Role Summary

As a Senior Software Engineer, you will play a key role in developing and maintaining the game’s Unity client across a large live-service mobile game codebase. You will contribute to high-impact initiatives spanning gameplay systems, live-ops features, client infrastructure, performance, build reliability, platform integrations, and maintainability. This role is ideal for an engineer who combines strong technical execution with sound architectural judgment in a complex production environment.

Что предстоит делать

  • Design, build, and maintain major client systems across gameplay, UI, live events, progression, offers, social features, and platform services.
  • Contribute to the architecture of a large Unity/C# codebase with an emphasis on modularity, testability, performance, and long-term maintainability.
  • Implement and support data-driven and live-ops systems, including event configuration, feature flags, localization, and content delivered through structured data pipelines such as FlatBuffers.
  • Partner closely with design, product, QA, analytics, economy, backend, and live-ops stakeholders to deliver scalable client solutions aligned with roadmap and operational needs.
  • Improve engineering effectiveness through strong coding practices, tooling, CI/build processes, code reviews, debugging workflows, and technical documentation.
  • Investigate and resolve production issues across mobile client systems, platform integrations, monetization flows, and live event features.
  • Mentor junior and mid-level engineers, contribute to team technical decisions, and help raise the overall quality bar for the engineering organization.

Кого мы ищем

Experience & Technical Skills

  • 5+ years of professional software engineering experience, including experience on production game clients or similarly complex interactive applications.
  • Strong expertise in C# and object-oriented software design.
  • Solid experience with Unity in production environments, ideally on mobile live-service titles.
  • Experience building and maintaining client systems for gameplay, UI, event systems, or data-driven feature development.
  • Experience with mobile platform considerations for iOS and Android, including integrations, performance tuning, memory management, and release readiness.
  • Understanding of client-side data pipelines, remote content/configuration, serialization, and schema-driven systems.
  • Experience working with build automation, CI/CD workflows, and release pipelines for mobile games.
  • Strong debugging and profiling skills across runtime behavior, performance, memory, and production stability.
  • Familiarity with testing strategies and quality practices in medium to large codebases.

Cross-Functional and Game-Specific Knowledge

  • Familiarity with live-service game development, including progression systems, offers/monetization, event systems, social features, and analytics.
  • Experience partnering with backend and live-ops teams to support content updates and feature rollouts.
  • Ability to make thoughtful technical tradeoffs in a content-heavy, operationally active game environment.
  • Comfort working across teams and contributing to initiatives with multiple dependencies.

Soft Skills & Education

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills.
  • Demonstrated mentorship and knowledge-sharing ability.
  • High ownership, sound judgment, and a practical approach to solving complex technical problems.
  • Passion for games and for building stable, maintainable systems that support great player experiences.
