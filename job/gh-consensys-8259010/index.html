Что предстоит делать

This role is specifically within the Predict team, which is focused on integrating Prediction Markets directly into the MetaMask platform. As a Senior Software Engineer on this team, you will be instrumental in building the core infrastructure and user-facing features that enable millions of users to engage with prediction markets securely and intuitively within their wallet.

  • Designing and implementing the front-end components and user experience for prediction market interactions across MetaMask clients.
  • Developing robust and scalable backend services and APIs to fetch real-time market data, manage user positions, and facilitate transaction signing.
  • Ensuring seamless and secure integration with on-chain prediction market protocols.
  • Optimizing the performance and security of all Predict features, handling real-time data feeds and high-volume transaction throughput.
