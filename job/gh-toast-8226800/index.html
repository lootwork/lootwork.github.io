Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy.

О роли

Toast IQ Grow is an all-in-one growth and lifecycle solution designed to help restaurants attract more guests, keep them coming back, and increase revenue without adding extra work for operators. Delivering this value involves a complex mix of product, systems, operations and more.

The IQ Grow Flex team owns the top of the Toast IQ Grow funnel - everything from sale close to first campaign. We're building the AI-powered context, onboarding and activation layer that compresses time-to-value, captures restaurant context and removes human bottlenecks from the path to go-live.

This is a growth PM role. You'll start deep in onboarding, but the mandate is broader: find the leverage points across Guest products where product investment can drive direct business impact at scale.

A day in the life (Responsibilities)

  • Drive business growth, however you see fit: the mandate is outcome-oriented, but the tactics are up to the PM. Come in, identify and pursue the next high-impact bets.
  • Own restaurant context: Build tools that help Marketing Managers and AI Agents capture, synthesize, and act on restaurant context faster.
  • Drive onboarding efficiency: Reduce human dependency and compress time-to-live through product.
  • Drive activation and product value: Own the metrics that matter: time-to-first-campaign, feature adoption rates, and early retention signals. Identify what drives sustained usage, instrument the funnel, and partner with internal teams to experiment.

What you'll work with

  • A fast-moving team with a flexible growth mindset
  • Cross-functional enthusiasm: support and collaboration from a variety of stakeholders
  • AI-native product development: agents, LLMs, structured knowledge stores
  • High visibility and impact: IQ Grow is the fastest growing product in the history of Toast

What you'll need to thrive (Requirements)

  • 3+ years in product management, ideally with experience in a growth environment
  • Comfort with ambiguity: this is a new team with a big mandate and room to shape direction
  • Strong cross-functional instincts: you'll work across eng, design, ops, onboarding and multiple product teams
  • Interest in AI/agentic products: you don't need to be an expert, but you should be curious
  • Bias to ship: we move fast and learn from live products
