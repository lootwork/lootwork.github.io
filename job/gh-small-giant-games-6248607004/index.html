Кто мы

Small Giant Games is one of the top-performing mobile gaming companies in the world. We are proudly part of the Zynga & Take-Two Interactive family.

We believe small, skilled, and independent teams can accomplish gigantic things. We know the best teams are created by hiring the best individuals and by trusting these individuals with ownership and responsibility for the work they do. Transparency, low hierarchy, and a good work-life balance are not just buzzwords for us but critical parts of our culture. We focus on the essentials while constantly looking to learn more and improve our products, our processes, and how we work together as a company.

This role is a 6 Month Fixed Term Contract

You will join the Creative team as a Junior Marketing Artist with a strong focus on AI, helping us produce creatives faster and contributing to the growth of Empires & Puzzles.

Обязанности

·Produce UA videos, playables and static ads, using AI tools as a core part of your daily work.

·Help build and improve AI workflows for the creative team, together with the senior artists.

·Look for new AI tools, workflows and solutions that can speed up our creative production, and share them with the team.

·Support the motion graphics and video artists with AI-generated assets, variations and quick iterations.

·Play Empires & Puzzles and other mobile games, and follow market and ad creative trends to bring fresh ideas into our creatives.

·Learn from performance data and team feedback to keep improving your creatives.

Требования

·Very AI-savvy, with hands-on experience in generative AI tools for image, video or animation.

·Working knowledge of Photoshop and After Effects.

·Quick learner who can pick up new tools fast and is curious about emerging tech.

·Passionate gamer who plays Empires & Puzzles (or is willing to dive in) and keeps up with other mobile games.

·Follows mobile gaming and ad creative trends closely, with a basic understanding of the market.

·Strong, confident communication, and an energetic, proactive attitude.

Будет плюсом

·A portfolio showing AI-assisted creative work.
