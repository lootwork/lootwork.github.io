Кто мы

Founded in 2023, Small Axe Studios is 2K’s newest stand-alone studio, built to push the boundaries of game development and bring 2K’s next groundbreaking sports franchise to life. Headquartered in Burnaby, we are assembling a world-class team of visionaries, builders, and passionate gamers who thrive on creative collaboration, technical excellence, and bold ideas. At Small Axe Studios, we believe big things start with sharp focus—and we’re crafting an exceptional, player-first experience from the ground up. If you’re ready to tackle unique challenges, leave your mark on a brand-new IP, and help shape the future of interactive sports entertainment, we want you on our team!

Что нам нужно

Our studio is looking for an experienced Gameplay Animator who has a high quality bar and a passion for replicating authentic sports behaviors. In this role, you will work closely with AI and Animation Engineers, Designers and Producers to implement authentic sports behaviors to build highly polished world – class gameplay for what will be the next massive sports franchise in our industry!

Что предстоит делать

  • Work closely with the gameplay Animation Lead to ensure animation is on-brand, on-time and of professional quality.
  • Collaborate with Designers to support the design for animation-based features
  • Provide animation from the ideation stage through to final implementation of features.
  • Be an organized individual and support production by staying on top of mocap shot lists and mocap reference.
  • Deliver appropriate animations that support the strongest feature possible
  • Collaborate with Engineers and Designers to influence the technical implementation of features
  • Collaborate with the Animation Director to understand, and deliver on, the quality bar for the animations that you create
  • Collaborate with the Design Lead and Engineering team to implement your features in engine
  • Champion animation quality and authenticity within your features, bringing the animation perspective to all decision making

Кто нам подойдёт

  • A passion, and understanding of sports games
  • Experience developing and shipping AAA games on Unreal Engine 4 or 5
  • Experience working with on ‘second-to-second’ interactive animation features
  • Player experience focused
  • Open-minded, organized, proactive and collaborative
  • Excellent understanding of human mechanics – specific to sports behaviors

Будет плюсом

  • Passionate about playing and/or watching sports.
  • Passion for video games (not just ours)
