О AIRWALLEX

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

As Enterprise Risk Manager - UAE, you’ll help establish and evolve Airwallex’s enterprise risk management capability in the UAE while contributing to the broader global and regional risk agenda. You’ll operationalise Airwallex’s global enterprise risk architecture locally, covering risk appetite, taxonomy, governance standards, risk limits, escalation protocols, reporting and decision rights. You’ll partner with Regulatory Compliance, Legal, UAE leadership, Product, Engineering and Operations to support responsible growth as Airwallex operationalises its UAE entity and expands its regulated payments offering. You’ll translate risk data, incidents, issues, trends and key risk indicators into clear reporting for UAE management, regional leadership, risk committees and, where appropriate, the Board or regulators, while providing independent second-line challenge on initiatives, products, partnerships and material operating-model changes.

- Own the local implementation of Airwallex’s global enterprise risk architecture, including risk appetite, risk taxonomy, governance standards, risk limits, escalation protocols, reporting and decision rights.

- Partner with Regulatory Compliance, Legal and UAE leadership to align risk governance with applicable Central Bank of the UAE expectations and requirements relevant to Stored Value Facilities and Retail Payment Services.

- Deliver clear, decision-useful reporting by translating risk data, incidents, issues, trends and key risk indicators for UAE management, regional leadership, risk committees and, where appropriate, the Board or regulators.

- Provide independent second-line challenge on initiatives that may exceed approved risk appetite, regulatory expectations or the maturity of the control environment.

- Support risk assessment and approval for new products, payment services, customer propositions, markets, partnerships, systems changes and material operating-model changes in the UAE.

- Strengthen the UAE crisis management and resilience framework, including escalation triggers, governance, executive response mechanisms, business continuity planning and severe-but-plausible scenario exercises.

- Improve enterprise-wide risk reporting, dashboards, risk tools, automation and data quality while sharing UAE insights with the global risk team.

- Build strong relationships with local and global stakeholders and support consistent risk management standards across Airwallex’s regulated entities.

You’ll work from our Abu Dhabi office.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Business, Law, STEM, or another relevant quantitative field.

- 5 to 7+ years of experience in risk management, operational risk, enterprise risk, compliance, controls, or audit within financial services, fintech, payments, banking, or another highly regulated environment.

- Experience with defence frameworks, including risk appetite statements, risk taxonomies, governance processes, and escalation mechanisms.

- Familiarity with the UAE financial services regulatory environment, including practical exposure to the Central Bank of the UAE and requirements relevant to electronic money, Stored Value Facilities or Retail Payment Services.

- Demonstrated experience preparing risk papers and communicating effectively with executives, Board or committee audiences, regulators, or auditors, with excellent written and verbal communication skills.

- A high-ownership, pragmatic approach, with the ability to operate effectively in a fast-paced environment with evolving priorities and significant cross-functional collaboration.

- Experience adapting and operationalising enterprise risk frameworks across regulated entities or jurisdictions.

- Experience supporting crisis management, operational resilience, business continuity planning or severe-but-plausible scenario exercises.

- Strong stakeholder management skills, with the ability to provide constructive challenge and influence decisions across functions.

Preferred

- Fintech and payments experience, including exposure to cross-border payments, merchant acquiring, card scheme environments, local payment methods, or multi-entity regulated groups.

- Experience managing risk approvals and governance in a high-velocity product and engineering environment, where new features, products, markets, or operating models are launched frequently.

- Strong data literacy and comfort using dashboards, metrics, and technology-enabled tools to support risk oversight and reporting.

- A clear bias toward automation, AI, and scalable tooling to improve issue management, reporting, and second line efficiency, paired with the judgment to balance innovation, business agility, and control effectiveness.…
