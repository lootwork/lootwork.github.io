Job Responsibilities

  • Based on risk control operations needs, support risk monitoring, user behavior analysis, campaign governance, and case reviews for various marketing activities.
  • Conduct in-depth analysis combining internal and external data to provide valuable analytical insights supporting operational decisions, strategy iteration, and model optimization.
  • Continuously monitor data, define risk indicators, optimize workflows, and establish a standardized risk operations framework to improve operational efficiency.
  • Maintain strong relationships with marketing, operations, customer service, and other teams to ensure smooth execution of marketing activities and risk control operations strategies.

Job Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree or above; 2+ years of experience in data analysis and data operations, with relevant work experience in any of the following areas: business risk control, marketing anti-fraud, account security, or fund security.
  • Proficient in SQL with the ability to conduct data analysis independently; experience with R or Python is preferred.
  • Excellent business and risk insight capabilities; strong logical thinking and data sensitivity. Candidates should be able to identify risks from abnormal data and summarize the root causes.
  • Fluent English speaking ability is preferred.
