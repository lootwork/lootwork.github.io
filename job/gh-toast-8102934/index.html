Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy. We're looking for a Senior Product Designer to join our Web Experience Design team and help shape the next generation of AI-powered experiences on Toast's marketing website — from intelligent chatbots to personalized visitor journeys.

As a Senior Product Designer on our Web Experience Design team, reporting to the Senior Manager, Web Experience Design, you'll design customer-facing AI features that help prospects find the right information faster, get their questions answered, and feel a more tailored connection to Toast throughout their journey. You'll partner closely with Web Strategy, Web Engineering, Marketing, and our AI/ML partners to bring these experiences to life.

A day in the life (Responsibilities)

  • Design and iterate on AI-powered features embedded in Toast's website, including chatbots, conversational interfaces, and dynamic personalization experiences.
  • Translate complex AI capabilities into intuitive, human-centered interactions that feel natural and build trust with prospects.
  • Partner with Engineering and AI/ML teams to understand model capabilities and constraints, and design within them.
  • Design personalization logic and experiences that adapt content, messaging, or layout based on visitor behavior, segment, or intent.
  • Prototype and test AI-driven concepts, using data and user feedback to refine interaction patterns pre- and post-launch.
  • Collaborate with Web Strategy and Marketing stakeholders to ensure AI experiences align with campaign goals, brand voice, and business priorities.
  • Contribute to and help evolve design patterns and guidelines for AI-enabled experiences across the web ecosystem.
  • Clearly communicate design rationale, prototypes, and outcomes to your manager and cross-functional partners.

What you'll need to thrive (Requirements)

  • 4+ years of relevant product or web design experience, ideally within a B2B SaaS environment.
  • Exposure to designing for or with AI-powered features (chatbots, recommendation systems, generative content, personalization engines, etc.) — or strong enthusiasm and aptitude to grow into it.
  • Solid foundation in UI/UX principles, including interaction design, information architecture, and accessibility.
  • Comfort designing within real-world technical constraints and working closely with engineers to bring ideas to life.
  • A portfolio that shows thoughtful, user-centered problem solving — bonus points for conversational, adaptive, or AI-enabled interfaces.
  • Strong communication skills and the ability to clearly present design decisions to a range of stakeholders.
  • Ability to work through ambiguity, take feedback well, and iterate quickly.

Nice to have's

  • Are genuinely curious about AI and excited to explore what's possible in this space.
  • Like solving real human problems with emerging technology, not just chasing what's trendy.
  • Are comfortable taking a project from early exploration through to polished, shipped execution.
  • Enjoy close collaboration with engineers, PMs, and marketers to bring ideas to life.
  • Take ownership proactively, while welcoming direction on newer or more ambiguous projects.
  • Are deeply passionate about Toast's mission and excited to contribute to its growth.
