О Payoneer

Founded in 2005, Payoneer is the global financial platform that removes friction from doing business across borders, with a mission to connect the world’s underserved businesses to a rising global economy. We’re a community with over 2,500 colleagues all over the world, working to serve customers, and partners in over 190 countries and territories.

By taking the complexity out of the financial workflows–including everything from global payments and compliance to multi-currency and workforce management, to providing working capital and business intelligence–we give businesses the tools they need to work efficiently worldwide and grow with confidence.

We are seeking an Ops Data Analyst to join Payoneer Operations in Greater China. Working within an operations domain such as KYC or Customer Care, you will deliver KPIs, dashboards, data agents and data-driven workflows that help our teams run operations more efficiently and improve customer experience and retention.

Shanghai | Full Time | On-site

Что предстоит делать

  • KPI Design & Business Analytics
  • Contribute to KPI design and maintain consistent definitions for domain metrics (e.g., volume, SLA, backlog, productivity, quality).
  • Use SQL and Python to analyze large datasets, validate data, explain variances and perform root-cause analysis.
  • Support weekly, monthly and quarterly business reviews with accurate insights and clear recommendations.
  • Dashboards & Data Visualization
  • Build and maintain Power BI dashboards for Operations leaders and frontline teams.
  • Support self-service analytics with reusable reports and data models.
  • Communicate findings clearly, verbally and in writing.
  • Data Agents & Analytics Automation
  • Build data agents and simple web apps with approved AI tools (e.g., natural-language query assistants, automated insight summaries) to automate recurring analysis.
  • Automate data extraction and recurring reporting to reduce manual work.
  • Data-Driven Operational Workflows
  • Work with Operations, GTM and Platform team to embed data into workflows such as case alerting, workload forecasting and exception monitoring.
  • Help build monitoring and quality checks to detect operational risks and process defects.
  • Partner with Data Engineering to resolve data quality issues and keep metric definitions consistent.

Кто вы

Must-haves

  • 3+ years of related experience with a bachelor’s degree in data or business analytics.
  • Strong SQL and working Python skills.
  • Hands-on experience with Power BI or similar BI tools and operational KPIs.
  • Experience automating data extraction and recurring reports.
  • Ability to turn business questions into clear, structured analysis.
  • Excellent written and verbal English for working with global, cross-functional stakeholders.
  • Self-starter with strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

Nice-to-haves

  • Experience building data agents or LLM-powered analytics tools.
  • Knowledge of forecasting, A/B testing or other statistical techniques.
  • Experience in fintech or payments.
