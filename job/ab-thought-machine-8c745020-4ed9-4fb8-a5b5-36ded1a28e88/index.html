О команде

The Risk & Compliance team sits within Finance and is responsible for enterprise risk management, the internal controls framework, regulatory compliance, external certifications (including SOC 2, ISO 27001, ISO 22301 and PCI DSS), client audits and third-party risk management and procurement. We are a small, high-impact team that works across the whole business, from Sales and Engineering to Security, Legal and the Board.

О роли

The Supplier & Third Party Risk Manager will take end-to-end ownership of our third-party vendor ecosystem. Using our procurement platform (Omnea), you will lead commercial negotiations, proactively manage contract renewals, and establish a robust Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) programme to safeguard the company against commercial, operational, regulatory and security risk. This is a builder role: we have the tooling and the onboarding process in place, and we are looking for someone to turn it into a mature procurement and supplier management capability that delivers measurable cost savings and better outcomes from our suppliers.

Что предстоит делать

Establish and lead the TPRM programme

- Expand and maintain the enterprise-wide Third-Party Risk Management policy and framework.

- Partner closely with InfoSec, Legal, Compliance and the Data Protection Officer to build standardised due diligence workflows, and maintain an active third-party risk register.

- Ensure our supplier oversight meets the expectations of our regulated banking clients and supports our external certifications and client audits.

Proactive renewals management

- Take full ownership of the global contract renewal calendar.

- Partner with budget holders and FP&A well in advance of notice periods to assess supplier performance, review actual usage and ROI, and renegotiate terms or terminate contracts to eliminate unwanted auto-renewals.

Strategic sourcing and negotiation

- Support commercial negotiations for software, professional services, contractors and corporate overheads to optimise commercial terms and Total Cost of Ownership (TCO).

- Identify consolidation opportunities across enterprise-wide software spend and track savings delivered.

System ownership and process automation

- Act as the primary business owner for Omnea.

- Use the platform to automate intake requests, route conditional security risk assessments, track contract approvals, and maintain a centralised repository of active suppliers.

Vendor governance and performance

- Design and implement a structured vendor governance framework.

- Segment the supplier portfolio by criticality and establish ongoing monitoring (SLAs / KPIs) for high-risk and critical vendors.

- Produce regular reporting on supplier performance, risk and spend for senior management.

Business partnership

- Act as a commercial partner and advisor to department leaders across the business to support their purchasing needs, helping them navigate onboarding smoothly.

Кого мы ищем

Essential

- 3–6 years' experience in Procurement, Sourcing or Vendor Risk Management, ideally within a high-growth scaleup, regulated fintech or fast-paced financial services environment.

- Practical experience running third-party risk assessments, with an understanding of fundamental security and data privacy standards (e.g. ISO 27001, SOC 2 and GDPR).

- A track record of managing contract lifecycles, identifying consolidation opportunities, and using usage data to negotiate better renewal terms.

- Good negotiation skills with a track record of delivering cost savings and driving value from suppliers.

- A proactive, builder mindset, with the ability to design processes and establish strong internal relationships.

Desirable

- Experience with modern spend management and procurement orchestration platforms (direct experience with Omnea or similar tools is highly advantageous).

- Familiarity with regulatory expectations on outsourcing and third-party risk in financial services (e.g. DORA, EBA outsourcing guidelines, PRA SS2/21).

- A relevant qualification or progress towards one (e.g. CIPS).
