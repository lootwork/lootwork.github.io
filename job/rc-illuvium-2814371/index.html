О Illuvium

Illuvium is building an interconnected universe of blockchain-enabled games, combining high-quality game development, digital ownership and a global player community. We are looking for a hands-on Growth Marketing Specialist to help grow the Illuvium ecosystem across our games, products and community.

О роли

This is a broad, execution-focused individual contributor role for someone who can develop marketing campaigns and personally deliver the work.

You will run paid user-acquisition campaigns, support Illuvium’s social channels, deliver email marketing and coordinate selected influencer relationships. You will help us attract new players, re-engage existing community members and improve the performance of our marketing activity.

You should be comfortable moving between creative work, campaign execution and performance analysis. We are also looking for someone who actively uses modern AI tools to research audiences, develop campaign concepts, create and adapt content, and improve the speed and quality of their work.

Обязанности

  • Plan, launch and optimise paid user-acquisition and direct-response campaigns across relevant advertising platforms.
  • Manage campaign budgets, audience targeting and creative testing while monitoring acquisition, conversion and engagement metrics.
  • Plan and deliver social content, campaign copy, community announcements and email campaigns for launches and player re-engagement.
  • Coordinate influencer activity and collaborate with product, design, game development and community teams on campaigns and launches.
  • Use AI tools for market research, campaign ideation, content creation, audience analysis and workflow automation.
  • Report on campaign performance, share actionable insights and identify new growth opportunities.
