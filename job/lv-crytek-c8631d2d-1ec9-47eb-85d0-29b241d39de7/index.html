As Community Manager for Hunt: Showdown, you are the daily voice of the game in its community, and the voice of the community inside Crytek. You report to the Global Community Manager.

You run the channels, plan the content, keep conversation healthy, and make sure players feel heard. When something is going wrong, you are usually the first to know, and the first to say so internally.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own day-to-day community management for Hunt: Showdown across all community channels
  • Plan and deliver community content in line with the campaign calendar
  • Moderate and maintain a healthy community environment
  • Surface player sentiment, issues and trends to the Global Community Manager and development team
  • Support live beats, updates and launches with community activity
  • Partner with Creator Partnerships and Social on integrated content
  • Represent the community's perspective internally

Что вы приносите

  • 3+ years of experience in a community management role on a live game
  • Strong public writing skills and sound judgment on tone and messaging
  • Experience in community moderation, including handling sensitive and challenging situations
  • Genuine empathy for players and deep product knowledge
  • Ability to work effectively with development teams on player issues and feedback
  • Excellent written communication in English
  • Willingness to relocate to Frankfurt am Main, Germany
