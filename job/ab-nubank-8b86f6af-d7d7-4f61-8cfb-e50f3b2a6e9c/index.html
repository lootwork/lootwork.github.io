О роли

We’re looking for a senior Product Manager to own Nu Global’s growth agenda end to end. This is a dedicated Growth role: you will diagnose the funnel, identify the highest-impact constraints, and run a portfolio of growth programs that turns customer and market insight into measurable, sustained growth.

You are a relentless funnel optimizer—equally comfortable getting into the data, shaping the growth thesis, and driving execution with Product, Engineering, Design, Analytics, Marketing, Operations, Risk, and Legal. You will build a disciplined, high-velocity experimentation system and scale the interventions that work across markets. The goal is not to maximize sign-ups in isolation, but to acquire the right customers, help them reach value quickly, and grow engagement and advocacy while protecting customer trust.

Основные обязанности

- Growth strategy and portfolio: Translate Nu Global’s business goals into a clear growth strategy, measurable outcomes, and a prioritized roadmap, making trade-offs explicit.

- End-to-end funnel performance: Own the product growth funnel from acquisition and channel handoff through onboarding, verification, activation, first value, referral, and retention. Use data and customer insight to find and remove friction.

- Growth programs: Build and run a portfolio of coordinated growth programs, including coordination with other Nu markets (Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, US), from problem framing to scale.

- Experimentation at pace: Establish a fast, rigorous test-and-learn cadence: shape hypotheses, align on success and guardrail metrics, and decide whether to scale, iterate, or stop.

- Growth loops and journeys: Develop product-led growth mechanisms such as onboarding, referral, lifecycle journeys, and reactivation that can compound across markets.

- Analytics and measurement: Partner with Business Analytics on funnel instrumentation, cohort analysis, and experiment evaluation, making sound calls when attribution is imperfect.

- Cross-functional delivery and responsible growth: Lead through influence across functions and country teams, building growth that is scalable, compliant, and worthy of customer trust.

Требования

- Experience: 8–12 years of product management experience (Senior Manager level), ideally mostly in Growth.

- Global Expansion: Prior experience with Global Growth Brands such as Uber, Airbnb, Revolut, Wise, PayPal, King, ByteDance, Temu, Shein, Lovable, Duolingo, Open AI is a strong plus.

- Growth outcomes: Strong record of diagnosing funnel performance and delivering material improvements across acquisition, onboarding, activation, conversion, referral, or retention.

- Growth program execution: Experience leading growth programs end to end, from opportunity sizing and hypothesis through experimentation, launch, and scale.

- Analytical rigor: Ability to interrogate funnels and cohorts, define success and guardrail metrics, and evaluate experiments, even with imperfect attribution.

- AI fluency: Practical experience using AI to accelerate growth discovery, experimentation, or operations, with sound judgment on customer value, privacy, and risk.

- Leadership and execution: Hands-on, high-ownership style, able to move quickly from insight to action and align senior cross-functional partners.

- Languages: Fluency in English is mandatory. Portuguese and/or Spanish is a strong advantage. Nice to have: French, German, or Italian.
