О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

You’ll build and lead Airwallex’s Sales Development function in Bangalore, owning the outbound pipeline engine for the SME & Growth business.

- You’ll hire, coach and develop a high-performing SDR team, set clear performance expectations and build repeatable processes that generate high-quality pipeline at scale.

- You’ll partner closely with Marketing, Account Executives, Revenue Operations and People & Talent to improve targeting, qualification, Salesforce handovers, funnel conversion and pipeline health.

- You’ll scale an AI-assisted outbound engine, using prospecting technology and workflow automation to improve lead sourcing, account research, personalisation and sequence testing while maintaining appropriate quality and compliance guardrails.

- You’ll contribute to regional go-to-market strategy by refining value propositions, pitch narratives and local-market outreach.

This is an in-office role — you’ll be in our Bangalore office five days a week.

Кого мы ищем

ESSENTIALS

- Experience leading an outbound SDR or sales development team in a high-growth technology company.

- A track record of hiring and developing early-career sales talent and building repeatable processes that deliver against ambitious targets.

- Strong cross-functional partnership skills across Marketing, Sales, Revenue Operations and People & Talent.

- Experience with Salesforce or another modern CRM and sales engagement tools, plus a track record of implementing AI-enabled prospecting or workflow automation to improve personalisation and SDR efficiency.

- Comfort operating in a metrics-driven environment, owning targets including opportunity creation, pipeline value, conversion, activity levels and reporting.

- Excellent written, numerical and verbal communication skills, with the ability to simplify complexity and coach effectively.

- Strategic thinking, pragmatic execution and a bias toward action, continuous improvement and ownership.

Желательно

- Familiarity with payments, FX, cards, treasury, spend management or complex B2B SaaS.

- Experience contributing to regional go-to-market strategy, including value propositions, pitch narratives and local-market outreach.

YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF

- You have a track record of hitting your numbers, and you’re motivated by stretch goals.

- You own the outcome and you don't wait for permission to fix what's broken.

- You're consistently looking to take on new and diverse challenges, expand your knowledge beyond your current areas of expertise, and are comfortable stepping outside your comfort zone to do it.

- You say what you mean. You're direct, but you lead with respect.

LEARN MORE ABOUT YOUR TEAM

SME & Growth at Airwallex is about more than closing deals. We help small and mid-market businesses solve real financial challenges across borders: getting paid globally, managing multi-currency accounts, paying suppliers, and running spend across markets from a single platform. You're not selling a feature. You're helping founders and finance leaders replace fragmented financial operations with one global system that actually scales.

It's a function for sellers who like to build, not follow a script: curious, resourceful, and comfortable figuring things out in markets that are still wide open. You get real autonomy to create value, build relationships, and move opportunities forward.

If you want to sell a serious product, solve real problems for ambitious businesses, and build your career fast, this is a place to do it.
