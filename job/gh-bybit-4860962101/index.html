О вакансии

  • Act as the Manager in Charge (MIC) of Compliance and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) for a Hong Kong entity, overseeing the VASP license application with the SFC and ongoing compliance framework.
  • Act as the primary point of contact for regulators and supervisory authorities in Hong Kong, leading regulatory communications, examinations, inspections, and ongoing supervisory engagement.
  • Design, build, and maintain a comprehensive compliance framework covering AML, CTF, sanctions, KYC/KYB, transaction monitoring and regulatory reporting.
  • Own the end to end compliance program lifecycle, including policies, procedures, controls, risk assessments, monitoring, testing, and issue remediation.
  • Partner closely with Business, Product, Risk and Legal teams to embed compliance by design into business processes.
  • Ensure effective governance, documentation, and audit readiness across all compliance activities.
  • Build, mentor, and lead a high performing global compliance team, setting clear accountability and performance standards.
  • Monitor global regulatory developments, assess impact, and proactively adapt to the compliance program.

Требования и навыки

  • Bachelor’s degree in Law, Finance, Business Administration, or a related discipline from Singapore, Hong Kong, Malaysia, UK, or US.
  • 6-10 years of compliance experience in a bank, financial institution, fintech, or crypto firm.
  • Prior experience acting as the Manager in Charge (MIC) of Compliance and AML in Hong Kong licensed institutions will be an advantage.
  • Extensive experience interacting directly with relevant regulatory agencies in Hong Kong.
  • Exposure to regulatory governance, licensing, or compliance monitoring within a regulated financial institution.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to interpret regulations and apply them pragmatically to new products and business models.
  • Comfortable working in dynamic and ambiguous environments, with the flexibility to adapt regulatory frameworks as the business and regulatory landscape evolve.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder-management skills, with the ability to engage effectively with regulators, senior management, and cross-functional teams.
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and deliver high-quality work under tight timelines.
  • Excellent command of written and spoken English and Chinese.
  • Strong team player, good work attitude, takes ownership and has pride in deliverables
