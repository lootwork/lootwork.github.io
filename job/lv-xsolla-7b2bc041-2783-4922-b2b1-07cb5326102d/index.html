О роли

We are hiring a Staff Software Engineer to own the smart wallet and account abstraction layer of Xsolla ZK, Xsolla's Web3 platform on a zkSync-based L2. This is an individual contributor role with a large technical scope. You are the technical anchor for how millions of players get an on-chain account they never have to think about.

The product. Every player who signs in with Xsolla ID receives a deterministic, non-custodial smart account. The account is derived from their identity claim, is identical on every device, and its private keys never leave the client. It is not a separate wallet screen: it is the player's inventory. It backs:

  • minting and gifting;
  • trading on our Bazaar marketplace;
  • Merkle-based reward airdrops;
  • gas sponsorship;
  • an in-app dApp browser that exposes the same signer to third-party applications through an EIP-1193 provider.

Обязанности

  • The technical strategy and architecture of the smart wallet platform: account model, signing, session management and gas abstraction at scale
  • The smart account and permissioning model, evolved to meet both product and compliance needs
  • The security model where our signer meets third-party applications: what a player is actually approving, and proof that the UI shows it
  • The custody and recovery roadmap, from today's interim mechanisms (including raw key export) to MPC-based custody
  • Cross-team technical decisions on account architecture, SDK contracts, data modeling and platform reliability, with buy-in from the wallet, marketplace, SDK, mobile and security teams
  • Engineering standards in the Web3 domain
  • Production escalations in the wallet and account abstraction domain

Что предстоит делать

  • Design and evolve the ERC-4337 user operation lifecycle (prepare → sign → submit → confirm), EIP-7702 delegation, deterministic address derivation from identity, and the session-key and permission primitives (including origin-bound Smart Sessions) that let players act without a prompt on every transaction
  • Set the paymaster and gas-sponsorship strategy: what we sponsor, how we meter and cap it, how we defend it from abuse, and how it degrades when sponsorship is unavailable
  • Define EIP-1193 / EIP-6963 provider behavior, origin-bound and policy-versioned sessions, trust tiers, and the transaction and signature confirmation flow
  • Design recovery paths (passkeys, guardians, social recovery) that a non-crypto-native player can complete without losing assets
  • Identify systemic risks (key management, signature phishing, reorgs and indexer staleness, bundler and RPC dependencies, contract upgrade paths) and lead initiatives that resolve them before they become incidents
  • Write RFCs and design docs; review critical code, contracts and designs
  • Work with product, security, legal and infra teams to align on the roadmap and turn business and regulatory needs into well-scoped technical plans
  • Create leverage for the engineering org through documentation, tooling and mentorship
  • Serve as the escalation point for complex production issues: a stuck user operation, a drained paymaster, a signing prompt a player didn't understand
  • Set up and maintain AI tooling in the repositories (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor: rules, context, harness) so the team uses it effectively

Обязательно

Experience

  • 8+ years of engineering in the Ethereum ecosystem working with crypto wallets and smart wallets
  • Designing or operating a production smart wallet or account abstraction system serving real users and real value
  • Shipping and operating systems where a bug meant lost funds. You can talk concretely about the guardrails, reviews and rollback paths you put in place

Account abstraction and smart wallets

  • ERC-4337 in depth: EntryPoint (v0.6 through v0.8), the UserOperation lifecycle, bundler and paymaster roles, validation rules (ERC-7562), gas estimation and its failure modes, counterfactual deployment, signature validation for undeployed accounts (ERC-6492)
  • EIP-7702 and the post-Pectra landscape: EOA delegation, its interaction with EntryPoint v0.8, and the risks it introduces (residual key authority, delegation phishing, storage collisions)
  • Building on or extending production smart account implementations; modular account standards (ERC-7579 / ERC-6900)
  • Session keys and delegated permissions: scoped, time-limited, policy-bound authority; TTLs and contract allowlists, origin binding, permission revocation; ERC-7710 / ERC-7715
  • Signature schemes and wallet interfaces: EIP-712 typed data, ERC-1271 contract signatures, WebAuthn / passkey (P-256) signers; EIP-1193, EIP-6963, EIP-5792 batched calls, ERC-7677 paymaster interfaces

Smart contracts and EVM

  • Solidity and the EVM: reading, reviewing and writing account, paymaster and module contracts; gas-aware design; upgradeability and migration patterns; working effectively with external auditors
  • L2 specifics and their effect on wallet design: native account abstraction on zkSync-family chains, transaction and fee models, finality and reorg behavior, RPC and indexer semantics
