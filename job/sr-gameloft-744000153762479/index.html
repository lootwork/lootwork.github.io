Company Description

О Gameloft

Here at Gameloft, we create moments of happiness for our players all over the world through exciting gaming experiences. This is our mission, and it has been the same for over 25 years. We are the proud creators of Asphalt, Dungeon Hunter, Modern Combat, and over 200 other great games.

There is a game for everyone, and we are now expanding to cross-platform games on PC and consoles, with titles such as Disney Speedstorm and Disney Dreamlight Valley, to provide our players with even more amazing adventures.

With more than 3,000 passionate Gamelofters across the globe, we take great pride in our differences and draw on them to create games just as diverse as we are.

If you’re looking for a new challenge and want to work with a bold and talented group of people, don’t hesitate to join the game!

Job Description

Обязанности

  • Collaborate with the studio stakeholders and game team leads to define and execute the game vision as a whole.
  • Direct the tone, gameplay, and meta structure of the game, focusing on innovation and a thriving AA Experience.
  • Lead the design team to ensure a cohesive experience across the game.
  • Coordinate with level design teams to ensure synergies between mechanics and progression.
  • Anticipate and mitigate threats to quality and innovation.
  • Build a collaborative relationship with the other departments, including Art, UI, Tech, Online, Data, Audio, Production and Human Resources.
  • Allocate and plan resources and roadmap in collaboration with managers and producers.
  • Participate in the recruitment process for game designers.

Требования

Требования

  • 6+ years of experience in game design, including lead or principal GD positions.
  • Released a minimum of two titles while serving as a lead, principal, or senior game designer.
  • Proven experience in 3Cs and gameplay systems design of PC/Console action genres (sports, racing, combat, shooters, open world, etc.)
  • Experience prototyping with Unity or Unreal.
  • Proficiency in core game design aspects like meta-game & progression systems, balancing, UI/UX, level design, and narrative.
  • Proficient communication and leadership skills.
  • Up-to-date knowledge of the gaming market and trends.
  • Skillfulness in retaining a defined creative strategy while being open to modifications.
  • Experience with standard industry tools like Jira, Confluence, Figma, etc.
  • Fluent in English

Bonus assets

  • Experience with racing and/or action games.
  • Experience with multiple platforms (PC, console, mobile)
  • Experience with both premium games and games as a service (either DLC, subscription or F2P based).
