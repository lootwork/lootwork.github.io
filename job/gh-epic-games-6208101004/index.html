QUALITY ASSURANCE

What We Do

The Quality Assurance team owns the testing process, risk management, and quality evaluation of all products and functions across Epic. Our team works closely with developers and production to ensure the best possible user experience for our community and customers.

Что предстоит делать

People within the QA Engineer career track work with product owners, developers, and QA management to own technical feature sets within our Fortnite Product. You will develop a test plan for certification and release testing of new features. You will develop and implement automation test coverage and utilize technical tooling to expand breadth and add depth to QA coverage of multiple features in a dynamic and fast-paced environment.

In addition, you will facilitate functional, qualitative, and performance testing efforts while using a mix of manual and technical testing skills to execute a variety of testing techniques across your product to analyze and report on quality.

You are expected to work in a highly autonomous environment while working closely with Release Managers, engineers, and production staff, using a combination of quality assurance, data analysis, and technical skills to drive appropriate testing techniques through this critical stage of release cycles and help push those improvements into future releases.

In this role, you will

  • Develop and implement automated test cases within a framework and assist in debugging issues for peers to ensure the team stays as productive as possible
  • Responsible for feature level test planning and test execution of individual releases, as well as conduct whitebox functional testing as well as technical testing including defect exploration and verifications using various OOP languages
  • Generate analysis of outstanding defect load and recommend new areas of testing and areas to prioritize for fixes, and proactively manage incoming defects and work autonomously on highest value tasks, be it test writing or test execution
  • Work with product owners, release managers, developers and QA management to define release acceptance criteria, identify risks, and plan testing coverage for upcoming features / releases, and examine technical gaps and automation initiatives between geographically distributed teams, vendors, and agencies to deliver the highest quality product
  • Use a mix of manual and technical testing skills to execute a variety of testing techniques across your product to analyze the quality of said product while troubleshooting reported defects and internal issues
  • Report and track for data quality, and use that data to drive conversations around product quality and development health, supporting developers before, during, and after deployments, as well as utilize telemetry and data to adapt test coverage dynamically in response to changing player behaviour in live service
  • Test communications between the client and backend services

Кого мы ищем

  • 3+ years experience programming with C++, Java or C# required
  • Ability to confidently collaborate with senior members on technical topics and features
  • Ability to assess scope and requirements and help drive release milestones
  • Must have a strong background in reporting and tracking for data quality
  • Software or games testing experience in a fast paced live service environment
  • Technical understanding of game engine or entertainment technologies, including automation, functional, qualitative, and performance against consistently iterative development, as well as previous experience working with data and/or analytics

This role is open to multiple locations across the US (including WA).
