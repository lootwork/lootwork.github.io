Join FxPro: a leading international fintech company. Be a part of our expanding international team, with offices in Limassol, London, Nassau, Dubai, and Yerevan. FxPro boasts a diverse workforce of over 600 employees representing 50 nationalities, making it an exciting and dynamic workplace. At FxPro, we see each team member as an integral part of our success story.

We are expanding our team due to exciting developments planned for the near future. This is a hands on role focused on individual contribution, strong execution, creativity, and day-to-day involvement within our growing in-house creative team.

We are looking for a talented designer who is passionate about creating high-quality visual work and collaborating closely with different teams across the business. The role is full-time and office based in Limassol, Cyprus.

Обязанности

  • Develop high-quality visual content across multiple platforms (web, print, digital, social media, presentations, etc.)
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams as part of the marketing team (product, UX/UI, and developers)
  • Contribute to concept development and visual storytelling
  • Maintain and evolve brand guidelines

Требования

  • 3+ years of relevant experience working as a Graphic, Visual, or Web Designer
  • Strong portfolio showcasing design work across a range of media
  • Proficiency in Figma, Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator), and experience with AI tools. 3D software is a plus
  • Familiarity with branding, web design, marketing design, and digital advertising formats
  • Industry-specific experience in fintech is a plus
  • Strong English communication skills, both written and verbal

Interview steps

  • Recruiter screen (~30 minutes)
  • Technical Interview (1 hour)
  • Test task
  • Final Interview with managers (1 hour)

Our benefits

  • Excellent compensation package
  • Medical insurance
  • Provident fund
  • In-house gym with a personal trainer
  • Free daily lunch catering, snacks, and beverages
  • Company discount card for various products & services
  • 21 days of annual leave and 10 days of sick leave annually
  • Shuttle bus service from Limassol
  • Birthday gift
  • Relocation bonus and visa/work permit support
