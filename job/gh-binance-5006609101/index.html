Binance is a leading global blockchain ecosystem behind the world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume and registered users. We are trusted by 300+ million people in 100+ countries for our industry-leading security, user fund transparency, trading engine speed, deep liquidity, and an unmatched portfolio of digital-asset products. About the Role: We are looking for a proactive, detail-oriented intern to join our affiliate product team as the bridge between business and product. You will support product delivery, business insights, and operational efficiency across our affiliate/referral programs, working closely with Product, BD, and Operations teams.

Что предстоит делать

Product Delivery Support

  • Assist in drafting and organizing Business Requirement Documents (BRDs), ensuring information is complete and clear
  • Support new product launch processes, including writing release announcements, coordinating cross-team communication, and preparing BD training materials
  • Perform initial triage of user feedback, collect logs and relevant information, and escalate valid issues to the product and engineering teams

Business Insights Support

  • Assist with competitor research and compile feature comparisons of affiliate/referral products
  • Coordinate KOL and BD interviews, document and summarize key feedback, and help shape product improvement recommendations

Operational Efficiency Support

  • Help review existing operational processes, identify standardization opportunities, and contribute to SOP documentation
  • Maintain the internal BD/ARM knowledge base, updating product feature guides and FAQs to enable team self-service

In a Nutshell: As the bridge between business and product, you will drive smoother iteration of our affiliate products and stronger cross-team collaboration through documentation, process follow-up, and information synthesis.

Требования

  • Current undergraduate or graduate student (any major; Business, Computer Science, or Marketing preferred)
  • Genuine curiosity about product operations, user growth, and affiliate/referral programs
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills in both Chinese and English
  • Detail-oriented and organized, with excellent documentation and information synthesis skills
  • Self-driven and proactive, comfortable working across teams
  • Knowledge of crypto/Web3 or affiliate marketing is a plus
  • Ability to commit to 5 days per week for 6 months is preferred
