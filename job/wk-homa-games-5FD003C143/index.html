The team

You join an 8-person puzzle studio in Istanbul, founded by Peak and Zynga alumni. It's backed by Homa's puzzle, data, art, and UA teams. Its new puzzle game is heading to global launch, and fresh levels are now its biggest growth lever.

The opportunity

You own the levels players see every week. You work every day with Homa's product director and the studio's level designers, artists, and engineers. You use a level editor and AI tools that generate layouts and estimate difficulty. AI makes you faster, and you stay the brain behind every level. You work remotely from Türkiye. The team is small, and the bar is high.

As the studio grows, you can own a family of mechanics or a pack of levels. You can also lead a group of level designers, or move toward a product role.

Ваши обязанности

  • Build. Your levels keep fresh content reaching players, every single week.
  • Tune. Your difficulty curve makes levels feel hard but fair, so players keep trying.
  • Introduce. New mechanics land smoothly because your levels teach them step by step.
  • Test. You play every level before release, so players only see levels that work.
  • Optimize. You use win rate, attempts and churn data to turn weak live levels into strong ones.
  • Shape. Your ideas on new mechanics and content push the game forward.
  • Accelerate. You use AI to generate layout variants, explore difficulty options and check solvability.

What we look for

  • Ownership. You drive outcomes. When something breaks, you fix it instead of explaining why it broke.
  • Proactive. You don't wait for instructions or perfect conditions. You spot what needs doing and do it.
  • Pragmatism. You look for the simplest solution that works, use whatever tools are available, and ship.
  • AI-driven. AI is already part of how you work every day. You've used it to automate, speed up, or rethink.
  • Sharp thinking. You learn fast, reason from facts, and structure your ideas clearly.
  • Motivated. You're ambitious and grounded. You see this opportunity as a chance to learn faster than anywhere else.

Требования

  • Experience. 1+ year in level design, game design or QA on mobile games, ideally puzzle games.
  • Languages. Fluent Turkish and English. You use both every day.
  • Volume with quality. You ship every week and never let quality slip.
  • Tool speed. You work fast in a level editor and pick up new tools quickly.
  • Data sense. You track level data and use it to tune your levels.
  • Puzzle instinct. You play puzzle games a lot and can explain why a level works.
  • AI native. You turn AI into output. You've built tools that generate layouts, check solvability or analyse level data.

Bonus points if you have

  • Shipped levels on a live mobile puzzle game.
  • Experience with Unity or a similar game engine.

Even if you don't check every box in our requirements, we encourage you to apply. We value diverse perspectives and backgrounds, and we're more interested in your potential and passion than a perfect match to our checklist.

Our process (2 weeks max)

  • Call with our recruiter (30 min). A generalized conversation, and to tell you more about the role.
  • Level design case study. We look at your thinking and your technical skills.
  • Technical interview with the studio's level designers to dive deeper into technicalities.
  • Final interview with the studio founder and Homa's Puzzle lead. Focused on motivation, values, and fit.
