Обязанности

  • Execute brand marketing initiatives in Japan to support brand awareness, campaign objectives, and local market presence.
  • Support the development and rollout of integrated marketing campaigns in alignment with business priorities and local market needs.
  • Manage Binance Japan’s social media operations across key platforms, including content scheduling, publishing, community monitoring, and performance tracking.
  • Create, adapt, and localize social media content that is aligned with brand guidelines and relevant to local audiences.
  • Monitor social media trends, audience behavior, and competitor activities, and provide practical recommendations to improve content performance and engagement.
  • Plan and execute online and offline events, including community events, brand activations, industry conferences, and partner-related initiatives.
  • Coordinate with internal stakeholders and external vendors to ensure timely and high-quality delivery of marketing campaigns and events.
  • Handle day-to-day event operations, including timeline management, logistics coordination, vendor communication, on-site support, and post-event follow-up.
  • Work closely with regional and global teams, as well as local business, PR, compliance, and product teams, to support the execution of marketing initiatives in Japan.
  • Track and report campaigns, social media, and event performance, and help identify areas for optimization and improvement.
  • Ensure marketing materials and activities are executed in accordance with brand standards, local requirements, and internal processes.
  • Contribute ideas and execution support for initiatives that enhance brand visibility, audience engagement, and community growth in the local market.
  • Operate effectively across a wide range of marketing activities, from large-scale brand campaigns and high-impact initiatives to detailed day-to-day execution and coordination.

Требования

  • 10+ years of marketing experience, with solid hands-on experience in brand marketing and campaign execution.
  • 7+ years of experience in brand marketing, with a strong understanding of brand communication, campaign planning, and local market execution.
  • 5+ years of experience managing social media operations, including content planning, publishing, community monitoring, and performance analysis.
  • 3+ years of experience planning and executing online and offline events, including logistics coordination, vendor management, and on-site support.
  • Proven ability to manage multiple marketing projects simultaneously and deliver high-quality execution in a fast-paced environment.
  • Comfortable working across both large-scale marketing initiatives and detailed day-to-day execution, with a hands-on and flexible approach.
  • Strong understanding of the Japan market, local consumer behavior, and culturally relevant communication.
  • Native level written and verbal communication skills in Japanese, with the ability to create clear, engaging, and locally relevant marketing content.
  • Business-level English skills to collaborate effectively with regional and global stakeholders.
  • Strong coordination and project management skills, with attention to detail and the ability to manage timelines and priorities effectively.
  • Comfortable working cross-functionally with internal teams such as business, PR, compliance, product, and regional marketing teams.
  • Data-aware mindset with the ability to track performance, interpret results, and support optimization of campaigns and content.
  • Self-motivated, hands-on, and execution-focused, with a practical approach to problem-solving.
