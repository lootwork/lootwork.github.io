Binance is a leading global blockchain ecosystem behind the world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume and registered users. We are trusted by 300+ million people in 100+ countries for our industry-leading security, user fund transparency, trading engine speed, deep liquidity, and an unmatched portfolio of digital-asset products. Binance offerings range from trading and finance to education, research, payments, institutional services, and more. Our mission is to increase the freedom of money and financial access for people around the world.

The Know Your Customer Technology Team at Binance is dedicated to building a state-of-the-art platform that ensures a seamless onboarding experience for our customers. Our focus is on developing a large-scale KYC platform, launching innovative products, and integrating regional and global compliance vendors. We prioritize the responsible use of AI/ML technologies while adhering to both global and local regulatory standards. Join us and be part of a team that is at the forefront of innovation and compliance in the financial technology industry.

Обязанности

  • Design, develop, and maintain advanced web applications for Binance's internal teams, including AI-driven products and other business-critical applications.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional and cross-country teams to define, design, and implement new features across various projects.
  • Integrate and efficiently utilize third-party data sources, APIs, and AI models where applicable.
  • Develop and execute comprehensive unit tests to ensure code robustness, including handling edge cases, usability, and reliability.
  • Manage and oversee the feature launch release lifecycle, ensuring seamless integration across all product areas.
  • Troubleshoot issues, fix bugs, and optimize application performance

Требования

  • 8+ years of experience in developing production-ready web applications using JavaScript.
  • BS or MS degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field required.
  • Must have experience with React and Vue; experience with other frontend frameworks is welcome.
  • Proven track record of delivering projects with high-quality UI across diverse application areas.
  • Preferred work experience in an exchange (cryptocurrency or traditional), financial or banking institution, eCommerce, or payments processing.
  • Strong experience in working with remote data via REST, JSON, and AI model APIs.
  • Demonstrated experience in using AI tools and technologies in daily work to enhance productivity and innovation.
  • Interest and knowledge in the crypto/blockchain/technology space preferred, with a keen interest in AI technologies.
  • Experience in KYC or Compliance is a plus.
