О роли

We are currently seeking a skilled Lead QA Engineer with a background in game development. We'd like to see a specialist who can confidently maintain our course of continuous improvement of our product's quality and keep up with our busy schedule and active rhythm of development.

Основные обязанности

  • Directly participating in product testing
  • Defining and controlling task/project testing quality indicators
  • Developing testing rules and maintaining high standards
  • Managing the QA team (Manual & Automation)
  • Distributing test tasks among team members and controlling their implementation
  • Record-keeping for the QA department (including test cases and checklists)
  • Preparing the product for release
  • Leading the project - constant readiness to provide a full and clear report on the status of the project, plus risk analysis
  • Participating in the estimation and planning of tasks for the latest release version

Обязательные навыки и опыт

  • 3+ years in a similar QA role in mobile game development
  • Strong understanding of the mobile game development lifecycle
  • Experience leading QA teams of 10+ people
  • Strong planning, organization, and team management skills
  • Experience with QA KPIs, team development, test documentation, and testing strategy
  • Experience collaborating with automation teams
  • Experience with A/B testing and monetization
  • Knowledge of SQL and experience working with relational databases
  • Experience with Git and CI/CD
  • Experience with API and network debugging tools such as Swagger/OpenAPI, Charles, and Fiddler
  • Strong practical use of AI in testing, with the ability to integrate AI-driven solutions into QA workflows and pipelines
  • English and Russian spoken and written at B2 level and above

Будет плюсом

  • Ability to read XML and JSON
  • Knowledge of MongoDB (queries must be written often)
  • Experience analyzing testing effectiveness
  • Ability to localize problems based on statistical data
  • Experience developing work processes with test management systems (TestRail)
  • Experience with Android debug bridge, iMobileDevice
