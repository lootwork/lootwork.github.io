О роли

Bybit EU is looking for a Head of Marketing EU to lead our marketing strategy and execution across European markets. This senior leadership role covers brand, growth, product marketing, customer acquisition, communications, content and channel strategy, with a strong focus on crypto trading, advanced trading products and derivatives.

Reporting directly to the EU Group CEO, you will work closely with Product, Country Managers, Compliance and global Marketing teams to drive sustainable user, trading and revenue growth.

Key Responsibilities

Strategy & Growth

  • Define and execute the European marketing strategy across brand, acquisition, activation, engagement and retention.
  • Develop market-specific strategies for priority European markets.
  • Own the European marketing budget, KPIs and investment allocation.
  • Drive measurable growth across users, trading activity, revenue and customer lifetime value.

Crypto, Derivates & Product Marketing

  • Lead go-to-market strategy for crypto trading and advanced products, including spot, futures, perpetuals, options and derivatives, subject to applicable regulatory approvals and product availability.
  • Develop clear propositions for retail, active trader, VIP and institutional audiences.
  • Translate complex trading products and market concepts into compelling customer communications.
  • Partner with Product and Commercial teams to bring customer and market insights into product development.

Channel, Content & Distribution

  • Own the European channel strategy across paid, owned and earned media, CRM, affiliates, KOLs, influencers, partnerships, community, PR, events, content and SEO.
  • Define the optimal channel and content mix by market, audience, product and campaign objective.
  • Lead content, editorial, educational and organic growth strategies.
  • Drive full-funnel acquisition from awareness through KYC, deposit, first trade, retention and reactivation.
  • Manage agencies and external partners and continuously test new channels and formats.

Brand, Communications & Community

  • Build and strengthen the Bybit brand across Europe while maintaining global brand consistency.
  • Lead European product positioning, messaging and integrated campaigns.
  • Own PR, media relations, thought leadership and reputation management in partnership with Corporate Communications.
  • Develop strong relationships with crypto communities, traders, affiliates, KOLs, media and ecosystem partners.
  • Lead Bybit’s presence at relevant crypto, fintech, trading and institutional events.

Data, Lifecycle & Marketing Operations

  • Lead lifecycle marketing across onboarding, activation, engagement, retention and reactivation.
  • Own marketing analytics, attribution, automation and MarTech capabilities.
  • Maintain strong customer and market insight through segmentation, competitor analysis and proposition testing.
  • Use data and experimentation to improve channel efficiency and campaign performance.

Leadership & Compliance

  • Build and lead a high-performing European marketing function.
  • Set clear objectives, KPIs and accountability across the function.
  • Partner closely with Legal and Compliance to ensure marketing activities meet applicable European regulatory requirements.
  • Ensure appropriate governance around financial promotions, customer targeting, product communications and risk disclosures.
