With multiple awards to our name, Larian Studios has proven that we’re dedicated to delivering high-quality role-playing games. As we move on to new projects, the studio that brought you Baldur’s Gate 3 & Divinity: Original Sin 2 is looking for a Junior Web Dev QA Analyst.

Обязанности

  • Collaborate closely with the Digital Team, ensuring clear communication, alignment, and timely follow-up on assigned tasks.
  • Execute testing instructions from the Digital Team and proactively support investigations, validation, and regression testing.
  • Develop, maintain, and improve web automation scripts for automated testing.
  • Maintain end-to-end automation scripts and contribute to the stability of the automation framework.
  • Develop an understanding of Digital QA workflows and their application across Digital and Gameplay QA contexts.
  • Support Gameplay QA feature testing when required, based on project priorities and workload availability.
  • Execute testing instructions and validate regressions from the Gameplay QA Team.

Требования

  • Solid knowledge of HTML and CSS, and basic JavaScript or TypeScript.
  • Ability to prepare test cases and test scenarios.
  • Basic understanding of web applications, HTTP, and APIs.
  • Ability to use browser developer tools.
  • Familiarity with cross-browser, cross-platform, and cross-device testing.
  • Familiarity with Git concepts such as branches, commits, pull, push, and merge requests.
  • Strong communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with QA and Digital teams.
  • Proactive mindset with a strong willingness to learn and develop new skills.
  • Ability to work independently and manage assigned tasks with minimal supervision.
  • Strong interest in identifying bugs, investigating issues, and contributing to the delivery of a high-quality user experience through QA practices.

Будет плюсом

  • General experience using the Playwright test automation framework.
  • General understanding of REST APIs and their role in testing workflows.
  • Familiarity with reporting and task-tracking tools such as Jira.
  • General knowledge of GitLab CI/CD workflows.
  • Knowledge of WCAG Guidelines.
  • Basic understanding of SQL and relational databases, with the ability to write simple queries for data retrieval and use database administration tools such as MySQL Workbench or similar.

Что мы предлагаем

  • An international team of over 550 employees spread across 7 studios around the world, each of which have their own unique qualities, and maintain a small-studio feel
  • An open, collaborative environment where ideas are welcome and encouraged
  • A managerial team consisting of highly experienced game developers at the top of their fields
  • Sharing hobbies and passions with like-minded colleagues
  • Inclusive environment with many activities organized by the studios or the employees
  • Commitment to making the best game possible for the fans, including listening to community feedback
