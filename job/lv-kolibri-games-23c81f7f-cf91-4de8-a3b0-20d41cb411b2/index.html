Senior Product Manager, LiveOps – Idle Bank Tycoon (f/m/d)

Idle Bank Tycoon (IBT) lets players around the world build their own banking empire, one upgrade at a time. As Senior Product Manager, you’llown the Live side of Idle Bank Tycoon (IBT), from strategy to execution, and you’ll be responsible for the game’s Live performance.

Our Lead Designer owns feature design and the macro economy. You and your team own the numbers behind it: you set the costs and rewards of every game element, in both soft and hard currency, and keep them well-tuned for as long as the game runs.

You’ll report to the Head of Game and work closely with the Producer and the Lead Designer. You’ll bring the right data into every conversation so the team can make good choices together.

Обязанности

  • Manage and grow the Live squad of Idle Bank Tycoon, a cross-functional team that includes a game economy designer and an analyst
  • Shape the Live strategy and roadmap (events, offers, economy tuning). Set the LiveOps vision and goals with your team, and take Live events and offers from idea to launch
  • Be responsible for the Live economy and its balancing. Define and fine-tune the costs, rewards and prices of all game elements (features, upgrades, events, offers) in soft and hard currency, from launch values to ongoing balancing, and set up Live events and offers in our live content tool
  • Turn each new feature’s design into concrete cost and reward values that fit the economy and the Live roadmap
  • Keep a close eye on game metrics, dig into KPIs and recommend improvements that support long-term LTV. Get to know different player audiences and build segments with tailored balancing and offers
  • Run A/B and multivariate tests on the economy, events and offers to improve retention, conversion and monetization while reducing friction
  • Collaborate on KPI dashboards, and help us work out which tools and systems we need to build or improve so we can make the best in-game choices
  • Study the economies and live operations of games in relevant subgenres, and share practical insights with senior stakeholders and the wider team

REQUIRED SKILLS

  • 5+ years in mobile F2P product, including at least one Live game at scale
  • Experience shaping the Live strategy of a similar mobile F2P game, ideally in the idle / tycoon genre
  • Hands-on experience running a LiveOps / event calendar, from planning to performance review
  • Experience managing a cross-functional Live team (economy design, analytics) and delivering improvements step by step, together
  • Experience A/B testing economy, event and offer assumptions to improve KPIs
  • Hands-on experience with BI and experimentation tools (e.g. Looker, Amplitude), and turning complex data into clear, practical recommendations that grow revenue
  • Strong communication skills and a collaborative way of working

DESIRABLE SKILLS

  • Familiarity with tools such as Jira, Confluence, Google Play Console and App Store Connect
  • Experience with economy design in hybrid monetization (IAP and Ads), ideally for idle or tycoon games
