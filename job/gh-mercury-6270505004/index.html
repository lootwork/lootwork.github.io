In 1933, Harry Beck introduced a radically different map of the London Underground. Rather than faithfully depicting the geography of London, he simplified the sprawling transit network into clean lines, clear connections, and a system that anyone could navigate. Distances were distorted, angles were standardized, and unnecessary details disappeared. The result was something far more useful than a geographically accurate map: a way for millions of people to understand a complex system and confidently find their way through it.

Beck understood something fundamental. The best way to explain something complicated isn't to show people everything. It's to understand what matters to them and make that unmistakably clear.

At Mercury, we believe great product marketing works the same way. Banking* and payments can be complex, but the value they create for customers shouldn't be. Our job is to understand what people need, find the essence of what we're building, and help customers see how it can make their lives better.

We're a financial technology company building products that help ambitious businesses do more with their money. From everyday banking and payments to international money movement and emerging capabilities like agentic payments, we're making complex financial workflows feel remarkably simple.

We're looking for a Product Marketing Manager to help bring these products to market, communicate their value, and make sure customers discover and use what we're building.

This role sits at the intersection of Product, Marketing, and Revenue. You'll develop positioning, lead go-to-market strategies, drive successful product launches, and identify opportunities to increase adoption and revenue.

We care deeply about craft, but successful product marketing is about more than a beautiful campaign or a well-executed announcement. It's about understanding customers, building an effective plan, and driving meaningful results.

*Mercury is a fintech company, not an FDIC-insured bank. Banking services provided through Choice Financial Group and Column N.A., Members FDIC.

In this role, you'll

  • Lead impactful product launches. Own end-to-end go-to-market strategy and execution for new banking and payments capabilities, including emerging agentic features. Develop launch plans, align cross-functional teams, prepare customer-facing partners, and measure impact on product adoption and business goals.
  • Get close to our customers. Build a deep understanding of how founders and finance teams manage money, what challenges they face, and why they choose one financial product over another. Turn these insights into compelling positioning, messaging, and GTM strategies.
  • Execute AI-first. Use AI to work smarter, move faster, and produce better outcomes. You’ll build and use existing workflows, automations, and repeatable processes that improve how you approach research, launch execution, competitive intelligence, and other core responsibilities.
  • Help more customers discover and use Mercury. Partner with Product, Lifecycle, Activation, and Revenue to drive adoption of banking and payments capabilities. Identify growth opportunities, test hypotheses, and improve product usage over time.
  • Become an expert on our market. Develop a sharp understanding of the banking and payments competitive landscape, including emerging fintech products and traditional financial institutions. Translate market insights into differentiated positioning and actionable recommendations.
  • Equip our Revenue teams to win. Build trusted relationships with customer-facing teams and develop the competitive intelligence, battlecards, messaging, and training they need to communicate Mercury's value confidently and accurately.
  • Work with exceptional cross-functional partners. Collaborate with Product, Brand, Creative, Lifecycle, and Revenue to bring go-to-market strategies to life. Help shape strong campaign briefs and channel strategies, drawing on specialized creative expertise when needed.
  • Connect marketing to business impact. Define clear objectives for launches and growth initiatives, track meaningful performance indicators, and communicate what worked, what didn't, and what we should do next.
  • Own the full picture. Manage multiple projects and product areas simultaneously, make thoughtful tradeoffs, and prioritize the work that will have the greatest impact on Mercury and its customers.
