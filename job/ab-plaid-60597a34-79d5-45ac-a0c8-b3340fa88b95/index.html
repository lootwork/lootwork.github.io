We believe that the way people interact with their finances will drastically improve in the next few years. We’re dedicated to empowering this transformation by building the tools and experiences that thousands of developers use to create their own products. Plaid powers the tools millions of people rely on to live a healthier financial life. We work with thousands of companies like Venmo, SoFi, several of the Fortune 500, and many of the largest banks to make it easy for people to connect their financial accounts to the apps and services they want to use. Plaid’s network covers 12,000 financial institutions across the US, Canada, UK and Europe. Founded in 2013, the company is headquartered in San Francisco with offices in New York, Seattle, Washington D.C., Raleigh, London, and Amsterdam.

Data Governance builds the infrastructure that ensures Plaid responsibly manages sensitive consumer financial data. This includes identifying and managing PII (including deletions), enforcing consent and other data use limitations, upholding data minimization, and anonymizing data, while unblocking velocity for internal teams.

Обязанности

- Own the product strategy and execution for privacy, data governance, and responsible data use across Plaid’s platform

- Ensure Plaid’s products and internal systems handle consumer data in ways that are compliant, transparent, secure, and aligned with Plaid’s values and commitments

- Serve as the connective PM between Legal, Security, Data, Engineering, and Product teams to operationalize privacy requirements into scalable product solutions

- Define and drive the roadmap for privacy-related platform capabilities (e.g., consent, data minimization, retention, deletion, access controls)

- Translate evolving regulatory requirements into clear product requirements and system designs

- Partner with Legal, Policy, and GTM to proactively identify privacy risks and opportunities in new and existing products

- Work closely with Engineering to design privacy-by-default and privacy-by-design architectures

- Balance strong privacy protections with usability and performance for customers and developers

Требования

- 3+ years of product management or equivalent experience in a B2B environment

- Ability to influence and collaborate with cross-functional partners in Legal, Engineering, Policy, and Compliance to balance risk with product innovation

- Strong communication skills, both written and verbal; able to simplify complex concepts for diverse audiences

- Experience working on platform, infrastructure, or highly regulated products

Будет плюсом

- Experience with design and implementation of privacy initiatives to deliver best-in-class privacy practices,

- Experience with data protection domains such as data lifecycle management, access control, or automated policy enforcement.

- Experience in fintech or API-driven B2B products

- Experience in data management, observability, or quality
