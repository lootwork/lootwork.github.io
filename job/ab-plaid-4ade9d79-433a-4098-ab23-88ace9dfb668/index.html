We believe that the way people interact with their finances will drastically improve in the next few years. We’re dedicated to empowering this transformation by building the tools and experiences that thousands of developers use to create their own products. Plaid powers the tools millions of people rely on to live a healthier financial life. We work with thousands of companies like Venmo, SoFi, several of the Fortune 500, and many of the largest banks to make it easy for people to connect their financial accounts to the apps and services they want to use. Plaid’s network covers 12,000 financial institutions across the US, Canada, UK and Europe. Founded in 2013, the company is headquartered in San Francisco with offices in New York, Seattle, Washington D.C., Raleigh, London, and Amsterdam.

We are Plaid's Product Marketing team, and we own how the world understands what Plaid's platform does. Fraud Product Marketing owns one of the most important chapters in that story: how Plaid's Fraud Intelligence products — Protect, Signal, and identity verification — turn the Plaid Network into decisions that stop fraud before it lands, with less friction for the people who deserve access.

We work from evidence, not opinion. We treat positioning as a discipline, not a tagline exercise. And we build for the field: if sellers don't use it and buyers don't believe it, it doesn't count.

As Fraud Product Marketing Lead, you will own positioning, go-to-market, and enablement for Plaid's Fraud Intelligence portfolio — and lead the team behind it. You'll take Protect from beta into scaled adoption, define how Plaid shows up against fraud incumbents, and make sure the story we tell in campaigns is the story that wins in the room. You will report to the Head of Product Marketing and work directly with Fraud Product, Risk, the Anti-Fraud sales team, Brand, and Comms.

This is a player-coach role. You'll set the strategy and also write the positioning, run the customer interviews, and build the first pitch. Plaid teams are lean by design, and that's a feature.

Обязанности

- Own positioning, messaging, and narrative for Protect, Signal, and identity verification across new-logo and installed-base motions.

- Lead the GTM plan to move Protect from beta to scaled adoption: ICP definition, launch strategy, target-account plays, proof points, pricing/packaging inputs, and success metrics.

- Build and maintain fraud sales enablement — pitch, discovery, objection handling, competitive battlecards, customer evidence — for the Anti-Fraud overlay team and segment AEs, and measure adoption.

- Be the voice of the fraud buyer inside Plaid: win/loss, customer and call research, analyst and market intelligence, fed back into product roadmap and GTM decisions.

- Partner with Brand Marketing and Comms on the always-on fraud narrative and category-level campaigns so campaign and sales stories reinforce each other.

- Represent Fraud in company-level narrative work: platform positioning, product architecture, and investor, analyst, and major event moments.

- Manage, coach, and grow the Fraud PMM team; set priorities, hold the quality bar, and build the bench as the business scales.

Требования

- 10+ years of B2B product marketing, with 3+ years leading PMM teams, in fintech, infrastructure, or a technical/regulated industry.

- Several years of marketing fraud, risk, identity, or trust & safety products; you know the buyer, the incumbents, and the evaluation criteria.

- Proven positioning rigor: you've rebuilt positioning for a product that wasn't landing and can show the result.

- Experience taking a product from early adoption to scale, including ICP definition, launch, and enablement.

- Sales enablement sellers actually use, with evidence of adoption and impact.

- Evidence-driven: fluent in win/loss, call analysis, customer research, and market data.

- A strategic thinker who is also a doer: sets the plan and ships the work.

- Strong cross-functional operator who can align Product, Sales, Brand, and Comms without direct authority.

- Practiced at using AI as a research, drafting, and enablement multiplier while holding the bar on accuracy and claims.

Будет плюсом

- Experience marketing a data-network or consortium-based product.

- Experience selling a second or third product into an installed base at a multi-product platform.

- Familiarity with identity verification, KYC/AML, account takeover, payments fraud, or ACH return risk.

- Analyst relations experience in the fraud or identity category.
