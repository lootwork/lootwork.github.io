О команде

The risk platform team at Airwallex is responsible for managing risk across all Airwallex products, including topics like GTPN, Payments, Issuing, Onboarding, Account Takeover, Lending, and Credit. The risk landscape is constantly changing, and threats are becoming increasingly sophisticated. We are at the forefront of innovation in risk management.

Что предстоит делать

As a Senior Data Scientist, you will tackle challenging risk problems across credit and fraud (e.g., account takeover), and help build data-driven decision systems that deliver meaningful business impact. You will be responsible for translating business problems into analytical frameworks, strategies, models, and scalable data solutions.

Обязанности

- Build real-time ML models to protect Airwallex customers and platform assets, such as Account Takeover prevention and fraud decisioning

- Develop credit risk models and analytical frameworks to assess merchant risk and support the growth of Airwallex’s lending business

- Translate business requirements into analytical, modeling, and strategy problems across fraud, credit, onboarding, and related risk domains

- Design performance monitoring, metrics frameworks, and decision-support tools to improve risk visibility and decision quality

- Partner with Product, Engineering, Operations, and other cross-functional teams to turn data, models, and insights into production strategies and measurable business impact

Кто вы

We're looking for people who meet the minimum qualifications for this role. The preferred qualifications are great to have, but are not mandatory.

Minimum qualifications

- 3+ years of experience in analytics, data science, risk strategy, or a related quantitative role

- Bachelor’s degree or above in a quantitative field such as Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Computer Science, Engineering, or a related discipline

- Proficient in Python and SQL

- Strong problem-solving skills and experience working with large datasets

- Experience partnering with cross-functional stakeholders to solve business problems

Будет плюсом

- Experience in credit, fraud, or related risk domains

- Hands-on experience in model development, validation, or monitoring

- Experience designing risk rules, decision strategies, or performance monitoring frameworks

Why Join Us

- Opportunity to work on impactful problems that directly influence payments success and business outcomes.

- Collaborate with a talented, cross-functional team in a fast-paced, high-growth environment.

- Drive the adoption of advanced data science techniques and experimentation to improve customer and business outcomes.
