О this Role

As our Senior Learning Experience Designer (LXD), you will be the creative and execution engine of our Global Core L&D team. You understand that real behavior change doesn't happen through generic, off-the-shelf e-learning modules or endless powerpoint workshops. Instead, you design multi-touch, human-centric learning journeys that inspire our Team Leads (TLs) and Individual Contributors (ICs) to own their growth and lead high-performing teams.

We are looking for a strong deliverer—someone who combines human-centered UX design psychology, striking visual design, and interactive facilitation to turn strategic initiatives into tangible, engaging learning interventions. In this role, you will launch fast, iterate based on real feedback, and champion modern, AI-augmented learning methods to scale our flagship programs globally.

Что предстоит делать

  • Design Next-Level Learning Journeys: Build end-to-end, multi-touch learning experiences across key culture pillars: Leadership Effectiveness, Team Effectiveness, Feedback Culture, and "Own Your Growth."
  • Execute Across Formats: Go beyond traditional training to design gamified interventions, coaching toolkits, micro-learning assets, interactive digital workbooks, and immersive live workshops.
  • Visual Design & UX Excellence: Leverage modern design tools (Figma, Canva, Adobe Illustrator, Articulate 360) to craft visually stunning, highly intuitive learning materials that reflect Adyen's high standard of brand quality.
  • Pioneer Modern L&D Tech & AI: Experiment with and implement practical AI tools, workflow automation, and platforms (e.g., Coachello) to hyper-personalize learning and eliminate administrative friction.
  • Rapid Prototyping & Iteration: Prototype pilots quickly ("launch fast & iterate"), test them with learners across regions (EMEA, NORAM, APAC, LATAM), and continuously refine curriculum based on real-time feedback and data metrics.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with L&D Strategic Leads, Learning Business Partners (LBPs), and subject matter experts (SME) to translate business needs into intuitive, practical toolkits.

Что у вас должно быть

  • Proven LXD & UX Track Record: 5+ years of experience designing and scaling innovative learning experiences in global, fast-paced environments. Strong understanding of learning psychology, UX/UI principles, and human-centered design.
  • Senior Stakeholder Partnership: Experience designing for complex audiences, including senior leaders and the C-suite, and shaping the design approach with autonomy rather than only executing requests.
  • Strong Delivery & Execution Focus: A passion for "doing" and building—you love turning high-level concepts into beautifully executed, concrete assets and toolkits.
  • Visual Design Mastery: High design aesthetic with hands-on proficiency in visual design and authoring tools such as Figma, Articulate Rise/360, Canva, or Adobe Creative Suite.
  • AI & Tech Savviness: Practical curiosity and hands-on experience using generative AI tools and modern HR/L&D tech to streamline design workflows and create interactive assets.

Nice-To-Haves

  • Confident in-person and virtual facilitator who can engage diverse global audiences, hold space for deep conversations, and manage dynamic room environments.
  • Practical experience with psychometric tools (360s, DISC, Strengths Finder) and coaching frameworks.
  • Basic understanding of learning data pipelines, metrics tracking, and learner analytics.
  • Experience working in a hub-and-spoke global L&D model.
