Кто мы

Tripledot Studios is one of the largest independent mobile games companies in the world.

We are a multi-award-winning organisation, with a global 2,500+ strong team across 12 studios.

Our expanded portfolio includes some of the biggest titles in mobile gaming, collectively reaching top chart positions around the world and engaging over 25 million daily active users.

Tripledot’s guiding principle is that when people love what they do, what they do will be loved by others.

We’re building a company we’re proud of. One filled with driven, incredibly smart and detail-orientated people, who LOVE making games.

Our ambition is to be the most successful games company in the world, and we’re just getting started.

Работа в студии Ludios

О Ludios

Ludios takes its name from Latin; it means "The Game People". Led by Eyal, one of the co-founders of Tripledot Studios, and some of the most experienced people in mobile games, we are a studio that makes and publishes our own casual, free-to-play mobile games.

Our team is based across APAC, with the majority of our development happening in Jakarta and Melbourne. We are data-driven, creative, and genuinely passionate about the industry we work in.

Right now, we are working on a number of exciting projects, including one built around a globally recognised IP, alongside several original titles we are equally excited about.

If crafting games and reaching millions of players is what gets you out of bed, we would love to hear from you.

О роли

The role focuses on driving user acquisition for mobile games by managing and optimising marketing campaigns across multiple channels. You’ll analyse performance, identify growth opportunities, collaborate with creative and product teams, and manage relationships with advertising partners like Google and Facebook. We’re looking for someone analytical, creative, proactive, and adaptable, with strong English communication skills and a data-driven mindset. Experience in digital advertising is a plus.

Основные обязанности

  • Managing the user acquisition process for mobile games - Planning and execution of marketing campaigns.
  • Overseeing marketing campaigns across multiple channels and platforms.
  • Producing comprehensive reporting, analysis, and identifying new opportunities to support our key games.
  • Experimenting and analyzing campaigns for existing and new games.
  • Developing briefings for ad creatives and helping with the production of marketing materials.
  • Managing business relationships and day-to-day communications with partners (such as Google and Facebook).
  • Working closely with the product team to improve the game to help drive revenue.

Обязательные навыки и опыт

  • Analytical and metric-driven.
  • Good communication skills in English (required to communicate in)
  • Fast learner with the ability to be adaptable.
  • Organised with close attention to detail
  • Ability to work in a collaborative, fast-paced environment.
  • Self-starter who takes initiative
  • Creative minded
  • Experience with digital advertising – a big plus!
