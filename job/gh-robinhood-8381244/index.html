Staff Software Engineer, Customer Care Platforms (SupportX & Agentic Systems)

Location: Menlo Park, CA (Hybrid: 3 days/week in office)

О Team & Role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to some of the world's biggest financial problems.

We're looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact.

Robinhood isn't a place for complacency. It's where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We're a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

The SupportX team is at the forefront of building intelligent, agentic AI products across Robinhood. Our team pioneered Robinhood's first generative customer-facing systems and has since evolved into driving cutting-edge agentic workflows, including the Robinhood Cortex assistant and portfolio digest experiences.

We build the agent harnesses, custom tools, and autonomous intelligence that empower Robinhood customers to conduct deep research, understand real-time market movements, and unlock self-service solutions. We operate with high agency, iterating from zero to one while partnering across engineering, product, design, and AI platform teams to craft high-impact, context-aware AI experiences.

As a Staff Software Engineer on this team, you will serve as the technical lead driving our agentic architecture and next-generation AI platforms. You will take ambiguous, zero-to-one concepts from rapid prototypes to robust, production-grade systems, establish best practices for agent evaluation, build resilient tool harnesses, and lead architectural decisions that power intelligent, context-aware user experiences.

This is a high-visibility, high-impact role where you will directly shape the future of agentic experiences at Robinhood.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Architect, build, and scale agentic systems, autonomous workflows, and custom tool harnesses powering the Cortex assistant and intelligent customer support experiences
  • Drive zero-to-one rapid prototyping, taking open-ended product concepts from initial ideas to functional demos and guiding them into resilient production systems
  • Design and implement evaluation frameworks to measure, test, and improve the reliability, safety, and accuracy of AI-generated responses
  • Provide technical leadership and mentorship across the team, raising the engineering bar for design quality, code quality, and operational excellence
  • Partner closely with Product, Design, and AI Platform teams to shape product direction and define the roadmap for agentic systems across Robinhood
  • Stay hands-on as a builder in a lean environment, balancing technical leadership with direct execution

Что вы приносите

  • 8+ years of professional software engineering experience building and scaling production systems
  • Proven track record architecting and shipping agentic products, LLM-based workflows, or tool-use and orchestration systems in production
  • Hands-on experience building evaluation processes, validation frameworks, or quality systems for AI and LLM-based products
  • Strong zero-to-one mindset with a track record of turning ambiguous concepts into functional prototypes and production systems
  • Exceptional technical leadership, product judgment, communication, and cross-functional collaboration skills
  • Ability to operate effectively in a fast-moving environment with high ambiguity and high ownership

Будет плюсом

  • Experience building customer-facing AI products in support, research, or financial-product domains
  • Experience partnering closely with platform or infrastructure teams while owning the product-facing technical strategy
  • Experience with high-frequency or context-rich AI systems where quality, trust, and iteration speed matter deeply
