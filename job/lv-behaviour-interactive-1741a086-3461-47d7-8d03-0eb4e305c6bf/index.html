Are you ready to dive into the world of cutting-edge video game development? Headquartered in Montreal, Behaviour Interactive is at the forefront of the gaming industry, crafting unforgettable experiences across PC, console, and mobile platforms. We’re a dynamic, independent studio of over 1200 passionate employees, with studios in Toronto (Behaviour Toronto), Rotterdam (Behaviour Rotterdam) and in the UK (Behaviour UK–North & South). Our day to day revolves around two core business focuses: Services and Original Games. Our Services team collaborates with gaming and entertainment giants like Disney, Sony, Activision, and Nintendo, delivering exceptional work-for-hire development and pioneering immersive location-based entertainment (LBE). Meanwhile, our Original Games unit pushes our creative limits, with our signature IPs, including the iconic Dead by Daylight. Join us and be a part of the future of interactive entertainment!

At Behaviour Interactive, we believe in giving everyone a fair shot. We embrace a diverse and creative environment where everyone is celebrated. We value folks of all ages, sex, ethnicity, colour, ethnic or national origin, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, civil status, disability, or any other protected characteristics as per applicable law.

As a Project Manager within our Immersive team, you will be a driving force behind the successful delivery of Behaviour's immersive and interactive experiences. You'll coordinate cross-functional teams, track milestones, anticipate risks, and maintain clear communication across disciplines, all while supporting projects that blend technology, storytelling, and innovation. Your ability to bring structure to complex initiatives will help transform creative concepts into seamless, engaging experiences that captivate audiences both in physical and digital spaces.

В этой роли вы будете

  • Contribute to the planning and delivery of immersive and interactive experiences by identifying dependencies, anticipating risks, and supporting mitigation strategies.
  • Track milestones, tasks, and deliverables in JIRA and Confluence, ensuring production plans remain aligned with creative, technical, and client expectations.
  • Support multidisciplinary teams in their day-to-day activities, proactively removing blockers and maintaining momentum throughout the production lifecycle.
  • Facilitate clear communication across disciplines to ensure alignment on project goals, creative vision, technical requirements, and audience experience objectives.
  • Keep the team aligned on the right priorities throughout production, promoting motivation and timely delivery of milestones.
  • Keep stakeholders informed of progress, risks, and issues, and produce appropriate documentation, plans, and status reports.

Кого мы ищем

  • Minimum of 6 years of experience managing projects within the video game, interactive entertainment, experiential, or immersive experiences industry.
  • Minimum of 3 years of experience coordinating multidisciplinary teams across creative, technical, and production disciplines.
  • Strong understanding of production planning, project lifecycles, scheduling, and risk management in fast-paced, collaborative environments.
  • Experience using project management tools such as JIRA, Confluence, and other production tracking platforms.
  • Strong organizational and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to balance multiple priorities, anticipate challenges, and drive projects to successful delivery.
  • Excellent communication skills and fluency in both French and English.

DANS CE RÔLE, VOUS

  • Contribuer à la planification et à la réalisation d’expériences immersives et interactives en identifiant les dépendances, en anticipant les risques et en soutenant la mise en place de solutions adaptées pour les prévenir ou en réduire les impacts;
  • Assurer le suivi des milestones, des tâches et des livrables dans JIRA et Confluence, en veillant à ce que les plans de production demeurent alignés avec les attentes créatives, techniques et des client·e·s;
  • Soutenir les équipes multidisciplinaires dans leurs activités quotidiennes, en éliminant de manière proactive les obstacles et en maintenant la progression tout au long du cycle de production;
  • Assurer une communication claire entre les différentes disciplines afin d’assurer l’alignement sur les objectifs du projet, la vision créative, les exigences techniques et les objectifs liés à l’expérience du public;
  • Veiller à ce que l’équipe reste concentrée sur les bonnes priorités tout au long de la production, en favorisant la motivation et la livraison des milestone dans les délais prévus;
  • Tenir les parties prenantes informées de l’avancement, des risques et des enjeux, et produire la documentation, les plans et les rapports de suivi appropriés.…
