At Socialpoint, we are looking for an AI Artist Intern to join the Two Dots team. In this role, you'll create LiveOps content and support new features using AI image generation, from first exploration to final in-game asset, with guidance and mentorship from the art team. We're looking for someone with strong visual taste and clear communication, who uses AI tools with judgment, not just speed.

Your quest will involve

  • Creating LiveOps content (offers, seasonal wheel, battle pass) with AI image generation and editing tools.
  • Creating icons for currencies, tokens and rewards that stay readable at small sizes on mobile.
  • Matching the established Two Dots art style so assets stay consistent from one release to the next.
  • Supporting feature development with fast visual explorations and mockups.
  • Building and documenting repeatable AI workflows for recurring content.
  • Testing new models and tools, and sharing what works and what doesn't with the team.

To join our Guild you'll need

  • A high level of English, both written and spoken (English is our official language!)
  • A portfolio that shows AI as part of your process, not only final images: include references, iterations and the edits you made. Student and personal projects count.
  • Strong visual taste: a solid sense of shape, color, composition and readability.
  • Hands-on experience with AI image tools (e.g. Midjourney, Flux, GPT Image, Nano Banana, ComfyUI), and the ability to explain what the ones you've used do well and where they fall short.
  • Comfort refining AI output in Photoshop or similar: fixing errors, compositing, color correction and clean edges.
  • Experience building your own workflows, even small ones, and explaining the choices behind them.
  • Excellent communication skills: you explain your decisions, share work early and take feedback well.

Дополнительный плюс

  • Node-based workflows (ComfyUI) or training custom models and LoRAs.
  • Experience with Layer AI or similar production platforms.
  • Knowledge of game UI art: icons, pop-ups, buttons.
  • Figma basics.
  • A passion for mobile puzzle games.

What you'll get

  • Real LiveOps content shipping to players in Two Dots, a live game with more than 10 years in market.
  • Hands-on experience with the AI production pipelines of a professional art team.
  • Mentorship under the Art Director, with day-to-day support from a senior artist, while also learning from the Technical Art and UI/UX teams.

Due to the COVID-19 crisis, we have employed a hybrid working-from-home/office system. So, what does this mean? All we ask is that you live within commuting distance of our office in Barcelona so you are able to come into the office for important meetings/events.
