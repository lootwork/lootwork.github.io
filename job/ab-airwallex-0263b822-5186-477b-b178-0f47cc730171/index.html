О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,300 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Что предстоит делать

- Design, deploy and maintain LAN, WAN, VPN and cloud networks.

- Operate global connectivity, including leased lines, internet connections and data-centre networks.

- Configure and troubleshoot routers, switches, firewalls, load balancers and cloud networking services.

- Manage mixed-vendor environments such as Juniper, Cisco, Palo Alto, Aruba or equivalent platforms.

- Work with BGP, route filtering, subnetting, IPsec VPNs and network security controls.

- Respond to network incidents, lead troubleshooting and complete post-incident reviews.

- Support network projects, upgrades and change activities with minimal business disruption.

- Partner with Security, Engineering, IT and Workplace teams on network solutions.

- Maintain network diagrams, documentation, runbooks and operational procedures.

- Participate in a global on-call rotation.

Кто вы

- 5+ years of hands-on network engineering experience in a production environment.

- Strong routing and switching fundamentals, equivalent to CCNA level or above.

- Experience with BGP, VPNs, network troubleshooting and change management.

- Practical experience with GCP or AWS networking; GCP experience is preferred for this role.

- Experience with at least two networking vendors, such as Juniper, Cisco, Palo Alto, Aruba or Arista.

- Working knowledge of Linux and scripting with Python or Bash.

- Experience with Terraform, Git or CI/CD-based infrastructure automation is a plus.

- Strong written and verbal English communication skills.

- Experience in banking, fintech or other regulated, high-availability environments is highly preferred.

- A relevant networking certification such as CCNA, JNCIA, HCIA or equivalent is preferred.

- A stable employment history with sustained ownership of production systems.
