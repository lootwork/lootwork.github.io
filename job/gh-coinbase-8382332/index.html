The International team at Coinbase plays a critical role in Coinbase’s mission by providing access to the crypto economy to millions of users across the globe. As a Product Manager in the International team, your role will involve launching and improving the retail payment flows across markets thereby ensuring a smooth on and off ramp experience for our users .

Что предстоит делать

  • Launch and own new on and off ramp payment rails across markets globally
  • Improve the payments experience for international users, thereby ensuring a seamless access to crypto assets.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams such as Engineering, Design, Compliance, Legal, and Marketing to prioritize and deliver product enhancements.
  • Define and track key metrics to measure the success of payment optimization initiatives.
  • Stay updated on market trends, user feedback, and competitive landscape to inform product decisions.
  • Work closely with international teams and stakeholders to align on goals and priorities.

What we look for in you (ie. job requirements)

  • 2+ years of product management experience, preferably in B2C products
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills to work effectively with cross-functional teams.
  • Data-driven mindset with the ability to analyze metrics and make data-informed decisions.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and ability to prioritize competing demands.
  • Adaptability and cultural sensitivity to work with diverse international markets.
  • Past experience around payment flows and optimisations is a good to have
  • Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.
