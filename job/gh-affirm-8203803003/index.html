At Affirm, we exist for the moments that matter—giving people a clear, predictable way to pay over time, with no hidden fees, no surprises, and no tradeoffs on what matters most.

As a Staff Partner Solutions Engineer, you will be the senior technical owner for some of Affirm’s most strategic card platform partnerships. You will work across Product, Engineering, Commercial, Operations, and external partner teams to design, launch, and scale integrations that bring Affirm’s flexible financing products into new distribution channels.

This role requires someone who can go deep technically, lead through ambiguity, and translate complex internal architecture into clear partner-facing implementation strategies. You will help define the technical go-to-market model, launch readiness framework, support paths, and long-term operating model for an expanding area of Affirm integrations.

Что предстоит делать

  • Serve as the technical lead and trusted advisor for strategic partners across Affirm’s card and digital banking ecosystems.
  • Build strong external technical relationships and communication channels with partner product, engineering, implementation, and support teams.
  • Own solution discovery, integration design, partner testing, launch readiness, and ongoing technical support across our partner portfolio.
  • Translate internal product and engineering architecture into partner documentation, implementation playbooks, test plans, support runbooks, and escalation paths.
  • Partner with Engineering and Product to identify integration gaps, data quality issues, operational risks, and partner-specific requirements before they become launch blockers.
  • Socialize solution designs, tradeoffs, and launch plans across internal and external stakeholders, building consensus across Product, Engineering, Commercial, Legal, Operations, and partner teams.
  • Lead technical triage for partner issues, including API behavior, latency, error rates, data mismatches, incident management, RCA follow-up, and partner communications.
  • Help establish technical operating rhythms for new partner categories, including external architecture reviews, readiness reviews, incident response, and technical health checks.

What We Look For

  • 6+ years in Partner Solutions Engineering, Solutions Architecture, Partner Engineering, customer-facing Software Engineering, or a similar technical role.
  • Experience designing launch readiness, partner certification, UAT, incident management, observability, and support models for external integrations.
  • Experience supporting complex external integrations across card, payments, or banking platform environments.
  • Deep knowledge of card and payments systems, including how transaction state, money movement, reconciliation, and partner data flows operate across multi-party financial systems.
  • Strong understanding of REST APIs, webhooks, JSON, SDKs, authentication, observability, and secure integration design.
  • Ability to read code, inspect logs, debug API behavior, write scripts, and collaborate credibly with software engineers.
  • Experience leading cross-functional technical programs by managing dependencies, assessing program health, communicating risks to leadership, and holding internal and external teams accountable to launch milestones.
  • Demonstrated ability to build durable cross-functional and cross-domain relationships across engineering, product, business, and partner teams.
  • Ability to design scalable integration patterns that solve partner needs without creating partner-specific technical debt.
