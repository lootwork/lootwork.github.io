Easybrain is looking for a Data Analyst to help shape product decisions across our mobile games.You will explore player behaviour, look for growth opportunities and design experiments that improve player experience and business performance.

Обязанности

- Work closely with product managers, game designers, monetization specialists to formulate and prioritise hypotheses;

- Design and analyse A/B tests, from hypothesis formulation and sample size estimation to interpretation and rollout recommendations;

- Analyse and monitor key product metrics, investigate changes, and identify opportunities for growth;

- Research player behaviour, progression and game balance to inform product improvements;

- Turn analytical findings into actionable recommendations and communicate them clearly to stakeholders;

- Use AI-assisted tools to accelerate analytical workflows while ensuring the accuracy of results.

Требования

- Strong quantitative background in mathematics, statistics and A/B test design;

- Experience using Python for data analysis;

- Strong SQL skills for querying and analysing large datasets;

- Understanding of key product metrics, including retention, engagement, and monetization;

- Practical experience using AI tools for coding, analysis, or automation;

- Russian language is a necessity;

- English at Intermediate level or above.

Будет плюсом

- Experience in mobile games and mobile app analytics;

- Experience with cohort analysis, audience segmentation, LTV forecasting, and evaluating user acquisition performance;

- Experience leading analytical projects from problem definition to implementation and impact assessment.

Условия и льготы

Besides the engaging tasks, support from experienced colleagues, challenge, and drive, we offer:

- Full support in relocating to countries where our offices are located;

- High-end market salary with performance bonuses;

- All needed equipment;

- Regular company events and monthly Friday meetings;

- Social benefits (private medical cover, sports reimbursement, etc.);

- Paid vacations, sick days;

- English, Greek, and Polish online language classes;

- Reimbursement for education and professional development.
