О роли

Binance is building and strengthening its enterprise-wide risk management function. We are looking for a Senior Enterprise Risk Manager to support the development, implementation, and continuous enhancement of our Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) framework.This is a senior individual contributor role in the ERMF team. You will be a core member of the enterprise risk team, focused on operationalizing the ERM framework across the organization — from risk identification and assessment to monitoring, reporting, and governance support.

The ideal candidate combines strong knowledge of enterprise risk frameworks with hands-on experience implementing risk management processes in a complex, global organization.

Что предстоит делать

Core ERM Delivery

  • Develop and maintain enterprise risk registers, risk assessments, risk appetite frameworks, and KRI monitoring — covering both enterprise and operational risk domains.
  • Execute and coordinate Risk and Control Self-Assessments (RCSA) across business functions, tracking control effectiveness and remediation.
  • Conduct risk assessments for new products, business initiatives, and market expansion activities, collaborating with first-line teams to strengthen risk ownership.

Risk Governance & Reporting

  • Prepare risk reports, dashboards, and management presentations for senior stakeholders and risk committees.
  • Support the preparation of risk management materials for internal governance committees, regulators, and external auditors.
  • Analyse risk data to identify trends, concentrations, and emerging threats, leveraging analytics and automation where possible.

Cross-functional Support

  • Support operational risk incident management, root cause analysis, and remediation tracking.
  • Assist regional entities in implementing risk frameworks aligned with applicable regulatory requirements.
  • Engage with compliance, legal, finance, technology, and business teams to ensure consistent application of enterprise risk standards.

Note: This role covers the full spectrum of enterprise risk management, but day-to-day priorities will be set by the team lead based on organizational needs. The successful candidate is not expected to be a specialist in every area listed above; strong fundamentals in ERM and operational risk, with the ability to adapt across domains, are key.

Кого мы ищем

  • 7+ years of experience in risk management within financial services, fintech, payments, digital assets, or other regulated industries, with strong exposure to enterprise and operational risk.
  • Hands-on experience implementing or enhancing ERM frameworks, including risk assessments, risk registers, risk appetite, and risk reporting.
  • Strong operational risk experience — RCSA, KRI development, incident management, control assessments.
  • Familiarity with established risk frameworks such as COSO ERM, ISO 31000, or Basel operational risk principles.
  • Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills, with experience working across business functions and senior management.
  • Bilingual English/Mandarin is required to be able to coordinate with overseas partners and stakeholders.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience in cryptocurrency exchanges, digital assets, fintech, or other highly regulated financial environments.
  • Exposure to regulatory frameworks from authorities such as FCA, MAS, HKMA, ADGM/FSRA, VARA, or MiCA.
  • Experience in third-party risk management, business continuity, or operational resilience.
  • Experience using SQL, Python, BI tools, or AI-enabled solutions for risk analytics.
  • Professional certifications such as FRM, PRM, CRISC, or other relevant qualifications.
