The Experimentation Platform team at Robinhood builds the systems and infrastructure that power our company-wide experimentation efforts. Our mission is to enable rapid, trustworthy, and statistically sound experimentation at scale, so teams can learn quickly and make confident decisions. We partner closely with engineering, product, and data teams across the company. The team's work has a direct impact on product velocity and business outcomes. Together, we're driving a culture of data-informed innovation across Robinhood.

As a Senior Data Scientist on the Experimentation Platform team, you'll play a key role in shaping the statistical foundations of our experimentation systems. You'll collaborate closely with engineering, product, and data partners to advance best practices in A/B testing, improve experiment velocity, and build capabilities that help Robinhood scale experimentation effectively. You'll also explore how AI-powered capabilities can automate workflows and strengthen decision-making, while serving as a trusted statistical partner to data science and product teams. This is a high-impact role with the opportunity to define how we learn and iterate across our products!

This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA office, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Enhance the statistical methodologies underpinning the experimentation platform, including A/B testing and advanced techniques
  • Evaluate current experimentation practices across Robinhood and identify areas for improvement
  • Partner with engineering to build scalable tools and infrastructure for experimentation
  • Identify and drive opportunities to integrate AI-powered capabilities into the experimentation platform, automating workflows, accelerating experimentation velocity, and improving decision-making
  • Develop and champion experimentation best practices, enabling teams to design, analyze, and interpret experiments with statistical rigor
  • Serve as a statistical expert and trusted partner to data science and product teams, providing guidance on complex experimental designs and causal inference challenges
  • Shape the experimentation platform roadmap by identifying high-impact opportunities, prioritizing initiatives, and driving solutions from concept through implementation and adoption

Что вы приносите

  • Master's or PhD in a quantitative field (e.g., statistics, economics, mathematics, engineering, natural sciences)
  • 3+ years of experience with A/B testing, causal inference, experimental design, and statistical analysis in a tech environment
  • Deep expertise in statistical methods such as multivariate testing, adaptive experimentation, Bayesian inference, and machine learning techniques
  • Proficiency in Python or R for data analysis and experimentation workflows
  • Experience partnering with engineering and product teams to translate statistical methodologies into production-ready tools and platform capabilities
  • Strong communication skills, with the ability to explain complex statistical concepts and influence technical and non-technical stakeholders
  • Experience building experimentation platforms or integrating AI-powered capabilities into data science workflows is a plus
