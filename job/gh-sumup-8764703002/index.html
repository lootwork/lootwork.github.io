Affiliate Marketing Intern

Location: Berlin, Germany | Employment type: Fixed-term internship (6-12 months)

Team description

SumUp's Affiliate Marketing team helps small businesses discover our products through a fast growing network of partners across Europe, a network that's more than doubled in size over the past year. Sitting within the wider Performance Marketing group, this small team manages web partnerships and influencers from first outreach through to long term growth, working closely with an agency, designers, and content colleagues. This internship offers genuine hands-on exposure to how a specialist marketing channel runs day to day, from partner research to campaign activation to reporting.

Что предстоит делать

  • Support partner research, onboarding and activation across an agreed market or partner segment, using existing playbooks
  • Help keep partner offers, content, visuals and commission information accurate and up to date
  • Create and follow up on tickets for assets, tracking and landing page updates
  • Assist with routine reporting, including partner evaluations and performance summaries
  • Work alongside the agency, designers and content colleagues to keep campaigns and promotions on track

You'll be great for this role if…

  • Currently studying towards, or recently graduated with, a degree in Marketing, Business, Communications or a related field
  • Basic understanding of performance marketing, online customer journeys and campaign KPIs
  • Comfort working with spreadsheets, structured data and reporting tools, with a willingness to learn new platforms
  • Genuine interest in AI and new tools, with a critical eye on what they produce
  • Strong attention to detail and ownership over tasks, even when priorities shift
  • Excellent written and spoken English, with clear and proactive communication across teams

A peek into our selection process

  • Application review: our team reviews your application.
  • Case task: if your profile matches our requirements, we'll send you a small business case to complete within 5 days.
  • Case presentation: we'll review your business case and, if it meets the requirements for the role, invite you to an interview with our Affiliate Marketing Lead.
  • Team and stakeholder interview: if you're successful in the first interview, you'll move on to a team interview where you will discuss the case.
  • Offer: we'll extend an offer to our top candidate.

💰 This is a 6-12 month paid internship with a fixed hourly compensation of €13.9.
