As the Senior Performance Marketing Manager, SEM, you’ll own Robinhood’s US paid search program end to end, from strategy and budget to bidding, measurement, and testing. You’ll work closely with teams across marketing, creative, MarTech, product, and data science to build campaigns, experiments, and messaging that increase funded customers and revenue across our core products and services. You bring a quantitative approach to prioritization and problem solving, and you’re as comfortable hands-on-keyboard in the ad platforms as you are deep in the data.

You’ll thrive in a fast-paced environment, supported by an incredible group of engineers, analysts, content strategists, brand marketers, and product innovators working toward our shared mission: to democratize finance for all.

This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA and New York, NY offices, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own and scale our brand and non-brand SEM campaigns for all of Robinhood’s US product lines.
  • Build AI tools to plan, forecast, and pace budget, shifting spend toward the campaigns, keywords, and products with the highest marginal return.
  • Lead bidding and conversion strategy, including value-based bidding and offline conversion signals.
  • Develop measurement methodologies, such as geo holdouts and brand search incrementality tests, to understand SEM’s incremental impact and points of diminishing returns.
  • Partner with creative, MarTech, and data science teams to develop a rapid A/B testing process for ad copy and landing pages that improves click-through and conversion rates.
  • Partner with product and web teams to build high-converting funnels and landing pages for search traffic.
  • Work with Legal and Compliance to ensure ad copy and landing pages are compliant.
  • Proactively and independently drive a growth roadmap that increases the funded customers and revenue SEM delivers, and share results and recommendations with marketing leadership.

Что вы приносите

  • 7+ years of performance marketing experience with deep, hands-on SEM ownership, ideally in consumer fintech, financial services, or another regulated, data-driven category.
  • Experience building AI tools to optimize copy, manage workflows, and automate reporting.
  • Deep expertise in Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising, including Smart Bidding, account structure, query management, and automation.
  • Analytical mindset and experience setting up and running rigorous tests, including A/B tests, geo holdouts, and matched market tests.
  • A track record of managing large budgets against efficiency targets such as CAC, ROAS, or LTV:CAC.
  • A results-oriented approach, with the humility, drive, and creativity to get it done.
  • Experience with marketing strategy, including opportunity assessment, customer segmentation, and message testing.
  • Proficiency in SQL, enough to pull and analyze your own data.
  • Clear written and verbal communication, with the ability to turn analysis into recommendations for senior leaders.
