Senior Security Engineer - Penetration Tester

Office - León, Spain. Hybrid: 4 days in the office, 1 day working from home

О роли

Ebury is investing heavily in product security across its global financial services. Security engineering is a technical, hands-on team that works with software and platform engineers to build security into what they ship.

We don't own security policies, internal audit or other GRC work, IT security (such as anti-malware) or security operations, including incident response.

We work on engineering problems: vulnerability management, AI threat modeling, SSDLC, DRP, infrastructure hardening and other security engineering projects.

As a Senior Security Engineer Penetration Tester, you will test Ebury's security across web applications, mobile apps, APIs and cloud infrastructure. You'll combine manual testing with continuous automated testing, coordinate third-party pentests, and help engineering teams understand and fix what you find.

Основные обязанности

  • Perform manual penetration tests against Ebury's web applications, mobile apps, APIs and cloud infrastructure, including business logic flaws.
  • Set up, configure and tune web, mobile, infrastructure and cloud vulnerability assessment tools for continuous automated testing, and monitor Ebury's Internet-exposed surface, alerting stakeholders when needed.
  • Emulate an attacker in internal exercises such as table-tops, purple-team drills and disaster recovery simulations.
  • Schedule and prepare environments for third-party pentests and vulnerability assessments, for proactive testing or compliance, and coordinate the testers with internal teams.
  • Own the inventory of pentest and vulnerability assessment findings, and prioritize them using CVSS or similar.
  • Explain findings and their business impact to developers and non-technical stakeholders, guide remediation to the root cause, and re-test fixes.
  • Lead threat modeling sessions using STRIDE, PASTA, or similar frameworks to identify web and mobile attack vectors, abuse cases and security test scenarios.

Required qualifications

  • 5+ years of experience in pentesting, offensive security, application security, or security research.
  • Strong programming skill in at least one language, preferably Python or Golang, to write exploits, automate reconnaissance and build internal tooling.
  • Strong understanding of web and mobile vulnerabilities and their root causes, and of modern attacker TTPs.
  • Strong understanding of attack paths, misconfigurations and privilege escalation in cloud infrastructure, preferably AWS and GCP, including serverless, Kubernetes and PaaS.
  • Hands-on experience with vulnerability assessment and automated pentesting tools, and with methodologies such as PTES and OWASP WSTG.
  • Strong knowledge of secure architecture patterns and anti-patterns.
  • Experience in financial services or high-security environments.
  • Ability to evaluate risk against technical and business requirements.
  • Clear communication with developers and non-technical stakeholders, and good technical writing for reports and post-mortems.
  • Collaborative approach to working with engineering teams.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with payment systems and transaction security.
  • Published security research, CVEs, bug bounty record, or open-source offensive tooling.
  • Relevant certifications (OSCP, OSWE, OSEP, and similar).

Что мы предлагаем

  • Work on the security of fintech products used by thousands of businesses, across web, mobile and cloud.
  • Work with engineers in 25+ countries.
  • Use and build AI-driven internal red-teaming agentic platforms.
  • Security tooling and testing infrastructure.
  • Investment in professional development and certifications.
  • Progression path to Staff and Principal roles.
