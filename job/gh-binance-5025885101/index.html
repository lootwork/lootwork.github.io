О роли

As a Brand Intern within the Binance Brand Design team, you will play a key role in bringing our global brand identity to life. We are looking for a creative individual with a sharp art taste, strong graphic design fundamentals, and a strong appetite for exploring cutting-edge AI design workflows to scale brand communication worldwide.

Что предстоит делать

  • As part of the Brand Design team, design high-impact brand assets and product launch assets using both traditional design fundamentals and cutting-edge AI design tools
  • Collaborate closely with stakeholders and internal design team to deliver owned brand collateral and templates with a refined art taste and strong aesthetic standards
  • Collaborate with teams to build toolkits ensuring global brand consistency
  • Proactively identify emerging design trends, and contribute to robust internal creative conversations
  • Proactively explore emerging design trends and AI-driven creative techniques

Кого мы ищем

  • Major in Graphic Design, Brand Design, Fine Arts, or related fields
  • Basic knowledge of layouts, typography, colour, and other brand and graphic design fundamentals
  • Hands-on experience or strong fluency in AI design tools (e.g., Midjourney, Weavy, Higgsfield or AI-integrated workflows)
  • A clear communicator who can express complex ideas clearly and succinctly, while collaborating closely with teams outside of the Creative Studio
  • Experience with Figma, Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator is a must
  • Fluency in English is required to be able to coordinate with overseas partners and stakeholders. Additional languages would be an advantage

Будет плюсом

  • Knowledge and interest in the world of Binance and cryptocurrency
  • Knowledge of 3D or motion

Why Binance

  • Shape the future of finance
  • Collaborate with world-class talent in a user-centric global organization with a flat structure
  • Tackle challenging projects in a fast-paced and innovative enviornment with autonomy
  • Thrive in a results-driven workplace with opportunities for career growth and continuous learning
  • Competitive salary and company benefits
  • Remote-first, work-from-home arrangement (may vary depending on the nature of the role and team)
