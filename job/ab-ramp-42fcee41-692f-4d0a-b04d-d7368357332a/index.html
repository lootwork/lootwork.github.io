О RAMP

Ramp is building the smart infrastructure for finance teams, embedded in the transaction flow of every dollar a business spends. We automate how over $275B in annualized spend flows in and out of 85,000+ companies: authorizing payments, flagging risk, categorizing spend, and closing books.

The problems are high-stakes, data-dense, and unforgiving.

We hire people with high agency and high urgency. We look for slope over intercept. We care less about where you trained and more about what you’ve built. At Ramp, everyone is a builder who owns problems end to end and makes consequential decisions that shape the outcome.

The median Ramp customer saves 7.5% and grows revenue 16% in their first year – far in excess of businesses operating without Ramp. We believe every ambitious company deserves the same.

If you want to build systems that directly shape how companies move and manage billions, Ramp is the place to do it.

О роли

Ramp is hiring a Mid-Market Account Based Marketing Manager to extend Enterprise Marketing's proven account-based engine into our Mid-Market segment. You'll partner with Mid-Market AEs to help win our highest-value opportunities by reaching executive buyers, overcoming specific deal obstacles and differentiating Ramp, while building scalable, signal-led patch programs that give SDRs and AEs relevant reasons to engage the right buyers and turn priority accounts into meetings and qualified sales-qualified leads (SQLs). As the Mid-Market AE team continues to scale and grow throughout 2027 and more outbound runs through dedicated priority account programs, this role makes our account engagement more intentional and relevant rather than simply higher volume, leading with the Ramp solutions that best fit each buyer's needs. This is a high-ownership individual contributor role on the Enterprise Marketing team.

Что предстоит делать

- Partner with Mid-Market AEs on high-value, in-flight opportunities, using tailored outreach and coordinated engagement to reach executive buyers, overcome specific deal obstacles and introduce the Ramp solutions that best fit each buyer's needs, rather than one-size-fits-all product entry

- Build and run scalable, signal-led patch programs that give SDRs and AEs relevant reasons to engage the right buyers and convert priority accounts into meetings and qualified SQLs

- Run tiered plays across 1:many cohorts, 1:few groups by industry, buyer or use case, and 1:1 programs for the highest-value opportunities

- Align priority cohorts with Mid-Market leadership, Enterprise Marketing and RevOps through a monthly prioritization review and weekly async intake

- Measure impact on incremental contribution profit, account progression, conversion and ACV, including holdout-based lift tests

WHAT YOU NEED

- 4+ years of Account-based marketing experience supporting Mid-Market new business sales

- Experience building repeatable patch (account-level) programs with sales teams

- A track record of moving named accounts forward. You can point to specific deals your campaigns helped open or advance and explain how

- A builder’s mindset. You’ve built an ABM program or taken another experimental channel from zero to one, from designing the first test to deciding what to scale

- A close partnership with Sales. You’ve joined deal reviews, contributed to account plans, and advocated for your ideas with AEs, Sales leaders, and your own team

- Hands-on experience with Salesforce, intent data tools like 6sense or Demandbase, gifting platforms, direct mail vendors, and event operations. You pull your own reports and use them to make decisions

- Sharp creative judgment about what earns a CFO, Controller, or VP of Finance’s attention. You’re willing to run a bold, imperfect play and use what you learn to improve the next one

- The ability to execute from idea to delivery. You’re comfortable writing a Salesforce report, briefing an agency, negotiating with a venue, or getting a custom gift onto an executive’s desk by end of day

- You use AI in your day-to-day work. Tools like Claude, Cursor, and ChatGPT are part of your regular workflow, alongside Gong and Notion

Nice-to-Haves

- B2B SaaS or fintech marketing to finance decision-makers (CFOs, Controllers, VPs of Finance)

- Combining executive 1:1 outreach with repeatable 1:few and 1:many programs

- Coordinating multichannel account engagement with SDR, field and creative teams

- Partnering with RevOps or analytics to measure program lift beyond influenced pipeline

- Supporting a growing sales organization with evolving territories
