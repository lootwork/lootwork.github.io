О вакансии

Wargaming Kyiv is seeking a Senior 2D Artist for the World of Tanks team.

In this role, you will be tasked with the visual development of our games. You will collaborate closely with art directors, game designers, and developers to create illustrations and interface assets that align with the game's narrative and visual style. Your artistic vision and expertise will help define the unique and captivating experience our players will explore.

Что предстоит делать

  • Create 2D art content according to the game's current art style, such as UI content, illustrations, and concepts.
  • Work in a collaborative environment. Not only with the art team, but also with the UX team, historical consultants, developers, etc.
  • Provide mentorship and guidance to junior artists, fostering a culture of continuous learning and artistic excellence.

Кого мы ищем

  • 3+ years of experience in the game development industry and a love for computer games.
  • Proficiency with Photoshop.
  • Advanced skills with 3D modeling tools (Blender, ZBrush) for creating 3D scenes and high-quality renders.
  • Strong illustration and artistic skills, with a keen eye for form, color, and composition.
  • Proactive mindset and ability to propose creative ideas and visual solutions.
  • Experience with AI tools.
  • Ability to match the style of World of Tanks.
  • Time management and the ability to work within deadlines.
  • Ability to take and give constructive feedback.
  • Organized and clean workflow.

Что поможет выделиться

  • Willingness to incorporate new tools and to be creative about workflows to efficiently produce production content.
  • Intermediate English or higher.

Формат работы

  • Remote (from anywhere within Ukraine).
  • This role isn't eligible for relocation & immigration support.

Please submit your CV in English to ensure smooth processing and review.

О Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming's titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
