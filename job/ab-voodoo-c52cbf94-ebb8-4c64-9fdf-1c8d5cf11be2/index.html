О VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

Команда

Our Growth team is a transversal entity aiming to maximize and deliver the potential of any mobile product. It combines user acquisition, creative content production, monetization, and the tech capabilities required to turn applications into global hits. With over 150 experts, we operate at the forefront of mobile marketing and ad tech across various types of games (hypercasual, hybrid, 100% IAP, blockchain) and consumer apps.

Роль

We're looking for a Growth Monetization Analyst Intern to support ad revenue optimization and growth strategies across a portfolio of mobile games & apps. Reporting to the Monetization Lead, you will use your analytical mindset, strategic thinking, and problem-solving skills to help maximize performance and turn strong products into top-performing titles.

- Manage and optimize ad monetization and user acquisition strategies across multiple networks and platforms.

- Analyze key metrics and A/B tests to maximize ad performance, user LTV, and overall ROI.

- Provide data-driven insights to enhance growth, monetization, and product decisions.

- Collaborate with product, tech, and data teams on SDK integrations, dashboards, and automation tools.

- Stay ahead of industry trends and share best practices to drive continuous improvement.

Профиль кандидата

- You are currently enrolled in a relevant Masters degree program (Marketing, Business, Data Analysis, or a related field) in top business & engineering schools and looking for a 6 months end-of-studies internship

- Strong analytical skills, detail-oriented, and excellent organizational capabilities

- Proactive problem-solving mindset

- Advanced proficiency in Excel; familiarity with statistical analysis tools is an advantage

- Creative and innovative thinker

- Fluent in English, both written and spoken

- Interest or experience in mobile gaming and mobile ad technology ecosystem is preferred
