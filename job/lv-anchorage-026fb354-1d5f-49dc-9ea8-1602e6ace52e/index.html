At Anchorage Digital, we are building the world’s most advanced digital asset platform for institutions to participate in crypto.

Anchorage Digital is a crypto platform that enables institutions to participate in digital assets through custody, staking, trading, governance, settlement, and the industry's leading security infrastructure. Home to Anchorage Digital Bank N.A., the first federally chartered crypto bank in the U.S., Anchorage Digital also serves institutions through Anchorage Digital Singapore, Porto by Anchorage Digital, and other offerings.

The company is funded by leading institutions including Andreessen Horowitz, GIC, Goldman Sachs, KKR, and Visa, with its Series D valuation over $3 billion. Founded in 2017 in San Francisco, California, Anchorage Digital has offices in New York, New York; Porto, Portugal; Singapore; and Sioux Falls, South Dakota. Learn more at anchorage.com, on X @Anchorage, and on LinkedIn.

With the evolving landscape of digital threats and an increasing volume of sophisticated attacks by malicious actors, we have a security policy mandating all new hires complete an in-person onboarding process - no exceptions. Security is our foundation and we prioritize physical identity verification and secure IT setup. While our security and IT logistics require physical presence to protect our infrastructure from sophisticated global threats, this move is equally about our Village culture. We believe that a laptop can be shipped, but our culture cannot.

We are looking to add a Senior Product Marketing Manager, Stablecoin-as-a-Service and Tokenization to our team.

You will own how Anchorage Digital positions and takes to market two product lines: Stablecoin-as-a-Service, the issuance platform that lets a fintech, bank, payments company, or protocol launch its own branded, federally regulated dollar stablecoin with Anchorage Digital Bank as issuer of record; and Tokenization, including tokenized deposits infrastructure for banks and the tokenized assets our platform supports.

Your buyers are the people deciding whether to launch a stablecoin or put deposits onchain: heads of product and payments at fintechs and banks, treasurers, protocol founders, and the legal and compliance teams behind them. You will shape the story they hear, the proof they see, and the material our sales team uses to close them.

This is a generalist product marketing role on a small team. Product marketing is the core of the job, and you will also carry brand, events, and communications work for your product lines: the launch announcement, the conference talk, the co-marketing plan with a client who just issued on our platform. You should be as comfortable writing a positioning doc as briefing a designer or prepping an executive for a panel.

We have created the Factors of Growth & Impact to help Villagers better measure impact and articulate coaching, feedback, and the rich and rewarding learning that happens while exploring, developing, and mastering the capabilities and contributions within and outside of the Senior Product Marketing Manager, Stablecoin-as-a-Service and Tokenization role:

TECHNICAL SKILLS

  • Write the positioning, messaging, and ICP definition for Stablecoin-as-a-Service and Tokenization, and keep them current as the products and the market move.
  • Build and maintain the sales toolkit: pitch decks, one-pagers, solution pages, competitive battlecards, FAQs, and client case studies, and train Sales on how to use them.
  • Run product and client launches end to end: the announcement, the press and social plan, the website and in-app updates, and the co-marketing with the client whose stablecoin just went live.
  • Produce content for an institutional audience, from web copy and blog posts to white papers, webinars, and conference talks, in the Anchorage Digital voice.

COMPLEXITY AND IMPACT OF WORK

  • Translate a regulated issuance platform into a story a fintech CEO, a bank treasurer, and a protocol founder each recognize as their own, and decide which story leads where.
  • Build competitive positioning against other issuers and issuance platforms, and make the case for issuing through a federally chartered bank in plain terms a buyer can repeat internally.
  • Own the brand, events, and communications calendar for your product lines, including conference presence, speaking submissions, and executive prep.

ORGANIZATIONAL KNOWLEDGE

  • Develop fluency in how issuance, reserves, minting, redemption, and tokenized deposits work on our platform, and in the GENIUS Act requirements that shape what we can say.
  • Partner with Product on roadmap priorities using client interviews, win and loss analysis, and market research.
  • Work with Legal and Compliance so every external claim survives review the first time.

COMMUNICATION AND INFLUENCE

  • Gather client, prospect, and competitor insight and share it across Marketing, Product, Sales, and the Stablecoin Solutions team.
  • Act as the marketing point of contact for the Stablecoin-as-a-Service and Tokenization business, and bring the broader Marketing team (design, demand generation, social, communications) into your launches.…
