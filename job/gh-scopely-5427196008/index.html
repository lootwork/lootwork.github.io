We are looking for a Engineering Integration Lead in Barcelona, Spain, on a hybrid 3 days a week. We can support with visa sponsorship from any location.

At Scopely, we create games for everyone - and want to ensure that the people behind our games reflect that! We are committed to creating a diverse, supportive work environment where everyone is treated with respect. We are committed to providing equal employment opportunities and welcome individuals from all backgrounds to join us & embrace the adventure!

We are in the early stages of development on an ambitious, unannounced, cross-platform Strategy/MMO title, creating a team of talented and passionate game makers to join us on this exciting journey!

The Technical Release Manager will own the complete path from local changes to validated availability across all development lanes. You will set the technical strategy and operating model for how hundreds of contributors safely exchange changes while maintaining velocity. This is a senior technical leadership role that requires both deep technical credibility and cross-functional coordination skills

Что предстоит делать

  • Own how infrastructure, tooling, contributor workflows, and integration policies work together to move changes through development, including branch, merge, propagation, validation, and exception policy.
  • Set the lifecycle contract for each development lane: why it exists, what belongs there, how it stays synchronized, who handles conflicts, and when it retires.
  • Surface dependencies and likely collisions early enough for production teams to sequence work or knowingly accept rework, then preserve ordering and context as changes move between lanes.
  • Give contributors one clear view of change status, blockers, ownership, and recovery, with downstream failures traceable to their source.
  • Improve conflict detection and target-lane validation, and automate routine merge actions, propagation, backports, cleanup, and notifications.
  • Study how engineers, artists, designers, QA, and production actually work, accounting for differences in technical knowledge and working habits, then remove unnecessary ceremony, waiting, and specialist dependence.
  • Measure whether changes reach their destinations safely and efficiently, contributors spend less time managing the process, and validation and recovery give people confidence in the system. Use those findings and operational experience to improve policy and tooling.
  • Coordinate work across Developer Experience, QA, CI/build, production, and domain teams, with clear owners and backup coverage for critical operations.

Кого мы ищем

  • Strong engineering background, with the ability to analyze source-control history, diagnose CI and integration failures, and build supporting tooling.
  • Technical depth in at least one relevant area, such as source control, CI/CD, build and release systems, or developer tooling, and the ability to learn how the surrounding systems fit together.
  • Technical judgment and the ability to lead improvements across team boundaries, making difficult tradeoffs among speed, stability, and delivery goals.
  • Curiosity about how different disciplines work and flexibility in designing workflows for people with different technical knowledge, tools, and working habits.
  • Good product sense for internal tools and processes: find the constraint, design a supported path, measure it, and adapt.
  • Ability to explain complex technical and operational tradeoffs clearly to senior leaders, engineers, artists, designers, QA, and production.
  • Persistence in untangling messy operational problems, with the instinct to turn recurring manual work into durable systems.
  • An understanding of contributor desktop workflows and how they interact with shared infrastructure, with awareness of both Windows and Mac environments.

Дополнительный плюс

  • Current or recent leadership of cross-functional technical programs or ownership of a project-wide development system.
  • Game development, Unity, or another large and tightly coupled content-production environment.
  • Git LFS, binary or non-mergeable assets, large monorepos, or parallel development mainlines.
  • Operating high-volume merge queues or other change systems at substantial organizational scale.
  • Multiple simultaneous live releases or development tracks.
  • Startup, indie, or small-studio experience spanning engineering, pipelines, tools, and direct support for artists or content creators.
