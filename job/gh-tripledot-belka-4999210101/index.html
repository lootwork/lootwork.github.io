PeopleFun is an award-winning creator of mobile games for players across the globe. Job Summary

PeopleFun is seeking a Lead Product Manager to help shape the next chapter of Wordscapes, our flagship word game with more than 100M Google Play downloads. This role will own product strategy for a proven live game and identify the experiences and systems that will keep it growing.

You will lead the LiveOps pod, own and prioritize the LiveOps roadmap, and shape a sustainable cadence of features, events, content, offers, and experiments. The ideal candidate combines strong product judgment, hands-on analysis, and the ability to balance near-term performance with long-term player value.

Обязанности

  • Own product management for Wordscapes and lead the cross-functional pod responsible for the game’s roadmap, execution, and performance.
  • Drive sustainable growth by improving recurring engagement, retention, and monetization across the player lifecycle.

Own and prioritize the LiveOps roadmap and calendar, balancing recurring events, content, offers, experiments, and larger feature investments.

  • Own key game KPIs and use player, cohort, funnel, segment, event, and feature data to identify opportunities and make product decisions.
  • Partner with Design, Production, Engineering, and Analytics to deliver features, events, content, offers, and experiments with clear goals, success metrics, and guardrails.
  • Own and evolve the game economy, progression systems, rewards, and offers to support player motivation, retention, monetization, and long-term game health.
  • Review event and feature performance, identify follow-up actions, and apply learnings to future LiveOps cycles and roadmap decisions.

Требования

  • Bachelor’s Degree (graduate degree preferred) in Economics, Statistics, Mathematics, Finance or a related field.
  • 3+ years of experience in game product management, ideally on a scaled free-to-play mobile game or long-running live product.
  • Proven experience owning LiveOps for a free-to-play game and improving retention, engagement, or monetization through recurring events, content, offers, or features.
  • Experience owning a product roadmap and leading a cross-functional team through strategy, execution, measurement, and follow-through.
  • Strong understanding of free-to-play business models, LiveOps, game economies, and monetization systems.
  • Highly data-driven, with the ability to analyze cohorts, funnels, segments, and experiments and translate insights into product decisions.
  • Experience designing and interpreting A/B tests, including defining hypotheses, success metrics, guardrails, and follow-up actions.
  • Strong prioritization, initiative, judgment, and communication skills, with the ability to make clear decisions as information and priorities evolve.
  • Ability to travel to the Dallas office 3-4 times per year.
