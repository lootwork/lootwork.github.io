О VOODOO

Voodoo is a consumer tech company founded in Paris in 2013, with one mission: entertain the world. Today we are 1,000 people, generating $1b+ in annual revenue, profitably. Our games and apps have passed 7 billion downloads and reach 200 million+ active users every month, making Voodoo the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in downloads, after Google and Meta. We were backed by Goldman Sachs, Tencent and GBL along the way.

This position is Paris-based, with relocation support available.

Команда

Join Playbook, an agile internal studio dedicated to Castle Busters, one of our flagship mid-core titles. You’ll be an integral part of a focused team chartered with transforming this promising game into a market-leading, long-term hit.

Роль

We’re seeking a talented and seasoned Game Designer who is deeply passionate about RPG/mid-core games and laser-focused on creating the next big hit. With extensive experience in the industry, you'll play a pivotal role in delivering fun to millions of players. As the driving force behind the game's success, you'll ensure the game reaches the highest standards of quality and innovation.

- Own features from ideation to implementation and post-release optimization

- Collaborate with Product, Analytics and Art throughout features implementation

- Own game balancing, pace, feel

- Always back up your ideas with available data points

- Ability to deconstruct and analyze mobile games mechanics

- Get involved in all aspects of game development: UI, UX, Game Design, logic, performance, etc.

- Benchmark the market to stay on top of new trends

- Understanding of models and tools to analyze gameplay and user behavior to inform design

- Proficiency in Unity a huge plus

- Familiarity with Figma, Miro or similar visual collaboration software

Профиль кандидата

- At least 3 years of professional experience as a game designer

- Experience working on mobile games, F2P or RPG

- Creative and hacker mindset

- Good sense of gameplay experience, and special attention to detail

- Communication skills in English, both written and verbal

- Knowledge of casual games

- Deep collaboration mindset, including excellent interpersonal and communication skills, and a positive mindset towards receiving, acting on, and giving feedback

- Accountability, independence, and responsibility when given ownership over your work

- An eagerness to learn new ways to perform your tasks in a more efficient way, and to improve yourself day after day

Условия и льготы

- Best in Class compensation

- Other benefits according to the country you reside.
