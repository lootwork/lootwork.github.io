О Payoneer

Founded in 2005, Payoneer is the global financial platform that removes friction from doing business across borders, with a mission to connect the world’s underserved businesses to a rising global economy. We’re a community with over 2,500 colleagues all over the world, working to serve customers, and partners in over 190 countries and territories.

By taking the complexity out of the financial workflows–including everything from global payments and compliance to multi-currency and workforce management, to providing working capital and business intelligence–we give businesses the tools they need to work efficiently worldwide and grow with confidence.

This is an 12-month temporary position, with potential for extension depending on business needs.

We’re looking for an experienced hands-on Product Manager to join our Product Compliance & Risk domain and drive how Payoneer secures customer accounts and protects the platform.

Every login, device, API call, and sensitive action on Payoneer is a trust decision: is this really the customer, and is it safe to let them through? Account Security answers that across the account lifecycle, from customer authentication and access (login, MFA, identity) to platform protection (bots, devices, APIs), keeping funds and data secure without slowing legitimate businesses.

In this role you’ll take an active part in defining the strategy and roadmap for Account Security, from problem definition through delivery and measurable impact.

You’ll balance security and friction against clear KPIs, and prepare our security and access management for an agent-first world, where AI agents act on behalf of customers alongside people.

This role sits within Product Compliance & Risk, whose mission is to build the risk and compliance foundation that enables Payoneer’s up-market growth, while strengthening trust and security across the Payoneer platform.

Here you’ll partner with engineering, cyber, fraud, and risk teams to shape how Payoneer protects its customers and platform across the full customer lifecycle.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own outcomes & capabilities - drive the Account Security strategy, vision, and roadmap, from problem definition to delivery and measurable impact
  • Make clear tradeoffs - balance security and customer friction so that protection and customer experience move together, not against each other, and prioritize quick wins against long-term direction
  • Own the KPIs - define and monitor the metrics that matter, such as bot detection coverage and false-positive friction, and take action through data, experimentation, and iteration
  • Partner and influence - work closely with engineering, cyber, fraud, risk, and business stakeholders to deliver high-quality, end-to-end solutions
  • Shape the platform - drive build-vs-buy decisions and the architecture of the identity, access, and risk-signal layers, and how they connect to the wider fraud lifecycle
  • Lead the shift to agent-first security - prepare our authentication, access, and permissions model for AI agents acting on behalf of customers, and leverage AI and machine learning to drive adaptive, risk-based controls

Кто вы

  • You have 5+ years of product management experience in high-stakes, complex technological environments
  • You have hands-on cyber security, identity and access, or fraud experience, owning a domain end-to-end for at least 2 years (must-have)
  • You understand authentication, authorization, and access management - MFA, session and device risk, API security - and can partner effectively with security architects and engineers
  • You know how to set the right metrics and KPIs for a product, and you relentlessly drive toward them through data, experimentation, and iteration
  • You have an owner mindset: you take accountability for outcomes, not just delivery, and you’re comfortable working independently

Extra points will be given on

  • Experience in fintech or payments, especially cross-border or global, multi-market products where local patterns and regulation shape decisions
  • Experience with identity and security vendors and build-vs-buy decisions - identity providers, SSO, bot defense, or device intelligence
  • Experience with AI-powered or agentic capabilities - agent identity and delegated access, adaptive authentication, anomaly detection, or LLM-assisted workflows
