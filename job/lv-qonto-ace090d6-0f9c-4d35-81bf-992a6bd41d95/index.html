Join us as Product Designer in the AI Lab to design AI agents end-to-end — from discovery to delivery — and accelerate Qonto's AI roadmap alongside a team of ~12 engineers, a Product Designer, a PM, and a PMM building on top of one of the most advanced in-house agent platforms in European fintech.

You will report to Sophie Cornay, Head of the AI Lab, and work within a fast-moving team of ~15 people shipping AI agents at high cadence.

➡️ What you'll do

  • Design end-to-end experiences for AI agents (from concept to shipped UI/UX) across Qonto.
  • Turn ambiguous problems into clear product flows through discovery, prototyping, and iteration.
  • Run user research (interviews, tests) and translate insights into actionable design decisions.
  • Partner daily with engineers and product to ship quickly while keeping quality high.
  • Define interaction patterns for AI (prompting, guardrails, error states) that are transparent and safe.

➡️ What we’re looking for

  • Senior product design experience building products from scratch in fast-moving environments.
  • Strong problem-solving and product thinking (you connect user needs, business impact, and feasibility).
  • Excellent communication and facilitation skills with cross-functional teams.
  • High curiosity for AI and comfort operating in uncertainty and rapid iteration cycles.
  • Hands-on craft: you can prototype, document decisions, and sweat the details when it matters.

➡️ What we can offer you

  • Close Collaboration with Dean Siriscevic, Staff Product Designer Lead in the AI Lab — you'll collaborate daily on design direction, craft standards, and shipping product together (including close collaboration with the Qonto Design Studio).
  • An "agent highway" already built: The AI Lab has existing infrastructure, UI patterns, and agent frameworks. You're building on a real foundation — at speed, not from scratch.
  • A technically dense team: ~12 engineers, a Product Designer, a PM, and a PMM, all focused on AI agents. Steep learning curve, high signal density, fast feedback loops.
  • Room to shape how AI scales at Qonto: As the Lab matures, PMs here will influence how agent ownership transitions across teams. You'll help define what that model looks like.
  • Ship fast, ship well: The AI Lab runs at a cadence you'll rarely find elsewhere — without trading quality for speed. Here’s how we build.

➡️ Your future manager

You’ll report to Sophie Cornay, AI Lab - but don't expect hand-holding.

Sophie has led cross-functional teams through the full product lifecycle at both fast-growing startups and established tech companies. She partners directly with Product and Tech leadership to align the AI roadmap with Qonto's strategic goals.

Her approach: give Product Designers real ownership, create space for experimentation, and empower the squad to adapt their ways of working in pursuit of real impact.
