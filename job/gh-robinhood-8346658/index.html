Join us in building the future of finance.

Our mission is to democratize finance for all. An estimated $124 trillion of assets will be inherited by younger generations in the next two decades. The largest transfer of wealth in human history. If you’re ready to be at the epicenter of this historic cultural and financial shift, keep reading.

О team + role

As a Product Manager at Robinhood, you’ll be responsible for leading teams to develop products that improve the everyday lives of our customers. You’ll work with experts in engineering, data, design, marketing, operations, and research to bring ideas to life that will help us democratize our financial system.

The Brokerage Platform team provides core brokerage services that power Robinhood’s three strategic pillars: #1 for Active Traders, Lifelong Ownership, and Global Ownership. Our teams are responsible for all market data, reference data, order routing and execution, clearing and settlement, and customer buying power and margin. We sit on the critical build path for most of Robinhood’s new products and where possible we use our resources to increase Robinhood’s overall product velocity, drive incremental revenue and margin, and build core functionality that enables future growth for Robinhood.

As a Brokerage Platform PM, you will work closely with product and engineering from across Robinhood. You will shape Brokerage Platform priorities and roadmap and also have influence over the company roadmap. You will work closely with partners in engineering, data science, and operations to deliver, scale, and maintain our critical brokerage systems.

The role is located in the office location(s) listed on this job description which will align with our in-office working environment. Please connect with your recruiter for more information regarding our in-office philosophy and expectations.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the vision and roadmap for one or more Brokerage Platform domains.
  • Balance partner-team requests, maintenance work, and platform investment, and lead engineering, data science, and operations to deliver and scale the systems on Robinhood’s critical build path.
  • Champion new initiatives with clear business impact and drive prioritization, requirements, timelines, go-to-market, and alignment with legal & compliance and operational stakeholders.
  • Increase Robinhood’s product velocity through platform strategy and AI. Build configurable paved roads so partner teams can self-serve new venues, account types, geographies, and order-types.
  • Turn regulatory, operational, and vendor constraints into clear requirements and launch plans and work cross-functionally to bring new offerings to market.

Что вы приносите

  • Bachelor’s or Master's Degree
  • 4+ years of direct product management experience building high-quality platform products
  • 5+ years of experience in brokerage, market-making, or institutional trading
  • Familiarity using research, experimentation and product analytics to connect platform decision making with customer experience
  • Ability to focus on a problem at hand while thoughtfully considering overarching company strategy
  • Openness to all solutions and ability to build conviction in a solution based on evidence
  • Passion for financial products and improving people’s financial lives
