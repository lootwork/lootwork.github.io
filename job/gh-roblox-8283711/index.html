Что предстоит делать

Are you a seasoned engineering leader with a passion for reliability and scalability? We’re looking for an exceptional Software Engineering Manager to join our Site Reliability Team at Roblox. In this pivotal role, you will drive the evolution of our systems, ensuring they meet the highest standards of performance, reliability, and efficiency. You’ll collaborate with cross-functional teams to build robust infrastructure that supports our growth. If you have a track record of leading high-impact software engineering teams and a knack for solving complex technical challenges, we want to hear from you. Join us in shaping the future of our platform and delivering unparalleled value to our users.

At Roblox, our vision is to achieve 1 billion daily active users. We believe this leader will be instrumental in driving us towards that ambitious goal.

Your Role

  • Drive Reliability: Collaborate with cross-functional product partners to enhance and elevate system reliability.
  • Champion Production Health: Engage directly with production environments, analyzing key trends and deriving actionable insights. Take ownership of maintaining and improving production health.
  • Build and Implement: Develop and enforce production guardrails to ensure scalable and reliable service operations.
  • Reporting Line: Report directly to the Head of Reliability Engineering.

Your Qualifications

  • Team Leadership: Demonstrated ability to build, lead, and develop high-performing engineering teams.
  • System Expertise: In-depth knowledge of large-scale distributed systems and infrastructure.
  • Software Engineering Background: Strong foundation in software engineering principles and practices.
  • Scalable Design Experience: Extensive experience in guiding teams on designing and implementing scalable systems. Expertise in creating robust architectures that efficiently manage growth and maintain performance under varying loads, ensuring systems are resilient and adaptable to evolving demands.
  • Engineering Management: Over 3 years of experience in engineering management roles, with a proven track record of leading successful projects and teams.
  • Educational Background: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent experience.

You Are

  • A Visionary Leader: Capable of leading teams and fostering a problem-solving culture within large-scale engineering projects.
  • Hands-On and Technical: Equipped with strong technical skills and a hands-on approach to achieving team objectives.
  • Strategic Planner: Skilled in project management, strategic planning, and developing detailed roadmaps for successful delivery.
