With multiple awards to our name, Larian Studios has proven that we’re dedicated to delivering high-quality role-playing games As we move on to new projects, the studio that brought you Baldur’s Gate 3 & Divinity: Original Sin 2 is looking for a Junior Web Project Manager to join the team and help us in crafting the high calibre games our fans have come to expect.

Обязанности

  • Help the team stay organised and keep track of what is happening across our projects.
  • Support the team in keeping Jira tickets and boards organised and up to date, including agreed priorities and statuses.
  • Track action items and follow up with their owners.
  • Organise team meetings, prepare simple agendas, take notes, and share follow-up actions.
  • Help with communication between our team and other departments.
  • Help organise planning sessions, workshops, and other team activities.
  • Maintain project documentation and shared materials.

Требования

  • Strong organisation skills and the ability to keep track of multiple tasks at once.
  • Some experience in project coordination, team support, administration, or a similar role.
  • Advanced experience with Jira.
  • Clear and confident communication in English, both written and spoken.
  • Good attention to detail and the ability to follow tasks through to completion..
  • A proactive and helpful approach.
  • Comfortable working with different teams and personalities.
  • Comfortable using everyday tools such as Google Workspace or Microsoft Office suite (Excel, PowerPoint etc) or other relevant software

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with Confluence, Miro, Figma, or similar collaboration tools.
  • Experience working with artists, designers, programmers, or other creative teams.
  • Some knowledge of Agile, Scrum or Kanban practices.

Что мы предлагаем

  • An international team of over 500 employees spread across 7 studios around the world, each of which have their own unique qualities, and maintain a small-studio feel
  • An open, collaborative environment where ideas are welcome and encouraged
  • A managerial team consisting of highly experienced game developers at the top of their fields
  • Sharing hobbies and passions with like-minded colleagues
  • Inclusive environment with many activities organized by the studios or the employees
  • Commitment to making the best game possible for the fans, including listening to community feedback
