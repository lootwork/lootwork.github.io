The role & the team

We are looking for a Partner Technical Solution Engineer to join our business Partnerships team. You’ll have the opportunity to become the technical go-to person for our integration partners, supporting them from pres-ales through production launch, and representing their voice to our Product and Tech teams.

The Partnerships Team is part of the Pennylane Sales team, dealing with Referral Partners and Integrators reselling Pennylane on the PME & ETI market, growing this area of business to a >50% Indirect recurring revenue.

Ваши обязанности

Partner pre-sales

- Act as an externalised technical export engaged by our partners in key pre-sales opportunities or transformations to lock Pennylane at Partner or customer site.

- Co-lead demos and technical workshops with partners and Sales Account Executives for mid-sized business prospects.

- Design integration architectures between Pennylane and the customer's information systems (ERP, CRM, industry-specific tools, PA/PDP platforms, banks) using the API, connectors, SFTP or EBICS.

- Respond to security and architecture questionnaires (authentication, SSO/MFA, OAuth2, hosting).

Project escalation management

- Troubleshoot ongoing integrations: API errors, e-invoicing rejections (Factur-X, UBL, CII, BR-FR rules) and connector configuration issues.

- Understand incidents and resolution paths in order to coordinate Support, Product and Engineering teams through to resolution.

- Lead follow-up meetings with the end customer and the integrator on critical projects.

Ecosystem enablement

- Train partners' technical and functional teams on the API, AI new modules and e-invoicing.

- Manage beta programs and soft launches with champion partners (selection, training, feedback collection).

- Create and maintain reference materials: integration guides, FAQs, use cases and recordings.

Representing the partner voice to Product

- Communicate structured and prioritized requirements (missing endpoints, feature enhancements, documentation).

- Contribute to the quality of the public API documentation.

- Keep partners informed about the roadmap transparently.

- Analyze the needs of partners and provide effective technical solutions

- Ensure that developments meet users expectations

- Act as a liaison between partners and internal stakeholders (partnerships, product, sales teams…)

- Build and maintain solid tech-relationships with partners

- Gather and share partners qualitative feedback with the product team to improve our product and/or our APIs

- Identify key uses cases and best practices and work with our Product & API teams to display valuable documentation and help our team to scale.

- Be creative to develop content towards Patrners developers and techs (dev toolkit, guidelines etc.)

Профиль кандидата

- 5+ years of experience in technical pre-sales, integration consulting or Solutions Engineering, ideally at a SaaS vendor or an ERP/accounting systems integrator.

- Strong command of REST APIs: OAuth2 and token-based authentication, webhooks, Postman, and reading JSON/XML payloads. Scripting skills are a plus.

- A solid understanding of accounting and finance: journal entries, account matching, VAT, cost accounting and bank reconciliation. You are equally comfortable speaking with a CFO or a developer.

- Knowledge of e-invoicing (Factur-X, UBL, CII, PA/PDP, PPF/directory, e-reporting) is a strong asset.…
