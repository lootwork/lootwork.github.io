Lighting Artist

Apply now, this is more than just a job. This is an opportunity to become a part of something truly special!

Within this role you will be

  • Working closely with the Creative Director, and other artists to shape and maintain the game's visual style and lighting quality,
  • Creating dramatic and compelling lighting that supports photorealistic rendering, enhances environmental storytelling, and establishes depth and layering in scenes,
  • Leveraging game engine tools (e.g., Unreal Engine) to achieve high-quality lighting while respecting artistic and technical constraints,
  • Optimizing lighting to balance visual quality and performance.
