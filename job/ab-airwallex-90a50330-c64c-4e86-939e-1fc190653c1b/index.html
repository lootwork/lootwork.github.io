О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 676,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,300 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

Own the lifecycle growth strategy for Airwallex’s self-serve small-business customers, helping them move from onboarding and KYC through activation, first transaction and deeper product adoption. You’ll turn customer, product and behavioural data into practical growth opportunities, then design and run evergreen campaigns, experiments, automations and AI-enabled workflows that improve conversion, go-live activation and expansion across products without dedicated sales coverage.

- Lead Lifecycle Strategy: Design the end-to-end lifecycle engagement and build scalable lifecycle journeys for self-serve customers in EMEA and products without dedicated sales coverage.

- Own Digital Channels for Self-Serve: Lead the strategy and operational health for email, push, and in-app messaging. Ensure these high-impact channels are optimized to deliver personalized engagement and consistent growth across managed regions.

- Drive End-to-End Campaign Execution: Transform high-level lifecycle strategies into high-performing programs. Design and optimise evergreen campaigns, experiments and automations that improve conversion, go-live activation and expansion. Partner with Product, Sales, Marketing, Data and regional teams to turn customer, product and behavioural data into measurable growth opportunities

- Regional Funnel Accountability: Take full ownership of E2E growth metrics within your region. Leverage deep-dive quantitative analysis to identify high-impact opportunities for experimentation, ensuring data-driven decision-making at every touchpoint.

- Drive Regional Experimentation: Initiate and lead a high-velocity experimentation roadmap within your region, testing innovative tactics to uncover local growth levers and scale successful pilots into global frameworks.

- User-Centric Communication: Champion a "customer-first" messaging philosophy, delivering personalized and compelling content that resonates with users and reduces friction throughout the lifecycle.

Кто вы

We’re looking for people who meet the minimum requirements for this role. The preferred qualifications are great to have, but are not mandatory.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

- 5+ years of experience leading growth initiatives across the customer lifecycle.

- Experience in a product-led growth (PLG) environment, with a strong customer-first and customer-centric mindset.

- Strong analytical skills with the ability to interpret campaign, product and behavioural data, identify trends, prioritize campaign ideas and develop data-backed recommendations.

- Experience working with high volumes of data and communicating clearly through data and analytics.

- Hands-on experience designing and executing lifecycle or CRM programmes that drive customer conversion, activation, retention and product adoption.

- Experience running A/B tests and applying structured experimentation to improve performance.

- Strong organisational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to manage multiple initiatives simultaneously.

- Clear and structured communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with cross-functional partners, especially across Sales, Marketing and Data Science.

- A proactive and results-oriented approach, with a track record of getting things done and delivering impact in fast-paced environments.

- Bachelor’s degree.
