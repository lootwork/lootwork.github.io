О роли

The role provides senior compliance expertise and leadership to the ADGM Recognised Bodies (i.e., recognised investment exchanges, clearing houses, and custodians) and the ADGM financial services provider entity.

Обязанности

  • Review and approve new products, features, and business initiatives to ensure alignment with FSRA rules and requirements
  • Regulatory Liaison: Act as a key point of contact for new product request/proposal submission, regulatory queries, and approvals concerning product scope and respective FSRA compliance
  • Risk & Impact Assessment: Conduct compliance risk assessments, gap analyses, and operational testing for new financial instruments, assets, products or features
  • Generate product compliance plan with joint efforts from cross functions to ensure it is managed in accordance with the BU’s objectives
  • Policy Implementation: Draft, update, and maintain internal compliance policies, frameworks, and monitoring controls.
  • Provide advice to staff, training and support as necessary to ensure adherence with the product compliance plans
  • Assist with internal compliance audits.

Требования

  • Minimum of 10 years in regulatory compliance, specifically within fintech, crypto, or traditional financial services.
  • Deep familiarity with FSRA rulebooks in particular to GEN, COBS, MIR,, etc.
  • Track record on regulatory engagement on product compliance aspect and hands on experience in responding regulatory queries on new product approval process
  • Ability to effectively organize rulebook gap analysis and risk assessment with good execution credentials and manage detailed proposal for FSRA’s review and approval
  • Ability to provide and communicate advice & guidance in a practical & commercial manner with internal stakeholders
  • Strong attention to detail
