We're looking for a talented Game Artist to join our Game team and create eye-catching visuals for our mobile games!

You'll possess a keen eye for detail and a knack for crafting compelling graphics that resonate with mobile audiences.

Collaborate with the Product team, you'll research and design UI/UX and game assets that bring our mobile games to life and engage players worldwide.

Что предстоит делать

  • Design user interfaces (UI) that are visually appealing, intuitive, and user-friendly for mobile games.
  • Collaborate closely with Developers, and Product Owners to ensure a cohesive and delightful player experience.
  • Stay up-to-date on mobile games UI/UX trends and best practices, continuously improving the game's usability and aesthetics.
  • (Optional) Assist Marketing Team to prepare the material such as icons, screenshots, etc.

QUALIFICATION

  • Technical skills requirements:
  • Minimum 2-3 years working with Graphic design softwares (Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma..).
  • Can independently conduct research, update knowledge about mobile game trends.
  • Having experience working in UI/UX design is a plus.
  • Having experience working with the Mobile Game industry is a plus.
  • Having experience working in animation design is a plus.
  • Graduated in Graphic Design, Game Design or related fields.
  • Basic knowledge and passion for music, digital marketing, and mobile games.
  • Soft skills requirements:
  • A reliable person and able to work independently, with responsibility.
  • A creative person, flexible and adaptive to work with multiple games portfolio.
  • A communicative person who can discuss with the team about their ideas, projects and negotiate feedback effectively.
  • An energetic and open-minded collaborator.
  • A multi-tasker, who proactively seeks out opportunities to support the team.
  • Portfolio is required as part of the application

  • Competitive salary, 100% salary during probation

  • Full SHUI as Vietnamese Law + Premium Private Medical Insurance and annual health check
  • Minimum 12 days paid annual leave + 10 days paid sick leave.
  • 12 days working from home per year
  • Annual flexi-budget (9.400.000 VNĐ/year) for well-being & personal development
  • Free daily lunch with 10+ menu options.

  • Monthly/Bi-monthly team activity allowance
  • Company learning trip will be top of countries that we gain most profit. 1st is Korea, next destination is Japan
  • Sponsored sports activities: badminton, soccer, ...
  • Sponsored overseas conferences and industry events for all levels.
  • Modern equipments/AI tool provided.
