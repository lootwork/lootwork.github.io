О Persona

Persona https://withpersona.com/ is the configurable identity platform built for businesses in a digital-first world. Verifying individuals and organizations is harder — but more important — than ever, with AI enabling fraudsters to launch sophisticated accounts at scale and regulations evolving rapidly.

We’ve built Persona to support practically every use case and industry — that’s why we’re able to serve a wide range of leading companies. For example, Reddit relies on Persona for age assurance and verification to comply with online safety regulations, protecting younger users while maintaining a seamless experience. Meanwhile, OpenAI relies on Persona to keep bad actors out, protecting one of the world’s most powerful AI platforms from large-scale abuse in a time when AI is reshaping the way we work and live.

We’re growing rapidly and looking for exceptional people to join us!

О роли

Persona is seeking a Privacy Counsel to help guide our global privacy program and support our go to market team. In this role, you’ll work at the intersection of cutting edge privacy law, compliance, and commercial enablement. You’ll partner closely with Sales and Compliance teams as well as Commercial Counsel to address privacy and compliance questions in real time, support compliance reviews, and help ensure Persona’s practices align with evolving global regulatory requirements.

WHAT YOU'LL DO AT PERSONA

- Work closely with the General Counsel to advise on data privacy regulations and support sales and post-sales teams on calls with customers to educate them on our state of the art compliance.

- Become an expert in how our product supports compliance with various regulations including KYC/AML regulations, privacy regulations, and age verification regulations,

- Support customer due diligence efforts, including reviewing and completing questionnaires, DPIAs, and security/privacy assessments.

- Assist the General Counsel by monitoring regulatory developments, provide guidance on global privacy and compliance obligations, and assist in designing and operationalizing legal compliance.

- Assist in managing relationships with regulators and industry groups.

- Coordinate with our compliance team to ensure compliance with contractual and privacy obligations.

- Collaborate cross-functionally with the legal team, Security, Product, and Compliance teams to implement privacy-forward processes and documentation.

- Draft and maintain internal and external privacy and compliance policies, templates, and FAQs.

- Provide general legal and compliance support on other matters as needed.

WHAT YOU'LL BRING TO PERSONA

- 3+ years of relevant legal or compliance experience in a law firm, in-house, or regulatory environment.

- Ideally have a J.D. and active membership in at least one state bar or international attorney license.

- Strong working knowledge of global data privacy and protection laws, including GDPR, LGPD, CCPA/CPRA, and other emerging frameworks.

- Excellent communication skills and the ability to translate complex regulatory concepts into practical business guidance.

- Comfort working directly with external customers in both pre-sales and post-sales settings.

- Quick learner who can quickly learn an unfamiliar regulation and apply it to matter at hand.

- Ability to manage multiple projects, prioritize effectively, and thrive in a fast-paced environment.

As part of our interview process, all candidates will be asked to verify their identity with Persona. This step is used solely to confirm that candidates are who they say they are, and will have no impact on hiring decisions.
