О VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

Команда

New Apps is Voodoo's zero-to-one small and elite team, building the next wave of B2C AI apps on mass-market segments. We're exploring whatever looks huge next: social entertainment, fitness, health, learning, etc. Think startup speed with big-company muscle behind it.

The team is kept deliberately lean, which means fewer layers, more ownership, and faster decisions.

Роль

We're looking for someone who understands why a video gets 500 views and another gets 5 million.

Вам предстоит

- Create high-quality video and static content for mobile apps, specifically tailored for Meta Ads

- Ideate and execute creative concepts that go beyond standard, templated executions

- Become a creative expert: benchmark competitors, identifying opportunities from trends, user behaviors and cultural moments

- Understand how your creations perform and why: reading hook rate, CTR, CVR, IPM, and retention signals to iterate quickly and effectively

- Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams (Dev, UA, Product) to stay aligned with marketing strategy and business priorities

Профиль кандидата

- Fully proficient with video editing and content creation

- Hands-on experience with AI creative tools (Scenario, Seedance, Kling, Veo, Runway, etc.)

- Creative and have a hacker mindset, turning "we should make something about this" into a finished video by the end of the day

- Strong instincts for content and understand what captures attention online

- Fluent in English, both oral and written

- Nice-to-have: experience with UI/UX, to contribute to onboarding funnel content
