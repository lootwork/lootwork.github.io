OBJECTIVES

Evolve and maximize the business value of Amanotes' core backend platforms and portals, bridging the gap between music licensing, game studio operations, and engineering delivery. You will own the roadmap and execution for our core platform products: Music Catalog & Licensing management, Game Level Design, Product Management.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

  • Backlog Management: Translate complex requirements from Music Licensing, Game Studios, and AdOps into clear, prioritised product specifications and backlogs
  • ACM Ownership: Own the lifecycle of ACM (Amanotes Content Management), ensuring high metadata reliability and a seamless track/artist ingestion pipeline
  • Licensing Digitization: Partner with Music Licensing and Finance to build a centralized, auditable digital licensing workflow within ACM
  • SnapBeat Evolvement: Drive the roadmap of SnapBeat, introducing automated level design features, metadata templates, and real-time multi-game previews
  • PMP Scalability: Oversee PMP (Product Management Portal) features, including AdOps waterfall setups, revenue share reporting, and App Store automation
  • Agile Collaboration: Lead sprint planning and agile backlog grooming with backend, web, and SDK engineering teams to ensure high-quality, timely releases
  • Metrics Monitoring: Define and track key platform performance indicators, connecting platform enhancements directly to business efficiency metrics

QUALIFICATIONS

  • Experience: Minimum 3 years of experience as a Product Owner or Product Manager, managing internal portals, CMS, or developer-facing platforms
  • Technical Fluency: Solid understanding of software development lifecycles, APIs, database concepts, and SQL queries
  • Stakeholder Management: Proven ability to collaborate with and align diverse stakeholders (licensing, operations, creative, and engineering) in an agile environment
  • Data-Driven Mindset: Excellent analytical skills, utilizing qualitative and quantitative data to make prioritized, ROI-driven decisions
  • Communication: Exceptional communication and interpersonal skills to translate business needs into precise technical requirements

NICE TO HAVE

  • Industry: Background in music-tech, mobile gaming, or entertainment industries
  • Domain: Familiarity with music licensing models, rights management, or metadata ingestion
  • Emerging Tech: Basic understanding of AI/ML generative workflows or automation systems
