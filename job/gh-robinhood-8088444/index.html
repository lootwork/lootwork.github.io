The Robinhood Wallet team’s mission is to democratize access to financial services worldwide by leveraging DeFi protocols and Robinhood Chain! We build secure, intuitive, and decentralized products that enable our users to custody and manage their digital assets. Our team works at the intersection of traditional finance and web3, driving progress in cryptocurrency and blockchain applications. We work closely across multiple engineering and design areas to deliver highly reliable, high-performance systems for customers worldwide.

As a Software Engineer, you will build and own the backend services, infrastructure, and data systems that support Robinhood Wallet products. You will develop new features and system enhancements to deliver a highly secure and efficient trading experience for cryptocurrencies and tokenized stocks, including perpetual futures, to customers worldwide. In this role, you will design scalable architectures and maintain high-quality codebases. You will also collaborate with other engineering teams to guide key technical decisions and raise our team's engineering standards.

This role is based in our New York, NY or Menlo Park, CA office(s), with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week. At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Design, build, test, and deploy scalable backend services that power Robinhood Wallet.
  • Develop product-facing features and platform enhancements that meet strict correctness and scalability standards.
  • Identify and implement improvements to system performance, engineering productivity, and risk reduction.
  • Collaborate closely with client teams, product managers, designers, and external vendors to deliver reliable technical solutions.

Что вы приносите

  • 2+ years of experience in software development.
  • Expertise in backend development using languages such as Go, Python, or Java, with deep knowledge of cloud-native architectures.
  • Strong grasp of system design, data modeling, and software engineering fundamentals.
  • Proven ability to simplify complex problems, make sound tradeoffs, and drive alignment across product, engineering, and design teams.
  • Strong sense of personal accountability with a track record of delivering successful, end-to-end products and raising team-wide technical standards.

Дополнительный плюс

  • Experience working for a DeFi protocol, a crypto wallet company, or another blockchain-related company.
  • Familiarity with technologies like EVM blockchains and smart contracts.
