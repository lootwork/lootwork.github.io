We are seeking an experienced risk expert and leader to join our team as Group Head of Enterprise Risk. The successful candidate will be responsible for developing and overseeing the implementation of the Bank and Group’s Enterprise Risk Management Framework (ERMF). The individual will also support the Group Director of Enterprise and Operational Risk in further embedding associated risk activities, such as risk policy management and risk culture throughout the organisation, contributing to ongoing risk management maturity.

Key accountabilities

  • Own and maintain the Group and Bank ERMF, ensuring roles, responsibilities, and accountabilities for risk management are clearly understood across all three lines of defence.
  • Complete the annual review and refresh of Group and Bank ERMF, for approval by the Board, and develop and maintain supporting standards, procedures and guidance documents as needed.
  • Communicate changes to the Group and Bank ERMF to all relevant stakeholders, across all three lines of defence.
  • Act as an SME for enterprise risk management and provide advice and challenge on the application of the ERMF and risk management practice to all three lines of defence.
  • Research leading practices on enterprise risk management and develop proposals to continuously improve components of the ERMF.
  • Own and maintain the Risk Taxonomy. Complete periodic review and refresh of the Taxonomy, for approval by the CRO.
  • Monitor the effectiveness and level of embedding of the ERMF and take action to improve both where needed, and report on findings to the Board.
  • Regularly refresh, monitor and report on the Group and Bank’s Principal and Material Risks, obtaining input from relevant stakeholders across all three lines of defence.
  • Own and develop the Group and Bank’s approach to emerging risks.
  • Own and develop the Group and Bank’s Emerging Risk Register and emerging risk approach.
  • Develop and maintain the Risk Appetite Framework. Complete annual review and refresh of the Risk Appetite Framework, for approval by the Board, and develop and maintain supporting standards, procedures and guidance documents as needed.
  • Coordinate the annual review and refresh of the Risk Appetite Statement, for approval by the Board, and provide SME advice and guidance to risk leadership as they refresh the individual Risk Category statements and metrics.
  • Maintain the risk policy inventory and refresh schedule, and coordinate with the Risk Reporting team and Policy Owners to ensure refreshes are undertaken in good time.
  • Ensure adequate coverage of the Risk Taxonomy across the risk policy suite.
  • Provide SME advice and guidance to Policy Owners on the creation, maintenance, tiering and rescission of risk policies.
  • Review and challenge all proposed Level 1 and 2 risk policy updates to ensure clarity and alignment to ERMF standards.
  • Coordinate the committee approvals process for all tier 1 and 2 risk policy updates on behalf of the Policy Owner.
  • Complete annual review and refresh of the Policy Management guidelines and procedures.
  • Support the effective communication of risk policy changes to all relevant stakeholders, across all three lines of defence.
  • Lead on the reporting, monitoring and development of risk culture.
  • Work with Learning & Development to design and maintain an effective risk management learning and development programme across all three lines of defence. Provide advice and support to the RLT in designing ad hoc / bespoke risk training as needed.
  • Provide material on enterprise risk management for external reports, such as the Annual Report & Accounts, Pillar 3 Disclosures and ICAAP, and in response to requests from third parties, such as the regulators, investors and/or potential (Engine) clients.
  • Support the completion and continuous improvement of key enterprise risk processes, including the development of Group risk artefacts: risk policy management, risk appetite maintenance, risk taxonomy and risk framework developments.
  • Support the development of the Bank’s risk data and MI strategy and framework.
  • Develop and implement clear standards and procedures for the aggregation and escalation of risk matters to ERC, BRC and Bank Board.
  • Manage the process to track, embed and report on the status of material policy exceptions and risk acceptance requests.
  • Support the process for completing the annual SMF attestation for the Group Chief Risk Officer.
  • Manage, assess, test and develop risk and controls owned within the Risk function and in particular those sporting the CRO’s compliance requirements.
