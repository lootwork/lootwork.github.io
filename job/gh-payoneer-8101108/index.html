О Payoneer

Founded in 2005, Payoneer is the global financial platform that removes friction from doing business across borders, with a mission to connect the world’s underserved businesses to a rising global economy. We’re a community with over 2,500 colleagues all over the world, working to serve customers, and partners in over 190 countries and territories.

By taking the complexity out of the financial workflows–including everything from global payments and compliance to multi-currency and workforce management, to providing working capital and business intelligence–we give businesses the tools they need to work efficiently worldwide and grow with confidence.

Chargebacks & Payment Disputes Team Manager

Role Summary

You will lead and develop Payoneer's global Chargebacks & Payment Disputes team, spanning three hubs and multiple time zones, serving as the company's center of expertise for managing payment reversals, disputes, and recovery across card payments, ACH debit, and other payment methods. You will own team performance — productivity, recovery rates, service levels, and quality — while building strong working relationships with banks, payment providers, and internal stakeholders across Risk, Compliance, and Product. You will drive process improvement, automation, and control enhancements that reduce risk and improve the customer and stakeholder experience. This is a hybrid, full-time role based in Israel.

Hybrid

Full-time

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead and develop a global team across three hubs and multiple time zones, driving collaboration and accountability.
  • Oversee team capacity, priorities, and service delivery to ensure execution aligns with business objectives.
  • Maintain and improve dispute management processes, policies, and controls across card payments, ACH debit, and other payment methods.
  • Manage relationships with banks, payment providers, customers, and external vendors, including their operational and risk teams.
  • Partner with Risk, Compliance, Reconciliation, Business, Product, and Customer Care on investigations and dispute resolution.
  • Lead process improvement, automation, and optimization initiatives to boost efficiency and reduce risk.
  • Own team performance across quantitative metrics (productivity, recovery rates, backlog, SLAs) and qualitative metrics (accuracy, compliance, audit readiness).
  • Use operational reports and data analysis to identify trends, risks, and opportunities for improvement.

Кто вы

  • 5+ years of experience leading operational teams.
  • Experience in chargebacks, disputes, payments operations, risk, or fraud is highly desirable; strong operational leaders from fintech/payments who can quickly build domain expertise will also be considered.
  • Proven experience managing teams across multiple locations, cultures, and time zones.
  • Strong operational leadership track record, achieving KPIs, SLAs, and quality targets.
  • Outstanding people-management skills: coaching, mentoring, development, and building high-performing teams.
  • Excellent prioritization and organizational skills, able to manage competing business needs.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with exceptional attention to detail.
  • Highly independent, with a strong sense of ownership and accountability.
  • Advanced Excel skills and excellent written and verbal English communication.
