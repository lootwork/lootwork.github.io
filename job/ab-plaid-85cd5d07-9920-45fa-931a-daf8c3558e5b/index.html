We believe that the way people interact with their finances will drastically improve in the next few years. We’re dedicated to empowering this transformation by building the tools and experiences that thousands of developers use to create their own products. Plaid powers the tools millions of people rely on to live a healthier financial life. We work with thousands of companies like Venmo, SoFi, several of the Fortune 500, and many of the largest banks to make it easy for people to connect their financial accounts to the apps and services they want to use. Plaid’s network covers 12,000 financial institutions across the US, Canada, UK and Europe. Founded in 2013, the company is headquartered in San Francisco with offices in New York, Seattle, Washington D.C., Raleigh, London, and Amsterdam.

We are the Fraud ML & AI team within Plaid’s Fraud organization, partnering closely with our Fraud Data Science team to build intelligent, scalable fraud detection solutions. By leveraging the unique patterns and relationships across Plaid’s financial network, we develop machine learning models that help customers detect and prevent fraud while minimizing friction for legitimate users. Our team owns the end-to-end machine learning lifecycle, from signal discovery and experimentation to feature engineering, model training, deployment, and production monitoring. Together, we’re advancing how machine learning and AI protect the financial ecosystem.

As a Staff Machine Learning Engineer, you will set technical direction for major fraud modeling projects and lead them from experimentation through deployment and ongoing improvement. You will identify new signals and modeling opportunities in Plaid’s network data and turn them into models that help customers detect fraud.

You will remain hands-on in modeling and engineering while helping other MLEs deliver complex projects. You will work with Product, Fraud DS, Engineering, and ML Infrastructure to evaluate models rigorously, deploy them reliably, and improve them after launch.

Обязанности

- Turning network data and customer fraud feedback into new signals and stronger models that adapt to evolving attacks.

- Translating model improvements into lower fraud losses, fewer false positives, and stronger conversion for customers.

- Experimenting with traditional and state-of-the-art ML approaches and applying AI to fraud detection and investigation.

- Leading complex initiatives from problem definition through release, with direct contributions to modeling and engineering.

- Improving evaluation, training, deployment, and monitoring so the team can test and ship model improvements reliably.

- Setting technical priorities for major Fraud ML initiatives, resolving cross-team dependencies, and mentoring other MLEs.

Требования

- 10+ years of relevant experience, including at least 6 years building and deploying production ML systems. Level candidates on demonstrated Staff scope rather than tenure alone.

- Hands-on experience developing and deploying ML models with measurable product or customer impact.

- Strong ML and statistical fundamentals, with rigorous approaches to data quality, evaluation, error analysis, and generalization across time and populations.

- Fluency with LLMs and AI-assisted development tools, including using them to accelerate experimentation, implementation, and debugging while validating generated code and results.

- Knowledge of traditional and modern ML methods and experience evaluating their tradeoffs.

- Strong Python and practical SQL skills, with experience in modeling frameworks and reproducible data and training workflows. Experience with comparable tools and platforms is welcome.

- Experience operating production ML systems, including feature consistency, model deployment, monitoring, and investigation of failures.

- A record of leading complex technical initiatives, aligning stakeholders, and helping other engineers succeed through mentorship and technical guidance.

Preferred experience

- Fraud detection or risk modeling experience is strongly preferred.

- Experience applying graph algorithms, graph neural networks, or representation learning to detect fraud patterns and improve model performance.

- Experience training sequence or foundation models, applying model interpretability techniques, or deploying ML systems across multiple customers.
