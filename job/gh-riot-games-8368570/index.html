Principal 3D Environment Artist - VALORANT, Maps

As part of the Environment Art team, you will channel your knowledge of concepting, modeling, and texturing to help tell stories through visually compelling environments. Working within both design and technical constraints you will build optimized environments that elevate the purpose and mood of the game. Your craft expertise and eye for structure, form, shape language, and design will help develop and build fantastic new worlds for our players to explore.

As Principal 3D Environment Artist for Maps on VALORANT, you will create environment assets for Maps, ensuring its cohesion in style and quality supports VALORANT art direction. You will partner closely with your cross-discipline team to deliver iconic, creative, and resonant experiences that amplify competitive gameplay for players. You will report directly into the Environment Art Director.

Обязанности

  • Establish the craft quality bar for the look and feel of 3D environment assets ranging from hard surface structures to organic foliage, including the technical requirements needed to realize the VALORANT IP in new and creative ways
  • Use your understanding of optimized systems for asset creation, taking into account efficiency, speed, and cross dependencies, to ensure the art is within technical performance targets
  • Full ownership of art from design blockout to polish
  • Work directly with the design team to make sure art meets the requirements of gameplay, metrics and technical constraints
  • Work with and establish the Environment Art workflow keeping in mind gameplay, technical constraints and art direction
  • Creating environment art that not only meets the high visual bar from art direction, but also is performant and respects gameplay constraints
  • Identify opportunities for improvement in the production pipeline and collaborate with tech art to develop tools and workflows
  • Active member of craft community that often leads meetings and initiatives to level up members of craft
  • Ability to mentor, provide feedback and lead artists in a map throughout the development stages
  • Mentor and develop artists at all levels through workshops and detailed documentation.
  • Fully support various departments with Environment Art where needed
  • Take art direction and design feedback and execute quickly on solutions

Required Qualifications

  • 8+ years of game industry experience as an Environment Artist
  • Shipped 3+ project as an Environment Artist with at least one of those being a First Person Shooter
  • Experience creating immersive and optimized environments with a focus on FPS, gameplay and technical constraints
  • Experience working with modern game engines such as Unreal, Unity, or proprietary technologies
  • Expert experience in modeling, sculpting, texturing and materials for stylized games
  • Expert experience in programs such as Maya, Zbrush, Substance Painter/designer, and Photoshop
  • Strong communication skills and experience in working closely with design
  • Portfolio demonstrating a range of world building, level art, props, textures, with examples of stylized work

Desired Qualifications

  • Experience with foliage modeling tools (Speedtree, Houdini, Plant Factory)
  • Strong understanding of Substance and PBR workflows for Texture Creation
  • Stylized 2D Concept Artwork
  • Experience mentoring and leading artists in previous projects
  • Deep knowledge of the VALORANT IP

As a part of the application process, candidates will be expected to provide a portfolio or demo reel of their work and may be asked to complete an art test.

For this role, you'll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players' experiences, this could be your role!
