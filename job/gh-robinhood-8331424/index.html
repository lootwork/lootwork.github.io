О team + role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world's biggest financial problems. We're looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn't a place for complacency, it's where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. Our Compliance team helps Robinhood move quickly while staying grounded in strong governance, regulatory rigor, and consumer protection. We work closely with cross-functional partners to support compliance testing, monitoring, and oversight across a growing product suite. Our DeFi-focused compliance work sits at the intersection of decentralized technology and regulated finance, where the questions are new and the pace is fast. We value people who are easy to work with, come prepared, and bring real depth to the table. It's an exciting time to join as we expand our chain and product offerings!

As the Crypto Compliance Lead, Product, you will help shape how Robinhood approaches compliance for emerging DeFi products, including some that haven't been announced yet. You will bring deep expertise in compliance frameworks, regulatory requirements, and consumer protection, including AML and sanctions, to support rapid product launches. You will contribute to compliance governance, testing, and monitoring, and you'll partner with colleagues across the business to build practical, scalable approaches. Your work will help ease the load on current teams and give product partners clear, confident guidance. This is a chance to apply your expertise at the cutting edge of DeFi and regulated products globally.

This role is based in our New York, NY and Menlo Park, CA offices, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Provide compliance guidance and oversight for products, with a focus on DeFi products, including products that are still in development
  • Support compliance governance, including frameworks, policies, and controls that fit DeFi and traditional financial products
  • Conduct and support compliance testing and monitoring to confirm that controls are working as intended
  • Apply regulatory knowledge across consumer protection, AML, and sanctions to new and evolving product use cases
  • Partner with cross-functional teams to support rapid product launches without compromising compliance standards
  • Translate complex regulatory requirements into clear, practical direction for business and product partners
  • Help reduce pressure on existing compliance teams by contributing substantive, hands-on expertise

Что вы приносите

  • Strong compliance experience in DeFi and traditional finance
  • Deep, working knowledge of compliance frameworks, governance, and regulatory requirements
  • Expertise in blockchain, smart contracts, staking, DeFi lending
  • Solid understanding of consumer protection, including AML and sanctions
  • Proven ability to go beyond surface-level knowledge and offer well-reasoned, in-depth compliance expertise
  • Comfort working in fast-moving environments where products are launched quickly and the regulatory landscape is evolving
  • A collaborative, well-prepared working style, with the ability to build strong relationships across teams
  • Brokerage experience, or familiarity with how traditional brokerage compliance can inform crypto and DeFi is a plus
