Job Description

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON

What you will focus on

Great games start with great design. As a Game Designer at Gameloft Saigon, you'll shape the mechanics, systems and levels that keep players coming back. You'll turn ideas into playable features, test them fast and refine them with artists, programmers and producers.

What it means on a daily basis

  • Design gameplay mechanics, levels, combat and systems that are intuitive and fun.
  • Write and maintain clear design documents covering mechanics, features, flow and UI.
  • Prototype ideas and iterate using feedback from the team and playtests.
  • Balance difficulty, pacing and game economy to match the game's vision.
  • Work with artists, programmers and producers so features are implemented as intended.
  • Support playtests, and track and help resolve design issues and bugs.
  • Follow industry trends and competitor games.
  • Guide and mentor junior designers and interns.

Who you would be working with

  • You will be reporting to the Game Design Lead and Studio Design Deputy Director.
  • Closely collaborating with the project team members: Artists, Programmers, Producers, Data Analysts, Product Managers, etc.

Recruitment journey

  • Screening call with recruiter
  • Distance test
  • Interview with Game Design Lead and Studio Design Deputy Director

Требования

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED

  • Proven experience in game design with a portfolio of successful projects.
  • Solid game design knowledge: mechanics, level design, UI/UX and reward systems.
  • Good understanding of the market and players for mobile, PC and console games.
  • Confident in a game engine (Unity, Unreal or similar).
  • Good knowledge of monetization, retention and balancing complex systems.
  • Good understanding of player psychology and data-driven design.
  • Good spoken and written English.

Кто вы

  • Creative, original and a strong problem-solver.
  • Clear communicator who can present and pitch ideas persuasively.
  • Team player who supports colleagues and works well independently.
  • Open to feedback, flexible and quick to adapt to change.
  • Self-disciplined and passionate about games, both new releases and retro.
  • Meticulous and quality-focused, able to meet deadlines under pressure.
