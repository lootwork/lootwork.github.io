О роли

We’re looking for a hands-on Sales Compensation Systems & Data Engineer to build and maintain the technical foundation of our sales compensation operations. You’ll own data pipelines, integrations, data models, and calculation logic that produce accurate, reliable, and explainable commission results.

You’ll partner with Sales Compensation Operations, Finance, Payroll, and technical teams to translate approved compensation plans and business requirements into tested, maintainable systems.

Вам предстоит

  • Lead technical design and implementation of commission-related systems, integrations, and data flows across sales, finance, and payroll.
  • Build and maintain pipelines that ingest, transform, validate, and deliver data used in commission calculations and payments.
  • Design data models supporting sales crediting, participant eligibility, plan versions, effective dates, adjustments, and historical traceability.
  • Translate approved compensation plans into maintainable calculation logic and platform configurations. Identify ambiguous requirements and work with business owners to confirm and document approved interpretations before implementation.
  • Build automated tests covering calculations, integrations, plan changes, edge cases, and regression scenarios.
  • Implement appropriate access controls, audit trails, and reproducible processing so commission results can be traced, reconciled, and reproduced.
  • Establish access controls, audit trails, and reproducible processing to protect sensitive data and support reconciliation.
  • Own technical release practices, including version control, code review, automated testing, deployment validation, rollback planning, and technical documentation.

You Have

  • 5+ years of experience in systems engineering, data engineering, or hands-on business-systems development.
  • Hands-on engineering experience with sales commission systems, including implementing calculation logic, crediting rules, adjustments, and payment workflows.
  • Expert-level SQL, including owning design, development, and documentation, and the ability to navigate new datasets and answer complex questions independently.
  • Experience designing data models and reliable pipelines, including handling historical changes, reprocessing, and reconciliation.
  • Advanced Excel proficiency (complex formulas, pivot tables) and experience with reconciliation tooling and payments.
  • Experience integrating business systems through APIs, files, or other interfaces.
  • Practical knowledge of software testing, monitoring, access controls, and controlled releases.
  • Excellent communication skills: able to own stakeholder relationships, present findings and recommendations, and drive clear next steps in areas without clear ownership.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience extending or integrating incentive compensation platforms such as Pigment, Xactly, CaptivateIQ, or Spiff.
  • Familiarity with CRM, financial, payroll, and cloud data warehouse systems.

Technologies We Use and Teach

  • Pigment for ICM/quota issuance, Salesforce as the system of record & end user UI in Looker.
