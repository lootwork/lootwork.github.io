О Opportunity

OKX is building a vibrant in-app social community — spanning content, livestream, and creator ecosystems — that connects users, creators, and partners while driving trading engagement across the platform. You'll own the end-to-end Community & Live product, and contribute to north star goals including DAU, high quality creator ratio, and trading conversion within the community, underpinned by a robust safety & moderation stack.

Что предстоит делать

Community & Livesteam Product

  • Own the community and livestream product roadmap: content strategy, content curation, and content recommendation to improve discovery, engagement, and retention.
  • Drive user segmentation and interest discovery — build the frameworks and tooling to understand user cohorts and personalize content and community experiences at scale.
  • Define and improve community content quality standards; own the content/community ecosystem governance roadmap.

Creator & Fan Ecosystem

  • Build the creator and fan economy: creator incentive programs, tasks/missions, creator leveling systems, and tiered benefits that deepen streamer-fan and creator-fan relationships.
  • Build up creator lifecycle product capability, including discovery, engagement, onboard, grow, retain, and off-board phases.
  • Drive community's user education, product adoption, and trading conversion via innovative ways including internal advertising, fan economy mechanics, and trader leaderboards to achieve strong content- and trader-driven growth.

Community Monetization & Partnerships

  • Design and run community growth campaigns that convert community engagement into platform value (acquisition, activation, trading conversion).
  • Partner with marketing and growth teams to develop content strategies to capture and amplify opportunities from trendy events in the industry.

What We Look For In You

  • 5+ years of experience as a product manager, with solid experience shipping social media, community, livestreaming, creator platforms, growth campaigns, or social products.
  • Hands-on with Trust & Safety or content moderation tooling.
  • Adequate understanding of the crypto industry, trading funnels, and risk/compliance constraints.
  • Able to think and communicate logically, data-sensitive, and able to accurately grasp customer mindsets; strong ability to identify and break down problems quickly.
  • Strong in execution, with good communication and project management skills; able to mobilize cross-functional resources to solve problems and deliver projects on time.
