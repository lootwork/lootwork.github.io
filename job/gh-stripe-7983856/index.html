Кто мы

О Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.

О команде

Treasury for Platforms sits at the center of Stripe's evolution from a payments processor to a complete financial infrastructure provider. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and Treasury extends that mission by enabling marketplaces, software-as-a-service platforms, and fintechs to embed fully featured financial services directly into their own products.

By handling bank partnerships, compliance, and regulatory requirements, we allow platforms to launch quickly while focusing on the core parts of their business. We pair this with embedded user interfaces, managed support, and rich treasury features so our users can build great financial experiences.

Today, our platforms serve a highly diverse set of use cases, from payout cards for on-demand workers and operational accounts for in-field purchases to financial accounts for small businesses. We proudly power industry-leading products like Shopify Balance and Ambrook Wallet, serving a rapidly growing roster of users across the United States.

Что предстоит делать

We are seeking a Product Manager to own and drive critical Treasury for Platforms infrastructure and features, including new account types, checkbooks, and accounting integrations. You will take these products from alpha to general availability across consumer and commercial Treasury. You will work directly with platforms by joining sales calls, running product demos, and shaping integration paths. You will be the most technically fluent person in the room with the user and the most user-fluent person in the room with engineering. Ultimately, you’ll turn every platform into a financial hub for its users.

Обязанности

  • Deeply understand the financial management needs of our diverse user base and how they can benefit from strong treasury features.
  • Develop and communicate a clear product strategy and roadmap that aligns with Stripe’s broader vision and market demands.
  • Drive product development from ideation to launch, ensuring the delivery of high-value experiences for our users.
  • Work closely with engineering, design, legal, and other teams to ensure cohesive execution and alignment across all phases of product development and launch.
  • Advocate for the Treasury for Platforms vision internally and externally, ensuring key stakeholders are informed and engaged.

Кто вы

We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • 6+ years of product management experience, with a strong track record of launching impactful products.
  • An ownership mindset and willingness to do what it takes to solve problems and delight consumers.
  • Ability to handle and prioritize work in a fast-paced, dynamic environment while managing multiple projects and deadlines.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills, with a talent for distilling user problems into precise, actionable framing.
  • Rigorous approach to data, including comfort digging into metrics, writing SQL when needed, and thinking from first principles to deliver the right result.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience in treasury services and financial account management.
  • Experience building platforms with multiparty transaction capabilities.
  • Experience managing high-stakes external partnerships.
  • Understanding of international payments, foreign exchange, and compliance.
