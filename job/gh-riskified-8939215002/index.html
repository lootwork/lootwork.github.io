О роли

The Data Applications team builds the data platform that Riskified’s development teams build on. Our goal is to make it self-service: teams get databases, access and data pipelines on Aerospike, CockroachDB, Elasticsearch, Neo4j and PostgreSQL without waiting on us.

We also run the data infrastructure behind Riskified’s AI-driven fraud detection. Our pipelines and low-latency stores feed the models that decide on every order in real time, and connect them to the data lake.

As a Senior Data Platform Engineer, you will own large parts of this platform, from self-service tooling and data pipelines to production clusters of 100+ nodes. You will also be a go-to database expert for R&D.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build self-service tooling and infrastructure as code so development teams can create and manage databases, access and pipelines on their own.
  • Build the data infrastructure behind our AI fraud models: pipelines that move data between the data lake and low-latency operational stores, in batch and through change data capture (CDC).
  • Design and build new data services, such as low-latency access to data stored on S3.
  • Act as the database expert for R&D: review data models and access patterns, and help teams choose the right data store.
  • Use AI coding agents in daily work and build automation around them for the team.
  • Own large-scale production database clusters (key-value, distributed SQL, search and graph), including performance, cost, and major upgrades and migrations with zero downtime.
  • Share an on-call rotation and lead incident investigations across the database, infrastructure and application layers.

Требования

  • Experienced data platform, database reliability or big data infrastructure engineer, with a B.Sc. in Computer Science or equivalent experience.
  • Deep hands-on experience running at least two distributed data stores in production at high scale, such as Aerospike, CockroachDB, Elasticsearch/OpenSearch, Neo4j, PostgreSQL, Cassandra or Redis.
  • Strong grasp of distributed-systems fundamentals: replication, partitioning, consistency models and failure modes.
  • Strong coding skills in Python.
  • Production experience with Terraform and AWS, including on-call ownership of production incidents.
  • Clear communication in English, and a track record of guiding other engineers and teams.

Big plus for this role

  • Lakehouse tools: Spark, Databricks, Delta Lake, Apache Iceberg and Airflow.
  • Kafka and CDC: CockroachDB changefeeds, Debezium or AWS DMS.
  • Aerospike operations: strong consistency, cross-datacenter replication (XDR) and capacity planning.
  • Coding in Scala, Java, Go or Node.js/TypeScript, such as client libraries or Terraform providers.
  • Kubernetes, and debugging performance at the Linux level (CPU, memory, disk I/O, network).
  • Hands-on use of AI coding agents such as Claude Code for infrastructure work.
