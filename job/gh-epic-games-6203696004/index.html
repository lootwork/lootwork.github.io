Что предстоит делать

Epic Games is searching for a Lead Technical Animator with a passion for expressive character movement to join our team. In this talented team of veteran and emerging artists, you’ll collaborate across teams and contribute directly to Fortnite and other initiatives as part of our collaboration with Disney. Joining this team means you’re motivated to seek solutions to problems and work with other disciplines to make positive changes to workflows, tools, and technologies that directly impact cosmetic assets and gameplay.

In this role, you will

  • Work closely with technical animators, Leads, Directors, and Outsource Managers on the team to ensure assets are set up for success in the different stages of production
  • Communicate and drive alignment around strategy for technical animation and adjacent cosmetics teams
  • Work with production to schedule, prioritize, and delegate assets for the team, both internal and vendor
  • Work closely with animators, modelers, and designers to ensure a smooth development pipeline that delivers high-quality assets
  • Assisting with pipeline tools and working with engine and project leads on the continuous improvement of those engine tools
  • Mentor rigging-focused technical animators
  • Rigging characters, weapons, creatures, and vehicles
  • Setting up assets in Unreal Engine 5; including control rigs, cloth, physics, and blueprints
  • Maintain detailed documentation, video reference, and examples of art asset production, procedures, and engine requirements to support internal and external artists

Кого мы ищем

  • Extensive experience rigging characters in Maya and/or Unreal Engine
  • A portfolio demonstrating rigging for a range of characters, bipeds, and creatures, both body and faces
  • Attention to aesthetic detail, especially with regard to character deformation and physics movement
  • Familiarity with the Unreal Engine 5 art pipeline
  • A positive individual with strong written communication and teamwork skills
  • Exceptional problem-solver
  • Control Rig experience a plus
  • Python programming is a plus
  • You’re user-focused, passionate, solutions-focused, and innovative. These traits equal success at Epic and influence everything we do

This role is open to multiple locations across the US (including CA, NYC, & WA).
