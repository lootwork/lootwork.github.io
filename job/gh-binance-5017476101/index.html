Что предстоит делать

  • Assist in the development and implementation of the global Compliance Risk Management Program, designed to identify, assess, manage, and monitor Compliance Risk effectively.
  • Assist with the Anti-Financial Crime (AFC) EWRA process on at least an annual basis.
  • Lead risk assessments of additional Financial Crime segments as required, including Anti-Bribery and Corruption, Sanctions, Transaction Monitoring, and Market Abuse.
  • Liaise with the Business Unit Compliance team to participate in the risk assessment of new, changing, or existing products, i.e., Product Risk Assessments.
  • Work with various stakeholders, such as Product, Local Entity Compliance, and Central Compliance teams, to document and maintain control inventories in adherence to regulatory requirements and external and internal policy frameworks.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders at various levels globally to ensure that the risk assessment methodology and execution meet expectations and business needs.
  • Support the regional, business, and product teams in developing and managing policies, procedures, and processes used for risk mitigation and ongoing compliance risk assurance, i.e., building out the Compliance Risk control environment.
  • Assist with all pillars of the Compliance Risk Management framework (e.g., Regulatory Risk Management), including but not limited to horizon scanning, regulatory perimeter reviews, and impact assessments related to upcoming regulatory changes.
  • Assist in the development of timely, effective, and efficient management information on Compliance Risk insights and performance.
  • Produce MI/metrics to be provided to senior management on a continuous basis, outlining areas of heightened risk and the status of recommendations from the AFC EWRA exercise.
  • Monitor and report RAS KRIs on an ongoing basis, with clear recommendations where risk levels fall outside the established appetite.

Кого мы ищем

  • 5+ years as a compliance professional in a regulated financial services environment, 2+ years of direct experience in Compliance Risk and EWRA implementation.
  • Proven experience with prompt engineering, AI-driven automation, or fine-tuning workflows to drive Compliance related strategic initiatives using leading generative AI models.
  • Proficiency in Artificial Intelligence/ Technology i.e. strong foundation in tech frameworks which includes coding skills (e.g. SQL/ Python)
  • Degree in a business management, data science, finance, or economics field.
  • Ability to work independently in a fast-paced, global organization while maintaining close contact with relevant teams across multiple locations.
  • Excellent problem-solving and communication skills with the ability to articulate complex concepts into a format digestible by a diverse audience.
  • Proactive, diligent team player.
