О команде

The Employee Relations and People Risk & Compliance team is a relatively new center of excellence here at Stripe. We are striving to build a respectful, balanced, well-skilled team focused on enabling the People team, managers, and ICs to navigate challenging career moments while balancing compliance, empathy, and risk, resulting in success and engagement for all.

Что предстоит делать

We are looking for an exceptional leader for the Employee Relations, People Risk & Compliance function to join our team at Stripe with coverage for our locations in EMEA and APAC. This individual will have deep subject knowledge and expertise in employee relations, a strong understanding of people related risk in the regulatory space and will be an instrumental player/coach, who will lead the team focusing on improving and implementing frameworks, guidelines, approaches, and best practices. The International Employee Relations, People Risk & Compliance Leader will be highly cross functional and collaborative, partnering to inform and support Stripes and cross functional colleagues, both within and outside of the People team. This role reports into the Global Employee Relations, People Risk & Compliance leader at Stripe.

Обязанности

Case Management

  • Provide support to or personally lead medium to high complexity employee relations investigations, making recommendations on appropriate courses of action, matching investigation strategy to the complexity, risk, sensitivity, and confidentiality of the investigation.
  • Provide guidance on complex performance management cases, depending on what support needs are determined with People Partners and People Consultants.
  • Ensure cases are handled fairly, consistently, transparently, in a timely fashion, and in line with company culture, policies, and appropriate local laws.

Advising

  • Counsel People Partners, People Consultants, and business leaders on ER issues, trends and risks to foster sound business decisions, rooted in commerciality and aligned with Stripe’s Operating Principles.
  • Ensure that optimal decisions are made by leaders, People Partners and other stakeholders through influencing, coaching, and by providing technical expertise and sound strategy.
  • Review and refresh training offerings depending on company needs and external regulations.
  • Establish best practices and frameworks for critical business and regulatory activities and/or policies, in partnership with our employment legal team.
  • Develop and manage proactive and positive relationships with works councils and unions where applicable
  • Lead the International team, building an exceptional performance bench
  • Collect, analyze, interpret and make recommendations on ER related data.
  • Ensure alignment to the larger strategy of the People team and Stripe.

Scaling

  • Improve, socialize and rollout processes, approaches, guidelines, practices and operating procedures to use in EMEA, APAC and globally.
  • Develop and implement appropriate risk frameworks relevant to the People team
  • Develop and conduct training for leaders, managers, employees, People Partners, People Consultants, Talent Partners, and other stakeholders.
  • Build and maintain an organized case management system; ability to run analytics and metrics, produce dashboards, and drive monitoring.

Кто вы

We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree required; JD, MBA, or other Advanced Degree preferred
  • 10+ years of Employee Relations/Human Resources experience managing complex global investigations and people related risk required
  • 5+ years of direct management/supervisory experience leading cross-border teams required
  • Experience setting up and managing productive relationships with works councils and/or unions
  • Comprehensive understanding of the regulatory environment in which Stripe operates
  • Superior written oral communication skills
  • Strong analytical skills
  • Ability to remain positive, collaborative and objective while under pressure
  • Proven track record of positively influencing leaders, teams, and stakeholders
  • Ability to be a coach, mentor, advise, and partner with stakeholders at varying levels of seniority
  • Strong knowledge of ER, people risk labour relations, training, and conflict resolution, along with direct experience leading complex global investigations at all levels, including executive level
  • Familiarity with and extensive experience in employment laws in the EMEA region (and APAC preferred)
  • Must be able to work under pressure and deal with complex issues, while maintaining composure
  • Experienced decision maker who uses good reasoning and sound judgment required
  • Ability to be discreet and maintain confidentiality is required
  • Must be self-motivated and have an enhanced ability to prioritize/plan in a fast-paced, high growth environment
  • Open to travel (some travel may be required)
