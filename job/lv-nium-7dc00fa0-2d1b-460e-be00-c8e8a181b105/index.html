Nium, the Leader in Real-Time Global Payments

Nium, the leading global infrastructure for real-time cross-border payments, was founded on the mission to deliver the global payments infrastructure of tomorrow, today. With the onset of the global economy, its payments infrastructure is shaping how banks, fintechs, and businesses everywhere collect, convert, and disburse funds instantly across borders. Its payout network supports 100 currencies and spans 190+ countries, 100 of which in real-time. Funds can be disbursed to accounts, wallets, and cards and collected locally in 40 markets. Nium's growing card issuance business is already available in 34 countries. Nium holds regulatory licenses and authorizations in more than 40 countries, enabling seamless onboarding, rapid integration, and compliance – independent of geography. The company is co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore.

О роли

We are looking for a Senior Analyst to join our Card Operations team in Chennai. You will support the Card Operations Manager in overseeing the cards segment of Client Operations, act as the primary point of contact for client queries and internal support, and lead the team’s day-to-day operations.

This is a hands-on role for someone who can own complex investigations, guide Analysts and deputise for the manager when needed.

Основные обязанности

  • Act as the primary escalation point for internal and client queries; ensure all Card Operations tickets are handled correctly and on time.
  • Oversee investigations of pending card transactions and flow-of-funds issues on client accounts and cards.
  • Process card settlement and funding movements, and ensure automated runs complete successfully.
  • Review settlement and authorisation exceptions across all banking and issuer partners in a timely manner.
  • Post manual deposits, reconcile client funding accounts and resolve intercompany issues.
  • Oversee the accuracy of card monitors and resolve monitor-related issues.
  • Identify and report technical issues and follow them through to resolution.
  • Drive the improvement, documentation and maintenance of internal processes.
  • Support project teams with delivery and launch tasks assigned to Card Operations.
  • Support month-end deliverables, external audits and ad-hoc reporting.
  • Provide daily, weekly and monthly updates to the Card Operations Manager on status, progress and issues.
  • Deputise for the Card Operations Manager and cover for team members when needed.

Требования

  • 3–6 years of experience in card operations, with hands-on exposure to card issuing programs
  • Ability to train, guide and review the work of Analysts
  • Strong stakeholder management and experience with cross-functional teams across time zones.
  • Strong command of Excel and Microsoft Office; SQL or database experience is an asset
  • Strong command of the end-to-end card transaction cycle – authorisation, clearing, settlement, chargebacks/disputes, reversals and refunds.
  • Clear understanding of each party’s role – cardholder, merchant, acquirer, issuer, processor and card schemes (Visa / Mastercard)
  • In-depth knowledge of scheme settlement, funding and reconciliation; able to independently resolve auth-vs-clearing and settlement-vs-bank breaks.
  • Outstanding spoken and written English – mandatory, no exceptions; confident with clients, banking partners and card schemes.
  • Passion and curiosity for finance and technology, results-oriented and proactive, with sound judgement and strong attention to detail.
  • Flexible approach, including additional hours when required and ability to represent Nium and its values.
  • This role is based in Chennai with 3 days a week in the office. It operates on night shift or rotational shifts; the rotation is set according to business requirements and may change as the business evolves.
