ABOUT VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

This position is Paris-based, with relocation support available.

TEAM

You will be an integral part of an established internal studio, a dynamic and focused team dedicated to one of our most promising prototype in scale. This studio is specifically chartered to improve this high-potential game up to the successful and market-ready title it is meant to become.

In this exciting role, your initial focus will be on Cup Heroes, a game that has already demonstrated significant promise with its compelling core mechanics and engaging gameplay. You'll have the opportunity to contribute directly to the growth and refinement of Cup Heroes, to continue building a truly exceptional player experience. This is a unique chance to join a project with established momentum while still having a significant impact on its future direction within a dedicated and agile studio environment.

ROLE

We’re seeking a talented Producer who is deeply passionate about games and laser-focused on creating the next big hit. You'll play a pivotal role in delivering fun to millions of players. As one of the driving force behind the game's success, you'll ensure the game reaches the highest standards of quality and innovation.

1. Project management & Planning

- Define scope and create schedules

- Drive resource allocation in the studio

- Track progress and mitigate risks

2. Product quality & vision

- Champion the game vision

- Oversee QA for quality

- Provide feedback on gameplay and player experience

3. Production process & Optimization

- Establish and refine development workflows

- Identify and resolve bottlenecks

- Implement best practices

With this role, you will have a strong impact on the company by contributing daily to important decisions with a senior, multidisciplinary team of passionate and talented people working on one of the most ambitious Voodoo game. Thanks to your creativity and your teamwork, let’s entertain millions of people with our game!

PROFILE

- Production mastery: A true expert in agile processes, driving smooth and effective production workflows

- Product obsession: Highly motivated by crafting a hit, with an unwavering focus on product excellence

- Industry experience: previous experience as a project manager in either video games, consulting firms or tech industry companies

- Entrepreneurial spirit: Resilient, with the energy to navigate challenges and motivate the team through highs and lows
