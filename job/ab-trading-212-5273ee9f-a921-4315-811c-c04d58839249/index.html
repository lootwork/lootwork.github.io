О роли

As a Compliance Associate in the Advisory team at Trading 212 UK, you'll help the business meet its FCA obligations through day-to-day second line advice, review and approval. You'll handle time-sensitive requests from across the business, including financial promotions, client communications, employee compliance and regulatory queries, and help keep the UK compliance framework up to date.

Что предстоит делать

- Review and approve financial promotions for Trading 212 in line with FCA rules, Consumer Duty and internal guidelines.

- Review and approve client communications.

- Respond to compliance queries from the business on a wide range of subjects.

- Review and approve professional client opt-up applications.

- Support partner and affiliate onboarding reviews.

- Deliver compliance inductions and support the Compliance training plan, including training materials.

- Support horizon scanning, impact assessments, gap analyses and implementation of regulatory change.

- Review and update policies, procedures and the Compliance Manual.

- Support SM&CR administration, FCA notifications, regulatory reporting and responses to FCA requests.

- Support product governance, appropriateness and Consumer Duty work on new products and changes.

- Collect data and help produce monthly MI.

- Support senior members of the team with projects and ad hoc tasks.

WHAT YOU NEED TO HAVE

- 2–3 years' compliance experience within an FCA-regulated firm, ideally in retail investments, brokerage or payments.

- Experience reviewing financial promotions or client communications is strongly preferred.

- Working knowledge of FCA rules, including COBS 4, PRIN and Consumer Duty, SYSC and SM&CR.

- Ability to manage a high volume of time-sensitive work accurately and prioritise under pressure.

- Strong attention to detail and record-keeping.

- Strong IT skills.

- Bachelor’s degree in Law, Finance, Economics, Business or any other relevant field.

- Strong attention to detail and ability to work in a high-performance

- environment

- Ability to work independently and as part of a team

- A strong drive for excellence and a continuous improvement mindset

- Strong time management and organizational abilities

- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
