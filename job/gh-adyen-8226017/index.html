Data Analyst

In this role, you will transform complex data into clear insights that guide commercial decision-making. You will contribute to the development of scalable analytics solutions, from dashboards to data pipelines, while collaborating closely with senior data analysts, data engineers, and cross-functional partners across the business.

This is a high-impact role with a unique opportunity to develop. You'll be joining a team of experts, and we're looking for someone who is passionate about data and technology, eager to learn, and proactive in finding opportunities to create value.

Что предстоит делать

  • Support technical contributions: Support cross-functional data needs by collaborating with stakeholders across Commercial, Finance and Engineering teams.
  • Data quality and governance: Play an active role in data governance initiatives by cleaning, transforming, and documenting datasets, escalating data inconsistencies and ensuring accuracy and reliability.
  • Automation: Leverage AI to automate manual and repetitive processes.
  • Developing insights: Analyze datasets and translate findings into actionable insights for the commercial teams.
  • Scalability: Develop and design scalable, reliable, and sustainable data products that can be re-purposed for different use cases.

Кто вы

Must haves

  • 1-3+ years of experience in data analysis, data wrangling, or related analytical roles.
  • Proficiency in SQL and Python (Pyspark beneficial but not needed) for data analysis and processing.
  • Good analytical skills: adept at extracting meaningful insights from complex datasets and translating them into actionable recommendations.
  • Basic understanding of data validation, reconciliation, and data quality practices.
  • Strong attention to detail, particularly in data accuracy and control-oriented environments.
  • Good communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively within a global, multicultural team.
  • Fluency with AI tools to automate repetitive tasks (first-draft code, boilerplate documentation, exploratory queries).
  • You are proactive and have a deep curiosity for how things work and an analytical mindset that helps you solve problems.
  • You are comfortable working in ambiguity and eager to shape your own path.

Plus

  • Familiarity with analytical and statistical techniques (e.g., distributions, correlations, hypothesis testing, regression analysis) and with validation frameworks.
