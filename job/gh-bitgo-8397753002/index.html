BitGo (NYSE: BTGO) is the digital asset infrastructure company delivering custody, wallets, staking, trading, financing, stablecoins, and settlement services from regulated cold storage. Since 2013, BitGo has focused on accelerating the transition of the financial system to a digital asset economy. BitGo maintains a global presence and multiple regulated entities, including BitGo Bank & Trust, National Association, the first federally chartered digital asset trust bank owned by a publicly traded company. Today, BitGo serves thousands of institutions, including many of the industry's top brands, financial institutions, exchanges, and platforms, and millions of investors worldwide. For more information, visit www.bitgo.com.

BitGo is looking for a Product Designer to help shape the next generation of fintech experiences for institutional clients. You’ll join a growing Design team and work cross-functionally to define, build, and refine the products at the core of BitGo’s Platform. As a Product Designer at BitGo, you’ll work end-to-end—from early definition and vision through execution and launch. You’ll be a passionate advocate for users, balancing business goals with real user needs, and bringing bold ideas to life in a fast-paced environment. You’ll have a direct impact on major product surfaces during a key growth phase for the company. We operate with the agility of a startup and the stability of an industry leader—offering a rare opportunity to tackle complex, high-impact problems in modern finance.

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead end-to-end design work—from framing ambiguous problems and defining user needs to shipping polished, high-quality product experiences.
  • Conduct and participate in research, ideation, prototyping, usability testing, and iterative refinement.
  • Translate insights into personas, journeys, system maps, flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity UI.
  • Design clear, intuitive interfaces for complex workflows, data-heavy environments, and institutional-grade products.
  • Partner closely with PMs, engineers, and cross-functional teams to bring work to life with clarity and momentum.
  • Facilitate critiques, workshops, and collaborative sessions to align teams and sharpen product direction.
  • Contribute to and evolve our design system—improving consistency, quality, and velocity.
  • Raise the bar for craft, user advocacy, and design excellence across the organization.

Кого мы ищем

  • 5+ years of experience designing for web or mobile products in fast-paced environments.
  • Strong command of UX fundamentals, interaction patterns, and product thinking.
  • Demonstrated ability to simplify complexity and design intuitive experiences for technical or systems-heavy products.
  • Confident communicator who can articulate design decisions grounded in user insight, data, and clear rationale.
  • Strong visual design and craft: typography, hierarchy, layout, component thinking.
  • A portfolio that showcases not just polished screens, but thoughtful process, decision-making, and measurable impact.
  • Curiosity, humility, and a growth mindset—continuously developing your skills and pushing your work forward.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience in fintech, trading platforms, blockchain, or institutional financial products.
  • Contributions to design systems or UI libraries.
  • Familiarity with front-end technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React) and constraints.
  • Experience using of AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, v0 in their day to day process.

Why BitGo

Transforming financial markets requires bold thinking, technical excellence, and a deep sense of ownership. At BitGo, you’ll join a team that values craftsmanship, transparency, and high standards—and you’ll have the opportunity to shape critical product surfaces used by leading institutions around the world.

We’re building the infrastructure for the next era of finance. Joining BitGo means being able to look back and say you were part of the team that moved an industry.
