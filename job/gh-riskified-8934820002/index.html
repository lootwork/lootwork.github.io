О роли

Product Data Analysts at Riskified are a core part of the Product team. They work side by side with PMs and with our engineering, data science, design, growth, marketing, and operations teams to shape what we build and measure its impact. We are looking for people who solve complex problems with varied skills, and who learn and adjust quickly as the company and our product platform continue to expand. Our philosophy centers on end-to-end ownership: every team member is empowered and accountable. We hold ourselves to high standards, have ambitious goals, and are not afraid of trying new things.

Riskified helps leading global merchants reduce fraud and chargeback losses. We are looking for a Product Data Analyst to take our chargeback analytics to the next level, in a deep, high-impact role. You’ll join the Product team and work closely with PMs, R&D, Data Science, Operations and Sales.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own performance analytics for our chargeback products.
  • Go deep on what drives chargeback outcomes, and turn insights into product improvements.
  • Design and analyze experiments to improve product performance.
  • Partner with Data Science on predictive models.
  • Give Sales and Account Management data-backed insights to show merchants the value we deliver.
  • Track product health and adoption, and build it so it runs automatically.
  • Present findings to product and business leadership.

Требования

  • 5+ years in product, business or data analytics, ideally in payments, fintech, eCommerce or risk.
  • Expert SQL and strong Python, with the ability to independently extract, transform and interpret complex datasets.
  • Experience with BI tools (Looker, Mixpanel or similar).
  • Solid statistics: experiment design, significance, and avoiding bias in messy real-world data (small samples, long delays before outcomes are known).
  • A record of analysis that changed a product or business decision, not just reporting.
  • Able to work independently, choose the right questions, and push back with data.
  • Clear communicator in English, including with senior leadership and Sales.
  • BA/BS degree or equivalent experience.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with chargebacks, card schemes (Visa, Mastercard) or payment gateways.
  • Experience working with Data Science on predictive models.
