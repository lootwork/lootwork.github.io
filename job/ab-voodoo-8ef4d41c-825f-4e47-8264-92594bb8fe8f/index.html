О VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

Команда

Our Growth team is a transversal entity aiming to maximize and deliver the potential of any mobile product. It combines user acquisition, creative content production, monetization, and the tech capabilities required to turn applications into global hits. With over 150 experts, we operate at the forefront of mobile marketing and ad tech across various types of games (hypercasual, hybrid, 100% IAP).

For this role, you would be joining Paper.io 2 team: we are leaving hypercasual era behind, and building a brand through top-notch quality and polish, including in our creatives.

Роль

- Create mobile video ads that engage millions of people

- Become a creative expert in mobile gaming: benchmark what our competitors are doing, experiment with new ideas

- Develop a deep understanding of how your creations perform and why

- Work within a small creative squad with amazing people from diverse backgrounds and nationalities

- Contribute to transversal initiatives (e.g., testing new tools)

Профиль кандидата

- Fully proficient with video editing and content creation

- Expert in After Effects - Nice to have: 3D softwares and/or Unity

- Creative and have a hacker mindset

- Understand how your creations drive marketing performance

- Fluent in English, both oral and written

- At least 3+ year of experience in a similar role

- Hands-on experience with AI creative tools (Scenario, Seedance, Kling, Veo, Runway, etc.)

- Deeply familiar with the mobile gaming market, in particular, hyper casual and casual
