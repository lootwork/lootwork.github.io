О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

In this role, you’ll be the technical lead for customers and partners across the sales cycle, helping shape integrations around their business outcomes and supporting them through to production. You’ll work closely with commercial and cross-functional teams to move opportunities forward and improve how customers and partners integrate with Airwallex.

- Lead technical conversations with customers and partners from discovery and qualification through solution design, integration, and go-live.

- Shape integrations around customer and partner business outcomes by recommending the right Airwallex APIs, products, scopes, and integration patterns.

- Own customer and partner integrations end to end, coordinating technical project plans, milestones, integration checkpoints, and go-live readiness from sandbox to production.

- Help customers, partners, and partners’ end customers troubleshoot live integrations using API logs, webhooks, error responses, and structured technical diagnosis.

- Turn recurring integration challenges, product feedback, and developer support trends into better documentation, reusable solution assets, and improvements to onboarding and support.

- Partner with Sales, Partnerships, Product, Engineering, Compliance, Implementation, and Support to keep opportunities and integrations on track.

- Track measures such as time to integrate, time to first transaction, and issue resolution time, using what you learn to improve the customer and partner experience.

You’ll work from our Bangalore office four days a week and work from home one day a week.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- 3+ years of experience in solutions engineering, sales engineering, technical account management, integration engineering, or a similar customer- or partner-facing technical role.

- Hands-on experience with REST APIs, webhooks, authentication, and JSON, with the ability to read API documentation and troubleshoot integrations using tools such as Postman, cURL, or logs.

- Working knowledge of at least one programming or scripting language, such as Python, JavaScript/Node.js, or Java, sufficient to read customer and partner code and build sample snippets.

- Experience running technical discovery, translating business and technical requirements into clear recommended solutions, and explaining technical trade-offs to technical and non-technical audiences.

- A structured, methodical approach to diagnosing issues across systems quickly.

- Strong organisation and follow-through when managing multiple integrations at once.

- Excellent written and spoken English, with confidence working with customers, partners, and teammates across time zones.

- A bachelor’s degree in a related field or equivalent practical experience.

Preferred

- Experience in fintech, payments, banking-as-a-service, or embedded finance.

- Experience supporting platform, marketplace, or SaaS integrations.

- Familiarity with payments concepts such as card acquiring, payouts, FX, KYC/KYB onboarding, and reconciliation.

- Exposure to SQL, observability or logging tools, and sandbox-to-production release processes.

- Experience creating developer documentation or customer and partner enablement materials.

- Experience working in a high-growth technology or fintech environment.

YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF

- You own the outcome and you don't wait for permission to fix what's broken.
