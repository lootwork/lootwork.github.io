О роли

The Principal Operations Product Lead, SIPS & Crypto will build and improve the operating model behind SoFi’s Invest and Crypto products as they scale. Reporting to the Director of Operations Enablement, SIPS, Crypto & Member, this is a senior individual contributor role with broad influence across Product, Engineering, Operations, Risk, Compliance, and frontline teams. This role turns product strategy into member ready operations. You will define how products are launched, supported, controlled, and improved. You will represent Operations in product planning, set operational requirements and priorities, and help teams make clear tradeoffs across member experience, risk, cost, and speed. This role is for someone who gets energized by being hands on while thinking two steps ahead.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build the Operating Model:
  • Lead operational readiness for Invest and Crypto launches, major product changes, and new capabilities.
  • Define service models, workflows, controls, exception handling, escalation paths, and launch requirements with Product and Engineering.
  • Establish clear operating procedures for automated and manual work, and identify where better tooling or automation is needed.
  • Design scalable support for investing and digital-asset experiences, including account servicing, trading support, asset transfers, deposits and withdrawals, custody-related workflows, fiat movement, reconciliations, and member exceptions.
  • Partner with Risk, Compliance, Legal, Security, Finance, and Treasury to build controls, audit trails, and safeguards that protect member assets and meet regulatory requirements.
  • Improve the Member and Frontline Experience:
  • Set requirements for frontline readiness, including knowledge content, training, communications, and launch support plans.
  • Turn product and technical requirements into clear guidance for frontline teams.
  • Use member and frontline feedback to identify contact drivers, product defects, manual work, and gaps in self-service.
  • Work with Product and Engineering to prioritize operational improvements, internal tools, and automation that reduce friction and operational risk.
  • Own operational performance
  • Define and monitor key measures of operational health, including contact rate, NPS, first-contact resolution, handling time, error rates, and cost to serve.
  • Investigate performance issues, identify root causes, and recommend product, process, tooling, staffing, or vendor changes.
  • Build the business case for investments that improve member outcomes, efficiency, or control effectiveness.
  • Assess the operational impact of product-roadmap decisions and advise leaders on sequencing, capacity, risk, and implementation tradeoffs.

What you’ll need

  • 10+ years of experience in financial services, fintech, product operations, operations strategy or a similar field.
  • Experience with brokerage, investing, trading, digital assets, stablecoins, payments, or custody products.
  • Deep knowledge of crypto operations or the broader digital-asset ecosystem, including transfers, custody, wallets, fiat movement, reconciliations, and asset safeguards.
  • Strong analytical skills, including experience using operational data to identify issues, quantify impact, and set priorities.
  • Demonstrated ability to lead complex work across Product, Engineering, Operations, Risk, Compliance, and other partners without direct authority.
  • Clear, concise communication skills and the ability to explain operational, technical, and risk topics to senior leaders and non-technical partners.
  • Strong judgment, attention to detail, and comfort working through ambiguity in a fast-moving environment
