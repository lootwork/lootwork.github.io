Scopely is looking for a UI Artist to join our MONOPOLY GO! team in Barcelona (Spain) on a hybrid basis!

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues, or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing and innovating the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people around the world daily.

MONOPOLY GO! is a global phenomenon and the biggest mobile game launch in history, captivating millions of players worldwide. The team is based in Europe and the US and works daily to create captivating new experiences for our players.

UI Artist

Every Monopoly GO! season brings new experiences to life, and UI is where those experiences become clear, expressive, and unmistakably ours. As a UI Artist, you'll turn ideas into polished interfaces that balance visual appeal with intuitive design, helping every screen feel as good as it looks.

Getting that balance right means continually expanding how you approach the work. You'll build fluency with AI and emerging tools alongside strong UI fundamentals, learning where they can broaden exploration, improve iteration, and help you make stronger design decisions, with experienced artists around you to help develop your judgment and approach.

If you're looking for new design challenges, opportunities to broaden your visual and technical skills, and the support to turn strong fundamentals into a more confident and versatile craft, you'll find plenty of room to grow here.

Что предстоит делать

  • Create mockups and polished UI assets for Monopoly GO! features, translating flowcharts and wireframes into usable UI systems, with guidance from a Senior UI Artist.
  • Apply AI-assisted tools within your UI workflow, using sound judgment to identify where they improve quality, iteration, or efficiency.
  • Collaborate with game designers, UX designers, concept artists, animators, and engineers to keep the player experience consistent.
  • Present your UI work clearly within your immediate team, and advocate for strong UI craft.
  • Iterate quickly on feedback to keep your work polished and production-ready.

Кого мы ищем

  • 3+ years practical art/design experience in games, preferably mobile.
  • Demonstrated fluency with AI-assisted tools relevant to UI production, with experience applying them to real production tasks while maintaining quality.
  • Strong graphic design and illustration skills for casual/stylized games, with experience in typography and logo design.
  • Solid understanding of UI/UX best practices and interaction design fundamentals, backed by mastery of Photoshop, Illustrator, and Figma.
  • Strong cross-functional communication skills, with the ability to iterate toward the best

Bonus

  • Experience navigating brand guidelines and IP stewardship of at least one externally-licensed IP.
  • Unity experience.
  • A passion for mobile gaming.
