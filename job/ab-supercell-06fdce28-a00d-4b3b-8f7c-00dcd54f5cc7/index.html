MARKETING TECH TEAM BUILDS AND RUNS THE TECHNOLOGY BEHIND SUPERCELL'S MARKETING: AUTOMATED USER ACQUISITION (UA) DECISION SYSTEMS, ATTRIBUTION AND MEASUREMENT, CRM AND LIFECYCLE TOOLING, FIRST- AND THIRD-PARTY DATA INTEGRATIONS, MARKETING AND PRIVACY CLIENT SDKS, COMPLIANCE FLOWS, AND MARKETING DATA PIPELINES. THESE SYSTEMS KEEP THE PLAYER EXPERIENCE SMOOTH WHILE GIVING GAMES AND MARKETING TEAMS TRUSTWORTHY DATA AND RELIABLE TOOLS SO MARKETING INVESTMENT LANDS IN THE RIGHT PLACES.

You will lead Marketing Tech within the Marketing Data Team (part of Supercell's Data and Insights organisation), partnering closely with Marketing Data Science on measurement, predictive modelling, and marketing decision support. Together, we support all Supercell games (Brawl Stars, Clash of Clans, Clash Royale, Hay Day, and new games) and every marketing function helping them with data, tools and automation.

Roughly half of this role is cross-team project ownership and stakeholder leadership; the other half is people leadership, MarTech direction, and shifting how we work through production automation and AI workflows that redesign recurring work for game teams, marketers, and engineers. Reporting to the Head of Marketing Data, you will lead and grow a distributed team of seasoned engineers.

Что предстоит делать

- Lead and grow the team. Hire, manage, and support an employee-and-contractor engineering mix across locations. Build lasting ownership over what ships and strong internal visibility for MarTech's impact across Supercell.

- Own and prioritise the MarTech roadmap. Set and ruthlessly prioritise the 3-24 month roadmap across automation, attribution, marketing and community data, and lifecycle tooling. Own platform adoption, production reliability, and cost, partnering with engineers on technical trade-offs to decide what to build, buy, improve, refactor, or retire.

- Drive cross-team delivery. Align game teams, game technology, Legal, Data Platform, Marketing, and external partners across regions and time zones, turning ambiguous asks into scoped execution plans with clear owners.

- Make every new game launch-ready. Build customisable, repeatable playbooks and tooling so new games launch with working attribution, compliance, and UA technology on day one.

- Own marketing privacy engineering. Run shared consent and initialisation systems across multi-level player consent, age gates, ATT, and marketing SDKs, partnering with Legal, game and platform technology. Keep first-session friction low while making consent and signal loss clear and improvable.

- Build production automation and agentic workflows. Redesign recurring work, from testing and feature-request triage to campaign operations, and deliver bounded AI-automated decisions for UA and lifecycle systems within agreed limits, backed by monitoring, approvals, rollback, and clear ownership for budget-moving actions. Match the mechanism to the problem (AI assistance vs autonomous agents), measure outcomes, and expand autonomy with evidence and guardrails.

Что у вас должно быть

- Lead a distributed team of seasoned engineers. Proven track record managing and developing senior engineers across locations and time zones. You foster team cohesion, coach engineers on stakeholder communication, and know when to step in versus delegate.

- Turn ambiguity into business impact. You cut through competing demands, build alignment across diverse stakeholders, and drive messy cross-team problems to shipped outcomes, staying candid about trade-offs, risks, and uncertainty.

- Bring relevant domain and compliance depth. You have led engineering initiatives in data platforms, marketing automation, MarTech, AdTech, e-commerce, consumer mobile, or adjacent domains, and can ramp quickly on mobile UA and attribution needs. You also have practical experience partnering with Legal and compliance teams on privacy or regulated data requirements.

- Have strong technical understanding. You understand data pipelines, partner APIs, event streaming, server-to-server (S2S) integrations, data quality, governance, cloud tech, and mobile SDKs well enough to ask sharp questions, spot risks, and evaluate trade-offs, while trusting senior engineers to own architectural decisions and execution.

- Have shipped production automation and practical AI. You have put reliable automation into production, driven real team adoption so people depend on it, and delivered practical AI workflows with clear value, failure modes, and human-in-the-loop guardrails.

- Put players first and thrive with autonomy. You balance player trust and smooth in-game experience with marketing signal quality, make progress without rigid structure, and learn fast when wrong.

Будет плюсом

- Background in mobile gaming or consumer mobile apps

- Experience leading a dedicated MarTech or AdTech team

- Experience in user acquisition (UA) tech and data (e.g. attribution, measurement partners, or ad network integrations)

- Experience operating autonomous AI agents in production

- Hands-on data or software engineering background

Где предстоит работать

You can be based in either Helsinki or London. Expect significant time at our Helsinki HQ with key stakeholders, including regular travel if based in London.
