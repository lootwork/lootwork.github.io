Scopely is seeking a technically rigorous Finance Engineer to join the team behind MONOPOLY GO!, one of the fastest-growing mobile games in the world. This is a rare opportunity to sit at the intersection of finance and engineering inside a dynamic, rapidly scaling global gaming company — building the systems, pipelines, and automation that the game's finance organization runs on. The role is based in Culver City, CA, on a hybrid basis.

MONOPOLY GO! is a global phenomenon and the biggest mobile game launch in history, captivating millions of players worldwide.

In this role you will own the technical backbone of Product Finance for MONOPOLY GO!: the data models behind OpEx reporting, the automation that drives month-end close, the pipelines that feed headcount and cost allocations, and the AI-assisted tooling that replaces manual reporting work. You will write the code, build the integrations, and design the data structures — then put them into production and maintain them. Critically, the people who use what you build are analysts and finance managers, not engineers, so you will be judged as much on whether your tools get adopted as on whether they work.

To succeed in this position, you must be able to move fluidly between a spreadsheet, a SQL console, and a Python script, and you must care as much about reconciling to the penny as you do about clean, reusable tooling. This role offers a unique blend of financial engineering and deep analytics, operating at the scale of a standalone company.

You'll work directly with Finance Managers, the VP of Monopoly GO Finance, and Scopely's data and systems teams. We prioritize your growth; you will receive strong support from senior partners during your ramp-up, ensuring you have the mentorship and guidance needed to navigate Scopely's global portfolio effectively.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build, Don't Just Report: You'll design and ship the reporting infrastructure — OpEx models, accrual engines, headcount and allocation pipelines — rather than rebuilding the same workbook every month.
  • AI-Powered by Default: This is an AI-first FP&A team. You will be expected to use Claude, Claude Code, and agentic tooling at an expert level to automate recurring deliverables, and to set the standard for how the wider finance organization does the same.
  • Product, Not Scripts: Your users are the finance team. The win condition is an analyst running your tool on their own and trusting the number that comes out — so you'll own the usability of what you ship, not just its correctness.
  • Top Talent Environment: Join a high-caliber team with backgrounds in consulting, banking, private equity, and high-growth tech from companies such as Amazon, Google, and Netflix.
  • Real Scale, Real Data: MONOPOLY GO! generates data volumes that break manual processes. The systems you build will be load-bearing for the game's senior leadership.
  • Career Trajectory: High performers have a path to fast promotions and will gain experience in complex modeling, financial systems architecture, and automation across Scopely's game portfolio.
  • Learning Culture: Access to internal leadership development, mentorship, and cross-functional mobility across Scopely's global portfolio.
