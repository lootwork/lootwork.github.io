Scopely is looking for an Associate Product Marketing Manager to join our Monopoly GO! Game in Culver City on a hybrid basis, working 2 days a week from the hub.

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues, or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing and innovating the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people around the world daily.

Monopoly GO! is a worldwide phenomenon, reaching tens of millions of players worldwide. This role is an opportunity to be part of a world-class team of marketers leading promotional efforts for a multi-billion-dollar game. The team is based in Canada, Europe and the US, and works every day to create captivating new experiences for our players.

Что предстоит делать

  • Support GTM planning and campaign execution across seasonal content, in-game events, new product features, and major IP collaboration launches, partnering with Product, LiveOps, UA, CRM, Community, and other cross-functional teams to keep launches on track and unblock execution.
  • Drive creative development for marketing campaigns, including trailers, key art, User Acquisition creatives, and other promotional assets. Act as the primary point of contact between internal creative teams, external agencies, and marketing stakeholders to manage timelines, feedback, approvals, and asset delivery.
  • Support app stores promotional initiatives, including featuring opportunities, in-app event cards,co-marketing campaigns across App Store and Google Play.
  • Support major IP collaborations, partnering closely with external licensors and internal stakeholders throughout campaign development. Serve as the primary point of contact for licensor reviews, approvals, and day-to-day communications to ensure assets meet partner guidelines and deadlines.
  • Become an expert in MONOPOLY GO!, its roadmap, and live operations, using deep product knowledge to support marketing initiatives, partnership opportunities, and cross-functional decision making.

Кого мы ищем

  • Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business, Communications, Advertising, or a related field.
  • 2–3 years of experience in product marketing, brand marketing, project management, or a similar role.
  • Passion for games and a strong desire to learn product marketing in a live-service environment.
  • Excellent organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple projects, stakeholders, and deadlines simultaneously.
  • A proactive, self-starter mindset with strong attention to detail and a willingness to take ownership of projects from planning through execution.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills, with experience collaborating across cross-functional teams and external partners.
  • Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, evolving environment and adapt as priorities shift.
  • Experience supporting creative development, marketing campaigns, or go-to-market initiatives is a plus.
  • Experience working with licensed IPs, platform partnerships (App Store/Google Play), or User Acquisition is a plus.
  • Experience within the gaming or entertainment industry is preferred but not required. Experience working on AAA or mobile games is a plus.
