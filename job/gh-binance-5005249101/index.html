Responsibility

  • Support the OTC desk in driving trade volume by engaging with clients to understand their liquidity and trading needs, and assisting with trade execution across all OTC products
  • Learn and assist with pricing and execution across multiple OTC products using internal trading systems, under the guidance of senior traders
  • Work closely with the Sales team to identify new OTC clients and business opportunities
  • Assist with ad-hoc tasks related to trade investigations, risk management, post-trade analysis, and algorithm performance analysis
  • Collaborate with trade operations and the developers team to improve middle-office operations, reporting, and internal pricing and execution systems

Требования

  • Recently completed a Bachelor's degree (Master's preferred) in finance, engineering, mathematics, or a related field
  • Prior internship or up to 3 years of experience in trading, sales & trading, or financial markets preferred; exposure to the OTC trade lifecycle is a plus
  • Foundational understanding of crypto markets, liquidity concepts; eagerness to learn pricing and execution methods and the roles of exchanges, liquidity providers, and market makers
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills; self-motivated and detail-oriented
  • Interest in the crypto/DeFi industry is highly preferred
