This is Adyen

Adyen provides payments, data, and financial products in a single solution for customers like Meta, Uber, H&M, and Microsoft - making us the financial technology platform of choice. At Adyen, everything we do is engineered for ambition.

For our teams, we create an environment with opportunities for our people to succeed, backed by the culture and support to ensure they are enabled to truly own their careers. Authentication & Tokenization Team

Operating at the intersection of high-grade security and conversion optimization, our Authentication and Tokenization systems deliver robust solutions for global commerce. The Adyen Tokenization solution securely vaults payment credentials to enable seamless, friction-free checkout experiences worldwide while ensuring adherence to localized market regulations. Our Authentication engine dynamically optimizes transaction flows to eliminate checkout friction while maintaining strict compliance.

Both products are core pillars of Adyen Uplift, an advanced, AI-powered payment optimization suite designed to drive peak performance for global enterprise merchants. By capitalizing on Adyen's vast, unparalleled global payments data, Adyen Uplift offers intelligent, AI-powered solutions that help businesses maximize payment conversions, streamline fraud management, and reduce the overall cost of transactions.

Based in one of the world's most dynamic fintech hubs, our engineering team navigates intricate compliance frameworks, including the RBI's Card-on-File Tokenization (CoFT) and multi-factor authentication mandates. We design and scale low-latency, highly resilient distributed systems that process millions of daily transactions. Joining this team means taking complete, end-to-end ownership of mission-critical architecture that directly impacts enterprise merchants and consumers across APAC, Europe, and the Americas.
