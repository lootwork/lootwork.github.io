О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the only unified payments and financial platform for global businesses. Powered by our unique combination of proprietary infrastructure and software, we empower over 250,000 businesses worldwide – including Brex, Navan, Qantas, SHEIN and many more – with fully integrated solutions to manage everything from business accounts, payments, spend management and treasury, to embedded finance at a global scale.

Proudly founded in Melbourne, we have a team of over 2,300 of the brightest and most innovative people in tech across 27 offices around the globe. Valued at US$11 billion and backed by world-leading investors including T. Rowe Price, Visa, Mastercard, Robinhood Ventures, Sequoia, Salesforce Ventures, DST Global, and Lone Pine Capital, Airwallex is leading the charge in building the global payments and financial platform of the future. If you’re ready to do the most ambitious work of your career, join us.

ACCOUNT EXECUTIVE, SME & GROWTH

The Account Executive team is a core driver of growth within Airwallex. In this role, you will be responsible for driving new business across our Acquiring, Card, FX, and Payment API product suite, targeting eCommerce retailers, marketplaces, online travel agents, and other high-potential verticals.

As an Account Executive, you will combine deep payments expertise with a strong commercial mindset to deliver consistent results across the SME & Growth segment.

We are seeking individuals who bring high energy, strong ownership, and a growth mindset, coupled with exceptional emotional intelligence and a passion for mentoring others.

Join our team of passionate tech sales professionals and help drive growth and innovation in the dynamic fintech landscape. Your leadership, experience, and execution will be instrumental in empowering businesses across Australia with Airwallex’s cutting-edge solutions.

Обязанности

- Proactively identify, hunt, and convert new business opportunities across Australia, leveraging your network, industry expertise, and strategic prospecting.

- Own and drive the full sales cycle end-to-end, from prospecting through to close, across SME Accounts accounts.

- Lead complex commercial negotiations, partnering closely with Legal, Finance, and Strategy to execute agreements.

- Build, manage, and maintain a high-quality pipeline, while contributing to the definition and evolution of sales best practices for the Australian business.

WHAT YOU WILL BRING

- Proven success in tech sales, with a strong track record of consistently exceeding quotas and driving new business growth.

- Demonstrated ability to operate as a trusted individual contributor, with the maturity and leadership capability to influence team outcomes.

- Exceptional communication and rapport-building skills, with the ability to engage stakeholders at all levels, from operators to senior executives.

- Strong commercial acumen and negotiation skills, with experience navigating complex sales cycles.

- A highly proactive and independent mindset, with a strong sense of ownership and accountability for results.

- A collaborative approach, with the ability to work cross-functionally to deliver best-in-class customer outcomes.

- A passion for mentoring and developing others, with a willingness to invest in team success beyond your own deals.

- The ability to simplify complex solutions and articulate clear, compelling value propositions tailored to customer needs.

- High attention to detail and a strategic mindset when crafting proposals and closing opportunities.

Airwallex does not accept unsolicited resumes from search firms/recruiters.
