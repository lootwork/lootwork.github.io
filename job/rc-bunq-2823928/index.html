At bunq, we’re on a mission to Make Life Easy for our users by creating the most user-centric banking experience out there. That starts with keeping their data safe. As AI makes attackers faster, we’re seeing more critical zero-day vulnerabilities than ever, and we need to stay ahead of them.

That’s where you come in. As our Security Engineer, you’ll own how 700 colleagues get secure access to our systems, and you’ll help defend bunq against attacks as they happen. You’ll design and build security processes that AI can’t run on its own, and automate everything that it can.

Take Ownership 🚀

You’ll keep bunq secure, from access control to incident response. Here’s how you’ll do it:

  • Own access control governance in Okta Identity Governance, automating how access is granted, revoked and reviewed, so every employee and contractor has the right access from day one.
  • Investigate and triage security incidents and vulnerability findings, decide whether they’re malicious, and escalate or fix them fast.
  • Drive the replacement of our security monitoring system with an AI-based platform that detects and stops threats automatically, and build automations so the team can focus on real security issues.
