Discord has a highly engaged community of millions of daily active users who use the platform for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does: play video games. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming, and we are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to hang out before, during, and after playing games.

We are looking for a Senior Manager, Developer Solutions who is passionate about building partnerships in the games industry that accelerate adoption of Discord’s engagement and monetization solutions (social layer SDK, Nitro Rewards, Social Commerce, Collectibles). You will build partnership and sales strategies, as well as lead deal execution, including sourcing and negotiating complex, high-impact partnerships. You will interact with senior executives at our partner organizations and be a trusted evangelist for Discord’s products and priorities. Internally, you will be an advocate for our partners and bring the “voice of the developer” to inform business and product decisions. This is a senior-level individual contributor role and will report into the VP of Developer Solutions.

Что предстоит делать

  • Identify high-fit games and developers for Discord’s Social SDK, commerce, and analytics offerings
  • Own a territory / book of business of AAA and AA game developers with quarterly goals tied to your performance
  • Own the sales / deal process from prospecting, qualification, decision, terms negotiation to deal close
  • Understand developer needs in regard to monetization, user growth, and social mechanics. This includes identifying the optimal path and selection for third party technology adoption
  • Be a subject matter expert on the team around deal construction for player rewards in gaming, especially when tied to subscription products and monetization
  • Build and manage a healthy, predictable pipeline through outbound prospecting, targeted outreach, and strategic account mapping. Fully document your progress using CRM tools
  • Lead strategy, structuring, and negotiation of key partnership agreements, including developing clear commercial frameworks and defining technical scope.
  • Align GTM and execution with leaders across Product, Engineering, Ad Sales, Operations, Marketing, Finance, Legal and Executive Leadership; remove roadblocks and ensure partner success and achieve defined KPIs.
  • Work alongside Customer Success to identify upsell / cross-sell opportunities and deepen relationships within existing customers.

What you should have

  • 5-7+ years of proven experience in business development, sales and partnerships experience
  • 3+ years of experience in structuring and closing high impact deals in the player rewards and consumer subscriptions categories
  • Experience in enterprise SaaS / SDK adoption, game developer tools, analytics / insights sales or related roles at software or platform companies is a plus
  • Strong track record of winning new logos and closing complex deals with enterprise customers
  • Proven track record sourcing, negotiating, and scaling complex partnerships with measurable impact on revenue, adoption, and monetization
  • Proven ability to work with internal cross-functional teams at early stages to define and articulate partner value within the context of Discord
  • Deep knowledge and experience in the gaming space, specifically
  • Demonstrated ability to lead cross-functional execution with clear ownership and accountability
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with experience producing clear, executive-ready materials (e.g., strategy memos, deal summaries, business cases, and partner communications)

Candidates must reside in or be willing to relocate to the San Francisco Bay Area (Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, Napa, San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Solano, and Sonoma counties) or New York Metropolitan Area. Relocation assistance may be available.
