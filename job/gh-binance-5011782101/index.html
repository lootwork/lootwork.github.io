О роли

We are seeking a Market Manipulation Risk Strategist to join our Risk Strategy team. You will build and refine detection and control strategies against market abuse across spot and derivatives markets including spoofing, layering, wash trading, self-trading, momentum ignition, pump-and-dump, cross-market manipulation, and misappropriation schemes. You will translate trading microstructure knowledge into surveillance logic, work closely with data scientists to operationalize detection models, and partner with Compliance and Market Surveillance on regulatory frameworks.

Обязанности

  • Design and maintain risk strategies detecting manipulative and abusive trading behaviors across spot, perpetual futures, options products and other trading products.
  • Conduct in-depth investigations into suspected manipulation rings: order flow analysis, self-match/crossing patterns, order book microstructure anomalies, and cross-account linkage
  • Build behavioral profiles for emerging manipulation typologies in low-liquidity instruments, leveraged products, and leveraged liquidation cascades
  • Quantify risk impact (market distortion, potential losses, exposure concentration) to support strategy prioritization and management reporting
  • Partner with trading product, compliance, and other related teams on surveillance rule and control measure design

Required Qualifications

  • Hands-on derivatives trading experience: solid understanding of futures, perpetual contracts, and options mechanics including leverage, margining, liquidation mechanisms, insurance fund mechanisms, and ADL (Auto-Deleveraging) settings.
  • Trading risk management experience, with a focus on market manipulation detection and prevention of abusive schemes (spoofing/layering, wash trading, front-running, insider/dealer misappropriation)
  • Strong data analysis skills: SQL required; Python preferred; able to independently design and validate detection logic against order-level and trade-level datasets
  • Ability to reason from the attacker's perspective: how manipulators construct positions, disguise intent, and exploit product rules
  • Experience collaborating across risk, compliance, product, or legal functions

Будет плюсом

  • Work experience at a crypto exchange, brokerage, or trading venue in surveillance, trading risk, or market conduct roles
  • Personal quantitative trading track record: candidates who actively trade derivatives are encouraged to share verifiable account performance (PnL statements) as evidence of practical market understanding
  • Familiarity with traditional finance market abuse frameworks and surveillance vendors
  • Familiarity with crypto on-chain fund-flow tracing.
  • Experience with graph analysis / entity-linking and crypto on-chain fund-flow tracing for uncovering collusion networks across accounts.
  • Understanding of the application of market anomaly signals, such as VPIN, HHI.
