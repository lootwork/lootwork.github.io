О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

You’ll define the design strategy and execution for Airwallex’s end-to-end developer experience, spanning APIs, SDKs, developer tools, documentation, ecosystem integrations, and white-label customer management tools. You’ll shape how customers understand and use complex financial products and workflows, partnering with product and engineering leaders to frame problems, prioritise opportunities, and embed design early in product strategy.

- Define the design strategy for Airwallex’s developer experience across APIs, SDKs, developer tools, documentation, ecosystem integrations, and white-label customer management tools.

- Shape how customers understand and use complex financial products and workflows by framing problems, prioritising opportunities, and embedding design early in product strategy.

- Establish moving money into, within, and out of Airwallex as a user experience foundation that other product areas, including Spend Management and Subscription Billing, can build on.

- Lead end-to-end experience design across research, object modelling, interaction design, testing, and UI design, while raising the quality bar for complex, data-rich experiences across desktop, mobile web, and native mobile apps.

- Build a collaborative, growth-oriented design practice through stronger feedback rituals, shared standards, design-system adoption, and product-engineering partnerships across regions.

- Lead and grow a high-performing team of senior and staff-level product designers, setting clear expectations for impact, craft, and ownership.

You’ll work from our San Francisco office on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- 12+ years of experience in product or UX design, including significant experience with complex, system-heavy products such as B2B SaaS, developer platforms, financial products, or payments products.

- 4+ years of experience leading designers, including senior designers or design managers.

- Experience with developer platforms, APIs, SDKs, and technical tooling, ideally in fintech, payments, financial infrastructure, or embedded finance.

- Deep strengths in interaction design, information architecture, and UX for complex workflows and data-heavy products.

- Systems thinking across flows, states, permissions, object relationships, and edge cases, with the ability to work at both screen and system level.

- Proven ability to set a design vision, align stakeholders, and lead multi-quarter initiatives across multiple domains and teams.

- Confidence debating trade-offs with senior product and engineering leaders and advocating clearly and pragmatically for customer outcomes.

- Experience partnering closely with user research and quantitative analysis to inform decisions, validate designs, and measure impact.

- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to make complex financial and technical concepts accessible to diverse audiences.

- Comfort working in a high-growth environment with evolving priorities and ambiguous constraints.

Preferred

- Background working with API products and developer-facing experiences.

- A degree in Design, HCI, Computer Science, or a related field.
