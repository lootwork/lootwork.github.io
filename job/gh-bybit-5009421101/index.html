О роли

We are looking for an experienced HR Business Partner to support our global Legal & Compliance business unit, partnering closely with the Chief Legal & Compliance Officer (CLCO) and senior functional leaders.

The ideal candidate brings strong HRBP expertise, hands-on experience supporting Legal and/or Compliance functions in a regulated industry, and the ability to navigate complex organizational and people challenges. This role requires a commercially minded, proactive HR partner who can confidently advise senior leadership and drive effective people solutions.

Обязанности

  • Partner closely with the CLCO and senior leaders across Legal, Compliance, AML, Financial Crime, and related functions, providing strategic and hands-on HR support.
  • Understand the business priorities, organizational structure, regulatory environment, and talent challenges of Legal & Compliance functions, translating them into effective people strategies.
  • Advise leadership on organization design, workforce planning, succession planning, talent development, and organizational effectiveness.
  • Drive performance management, talent reviews, promotions, retention, and leadership development initiatives.
  • Manage complex employee relations matters, including disciplinary cases, performance issues, employee disputes, and sensitive investigations.
  • Partner with HR COEs and Talent Acquisition on critical hiring, compensation, organizational changes, and people-related initiatives.
  • Leverage HR data and business insights to identify talent gaps, organizational risks, and opportunities for improvement.
  • Ensure effective implementation of HR policies and practices in alignment with applicable employment regulations and internal governance standards.

Требования

  • 5+ years of HRBP experience, with proven experience partnering with senior business leaders.
  • Hands-on HRBP experience supporting Legal, Compliance, Risk, AML, or Financial Crime functions, preferably within banking, financial services, fintech, or cryptocurrency.
  • Strong understanding of Legal & Compliance organizational structures, specialist talent requirements, and regulatory-driven business challenges.
  • Proven experience in organization design, workforce planning, talent management, performance management, and complex employee relations.
  • Strong stakeholder management skills, with the confidence and maturity to advise and challenge C-level executives and senior leadership.
  • Commercially minded, analytical, and hands-on, with strong problem-solving skills and the ability to operate independently.
  • Fluent English is mandatory, with excellent communication and executive stakeholder engagement skills. Mandarin Chinese is a strong advantage.
  • Experience working in a fast-paced, international, and highly regulated environment is preferred.
