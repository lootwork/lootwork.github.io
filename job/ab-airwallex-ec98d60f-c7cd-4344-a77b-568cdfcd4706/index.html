Что предстоит делать

Based out of our Melbourne office, this role will be focused on generating and qualifying leads that will build Airwallex’s ANZ sales pipeline. As a Sales Development Representative (SDR), you are excited about engaging with prospects to find out about their needs, adept at identifying high-value opportunities, and capable of managing early sales funnel activities.

This role is based in Melbourne.

Обязанности

- You are used to delivering value in complex situations and are energised by learning about new and existing products.

- Initiate contact with potential customers through diverse outreach strategies including email or cold calling and follow up on designated touch points in a customer journey

- Move quality leads through the funnel while connecting them to a salesperson and arranging meetings

- Develop relationships with prospects to uncover needs through effective questioning to qualify interest and viability to prepare hand-off to sales

- Update activity and contact information within the CRM system and support reporting efforts

- Collaborate and provide feedback and insights to cross functional teams, including Marketing to help improve targeting and messaging

- Work closely with your designated Account Executive on the handover & first call

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

- Track record of top performance or prior success in an SDR or BDR-related role

- Superior verbal and written communication skills

- Strong interest in technology and/or financial services

- Self starter who can operate in a hyper growth environment

- Bachelor's degree

Будет плюсом

- Target oriented - you are motivated by stretch targets that facilitate business growth and reward you for direct impact

- Customer focused - you are excited to learn about your prospect’s business and rely on data to help solve issues

- Process driven - you enjoy developing, documenting, and following procedures to ensure they are efficient, scalable, and defensible

- A team player - you are a natural at building relationships with your colleagues and can also work independently at ease

- A master prioritiser - you leverage sound judgment to quickly assess all the tasks at hand and expertly prioritise them

- Obsessively curious - you know when to dig a little deeper to get to the bottom of a problem and solve it; in doing so, you ask the right questions necessary to arrive at the solution
