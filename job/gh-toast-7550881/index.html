Кто мы

Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy.

Are you bready* for a change?

Toast currently serves hundreds of multi-unit brands, including Caribou Coffee, Choice Hotels, Craveworthy Brands, WaBa Grill, Nothing Bundt Cakes, Papa Gino's & D'Angelo, Costa Vida Fresh Mexican Grill, and The Human Bean, among others. We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join our enterprise drive-thru team and help us build the best-in-class drive-thru solutions that enable significant future growth. You will get to work across the entire Toast platform, including our scalable backend, customer-facing web apps, and everything else in between. As a member of the Drive-Thru Enterprise team, you will help ensure our products are built to meet the evolving needs of these customers. You can read more on our Toast for Enterprise Page.

О this roll* (Responsibilities)

  • Design, build, deploy, and maintain highly resilient and scalable features across Toast’s enterprise management products
  • Hands-on design and implementation of Android applications in Kotlin
  • Lead complex projects that improve the functionality and reliability of our products
  • Ensure quality by building automated testing for changes
  • Contribute to the overall quality of the Android and backend systems
  • Collaborate with UX, PM, QA, and Engineering to define, design, and build best-in-class solutions in a complex and fast-moving environment
  • Guide the growth & development of peer engineers with mentoring and coaching

Do you have the right ingredients*? (Requirements)

  • 5+ years of prior experience developing and maintaining Android applications
  • Experience with distributed systems, enterprise messaging systems, and/or microservices
  • Proficient with modern API standards and technologies (e.g., REST, GraphQL)
  • Strong leadership and mentoring skills
  • Excellent communication skills, with the ability to articulate complex technical concepts to diverse audiences
  • Passionate about driving innovation and continuously improving engineering practices
  • Proven track record of technical leadership and contribution to complex projects
  • Has a growth mindset with an emphasis on improving yourself and your team
  • Balances getting things done with platform stability
  • Bachelor’s degree or higher in computer science

What we use to make our dish (Our Tech Stack)

  • A core of Java & Kotlin based backend services.
  • React SPAs leveraging Tailwind, GraphQL, and Storybook.
  • Android app built in Java & Kotlin.
  • PostgreSQL and DynamoDB as core persistence.
  • Pulsar, Camel, and RabbitMQ for message-based integrations.
  • And many more technologies that help us build a top-class platform.
