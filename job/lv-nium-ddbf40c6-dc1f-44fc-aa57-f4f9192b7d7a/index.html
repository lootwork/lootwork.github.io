О роли

As the Senior Manager, Regulatory Compliance & Deputy MLRO (Lithuania), you will play a key role within Nium's global Compliance function, acting as a trusted advisor to the business and Deputy to the Head of Compliance/MLRO for Lithuania.

This is a highly visible position that combines regulatory compliance, financial crime prevention, sanctions oversight, regulatory engagement, and strategic risk management. You will work closely with local and global stakeholders to ensure that Nium's operations continue to meet Lithuanian and EU regulatory requirements while supporting business growth and innovation.

You will have the opportunity to contribute both locally and globally, providing subject matter expertise on Lithuanian regulatory requirements and supporting enterprise-wide compliance initiatives.

Основные обязанности

  • Support the management and continuous enhancement of NIUM's Lithuanian Compliance and AML/CFT framework, policies, procedures, and controls.
  • Act as Deputy MLRO and support the Head of Compliance/MLRO in fulfilling regulatory obligations and maintaining effective AML/CFT and sanctions frameworks.
  • Perform and maintain enterprise-wide and local AML/CFT risk assessments, ensuring risks remain appropriately identified, assessed, documented, and mitigated.
  • Lead and coordinate annual AML/CFT independent audits and regulatory reviews, including remediation activities where required.
  • Assist in preparing quarterly, annual, and ad-hoc compliance reporting for senior management and the Management Board.
  • Review complex compliance and financial crime escalations, including high-risk customer cases, suspicious activity investigations, and potential reporting obligations.
  • Prepare and submit Suspicious Transaction Reports (STRs) and other required notifications to the Lithuanian Financial Crime Investigation Service (FCIS/FNTT), where appropriate.
  • Manage communications and engagement with the Bank of Lithuania, FCIS/FNTT, and other competent authorities, including regulatory inquiries, inspections, and information requests.
  • Respond to law enforcement requests and support internal investigations where required.
  • Ensure ongoing compliance with applicable AML/CFT, sanctions, payments, and broader regulatory requirements.
  • Oversee sanctions screening outcomes and make decisions relating to asset freezing, sanctions compliance measures, and reporting obligations.
  • Prepare and submit mandatory regulatory reports, including periodic and ad-hoc reporting to regulatory authorities.
  • Deliver compliance and financial crime training and awareness programs across the organization.
  • Support new product launches, change initiatives, and strategic projects by providing regulatory and financial crime compliance expertise.
  • Provide Lithuanian regulatory expertise and local market insight to global compliance and business initiatives.
  • Assist the Head of Compliance/MLRO in any additional responsibilities necessary to ensure an effective compliance framework.
