Senior Data Engineer - Platform

Porto Office - Hybrid: 4 days in the office, 1 day working from home

Join Our Data Team at Ebury Porto Office.

Ebury's strategic growth plan would not be possible without our Data team and we are seeking a Senior Data Engineer to join our Data Platform team!

Our mission is to develop maintain and improve Ebury's Data Platform with a focus on building scalable, robust, and innovative frameworks.

As a Senior Data Platform Engineer, you will work closely with the rest of the team to help develop and improve the Data Platform.

Что мы предлагаем

  • Competitive salary and benefits package
  • Discretionary bonus based on performance
  • Continued personal development through training and certification
  • We are Open Source friendly, following Open Source principles in our internal projects and encouraging contributions to external projects

Обязанности

  • Oversee the design, development, and maintenance of a robust data platform to be consumed across the company
  • Collaborate with stakeholders to understand their data needs and deliver actionable insights
  • Drive innovation and explore emerging technologies to enhance our data capabilities
  • Establish and enforce data governance policies and standards to ensure data quality and security

О вас

  • Proficiency in Python for data transformation and validation
  • Proficiency in SQL for querying and managing large datasets, as well as experience working with structured and unstructured data sources
  • Data engineering and modelling skills such as slowly changing dimensions and dimensional modelling
  • Graph database modelling encouraged
  • Hands-on experience with data warehousing, data lakes, and cloud platforms, including designing and implementing data solutions at scale
  • Self-motivation and curiosity, with a desire to learn and grow in a fast-paced environment, continuously improving both technical and business skills
  • Experience with GCP (Google Cloud Platform) tools and services, such as BigQuery, Cloud Run, and Cloud Functions
  • Fluency in English (Spanish, a plus)
