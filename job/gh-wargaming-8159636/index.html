О вакансии

We’re excited to invite you to join the World of Warships team at Wargaming, where we’re looking for a Senior Frontend Developer.

As a member of the World of Warships team, you’ll have the opportunity to collaborate with a talented and passionate group of professionals, contribute to creating engaging and seamless experiences for our players, and make a meaningful impact on the direction and success of the game.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own and develop frontend/UI systems within the product and web ecosystem, including reusable components, UI patterns, frameworks, tools, and shared frontend solutions.
  • Develop and maintain interactive web experiences, including event pages, web adventures, game mechanics, mini-games, widgets, portal modules, and other content-driven experiences.
  • Translate UI/UX designs and wireframes into robust, responsive implementations that accurately reflect the intended user experience.
  • Develop frontend solutions using React, TypeScript, Composition API, and related tooling, while maintaining and gradually improving existing vanilla JavaScript code.
  • Work with REST APIs, and data-driven interfaces, implementing asynchronous client-server interactions and dynamic content.
  • Implement complex animations and interactive mechanics, with a strong focus on UX, responsiveness, and frontend performance.
  • Optimize animation-heavy and mobile-heavy interfaces, including asset loading, lazy loading, rendering, browser behavior, and overall frontend performance.
  • Ensure reliable functionality across devices, browsers, platforms, regional versions, and language configurations.
  • Investigate and resolve complex frontend issues, including UI behavior, rendering, input handling, browser compatibility, integrations, performance, and production stability.
  • Refactor and improve existing and legacy codebases, maintaining high standards of production quality, maintainability, and scalability.
  • Collaborate with UX/UI designers using Figma to verify implementations, discuss technical feasibility, and prepare or export assets when required.
  • Participate in technical and design discussions, code reviews, and architectural decisions, contributing to engineering standards and best practices.
  • Document frontend architectures, APIs, component behavior, integrations, and technical guidelines.
  • Mentor less experienced engineers on frontend development, UI systems, debugging, performance, and maintainability.
  • Collaborate with designers, game and engine engineers, QA, production, product, and other stakeholders to deliver high-quality interactive experiences.

Кого мы ищем

  • 5+ years of commercial frontend development experience with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern frontend technologies.
  • 3+ years of practical experience with React.
  • Strong knowledge of JavaScript (ES6+), TypeScript, HTML5, CSS3, and SCSS.
  • Strong understanding of component-based UI architecture, responsive design, rendering behavior, and cross-browser compatibility.
  • Experience developing reusable UI systems, components, patterns, frameworks, or frontend tools.
  • Experience implementing complex animations and interactive interfaces, with a strong understanding of browser rendering and performance optimization.
  • Experience with mobile web development and responsive interfaces.
  • Experience working with REST APIs, asynchronous client-server interactions, and data-driven interfaces.
  • Experience working with existing or legacy codebases, including refactoring and maintaining production-quality code.
  • Ability to evaluate technical requirements and designs from the perspectives of usability, feasibility, performance, compatibility, integration, and maintainability.
  • Confident use of Figma and ability to collaborate effectively with designers.
  • Ability to work independently, investigate technical problems, and find practical solutions.
  • Strong attention to implementation quality, interface details, consistency, and user experience.
  • Experience participating in code reviews and technical discussions.
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills in multidisciplinary teams.
  • Strong spoken and written English for technical communication and documentation.
  • Russian language skills would be an advantage.

Что поможет выделиться

  • Good sense of visual design, composition, and understanding of UI/UX principles for interactive web interfaces.
  • Experience with Three.js, WebGL, or other 3D/web graphics technologies.
  • Experience with GSAP, Motion, or similar animation libraries.
  • Experience with Node.js for frontend tooling, scripts, and build systems.
  • Experience working on multilingual and multi-regional projects.
  • Experience developing mini-games
  • Experience in the game industry or with game-related web products.
  • Links to relevant projects demonstrating interactive, animation-heavy, or technically complex frontend work.

Формат работы

  • Onsite during probation period and hybrid (3 days of work from the office) after finishing probation period.
