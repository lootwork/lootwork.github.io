Binance is a leading global blockchain ecosystem behind the world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume and registered users. Our mission is to increase the freedom of money and financial access for people around the world.

Требования

  • Optimize user growth & marketing strategies / analysis based on our current global ecosystem
  • Lead full-funnel user journeys analysis: acquisition → activation → conversion → retention
  • Drive multi-channel user acquisition (KOLs, Paid Ads, SEO, referrals, communities, social etc.)
  • Set up & track key metrics: CAC, LTV, retention, conversion, viral coefficient
  • Run rapid A/B tests, iterate growth models, and improve user quality
  • Tailor strategies for local markets with deep user insights
  • Collaborate cross-functionally to bring strategies to life

Обязанности

  • 8+ yrs in user growth / performance / digital marketing
  • Global market experience (MENA, LATAM, SEA, Africa, etc.)
  • Strong data mindset, familiar with growth tools & KPIs
  • Internet, Web3, or fintech background preferred
  • Bilingual English/Mandarin is required to be able to coordinate with overseas partners and stakeholders. Additional languages would be a strong advantage
  • Adaptable, entrepreneurial, and hands-on
