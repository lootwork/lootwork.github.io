О роли

We're looking for a Marketing Operations Manager to join our Marketing Operations, RevOps team at Pleo. In this role, you'll help build the operational infrastructure needed to support our upmarket expansion into Mid-Market and Enterprise segments and be part of our Marketing Ops team as we scale our spend management platform across growth companies in Europe. If you're excited about designing account-based go-to-market systems, establishing AI enablement across operational workflows, and solving complex cross-functional challenges, and are passionate about marketing operations, RevOps, and systems architecture, then this is the opportunity for you!

WHO YOU’LL BE WORKING WITH AND REPORTING TO

You’ll report to our Associate Director, Marketing Operations and work closely with teams in Marketing Operations, RevOps, and Sales Ops. Our team is highly collaborative and dedicated to powering how the marketing team runs—from campaign operations and reporting cadence through to AI adoption and process improvement. You’ll also have the chance to partner with teams across Data, BAT (Business Automation & Tools), CX Ops, and Engineering to ensure success and maintain a single account truth across systems.

Что предстоит делать

As a Marketing Operations Manager, you will

- Build and refine marketing operational frameworks – Lead initiatives to continuously improve campaign workflows, request intake, approval processes, team governance, MQL→MQA routing logic, and SDR/BDR handoff processes.

- Own account intelligence and reporting cadence – Design account scoring models for Mid-Market and Enterprise, maintain executive dashboards, integrate intent/firmographic/behavioral signals into segmentation, and manage account assignment logic.

- Drive AI adoption across Marketing Ops – Translate AI capabilities into operational reality by building processes, governance, and enablement that accelerate execution and scale output within the team.

- Ensure tooling health and systems ownership – Manage HubSpot workflows, automations, and integrations while coordinating with BAT and Engineering on tech stack builds, QA standards, and enterprise tooling requirements.

- Enable team capabilities and documentation habits – Upskill Marketing and Marketing Ops teammates through shared standards, QA checklists, repeatable guides, templates, and onboarding resources to raise the operational floor.

Что вы приносите

You’ll thrive in this role if you have

- HubSpot Fluency – A solid understanding of designing workflows, routing logic, and automation in HubSpot, including its limitations and when to extend outside the platform.

- Account Operations Mindset – Track record of building ABM operational processes, MQL-to-MQA scoring rules, multi-stakeholder buying committee motions, and AI/automation adoption that sticks.

- Sales Ops Partnership – Ability to collaborate seamlessly with Sales Ops on account strategy, territory strategy, handoff logic, and rep productivity without jargon.

- Data & Documentation Habits – Excellent habits in building auditable specs, validating data quality, and diagnosing root causes in data flows alongside Data and BAT teams.

- Experience with multi-touch attribution modeling, lifecycle marketing operations (nurture flows, recycling, engagement scoring), and ABM platforms / account intelligence tools.

- A background in seeing a company move upmarket to Mid-Market/Enterprise and building scalable, long-term GTM operations.

WHY IS THIS ROLE A GOOD FIT FOR YOU

This role is a good fit for you if

- You are an operational thinker who naturally designs sustainable systems, governance, and frameworks rather than executing quick-fix tasks.

- You love working cross-functionally across Marketing, Sales Ops, and Data teams to streamline GTM processes and remove friction.

- You are energized by driving practical AI adoption and upskilling team members through documentation, templates, and shared standards.

This role is not a good fit for you if

- You prefer operating in established, rigid systems rather than designing new account-based infrastructure in ambiguous environments.

- You look at marketing operations as purely executing inbound lead lists without considering account context or buying committees.

- You rely on tribal knowledge rather than maintaining clear, auditable documentation and QA standards for your builds.

HOW YOU’LL DEVELOP IN THIS ROLE

In your first 6 months at Pleo, you’ll

- Lead the launch of our account segmentation model to directly power SDR assignments and Mid-Market/Enterprise routing.

- Collaborate with Data and Marketing Ops teams to clean up attribution, reporting, and lifecycle data so performance metrics are fully trusted.…
