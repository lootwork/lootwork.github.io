О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,300 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

Обязанности

- Conduct audits to gather use cases and UX patterns across our digital products, create reusable component and pattern definitions that can support a diverse range of use cases.

- Identify inconsistencies in existing component usage and design, and deviations from the existing design system

- Work collaboratively with teams to ensure alignment and adoption of a consistent set of components and patterns

- Work collaboratively with developers to implement and quality check components

- Crafting component and pattern usage guidelines

- Enforce processes to guide the appropriate usage and upkeep of the design system

- Champion the value and advantages of the design system to internal stakeholders, advocating for its adoption within our organization, and demonstrating its positive impact on user experience and efficiency.

- Continuously enhance and adapt the design system based on user feedback, industry best practices, and emerging design trends.

Требования

- 7+ years as a designer, with preferably 2+ years of experience maintaining a design system

- A portfolio that demonstrates a deep understanding of product design, craftsmanship, and innovative solutions from conception to launch

- Strong UI design skills with proficiency in Figma

- Content strategy, HTML/CSS knowledge is a bonus

- Excellent methodical and critical thinking skills
