Роль

Paper Party is the multiplayer mode of Paper.io 2. We are looking for a dedicated Game Designer to improve the modes we already have and create new ones from scratch.

All modes will keep the Paper universe, camera and controls. Beyond that, there is a lot of creative freedom. You will explore new rules, objectives, win conditions, pacing and team setups to create a broad range of fun and replayable multiplayer experiences.

Вам предстоит

- Design and prototype new gameplay modes

- Improve existing Paper Party modes

- Define simple, readable rules for one-finger controls

- Iterate quickly through testing, feedback and playtests

- Balance accessibility, competition, pacing and replayability

- Identify weak ideas early and confidently cut or reshape them

- Work closely with the Paper.io 2 team to turn concepts into playable experiences

- Document your designs clearly

- Fine-tune modes directly in Unity whenever possible

This is a hands-on role for someone who enjoys moving quickly from an idea to a playable prototype, testing it and improving it.

Профиль кандидата

- 5+ years of experience in mobile F2P games, designing new games, gameplay modes or prototypes from scratch

- Experience shipping player-facing content, ideally in mobile games

- A strong understanding of multiplayer and competitive gameplay

- A good sense of pacing, clarity and player motivation

- A pragmatic and iterative approach: prototype, test, cut and retry

- The ability to work independently and take full ownership

- Strong English communication and collaboration skills

Будет плюсом

- Strong Unity experience

- Ability to prototype directly in the engine

- Experience with mobile controls, multiplayer or party games

- Experience working on live games or live content
