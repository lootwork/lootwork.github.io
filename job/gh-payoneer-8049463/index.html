О Payoneer

Founded in 2005, Payoneer is the global financial platform that removes friction from doing business across borders, with a mission to connect the world’s underserved businesses to a rising global economy. We’re a community with over 2,500 colleagues all over the world, working to serve customers, and partners in over 190 countries and territories.

By taking the complexity out of the financial workflows–including everything from global payments and compliance to multi-currency and workforce management, to providing working capital and business intelligence–we give businesses the tools they need to work efficiently worldwide and grow with confidence.

Payoneer is becoming an AI-native company, where AI is the operating layer powering how we create products, build software, transform business operations, and serve millions of customers.

We are looking for an exceptional Product Leader to define and lead the product strategy for Payoneer's Core AI Platform—the AI operating layer enabling trusted, scalable AI across Product, Engineering, and Business.

This is one of the company's most strategic product leadership roles. Partnering closely with leaders across Product, Engineering, Data, Architecture, Security, and Business, you will define the platform strategy, lead the first generation of AI-native solutions, and establish reusable patterns that accelerate AI adoption across the company.

You will also shape how Payoneer leverages the rapidly evolving AI ecosystem—making strategic build, buy, partner, and integrate decisions to combine differentiated internal capabilities with best-in-class external technologies into a trusted, enterprise-grade AI platform.

The Core AI Platform enables three strategic outcomes: transforming how products are built, enabling every business function to redesign and automate workflows through AI, creating agentic customer experiences across Payoneer's products.

  • Own the long-term vision, strategy, and roadmap for Payoneer's Core AI Platform.
