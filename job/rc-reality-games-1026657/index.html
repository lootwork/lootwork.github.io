Join us at Reality Games, an ambitious gaming studio based in the iconic Railway Station building in the heart of Krakow’s historic center. With over 25 million players worldwide enjoying our flagship title Landlord Real Estate Tycoon, we're now launching an exciting new project: Monopoly World – an innovative mobile game that transforms the classic Monopoly experience into a real-world adventure. Designed for Unity Mobile, the game combines timeless gameplay with location-based features and global impact.

Role Overview

We're looking for a passionate and experienced Senior UI/UX Artist to help bring our vision to life. In this role, you'll play a critical part in shaping the user experience and interface of Monopoly World, working closely with the Principal UX/UI Mobile Game Artist and Head of Product. You’ll merge creative vision with user-centered design to craft engaging, intuitive, and visually compelling interfaces that delight players around the globe.

Check out our trailer for the Monopoly World project

Key Responsibilities

  • Design intuitive and engaging user interfaces for mobile platforms (iOS/Android).
  • Translate complex game systems into clear, player-friendly UI/UX solutions.
  • Collaborate with the Principal UX/UI Mobile Game Artist and Head of Product to align design elements with gameplay mechanics and user expectations.
  • Create wireframes, user flows, mockups, and high-fidelity prototypes.
  • Conduct user research and usability testing to validate design concepts and iterate based on feedback.
  • Work closely with Unity developers to implement UI elements and ensure visual consistency and performance across devices.
  • Maintain and evolve a cohesive visual language and UX standard across the project.
  • Take ownership of the full UI/UX pipeline—from concept to implementation and iteration.
