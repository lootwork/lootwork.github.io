О роли

We're looking for a talented Unity Animator to join our team and bring our games to life through engaging animations and visual effects. You'll work closely with Art, Development, and Technical Art teams to create, integrate, and optimize animations and VFX, ensuring a polished and engaging player experience.

- Create and implement animations directly in Unity.

- Work with Unity animation systems, particle systems, and VFX.

- Collaborate with developers, artists, and technical artists on animation workflows and integrations.

- Create animation prototypes and video demos to showcase concepts.

- Optimize animations and visual effects for mobile performance.

- Explore AI tools to improve creative and technical workflows.

Профиль кандидата

- Experience in Unity animation within the gaming industry.

- Strong knowledge of Unity animation systems and technical integrations.

- Experience with particle systems and VFX.

- Understanding of mobile game optimization.

- Ability to work closely with Art, Development, and Technical Art teams.

- Creative, detail-oriented, and solution-driven.

- AI-first mindset: Comfortable using AI tools daily to improve workflows and productivity.

- Fluent in English.
