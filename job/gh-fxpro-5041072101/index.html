Join FxPro: a leading international fintech company. Be a part of our expanding international team, with offices in Limassol, London, Nassau, Dubai, and Yerevan. FxPro boasts a diverse workforce of over 600 employees representing 50 nationalities, making it an exciting and dynamic workplace. As an Alternative Payment Method Specialist, you will play a crucial role in ensuring the seamless operation and continuous optimization of our payment systems, driving efficiency, reliability, and smooth transaction processing across various payment methods.

Обязанности

  • Market Trends & Solution Scouting: Stay ahead of industry trends in payments, acquiring, and Alternative Payment Methods (APMs) to proactively identify, evaluate, and recommend new payment solutions for clients.
  • Performance & Routing Optimization: Review transaction data, identify decline issues, and configure payment routing or cascading rules to maximize conversion rates and reduce processing costs.
  • Growth & Competitor Analysis: Drive retention and growth by conducting competitor analysis and expanding the Group's payment coverage across new regions and partner solutions.
  • Provider & Relationship Management: Manage day-to-day relationships with existing and new Payment Service Providers (PSPs)
  • Settlements & Fee Reconciliation: Monitor provider balances, oversee daily settlements, and audit PSP invoices to ensure transaction fees match agreed pricing schedules.
  • Fraud Monitoring & Reporting: Monitor transactions for unusual activity, investigate flagged issues, and analyze payment system data to produce regular performance and failure reports.
  • Disputes & Incident Management: Act as the primary point of contact to escalate and resolve live payment outages or stuck transactions.
  • Testing & Client Journey: Test new payment systems and integrations in staging before live release, while helping improve the client's deposit and withdrawal journeys.
  • Cross-Team Collaboration & Training: Collaborate closely with Tech, Support, Finance, and Partnerships teams, providing internal training and support on new or existing payment functionalities.
  • Documentation & Compliance: Maintain up-to-date documentation of all internal APM processes and uphold all relevant compliance practices, procedures, and regulations.

Требования

  • 2-3 years of experience in financial services, CFD, iGaming, or gambling within payments
  • Strong data analysis skills and high computer literacy (Excel, BI tools)
  • Practical knowledge of payment systems, APMs, and payment flows
  • University degree, preferably in Finance or Economics
  • Good understanding of relevant laws and regulatory requirements
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced, multicultural environment
  • Logical, business-oriented mindset
  • Excellent command of English
