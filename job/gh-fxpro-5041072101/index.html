As an Alternative Payment Method Specialist, you will play a crucial role in ensuring the seamless operation and continuous optimization of our payment systems, driving efficiency, reliability, and smooth transaction processing across various payment methods.

Обязанности

  • Follow developments in payments, acquiring, and alternative payment methods to identify, assess, and recommend new payment solutions for clients
  • Analyse transaction data, identify the causes of declines, and set up payment routing and cascading rules to improve conversion rates and lower processing costs
  • Support client retention and business growth through competitor analysis and by extending the group's payment coverage to new regions and partner solutions
  • Manage day-to-day relationships with current and new Payment Service Providers (PSPs)
  • Monitor provider balances, oversee daily settlements, and audit provider invoices to confirm that transaction fees match the agreed pricing
  • Monitor transactions for unusual activity, investigate flagged cases, and prepare regular reports on payment performance and failures
  • Serve as the main point of contact for escalating and resolving live payment outages and stuck transactions
  • Test new payment systems and integrations in the staging environment before release, and contribute to improving the client deposit and withdrawal journey
  • Work closely with the Tech, Support, Finance, and Partnerships teams, and provide internal training and support on new and existing payment functionality
  • Keep documentation of internal alternative payment processes up to date and ensure adherence to all relevant compliance procedures and regulations

Требования

  • 2-3 years of experience in payment operations within fintech, financial services, Forex and CFD, iGaming, or gambling
  • Hands-on experience with alternative payment methods and a solid understanding of end-to-end deposit and withdrawal payment flows
  • Working knowledge of payment gateways, cashier and routing logic, cascading rules, settlement cycles, fee reconciliation, and provider onboarding and KYC processes
  • Practical knowledge of payment systems, APMs, and payment flows
  • Experience in user acceptance testing of payment integrations and confidence in reading basic API documentation and decline error logs
  • Strong data analysis skills and high computer literacy (Excel, BI tools)
  • Good understanding of relevant laws and regulatory requirements
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills
  • Experience in handling multiple priorities effectively
  • Professional fluency in English. An additional language is considered a plus
