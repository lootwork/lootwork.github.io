О Payoneer

Founded in 2005, Payoneer is the global financial platform that removes friction from doing business across borders, with a mission to connect the world’s underserved businesses to a rising global economy. We’re a community with over 2,500 colleagues all over the world, working to serve customers, and partners in over 190 countries and territories.

By taking the complexity out of the financial workflows–including everything from global payments and compliance to multi-currency and workforce management, to providing working capital and business intelligence–we give businesses the tools they need to work efficiently worldwide and grow with confidence.

We are looking for an experienced Senior DevOps Engineer to join our Platform Services team. In this role, you will design, build, and operate scalable platforms and infrastructure across a hybrid on-premises and multi-cloud environment, enabling engineering teams to develop, deploy, and run services efficiently, securely, and reliably.

You will work in a complex, large-scale production environment that combines on-prem infrastructure with AWS, GCP, Kubernetes, Infrastructure as Code, automation, observability, and AI-driven technologies.

Что предстоит делать

  • Design, build, and maintain hybrid infrastructure spanning on-premises platforms, AWS, GCP, and multi-cloud architecture
  • Operate and scale Kubernetes-based containerized production environments
  • Implement and manage Infrastructure as Code using Terraform and Helm
  • Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and automation with Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, and Argo CD
  • Drive platform engineering initiatives — developer self-service portals, internal tooling, and golden paths
  • Establish and improve observability and reliability practices using Prometheus, Grafana, and Coralogix
  • Write automation scripts and tooling in Python, Bash, and PowerShell
  • Own cloud networking, security, IAM, secrets management, and governance across environments
  • Lead production troubleshooting, incident management, and root cause analysis
  • Architect solutions and provide technical leadership in close collaboration with R&D teams
  • Leverage AI-based tools to improve automation, incident analysis, operational efficiency, and developer workflow

Кто вы

  • 6+ years of hands-on experience in DevOps, Platform Engineering, Infrastructure Engineering, or similar roles
  • Strong experience with hybrid on-premises and multi-cloud environments — especially AWS and GCP
  • Solid hands-on knowledge of Kubernetes, CI/CD, Infrastructure as Code, automation, and observability
  • Ability to design and lead infrastructure/platform projects in close collaboration with R&D teams
  • Strong troubleshooting skills and the ability to investigate complex production issues end-to-end
  • Ability to work independently, take ownership, and drive projects from design through production
  • A solution-oriented mindset, strong collaboration skills, and passion for improving developer experience
  • Curiosity and a hands-on approach to adopting AI-driven tools for DevOps and platform workflow
  • Available to participate in a 24/7 production on-call rotation as part of the DevOps team is required
