О нас

Traditional banking has never really given you control over your financial life. Satispay exists to change that: to empower people through finance. That means making financial services easy and accessible for over 6.5M users. We started with payments, then benefits, investing, cards... and we’re not done. Not even close. We follow the same logic every time: find a real problem, solve it properly, then look for the next one. The destination? To build the most loved financial platform in the world. If you own outcomes rather than tasks, stay close to what users need, and refuse to wait for someone else to fix what's broken — you'll find people here who work exactly in the same way. And together, you'll build something worth being proud of.

Что предстоит делать

As our Staff Software Engineer, Consumer Wealth Domain, you'll play a defining and strategic role in shaping how our users grow and manage their savings and investments. Here's what that looks like in practice:

- Define Technical Strategy – Define the technical strategy for the Consumer Wealth domain, architecting complex subsystems that power savings and investment features with cross-product considerations.

- Advance the Architecture – Design and build scalable, high-quality systems that solve complex financial engineering problems, including managing and simplifying complex back-end systems.

- Technical Thought Leadership – Build cross-organizational relationships to drive technical alignment, represent the technical vision, and stay ahead of emerging technology trends in FinTech and wealth management.

- Develop Talent – Develop the next generation of technical leaders and champion a culture of high-quality engineering and continuous improvement.

- Generate Impact – Play a vital role in helping users grow their wealth through contributions to the Consumer Wealth back-end infrastructure (Java, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, AWS).

Who we're looking for

This role needs a visionary problem-solver who excels in cross-team collaboration with deep technical expertise.

- Extensive Experience – At least 7 years in back-end software engineering within product-based companies, with a proven track record of architecting, building, and maintaining microservices and complex cloud environments.

- Domain Expertise – Deep experience with Java, a strong understanding of cloud platforms (AWS is a plus), and interest in financial products and wealth management systems.

- Strategic Communication – Ability to communicate in a structured and convincing manner, building trust and cross-organizational relationships to achieve technical alignment.

- Thought Leadership – Proven ability to generate intellectual property, lead technical vision, and capitalize on emerging technology trends.

- Passion and Language Skills – Passion for FinTech or startup environments and fluency in English.

Don't worry if you don't tick every box. We believe in the power of different viewpoints and strengths. Your unique perspective is important as we build something special: if you're passionate and can make a difference, we truly encourage you to apply.
