Что предстоит делать

  • Modernize and Scale the RDS Fleet: Ensure the reliability of our existing RDS footprint. You will use Terraform to maintain the databases and automate strategies for performance tuning
  • Evolve Observability & Monitoring: Build deep, proactive monitoring and alerting for our global database fleet. You will leverage Java-based data collection pipelines and metrics platforms (Prometheus, Grafana, Dash0, Sentry, Humio) to detect performance regressions, slow queries, and health issues before they impact customers
  • Fortify Business Continuity (BCP): Design and implement rigorous Business Continuity Planning and Disaster Recovery strategies. You will build Java-based automation that ensures data durability and rapid recovery across regions, guaranteeing our databases remain resilient.
  • Architect Cross-Cloud Portability: Use CNPG (CloudNativePG) and cloud-native patterns to ensure our database layer remains provider-agnostic, allowing seamless deployment across clouds
  • Build Java-Driven Applications: Enhance production-grade Java microservices to orchestrate database monitoring, automated failover, and replication workflows

Требования

  • PostgreSQL Experience: You have hands-on experience working with Postgres databases. You understand core database concepts like query execution, indexing, locking, and performance tuning
  • Systems Thinker: You believe infrastructure and database orchestration should be driven programmatically via Terraform and custom Java automation
  • Distributed Systems Enthusiast: You are excited by the challenge of "multi-everything"—multi-tenant and multi-region data platforms—while ensuring data integrity, clean relational modeling, and mobility
  • A Security & Observability Mindset: You focus on building deep observability (Prometheus/Grafana/Dash0/Sentry/Humio) and automated security guardrails directly into your Java services so the fleet is secure and monitored by design
