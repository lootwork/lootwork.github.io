О роли

We are exploring the frontier intersection of AI and blockchain/cryptocurrency, seeking passionate and innovative interns to join our research team. You will participate in technical research and prototype validation at the intersection of AI and Web3/OnChain, exploring innovative applications of large models, agents, and other AI technologies in on-chain scenarios, and rapidly turning cutting-edge ideas into verifiable prototypes.

Обязанности

  • Track frontier technologies and industry developments in AI and blockchain/cryptocurrency; conduct technical research and produce valuable analysis and insights.
  • Paticipate in pre-research topics in the AI × Crypto direction, exploring the feasibility of large models, agents, and other technologies in on-chain trading, data analysis, and intelligent application scenarios.
  • Rapidly build technical prototypes (PoCs) to validate innovative ideas and produce reproducible experimental conclusions.
  • Participate in application-level exploration and development, advancing pre-research outcomes into demonstrable and evaluable product prototypes.
  • Collaborate with algorithm and engineering teams to participate in discussions and drive topics toward implementation.

Требования

  • Currently enrolled Master's or PhD student in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Mathematics, Financial Engineering, or related field; able to commit to stable weekly internship hours.
  • Strong interest in the intersection of AI and blockchain/cryptocurrency; foundational knowledge of at least one direction (e.g., large language models, agents, or blockchain, smart contracts, DeFi).
  • Proficient in Python with solid programming and engineering implementation skills; able to independently complete prototype development.
  • Strong learning ability and curiosity; able to quickly pick up new technologies and directions; proactive in exploring unknown problems.
  • Good communication and documentation skills; able to clearly express technical research conclusions and experimental results.

Bonus

  • Experience with LLM/agent-related projects, papers, or open-source contributions.
  • Familiarity with blockchain development (Solidity, on-chain data analysis, wallet/transaction interaction) or practical experience with DeFi and on-chain trading.
  • Personal projects, hackathon participation, or internship experience in AI × Crypto.
  • Papers published at top conferences (NeurIPS, ICML, ACL, etc.) or outstanding open-source projects.
