Кто мы

О Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.

О команде

Stripe's Experimental Projects team is our in-house innovation lab where we rapidly transform ideas into working prototypes. We operate as an idea incubator, building and testing new concepts with real users, sometimes going from concept to functional prototype in just days.

Что предстоит делать

As a Software Engineer on the Experimental Projects team, you'll be at the forefront of innovation at Stripe, turning promising ideas into tangible solutions that could shape the future of our platform. You'll have the autonomy to identify opportunities, rapidly build prototypes, and work directly with users to validate and iterate on your solutions.

This role combines technical excellence with creative problem-solving – you'll work across our entire technology stack to bring ideas to life, sometimes in just days.

Your work will help us discover new ways to empower businesses worldwide, contributing directly to Stripe's mission of increasing the GDP of the internet. Whether it's creating new developer tools, exploring novel payment experiences, or inventing entirely new products, you'll have the freedom to experiment and the resources to make a significant impact.

Обязанности

  • Drive projects from ideation to implementation, taking ownership of the full development lifecycle
  • Build across the stack, from APIs, services and infrastructure to data pipelines and data products to the frontend when needed
  • Partner with teams across Stripe to integrate with existing systems and infrastructure
  • Use AI coding tools and agents to go from idea to working prototype fast, and build products for AI and agent use cases
  • Collaborate directly with users to understand their needs and gather feedback
  • Prototype and validate ideas quickly, pivoting or adjusting based on real-world usage

Кто вы

We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • 2+ years of professional, hands on software engineering experience (backend, infrastructure, data, or full-stack)
  • Excels at rapid prototyping while maintaining appropriate quality standards
  • Demonstrates strong product intuition and user empathy
  • Comfortable working with ambiguity and navigating large organizations to get things done
  • Experience building from 0→1 and shipping to real users (internal or external)

Будет плюсом

  • Enjoys iterating on the development stack in order to maximize velocity, especially using AI-based tools
  • You keep a close eye on emerging technology, especially AI, and you're excited about spotting what it means for Stripe's users
  • You have a passion for working on side-projects, open source, or similar self-driven initiatives
