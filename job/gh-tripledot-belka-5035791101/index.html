О роли

We’re looking for a Lead Animator to own the quality, direction, and technical execution of animation across our live mobile games. You’ll bring our games to life through polished, engaging animations, from UI transitions and character animations to exciting wins, rewards, and celebrations that make every moment feel satisfying.

This is a highly technical, hands-on role. You’ll work extensively in Unity, building and implementing animations, VFX, and motion systems directly in-engine, while also creating character and asset animations in Spine. You’ll work closely with art, design, and development to create experiences that look fantastic, perform efficiently, and can be reused and scaled across our games.

AI is central to how we work. You’ll use it to accelerate animation workflows, prototype ideas, develop scripts and shaders, and explore new ways to improve the quality and efficiency of our animation pipeline.

We’re a small, ambitious team, so you’ll have real ownership over how our games look and feel in motion. You’ll spend most of your time creating, implementing, and solving technical challenges, while also providing creative direction and leadership to elevate the quality of our work.

Обязанности

  • Own animation quality and direction across our live titles, establishing motion guidelines, animation standards, and consistent approaches to timing, transitions, and visual feedback.
  • Create and implement animations directly in Unity, including UI transitions, popups, currency animations, progress bars, and feature presentations.
  • Build impactful reward and celebration sequences, combining animation, VFX, UI, and audio to create satisfying wins, jackpots, and other key gameplay moments.
  • Develop VFX in Unity, using particle systems, flipbooks, shaders, and other techniques to create polished, engaging effects.
  • Create character and asset animations in Spine, including rigging, expressive character animation, and integration into Unity.
  • Own the technical animation pipeline, working with the Lead Technical Artist to establish standards for Spine assets, animation events, naming conventions, and efficient implementation.
  • Optimize animations for mobile performance, ensuring motion and VFX run smoothly across devices, including lower-end Android hardware.
  • Build reusable animation systems and tools, creating prefabs, templates, and workflows that improve efficiency and reduce custom work for live events.
  • Lead and support the Senior Sound Designer, aligning animation and audio priorities, reviewing work, and ensuring a cohesive player experience.
  • Champion AI-assisted animation workflows, using AI for prototyping, scripting, shader development, and identifying opportunities to improve the team’s creative and technical processes.

Требования

  • 8+ years of experience in game animation, motion design, and VFX, with several shipped free-to-play mobile titles.
  • Expert-level Unity animation skills, including Animator, Timeline, particle systems, Shader Graph, and tweening tools such as DOTween.
  • Strong technical animation capabilities, including working knowledge of C# and the ability to implement, connect, and troubleshoot animations directly in Unity.
  • Advanced Spine animation experience, including rigging, character animation, timing, acting, and integrating Spine assets into Unity.
  • A strong understanding of mobile game performance, with experience optimizing animations, effects, and assets for a wide range of mobile devices.
  • Exceptional animation fundamentals, with a strong sense of timing, easing, weight, anticipation, and visual feedback.
  • Experience leading or mentoring creative team members, providing direction, feedback, and maintaining high standards of quality.
  • A hands-on, technically minded approach, comfortable moving between creative animation, technical implementation, troubleshooting, and workflow improvements.
  • Practical experience using AI tools to support creative or technical workflows, with curiosity about new techniques and applications.
  • A strong portfolio of shipped game animations, including in-engine Unity captures demonstrating both creative quality and technical execution.
  • Ability to work from our Toronto office (hybrid) or be based in Montreal. Reasonable relocation support is available and remote work may be considered for exceptionally qualified candidates in select locations.
