Kabam is looking for a Contract Video Artist to support the creative services team for 1 year. You will be responsible for capturing high-quality gameplay footage and imagery and supporting video editing tasks across our portfolio of titles - including Marvel Contest of Champions and Shop Titans - for use in marketing materials, both static and video.

This role is a full-time, on-site position based 5 days a week at our Downtown Vancouver studio.

IN THIS ROLE, YOU CAN EXPECT TO

  • Assist in video editing and content preparation for upcoming video releases for major titles including Marvel Contest of Champions and Shop Titans.
  • Capture gameplay footage and screenshots in Unity across multiple Kabam titles.
  • Organize and deliver raw capture in a way that's easy for editors and designers to use.
  • Stay up to date on in-game events, updates, and content across our titles so you can proactively capture relevant moments.
  • Flag bugs, visual glitches, or inconsistencies encountered while capturing from in-development builds.
  • Occasional support in live stream operations as required.

TO BE SUCCESSFUL IN THIS ROLE, YOUR BACKGROUND INCLUDES

  • 3+ years of video editing experience in both short-form and long-form content: game industry experience preferred.
  • Working knowledge of game engines or 3D software (Unity, Unreal Engine, etc.).
  • Interest in game development and/or marketing.
  • Familiarity with Kabam titles (Marvel Contest of Champions & Shop Titans).
  • Hands-on experience with Adobe Creative Suite, especially After Effects and Premiere Pro.
  • Moderate proficiency in audio editing and intermediate-level proficiency in designing and animating motion graphics.

Excited by this opportunity? Kabam is dedicated to growing our diverse and inclusive workforce, so if your past experience doesn’t perfectly match the listed requirements we encourage you to apply anyways - you could be a great fit for this or other positions.
