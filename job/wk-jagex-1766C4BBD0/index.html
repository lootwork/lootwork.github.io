Office location: Cambridge, UK – This position can accommodate flexible working options. Candidates must be based (or be willing to relocate) within a comfortable commuting distance of our Cambridge office to attend onsite as required.

Are you looking to start a career in cloud and reliability engineering, working on the infrastructure behind some of the world's biggest and longest-running online games?

As a Junior Cloud Reliability Engineer in our Developer Platform team, you'll help support the backend infrastructure powering RuneScape, Old School RuneScape, and RuneScape: Dragonwilds. Working within a small team, you'll gain hands-on experience across cloud infrastructure, networking, Linux systems, and game services while learning how to keep hundreds of thousands of players online every day.

This role offers a lot of development opportunities. You'll begin by supporting existing systems and responding to day-to-day operational requests before progressing to independent ownership of infrastructure projects as your technical skills and experience grow.

Что предстоит делать

  • Learn our backend infrastructure which will cover game world systems, networking, build pipelines, version control, data center hardware, and cloud infrastructure.
  • Handle day-to-day support requests and troubleshoot issues across our live systems, escalating quickly when needed and following up to understand the root cause.
  • Support development teams in managing cloud-native workloads and consuming our cloud platform through Infrastructure-as-Code.
  • Contribute to the management of our Linux virtual machine fleet, including monitoring, configuration management, and workload optimisation.
  • Participate in incident response and post-mortems, developing your understanding of how complex systems fail and how to make them more resilient.

Кого мы ищем

  • Comfortable working in a Linux terminal environment and writing bash scripts.
  • Familiarity with at least one programming language such as Python - we’re open to any language if you can demonstrate how you think through problems.
  • A solid understanding of networking concepts and the OSI model.
  • A genuine problem-solving mindset - you enjoy working through complex issues, aren’t satisfied with surface-level answers, and naturally ask how and why things work the way they do.
  • Good communication skills and a desire to keep learning, including the confidence to ask questions and challenge existing approaches.
  • Prior infrastructure or SRE experience is a plus.
