Your job will be to work as a facilitator, keeping different departments abreast of things and making sure that all the pieces come together. You should merge business knowledge with a passion for games!

You will be working closely with the Product Owner to maintain comprehensive project plans, documentation, and statuses throughout the life of the projects; Proactively and continuously follow up with all project participants and track statuses; Participate in all team meetings, and being responsible for reporting project statuses; Work collaboratively with all stakeholders; Perform multiple tasks and duties as assigned by Product Owner, eg.

Обязанности на этой позиции

  • Creating project documentation, and co-creating tasks for the team;
  • Daily monitoring of selected KPIs;
  • Participation in the creation of a product strategy/roadmap;
  • Monitoring of user feedback, and communication with players;
  • Planning marketing activities (promotion, tournaments, etc.);
  • Verification of language versions for key markets
