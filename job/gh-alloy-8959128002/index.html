О команде

Alloy has expanded beyond onboarding into ongoing fraud prevention, sold through digital banking platform partners to the banks and credit unions that run on them. Their clients use Alloy to stop account takeover and scams before money moves, while good customers bank with less friction. We've built the distribution, and now every strong client result becomes the story that wins the next one. Fraud Signal, Alloy's machine learning fraud model, is at the center of that flywheel, and you'll be the product manager who makes it deliver. Alloy operates in a hybrid-work environment. We look to foster collaboration and community by having our local employees onsite three days a week.

Что предстоит делать

Your job is simple to say and hard to do: deliver fraud outcomes for clients on our digital banking partners without adding friction for good customers, and turn every result into the reason the next client buys.

  • Own Fraud Signal performance for ongoing fraud, starting with account takeover. Partner with our ML and engineering teams on which features matter, which data we're missing, how we evaluate performance, and what good looks like for each client.
  • Get deep into each partner's data: which events and fields come through, how complete and reliable they are, and what we could get that we don't today. Turn gaps into concrete asks of partners and feature priorities for Fraud Signal, and work with Alloy's integration teams so every signal we need has a home in the platform.
  • Build how we measure fraud outcomes. Drive the confirmed fraud labels, client feedback loops, and client-level performance reporting that tell us and our clients whether we're catching fraud.
  • Design the policies that work alongside Fraud Signal to stop fraud while keeping friction low for good customers, and make them repeatable across clients instead of hand-tuned one at a time.
  • Turn results into proof. Get live clients to outcomes strong enough to show, and give Sales, Solutions Engineering, and our partners the evidence to sell with confidence.
  • Turn emerging fraud patterns into an advantage. When you spot a new pattern at one institution, protect every client from it and partner with Product Marketing to turn it into a story the market hears.

Кого мы ищем

Must-haves

  • Hands-on experience building or directly shaping ML models for financial institutions, ideally fraud models. You understand feature engineering, label quality, class imbalance, threshold tuning, and model evaluation well enough to challenge an ML team and shape its roadmap without managing it.
  • 5+ years across product management, data science, or ML roles, including ownership of a model-driven product in financial services or another regulated B2B setting.
  • A track record of turning model performance into business outcomes, not just better offline metrics.
  • Comfort working directly in the data. You can query event data yourself, spot quality and coverage gaps, and trace a weak result back to a missing or broken input.
  • Client-facing experience. You've presented results to clients and worked with Sales or Solutions Engineering to turn them into wins.

Nice-to-haves

  • Experience with device, behavioral, or session-based signals in online and mobile banking
  • Designed fraud outcome or labeling programs, such as chargeback, dispute, or confirmed fraud feedback loops
  • Worked on fraud strategy or rules and policy design inside a bank, credit union, or fintech
  • Worked directly with technology partners or data providers to define data-sharing requirements or integration specs
