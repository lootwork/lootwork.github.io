Кто мы

Tripledot Studios is one of the largest independent mobile games companies in the world.

We are a multi-award-winning organisation, with a global 2,500+ strong team across 12 studios.

Our expanded portfolio includes some of the biggest titles in mobile gaming, collectively reaching top chart positions around the world and engaging over 25 million daily active users.

Tripledot’s guiding principle is that when people love what they do, what they do will be loved by others.

We’re building a company we’re proud of. One filled with driven, incredibly smart and detail-orientated people, who LOVE making games.

Our ambition is to be the most successful games company in the world, and we’re just getting started.

This role will be working at our Clipwire Games studio.

Clipwire Games is one of Canada's top grossing mobile game developers, and the creator of Monopoly: Bingo!, TPIR: Bingo! and Bingo Story. Founded in Toronto over 10 years ago, we’ve had tremendous success and are poised for another decade of record growth. Our studio is built on strong values, work ethic, and intelligent data driven growth. We work hard, have fun, innovate, and level-up every day.

To work at Clipwire means becoming part of our future right now. We have reached our current growth rate because we support, promote, and develop our team so that they can make great games. Come find out what all the buzz is about!

О роли

We’re looking for a Senior Product Manager to own the performance and direction of our live titles. Reporting to the Product Director, you’ll drive the product roadmap and priorities, turning player insights and data into features, events, offers, and economy changes that move our core KPIs. You’ll have real ownership over product decisions, from identifying opportunities to execution and measuring results.

AI is central to how we work. You’ll use it every day across analysis, research, specs, design ideation, and reporting. You’ll have the freedom to build your own workflows and tools, experiment with new approaches, and help the broader team get more out of AI. We’re looking for someone who’s genuinely curious about what’s possible with AI and excited to push the boundaries of how it can improve the way we build games.

We’re a small and ambitious team, so this role is a hands-on role with a real opportunity to influence our products. Your day to day might include digging into the behaviour of a player segment, prototyping a new feature, strategizing LiveOps calendar, or sharing insights with the team. You’re analytical, decisive, and organized, with strong product instincts,a deep understanding of mobile games, and the ability to move comfortably between strategy and execution.

Обязанности

  • Own the product roadmap and day-to-day product decisions for our live titles, with clear goals and accountability for results.
  • Lead LiveOps strategy and execution, planning events, promotions, and content cycles ahead of time, measuring their performance, and continuously improving the approach.
  • Champion AI across product development, using AI extensively in your own work, building tools and workflows, and identifying opportunities to improve how the team approaches analysis, content development, design ideation, and reporting.
  • Drive game design priorities alongside the Product Director and Lead Game Designer, translating the roadmap into clear feature goals, requirements, and success benchmarks.
  • Own features from concept through launch and beyond, working across design, development, art, and QA to bring ideas to life, evaluate their performance, and iterate.
  • Align product priorities with production, ensuring the roadmap and LiveOps calendar are coordinated with development, art, and QA delivery plans.
  • Drive monetization initiatives across IAP offers, pricing, D2C programs, and ad placements, working closely with the Game Economy Designer and Growth team.
  • Design and analyze A/B tests and experiments, working directly with data to validate hypotheses and turn findings into actionable product decisions.
  • Monitor product health and performance, tracking retention, monetization, and engagement KPIs, responding quickly to changes, and communicating results so teams understand the impact of their work.
  • Lead market and competitor research, identifying opportunities, emerging trends, and insights that inform product strategy and priorities.

Требования

  • 5+ years of product management on live free-to-play mobile games, with social casino being a nice to have
  • A track record of results, showing movement of KPIs from ideated and launched features, mechanics, initiatives and activations
  • Deep, daily AI use in your own work and a real enthusiasm for it. You’ve built workflows, automations, or tools, and experience positive change because of them
  • Good judgement about AI output: you know what needs to be checked, and you validate and back the data behind every decision
  • Deep familiarity with game analytics and mobile KPIs, and comfort working directly with data
  • Strong commercial instincts, with an understanding of how product decisions influence revenue, profitability, player lifetime value, and long-term product health
  • An exceptional understand of free-to-play economies, monetisation, and LiveOps strategies
  • Strong range, being able to move easily between data, design, art, and planning - you can pick up whatever moves the product forward
  • Clear communication that aligns the team and confidence sharing your reasoning behind decisions.
  • Ability to work from our Toronto office (hybrid) or be based in Montreal. Reasonable relocation support is available and remote work may be considered for exceptionally qualified candidates in select locations.
