О GROWTH TEAM

Growth Marketing at Alan covers a broad range of opportunities, including acquisition, segment and market growth, partnerships, content, experimentation, and international expansion.

We are looking for adaptable marketers who can take ownership of a defined growth problem, learn quickly, and evolve as our priorities change.

YOUR MISSION

As a Growth Marketer, you will independently own an ambiguous but defined Growth problem from diagnosis through execution. You will prioritise the highest-impact actions, coordinate the relevant stakeholders, and connect your work to measurable business outcomes.

Concretely, our team works on missions such as

- Developing our channels: scaling paid acquisition, growing visibility in AI search and LLMs like ChatGPT, or growing our strategic partnerships

- Crafting the go-to-market strategy for our different segments (e.g. retirees, freelancers, companies,...)

- Producing performance creatives with AI workflows to create assets at scale

Что предстоит делать

- Independently own an ambiguous but defined Growth problem from diagnosis through execution.

- Prioritise high-impact opportunities and make clear trade-offs.

- Design and run experiments, campaigns, or programmes end to end.

- Coordinate the relevant stakeholders and move initiatives forward with limited direction.

- Use business and performance data to make decisions and establish a clear learning loop.

- Adapt your approach based on results, customer insight, and feedback.

- Connect your work to measurable business outcomes.

Кого мы ищем

- Experience owning a meaningful Growth, acquisition, segment, market, or related Marketing scope.

- Strong problem-framing, prioritisation, and experimentation skills.

- Ability to make decisions based on evidence and business impact.

- Hands-on execution combined with an understanding of adjacent teams and activities.

- Ability to work with limited direction in an ambiguous environment.

- Clear communication and constructive stakeholder management.

- Curiosity, adaptability, and willingness to expand your scope over time.
