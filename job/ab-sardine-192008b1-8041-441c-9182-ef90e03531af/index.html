О роли

Browsers are steadily closing the door on how device identity has worked on the web. Sardine's Device Intelligence & Behavior Biometrics team builds the signals that tell genuine users apart from fraudsters, bots, and AI agents, and we need an engineer to own how our Web SDK identifies devices reliably across Safari, Firefox, Chrome, Brave, and in-app WebViews, and how it reaches the browser as a true first party.

You will own the Web SDK, its client-side identity storage, and the CDN reverse proxy integrations that sit in our customers' traffic. This is a production engineering role with a strong investigative side: much of the work starts with understanding exactly how a browser treats a cookie, a request, or a domain, and ends with shipping a change that keeps working for thousands of end users.

Что предстоит делать

- Lead our move to privacy-resilient first-party integrations: opt-in CHIPS partitioned cookies, true first-party cookie flows, and in-band CDN reverse proxy integrations.

- Build and extend reverse proxy integrations across CDNs and origin setups (Cloudflare, AWS Lambda@Edge / CloudFront, Fastly, Akamai, F5, NGINX), including per-customer randomized paths for SDK loading and event ingestion.

- Modernize the SDK's client-side identity storage layer, and design graceful degradation when persistence is limited.

- Design device identity handoff between our native mobile SDKs and in-app WebViews, resistant to spoofing and abuse.

- Instrument and measure device identifier longevity and churn by browser, version, and integration mode, and alert on regressions.

- Track browser privacy changes (ITP, ETP / dFPI, CHIPS, Storage Access API, tracker lists) ahead of release, and turn them into SDK and integration decisions.

- Own the performance, bundle size, and reliability of an SDK that runs on login, signup, and payment flows of fintechs and enterprises.

- Work with backend (Go), mobile, dashboard, and customer success teams to roll out changes across existing customers without breaking installs.
