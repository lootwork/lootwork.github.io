Product Marketing Manager for Nu Global

This is an individual contributor role with no direct reports.

О роли

Launched in 2026, Nu Global is a global account that people can use to earn, send, and move money worldwide: a daily-yield balance held in digital dollars and digital euros, fee-free transfers to 35+ countries across both regions, a card for spending abroad, and access to curated digital assets. The Product Marketing Manager for Nu Global owns go-to-market to the existing customer base, lifecycle communications (CLM), the Nu Global website experience, the App Store and Google Play listing, and product-led content across channels.

CLM is the heaviest of these work streams and a core part of the role. You will reach customers through email, push notifications, in-app messages and marketing screens, using Nu's proprietary CRM platform, so the quality of this work directly drives adoption and engagement of Nu Global products.

The role is execution-heavy and sits in a small team: a lead PMM, a growth marketer and a partnership activation marketer. You own the delivery and results of your work streams, with direction on priorities from the lead PMM.

YOU'LL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR

Your week is split between running lifecycle programs and keeping the other owned surfaces (website, store listing, content) aligned with what the product is launching. The main challenge is delivering a high volume of work with quality while keeping customer touchpoints prioritized and not overloaded.

- Lifecycle programs (CLM): build, launch and optimize programs end to end: audience segmentation, journeys and triggers, email, push notifications, in-app messages and marketing screens, including QA, scheduling and frequency control.

- Lifecycle roadmap: maintain an always-on communications calendar across onboarding, activation, engagement and cross-sell, coordinated with the lead PMM, Growth and Product.

- Go-to-market to existing customers: plan and execute the communication of launches, new products and feature updates, driving adoption by segment, market and language.

- Website experience: own page content, messaging updates and conversion improvements for the Nu Global website, in partnership with Design, Product and CX.

- Store listing: own App Store and Google Play listing copy, creatives and releases, aligned to product launches and positioning.

- Product-led content: adapt product narratives into channel-specific content (CRM, web, store, social) and keep positioning and tone consistent across surfaces and markets.

- Experimentation and measurement: run A/B and holdout tests on messaging, timing and channel; define KPIs per program (delivery, open, click, activation, adoption, conversion); report results and turn them into next iterations.

- Cross-functional alignment: work with Product, Engineering, Legal, Compliance, CX, Business Analytics and local market teams on launch timing, product readiness, and legal and eligibility requirements before activating the customer base.

- Documentation and governance: maintain playbooks, templates, access and naming conventions for lifecycle programs and other owned surfaces, so operations stay auditable and scalable.
