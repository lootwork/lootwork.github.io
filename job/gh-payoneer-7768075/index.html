О Payoneer

Founded in 2005, Payoneer is the global financial platform that removes friction from doing business across borders, with a mission to connect the world’s underserved businesses to a rising global economy. We’re a community with over 2,500 colleagues all over the world, working to serve customers, and partners in over 190 countries and territories.

By taking the complexity out of the financial workflows–including everything from global payments and compliance to multi-currency and workforce management, to providing working capital and business intelligence–we give businesses the tools they need to work efficiently worldwide and grow with confidence.

🚀 Principal Mobile Engineer – Payoneer

Are you a mobile wizard who dreams in Swift or Kotlin, and maybe even Flutter or React Native? Do you obsess over performance, seamless UX, and pushing mobile experiences to the next level? If yes, we want you on our team at Payoneer, where we power global payments for millions of businesses worldwide.

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead the charge in designing, building, and scaling high-performance mobile apps for Android and iOS.
  • Mentor and inspire a team of talented mobile engineers, sharing your knowledge of native as well as cross-platform technologies like Flutter, React Native, or Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP).
  • Set the bar for mobile performance, reliability, and user experience, making sure our apps are blazing fast and bulletproof.
  • Collaborate with product, design, and backend teams to deliver innovative features that delight our global users.
  • Stay ahead of the curve in mobile tech trends and influence architecture decisions across the mobile domain.

Что вы приносите

  • 9+ years of mobile development experience, with strong hands-on expertise in Android or iOS.
  • Deep understanding of at least 1 cross-platform framework (Flutter / React Native / KMP) , bonus points if it’s React Native.
  • Deep understanding of mobile fundamentals, including memory management, threading, app lifecycle, and UI performance optimization.
  • A passion for measuring and improving app performance, metrics such as start-up times, frozen frames, app size, etc
  • Excellent communication and leadership skills: you can mentor, inspire, and grow the team.
  • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, and code quality practices is a plus.

Why You’ll Love Working at Payoneer

  • Impact millions of users globally through cutting-edge mobile experiences.
  • Be part of a culture that values innovation, ownership, and thinking outside the box.
  • Flexible work options and a team that’s as fun as it is brilliant.
