With multiple awards to our name, Larian Studios has proven that we’re dedicated to delivering high-quality role-playing games As we move on to new projects, the studio that brought you Baldur’s Gate 3 & Divinity: Original Sin 2 is looking for an Associate VO Producer to join the team on a 12 month contract and help us in crafting the high calibre games our fans have come to expect.

Обязанности

  • Operating an in-house scheduling system to coordinate VO and Mocap bookings with a large cast and crew across multiple internal recording studios.
  • Penciling and confirming sessions, preparing scripts, raising purchase orders, reviewing invoices, organising contracts with the legal department.
  • Build and develop positive external relationships with talent agencies and freelance suppliers.
  • Collaborate with the casting team, prepare audition materials, collate feedback, maintain casting database, offer contracting and actor onboarding.
  • Reviewing scripts and coordinating session prep materials for directors and studio crew.
  • Learn the technical VO pipeline to understand the flow of recording assets and assist in troubleshooting any post-session issues.

Требования

  • Production experience in a VO, mocap, film / TV or post-production environment.
  • Experience with scheduling and project management software and the ability to quickly identify capacity problems and propose scheduling solutions.
  • The role will have a lot of external communication so excellent written and spoken skills are required.
  • Confident working in spreadsheets and enthusiasm to learn new software and production tools
  • Strong organizational skills and able to handle documentation as needed.

Будет плюсом

  • Video / audio editing (e.g for preparing casting clips or video reference materials)
  • Experience in casting and actor contracting
  • Experience in asset management and post-production pipelines
  • Experience working with budgets and cost tracking

Что мы предлагаем

  • An international team of over 500 employees spread across 7 studios around the world, each of which have their own unique qualities, and maintain a small-studio feel
  • An open, collaborative environment where ideas are welcome and encouraged
  • A managerial team consisting of highly experienced game developers at the top of their fields
  • Sharing hobbies and passions with like-minded colleagues
  • Inclusive environment with many activities organized by the studios or the employees
  • Commitment to making the best game possible for the fans, including listening to community feedback
