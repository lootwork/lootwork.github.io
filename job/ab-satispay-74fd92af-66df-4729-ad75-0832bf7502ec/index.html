О нас

Traditional banking has never really given you control over your financial life. Satispay exists to change that: to empower people through finance.

That means making financial services easy and accessible for over 6.5M users. We started with payments, then benefits, investing, cards... and we’re not done. Not even close.

We follow the same logic every time: find a real problem, solve it properly, then look for the next one. The destination? To build the most loved financial platform in the world.

If you own outcomes rather than tasks, stay close to what users need, and refuse to wait for someone else to fix what's broken — you'll find people here who work exactly in the same way. And together, you'll build something worth being proud of.

Что предстоит делать

As our Field Business Developer, you’ll be the person who brings Satispay to independent merchants across Milan, owning your territory from the first knock to the signed deal. You’ll be on the front lines of our expansion, driving the adoption of Satispay’s solutions among independent merchants. You’ll work autonomously to hit and exceed ambitious sales targets.

Here's what that looks like in practice

- Territory management & hunting - Analyse the Milan metropolitan area to find, target and win high-potential independent merchants.

- End-to-end sales cycle - Run the whole sales process on your own, from the first cold approach and product presentation to negotiation and closing.

- Pitching complex solutions - Explain the value of our business solutions clearly, tailoring your pitch to the needs of each merchant.

- Pipeline mastery - Keep your CRM spotless: log your daily activities, track your funnel, forecast your results and optimise your daily routes with data.

Who we’re looking for

We need a problem-solver who loves teamwork and gets things done. If you’re curious and ready for real ownership, you’ll fit in!

- Field sales experience - 3-5 years in B2B field sales to independent merchants, especially in hotels, restaurants and cafés (HoReCa) or local retail, selling either physical products or complex business services.

- Strategic & analytical approach - You can evaluate a territory, spot the best opportunities quickly and prioritise your daily hunting.

- Advanced commercial skills - You’re a natural negotiator with a consultative approach, able to break down and sell complex solutions to business owners.

- Autonomy & drive - You’re a self-starter who manages your own schedule, stays resilient under challenging targets and has a proven track record of overachieving.

- Local knowledge - You know Milan and speak the language of local business owners.

- CRM skills - You’re highly proficient with CRM tools (e.g. HubSpot, Salesforce) and digital tracking systems to manage leads and monitor your performance.

- Mobility - You hold a valid driving licence and own a vehicle to cover your territory in and around Milan.

- Languages - Full professional fluency in Italian and English (written and spoken).

Don't worry if you don't tick every box. We believe in the power of different viewpoints and strengths. Your unique perspective is important as we build something special: if you're passionate and can make a difference, we truly encourage you to apply.
