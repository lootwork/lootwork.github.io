Job Description

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON

Main challenge

As a Lead VFX Artist, you will have the opportunity to build, shape, and elevate the VFX discipline across our PC and console projects.

This is a high-impact leadership role where you'll guide a talented team, influence studio-wide standards and workflows, and help define how VFX are created and delivered across our games.

Alongside the Studio Art Director and key stakeholders, you'll combine artistic vision, team leadership, and technical innovation to bring exceptional player experiences to life.

What it means on a daily basis

VFX Team Leadership

  • Lead, mentor and develop the VFX team, with clear goals and regular feedback.
  • Set priorities and ensure quality and delivery expectations are met.
  • Foster a collaborative, feedback-driven environment.
  • Take part in recruitment and support talent development.

Visual Direction & Quality

  • Partner with the Studio Art Director to define and maintain the VFX quality bar and guidelines.
  • Review the team’s work to ensure VFX are readable, consistent and within performance budgets across target platforms.

Cross-Team Collaboration

  • Collaborate with Art Leads, Producers, Designers, Gameplay, Tech Artists, Engineers and other teams to define VFX needs and resolve dependencies.

VFX Pipeline & Production

  • Oversee the VFX pipeline from concept to final in-engine integration.

Standards & Workflow Development

  • Define and continuously improve VFX standards, workflows, tools and production practices.

What success will look like

After 3 months

  • Built strong partnerships across the studio.
  • Assessed team strengths, workflows and opportunities for improvement.
  • Established a clear vision for the evolution of the VFX discipline.

After 6 months

  • Improved VFX workflows and team effectiveness.
  • Increased consistency and quality across productions.
  • Became a trusted leader and strategic partner within the studio.

Who you would be working with

You will report directly to the Studio Art Director and lead the local VFX team, currently composed of one Principal VFX Artist and two Junior VFX Artists. You will work closely with Art Leads, Producers, Designers, Gameplay teams, Tech Artists, Engineers and other development teams across the studio.

Your recruitment journey

  • Portfolio Review – The Studio Art Director will review your portfolio to assess how your experience and work align with the studio's needs.
  • HR Screening Call – A 40-minute online chat where you'll learn more about the studio and we'll get to know you better, discussing your experience, background, and expectations.
  • Technical Interview – A 1-hour online interview with the Studio Art Director, who will also be your direct manager. During this discussion, you'll have the opportunity to go into more detail about your experience, skills, and approach to your work.

Where this role can take you

This role offers the opportunity to grow as a VFX leader while making a lasting impact on our games, our team, and our technology. You will help shape VFX standards, workflows, and production practices across multiple projects, while influencing the future direction of the discipline within the studio.

Требования

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED

  • 5+ years creating real-time VFX for games, including experience in a lead role and at least one shipped title.
  • A strong portfolio showing both artistic and technical skill, including effects that improve gameplay readability, player feedback and immersion.
  • Strong art fundamentals: timing, shape language, colour, composition and motion.
  • Expert knowledge of real-time particle systems, materials and engine-side VFX authoring, with a solid grasp of performance budgets and optimisation for target platforms.
  • Hands-on experience with industry-standard tools such as Houdini, Maya or 3ds Max, and EmberGen, for simulations, meshes, flipbooks and texture creation.
  • Proven experience leading, mentoring and developing VFX artists, setting the quality bar and giving clear, constructive feedback.
  • Strong planning and prioritisation skills: estimating work, managing schedules and balancing creative ambition against production constraints.
  • Experience working with Art Direction, Design, Animation, Tech Art and Engineering to align VFX with the game’s vision.
  • Curiosity to explore new technologies, workflows and tools, including proprietary game development solutions.
  • Shader development (HLSL or GLSL), node-based material authoring or scripting (Python or similar) would be an advantage.
  • Animation experience, or experience reviewing outsourced VFX work, would be a plus.
  • Fluent written and spoken English, and comfortable working in an international team.
