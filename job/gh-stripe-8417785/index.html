Кто мы

О Stripe

Stripe, LLC. is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

Что предстоит делать

Обязанности

  • Wrangle large, highly complex datasets using SQL into actionable insights and become a data expert for a business area.
  • Develop metrics and large-scale dashboards to track the health of the company’s user-facing and internal teams.
  • Perform analyses, using SQL and other analytical tools and languages to identify trends, conduct research, and build models, all to deliver objective, critical insights that inform leadership of opportunities, risks, and strategic priorities.
  • Create narratives and documents that tell the story of the business area to influence senior Support Operations leadership and the company’s executive team decision making.
  • Influence and drive cross-functional change by partnering with stakeholders across a wide internal network of globally distributed teams, including Support Ops, Finance, Data Science, Product/Engineering and more.
  • Design, optimize and drive operational reviews and rhythm of the business.
  • Guide the planning and goal-setting process for your business area, and progress checks throughout the year.
  • Bring and consistently apply the ROI lens to all operational decisions to make better-informed trade-offs.
  • Partner on forecasting and planning processes for Support Operations, including near/medium/long-term planning to enable leadership to make strategic decisions.

Кто вы

Minimum requirements

Must have a Bachelor's Degree or foreign equivalent degree in Data Science, Marketing, Business Administration, or a related field and two (2) years of related work experience in Analytical and strategic roles in Management Consulting, Operations, Support, Risk, or Financial Services.

Must also have two (2) years of experience in each of the following:

  • SQL and understanding of data relationships;
  • Tableau;
  • Understanding of operations principles, including capacity planning and process improvement;
  • Advanced statistical and quantitative knowledge and modeling techniques including experimental design;
  • Stakeholder management and influencing cross-functional teams to drive progress;
  • Influencing business decisions by creating high quality presentations and documentations using Google Suite;
  • AI/ML technologies and their application in business-driven technical solutions;
  • Turn business strategy into operational roadmaps, aimed at increasing efficiency and user experience; and
  • Proven ability to translate data into compelling and easy-to-understand insights, stories, and narratives that resonate with technical and non-technical audiences.
