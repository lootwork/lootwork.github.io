О TIDE

At Tide, we help SMEs save time and money in the running of their businesses by not only offering business accounts and related banking services, but also a comprehensive set of highly usable and connected administrative solutions, from invoicing to accounting.

Tide is transforming the small business banking market and now supports over 2 million members globally across the UK, Germany and France.

Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love.

Tide facts

  • Tide is available for UK, German and French SMEs
  • Over 2 million members globally and rapidly growing
  • Over $300 million raised in funding
  • 3,000 Tideans globally
  • We have offices in Central London, with a member support and technology centre in Sofia, Bulgaria, technology centres in Serbia, Romania, Lithuania and Hyderabad and offices in Gurugram, New Delhi, Berlin, Paris and Luxembourg

О роли

Here at Tide, we are looking for a seasoned Manager who

  • has a good understanding of the Trust and Safety domain with expertise in an e-commerce environment.
  • Willingness to operate in a fast paced growing product line within Tide called Commercial Services that primarily focus on enabling Small and Medium Enterprises (SME Businesses) to sell their products online
  • has understanding on Financial Crime Typologies including money laundering, fraud, online safety, trust and safety
  • is passionate and proactive, who will help ensure Tide is taking all relevant steps to detect and prevent all aspects of fraud, financial crime.
  • understands Trust and Safety in the context of financial crime in a global financial set up with specific focus towards UK and Europe geography.
  • has exceptional communication skills in English (both Written and Speaking).
  • can motivate the team and work in an extremely fast paced environment
  • can use expert judgement to recommend articulating risks

As a Manager Commercial Services (AML&PA), you’ll be

Основные обязанности

  • Strategic Leadership and Operational Management:
  • Lead, scale, and empower a high-performing team of Trust and Safety Specialists who also will work on AML Transaction Monitoring and Payment Sanctions Screening
  • Primary focus will be towards Trust & Safety operations but the role scope will cover AML Transaction Monitoring and Payment Sanctions Screening.
  • Drive operational excellence across ongoing monitoring, alert review, and risk assessment workflows to maintain strict SLA compliance.
  • Define performance metrics, oversee talent development, and champion continuous training in Trust and Safety Risks and financial crime typologies.
  • Establish resource allocation strategies to efficiently balance operational workload and backlogs across financial services oversight.

Risk Governance and Quality Assurance

  • Serve as the final internal escalation authority for complex Trust & Safety investigations.
  • Ensure robust control mechanisms are in place to address evolving financial crime and trust threats specific to Commercial Services product offerings (online listings)
  • Extreme ownership in leading conversations with cross functional stakeholders such as Product Development team, engineering teams, policy and procedures to drive a rapidly growing product line
  • Manage and prioritize alert backlogs to mitigate risk and ensure timely resolution.
  • Subject Matter Expertise and Compliance:
  • Maintain deep domain expertise in financial services, Online safety Act, international sanctions screening regimes, and AML risks
  • Provide strategic oversight on emerging Trust and Safety standards, financial crime typologies, and illegal transaction pattern detection.
  • Act as a subject matter expert (SME) on complex cases, offering guidance on investigation strategies and documentation.
  • Process Optimization and System Enhancements:
  • Partner with Product, Engineering, and Analytics teams to optimize automated rules to detect risks related to Trust and Safety standards and financial crime typologies
  • Leverage data analytics to identify risk trends, eliminate operational bottlenecks, and minimize false positives in payments sanctions screening.
  • Analyze data to identify trends, patterns, and inefficiencies, and recommend solutions to reduce false positives and improve detection effectiveness.
  • Stakeholder Management and Reporting:
  • Present strategic KPI metrics, risk assessments, and performance dashboards to senior executive leadership.
  • Act as key liaison across 2nd Line of Defence, Legal, Regulatory Compliance, and external financial partners for UK and EU markets.
  • Assist in preparing for internal and external audits and regulatory examinations where applicable
  • Engage with employees of partner banks (other than Tide)

Кого мы ищем

  • You have 8+ years of experience in a Trust and Safety Domain. Any experience within Transaction monitoring, Financial Crime risk related role in financial services will be a great advantage
  • You have 5+ years of managerial experience leading teams.
  • A genuinely curious mindset, asking the right questions at the right times, with a problem solving attitude.
  • You have a keen eye for process improvements, looking how we can work leaner and more robustly.
  • You have an analytical mind and are able to digest large data sets and spot patterns and trends with ease
  • You are a quick learner, explorer and multitasker
  • You have exceptional communication skills, written and verbal. Are able to communicate with our members, internal stakeholders & regulatory bodies if required
  • You have C1/C2 level of spoken and written English
  • CAMS, ICA (or similar industry certifications) will be considered as an advantage.
