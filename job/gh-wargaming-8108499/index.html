Job Overview

Bring your passion for creating precise hard-surface models and realistic vehicles into the world of AAA games. Wargaming Kyiv is looking for a skilled 3D Artist to help bring iconic armored vehicles to life in some of our most well-known titles. If you have a sharp eye for detail, strong modeling expertise, and a solid understanding of vehicle design, we’d love to hear from you.

What will you do?

  • Create photorealistic and game-ready 3D models of vehicles, props, and other hard surface assets.
  • Handle the full production cycle, from high-poly sculpting to in-engine export and setup.
  • Utilize cutting-edge CG technology to push the quality bar even higher.
  • Continuously grow your expertise through training, personal development, and knowledge sharing within the team.

What are we looking for?

  • 3+ years of experience in the game development industry as a 3D Artist.
  • Strong hard surface modeling skills, particularly in vehicle creation and mechanical structures.
  • Expertise in high-poly and low-poly modeling, UV mapping, and normal map baking.
  • Solid understanding of PBR texturing workflows and materials.
  • Proficiency in Maya, Substance Painter, ZBrush, and Photoshop.
  • Ability to work from technical documents, blueprints, and real-world references.

What additional skills will help you stand out?

  • Experience with Substance Designer, UVLayout, and Marvelous Designer.
  • Hands-on experience with photogrammetry to enhance realism.
  • Strong drawing or concepting skills to visualize ideas before modeling.

Work mode

  • Hybrid (2-3 days of work from the office) // Remote (from anywhere within Ukraine).
  • This role isn't eligible for relocation & immigration support.

About Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming's titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
