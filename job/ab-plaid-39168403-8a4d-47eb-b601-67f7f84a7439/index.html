О команде

The Fraud team is building the largest network of trusted identities in the US. We help customers onboard legitimate users and stop bad actors before they cause downstream harm. Our products span the risk lifecycle: Identity Verification and Monitor for onboarding and compliance, and Protect, our fraud intelligence platform.

We are extending that network to AI agents. Agents are starting to open accounts, link accounts, move money, and transfer between wallets for consumers. The protocols forming around them standardize how an agent identifies itself and what it may do, and leave the question of whether to trust it to the business on the receiving end. Agent Trust, our Know Your Agent (KYA) product, answers it, on top of the identity, consent, and risk infrastructure Plaid already runs.

You will take over Agent Trust: the decision on whether an action an AI agent takes on a financial account can be trusted. You will define how Plaid identifies agents, verifies the people behind them, and evaluates what they are authorized to do, and how that connects to portable identity, reusable KYC, and consumer permissions. You will sell that decision to financial institutions and to Plaid customers, and you will carry Plaid’s requirements into the standards bodies shaping this space, with the fraud engineering and data teams behind you.

Обязанности

- Own the vision, strategy, roadmap, and OKRs for Agent Trust. Decide what Plaid offers, to whom, and how it is packaged and priced.

- Define how Plaid identifies agents, verifies the humans behind them, and evaluates what they are allowed to do, for new accounts and existing ones.

- Connect Agent Trust to portable identity, reusable KYC, and consumer permission management, with the product teams that own those surfaces.

- Work directly with financial institutions and Plaid customers as the product expert: discovery, design partnerships, and GTM enablement.

- Represent Plaid’s product requirements in the industry groups and protocol working groups writing the specs for agent identity and authorization, with Partnerships and Legal.

- Partner with data science and engineering on what the product needs: signals, labels, success metrics, and the requirements that tie them together.

Требования

- 4+ years of product management or equivalent product ownership, with meaningful time in fraud, identity, authentication, or payments risk

- Hands-on depth in identity and authorization protocols (OAuth 2.x and OIDC, FIDO and WebAuthn, FDX or Open Banking consent), or in bot, device, and agent detection systems

- Track record of growing a new product line, from early design partners and first customers to repeatable sales

- Experience building with and selling to enterprise customers or financial institutions

- Clear writing, and the ability to translate between protocol engineers, data scientists, GTM, and executives
