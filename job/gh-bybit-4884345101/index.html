Job Responsibilities

  • Daily Monitoring & Operations: Responsible for the daily overall monitoring of transaction risk control strategies, maintaining data dashboards, and conducting routine strategy operations to ensure the smooth and stable operation of the risk control system.
  • Risk Feature Mining: Assist risk control analysts in processing and extracting massive amounts of business data; perform data mining and deep analysis to accurately identify abnormal transaction behaviors and potential risk features.
  • Strategy Effectiveness Evaluation: Assist in tracking and evaluating the interception effectiveness and false positive rates (FP rates) of existing risk control strategies, providing data support for strategy iteration and optimization.
  • Risk Reporting: Assist in organizing daily risk control data and participate in drafting business risk analysis reports, as well as weekly and monthly updates.

Job Requirements

  • Educational Background: Current undergraduate or postgraduate students. Majors in STEM fields such as Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Financial Engineering, or Big Data are preferred.
  • Data Skills: Possess solid data analysis capabilities; proficient in SQL to efficiently extract required data from databases; skilled in using Excel for data processing.
  • Core Competencies: Highly sensitive to data, with excellent logical thinking and problem-solving/deconstructing skills; rigorous, detail-oriented, and possesses a strong sense of responsibility.
  • Communication Skills: Strong communication and expression skills, a good team player, and able to integrate into the team quickly.
