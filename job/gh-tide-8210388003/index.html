О TIDE

At Tide, we help SMEs save time and money in the running of their businesses by not only offering business accounts and related banking services, but also a comprehensive set of highly usable and connected administrative solutions, from invoicing to accounting.

Tide is transforming the small business banking market and now supports over 2 million members globally across the UK, Germany and France.

Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love.

О команде

The Ledger team owns the core ledger. It's the system of record behind every account, balance and payment at Tide. Payments, cards and lending all sit on top of it. If it's slow or wrong, members can't pay people, and that hits their business directly. The team works on this one problem.

О роли

Tide is growing into new markets and new products, and the ledger has to scale with that. We're rewriting it and migrating to the new version. The goals are better performance for members and a foundation that keeps up as volumes and products grow. You'll help design it and deliver it.

This is a hands-on role in one team, working on one problem. You'll do architecture, system design, coding and mentoring. You won't have direct reports, but you'll own the technical direction of the ledger. You'll partner closely with the team's Engineering Manager and Product Manager, and lead by example.

We're also changing how we build and test. The team uses AI and agentic tools to deliver working software. We're applying the same approach to functional and performance testing, so we can deliver this work in a fraction of the time it would have taken before. You'll help shape how that works.

Что предстоит делать

  • Design and own the architecture of the new ledger, and make the trade-off calls
  • Write production code and review the team's, so the quality bar is clear
  • Plan and lead the migration from the current ledger: staged rollouts, parallel running, reconciliation and rollback, with a small blast radius for every change
  • Use AI and agentic tools to deliver faster, and help build AI-assisted functional and performance testing
  • Own performance and reliability: latency budgets, SLOs and capacity as volumes grow
  • Mentor and coach engineers at every level
  • Partner with the Engineering Manager and Product Manager, and with the teams that build on the ledger
  • Contribute to our Communities of Practice

Кого мы ищем

Experience and background

  • You've built and run high-volume, real-time distributed systems with hard constraints, such as the time allowed to authorise a transaction
  • You've worked on systems that are too important to fail, where an outage stops customers doing business. That could be in payments, FX, trading, gambling, finance or similar. Payments or banking experience is a bonus, not a requirement
  • You've changed critical systems safely: rewrites, migrations and rollouts where you kept the blast radius small
  • You've used agentic AI tools such as Claude to deliver working software, and changed how you work to get the best out of them. You believe in this approach
  • You've mentored other engineers and set technical direction for a team, without being their line manager

Technical depth

  • Strong backend engineering in a strongly typed language. Java is preferred, but design and architecture skills matter more than the language
  • Distributed systems: consistency, idempotency, concurrency, ordering and failure modes
  • Relational databases at scale, ideally PostgreSQL: transactions, locking, and data modelling for correctness and performance
  • Performance engineering: profiling, latency budgets and load testing
  • Event-driven and asynchronous design
  • Running services in a cloud-native environment (we are AWS-heavy)
  • Testing: functional, performance and resilience

How you work

  • You make pragmatic trade-offs and can explain them
  • You lead by example
  • You coach and develop the engineers around you
  • You use your authority well: you explain your decisions and bring people with you
  • You can talk to non-technical stakeholders without losing precision
  • Our tech stack
  • Java and PostgreSQL, running on AWS
  • Event-driven architecture with messaging over SNS and SQS
  • Observability with Coralogix
  • GitHub for CI/CD with automated testing and security scanning. New joiners typically have something in production within their first two weeks
  • AI coding and agentic tools, including Claude, as part of everyday engineering
