О роли

As a Marketing Specialist, you will help grow Nubank's PJ segment through integrated campaigns, performance analysis and optimizations, and a strong cross-functional execution. You will be the driving force behind our brand touchpoints, ensuring our strategic goals, as well as product features and benefits, reach customers in a clear and impactful way. You will work at the intersection of Product, Content, and different external and internal partners to transform business objectives into delightful communications.

You’ll be Responsible for

- Campaigns: Act as a key contributor in major product launches, ensuring that product narratives are perfectly integrated into the overall campaign concept.

- Bottom-funnel optimization: Coordinate the PJ bottom-funnel workstream, including testing, budget optimization, and creative performance improvements focused on generating incremental releases.

- Content & Narrative Guardianship: Brief the content team and internal channels, ensuring all creative assets are aligned with the brand tone of voice and business objectives.

- Internal and External Partners Management: Manage the end-to-end flow with cross functional teams and external partners, ensuring creative excellence, budget efficiency, and timely delivery.

- Measurement & Success Metrics: Define success metrics, monitor performance funnels, build reports and use data insights to discuss and redefine marketing tactics whenever necessary.

WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR SOMEONE WHO HAS

- Experience: Previous experience in similar positions

- Languages: Fluency in English and Portuguese is mandatory.

- Autonomy & Initiative: You are able to execute tasks independently and lead projects with defined scopes. You thrive when given room for independent decision-making, while knowing when to seek managerial support for highly complex or critical scenarios.

- Problem Solving: Ability to navigate challenging and sometimes ambiguous scenarios involving multiple stakeholders, conducting trade-off analyses to derive clear solutions.

- Analytical Ability: Competence in analyzing structured data from multiple sources (dashboards, surveys) to generate insights that inform team decisions and short-term plans.

- Strategic Alignment: Ability to articulate how your current work contributes to quarterly goals and milestones, balancing immediate deliverables with upcoming dependencies.

- Communication & Influence: Skill in communicating ideas logically and concisely, adapting your communication style to different audiences and stakeholders, and influencing them to drive alignment and action.
