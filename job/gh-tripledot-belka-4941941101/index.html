Who are we?

Tripledot Studios is one of the largest independent mobile games companies in the world.

We are a multi-award-winning organisation, with a global 2,500+ strong team across 12 studios.

Our expanded portfolio includes some of the biggest titles in mobile gaming, collectively reaching top chart positions around the world and engaging over 25 million daily active users.

Tripledot’s guiding principle is that when people love what they do, what they do will be loved by others.

We’re building a company we’re proud of. One filled with driven, incredibly smart and detail-orientated people, who LOVE making games.

Our ambition is to be the most successful games company in the world, and we’re just getting started.

Role Overview

We are looking for a passionate Product Manager to join our team in Barcelona. The role will entail leading a product and elevating it to the next level. The role requires having a holistic view of the entire app, including Roadmap, UA and Monetization.

You will be using real-time data, industry trends and experience to make product decisions, see them through production and use data to validate your hypothesis.

Key Responsibilities

  • Own the product strategy, roadmap and priorities for a mobile game or a significant area of a live game.
  • Use player behaviour, game performance and market data to identify opportunities and make clear product decisions.
  • Define, prioritise and evaluate A/B tests across monetisation, gameplay, progression and level performance.
  • Optimise hybrid monetisation systems across in-app purchases and advertising.
  • Partner with game designers, level designers, developers, artists and analysts to translate product goals into focused execution.
  • Collaborate with user acquisition partners to understand marketability and support effective launch and scaling decisions.
  • Establish success metrics, monitor product KPIs and communicate insights and recommendations to stakeholders.
  • Make evidence-based recommendations on whether to iterate, scale or stop development of new game concepts.
  • Support a Product Analyst as the role and team evolve.

Required Skills, Knowledge and Expertise

  • Significant product management experience within mobile games
  • Strong analytical skills and a track record of using data to diagnose performance, prioritise opportunities and assess impact.
  • Experience with live-game optimisation, including experimentation and A/B testing.
  • Experience working with mobile-game monetisation; ideally hybrid monetisation experience is preferred.
  • Adaptability to work across both new and live products.
  • Strong organisation, ownership and communication skills in a fast-changing environment.
  • Experience in casual mobile games; puzzle-game experience is preferred.
  • A background or education in analytics, data science or a related quantitative discipline is advantageous.
