Binance is a leading global blockchain ecosystem behind the world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume and registered users. We are trusted by 300+ million people in 100+ countries for our industry-leading security, user fund transparency, trading engine speed, deep liquidity, and an unmatched portfolio of digital-asset products. Binance offerings range from trading and finance to education, research, payments, institutional services, and more. Our mission is to increase the freedom of money and financial access for people around the world.

Обязанности

  • Drive and manage P2P platform growth, including new and active users, liquidity, order success rates and disputes.
  • Collaborate closely with product, risk, compliance, customer support, and local teams to maintain operational efficiency and controlled risk levels.
  • Adequate understanding of the local market to independently navigate regulatory environments through cross team collaboration to ensure compliance and support expansion of fiat on/off-ramp capabilities.
  • Monitor key indicators such as spread competitiveness, success rate, completion rate, cancellations, and fraud patterns.
  • Analyze market trends, pricing dynamics, payment methods, and competitive landscape across markets.
  • Identify operational bottlenecks and leverage data insights to propose and implement improvements in workflows, user experience, and channel performance.
  • Manage merchant ecosystem development including onboarding, performance monitoring, incentive programs, and quality assurance.
  • Coordinate operational workflows such as merchant vetting, transaction monitoring, quality reviews, and policy enforcement to uphold channel integrity.
  • Monitor key performance indicators including liquidity balance, spread competitiveness, transaction success rates, cancellations, and fraud patterns.
  • Provide actionable business insights to internal teams to inform strategy, product roadmap, and market expansion plans.
  • Product mindset to collaborate effectively with product teams on feature enhancements and local rollouts.
  • Partner with marketing and community teams to support regional campaigns, payment method adoption, and user education initiatives.
  • Collaborate with customer support and dispute resolution teams to ensure timely and effective handling of user issues.

Требования

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business, Economics, Finance, Engineering, or a related field.
  • 5+ years of experience in crypto exchanges, fintech, banking, or payment platforms.
  • Strong understanding of fiat payment channels and on/off-ramp mechanisms across multiple markets.
  • Strong understanding of crypto landscape, particularly across CIS, including major markets, regulations and user behaviors.
  • Data-driven approach with the ability to analyze performance metrics and translate insights into actionable improvements.
  • Ability to operate in a fast-paced, high-pressure environment with attention to detail and a sense of ownership.
  • Fluency in Ukrainian or Russian language is needed for this position
