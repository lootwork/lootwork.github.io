The International Expansion team at Robinhood builds the systems and product experiences that help bring Robinhood to customers around the world. The team works across onboarding, funding, account setup, localized experiences, and the platform capabilities needed to launch and scale in new markets. In close partnership with product, design, and cross-functional teams, they focus on building reliable, scalable solutions that make global expansion faster, safer, and more seamless for customers.

We are looking for a Web Engineer to join our team. As a Web Engineer, you will lead the web development process and build products from inception to deployment. We also work closely with cross-functional teams like design, product management, and internal platforms to deliver high-impact features within multidisciplinary product teams. This individual will be helping solve challenges on, a web first, high-performance and world-class trading product centered on the web.

This role is based in our Toronto, Canada office, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Create high-quality, performant products that leverage technologies like React, TypeScript, and CSS-in-JS
  • Own and drive features from inception and design to implementation and launch, being the web expert on your team
  • Build and improve tools to support Robinhood's operations and platforms, providing leverage across the company
  • Collaborate with design and product experts to rapidly iterate and ship financial products
  • Build frameworks and abstractions to materially accelerate and empower other developers

Что вы приносите

  • 2+ years of full-time professional experience with modern Web standards, technologies, and frameworks
  • Skilled at writing and efficiently debugging JavaScript in Node and browser environments
  • Obsessed with building a best-in-class developer experience
  • Have a track record of successfully leading projects and delivering large scope technical projects at scale
  • Constantly setting a high standard for code quality, advocating for best practices in testing, and improving code review processes
