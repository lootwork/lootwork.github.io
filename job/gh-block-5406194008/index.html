О роли

At Block, we strive to build the best products in the world — including the ones our customers never see. The Justice Engineering team builds the systems Block uses to meet its legal obligations to law enforcement, courts, and government agencies.

Every month, Block receives thousands of subpoenas, civil notices, tax levies, liens, and receiverships. Getting each one right matters in both directions: responding late or incompletely exposes Block to financial, criminal, and reputational penalties, while producing more data than a request calls for breaks the trust our customers place in us. Our services automate that work end to end — identifying the right customer from a fragment of data, assembling and securely delivering records, freezing and releasing accounts, moving money, and notifying customers — for the Counsel and Compliance teams who respond to these requests on Block's behalf.

As a full stack software engineer on Justice Engineering, you will design, build, and operate these services. We're a small team with a large surface area, so the scope is real: you'll own well-scoped features end to end — from design through testing, launch, and operation — across backend services, the internal web tools our partners work in, and the pipelines that connect us to dozens of upstream systems across Block. You'll also help shape where we're going next: LLM-powered agents that read and triage incoming legal requests, a secure portal for verified external requesters, and the evolution of our tools into a platform other Block teams build on.

Вам предстоит

  • Write high-quality, well-tested code, and participate actively in design reviews and code reviews

  • Partner directly with Counsel, Compliance, and Operations to translate nuanced legal requirements into durable automated workflows
  • Participate in operational ownership of engineering systems, including on-call, incident response, and postmortems
  • Reduce toil and modernize services that grew quickly, leaving each one more reliable than you found it
  • Demonstrate AI fluency by leveraging AI-powered tools throughout the development lifecycle to improve velocity and quality, while applying sound judgment in their use
  • Hold your teammates — and yourself — to high engineering standards
  • Learn from and support other engineers through code reviews and technical discussion
