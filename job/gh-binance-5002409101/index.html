Binance is a leading global blockchain ecosystem behind the world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume and registered users. We are trusted by 300+ million people in 100+ countries for our industry-leading security, user fund transparency, trading engine speed, deep liquidity, and an unmatched portfolio of digital-asset products. Binance offerings range from trading and finance to education, research, payments, institutional services, and more. Our mission is to increase the freedom of money and financial access for people around the world.

Responsiblities

  • Set the product direction and roadmap for backend trading systems.
  • Improve core systems like matching engine, risk engines, pricing models for derivatives products including but not limited to Futures, Margin, Options, Event Contracts etc.
  • Work with the trading engineering teams to turn complex trading needs into technical solutions.
  • Lead the full product lifecycle from planning to release and ongoing/new feature launch.
  • Monitor backend performance and fix issues before they impact users.
  • Ensure systems comply with standards, regulations, and best practices.

Требования

  • Product experience is not a must but preferred.
  • Deep understanding of Financial products like Margin, Futures, Perpetual swaps, options etc.
  • Technical or quantitative background (e.g., CS, Financial Engineering, Math).
  • Ability to understand trading logics, risk methods, and translate them into product requirements.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Proficiency in Mandarin is preferred to coordinate with global stakeholders and overseas partners.

Why Binance

  • Shape the future of finance
  • Collaborate with world-class talent in a user-centric global organization with a flat structure
  • Tackle challenging projects in a fast-paced and innovative enviornment with autonomy
  • Thrive in a results-driven workplace with opportunities for career growth and continuous learning
  • Competitive salary and company benefits
  • Remote-first, work-from-home arrangement (may vary depending on the nature of the role and team)
