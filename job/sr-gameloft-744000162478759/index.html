Job Description

Bring Game Worlds to Life Through Sound

At Gameloft, sound is more than background. Its creates emotion, immersion, and memorable gameplay experiences. We are looking for a Sound Design Intern who is excited to explore the world of game audio and contribute to creating engaging experiences for players around the world.

This internship offers hands-on experience in game audio production and the opportunity to work alongside experienced sound designers, composers, game designers, artists, and programmers throughout the development process.

If you are passionate about games, interactive audio, and eager to grow your skills in a professional game development environment, this role is for you.

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON

Your Main Challenge

Support the Audio Team in creating, editing, implementing, and maintaining high-quality audio assets while learning how audio contributes to the overall player experience.

Main Responsibilities

  • Assist in creating, editing, and implementing sound effects and audio assets for games.
  • Support audio integration using game engines and audio middleware.
  • Manage, edit, and maintain large libraries of voice-over (VO) recordings and other audio assets, ensuring proper organization and file management.
  • Organize and maintain audio libraries, project files, and production documentation.
  • Assist with audio testing, troubleshooting, and quality assurance.
  • Collaborate with designers, programmers, and the audio team on project requirements.
  • Explore new audio technologies, tools, and workflows, including AI-assisted production.

WHAT SUCCESS WILL LOOK LIKE

Within your first 1–2 months, you will become familiar with our tools, workflow, and audio production pipeline while supporting ongoing project activities.

By the end of the internship, you are expected to independently handle assigned audio tasks, contribute to production deliverables, and demonstrate growth in both technical and creative audio skills.

WHO YOU WILL BE WORKING WITH

You will work closely with the Audio Team while collaborating with:

  • Audio Manager
  • Sound Designers & Composers

THE JOURNEY

  • Online Preliminary call with recruiter
  • Distance Technical test
  • Online interview with Creative Audio Director and Audio Project Manager

Требования

Professional Skills

  • Currently studying Audio Production, Sound Design, Music Technology, Game Development, or a related field.
  • Basic proficiency with DAWs such as Reaper, Pro Tools, Audition, or similar software.
  • Understanding of sound design fundamentals, audio editing, and asset management.
  • Passion for video games, interactive audio, and emerging technologies.
  • Familiarity with Unity, Unreal Engine, Wwise, or FMOD is a plus.
  • Portfolio or demo reel showcasing audio-related work is encouraged.
  • Basic experience with AI-assisted audio tools, generative AI, or AI integration into creative workflows
  • Basic Familiarity with AI APIs, audio processing applications.

Кто вы

  • Creative, curious, detail-oriented, and eager to learn.
  • Good communication, teamwork, and openness to feedback.

Additional Information

Life at Gameloft

At Gameloft, you’ll work with a global team of game makers, collaborating across studios to create games played by millions while growing your skills in a creative, flexible, and supportive environment.
