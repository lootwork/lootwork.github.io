Be the person who takes bunq to the UK.

bunq is building its way into UK banking, and the FCA is watching. We’re applying for an EMI license with a Part 4A permission, while keeping our run-off plan with the PRA on track. You’ll own the compliance and MLRO functions for a live regulatory application, with a direct line to senior leadership and a hand in every big decision. When the FCA asks, you answer, on time.

Take Ownership

  • Write the rulebook: the compliance manual, conflict of interest policy, AML controls and data protection rules, built for how bunq actually works
  • Build the Compliance Monitoring Program (CMP), so we know we’re compliant after the license lands
  • Make Consumer Duty real for our users
  • Shape the Regulatory Business Plan (RBP) together with senior leadership
  • File Form A submissions and structure Statements of Responsibilities for our senior people
  • Answer regulator inquiries before the deadline, not on it
  • Keep our register of UK laws and regulations current
  • Draft the UK Compliance Annual Report for the global CRO and UK Board
