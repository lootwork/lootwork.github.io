О Ludios

Ludios takes its name from Latin; it means "The Game People". Led by Eyal, one of the co-founders of Tripledot Studios, and some of the most experienced people in mobile games, we are a studio that makes and publishes our own casual, free-to-play mobile games. Our team is based across APAC, with the majority of our development happening in Jakarta and Melbourne. We are data-driven, creative, and genuinely passionate about the industry we work in. Right now, we are working on a number of exciting projects, including one built around a globally recognised IP, alongside several original titles we are equally excited about. If crafting games and reaching millions of players is what gets you out of bed, we would love to hear from you.

О роли

You will work on creating 3D environment props and assets for a live game. The 3D Artist needs to be able to successfully produce any type of environmental art assets assigned to them and deliver to defined deadlines. Quickly adapt to new art styles and any technical requirements and pipelines. Be diligent in delivering quality following art guidelines and style from the Lead Artist and Art Director, and also be efficient in following the production schedule.

Обязанности

  • Produce models and textures of 3D environment Props, objects, and scenes for the game engine
  • Understand and follow art requirements and specific needs of the project
  • Promptly learn any new tools or techniques
  • Execute tasks assigned to them according to the project schedule while meeting quality expectations
  • Use checklists to perform initial art and technical quality checks before sending art assets to the Team Leader for review
  • Fix all problems noted by the Team Leader and Art Director
  • Promptly report to the Team Leader about any difficulties encountered
  • Support Junior Artists, helping them to deal with tasks when needed

Требования

  • Strong portfolio showing examples of 3d props and environment examples
  • Excellent 3D experience in one or more software: Blender, 3D Studio Max, Maya, Softimage
  • Good knowledge of Photoshop and texture creation
  • Good basic art skills in art fundamentals (colour composition and form)
  • Passionate about games
  • Unity experience is a plus
