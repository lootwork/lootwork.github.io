Задачи

  • To lead the impact of data science on different areas of business, that include data management, data analytics & data innovations through Machine Learning, AI
  • Being the visionaries & execution driver of all data-enabled initiatives in Amanotes. Provide transformative direction and the general technical leadership for Amanotes Data Ecosystem. Coordinate cross-function to leverage data to achieve impactful business outcomes

Что предстоит делать

  • Data Management
  • Supervise the data and information system to ensure adequacy, accuracy, and legitimacy of data products (including but not limited to: reports, insights, analytics tools, models)
  • Establish & Supervise Amanotes data governance and policies
  • Facilitate & Communicate Data Visions and Strategies across functions & business units
  • Data Operations (Data Supports)
  • Anticipate the upcoming trends and business demands to propose impactful data deliverables
  • Supervise the pipeline to deliver data with high quality, timeliness, usefulness; to enable data usability, availability, and efficiency on the Information System (BI, Reportings, Dashboard)
  • Ensure Data Support Excellence in performing data ad-hoc requests
  • Oversee the end-to-end MLOps lifecycle, including automated pipeline deployment for model training, evaluation, and inference at scale
  • Implement robust monitoring and alerting frameworks to track model health, data drift, and performance degradation in production
  • Data Analytics & Insights
  • Define Analytics opportunities to support the growth of Amanotes
  • Develop the Analytics Capacities in Amanotes: Through Data & Data Science techniques, Mastering knowledge about our customers, products, and business

Требования

  • Bachelor's degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Information Technology, Economics, Statistics, Applied Math, Business Administration, or any other related field
  • 3-5 years experience in a senior-level data management role
  • Experience in designing, managing, and delivering data solutions with a proven track record of managing end-to-end delivery from ideation to R&D to production with multiple stakeholders involved (managing timelines, development, deliverables, and expectations)
  • Experience in managing senior stakeholders and communicating complex, technical information in an engaging way to both technical and non-technical audiences
  • Technical understanding or enthusiasm in areas of BI, AI (Incl. Machine Learning), Reporting and visualization, infrastructure, architecture, and governance, are appreciated
  • Demonstrated leadership ability, confidence, and executive presence – ability to lead and motivate employees within the organization
  • Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and data analysis skills
  • High Adaptability (quick-learner & able to adapt quickly with changes in high uncertainty environment)
  • Radical open-mindedness and radical transparency
  • Excellent verbal and written communication, in English and Vietnamese
  • Proven experience with MLOps tools (e.g., MLflow, Kubeflow, DVC) and implementing CI/CD pipelines for machine learning automation
  • Strong understanding of cloud infrastructure (GCP/AWS) and infrastructure-as-code for managing ML production environments
  • High level of strategic thinking & critical thinking
  • Exceptional team management skills

Будет плюсом

  • Master’s degree or Ph.D. in Data Analytics, Data Scientists, or related fields
  • Working experience related to the mobile/music/game industry is a plus
  • Knowledge of Modern Data Stacks, i.e. GCP, AWS, and MLOps is a plus
  • Passion for Music (such as playing musical instruments and love to sing karaoke) is a huge asset
  • Showcase in designing initiatives and leading the organization’s data strategy
