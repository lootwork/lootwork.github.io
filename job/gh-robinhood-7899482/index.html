The Security Platform team is responsible for the secure lifecycle, governance, and protection of Robinhood’s most sensitive user data. This team builds foundational systems such as secure data pipelines, tokenization services, and privacy compliance infrastructure to ensure customer data is handled responsibly and in line with regulations. The team works closely with security, infrastructure, and product engineering partners to ensure data is both usable and protected. You will contribute to systems that support authentication, third-party integrations, and emerging AI-driven use cases.

As a Senior Software Engineer, you will design and build backend systems that securely process and manage customer data across Robinhood’s platform. You will own the systems that handle authentication, authorization, and privacy-preserving data operations. This role involves close collaboration with engineers focused on access management, infrastructure, and data systems. Your work will directly support efforts to improve system reliability, strengthen data protections, and enable new product capabilities using secure data.

This role is based in our Bellevue, WA, and Menlo Park, CA offices, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build backend services and data pipelines that securely store, process, and route customer data
  • Develop systems for token issuance, user authentication, and anonymization to support privacy requirements
  • Define and execute the vision for authentication across next-generation AI platforms, intelligent agents, and MCP ecosystems, shaping how secure identity works in an AI-first world
  • Implement data retention and deletion workflows aligned with regulatory standards such as CCPA and GDPR
  • Partner with access management engineers to enforce least-privilege access controls across systems
  • Contribute to monitoring and auditing systems that track how data is accessed and used

Что вы приносите

  • You have 5+ years of software engineering experience focused on backend systems, data infrastructure, or security
  • You are proficient in at least one backend language such as Python, Go, or Java
  • You have experience building and operating services in cloud environments such as AWS and Kubernetes
  • You have worked with distributed systems such as databases, messaging systems, or data processing frameworks (e.g., PostgreSQL, Kafka, Spark, Snowflake)
  • You understand data protection principles such as encryption, secure data handling, and privacy-focused system design
