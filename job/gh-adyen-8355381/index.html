Salesforce Developer, Service Cloud

We build the Salesforce platform our Operations and Support teams use every day. We are early in rolling out Service Cloud across a complex, global business and IT environment, and we are looking for a developer who wants to shape how it is built.

This is a hands-on engineering role. Most of your time goes into designing and writing code: Apex, Lightning Web Components and integrations with Adyen's back-office systems. Because the team is small, you will also own a share of the admin work, from building Flows and configuring objects to managing permissions and fixing user issues. We are looking for someone who sees that as part of owning the platform, not as a distraction from it.

Что предстоит делать

  • Design, build and maintain Service Cloud features using Apex, triggers, LWC and SOQL.
  • Leverage AI-assisted development to improve engineering productivity, code quality and delivery efficiency.
  • Build integrations between Salesforce and Adyen's in-house middleware and back-office systems using REST, SOAP and Pub/Sub APIs.
  • Configure the platform declaratively where that is the better choice: Flows, validation rules, page layouts, record types, case management setup.
  • Manage users, profiles, permission sets and sharing rules together with Security and Compliance.
  • Refine user stories with Operations specialists and the team, and propose the right balance between configuration and code.
  • Review code, approve pull requests, and resolve merge conflicts in our CI/CD pipeline (Gearset, GitHub).
  • Refactor existing Apex classes and Flows for performance and maintainability.

Кто вы

  • You have 3-6 years of hands-on Salesforce development experience, ideally including Service Cloud.
  • You write solid Apex, triggers, SOQL and Lightning Web Components, and know Salesforce design patterns, governor limits and testing best practices.
  • You have built integrations using REST/SOAP APIs or platform events.
  • You are comfortable with declarative tools (Flows, configuration, security model) and happy to pick up admin tasks when needed.
  • You are comfortable with Git, branching and resolving merge conflicts, and have worked with a CI/CD tool such as Gearset or Copado.
  • You can explain technical trade-offs to non-technical stakeholders.
