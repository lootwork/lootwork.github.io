Lead Salesforce Developer

We are seeking a highly skilled and innovative Lead Salesforce Developer to join our dynamic team. The ideal candidate will possess deep expertise in Salesforce development, Integration, architecture, and a proven track record of implementing intelligent, self-optimizing systems. This is a unique opportunity to shape the future of our customer engagement and operational efficiency using cutting-edge technology.

As a key member of the Business Technology and Transformation (BTT) team, this Lead Salesforce Developer will partner with Product Managers, architects, and engineering leadership to architect and deploy cross-engineering solutions. You will play a foundational role in executing Toast's strategic roadmap, driving the platform's evolution through technical excellence and forward-thinking innovation.

Основные обязанности

  • Salesforce Architecture & Development: Lead the design, development, and implementation of complex, scalable solutions within the Salesforce platform (Sales Cloud, Salesforce CPQ, Service Cloud, etc.) using Apex, Lightning Web Components (LWC), Visualforce, and other Salesforce technologies. Salesforce CPQ configuration and implementation.Deep understanding of the nuances within the Quote-to-Cash domain. Salesforce Integration Patterns and technologies for integrating with upstream/downstream systems
  • Technical Leadership: Mentor and guide a team of Salesforce developers, conducting code reviews, establishing best practices for Design and Development, and ensuring high-quality, maintainable code. Lead troubleshooting exercise for complex issues
  • Collaboration: Work closely with Product Managers, Business Analysts, and Stakeholders to translate business requirements into technical specifications and deliver innovative solutions.
  • Deployment and Governance: Manage release cycles, deployment processes (using tools like Azure DevOps, GitLab, or Copado), and maintain platform health, security, and governance standards.
  • Stay Current: Continuously research and evaluate new Salesforce features, industry trends, and advancements in AI/Agentic technologies to recommend and implement relevant improvements.
  • AI Proficiency: Stays current on the AI tools and technologies available, and applies them to meaningfully increase velocity on day-to-day work. Has hands-on experience with at least one AI tool, using it as a regular part of their workflow. Acts as an advocate for AI adoption, and open to exploring new tools and use cases as they emerge. Extends that impact beyond their own work by helping teammates ramp up, sharing tips and workflows, and guiding the team toward broader and more effective AI usage

Required Qualifications

10+ years of Salesforce solution design, architecture, and development experience (Apex, Lightning, Visualforce) in an enterprise environment.

  • Advanced experience with sharing and security, visibility, data models, Flows, and other Salesforce platform features and components.
  • Experience with Salesforce Sales Cloud or Service Cloud.
  • Experience with Salesforce CPQ configuration and implementation, including Product and Pricing Rules.
  • xExperience with Salesforce discounting, contracts, quote templates, and renewals.
  • Experience in the Quote-to-Cash domain, preferably with CPQ tools (Salesforce CPQ, Conga, or Industry CPQ).
  • Experience integrating Salesforce with other systems in an enterprise environment.
  • Experience with middleware technologies (Workato preferred, or equivalent).
  • Experience leading a team of developers on project and feature delivery.
  • Advanced understanding of the software development life cycle and Agile methodologies.
  • Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making skills.
  • Ability to work independently and support other team members as needed.
  • Salesforce Platform Developer I & II certifications required; Salesforce CPQ certification a plus.
  • Strong stakeholder management and communication skills.
  • Ability to adapt and learn quickly.
  • Engineering degree or equivalent educational background required.
  • Hybrid work model: 2 days per week in office.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with advanced DevOps practices for Salesforce (CI/CD pipelines, automated testing), version control tools (GitHub) and Copado
  • Knowledge of data modeling and management in large-scale Salesforce environments.
  • Experience using Jira or similar
