Что предстоит делать

As a Senior Product Manager, GTPN (Global Treasury & Payment Network), you will drive complex, technically oriented product initiatives across our transfers and collections infrastructure and API platform. This role combines product ownership with strong technical orientation, particularly where success depends on platform capabilities, infrastructure, partner integrations, operational flows, and cross-team dependencies.

You will own the product problem, business outcomes, scope, prioritisation, and requirements, while partnering closely with engineering on technical strategy and implementation. This is a high-impact, strategic role with the opportunity to shape the future of global payments and financial infrastructure. This role is based in Singapore.

Обязанности

- Own the end-to-end product lifecycle for GTPN APIs and money movement infrastructure, from solutioning to delivery and ongoing optimization.

- Develop and champion the strategic vision and roadmap for the platform, balancing long-term innovation with short-term business needs.

- Engage directly with enterprise customers, developers, and partners to understand their needs, gather feedback, and validate product features.

- Translate complex use cases into scalable, secure, and intuitive APIs in collaboration with engineering teams.

- Lead cross-functional teams—including engineering, design, sales, compliance, and data science—to deliver impactful solutions.

- Use data-driven methods to define and measure success metrics, prioritize requirements, and communicate plans and launches.

- Represent GTPN in strategic conversations with senior stakeholders and enterprise partners.

- Stay ahead of industry trends in payments, APIs, and financial technology, and leverage AI/ML where relevant to drive product innovation.

Кто вы

We're looking for people who meet the minimum requirements for this role. The preferred qualifications are great to have, but are not mandatory.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

- 5+ years of product management experience, with at least 2-3 years focused on platform/API products in fintech, payments, or financial infrastructure.

- Proven track record of defining, building, and launching successful, scalable API-powered platforms or products.

- Deep technical understanding of API design principles, security, and developer experience.

- Experience considering both user requirements and commercial strategy to drive meaningful product growth.

- Strong ability to manage trade-offs between long-term vision and short-term execution.

- Excellent communication skills, with the ability to convey product strategy to both technical and non-technical audiences.

- Comfortable working in data-driven, cross-functional teams in a fast-paced, global environment.

- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.

- Flexibility to work across multiple time zones and with global teams.

Будет плюсом

- Experience with global payment networks or banking partners.

- Familiarity with OpenAPI/Swagger, Postman, GitHub, or API client tooling.

- Hands-on experience as a developer is a plus.

- Experience in hyper-growth startup environments, financial infrastructure, or technology.

- Master’s degree in a relevant field.

- Passion for solving complex problems at the intersection of finance, artificial intelligence, and technology platforms.
