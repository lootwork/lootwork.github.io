О Company

Gemini is a global crypto and Web3 platform founded by Cameron and Tyler Winklevoss in 2014, offering a wide range of simple, reliable, and secure crypto products and services to individuals and institutions in over 70 countries. Our mission is to unlock the next era of financial, creative, and personal freedom by providing trusted access to the decentralized future. We envision a world where crypto reshapes the global financial system, internet, and money to create greater choice, independence, and opportunity for all — bridging traditional finance with the emerging cryptoeconomy in a way that is more open, fair, and secure. As a publicly traded company, Gemini is poised to accelerate this vision with greater scale, reach, and impact.

The Department: Invest / Trade

The Role: Product Manager

Gemini is building the best way to trade and invest — and we just launched a new stock trading product to do it. We're looking for a Product Manager to help own this from the ground up: shaping how customers buy, sell, and track stocks on Gemini, and making the case that a crypto-native platform can be the best place to trade equities too.

The ideal candidate combines a drive for innovation with a thorough understanding of both traditional and digital assets trading and performance, aiming to set new benchmarks in user experience and operational excellence. Working closely with Engineering, Design, Data, and Marketing, as well as across the Product organization, you'll employ a data-driven decision-making process and a growth mindset to ensure our platform's features and functionality lead the market in innovation, efficiency, and excellence.

This is a highly visible role at the center of our Stocks roadmap. You'll be one of the first PMs on a product that's still being defined, designed for a builder who wants to set the strategy and create exciting new user experiences.

This role is required to be in person twice a week at our New York City, NY office.

Обязанности

  • Own specific parts of the stock trading experience end-to-end from the first customer insight to a shipped feature customers use.
  • Dig into customer behavior, support tickets, market research, and your own trading instincts to find the highest-leverage problems to solve next.
  • Write clear specs, make the tradeoffs explicit, and drive features from idea to launch alongside Engineering, Design, Data, and Marketing.
  • Sweat the details including onboarding flows, order execution, statements, and edge cases. For a product this new, the details are the product.
  • Ship fast, measure, and iterate.

Требования

  • 2-4 years of product management experience, ideally at a high-growth tech company or fintech.
  • AI pilled - you leverage Claude and other AI tools in your day to day.
  • A genuine interest in stock trading and investing. You don't need to have been a professional trader, but you should be someone who actually trades or follows markets.
  • A bias toward action: you'd rather ship something small and learn than wait for the perfect plan.
  • Strong written communication and interpersonal skills. You can turn a messy problem into a clear spec or a tight Slack update.
