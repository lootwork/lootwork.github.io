YOUR MISSION

Pathfinder is the routing algorithm behind the 1inch swap aggregator. In traditional finance it’s called a smart order router (SOR). It searches many liquidity sources in real time to find users the best available rate, under tight latency and cost limits. You’ll own Pathfinder’s architecture, performance and evolution. Your work makes routing faster, cheaper to run and more reliable at production scale, and it directly affects the rate users get on every swap.

Что предстоит делать

  • Shape the architecture of Pathfinder and its surrounding services, with a focus on latency, throughput and cost per swap.
  • Build efficient, low-latency software in Go and Rust.
  • Find and remove bottlenecks across the stack, from routing logic to infrastructure, with a focus on tail latency (p99, the slowest 1% of requests).
  • Run Pathfinder in production, including deployment on Kubernetes, monitoring, load testing, incident response and root-cause analysis.
  • Improve the routing logic using graph-based pathfinding and optimization techniques such as mixed-integer linear programming (MILP).
  • Use AI tools to speed up development and improve routing quality.
  • Contribute to code reviews and architecture discussions, and mentor other engineers.

Кто вы

  • You have five or more years of experience building and running production systems in high-load or performance-critical environments.
  • You have run CPU-heavy services at scale, for example 1,000+ requests per second on 50+ CPU cores and 100+ GB of memory, with strict latency targets (p50 under 100 ms).
  • You have deep expertise in Rust, Go or C++, including the memory model, allocation patterns and garbage collection tuning.
  • You have strong high-load system design skills: queueing, backpressure, caching, batching, load balancing, timeouts and retries, and serialization overhead.
  • You have hands-on experience with metrics, distributed tracing, profiling and load-testing tools. You know Kubernetes basics and how software maps to compute, latency and cost.
  • You have hands-on DeFi experience with DEXes, AMMs, on-chain data or blockchain products.
  • You know EVM fundamentals: transactions, blocks, gas (gas limit, gas used, gas price) and ERC-20 tokens.
  • You use AI tools in your engineering work, question their output and take responsibility for the code you ship.
  • You own critical systems end to end. You debug complex problems, measure the impact of your changes and drive initiatives through ambiguity.
  • You explain technical trade-offs clearly and work well with engineering and product teams.

Будет плюсом

  • Deep knowledge of AMM pricing across pool types, such as Uniswap, Curve and Balancer
  • A strong algorithms background in graph theory, routing or combinatorial optimization
  • Experience with smart order routing in traditional finance or crypto
  • A degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, Operations Research or a related field, or equivalent practical experience
