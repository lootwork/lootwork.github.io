Help shape the next era of an iconic survival franchise.

For over a decade, 7 Days to Die has captivated more than 20 million players worldwide. Now, with Behaviour Interactive joining forces with The Fun Pimps, we’re entering an exciting new phase of growth, bringing together deep live game expertise and the creative legacy behind the franchise. This is a rare opportunity to help elevate a beloved game, scale its long-term potential, and shape what comes next for millions of players.

Based in Montréal and reporting to the Associate Director, Community Management, the Community Developer for 7 Days to Die serves as the critical bridge between players and the development team. This role is responsible for transforming player feedback into actionable insights, strengthening relationships with the community, and ensuring the player perspective helps inform the evolution of the game.

В этой роли вы будете

  • Own and continuously improve the player feedback loop, from feedback collection and analysis to internal communication and player-facing follow-up.
  • Monitor player sentiment and community discussions across Discord, Steam, Reddit, forums, and social platforms to identify trends, opportunities, and emerging issues.
  • Translate qualitative and quantitative feedback into clear recommendations that support game development, live operations, and marketing initiatives.
  • Plan and execute community engagement initiatives, including events, livestreams, playtests, and feedback programs.
  • Build trusted relationships with players, creators, community leaders, and the modding community to strengthen player advocacy and insight gathering.
  • Collaborate closely with Production, Product.

Кого мы ищем

  • 5 to 9 years of professional experience, including at least 3 years in game community management or a related player-facing role.
  • Strong expertise with community platforms such as Discord, Steam, Reddit, X, YouTube, and Twitch.
  • Demonstrated experience gathering, analyzing, and synthesizing player feedback using community analytics, surveys, and social listening methodologies.
  • Proficiency with tools such as Gamesight, Power BI, Tableau, Excel, Confluence, Jira, Monday.com, and Microsoft 365.
  • Excellent communication, relationship-building, and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence through insights and data.
  • Experience with live-service games, survival or sandbox titles, modding communities, or bilingual proficiency in French and English is considered an asset.
