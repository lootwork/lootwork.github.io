This is Adyen

Adyen provides payments, data, and financial products in a single solution for customers like Meta, Uber, H&M, and Microsoft - making us the financial technology platform of choice. At Adyen, everything we do is engineered for ambition.

For our teams, we create an environment with opportunities for our people to succeed, backed by the culture and support to ensure they are enabled to truly own their careers. Together, we deliver innovative and ethical solutions that help businesses achieve their ambitions faster.

О команде

The Adyen Internal Communications team is looking for a Senior Communications Business Partner to help scale our global Tech organization (Product & Engineering).

In this role, you will build foundational communication systems and content that shape how thousands of colleagues connect, collaborate, and deliver impact globally.

The role requires a high degree of autonomy, comfort with ambiguity, strong decision-making, and the flexibility to travel (particularly to the US a few times per year).

Что предстоит делать

  • Become as a strategic partner to Tech leadership, shaping and executing internal communications that align with business priorities.
  • Design a communications matrix that works across global, local, and functional levels.
  • Be a multiplier: empower teams by creating scalable communication approaches that enable - not bottleneck - them.
  • Lead content strategy and storytelling for high-impact Tech events, announcements, and initiatives.
  • Develop communication plans that bring clarity, consistency, and momentum to cross-functional programs.
  • Translate complex technical topics into clear, compelling narratives that resonate with diverse audiences.

Bring creative, non-traditional approaches rather than replicating what's been done before.

Кого мы ищем

  • Proven expertise: at least 10+ years of experience in internal communications, ideally supporting technology or product teams in a global organization.
  • Someone who can think big-picture but also loves rolling up their sleeves and getting the details right.
  • A listener who asks smart questions, isn’t afraid to challenge or be challenged, and cares about learning and improving.
  • A collaborator who enjoys partnering with different teams and working in a constantly evolving environment.
  • A strong storyteller and writer who can simplify complexity without flattening it.
  • Someone who is collaborative and relationship-driven - skilled at building trust with stakeholders and aligning across teams.
  • A person who is comfortable in a fast-paced, scaling environment and can bring structure where it doesn’t yet exist.
  • Curious by nature, excited to really understand how the Tech function works, and willing to dive deep.
