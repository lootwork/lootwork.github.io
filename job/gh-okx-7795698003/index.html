О Opportunity

As a Technical Expert, you will own the core build-out of the company's R&D Efficiency Platform, proactively identifying and eliminating risks across the delivery pipeline through CI/CD, DevSecOps, Change Management, and AI-Native upgrades—reducing delivery cost while improving quality and efficiency via platformization and automation.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build core capabilities of the R&D Efficiency Platform and onboard business systems to improve overall engineering efficiency and delivery quality;
  • Design and optimize CI/CD pipelines (GitLab, Pipeline, ArgoCD), driving standardization, automation, and observability;
  • Drive the AI-Native evolution of CI/CD, introducing AI Agent / LLM for intelligent code review, pipeline self-healing, smart test orchestration, and change-risk prediction;
  • Build DevSecOps capabilities, embedding Shift-Left Security across the lifecycle with SAST, DAST, SCA, image/secret scanning, and security gates to ensure supply-chain security;
  • Establish standardized Change Management, covering requests, risk assessment, approvals (CAB), canary releases, rollback, and change traceability;
  • Apply Kubernetes, microservice governance (Nacos, Apollo, Kong), and Aliyun / AWS cloud services to ensure platform stability and scalability.

What We Look For In You

  • Solid Computer Science fundamentals (OS, networking, distributed systems, data structures & algorithms);
  • Proficient in DevOps toolchains, CI/CD, Kubernetes, and microservice stacks;
  • Familiar with DevSecOps concepts and security toolchains, understanding supply-chain security and compliance;
  • Knowledge of AI / LLM in engineering-efficiency and CI/CD scenarios, with an AI-Native engineering mindset;
  • Familiar with the Java stack, with broad awareness of multi-language and business-side technologies;
  • Strong product sense, ownership, and ability to work under pressure;
  • Fluent English communication for efficient cross-regional collaboration.
