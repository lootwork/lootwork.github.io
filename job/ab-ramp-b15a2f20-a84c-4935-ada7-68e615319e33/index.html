О RAMP

Ramp is building the smart infrastructure for finance teams, embedded in the transaction flow of every dollar a business spends. We automate how over $275B in annualized spend flows in and out of 85,000+ companies: authorizing payments, flagging risk, categorizing spend, and closing books.

The problems are high-stakes, data-dense, and unforgiving.

We hire people with high agency and high urgency. We look for slope over intercept. We care less about where you trained and more about what you’ve built. At Ramp, everyone is a builder who owns problems end to end and makes consequential decisions that shape the outcome.

The median Ramp customer saves 7.5% and grows revenue 16% in their first year – far in excess of businesses operating without Ramp. We believe every ambitious company deserves the same.

If you want to build systems that directly shape how companies move and manage billions, Ramp is the place to do it.

О роли

We’re looking for a Growth Lead to build the growth engine for a new product line. Your mandate includes both acquiring customers that don’t use Ramp today, and driving adoption of the new product among existing customers. You’ll help shape how customers discover the product, understand its value, and decide to adopt it.

We are looking for someone who can determine which growth channels are most pertinent for their product, figure out the strategy for those channels and then successfully execute on the strategy. You’ll decide what to test, launch campaigns, and turn what works into repeatable growth. You should be equally comfortable developing a strategy, writing a landing page, digging into funnel performance, and building an automated workflow.

Что предстоит делать

- Set the growth strategy and execute on it. Identify the audiences, messages, and channels with the greatest potential. Turn ideas into live experiments, and scale up the successful experiments into perpetual lead generators.

- Acquire customers beyond Ramp’s existing base. Build campaigns that introduce the product to new businesses and convert interest into qualified pipeline. Develop a clear understanding of customer needs and translate it into compelling reasons to act.

- Make the product part of the broader Ramp sale. Partner with sales to increase your product’s attach rate in deals that were initiated through other products. Improve the positioning, campaigns, and sales touchpoints that help customers understand where the product fits and why it matters.

- Drive adoption across existing customers. Build cross-sell programs that drive adoption of the product among existing Ramp customers

- Build and operate the channel mix. Launch and optimize landing pages, conversion funnels, paid search and social, SEO, sponsorships, direct mail, and events.

- Use automation to multiply your impact. Create workflows that reduce manual work across campaign execution, experimentation, and reporting. Use AI thoughtfully to move faster without making every initiative dependent on additional resources.

WHAT YOU NEED

- Minimum 2 years of experience in management consulting, investment banking, private equity, venture capital, and/or growth / operations at a fast-growing startup

- Demonstrated ability to dissect complex problems, identify solutions, and drive these solutions to completion with a high degree of autonomy

- Proficiency with data analytics with an ability to draw key insights from both quantitative and qualitative data

- High level of resourcefulness and a track record of being a fast learner

- Communication skills to influence others at all levels and across all functions within the organization to rally behind shared business goals

Как выглядит успех в этой роли

You’ve built a clearer understanding of which audiences, messages, and channels drive growth for the product. Promising experiments have become repeatable programs. More new customers are discovering the product, and more existing Ramp customers are adopting it.

You should also build a way of working that gets faster and smarter with each experiment, not a collection of campaigns that depend on constant manual effort. Thinking from first principles should lead you to discover growth tactics that are new to the company.
