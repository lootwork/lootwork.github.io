О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

ACCOUNT EXECUTIVE, MID MARKET

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

You'll help expand Airwallex's cross-border customer base by developing new business, understanding customer needs, and turning relevant opportunities into long-term relationships.

- Drive new business growth by identifying, engaging, and signing cross-border customers through direct outreach, referrals, industry events, and commercial partnerships.

- Build trusted relationships with customers in advertising and adjacent entertainment, online travel, online education, and other relevant verticals, understanding their payment challenges and shaping targeted solutions with internal Product partners.

- Guide customers through the full lifecycle from registration and KYC to launch and ongoing transactions, helping them adopt Airwallex reliably and grow their usage over time.

- Maintain a strong awareness of transaction compliance, using sound judgment to identify potential risks and assess the reasonableness and authenticity of customer activity.

- Stay close to market developments, identify new commercial opportunities, and share insights that help Airwallex improve its solutions and build sustainable growth.

This is an in-office role — you'll be in our Shenzhen office five days a week.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- 5+ years of sales experience, with a track record of developing customers and delivering revenue outcomes.

- Experience building and maintaining strong customer relationships through clear, effective communication.

- Strong self-motivation, a results-oriented approach, and personal accountability for outcomes.

- Strong learning ability, commercial judgment, adaptability, and resilience.

- The ability to identify market opportunities, analyse customer needs, and develop and maintain a high-quality customer pipeline.

- A bachelor’s degree or above.

Preferred

- Experience in cross-border payments, cross-border e-commerce, direct-to-consumer commerce, ERP, overseas advertising agencies, or a related industry.

- Experience selling to customers in advertising and adjacent entertainment, online travel, online education, or other relevant verticals.

- Experience working with internal Product or Solutions teams to translate customer needs into industry-specific solutions.

- Experience managing customer onboarding, KYC, launch, and ongoing transaction activity.

YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF

- You have a track record of hitting your numbers, and you’re motivated by stretch goals.

- You stay close to your industry, follow trends, and bring outside perspective into your work.

- You own the outcome and you don't wait for permission to fix what's broken.

- You're comfortable making decisions with incomplete information and moving quickly anyway.

- You've operated in a compliance-heavy environment and know how to move fast without cutting the corners that matter.

LEARN MORE ABOUT YOUR TEAM

SME & Growth at Airwallex is about more than closing deals. We help small and mid-market businesses solve real financial challenges across borders: getting paid globally, managing multi-currency accounts, paying suppliers, and running spend across markets from a single platform. You're not selling a feature. You're helping founders and finance leaders replace fragmented financial operations with one global system that actually scales.

It's a function for sellers who like to build, not follow a script: curious, resourceful, and comfortable figuring things out in markets that are still wide open. You get real autonomy to create value, build relationships, and move opportunities forward.

If you want to sell a serious product, solve real problems for ambitious businesses, and build your career fast, this is a place to do it.
