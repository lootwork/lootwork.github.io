The Role 🚀

We’re hiring a Social Content Manager to join the team at Monzo. We’re looking for a brilliant social strategist and storyteller to help us scale our next chapter: showing up less like a standard brand account and more like an authoritative, high-craft Media Publisher.

Through witty, relatable, and deeply educational content, we’ve already proven that we can challenge what a bank looks like online. But as we expand, our goal is to drive deep trust and authority around the culture of money. We want to take complex financial concepts and turn them into highly engaging, platform-native formats that position Monzo as THE banking partner of choice.

This role is to build always-on, high-performing organic ecosystems that drive real brand love and broaden our product association across our portfolio.

We’ve built a great foundation, but we want to go further. As our Social Content Manager, you’ll be the creative engine behind our feeds. You won't just follow internet culture; you’ll help us create it, turning complex financial concepts into scroll-stopping, uniquely Monzo content.

The Team 💫

You’ll join a tight-knit, collaborative social team. You’ll report directly to our Head of Social and Influencer, working alongside another Social Content Manager (who focuses on campaign strategy) and a dedicated Social Creative Producer.

Monzo is growing up, and we’re ready for our next stage of growth. We’re moving away from the old playbook to build a high-quality, always-on social ecosystem with partners at the top of their game, and we need someone to lead the charge. This role is a massive piece of the puzzle. You’ll have the creative autonomy to experiment, tear up the rulebook, and directly define how Monzo shows up across the internet.

You’ll play a key role by...

  • Champion platform-native creativity: Bring a deep, nuanced understanding of social platforms, including TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook and Threads. You know what makes audiences click, share, and engage, and you’ll use this expertise to make sure our content always feels native, timely, and culturally relevant.
  • Co-create with agency partners and creators: Act as a key creative bridge, collaborating hand-in-hand with external agency partners and creators. You'll help brief, guide, and refine their output to design and execute repeatable, episodic content formats that build long-term audience loyalty.
  • Collaborate cross-functionally: Work seamlessly across internal teams, partnering with Brand Designers, Copywriters, and the broader marketing org, to align social-first concepts with our overarching brand strategies and business goals.
  • Translate platform insights into action: Keep a pulse on performance metrics and platform trends. You’ll translate data and audience behavior into actionable creative insights, continuously refining your concepts to keep our brand at the cutting edge. You won’t be afraid to experiment and learn from the data.

We’d love to hear from you if…

  • You balance strategic partnerships with hands-on hustle. You’re completely comfortable collaborating with agency partners to bring big ideas to life, but you’re equally excited to roll up your sleeves and get stuck into the day-to-day execution.
  • You have a deep understanding of social platforms. You know how to hook an audience in the first two seconds, you understand algorithm behavior, and you have a portfolio of social-first content you’re incredibly proud of that shows your ability to translate concepts across channels.
  • You’re driven by commercial performance. You don’t just create for the sake of views or vanity metrics; you have a strong commercial instinct and a genuine interest in how creative storytelling drives real business growth.
  • You thrive in a lean, agile environment. You’re excited to be a key player in a tight-knit, in-house team where your impact is felt daily and collaboration is second nature. You’ll be an expert at understanding how our always on strategy can support other key brand moments, collaborating seamlessly with our campaign team to bring this to life seamlessly.
  • You have high empathy and good judgment. Money can be a sensitive topic. You know how to be funny and provocative without punching down or taking unnecessary risks with our brand reputation.
  • You have impeccable planning skills. You’ll have your finger on the pulse of money and wider culture to look at how we integrate trending conversations in to our brand world, tastefully and with meaningful value for our audience.
