Staff Software Engineer, (Money Flows: Payouts)

О команде

Payouts is one of five Money Flows domains at Ebury. The team owns the Core payout platform, which sends client money to beneficiaries worldwide.

О роли

You will have ownership of large, mission-critical payment services. You’ll start delivering to production in your first week, with a clear path to technical leadership for those who show exceptional performance. You’ll work with Product, Operations, Treasury, Compliance and other Money Flows domains.

Основные обязанности

  • Design, build, test and deploy scalable payout services: routing, partner and bank integrations, payment message generation, validations and exception handling.
  • Integrate new payment rails and partners (for example SEPA Instant, SEPA Direct Debit, and local clearing partners). Own these from technical design through go-live and post-launch monitoring.
  • Implement payment messaging standards such as ISO 20022 (pacs.008), SWIFT and SEPA. Work with partner banks to resolve mapping and format issues.
  • Increase straight-through processing and the share of payments paid locally, reducing manual payments, cost and settlement time.
  • Build and maintain tools for Operations and Front Office, such as routing rule management and dashboards.
  • Contribute to architecture and design reviews across Money Flows, including cross-domain dependencies with Accounts, Collections and Beneficiaries.
  • Uphold engineering standards: code review, automated testing, CI/CD, observability and operational resilience, including disaster recovery readiness.
  • Mentor engineers and help shape the team’s technical direction.
  • Use AI tools to speed up your work, and fully understand and own everything you ship.

Required experience

  • Typically 7+ years writing production-grade code, though competency, attitude and ability matter more than the number.
  • Strong grounding in data structures, algorithms, software design and complexity analysis.
  • Proficiency in at least one modern language [Java / Python].
  • Cloud (AWS, GCP, Azure) and containerisation (Docker, Kubernetes) experience.
  • Experience designing distributed, event-driven or API-based services where correctness, idempotency and auditability matter.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, and the ability to break down ambiguous problems.
  • Clear communication with technical and non-technical stakeholders, including partner banks and Operations.
  • A Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related discipline, or equivalent experience.

Desirable not mandatory

  • Hands-on knowledge of payment schemes and standards (SEPA, SEPA Instant, SWIFT, FPS, ISO 20022, SEPA Direct Debit).
  • Experience integrating with banks or payment providers.
  • Familiarity with FX, liquidity or treasury flows, or with regulated payment products.
  • Experience with data and observability tooling for monitoring payment flows.
  • Open-source contributions or a portfolio showing technical curiosity.

Что мы предлагаем

  • Competitive salary, performance-based bonus and a comprehensive benefits package.
  • Dedicated mentorship from senior engineers and a structured career path, with accelerated progression for exceptional performance.
  • Complex, regulated, high-volume problems with measurable business impact.
  • An inclusive, high-performance culture in a high-growth global FinTech.
