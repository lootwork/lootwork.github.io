Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy.

At Toast, the IQ Grow team is on a mission to scale new, high-impact go-to-market initiatives. By combining the cutting-edge innovation of AI agentic solutions with human-in-the-loop workflows and touchpoints, we are redefining how restaurants connect with their guests. Our goal is to deliver a truly personalized marketing experience that empowers Toast customers to build lasting relationships, drive engagement and grow their businesses. If you're excited about pioneering at the intersection of advanced AI and human-centered design to make a real-world impact, we’d love to have you on the team! You will be a key part of a high-impact, high-ownership engineering team solving complex challenges in a fast-moving product space. Your engineering leadership will be balancing technical excellence alongside a strong product mindset to solve for continuous growth within both the team and our product space.

A day in the life (Responsibilities)

  • Design, build and maintain high performing, flexible, and scalable solutions that help restaurants deliver a best-in-class guest experience : define and execute the technical architecture for AI agents within Marketing and Demand Generation domains, ensuring systems are scalable, reliable, and secure.
  • Collaborate closely with Product, Design and Engineering stakeholders
  • Maintain a strong hands-on presence, writing code, prototyping new patterns, and resolving complex technical bottlenecks under load.
  • Collaborate with Principal and Senior Principal Engineers across Toast to align on best practices, agent patterns, and spec-driven development paradigms.
  • Guide, mentor, and elevate engineers within the Team area, fostering a culture of technical excellence and continuous learning.
  • Ship features end-to-end, from design through rollout, monitoring for success and iterating based on real-world use
  • Be an awesome teammate who's all in on collaboration and collective success
  • Leverage cutting edge AI tools to enhance you and your team’s development workflow, improve velocity, and help pioneer new approaches to building - contributing to a culture of innovation and productivity across the team.
  • Mentor less experienced engineers and up-level the team through design reviews, constructive feedback, and agent-assisted engineering practices

What you'll need to thrive (Requirements)

  • 10+ years of backend/full-stack experience, delivering products that drive impact
  • Ability to read and integrate with frontend applications; showing curiosity to work across the stack when needed
  • Strong enthusiasm for agentic development practices, LLM integration, and modern software paradigms.
  • Effectively works with ambiguity, agile delivery, and shipping usable software incrementally
  • Passionate about making users happy and seeing people use your product in the wild
  • Strong on software development fundamentals — code quality, unit tests, clean code — and open to evolving your workflow with AI tooling
  • Strong collaboration skills: you communicate clearly with engineers, product, and business stakeholders, and adapt technical detail to the audience
  • Curiosity and a growth mindset: you seek feedback, embrace change, and want to grow toward broader ownership
  • Balanced expertise: Ability to design high-level technical solutions on a whiteboard while remaining deeply connected to code-level execution and system performance.
  • Effective communication: Ability to articulate complex technical ideas clearly to both technical peers and product leadership.

Our Tech Stack

  • A core of Java & Kotlin based backend services
  • React SPAs leveraging Tailwind, GraphQL and Storybook.
  • Android app built in Java & Kotlin running in the restaurant
  • Claude Code and similar AI agentic development tools as part of daily engineering
  • PostgreSQL and DynamoDB as core persistence
  • And many more technologies that help us build a top class platform.

**This is a hybrid role requiring in-office collaboration two days per week in our Dublin office.**
