О нас

Satispay began by rethinking the simple act of a payment to remove the friction from our daily routines. But we didn’t stop there. Today, we are building a complete financial platform designed to empower people and concretely improve their lives. By giving our 6 million users a clear, open path to pay, save, and invest, we are evolving into the definitive destination for every financial need.

Что предстоит делать

As our Product Marketing & Growth Manager, you’ll be the person who successfully launches new product features and scales Satispay’s product across markets to drive user adoption and engagement. Here's what your day-to-day will look like:

- B2B Product Marketing Plans – Lead the creation and execution of marketing strategies to drive new product launches, ensuring alignment with overall business objectives and user needs.

- Cross-Functional Collaboration – Coordinate with Product, CRM, RevOps, Performance, and Creative teams to align on go-to-market strategies, product positioning, and messaging for effective launch campaigns.

- B2B Acquisition and Retention – Plan, forecast, and roll out acquisition channels for selected audiences to achieve targets, and coordinate retention campaigns with the CRM team to enhance retention.

- Continuous Improvement – Continually assess program effectiveness, employing industry best practices and seeking opportunities for improvement.

Who we're looking for

We need a problem-solver who loves teamwork and gets things done. If you're curious and ready for real ownership, you'll fit in! Does this sound like you?

- Product Marketing Experience – You have strong marketing experience in a dynamic environment, with expertise in product marketing, go-to-market strategies, and user acquisition campaigns. B2B product experience is a plus.

- Project Management & Organization – With strong prioritization and stakeholder management abilities, you excel at leading projects with multiple cross-functional teams.

- Analytical & Data-Driven – You leverage data to inform strategies, track performance, and uncover insights for ongoing improvements.

- Adaptability & Resilience – You thrive in a fast-paced environment, taking on challenges positively and continuously raising your performance standards.

- Language Proficiency – Fluency in Italian and English is essential.
