О роли

We're now looking for a senior product leader to own and scale the Investments & Pensions pillar - the beating heart of our wealth proposition. As Group Product Manager for Investments & Pensions, you'll define the entire roadmap, shape the user experience and ensure our products scale globally while maintaining simplicity and clarity for our customers.

Требования

Что предстоит делать

  • Leading the Wealth, Trading & pensions product, defining the strategy, roadmap, and user experience across trading platforms, investment products, and wealth management features
  • Shaping product vision and strategy, balancing business objectives, customer needs, and UX excellence
  • Collaborating closely with Design and Engineering teams to deliver elegant, intuitive, and scalable solutions
  • Developing and mentoring product team members to achieve strategic goals
  • Owning end-to-end product roadmaps and prioritisation in a fast-paced, data-driven environment
  • Leveraging AI and automation to enhance trading decisions, portfolio management, and user interactions
  • Drive key commercial metrics: AUM growth, investment activation and retention, pension consolidation rates, and revenue per customer.
  • Partner with Compliance and Legal to navigate FCA regulatory requirements, Consumer Duty obligations and policy changes
  • Represent the Investments & Pensions pillar in senior leadership forums, contributing to company-wide strategy

Кого мы ищем

  • 7+ years of product management experience, preferably in fintech, trading, or wealth management. You understand investment products from first principles.
  • 3+ years of experience managing multiple product squads or teams in product-led organisations
  • A player-coach mentality - you're energised by building & shipping features yourself, not just managing through others. You've led small PM teams (1-3 reports) while staying hands-on with the product.
  • Strong UX sensibility - you care deeply about how products feel, not just what they do. You can partner with designers to turn complex financial flows into experiences customers trust and enjoy.
  • A proven ability to define and drive product strategy, influence stakeholders, and communicate vision across leadership and teams
  • High analytical skills and business sense to guide prioritisation based on ROI, impact, and customer value
  • Experience delivering sophisticated products under ambiguous conditions in a fast-moving environment
  • Nice to have : Experience in trading, investment platforms, or equity research, with a track record of translating complex financial concepts into intuitive products
