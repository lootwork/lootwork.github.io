О RAMP

Ramp is building the smart infrastructure for finance teams, embedded in the transaction flow of every dollar a business spends. We automate how over $275B in annualized spend flows in and out of 85,000+ companies: authorizing payments, flagging risk, categorizing spend, and closing books.

The problems are high-stakes, data-dense, and unforgiving.

We hire people with high agency and high urgency. We look for slope over intercept. We care less about where you trained and more about what you’ve built. At Ramp, everyone is a builder who owns problems end to end and makes consequential decisions that shape the outcome.

The median Ramp customer saves 7.5% and grows revenue 16% in their first year – far in excess of businesses operating without Ramp. We believe every ambitious company deserves the same.

If you want to build systems that directly shape how companies move and manage billions, Ramp is the place to do it.

О роли

We’re looking for a Technical Program Manager who can work across engineering, product, and business teams. You’ll bring the technical judgment to earn engineers’ trust and the ability to create clarity and momentum across complex programs. This is a hands-on role with executive visibility and direct impact on how Ramp’s engineering organization scales.

You’re energized by complexity and curious about how AI can improve the way engineering teams work. You experiment with AI tools yourself, understand how they are changing software development, and bring that perspective to the programs you lead.

Что предстоит делать

- Lead large technical programs across engineering and partner teams, from infrastructure scaling to reliability and incident response.

- Connect platform and product engineering teams, keep context flowing, surface dependencies, and drive effective execution.

- Own programs from scoping and kickoff through delivery, measurement, and retrospective.

- Partner with engineering leaders on roadmaps, identify how projects overlap or depend on one another, and keep programs aligned with organizational goals.

- Help engineers, engineering managers, and partner teams communicate business and engineering priorities across the organization.

- Strengthen Ramp’s incident response program by improving processes, facilitating retrospectives, and helping teams act on what they learn.

- Help define how TPMs work at Ramp by setting standards, creating playbooks, and improving program practices.

What You’ll Need

- 5+ years of technical program management experience, ideally in a high-growth startup.

- Hands-on fluency with AI tools such as Cursor or Claude Code, and a point of view on how they can improve day-to-day work and software development.

- The ability to execute directly while improving how the broader team works.

- A track record of building trust quickly and working effectively across teams.

- A BA/BS in computer science, engineering, information systems, analytics, or another technical or analytical field.

- Strong systems thinking: you can connect architecture, product, compliance, and timelines, and spot risks early.

- Experience leading cross-functional programs toward ambitious goals with urgency and ownership.

Будет плюсом

- Experience with cell-based or multi-region architectures, or international expansion.

- Hands-on experience with infrastructure, CI/CD, or observability tools.

- Experience leading reliability programs.

- Experience building programs in rigorous, fast-moving organizations.

- A background in software engineering.

- Familiarity with how LLM-based systems are built and deployed, enough to have substantive conversations with the engineers building them.
