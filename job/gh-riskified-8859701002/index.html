О роли

We are looking for a technically skilled, customer-facing Integration Engineer to join our team at Riskified.

In this role, you will work closely with global enterprise merchants, primarily during the post-sales journey, while occasionally supporting pre-sales technical discussions.

You will be responsible for leading customers through the end-to-end technical integration process, from initial discovery and solution design to implementation, testing, and successful go-live.

The position combines technical expertise, customer engagement, solution architecture, and project management, requiring the ability to understand complex merchant environments and translate business requirements into scalable technical solutions.

Что предстоит делать

  • End-to-End Integration Ownership: Lead technical integration projects with international enterprise customers, from initial onboarding and technical scoping through implementation, testing, and production launch.
  • Customer-Facing Technical Leadership: Act as the primary technical point of contact for merchants throughout the integration lifecycle, ensuring clear communication and alignment between technical and business stakeholders.
  • Technical Architecture & Solution Design: Develop a deep understanding of each customer's technical architecture, infrastructure, and business flows, and determine how Riskified's APIs, SDKs, and solutions can be integrated effectively.
  • API & SDK Integration: Guide customers through API and SDK implementations, troubleshoot integration challenges, and ensure adherence to technical requirements and best practices.
  • Technical Project Management: Drive integration timelines, coordinate dependencies, identify potential risks and blockers, and ensure projects progress toward successful go-live milestones.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work closely with internal teams, including R&D, Product, Customer Success, and Sales, to resolve technical challenges and deliver a seamless customer experience.
  • Pre-Sales Technical Support: Occasionally participate in technical discovery sessions and solution discussions with prospective customers, evaluating integration feasibility and architectural requirements.
  • Problem-Solving & Troubleshooting: Investigate complex technical issues, analyze API requests and responses, and provide praסctical solutions throughout the integration process.

Требования

  • 3-5+ years of proven experience in a customer-facing technical role, such as Integration Engineer, Solutions Engineer, Technical Implementation Engineer, or Technical Account Manager.
  • Strong understanding of REST APIs, SDKs, HTTP protocols, JSON, webhooks, and system integrations.
  • Ability to understand and analyze complex customer architectures, system dependencies, and end-to-end data flows.
  • Demonstrated experience leading technical integration or implementation projects with international enterprise customers.
  • Strong technical project management capabilities, including managing timelines, stakeholders, dependencies, and multiple concurrent projects.
  • Excellent communication skills, with the ability to explain complex technical concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences.
  • Strong analytical thinking, troubleshooting abilities, and a proactive, solution-oriented mindset.
  • Ability to work independently, take ownership, and operate effectively in a fast-paced, cross-functional environment.
  • Fluent English, both written and spoken.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience working in the eCommerce, payments, fraud prevention, or fintech industries.
  • Familiarity with eCommerce platforms, payment service providers (PSPs), and payment gateways.
  • Experience working with tools such as Postman, SQL, Jira, or similar technical and project management platforms.
  • Previous experience collaborating with globally distributed teams and enterprise-level stakeholders.
