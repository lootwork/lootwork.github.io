Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device. We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

Roblox’s Safety Operations team is dedicated to protecting our users, creators, and advertisers from individuals who want to cause harm on our platform. The Moderation Operations team are subject matter experts for content safety policies, and apply their knowledge to empower moderation at scale across the Roblox platform. This is a critical team for our users, developers, creators, and advertising partners!

The Product Support Specialist, Ads & Economy supports advertisers, creators, and moderation operations across Roblox’s ads, economy, and Marketplace products. This role collaborates closely with stakeholders across Ads Safety, Policy, Legal, Ads and Economy Product & Engineering, Creator Support, and safety leadership, and improves the tooling, processes, and workflows designed to scale Roblox’s Ads & Economy requirements.

Please note: This role may review graphic, controversial, and sometimes offensive content in line with Roblox’s policies. This role will also require some on-call or shift support.

You will report to the Manager, Moderation Product Support.

Вам предстоит

  • Act as policy enforcement subject matter expert for Ads and Marketplace Safety, owning high-visibility escalations end-to-end — assessing content against Roblox Advertising Standards, Community Standards, Marketplace policies, and internal enforcement guidelines
  • Implement and maintain standard operating procedures, playbooks, and enforcement guidance for ads and Marketplace queues, keeping enforcement guidance in sync with policy as it evolves
  • Triage and resolve escalations from advertisers, agencies, and creators, ensuring exceptional customer experience and meeting performance metrics
  • Communicate effectively with internal customers (leadership up to Roblox c-suite) and cross-functional stakeholders, performing comprehensive analysis of ambiguous incidents and making business-critical decisions under time constraints
  • Maintain professionalism, confidentiality, and good judgement when handling brand-sensitive and regulatory-sensitive matters
  • Manage, maintain, and develop highly effective processes that meet or exceed relevant service level agreements
  • Coordinate with cross-functional partners and a large team of Roblox moderation agents and their respective staff
  • Analyze operational and QA data and conduct root cause analysis to identify trends, bottlenecks, and optimization opportunities, crafting recommendations for improvement
  • Participate in an on-call or shift rotating basis to ensure coverage, which may include weekends

You Have

  • 5+ years of experience handling escalations or content review at a gaming, ads platform, marketplace, gaming, social media or other communications platform company.
  • Experience with managing escalation requests and responses, or online safety issues in a compliance enforcement
  • Strong program management skills, experience developing processes to improve operational efficiency
  • Experience using data to lead decision-making
  • Strong collaboration skills, with the ability to work with cross-functional teams, specifically product, engineering, and BPO workforces
  • Experience on a trust and safety, and/or have worked closely with policy or content moderation
  • Familiarity with ad tech or virtual economies and digital goods is a plus
  • An interest in online safety, policy development, regulations and emerging trends .

Roles that are based in an office are onsite Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday, with optional presence on Monday and Friday (unless otherwise noted).
