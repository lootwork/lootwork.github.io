О роли

We’re looking for a Game Economy Designer to own the design, balance, and performance of the economies across our live mobile games. Reporting to the Lead Game Designer, you’ll be responsible for understanding how players earn, spend, and interact with our game currencies, ensuring our economies support engaging gameplay, healthy progression, and sustainable monetization.

This is a highly analytical, hands-on role. You’ll build and maintain economy models, analyze player behaviour, simulate changes, and work closely with game designers and product managers to balance rewards, progression, difficulty, and monetization systems. You’ll turn complex data into practical recommendations that improve player engagement, retention, and revenue.

AI is central to how we work. You’ll use it to accelerate analysis, build models and simulations, explore balancing scenarios, and develop tools and workflows that help us make smarter, faster decisions.

We’re a small, ambitious team, so you’ll have significant ownership and the opportunity to directly influence the performance of our games. We’re looking for someone who combines strong analytical skills with a deep understanding of free-to-play game economies, player psychology, and the commercial drivers behind successful mobile games.

Обязанности

  • Own game economy design and balance across our live titles, managing currency sources and sinks, inflation, progression pacing, and reward values.
  • Develop and maintain economy models, using spreadsheets, simulations, and player data to understand economic health, predict outcomes, and identify opportunities for improvement.
  • Design and balance monetization systems, including reward schedules, payout rates (RTP), difficulty curves, and segment-based tuning.
  • Support feature and systems design, working with game designers to define rewards, costs, payouts, and progression structures that support engaging gameplay and healthy economies.
  • Own LiveOps economy balancing, defining how players earn and spend during events, evaluating performance against targets, and continuously optimizing the experience.
  • Analyze player behaviour and economy performance, identifying imbalances, friction points, and opportunities to improve retention, engagement, and monetization.
  • Collaborate on offers and pricing, working with the Senior Product Manager to evaluate offer value, and supporting VIP and win-back strategies with the Community & Comms team.
  • Model and validate economy changes, building simulations, forecasting their impact, and comparing predictions against live performance to inform future decisions.
  • Communicate economy insights and recommendations, translating complex models and analysis into clear, actionable guidance for designers, product managers, and leadership.
  • Champion AI-assisted economy design, using AI to improve modelling, analysis, simulations, and the efficiency of our design workflows.

Требования

  • 5+ years of experience in game economy, systems, or monetization design, working on live free-to-play mobile games.
  • Deep understanding of free-to-play economies, including currency sources and sinks, reward structures, progression systems, and player spending behaviour.
  • Exceptional analytical and modelling skills, with advanced spreadsheet proficiency and experience using SQL and/or Python to work directly with data.
  • Proven experience balancing complex game economies, including multiple currencies, progression systems, and regular LiveOps events.
  • Strong understanding of player psychology, including habit loops, reward motivation, engagement, and the relationship between economy design and monetization.
  • Experience designing and tuning economic systems, including payout rates, reward schedules, difficulty curves, and player segmentation.
  • Strong commercial instincts, understanding how economy decisions influence retention, engagement, revenue, and long-term player value.
  • Practical experience using AI tools to support analysis, modelling, or design workflows, with an interest in exploring new applications.
  • Clear communication and collaborative skills, with the ability to explain complex economic concepts, justify recommendations, and work effectively with cross-functional teams.
