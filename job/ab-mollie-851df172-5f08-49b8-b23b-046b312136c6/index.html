Your impact

We’re looking for a SEO/GEO intern to join Mollie's SEO team. You'll work closely with Misael, our SEO Manager, and get hands-on exposure to both classic search engine optimisation (SEO) and the fast-moving world of generative engine optimisation (GEO) — learning the ins and outs of how brands like Mollie show up in answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and beyond.

You'll own real tasks, see your work go live on mollie.com http://mollie.com, and build a genuine, practical skill set in one of the most in-demand areas of marketing right now.

If you're curious about SEO, eager to learn GEO/AEO, and ready to see your work shape how Mollie shows up in both search engines and AI answer engines, this is your mission.

What you'll ship

- Content management: Update and maintain content in our Framer CMS, including publishing new pages and keeping localised content accurate and consistent across our European markets.

- Keyword research: Conduct keyword research to uncover content and optimisation opportunities across both traditional search engines and AI-driven answer engines.

- Technical SEO: Run technical SEO audits (covering areas like crawlability, indexation, site speed, and structured data) and help execute the fixes and optimisations that follow.

- General support: Support the wider SEO/GEO team with ad hoc analysis, reporting, and administrative tasks that keep projects moving.

What you'll bring

- Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's programme (Marketing, Communications, Business, Digital Media, or a related field), with the flexibility to combine your studies with a few days a week at our Amsterdam office.

- Proficient in English, written and spoken.

- Strong administrative skills — you're organised, reliable, and comfortable juggling several small tasks and content updates at once without losing track of details.

- A genuine interest in SEO, GEO/AEO, and how AI is changing the way people search for and find information online. No prior professional experience required — we'll teach you the rest.

- Comfortable working in spreadsheets (Excel or Google Sheets) and confident picking up new tools and platforms quickly.

- Dutch, French, German or Italian is a plus (useful for reviewing localised content) but not required.

- Framer editing experience is a plus but not required.
