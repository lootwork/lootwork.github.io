О роли

Purple Journey is Nubank’s AI-first platform for performance, talent and learning. Its agents support experiences across assessments, calibration, always-on coaching, leveling and mobility — not as a single chatbot, but as a system that managers and Nubankers can trust.

As a Staff Software Engineer, AI Agents, you will own how these agents work end to end: orchestration, agent design and the engineering quality bar. You will help ensure that new agents ship as reliable, secure and maintainable products—not one-off demonstrations.

This role combines deep backend and systems engineering with production AI. You will work with sensitive People data, human-in-the-loop workflows and high-exposure stakeholders across Brazil, the United States and Canada.

YOU’LL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR

- Designing the orchestration model: how agents are composed, routed, scheduled, handed off to humans and operated across product surfaces and services.

- Creating and evolving agents for real People workflows, including tools, contracts, identity, scope, failure modes and regeneration strategies—not only prompts.

- Establishing a strong engineering quality bar through evaluations, testing, observability, cost and latency controls, privacy, authorization, versioning, CI gates and production incident readiness.

- Building reliable infrastructure to process data from multiple internal platforms, including collaboration and knowledge systems such as Slack, Google Docs and Confluence.

- Defining standards that other engineers and coding agents can follow across architecture, reviews, skills and contracts.

- Partnering with Product, People, Data, Security, Compliance and platform teams to align technical decisions with business and user needs.

- Mentoring engineers and helping raise the technical bar across a growing, cross-functional team.

- Supporting the safe adoption of AI-powered products by engineers and non-engineers across Nubank.

WE ARE LOOKING FOR A PERSON WHO HAS

Required

- Proven Staff-level technical leadership delivering complex software systems in production.

- Hands-on experience with production agent or LLM systems, including orchestration, tool use, structured outputs and real-world failure modes.

- Strong backend and systems engineering experience across multiple services, contracts, authentication, authorization and data flows.

- A high bar for quality and operability, including evaluations, testing, monitoring, incident response and continuous improvement.

- Experience designing systems that handle sensitive data, privacy constraints and human-in-the-loop product decisions.

- Ability to work through ambiguity, make architectural trade-offs and influence senior stakeholders.

- Strong written and verbal communication skills in English.

- Willingness to work in a hybrid model from an approved Nubank office location.

Будет плюсом

- Experience with People, HR, talent, performance or learning products.

- Experience building AI systems in regulated or privacy-sensitive environments.

- Familiarity with agent evaluation frameworks, prompt and context management, tool contracts or multi-agent architectures.

- Experience working across international teams and multiple time zones.
