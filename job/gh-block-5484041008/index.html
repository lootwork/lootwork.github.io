О роли

We’re looking for an Engagement Marketing Lead to own engagement for Afterpay customers in the US, across the Afterpay app and Afterpay on Cash App, and to extend that work to the commerce and lending products within Cash App, including Cash App Pay and Borrow. More and more customers in the US use both Cash App and Afterpay, and you’ll decide how and when they hear about flexible ways to pay across both products.

The goal is to grow active customers and gross profit by helping people discover, adopt, and keep using flexible ways to pay, through lifecycle programs, cross-sell between Cash App and Afterpay, and the engagement behind new commerce and lending features. You’ll sit on the Engagement Marketing team and act as the senior engagement partner to Afterpay business and product leadership on Afterpay strategy, and to the lending team on overall lending strategy. You’ll also work with Brand and the rest of Growth to drive go-to-market strategy for launches. You’ll trade learnings and best practices with peer teams, including Afterpay International.

Some of this work is well established, with live journeys and a clear measurement history. Some of it has never had a dedicated owner. You’ll decide where to invest, build the opportunity case, and set the roadmap.

What's Unique About This Role

  • The AI frontier. You'll help define how engagement marketing evolves as decisioning and workflow automation take on more of the work, building on automation we already run today.
  • Growth for Afterpay in the US, plus two core verticals. You’ll own the growth strategy for Afterpay in the US and oversee commerce and lending, deciding the right moment to introduce one product versus another to customers who use both Cash App and Afterpay.
  • Room to define the work. Parts of the commerce and lending experience inside Cash App are open space. You'll size them, prioritize them, and decide what gets built.
  • Economics-led prioritization. We evaluate customer actions by their incremental profit, so the best idea is the one that moves actives and gross profit, not the one that sounds best. You'll make the case for what to invest in and what to leave out.

Вам предстоит

  • Own the engagement and growth strategy for Afterpay customers in the US, across the Afterpay app and Afterpay on Cash App, and for the broader commerce and lending suite, with active customers and gross profit as the north star
  • Influence strategy across Product, Analytics, Martech, Creative, and Brand as a true peer, bringing a clear, data-backed point of view and building alignment when priorities compete
  • Partner with Afterpay business and product leadership on Afterpay strategy, and with the lending team on overall lending strategy
  • Lead go-to-market strategy for launches and new features, including positioning and launch narratives, working with Brand and the rest of Growth
  • Set the multi-quarter strategy and roadmap across lifecycle journeys, cross-sell, incentives, and product launches
  • Optimize cross-sell between Cash App and Afterpay, including incentive design and testing
  • Run rigorous experiments, read ambiguous results with judgment, and apply learnings across Afterpay in the US and Cash App
  • Size opportunities, build impact models, and diagnose program performance yourself, without waiting on an analyst
  • Work with Partner Marketing and Creative so seasonal, merchant, and holiday moments reinforce the lifecycle strategy
  • Push the frontier on AI and automation, helping decide how customer programs evolve as more decisioning is automated
  • Share best practices and learnings with peer engagement teams, including Afterpay International

You Have

  • 8+ years of experience in lifecycle marketing, CRM, growth marketing, growth strategy, business operations, or a consumer-facing analytical role in a B2C tech environment
  • A track record of owning a growth portfolio end-to-end, from opportunity sizing through measurable impact
  • A track record of influencing cross-functional partners without direct authority, including advocating for your recommendation when others see it differently
  • Strong analytical self-sufficiency. You can size an opportunity, build an impact model, and diagnose a program from first principles. Experience with data modeling or opportunity sizing frameworks required; SQL and data visualization experience strongly preferred
  • Experience designing and measuring incentive or offer programs, including incremental impact
  • Hands-on experience with a CRM or marketing automation platform (e.g., Braze, Iterable, Salesforce Marketing Cloud)
  • Strong experimentation experience. You know when to test, when to scale, and when to stop
  • Comfort moving between strategy and hands-on execution
  • Fluency with AI tools as a core part of how you work
  • Clear, takeaway-first communication, including crisp recommendations for senior leadership

  • Experience driving cross-sell between two products or brands
  • Familiarity with regulated or trust-sensitive products (e.g., fintech, financial services, or healthcare)
