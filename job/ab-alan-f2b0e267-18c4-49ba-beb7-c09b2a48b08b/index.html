HEALTH CAN’T WAIT.

Not for symptoms to get worse. Not for a six‑month appointment. Not for a system to catch up. But that’s exactly how healthcare works today. You wait, until you can’t.

Alan exists to end the wait.

Health is a universal right, and we believe this right can only become real when it’s coupled with prevention. We need to stop treating health as something we repair and start treating it as something we build, every day. It’s not solely a question of willpower. It’s the healthcare system itself that needs to work for everyone, in a sustainable way.

So we are building the new standard in prevention insurance. Alan is the first company that integrates insurance, prevention, and care into a single, acclaimed user experience.

⭐️ SENIOR BRAND DESIGNER AT ALAN

Our Brand at Alan is precious to us, and is evolving as we grow!

As part of the Design team, the Brand designer plays a large role in cementing our brand, creates visually compelling assets to support our messaging, and uses their experience to grow a strong brand design practice.

Your missions will include

- Define and evolve Alan's visual identity and brand systems, and make sure they hold up across every touchpoint.

- Own briefs end-to-end: understand the business problem, help shape the strategy, then explore, prioritize, and ship the solution.

- Create and art-direct campaigns and assets across the web, including our landing pages, email, advertising, and social channels, bringing the brand to life through art direction, motion, and video.

- Partner closely with Product, Marketing, and Brand to translate business objectives into visuals that convert.

- Extend brand design work into our Health & Prevention area, alongside our core Marketing & Brand responsibilities.

- Scale production to deliver high volumes of assets without losing quality or brand consistency.

- Leadership & mentoring

- You lead through influence by shaping roadmaps, aligning stakeholders around well-reasoned proposals and trade-offs, and proactively communicating progress and plans.

- You raise the bar for others by coaching and mentoring designers and non-designers, and helping Alaners grow through thoughtful feedback and technical or creative leadership.

OUR CURRENT DESIGN PRIORITIES 🌟

Our Design community red thread is to grow our brand design practice and ensure consistency in order to build an established and memorable brand.

To succeed in this mission, we currently

- Consolidating and scaling our visual identity across every touchpoint: web, email, ads, social, and increasingly our own landing pages, where brand expression and conversion design meet.

- Growing our motion and video capability: it's a big plus for this role, not a nice-to-have.

- Creating impactful physical and event experiences that bring the brand to life beyond screens.

- You are at the cutting edge of AI-enabled design and creative workflows, actively experimenting with emerging models and tools and turning them into practical ways to improve the quality, speed, and impact of your work.

- Extending brand identity into our Health & Prevention area, alongside our core Marketing & Brand responsibilities.

- Shaping how Alan's mascot and visual universe show up, used deliberately, not everywhere.
