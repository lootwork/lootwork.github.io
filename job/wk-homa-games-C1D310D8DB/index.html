Homa is a global mobile game developer and publisher creating games people love. We partner with studios and internally develop games, having launched over 80 titles, reached over 2 billion downloads, and seen our game All in Hole break into the global top-50 grossing charts. These are milestones, not the finish line.

With deep expertise in product and technology, we built Homa Lab, our proprietary platform that gives developers the market intelligence, data tools, and game tech, with AI built-in, to find product–market fit fast and scale mass-market games into lasting experiences players enjoy for years.

This is our flywheel: Homa Lab helps studios spot the right ideas early, turn them into games quickly, and reach millions of players. Every launch brings back data and insights that fuel the next hit, making each turn of the loop faster, smarter, and more likely to win. AI supercharges this process, running through everything we do, from everyday tasks to our guiding principles.

What powers the loop is talent density. 220+ people from 30+ nationalities, bringing expertise from the world’s best studios and companies. We uphold ambition, curiosity, humility, and focus, and deliver with speed, excellence, and candor.

Since the start, we’ve raised $165M from Headline, Northzone, Eurazeo, Bpifrance, and the founders of King, Sorare, and Spotify, people who’ve built and backed the games and platforms we admire, now supporting our drive to create the next ones.

At Homa, we are building the next billion-player experiences from the ground up and shaping the future of entertainment.

Mission

As a Strategic Project Manager Intern, you will lead a high-impact initiative at the intersection of strategy, AI, and communication.

You will develop a true 360° vision of a fast-moving gaming company, contributing to projects with direct business impact. Based on your profile, strengths, and evolving business needs, you will be embedded in one or several of our core teams.

The types of assignments you could own include

  • AI: Embedding with our dedicated AI unit to explore, design, and implement AI-powered workflows across the company — including building and scaling Homa's AI-powered content engine (prompt libraries, automation workflows, and quality standards that turn executive strategy into clear, consistent communication). A fast-moving, high-impact space at the frontier of what's possible in gaming.
  • Product & Gaming: Embedding within product teams to support roadmap prioritization, market analysis, and go-to-market strategy on live or upcoming game titles.
  • People & Transformation: Acting as a business partner to HR leadership, supporting talent strategy, retention, team structure, and organizational change. Ideal for someone who sees people operations as a lever for business.
  • Finance: Supporting financial modeling, business case development, and strategic planning alongside our finance leadership. A strong fit for profiles from PE, consulting, banking, or corporate finance.

Требования

We are looking for motivated, curious, and passionate individuals eager to kickstart their career in gaming. You don’t need years of experience; what matters most is your hunger to learn, grow, and make an impact.

If you recognise yourself below, this role is for you.

  • Currently pursuing a degree in Business, Strategy, or related fields.
  • Prior internship or strong self-driven project in gaming, consulting, tech, or AI is a plus.
  • Business-driven and analytical - you link communication to strategy and measurable impact.
  • AI-native mindset - you use AI to automate, scale, and 10× your productivity.
  • Strong communication, presentation, and research skills.
  • Proactive, autonomous, and highly organized.
  • Fluency in English.
  • Passion for gaming, tech, or AI is a strong plus.
  • Eligible for a 6-month onsite internship in Paris (internship agreement required).

We care more about your potential, rigor, and ambition than a perfectly matching CV. Even if you don’t check every box in our requirements, we encourage you to apply.

Why Join?

This is not a traditional internship. You will

  • Work directly with executives on a company-wide strategic initiative.
  • Build systems that will shape how Homa communicates long-term.
  • Gain exposure to strategy, AI, and operations inside a fast-growing gaming company.
  • Learn from multiple teams and experienced mentors.
  • Deliver tangible, measurable impact in 6 months.
  • Be part of a passionate, dynamic, and international team.

If you’re looking for comfort, this isn’t it. If you’re looking for ownership, learning velocity, and impact, welcome.

Our Culture

At Homa, we prioritise talent, energy, and determination over traditional credentials. We’re building a global community of brilliant, driven people who think boldly, act with pragmatism, move fast, and push boundaries. We believe true innovation comes from diverse perspectives, deep collaboration, and curiosity without limits. Our culture is grounded in four core values:

  • Humility: We recognise that we are always learning, we can always improve and grow - we stay grounded in a clear view of ourselves.
  • Curiosity: We continuously seek the “why” behind the “what” - we explore with purpose and practicality.
  • Focus: Our curiosity takes us deeper, not wider - we obsess over the details and let pragmatism guide our attention.
  • Ambition: We strive for the best - we don’t settle for “good enough,” - we use common sense to reach higher.
