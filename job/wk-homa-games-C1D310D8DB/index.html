Homa is a global mobile game developer and publisher creating games people love. We partner with studios and internally develop games, having launched over 80 titles, reached over 2 billion downloads, and seen our game All in Hole break into the global top-50 grossing charts. These are milestones, not the finish line.

With deep expertise in product and technology, we built Homa Lab, our proprietary platform that gives developers the market intelligence, data tools, and game tech, with AI built-in, to find product–market fit fast and scale mass-market games into lasting experiences players enjoy for years.

This is our flywheel: Homa Lab helps studios spot the right ideas early, turn them into games quickly, and reach millions of players. Every launch brings back data and insights that fuel the next hit, making each turn of the loop faster, smarter, and more likely to win. AI supercharges this process, running through everything we do, from everyday tasks to our guiding principles.

What powers the loop is talent density. 220+ people from 30+ nationalities, bringing expertise from the world’s best studios and companies. We uphold ambition, curiosity, humility, and focus, and deliver with speed, excellence, and candor.

Since the start, we’ve raised $165M from Headline, Northzone, Eurazeo, Bpifrance, and the founders of King, Sorare, and Spotify, people who’ve built and backed the games and platforms we admire, now supporting our drive to create the next ones.

At Homa, we are building the next billion-player experiences from the ground up and shaping the future of entertainment.

О this opportunity

You will work alongside our Chief of Staff and teams across Homa, contributing to strategic initiatives that support the company’s growth. This internship offers hands-on exposure to business challenges, with opportunities to collaborate with senior leaders and learn how a fast-moving gaming company operates.

You will develop a 360° understanding of Homa while building your analytical, project management, and communication skills. Based on your strengths, interests, and business needs, you will contribute to one or several core teams, taking on increasing responsibility with guidance from experienced colleagues.

Mission

As a Strategic Project Manager Intern, you will help turn business questions into clear insights and practical actions, leading high-impact initiatives at the intersection of strategy, AI, and communication, contributing to projects with direct business impact. The types of assignments you could own include:

  • Business partnering: Work with stakeholders to understand their needs, structure problems, and identify next steps.
  • Operational improvement: Help map processes, identify inefficiencies, and implement practical improvements.
  • Strategic analysis: Conduct market research, competitive benchmarking, and quantitative analysis to support decision-making.
  • Project execution: Support projects from scoping to implementation, coordinating actions and tracking progress and results.
  • AI: Working with our dedicated AI unit to explore, design, and implement AI-powered workflows across the company, including building and scaling Homa's AI-powered content engine.
  • Communication: Prepare clear presentations, concise recommendations, and progress updates for internal stakeholders.

Требования

We’re looking for curious, driven students who want to learn quickly and contribute to meaningful projects. You don’t need to arrive with all the answers: we value structured thinking, initiative, and the willingness to ask questions and act on feedback. You are a great fit if you have:

  • Current studies in Business, Engineering, Strategy, Finance, or related fields.
  • Prior internship in startups, gaming, consulting, tech, or AI is a plus.
  • Business-driven and analytical: you link communication to strategy and measurable impact.
  • AI-native mindset - you use AI to automate, scale, and 10× your productivity.
  • Strong communication, presentation, and research skills.
  • A pragmatic approach: you prioritize effectively and enjoy turning ideas into action.
  • Comfort with ambiguity and variety: you’re open to unfamiliar topics and changing priorities.
  • Fluency in English.
  • Eligible for a 6-month onsite internship in Paris (internship agreement required).
  • A plus: Entrepreneurial initiatives, or personal projects. If you’ve built a game, launched an app, organized an initiative, or shipped something of your own, we’d love to hear about it.

👉 Even if you don’t check every box, we encourage you to apply. We value diverse perspectives and backgrounds, and we’re interested in your potential, motivation, and approach to learning.
