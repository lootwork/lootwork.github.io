Что предстоит делать

This opportunity is on our Product Data Platform (PDP) team, which builds and operates the high-performance data, query, and search infrastructure that powers critical Brex product experiences, including AI-powered data products like our agentic reporting experience. PDP's mission is to make Brex customizable, scalable, and reliable for finance teams, and that requires deeply optimized, production-grade distributed systems.

As a Staff Software Engineer, you'll shape the technical direction of the platform across multiple teams. Your mandate is to identify the highest-impact architectural problems across the data products group, including the cross-cutting ones that fall between teams, and drive them to resolution. You'll be accountable for the group's technical quality, move between long-horizon strategy and hands-on execution, and lead through influence rather than authority.

Обязанности

  • Lead technical strategy for critical PDP initiatives, balancing long-term architecture with near-term delivery across multiple teams.
  • Identify and resolve systemic performance, consistency, and scalability problems, including cross-team gaps no single team can own.
  • Own architecture direction for query performance: query plans, execution paths, cost models, indexing strategies, and schema and access-pattern design.
  • Design and evolve distributed systems with thoughtful tradeoffs around consistency, latency, caching, cost, and failure modes.
  • Raise the technical bar across the group through design reviews and architecture decisions, and dive in hands-on where it matters most, including P95/P99 latency issues in production.
  • Proactively identify scaling risks and reliability gaps, and turn findings into plans the group aligns behind.
  • Mentor senior engineers and grow the next generation of technical leaders within PDP.
  • Contribute directly on high-stakes initiatives and critical path decisions, primarily in the JVM ecosystem (Kotlin/Java).

Требования

  • 8+ years of backend engineering experience building and operating production systems at scale, including as a technical leader on multi-team initiatives.
  • Track record of leading architecture and delivery for large-scale distributed systems in a platform or infrastructure environment.
  • Deep hands-on expertise with relational databases (Postgres or Aurora strongly preferred), including query plan analysis, indexing strategies, and real-world optimization.
  • Ability to drive ambiguous, cross-cutting problems to resolution without a defined charter.
  • Strong written and verbal communication, with the ability to influence stakeholders and align teams without direct authority.
  • Experience mentoring senior engineers.

Bonus

  • Hands-on experience with OpenSearch / Elasticsearch or similar systems in production.
  • Experience with streaming data platforms (Flink, Kafka) or semantic/analytics layers in production.
  • Experience with multi-tenant or data-heavy platforms in regulated environments.
  • Experience building platform foundations for AI or agentic product experiences.
