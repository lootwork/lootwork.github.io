О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

You’ll shape how SME customers in DACH realise lasting value from Airwallex by building clear, scalable customer journeys from handover and onboarding through activation, adoption, retention and expansion readiness.

- Build clear, scalable customer journeys across handover, onboarding, first value, adoption, retention, reactivation and expansion readiness.

- Create segmented, cohort-based and trigger-led lifecycle programmes using customer context and product signals.

- Develop customer education and enablement experiences, including onboarding materials, webinars, enablement sessions, campaign content, FAQs and self-service resources.

- Establish repeatable playbooks, journey standards, handover requirements, engagement triggers and internal workflows that create a consistent customer experience.

- Use customer insight, product signals and operational data to identify friction, define success measures and continuously improve lifecycle programmes.

- Partner with Account Management, Sales, Product, Risk, FinOps, Support, Operations and RevOps to improve customer outcomes and contribute to the operating model, dashboards, tooling requirements and ways of working for a growing DACH Customer Success function.

You'll work from our Berlin office three days a week.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- 3+ years of experience in Customer Success, Lifecycle Management, Customer Enablement, Customer Operations, Onboarding, Learning & Development or a comparable customer-facing or operational role.

- Experience designing and improving customer journeys, lifecycle programmes, enablement initiatives, operational processes or playbooks.

- Ability to turn customer insight and data into clear priorities, practical improvements and measurable outcomes.

- Strong written communication and content skills, with the ability to make complex products or processes straightforward for different audiences.

- Strong analytical and operational skills, including experience defining success measures and using CRM, analytics or campaign tools to monitor results.

- Ability to work across functions, clarify ownership and drive programmes from idea through implementation and iteration.

- Confidence engaging with customers and internal stakeholders, including in workshops, onboarding or enablement sessions.

- Excellent written and verbal communication skills in German and English.

- A structured, pragmatic approach and the ability to balance detail with progress in a fast-moving environment.

Preferred

- Experience in fintech, payments, SaaS, financial services or another technical product environment.

- Experience with lifecycle marketing or customer engagement platforms, customer education, scaled success programmes or digital adoption.

- Familiarity with Salesforce, Looker, Zendesk, Google Workspace, SQL or similar CRM, analytics and support tools.

- Experience working with KYC/KYB, risk, compliance, payment operations or other regulated financial workflows.

- Experience in a high-growth environment where processes, ownership and operating models are still being built.

YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF

LEARN MORE ABOUT YOUR TEAM

SME & Growth at Airwallex is about more than closing deals. We help small and mid-market businesses solve real financial challenges across borders: getting paid globally, managing multi-currency accounts, paying suppliers, and running spend across markets from a single platform. You're not selling a feature. You're helping founders and finance leaders replace fragmented financial operations with one global system that actually scales.

It's a function for sellers who like to build, not follow a script: curious, resourceful, and comfortable figuring things out in markets that are still wide open. You get real autonomy to create value, build relationships, and move opportunities forward.

If you want to sell a serious product, solve real problems for ambitious businesses, and build your career fast, this is a place to do it.
