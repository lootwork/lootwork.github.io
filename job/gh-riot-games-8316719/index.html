Senior Game Producer - VALORANT, Maps

As Producers at Riot, we lead passionate teams to create products and experiences that deliver maximum player value. We guide development, clear blockers, and help teams deliver while creating a sustainable, collaborative environment.

As a Game Producer III on the VALORANT Maps team, you will lead production planning and execution for a multidisciplinary pod building the map experience. You will partner with level design, environment art, look development, engineering, technical art, creative, product, and QA to translate product and milestone goals into clear plans and demonstrable progress. You will help the team establish and improve the workflows, technical foundations, and cross-discipline partnerships needed to develop a large-scale map sustainably. You’ll connect day-to-day execution with broader team goals by aligning priorities, surfacing risks and dependencies early, and helping the team make informed tradeoffs as the map and production pipeline evolve. This role will report into the Sr. Director of Game Production.

Обязанности

  • Build and maintain the Maps team’s integrated plans, roadmap, and prioritized backlog in alignment with milestone goals
  • Hold teams accountable to predictable delivery of commitments
  • Partner with pod and discipline leads to ensure strategic alignment between team work and goals
  • Facilitate effective planning and team rituals that support communication, realistic commitments, and timely blocker removal
  • Coordinate collaboration across level design, art, gameplay, engineering, technical art, creative, and QA; proactively manage dependencies and build aligned delivery partnerships
  • Guide day-to-day execution toward quality goals; adapt plans as new information emerges and balance scope, timing, and quality
  • Iterate on the production processes to continuously improve our velocity, collaboration, and visibility
  • Promote healthy collaboration, constructive conflict, psychological safety, and continuous improvement across the pod and its partners
  • Share production practices and mentor less-experienced producers or collaborators where appropriate

Required Qualifications

  • 6+ years of experience in software or game development
  • 2+ years in a leadership position, working directly on the production of a game
  • Experience leading production for cross-functional game development efforts through planning, execution, delivery, and iteration
  • Experience leading teams with long term content development pipelines
  • Experience working with environment art workflows and various disciplines (Level Design, Env Art, Lighting, VFX, Tech Art)
  • Experience in working with live content/ops
  • Experience working on in-game levels and maps
  • Strong communication, facilitation, and problem-solving skills, including the ability to create alignment across teams with different perspectives

Desired Qualifications

  • Experience in game development and a good understanding of how artists, engineers, and game designers build assets and game features for a modern game
  • Experience producing maps, levels, worlds, or other content with interconnected design, art, and technology workflows.
  • Experience coaching producers or discipline leads.
  • Experience coordinating co-development partners or outsourced work.
  • Experience in FPS games and shipped FPS projects
  • Experience handling large seasonal content changes
  • Experience working with builds and technical support for art teams
  • Experience working on console, mobile & PC games.
  • Familiarity with VALORANT

For this role, you'll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players' experiences, this could be your role!
