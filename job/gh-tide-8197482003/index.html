О TIDE

At Tide, we help SMEs save time and money in the running of their businesses by not only offering business accounts and related banking services, but also a comprehensive set of highly usable and connected administrative solutions, from invoicing to accounting.

Tide is transforming the small business banking market and now supports over 2 million members globally across the UK, Germany and France.

Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love.

Tide facts

  • Tide is available for UK, German and French SMEs
  • Over 2 million members globally and rapidly growing
  • Over $300 million raised in funding
  • 3,000 Tideans globally
  • We have offices in Central London, with a member support and technology centre in Sofia, Bulgaria, technology centres in Serbia, Romania, Lithuania and Hyderabad and offices in Gurugram, New Delhi, Berlin, Paris and Luxembourg

О команде

The Accounts team is the platform layer behind how members open, manage, and understand their accounts at Tide. It owns account opening, regulatory compliance, account management, account switching (CASS), and the full transaction experience: the timeline at the centre of the app, transaction details and counterparty information, transaction history, statements, and exports. Every member who opens the app and looks at their balance or recent activity relies on this team. Tide is a global product used across regions, so the team works with different banking partners, regulatory regimes, and account structures, and it sits on top of Tide’s core ledger.

О роли

As an Engineering Manager at Tide, you will lead the Accounts team. You will set its technical direction, own its delivery and reliability, and grow its engineers across backend, mobile, and web.

This is a product-facing platform team. A large share of the work shows up directly in the app, so you will work closely with product and design. It is also a financial system: a transaction that renders wrongly, a statement that does not reconcile, or an export that misses a record is a correctness problem, not a cosmetic one.

Что предстоит делать

  • Leading the Accounts team across backend, Flutter mobile, and Angular web, setting its technical direction and holding it to delivery.
  • Defining and maintaining the interface with the ledger: what the ledger guarantees, how Accounts services consume it, and who owns which data and schemas.
  • Owning account opening, account management, and account switching (CASS), including the regulatory compliance that comes with them.
  • Owning the member-facing transaction experience end to end: the timeline, transaction details, counterparty information, transaction history, statements, and exports.
  • Setting technical direction for services that are read-heavy, data-rich, and tightly coupled to the app: performance, pagination and search at scale, data consistency with the ledger, and long-term architecture.
  • Owning system stability and operational reliability for your services: SLOs, observability, on-call practice.
  • Partnering with product, design, compliance, and operations to make sure what we ship is accurate, supportable, and compliant across all markets.
  • Growing engineers at every level, across backend, mobile, and web. The team spans early-career through senior.
  • Partnering with other teams at Tide that build on the Accounts platform, such as investment accounts and savings accounts, so the platform meets their needs.
  • Aligning with other EMs across Banking on shared platform and infrastructure decisions.

Кого мы ищем

Experience and background

  • Meaningful engineering management experience. You have led teams through real delivery, not just been a strong tech lead. What matters is the quality of that experience, not the number of years
  • Experience leading product-driven teams. You have worked closely with product and design and been accountable for customer outcomes, not just output
  • A solid backend engineering foundation before moving into management, enough to earn credibility with the team and get into the detail when it matters
  • Experience leading distributed, remote-first teams across time zones
  • Proven experience aligning with senior leadership on deliveries and managing dependencies with neighbouring teams
  • Strong grasp of distributed systems: data consistency across services, reliability patterns, SLOs and SLAs, observability tooling

Technical depth

  • Technically fluent: you can read the code, follow the architecture, and engage meaningfully with design decisions and production problems. You do not need to be shipping features regularly, but you need to understand what your team is building and why.
  • Backend engineering in a strongly-typed language. Our stack is Java/Spring Boot, but Java experience is not a requirement. We want someone who can learn and work in our stack with confidence.
  • Experience with microservices in a cloud-native environment (we are AWS heavy).
  • Designing and operating APIs and data services that feed mobile and web clients: latency, pagination, caching, and backwards compatibility with released app versions.
  • Relational database engineering, including data modelling and query performance for read-heavy workloads over large transaction histories.
  • Event-driven and async systems experience.
  • Familiarity with mobile and web delivery (Flutter and Angular, or similar), enough to make good trade-offs with client engineers.
  • Exposure to agentic or AI-integrated systems, and engineering practises.
