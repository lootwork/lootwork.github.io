TradePMR is Robinhood's custody and technology platform for registered investment advisors. We're in the middle of one of the most ambitious builds in Robinhood's history: building a next-generation custody platform that lets advisors work seamlessly across custodians, with AI-native insights, digital onboarding, and access to cutting-edge, first-of-a-kind asset classes — delivered as cohesive platform. A core part of this vision is bringing the same intuitive, retail-grade tools and experiences Robinhood has built for individual investors to financial advisors and the clients they serve.

The advisors we serve are growth-oriented, multicustodial RIAs, and they run their practices on a stack of portfolio management, CRM, financial planning, and increasingly AI-native tools. Our job is to make TradePMR the easiest, most modern custodian in that stack to build and work with — foundational APIs today, and an increasingly partner-first platform as the industry moves toward agentic, AI-driven advisor workflows tomorrow.

This role sits at the center of that effort: part outward-facing partner/advisor advocate, part technical product owner for our integration platform.

This role is based in our Clearwater, Fl, Menlo Park, CA and New York, NY offices, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own our third-party integration strategy end-to-end — from prioritizing and scoping which APIs and connectivity we build (including emerging AI-agent standards like MCP) to defining the sandbox, documentation, authentication, and governance that make us easy to integrate with.
  • Be the primary, outward-facing product owner for our technology-partner ecosystem — leading commercial and technical conversations with PMS/CRM vendors and AI-native advisor tools, scoping statements of work, setting production-access requirements, and representing Robinhood at industry events and with key RIA-industry influencers.
  • Build and run the framework for how we tier, prioritize, and resource integration requests across dozens of vendors and advisor firms at once — balancing large-firm strategic bets against overall platform coherence and engineering capacity.
  • Partner closely with engineering to translate advisor and partner needs into technical requirements and make the build-vs-partner-vs-defer calls — comfortable reading an API spec or discussing an SSO/OAuth flow directly with a partner's engineering team.
  • Develop a clear point of view on how AI and agentic workflows are changing custodial integrations
  • Represent the integrations function in Robinhood's broader next-generation custody roadmap, working closely with cross-functional partners across product, engineering, and operations to keep near-term integration work aligned with our long-term vision for advisor-facing custody.

Что вы приносите

  • 6+ years of product management experience, with a meaningful portion spent in a technical, platform, or API/integrations-focused PM role.
  • Experience working at a high-growth fintech company, with a track record of navigating the regulatory, compliance, and legacy-system realities that shape technical integration work in financial services.
  • A strong, informed point of view on how AI is reshaping fintech and advisor workflows — ideally backed by direct experience shipping AI-enabled features or evaluating AI-native vendors and partners.
  • Genuinely technical and a proven builder — comfortable as the primary point of contact with external partner engineering teams, able to reason through API design, auth (SSO/SAML/OAuth), and data models, with a track record of shipping developer-facing platform capabilities (documentation, sandboxes, versioning) in a regulated or enterprise B2B environment.
  • Experience running a partner or vendor ecosystem: prioritization frameworks, SOWs/contracts, production-access gating, and managing a portfolio of integrations at very different maturity stages at the same time.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication — you can flex between a technical design conversation with engineering and a strategic partnership conversation with an executive or advisor.
  • Comfortable with ambiguity: this role is being created by combining two existing scopes into one, and part of the job is defining what "great" looks like as you go.
  • Bonus Points
  • Experience in wealth management, RIA custody, or advisor technology, or working with custodial/clearing operations (e.g., First Clearing, Schwab, Fidelity).
  • Experience building or scaling a formal integration/partner program (tiering, vendor onboarding, developer portal) from a relatively early stage.
