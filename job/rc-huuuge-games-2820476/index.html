Introduction

At Huuuge Games we make top-grossing mobile games that bring people together through fun and social mobile gaming.

Join the Huuuge Casino team and become the voice of one of the top social casino games in the world. Every day, millions of players read your words: in the offers they open, the events they join and the new features they discover for the first time.

As a Copywriter, you will work closely with the Monetization, Art, and Product teams to turn game mechanics into clear, exciting copy that players understand at a glance. From naming a new collection to writing the launch of a new feature, your work will shape how players experience the game.

This is a hands-on role for a writer who cares about accuracy as much as creativity, enjoys working with designers on how a message looks, and wants to see the direct impact of their work on millions of players.

Become a part of an exciting adventure bringing fun & truly social experience to millions of players around the world from our office in Warsaw.

Как вы повлияете на результат

  • Write in-game copy for offers, announcements, the shop and banners across the game.
  • Create the full launch copy for new features, from teaser and announcement to info screens, missions and banners.
  • Develop creative concepts and names for events and seasonal themes.
  • Turn game design documents into short, accurate player-facing copy, using the right in-game terms and mechanics.
  • Work with designers to shape how the main message appears on each asset, so players understand it at a glance.
  • Use and build your own AI tools, and make them your copywriting superpower
