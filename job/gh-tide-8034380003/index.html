О TIDE

At Tide, we help SMEs save time and money in the running of their businesses by not only offering business accounts and related banking services, but also a comprehensive set of highly usable and connected administrative solutions, from invoicing to accounting.

Tide is transforming the small business banking market and now supports over 2 million members globally across the UK, Germany and France.

Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love.

Tide facts

  • Tide is available for UK, German and French SMEs
  • Over 2 million members globally and rapidly growing
  • Over $300 million raised in funding
  • 3,000 Tideans globally
  • We have offices in Central London, with a member support and technology centre in Sofia, Bulgaria, technology centres in Serbia, Romania, Lithuania and Hyderabad and offices in Gurugram, New Delhi, Berlin, Paris and Luxembourg

О команде

The Core Infrastructure team provides a containerised cloud environment based on EKS that is the foundation of Tide’s Platform Engineering provision to product engineering teams.

The team mission is to provide product engineers a platform so they can build great products for our members without directly provisioning cloud infrastructure.

О роли

Tide is hiring for a proven Engineering Manager to lead the team.

As the successful candidate you will

  • Lead: Hire, coach, and set clear goals for a team of engineers.
  • Plan: Develop a roadmap for architecture, upgrades, autoscaling, and capacity for our infrastructure, databases, and Kubernetes clusters.
  • Protect: Make sure our platforms meet their targets for availability and security.
  • Empower: Reduce the time that product engineers need to deploy and run services on the platform.
  • Control cost: Help product engineering teams make cost-efficient choices for services that run on our platform.
  • Collaborate: Share team context and build cross-team relationships.
  • Balance priorities: Weigh technical decisions against team and company goals.
  • Measure: Track team productivity with metrics such as DORA and SPACE, and drive measurable improvement.

Кого мы ищем

You are an engineering leader who stays technically hands-on. You know that the biggest challenges need effective delegation and team empowerment.

You have

  • 10+ years of experience in distributed computing
  • 3+ years in engineering leadership roles
  • A degree in Computer Science or a related field
  • Detailed knowledge of AWS, GCP, or both, in a regulated environment that requires security guardrails
  • Experience running Kubernetes in production with GitOps (ArgoCD preferred)
  • An understanding of how to use AI tools such as Claude, and how to help your engineers and customers use them efficiently
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills
  • Experience managing stakeholders across teams and functions

You will stand out if you have

  • Platform engineering experience that abstracts Kubernetes complexity into a stable internal product
  • Experience creating and maintaining Kubernetes operators

OUR TECH STACK

  • Cloud: 100% containerised on AWS EKS
  • Infrastructure as code: Terraform and Terragrunt manage all platform infrastructure
  • Deployments: ArgoCD with a GitOps approach
  • Custom infrastructure: Helm and Crossplane manage the databases, S3 buckets, and other resources that each container needs
  • Source control and CI/CD: GitHub and GitHub Actions
  • Observability: Coralogix
  • Product backend: Java, Python, and Go
  • Client apps: Angular and Flutter
