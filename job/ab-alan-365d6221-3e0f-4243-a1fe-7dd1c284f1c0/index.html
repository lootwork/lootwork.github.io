🚀 WHAT YOU'LL BUILD AND WHO YOU'LL WORK WITH

You would join the team building Mo, Alan's AI health companion, on its medical side: everything that makes Mo safe, accurate and useful when a member asks a health question - triage, medical guidance, follow-up, and the handover to our own doctors.

Mo has been available to all our French members (1M+) since Q1 2026. Members ask it about symptoms, treatments, test results and what to do next, and Mo can route them to a doctor in Alan's Clinic through chat or video when it matters. Your mission is to make those medical conversations more accurate, safer and more helpful, to bring a much deeper level of medical expertise into our AI agents, and to connect Mo even more closely with the care Alan delivers.

Concretely, you will

- Build the LLM-powered agents and tools behind Mo's medical conversations (triage, symptom assessment, personalised guidance, proactive follow-up), using the member's health profile and the right context at the right moment

- Go deeper in medical expertise: work with doctors and clinical leads to turn clinical knowledge, guidelines and medical judgement into specialised agents and tools

- Connect Mo with Alan's care and healthcare system: hand conversations over to a doctor and take members from a question to a consultation, a prescription or a follow-up

- Own safety and evaluation: guardrails, doctor review of Mo's answers, datasets, automated evals and monitoring, so we know Mo is right before members see it

- Extend Mo to the countries where Alan operates, with one shared core and local medical rules

⚡ WHY THIS ROLE IS SPECIAL

- Medical expertise in the room: Doctors, clinical leads and ML scientists are part of the team. You build with them, and you help our agents get as close as possible to real medical expertise.

- Applied AI in a high-stakes domain: You'll ship an agent that answers real health questions for over a million members, where accuracy and safety come first.

- Frontier problems: Agentic workflows, tool use, evaluation, safety and guardrails, cost and latency trade-offs - the hardest open questions of production LLM systems, with real medical constraints.

- Ownership & Growth: Own the use cases you ship end to end, influence the architecture, mentor other engineers and help shape how we hire.
