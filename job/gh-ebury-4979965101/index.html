FCC Programme and Governance Analyst

Ebury Malaga or Leon Office - Hybrid: 4 days in the office, 1 day working from home per week

Ebury is seeking an efficient, detail oriented Financial Crime Compliance (FCC) Programme and Governance Analyst to join our Compliance Operations team, to support various projects and ongoing initiatives regarding data and governance. The programme and governance analyst has an eagle eye for detail, a drive for efficiency and great time management skills. Their duties will consist of creating reports, ensuring data is reflected accurately, collaborate with the data team to increase data automation and liaising with responsible owners for updates and ensuring that the data and governance projects and deliverables meet company goals and standards.

Что предстоит делать

● Monitor every step of the data process for successful and timely completion

● Maintain various governance packs and ensure data is reflected correctly

● Support the Compliance Project Manager in the planning of new projects

● Prepare meeting packs for various meetings and prepare minutes

● Create and continuously improve data reporting

● Collaborate with the data team to work on automation projects

● Perform data quality checks

● Perform data reviews and deep dives

What you’ll need

● Demonstrated experience in data and governance activities

● Attention to detail

● Proven organisational skills, drive to work in a structured way

● Excellent verbal and written communication skills

● Ability to prioritise and complete tasks in a timely manner

● Firm understanding of processes that increase efficiency

● Knowledge of Google Sheets (especially formulas), Docs and Slides

● Dedication to fostering a collaborative team

● Solution-oriented mindset

● C1 English

Preferred qualifications (nice to have)

● Familiarity with Financial Crime Compliance (FCC), Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Counter Terrorist Financing (CTF)

● Ability to generate analytical reports to measure programme impact

● Familiarity with Jira
