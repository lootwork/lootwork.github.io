Block is one company built from many blocks, all united by the same purpose of economic empowerment. The blocks that form our foundational teams — People, Finance, Counsel, Hardware, Information Security, Platform Infrastructure Engineering, and more — provide support and guidance at the corporate level. They work across business groups and around the globe, spanning time zones and disciplines to develop inclusive People policies, forecast finances, give legal counsel, safeguard systems, nurture new initiatives, and more. Every challenge creates possibilities, and we need different perspectives to see them all. Bring yours to Block.

Since we opened our doors in 2009, the world of commerce has evolved immensely, and so has Square. After enabling anyone to take payments and never miss a sale, we saw sellers stymied by disparate, outmoded products and tools that wouldn’t work together.

So we expanded into software and started building integrated, omnichannel solutions – to help sellers sell online, manage inventory, offer buy now, pay later functionality, book appointments and engage loyal buyers. Across it all, we’ve embedded financial services tools at the point of sale, so merchants can access a business loan and manage their cash flow in one place. Afterpay furthers our goal to provide omnichannel tools that unlock meaningful value and growth, enabling sellers to capture the next generation shopper, increase order sizes, and compete at a larger scale.

Today, we are a partner to sellers of all sizes – large, enterprise-scale businesses with complex operations, sellers just starting, as well as merchants who began selling with Square and have grown larger over time. As our sellers grow, so do our solutions. There is a massive opportunity in front of us. We’re building a significant, meaningful, and lasting business, and we are helping sellers worldwide do the same.

О роли

Square is looking for a hands-on B2B content creator and storyteller to help Australian sellers understand what’s possible for their businesses with Square. You’ll turn seller insights, business priorities, product capabilities, and industry trends into clear, compelling stories that build trust and support growth.

You’ll own the content strategy and its delivery for Australia, within the wider Australian marketing strategy. Seller stories will be central to your work, alongside product narratives, practical business content, and industry perspectives. You’ll start with the audience, the business objective, and the story, then decide which formats and channels will bring it to life. A strong idea might become a seller film, an article, a series of social posts, or an asset that helps sales have a better conversation.

This is an individual contributor role that combines strategic thinking with hands-on creation. You’ll develop ideas, interview sellers, write and edit content, and take work from brief to publication. Your remit includes organic social, YouTube, creator and influencer partnerships, and seller case studies. You don’t need to be a specialist in every channel, but you do need strong creative judgement and the ability to adapt a story for different audiences and formats.

You’ll report to the Head of Marketing Australia and help shape the Australian marketing plan through your content expertise. You’ll work closely with sales, account management, and the sellers we feature, bringing in agencies and production partners where specialist support will make the work stronger.
