Кто мы

Modulr is the payments automation platform that streamlines money movement with accuracy, control and reliability – built to scale. We process more than 200m transactions and over £180bn in payment value on an annualised basis, and we’re trusted by over 6,000 businesses from fast-growing SMEs to global enterprises.

We automate how money moves into and out of a business (payroll, supplier payments, spend management and payment collection) through a single platform, connected to the accounting and payroll systems finance teams already use, and underpinned by principal memberships of Visa and Mastercard and direct access to central bank infrastructure.

As a regulated payments provider with over a decade of experience, we’re the platform finance teams trust to run mission-critical operations. Modulr is backed by PayPal, FIS, General Atlantic, Blenheim Chalcot, Frog Capital and Highland Europe, with over 400 employees across London, Edinburgh, Amsterdam and Mumbai.

Find out more about us on our website.

Что предстоит делать

Reporting into the Marketing team, working alongside, content, product marketing, and PR, and supporting demand generation, pipeline progression, and profile elevation. Working closely with sales and product on key events and campaigns

  • Plan and coordinate end-to-end logistics for events, from initial briefing through to post-event review, including trade shows, sponsorships, and large-scale flagship conferences
  • Source, negotiate with, and manage vendors and existing suppliers, such as venues, caterers, and branding and merchandise providers
  • Build and maintain detailed event timelines, briefing documents, and checklists, regularly reviewing and reprioritising tasks as plans evolve
  • Lead on-site delivery on live event days, troubleshooting issues and ensuring a strong, consistent brand presence
  • Coordinate creative assets, presentations and marketing collateral, liaising with internal stakeholders on key updates and decisions
  • Support webinar coordination and vendor management, ensuring timely execution and post-webinar follow up.

Кто вы

  • AI: Motivated to learn and develop AI skills that streamline processes, improve efficiency, and drive engagement using data and experiential activations in events
  • Experience: 1-3 years of hands-on experience of in-person event coordination and organisation.
  • Industry Context: Experience in tech industry, events agency or fast-paced B2B environment is highly valued.
  • Skills: Strong project coordination, communication, cross-functional collaboration, and analytical skills.

Будет плюсом

  • Background in Fintech or working in a B2B Saas environment
  • Experience working in a scaleup or startup environment
