As part of the Real-Time VFX team, you’ll design and help implement the sleek, dynamic and dramatic special effects that add atmosphere and depth to gameplay. With your experience, you’ll create aesthetically captivating visual effects that communicate essential game information. You’ll use your artistic and technical skills to breathe life into undead spells, channel primal emotions and capture the essence of hundreds of champions.

As a Principal VFX Artist on the unpublished MMO team, you will be responsible for driving the style of our real-time VFX systems and pipelines. This role focuses on artistic problem solving while collaborating with Tech Art to create scalable, modular systems that will support our open world, content, and gameplay for years to come. VFX in our game is foundational. It’s not just a layer of polish; it shapes gameplay clarity, gameplay feedback, and the identity of the world itself. You'll partner across Design, Animation, and Engineering to prototype abilities, define visual language hierarchies, and create reusable systems that support thousands of effects across characters, environments, and interactions.

Обязанности

  • Own and steward the artistic quality bar for real-time VFX, setting clear benchmarks for style, craft, readability, and consistency across the game.
  • Personally author benchmark effects that demonstrate the intended visual language across characters, environments, and world interactions.
  • Establish visual hierarchy principles and partner with Design and Animation to make abilities, impacts, and other gameplay events clear in complex encounters.
  • Partner with Tech Art and Engineering to turn the artistic benchmarks into reusable VFX patterns, modular workflows, and tools that support both rapid prototyping and production.
  • Collaborate with Tech Art and Engineering to profile effects and define practical performance and scalability targets for open world spaces and different quality settings.
  • Represent VFX in cross-discipline reviews and planning - communicating tradeoffs, guiding adoption of the visual standards, and helping unblock teams without implying that this role alone owns technical architecture or people management.

Required Qualifications

  • 8+ years of experience creating real-time VFX for AAA games.
  • Strong experience working within Unreal Engine and using skills such as Niagara, Blueprint, Niagara Scratchpad, and Material Graphs.
  • Strong understanding of visual hierarchy, readability, and prioritization in complex gameplay spaces.
  • Experience with modular and systemic VFX development, creating reusable effects that scale across large environments.
  • Familiarity with performance optimization for large scale battles and high density environments.
  • Experience shipping at least one AAA game title.

Desired Qualifications

  • Experience working on MMOs or large-scale multiplayer games, with a focus on readability and system scalability
  • Experience with Houdini, Blender, Substance Designer, Adobe Animate, After Effects, Photoshop, Maya, or 3DS Max.
  • Familiarity with Python or other programming languages such as C++, C#, Python, Houdini’s VEX, HLSL, etc
  • Familiarity with scalable large world-building workflows
  • Experience with performance benchmarking, streaming budgets, and optimization in large-scale maps

When submitting your application, please provide a portfolio of high-quality real-time VFX work, including examples of systematic thinking, stylized effects, technical implementation, and gameplay-focused VFX.
