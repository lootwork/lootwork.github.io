Team description

The Treasury team at SumUp in São Paulo keeps cash moving behind every merchant payout in Brazil, working through the Brazilian Payment System and alongside regulators, including the Central Bank of Brazil. As Treasury Coordinator, you'll own the liquidity side of this work in a newly structured team, leading two analysts and making sure there's always enough cash to cover planned settlements, even at peak times. It's a chance to shape how a critical, regulated function runs, from clearer review and escalation routines to automations that replace manual work, while staying close to the decisions that protect merchants.

Take a look inside our São Paulo office.

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead daily activity across the STR, SPI and SELIC desks, making sure payments are accurate, on time and compliant
  • Monitor financial transactions, including on some weekends, and block suspicious movements to protect cash and prevent fraud
  • Decide the amounts of money moved and support the funding and liquidity needs of the bank's products
  • Coach and review the work of two analysts, setting up clear review, escalation and development routines
  • Build and track KPIs for daily work, spot outliers and explain the main variances to leadership and stakeholders
  • Find and lead automation and process improvements across teams, replacing manual steps wherever possible

What you'll bring

  • Solid working knowledge of financial operations, including the Brazilian Payment System (SPB) and asset and liability management (ALM).
  • Experience leading or supervising analysts, with a coaching mindset and comfort giving and receiving feedback.
  • Advanced Excel skills and strong attention to detail when consolidating and reconciling data from different sources.
  • Ability to plan workload and manage several demands at once, while staying reliable in a time sensitive, regulated setting.
  • Fluent English and clear, concise communication with teams across the business, leadership and regulators.
