About the role.

The Product Manager, Decisioning owns the decision layer behind Zilch’s credit and eligibility products – the rules, models and data that determine who we approve, how much we lend, and how customers grow with us over time. Reporting to the Product Director and sitting within the Customer product team, this role turns credit risk strategy and product requirements into well-defined, well-delivered changes that balance growth, great customer outcomes and responsible lending.

This is a delivery-focused role at the intersection of product, credit risk and engineering. You’ll work with a dedicated engineering squad and partner closely with our Credit Risk team and with the product managers across Onboarding, Credit and Loyalty who rely on Decisioning to launch and evolve their products. Zilch is the ideal environment for a product manager who enjoys getting deep into rules and data, wants to see their work move approval rates and losses, and is looking to grow their scope in a fast-moving, regulated fintech.

Day to day responsibilities.

  • Own the Decisioning backlog and delivery plan, taking in requests from Credit Risk and other product teams and turning them into clear requirements, prioritised work and shipped changes.
  • Translate credit policy and risk strategy into decision logic – approve and decline rules, starting limits, limit increases and graduation, and product-line eligibility – configured and tested in our decisioning platform.
  • Run champion-challenger experiments and staged rollouts for new models, data sources and rule changes, tracking their impact on approval rates, credit losses and GMV.
  • Partner with other PMs to define the decisions their products need – from instant offers at onboarding to eligibility for new credit and payment features – so Decisioning helps them launch at pace.
  • Work hand in hand with engineering to keep the decisioning service and its third-party data integrations (credit bureaux, open banking and fraud data) reliable, performant and well documented.
  • Identify and automate manual risk processes, such as moving customers between product lines, to reduce operational load and speed up decisions.
  • Monitor decision performance through clear KPIs, spot issues early, and ensure every change is documented, auditable and compliant, working with Risk and Compliance on governance and sign-off.

We’re looking for.

  • Experience as a product manager, product owner or business analyst within a fintech, lender or other FCA-regulated financial services business.
  • Hands-on experience of credit or lending decisioning – approvals, limits, affordability or credit policy – ideally in BNPL, cards or consumer lending.
  • Practical experience configuring or delivering changes in a decisioning platform or rules engine (e.g. Taktile, Provenir, Experian PowerCurve or similar).
  • Working knowledge of the UK consumer credit framework, including Consumer Duty and creditworthiness requirements, and how it shapes decision design.
  • Strong delivery skills: writes clear requirements, works well with engineers, and runs a backlog with pace and rigour.
  • Confident, independent stakeholder manager, able to align Risk, Engineering and other product teams around priorities and trade-offs.

Nice to have.

  • Comfortable using SQL or similar tools to pull and interrogate data yourself, size changes and read experiment results.
  • Experience using credit bureau, open banking or transaction data in decisioning.
  • Exposure to fraud or FinCrime controls and how they interact with credit decisions.
