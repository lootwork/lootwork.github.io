SEON is the command center for fraud prevention and AML compliance, helping thousands of companies worldwide stop fraud, reduce risk and protect revenue. Powered by 900+ real-time, first-party data signals, SEON enriches customer profiles, flags suspicious behavior and streamlines compliance workflows - all from one place. SEON provides richer data, more flexible and transparent analysis, and faster time to value than any other provider on the market. We’ve helped companies reduce fraud by 95% and achieve 32x ROI, and we’re growing fast, thanks to our partnerships with some of the world’s most ambitious digital brands like Revolut, Wise, and Bilt.

This position will join SEON’s growing Legal & Compliance team, reporting to our VP & General Counsel. You will focus on privacy and data protection, product compliance and internal controls, working closely with our Product, Engineering, Security and Revenue teams across our global offices.

This role can be based in our Budapest, Hungary office on a hybrid schedule or remote Europe.

Что предстоит делать

- Privacy and data protection: Support our global privacy program, including records of processing, DPIAs and legitimate interest assessments, data subject requests, international transfers and privacy notices, and support our Data Protection Officer function

- Product compliance: Work with product and engineering teams to turn regulatory requirements, including GDPR and the EU AI Act, into practical product requirements

- Customer due diligence: Help our Revenue teams answer privacy and compliance questions from customers, many of them regulated financial businesses

- Internal controls and audits: Support our security and compliance certifications (such as SOC 2 and ISO 27001), including evidence collection and control testing

- Policies and vendor risk: Help maintain our internal compliance policies and risk register, and review vendors for privacy and security risk

- Process improvement: Use our tools and AI to make compliance work faster, more consistent and easier to audit

WHAT YOU’LL BRING

- A degree in law or a related field

- 2+ years of privacy, data protection or compliance experience (SaaS, fintech, other regulated business or law firm)

- Working knowledge of GDPR

- Ability to explain regulatory issues clearly to technical and non-technical colleagues

- Fluent in English on a professional level

- Ability to thrive in a dynamic company environment

- Willingness to take active ownership of tasks and processes

- Working knowledge of and an interest in leveraging AI capabilities

(AMAZING IF YOU ALSO HAVE)

- IAPP certification (CIPP/E, CIPM or CIPT)

- Experience with SOC 2, ISO 27001 or the EU AI Act

- Background in fraud prevention, AML or financial services
