Join FxPro: a leading international fintech company. Be a part of our expanding international team, with offices in Limassol, London, Nassau, Dubai, and Yerevan. FxPro boasts a diverse workforce of over 600 employees representing 50 nationalities, making it an exciting and dynamic workplace. At FxPro, we see each team member as an integral part of our success story.

We are looking for a Financial BI Analyst with strong financial analysis expertise and hands-on Power BI skills, ideally with a background in banking, hedge funds, investment funds, or similar financial institutions. You will analyse financial data to help improve business performance, and provide insights and feedback to the growth team and management.

Обязанности

  • Perform financial analysis: P&L, cash flow, balance sheets, financial ratios, and cohort analysis
  • Design, develop, and maintain Power BI solutions: data models, DAX measures, and dashboards to support financial reporting
  • Talk to stakeholders (finance, operations, product) to understand their needs and turn them into analytics solutions
  • Work with data engineers to ensure data coming from source systems is accurate and well-organised
  • Create reports and present financial findings to stakeholders in a clear, actionable way
  • Spot inefficiencies in financial processes and suggest improvements
  • Analyse the user journey and identify areas for product improvement in a banking context

Требования

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Finance, Economics, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related field
  • Proven experience in financial analysis (banking, hedge funds, investment funds, or similar)
  • CFA, FRM, or similar certification is a strong plus
  • Strong hands-on experience with Power BI and Excel (DAX, M language, data modelling, dashboards)
  • Proficiency in SQL (at least at a basic level)
  • Ability to explain complex financial data in simple terms to non-technical audiences
  • Collaborative and comfortable working across different teams
  • Detail-oriented and takes data quality seriously
  • Proactive in identifying problems and suggesting solutions
  • Understanding of product analytics in a banking context
