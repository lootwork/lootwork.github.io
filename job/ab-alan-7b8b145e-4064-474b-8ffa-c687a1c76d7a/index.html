О GROWTH TEAM

Growth Marketing at Alan covers a broad range of opportunities, including acquisition, segment and market growth, partnerships, content, experimentation, and international expansion.

We are looking for adaptable marketers who can take ownership of a defined growth problem, learn quickly, and evolve as our priorities change.

Concretely, our team works on missions such as

- Developing our channels: scaling paid acquisition, growing visibility in AI search and LLMs like ChatGPT, or growing our strategic partnerships

- Crafting the go-to-market strategy for our different segments (e.g. retirees, freelancers, companies,...)

- Producing performance creatives with AI workflows to create assets at scale

YOUR MISSION

As a Senior Growth Marketer, you will own broad and strategically important Growth outcomes. You will challenge the initial framing, make high-impact choices, and build scalable ways of working across teams, segments, or markets.

Что предстоит делать

- Own broad and strategically important Growth outcomes.

- Challenge the initial framing of complex problems and identify the highest-leverage opportunities.

- Connect Growth decisions to company objectives, market strategy, and long-term business impact.

- Make explicit choices about where the organisation should and should not invest.

- Build repeatable systems for insight, experimentation, measurement, and execution.

- Influence senior stakeholders across teams, markets, and functions.

- Anticipate second-order effects and create leverage beyond your own execution.

- Raise the quality of Growth thinking and execution across the team.

Кого мы ищем

- A strong track record of owning broad or strategically important Growth outcomes.

- Experience making complex trade-offs across business priorities and stakeholder groups.

- Ability to challenge the framing of a problem and identify systemic constraints.

- Strong business judgment, including an understanding of economics, measurement, and long-term impact.

- Experience building scalable systems, standards, or ways of working.

- Ability to influence senior stakeholders without relying on formal authority.

- Strong learning agility and comfort operating in complex, ambiguous environments.

- Ability to create leverage beyond your individual execution and raise the quality of the surrounding team.

- Advanced written and verbal communication skills in English
