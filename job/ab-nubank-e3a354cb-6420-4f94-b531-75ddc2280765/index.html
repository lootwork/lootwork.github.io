STAFF PRODUCT DESIGNER (IC7), CREDIT BUILDER

Location: Miami (hybrid). We'll also consider candidates near other Nubank hubs, including São Paulo.

Team: Global Credit Card, Credit Builder

О роли

Millions of people across Latin America have never had a credit card. For many of them, Nubank is the first institution that gives them a real path to one. Credit Builder is where that path starts.

Our Credit Builder card is an entry-level credit product. Customers add money to unlock spending power, and we coach them step by step toward a full credit limit. The goal isn't to keep people on a starter product. It's to move them up and out of it, and most of our success stories do exactly that. The experience is built around gamification. A guided timeline walks customers through adding to their money box, increasing their limit, setting up autopay and building healthy habits. It has become one of the highest-NPS experiences at Nu and a model for gamification across the company.

Today Credit Builder runs in Brazil, Mexico and Colombia. Next year our scope roughly flips. We'll bring our under-18 credit product, our credit score product (NuScore) and Credit Builder into more countries, possibly including the US. We're also planning a significant revamp of the core experience.

We're hiring a Staff Product Designer to help set the craft bar for this work. You'll be one of the most senior designers in Global Credit Card, working on both the high-visibility revamp and the day-to-day product work that keeps a fast-moving, multi-country team shipping.

Что предстоит делать

- Lead design for Credit Builder and related underserved-credit products across Brazil, Mexico, Colombia and new markets, including the US.

- Drive the upcoming revamp of the Credit Builder experience, raising the bar on visual craft, interaction design and motion.

- Design global solutions that hold together as one system while adapting to local needs, such as regulatory requirements, cultural context and market-specific constraints.

- Push gamification and financial-coaching patterns further, helping customers understand how credit works and how to grow it.

- Mentor and level up the more junior designers on the team, through critiques and day-to-day collaboration.

- Partner closely with product managers, engineers, business analysts and a dedicated researcher, plus the market design teams in Brazil, Mexico and Colombia and adjacent teams such as Growth, Core and Collections.

- Get hands-on with the full range of work, from the most ambitious projects to quick everyday design tasks, and move fast across both.

Кого мы ищем

- An exceptional craft bar: polished visual and interaction design, strong systems thinking, and comfort with motion.

- Proven experience designing complex products, ideally in fintech, financial services or other regulated or multi-market settings.

- Strong strategic judgment, and comfort with ambiguity, competing priorities and shifting scope.

- Excellent stakeholder management across countries, time zones and functions, with the ability to align global and local partners.

- A low-ego, hands-on mindset. On a lean team covering many products and markets, everyone pitches in, including senior folks.

- Experience mentoring designers and raising the quality of a team's work.

- A portfolio that shows both depth of craft and the thinking behind it.

Будет плюсом

- Spanish and/or Portuguese.

- Experience with gamification, behavior-change design or financial-education products.

- Experience designing for underserved or first-time credit customers.

- Willingness to travel occasionally to work in person with market teams. Team leadership typically visits partners about twice a year.
