ole Overview

We are looking for a Web3 Content Creator & Product Education Specialist to produce educational articles and guides, script and present videos, and create supporting course materials that help users understand, explore and activate products across the Bybit ecosystem.

This is a hands-on content execution role. The successful candidate must have proven experience producing written educational content and scripting, presenting and hosting video content within crypto, blockchain, trading or Web3.

The role will work closely with business units, product teams and the Learn team to turn new products, features, token listings and platform updates into clear written content, tailored videos, tutorials and supporting educational materials.

The objective is not only to explain how Bybit products work, but also to help users confidently take the next step and use them.

Key Responsibilities

Written Content & Product Education

  • Write original educational articles, product guides, explainers, tutorials and how-to content covering Bybit products, crypto markets, blockchain, trading and Web3
  • Present complex crypto and trading concepts in clear, practical, retail-friendly language
  • Adapt content for beginner, intermediate and experienced users
  • Ensure content explains product value, functionality, risks, use cases and user actions
  • Create or adapt assigned lesson content, scripts, quizzes and supporting materials based on course structures and learning journeys set by the Learn team
  • Ensure assigned course materials are accurate, clear and aligned with the intended audience and learning objectives
  • Independently research, draft, revise and finalize written content to publication-ready standards
  • Own assigned content deliverables from brief through review and publication, coordinating with relevant stakeholders and raising risks or dependencies early

Video Scripting & On-Camera Presentation

  • Script, present and host short-form and long-form educational videos covering Bybit products, features and campaigns, working with production support on editing and post-production
  • Develop video scripts, walkthroughs, product demonstrations and explainer series
  • Maintain a consistent, credible and engaging creator voice on camera

Product Launch Content

  • Work with product and business teams to understand upcoming releases, features, campaigns and token listings
  • Produce timely written explainers, FAQs and walkthroughs, and script and present videos to support product launches
  • Translate technical product information into clear, engaging educational content
  • Ensure content is aligned with launch timelines and business priorities
  • Follow up on missing product information and complete revisions promptly

Content Operations & Distribution

  • Repurpose long-form educational content into short videos, clips, posts, guides and social assets
  • Publish assigned content across agreed channels, working with relevant channel owners where needed
  • Coordinate reviews, approvals and production handoffs with editors, designers, SEO, localization, compliance and product stakeholders
  • Flag outdated, duplicated or underperforming content for review
  • Conduct accuracy and quality checks on own deliverables before submission
  • Track the performance of assigned content, including reach, watch time, engagement and activation, and share insights with the Learn team

AI-Assisted Content Production

  • Use AI tools to support research, scripting, localization, repurposing and lightweight content production, including voiceovers, animations, product walkthroughs and visual explainers
  • Experiment with emerging AI content tools to improve production speed and scalability
  • Ensure AI-generated materials remain accurate, credible and aligned with Bybit's quality standards
