О команде

Inside our team

  • Strong data engineering foundation team with sustainable data pipelines.
  • Highly mature Data team with a mindset of Data as a Product.
  • Highly data-driven culture across multiple teams with a variety of data problems requiring diversified analytics methods. You will learn and grow deeply in Analytics while solving the problems.

О роли

  • As an Amanotes’ product Data Analyst, you have the chance to deal with data of 3+ billion users to generate accurate, actionable, and timely insights for the products team, in order to boost user understanding and product optimization

Что предстоит делать

  • Insights are key. We need deep-dive insights to improve user experience and product/process optimization, driving actions from top-down levels to bottom-up.
  • Use analytical techniques such as EDA, statistical tests, time series analysis, funnel analysis, user journey analysis, clustering and other segmentation methods, etc. to extract patterns and user/product insights from behavioral user data.
  • Conduct pre-AB test exploratory analysis to provide data-driven insights for product feature design.
  • Conduct post-AB test deep-dive explanatory and exploratory analysis to validate key hypotheses, providing solid learning for next steps.
  • Construct dashboards to monitor business performance.
  • Proactively communicate with the business team (cross-functional teams) to understand their needs and to transform business questions into analytics and build data models to solve them.
  • Collaborate closely with the Data Engineering team to understand the centralized data warehouse and pipeline, extracting data from multiple sources.

Требования

  • Bachelor’s degree in Business, Economics, Statistics, Math, Computer Science, or related majors.
  • +3 years of experience in the data/analytics field.
  • Strong product mindset, striving to improve user experience and product optimization; not stopping at data but providing product-driven solutions and consultation.
  • Very good at logical thinking and able to learn new things quickly, attention to details.
  • Strong analytics/statistics skills with experience working with a large amount of data (clean, visualize, extract insights, etc.).
  • Strong experience using SQL/Python/R for data extraction and manipulation.
  • Be confident to present the ideas and translating technical concepts and data into understandable reports/presentations.
  • Be able to give clear and actionable recommendations based on data findings.
  • Prefer experience on user segmentation modeling and product solutions based on models.
  • Have a growth mindset, especially be comfortable with agile, strategic projects with ambiguous approaches.
  • Nice to have: basic understanding of game industry and good command of English.
