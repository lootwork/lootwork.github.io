Senior Software Engineer, Full Stack, Government Products

Join us in building the future of finance.

Our mission is to democratize finance for all. An estimated $124 trillion of assets will be inherited by younger generations in the next two decades. The largest transfer of wealth in human history. If you're ready to be at the epicenter of this historic cultural and financial shift, keep reading.

О Team & Role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world's biggest financial problems. We're looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn't a place for complacency, it's where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We're a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

The Government Products team focuses on building and scaling systems that support long-term investing and account management for millions of customers. This team works closely with product, data, and infrastructure partners to deliver reliable, secure, and intuitive experiences. You will help shape systems that enable customers to plan for their financial future with confidence!

As a Senior Software Engineer, Full Stack, you will serve as a key technical contributor for this 0-to-1 initiative. You will be responsible for designing and building the features, services, and cross-functional integrations that span our Go backend and React web experience, working securely with external institutional partners. This is a high-visibility, high-impact role where you will directly shape a platform built to serve millions of future customers

This role is based in our New York, NY and Menlo Park, CA offices, with in-person attendance expected 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Design, build, and ship features across Go backend services and React-based front-end experiences for a brand-new, standalone product optimized for security, reliability, and scale.
  • Contribute to the architectural strategy and technical decisions of a 0-to-1 platform, setting foundational engineering patterns built to last.
  • Partner closely with Product, Design, and external stakeholders to translate complex requirements into secure, well-tested, and maintainable systems.
  • Move fluidly between backend and front-end work as priorities shift, helping the team stay flexible and deliver quickly.
  • Mentor engineers through design discussions and code reviews, fostering technical excellence and raising the engineering bar for the team.

Что вы приносите

  • 5+ years experience building and maintaining production backend services, ideally in Go or Ruby, with a solid grasp of API design, domain modeling, and reliable data flows.
  • Strong experience building front-end applications with JavaScript frameworks such as React, with comfort working in Node and backend-for-frontend architectures.
  • Demonstrable execution in a zero-to-one product space, navigating technical ambiguity, making sound technical decisions, and taking features from design through production.
  • A track record of guiding the work of other engineers through design reviews, code reviews, and mentorship.
  • Excellent communication skills with the ability to partner closely with Product, Design, and external institutional partners.
  • Experience with financial products, ledgering, reporting, or investment technology is a plus.

Что мы предлагаем

  • Challenging, high-impact work to grow your career
  • Performance driven compensation with multipliers for outsized impact, bonus programs, equity ownership, and 401(k) matching
  • Best in class benefits to fuel your work, including 100% paid health insurance for employees with 90% coverage for dependents
  • Lifestyle wallet - a highly flexible benefits spending account for wellness, learning, and more
  • Employer-paid life & disability insurance, fertility benefits, and mental health benefits
  • Time off to recharge including company holidays, paid time off, sick time, parental leave, and more!
  • Exceptional office experience with catered meals, events, and comfortable workspaces.

Position Restriction: This position is restricted to US citizens or lawful permanent residents
