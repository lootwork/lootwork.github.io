О роли

The Globalization team is dedicated to expanding Block’s multiple brands worldwide. Our mission is to build on Block’s success and continue to deliver innovative, seamless, and fully localized experiences for multilingual Sellers and Customers domestically and abroad.

We are seeking a Quality Program Manager, Globalization to oversee and enhance our quality management at scale. This role involves managing key programs across quality planning, assurance, and control processes. It requires strong collaboration with both cross-functional partners and our suppliers to ensure the highest standards of quality. The ultimate goal is to measure, optimize, and sustain quality throughout the localization process in order to protect customer trust, support conversion in global markets, and reduce regulatory risk.

Вам предстоит

  • Own and evolve the overall quality program and its strategy for the Globalization team, aligning with broader business goals and KPIs.
  • Build robust quality processes that meet the company’s evolving needs, including creating and maintaining clear documentation, providing training, and offering 1:1 support. Serve as the quality expert in the development of linguistic resources and team processes.
  • Collect quality data to guide decisions on strategy, budget, initiatives, and vendor resources. Regularly report quality findings in a variety of forums and own data pipelines and dashboards.
  • Collaborate with localization vendors and internal teams to analyze quality data, identify opportunities for improvement, manage escalations, develop and refine processes, and maintain accountability for quality metrics.
  • Partner with legal, risk, compliance, and product teams to ensure Block’s localization quality program meets regulatory requirements across markets.
  • Assess existing tools and collaborate cross-functionally to optimize them through automation as a way to enhance scalability, productivity and efficiency.
  • Use AI tools, such as LLM-based translation quality checks, to scale quality evaluation.

You Have

  • 5+ years of experience in the localization industry, with a focus on program management, vendor management, localization quality assurance, and delivering multi-layered projects involving cross-functional collaboration.
  • Skilled at defining and delivering results end-to-end against ambiguous problems.
  • Comfortable with data analytics - including demonstrated facility with data management tools and advanced analytical platforms, such as Snowflake, Datadog, or similar.
  • Hands-on experience with Artificial Intelligence, such as MT/AI quality evaluation and process automation.
  • Experience running QA programs, including Terminology Management and Style Guide creation, and familiarity with quality measurement frameworks/models (e.g. MQM/DQF).
  • A clear perspective on when and where AI tools and agents help or hurt translation quality and its oversight.
  • Experience working with multiple TMS, CMS, and other translation and content management tools.
  • Ability to describe linguistic challenges, cultural questions and concepts to stakeholders.
  • Strong communication skills paired with excellent presentation skills, especially of complex data for a variety of audiences.
  • Strong relationship management skills, with the ability to influence others and build trust.
  • Ability to excel in a fast-paced, multicultural team.

Technologies We Use and Teach

  • Contentful, Github, Airtable, Jira, Phrase, Internal tools
