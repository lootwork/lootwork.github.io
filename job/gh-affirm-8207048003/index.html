О роли

Affirm’s Marketing Legal team provides legal counsel on the marketing and advertising of our financial products across the U.S., Canada, U.K., and Australia, with more markets on the horizon.

We’re looking for a Senior Counsel to be an independent, trusted advisor on our high-visibility work, and to help build the guardrails that let a lean team scale as Affirm grows internationally.

This role is for someone who can balance the legal requirements of consumer credit and advertising across multiple geographies with real creative ambition. You should get energy from finding “yes, here’s how” rather than stopping at “no.” You’ll partner closely with Marketing, Brand, Product, Partnerships, Compliance, and other international teams, and report to the Director, Marketing Legal.

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead legal strategy for campaigns. Own legal review for Affirm’s multi-channel campaigns from concept through launch, and serve as a go-to advisor on influencer, partner, and seasonal marketing, making practical risk calls that keep work moving.
  • Support international growth. Guide marketing across our current and new markets, partnering with cross-functional teams and local counsel to adapt campaigns and disclosures to local requirements.
  • Build scalable legal resources. Create legal templates, guidance, and self-serve resources for internal teams, merchants, and partners, working closely with Compliance in every market we support.
  • Shape B2B and product-adjacent messaging. Review sales and merchant-facing materials, in-product messaging, and required credit disclosures.
  • Counsel on claims and disclosures. Assess claim substantiation and draft clear, accurate disclosures across all media.
  • Structure promotions. Draft rules for and review sweepstakes, contests, and incentives in the U.S. and internationally.
  • Stay current on credit and BNPL marketing rules. Track state and international laws governing how credit and BNPL products are marketed, and partner with Product & Regulatory Legal to turn developments into practical marketing guidance.

What We Look For

  • 8+ years of marketing and advertising law experience, ideally a mix of law firm and in-house
  • JD from an accredited law school and active membership, in good standing, in a U.S. state bar
  • Deep knowledge of U.S. advertising and consumer protection law, including the FTC Act, UDAAP, state consumer protection laws, endorsement and influencer rules, and promotions laws
  • Experience advising on marketing and advertising across multiple international jurisdictions, with the ability to get up to speed quickly on new regimes and work effectively with local counsel (specific country expertise not required)
  • Proven ability to work effectively across multiple time zones, partnering with Legal, Compliance, Product, and in-market teams around the world
  • A background in a regulated industry, such as financial services, healthcare, gaming, alcohol, or consumer health, and a clear understanding of how regulatory scrutiny shapes creative and disclosure decisions
  • A track record of independently owning high-visibility, multi-channel campaigns, including social, influencer, paid media, email, and in-app
  • Sound, risk-calibrated judgment: you can tell a must-fix from a nice-to-have, and explain why
  • Clear, concise communication with business partners at all levels, in writing and in virtual settings, suited to a fully remote team
  • A builder’s mindset: experience creating playbooks, templates, or processes that help a lean team scale
  • Thrives in a fast-paced, high-growth environment: low ego, high ownership, and ready to pitch in wherever needed

Additional Strengths

  • Marketing legal experience with payments, consumer credit, or BNPL products, especially outside the U.S.
  • Familiarity with U.K. financial promotion rules or other non-U.S. financial services advertising regimes
  • In-house experience at a fintech or financial services company
