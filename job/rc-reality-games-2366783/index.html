About Us

Reality Games is a fast-growing mobile gaming company with a focus on harnessing big data and AI to create immersive, location-based gaming experiences. We’re passionate about innovation, and we’re scaling globally with our latest project, Monopoly World.

About the Project

Monopoly World is our bold re-imagining of the most iconic board game ever made, rebuilt for mobile and already captivating millions of players. We’re building a revolutionary, location-based mobile game based on the world’s strongest gaming IP — transforming the real world into a living, breathing game board. Players walk, buy, trade, and build real-world properties, powered by geolocation, real-time data, and AI personalization. Our mission is not just to adapt a famous brand, but to redefine how players experience the world around them. This means pioneering a complete sonic identity for a global franchise — something that has never been done before in this space.

About the Role

Location: Kraków – On-site (office only)

Contract: Full-time

We’re looking for a hands-on, fast-moving Performance Creative Producer who will manage the end-to-end production of performance ads for Monopoly World. This includes sourcing creators, purchasing ads, coordinating designers, giving feedback, and delivering high-quality assets for UA testing. This is a pure production role—operational, quick, and quality-driven.

You must be someone who thrives on speed and can get great ads produced within 24–48 hours.

Why this role matters?

Monopoly World’s growth relies heavily on top-performing creatives.

Your work will be directly responsible for scaling our global user acquisition.

Work happens in our Kraków office alongside the creative, production, and UA teams.

Join us full-time at our stunning office located in the heart of Krakow! Situated in a historic building just steps away from the vibrant city center, our workspace blends timeless charm with modern amenities, providing an inspiring setting for your personal and professional growth.

Key Responsibilities

  • Source UGC creators, editors, animators, and designers worldwide.
  • Negotiate pricing and deadlines for ad production.
  • Lead fast and effective calls with creators and production partners.
  • Give clear briefs and sharp, actionable feedback.
  • Evaluate creative quality — understanding what performs and why.
  • Coordinate designers, animators, video editors, and copywriters.
  • Manage deadlines, priorities, and delivery pipelines.
  • Work closely with User Acquisition on creative testing.
  • Maintain processes, templates, and structure for creative production.
  • Deliver assets in all required formats (Meta, TikTok, Google, Webshop).
  • Track trends in mobile gaming creatives and generate new ideas.
