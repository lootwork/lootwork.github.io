Что предстоит делать

As the Manager for Growth Strategy & Operations, you'll build and run the experimentation engine behind Airwallex's global growth: what we test, how we test it, and how we know when we're right. You'll design and manage the end-to-end execution of growth experiments and campaigns. You'll work with cross-functional partners to translate strategic hypotheses into operational workflows, manage experiment design and tracking, coordinate launches and rollouts, synthesize learnings to inform next iterations and build scalable systems.

You'll act as the operational backbone of the growth function, ensuring experiments run efficiently, dependencies are managed, and insights are captured. You'll own parts of our growth campaign calendar and experiment roadmap, working with Growth, Commercial, Data, Product, Engineering, Legal, Marketing to orchestrate launches. This role requires strong project management, analytical thinking, and the ability to balance speed with rigor.

This role is based in San Francisco, CA.

Обязанности

- The experimentation backlog and roadmap. Consolidate hypotheses from Commercial, Lifecycle, Growth Innovation, Product and Marketing into a single prioritized backlog. Rank by expected impact, confidence, effort and strategic fit, and keep the prioritization framework current as the bar moves. Maintain the global campaign registry and forward calendar so teams can see clashes and share learnings.

- Experiment design and measurement. Partner with each campaign or experiment owner to define the hypothesis, primary metric, and target effect size before a single thing gets built. Set the measurement approach up front: control or holdout, audience size and statistical power, and the criteria for calling a result. Pressure-test feasibility across audience, budget and technical requirements, and flag what has to be built first.

- Build, QA and launch. Turn approved designs into live campaigns across our Martech stack (Hightouch, Marketo, Infobip, Contentful). Produce copy and creative variants with the campaign owner, draft terms with Legal where needed, and run copy and design approvals end to end. QA the full journey before go-live, apply consent, suppression and frequency-capping rules per market, and run the rollback and comms protocol if a campaign misfires.

- Measurement and readout. Own the tracking taxonomy e.g. campaign IDs, tagging conventions so results stay comparable across campaigns and regions. Build and maintain reporting dashboards with Data Science in Hex and Looker, monitor performance, run ad-hoc analysis and troubleshooting, and share results and learnings in cross-functional forums.

- Systems, automation and AI. Find the gaps in tooling and process, and codify repeatable work incl. checklists, QA scripts, readout templates, a registry library into tooling that scales. Use AI to do this well, with clear guardrails: human review before anything customer-facing goes live.

- Budget and forecasting. Partner with FP&A on the budgeting and forecasting process. Set expected impact and cost targets before launch, own the central budget once allocated, track utilization across campaigns, and report actuals against forecast.
