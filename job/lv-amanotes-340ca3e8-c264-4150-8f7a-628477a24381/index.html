О вакансии

We're looking for a Senior UA Marketing (Mobile Apps) to own and scale user growth across our AI and Utility app portfolio. You'll manage significant advertising budgets, optimize performance across multiple channels, and play a key role in driving product growth in global markets.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own and execute UA strategy across the company for a portfolio of 3-5 mobile apps titles, managing large-scale budgets on channels such as Meta, Google Ads, TikTok, Unity Ads, IronSource, AppLovin, DSPs, and more
  • Act as your team’s expert in one of Meta, Tiktok, Google UAC, or SDK networks, meeting regularly with our stakeholders while actively running campaigns that hit KPI & ROAS targets and grow daily active users
  • Define UA launch strategies for new titles, including pre-registration campaigns
  • Define conceptual creative strategies and testing processes
  • Collaborate closely with Product, Monetization, Creative, and Data teams to drive growth
  • Stay ahead of trends by conducting market research and competitive analysis

*** Duties and responsibilities outlined above are representative, but not all-inclusive.

Требования

  • 3+ years of hands-on UA experience in mobile app industry
  • Strong preference for candidates with experience in UA for apps in Utility or AI genre
  • Deep knowledge of the mobile growth stack: MMPs, mediation, analytics, SDK ad networks, DSPs, etc.
  • Self-driven, be able to communicate goals, needs, and reports that provide insight into game performance and recommendations on improving metrics relevant to user acquisition campaigns
  • Experience with ad monetization and strong working relationships with various ad networks on both the monetization and user acquisition side.
  • Fluent English (written + speaking) is a must

Чтобы дополнить команду, вы должны

  • You are on top of the latest apps, trends, and what’s happening in live events
  • Solution-oriented, self-motivated and have the attitude to get things done
  • Comfortable with uncertainties and changes in a fast-paced environment
  • Curious, proactive learning and knowledge-hungry learner
