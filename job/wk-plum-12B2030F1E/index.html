О роли

You will be joining our Infrastructure squad as a Senior Site Reliability Engineer to ensure that Plum’s systems are resilient, secure, scalable, observable and fully capable to support our growth. You will support our Engineering function to use our infrastructure in the most efficient way. You’ll proactively identify areas of improvements and propose initiatives to make the SRE function more streamlined and with reduced overhead.

What you will be doing

  • Operate, secure and scale our infrastructure (GCP, AWS, Kubernetes, PostgreSQL, RabbitMQ, Redis)
  • Automate aspects of systems using infrastructure management tools of the trade (we use Terraform)
  • Ensure our metrics give an accurate picture of how the system is performing (we use Prometheus, OpenTelemetry, Grafana)
  • Build and maintain SLIs and SLOs for our infrastructure, and provide a platform for squads to build their own SLOs on top of collected metrics
  • Optimize and oversee our CI/CD infrastructure (Github Actions, CircleCI, ArgocD, Argo Workflows), empowering engineering teams with seamless build, test, and deployment automations
  • Lead incident response and troubleshoot issues, correcting and improving systems to prevent incidents and grow at scale
  • Build, improve and maintain Developer Experience tooling
  • Collaborate cross-functionally with Engineering squads, Security, Compliance to set up and improve Standard Operating Procedures

For this role, we'd like to see

  • Working experience of 5+ years as a Site Reliability Engineer, DevOps or in a similar position
  • Proficiency in managing cloud infrastructure with tools like Terraform/OpenTofu
  • Strong expertise in administration of Kubernetes clusters (preferably GKE)
  • Strong expertise in observability (Logging, Monitoring, Tracing)
  • Strong experience in scripting and automation
  • Experience in Python, ability to navigate large codebases
  • Familiarity with Relational Databases (preferably PostgreSQL)
  • Passion for improving engineering workflows and developer productivity
  • Analytical, troubleshooting attitude
  • Proactive approach on problems, able to identify them and propose solutions
  • Passion for continuous improvement and challenging the status quo
  • Excellent communication skills in English (verbal and written)

Good to have

  • System design/administration background
  • Security awareness with secure-by-default mindset
