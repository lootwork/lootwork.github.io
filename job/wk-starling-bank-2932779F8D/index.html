Starling is the UK’s first and leading digital bank on a mission to fix banking. Our vision is fast technology, fair service, and honest values – all at the tap of a phone, all the time. We built a new kind of bank because we knew technology had the power to help people save, spend and manage their money in a new and transformative way.

We’re a fully licensed UK bank with the culture and spirit of a fast-moving, disruptive tech company. We’re a bank, but better: fairer, easier to use and designed to demystify money for everyone. We employ more than 3,000 people across our London, Southampton, Cardiff and Manchester offices.

We’re looking for a Content Designer to join our team and shape the experience our customers get in the Starling app. From notifications and navigation to FAQs and T&Cs, you’ll be responsible for writing and editing the messages we need to get across in a clear, concise, and consistent way. You’ll help drive behaviour at every stage of the customer lifecycle, maintain copy quality and consistency, and build a product that changes the way people bank – for good.

Hybrid working

We have a hybrid approach to working here at Starling – our preference is that you're located within a commutable distance of one of our offices so that we're able to interact and collaborate in person. For this role, we're asking that you attend the office a minimum of 1 day per week.

Что предстоит делать

  • Crafting precise, accessible, and on-brand copy for the Starling app, online bank, and customer comms
  • Getting under the skin of complicated concepts and finding ways to explain them clearly
  • Working closely with product designers, managers, and user researchers to create intuitive and effective customer experiences
  • Conducting research to inform and review copy decisions, and advocating for the best experience for all our users
  • Exploring solutions that satisfy regulatory requirements and meet customer needs
  • Spotting and solving content design problems, and maintaining consistency – including in all the nooks and crannies
  • Developing guidance, templates, and frameworks to scale the impact of content design at Starling

Требования

This might be for you if

  • you’ve worked in UX writing or content design roles before, ideally for an app
  • you have a knack for demystifying complex subjects, and writing copy that helps people find or do what they need
  • you understand how to use data to drive decisions, and explain those decisions strategically
  • you can explain why you chose one word over another, and change your mind when research or new information tells you to
  • you’re comfortable asking as many questions as it takes to understand a brief or piece of feedback
  • you have an eye for the small stuff – you notice the inconsistent button label, the unclear error message, the figure that doesn’t match
  • you’re enthusiastic about collaborating with other teams, and curious about others’ points of view

Как откликнуться

Please send us a cover letter as well as a CV – this helps us get a better understanding of who you are and the experience you’ll bring.

Please also attach or include a link to your portfolio, if you have one. If you don’t, please include an example in your cover letter of a piece of writing you’re proud of – tell us what the problem was, what you wrote, and what changed as a result.
