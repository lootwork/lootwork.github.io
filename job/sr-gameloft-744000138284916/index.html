Job Description

Gameloft Barcelona is looking for a Video Artist Intern to join our Creative Agency team and help create engaging video content for Asphalt Legends, one of the world's leading racing game franchises.

As a Video Artist Intern, you will gain hands-on experience capturing gameplay, editing marketing videos, and creating content for social media, User Acquisition campaigns, app stores, and other digital platforms. Working alongside experienced artists and marketers, you'll learn how high-quality video assets are produced for a live-service game enjoyed by millions of players worldwide.

Что предстоит делать

  • Capture exciting gameplay footage using our internal tools and game engine (training provided!).
  • Edit short-form videos for marketing campaigns, social media, and User Acquisition.
  • Help create gameplay screenshots and adapt visual assets for different platforms.
  • Support the team with Photoshop and After Effects tasks when needed.
  • Organize video projects and prepare assets following creative briefs and technical requirements.
  • Collaborate with artists, marketers, and creative leads to deliver engaging content.
  • Learn how video content is created, reviewed, and optimized for different audiences and platforms.

This internship is a great opportunity to develop your video editing and content creation skills while gaining real industry experience in an international team passionate about games and creativity.

Требования

  • Undergraduate.
  • Passion for video games, video editing, and digital content creation.
  • Basic knowledge of Adobe Premiere Pro.
  • Basic knowledge of Photoshop and/or After Effects is a plus.
  • A creative eye for composition, pacing, and visual storytelling.
  • Organized, proactive, and eager to learn.
  • Fluent in English, both written and spoken.
  • Portfolio, demo reel, or personal video projects showcasing your work.
  • Curiosity, creativity, and a positive mindset.
