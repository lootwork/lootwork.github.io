О роли

- At Nubank, hiring and moving talent across borders is a core part of how we grow. The Talent Acquisition team operates at high speed and scale, and the systems that support it need to match that pace. As a Senior IT Engineer in the Automations & Internal Systems pillar, you will design and build the internal systems and automations that power our recruiting operation.

- This is a high-impact, AI-first engineering role. You will understand real operational problems, build solutions using the tools that fit best (from n8n and AI APIs to production-grade services), and progressively evolve the most critical solutions into robust systems on Nubank’s core engineering stack. You’ll work on projects ranging from recruiting workflows to cross-functional, business-critical systems, including regulated processes such as background checks.

YOU'LL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR

- Internal Systems Development: Designing, building, and evolving internal systems that support Talent Acquisition and related operations, including new checks and integrations with external vendors for regulated processes.

- From Automation to Production Services: Taking solutions originally built in low-code tools like Apps Script and rebuilding them as scalable, maintainable services on Nubank’s core engineering stack (Clojure, Datomic, AWS), as part of our migration toward canonical technologies.

- Automation Development: Designing and building robust automations and cross-system integrations, using n8n as our main platform together with internal APIs and tools.

- AI-First Engineering: Applying an AI-first mindset to everything you build, leveraging tools like Claude Code, Cursor, and Glean to develop intelligent, agentic solutions that replace or augment manual processes.

- Systems Integration: Designing and maintaining reliable data flows between recruiting systems and other internal platforms, ensuring data integrity and security.

- Problem Discovery: Partnering with business stakeholders and recruiters to understand their challenges, prioritize opportunities, and define the right solution, with a focus on efficiency, accuracy, and user experience.

- Collaboration: Working alongside other technical partners in the Talent Efficiency pillar, accountable for automations and internal systems, as well as recruiters and business partners, to deliver solutions aligned with real operational needs.

- Documentation & Knowledge Sharing: Maintaining clear documentation, including flow diagrams, technical docs, and user guides, to ensure maintainability and knowledge transfer.

- Innovation: Staying current with emerging engineering and AI trends, and proactively evaluating new tools and methodologies.

WE ARE LOOKING FOR A PERSON WHO HAS

- Solid professional experience in software development, including building and maintaining backend services or internal systems.

- Proficiency in at least one general-purpose programming language (such as Python, Java, Kotlin, Go, or Clojure).

- Proven experience building with AI/LLM APIs, including prompt engineering, agentic workflows, and tools such as Claude, Gemini, or GPT.

- Solid experience with RESTful API design and integrations with external web services.

- Strong knowledge of development best practices: code versioning, testing, code review, and design patterns.

- Ability to analyze, document, and optimize processes before building solutions for them.

- Clear and effective communication skills, able to translate technical concepts for non-technical stakeholders.

- Advanced English proficiency.
