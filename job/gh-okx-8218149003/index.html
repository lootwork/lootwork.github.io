Кто мы

At OKX, we believe that the future will be reshaped by crypto, and ultimately contribute to every individual's freedom.

OKX is a leading crypto exchange, and the developer of OKX Wallet, giving millions access to crypto trading and decentralized crypto applications (dApps). OKX is also a trusted brand by hundreds of large institutions seeking access to crypto markets. We are safe and reliable, backed by our Proof of Reserves.

Across our multiple offices globally, we are united by our core principles: We Before Me, Do the Right Thing, and Get Things Done. These shared values drive our culture, shape our processes, and foster a friendly, rewarding, and diverse environment for every OK-er. OKX is part of OKG, a group that brings the value of Blockchain to users around the world, through our leading products OKX, OKX Wallet, OKLink and more.

О команде

  • Singapore is an important part of OKX's global strategy and a major hub for digital assets. Licensed by the Monetary Authority of Singapore, OKX SG is working to build a trusted, easy-to-use platform for clients who want to trade digital assets.
  • Our ambition is to become a leading exchange in Singapore. Achieving that means understanding the needs of local clients, building strong relationships and continually improving the products and experiences we offer.
  • Joining the Singapore team is an opportunity to help shape a growing business. You'll work with colleagues across functions and markets, take ownership of meaningful challenges and see the impact of your work on our clients and our growth.

О Opportunity

  • We are seeking a Senior Marketing Manager to define how OKX Singapore tells its story and connects with clients.
  • You'll take ownership of client communications, creative storytelling and product marketing, turning business priorities and product capabilities into messages that resonate. This is a role for someone who combines strategic thinking with creative judgment and enjoys bringing ideas to life.
  • You'll have the autonomy to develop our voice, strengthen how we communicate and make our products easier to understand. Working with local and global teams, you'll see the impact of your work on client trust, engagement and adoption.

Что предстоит делать

  • Digital Marketing & Client Communications

- Own client communications across email, in-app messaging, social media and other digital channels, strengthening engagement and product adoption.

- Lead campaign communications, translating business objectives and client insights into compelling messaging.

- Use channel performance data and client insights to continuously improve communications and engagement.

  • Creative Strategy & Storytelling

- Shape OKX Singapore's brand narrative and content strategy, building a distinctive, credible voice.

- Lead storytelling across written and visual formats, from thought leadership and social content to videos, short-form content and product explainers.

- Own creative direction from concept to delivery, working with internal production teams, PR and external partners to bring ideas to life.

  • Product Marketing & Cross-functional Collaboration

- Develop differentiated product positioning with Product and Commercial teams, grounded in client and market insights.

- Lead product launch communications and client education to drive awareness and adoption.

- Drive cross-functional alignment to ensure consistent, compliant communications.

What We Look For In You

  • Experience in marketing communications, content strategy or product marketing, with a track record of building brands and driving client engagement.
  • Strong storytelling and creative judgment, with the ability to turn complex products and ideas into clear, compelling communications.
  • Comfort working with data and client insights to inform messaging and communications strategy.
  • Strong collaboration skills and confidence aligning internal teams, creative partners and senior stakeholders.
  • A hands-on approach and willingness to take ownership from strategy through execution.

Perks & Benefits

  • Various team building programs and company events
  • Wellness and meal allowances
  • Comprehensive healthcare schemes for employees and dependants
  • More that we love to tell you along the process!
