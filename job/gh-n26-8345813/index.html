О opportunity

As a newly established function, the Business Risk team sits in the 1.5 line of defense at N26, bridging the Finance organization and the Risk and Compliance functions. This is an exciting, high-impact opportunity to help shape the function from the ground up; building frameworks, establishing best practices, and ensuring the Finance department retains full compliance while maintaining efficient processes.

Working alongside the Business Risk Manager, you will drive operational excellence, strategic process design, and rigorous risk management across the department. Your core focus includes steering ongoing audit coordination and facilitating the prompt remediation of audit findings. In addition, you will help standardize documentation, streamline workflows, and steer cross-functional initiatives, ranging from IT onboarding to New Product Approval (NPP) tracking, in close collaboration with Risk, IT, and broader business stakeholders.

The ideal candidate is highly organized, reliable, and energized by the prospect of building structures in an evolving environment. A foundational understanding of the banking sector, finance operations, and core risk management principles will set you up for success.

In this role, you will

  • Assist the Finance Business Risk team in coordinating ongoing audits, and coordinating the resolution of audit findings or self identified issues
  • Assist the Finance Business Risk team in coordinating regular reviews and quality management of key documentation
  • Analyze data to provide consistent and on demand risk related metrics to Business Risk management and wider Finance organisation
  • Assist in creating executive summaries and overviews for internal and external stakeholders
  • Assist in research regarding changes in relevant regulations and their impact on Finance teams and Bank as a whole
  • Provide general support in project management for IT, NPP, and other initiatives within Finance

What you need to be successful

Background

  • 1-2 years of professional experience in a coordination or support role, ideally within the banking, financial services, or fintech sector
  • Bachelor's degree in Economics, Business Administration, Law, Finance, or a related field
  • Prior exposure to financial processes, operational risk management principles, or internal controls
  • Familiarity with banking regulatory frameworks (e.g., MaRisk, DORA, BAIT) is an advantage

Skills

  • Proven ability to coordinate cross-functional initiatives, track deliverables, manage stakeholder expectations, and stay on schedule
  • Strong skill in synthesizing data from diverse sources to track risk metrics and generate actionable operational reports
  • Clear and structured communication: Excellent written and verbal English communication skills, with the ability to distill complex topics into clear executive summaries, presentations, and process documentation
  • Proficiency in Google Workspace, Microsoft Excel, and modern collaboration or project management platforms (e.g., Jira, Confluence, Asana)
  • Critical thinking, ability to learn fast and interpret regulations, identify control gaps, and map efficient operational workflows
  • Professional proficiency in German is a strong asset
