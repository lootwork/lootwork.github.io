Binance is a leading global blockchain ecosystem behind the world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume and registered users. We are trusted by 300+ million people in 100+ countries for our industry-leading security, user fund transparency, trading engine speed, deep liquidity, and an unmatched portfolio of digital-asset products. Binance offerings range from trading and finance to education, research, payments, institutional services, and more. Our mission is to increase the freedom of money and financial access for people around the world.

Обязанности

  • Support the rollout and implementation of 2C AI product features;
  • Translate product requirements from stakeholders into clear execution plans and documentation;
  • Work closely with engineering, design, marketing, and operations teams to support delivery;
  • Track progress, manage timelines, and help resolve execution issues;
  • Monitor feature performance and collect user feedback for future improvements;
  • Support pilot launches, global rollouts, multilingual expansion, and ongoing optimization;
  • Help improve user experience by identifying issues and sharing suggestions with stakeholders.

Требования

  • 3–4 years of experience in project management, product operations, or a related role;
  • Experience supporting consumer-facing AI products is preferred;
  • Strong execution skills and ability to manage cross-functional projects;
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced, high-pressure environment;
  • Able to handle launch pressure and user feedback professionally;
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills;
  • Growth mindset, high EQ, and strong ownership.

Why Binance

  • Tackle challenging projects in a fast-paced and innovative enviornment with autonomy
  • Thrive in a results-driven workplace with opportunities for career growth and continuous learning
  • Competitive salary and company benefits
  • Remote-first, work-from-home arrangement (may vary depending on the nature of the role and team)
