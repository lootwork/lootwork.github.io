О команде

Data Engineering owns Veriff's analytical data platforms: a Data Warehouse (AWS Redshift, dbt, Airflow, Cube) and a Data Lake (S3, Glue, Athena) running across EU and US regions. We serve analysts, data scientists, ML, and product teams. We are a small, senior team based mostly in Tallinn. Because Veriff handles identity data, our job is to make data easy to use while keeping it secure, compliant, and cost-efficient.

The opportunity

We are moving towards a governed lakehouse that teams can use self-service. You will join our Data Engineering team and work closely with senior engineers. Your initial focus will be governance across both platforms: access control, handling of personal data, data lifecycle and deletion, and platform cost. You will own projects end-to-end, with support and mentoring from the team.

What you'll achieve in your first 3 months

  • Learn how Veriff works and how data flows from our product into the Data Lake and Data Warehouse
  • Get comfortable with our architecture and tooling, and ship your first changes to production
  • Start your first project in data governance or cost optimization

What you'll achieve by 6 months

  • Deliver your first project end-to-end
  • Help keep Data Warehouse costs flat or lower through query and compute tuning
  • Become a go-to person for data governance across the Data Lake and Warehouse

What you'll bring — essential

  • Experience building and running production data pipelines
  • Strong SQL and Python
  • Hands-on experience with a cloud data platform, ideally AWS (S3, Glue/Spark, Athena/Trino, Redshift)
  • Experience working with sensitive or personal data, and a security-first mindset
  • Ownership: You take a project from idea to production and communicate clearly along the way

Будет плюсом

  • Redshift, Lake Formation, or lakehouse / open table formats (Iceberg, Delta, Hudi)
  • dbt and Apache Airflow
  • GDPR data deletion, data lifecycle, or metadata/catalog tools
