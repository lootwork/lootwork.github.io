Job Description

We are looking for a Cybersecurity Analyst to join our team! In this role, you'll analyze security incidents, respond to requests, and support Security Engineers in their daily work. You'll collaborate closely with the Cybersecurity team, IT, DevOps, and other departments to ensure robust security practices across the organization.

You'll contribute to improving our overall security posture, strengthening incident response processes, and working with key stakeholders. You'll also have the opportunity to provide input on our security systems, manage various aspects of their operations, and oversee external access to company resources.

If you're ready to take on new challenges in cybersecurity within the game development industry, we encourage you to apply!

Daily Responsibilities

  • Handle and remediate information security incidents.
  • Manage requests submitted to the Cybersecurity team through a ticketing system.
  • Update and maintain internal documentation of processes and procedures.
  • Recommend technical and organizational solutions to enhance the security of information processed in our environments.
  • Improve cybersecurity processes and adapt them to the game development environment.
  • Collaborate with teams responsible for developing and implementing new processes.
  • Coordinate activities with external partners.

Требования

  • At least 3 years of experience in cybersecurity or IT infrastructure.
  • Very good analytical and technical skills.
  • Practical knowledge of:
  • Application security standards and regulations.
  • EDR/XDR/MDR tools.
  • Incident response.
  • Google Workspace security.
  • PAM or similar secure access management solutions.
  • SIEM/ELK and other methods of analyzing security logs and events.
  • Endpoint security across Linux, Windows, and macOS.
  • Very good command of English, both written and spoken.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience working in a SOC, CSIRT, or CERT teams.
  • Certifications confirming cybersecurity competencies.
  • Familiarity with game development tools.
  • Experience working in an Agile environment.
