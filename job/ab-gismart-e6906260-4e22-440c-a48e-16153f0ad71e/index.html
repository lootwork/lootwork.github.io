Обязанности

- Engage with key stakeholders (product managers, marketers) to identify the product needs and shape the analytics strategy.

- Prioritize tasks based on business value and potential impact, balancing urgent requests with long-term strategic initiatives.

- Build and maintain dashboards, reports, and key metrics to support data-driven decision-making.

- Design, run, and analyze A/B tests to validate hypotheses, measure the impact of product changes, and drive continuous improvement.

- Collect requirements and maintain the event logging template to ensure accurate and comprehensive tracking of user interactions and key product events.

- Ensure data quality, uncover insights, and provide actionable recommendations to improve the product and marketing performance.

Требования

- 2+ years of experience in a similar analytics role.

- Advanced SQL proficiency for querying and analyzing large datasets.

- Strong Python skills for data manipulation and analysis (pandas, data visualization, statistics).

- Solid understanding of statistics, including experiment design, statistical tests, correlation analysis, and segmentation.

- Hands-on experience with BI tools such as Tableau or Power BI.

- Effective communication to present analysis results clearly and compellingly.

Would be a plus

- Experience in full-cycle product analytics — owning both product and marketing analytics, independently prioritizing analytical tasks, and driving data-informed decisions.

- Experience with subscription monetization products — understanding key metrics, user retention, churn analysis, and revenue optimization strategies.

- Basic knowledge of Data Science — familiarity with machine learning concepts, predictive modeling, or clustering techniques.

Employee Benefits

- Remote-First Culture: Our team is diverse and extensive, just like our product portfolio. We provide a flexible working schedule and let you work anywhere in the world, either remotely or in one of our corporate hubs.

- Сoworking Compensation: We provide 100% compensation for coworking spaces in Ukraine (except Kyiv, where we have a cozy, autonomous hub) and 50% compensation for coworking spaces worldwide to ensure everyone has a comfortable and inspiring workspace.

- Free Office Lunches: We want you to stay fueled and focused, so we provide delicious free lunches for everyone working from our hubs. It’s one less thing for you to worry about during a productive workday.

- Public Holidays Policy: Gismart observes public holidays based on the Ukrainian or Polish holiday calendars. Employees working in other locations are entitled to 12 public holidays per year.

- 100% Sick Leave Compensation

- Mental and Physical Health: To help you stay happy and healthy, Gismart will partially cover your medical insurance, sports activities, and therapy costs.

- Personal Equipment Policy: Of course, we’ll provide you with all the equipment you need or cover the maintenance cost for your own tools.

- Personal Learning and Development: We love to see you learn and grow, so Gismart will cover 70% of your professional development courses. We’ll also pay for 100% of corporate English courses.

- Time-Off Policy: Every employee gets 18 working days of paid vacation and 3 personal days per year, with an extra day added for each year at Gismart.

- Corporate Events: We are remote-first, but we love meeting up in person. Whenever possible, we organize corporate events and team-building activities across all Gismart hubs.
