Staff Software Engineer, Android

Location: Remote USA

Кто мы

Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy.

Are you bready* for a change?

Toast currently serves hundreds of multi-unit brands, including Caribou Coffee, Choice Hotels, Craveworthy Brands, WaBa Grill, Nothing Bundt Cakes, Papa Gino's & D'Angelo, Costa Vida Fresh Mexican Grill, and The Human Bean, among others. We are looking for a Staff Software Engineer to join our enterprise drive-thru team and help us build the best-in-class drive-thru solutions that enable significant future growth. You will get to work across the entire Toast platform, including our scalable backend, customer-facing web apps, and everything else in between. As a member of the Drive-Thru Enterprise team, you will help ensure our products are built to meet the evolving needs of these customers. You can read more on our Toast for Enterprise Page.

О this roll* (Responsibilities)

  • Define the long-term technical strategy and architecture for the Drive-Thru domain, balancing immediate delivery with long-term maintainability.
  • Standardize Android best practices across the organization, influencing how multiple teams build and deploy mobile features.
  • Architect the interface between mobile clients and complex backend services to ensure offline-resiliency in high-volume drive-thru environments.
  • Design, build, deploy, and maintain highly resilient and scalable features across Toast’s enterprise management products.
  • Identify high-impact opportunities within the Drive-Thru ecosystem and partner with Product and UX leadership to define the roadmap.
  • Bridge the gap between Android and backend systems to ensure a seamless, end-to-end enterprise experience.
  • Guide the growth & development of peer engineers with mentoring and coaching.
  • Foster a culture of engineering excellence by driving innovation and continuously improving engineering practices.

Do you have the right ingredients*? (Requirements)

  • 8+ years of experience developing and maintaining Android applications
  • Experience with distributed systems, enterprise messaging systems, and/or microservices
  • Proficient with modern API standards and technologies (e.g., REST, GraphQL)
  • Proven track record of leading teams through major architectural transformations and delivering high-impact, scalable solutions in multi-product environments.
  • Excellent communication skills, with the ability to articulate complex technical concepts to diverse audiences
  • Passionate about driving innovation and continuously improving engineering practices
  • Proven ability to influence non-technical stakeholders and align technical debt reduction with business value
  • Bachelor’s degree or higher in computer science

What we use to make our dish (Our Tech Stack)

  • A core of Java & Kotlin based backend services.
  • React SPAs leveraging Tailwind, GraphQL, and Storybook.
  • Android app built in Java & Kotlin.
  • PostgreSQL and DynamoDB as core persistence.
  • Pulsar, Camel, and RabbitMQ for message-based integrations.
  • And many more technologies that help us build a top-class platform.
