THE WORK

Join Ripple Labs Inc. in London, UK as a Product Operations Analyst — Customer Activations This role is uniquely positioned to transform customer experiences by seamlessly integrating them into our innovative products. You'll be at the forefront of a global financial revolution, working cross-functionally to ensure flawless activations and world-class customer support.

Что предстоит делать

  • Working cross-functionally with your counterparts in a global business
  • Own and coordinate end-to-end activations across multiple Ripple products, coordinating with Treasury, Compliance, Product Ops, CPS, CPE, and Engineering teams to get the job done.
  • Detect and bring up blockers to ensure smooth progress and resolution.
  • Guide institutional customers through setup steps with clear, professional communications.
  • Investigate and resolve edge case scenarios, ensuring strict adherence to data accuracy.
  • Report weekly activation status to collaborators with verified information.
  • Manage dormant account processes and ensure human review before deactivation.
  • Scale activation functions to new products and drive systemic fixes for continuous improvement.

WHAT YOU'LL BRING

  • 4-5 years of proven experience in client onboarding/activation, payment operations, or customer operations within fintech, payments, banking, or digital assets.
  • A 'self-starter' mentality with outstanding coordination skills for multi-team, multi-step processes.
  • Strong investigative and problem-solving abilities to trace money and data across systems.
  • High attention to detail, documentation in the field, and comfort in a compliance-sensitive environment.
  • Excellent written communication skills with institutional clients and internal collaborators.
  • Proficiency with CRM (Salesforce) and ticketing (Jira) tools.
