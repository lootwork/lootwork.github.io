О роли

At Plum, we’re looking for engineers who care deeply about technology, customer impact, and building things that scale.

We’re looking for a Staff AI Engineer to work across our AI Platform and Wealth Advisor teams, the teams behind the intelligence that powers Plum. The AI Platform team builds the foundations (model orchestration, retrieval, evaluation and guardrails) that every AI experience at Plum runs on, while the Wealth Advisor team uses those foundations to deliver trusted, personalised financial guidance to millions of customers. Together, they tackle some of Plum’s most important and technically interesting challenges: making AI-driven financial advice safe, accurate and accessible to everyone.

You’ll help shape the technical direction of AI at Plum, connecting LLM-powered products, backend systems, and financial infrastructure to create a platform that’s reliable, scalable, and built for rapid innovation.

Что предстоит делать

  • Architect and design the core components of our AI platform, from model orchestration to retrieval (RAG), agentic workflows, prompt and context management, so that AI capabilities can be built once and adopted across every Plum team
  • Lead the end-to-end development of LLM-powered customer experiences for the Wealth Advisor, from system design and tool integration through deployment, monitoring and iteration in production
  • Build rigorous evaluation systems including golden datasets, LLM-as-judge grading, regression gates on prompt/model changes and offline-to-online metric correlation, so we can ship AI improvements quickly with confidence
  • Design the guardrails, safety and observability layers (tracing, cost and latency monitoring, content and action controls) that make AI-driven financial guidance trustworthy in a regulated environment
  • Drive the AI Strategic Vision and roadmap, evaluating emerging models, tools and techniques and ensuring our architecture supports high-volume experimentation, rapid iteration and future business needs
  • Promote Continuous Improvement by driving architectural standards, engineering best practices, and operational excellence for AI systems across multiple teams
  • Provide guidance to and mentor Senior Engineers and other team members, raising the bar on AI engineering craft and fostering a culture of technical excellence and career growth
  • Identify root causes of systemic issues, from model quality regressions to architectural bottlenecks, determining short-term mitigations and long-term strategic resolutions
  • Partner closely with Staff Engineers, Product Managers, Data Scientists and Compliance to design AI solutions that unlock new growth opportunities while meeting the standards expected of a regulated financial product
