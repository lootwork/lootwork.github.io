In 1945, elevator operators walked out of New York's office buildings. The city ground to a halt with millions of workers stuck on the street and a hundred million in losses. Automatic elevators had existed since the 1890s. Nobody would ride one without a person at the controls. It took the strike, and the decade after it, building trust car by car, building by building, before people would step into a box and press a button themselves.

At Mercury, we build for the building class: the people who see something that doesn't exist and decide to make it real — with their time, their money, their team. Whether that's a side project or a scaling unicorn, we build the financial stack that gets them there.

We believe the future of financial software is infrastructure that works on your behalf. We’re looking for a product leader to accelerate our AI bets – Command, CLI, MCP – and our API as we build towards a Mercury that is programmable and intelligent. This is a role for a builder who is capable across the stack with hands-on experience building with AI.

*Mercury is a fintech company, not an FDIC-insured bank. Banking services provided through Choice Financial Group and Column N.A., Members FDIC.

As part of the journey, we would expect you to

  • Develop a deep understanding of how Mercury's most technical customers use our product today, and what they're trying to do that they can't yet
  • Lead the product vision for how our AI products – Command, CLI, MCP – transform the way people interact with their finance stack: from asking questions about their business to taking action on their behalf
  • Define Mercury's strategy for connecting to the broader ecosystem of AI tools; build and automate the workflow end to end using Command, CLI, MCP, and the rest of our AI stack
  • Own the roadmap for Mercury's API and drive it from niche, read-heavy usage toward a comprehensive platform that enables customers to build on top of Mercury
  • Partner with engineering to make hard technical tradeoffs about interfaces, API design, data access, and capabilities: you should be able to hold your own in these conversations
  • Work closely with Mercury's growth and partnerships teams to ensure our AI products are a competitive advantage in acquisition and retention
  • Navigate a high-velocity environment where leadership cares deeply about this space and expects you to move fast and think originally

Some things that might make you successful in a role like this:

  • You're genuinely excited about what's happening in AI right now and are actively building with these tools
  • Have strong opinions about AI products and the judgment to recognize fast when one of them is wrong. You're not waiting for someone to tell you what's possible
  • 10+ years of experience building — as a founder or product manager — including meaningful time owning product decisions end-to-end
  • Be dangerous across the stack and able to grapple directly with complex concepts; hands on experience building AI products with AI, and knowledge about RAG, evals, agent harnesses a plus
  • You understand what it means to build for developers and technical users
  • You thrive in a culture where leadership is deeply engaged
  • You can take a nascent product area with real traction and turn it into a coherent strategy with a clear roadmap

Learn more about what we're building

  • Mercury Command: Complete financial work end-to-end with AI. Tell Mercury what to do in natural language; it stages the action and executes with your approval
  • Mercury MCP & CLI: Give your AI agents direct access to verified financial data, or work with your Mercury account straight from the terminal
  • The bank of the future knows your business: Immad on what we're building toward and why now is the moment
