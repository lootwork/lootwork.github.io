Riot engineers bring deep knowledge of specific technical areas but also value the opportunity to work in a variety of broader domains. As Software Engineers, we work with both new and current technology, creating innovative solutions to overcome high-volume obstacles and to deliver player value.

As a Software Engineer on the Infrastructure Team (Protect), you’ll be part of a team that is focused on developing solutions to ensure the secure, scalable operations of Riot's global network backbone and UDP game traffic in collaboration with other infrastructure and game teams. The Infrastructure group at Riot collaborates with our game teams to build the foundation that enables our games to operate globally and securely at scale, ensuring the highest levels of infrastructure performance, availability, application delivery, and quality for our players. You will report directly to the Manager of Software Engineering on the team.

Обязанности

  • Break down and independently execute on work with general direction from team tech leadership
  • Adhere to quality design, coding standards, and general engineering best practices
  • Continually look for efficiency and improvement opportunities, propose solutions, and participate in code reviews
  • Work with team technical leadership to ensure successful implementation
  • Build healthy relationships with other engineers on the team
  • Participate in routine, emergent, and on-call work including analysis focused on uptime, SLAs, and RCAs

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years experience as a software engineer with focus on infrastructure or tooling in a fast-paced, large-scale environment
  • Experience with full software development life cycle
  • Experience in one or more object oriented languages (e.g. Go, C++, Python, Java)
  • Experience with large-scale cloud and/or physical infrastructure ecosystems
  • Understanding of core infrastructure services, distributed systems, microservices, and software at a large scale

Desired Qualifications

  • Experience in fast-paced and matrixed organizations
  • Experience operating and supporting agile development, production services, and high availability
  • Experience developing using cloud infrastructure (e.g., AWS, GCP) using containers and orchestration (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes), managed using infrastructure as code (e.g., Terraform, Ansible, Pulumi)
  • Experience with CI/CD pipelines (e.g., Jenkins, GitHub Actions, Argo CD)
  • Experience working with the Linux kernel - writing software/firmware for hardware environments
  • Strong understanding of networking - HTTP down to the network layer (TCP/IP, routing, DNS, etc.)

For this role, you'll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes your fellow Rioters, who are the customers of your work. Being a dedicated fan of games is not necessary for this position!
