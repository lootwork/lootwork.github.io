О team + role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world’s biggest financial problems. We’re looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn’t a place for complacency, it’s where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We’re a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

As a Senior Software Developer on this high-impact team, you’ll collaborate across engineering, business, operations, and vendor channels to support critical infrastructure. Most product initiatives begin with us, as we enable reliable, scalable access to financial instrument data across Robinhood! Your work will help drive new feature rollouts and product expansions across multiple lines of business!

The WonderFi team including Coinsquare and Bitbuy, is now part of Robinhood and gives the company a direct path into the regulated Canadian crypto market through WonderFi’s licenses, platforms, and existing customer base. As part of the transition, Robinhood is gradually migrating eligible WonderFi customers onto the Robinhood Canada platform, with Bitbuy and Coinsquare accounts moving to read-only mode after migration while customers retain access to past transactions and tax documents for a period of time.

These roles are based in our Toronto, ON, Canada office, with hybrid attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

Что предстоит делать

● Build scalable systems and backend components, balancing stability, performance, and maintainability

● Design, implement, test, and deploy production-ready features with high correctness and low-latency requirements

● Partner with frontend and data teams to refine APIs used by millions of customers

● Mentor junior developers and technically lead complex projects across teams

● Identify and implement improvements for system performance and team workflows

Что вы приносите

● 5+ years of professional software development experience

● 3+ years designing and building large-scale, distributed, low-latency systems

● Proficiency in highly concurrent backend system development and intuitive API design

● Demonstrated ability to deliver long-term impact on systems or team-wide architecture

● Strong judgment and prioritization skills, with the ability to lead initiatives and mentor peers
