Position Overview

We are seeking a detail-oriented, highly organised, and motivated Operations Analyst for a 12-month fixed-term contract based in Manila.

In this role, you will be primarily responsible for day-to-day Ongoing Screening (OGS) alert reviews, including Sanctions, Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs), and Adverse Media screening. This position is ideal for a fresh university graduate looking to launch a career in financial compliance, risk management, or operations in a fast-paced environment. No prior KYC experience is required—full training on internal systems and screening processes will be provided.

Основные обязанности

  • Screening & Alert Review: Perform daily review and dispositioning of Ongoing Screening (OGS) alerts for Sanctions, PEPs, and Adverse Media in accordance with company standard operating procedures (SOPs).
  • Alert Triage & Escalation: Promptly identify true hits and high-risk matches (such as potential sanction target matches) and escalate them to the Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO) in a timely manner.
  • Data Reconciliation & System Support: Assist with manual reconciliation during system migrations and updates (e.g., alert validation and false-positive filtering).
  • Record Keeping: Maintain accurate, concise, and audit-ready review notes and documentation for all cleared or escalated alerts.
  • Team Collaboration: Work closely with the global Compliance Regulatory and Operations teams as well as the Account Management/Business Development teams to ensure daily operational targets and SLA benchmarks are met.

Qualifications & Key Requirements

Education & Experience

  • Education: Bachelor’s Degree in Business, Finance, Criminology, Legal Management, Information Technology, or a related discipline.
  • Experience: Open to fresh graduates or entry-level candidates with 0–2 years of experience in back-office operations, data review, or administration. (Prior KYC/AML experience is a plus, but not mandatory).

Skills & Competencies

  • Attention to Detail: Meticulous approach to reviewing data, identifying inconsistencies, and following strict process guidelines.
  • Analytical Thinking: Ability to evaluate facts logically and exercise sound judgment when assessing match relevance.
  • Communication Skills: Strong written and verbal English communication skills for drafting review notes and internal escalations.
  • Tech-Savvy: Comfortable navigating financial software platforms, web-based tools, and standard office applications (Google Workspace, MS Excel, Word, Slack).
  • Time Management: Able to manage daily alert volume, prioritise urgent tasks, and perform effectively under regular operational deadlines.
  • Fast learner: Able to quickly acquire knowledge and be equally adept to the pace of the Fintech industry
