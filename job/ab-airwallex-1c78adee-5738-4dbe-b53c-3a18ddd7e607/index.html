О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

CUSTOMER SUCCESS MANAGER, SME & GROWTH, CN

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

Existing SME and growth customers in China need proactive support, efficient service journeys and a clear path to getting more value from Airwallex. You'll connect customer insight, service operations and cross-functional delivery to improve their experience and long-term value.

- Own the day-to-day success of Airwallex’s existing SME and growth customers in China, building strong relationships and helping them get more value from the platform.

- Build and operate customer self-service platforms and automated support tools, improving internal and external service workflows.

- Use self-service tools, automation and efficient processes to proactively support existing customers, respond quickly to enquiries, and improve service efficiency, customer satisfaction and product experience.

- Partner with Strategy, Product and Engineering to identify customer pain points and translate them into product improvements.

- Work with Marketing, Sales and Account Management to re-engage dormant customers, identify high-potential opportunities and increase customer value.

- Partner with Operations, Risk and Compliance to identify and manage potential business risks.

You'll work from our Shenzhen office five days a week.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- Bachelor's degree or above, with fluency in both Chinese and English.

- At least two years' experience in customer service, customer experience or customer success.

- Experience combining frontline customer operations with the design and optimisation of service platforms and tools.

- Strong data analysis and decision-making skills.

- A track record of delivering results and improving customer outcomes.

- Experience collaborating across teams to deliver complex projects.

Preferred

- Experience in a B2B or enterprise-services environment.

- Experience at a leading cross-border or global company.

- Experience building or operating customer self-service platforms, automated support tools or scalable service workflows.

- Experience working closely with Product, Engineering, Marketing, Sales, Operations, Risk or Compliance teams.

YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF

- You're comfortable owning the roadmap yourself.

- You own the outcome and you don't wait for permission to fix what's broken.

- You connect dots across functions and hold the big picture without losing grip on the details.

- You simplify complexity rather than hiding behind it.

- You say what you mean. You're direct, but you lead with respect.

- You know when to align and when to just decide. Collaboration isn't a way to avoid accountability.

- You value in-person collaboration and are happy being in the office five days a week.

LEARN MORE ABOUT YOUR TEAM

SME & Growth at Airwallex is about more than closing deals. We help small and mid-market businesses solve real financial challenges across borders: getting paid globally, managing multi-currency accounts, paying suppliers, and running spend across markets from a single platform. You're not selling a feature. You're helping founders and finance leaders replace fragmented financial operations with one global system that actually scales.

It's a function for sellers who like to build, not follow a script: curious, resourceful, and comfortable figuring things out in markets that are still wide open. You get real autonomy to create value, build relationships, and move opportunities forward.

If you want to sell a serious product, solve real problems for ambitious businesses, and build your career fast, this is a place to do it.
