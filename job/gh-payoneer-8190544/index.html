О Payoneer

Founded in 2005, Payoneer is the global financial platform that removes friction from doing business across borders, with a mission to connect the world’s underserved businesses to a rising global economy. We’re a community with over 2,500 colleagues all over the world, working to serve customers, and partners in over 190 countries and territories.

By taking the complexity out of the financial workflows–including everything from global payments and compliance to multi-currency and workforce management, to providing working capital and business intelligence–we give businesses the tools they need to work efficiently worldwide and grow with confidence.

Digital Marketing Intern

Role summary - As a Digital Marketing Intern, you will help craft high impact digital experiences, optimize our website journeys, and power data-driven marketing operations that directly contribute to Payoneer’s growth.

Shanghai

On-site

Part-time/ Internship

As a Digital Marketing Intern, you’ll contribute to shaping how users discover, understand, and engage with Payoneer—whether through website content, optimized user journeys, or automated marketing campaigns. You will gain hands-on exposure to end-to-end digital execution, from content creation to conversion funnel optimization, and play an important part in turning insights into measurable business impact.

What you’ll do -

  • Be the Voice of Our Users: Go beyond testing—become an advocate for an exceptional experience. You'll refine website journeys and forms, transforming casual clicks into delighted customers and directly boosting our conversion metrics.
  • Shape Our Digital Story: Take the lead in online content development, especially through GenAI‑powered content creation and localization workflows. You’ll generate fresh, high‑quality website content that reflects Payoneer’s global brand voice while tailoring narratives for China audiences. Your work will help us better communicate our value, inspire trust, and turn visitors into loyal followers.
  • Power Our Marketing Engine: Get hands-on with the tools that drive real growth. You'll help execute automated marketing campaigns (via WeCom, email, SMS) and see firsthand how your work nurtures leads and ignites action.

Who you are -

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Marketing, International Economics & Trade, or a related field.
  • Adequate capability in industry/market research and analysis.
  • Proficient in using AI tools.

What you will gain -

  • Hands-on experience in digital marketing execution and website optimization.
  • Exposure to how websites drive lead generation, conversion, and business growth.
  • Practical understanding of user behavior, conversion funnels, and marketing-oriented web design.
  • A solid foundation for future roles in Digital Marketing, Growth, or Marketing Operations.
