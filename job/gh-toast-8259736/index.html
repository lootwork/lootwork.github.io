Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy. We're building a world-class Customer Marketing team that creates meaningful connections with the restaurant and retail operators we serve.

We are seeking a Senior Manager, Lifecycle Marketing to build, run and optimize the lifecycle programs that drive customer engagement, adoption, retention, expansion and cross-sell growth. Reporting to the Director, Lifecycle Marketing, this leader will turn lifecycle strategy into live, automated customer journeys and own their day-to-day performance.

The ideal candidate is a hands-on lifecycle marketer with strong marketing automation skills and a track record of launching personalized, behavior-based journeys that move business metrics. They are comfortable building in Marketo, analyzing results; they are eager to apply AI, automation and predictive signals to make lifecycle programs more efficient, more relevant and more effective at driving cross-sell.

A day in the life (Responsibilities)

Lifecycle Journey Management

  • Build and manage lifecycle programs across conversion, onboarding, activation, adoption, retention, renewal support and cross-sell, within the strategy set by the Director.
  • Design customer journeys that align messaging, offers, channels and timing to customer needs and business priorities.
  • Translate key lifecycle moments and customer signals into triggers, segments and journey logic.
  • Launch segmented, behavior-based programs that improve engagement, product usage, retention and revenue growth.
  • Work with Product Marketing, Customer Success, Sales, Revenue Operations and Analytics to plan, launch and measure programs.

Cross-Sell Programs

  • Run lifecycle-driven cross-sell programs end to end, including audience selection, journey build, campaign execution, testing and performance optimization.
  • Build cross-sell audiences and journeys using customer behavior, product usage, firmographic data, lifecycle stage and propensity signals.
  • Coordinate with Sales and Customer Success on cross-sell handoff points, enablement materials and follow-up processes.
  • Execute integrated campaigns that support both self-serve and sales-assisted expansion motions.
  • Track and report cross-sell impact on pipeline, bookings, conversion, attach rate, engagement and incremental revenue.

AI-Enabled Lifecycle Marketing

  • Implement AI-powered workflows, automation and personalization across lifecycle programs.
  • Pilot and scale AI use cases in campaign build, segmentation, content creation, journey orchestration and testing.
  • Apply predictive analytics and customer signals to personalize messaging, channel selection, product recommendations and cross-sell offers.
  • Work with Global Analytics, Product and Marketing Operations to put propensity scores, next-best-action logic and automated insights into live journeys.

Optimization

  • Maintain lifecycle dashboards and reporting tied to engagement, adoption, retention, expansion and revenue.
  • Run the test-and-learn roadmap across journey design, messaging, segmentation, channel mix, timing, personalization and offers.
  • Analyze campaign and journey performance and act on opportunities to optimize and scale.
  • Share lifecycle insights and recommendations with marketing, product, sales and customer success teams.

What you’ll need to thrive (Requirements)

  • 10+ years of experience in lifecycle marketing, customer marketing, growth marketing, CRM marketing, marketing automation or related roles.
  • 2+ years of experience managing people or leading lifecycle, CRM or marketing automation programs.
  • Hands-on experience running cross-sell, expansion, upsell or customer growth programs with measurable results.
  • Strong technical skills in marketing automation, journey orchestration, segmentation, campaign operations and personalization.
  • Advanced, hands-on proficiency in Marketo or a similar platform.
  • Experience building complex automated journeys using behavioral triggers, customer attributes, lifecycle stages, scoring, suppression logic and dynamic content.
  • Solid understanding of customer lifecycle management, retention, adoption, engagement, renewal support and expansion motions.
  • Experience using data to build audiences, measure performance and optimize campaigns.
  • Familiarity with CRM, CDP, product analytics, web analytics and data warehouse environments such as Salesforce, Snowflake, BigQuery or similar.
  • Experience applying AI, predictive analytics, personalization or experimentation in marketing programs.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience in B2B SaaS, technology, or fintech
  • Experience supporting product-led, sales-led or hybrid go-to-market motions.
  • Knowledge of customer segmentation, propensity modeling, lead scoring, product usage signals, customer health scoring and revenue attribution.
  • Experience with AI-enabled marketing tools, generative AI workflows, decisioning engines or predictive recommendation models.
  • Working knowledge of SQL, no-code data tools or modern data stack concepts.
  • Strong understanding of privacy, consent, deliverability and compliance requirements for customer communications.
  • Bachelor's degree in marketing, business, analytics or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
