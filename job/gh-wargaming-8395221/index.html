О вакансии

Wargaming is looking for a talented 3D Vehicle Artist to join the World of Tanks Modern Armor team. In this role you will create high-quality armored vehicle assets for the console version of the game, collaborating with a passionate team of artists and designers to deliver visually outstanding content for millions of players.

Reports to

Lead 3D Vehicle Artist

Что предстоит делать

  • Create and process console-ready tank models based on PC source assets, blueprints, and references
  • Set up and organize Maya and 3ds Max scene files following studio pipeline conventions
  • Perform rigging, skinning, and export of vehicle assets for use in engine
  • Import vehicle models into the game engine using proprietary tools (Tank Importer, Forge), set up game object templates, and configure material and texture assignments
  • Process and prepare PBR texture sets using Substance Suite and Photoshop
  • Configure game object templates and material assignments in proprietary tools
  • Maintain asset and XML data in version control and collaborate with the Lead Vehicle Designer on configuration
  • Perform in-engine QA to validate visual quality, performance, and correctness before delivery

Кого мы ищем

  • 3+ years of experience as a 3D Artist in game production
  • Solid hard-surface modeling and texturing skills
  • High proficiency in Maya, 3ds Max, Substance Painter, and Photoshop
  • Good understanding of the full game-ready asset creation pipeline (High Poly, Low Poly, UV, Bake, PBR Texturing, Engine Implementation)
  • Experience with version control systems (Perforce, SVN, or similar)
  • Strong attention to detail and ability to follow strict naming and file conventions

Что поможет выделиться

  • Experience with vehicle or military hard-surface assets
  • Familiarity with rigging and skinning workflows in Maya
  • Knowledge of tank anatomy and historical vehicle reference
  • Prior experience on vehicle-based titles such as World of Tanks or similar

Формат работы

  • Hybrid (3 days of work from the office)
  • This role isn't eligible for relocation & immigration support
