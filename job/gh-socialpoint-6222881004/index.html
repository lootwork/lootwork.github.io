Position Overview

Join our Central Analytics team to build AI-powered tools and scalable data solutions that simplify analysts' daily work and drive game growth. Work alongside experienced Analytics Engineers, partnering closely with game and marketing analysts to turn automation opportunities into impactful solutions.

We're looking for someone who learns quickly, embraces new technologies, and thrives in a collaborative, fast-paced environment.

What will be your main tasks working with us?

  • Design, build, and maintain end-to-end AI pipelines and agentic workflows that automate analytical tasks and streamline analysts' daily work.
  • Develop scalable AI-powered tools and applications, integrating LLMs with internal data sources, APIs, and analytics platforms.
  • Build reusable Python frameworks and services, ensuring AI solutions are reliable, maintainable, and scalable across game and marketing analytics teams.
  • Design and maintain data pipelines and cross-game data marts, using Python and SQL to deliver clean, reliable, and accessible data.
  • Partner closely with analysts to identify automation opportunities, develop practical solutions, and drive the adoption of AI tools and engineering best practices.

Why should you join us?

  • We are committed to continuous improvement, so you'll never stop learning.
  • We are a collaborative team that learns from each other every day.
  • We run large-scale production pipelines spanning ETLs, analytical tools, and AI/ML models.
  • We focus on delivering innovative solutions to real-world problems.

Требования

  • A bachelor's degree in a quantitative field (e.g., Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, or Physics).
  • Advanced SQL skills, including experience with CTEs, query optimization, and large-scale data warehouses.
  • Advanced experience building and maintaining production data pipelines using tools such as Airflow, Docker, S3, and CI/CD workflows.
  • Hands-on experience integrating LLMs or developing generative AI tools and workflows.
  • Advanced Python skills for developing production-ready data and analytics solutions.
  • A collaborative team player who builds trust, communicates openly, and contributes to shared success.
  • A fast learner who is proactive and comfortable working with ambiguity.
  • Detail-oriented, with a strong commitment to accuracy and high-quality outcomes.
  • Pragmatic, with the ability to balance short-term delivery needs with long-term technical quality.

Nice-to-haves

  • Experience in the mobile gaming industry.
  • Experience in marketing analytics.
  • Experience creating and maintaining dashboards in Tableau or a similar BI tool.
