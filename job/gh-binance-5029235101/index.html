О роли

You will support a risk-based monitoring programme across in-scope processes and controls, working alongside the Enterprise-wide Risk Assessment team and Compliance business units to assess control criticality, determine monitoring frequency, and support delivery of the annual monitoring plan. You will also support periodic reviews to assess whether controls are operating as designed, and help document outcomes for feedback.

Что предстоит делать

  • Run the monitoring plan — execute the risk-based monitoring plan across all in-scope processes and controls.
  • QA the frontline work — test retail and corporate transaction monitoring alerts and KYC files.
  • Test control effectiveness — assess whether controls are properly designed and operating effectively, including input into control criticality assessments and the monitoring frequency matrix.
  • Document everything — record results, observations, findings and improvement areas accurately, and independently manage and resolve disputed QA findings.
  • Report and track — prepare reports and MI, and track follow-up actions, remediation items and outstanding issues through to closure.
  • Coordinate across teams — work with Transaction Monitoring, SAR Investigations and KYC teams (local and offshore), and keep process consistent across jurisdictions.
  • Flag risk and upskill — identify unusual activity and AML flags, support risk assessments, periodic/trigger reviews and EDD, and take part in AML/CFT training.

Кого мы ищем

  • 1–3 years of experience in risk, controls, compliance, audit or a related area.
  • Basic understanding of internal controls and control effectiveness concepts.
  • Strong attention to detail, documentation and organisational skills.
  • Good communication and stakeholder coordination skills.
  • Proficiency in Excel and PowerPoint.
  • Experience in financial services or a regulated environment is an advantage.
