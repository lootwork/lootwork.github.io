THE WORK

As a Staff Software Developer, you will own the platform delivering financial clarity to businesses from SMEs to Fortune 500 enterprises. You will build full-stack, cloud-native solutions on Azure. You will link modern technology with leading financial organizations and lead the engineers around you. You will set technical direction, champion continuous improvement, and facilitate no/low-code bank connector configurations that connect our platform to the world's leading banks. If you enjoy working with intricate decentralized architectures and thrive in a you-build-it-you-run-it culture, this opportunity is for you. You will help shape how the world manages its finances.

Что предстоит делать

  • Work alongside architects and product managers to develop, estimate, and deliver new features spanning the full stack — front-end, server-side, and database tiers. Invent solutions that matter at scale.
  • Harness the full power of Azure to architect cloud-native solutions that are scalable, resilient, and built to perform under real-world demand
  • Drive continuous improvement across our software applications, owning delivery pipelines and feature flags to ensure seamless, confident integration and deployment
  • Champion quality at every layer — leading rigorous unit, integration, performance, and security testing to ship software that is genuinely production-ready
  • Own reliability across the full environment lifecycle, using observability tools and logging to swiftly diagnose and resolve issues in development, test, and production
  • Be a force multiplier for the team — staying ahead of tech trends, engaging with the broader tech community, mentoring fellow engineers, and leading technical design and code reviews
  • Set the standard relentlessly — finding opportunities to sharpen team operations, processes, methodologies, and technology choices in pursuit of engineering excellence
  • Ensure every piece of work meets the highest quality, operational, and architectural standards, directly contributing to the organization's success
  • Play a unique and strategic role in facilitating the configuration of no/low-code bank connectors with top-tier financial institutions, bridging modern platforms with the world's leading banks
  • Embrace full ownership through the team's YBIYRI (you-build-it-you-run-it) model, including active participation in the on-call pager rotation

WHAT YOU'LL BRING

  • Someone who takes initiative to guide others and collaborate across teams, writes stellar code, solves complex problems under pressure, and brings creative energy to every challenge
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent professional experience, with at least 8 years of hands-on application development experience
  • An expert in application development best practices, cloud architecture, and deployment, with deep experience building robust solutions in complex distributed systems
  • Deep expertise in ASP.NET/Core and C#, with expert-level Microsoft SQL Server database development and daily use of Visual Studio
  • Extensive hands-on experience with Azure Services, including designing and implementing CI/CD pipelines within Azure DevOps
  • Highly accomplished in building web applications using Angular with TypeScript, with strong command of TDD and BDD practices and familiarity with JavaScript frameworks
  • A proven mentor and multiplier — growing fellow engineers, influencing across teams, and championing continuous improvement as an agent of change
  • Highly analytical, data-driven, and deeply committed to quality, with a keen understanding of customer needs and the vision to advance both technology and product
  • A self-starter and fast learner who communicates effectively with technical and non-technical partners, thrives in cross-functional global agile teams, and delivers with minimal supervision
  • Advanced skills in fixing technical issues, solid understanding of Windows operating system principles, and experience in the financial industry is a plus
