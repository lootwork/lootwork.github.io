О команде

The SMB & Mid-Market Inbound Sales team is a key growth engine behind Airwallex’s scale. We work with inbound SMB and mid-market businesses, turning demand into customers through a fast, strategic sales process.

We’re looking for an experienced, high-energy sales professional who loves the phone, moves fast and knows how to turn conversations into customers.

This is a high-velocity, phone-first acquisition role. You’ll own inbound opportunities from first conversation through to activation, working with SME and mid-market businesses and managing a large pipeline at different stages of the sales journey.

You’ll bring a strong foundation in new-business sales, with experience progressing and closing deals with mid-market customers.

Что предстоит делать

- Own and convert inbound opportunities from qualification through to activation

- Run strong discovery conversations to uncover customer needs and commercial opportunities

- Progress and close deals with SME and mid-market businesses

- Pick up the phone and create momentum. Calling is a core part of how you sell

- Manage a large pipeline of customers across different stages of the sales cycle

- Handle objections, create urgency and confidently move customers towards a decision

- Use data to understand your pipeline, performance and where to focus

Кто вы

- You understand sales: You have proven new-business sales experience and a track record of closing deals.

- You love the phone: You enjoy speaking with customers, creating opportunities and moving deals forward through conversation.

- You're structured and organised: You can manage a high volume of customers across different stages, identify what needs your attention and prioritise the actions that will have the greatest impact.

- You're data-driven: You understand your numbers, use data to identify gaps and opportunities, and use those insights to decide where to focus.

- You're resilient: You stay positive and focused through challenges, adapt quickly and maintain momentum in a fast-paced sales environment.

- You learn quickly and independently: You pick up new products, processes and concepts quickly and can apply them without constant direction.

- You take ownership: You take responsibility for your results and proactively work out what needs to happen next.

- You move with pace: You're comfortable in a fast-moving environment, act with urgency and can manage multiple priorities at once.

- You're team-oriented: You want to win, but you also want to see the people around you win. You share what works, support the team and contribute to a high-performing culture.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

- 2+ years of proven success in a closing role, with consistent performance against quota

- Experience selling technology, payments or financial services

- Strong understanding of how businesses operate and financial services, with the ability to demonstrate this in customer conversations

- Demonstrated ability to operate as a self-starter in a fast-paced, high-growth environment
