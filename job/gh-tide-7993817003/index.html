О TIDE

At Tide, we help SMEs save time and money in the running of their businesses by not only offering business accounts and related banking services, but also a comprehensive set of highly usable and connected administrative solutions, from invoicing to accounting.

Tide is transforming the small business banking market and now supports over 2 million members globally across the UK, India, Germany and France.

Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love.

О роли

The Banking Payments team is responsible for driving engagement, usage and commercial performance across Tide’s banking payment products and member payment journeys.

We are looking for an exceptional Lead Country Commercial Manager to drive growth and commercial performance across Banking Payments in the UK. This role sits at the heart of Tide’s Payments organisation and will work cross-functionally with Product, Finance, BI, CRM, Operations and Member Success teams to accelerate payment usage, optimise member journeys and deliver strategic commercial initiatives.

This role requires a highly commercial and analytical operator who can translate data and member insight into actionable strategies, campaigns and product opportunities.

SOME OF THE THINGS YOU’LL BE DOING

Revenue Ownership & Forecasting

Partner closely with Finance and key stakeholders to set, track and refine commercial projections for Banking Payments, ensuring clear accountability against revenue and engagement targets.

Analytics-Driven Insight

Work closely with BI and analytics teams to understand member payment behaviours, identify opportunities and measure performance across key initiatives.

Product & Commercial Strategy

Partner with Product teams to shape payment propositions, improve member journeys and influence roadmap prioritisation using commercial insight and member feedback.

Launch Strategy & GTM

Lead the commercialisation and go-to-market strategy for Banking Payment features and initiatives, ensuring coordinated execution across CRM, in-app, servicing and operational channels.

Lifecycle & Engagement Strategy

Work closely with CRM and marketing teams to develop “always on” payment engagement strategies that drive activation, usage and retention.

Stakeholder Management

Collaborate across Product, Finance, Operations, Engineering and Senior Leadership teams to align on priorities, performance and strategic direction.

Commercial & Competitor Intelligence

Monitor payment trends, competitor propositions and market developments to identify opportunities that strengthen Tide’s Banking Payments offering.

Member Obsession

Become an expert in member payment behaviours and needs, ensuring the member voice is central to commercial and product decision-making.

Кого мы ищем

  • Strong commercial acumen with experience driving measurable business impact
  • Proven experience managing commercial performance within payments, fintech or banking
  • Strong analytical mindset with ability to translate insights into execution plans
  • Experience delivering GTM and lifecycle engagement strategies
  • Excellent stakeholder management and cross-functional collaboration skills
  • Strong project management capabilities within fast-paced environments
  • Self-starter mentality with high ownership and accountability
  • Experience working closely with Product, CRM, Finance and BI teams
  • Ability to operate strategically while remaining execution-focused

Expected Outcomes of the Role

Within 12 months, this role would

  • Establish dedicated commercial ownership for Banking Payments
  • Improve payment engagement and usage metrics
  • Accelerate commercial experimentation and CRM activity
  • Improve visibility and forecasting of Banking Payments performance
  • Support successful launches of Banking Payment initiatives
  • Strengthen alignment across Product, CRM, Finance and Operations
  • Increase focus on member payment journeys and behaviours
  • Create additional leadership bandwidth across Cards and Payments
