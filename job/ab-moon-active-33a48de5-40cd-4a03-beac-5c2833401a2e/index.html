Moon Active is one of the world’s fastest-growing mobile game companies, providing entertainment to millions of active users across the universe. The company is headquartered in the heart of Tel Aviv.

We’re looking for a motivated and ambitious Technical Artist to join our rapidly growing Art & Design team. As a Technical Artist, you’ll work closely with our Creative, Product and Development teams to shape Moon Active games.

Обязанности

- Integrate creative assets (art and visual effects) into the game.

- Investigate and implement new techniques and practices.

- Optimize workflow processes using tools, scripts and AI so each teammate can focus on their core contribution.

- Contribute to technical solutions and pipeline design for the production and delivery of art assets and help leverage our technology.

- Be responsible for creating a smooth pipeline for designs and assets incorporating significant amounts of 2D elements.

Требования

- Portfolio - Must

- Knowledge in 2D animation, Unity and Photoshop.

- Experience in Unity or ability to undergo Unity training.

- Proven track record of both artistic and technical creativity with a passion for solving problems.

Advantage

- Knowledge of AI tool development and scripting in Unity.
