Join us to build the future of corporate treasury and the infrastructure that powers the Internet of Value.

THE WORK

As a Senior Software Developer, you will build and own the platform powering financial clarity for businesses from SMEs to Fortune 500 enterprises. You will own full-stack feature delivery across front-end, server-side, and database tiers — leveraging Azure to craft scalable, resilient cloud solutions at the core of a fast-growing SaaS product. You will shape technical direction and mentor fellow engineers within a you-build-it-you-run-it team that holds itself to the highest standards. If you are energized by complex distributed systems and want to make a measurable impact on how the world manages its finances, this is the opportunity for you.

Что предстоит делать

  • Partner with architects and product managers to design, estimate, and deliver cutting-edge features across the full stack — front-end, server-side, and database tiers
  • Leverage Azure services to build powerful, scalable, and resilient cloud-native solutions
  • Improve software applications and own delivery pipelines and feature flags, ensuring seamless integration and deployment
  • Drive rigorous unit, integration, and non-functional testing — including performance and security — to guarantee production-ready software
  • Use observability tools and logging to diagnose and resolve issues across development, test, and production environments
  • Stay ahead of tech trends, engage with the broader tech community, mentor fellow engineers, and lead technical design and code reviews
  • Continuously improve team operations, processes, methodologies, and technology choices, holding every practice to a high standard
  • Ensure all work meets quality, operational, and architectural standards, contributing to the organization's broader success
  • Embrace the team's YBIYRI (you-build-it-you-run-it) model, including active participation in the on-call pager rotation

WHAT YOU'LL BRING

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or relevant equivalent experience, with at least 5 years of application development experience
  • Experienced and proficient in application development guidelines, cloud architecture, and deployment
  • Deep expertise in ASP.NET/Core and C#, with advanced knowledge of Microsoft SQL Server
  • Hands-on experience with Azure Services, Azure DevOps, and designing and implementing CI/CD pipelines
  • Extensive experience building web applications using modern JavaScript frameworks, with a strong grasp of TDD and BDD practices
  • Proven track record to influence and mentor developers within an agile team environment
  • Skilled in reviewing and estimating user stories as part of an agile delivery process
  • Strong troubleshooting skills with hands-on experience in production application support
  • Solid understanding of Windows operating system principles and administration
  • Experience in the financial industry is a plus
