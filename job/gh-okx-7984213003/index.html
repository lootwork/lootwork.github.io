Кто мы

At OKX, we believe that the future will be reshaped by crypto, and ultimately contribute to every individual's freedom.

OKX is a leading crypto exchange, and the developer of OKX Wallet, giving millions access to crypto trading and decentralized crypto applications (dApps). OKX is also a trusted brand by hundreds of large institutions seeking access to crypto markets. We are safe and reliable, backed by our Proof of Reserves.

Across our multiple offices globally, we are united by our core principles: We Before Me, Do the Right Thing, and Get Things Done. These shared values drive our culture, shape our processes, and foster a friendly, rewarding, and diverse environment for every OK-er.

OKX is part of OKG, a group that brings the value of Blockchain to users around the world, through our leading products OKX, OKX Wallet, OKLink and more.

What We Look For In You

  • Bachelor’s degree or above, with 2–5 years of experience in business development, strategic partnerships, channel development, or related commercial roles within brokerage, fintech, wealth management, trading, or financial services.
  • Experience working with international or multi-market financial platforms is preferred.
  • Strong understanding of brokerage, investment, or trading ecosystems, including partnership structures, distribution channels, customer acquisition models, and commercial incentive mechanisms.
  • Existing relationships with brokerage firms, financial platforms, financial content creators, investor communities, or other relevant financial ecosystem partners are a plus.
  • Familiarity with, or strong interest in, digital assets and crypto, with the ability to understand the evolving relationship between traditional finance and emerging digital-asset products.
  • Strong commercial judgment, negotiation, stakeholder management, and analytical skills, with a proven ability to translate partnerships into measurable business outcomes.
  • Results-oriented, highly self-motivated, and comfortable operating in a fast-paced, international, and evolving business environment.
  • Strong cross-cultural communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively with stakeholders across different regions and functions.
  • 本科及以上学历，具备2–5年券商、金融科技、财富管理、交易平台或其他金融服务领域的商务拓展、战略合作、渠道拓展或相关商业经验。
  • 有国际化金融平台或跨区域市场工作经验者优先。
  • 熟悉券商、投资或交易生态，包括渠道体系、客户获取模式、合作机制及商业激励模式。
  • 在券商、金融平台、财经KOL/内容创作者、投资者社区或其他相关金融生态中拥有合作资源者优先。
  • 对数字资产及加密行业具备一定理解或强烈兴趣，能够理解传统金融与新兴数字资产产品之间不断演进的用户及商业需求。
  • 具备较强的商业判断、商务谈判、利益相关方管理及数据分析能力，能够将合作机会转化为可量化的业务成果。
  • 结果导向、自驱力强，能够适应快速变化、国际化的业务环境。
  • 具备良好的跨文化沟通及跨区域协作能力。
