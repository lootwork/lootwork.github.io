Что нам нужно

Visual Concepts Entertainment, the developers of the critically acclaimed NBA 2K video game franchise, is seeking an experienced temporary Animator! This position will report directly to the Art Director.

Что предстоит делать

  • Polishing multiple gameplay move sets from Mocap data using Maya
  • Address feedback in a timely manner
  • Work closely with the lead and senior animators to modify assets meeting game-ready requirements
  • Efficiently meet project schedules
  • Cleaning up any solving issues and improving the data itself by adding handposes and IK tokens for various features
  • Editing any prop data captured and ensuring character interactions with it look at the necessary level of quality expected by the supervisor
  • Responsible for delivering a list of daily animations edited for the supervisor to review and make revisions accordingly

Who Will Be A Good Fit

  • Bachelors degree and 2-4 years of relevant experience
  • A detailed knowledge of Maya
  • Proficiency animating realistic body motion with a good eye at observing and animating the subtleties of player movement
  • Ability to quickly adapt to iterative processes and technical innovation
  • Efficiently handle their workload on their own initiative
  • Comfortable with schedules and fast turnaround times
  • Ability to assist other areas of animation outside of gameplay (while still working with body Mocap data).
  • Ability to maintain motivation, has excellent work ethics, and works well with others
  • Experience using MotionBuilder for Mocap data
  • Working knowledge of Mel or Python scripting is a plus
  • Previous Mocap experience is helpful
  • Knowledge and love of basketball
  • Love of video games, not just ours!
