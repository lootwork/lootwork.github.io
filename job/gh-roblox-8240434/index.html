Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device. We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

As a Product Designer on Marketing Systems (BizApps), you'll be an individual contributor dedicated to optimizing and expanding Roblox's web presence. Marketing Systems maintains and improves our company websites, builds new ones, and keeps Roblox's web surfaces aligned with our design systems and brand identity. You will focus on UX architecture, design language, and visual consistency across platforms, working with multiple teams at Roblox to deliver a cohesive experience across the company's online presence.

You have experience in web design and understand how design systems and branding contribute to a seamless digital experience. You develop long-term design solutions that improve the user experience on websites and mobile platforms.

Вам предстоит

  • Audit, research, strategize, and lead design for our web platforms and company websites
  • Partner with the design systems and brand teams to ensure visual consistency across Roblox's web presence
  • Create intuitive, functional, and visually polished designs for web and mobile experiences
  • Build a visual design language that aligns with Roblox's brand and web strategies
  • Work with PMs, engineers, and other team members to bring designs to life, ensuring alignment across functions
  • Conduct UX research, including interviews, surveys, and usability tests, to guide and validate design decisions
  • Deliver wireframes, prototypes, design specs, and high-fidelity visuals that communicate design intent
  • Establish a scalable design system for web and mobile products that maintains consistency and quality
  • Evolve the design system as new features and user needs emerge

You have

  • 4+ years of experience in web design, UX, product design, or a related field
  • Expertise in web and mobile design, with a strong understanding of design systems, branding, and visual language
  • Experience working with cross-functional teams, including PMs, engineers, and brand designers
  • A deep understanding of the full design process, from ideation to execution, including design sprints, UX research, and iteration based on user feedback and metrics
  • A portfolio that demonstrates beautiful, functional, user-friendly web and mobile experiences, with a focus on consistency, usability, and flow
  • Strong communication skills, with the ability to advocate for design decisions and collaborate with stakeholders
  • Proficiency in design tools (e.g., Figma, Adobe Creative Suite) and familiarity with design systems and component libraries
  • A proactive attitude and a passion for staying current with design trends and best practices
