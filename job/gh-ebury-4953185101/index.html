Graduate Software Engineer

Location: Madrid (Hybrid: 4 days office / 1 day WFH)

Ready to ship real code on day one? Join Ebury as a Graduate Software Engineer and leapfrog the standard junior learning curve. We don’t relegate new grads to bug-fixing or background tasks—you’ll be building mission-critical features, solving complex financial tech challenges, and pushing production code within your first week.

If you want an accelerated path to technical leadership in a fast-growing global FinTech, this is your launchpad.

Why Join Ebury?

  • Ship Code Fast: Push production code in Week 1 and see your work impact global business operations immediately.
  • Fast-Track Career Growth: A structured progression plan with clear milestones to move you into mid-level and leadership roles quickly.
  • Dedicated Mentorship: Direct pairing with senior engineers who care about your technical growth and professional development.
  • Modern Tech & AI-First Mindset: We encourage you to use cutting-edge AI tools to augment your workflow, speed up learning, and write smarter code.
  • Competitive Package: Attractive salary, performance bonuses, and a comprehensive health/wellness benefits package.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build & Scale: Design, test, and deploy scalable software solutions powering our international financial platform.
  • End-to-End Ownership: Take features from dynamic design sessions and architectural reviews through to deployment and maintenance.
  • Collaborate: Work alongside cross-functional engineering, product, and design teams to solve high-stakes challenges.
  • Level Up Engineering Standards: Participate in code reviews, drive CI/CD pipelines, and contribute to standard-setting technical discussions.

Кого мы ищем

  • Education: Degree (BSc or MSc) in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related technical field.
  • Core Fundamentals: Solid grasp of algorithms, data structures, software design, and complexity analysis.
  • Coding Skills: Proficiency in at least one modern language (e.g., Python, Java, Go).
  • Mindset: Strong analytical problem-solving skills, accountability for the code you write, and a collaborative team attitude.

Дополнительный плюс

  • Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure) or containers (Docker, Kubernetes).
  • Software engineering internship experience or a cool side project/open-source portfolio.

О нас

Ebury delivers sophisticated, integrated solutions — business accounts, hedging, and financing — on a single platform with a seamless workflow. Our success is built on a simple premise and singular purpose: To help businesses operate and scale globally.

Since its founding in 2009, Ebury has always been a fast-growing leader in fintech. Today, we bring together 2000+ Eburians across nearly 70 cities and we’re always looking to add to our team.

At the heart of our offering is a proprietary platform, purpose-built to help businesses seamlessly streamline and manage global cash flow. We focus on continuous product evolution and innovation to build the infrastructure for borderless growth and help our clients scale at every stage.

The opportunities at Ebury are as diverse as our people, ranging from business development to engineering roles across our tech pillars.

We believe in inclusion. We stand against discrimination in all forms and are against the intolerance of differences that makes us a modern and successful organisation. At Ebury, you can be whoever you want to be and still feel a sense of belonging no matter your story.

Important Security Notice for Job Applicants

Ebury only accepts applications via official Ebury channels. We will only contact you from an @ebury.com domain. Always check the emails you receive from us, and note that we will never request payment for any part of the recruitment process. Stay safe and report suspicious activity.

All hiring decisions, including application reviews, shortlisting, and final selections are evaluated and conducted by human talent acquisition professionals and hiring managers.
