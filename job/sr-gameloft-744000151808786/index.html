Job Description

Main challenge

We are looking for a Java Developer to join our Business Intelligence department, Sales team, in our Bucharest studio.

You will work on developing new applications while also maintaining, supporting and improving complex intranet applications used by the team. The role combines development with opportunities to work on reporting, automation, data-related optimizations and application improvements.

What it means on a daily basis

  • Develop new applications using advanced technologies
  • Maintain, support and improve complex intranet applications
  • Develop and maintain custom reports
  • Work on imports from external and internal tools
  • Automate different stages of operations
  • Contribute to database fine-tuning and optimizations
  • Work with bug tracking applications

What success will look like

  • Ability to work on both new development and existing applications
  • Structured approach to problem-solving
  • Communicate effectively and integrate quickly into the team
  • Work independently while also being a team player
  • Initiative and openness to new challenges

Who you would be working with

  • Business Intelligence / Sales team
  • Developers and other technical stakeholders
  • Internal teams using the applications and reporting solutions

Your recruitment journey

  • HR interview
  • Technical Test
  • Final Interview

Требования

What you need to succeed

  • Good Java skills
  • Good MySQL skills
  • Fluent in English, both written and spoken
  • AWS tech, Spring Boot and Looker represent big pluses

Кто вы

  • Good communicator
  • Analytical person
  • Ability to work effectively as part of a team
  • Self-motivated and resourceful mindset

Additional Information

What's in it for you

  • 2 months/year full remote work from anywhere
  • MyBenefits platform - 45 RON/day
  • Extra days off for seniority, birthday and company anniversary
  • Short Fridays, so you can start your weekend at 4PM
  • Medical subscription with Regina Maria

Life at Gameloft

  • Hybrid work model (3 days in the office / 2 days from home)
  • Free coffee, fruits, and relaxation areas
  • Inclusive and international work environment;
  • Supportive team culture and open communication style;
