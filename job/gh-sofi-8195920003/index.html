О роли

As a Credit Reporting Bureau Early Warning System (EWS) Governance Analyst you will be responsible for documenting the specialty credit bureau EWS disputes framework. The role will help onboard the newly established EWS (third party partner) program within the Credit Bureau Reporting (CBR) ecosystem. In addition, you will be responsible for continuously improving EWS credit bureau dispute policies, controls and procedures. Continuous regulatory learning and training is critical to maintain skills and knowledge needed to ensure the program is compliant. This role works with resources across the company - tech, legal, compliance, Fraud, Operations, etc. The ability to understand and document the work within multiple platforms and services to analyze information is critical to the success of this role.

Что предстоит делать

  • Support Credit Bureau leadership govern and document EWS policies, SOPs and processes.
  • Build EWS Governance Playbook
  • Coordinate internal and external exam documents and meeting invites
  • Coordinate EWS intent & change intake processes
  • Coordinate EWS owned issues and milestones
  • Assist in building, managing and executing QA results for EWS specialists following SOPs and intended outcomes
  • Gather and facilitate the communications for EWS Credit Bureau Disputes reports and metrics
  • Governing the investigation process for EWS direct and indirect disputes from EWS according to department procedures and by department deadline.
  • Govern platforms the EWS disputes specialists use to perform an investigation - ex. Accesses, permissions and usage controls
  • Document and deliver evidence of control execution
  • Identify and share process improvements
  • Recognize, escalate and be a part of the solution for breakdowns within EWS bureau processes and data
  • Work independently and as part of a team on ad hoc tasks

What you’ll need

  • Bachelors degree preferred with 2+ years experience in the financial industry or 4+ years related FCRA or other related regulations experience
  • Strong written communication skills, including the ability to document complex research findings clearly
  • Ability to interpret business rules and apply them consistently to cases
  • High attention to detail
  • High level of ethics and integrity
  • Possess strong problem solving and critical thinking skills
  • Be a self-started requiring some direction with the ability to work independently and on a team
  • Proficient in MS Office (Proficiency in Excel required)
  • The ability to handle confidential or sensitive information with discretion
