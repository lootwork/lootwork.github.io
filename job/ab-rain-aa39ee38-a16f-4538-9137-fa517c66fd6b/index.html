О команде

Security Engineering makes sure Rain is built right. We run our own AI-driven red team against our code, contracts, and cloud, gate pull requests before they merge, and set the baselines for how every system is configured. This role owns blockchain security end to end: the smart contracts on EVM chains, Solana, and Stellar that hold and move customer funds, the keys and multisigs that control them, and the backend services that act on what happens on chain.

You'll be Rain's blockchain security expert. The calls are yours: what's real, how bad it is, and what has to be fixed before a contract ships or an upgrade goes out. You'll work closely with the leads of our Solana, EVM, and blockchain infrastructure teams, hold the line when it matters, and make the case so they agree, not just comply.

Что предстоит делать

- Review smart contract changes and upgrades before they ship, across Solidity (EVM), Rust (Solana), and Soroban (Stellar). Find the bug that matters, and often write the fix yourself

- Run our contract audits end to end: scope them, work with outside auditors, triage findings from audits, our AI red team, and our bug bounty, set severity, and approve the fixes

- Own admin key and role security: multisig and Safe setups, signing ceremonies, role separation, timelocks, and who can upgrade, pause, or move what

- Strengthen treasury and fund-flow protections so no single key, signer, or bad transaction can drain money

- Find the blind spots in our on-chain monitoring: work out what we can't see today, and tell our blockchain detection and response team which detections to build and why

- Review how our backend reads and reacts to chain events (indexers, event handlers, signing services), so off-chain code can't be tricked by on-chain input

- Own the upgrade process so every upgrade is done securely: who deploys, from where, and with what checks. Make sure no contract, chain, or customer program gets left behind on an old version, and prove that what's live matches what was reviewed

- Build the tools that make this repeatable: fuzzing and invariant tests, and simulations of upgrades and admin actions before anyone signs them

- Lead the technical response when something goes wrong on chain, and own the plan for where blockchain security goes next

You are likely to succeed if you have

- 4+ years in smart contract security, protocol security, or security-minded blockchain engineering

- Deep, hands-on EVM and Solidity, plus real working knowledge of at least one non-EVM chain (Solana or Stellar/Soroban)

- You've found real bugs in contracts that hold real money, through audits, contests, bounties, or your own team's code. You know a scary-sounding finding from a real one

- You've designed or run key management for production contracts: multisigs and Safe, KMS or HSM signing, timelocks, and role-based access

- Fluent with Foundry, fuzzing and invariant testing, transaction simulation, and reading chain state directly

- You can read TypeScript backend code well enough to follow how it talks to the chain

- You've owned security decisions. You can tell a senior engineer their design needs to change and keep a good working relationship

- You'd rather ship a check than write a document, and you use AI tools heavily without trusting them blindly

Bonus attributes

- Stablecoin, payments, or card issuing background

- Public audit reports, audit contest placings, or bug bounty payouts

- Hands-on response to a live on-chain exploit or key compromise

- Formal verification or contract static analysis (Certora, Halmos, Slither, or similar)

- Experience building security scanners or LLM-based review tools
