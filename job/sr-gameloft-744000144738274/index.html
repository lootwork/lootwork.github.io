Job Description

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON

Main challenge

This role is for those eager to learn the fundamentals of game design and the full game development process. They'll build essential skills and knowledge, then apply them to support real design tasks on live projects, all while leveling up their careers.

What it means on a daily basis

  • Study game design fundamentals and analyze game aspects, including design concepts, controls, and fun factors, across genres
  • Learn the game-making process on a live project and support the main GD on minor tasks.
  • Contribute to design discussions and decisions.
  • Write and maintain game design documents (GDDs) for small features, simple gameplay, and improvements to existing features and content (missions, quests, levels), guided by the project game designer.
  • Learn to find and fix design-related bugs using in-house tools.
  • Playtest builds with the team.

Who you would be working with

  • Studio Game Designer Lead, Senior Game Designers, and Game Designer Project Leads
  • Producers, Artists, and Programmers
  • Cross-functional game development teams

Your recruitment journey

  • (1) Screening call
  • (2) Test
  • (3) Interview
  • (4) Offer

Qualifications

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED

  • Currently pursuing a degree in a related field such as Game Design/Development, Information Technology, Multimedia, Art, Computer Applications, or Product Design
  • Strong interest in gameplay design, game features, and game balancing
  • Good knowledge of video games, game genres, player types, and what makes games enjoyable
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills in English
  • Fast learner, adaptable, and a strong team player who works well under pressure
  • Familiarity with Gameloft games

WHO YOU ARE

  • Passionate about video games and industry trends
  • Creative thinker with strong problem-solving abilities
  • Adaptable and open to feedback
  • Strong storytelling and communication skills
  • Organized, detail-oriented, and quality-focused
  • Able to work independently and collaboratively under pressure
  • Self-motivated with a strong sense of ownership and commitment
  • Willing to learn, share knowledge, and support team growth
