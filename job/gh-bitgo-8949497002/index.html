О команде

The Growth team at BitGo is responsible for expanding the user experience and feature set at BitGo. There are two main applications that we’re developing at the moment: The first mobile app is hyper optimized to help institutions manage workflows, such as moving billions on-chain, at scale without compromising security.

The second mobile app is focused on making crypto accessible to everyone and broadly applicable in day to day life. This requires unwavering focus on the end to end experience (from onboarding to first on-chain activity) and understanding of crypto’s edge over other mediums (e.g. payments).

As Senior Product Manager, you will lead a team of engineers and designers to launch our mobile apps, grow usage, and make something users can’t stop raving about.

Что предстоит делать

  • Develop retail-first mobile experiences, making buying, selling, staking, lending, and on-chain interactions frictionless.
  • Own and define the end-to-end mobile strategy, ensuring seamless and secure user experiences.
  • Optimize approval workflows for institutions, making transactions fast, intuitive, and hyper-secure.
  • Drive the roadmap and set KPIs for your team and vertical
  • Experiment with different user acquisition strategies to see what works and what doesn’t
  • Work closely with engineering, security, fraud, risk and compliance teams to balance usability with institutional-grade security.
  • Analyze engagement metrics to drive user retention and feature adoption.
  • Partner with Marketing and Growth teams to expand usage and onboard new users.
  • Stay ahead of mobile crypto trends, continuously iterating on the mobile experience.

Обязанности

  • Define, prioritize, and launch high-impact features that enhance user engagement and security.
  • Partner with engineering, design, security, and compliance teams to ship best-in-class mobile experiences.
  • Ensure feature parity across iOS and Android, optimizing for global adoption.
  • Drive onboarding optimizations, improving activation rates for both institutional and retail users.
  • Establish key mobile performance metrics, ensuring BitGo’s apps set the industry standard for usability and security.
  • Act as the voice of the user, ensuring product decisions are aligned with institutional and retail needs.

Кто вы

  • 5+ years of product management experience, preferably in scaling consumer applications to a large number of users.
  • Significant experience in improving growth metrics such as DAUs and Assets under custody.
  • Deep experience in mobile UX/UI, with a strong understanding of secure and frictionless design.
  • Knowledge of crypto wallets, custody, staking, and blockchain transactions is a major plus.
  • Strong analytical mindset—comfortable making data-driven decisions.
  • Proven ability to work cross-functionally, aligning engineering, design, and compliance teams.
  • Ability to execute in a fast-moving, high-growth environment while keeping security at the forefront.
