О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

The Senior Integrated Marketing Manager, MY will connect Malaysia’s commercial priorities with brand, product marketing, growth marketing, and customer acquisition. You’ll lead integrated campaigns from strategy through execution and use performance insights to improve customer and revenue outcomes.

- Own the end-to-end development, execution, and performance analysis of integrated marketing campaigns across Malaysia.

- Develop market-specific marketing strategies by identifying priority customer segments, growth opportunities, and initiatives that accelerate customer acquisition, activation, and revenue growth.

- Lead strategic campaigns across paid, owned, and earned channels, working closely with Growth, Product Marketing, Brand, PR, and the wider Commercial team to deliver integrated, on-brand experiences.

- Define campaign objectives, KPIs, forecasts, and measurement frameworks; analyse performance across the customer journey and turn insights into clear recommendations for optimisation.

- Partner with the global Growth Marketing team to ensure the growth marketing roadmap reflects Malaysia’s strategic projects and priorities.

- Support annual and quarterly planning, OKRs, and key cross-functional forums, representing Marketing when required.

- Prepare and present post-campaign analysis and reporting, including actionable recommendations for continuous improvement.

- Oversee campaign budgets and resource allocation, demonstrating return on investment and identifying opportunities to improve outcomes.

- Localise global marketing strategies for Malaysia, taking market nuances and audience insights into account.

- Use AI creatively as a force-multiplier to improve marketing efficiency and effectiveness.

This is an in-office role — you’ll be in our Kuala Lumpur office five days a week.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- 7+ years’ experience in growth marketing, integrated marketing, campaign management, or a similar function within B2B, fintech, or technology sectors.

- Demonstrable experience leading 360-degree marketing campaigns across performance marketing, brand, events, content, and PR channels.

- Proven ability to use data to drive decisions, including building and interpreting dashboards, analysing trends, and translating insights into clear recommendations.

- Experience reporting on multi-channel marketing performance and return on investment.

- A strategic, creative, and results-oriented approach to delivering high-impact campaigns that drive business outcomes.

- Strong project management and organisational skills, with meticulous attention to detail.

- Experience managing multiple stakeholders, timelines, and priorities in a matrixed environment.

- Excellent communication and influencing skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with internal and external partners.

- A proactive, ownership-oriented approach to problem-solving and execution.

- Bachelor’s degree or equivalent in Marketing, Communications, Business, or a related field.

Preferred

- Experience working in or with high-growth start-ups or technology companies.

- Experience supporting the localisation of global marketing strategies for Malaysia or another Southeast Asian market.

YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF

- You're comfortable owning the roadmap yourself.

- You're not precious about your ideas. You push hard for what you believe in, but you pivot fast when the data says otherwise.

- You connect dots across functions and hold the big picture without losing grip on the details.

- You communicate clearly and directly across cultures, and you're thoughtful about how context shifts meaning across borders.

- You value in-person collaboration and are happy being in the office five days a week.

LEARN MORE ABOUT YOUR TEAM

Marketing at Airwallex is a growth function, not a support one. The brief is ambitious: build the brand to match the platform, well-established in some markets and still emerging in others. This team leads across product marketing, brand, demand generation, and regional GTM: understanding what our platform actually does, then building the stories that make it land with real businesses, not just buyers on paper. They work closely with product, sales, and leadership, so the link between marketing activity and business outcome is direct.

If you want to build a global brand through insight, sharp positioning, and creative that earns attention and trust, this is where marketing has real leverage.
