О Payoneer

Founded in 2005, Payoneer is the global financial platform that removes friction from doing business across borders, with a mission to connect the world’s underserved businesses to a rising global economy. We’re a community with over 2,500 colleagues all over the world, working to serve customers, and partners in over 190 countries and territories.

By taking the complexity out of the financial workflows–including everything from global payments and compliance to multi-currency and workforce management, to providing working capital and business intelligence–we give businesses the tools they need to work efficiently worldwide and grow with confidence.

Program Manager, Stablecoin and PAYO Digital Bank

Location: New York/Washington DC/Miami

Position Summary

Cross-border payments are about to be rebuilt. Help us build the version that wins.

Payoneer is building a stablecoin-powered payments platform and a regulated national

trust bank, leveraging new technology to deliver the next generation of payment infrastructure for global commerce.

This role serves as the operating backbone for the initiative, orchestrating product delivery,

go-to-market execution, trust bank launch, and board-level governance into a single

coherent program. The Program Manager will partner with senior leadership to translate

strategy into milestones, milestones into action, and action into outcomes.

This is a high-visibility role for a versatile program manager who can hold complexity across

commercial, product, and regulatory workstreams. If you want a tidy lane, this is not the

role. If you want to help build something that does not yet exist in U.S. financial services,

sitting next to the executive driving it, keep reading.

Key Responsibilities

Program & Strategy Operations

  • Maintain the integrated plan across product, GTM, regulatory, and bank stand-up workstreams
  • Establish and run the program's governance model — steering committees, working groups, decision logs, risk registers, and reporting cadences
  • Translate strategy into milestones and milestones into weekly action across a cross- functional team
  • Drive disciplined prioritization across competing demands on a small, senior team Product Launch
  • Partner with Product and Engineering leadership to maintain an integrated roadmap for stablecoin and bank capabilities, including wallet functionality, issuance, custody, reserve management, and cross-border payments use cases.
  • Track milestones, dependencies, and launch readiness across discovery, build, pilot, and general availability.
  • Run program rituals that keep product delivery synchronized with commercial commitments and regulatory constraints.Go-to-Market Execution
  • Drive the program plan for stablecoin GTM including segmentation, pricing, packaging, partnership pipeline, sales enablement, and launch sequencing
  • Coordinate across Marketing, Sales, Partnerships, and Product Marketing to ensure messaging, collateral, training, and commercial readiness are in place ahead of each launch
  • Maintain visibility into pipeline, pilots, and early commercial KPIs — surface what is working, what is not, and what needs leadership attention Bank Stand-Up
  • Own the integrated plan for PDB across the path to charter approval and opening
  • Coordinate workstreams spanning executive hiring, governance and policy buildout, technology and operations readiness, risk and compliance program design, and external advisor management
  • Ensure commercial and product plans are sequenced realistically against charter and regulatory dependencies Board & Executive Governance
  • Own the operating cadence and content pipeline for PDB board and committee engagement
  • Manage agendas, pre-reads, and follow-ups; partner with executive leadership to shape narrative and decisions sought
  • Track board commitments and ensure timely, high-quality close-out
  • Produce executive-grade communications for CCO, CEO, board, and partner audiences, translating technical, regulatory, and commercial complexity into clear narratives on progress, risk, and decisions needed

Required Qualifications

  • 7–10 years of experience spanning consulting, project/program management and operational roles inside a company building something new, with at least 3 years managing complex cross-functional programs across product, commercial, and operational workstreams
  • Track record of delivering programs spanning product launches, GTM execution, and operational stand-up in a fast-moving environment
  • Strong working knowledge of program management methodologies (Agile, Waterfall, hybrid) and the judgment to apply the right approach for the work• Excellent written and verbal communication skills — equally comfortable with a board pre-read and a one-page exec update, including experience preparing executive and board-level materials
  • Proficiency with program management and collaboration tooling (Smartsheet, Jira, Confluence, MS Project, or equivalents)
  • Bachelor's degree in business, engineering, finance, or a related field

Будет плюсом

  • Experience in financial services, fintech, payments, or another regulated industry
  • Exposure to digital assets, stablecoins, or cross-border payments
  • Prior experience supporting board or committee operations
  • PMP, PgMP, SAFe, or equivalent certification
  • MBA or advanced degree in a relevant field
  • Experience supporting a new business, product line, or regulated entity stand-up
