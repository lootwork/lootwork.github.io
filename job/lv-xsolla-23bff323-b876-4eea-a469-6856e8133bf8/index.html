О нас

Xsolla is a global commerce company with robust tools and services to help developers solve the inherent challenges of the video game industry. From indie to AAA, companies partner with Xsolla to help them fund, distribute, market, and monetize their games. Grounded in the belief in the future of video games, Xsolla is resolute in the mission to bring opportunities together, and continually make new resources available to creators.

Headquartered and incorporated in Los Angeles, California, Xsolla operates as the merchant of record and has helped over 1,500+ game developers to reach more players and grow their businesses around the world. With more paths to profits and ways to win, developers have all the things needed to enjoy the game.

For more information, visit xsolla.com.

О вас

We are looking for a Senior Full-stack Engineer who is self-motivated, product-minded, and technically rigorous to join our team. Our product is built in three parts: backend services in Node.js and TypeScript, frontends in React and Next.js, and a set of Go applications and services alongside them. You will work across all three, and the balance shifts from quarter to quarter depending on what is in flight.

Strong Go and TypeScript skills are essential, along with experience owning services end to end, from the HTTP layer down to storage, and designing API contracts rather than only implementing them. Working fluently with AI coding agents is part of how this team operates day to day. The ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams (product, design, backend, and QA) and drive technical decisions will be key to your success in this role.

If you enjoy moving between a Go service, a Node API and a React frontend in the same week, and you care about the details that make each of them correct, we would love to hear from you!

Обязанности

  • Building and maintaining Go applications and services: new ones where Go fits better than Node, plus platform services and tooling.
  • Developing backend functionality and services in Node.js and TypeScript.
  • Building frontends, either as custom React applications or as storefronts on the Xsolla Shop Builder platform.
  • Designing and maintaining our content API (contracts, response codes, the OpenAPI spec, access control) and integrating with other Xsolla services.
  • Writing automated tests and the infrastructure behind them.
  • Collaborate closely with product owner, designer, backend dev, and QA; contribute to technical design and code reviews.

Требования и навыки

  • Go in production, 2+ years. Services you have owned end to end, from the HTTP layer down to storage.
  • Node.js backend, 2+ years, TypeScript, with a document database (we use MongoDB).
  • React in production, 2+ years, including the Next.js App Router, and care for the states that usually get skipped: loading, error with retry, empty, mobile breakpoints.
  • AI-assisted development as a daily habit. Working with coding agents on real tasks: giving them enough context to be useful, reviewing what they produce as critically as a colleague's pull request, and knowing which work to hand over and which to write yourself. Be ready to walk us through a recent example.
  • Comfort in an unfamiliar codebase. Picking up a service you did not write and following its conventions rather than importing your own.
  • API design, not just implementation. Meaningful response codes, machine-readable errors, a spec that matches actual behaviour.
  • Correctness under concurrency. Race conditions, idempotency, retries, concurrent writes. In multi-tenant systems, the habit of asking which tenant owns the data on every read and write.
  • Testing as a default, not a phase. Unit, integration and functional tests, kept fast and isolated.
  • Solid engineering fundamentals: coding standards, unit testing, and version control (GitLab).
  • English at B2 or above, including technical documentation, tickets and design docs.

Будет плюсом

  • gRPC and Protobuf beyond a tutorial: service contracts, generated clients, versioning. Some Xsolla platform services we integrate with are gRPC-only.
  • Search engines (Typesense, Elasticsearch, Meilisearch) and keeping them in sync with the primary database.
  • Infrastructure: Kubernetes and GitLab CI (multi-stage pipelines, immutable image tags, deploys by digest), and AWS at the level of configuring content delivery and working out why a file is served the wrong way.
  • Observability and performance work: Datadog, Sentry, Prometheus, profiling, caching, latency budgets and SLOs.
  • Headless content platforms.
  • Gaming or payments, and regulatory work such as GDPR and consent flows.
