Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy.

Toast is looking for a Vice President to lead Product Marketing, Competitive Intelligence, and Market Insights — the team responsible for how Toast understands its market, positions its platform, and equips the business to win against the competition. This leader will own go-to-market strategy and execution for Toast's product portfolio, run a rigorous competitive intelligence function, and drive the market research and segmentation work that informs how Toast builds, prices, and sells.

This is a senior leadership role with high visibility to the executive team and board. The right candidate combines strategic GTM thinking with hands-on execution, has a strong point of view on AI's impact on restaurant technology, and can operate as a trusted advisor to Product, Sales, and Marketing leadership.

A day in the life (Responsibilities)

  • Own product marketing strategy and execution across Toast's platform — positioning, messaging, launch narratives, and sales enablement for major releases (e.g., Toast IQ, agentic AI capabilities)
  • Lead competitive intelligence: build and maintain a rigorous view of competitor strategy, pricing, and product moves; brief executive and VP+ leadership on competitive risk and opportunity
  • Run market insights and segmentation: translate market research into actionable frameworks that inform product, pricing, and GTM decisions
  • Partner closely with Product, Sales, and Enterprise leadership to align GTM strategy with the product roadmap and commercial priorities
  • Build and develop a high-performing team of senior, athlete-model marketers and analysts
  • Represent the voice of the market to executive leadership — shaping how Toast thinks about its position, its competitors, and where the industry is headed
  • Drive narrative and thought leadership for major company moments (fall/spring releases, board and investor materials, industry events)
  • Define and track KPIs for product marketing impact — launch adoption, win rates, and competitive displacement — and report progress to executive leadership
  • Champion AI adoption across the team — bringing AI into the product marketing tech stack and rethinking how the team operates to drive greater speed and efficiency in how it executes

What you'll need to thrive (Requirements)

  • 10-15+ years in product marketing, competitive intelligence, or strategy roles, with 5+ years in senior leadership
  • Track record building and scaling high-performing marketing/insights teams
  • Strong strategic and analytical skills — comfortable moving between board-level narrative and detailed competitive/market analysis
  • Experience partnering directly with Product and Sales leadership on GTM strategy
  • Excellent written and verbal communication; able to influence senior stakeholders without formal authority
  • Background in restaurant tech, vertical SaaS, fintech/payments, or B2B platforms a plus
  • Comfortable operating in ambiguity and building structure where none exists

How Toast Uses AI in its Hiring Process

Throughout the hiring process, our goal is to get to know you.
