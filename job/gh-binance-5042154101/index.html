О роли

We're looking for a hands-on Campaign Manager to own integrated marketing campaigns for our SEA market — from strategy and brief through to execution, measurement and optimisation. You'll sit at the intersection of acquisition, CRM and creative, driving user growth at every stage of the funnel: acquisition, KYC, first deposit, first trade, and trading volume. If you like moving fast, testing ideas and seeing your work show up directly in the numbers, this is the role.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own integrated campaigns end to end — strategy, brief, execution and performance measurement — across assigned SEA markets.
  • Drive user acquisition, KYC conversion, first-time deposits, first-time trades and trading volume growth against clear targets.
  • Build CRM and lifecycle marketing strategies that lift user activation, engagement, retention and reactivation.
  • Manage CRM communications across email, push notifications, in-app messages and automated user journeys, with targeted segments built on user behaviour and trading activity.
  • Plan and coordinate creative assets for paid campaigns across Google Ads, Meta Ads and other digital platforms, partnering with designers and content teams on visuals and messaging.
  • Run continuous experimentation — A/B testing on campaign messaging, CRM communications and ad creatives — to improve engagement and conversion.
  • Manage campaign budgets, promotional incentives, timelines and performance reporting, partnering with regional marketing, creative, product, data and local teams to surface growth opportunities.

Кого мы ищем

  • Hands-on experience executing integrated marketing campaigns end to end, from strategy and brief through to measurement.
  • Demonstrated CRM and lifecycle marketing experience — segmentation, automated journeys, and channels spanning email, push and in-app messaging.
  • Practical experience planning and running paid campaigns on Google Ads, Meta Ads and comparable digital platforms.
  • Comfort with A/B testing and performance data: able to read results, form a view, and act on it.
  • Working understanding of cryptocurrency terminology and market trends.
  • Fluency in English is required to be able to coordinate with overseas partners and stakeholders. Additional languages would be an advantage.
  • Excellent organisational skills, with the ability to multitask and manage time well across competing priorities.

Будет плюсом

  • Prior experience in crypto, fintech or another fast-scaling environment.
  • Experience managing promotional budgets and incentive programmes.
