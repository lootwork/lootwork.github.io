О Betterment

Betterment is a leading, technology-driven financial services company that offers investing, savings and retirement solutions for retail investors and investment advisors as well as financial wellness solutions, including a 401(k) for small and medium-sized businesses. Our team is passionate about our mission, to empower people to build wealth with confidence and ease. We’re headquartered in NYC and offer hybrid NY-based positions (four days/ week in-office, with no required office days during the summer and winter holidays).

О роли

You're a natural systems thinker who gets energized by turning complex, ambiguous requirements into something clean and usable. In this role, you'll sit at the intersection of regulatory compliance and operational execution — taking dense regulatory frameworks and translating them into workflows, procedures, and documentation that the rest of the business can actually act on.

This isn't just a "keep the lights on" compliance role. You'll be building and maintaining the structural backbone of Betterment's compliance programs — designing how processes work, not just following them.

This role is based out of our NYC office, with in-office attendance required Monday through Thursday. Salary range is disclosed per NYC requirements; actual compensation varies based on experience and performance.

  • New York City: $90,000 - $105,000

A Day in the Life

  • Translate SEC and FINRA regulatory requirements into clear, actionable compliance workflows and process documentation
  • Design and maintain compliance program infrastructure — building and managing workflows in tools like Jira and Claude to track, route, and execute compliance processes at scale
  • Build and maintain procedures, process maps, and control documentation that are accurate, readable, and built to scale
  • Identify where compliance processes break down or create friction, and propose structured solutions
  • Partner with teams across the business to implement compliance requirements in ways that are practical and well-understood
  • Support day-to-day program management across a range of compliance workstreams, keeping processes moving

Кого мы ищем

  • 1–2 years of experience in compliance, operations, program management, or a related field
  • A systems-oriented mindset — you naturally want to understand how things work end-to-end, and you notice when something's missing or misaligned
  • Strong documentation instincts: you write clearly, organize logically, and leave processes better than you found them
  • Comfort with ambiguity — you can take a messy regulatory requirement or undefined workflow and find the through-line
  • Attention to detail without losing sight of the bigger picture
  • Ability to work across business units and communicate compliance requirements without the jargon
  • Curiosity about how technical systems and process flows are built, and a willingness to roll up your sleeves on the structural elements of program design
