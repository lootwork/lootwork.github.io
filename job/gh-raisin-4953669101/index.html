Raisin is the world’s leading platform for savings and investment products. Founded in 2012, the FinTech connects consumers with banks in the EU, the UK and the US. This gives consumers better interest rates and banks a diversified form of refinancing. Our vision is to offer savings and investments without barriers and thus open up the global 160 trillion euro market.

Raisin currently employs more than 800 people from over 75 countries worldwide. Today, the platform holds over 80 billion euros in assets from more than one million investors which have accrued over 5 billion euros in returns.

Team

The B2C Channels EU team drives customer growth across our European markets by creating engaging customer experiences, optimising our sales funnels, and building long-term loyalty to our brand.

Ваши обязанности

  • Support in the development of new product categories and features.
  • Support in the conception and execution of performance marketing activities tailored to the needs of current and future customers.
  • Help manage ongoing customer service operations and optimize the onboarding journey for speed and ease.
  • Support in assessing and improving the user experience for customers and increasing conversion.
  • Collaborate with product, operations, and customer service teams to improve operations and internal processes.
  • Track and analyze the execution of our growth strategy, general market developments, and competition in the Dutch market.
  • Help draw conclusions and implement improvements.
  • Gathering and synthesising key insights such as competitive analysis, as well as preparing reporting.
  • Support the daily operations of the Dutch Raisin business.
  • Help maintain and improve the website.
