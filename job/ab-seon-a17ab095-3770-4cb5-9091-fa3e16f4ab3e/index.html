Job purpose

We're looking for a Senior Motion Designer who is a strong craft practitioner and an even stronger thinker, someone who can lead creative direction, pressure-test briefs, and build systems, not just produce assets. Motion is the core craft requirement.

Обязанности

- Motion design & production

- Lead motion design across brand touchpoints, including social, digital campaigns, video, and event assets

- Develop concepts and storyboards before production starts

- Own video editing for digital and content marketing from brief to final delivery

- Set the quality bar for motion across the team and raise it over time

- Apply a consistent brand lens to everything, and push back when a brief would compromise it

- Creative direction & stakeholder collaboration

- Take briefs from demand generation, content, and field marketing and the rest of the business and interrogate them before executing

- Facilitate alignment between creative intent and campaign goals across functions

- Communicate clearly (in English) in internal alignments and cross-functional reviews

- Act as a creative partner to stakeholders, not just an executor of requests

- Systems & infrastructure thinking

- Build tools, templates, and reusable assets that let the broader team self-serve

- Contribute to the evolution of the brand's motion principles and visual language

- Scout trends and emerging tools as part of the role, bringing perspective back to the team

- Team & cross-functional collaboration

- Operate as a senior practitioner the wider design team can look to for direction

- Pull the work and the team toward higher-order thinking through quality and approach

- Support broader brand and creative needs in collaboration with the design team

Требования

- Professional proficiency in English

- Strong portfolio demonstrating high-quality motion and brand design

- Advanced proficiency in After Effects and Premiere Pro

- Strong supporting skills in Figma, Adobe Illustrator, and Photoshop

- A demonstrable feel for brand: your portfolio shows motion as a brand expression

- Demonstrable ability to develop a concept, not just execute one

- Several years of senior-level motion design experience in a fast-paced environment, agency or in-house

- Experience interrogating briefs and proposing creative direction rather than executing on request

- Strong communication skills and a collaborative, detail-oriented approach

- Ability to manage multiple projects and adapt to varying levels of complexity

- Nice to have: Familiarity with 3D, illustration, or other adjacent visual disciplines
