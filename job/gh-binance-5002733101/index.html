О роли

We are looking for highly motivated interns to join our engineering team and work on **high-performance, low-latency systems** that operate under demanding throughput and latency requirements.

You will work closely with experienced engineers on performance-critical infrastructure, including high-throughput messaging, concurrent processing pipelines, networking, memory optimization, and latency-sensitive systems.

This internship is designed for candidates with strong fundamentals and exceptional learning potential. **Prior experience in low-latency or trading systems is not required.** We care more about your ability to understand complex systems, reason from first principles, and systematically make software faster and more efficient.

Обязанности

  • Design and optimize high-throughput, low-latency systems, including messaging infrastructure, concurrent processing pipelines, and networking.
  • Identify and resolve performance bottlenecks across CPU, memory, operating systems, networking, and concurrency.
  • Optimize memory allocation, data structures, serialization, and multithreaded processing to improve system efficiency.
  • Explore advanced optimization techniques, including lock-free architectures, CPU affinity, and memory management.
  • Build benchmarking and profiling tools to measure throughput, CPU efficiency, and latency (p50/p99/p999).
  • Collaborate with experienced engineers to investigate complex system performance issues and deliver measurable improvements.

Требования

  • Currently pursuing or recently completed a Master's, MPhil, or PhD in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Computer Systems, or a related technical field.
  • Strong foundation in data structures, algorithms, operating systems, networking, memory management, and concurrency.
  • Proficiency in at least one programming language, preferably C++, Rust, or Java.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to break complex problems into measurable hypotheses.
  • Interest in understanding how software interacts with hardware and how system performance can be improved.
  • Strong learning agility, curiosity, and the ability to quickly understand unfamiliar systems and technologies.
  • Prior experience in low-latency or trading systems is not required.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with high-performance computing, low-latency systems, multithreaded programming, or distributed systems.
  • Knowledge of lock-free data structures, CPU caches, NUMA, SIMD, memory pools, or CPU affinity.
  • Experience with Linux systems programming, network programming, RDMA, DPDK, io_uring, or eBPF.
  • Familiarity with profiling, benchmarking, and performance analysis tools, such as perf or FlameGraph.
  • Exposure to compiler/runtime optimization, database or storage internals, or high-performance serialization.
  • Contributions to open-source systems projects or relevant academic research.
  • Strong competitive programming background, such as ICPC, IOI, NOI, CCPC, or Codeforces.
