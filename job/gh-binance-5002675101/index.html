О Binance Accelerator Program

Binance Accelerator Program is a 6-months program designed for Early Career Talent to have an immersive experience in the rapidly expanding Web3 space. You will be given the opportunity to experience life at Binance and understand what goes on behind the scenes of the worlds’ leading blockchain ecosystem. Alongside your job, there will also be a focus on networking and development, which will expand your professional network and build transferable skills to propel you forward in your career. Learn about BAP Program HERE

Who may apply

Current university students and recent graduates

Обязанности

  • Participate in Binance APP testing, ensuring high-quality delivery across Android and iOS platforms.
  • Understand business scenarios and technical design; design, develop, and execute test cases, and organize test case reviews.
  • Execute functional and regression testing, identify and track bugs, and ensure timely fixes and delivery.
  • Collaborate closely with developers and product managers to drive issues to resolution.
  • Leverage AI tools to enhance testing efficiency and coverage.

Требования

  • Undergraduate or postgraduate in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field
  • Solid understanding of software engineering and QA methodologies
  • Familiar with Java and MySQL
  • Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills, with good attention to detail and patience for APP testing
  • Good communication and collaboration skills in a fast-paced environment
  • Fast learner with a strong work ethic
  • Able to commit full-time (5 days/week) for at least 6 months
  • Familiarity with AI is a plus
