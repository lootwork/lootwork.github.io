Кто мы

Tripledot Studios is one of the largest independent mobile games companies in the world.

We are a multi-award-winning organisation, with a global 2,500+ strong team across 12 studios.

Our expanded portfolio includes some of the biggest titles in mobile gaming, collectively reaching top chart positions around the world and engaging over 25 million daily active users.

Tripledot’s guiding principle is that when people love what they do, what they do will be loved by others.

We’re building a company we’re proud of. One filled with driven, incredibly smart and detail-orientated people, who LOVE making games.

Our ambition is to be the most successful games company in the world, and we’re just getting started.

This role is based within our studio, Belka Games

Belka Games is a game development company ranked by Sensor Tower among the top 30 highest-grossing game developers in Europe. Our games have reached more than 110 million players worldwide

О роли

We are looking for a Product Director to lead and strengthen our Midcore Direction. The key goal at the current stage is a successful global launch of our game Train of Hope and its further scaling.

As part of the Belka Games leadership team, you will contribute to the company’s broader product strategy and long-term development.

The role includes responsibility for the product strategy and business performance of Train of Hope, including its revenue, profitability, long-term product value, and investment efficiency. You will also build a strong product team and look for new growth opportunities for the game, from the best solutions on the market to IP collaborations and partnerships.

Key Responsibilities

Part 1 - Product ownership and global launch

  • Define and implement the product strategy and long-term vision for Train of Hope: target audience, positioning, core gameplay pillars, and a 1-3 year development horizon
  • Own the preparation and execution of the global launch: target KPIs and readiness criteria, go/no-go decisions, the launch plan together with Marketing and Development, and post-launch scaling
  • Drive the business performance of the direction, including revenue growth, profitability, long-term product value, and investment efficiency
  • Drive improvements in long-term retention, engagement, payer conversion, ARPPU, monetization, LTV, and other key product and business metrics
  • Drive an AI-first approach to content production: introduce AI tools to automate and optimize the creation of locations, characters, art, text, and localization, reducing production time and cost without compromising quality
  • Grow higher-margin monetization and distribution channels, including direct-to-consumer sales
  • Balance short-term priorities with long-term product investments and allocate team resources accordingly
  • Make decisions on the launch, scaling, iteration, or termination of in-game features and product initiatives
  • Ensure high-quality and timely delivery of product updates together with the Development Director and Producers, and reduce product and technical risks of server-side and social features
  • Build transparent cross-functional decision-making processes and align Product, Game Design, Narrative, Art, Development, Analytics, Marketing, and Finance around shared goals and priorities
  • Partner with the CMO on player acquisition and re-engagement economics and UA scaling decisions for the global launch, taking into account the longer payback of midcore audiences
  • Partner with the Belka Games leadership team on the company’s product strategy and long-term development
  • Hire and develop strong product leaders and key specialists with midcore expertise (meta and combat design, competitive economy, narrative), and build an autonomous and scalable team

Part 2 - Market research and new growth opportunities

  • Continuously research the midcore market, audience, and competitors: successful mechanics, LiveOps and monetization practices, genre and setting trends, and changes in user acquisition costs
  • Identify successful solutions on the market and adapt them for Train of Hope, managing a pipeline of in-game hypotheses and experiments
  • Source and evaluate IP collaborations for Train of Hope: selecting IPs and partners, building the business case, integrating collaborations into content and LiveOps, and measuring their impact on acquisition, retention, and revenue
  • Evaluate other growth opportunities for the game: new markets and localizations, new platforms, partnerships and cross-promotion, new game modes and content formats
  • Apply a data-informed and hypothesis-driven approach to prioritizing and validating new opportunities
  • Define success criteria in advance and evaluate initiatives against relevant midcore product, market, and business benchmarks
  • Develop business cases for major initiatives and partnerships, including the opportunity, expected impact, required resources, timelines, and key risks
  • Systematize learnings from experiments, collaborations, and post-release analysis of content and features, scale effective practices, and eliminate ineffective approaches
