О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

The Financial Platform team builds the financial infrastructure that powers Airwallex’s global expansion. You'll develop backend services that manage real-time balances, account for every transaction, and support accurate global settlements at scale.

- Build and evolve the Client Wallet, Transaction Processing Engine, and Internal Ledger.

- Develop reliable backend services that strengthen financial integrity, data accuracy, and operational control across the platform.

- Partner with Finance and product teams across Payments, GTPN, Issuing, FX, and Billing to support complex financial workflows.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- Strong backend software engineering experience, with proficiency in a language such as Java or Kotlin.

- Experience designing, building, and operating reliable services in production.

- Strong understanding of software architecture, distributed systems, data integrity, and system scalability.

- Experience delivering complex projects and making sound technical decisions in an evolving environment.

- Clear communication skills and the ability to work effectively with engineering, Finance, Product, Risk, and other cross-functional partners.

- A strong focus on security, reliability, and operational excellence.

Preferred

- Experience building fintech, payments, accounting, ledger, reconciliation, wallet, treasury, or other financial systems.

- Understanding of accrual-based accounting principles and bookkeeping practices.

- Experience working in a high-growth or globally distributed technology environment.

- Experience with financial reporting, settlement, transaction processing, or reconciliation systems.

LEARN MORE ABOUT YOUR TEAM

The Financial Platform team is the core engine powering Airwallex's global expansion. We own the fundamental financial stack, including the Client Wallet, Transaction Processing Engine, and Internal Ledger. Together, these mission-critical systems manage real-time balances, account for every transaction, and execute global settlements at massive scale. Our mandate is to safeguard financial integrity and ensure absolute precision across all settlements while scaling our infrastructure to fuel hyper-growth. If you want to build high-visibility architecture where your code directly secures and drives our global platform, this is where you belong.
