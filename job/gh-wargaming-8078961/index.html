Job Overview

World of Warships: Legends team in Belgrade is looking for a Game Designer. As a member of the World of Warships team, you will have the opportunity to collaborate with a talented and passionate group of professionals and make a meaningful impact on the direction and success of the game.

World of Warships: Legends game is a free-to-play naval action MMO that puts players in command of some of the most iconic ships in history. From agile destroyers to mighty battleships, players can choose from a variety of classes and nations as they battle it out on the high seas.

What will you do?

  • Design, implement, and balance new gameplay and content features for World of Warships Legends
  • Partner with internal and external teams to execute development goals
  • Work with the analytics team to optimize and improve day-to-day user behavior
  • Create and support game design documentation

What are we looking for?

  • 2+ years of Game Design experience (ideally on console projects)
  • Solid understanding of at least one game design area: good experience with moment-to-moment gameplay (3Cs, weapons, abilities, etc.) or session-to-session gameplay (economy, progression, customization, etc.)
  • Positive attitude and ability to accept feedback, working in a multidisciplinary team
  • Excellent communicator (spoken and written) with good interpersonal skills
  • Upper-intermediate English to participate in daily meetings and discussions with English-speaking teammates
  • Be aware of industry trends, and know a wide range of games across multiple platforms and genres with a general understanding of the main game design approaches and principles

What additional skills will help you stand out?

  • Programming experience
  • Upper-intermediate Russian language skills
  • Level design experience
  • UX/UI design understanding

Work mode

  • Onsite during probation period and hybrid after that period (3 days' work form office and 2 days' work from home).

About Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming's titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
