О Role & Team

The Cryptographic Services & Infrastructure team secures and manages cryptographic keys and enables Robinhood engineering teams to safely leverage core capabilities like encryption and decryption.

Join a high-impact team responsible for protecting some of Robinhood's most sensitive financial and customer data! In this role, you will help build and operationalize Robinhood's new in-house card-processing service—a critical initiative that brings core capabilities in-house and offers meaningful ownership across software, infrastructure, security, and operations. While prior cryptography or security experience is highly preferred, we are excited to empower strong engineers with a passion for security to learn and grow their skills on the job.

This role is based in our Bellevue, WA and Menlo Park, CA offices, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

What you'll do day-to-day

  • Design, build, and maintain production services and infrastructure supporting key management, encryption, and decryption functions.
  • Help architect, launch, and scale Robinhood’s new in-house card-processing environment to protect sensitive card, financial, and tax-related data.
  • Write clean, maintainable production code and build operational tooling across software, infrastructure, and security controls.
  • Collaborate with internal engineering teams to support migrations, drive adoption of shared cryptographic capabilities, and ensure safe system design.
  • Participate in a rotating on-call schedule to maintain high availability, security, and operational reliability as processing volume grows.

Что вы приносите

  • Experience building, operating, or supporting production services, distributed systems, or infrastructure.
  • Proficiency writing production code in at least one modern programming language (such as Python, Go, Java, C#, or C++) with a strong ability to reason about complex systems.
  • Comfort working across multiple technical domains, including software development, infrastructure, and operational reliability.
  • Clear and concise communication skills, with a demonstrated ability to explain technical decisions and proactively collaborate when facing challenges.
  • Curiosity and willingness to learn security engineering principles and participate in a light on-call rotation.

Дополнительный плюс

  • Prior experience in site reliability engineering (SRE), platform engineering, or cloud security.
  • Familiarity with key management, secrets management, or core cryptographic concepts (such as symmetric encryption or public/private key pairs).
  • Experience handling sensitive financial, payment, or card data in regulated environments.
  • Background in supporting internal customer migrations or driving platform adoption across engineering teams.
