Join us and be part of creating Monopoly World – an innovative mobile game that brings the classic board game to life in the real world. As a Game Designer, you will play a key role in shaping the core gameplay experience, systems, and features that make our game engaging, strategic, and globally scalable.

This is your opportunity to leave your creative fingerprint on a product where players explore the real world, collect venues, and build their own empires.

Check out the Monopoly World trailer

We’re looking for a Game Designer to join our team on-site in our Kraków office, located in the historic building of the Old Kraków Train Station. This unique workspace fosters creativity and collaboration, perfectly aligning with the close-knit teamwork we value.

To support candidates relocating to Kraków, we’re offering a relocation package. More details about this will be shared during the recruitment process.

Обязанности

  • Design and balance core gameplay mechanics, systems, and progression loops.
  • Create feature specs and documentation for new game systems, events, and content.
  • Collaborate closely with developers, artists, and producers to bring your ideas to life.
  • Use data and player feedback to iterate on features and improve retention and monetization.
  • Prototype and test new gameplay ideas to validate design choices.
  • Own the end-to-end design process for specific features – from ideation to implementation.
