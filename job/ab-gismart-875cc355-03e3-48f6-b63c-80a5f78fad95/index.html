О нас

We have achieved a significant milestone of over 1 billion downloads worldwide, taking a step toward our mission of cultivating the well-being of people worldwide. Обязанности

- Own end-to-end production of performance creatives (video and static) for paid user acquisition (focusing on Meta)

- Build and run the creative pipeline from briefing to delivery: ideation, briefing, production, launch, iteration

- Partner with the UA team daily - translate performance data and learnings into new creative concepts, hooks, and hypotheses

- Plan weekly creative output and hit volume targets without sacrificing quality

- Manage internal designers; source and onboard new talent as we scale

- Track creative performance (CTR, IPM, ROAS, hook rate, hold rate) and drive A/B testing on hooks, scripts, formats, and lengths

- Run continuous trend research and competitor analysis across mobile and web ad creatives

- Maintain a clean creative library, briefing templates, and reusable assets

Требования

- 3+ years of experience as a Creative Producer / Creative Strategist / Creative Marketing Manager for subscription web funnels, apps

- Demonstrable track record of producing high-performing UA creatives (Meta)

- Strong understanding of performance creative principles: hooks, retention, CTAs, platform-native best practices

- Excellent project management skills - comfortable shipping dozens of creative variations per month under tight deadlines

- Data literacy - confident reading creative dashboards, identifying winners, and writing briefs that translate insights into actions

- Excellent briefing and feedback skills

- English at Intermediate (B1)

- Self-driven mindset, comfortable in zero-to-one and ambiguous environments

Будет плюсом

- Experience hiring and managing freelance creative networks (motion graphics, UGC, voice-over)

- Experience working in the health & wellness niche

- Experience setting up creative testing frameworks and reporting from scratch

- Experience with Applovin, TikTok, and Snapchat

- Hands-on skills in After Effects, Premiere Pro, or CapCut

- 100% Sick Leave Compensation

- Personal Equipment Policy: Of course, we’ll provide you with all the equipment you need or cover the maintenance cost for your own tools.

- Personal Learning and Development: We love to see you learn and grow, so Gismart will cover 70% of your professional development courses. We’ll also pay for 100% of corporate English courses.

- Time-Off Policy: Every employee gets 18 working days of paid vacation and 3 personal days per year, with an extra day added for each year at Gismart.

- Corporate Events: We are remote-first, but we love meeting up in person. Whenever possible, we organize corporate events and team-building activities across all Gismart hubs.
