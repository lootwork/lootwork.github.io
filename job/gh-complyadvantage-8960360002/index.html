We are seeking a Technical Support Analyst to join our operational unit in Manila, supporting clients throughout the Globe leveraging our advanced financial crime intelligence architecture. Serving as a crucial liaison between customer organizations and internal engineering, you will triage incoming technical inquiries, delivering rapid initial resolutions while seamlessly escalating complex cases with thorough context. Collaborating alongside L1 Analysts, regional analysts in Singapore, and Customer Success managers, you will drive operational efficiency to ensure no client requests are left unaddressed.

As a Technical Support Analyst, you will

  • Act as the primary point of contact for regional support requests submitted via email and modern ticketing channels
  • Execute precise ticket triage, resolving front-line issues directly or escalating to specialized internal teams with detailed technical context
  • Drive L1 issue resolution independently, prioritizing high first-contact resolution rates as a primary standard
  • Maintain proactive client communication across every stage of ticket lifecycle to provide continuous transparency
  • Manage performance metrics covering response speed, resolution times, and customer satisfaction while mitigating potential SLA breaches
  • Develop comprehensive expertise across software products while expanding collective internal documentation with novel solutions
  • Uphold rigorous data privacy standards and security policies when handling sensitive customer information
  • Identify recurring operational trends within support channels and partnering with product teams to eliminate core system issues
  • Leverage automated tooling and AI capabilities to scale ticket volume capacity while maintaining service excellence

What we we are looking for

  • 1 to 3 years of experience within technical client support or helpdesk environments, preferably inside B2B SaaS organizations
  • Exceptional verbal and written English communication capabilities
  • Proven familiarity with enterprise service management platforms like Zendesk, Freshdesk, Intercom or Pylon
  • Technical aptitude surrounding web architectures, database querying, and RESTful API integrations
  • Robust analytical acumen paired with rigorous attention to detail and problem-solving skills
  • Demonstrated composure and structured task management during high-volume support operations
  • Capability to perform effectively within distributed teams across multiple time zones
  • Background utilizing issue-tracking software alongside exposure to regulatory compliance or financial technology solutions is advantageous
