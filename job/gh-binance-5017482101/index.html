О роли

We’re hiring several Investigation Analysts to join our Screening Escalations & Geofencing team on a 12-month contract. You’ll dig into suspicious access activity, evaluate risk alerts, and deliver timely, evidence-backed decisions that protect our platform and strengthen risk controls. This is a global, remote role, so you’ll work with colleagues across regions and time zones.

Что предстоит делать

  • Investigate geofencing alerts and abnormal login behavior to uncover potential risks
  • Analyze case details, IP data, and account activity to determine risk levels
  • Review cases, send Requests for Information (RFIs), and follow up with users as needed
  • Make well-supported block/unblock decisions in line with internal SOPs
  • Record findings clearly, escalate complex cases, and consistently meet SLA targets

Кого мы ищем

  • At least 2 years of experience in investigations, fraud prevention, financial crime, compliance, or risk operations.
  • Sharp analytical mindset, investigative instincts, and strong attention to detail
  • Sound judgment, with the ability to piece together cases from multiple data sources
  • Fluency in English is required to be able to coordinate with overseas partners and stakeholders.
  • Comfort handling high case volumes in a fast-moving environment

Будет плюсом

  • Exposure to fintech, e-commerce, banking, payments, or digital assets
  • Familiarity with IP analysis, geolocation, or detecting suspicious account behavior
  • Hands-on experience with case management or investigation tools
