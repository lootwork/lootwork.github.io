О роли

Our InfoSec team is looking for a Staff Security Engineer to lead how we defend Paddle in a rapidly changing threat landscape. AI is changing both sides of security. Attackers are using it to scale social engineering, find weaknesses faster and automate intrusion, while our own adoption of AI tools and agents creates new risks to manage. This is a hands-on, high-impact role for an experienced security generalist who wants to build our defences against these threats, and to use automation and AI to make our detection and response faster and more effective. You’ll be a senior technical leader across InfoSec and IT, working across both our Enterprise IT and Product environments, and helping our global team of Paddlers operate securely without slowing them down.

Что предстоит делать

- Lead our incident response capability, from playbooks and readiness exercises to acting as senior technical lead during security incidents.

- Improve our security operations, strengthening detection coverage, alert quality and triage, and turning manual operational work into engineered, repeatable processes.

- Build and evolve our defences against AI-enabled threats, including AI-driven phishing, impersonation and deepfakes, and accelerated vulnerability exploitation.

- Help Paddle grow our adoption of AI securely by designing guardrails, access models and monitoring for AI tools and agents, including the non-human identities and integrations they rely on.

- Use automation and AI to speed up detection, investigation and response, so the team can handle a growing threat volume without growing headcount at the same rate.

- Contribute to the secure configuration, posture management and monitoring of our AWS cloud infrastructure, working with the Platform team so security initiatives are delivered in a scalable and reliable manner.

- Strengthen identity and access security as capabilities evolve, including our approach to MFA, device trust and conditional access.

- Support GRC initiatives on technical control implementation and audit evidence automation, in line with SOC 2, PCI DSS and CIS Benchmarks.

- Partner with InfoSec, IT and Engineering leadership to set technical direction and deliver the department’s key security programmes, making design decisions and driving work across multiple business functions.

- Mentor and support other engineers across InfoSec, IT and the wider Engineering function, building a culture of continuous improvement, knowledge sharing and technical excellence.

SKILLS & EXPERIENCE

At Paddle we value a growth mindset and a willingness to learn, so if you're confident in most of these skills (not necessarily all) and you're excited to grow, we encourage you to apply.

We’d love to hear from you if you

- Have extensive experience as a security engineer or in a similar role, with breadth across incident response, security operations, security engineering and cloud security, and a strong track record of hands-on delivery.

- Have led or played a senior role in responding to real security incidents, from investigation and containment through to recovery and lessons learned.

- Bring strong cloud security expertise, including identity, resource configuration, logging and monitoring. Hands-on experience securing AWS, including containerised workloads and cloud-native deployments, is highly desirable.

- Have experience with detection and response tooling, such as SIEM, EDR and cloud security posture management platforms, and know how to make them effective rather than noisy.

- Understand how AI is changing the threat landscape, and are excited about using automation and AI to improve how security teams detect, investigate and respond.

- Are familiar with modern identity, device management and endpoint security solutions (e.g. Okta, Iru, Island, SentinelOne), as well as collaboration and networking tools (e.g. Google Workspace, Slack, Cloudflare Zero Trust).

- Can script or program to automate workflows and integrate systems. Experience with Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform preferred) is desirable.

- Have experience implementing technical controls against frameworks such as SOC 2, PCI DSS or CIS Benchmarks (nice to have).

- Are comfortable operating autonomously on high-impact, wide-reaching work, and working collaboratively across multiple business functions.

- Can communicate effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders, and are comfortable influencing at all levels of the business, including during high-pressure incidents.

- Are passionate about security and want to make a real impact at scale.
