О Binance Accelerator Program

Binance Accelerator Program (BAP) is a 3-6 month internship program designed for Early Career talent to have firsthand experience in the rapidly expanding digital assets space. You will be given the opportunity to develop your skills at Binance and understand what it’s like to work at the world's leading blockchain ecosystem. As part of your internship in the BAP, there will also be opportunities for networking and development, which will expand your professional network and build transferable skills to propel you forward in your career. Learn about the BAP Program HERE.

Who may apply

Current university students and recent graduates.

*Terms of employment / engagement shall be subject to contract and local applicable laws

О роли

Binance is looking for an intern to work at the centre of our AI, DeFi, and growth agenda. You'll act as a core member on company-level AI initiatives while shaping how users are reached, matched, and retained across our products. This is a hands-on role: you'll move from market research and product definition to strategy design, experimentation, and launch — with direct exposure to algorithm, engineering, and big-data teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead end-to-end work on company-level AI initiatives: industry research, product definition, prototype review, user testing, and post-launch data analysis
  • Track market trends, leading competitors, and on-chain data in DeFi and stablecoin, and produce decision-ready research memos; contribute to product design, write PRDs, and follow projects through development and launch
  • Design and iterate recommendation and ranking strategies — distribution logic, candidate pools, and ranking objectives — then validate with data and keep tuning
  • Build push, task, and coupon strategies: define who gets reached, when, and with what incentive, and quantify the incremental impact

Build and optimise lifecycle programmes: user segmentation, touchpoint design at each stage, and experiment-led retention and reactivation

  • Partner closely with algorithm, engineering, and big-data teams to translate strategy into specs and metric definitions, run A/B tests, read results, and drive them into production

Кого мы ищем

  • Currently enrolled in Finance, Computer Science, or a related field; senior undergraduate or graduate students preferred

Fluency in English is required to be able to coordinate with overseas partners and stakeholders. Additional languages would be an advantage.

  • Hands-on experience with recommendation, ranking, push, task/coupon, or lifecycle strategy, from an internship or campus project — you can explain what you designed, which metrics you watched, and what happened
  • Comfortable with data and able to interpret A/B test results independently
  • Experience working alongside algorithm, engineering, or big-data teams

Будет плюсом

  • SQL proficiency or familiarity with analytics tooling
  • A dual degree (Finance + CS or AI)
  • Daily use of AI tools (Claude, Cursor, etc.) to increase personal output, with demonstrable projects, demos, writing, or GitHub work
