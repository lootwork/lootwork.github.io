We are looking for a talented and passionate Senior Credit Decision Modelling Analyst to join our 2nd line Credit Risk team. Our team plays a critical role in developing and maintaining credit risk models and strategies across Starling, including the models used to assess our new lending applications. If you have experience in credit risk model development, and are eager to be part of the team that plays a vital role in credit risk management, we want to hear from you!

As a Senior Credit Decision Modelling Analyst, you will be responsible for:

  • Developing and implementing credit risk models and strategies.
  • Working closely with stakeholders to ensure that model developments meet business requirements.
  • Producing detailed model documentation, including methodology, assumptions, and limitations.
  • Monitoring model performance and conducting regular reviews to ensure accuracy and stability.
  • Collaborating with model validation teams to address any findings and ensure models are robust.
  • Presenting model developments to Senior leadership, and representing the Credit Risk Modelling team with internal and external stakeholders.

Требования

  • Minimum 3 - 5 years’ experience in developing credit risk models.
  • Proficient in the use of analytical tools such as Python/R/SQL/SAS and spreadsheets.
  • Graduate level education preferable, ideally in a numerical degree (or an equivalent work experience).
  • Good understanding of unsecured lending products, ideally covering both retail and SME.
  • Good knowledge of regulatory and risk management guidelines relating to credit risk.
  • Ability to articulate model development methodologies and findings in a succinct and clear way that is suitable for the target audience.
  • Organised, flexible and adaptable, with the ability to switch between tasks seamlessly.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork abilities.
