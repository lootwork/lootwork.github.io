Job Description

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON​

What you will focus on

  • Responsible for supporting the design and optimization of revenue strategies and helping adjust the balance between engaging player experiences and effective monetization models.
  • By proposing improvements to the product strategy based on player behavior and data analysis, A/B testing, and market research and through coordinated efforts with production and monetization teams (design, developer, marketing, live ops, data analysis, and economy design).

What it means on a daily basis​

Performances analysis

  • Identify the features, game modes, and player behaviors that need to be analyzed, and prioritize the areas with the highest impact.
  • Work with the DA team to conduct structured data analysis, from problem definition to recommendations.
  • Build and maintain shared performance dashboards and prepare monthly reports/product reviews to track KPIs and game performance against revenue and profitability targets.

Engagement Metrics and Monetization

  • Run A/B tests on events, offers, and shop configurations to improve engagement and monetization performance across player segments.
  • Document testing history, results, and key learnings; propose new features and improvements to increase monetization.
  • Review the GDD and support forecasting, post-launch performance tracking, and optimization recommendations, including initiatives to increase new and returning users.

Product Vision and Strategy

  • Use performance analysis to support the GM in defining the product vision, direction, and key focus areas for the coming year(s).
  • Help define the strategy and quarterly objectives needed to bring the vision to life, including the scope of each update.
  • Share product and monetization insights with the team to improve alignment, ownership, and commitment to the product’s evolution.

Требования

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED​

  • At least 2 years of experience in Product Management, Live Operation, Analytics, Monetization, or Consulting in product-oriented roles; gaming experience.
  • Good knowledge of top-grossing games and free-to-play (F2P) mechanics, with experience on at least 2 different titles, including one Live Game operated as a service.
  • Solid analytical and data modeling skills, with solid proficiency in Excel, PowerPoint, and basic knowledge of SQL, Python, or R.
  • A Bachelor’s degree in a related field; a Master’s degree and game design or game creation experience.

Кто вы

  • Detail-oriented, well-organized, and capable of performing effectively in a changing work setting.
  • Experienced at analyzing large datasets and turning them into actionable insights.
  • Someone who works well with others and has effective leadership and communication skills.
  • Motivated by video games and able to combine creative thinking with data-driven decision-making.
