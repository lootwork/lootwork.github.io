О роли

Please note: applications will be open until Tuesday 13th October 2026 at 08.00 CEST.

We will not review any applications before the closing window so please don't rush to apply and use the time to submit a high quality application.

We're looking for a Staff Engineer to join the Business Spend segment at Pleo. In this role, you'll help us build invoices, purchase orders, supplier invoices, cards for subscriptions and be part of Business Spend as we scale procurement to pay services. If you're excited about systems with real history and are passionate about building services that handle what a company spends to run itself, then this is the opportunity for you!

WHO YOU’LL BE WORKING WITH AND REPORTING TO

You’ll report to our Engineering Manager in Business Spend and work closely with teams in accounting systems and card infrastructure. Our team of 10 is highly collaborative and dedicated to invoices and vendors. You’ll also have the chance to partner with teams across product, design and compliance to ensure success.

Что предстоит делать

As a Staff Engineer, you will

- Partner with the Engineering Manager to own and be accountable for the whole architecture of Business Spend.

- Own significant parts of our supplier data migration, moving supplier and bank account logic out of a legacy service into a dedicated one, per customer and without downtime.

- Improve the reliability of our invoice payment flows, where roughly 2.5% of payments currently fail and every failure is a customer who can't pay a supplier.

- Turn our new vendor data model into working software, so that a supplier means the same thing across cards, invoices, and accounting.

- Help the 3 teams make consistent technical decisions, through design documents, code review, and the architecture forum, without being anyone's manager.

- Raise the people around you, through pairing, review, and writing things down clearly enough that someone in another time zone can act on them.

To put things into context, here's what's on our roadmap right now: extracting supplier management out of our legacy invoice service, meeting the ISO 20022 structured address requirements for outbound payments, and rebuilding how we model suppliers and vendors across the whole of Business Spend.

On the technical side, you can expect to work with the following stack: AWS, Github actions, Kotlin on the JVM, Kafka, Spring Boot, Gradle, Kubernetes, and Grafana for metrics, logs, and traces. Our frontend is TypeScript and React.

Что вы приносите

You’ll thrive in this role if you have

- Strong verbal communication. You can hold a room of engineers who don't share a first language and leave everyone clear on what was decided.

- Experience as the technical lead for a team spread across several countries and time zones. You adapt how you communicate rather than expecting everyone to adapt to you, and you're as effective in writing and async as you are live.

- Deep backend engineering experience in a modern statically typed language such as Java, Kotlin or TypeScript, on systems running in production at scale.

- A track record of shipping inside a legacy system that was being taken apart. Not just designing the target state, but doing the migration.

- The ability to change technical direction across teams you don't own, with specific examples of when you've done it.

- Writing that other people can act on. Design documents, decision records, migration plans that work without you in the room.

- A background in payments, fintech, accounts payable, ERP or B2B SaaS.

- Experience with event-driven systems and Kafka, and a habit of building observability into what you ship.

WHY IS THIS ROLE A GOOD FIT FOR YOU

This role is a good fit for you if

- You want to work on a genuinely unsolved problem. How a company models its suppliers across cards, invoices, and accounting is the hardest open question in our product, and we're answering it this year.

- You want your work to be visible. The team is small enough that one person changes the outcome, and you'll see the effect on customers within weeks.

- You like being the person others come to, and you'd rather earn that through the work than be handed it in a job title.

This role is not a good fit for you if

- You want a green field project. You'll spend a real share of your time in code you didn't write, with constraints you didn't choose.

- You want to design the target state and hand it over. Here you design it and then you build it.

- You need formal authority to get things moving. You'll need to rely on technical influence and earn trust across 3 teams.
