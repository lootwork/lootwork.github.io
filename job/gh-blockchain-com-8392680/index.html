О роли

We are seeking an experienced Travel Rule professional to drive and scale our global Travel Rule compliance programme. In this role, you will drive the operational, regulatory, and technical execution of virtual asset transfer compliance across key jurisdictions (including the UK, EU, US, and APAC). You will act as the primary subject matter expert bridging our legal, product, engineering, and financial crime operations teams to ensure seamless, compliant digital asset transactions.

Что предстоит делать

  • Programme Ownership: Design, implement, and maintain global Travel Rule policies, procedures, and controls aligned with FATF Recommendation 16, the EU Transfer of Funds Regulation (TFR), UK FCA rules, and FinCEN requirements.
  • Vendor & Protocol Management: Manage day-to-day operations and integrations with Travel Rule solution providers and messaging protocols (e.g., Notabene, TRUST, TRP, Sygna, Sumsub).
  • Counterparty VASP Due Diligence: Oversee onboarding, risk assessment, and ongoing due diligence for counterparty Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs), resolving interoperability and data-sharing gaps.
  • Transaction Monitoring & Escalations: Establish workflows for handling missing, incomplete, or mismatched originator and beneficiary information. Manage exception queues, rejected transfers, and Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) escalations.
  • Self-Hosted Wallet Verification: Implement and refine risk-based controls and ownership verification processes for transfers involving unhosted/self-custodied wallets.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with Product and Engineering teams to automate data transmission, reduce user friction, and troubleshoot API or protocol failures.
  • Regulatory & Audit Readiness: Maintain comprehensive audit trails, produce regular compliance metrics for senior leadership, and lead responses to regulatory examinations and external audits.

Required Qualifications

  • 2+ years of experience in AML/CTF compliance, financial crime operations, or regulatory risk within the cryptocurrency, digital assets, or payments sector.
  • Practical understanding of global Travel Rule frameworks and cross-border VASP obligations.
  • Hands-on experience working with Travel Rule messaging tools, blockchain analytics platforms (e.g., Chainalysis, Elliptic, TRM Labs), and API-driven compliance workflows.
  • Ability to interpret complex regulatory guidance and translate it into scalable operational and product requirements.
  • Strong stakeholder management skills with the ability to communicate clearly with engineers, external VASP partners, and internal partners.

WHAT YOU WILL NEED

  • Experience working with Notabene.
  • Relevant compliance certifications such as CAMS (Certified Anti-Money Laundering Specialist), CGSS, or ICA diplomas.
  • Direct experience managing Travel Rule interoperability challenges (the "sunrise issue") across multiple jurisdictions.
  • Familiarity with SQL, data analytics tools, or product management methodologies.
