О роли

We are looking for a proactive and results-driven PR & Communications Manager to lead Binance’s public relations and communications activities in Turkey. The successful candidate will be responsible for driving local media engagement, strengthening Binance’s reputation, identifying strategic speaking opportunities, and executing impactful PR initiatives aligned with regional and global communications objectives. This is a hands-on role requiring strong media relationships, excellent storytelling capabilities, sound judgment, and the ability to independently manage multiple communications projects in a fast-paced environment. The candidate must be based in Turkey, fluent in Turkish and English, and available to attend media engagements, industry events, and executive activities in person as required.

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead and execute PR and communications initiatives for Binance in Turkey, aligned with regional and global communications strategies.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with key Turkish media, including business, financial, technology, and mainstream outlets.
  • Proactively identify and secure earned media opportunities, including interviews, features, executive profiling, and thought leadership placements.
  • Identify and coordinate strategic speaking opportunities, industry conferences, media roundtables, and executive engagements.
  • Manage local PR activities around corporate announcements, product launches, partnerships, and key company milestones.
  • Develop and localize high-quality communications materials, including press releases, media pitches, executive talking points, Q&As, and briefing documents.
  • Support reputation management and crisis communications, including media monitoring, issue identification, and timely escalation of reputational risks.
  • Collaborate closely with regional and global communications teams, as well as local marketing, growth, legal, compliance, and other stakeholders.
  • Manage external PR agencies and communications partners where applicable, ensuring quality execution and measurable results.
  • Monitor media coverage, sentiment, and competitive activity, and deliver regular performance reports with actionable insights.
  • Represent the communications function at relevant local events and support on-the-ground media and executive engagements.

Кого мы ищем

  • 5–7 years of relevant experience in public relations, corporate communications, or media relations, preferably within technology, fintech, financial services, or related industries.
  • Native-level Turkish fluency and excellent professional English, both written and spoken.
  • Established relationships with Turkish media and a strong understanding of the local media landscape.
  • Proven experience in securing earned media coverage, managing media relations, and executing PR campaigns.
  • Experience coordinating executive interviews, thought leadership initiatives, speaking engagements, and industry events.
  • Excellent writing, storytelling, and content development skills, with the ability to translate complex topics into clear and compelling narratives.
  • Strong organizational and project management capabilities, with the ability to manage competing priorities independently.
  • Sound judgment, a proactive mindset, and the ability to operate effectively in a dynamic and fast-paced environment.
  • Must be based in Turkey and available to attend in-person media engagements, conferences, and company events as required.
