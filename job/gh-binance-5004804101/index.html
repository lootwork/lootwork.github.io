Обязанности

  • Develop and execute localized growth strategies and marketing campaigns to drive user acquisition, engagement, and retention in the local market.
  • Plan, manage, and optimize end-to-end campaigns across online and offline channels, including product launches, seasonal promotions, partnerships, and brand initiatives.
  • Work closely with cross-functional teams including Product, Operations, BD, Compliance, Creative, and Regional Marketing to ensure campaigns are aligned with business priorities and local market needs.
  • Analyze campaign performance and user behavior data to generate actionable insights and continuously improve campaign effectiveness.
  • Identify market trends, competitor activities, and user preferences in Korea to inform growth opportunities and campaign planning.
  • Collaborate with local and regional partners, influencers, communities, and media channels to expand market reach and strengthen brand presence.
  • Own campaign calendars, execution timelines, and reporting, ensuring projects are delivered on time and with high quality.
  • Support localization of global campaigns and tailor messaging, content, and channel strategy for the local audience.
  • Monitor budget allocation and campaign ROI, ensuring efficient use of resources and strong business impact.
  • Drive experimentation and test-and-learn initiatives to uncover new scalable growth channels and tactics.

Требования

  • 5+ years of experience in growth marketing, campaign management, digital marketing, or related roles, preferably in tech, fintech, crypto, or internet companies.
  • Strong understanding of the local market, including local user behavior, digital trends, media landscape, and cultural nuances.
  • Proven track record of planning and executing successful growth or marketing campaigns with measurable results.
  • Strong analytical skills with the ability to interpret data, track KPIs, and translate insights into strategy.
  • Experience working cross-functionally in a fast-paced, high-growth environment.
  • Excellent project management skills with strong attention to detail and execution ability.
  • Familiarity with performance marketing, CRM, lifecycle campaigns, partnerships, and community-led growth is a plus.
  • Knowledge of the crypto/Web3 industry is highly preferred.
  • Fluency in Korean and professional proficiency in English required to collaborate with global stakeholders.
