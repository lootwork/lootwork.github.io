Job Responsibilities

  • Responsible for product planning and delivery of Direct Stocks and Tokenized Stocks, covering core modules including accounts, assets, trading, market data, fund settlement, and corporate actions.
  • Responsible for interfacing with overseas brokers, clearing houses, custodians, and tokenized stock issuers — driving API integration, business process design, system joint testing, and product launch.
  • Design US equity trading product capabilities, including:
  • Order types: market orders, limit orders, conditional orders, etc.
  • Trading sessions: pre-market, regular hours, after-hours, and overnight trading
  • Buying power, order freezes, trade settlement deductions, and unsettled funds management
  • Market data display, NBBO, price protection, and abnormal market data fallback mechanisms
  • Corporate actions: dividends, stock splits, reverse splits, mergers & acquisitions, and delistings
  • Responsible for integrating Direct Stocks with the platform's existing account system, fund accounts, and trading infrastructure — designing fund flows including deposits, FX conversion, transfers, settlement, reconciliation, and withdrawal restrictions.
  • Responsible for mStocks product mechanism design, including token issuance, subscription/redemption, minting/burning, asset custody, price pegging, liquidity, and on-chain trading workflows.
  • Collaborate with compliance, legal, finance, risk control, engineering, and operations teams to drive product market access, investor suitability management, and risk control solutions across different countries and regions.
  • Continuously research and produce competitive analysis and product iteration proposals.

Требования

  • 3+ years of experience in trading products, with background in US equities, securities trading, brokerage, tokenized stocks, or RWA products.
  • Familiar with the Direct Stocks business model, with an understanding of the roles of brokers, introducing brokers, clearing brokers, custodians, and market makers.
  • Proficient in core securities trading processes, including:
  • Orders, fills, positions, and buying power
  • T+1 settlement and unsettled cash
  • Omnibus accounts and sub-accounts
  • Prefunding, net settlement, and reconciliation
  • Corporate actions and dividend tax
  • Familiar with US equity trading sessions, order types, time-in-force (TIF), circuit breakers, NBBO, SIP market data, and price protection mechanisms.
  • Understands the business architecture of Tokenized Stocks / mStocks, including on-chain issuance, reserve assets, subscription/redemption, liquidity management, and price pegging mechanisms.
  • Strong systematic product design skills — able to independently deliver business process design, product architecture, state machines, exception handling, and reconciliation solutions.
  • Strong English communication skills, capable of directly engaging with overseas brokers, vendors, and partner institutions to drive requirements and resolve issues.
  • Detail-oriented regarding trading mechanics and fund security, with strong cross-functional coordination and complex project management capabilities.
