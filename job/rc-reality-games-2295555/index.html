Join Our Creative Team as a 2D Graphic Designer! 🎮

Do you love turning ideas into eye-catching visuals? We’re looking for a Creative 2D Graphic Designer to help us bring our mobile games to life on the App Store and Google Play. If you enjoy working on dynamic projects, creating stunning graphics in multiple styles and languages, and collaborating with a passionate team, this role is for you!

Join us full-time at our stunning office located in the heart of Krakow. Situated in a historic building just steps away from the vibrant city center, our workspace blends timeless charm with modern amenities, providing an inspiring setting for your personal and professional growth.

What You’ll Do

✨ Design engaging promotional graphics for mobile games on App Store & Google Play.

✨ Create multiple design variations tailored for different markets (starting with 7, and more to come!).

✨ Integrate localized texts and translations into your designs.

✨ Experiment with AI-powered tools to craft backgrounds and supporting elements.

✨ Collaborate closely with our marketing team to keep visuals consistent, polished, and impactful.

✨ Ensure every asset is sharp, aesthetic, and 100% platform-ready.

What We Offer

We believe in autonomy and trust – your creative input will shape the visual identity of our games. You can expect attractive terms of cooperation, and the flexibility to work either from our office in an iconic building in the historic heart of the city.

Our collaboration begins with a 1-month paid trial to get to know each other, followed by a 3-month contract. For the right fit, we’re ready to extend into long-term cooperation. Join us and let’s create visuals that players all around the world will love

💡 Be part of the team redefining mobile gaming with Monopoly World!
