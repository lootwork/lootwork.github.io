With multiple awards to our name, Larian Studios has proven that we’re dedicated to delivering high-quality role-playing games. As we move on to new projects, the studio that brought you Baldur’s Gate 3 & Divinity: Original Sin 2 is looking for a Gameplay Animation Intern to join the team and help us in crafting the high caliber games our fans have come to expect.

Обязанности

  • Understand the requirements of the game and maintain the animation style of the production title.
  • Vocalize your obstacles and needs to your supervisor.
  • Respect the deadlines set for you by your supervisor.
  • Create charming and believable in-game animations ranging from the biggest creature to the smallest assets.
  • Maintain your own animations through polish and bug fixing.

Требования

  • A passion for animation.
  • Fundamentals of animation principles.
  • Fluent in both written and spoken English.
  • Basic understanding of game development processes.
  • Ability to work collaboratively in a studio environment
  • Self-motivated.
  • An active interest in video games, particularly in role-playing games
  • Must already be eligible to work in the country and possess any required documents (visa/permit).

Что мы предлагаем

  • An international team of over 600 employees spread across 7 studios around the world, each of which have their own unique qualities, and maintain a small-studio feel
  • An open, collaborative environment where ideas are welcome and encouraged
  • A managerial team consisting of highly experienced game developers at the top of their fields
  • Sharing hobbies and passions with like-minded colleagues
  • Inclusive environment with many activities organized by the studios or the employees
  • Commitment to making the best game possible for the fans, including listening to community feedback

В этой вакансии студия требует право работать в своей стране — гражданство, вид на жительство или рабочую визу. Даже если указана удалённая работа, кандидата из другой страны обычно не рассматривают. Проверьте условия в оригинале перед откликом.
