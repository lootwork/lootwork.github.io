О нас

Traditional banking has never really given you control over your financial life. Satispay exists to change that: to empower people through finance. That means making financial services easy and accessible for over 6.5M users.

We started with payments, then benefits, investing, cards... and we’re not done. Not even close. We follow the same logic every time: find a real problem, solve it properly, then look for the next one. The destination? To build the most loved financial platform in the world.

If you own outcomes rather than tasks, stay close to what users need, and refuse to wait for someone else to fix what's broken — you'll find people here who work exactly in the same way. And together, you'll build something worth being proud of.

Что предстоит делать

As our Senior Performance Marketing Manager, you'll take full ownership of the paid acquisition strategy for your assigned product portfolio, driving multi-channel growth and combining analytical rigour with strategic vision.

Here's what that looks like in practice

- Product Ownership – Own performance marketing strategy and execution end-to-end for assigned products, from planning through reporting and optimisation.

- Multi-Channel Acquisition – Plan, manage, and optimise full-funnel acquisition campaigns across key platforms, including Google (SEA, UAC, YouTube), Meta, ASA, LinkedIn, and TikTok.

- B2C & B2B Strategy – Develop and execute tailored acquisition strategies across both B2C and B2B models, adapting the channel mix, messaging, and KPIs to different audiences and sales cycles.

- Mobile Marketing – Apply advanced knowledge of mobile marketing dynamics, including attribution, deep linking, and mobile measurement partners (MMPs), to drive app-first product performance.

- Data-Driven Strategy – Define and monitor KPIs, budgets, and forecasts, leveraging rigorous data analysis to guide decisions and improve results.

- Cross-Functional Collaboration – Partner closely with Product, Data, Creative/Brand, and Sales teams to align performance strategy with broader company goals.

- Stakeholder Management – Build strong relationships across the organisation, presenting results, insights, and strategic recommendations with clarity and confidence.

- Continuous Testing – Monitor market dynamics and platform evolutions, actively proposing and testing new channel opportunities.

Who we're looking for

This role needs someone with real ownership and strategic vision who treats their channels and products as their own business, proactively solving problems and driving measurable business impact.

- Experience – 5–7+ years in Performance Marketing roles with direct ownership of six-figure budgets.

- Ownership & Accountability – 100% accountability for KPIs and outcomes, identifying challenges early and independently driving solutions.

- Strategic Vision – Ability to turn long-term product and channel vision into structured roadmaps with clear hypotheses, priorities, and trade-offs.

- Critical Thinking – Proactive approach to challenging the status quo across channel mix, budget allocation, tracking, creative, and cross-team workflows.

- Autonomous Decision-Making – High comfort making decisions with incomplete information, taking responsibility for results, and learning quickly.

- Channel Expertise – Hands-on experience managing campaigns across Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads, ASA, and Affiliation.

- Data & Analytics – Strong proficiency in interpreting complex performance metrics and translating data into actionable forecasts and strategies.

- Stakeholder Management – Excellent communication skills with the ability to influence and challenge stakeholders at every level.

- B2B & B2C Exposure – Experience navigating both business models is strongly preferred.

- AI-Savvy – Genuine interest and hands-on familiarity with modern AI tools to simplify workflows and optimise campaign performance.

- Languages – Fluency in Italian and English, both written and spoken.

Don't worry if you don't tick every box. We believe in the power of different viewpoints and strengths. Your unique perspective is important as we build something special: if you're passionate and can make a difference, we truly encourage you to apply.
