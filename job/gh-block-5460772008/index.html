It all started with an idea at Block in 2013. Initially built to take the pain out of peer-to-peer payments, Cash App has gone from a simple product with a single purpose to a dynamic ecosystem, developing unique financial products, including Afterpay/Clearpay, to provide a better way to send, spend, invest, borrow and save to our 50+ million monthly active customers. We want to redefine the world’s relationship with money to make it more relatable, instantly available, and universally accessible.

Today, Cash App has thousands of employees working globally across office and remote locations, with a culture geared toward innovation, collaboration and impact. We’ve been a distributed team since day one, and many of our roles can be done remotely from the countries where Cash App operates. No matter the location, we tailor our experience to ensure our employees are creative, productive, and happy.

The Team

Apollo leads Block's work on the Customer World Model: a continuously evolving representation of each customer's goals, context, history, constraints, and likely future needs. The Customer World Model powers Proactive Intelligence across Block's ecosystem, including Square, Cash App, and Afterpay. Instead of customers searching through products for the feature they need, the system understands what matters to them, anticipates what comes next, and takes timely action that improves their outcomes. Both are top priorities for Block.

О роли

We're hiring exceptional AI Researchers to take Customer World Model and Proactive Intelligence from promising research to reliable production systems. You'll set the technical direction, solve the hardest research and engineering problems, and own delivery all the way through production integration. The scope covers customer representations, forecasts, decision models, evaluation, real-time serving, and integration into the products customers use every day.

You'll shape problems that span several teams, connect applied research with engineering and product, and raise the effectiveness of everyone around you through shared infrastructure, clear technical standards, and mentorship. Our team already builds heavily with agentic development tools. As throughput grows, the judgment to frame the right problem, validate the result, and get it through the last mile matters more than ever. That judgment is what this role brings.

Вам предстоит

  • Translate product needs into research and engineering plans with clear, measurable success criteria.
  • Develop customer representations, forecasts, and decision models from large-scale event, transaction, and behavioral data.
  • Evaluate models rigorously for accuracy, uncertainty, and real customer impact, and build the evaluation frameworks that predict how they'll perform in production.
  • Own the path from prototype to production, including data dependencies, real-time serving, monitoring, and integration with product teams.
  • Lead cross-team technical decisions, act as a tie-breaker on hard tradeoffs, and build shared capabilities that reduce duplicated work across Block's businesses.
  • Expand automation across development, experimentation, evaluation, and operations so the whole team moves faster.
  • Mentor researchers and engineers, set technical standards, and help grow the team's research and engineering practice.

You Have

  • A track record of technical leadership at a Principal or equivalent level, setting direction for work that spans multiple teams.
  • [10+] years of experience in machine learning research or engineering, [or a PhD in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Statistics, Operations Research, or a related field with 7+ years of industry experience].
  • Deep expertise in modern machine learning, including representation learning, sequence or foundation models, and forecasting or decision-making under uncertainty.
  • Experience taking models from research to production at scale, and owning them through serving, monitoring, and iteration.
  • Strong judgment in evaluation: offline and online metrics, calibration and uncertainty, experimentation, and measuring real customer impact.
  • Fluency in Python and a modern ML framework such as PyTorch or JAX, and the ability to write production-quality code.
  • Clear communication and the ability to align research, engineering, and product partners on ambiguous problems.

Желательно

  • Experience building foundation models or embeddings over transaction, event-sequence, or behavioral data.
  • Experience with reinforcement learning, causal inference, counterfactual evaluation, or next-best-action systems.
  • Experience building real-time ML systems that power customer-facing products.
  • Experience in financial technology, payments, or another regulated domain.
  • Hands-on experience using agentic development tools to speed up research and engineering.
  • A record of research excellence through publications, open-source contributions, or equivalent work.

Success in Your First Year

  • You've set the technical direction for a core part of the Customer World Model or Proactive Intelligence, with success criteria the team and product partners agree on.
  • At least one capability you led is in production, monitored, and measurably improving a customer experience.
  • You've built a shared evaluation or infrastructure capability that other teams use instead of building their own.
  • The team ships faster and with higher confidence because of the standards, automation, and mentorship you brought.
