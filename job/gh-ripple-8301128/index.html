MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS

Must have a Bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, Computer Engineering or a related field plus 5 years of progressive, post-baccalaureate software development experience.

Alternatively, must have a Master’s degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, Computer Engineering or a related field plus 3 years of software development experience.

Must have 3 years of experience coding in Java with Spring or Spring Boot Framework.

Must have 3 years of experience with Messaging Stream Solutions.

Must have 3 years of experience in building systems backed by modern persistence technologies in payments, banking, e-commerce domains, backed by Aurora or DynamoDB.

Must have 3 years of experience working with at least one of the following web frontend frameworks:

  • Node Js,
  • React JS,
  • Angular Js

Must have 3 years of experience with Typescript, Golang, Scala, Rust, or Python.

Must have 3 years of experience building cloud applications on AWS, and technologies using Docker or Kubernetes.

Must have 3 years of experience using Agile development of distributed services, with a focus on robust software design, scalability and security
