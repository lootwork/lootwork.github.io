О вакансии

The Strategic Sourcing Analyst is a role responsible for driving end-to-end sourcing events, commercial negotiations, and contract structuring across key technology spend categories, including IT infrastructure, software, IT services, and staffing agencies. In this role, you will act as a trusted advisor to internal stakeholders, owning the entire sourcing lifecycle from initial market analysis and RFP execution to final contract drafting and vendor onboarding, ensuring optimal value and mitigated risk for the business.

“Negotiates high-value tech contracts and ensures we only pay for delivered results”

Обязанности

  • Lead single-source negotiations and competitive RFPs across multiple technology and services spend categories.
  • Negotiate and secure favorable tier discounts, pricing models, and commercial terms with key technology and service vendors.
  • Structure complex Statements of Work (SOWs), ensuring that agency fee disbursements are strictly tied to measurable delivery milestones and performance metrics.
  • Standardize contractor rate cards and implement labor caps across all staff augmentation and external consulting engagements.
  • Conduct comprehensive market analysis to inform sourcing strategies and benchmark pricing.
  • Draft, review, and refine contract terms in close collaboration with Legal, Security, and Finance teams.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with internal stakeholders, aligning sourcing strategies with broader organizational goals.
