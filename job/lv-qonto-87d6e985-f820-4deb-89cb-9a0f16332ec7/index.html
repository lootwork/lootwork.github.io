Join Qonto's Growth Operations team as an Apprentice and help build the data connections and integrations that power our growth channels. You'll work under the guidance of Hugo, our Lead Growth Operations.

➡️ What you'll do

  • Make our Growth Engine go further: You'll configure API integrations so data flows reliably between systems (Salesforce, Segment, Braze, Meta, etc.), our marketing tools and beyond.
  • Fuel outbound with data: You'll build data enrichment flows to provide the Sales teams with the best insights to convert prospect into loyal customers.
  • Boost our Paid Acquisition campaigns by owning website tracking and tagging.
  • Deliver powerful insights for business teams: You'll use SQL and Python or JavaScript, with AI tools to move faster, to turn messy data into insights teams can act on.
  • Investigate issues and improve our infrastructure: You'll take down recurring blockers and bring your own solution so the team gets stronger every time.
  • Be a sparring partner for Engineering: You’ll help challenge and shape technical choices with our engineers, so the integrations and data flows you build hold up at scale.

➡️ What we're looking for

  • Data confidence: You're comfortable with at least two of SQL, JavaScript or Python.
  • Integration mindset: You understand APIs and enjoy making different systems work together.
  • Technical and analytical background: You're studying software engineering, data science or a similar field.
  • AI-fluent: You use AI to move faster and stay critical about the output.
  • English: You can work and communicate in English every day; French is a plus.
  • Analytical: you know how to transform raw data into valuable insights for non technical stakeholders
  • Problem solver: you enjoy tackling structural issues and make other people's jobs easier with your solutions.

Curious and keen to learn fast? Apply regardless of the boxes you tick. You may have the missing piece we've been looking for.

➡️ What we can offer you

  • Three skill sets, one team: You'll work alongside the Ops, Product and Engineering sides of Growth Operations.
  • Room to shape your path: We encourage breadth across domains, so you grow into the areas that suit you.
  • A two-year runway: We invest in your onboarding and training upfront, so you have time to grow into your own projects.

➡️ Your future manager

You'll report to Hugo Peurois, Lead Growth Operations. Hugo has spent 3+ years at Qonto, moving from Data Analytics Manager to Senior Product Manager to leading Growth Operations. He wants his team to build knowledge across several domains rather than stay in one lane, and he invests in your development to make that happen.
