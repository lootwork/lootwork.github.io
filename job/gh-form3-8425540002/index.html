THE ROLE 📝

This is an exciting opportunity for a Senior Business Analyst to join the US Product team at Form3. The role will support the continued development and delivery of Form3’s US payments product suite, acting as a subject matter expert across the relevant payment schemes and working closely with Product, Engineering and external stakeholders.

Depending on your experience and background, you may focus on the ongoing development and optimisation of our live FedNow and RTP solutions, or support the build of new capabilities across ACH and Fedwire.

Основные обязанности

  • Take ownership of business analysis activities across one or more US payment product lines, depending on your experience and background.
  • Support the development and continuous improvement of our live FedNow and RTP solutions and/or support the design and build of new ACH and Fedwire gateway capabilities.
  • Develop a strong understanding of the relevant US payment schemes, including their rules, processes and technical requirements. Act as a subject matter expert for internal teams and external stakeholders.
  • Work closely with Product Managers and Engineering teams to translate product requirements into deliverable solutions.
  • Write user stories, define acceptance criteria and create functional and non-functional specifications.
  • Analyse the impact of product changes across APIs, services, data and operational processes.
  • Support product discovery, testing and delivery throughout the product development lifecycle.
  • Use data and customer insight to inform product decisions and identify opportunities for improvement.

WE ARE LOOKING FOR 🔍

We are looking for an experienced and versatile Senior Business Analyst with strong knowledge of US payments and the ability to take ownership of complex areas of work.

Essential

  • Significant experience as a Senior Business Analyst, Business Analyst, Product Owner or in a similar product-focused role within payments or financial services.
  • Strong experience working with US payment schemes, ideally including FedNow, RTP, ACH and/or Fedwire.
  • A detailed understanding of how relevant US payment schemes operate.
  • Experience working with payment gateway products, APIs and/or cloud-based technology.
  • Proven experience writing user stories, acceptance criteria and functional requirements.
  • Experience analysing complex requirements and their impact across products, services and platforms.
  • A strong understanding of the product development lifecycle.
  • Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills, particularly when working with Engineering teams.
  • The ability to work autonomously and effectively in a fast-paced, delivery-focused environment.

INTERVIEW PROCESS ✍️

Stage One: Screening Call with the Talent Team

Stage Two: Team Interview with a Senior Business Analyst and Senior Product Manager

Stage Three: Case Study Interview with two Heads of Product

We always aim to stick to the above process; however, there may be occasions when an additional interview stage is required for us to be sure we are hiring the right person!

HIRING LOCATIONS 📍

We're currently able to consider applications from candidates based in Ontario (Canada) and the following US states: Connecticut, Florida, New Jersey, New York and North Carolina.

This is a fully remote role. You'll be expected to travel occasionally for team and customer meetings, primarily to New York, with occasional travel to the UK.
