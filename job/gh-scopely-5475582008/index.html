Niantic Marketing drives community-focused initiatives for our global audience of tens-of-millions of users, demonstrating key partnerships and creative campaigns that reflect the spirit of our company's mission. О роли

We are looking for a Community Manager to strengthen and grow Pokémon GO's player community in India.

As a key growth market for Pokémon GO, India presents opportunities to expand our player community, build stronger engagement, and connect more players through meaningful in-game and real-world experiences.

This role will manage local community programs, organize and promote player events, develop our Community Ambassador network, and support marketing and communications initiatives that contribute to player engagement and market growth.

Working closely with APAC Marketing, Communications, Game teams, and external partners, the Community Manager will also translate player insights into actionable recommendations and explore AI-enabled solutions to improve the efficiency and impact of community operations.

Что предстоит делать

1. Community Events & Activations

  • Plan, promote, and execute community events and local activations, including Community Days, seasonal campaigns, and key Pokémon GO moments.
  • Develop initiatives that encourage player participation, strengthen community connections, and extend Pokémon GO's reach to wider audiences.
  • Collaborate with internal teams, Community Ambassadors, and local partners to deliver engaging player experiences.
  • Coordinate local event partners, vendors, and program budgets, ensuring effective execution and alignment with relevant approval processes.
  • Evaluate event performance and identify opportunities to improve participation, player engagement, and business impact.

2. Community Programs & Ambassador Development

  • Develop and execute community programs that strengthen player engagement, loyalty, and long-term community growth.
  • Manage and grow the Community Ambassador program, supporting community leaders in building active, inclusive, and sustainable local player communities.
  • Identify opportunities to expand community reach through grassroots initiatives, player advocacy, and local partnerships, helping attract new and returning players.
  • Promote community programs and collaborate with Marketing and Game teams to support broader Pokémon GO initiatives and market growth objectives.
  • Monitor program performance, community feedback, and participation to identify opportunities for improvement.

3. Community Insights & Cross-Functional Collaboration

  • Gather and analyze player feedback, community sentiment, and program performance to identify trends and opportunities.
  • Translate community insights into actionable recommendations that support player engagement, retention, and market development.
  • Partner with India and APAC Marketing, Communications, Product, and Game teams to align community activities with business priorities.
  • Support local marketing campaigns, communications, and partnerships to strengthen Pokémon GO's presence in India.

4. AI & Operational Improvement

  • Leverage AI and automation to improve community workflows, including survey development, localization, feedback collection, and reporting.
  • Identify opportunities to simplify repetitive tasks and develop scalable processes that can be reused across APAC markets.
  • Explore and prototype practical AI-enabled solutions that improve efficiency and support better community decision-making.
  • Apply appropriate human judgment, quality control, and data protection practices when using AI tools.

Кого мы ищем

  • 6+ years of relevant experience in community management, gaming, consumer marketing, events, or related disciplines, including demonstrated ownership of community programs or local activations.
  • Proven ability to independently manage community programs, player events, or grassroots marketing initiatives.
  • Strong understanding of gaming communities, player engagement, and how community programs can contribute to business growth.
  • Experience working with community leaders, ambassadors, creators, or external partners.
  • Strong project management, communication, and cross-functional collaboration skills.
  • Ability to analyze community feedback and translate insights into actionable recommendations.
  • Comfortable using AI tools and exploring automation to improve day-to-day workflows.
  • Knowledge of the Indian gaming market and local player communities.
  • Excellent written and spoken English; additional Indian languages are an advantage.
  • Passion for Pokémon GO and building meaningful player experiences.
