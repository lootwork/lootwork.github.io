OUR MISSION

At Trading 212, our mission is to enable everyone to build wealth. We reinvent how trading and investing work by creating exceptional products people love. Fostering a culture of excellence and high velocity is the key to our success.

Today, we serve over 5 million clients, with more than €50 billion in assets under management - a testament to the scale and trust we’ve built in just a few years.

Trading 212 is built by highly driven people who strive for excellence and value freedom.

We are looking for a Contracts Analyst based in Sofia, Bulgaria, to join our Global Legal team and support the business as we continue to grow.

Что предстоит делать

- Be the first pair of eyes and legal owner of commercial contract reviews across the Group.

- Draft, review, and negotiate a wide range of contracts, including vendor agreements, intra-group agreements, NDAs, and partnership agreements.

- Monitor contract lifecycles, including renewals and terminations, to ensure timely and accurate execution.

- Work closely with internal stakeholders across the Group to gather required information and ensure alignment on contractual terms and execution.

- Ensure that all contracting processes comply with relevant legal regulations, internal policies, procedures, and governance standards.

WHAT YOU NEED TO HAVE

- Strong academics in Law with a genuine passion for contract law and its practical application in a business context.

- At least 2 years of experience in a law firm or in-house role.

- Ability to review and manage contracts across multiple jurisdictions and governed by foreign laws.

- Self-starter mindset - work independently with a strong drive to deliver complete, high-quality outcomes and excellent attention to detail.

- Hands-on, curious approach to staying ahead of legal and regulatory developments.

- Fluency in Bulgarian and English (written and spoken)

Будет плюсом

- Previous experience in a similar position within financial services and in a global environment.

- German language skills.
