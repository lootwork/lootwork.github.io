О роли

We are building Nium's Product Compliance function as a strategic capability that enables safe, scalable global growth.

As a Product Compliance Manager, you will sit at the intersection of Product, Payments, Risk, Compliance, Legal, Engineering and AI. You will help ensure that Nium's products are designed, launched and operated in line with regulatory expectations across multiple jurisdictions.

This role goes beyond interpreting regulation. You will understand how products and payment flows work end-to-end, identify regulatory and financial crime risks, and work with Product and Engineering teams to design practical controls directly into the product and operating model.

We are looking for a builder and systems thinker who can turn complex regulatory requirements into scalable solutions.

Обязанности

Product Advisory & Risk

  • Partner with Product, Business and Engineering teams from ideation through launch and ongoing product evolution.
  • Understand customer journeys, product architecture and end-to-end payment flows.
  • Assess new products, features, payment methods, partnerships and material product changes.
  • Independently lead New Product Risk Assessments (NPRAs) and identify regulatory, financial crime and operational risks.
  • Translate regulatory obligations into clear product requirements and proportionate controls.
  • Challenge product designs where risks are not adequately addressed and help teams develop workable alternatives.

Product Assurance

  • Review existing products and processes for compliance gaps and emerging risks.
  • Assess the impact of new markets, products, payment flows and material changes.
  • Drive remediation of identified gaps and track controls through implementation.
  • Identify systemic risks and reusable solutions across products rather than solving issues individually.
  • Contribute to periodic Product Compliance health reviews and ongoing oversight.

Regulatory Intelligence

  • Monitor regulatory developments relevant to Nium's products and markets.
  • Assess how regulatory changes impact product design, processes and controls.
  • Develop reusable jurisdictional guidance, playbooks and compliance knowledge.
  • Partner with regional Compliance and MLRO teams to translate local requirements into scalable product solutions.

AI & Automation

  • Identify opportunities to use technology and AI to make Product Compliance more scalable and effective.
  • Help develop AI-assisted compliance workflows and knowledge-management capabilities.
  • Identify repetitive compliance activities that can be automated.
  • Contribute to initiatives such as Compliance Copilot and other Product Compliance tooling.

Требования

  • 5–7 years of experience in Product Compliance, Payments Risk, Financial Crime, Product Risk, Product Management, or a similar role within fintech, payments or financial services.
  • Strong understanding of digital products, payment flows and customer journeys.
  • Experience translating regulatory or risk requirements into practical product, process or control solutions.
  • Experience working cross-functionally with Product, Engineering, Business, Risk and/or Compliance teams.
  • Ability to independently manage ambiguous problems and drive them from assessment through recommendation and implementation.
  • Strong written and verbal communication, structured problem-solving and stakeholder management skills.
  • Comfortable influencing teams without relying on formal authority.

Strongly Preferred

  • Cross-border payments and remittances
  • Stablecoins, digital assets or crypto
  • Cards and card issuing
  • Fraud and financial crime
  • KYC/KYB and customer lifecycle controls
  • Multi-jurisdictional regulatory environments

Nice to have: SQL, APIs, workflow/automation tools and experience using AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.
