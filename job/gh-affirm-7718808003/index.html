Security Risk Management Lead

Affirm is reinventing credit to make it more honest and friendly, giving consumers the flexibility to buy now and pay later without any hidden fees or compounding interest.

Affirm values security as being critical to the company’s continued success. Our mission is to cultivate a culture of security at Affirm, enabling the company to succeed in building honest financial products. The Security Risk Management team builds and deploys common governance, risk, and compliance processes and controls, conducts audits, and ensures that technologies and business processes are built with data protection and compliance in mind.

The ideal candidate will design, develop, configure, and implement solutions to complex technical and business issues related to the Security Third Party Program and broader Security Risk Management program. They will work across highly complex functions, interface professionally with business stakeholders, and identify and recommend scalable functional, technological, and control solutions. They should be able to navigate ambiguity, drive multiple initiatives simultaneously, and act as a subject matter expert (SME) within the Security Risk Management team. This person will play a key role in evolving and automating the Security Third Party Program through thoughtful process design, workflow automation, and cross-functional partnership.

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead and mature Affirm’s Security Third Party Program, including the design, implementation, and continuous improvement of processes, controls, and operational workflows
  • Partner closely with Procurement, Legal, Engineering, IT, Compliance, Privacy, and business stakeholders to assess and manage security risk across third party relationships
  • What We Look For
  • 5+ years of experience in Information Security, Risk Management, Engineering and/or relevant roles
  • Previous experience leading the Security Engineering/GRC team/function is a plus
  • Excellent written and verbal communications skills
  • Experience engineering solutions via Python, Claude, Cursor or other agentic coding tooling
  • Experience with industry based information security & control frameworks (NIST Cyber Security Framework, ISO 2700x, SOC1&2(SSAE18), PCI DSS, NIST-800-53, FFIEC Cybersecurity Assessment Tool, SANS Top 20, etc.)
  • BA or BS degree in Information Security, Cyber Security, Computer Science or related field or commensurate experience
  • Attention to detail and experience with security practices and security tooling
  • Demonstrated ability to drive projects towards completion
  • Ability to understand and communicate technical issues to non-technical teams
  • Professional certification in Information Security or Risk Management (such as CISSP, CISM, CISA, CRISC, etc.) is a plus
  • Translate ambiguous business and security requirements into practical, scalable program solutions and decision frameworks
  • Design and implement automated workflows that improve the efficiency, consistency, and traceability of third party security reviews and related risk management activities
  • Identify opportunities to automate manual processes across the program, including intake, triage, evidence collection, tracking, reporting, escalations, and control validation
  • Drive program operational excellence by establishing repeatable processes, service-level expectations, metrics, and reporting for third party security risk management
  • Evaluate third party security controls, architectures, and risk posture, and provide clear recommendations to stakeholders and leadership
  • Conduct a light threat model on high risk integrations and partner with Security SMEs for additional diligence
  • Manage and prioritize a portfolio of complex security risk reviews and initiatives simultaneously, balancing business enablement with risk reduction
  • Partner with technical teams to implement or optimize systems and tools that support program automation and workflow orchestration
  • Develop dashboards, reporting mechanisms, and program insights that improve visibility into risk trends, bottlenecks, and program performance
  • Act as a trusted advisor and SME on third party security risk management, helping stakeholders make informed, risk based decisions
  • Contribute to the broader Security Risk Management strategy by identifying opportunities to scale, simplify, and strengthen security governance processes
