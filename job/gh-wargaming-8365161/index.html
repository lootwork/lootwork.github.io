О вакансии

Wargaming is looking for a proactive, excited and determined Product Analyst to join WoT Analytics team. In this role, you'll work at the intersection of data, product strategy, and business impact, transforming complex questions into actionable insights that directly influence product direction, user experience, and business outcomes.

You'll lead end-to-end analytical projects, partner closely with Product, Engineering, Design, and Business teams, and become a trusted advisor on everything from product performance to user behavior to monetization optimization. Your work will directly support decision-making processes across the organization.

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead exploratory analysis and apply statistical methods to solve complex product and business problems, validating hypotheses with data
  • Work autonomously on end-to-end analysis projects focused on product metrics, feature impact, player behavior, and opportunities to improve engagement, retention, monetization, or product health
  • Define and maintain product metrics, ensuring consistent KPI definitions and clear relationships between engagement, retention, monetization, and overall product health
  • Build dashboards and reports that support product, business, and operational decisions
  • Develop reproducible analytical workflows using modern analytics tools (Python/R, AB test platforms, BI tools, AI)
  • Leverage AI and machine learning techniques to enhance analytical processes, automate insights, and develop predictive models for business needs
  • Frame ambiguous problems into hypotheses and execution plans, aligning stakeholders on scope and success metrics
  • Promote data-driven decision-making and contribute to shared analytical standards, documentation, and best practices
  • Build deep domain expertise in your product area (e.g., monetization, retention, economy, live operations, platform analytics, user acquisition)

Кого мы ищем

  • 3+ years of experience in Product Analytics, Data Analytics, or a related role, preferably in digital products or gaming.
  • Analytical ownership: Ability to turn ambiguous product questions into structured investigations, actionable insights, and recommendations.
  • Strong SQL and Python skills: Proficiency in complex SQL queries and hands-on experience with Python for data processing and analysis.
  • Data visualization: Experience with BI tools such as Tableau, Looker, Power BI, or similar, and the ability to communicate insights through effective visualizations.
  • Statistical expertise: Solid understanding of experimental design, segmentation, hypothesis testing, and statistical methods.
  • AI-driven analytics: Experience applying AI/ML to business and product challenges, automate analytical workflows, and develop predictive solutions.
  • Product mindset and autonomy: Ability to independently navigate ambiguous problems, identify opportunities, and drive analytical projects from exploration to impact.
  • Communication and collaboration: Strong storytelling skills and experience aligning cross-functional stakeholders on analytical scope, success metrics, and recommendations.
  • English proficiency: B2 level or higher, written and spoken.

Что поможет выделиться

  • Experience in gaming, entertainment, or consumer product analytics
  • Experience with AB testing platforms and experimentation best practices
  • Experience working with Snowflake, Cloudera Impala / Hive, Oracle SQL
  • Knowledge and practical application of machine learning algorithms
  • Rich gaming experience in different games and over 1000 World of Tanks battles

Формат работы

  • Hybrid (2-3 days of work from the office)
