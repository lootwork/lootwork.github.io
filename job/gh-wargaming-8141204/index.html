О вакансии

Server Core team develops and maintains critical core services. We work on code that handles game clusters, manages game realm connectivity, stores player data, manages player inventory and assets, distributes game sessions in the most optimized way, and integrates server with external services. Given the large number of concurrent players, our work is important for ensuring stability, and a smooth gaming experience.

As a cohesive team, we value cooperation and problem-solving. If you're passionate about high-load systems and want to shape the future of a globally connected game, we would love to hear from you!

Reports to

Server Core Lead

Что предстоит делать

  • Own complex features end-to-end, from requirements to production
  • Decompose large problems into manageable tasks, define service/module boundaries
  • Ensure scalability, performance, and fault-tolerance
  • Improve engineering culture: tools, testing, observability, automation
  • Mentor mid-level engineers and support team decision-making

Кого мы ищем

  • 5+ years of professional development experience with C++ or other statically typed languages such as C#, Java, or Rust
  • Willingness to work primarily in Python; prior Python experience is a plus
  • Experience building and maintaining complex, stateful backend systems
  • Solid understanding of concurrency, multithreading, and asynchronous execution
  • Experience investigating and resolving performance issues
  • Hands-on experience with databases, query optimization, caching, and messaging
  • Skilled in monitoring, logging, and observability practices
  • Experienced in building reliable, secure backend systems
  • Guides less experienced developers
  • Makes sound technical decisions under ambiguity
  • Ensures delivery of features that create real user value
  • Raises the overall technical quality bar
  • Advanced level of English language

О Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming's titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
