The Robinhood Crypto Compliance team partners across the organization to develop, launch, and scale innovative crypto products and services safely and compliantly. As Robinhood’s primary point of contact for crypto compliance, the team provides real-time regulatory guidance, designs robust controls and testing, leads training and supervisory initiatives, and manages escalations across dynamic market environments.

As the Crypto Compliance Senior Lead, you will serve as the Deputy AML Compliance Officer for Robinhood Crypto (RHC) and Bitstamp USA, while extending strategic compliance support to Bitstamp Global. Reporting directly to the Director of Crypto Compliance, you will play a central role in designing, evaluating, and evolving our AML/BSA and financial crimes compliance frameworks. You will work cross-functionally with product, legal, operations, and engineering leaders to uphold the highest compliance standards as our crypto ecosystem expands.

This role is based in our Denver, Menlo Park, New York, Westlake office(s), with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Governance & Oversight: Serve as Deputy AML Compliance Officer for Robinhood Crypto (RHC) and Bitstamp USA, with advisory and operational support for Bitstamp Global.
  • Program Design & Monitoring: Design, implement, test, and monitor critical AML/BSA, OFAC/sanctions, and anti-financial crime controls to ensure compliance with federal, state, and international regulatory expectations.
  • Cross-Functional Advisory: Provide proactive regulatory advice and guidance to product, operations, legal, and business development teams on emerging crypto offerings and industry best practices.
  • Policy Architecture: Draft, update, and maintain comprehensive compliance policies, procedures, and internal playbooks reflecting evolving laws and business lines.
  • Inquiries & Examinations: Partner with the Director of Crypto Compliance on complex investigations and responses related to law enforcement requests, regulatory inquiries, examinations, and banking partner audits.
  • Issue Management & Remediation: Track, validate, and document the timely remediation of control gaps and audit/testing findings within the firm’s issue management systems.
  • Training & Enablement: Develop specialized crypto compliance training curricula and drive enterprise-wide compliance education programs.
  • Regulatory Horizon Scanning: Monitor global crypto regulatory developments and proactively translate evolving guidance into actionable policy, system, and control enhancements.
  • Special Projects: Lead and contribute to strategic compliance initiatives, product launches, and integration efforts across affiliated entities.

Что вы приносите

  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience, JD a plus but not required
  • 7+ years in a financial crimes (AML/BSA) compliance role at a regulated financial services company, including crypto or blockchain experience
  • Solid understanding of cryptocurrency markets and blockchain technology
  • Familiarity with anti-money laundering regulations: Bank Secrecy Act, NYCRR Part 504, FATF standards
  • Experience leading projects across technical and non-technical teams in a fast-changing environment
  • Exceptional attention to detail, data analysis, and problem-solving skills, with the ability to effectively summarize findings in a succinct and organized manner
  • Execution oriented mindset with the humility, drive, trustworthiness, and resourcefulness to get things done
  • Sound understanding of compliance and operational risks and internal control frameworks
  • CAMS certification, and experience with blockchain analytics software (E.g., Chainalysis, TRM Labs)
