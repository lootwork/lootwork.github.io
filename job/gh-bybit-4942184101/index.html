О вакансии

We are looking for a skilled Java Developer with strong mastery of core backend systems. In this role, you will drive the evolution of our core distributed transaction engine and high-concurrency Instant Messaging (IM) platform.

Beyond deepening core business applications, you will act as a key technical backbone for strategic AI Shopping Assistant projects, exploring the integration of the Java ecosystem with Large Language Models (LLMs) to drive intelligent business transformation.

Основные обязанности

  • Core Java Development: Design and build high-concurrency order processing centers, payment & settlement logic, and IM messaging engines; write high-quality, clean, and maintainable Java code.
  • Architecture Optimization & Evolution: Refactor technical debt, optimize JVM performance, database indexing, and caching strategies; resolve data consistency challenges in distributed environments.
  • AI Implementation: Participate in the architectural design of AI shopping guide/assistant projects, leveraging Spring AI or related frameworks to integrate LLM capabilities for smart recommendations, automated customer support, and other business scenarios.
  • System Stability: Lead core pipeline monitoring and troubleshooting to maintain high system availability during traffic spikes and promotional mega-sales.

Требования

  • Technical Background: 3–5 years of Java development experience with solid CS fundamentals; deep understanding of JVM memory models, GC mechanisms, and multi-threading/concurrency programming.
  • Framework Proficiency: Proficient in Spring Boot, Spring Cloud, MyBatis, and related mainstream frameworks, with a clear understanding of their core principles and extension points.
  • Domain Experience (At least one required):
  • Transaction Systems: Familiar with distributed transactions and state machine design; hands-on experience with complex, financial-grade order or settlement systems.
  • Messaging & Communication: Proficient in Netty network programming with a deep understanding of WebSocket/MQTT protocols; experience in handling push systems with millions of concurrent connections.
  • Middleware & Storage: Proficient in using message queues (RocketMQ/Kafka) for asynchronous decoupling; rich experience in MySQL performance tuning and Redis distributed locks.

Будет плюсом

  • Work Visa Status: Holders of Hong Kong Top Talent Pass Scheme (TTPS) or Quality Migrant Admission Scheme (QMAS) visas—or those eligible to apply—are highly preferred.
  • AI Vision: Attentive to AI industry developments; hands-on experience with LangChain, Vector Databases (e.g., Milvus, Pinecone), or RAG architecture is a strong plus.
