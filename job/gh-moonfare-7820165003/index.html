Investment Content & Communications Manager

Moonfare’s audience doesn’t read for entertainment. Private Office investors, family offices, GP Services partners and sophisticated individuals read for edge — and they can tell institutional-grade thinking from filler in a sentence. This role exists to produce the editorial output that earns their attention: writing they would bookmark and forward to a peer.

You will own Moonfare’s content slate and lead the editorial agenda that carries our point of view on private markets. We have a clear house view; your job is to help set it, research it and bring it to life — from a fund thesis to a market shift to proprietary research. You will be accountable for the signals that matter: finish rate, repeat readership, share rate and newsletter engagement above the editorial threshold. Not page views. The challenge is a sceptical, expert audience and a regulated product. The opportunity is end-to-end ownership of the editorial journey, direct access to investors, GPs and the deal pipeline, and the standing to set the terms of the conversation.

You join Moonfare’s Marketing team — lean and high-output, building the brand, generating demand and driving investor acquisition across global markets. It reports to the Head of Communications & Content and works closely with Investments, Fund Operations, Research and Investor Relations.

Вам предстоит

  • Lead the editorial agenda — driving Moonfare’s point of view on private markets, advising what we should be saying and when, ahead of the cycle rather than in reaction to it, and setting the forward calendar of theses and formats.
  • Set the standard across the slate — researching and producing everything from thought leadership and the annual Outlook to research reports, the newsletter, website copy and video and audio scripts, and defining the bar the rest of the slate is held to.
  • Translate fund opportunities, market events and proprietary research into accurate, well-sourced, investor-facing content — never optimised for SEO at the expense of credibility, never reliant on hype or unsupported superlatives.
  • Run the editorial calendar and production engine, owning the pipeline from brief to publication and building the templates that make each cycle sharper than the last.
  • Partner across the business — sourcing theses with the Investment and Fund teams, shaping and producing the Deal Talk series, supporting our monthly macro call, and coordinating with Research, Brand, Design and external agencies.
  • Hold the line on accuracy and compliance, applying Moonfare’s house style and claims discipline and writing within financial-promotions compliance, so review protects the brand without becoming a bottleneck.
  • Use AI to research, draft and accelerate, while owning the editorial judgment that protects accuracy and keeps the brand voice intact. AI speeds the work; it never lowers the bar.
  • Track the signals that matter and use them to sharpen the slate — knowing what worked, and why.

The next Moonfarian

  • 6+ years writing and editing investor-facing content, with published long-form work you can share
  • Experience in or around financial services — asset management, private markets, wealth management, banking or financial media (required)
  • Working understanding of private equity and private markets
  • Strong research skills — gathers, verifies and synthesises from credible sources
  • Versatile across formats: whitepapers, scripts, newsletters, website copy
  • Confident using AI to research and draft, with the judgement to own what ships
  • Excellent written and verbal English, with the self-direction to hold cadence across formats without dropping the bar. German or French a plus

If you feel you do not meet all of the requirements, we still encourage you to apply!
