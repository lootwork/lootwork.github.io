О Bybit EU

Bybit is one of the world’s leading cryptocurrency exchanges, committed to building a secure, compliant, and transparent digital asset ecosystem. As we expand our regulated European operations from our Vienna headquarters under the MiCA and DORA frameworks, we are looking for an ICT Risk Management Engineer to strengthen our technology resilience, operational risk controls, and regulatory readiness across our European infrastructure.

О роли

As an ICT Risk Management Engineer, you will bridge the gap between technical engineering, cybersecurity, and regulatory risk management. You will be helping to identify, assess, and mitigate Information and Communication Technology (ICT) risks across cloud infrastructure, trading systems, and third-party vendors—ensuring our technology stack meets the highest standards of operational resilience and European regulatory compliance (specifically DORA – Digital Operational Resilience Act).

Основные обязанности

  • ICT Risk Assessments & Control Design: Conduct technical risk assessments across applications, cloud infrastructure, APIs, and core systems; design and implement effective technical controls to mitigate identified vulnerabilities and weaknesses.
  • DORA & Regulatory Compliance: Support and drive technical compliance with European ICT regulatory frameworks, including DORA, PCI DSS and ISO/IEC 27001, translating regulatory mandates into actionable engineering controls.
  • Continuous Monitoring & Governance: Develop risk metrics, risk identification mechanisms, Key Risk Indicators (KRIs), and audit-ready documentation to provide leadership and regulators with clear visibility into our ICT risk posture.
  • Cross-Functional Advisory: Act as a trusted technical advisor to Product, Engineering, Legal, and Compliance teams during new feature launches, architecture reviews, and regulatory inspections.

Qualifications & Experience

  • Experience: 2–4+ years of experience in ICT Risk Management, Information Security Engineering, Technology Audit, or Operational Resilience within banking, fintech, crypto/digital assets, or a Big 4 technology risk practice.
  • Regulatory & Framework Expertise: Hands-on familiarity with DORA, ISO/IEC 27001/27005, NIST CSF, COBIT, or EBA ICT Guidelines.
  • Technical Foundation: Strong understanding of modern cloud architectures (AWS/GCP), network security, access controls (IAM), SDLC security, and resilient distributed systems.
  • Risk & Audit Acumen: Proven ability to conduct technical control testing, manage remediation tracking, and prepare evidence for internal audits or financial supervisory authorities (e.g., FMA).
  • Communication Skills: Fluency in English with the ability to translate complex technical risks into clear, business-focused recommendations for non-technical stakeholders (German is a strong plus).
  • Certifications (Preferred): CISA, CRISC, CISSP, CISM, or ISO 27001 Lead Auditor/Implementer.
  • Work Setup: Ability to work full-time on-site from our Vienna office.
