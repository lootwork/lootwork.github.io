О роли

We're looking for a Technical Program Manager, Customer Experience at Pleo. In this role, you'll help lead complex technical programs at the intersection of Customer Experience, Customer Success, Support, Onboarding, Product, Engineering, Data, Revenue Operations and AI. You’ll help turn Pleo’s CX transformation strategy into scalable technical capabilities that make it easier for customers to onboard, get support, realise value, resolve issues and grow with Pleo.

This is not a traditional project-management role focused on timelines, meetings and status updates. You’ll understand the customer and business problem, get into the technical details, translate requirements between CX and Engineering, challenge proposed solutions, manage technical dependencies, and drive programs from concept through implementation, adoption and measurable impact.

WHO YOU’LL BE WORKING WITH AND REPORTING TO

You’ll report to our Director, CX and work closely with teams in Customer Success, Support, Onboarding, Digital Content and Self-Service, Voice of the Customer, and Revenue Operations. Our team is highly collaborative and dedicated to acting as a technical bridge between CX and Pleo’s Product, Engineering, Data and systems organisations. You’ll also have the chance to partner with teams across Product, Engineering, Data and Analytics, Finance, Risk and Fraud, and Business Systems to ensure success and keep technical decisions tied to customer outcomes and business value.

Что предстоит делать

As a Technical Program Manager, you will

- Lead CX technology programs end-to-end, from problem definition and requirements through design, testing, launch, adoption and optimisation. Own scope, milestones, dependencies, risks and decisions, and confirm after launch that the technology changed adoption, customer impact and operations.

- Partner with CX leadership to define the technical roadmap for the customer experience transformation, and help establish the future-state CX technology architecture for Pleo’s next stage of growth.

- Translate customer and business problems into clear technical requirements and acceptance criteria for Product, Engineering, Data and systems teams, and explain architecture, trade-offs, risks and options to CX leaders in business language.

- Drive integration programs connecting CRM, Customer Success, Support, Onboarding, Product, billing, analytics and AI platforms. Document customer data flows: where data originates, where it moves, which system owns it and how it is used.

- Support a single customer view and improve customer health and risk infrastructure, combining usage, support, onboarding, payment, engagement and sentiment signals. Partner with Data on churn prediction, proactive risk detection and next-best-action.

- Drive AI-enabled CX programs for self-service, agent productivity, onboarding, Customer Success, knowledge retrieval, QA and virtual customer-facing capabilities. Set scope, release criteria and quality gates for the engineers and data scientists building AI agents, without line management of those teams.

- Automate repetitive CX workflows and reduce technical debt across customer-facing systems. Challenge automation that is covering up a broken process.

- Lead technical programs with Support (routing, case management, escalations), Customer Success (health, lifecycle triggers, forecasting) and Onboarding (digital journeys, implementation tracking, time-to-value).

- Own UAT coordination, business readiness and technical program governance for major CX releases, including decision logs, dependency and risk tracking, and architecture decisions. Escalate risks with recommendations, not just status.

- Make sure CX signals reach Product, so recurring friction and customer needs become visible and actionable.

Что вы приносите

You’ll thrive in this role if you have

- 5+ years of Technical Program Management, leading complex technology programs.

- Experience in SaaS, fintech, enterprise technology or another high-growth digital environment.

- Experience managing programs across multiple systems, integrations, APIs, data flows and technical dependencies, where no single team owns the whole solution.

- A solid grasp of the software development lifecycle, from requirements and architecture through testing, release and optimisation, in Agile or hybrid environments.

- Program fundamentals: requirements, scope, roadmap, dependency and risk management, technical governance, stakeholder management, UAT, release and change management, and adoption measurement.

- Strong analytical skills and comfort with operational and customer data.…
