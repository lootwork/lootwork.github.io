Job Description

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON​

What you will focus on

Create polished, technically compliant 3D and VFX content with minimal supervision. Work across modeling, UV mapping, texturing, optimization, and in-engine integration, while creating stylized, performant effects using particles and animated 3D meshes. Contribute to art briefs and visual direction, ensuring assets meet both artistic and technical requirements.

What it means on a daily basis​

  • Create low-poly 3D concepts for characters, environments, and props.
  • Produce high-quality high-poly assets that align with the art brief, project guidelines, and feedback.
  • Manage the full 3D production pipeline, including modeling, UV unwrapping, map baking (normal, specular, etc), texturing, rendering, and in-engine implementation.
  • Create real-time visual effects using a range of techniques, including particle systems, animated 3D elements, stylized propagation effects, shaders, and destruction simulations.
  • Follow the engine’s VFX and technical pipelines, standards, and constraints.
  • Optimize VFX assets and systems to improve performance while maintaining visual quality.

What success will look like

  • Deliver art assets that meet the technical requirements defined by the project’s Art and Technical Leads, ensuring smooth handoff to other artists and seamless integration into the game engine.
  • Adapt quickly to new tools, engines, workflows, pipelines, art styles, and creative directions.
  • Deliver assets within the timelines established by the Art and Project Leads, maintaining consistent progress in line with the expectations of the role’s seniority.

​Who you would be working with

  • You will be working with the Artist team, GD team, Dev team, and QA team
  • You will report to Studio Artist Lead, Producer

Where can this role take you?

  • Progress into specialized roles such as Character Artist, Environment Artist, Technical Artist, or VFX Artist.
  • Explore opportunities to contribute to 2D art, concept design, rigging, and animation.
  • Advance into senior positions, such as Senior 3D Artist, Art Lead, or Art Director, across new and live titles.

Требования

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED​

  • Solid foundation in 3D modeling and VFX creation with a focus on mobile game optimization.
  • Proficiency in industry-standard 3D software, such as Maya, 3ds Max, Blender, or ZBrush, as well as texturing tools like Substance 3D Painter and Photoshop.
  • Understanding of mobile performance constraints, including polygon budgets, texture atlasing, LODs optimization, VFX particle counts, and shader complexity while maintaining the intended visual quality.
  • Strong eye for form, silhouette, color, composition, and visual storytelling across stylized and realistic art styles.
  • Collaborative mindset, with openness to feedback and a willingness to work effectively across disciplines.

Кто вы

  • Take constructive critique positively.
  • You communicate clearly and work effectively.
  • Stay flexible when priorities or pipelines shift.
  • Have a real interest in video games and a keen willingness to learn new tools, pipelines, and techniques.
