О команде

The Company Secretariat (CoSec) team oversees business compliance across specific jurisdictions through integration with local regulatory authorities and third-party services. The team facilitates the timely submission of official filings and corporate modifications while embedding these processes seamlessly into the member journey. By abstracting market-specific legal and operational complexities within the Tide application, the team ensures that members can rely on robust, automated compliance management. Building upon the foundational architecture established in the UK, current initiatives focus on transforming the service into a market-agnostic platform capable of dynamically adapting to diverse data sources. This entails orchestrating complex cross-domain data integrations, ensuring accurate data transformation, and propagating post-filing updates and metadata to downstream systems once external third-party processing is complete.

  • Manage team members, support their professional development, distribute workload, and ensure alignment on deliverable goals.
  • Champion modern engineering practices by transitioning the team toward full-stack development and agentic workflows.
