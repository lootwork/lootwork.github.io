О вакансии

We are looking for an exceptional 3D Character Artist to join our team to be part of World of Tanks: Heat.

Reports to

3D Character Artist Lead

Что предстоит делать

  • Translate concepts into detailed character models, considering aspects such as appearance, costumes, accessories, and overall visual style, form and silhouette.
  • Collaborate with the Art Director, Art Manager and Technical Art to execute the art style.
  • Mentor Junior members of the team both artistically and technically.
  • Uphold the quality of the characters both in terms of consistency and style.

Кого мы ищем

  • 5+ years of industry experience as a 3D Character Artist or demonstration of at least 1 shipped AAA title.
  • Exceptional understanding of videogame 3D character art and related workflows.
  • A strong understanding of human anatomy with a deep understanding of creating realistic humans for videogames including hair and textures.
  • Hands-on experience working in game engines, including asset integration, optimization, and visual polish passes
  • Solid hard surface modeling skills
  • Strong knowledge of creating topology for animation.
  • Knowledge of 3D modelling and texturing software and pipelines (Maya, Blender, Marvelous Designer, Zbrush, Substance Painter, Photoshop).
  • A problem-solving mentality with a team first attitude.
  • Self-motivated with a productive attitude and the ability to work without direct supervision.
  • Good English communication and writing skills.

Please remember to apply with a portfolio

The ideal candidate must have a portfolio showcasing 3D Character Art that also demonstrates -

  • An eye for detail and realism.
  • A good eye for character art and design.
  • Realistic face modelling and anatomy.
  • Clothing that has been created with Marvelous Designer.
  • High poly sculpting and detailing.
  • Lowpoly modeling with realistic materials and textures rendered in a real time engine.
