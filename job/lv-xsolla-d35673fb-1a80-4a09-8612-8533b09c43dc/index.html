О вас

We are looking for a Baku Events Community Manager who is trustworthy, self-driven, motivated, and accountable to join our Global Experiential Marketing team. The ideal candidate is a natural relationship-builder who thrives on turning event moments into lasting community connections across the games industry in the Caucasus and Central Asia (CCA).

This is a regional role: while based in Baku, you will cover the whole CCA region, not only Azerbaijan. You will use your community management, content, and relationship-building skills to grow and nurture Xsolla’s network of partners, developers and clients across the region, working closely with the events team, Social Media Manager, and Marketing colleagues.

The right person for this role will be personable, organized, and confident engaging senior industry stakeholders, with a strong instinct for the kind of content and touchpoints that keep a community engaged between events.

This role is in-office/on-site in Baku and will require regular regional travel across CCA for events.

О нас

Xsolla is a global commerce company with robust tools and services to help developers solve the inherent challenges of the video game industry. From indie to AAA, companies partner with Xsolla to help them fund, distribute, market, and monetize their games. Grounded in the belief in the future of video games, Xsolla is resolute in the mission to bring opportunities together, and continually make new resources available to creators. Headquartered and incorporated in Los Angeles, California, Xsolla operates as the merchant of record and has helped over 1,500+ game developers to reach more players and grow their businesses around the world. With more paths to profits and ways to win, developers have all the things needed to enjoy the game.

For more information, visit xsolla.com.

Longevity Opportunity Vision — Enjoy the game!

Обязанности

  • Build and maintain Xsolla’s CCA community database of partners, developers and clients.
  • Plan and deliver community-driven events to drive community membership and Xsolla awareness across the region.
  • Manage guest lists, invitations and relationship tracking for invitation-only events and roundtables.
  • Plan and run community engagement activities around [key regional events/conferences in CCA] and other regional events.
  • Work with the Social Media Manager and Marketing teams to plan community-focused content and campaigns.
  • Draft copy and messaging for community communications, event invitations and follow-ups.
  • Support event registration pages for community events, including forms, copy and email templates.
  • Liaise with event organizers and local partners across CCA to identify community engagement opportunities.
  • Maintain the event roadmap tracker with community event details, dates and attendee information.
  • Log creative briefs (Jira) for community-facing materials and follow through to completion.
  • Host and support on-site community activities in Baku [и/или Xsolla space, если есть], ensuring a positive guest experience.
  • Capture community feedback and leads, ensuring timely hand-off to CRM and BD teams.
  • Produce post-event community reports, including engagement stats and relationship-building outcomes.

Требования

  • 3+ years of professional experience in community management, events, or marketing.
  • Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Communications, Business Administration, or related field.
  • Proven experience managing relationships with external stakeholders or VIP/member communities.
  • Proficiency in project management tools such as Atlassian Suite, Jira, Asana, Monday.com, and Google Workspace.
  • Fluent English and Russian; Azerbaijani is a strong asset.
