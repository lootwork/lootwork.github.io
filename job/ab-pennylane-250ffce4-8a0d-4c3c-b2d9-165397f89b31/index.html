WHY THIS POSITION IS IMPORTANT

Pennylane builds the financial management and accounting platform used by SMEs and accounting firms in France, Germany and Spain. As a product-led company, we invest first in the product, and AI is now at the core of it.

The ML & AI team (about thirty people in Paris, part of the Data organisation) builds the AI inside the product. Its first priority is Autopilot: automated accounting production with the accountant in control. Autopilot reads documents, books entries, matches bank transactions, reviews client files and prepares VAT returns with as little human intervention as possible.

These tasks raise real ML problems. Answers must be exact, every client is different, accounting constraints can be checked automatically, and we have millions of real examples corrected by accountants. This internship is a chance to work on one of these problems with real data, real users and a direct path to production.

HOW YOU WILL CONTRIBUTE

You will join the ML & AI team, within a Tech organisation of more than 400 people. You will work on one main topic for the whole internship, protected from day-to-day work, with a dedicated tutor.

Every topic follows the same path

- Evaluation first: in the first weeks, you agree with your tutor on a frozen evaluation set and the metrics that matter.

- A baseline in production: you ship a simpler first version that the team can put into production, around months 1 to 3.

- Then go further: you improve it and explore more advanced approaches. Research topics are designed to lead to a publishable result.

You will own the topic end to end: understanding the problem with accountants and product teams, defining success metrics, building, measuring and presenting your results.

TOPICS FOR 2027

Here is an overview of the topics we want to staff in 2027. We agree on the final topic and your tutor with you.

- Reading invoices and new types of documents with compact vision-language models (VLMs): fine-tuning, supervision from e-invoices, reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards.

- Knowing when the AI is right: calibrated confidence, selective automation and model routing for automated accounting decisions.

- Evaluating accounting agents: an open benchmark of long-horizon accounting tasks on simulated client files, with verifiable scoring.

- Learning from accountants' corrections: turning feedback into editable rules, with non-regression guarantees.

- Bank reconciliation: matching one transaction to several documents, as learned graph optimisation.

- An AI agent for Autopilot support tickets: from investigation to a pull request. This is an applied topic for profiles who prefer building systems close to production.

- Data foundations for AI: evaluation datasets, data quality and monitoring, and agents that let teams query and use data on their own.

AI WILL BE PART OF HOW YOU WORK

You will use coding assistants and AI agents every day: to explore the codebase and data models, write Python and SQL, run experiments, write tests and debug.

We expect you to give agents the right context and to check their output. You own the result, not the agent.

OUR CULTURE

You will join a hands-on engineering culture, where two technical co-founders are still among the most active contributors to the codebase.

You will work with ML and AI engineers, data specialists, software engineers, product managers and accountants. You will have strong mentorship and a lot of ownership: freedom to test ideas, challenge existing approaches and build things from scratch, with an R&D mindset.

The goal is not a demo. It is something the team can trust, reuse and ship.

YOU'RE THE RIGHT CANDIDATE IF YOU

- Are in the final year of a Master's degree or engineering school in machine learning, computer science, statistics or applied mathematics. Research internships of at least 4 months are also considered.

- Have strong ML foundations and solid Python, and have already trained, fine-tuned or evaluated models in academic, personal or professional projects.

- Care about evaluation: clean baselines, reproducible experiments and metrics that reflect the real problem.

- Are comfortable with real-world data: SQL, analysis, messy and imbalanced datasets.

- Already use AI ambitiously in your projects and can explain how you verify its output.

- Communicate clearly and enjoy working in a multidisciplinary team. No accounting knowledge is required, only curiosity.

PRACTICAL INFORMATION

- Duration: 5 to 6 months (final-year internship).

- Start date: March or April 2027.

- Location: Paris office, at least 3 days per week on site, together with your tutor.

- Supervision: a dedicated tutor from the ML & AI team.

- Resources: GPUs, API access to frontier models, real data under GDPR-compliant conditions, and our existing evaluation infrastructure.

- After the internship: an opportunity to join Pennylane full-time on a permanent contract.
