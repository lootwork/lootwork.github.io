BitGo (NYSE: BTGO) is the digital asset infrastructure company delivering custody, wallets, staking, trading, financing, stablecoins, and settlement services from regulated cold storage. Since 2013, BitGo has focused on accelerating the transition of the financial system to a digital asset economy. BitGo maintains a global presence and multiple regulated entities, including BitGo Bank & Trust, National Association, the first federally chartered digital asset trust bank owned by a publicly traded company. Today, BitGo serves thousands of institutions, including many of the industry's top brands, financial institutions, exchanges, and platforms, and millions of investors worldwide. For more information, visit www.bitgo.com.

We are seeking an exceptional Data Engineer - E3 to join our India Engineering Development Center. In this high-impact role, you will be a core technical execution powerhouse driving data platform engineering, deep performance optimization, data modeling, and lakehouse architecture for our global institutional crypto custody platform.

As an institutional digital asset custodian handling massive global transaction volume, data engineering is the backbone for real-time streaming pipelines, immutable double-entry financial accounting ledgers, and international regulatory compliance. You will own Tier-1 mission-critical pipelines, modern lakehouse modeling (Medallion Architecture, Apache Iceberg, Snowflake, PySpark, dbt), data governance (Data Products, Data Contracts, Shift-Left Quality Strategy), and operational telemetry (Grafana).

Обязанности

  • Tier-1 Data Pipelines & Lakehouse: Design, build, and maintain fault-tolerant Tier-1 data pipelines using PySpark, dbt, Apache Iceberg, Snowflake, and Medallion Architecture (Bronze/Silver/Gold) for multi-chain transaction events and financial ledgers.
  • Performance & Data Modeling: Optimize PySpark jobs, dbt transformations, Snowflake warehouses, and Iceberg tables through query, memory, storage, partitioning, file compaction, Z-ordering/clustering, and metadata optimization.
  • Lakehouse Migration: Lead migration of high-volume analytical, reporting, and historical workloads from Snowflake to AWS S3 and Apache Iceberg, reducing platform lock-in and compute costs.
  • Data Quality & Contracts: Implement Shift-Left Data Quality using Soda, Great Expectations, and dbt tests, with schema-enforced Data Contracts across ingestion and CI/CD pipelines.
  • Data Products, Lineage & BI: Build SLA-backed Data Products with clear ownership, governance, and end-to-end DataHub lineage, while enabling standardized semantic models for Looker, Tableau, and Power BI.
  • Financial Data & Reconciliation: Develop audit-ready transformations for double-entry accounting ledgers, staking rewards, fee analytics, daily on-chain/off-chain reconciliation, Proof of Reserves (PoR), and regulatory reporting.
  • Observability & Reliability: Build Grafana dashboards and alerting for pipeline freshness, sub-minute latency SLAs/SLOs, error rates, and resource utilization across batch and streaming CDC workflows.
  • AI-Driven Engineering: Leverage LLMs, Copilot, and agentic workflows to automate SQL generation, unit-test creation, engineering workflows, and CI/CD failure triage.
  • Cost & FinOps: Drive Snowflake and AWS S3 compute and storage optimization while maintaining Tier-1 freshness, availability, accuracy, and SLA/SLO requirements.

Skills & Experience

We are looking for teammates who share and practice our values: open communication, transparency, taking ownership, and a high level of craftsmanship. We are looking for coworkers who share our vision and mission: delivering trust in digital assets.

Обязательно

  • 6+ years of intensive, hands-on data engineering experience building and scaling high-throughput distributed platforms in production.
  • Expert mastery of Python, PySpark, SQL, dbt, Apache Iceberg, Apache Airflow, Snowflake, AWS (S3, EMR, Glue, ECS), and Apache Kafka.
  • Deep expertise in dimensional data modeling, Data Vault 2.0, Star Schema, and Medallion Lakehouse design patterns.
  • Proven experience enforcing Data Contracts, programmatic DQ Checks (Soda, Great Expectations), and a Shift-Left Strategy.
  • Production experience with Grafana telemetry/alerting and metadata tracking tools like DataHub.
  • Hands-on experience migrating analytical workloads from proprietary data warehouses to open lakehouse storage (AWS S3 / Iceberg).
  • Active practice utilizing AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot) to accelerate coding, debugging, and dbt modeling.
  • India business hours with intentional overlap for technical discussions with US engineering leaders.

Желательно

  • B.Tech / M.Tech in Computer Science or quantitative discipline.
  • Understanding of multi-chain blockchain primitives (EVM logs, UTXO chains), wallet indexing, or double-entry accounting ledgers.
  • Experience in Financial Services and/or Financial Technology
  • Understanding and strong interest in cryptocurrencies and blockchain industry
  • Experience with large-scale, real-time, and distributed applications
  • Experience with data governance, handling PII, data masking
