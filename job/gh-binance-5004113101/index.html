Обязанности

  • Conduct Second Line of Defence compliance reviews for new and existing corporate customers, including onboarding, periodic review, trigger review, and remediation cases
  • Assess corporate structures, ownership and control, and identify Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBOs) and authorized persons
  • Review and validate corporate documentation, including certificates of incorporation, registers, constitutional documents, proof of address, and other supporting evidence
  • Assess sanctions, PEP, adverse media, and other screening checks on entities and related parties
  • Assess customer risk factors, including jurisdiction, business activity, ownership structure, expected activity, and other relevant indicators
  • Escalate higher-risk, unusual, or complex cases in line with internal procedures
  • Support Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) reviews, where required
  • Ensure customer files are complete, accurate, and supported by a clear audit trail
  • Liaise with internal stakeholders to obtain missing information, resolve issues, and support case progression
  • Contribute to remediation exercises, control enhancements, and process improvement initiatives
  • Maintain awareness of applicable AML, sanctions, and KYB requirements and internal policy updates
  • Support quality assurance, audit, and regulatory review requests, as needed

Требования

  • 3+ years of experience in CDD, KYC, AML, compliance operations, or financial crime compliance
  • Strong understanding of corporate due diligence, beneficial ownership, and customer risk assessment
  • ACAMs or ICA preferred, not mandatory
  • Familiarity with sanctions, PEP, and adverse media screening processes
  • Ability to review and interpret corporate documents across different jurisdictions
  • Strong analytical skills and attention to detail
  • Ability to identify red flags and escalate appropriately
  • Good written and verbal communication skills
  • Ability to manage workload effectively and work to deadlines in a fast-paced environment
  • Comfortable working with case management systems, screening tools, and internal workflows
