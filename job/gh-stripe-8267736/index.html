О Stripe

Stripe, LLC. is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

Обязанности

  • Conducting technical interviews;
  • Creating software, tooling and distributed systems for automating and managing data center network hardware and compute stacks;

Кто вы

Minimum requirements

Must have a Bachelor's degree or foreign equivalent in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field, plus two (2) years of Software Development experience. Position also requires the following:

  • 2 years of experience with AWS cloud infrastructure and Terraform infrastructure automation;
  • 2 years of experience with reliability, scalability, and distributed systems principles;
  • 1 year of experience in Go and Ansible programming languages;
  • 1 year of experience in Puppet host configuration;
  • 1 year of experience in Ruby programming language;
  • 1 year of experience in Python programming language;
  • 1 year of experience in mentorship and leadership of junior software engineers;
  • 1 year of experience performing 24/7 on call rotations to support high-availability software systems;
  • 1 year of experience with monitoring and logging tools;
  • 1 year of experience with computer network concepts;
  • 1 year of experience in the knowledge of Linux operating system and subsystems;
  • 1 year of experience with cloud and application security best practices; and
  • 1 year of experience with PCI compliance monitoring, audit processes, and evidence collection.
