Our Story

Hello there. We’re Zopa.

We started our journey back in 2005, building the first ever peer-to-peer lending company. Fast forward to 2020 and we launched Zopa Bank. A bank that listens to what our customers don’t like about finance and does the opposite. We’re redefining what it feels like to work in finance. Our vision for a new era of banking puts people front and centre — we’ve built a business that empowers everyone to aim high, every day, to move finance forward. Find out more about our fantastic offerings at Zopa.com!

We’re incredibly proud of our achievements and none of it would be possible without the amazing team here. It’s not just industry awards we’re winning, we’ve also been named in the top three UK’s Most Loved Workplaces.

If you embrace unconventional challenges, are unafraid to think differently and are driven to make an outsized impact, you’ll thrive here at Zopa, so join us, and make it count. Want to see us in action? Follow us on Instagram @zopalife

At Zopa, we're redefining how people access finance. Our Point of Sale (POS) business helps customers spread the cost of life's biggest purchases by partnering with hundreds of merchants across the UK to offer seamless lending and payment experiences.

Within the POS Tribe, the Merchant Experience Squad sits at the centre of our B2B proposition. We own the products that merchants interact with every day—from integrations and APIs to ecommerce plugins and onboarding journeys—making it easier for businesses to offer Zopa's products to their customers.

As our merchant proposition continues to evolve, we're looking for a Senior Product Manager to help define what great merchant experiences look like. This is a role with genuine strategic ownership, where you'll partner closely with Engineering, Commercial and Product Marketing to shape product direction, influence investment decisions and become the voice of our merchants across the business.

If you enjoy solving complex B2B problems, balancing commercial opportunities with technical delivery and building products that create meaningful business impact, we'd love to hear from you.

A DAY IN THE LIFE

  • Define and evolve the product strategy for Merchant Experience, balancing immediate commercial opportunities with long-term platform investment.
  • Build trusted relationships with merchants to understand their challenges, identify unmet needs and turn customer insight into product direction.
  • Partner closely with Commercial teams to support merchant growth, improve onboarding and strengthen existing partnerships.
  • Work alongside Engineering to design scalable solutions across APIs, integrations and ecommerce platforms.
  • Make evidence-based prioritisation decisions using customer insight, product metrics and commercial performance.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders across Engineering, Commercial, Risk, Operations and Product Marketing to align on product priorities.
  • Improve how merchant feedback is captured, prioritised and translated into product outcomes.
  • Represent the voice of the merchant across the organisation, ensuring customer needs remain central to strategic decision making.
  • Balance technical investment with commercial opportunities, making thoughtful trade-offs that deliver long-term value.
  • Help shape the future of Zopa for Business as our B2B proposition continues to grow.

О вас

You'll likely bring many of the following

  • Strong experience owning and delivering B2B digital products.
  • A track record of working closely with merchants, partners or business customers rather than purely consumer-facing products.
  • Strong commercial judgement and an ability to balance customer value with business outcomes.
  • Confidence making complex prioritisation decisions using data, insight and product instinct.
  • Excellent stakeholder management skills, with experience influencing Engineering, Commercial and senior leadership teams.
  • Technical fluency that enables you to translate complex technical concepts into clear business outcomes.
  • Strong communication skills and the confidence to challenge priorities constructively when supported by evidence.
  • Experience working within Agile product teams.
  • A collaborative, proactive approach with the ability to operate independently in ambiguous environments.
  • Experience working within regulated or technically complex industries.

ADDED BONUS

It would also be beneficial if you have

  • Experience within Payments, Banking, FinTech or Credit.
  • Knowledge of FCA expectations, KYB requirements or payment scheme rules.
  • Experience using AI tools throughout the product lifecycle for research, prototyping or documentation.
  • Experience working on APIs, integrations or platform products.
