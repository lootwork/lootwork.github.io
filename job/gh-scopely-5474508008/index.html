О роли

Scopely is rapidly growing, and we are looking for a passionate Support Specialist in Barcelona who combines strong technical problem-solving skills with a proactive, relationship-oriented approach to supporting our game teams.

Playgami is Scopely’s internal SaaS platform for games. It provides the tools used by our game teams to operate multiplatform games, engage players and understand the data they generate.

As part of the Playgami Support team, you will provide reliable day-to-day support while also helping game teams understand how to use our products effectively. This means going beyond answering questions and closing tickets: you will seek to understand the context behind a request, identify the team’s underlying needs, provide guidance, follow up on outcomes and proactively look for opportunities to improve adoption and enablement.

This role starts within Support, but offers the opportunity to grow toward more proactive, game-facing and Customer Success-oriented responsibilities. We are looking for someone who is motivated not only by solving the immediate issue, but also by asking: “How can I help this game team succeed?”

Key Responsibilities

You will work with game teams around the world, helping them resolve technical and product-related questions and make effective use of Playgami. Some requests will be reactive, while others will require you to understand the broader context, guide the team toward the right solution and follow through after the immediate issue has been addressed.

You will act as a trusted point of contact between game teams and Playgami, connecting stakeholders with the right internal teams while ensuring that requests are handled with the appropriate level of ownership, urgency and context.

Что предстоит делать

  • Manage incoming requests by providing accurate, timely and thoughtful product and service information.
  • Troubleshoot issues, assess integration work, identify workarounds and ensure the right problems are escalated to the right teams.
  • Evaluate and prioritize requests based on urgency, impact and the needs of the game team.
  • Ask questions to understand the context, goals and underlying needs behind a request, not only the immediate technical problem.
  • Guide game teams on the effective use of Playgami products and help them connect product capabilities to their use cases.
  • Build trusted relationships with stakeholders and become a reliable point of contact for the teams you support.
  • Proactively follow up with game teams after an issue, training or enablement activity to ensure they are able to move forward successfully.
  • Identify opportunities where a game team could benefit from additional guidance, training, documentation or product education.
  • Deliver or coordinate product training and contribute to learning materials, playbooks and other enablement resources.
  • Identify recurring issues, knowledge gaps and adoption opportunities, and share these insights with Product and other Playgami teams.
  • Maintain internal troubleshooting and product documentation.
  • Contribute to improving the way Playgami supports, educates and partners with game teams.

Требования

  • Experience in Customer Support, Technical Support, Customer Enablement or another customer-facing role.
  • Strong curiosity and aptitude for learning digital products, integrations and technologies.
  • Ability to understand customer needs, ask effective questions and suggest practical solutions.
  • Clear written and verbal communication skills.
  • Ability to explain complex concepts in a simple and approachable way.
  • Proactive approach to stakeholder engagement and follow-up.
  • Strong sense of ownership and customer commitment.
  • Ability to manage multiple stakeholders and priorities.
  • High curiosity and motivation to continuously learn.

Будет плюсом

  • Previous experience working with SaaS platforms, digital products or integrations.
  • Formal technical education or experience in a technical environment.
  • Experience delivering product training, enablement sessions or user education.
  • Experience creating documentation, playbooks or learning materials.
  • Experience in gaming, live operations or a similarly complex product environment.
  • Experience in Customer Success, adoption or stakeholder enablement.

Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.…
