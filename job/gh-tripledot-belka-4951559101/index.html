Who are we?

Tripledot Studios is one of the largest independent mobile games companies in the world.

We are a multi-award-winning organisation, with a global 2,500+ strong team across 12 studios.

Our expanded portfolio includes some of the biggest titles in mobile gaming, collectively reaching top chart positions around the world and engaging over 25 million daily active users.

Tripledot’s guiding principle is that when people love what they do, what they do will be loved by others.

We’re building a company we’re proud of. One filled with driven, incredibly smart and detail-orientated people, who LOVE making games.

Our ambition is to be the most successful games company in the world, and we’re just getting started.

Role Overview

We are looking for a talented and experienced professional to join our CentralTech team! You will work on some of the largest titles in Tripledot's portfolio, ensuring the quality of existing features while supporting the development and release of new ones. The role involves close collaboration with the Monetization team, with a strong focus on validating advertising integrations and monetization features. At Tripledot, we play as a team – collaborating closely to deliver high-impact work through clear communication and continuous improvement.

Key Responsibilities

  • Analyze requirements and design high-quality test cases/checklists
  • Execute tests following best QA practices across Android and iOS platforms in Platform, LiveOps & Monetizastion areas
  • Manage bug lifecycle: identification, documentation, prioritization and communication
  • Report the results of the current testing efforts to the project stakeholders
  • Work closely with developers aligning on debug tools and test strategy
  • Investigate production incidents with an analytical mindset
  • Act as a voice for quality suggesting improvements to testing practices and workflows.
