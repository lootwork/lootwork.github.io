Job Description

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON​

What you will focus on

The Localization Team Lead is responsible for overseeing the day-to-day operations, quality, delivery, and performance of the Digital Assets Localization Team, including both 2D and Video. This role ensures the effective localization, adaptation, and delivery of promotional assets across multiple markets while ensuring team development, process improvements, and operational excellence.

What it means on a daily basis

  • Plan, prioritize, assign, and monitor incoming localization requests to ensure timely, accuracy, and high-quality delivery.
  • Oversee the localization, adaptation, resizing, and revision of digital assets, example: store screenshots, welcome screens, in-app assets, user acquisition (UA) creatives, cross-promo materials, trailers, app previews, packaging, and other promotional assets.
  • Act as the primary point of contact between the Localization Team and internal stakeholders, ensuring effective communication and alignment of priorities.
  • Conduct quality assurance reviews of localized 2D and video assets before delivery to ensure adherence to brand, localization, and creative standards.
  • Provide leadership, coaching, mentorship, and technical guidance to team members, supporting their professional growth and development.
  • Manage team performance, onboarding, goal setting, feedback, and employee engagement initiatives.
  • Identify opportunities to improve workflows, implement automation, and optimize localization production processes.
  • Develop, maintain, and update team documentation, guidelines, best practices, reports, and performance metrics.
  • Support resource planning and workload balancing across the team.
  • Perform hands-on localization work when required due to business priorities, workload fluctuations, or urgent deadlines.

​What success will look like​

  • The team consistently delivers localization projects on time and in alignment with business priorities.
  • Maintain efficient and scalable localization workflows across both 2D and video production.
  • High team engagement, retention, and performance.
  • Enable continuous improvement through process optimization and automation initiatives.
  • Build strong stakeholder satisfaction and confidence in localization delivery.
  • Maintain up-to-date documentation and ensure adherence to localization standards and best practices.
  • Reach department KPIs related to quality, delivery, productivity, and operational efficiency.
  • Maintain high-quality localized assets with minimal revisions and rework.

Who you would be working with​

  • You will be reporting to the Creative Agency Team Lead and leading a group of 4 Video/2D Localization Artists.
  • You will be also working alongside 17 designers and artists in the Creative Agency Team (including Graphic Designers, Illustration Artists, Motion Designers, Video Artists, and Project Manager).
  • You will closely collaborate with internal clients, including the marketing team and multiple game teams globally.

Where this role can take you

Advance to a more senior level or pivot horizontally to other roles within the Creative Agency Team or relevant departments, provided qualifications are met.

Требования

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED

  • Demonstrated experience leading creative, localization, production, or design teams.
  • Solid understanding of localization workflows for marketing and promotional assets.
  • Advanced proficiency in Adobe Creative Cloud, including Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, After Effects, and Premiere Pro.
  • Solid understanding of design principles, typography, composition, layout, and visual communication.
  • Experience working with both static and video asset localization.
  • Experience developing processes, documentation, and workflow improvements.

​WHO YOU ARE

  • High attention to detail and commitment to quality.
  • Effective organizational, planning, prioritization, and project management skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple projects, stakeholders, and deadlines in a fast-paced environment.
  • Effective leadership, coaching, and people management skills.
  • Clear and effective English communication skills, both written and spoken.
  • Ability to work with minimal supervision while maintaining a collaborative team environment.
  • Self-motivated, self-directed, and solution-oriented.
