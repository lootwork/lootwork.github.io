Join FxPro: a leading international fintech company. Be a part of our expanding international team, with offices in Limassol, London, Nassau, Dubai, and Yerevan. FxPro boasts a diverse workforce of over 600 employees representing 50 nationalities, making it an exciting and dynamic workplace. At FxPro, we see each team member as an integral part of our success story.

As a Project Manager you will have full responsibility for managing the delivery cycle of assigned projects, from controlling analysis and estimation through release to production, and for the line management of assigned team members within the payments team. In this role, you will own delivery planning and coordination within agreed business priorities. Product strategy and business prioritisation remain with the designated product and business owners.

Обязанности

  • Organising requirements analysis and ensuring that scope, acceptance criteria, assumptions, and dependencies are sufficiently defined and agreed with the relevant stakeholders to support estimation and delivery planning
  • Coordinating estimates with technical leads, analysts, QA engineers, and other contributors, and establishing realistic delivery plans based on team capacity, dependencies, and identified risks
  • Planning and communicating analysis timelines and agreeing delivery commitments once sufficient analysis and estimation have been completed
  • Managing team capacity, workload, and work sequencing within agreed business priorities, taking into account ongoing commitments and cross-team dependencies
  • Managing changes to project scope and assessing their impact on timelines, capacity, dependencies, and quality; obtaining the necessary stakeholder agreement before revising commitments
  • Coordinating dependencies and delivery commitments with other team managers
  • Coordinating testing and business acceptance, ensuring that agreed acceptance criteria are validated and that outstanding defects and issues are resolved or explicitly accepted by authorised stakeholders before release
  • Providing timely and accurate visibility into project status, delivery forecasts, risks, dependencies, and decisions required from stakeholders
  • Taking corrective action when delivery commitments are at risk, including replanning work within agreed priorities, proposing scope adjustments or phased delivery, and escalating decisions requiring changes to deadlines, priorities, or resources
  • Reviewing delivery performance and implementing improvements to planning, coordination, predictability, and ways of working
  • Providing line management for assigned direct reports, including technical leads, systems analysts, and QA lead.
  • Setting clear individual objectives and expectations, providing regular feedback, conducting performance reviews, and addressing performance concerns in accordance with Company procedures.
  • Supporting employee development through individual development plans, coaching, and the identification of relevant learning opportunities.
  • Supporting team motivation, effective collaboration, and sustainable workload, and addressing team issues or escalating them where necessary.

Требования

  • 3+ years experience in delivering complex software projects in cross-functional environments
  • Strong skills in prioritization, stakeholder management, and setting expectations
  • Experience with Agile, Scrum & Kanban methodologies
  • Experience in Forex, igaming or any other high risk industry is a big plus
  • Experience in the payment domain is a big plus
  • An outcome-focused mindset that values real impact over simply completing tasks
  • Clear communication skills and a collaborative, empathetic leadership style
  • Fluency in English
  • Ability to anticipate risks, manage challenges, and ensure predictable delivery
  • Ability to balance business requirements with technical constraints to make sound decisions quickly

  • Technical interview with 2 Project Managers (~60 minutes)
  • Final interview with Head of Backend Development (~60 minutes)
