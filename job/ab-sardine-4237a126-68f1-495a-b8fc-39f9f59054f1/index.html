Кто мы

Sardine is the leading agentic risk platform for fighting financial crime. Our integrated solution unifies data across risk teams to help organizations stop fraud in real time, prevent AI-driven attacks, and automate fraud and AML operations. Sardine’s platform is strengthened by one of the fastest-growing fraud consortiums in the market, spanning more than 6 billion profiled devices, 800 million consumers, and 3 million businesses worldwide. Leading companies including FIS, GoDaddy, Intuit, Edward Jones, ZoomInfo, and Checkout.com rely on Sardine to secure and grow trust in their products.

Our culture

- We hire talented, self-motivated individuals with extreme ownership and high growth orientation.

- We value performance and not hours worked. We believe you shouldn't have to miss your family dinner, your kid's school play, friends get-together, or doctor's appointments for the sake of adhering to an arbitrary work schedule.

О роли

As we expand globally, we’re looking for passionate QA automation engineer. You’ll be part of global QA team, working closely with both global team and client-facing team in APAC region. This role requires business-level English and Japanese skills.

You will design a clear set of test scenarios, decide what to automate and what to keep manual, and work with stakeholders across Engineering, Product, Professional Services, and our clients, including banks.

Что предстоит делать

- Own automated coverage for APIs and client-facing dashboard in sandbox and production

- Be the named QA on APAC bank integrations: structured test scenarios, UAT support, regression tests.

- Set the automation strategy for the surface you own: what runs in CI, what stays exploratory, and what is better left manual.

- Work with engineers across the full SDLC and shift left. Join design and planning so quality is in the conversation before code is written.

- Find, record, and document defects so engineer and customer partners can both act on them.

- Partner with Professional Services and Post-Sales on structured acceptance testing for bank and enterprise deployments.

- Own the health of the suites you run in CI so failures are real and actionable.

WHAT YOU'LL BRING

- Strong hands-on automation (API first, in TypeScript or JavaScript), with a clear strategy for what to automate. Our suites run on Cypress; Playwright or similar experience transfers.

- You can organize test scenarios so someone else can read the map: scope, priority, automation vs. evidence.

- 5+ years in software quality, including owning a product or integration end to end.

- Great written and verbal communication skills in English and Japanese for both internal and external communication.

- Strong ownership on everything around quality

- Hands-on with CI and a habit of keeping suites maintainable.

- Experience in Software Engineering is a plus

- Experience in working SaaS company is a plus

Nice to have: experience with banks or regulators in APAC (for example data privacy under APPI in Japan); Cypress or similar for the occasional UI path; reading Go (our services and their unit tests); Qase or another test-management tool; prior file-based or batch testing.
