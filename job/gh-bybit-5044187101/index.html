О роли

This strategic technical branding role sits within the CTO Office, with a core mission to build the CTO’s external technical influence from the ground up.

The ideal candidate combines strategic thinking, structured communication, and technical understanding to translate the expertise of the CTO and engineering teams, along with the company’s core technical capabilities, into clear, credible, and impactful narratives for external audiences.

This is not a conventional brand operations role or simply a writing position. Genuine technical understanding is essential—you must be able to understand engineering discussions, assess the accuracy of technical content, and engage directly with the CTO on technical topics. Coding skills are not required, but your capabilities must go beyond marketing copywriting.

Основные обязанности

  • Build the CTO’s technical brand from the ground up, including positioning, content strategy, communication channels, and publishing cadence.
  • Help the CTO articulate an external technical narrative and establish a consistent, distinctive voice over the long term.
  • Develop and produce technical articles, white papers, industry perspectives, speeches, interview scripts, media materials, and social media content.
  • Track trends across Web3, crypto exchanges, AI, security, and system reliability, and recommend relevant topics and perspectives to the CTO.
  • Benchmark the technical branding initiatives of peer exchanges, generate competitive insights, and align actionable recommendations with the CTO.
  • Collaborate with engineering, product, risk, security, PR, and compliance teams to identify technical strengths and ensure content accuracy and compliance.
  • Participate in relevant technical discussions, major project reviews, and reliability governance discussions to capture authentic engineering insights.
  • Proactively structure ambiguous problems, drive cross-functional alignment, and translate ideas into executable plans.

Требования

Обязательно

  • Experience within the past five years at McKinsey, BCG, or Bain (MBB) at the Associate level or above, or in strategy, business strategy, business performance analysis, or strategic operations at major technology companies such as ByteDance, Tencent, or Pinduoduo.
  • Genuine technical understanding, with the ability to follow engineering discussions, assess the accuracy of technical content, and engage directly with the CTO on technical topics.
  • Excellent structured thinking, problem-solving skills, business judgment, and written and verbal communication skills in both Chinese and English.
  • Experience driving complex initiatives from concept to execution, with the ability to exercise independent judgment and deliver results when the direction is not fully defined.
  • Ghostwriting experience, with the ability to develop content from the perspective of a CTO or senior technology executive rather than centering your own voice.
  • A portfolio of publicly available work, such as technical blog posts, white papers, speeches, industry reports, or brand campaigns.
  • A proactive, passionate approach, strong ownership, and the ability to learn quickly and close knowledge gaps in unfamiliar areas.

Желательно

  • Experience in Web3, crypto, or FinTech.
  • Experience managing developer communities or promoting open-source projects.
  • Experience working on technology-related initiatives at technology companies, FinTech firms, exchanges, or major tech companies.
  • Familiarity with incident reviews, system reliability, risk governance, or post-mortem processes.
  • An active personal presence publishing technical content.
