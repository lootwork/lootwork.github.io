Riot Games is looking for a Senior Security Engineer to strengthen and scale our information security risk management capabilities across enterprise environments, third-party relationships, business processes, and emerging technologies.

This role focuses on independently identifying, assessing, and managing complex security risks across Riot's global business. You will evaluate technologies, architectures, and business processes, identify threats and control gaps, and provide practical recommendations that balance security requirements with business objectives.

We are looking for an experienced security professional with broad technical expertise, strong analytical judgment, and an engineering mindset. Beyond traditional risk assessments, you will leverage automation, systems integration, and data analytics to improve how security risks are identified, monitored, measured, and managed.

You will report to the Senior Manager, Security Risk Engineering.

Обязанности

Security Risk Assessment & Management

  • Independently lead security risk assessments and threat modeling across business processes, technologies, applications, infrastructure, system integrations, third-party engagements, and AI-enabled solutions.
  • Evaluate security architectures, configurations, data flows, and technical controls through design reviews, evidence analysis, and hands-on validation to identify realistic attack paths and material security risks.
  • Assess third-party providers, their security posture, data protection practices, and integration risks, providing secure-by-design guidance and practical mitigation recommendations.
  • Support third-party security incident response by coordinating with internal incident response teams, vendors, and business stakeholders to assess exposure, contain risks, and track remediation and corrective actions.
  • Incorporate emerging threats, real-world attack techniques, and incident learnings into risk assessments and security recommendations.
  • Translate technical findings into prioritized remediation plans, influence risk-based decisions, and partner with stakeholders to drive risks through remediation or formal acceptance.
  • Improve security assessment methodologies, control requirements, and risk management processes to strengthen consistency and effectiveness.
  • Conduct occasional on-site security assessments requiring domestic or international travel (up to 15%).

Security Risk Engineering & Analytics

  • Develop or support automation and system integrations to improve risk assessments, evidence collection, control validation, and continuous security monitoring.
  • Aggregate and analyze security data to identify risk trends, control gaps, and changes in security posture.
  • Develop qualitative and quantitative risk metrics, dashboards, and reporting to communicate risk exposure, control effectiveness, and remediation progress.
  • Leverage emerging technologies, including AI, to improve risk identification, assessment efficiency, and ongoing monitoring.

Required Qualifications

  • 5+ years of experience in information security risk management, security engineering, security architecture, third-party risk management, or related technical security disciplines.
  • Demonstrated expertise in independently conducting complex technical security assessments and threat modeling, including evaluating architectures, validating security controls, and identifying realistic attack scenarios.
  • Broad technical expertise across cloud, network, infrastructure, identity and access management, endpoint, application, and data security, with the ability to critically evaluate unfamiliar technologies.
  • Proficiency in at least one programming or scripting language, such as Python, Go, or PowerShell, to support technical investigations, security assessments, and automation.
  • Strong understanding of security threats, attack vectors, vulnerabilities, and mitigation strategies, including the ability to apply emerging threat intelligence to practical risk assessments.
  • Experience applying recognized security frameworks and assessment methodologies, such as NIST, ISO 27001, CIS Controls, or similar standards.
  • Experience assessing security risks associated with third-party services, emerging technologies, and AI-enabled applications or integrations.
  • Strong analytical judgment, communication, and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to independently navigate ambiguity and influence security decisions.

Desired Qualifications

  • Experience with security engineering, hands-on technical testing, application and API security, automation, or continuous control monitoring.
  • Experience with security data engineering, risk quantification, and visualization using SQL, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, or similar technologies.
  • Experience implementing or improving enterprise risk management, third-party risk management, or GRC platforms.
  • Experience with AI security, AI governance, or AI-assisted security risk management.
  • Familiarity with content security principles, digital asset protection, and content creation, production, and distribution pipelines.
  • Security certifications such as CISSP, CISA, CRISC, CCSP, or equivalent.

For this role, you'll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes your fellow Rioters, who are the customers of your work. Being a dedicated fan of games is not necessary for this position!
