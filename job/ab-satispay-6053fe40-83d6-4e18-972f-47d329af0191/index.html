О нас

Что предстоит делать

As our Senior Strategic Project Manager within the Office of the CEO, you will be a primary execution driver behind our most critical cross-functional initiatives. You will serve as the strategic bridge connecting executive leadership with operational, regulatory, product, and business teams across our Luxembourg and Milan hubs.

While you will work side-by-side with internal Legal, Compliance, and Risk experts who own the regulatory subject matter, your core strength is senior strategic leadership and project execution: acting as a key leader within the organization to orchestrate complex initiatives across different verticals, driving high-impact project timelines, and turning corporate obligations into seamless operational workflows.

Here's what that looks like in practice

- Solve complex business challenges - Tackling intricate business problems by developing innovative, data-backed solutions with a consistent user-first approach.

- Stakeholder management and strategic partnership - Managing relationships with C-level stakeholders and acting as a thought partner for leadership on specific strategic areas.

- Strategic influence - Influencing major decisions through in-depth analysis and proven insights, integrating diverse perspectives into coherent strategies.

- Champion methodology and best practices - Developing and introducing new methodologies, best practices, or frameworks within the team or company, ensuring a consistent user-first approach.

- Champion Regulatory Requirements in Roadmap Design - Act as the project manager champion for what regulatory and supervisory requirements apply to a given initiative, ensuring they are proactively factored into project roadmaps at the design stage - partnering closely with Legal, Compliance, and Risk.

- Govern & Monitor Multi-Hub Projects - Establish project governance, track milestones across offices, proactively remove cross-departmental bottlenecks, mitigate delivery risks, and provide crisp, data-driven reporting to executive leadership.

- Anchor Luxembourg Operational Synergies - Serve as an authoritative on-the-ground project leader in Luxembourg, ensuring that financial institution governance requirements seamlessly harmonize with Satispay’s group-wide strategy and product velocity.

Who we're looking for

This role needs someone who is a problem-solver that loves teamwork, gets things done, and is ready for real ownership across regulated financial environments.

- Experience - Minimum 6–7 years of hands-on experience within a regulated environment -ideally within high-growth Fintech, Scale-ups, EMI/Banking institutions, or Financial Services.

- Regulatory Expertise - Hands-on knowledge and deep familiarity with compliance, legal, and regulatory matters.

- Master Project Operator - Proven track record of managing complex, end-to-end multi-stakeholder initiatives using structured project management frameworks.

- Fintech & EMI Licensing Awareness - Good high-level familiarity with the fintech landscape, EMI/payment institution dynamics, and European financial regulation concepts (PSD2/PSD3, AML/CFT, GDPR) — enough to translate requirements into project delivery without needing to be the legal specialist yourself.

- Cross-Border Leadership - Demonstrated ability to build consensus, establish momentum, and align priorities across distributed international offices and matrixed teams.

- Executive Presence - Exceptional communication skills with the ability to synthesize technical, operational, and regulatory concepts into clear, executive-level narratives and actionable plans.

- Mobility - Based in Luxembourg with a willingness for frequent, hands-on collaboration travel to our Milan headquarters.

- Languages - Fluent in English (written and spoken). Italian is a strong asset.

Don't worry if you don't tick every box. We believe in the power of different viewpoints and strengths. Your unique perspective is important as we build something special: if you're passionate and can make a difference, we truly encourage you to apply.
