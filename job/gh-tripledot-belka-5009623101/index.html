Кто мы

Tripledot Studios is one of the largest independent mobile games companies in the world.

We are a multi-award-winning organisation, with a global 2,500+ strong team across 12 studios.

Our expanded portfolio includes some of the biggest titles in mobile gaming, collectively reaching top chart positions around the world and engaging over 25 million daily active users.

Tripledot’s guiding principle is that when people love what they do, what they do will be loved by others.

We’re building a company we’re proud of. One filled with driven, incredibly smart and detail-orientated people, who LOVE making games.

Our ambition is to be the most successful games company in the world, and we’re just getting started.

Работа в студии Ludios

О Ludios

Ludios takes its name from Latin; it means "The Game People". Led by Eyal, one of the co-founders of Tripledot Studios, and some of the most experienced people in mobile games, we are a studio that makes and publishes our own casual, free-to-play mobile games.

Our team is based across APAC, with the majority of our development happening in Jakarta and Melbourne. We are data-driven, creative, and genuinely passionate about the industry we work in.

Right now, we are working on a number of exciting projects, including one built around a globally recognised IP, alongside several original titles we are equally excited about.

If crafting games and reaching millions of players is what gets you out of bed, we would love to hear from you.

О роли

As our Project Manager, you will be the operational heartbeat of the studio. You will bridge the gap between vision and execution, managing the development lifecycle to ensure our team delivers a world-class gaming experience to millions of players worldwide.

This role sits within our dynamic Jakarta studio, where we are embarking on our biggest, most ambitious project yet: a massive live-ops mobile game developed in direct collaboration with a world-renowned, household-name IP.

Основные обязанности

  • Run multiple concurrent projects with shared resources.
  • Manage Jira and dashboards including burndown charts, estimations, and priorities.
  • Refine and groom tasks and backlogs.
  • Run daily stand-ups, sprint planning, post mortems, etc.
  • Deploy and balance resources and workload to optimize velocity and quality.
  • Create studio transparency across the whole development cycle.

Обязательные навыки и опыт

  • Amazing communication skills with the ability to articulate issues, requirements, and
  • vision.
  • Ability to inspire teammates, motivate others, and achieve team
  • deadlines.
  • Experience producing and releasing games.
  • Experience owning and running development processes (e.g., SCRUM).
  • Experience with designing, planning, and executing Product and Game roadmaps.
  • Fluent in industry-standard management tools such as JIRA or similar.
  • You enjoy working closely with artists, game designers, and developers.
  • Professional-level English.
