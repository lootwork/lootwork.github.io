Lithic is the modern card issuing and processing platform empowering ambitious financial companies to build the future of payments.

Our infrastructure powers card programs for 100+ innovative clients, from fintechs reimagining credit and digital banking to platforms transforming disbursements and spend management. Companies like Mercury, Flex, and Novo rely on Lithic's developer-friendly APIs, direct network connections, and flawless reconciliation to launch and scale card programs in weeks, not years.

We're building a future where access to better financial products materially improves people's lives, free from the constraints of 30-year-old mainframes and legacy processors. We're proud to be backed by world-class investors who share that vision, including Bessemer Venture Partners, Index Ventures, Spark Capital, Stripes, and Mastercard, along with many others.

We're a team of 170+ across 26 states and 7 countries, headquartered in New York City.

Our Risk & Compliance team is hiring a Senior Compliance Analyst to own the judgment calls in our KYC/KYB, CDD/EDD, and sanctions programs. You'll make the decisions on escalated cases, handle the complex due diligence that needs senior judgment, and set the quality bar for first-line review. You'll report to our Senior Compliance Manager and work alongside our Lead Compliance Analyst, who owns review standards and bank-facing diligence. You’ll work alongside business facing, revenue generating teams and have a direct impact on the customer experience for some of Lithic’s largest enterprise clients. This is not a back office seat, Risk & Compliance at Lithic is a growth function.

Что предстоит делать

You will be the senior decision-maker for due diligence and sanctions escalations and the quality owner for first-line review. You'll also work the queue directly as needed, so you know the work before you oversee it.

Core responsibilities include

  • Make final decisions on escalated KYC/KYB and CDD/EDD cases: complex beneficial ownership (UBO) structures, foreign parents, high-risk or unclear industries, and client risk rating approvals
  • Own Enterprise due diligence for program manager onboarding end-to-end, and sign off on processor-only onboarding and periodic refreshes
  • Adjudicate escalated sanctions, PEP, and adverse media matches, and escalate true OFAC hits with a clear written rationale
  • Own sanctions program governance: screening thresholds, watchlist configuration, blocked account review, and annual summary reporting
  • Run quality review over first-line casework and alert dispositions, track remediation, and document your reasoning in a form that holds up to bank partner and regulatory review
  • Conduct vendor and third-party due diligence reviews
  • Execute CIP testing for programs that run their own KYB/KYC
  • Help write and update SOPs and runbooks
  • Provide case documentation and samples for bank partner audits and exam prep
  • Identify process gaps and propose improvements

What You'll Need

Minimum Qualifications

  • 3-5 years of compliance experience in fintech, payments, or a bank-partnered environment, with direct KYC/KYB and CDD/EDD casework
  • Hands-on experience with sanctions/OFAC screening and alert adjudication

Experience performing quality review over other analysts' casework

  • Experience with KYC/KYB tools (Persona or similar)
  • Solid grasp of the regulatory framework behind financial products (BSA/AML, USA PATRIOT Act CIP/CDD, beneficial ownership rules, OFAC)
  • Self-starter who can work independently and knows when to bring others in
  • Strong written and verbal communication
  • Comfort using AI tools in your work and checking their output critically
  • CAMS certified or actively working toward it

Будет плюсом

  • Regulatory compliance experience, including working knowledge of consumer protection rules (UDAAP, Reg E, Reg Z) and support for regulatory exams or audits
  • Background in high-risk verticals (MRBs, crypto-adjacent businesses, or similar)
  • Experience in card issuing, payments, or fintech with exposure to sponsor bank relationships
  • Experience working with outsourced or managed-service review teams
