HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

You'll help drive the development and implementation of Airwallex's Operational Risk Management Framework globally. As part of the Second Line of Defence, you'll provide independent, consolidated oversight of how the First Line identifies, assesses, and manages risk across Group entities.

- Develop and support the RCSA and incident management processes by maintaining the methodologies, standards, policies, taxonomy, guidance, templates, and procedures used for risk and control self-assessments and operational risk events.

- Provide independent review and challenge of First Line risk assessments, control ratings, incident classifications, root-cause analyses, and remediation plans, ensuring they’re timely, evidence-based, proportionate, and comparable across the business.

- Turn RCSA, incident, and loss data into clear management information by identifying trends, themes, and systemic control weaknesses, escalating repeat issues, and producing reporting and recommended actions for governance forums and relevant risk committees.

- Support the product approval and change risk framework by ensuring product changes are assessed for conduct and regulatory risk implications, monitoring risk sign-offs and post-implementation reviews, and escalating overdue actions.

- Maintain a clear 1LoD / 2LoD line by keeping ownership and accountability between First Line risk and control owners and Second Line oversight explicit and well documented.

- Enable the First Line through practical guidance, workshops, training, and coaching on risk identification, assessment, incident reporting, and root-cause methodologies, so RCSAs become useful management tools and high-quality incident reporting becomes business as usual.

You’ll report to the Head of Operational Risk and work from our London or Amsterdam office three days a week.

Кого мы ищем

ESSENTIALS

- 7+ years of progressive experience in operational risk management and controls within a financial services group, with proven experience in a Second Line (2LoD) capacity.

- Experience designing and implementing RCSA programmes in a regulated financial services environment.

- Hands-on experience running or overseeing operational risk event and loss data processes, including taxonomy, categorisation, and data quality assurance.

- Strong understanding of control design and control effectiveness assessment, including how residual risk is rated and challenged.

- Direct experience building or deploying AI agents.

- Strong understanding of Group and local regulatory expectations relating to operational risk and control frameworks, and how to apply Group-wide methodologies to local entity requirements.

- Ability to analyse risk data, identify trends and emerging issues, and communicate findings clearly.

- Strong facilitation, communication, and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to challenge constructively and influence senior First Line leaders.

- Ability to produce clear, practical, and well-maintained framework documentation, standards, and guidance.

- Practical experience enabling the First Line through risk guidance, workshops, training, and coaching.

Желательно
