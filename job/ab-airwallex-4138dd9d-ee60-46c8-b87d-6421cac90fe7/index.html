О команде

The Global Risk team at Airwallex plays a critical role in enabling the company's growth by building a strong, scalable, and globally consistent approach to risk management.

The team works across the business to define risk appetite, develop frameworks and policies, support strategic decision-making, and provide oversight of key risks as Airwallex expands across products, markets, and regulatory environments.

You'll be part of a highly collaborative group that partners closely with Product, Engineering, Operations, Commercial, Financial Crime Compliance, Regulatory Compliance, and Legal teams to help Airwallex grow responsibly and with confidence.

This is a team where you'll tackle complex, high-impact challenges, shape how risk is managed at a global fintech, and help build the foundations for long-term scale.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

You'll own the Group's Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA) and Incident Management framework as part of the Second Line of Defence, and provide independent, consolidated oversight of how the First Line identifies, assesses, and manages risk across Group entities.

- Own the Group RCSA and incident management frameworks: Maintain the methodologies, standards, policies, taxonomy, guidance, templates, and supporting procedures for the Risk and Control Self-Assessment (RCSA) and for identifying, recording, investigating, and reporting operational risk events, keeping them current and aligned with Group operating models, risk appetite, local entity requirements, and relevant regulatory expectations.

- Drive consistency across the Group: Promote consistent risk assessment, incident classification, and ownership across business units and Group entities.

- Provide independent review and challenge: Challenge First Line risk assessments, control ratings, incident classification, root-cause analysis, and remediation plans, ensuring they are timely, evidence-based, proportionate, and comparable across the business.

- Govern the lifecycle and drive actions to closure: Support governance of RCSA frequency, approvals, and evidence standards, and track actions arising from RCSAs and incidents through to completion, escalating overdue items with clear accountability.

- Turn data into insight and decision-ready MI: Analyse RCSA, incident, and loss data to identify trends, themes, and systemic control weaknesses, escalate repeat issues to the right owners and governance forums, and produce clear reporting and recommended actions for Operational Risk reporting, governance forums, and relevant risk management committees.

- Support regulatory notification: Work with Regulatory Compliance, Regulatory Legal, and First Line teams so material incidents are assessed and notified to the relevant regulators on time, in line with Group and local regulatory expectations.

- Enable the First Line: Give practical guidance on risk identification, assessment, incident reporting standards, and root-cause methodologies. Run workshops, training, and coaching so that RCSAs are a useful management tool rather than a compliance exercise and high-quality incident reporting becomes business as usual.

- Maintain a clear 1LoD / 2LoD line: Keep ownership and accountability between First Line risk and control owners and Second Line oversight explicit and well documented.

You'll report to the Head of Operational Risk and work from our London or Amsterdam office three days a week.
