О роли

The designated Chief Operating Officer (“COO” - Preapproval Controlled Function [“PCF”]-42) is accountable for the management and oversight of the operational functions of Trading 212 Money Europe Limited (the “Company”).

The COO will lead the Operations team and is responsible for designing, implementing and maintaining the Company’s Operational Framework. This means ensuring that the Company’s products and services are delivered safely, efficiently, and in accordance with the European Directives; Irish legislation; and the requirements of the Central Bank of Ireland. The role covers end-to-end day-to-day operations across the Company’s products, services and regulatory footprint, including:

- Customer operations;

- card programmes;

- payments and payment scheme operations;

- treasury and safeguarding;

- reconciliations; and

- operational and regulatory reporting

The COO is responsible for the Company’s operational resilience, including business continuity; incident management; and oversight of outsourced and third-party service providers, both intra-group and external. The COO is the point of internal escalation for operational issues. Working closely with Compliance, Risk, Product, and Technology, the COO ensures that new products and changes are built with the right controls, automation, and processes from the start. The COO also serves as a point of contact for external stakeholders, including the Central Bank of Ireland; card schemes; banking and safeguarding partners; and key service providers.

Что предстоит делать

- Develop, implement, and maintain the Company’s Operational Framework to ensure products and services are delivered in accordance with regulatory and legislative requirements.

- Oversee day-to-day operations, including customer management, card programs, payments, treasury, safeguarding, reconciliations, and reporting.

- Prepare clear dashboards and detailed reports for the Board and global governance committees.

- Lead the Operations team, supporting managers and fostering a culture of operational excellence.

- Serve as a trusted advisor, offering expert guidance on new products and initiatives. Work closely with compliance, risk, product, and technology teams to build the right structures, automation, and processes into each design.

- Help foster a strong, organisation-wide culture of compliance and best practices.

WHAT YOU NEED TO HAVE

- Professional certifications such as QFA, ACCA, PMP/Prince, and/or equivalent are an advantage.

- Educated to a degree level in Finance, Business, Technology, or a related field is desirable.

- 5+ years of senior Operations experience in Irish-regulated financial services (Payments) would be highly desirable.

- Prior experience in a Controlled Function or PCF role would be an advantage.

- Experience with retail clients and/or consumers is a requirement.

- In-depth knowledge of European and Irish legislation, regulations, and guidance relating to European Payment Services and Electronic Money Directives.

- Deep knowledge and demonstrable experience in developing and implementing operational frameworks, including policies, procedures, controls, Quality Control, and product launches.

- High standards of conduct and ethics.

- A “right first time” attitude and excellent attention to detail.

- Capable of working independently or as part of a team.

- Strong communication skills, both written and verbal.
