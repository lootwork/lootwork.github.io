We’re building Monopoly World – a game that combines iconic gameplay with real-world exploration. To make this world come alive, we’re looking for a Game & Marketing Artist – someone with versatile creative skills who feels at home both in the game and outside of it.

You’ll be switching hats between creating assets for gameplay and visuals that sell the game to the world. If you enjoy variety and love jumping between UI elements, game assets, and promo banners – this role is for you.

Check out our trailer for the Monopoly World project

Join us full-time at our stunning office located in the heart of Krakow! Situated in a historic building just steps away from the vibrant city center, our workspace blends timeless charm with modern amenities, providing an inspiring setting for your personal and professional growth.

In-Game

  • Design and produce 2D/3D assets, UI elements, icons, and illustrations.
  • Help maintain a consistent visual style across the game.
  • Collaborate with designers and developers.
  • Optimize assets for mobile performance.

Marketing & Branding

  • Create promotional graphics for the App Store, Google Play, social media, and ad campaigns.
  • Work with the product and UA teams to develop strong visual communication.
  • Contribute to the overall visual identity of the game and its presence in marketing.
