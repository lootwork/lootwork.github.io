Кто мы

We simplify wealth creation. Founded in 2014 in Vienna, Austria by Eric Demuth, Paul Klanschek and Christian Trummer, we’re here to help people trust themselves enough to build their financial freedom — for now and the future. Our user-friendly, trade-everything platform empowers both first-time investors and seasoned experts to invest in the cryptocurrencies, crypto indices, stocks*, precious metals and commodities* they want — with any sized budget, 24/7. Our global team works across different cultures and time zones, bringing our products to more than 7 million customers, making us one of Europe’s safest and most secure platforms that powers modern investing.

Headquartered in Austria but operating across Europe, our products are built by fast-moving, talented, “roll-up-your-sleeves-and-make-it-happen” kind of people. It’s these diverse perspectives and innovative minds operating as ONE TEAM that keep Bitpanda at the cutting edge of our industry. So if you’re someone who thinks big, moves fast and wants to make an impact right from day one, then get ready to join our industry-changing team. Let’s go!

Your Mission

The Onboarding & Activation team shapes the very first moments of a user’s journey with Bitpanda, turning a new signup into a verified, active account. We design and build the identity verification and activation experiences that millions of people rely on, working across web and mobile to create a path that feels effortless on the surface while meeting the technical and regulatory rigor a trusted financial platform demands.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build & Learn: Develop and maintain web applications using React, NextJS, and TypeScript, working closely with senior engineers to turn product and design concepts into clean code.
  • Debug & Troubleshoot: Investigate bugs, maintain existing features, and resolve edge-case issues by collaborating directly with Product Managers, Engineering Managers, and Backend Engineers to analyze and explain technical root causes.
  • Contribute to Core Codebase: Help write modular UI components, manage state logic, and improve frontend flows that directly impact our global user base.
  • Maintain High Engineering Standards: Follow established coding standards (and contribute ideas to improve them), writing unit and integration tests (Jest, React Testing Library) to keep our platform secure and reliable.
  • Grow & Share: Participate actively in code reviews, technical discussions, and team learning sessions to accelerate your engineering career.

Кто вы

  • CS Fundamentals: Strong understanding of core Computer Science principles, including algorithms, object-oriented programming (OOP), operating systems, and a foundational grasp of how web browsers work under the hood.
  • Web Tech & Practical Exposure: Hands-on experience with JavaScript (ES6+), HTML5, CSS3, React, and TypeScript, built through personal projects, open-source contributions, internships, or self-directed learning outside of university coursework.
  • Problem Solver & Analytical Thinker: Curious mindset with an aptitude for debugging complex issues and breaking down technical problems into clear solutions.
  • Quality & Best Practices: A desire to follow engineering best practices, write clean and maintainable code, and use testing frameworks to validate your work.
  • A strong interest or personal curiosity in cryptocurrency, fintech, stocks, or financial markets.
