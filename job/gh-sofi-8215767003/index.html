О роли

As the Fraud and Disputes Member Treatment & Operations Lead, you will own the design and evolution of how SoFi protects, communicates with, supports, restricts, and resolves member experiences across fraud and disputes.

You will define clear, risk-informed treatment principles and translate them into operating requirements that teams can execute consistently across products, channels, and fraud scenarios.

You will serve as the connective layer across Fraud and Disputes Operations, Risk Strategy, Product, Engineering, Legal, Compliance, and Communications—resolving complex tradeoffs among loss prevention, member protection, fairness, operational feasibility, and long-term member trust.

In this role, success is not just about policy or process. It is about ensuring that members receive timely, clear, and consistent treatment when they need SoFi most, while maintaining strong controls and scalable operational execution.

Что предстоит делать

Own Member Treatment Design (30%): Define and maintain end-to-end member-treatment principles across fraud and disputes, including segmentation, decision principles, restrictions, escalation paths, resolution experiences, and channel consistency.

Set treatment guardrails for high-impact decisions, ensuring member communications, frontline actions, and risk controls are timely, consistent, and defensible.

Translate Strategy into Operational Execution (25%): Convert fraud, disputes, policy, and regulatory requirements into clear operating standards for processes, communications, training, tooling, quality controls, and measurement.

Lead readiness for material treatment changes and emerging fraud events, aligning containment actions, member messaging, escalation pathways, and post-incident remediation.

Lead Cross-Functional Treatment Design (20%): Partner with Product, Engineering, Risk, Legal, Compliance, and Operations to define requirements for member-facing journeys, workflow, decisioning, agent tooling, and exception handling.

Facilitate design reviews and resolve complex tradeoffs across fraud types, products, channels, and member segments.

Own Member Communications and Scam Education (15%): Set the approach for fraud- and scam-related communications, including prevention education, in-product messaging, agent guidance, and post-incident outreach.

Use scam trends, member contacts, complaints, and engagement data to prioritize education campaigns and improve message effectiveness.

Establish Governance and Continuous Improvement (10%): Set decision rights, approval paths, and documentation for treatment changes and exceptions.

Use operational, risk, quality, complaints, contact-driver, and member-experience data to identify treatment gaps, quantify impact, and prioritize durable improvements.

What you’ll need

  • BA/BS degree required or equivalent practical experience.
  • 12+ years of experience in fraud, risk, disputes, member experience, operations strategy, product operations, or related financial-services work.
  • Deep knowledge of fraud and member-treatment practices in a regulated environment.
  • Demonstrated success leading complex, companywide programs or operating processes through indirect influence.
  • Ability to synthesize policy, data, operational realities, and regulatory expectations into clear recommendations and executable requirements.
  • Experience evaluating member impacts of fraud controls, decisioning, restrictions, investigations, and resolution processes.
  • Strong executive communication skills, judgment, and ability to resolve ambiguous, high-impact tradeoffs.
  • Experience partnering with Product, Engineering, Legal, Compliance, and Operations on member-facing changes.
  • Highly motivated self-starter with the ability to build alignment in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience across multiple fraud domains, including identity, scams, transaction fraud, first-party fraud, or disputes.
  • Experience with journey design, decisioning, controls, complaints, or regulatory remediation.
  • Experience designing member communications, prevention education, or agent guidance for high-risk or high-friction moments.
