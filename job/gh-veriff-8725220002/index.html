Physical document IDV is Veriff's flagship product: how millions of people prove who they are to fintechs, marketplaces, mobility platforms, and government agencies. It's fundamentally a machine learning system: document classification, data extraction, fraud and spoofing detection, coverage across thousands of document types and alphabets. Some versions also keep a human in the loop, so decision quality depends as much on operations as on models.

We are looking for a Senior Product Manager to own this core domain. You will sit directly between two demanding groups: machine learning engineers building models, and enterprise customers asking hard questions about accuracy, latency, and edge-case failure modes. Your job is to translate complex technical trade-offs into business outcomes.

You’ll help us protect honest people online by

  • Owning the technical roadmap for physical document IDV — accuracy, coverage, fraud detection, conversion — and defending it across the company
  • Partnering with data scientists and ML engineers on model and system trade-offs: framing the problem, defining what "better" means, focusing effort where it moves customer outcomes
  • Trading off accuracy, conversion, latency, and cost against live enterprise contracts
  • Being the voice of Veriff in complex customer calls: running technical deep-dives, answering detailed accuracy questions credibly, and knowing when to say we can't do it yet
  • Partnering with operations on the human-in-the-loop side of the product, and with our annotation teams on the data the models are trained and evaluated on
  • Working across product marketing, legal and privacy, analytics, and go-to-market to launch, position, and scale what you build

You are the right future Veriffian for the job if you have:

  • 4+ years of end-to-end B2B/B2B2C Product Management experience, ideally in identity, computer vision, fraud, or high-throughput ML systems.
  • ML Fluency: You do not build models, but you speak fluent precision/recall, understand loss functions, and know how thresholds impact user conversion.
  • Technical Independence: Ability to write queries, debug data pipelines, and conduct root-cause analysis without waiting for an analyst.
  • Customer Credibility: Comfort having tough, highly technical conversations with enterprise clients when systems fail or trade-offs must be made.
  • Experience thriving in a scale-up — high ambiguity, fast pace, changing priorities
  • Fluent English, written and verbal

You’re an especially awesome match if you have

  • Experience in identity verification, document security, computer vision, or fraud
  • Familiarity with regulated industries such as finance, mobility, or government tech
  • Previous software engineering or data science experience
  • A degree in a quantitative field

We care more about whether you can reason well about ML systems than how many years of your career have been spent inside one.
