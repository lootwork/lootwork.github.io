Robinhood is hiring an Events Marketing Manager to own the cohesive marketing plans for its growing roster of events. This is an amplification role. You'll take the approved event strategy and build the marketing plan and messaging for each workstream (RSVP, livestream, and post-event) to get people to register or buy tickets, tune in to the livestream, and keep them talking after the event ends. Your plans will cover all the major channels (paid and organic social, email and lifecycle marketing, PR), and you’ll also shape how the brand shows up on-site. This is not an event production or logistics role. Venue, travel, F&B, and on-site operations sit with other members of the Events team. We are looking for someone who thinks in funnels, channels, audiences, and reach, and who is just as comfortable owning a brief as running a daily performance check-in.

This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA or New York, NY offices, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

Event Marketing Strategy

  • Build the event marketing and amplification plan across 3 core workstreams (RSVP/ticket sales, livestream, and post-event), including goals, audience targeting, messaging hierarchy, asset needs, and channel mix.
  • Drive cross-functional alignment on the amplification plan with Channel Leads (Social, Lifecycle Marketing, Comms) and Marketing Leadership); own briefing to Creative team for assets.
  • Align with Product Managers on event-related product announcements and promotions, and incorporate into the event marketing plan and keynote.
  • Partner with Senior Brand Manager on ticketing/RSVP and pricing approach, and manage the registration types, flows and nurture strategy.
  • Collaborate with the Sponsorship Lead to define and execute the sponsor marketing strategy and deliverables included in each sponsor package (social, email, comms, etc).
  • Support Program Manager on Legal & Compliance review of marketing assets.

Promotional Plan Execution & Optimization

  • Execute the approved marketing plan across all channels with one promotional calendar that covers the full event arc (3 workstreams); ensures all assets go live on schedule and are on message.
  • Manage paid social planning and optimization, including budget tracking, platform partner relationships, and in-flight optimization against RSVP, ticket, and livestream tune-in goals.
  • Partner with the organic social team and Creative on teaser sequencing, social-first creative, and posting cadence to build momentum and reach.

On-Site Branding & Marketing

  • Own the on-site branding strategy along the attendee journey (e.g., signage, branded environments, swag); includes brief creation.
  • Build marketing plans for cross-functional on-site activations, such as CX and Research, in partnership with their teams.

Content Capture & Post-Event Promotion

  • Build the content pipeline to drive post-event momentum and reach: determine the post-event promotion plan that turns keynotes, sessions, and on-site moments into clips, highlights, and recap content that extends the moment and drives reach after the event ends.
  • Partner with the content capture and social teams for the on-site content capture, reviews, and go-live process.

Reporting & Measurement

  • Set goals for each workstream and channel (RSVPs and attendance, livestream views and CPV, engagement), benchmarked against prior events.
  • Track daily registration counts, cohort conversion, and channel performance to forecast attendance and optimize in flight.
  • Own the post-event program report: consolidate channel results against goals and prior events, with key learnings per work stream and attendee survey results (in partnership with Research).
  • Lead the marketing retro with channel partners and turn the learnings into recommendations for the next event.
