Technical Support Engineer (Finance)

As a Technical Support Engineer (Finance), you will become an expert in Adyen’s finance and treasury products, helping our merchants troubleshoot technical and financial issues and get the most out of our products.You’ll work closely with merchants to investigate issues across APIs, integrations and financial processes, and partner with Product and Engineering teams to resolve complex problems and improve our products.

Your team is highly merchant-focused and operates across time zones using a follow-the-sun model. A strong understanding of financial processes, technical troubleshooting skills and a customer-focused mindset will be key to succeeding in this role.

To find out more about what a day in the life of a Finance Support Specialist looks like, please see: Working in Finance Support at Adyen.

Что предстоит делать

  • Be the first point of contact for our merchants and commercial teams for financial-related issues. This means reactive/proactive communication
  • Educate and advise our merchants’ financial teams on how to best gain value from our platform, products and features
  • Receive and analyse merchant pain points and work closely with Adyen’s Finance Product and Development teams to improve or build new products to facilitate merchant’s operations

Кто вы

  • You have at least 3 years of experience, with exposure to financial products (issuing, business accounts, capital/business financing) within a banking or Fintech environment
  • You have strong analytical skills and are interested to know more about financial products for the payments industry and can easily get familiar with our internal accounting systems
  • You have a strong commercial acumen and are not afraid of being in direct contact with our merchants
  • You make quick informed decisions under pressure and prioritise appropriately based on urgency, necessity and both internal and external requests
  • You have excellent written and verbal communication skills in English. Any other language is a plus
  • You’ve had previous exposure to environments rich in uncertainty and you show a resulting track record of successfully dealing with ambiguity
  • You have advanced level of proficiency with MS Excel
