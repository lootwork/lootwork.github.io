About the role.

Strategic Insights owns Zilch’s key commercial data products, including the operational P&L, the expected loan economics view of the business, ensuring the numbers behind critical decisions are accurate, reconciled and well understood.

Sitting at the intersection of Finance, Data and Product, the team connects performance across the business, tracks progress against strategic targets and turns data into actionable insights that help Zilch grow and improve profitability.

You will lead a small team responsible for driving cross-functional performance reviews, identifying opportunities through LTV and commercial analysis, and tackling Zilch’s most pressing business challenges. This includes supporting strategic product initiatives, solving complex problems alongside business owners and filling critical analytics gaps across the organisation.

You will own complex work streams end to end with a high degree of independence, lead and develop others, and influence decisions across multiple teams and senior leadership.

Day-to-day responsibilities

People leadership.

- Line manage a small team of analysts, setting clear priorities and expectations and holding regular 1:1s.

- Develop the team through coaching, feedback and stretch work, and lead their performance reviews and growth plans.

- Review the team's analysis and models for accuracy and clarity before they reach stakeholders.

- Balance hands-on work with leadership, staying close enough to the detail to challenge and unblock the team.

- Contribute to hiring, onboarding and the wider analytics community at Zilch.

Operational P&L.

- Own the operational P&L table and its definitions (expected loan economics by product, cohort and customer segment), keeping it the trusted source for commercial decisions.

Data definitions, reconciliations and controls.

- Partner with the finance data team on metric definitions, data lineage and controls, so that the numbers used in key decisions are consistent, documented and verifiable.

- Maintain and develop the reconciliation between the operational P&L and management accounts, and explain and resolve variances with Finance.

- Put in place checks, ownership and sign-off for the datasets and reports the business relies on, and fix the root cause when issues come up.

Forecasting.

- Own short-term lending volume forecasts, track their accuracy and explain variances to Strategic Finance, Treasury and Credit.

- Feed forecasts and actuals into the annual plan and in-year reforecasts.

Performance and opportunities.

- Track company performance and attribute changes to their underlying drivers, including customer behaviour, product changes, pricing and credit decisions.

- Proactively surface commercial opportunities and issues, size them, and take them through to the teams who can act on them.

- Lead end-to-end analytical workstreams on strategic product initiatives and cross-functional business challenges, partnering with business owners to define problems, develop actionable insights and drive decisions.

- Support Corporate Strategy and the CFO with analysis and narrative for investor and other external materials.

Ways of working.

- Manage a broad set of senior stakeholders across Finance, Product, Credit, Data and Leadership: set expectations on scope and timing, push back where the evidence does not support a decision, and keep people informed without being asked.

- Use AI tools effectively to speed up analysis, modelling and drafting, while keeping strong controls on the output: check results against source data, document assumptions and methods, and own the narrative and recommendations you present. Set the same standard for the team.

Кого мы ищем

- 7+ years in data analytics, commercial analytics or strategic finance, ideally in lending, payments or fintech.

- Experience line managing analysts, including running performance reviews and developing people, ideally as a player-manager.

- Advanced SQL, and experience with a modern data stack (Snowflake and Looker, or similar).

- Familiarity with Python for data analysis; Experience in data modelling, experience of DBT is an advantage.

- Experience building and owning forecasts (time series, cohort or vintage based) and explaining forecast accuracy.

- Experience with data definitions, reconciliations and controls over reporting that others depend on for decisions.

- A strong grasp of P&L drivers and unit economics. Knowledge of credit product economics (losses, funding costs, fees) is an advantage.

- A first-principles thinker who brings structure to ambiguous problems.

- Clear written and verbal communication, with the ability to explain complex analysis to senior and non-technical audiences.

- Degree in a STEM, Finance or Economics field, or equivalent experience
