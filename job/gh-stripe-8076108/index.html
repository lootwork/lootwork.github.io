Кто мы

О Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

О команде

The Risk Program Management Office is a global team that leads complex, cross-functional programs across Operations, Product, Engineering, GTM, Partnerships, and Risk teams. We are a team of program management allstars who work to make it as seamless as possible for our users to navigate an evolving regulatory environment.

We are expanding our scope into one of Stripe's most consequential strategic priorities and launching in new markets. This work is new, fast-moving, and highly ambiguous — and we're looking for a leader who thrives in exactly that environment. In APAC, our portfolio spans established operations and GTM priorities as well as new licensing, market expansion, and product launches. The work ranges from strengthening and scaling existing programs to building entirely new capabilities from 0→1.

Что предстоит делать

This is a leadership role with the responsibility for defining the vision and scaling the portfolio of Risk Programs across APAC. You will develop and lead a high-performing team, determine where it should focus, and lead a broad mix of work from initial problem framing through implementation.

Working as part of the global Risk Programs team, you will represent our team in regional and global forums. You will serve as a trusted partner to senior leaders, build alignment across cross-functional teams, and establish the operating models, playbooks, and programs needed to deliver durable outcomes at scale.

Обязанности

Build and inspire an exceptional team

  • Develop, lead, and retain a senior program managers, helping them grow into strategic leaders who can shape decisions and mobilize organizations
  • Set a compelling vision for the APAC Risk Programs team and connect its work to Stripe’s topline priorities

Build from 0>1 a broad and consequential APAC portfolio

  • Take on the region’s most important and ambiguous Risk challenges—developing a point of view, defining the path forward, and leading the work from strategy through execution
  • Identify emerging needs, make thoughtful tradeoffs, and focus the team on the opportunities and problems that matter most

Deliver Operational and GTM programs

  • Manage an existing suite of programs and partner with Operations to forecast demand, manage capacity, and prepare teams to support users as requirements, products, and volumes change
  • Enable GTM teams to guide users through complex and evolving compliance programs with clear guidance, effective tools, and strong escalation paths

Mobilize Stripe and model execution excellence

  • Serve as a trusted advisor to senior regional and global leaders, bringing clarity, judgment, and a strong understanding of APAC to important decisions
  • Build durable partnerships across cross-functional teams to resolve tradeoffs and deliver shared outcomes

Кто вы

You are an inspiring people leader and strategic operator who builds trusted relationships and brings out the best in others. You develop a clear point of view amid ambiguity, influence without authority, and turn complex challenges into action.

Minimum requirements

  • 10+ years of experience in strategy and operations, business operations, program management, management consulting, or a related field
  • 5+ years of people leadership, with a track record of developing and retaining high-performing teams
  • Experience owning a broad portfolio of complex, cross-functional programs from strategy through execution
  • Demonstrated success building from 0→1 and strengthening established programs at scale
  • Strong executive communication and presence
  • Experience leading across cultures, geographies, and time zones
  • Fluency with AI tools and examples of using them to improve your own or your team’s effectiveness

Будет плюсом

  • Prior experience at a leading technology company or consulting firm
  • Prior experience working in Singapore and across APAC markets

Будет плюсом

  • Payments, fintech, or financial-services experience
  • Experience with licensing, market entry, or global expansion
