Кто мы

At OKX, we believe that the future will be reshaped by crypto, and ultimately contribute to every individual's freedom. OKX is a leading crypto exchange, and the developer of OKX Wallet, giving millions access to crypto trading and decentralized crypto applications (dApps). OKX is also a trusted brand by hundreds of large institutions seeking access to crypto markets. We are safe and reliable, backed by our Proof of Reserves. Across our multiple offices globally, we are united by our core principles: We Before Me, Do the Right Thing, and Get Things Done. These shared values drive our culture, shape our processes, and foster a friendly, rewarding, and diverse environment for every OK-er. OKX is part of OKG, a group that brings the value of Blockchain to users around the world, through our leading products OKX, OKX Wallet, OKLink and more.

Что предстоит делать

  • Develop and execute OKX’s retail growth strategy for Brazil, aligning local priorities with the company’s broader business objectives.
  • Build and scale growth initiatives across relevant channels, partnerships, product-led programs, community activations, and local campaigns.
  • Lead relationships with influencers and KOLs, from selection and negotiation to campaign execution and performance management.
  • Support local PR efforts, working with global internal teams and external agencies to strengthen brand visibility and reputation.
  • Own the strategy and execution of events, sponsorships, and offline activations that drive awareness, engagement, and user growth.
  • Define KPIs, analyze performance, and translate data into actionable insights that drive measurable business results.
  • Use AI tools to accelerate research, analysis, experimentation, personalization, content workflows, and operational efficiency.
  • Manage multiple workstreams, budgets, partners, and stakeholders in a fast-paced, results-oriented environment.

What We Look For In You

  • Minimum of 8 years of experience in Marketing/Growth in fast paced industry.
  • Strong familiarity with AI tools and a practical understanding of how to apply them to analysis, experimentation, personalization, and productivity.
  • A highly data-driven and results-oriented mindset, with a proven ability to turn insights into measurable growth.
  • Exceptional communication and stakeholder-management skills.
  • An entrepreneurial mindset, with strong ownership, adaptability, and bias toward action.
  • Excellent organization and project-management capabilities.
  • A genuine passion for crypto, blockchain technology, and digital assets.
  • Flexibility to adapt working hours to collaborate effectively across multiple time zones.
  • Fluency in Portuguese and English.
