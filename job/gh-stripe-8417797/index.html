О команде

The Batch Compute Experience team owns the platform that Stripe engineers use to build, run, debug and optimize Apache Spark jobs. Those jobs handle large-scale data processing for analytics, machine learning, finance and core product data pipelines. Hundreds of engineers and thousands of production jobs driving critical business processes, such as payouts and reconciliations, run on this platform.

Today, diagnosing and tuning a job means reasoning by hand across scattered telemetry, configuration, query plans and application code. Our goal is to make Spark at Stripe fast to iterate, reliable by default, and self-optimizing, so engineers can focus on business logic instead of Spark internals. We're doing this through three connected efforts:

  • Modernization: keeping Stripe's Spark runtime and dependency stack current, including Spark 3.5 → Spark 4, Scala 2.13 and Apache Iceberg. We're building automated upgrade and validation tools so that upgrading the whole fleet becomes routine and low-risk instead of a multi-year manual effort
  • Agentic Spark and optimization: building trustworthy, AI-powered workflows that diagnose failed or underperforming jobs, recommend fixes based on evidence, and carry them safely into configuration changes, code PRs or escalations. We also want to optimize configuration, query plans and code across the whole fleet
  • Operational excellence: making the platform reliable, observable and compliant, and defining user-experience metrics that show whether we're succeeding

Over the longer term, we want to turn the platform into a Spark control plane that keeps learning. It should detect problems before users notice them, validate fixes safely and apply them through policy-controlled automation.

Что предстоит делать

As a Staff Engineer on Batch Compute Experience, you'll set the technical direction for one of Stripe's most widely used data platforms. You'll lead multi-quarter efforts across teams, make high-stakes architectural decisions affecting thousands of production workloads, and raise the engineering bar for the team and its partners.

Обязанности

  • Lead Spark modernization across the fleet. Own the technical strategy for moving thousands of production jobs to newer Spark and Iceberg runtimes. This includes target selection, qualification, deciding the rollout unit (cluster, application or hybrid), and rollback planning, while coordinating dependencies with partner infrastructure teams
  • Make upgrades continuous and safe. Design and evolve automated upgrade and validation systems: shadow execution against production data, output-correctness comparison, sandboxed execution, and staged promotion. The goal is to make each new runtime upgrade cheaper than the last
  • Architect agentic Spark workflows. Build LLM-powered systems that diagnose failures, out-of-memory errors (OOMs), data skew and performance regressions. They should produce evidence-backed recommendations that are backtested and gated in CI, and you'll define the safety, provenance and human-approval requirements for every automated action
  • Build safe configuration and remediation paths. Design authorized, validated, reversible no-code configuration changes, along with pipelines that detect anti-patterns across the fleet and open code-fix PRs or tickets
  • Drive efficiency at scale. Identify, prove out and roll out the highest-value configuration and query-plan optimizations, such as shuffle and adaptive query execution (AQE) tuning, executor sizing, join strategy and partitioning. Turn the measured gains into real infrastructure savings without hurting correctness or reliability
  • Improve the developer experience. Shorten the inner dev loop, improve PySpark support and observability (including Python memory diagnostics), and unify how jobs are packaged and launched across schedulers such as Airflow and Flyte
  • Set the technical bar. Lead critical design reviews, define reliability standards (SLOs, observability, testing) for the services we own, mentor senior engineers, and steer the team away from structural and operational debt

Кто вы

We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • This is a Staff Engineer role - that typically means you have 10+ years of professional software engineering experience, with significant time spent building data infrastructure or distributed systems
  • A track record of leading complex, multi-quarter infrastructure projects from start to finish, especially migrations or upgrades that touch many consuming teams
  • Experience defining SLOs and running reliable platforms with on-call responsibilities
  • Experience designing safe rollouts for large fleets: staged rollout, reducing blast radius, validating correctness, and planning rollbacks
  • Strong product and developer-experience instincts: you build abstractions that are ergonomic, well documented and easy to adopt
