Обязанности

  • Build and maintain Golang services, APIs, and Agent workflows for AI-generated market briefings and news updates.
  • Design data flows, data models, and service architecture to support content generation and distribution.
  • Build high-concurrency, highly available systems for personalized content delivery, including scheduled pushes that must complete within tight windows—for example, within 10 minutes.
  • Monitor data quality, Agent workflow health, generation latency, and AI costs. Improve performance and reliability while keeping inference costs low.
  • Own existing and new services through development, deployment, maintenance, and troubleshooting, working closely with frontend, AI, data, DevOps, and QA teams.

Требования

  • 3–6 years of backend development experience, with Golang as the core tech stack and recent hands-on production experience
  • Proven experience building data pipelines, data platforms, or large-scale data processing backend systems
  • Experience with AI monitoring, observability tools, or Agent engineering; ability to integrate AI/LLM workflows into production backend systems
  • Solid foundation in distributed systems, microservice architecture, relational/NoSQL databases, and message queues (e.g., Kafka)
  • Familiarity with AI Agent frameworks, LLM integration, or related AI toolchain development is a plus
  • Strong communication skills; able to thrive in a fast-paced, product-driven environment
  • Bachelor's degree or above in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field
