Thirty Madison is now part of Remedy! You can read more about the acquisition here. We are excited to join forces and continue to revolutionize healthcare accessibility!

As a Data Analyst you’ll partner with our Clinical and Operations teams and make sure every patient gets fast, high-quality care. You'll be a key contributor to reporting on our clinical and operational SLAs, from time to provider review and prescription turnaround to patient messaging and fulfillment. You'll also dig into the data behind them to find where we can move faster without compromising quality.

This is a high-impact, highly visible role, and strong communication is central to it. You'll talk with clinicians, operations leads and executives every week. Your work will shape how clinicians, operations leads and executives set targets, staff the care team and improve the patient experience. Above all, you embody the Thirty Madison mission of providing access to healthcare for all who suffer from chronic conditions!

What you get to do every day

  • Contribute to SLA reporting. Help build and maintain trusted reporting on clinical and operational SLAs, such as provider response times, consult and prescription turnaround, patient message resolution and fulfillment. Help monitor performance and flag risks early.
  • Help improve the SLAs themselves. Analyze drivers of SLA performance, including volume, staffing, workflows, state licensure and patient mix. Recommend changes to targets, processes and capacity that improve efficiency and patient outcomes.
  • Translate analysis into action. Turn complex analyses into clear, actionable recommendations for non-technical audiences, including clinicians, operations leads and executives. Use concise written summaries, presentations and intuitive dashboards.
  • Scope the right questions. Partner with stakeholders to turn loosely defined business questions into well-scoped analyses. Push back or escalate when a request is unclear, the data can't support it, or priorities conflict.
  • Connect the data. Combine EHR, telehealth platform, and billing and claims data to give a full view of the patient journey, from intake through treatment and follow-up.
  • Raise the bar on data quality. Define metrics clearly, document logic, and work with data engineering to fix gaps at the source.

What you bring to the role

  • 2+ years of analytics experience at a clinical, digital health or telehealth company.
  • Working familiarity with EHR, telehealth platform, and billing and claims data.
  • Strong SQL skills and experience building dashboards in a BI tool such as Hex, Looker or Tableau.
  • Comfort with operational metrics such as SLAs, throughput, queue times and capacity.
  • An effective communicator who is comfortable talking with clinicians, operations leads and executives, and who explains findings clearly and confidently, in writing and in person.
  • Sound judgment about data limitations, and the confidence to say no or reframe a request.
  • Comfort using AI-assisted analytics tools as a multiplier, not a substitute. You use AI to move faster, for example drafting queries, exploring data or summarizing findings. You apply your own judgment to validate every output, and you fully own the accuracy of your work and the conclusions you share.
  • Working knowledge of HIPAA and care in handling protected health information.

Дополнительный плюс

  • Python for analysis, forecasting or capacity modeling.
  • Experience with dbt or another modern data stack.
  • Familiarity with clinical quality measures, provider workflows or multi-state telehealth operations.
  • Experience with workforce or queue modeling to inform staffing decisions.
