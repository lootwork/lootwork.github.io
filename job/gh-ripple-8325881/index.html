THE WORK

Join the Ripple Treasury Payments team in London! We build the payment workflows clients use to create, approve and release payments in bank formats like ISO 20022. Ripple Treasury facilitates over $13 trillion in payments a year, for clients from small businesses to the Fortune 500. Every payment is value moving the way Ripple believes it should.

You’ll work with C# / .NET 8, Azure, SQL Server, React and TypeScript, and help modernise a large .NET Framework codebase. AI tools are part of how we work every day.

Что предстоит делать

  • Take ownership of a core payment service within your first 6–12 months
  • Design, build and ship full-stack features across .NET 8 services and React front ends
  • Lead large projects from design to rollout with one or two other engineers
  • Modernise legacy code, building in automated tests and observability
  • Partner with product, engineering and client support, and join our on-call rotation
  • Mentor engineers through code review and pairing, and help us hire

WHAT YOU'LL BRING

  • 4–8 years of professional software development experience
  • Strong C# / .NET backend skills, including in large, established codebases
  • Experience with React, TypeScript and SQL
  • Daily, hands-on use of AI coding assistants
  • A record of delivering projects to production with limited supervision
  • Curiosity about payments: we’ll help you learn the domain
