At Kabam, we’re constantly raising the bar of excellence in free-to-play gaming. We hire for passion and diversity so that we can triumph in our collective skill. Our teams are made up of a fusion of personalities and interests bound together by a collaborative nature, fun-loving culture, and a drive to do what it takes to make great games. And this is where you come in...

You possess a combination of strong technical ability, a thorough understanding of art pipelines across modeling, animation and VFX, and a passion for tools and pipelines development and innovation, including optimizing the use of AI. You also have the ability to contribute to VFX production.

You are adaptable, you like technical challenges including developing art tools, establishing art strong pipelines and troubleshooting technical issues. You are recognized as a bridge between artists and engineers. You like working with people, helping their growth by teaching industry’s best practices.

Sound like a match? Kabam is looking for a contract Senior Technical Artist to join our growing studio in Montréal and evolve how we make games! We don’t just make games, we play and love them too.

The Contractor shall provide the following services on an independent, deliverables basis, exercising their own professional judgment, expertise, and methods in performing the Services.

IN THIS ROLE, YOU CAN EXPECT TO

  • Work closely with artists, project managers, engineers and the rest of the Technical Art team to create and improve art production workflows, tools, pipelines, and asset optimization/integration.
  • Work with art leads and production to identify where AI and automation can speed up art production, measure the time saved, and roll proven workflows out to the team.
  • Evaluate new AI tools and models, building prototypes, and recommending what to adopt based on quality, speed, cost and licensing.
  • Integrate AI tools into the existing art pipeline (Unity, Maya, Blender, Photoshop) so artists can run and review AI-assisted steps from the tools they already use.
  • Design and build AI-orchestrated art workflows that chain generative models, scripts and DCC tools (e.g. ComfyUI graphs, Python automation, agent-based tools) into repeatable, optimized art driven pipelines.
  • Train artists on AI-assisted workflows and document best practices, including guidelines on IP and responsible use.
  • Develop AI enabled art workflows & pipelines and collaborate with other disciplines to design the next generation of Ai native game prototyping and development process and technology.
  • Worki closely with the VFX artists, mentoring and teaching them on optimization.
  • Develop pipelines for creating beautiful and vibrant VFX.
  • Develop pipelines for lighting, including a day/night cycle, while contributing to lighting.
  • Contribute to rendering and shading techniques and processes.
  • Find creative solutions to realize artistic visions within the technical limits.
  • Automate validation, optimization, and Unity import for AI-assisted assets so they meet technical budgets and art direction.

TO BE SUCCESSFUL IN THIS ROLE, YOUR BACKGROUND INCLUDES

  • 5+ years development experience in Unity.
  • Solid game industry experience, specifically in a technical artist or VFX role.
  • Strong ability to troubleshooting artist technical problems.
  • Proficiency in Unity, Maya and/or 3Ds Max, Blender and creating tools in these programs.
  • Excited to experiment with AI tools by yourself and started to understand current AI automation tools and workflows.
  • Knowledge of art optimization techniques.
  • Excellent communication skills, both verbal and written.
  • Experience in Unity Scripting (C#).
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and prioritize tasks accordingly.

Excited by this opportunity? Kabam is dedicated to growing our diverse and inclusive workforce, so if your past experience doesn’t perfectly match the listed requirements we encourage you to apply anyways - you could be a great fit for this or other positions.

We invite you to apply now and start the conversation with us. Together, we can create and support some of the best games ever made and entertain the world!
