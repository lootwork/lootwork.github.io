О роли

We’re looking for a Performance Marketing Expert to join our Growth team and help drive and scale our paid advertising. This is a hands-on, end-to-end role for someone who thrives in a high-performance environment and wants to raise the bar every day.

You’ll own planning, execution, testing, optimisation, and reporting across multiple channels, with a strong focus on impact, efficiency, and measurable results.

Что предстоит делать

- Set up and manage paid campaigns across Google, Meta, Apple Search Ads, X, Reddit or other channels

- Ensure our martech stack enables best-in-class tracking, attribution, and reporting to support our growth

- Continuously improve performance through testing of creatives, copy, audiences, keywords, bidding strategies, and campaign structure

- Explore and validate new channels and campaign types

- Build clear documentation & reports to showcase your work beyond top of line metrics

WHAT YOU NEED TO HAVE

- Strong knowledge of marketing technology platforms, including integrating tools and processes to drive optimisation and automation

- Deep understanding of best practices for campaign structure, bidding, ad copy, creative, and extensions

- Proficiency with Google Ads products and programmatic advertising

- Technical knowledge of Google Tag Manager, MMPs and CDPs;

- Strong analytical ability to track, interpret, and present data clearly

- A habit of staying on top of industry trends, platform updates, and emerging opportunities

Дополнительный плюс

- Proven track record of success in app-first business

- Genuine interest in investing or finance
