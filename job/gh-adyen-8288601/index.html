Staff Machine Learning Platform Engineer

As a Staff Engineer in the ML Platform domain, you will act as a technical North Star, driving our long term vision and evolution of the whole ML platform ecosystem serving all ML teams across all the solutions in the company. Our platform is at a pivotal point: we are transitioning from traditional machine learning and the challenges in front of us are big and span the whole company.

You will shape self-service ML tooling that gives teams real freedom to move fast, with guardrails strong enough to keep it safe as agents join engineers and data scientists as everyday users of the platform. You will scale our infrastructure for very large models, from long-running training jobs that stay reliable to serving them in production.

We are looking for an engineer who pairs deep system design expertise with high agency, and who raises the effectiveness of everyone around them. At staff level you will work across squads and teams rather than inside a single project. You spot architectural bottlenecks before they become blockers, challenge existing approaches, and turn ambiguous, company-wide problems into a clear strategy.

Что предстоит делать

  • Drive Strategic Architecture: Architect and execute the technical strategy for our ml platform across the full ML lifecycle, from high-performance training and experiment tracking to real-time and batch model serving.
  • System Design & Modernization: Evaluate greenfield and brownfield architectures. Spot friction points in current systems, propose pragmatic improvements, and drive architectural shifts that reduce complexity.
  • Reliability & Observability: Set the standards for safe, stable, and performant model deployments in production, covering monitoring and drift detection. Lead the response to, and learning from, significant incidents.
  • Democratize ML Practices: Partner with product, data science, and engineering leadership across Adyen to understand their pain points, streamline their workflows, and raise the company-wide MLOps maturity bar. Treat internal teams as customers and measure platform success by their outcomes.
  • Multiply the Team: Mentor engineers, review designs, and raise the bar for engineering practices within the ML Platform team. Help shape how work is prioritized and sequenced across squads, and help grow the next generation of senior and staff engineers.

Кто вы

  • Proven Software Engineering Foundation: 10+ years of software engineering experience delivering production-grade software using both Python and Java, including at least 3 years at senior level or above. Leading large-scale systems, backed by strong software design, testing, debugging, CI/CD practices, and a record of technical decisions that held up over years.
  • ML Lifecycle & MLOps Experience: Extensive experience running ML systems in production. You know modern MLOps and model deployment patterns well, understand how ML algorithms depend on the infrastructure underneath them, and have helped set the direction of an ML platform
  • High Agency & Change Driver: Comfortable navigating ambiguity at the scale of an organization. You find the problems worth solving, take ownership, and lead initiatives independently across team boundaries..
  • Influence and Collaboration: Exceptional communication and system-design skills, with a proven ability to translate complex technical trade-offs for diverse stakeholders, align multiple engineering teams around a unified technical vision, and mentor and upskill engineers to elevate domain-wide technical execution.
  • Pragmatic & Iterative Mindset: You value a "launch fast, learn, and iterate" approach. You prefer simple, maintainable solutions over clever, overly complex ones, and you know when to say no to scope that doesn't pay off.
  • System Design & Architecture Expert: Deep expertise in building reliable, distributed software architectures. You can design systems from scratch or untangle legacy setups without losing sight of stability, you can show the impact of systems you designed and ran in production at scale.

Будет плюсом

  • Feature Platform on-premise: Experience setting up and/or operating high throughput and low latency feature platforms
  • GPU Training Infrastructure: Experience setting up, scaling, and operating GPU infrastructure for model training, including scheduling, capacity planning, and cost control.
  • Kubernetes & Infrastructure Fluency: Extensive experience operating and troubleshooting high-throughput workloads on production Kubernetes (both on-prem and cloud) alongside a solid understanding of system-level performance tuning (networking, storage, and operating system limits).
