With multiple awards to our name, Larian Studios has proven that we’re dedicated to delivering high-quality role-playing games As we move on to new projects, the studio that brought you Baldur’s Gate 3 & Divinity: Original Sin 2 is looking for an Infrastructure Engineer to join the team and help us in crafting the high calibre games our fans have come to expect.

Обязанности

  • Monitor the health, performance and availability of infrastructure and services, and respond to alerts and incidents
  • Investigate recurring issues, identify their root causes and implement improvements to service reliability
  • Support development teams with infrastructure issues, including version control, build and deployment workflows.
  • Maintain Linux and Windows servers, virtualised infrastructure and related services
  • Perform planned maintenance, including patching, updates and backup checks, while minimising disruption
  • Create scripts and automation to improve routine operations and reduce manual work
  • Support the network, security and other infrastructure teams with troubleshooting and changes
  • Maintain accurate documentation for configurations, changes and operating procedures
  • Coordinate with vendors and service providers when specialist support is required
  • Maintain local studio infrastructure, including server rooms, and coordinate site changes with the global IT team and studio management
  • Participate in incident response and planned work outside normal hours when required
  • Assist with user support, onboarding, offboarding and asset management where local support is needed

Требования

  • Working knowledge of Linux and Windows systems, including both servers and endpoints
  • Advanced knowledge of PC and laptop support, combined with a strong awareness of current hardware trends and innovations.
  • Familiarity with endpoint management, productivity platforms and collaboration tools.
  • Experience with CI/CD tools

Familiarity with private cloud and virtualized infrastructure

  • Experience with Google Workplace, Office365, Atlassian Suite
  • Understanding of networking fundamentals, including TCP/IP, LANs, WANs, VPNs and VLANs
  • Ability to work flexible hours around IT infrastructure upgrades and migrations
  • This is a full-time, on-site role, requiring your presence in the office to support teams collaboration

Будет плюсом

  • Experience administering server hardware
  • Experience with infrastructure as code and configuration management
  • Familiarity with containers and container orchestration
  • Experience with version control systems
  • Familiarity with storage systems and backup technologies

Что мы предлагаем

  • An international team of over 600 employees spread across 7 studios around the world, each of which have their own unique qualities, and maintain a small-studio feel
  • An open, collaborative environment where ideas are welcome and encouraged
  • A managerial team consisting of highly experienced game developers at the top of their fields
  • Sharing hobbies and passions with like-minded colleagues
  • Inclusive environment with many activities organized by the studios or the employees
  • Commitment to making the best game possible for the fans, including listening to community feedback
