MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS

Must have a Bachelor's degree or foreign equivalent in Computer Science, Computer Engineering or a related field, plus 5 years of progressive, post-baccalaureate experience in software development .

Alternatively, employer will accept Master's degree or foreign equivalent in Computer Science, Computer Engineering or a related field plus 3 years of experience in software development.

Of the required experience, must have 3 years of experience in/with:

  • Java, Scala or Python
  • Docker or Kubernetes infrastructure
  • Developing or testing large-scaled distributed systems
  • CI/CD implementation, building tools and frameworks, including Gitlab, Helm, or Terraform.
  • Spark or Flink

Of the required experience, must have 2 years of experience in/with:

  • AWS cloud resources including S3, Lambda, API Gateway, Kinesis, or Athena.
  • Data Lake or Databricks
  • RESTful APIs and server-side APIs integration
  • SQL

Telecommuting permitted up to 50% per week within same geographic location as assigned office location.
