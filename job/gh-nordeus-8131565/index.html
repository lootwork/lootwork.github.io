Are you someone who brings clarity when there’s a lot moving at once?

As a Senior Producer, you’ll own the execution picture across shifting priorities, cross-team dependencies, and decisions that can’t wait for every detail to be known. You’ll keep Top Goal teams focused on what they’re trying to achieve, with ownership of delivery outcomes rather than a predefined process.

Что изменится благодаря вам

This is not a coordination-only role. You’ll make complex delivery more predictable by maintaining a clear view of what’s happening, what’s at risk, and what needs a decision. When priorities or circumstances change, you’ll make sure the plan changes with them instead of quietly becoming outdated. That means fewer late surprises and a team that can keep moving without depending on one person to hold the full picture.

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead complex cross-functional initiatives end to end, from early planning through development, launch, and post-launch.
  • Turn product goals into clear, realistic delivery plans, with defined scope, estimates, dependencies, milestones, and quality expectations.
  • Keep delivery on track and transparent by maintaining a clear view of progress, risks, dependencies, and changes, and raising issues early.
  • Drive decisions when plans change - assess trade-offs, bring recommendations, make decisions within your scope, and follow through.
  • Improve how the team delivers by adapting your approach, building ownership within the team, and addressing recurring delivery problems.

С кем предстоит работать

  • You will work closely with a cross-functional Top Goal team spanning Product Management, Game Design, UX/UI, Art, Engineering, QA, Data Science, and Business Analytics.
  • Depending on the initiative, you will also work with other Producers, discipline leads, the Game Lead, central technology teams, marketing, and external partners.

Мы подойдём друг другу, если вы

  • Have 5+ years of experience leading complex product or game development projects across creative and technical disciplines, with ownership of delivery outcomes
  • Have led projects end to end and can show how you handled risks, changing priorities, and things that did not go to plan
  • Can turn incomplete or complex information into a clear, realistic plan while keeping sight of the bigger picture
  • Communicate risks and problems early, separate facts from assumptions, and come to discussions with a recommendation or proposed next step
  • Know how to create the right level of structure and ownership for a team, and can point to feedback that materially changed how you work

Дополнительный плюс

  • Experience developing and operating live mobile games, including event calendars and incident handling
  • Experience mentoring other Producers
  • Passion for football and an understanding of football audiences and products
