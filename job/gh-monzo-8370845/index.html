🚀 We’re on a mission to make money work for everyone.

We’re waving goodbye to the complicated and confusing ways of traditional banking.

After starting as a prepaid card, our product offering has grown a lot in the last 10 years in the UK. As well as personal and business bank accounts, we offer joint accounts, accounts for 16-17 year olds, a free kids account and credit cards in the UK, with more exciting things to come beyond. Our UK customers can also save, invest and combine their pensions with us.

With our hot coral cards and get-paid-early feature, combined with financial education on social media and our award winning customer service, we have a long history of creating magical moments for our customers!

We’re not about selling products - we want to solve problems and change lives through Monzo ❤️

Our Mobile team⭐

At Monzo, we believe managing your mobile plan should feel as simple and transparent as managing your money. With Monzo Mobile, we’re bringing that experience to connectivity, helping customers stay connected with a product that feels intuitive, flexible and genuinely useful.

We’ve launched our mobile offering and are now focused on the next phase of growth. That means reaching more customers, improving how they discover and engage with Monzo Mobile, and exploring how we can serve broader connectivity needs over time.

🔑You’ll play a key role by...

As a Product Lead, you’ll help shape the direction of Monzo Mobile as a business and product area. You’ll work across Product, Engineering, Design, Data, Marketing, Operations and Customer Experience to set strategy, drive execution and ensure we’re investing in the right opportunities to deliver meaningful customer and commercial outcomes.

This is an opportunity to lead a high-potential product area at Monzo, combining the pace and ambiguity of building something relatively new with the reach of a trusted brand. You should be excited about setting direction in uncertain spaces, leading through influence, and translating strategy into high-quality execution.

  • Setting the product strategy for Monzo Mobile, identifying where we can win in connectivity and translating that into clear priorities, bets and trade-offs.
  • Owning growth and business performance for the area, using customer, market and data insight to drive adoption, engagement and retention.
  • Leading multiple workstreams across the product area, ensuring teams are focused on the highest-impact opportunities and that short-term delivery ladders up to longer-term goals.
  • Partnering closely with Marketing, Operations and Customer Experience to improve the end-to-end customer journey and ensure the product scales effectively.
  • Building a strong understanding of the commercial drivers of the business to inform investment decisions, prioritisation and partner choices.
  • Working with external partners where needed to improve customer experience, delivery outcomes and commercial effectiveness.
  • Influencing senior stakeholders across Monzo and aligning adjacent teams around a coherent vision for the space.
  • Coaching and raising the bar for product thinking, execution and decision-making across the team.

🤩 We’d love to hear from you if…

  • You’ve led and scaled consumer digital products or product areas with meaningful customer and business impact.
  • You’re able to set strategy in ambiguous spaces and translate it into clear execution plans.
  • You’re highly analytical and comfortable using data to identify opportunities, make decisions and measure impact.
  • You’re commercially minded and can balance customer value with business outcomes.
  • You’re excellent at influencing senior stakeholders and aligning multiple teams behind a shared direction.
  • You care deeply about customer experience and know how to make complex products feel simple.
  • You’ve led large, cross-functional initiatives and are comfortable operating across product, marketing, operations and technology.
  • You’re thoughtful, low-ego and excited by what we’re building at Monzo.
