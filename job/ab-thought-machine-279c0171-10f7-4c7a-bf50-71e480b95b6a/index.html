The Risk & Compliance team sits within Finance and is responsible for enterprise risk management, the internal controls framework, regulatory compliance, external certifications and client audits. О роли

We are looking for a Risk & Compliance Analyst to join the team and strengthen our capability across risk management and compliance and controls. You will play a hands-on role in running our risk management framework, keeping us ahead of the regulatory landscape in our current and target markets, and supporting the testing and improvement of our controls framework. This is a broad role with excellent exposure to senior stakeholders and a real opportunity to shape how a fast-growing fintech manages risk.

DUTIES

Risk Management

- Support the annual enterprise risk assessment and departmental risk reviews, working with risk owners to identify, assess and track risks and mitigating actions.

- Maintain the risk register and help produce risk reporting and metrics for the Governance, Risk & Compliance Committee (GRCC), Senior Management and the Board Risk & Audit Committee (BRAC).

- Contribute to improvements in our risk reporting, including dashboards and establishing key risk indicators.

Compliance

- Run compliance horizon scanning, tracking regulatory and legislative developments relevant to Thought Machine and its banking clients.

- Lead the refresh of local regulatory assessments for our current and target markets.

- Provide day-to-day compliance advice to the business and support policy maintenance and attestation.

Client and Business Support

- Support Sales and Partnerships with due diligence requests, RFPs and client risk questionnaires.

Требования

Essential

- 2–4 years' experience in risk management, compliance, internal audit or controls assurance, ideally in financial services, fintech, SaaS or a Big Four / consultancy environment.

- Working knowledge of risk management frameworks and internal controls concepts (e.g. risk and control self-assessment, control design and operating effectiveness testing).

- A robust understanding of quantitative risk approaches and how these are applied in project and operational risk situations.

- Understanding of the regulatory environment for financial services and/or technology providers to regulated firms (e.g. operational resilience, outsourcing and third-party requirements, DORA, data protection).

- Strong analytical skills and attention to detail, with the ability to turn findings into clear, concise reporting for senior audiences.

- Excellent written and verbal communication, and confidence working with stakeholders at all levels.

- Highly organised, able to manage competing priorities and deadlines in a small team.

Desirable

- Exposure to SOC 1 / SOC 2 (ISAE 3402 / 3000), ISO 27001 or similar audit and certification programmes.

- Experience with GRC tooling, and an interest in using data, automation or AI to improve risk and compliance processes.

- A relevant qualification or progress towards one (e.g. ICA, IRM, CISA, ACCA/ACA, CIA).

We actively hire candidates who demonstrate technical excellence in their field and welcome people of all ages and backgrounds, providing everyone with equal access to professional development. You are encouraged to apply even if your experience doesn't accurately match the job description. We also encourage applications from those with different abilities, including candidates with ADHD, autism, dyslexia or dyspraxia.
