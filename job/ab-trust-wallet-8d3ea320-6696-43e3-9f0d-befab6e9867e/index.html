О company

Trust Wallet is the secure, self-custody Web3 wallet that empowers people to own and control their digital assets. Whether you're new to crypto or an experienced user, Trust Wallet makes it easier, safer, and more convenient to explore Web3, securely access dApps, manage your crypto, and buy and sell digital assets, all in one place.

Trust Wallet: Future Opportunities (Non-Engineering)

Why This Role Exists

At Trust Wallet, we believe exceptional talent creates exceptional outcomes.

While most companies hire only when a specific position opens up, we take a longer-term view. We’re always looking to connect with outstanding people in product, marketing, partnerships and operations who can raise the bar, bring fresh perspectives, and help us serve hundreds of millions of users worldwide.

Not every great candidate fits neatly into an open job description, and not every future opportunity exists today.

This posting is for people who are excited about our mission but don’t see a perfect match among our current openings. If you believe you can make a meaningful impact at Trust Wallet, we’d love to hear from you.

We hire for potential, not just open headcount.

Кого мы ищем

Every application is reviewed individually, but we’re especially excited to hear from people who:

- Take strong ownership and deliver measurable results

- Turn complex problems into clear, simple solutions

- Use data to make decisions and test ideas

- Communicate clearly and collaborate well across a globally distributed team

- Are curious about Web3, AI and emerging technologies

- Keep learning and improving

Web3 experience is welcome but not required for many positions.

What to Include in Your Application

To help us identify the right opportunity, please share

- Your preferred function or area

- Your strongest skills and key achievements, with numbers where possible

- Your current location and time zone

- Links to your LinkedIn, portfolio, writing samples, campaigns or projects (if applicable)

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION

This is a global and fully remote role. You must have the right to work for the country you are based.
