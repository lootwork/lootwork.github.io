О роли

Binance Square is where millions of users come to discover, discuss, and act on crypto. As our Content Governance Lead, you'll own the trust layer that makes that possible — defining how content is reviewed, how violations are detected and enforced, and how policy becomes product. You'll work at the intersection of product, algorithms, risk, and regional operations, and your decisions will directly shape the quality and credibility of one of the largest crypto content ecosystems.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own end-to-end content moderation and ecosystem governance strategy — review pipeline, violation detection, enforcement, appeals, and the penalty–visibility matrix
  • Build and iterate internal moderation tooling: review queues, labeling, case prioritization, and the ops feedback loop
  • Partner with algorithm and risk teams on content-quality detection, balancing recall, precision, and false-positive cost
  • Translate governance policy into product mechanisms — penalty matrix, KYC gating, block/report flows, traffic restriction — and keep PRDs aligned through delivery
  • Own governance metrics (violation exposure, moderation SLA and accuracy, appeal overturn, report success rate) and drive solutions from the data
  • Handle escalations from regional ops and Trust & Safety on high-risk scenarios: traffic farming, pump solicitation, spam, impersonation

Кого мы ищем

  • 3+ years in content or community products, including hands-on work with content safety, moderation, or governance
  • Solid grasp of moderation workflows, policy enforcement, and risk-control product design
  • Proven ability to turn ambiguous problems into prioritized, shipped solutions in a fast-changing environment
  • Strong data literacy — comfortable defining, instrumenting, and interrogating metrics

Будет плюсом

  • SQL proficiency
  • Experience in crypto, fintech, or another high-risk, high-scale content platform
  • Familiarity with Trust & Safety tooling or labeling platforms
