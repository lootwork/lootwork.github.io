О VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

Команда

Our Gaming team is made up of hybrid-casual, casual, and mid-core experts. The Core Games team supports internal and external studios worldwide in creating, developing, and launching new hit games, while the Live Games team focuses on increasing engagement and extending the success of our existing games.

Joining our Live Games team means collaborating with gaming industry experts on globally renowned, enduring games, while also embracing exciting new entrepreneurial ventures. With over 7 billion downloads worldwide and a portfolio of more than 10 resilient hybrid games generating over $20 million per year, we are the world’s largest and most successful hybrid publisher.

You will join the Midcore team within Live Studios. Our main mission is to transform hypercasual titles into deep, engaging midcore games by developing richer gameplay experiences, stronger progression systems, and long-term player engagement.

The studio is currently working on titles including Epic Plane Evolution, Path of Kings, Gear Fight and Archery Clash.

Роль

- Create mobile video ads that engage millions of people

- Become a creative expert in mobile gaming: benchmark what our competitors are doing, experiment with new ideas

- Develop a deep understanding of how your creations perform and why

- Work within a small creative squad with amazing people from diverse backgrounds and nationalities

- Contribute to transversal initiatives (e.g., testing new tools)

Профиль кандидата

- Strong experience in video editing and content creation

- Good knowledge of motion tools such as DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere Pro or CapCut Desktop. Experience with 3D software or Unity is a plus

- Creative, curious, and comfortable experimenting with new ideas

- Understanding of how video creatives contribute to marketing performance

- Fluent in spoken and written English or Russian

- Knowledge of the mobile gaming market, particularly hybrid-casual, casual, or midcore games

Условия и льготы

- Competitive salary and other benefits according to the country you reside in.
