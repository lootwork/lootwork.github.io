The Fraud Investigations team helps protect customers and the platform by reviewing suspicious activity, confirming what happened, and taking action to reduce risk. As a Fraud Investigation Analyst, you’ll review fraud alerts, analyze transactional and behavioral signals, and document outcomes in line with internal policy and regulatory requirements. You’ll also route higher-risk cases to the right partners and help limit customer impact through timely, accurate investigations—your work directly supports trust and safety across Robinhood!

This role is based in our Denver, CO and Westlake, TX office(s), with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Review and investigate fraud alerts to assess potential fraudulent activity using common fraud patterns and investigation steps
  • Analyze transactional and behavioral data to make risk-based decisions and complete clear, well-supported case documentation
  • Manage daily alert and case volume to meet quality and service-level targets
  • Partner with product, engineering, legal, compliance, vendor partners, and financial crime teams to gather information and escalate cases when needed
  • Identify recurring fraud trends and suggest specific process improvements based on investigation outcomes

Что вы приносите

  • 0–2 years of experience in fraud investigations or related financial crime, risk, compliance, or operations work, with exposure to account takeovers, identity theft, or money movement fraud
  • Clear written documentation skills and the ability to explain decisions using evidence from account activity
  • Strong attention to detail and comfort working with multiple data points (transactions, device signals, behavioral indicators)
  • Ability to prioritize work, manage queue volume, and meet defined quality and productivity goals
  • Strong collaboration skills when working with partner teams to resolve cases and reduce customer impact
  • Bonus points:
  • CFE, CAMS, CFCS, or similar certification
  • Series 7 and 63 licenses
