As a Graphic Designer, you will be a visual storyteller for our marketing team, responsible for maintaining brand integrity through high-quality communication design and creative excellence.

You’ll help us protect honest people online by

  • Create engaging and visually compelling marketing materials across digital, print and spatial media, including but not limited to advertisements, social media materials, presentations, and event designs.
  • Designing and developing a wide range of branded assets that strengthen and elevate our identity.
  • Executing designs within established frameworks and generating fresh ideas, innovative concepts, and reusable templates.
  • Collaborating with various teams to deliver high-quality design assets for both internal and external use.
  • Maintaining, organizing, and continuously improving our design library and AI skills to ensure consistency and efficiency

You are the right future Veriffian for the job if you have:

  • At least 3 years of experience in a similar role on similar tasks in an agency or in-house, creating various marketing materials, including digital channels and print.
  • A strong portfolio with your exact role and scope clearly outlined for each project.
  • Expertise in Figma and Adobe Creative Suite (at least Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign).
  • Strong understanding of design systems and layouts, with a keen eye for detail in both visuals and text.
  • High proficiency in written and verbal English communication.
  • Ability to collaborate with different teams, but also work independently.
  • Adaptability to work in a fast-paced, evolving industry.
  • Interest in AI’s applications in design.

  • Contribute to the brand story of a company backed by top-tier investors and global entrepreneurs
