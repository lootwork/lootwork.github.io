Team Support (Fixed-term)

We’re looking for a fixed-term (12 months) Team Support to help our central teams do their best work every day. You’ll take care of travel, team days, offsites and events, so the people you support can focus on what they do best. Join us in Helsinki and bring structure and care to a busy, creative environment.

Что предстоит делать

- Provide hands-on administrative support for your designated Supercell teams, from benefits questions to daily operations

- Own complex travel planning and coordination, including international and multi-team trips, from flights and hotels to ground transportation

- Plan and run team days, offsites and internal events that feel effortless and meaningful for everyone taking part

- Work closely with our central team leads to understand their upcoming needs and plan support proactively

- Collaborate with other Team Support professionals across Supercell to align how we support teams globally

Что у вас должно быть

- At least 5 years of experience in team support, travel arrangements and/or event production, ideally in a fast-paced, international environment

- A relevant educational background, such as executive assistant studies, hospitality management or business administration

- Experience from a company that has grown or scaled, where support processes needed to evolve, improve or become more automated

- Strong organisational skills and the ability to manage many moving parts without losing sight of the details

- Clear, thoughtful communication and a collaborative, service-minded approach

- A genuine interest in supporting others and enabling teams to focus on their best work

- A self-starter approach to AI: you’ve found ways to use it in your day-to-day work, you experiment, figure things out and bring the same energy to every new challenge

Как выглядит успех в этой роли

- Your teams feel supported, connected and able to focus on what they do best

- Daily logistics, from travel to events, run smoothly and efficiently

- You bring structure and care to a busy, creative environment, helping the whole company operate better

Где предстоит работать

This role is based in our Helsinki office, where we enjoy working together in person most of the time. We also welcome the occasional remote day when it suits the work. To be successful, we expect you to be able to do some travelling.

О SUPERCELL

Supercell is a games company from Helsinki, Finland, with offices https://supr.cl/jd-offices also in San Francisco, Seoul, Shanghai, and London. You might know us as the makers of Hay Day, Clash of Clans, Boom Beach, Clash Royale, and Brawl Stars. Our mission is to create great games https://supr.cl/jd-games that as many people as possible play for years and that are remembered forever.

So, how do we make great games? By putting together the best teams and giving them the freedom and independence to succeed. And by taking risks, failing, sharing learnings https://supercell.com/en/news/learning-from-failures/, and killing lots of projects.

HEY, YOU MIGHT LOVE IT HERE!

Independent cells and trust are at the core of our culture https://supr.cl/jd-why-you. But it takes more than that to make great games. We take good care of our people, providing them with the compensation, work environment, and resources they need to succeed while having fun along the way.

YOU ARE NOT YOUR JOB TITLE ™

Here, you won’t need to focus on chasing titles https://supr.cl/jd-job-title or climbing ladders. Internally, our job titles don’t include prefixes like Senior, Junior, Principal, or Director. Recognition isn’t tied to your title, as it doesn't define the impact you can have around here.

NOT SURE IF YOU SHOULD APPLY?

Many candidates with great skills and experience second-guess themselves. The bar is high, but if this role excites you, apply! We’re here to help you succeed. Also, we're happy to learn about any specific accommodations you may need to fully engage in our recruitment process.
