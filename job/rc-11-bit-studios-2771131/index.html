We’re looking for a Level Designer to join the team working on a new 11 bit studios unannounced project.

You’ll help us shape a large, diverse world combining a mix of natural environments with human-made spaces, focused on exploration, survival and first-person traversal. We’re looking for someone who enjoys exploring ideas, prototyping solutions and thinking about how to build engaging spaces at scale, rather than only working within already established patterns.

This position requires working in a hybrid model in our Warsaw office.

Ваши обязанности

  • Designing and iterating open-world locations, from early greyboxes to final layouts,
  • Creating natural environments and different biomes that support exploration, clear navigation and first-person traversal,
  • Designing gameplay spaces around survival mechanics, verticality, combat and environmental challenges,
  • Building varied locations using modular and scalable solutions,
  • Shaping both outdoor and indoor spaces, including shelters, abandoned locations and cave systems,
  • Working closely with Environment Art, Tech Art and Game Design to turn prototypes into convincing and performant locations.
