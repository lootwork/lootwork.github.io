О вакансии

Here at Wargaming, we are looking for an ActionScript UI Developer to strengthen the World of Tanks Modern Armor team.

You will join a talented and passionate team responsible for developing and maintaining the Game User Interface for our console game. Working closely with game designers, artists, QA engineers, and other developers, you will contribute to creating engaging experiences for our players and shaping the future of the game.

Reports to

Development Director

Что предстоит делать

  • Design, implement, and maintain UI features for the game client using ActionScript
  • Develop user interfaces of varying complexity, including screens, menus, HUD elements, and interactive UI components
  • Integrate UI solutions with the game client and existing game systems
  • Support and maintain existing UI code and solutions
  • Refactor existing code to keep it clean, reliable, and maintainable
  • Implement and support UI animations and interactive elements
  • Collaborate closely with game designers, artists, and QA engineers throughout the UI development process
  • Investigate and resolve UI-related issues and contribute to improving the overall quality of the game interface
  • Participate in R&D activities and contribute to the development of UI solutions and practices

Кого мы ищем

  • 2+ years of professional experience in UI development using ActionScript
  • Strong knowledge of ActionScript 3 and object-oriented programming
  • Experience developing UI for games or other interactive products
  • Understanding of UI architecture, patterns, and development principles
  • Experience working with UI animations and interactive components
  • Experience with version control systems
  • Experience working with design tools such as Figma
  • Intermediate English (B2) or higher

Что поможет выделиться

  • Professional experience in console or PC game development
  • Experience with Scaleform or other game UI frameworks
  • Experience working with complex UI animations
  • Knowledge of C++ or other programming languages
  • Experience with Python
  • Experience working with proprietary game technologies or game engines

Формат работы

  • This role is open in our Belgrade, Vilnius, and Nicosia offices
  • Onsite or hybrid in the office (2-3 days per week in the office)
  • This role is eligible for relocation & immigration support

О Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming's titles across all major gaming platforms.
