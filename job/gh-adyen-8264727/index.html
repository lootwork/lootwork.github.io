Team Lead - Software Engineer

As a Software Engineering Team Lead based in Singapore, you will lead a team of highly skilled software engineers responsible for building and evolving Adyen’s Alternative Payment Methods (APMs) platform for the APAC region. Your team plays a critical role in enabling merchants to offer a broad range of local and global payment methods, helping businesses reach more customers and deliver seamless payment experiences.

You'll work closely with Product Managers, Architects and Engineering teams to develop and scale integrations with alternative payment methods, including digital wallets, bank transfers, real-time payment schemes and other locally relevant payment solutions. You'll drive the technical direction of the team, ensuring our payment capabilities remain scalable, reliable and maintainable as we expand into new markets and support evolving payment methods.

As a people leader, you'll coach and develop engineers, foster a culture of ownership, and guide the team through complex technical challenges. As part of Adyen's global engineering organisation, you'll collaborate with teams across Europe, APAC and North America to build payment solutions that scale globally while meeting the specific needs of local markets.

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead, coach and develop a team of Software Engineers, supporting their technical and professional growth.
  • Foster a high-performing engineering culture built on ownership, collaboration and continuous learning.
  • Partner closely with Product Managers to translate business priorities and market needs into scalable technical solutions.
  • Drive the technical direction of the team, balancing product delivery with long-term platform scalability, resilience and maintainability.
  • Guide architectural discussions and engineering decisions relating to APM integrations, payment processing and platform capabilities.
  • Build and evolve integrations with alternative payment methods, including local payment schemes, digital wallets, bank transfers and other regional payment solutions.
  • Ensure payment integrations are reliable, secure and scalable, with appropriate handling of payment flows, transaction statuses, errors and reconciliation.
  • Collaborate with Engineering Managers, Architects and Team Leads across Adyen to improve engineering practices and execution.
  • Act as an escalation point for complex technical challenges, helping the team navigate ambiguity and make thoughtful engineering trade-offs.
  • Continuously improve engineering quality through better testing, observability, operational excellence and developer productivity.
  • Contribute to Adyen's APM strategy in APAC, balancing local market requirements and payment method expansion with long-term platform scalability, resilience and maintainability.

Кто вы

  • You have experience leading and developing high-performing software engineering teams.
  • You have a strong software engineering background building large-scale, distributed systems using Java or similar backend technologies.
  • You enjoy coaching engineers through technical mentorship, regular feedback and career development.
  • You're comfortable leading technical discussions, facilitating architectural decisions and balancing engineering quality with business priorities.
  • You have experience working closely with Product Managers and cross-functional stakeholders to deliver customer-focused solutions.
  • You have strong problem-solving skills and enjoy tackling complex technical challenges at scale.
  • Experience building payment systems or integrating alternative payment methods, such as digital wallets, bank transfers, real-time payment schemes or local payment methods, is highly desirable.
  • You understand the complexities of payment processing and integrations, including transaction flows, payment status handling, error management and reconciliation.
  • Experience working with APAC payment methods and local payment ecosystems is a plus.
  • You're passionate about building resilient, scalable systems and continuously improving engineering practices.
  • You thrive in collaborative, globally distributed engineering teams and communicate effectively with stakeholders across different functions and regions.
  • You embody Adyen's culture of ownership, collaboration and continuous improvement.
