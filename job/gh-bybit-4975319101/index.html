О нас

Established in 2018, Bybit is one of the world’s leading cryptocurrency exchanges and digital financial platforms, serving over 80 million users across more than 200 countries and regions. Powered by world-class technology and a user-first mindset, Bybit delivers a seamless ecosystem across trading, payments, wealth management, custody, institutional services, and Web3 — connecting users to the future of digital finance.

Our core values define how we build. We listen, care and improve to create products and experiences that put users first. Backed by a global team of ambitious builders, problem-solvers, and innovators, we foster a high-performance and fast-moving environment where talent is empowered to drive real impact at the global scale. Supported by 24/7 multilingual customer service and a strong commitment to innovation, we are shaping the future of finance through technology, collaboration, and bold execution.

Today, Bybit is recognized as one of the most trusted and transparent platforms in the digital asset industry, continuing to expand its global presence while building the infrastructure for the next generation of financial services.

Обязанности

  • Review marketing, advertising, sponsorship, branding and promotional materials from a regulatory compliance perspective.
  • Advise Marketing, BD and regional teams on applicable requirements, regulatory risks and mitigation measures.
  • Interpret and apply marketing, advertising and financial promotion rules across relevant jurisdictions.
  • Escalate matters to Legal, local Compliance Officers or external counsel where legal interpretation is required.
  • Maintain marketing compliance frameworks, review protocols, internal guidance and regulatory trackers.
  • Monitor regulatory developments impacting marketing and promotional activities globally.
  • Support the investigation and remediation of marketing-related compliance incidents.
  • Deliver compliance training to Marketing and business teams.
  • Prepare regular reporting on key risks, incidents and remediation actions.
  • Support regulatory enquiries or engagements relating to marketing activities where required.

Требования

  • 6+ years of experience in regulatory compliance, marketing compliance, financial promotions or a related role.
  • Strong understanding of marketing, advertising and financial promotion requirements in regulated industries, with demonstrated experience reviewing campaigns, sponsorships, promotions, customer communications, affiliates activities.
  • Ability to interpret regulatory requirements and translate them into practical business guidance.
  • Multi-jurisdictional experience is strongly preferred.
  • Strong stakeholder management, communication and project management skills.
  • Demonstrated ability to use AI and emerging technology to improve research, review and compliance workflows.
  • Candidates with a legal qualification will be given preference
  • Fluent in English

Профиль кандидата

  • Strong interest in crypto, digital assets and the evolving regulatory landscape.
  • Commercially minded and able to balance regulatory requirements with business objectives.
  • Comfortable challenging stakeholders and providing clear, practical advice.
  • Detail-oriented while able to assess broader regulatory and reputational implications.
  • Adaptable, ownership-driven and comfortable operating in a fast-paced, multi-jurisdictional environment.
