О нас

Established in 2018, Bybit is one of the world’s leading cryptocurrency exchanges and digital financial platforms, serving over 80 million users across more than 200 countries and regions. Powered by world-class technology and a user-first mindset, Bybit delivers a seamless ecosystem across trading, payments, wealth management, custody, institutional services, and Web3 — connecting users to the future of digital finance.

Our core values define how we build. We listen, care and improve to create products and experiences that put users first. Backed by a global team of ambitious builders, problem-solvers, and innovators, we foster a high-performance and fast-moving environment where talent is empowered to drive real impact at the global scale. Supported by 24/7 multilingual customer service and a strong commitment to innovation, we are shaping the future of finance through technology, collaboration, and bold execution.

Today, Bybit is recognized as one of the most trusted and transparent platforms in the digital asset industry, continuing to expand its global presence while building the infrastructure for the next generation of financial services.

Основные обязанности

  • Agent SDK and Runtime — Design and implement the orchestration engine, runtime, and core primitives. Ensure that agentic loops remain steerable and verifiable while preserving conversational and adaptive capabilities.
  • MCP Ecosystem Expansion — Expose internal systems as MCP servers, sub-agents, and agent skills. Establish unified mechanisms for tool discovery, dynamic registration, RBAC, rate limiting, and version management.
  • Agent Development Lifecycle (ADLC) — Build an end-to-end toolchain that accommodates the non-deterministic nature of Agent behavior, covering Spec → Eval → Build → Test → A/B Testing → Canary Release → Monitoring → Continuous Iteration.
  • End-to-End Ownership of Quality and Cost — Take ownership of accuracy, resolution rate, cost, and latency. Establish data-driven feedback loops for continuous Agent personalization and optimization.

Требования

Mandatory Requirements

  • Education and Experience
  • Bachelor's degree or above in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related discipline.
  • P7: 6+ years of experience, including at least 3 years in AI or Agent development. P7+: 8+ years of experience with proven zero-to-one Agent platform development experience.
  • Engineering Fundamentals
  • Proficiency in Go or Python, with strong production-grade system design capabilities and the ability to produce high-quality Technical Requirements Documents (TRDs).
  • LLM Application Engineering
  • Strong expertise in LLM APIs, prompt engineering, and the ReAct pattern.
  • Experience in context window management, including token budgeting, conversation history compression, and sub-agent context compression.
  • Production-Grade Agent Experience
  • Proven experience taking an Agent system from zero to production and establishing a continuous improvement lifecycle.
  • Experience must go beyond demos or proofs of concept, with actual production traffic and measurable effectiveness metrics.
  • Hands-on Agent Engineering
  • Familiarity with at least one Agent orchestration framework, such as LangGraph, Dify, Eino, Embabel, or OpenAI Agents SDK.
  • Practical experience with multi-agent collaboration, including Agent Teams, parallel execution, DAG-based orchestration, or handoffs.
  • Complementary Agent Capabilities — Deep Expertise in at Least One Area
  • RAG engineering: vector retrieval, hybrid search, and reranking.
  • Evaluation frameworks: LLM-as-a-Judge, Golden Sets, regression testing, and A/B testing.
  • Engineering Discipline
  • Ability to quantify Agent effectiveness and cost, with a strong focus on token efficiency.
  • Understanding of PII masking and prompt injection prevention.
  • Ability to identify regulatory and compliance boundaries in financial applications.

Будет плюсом

  • Zero-to-one experience building Agent simulation or benchmarking platforms comparable in scope to τ-Bench.
  • Experience with self-hosted LLM deployment, including inference frameworks such as vLLM and Triton, and models such as DeepSeek, Qwen, and GLM.
  • Domain-specific AI application experience: Crypto and financial AI (risk management, KYC, compliance), AIOps (alert RCA and build diagnostics), customer service AI, or AI-powered software engineering.
  • Publicly demonstrated AI security research, including prompt injection prevention, jailbreak mitigation, or AI-powered smart contract security.
  • Core contributions to open-source projects such as LangChain, LangGraph, Dify, MCP, Eino, or Vercel AI SDK.
  • Demonstrated technical influence through blogs, conference talks, research papers, or open-source contributions.
