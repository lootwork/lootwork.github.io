Position Overview：

Responsible for the product's worldview setting and meta narrative copy; enhance long-term retention and emotional attachment through characters, storylines and thematic packaging (benchmarking narrative-driven merge products such as Gossip Harbor). Serves overseas markets; native-level English writing is a core requirement.

Responsibilities：

  • Contribute to the product's worldview setting, character design and narrative line development
  • Write in-game copy: story dialogue, quest text, event narratives and character lines
  • Collaborate with the localization team to ensure cultural adaptation and language quality of narratives in target markets
  • Work with operations / art teams to integrate narrative into LiveOps events and meta scenarios
  • Research narrative preferences and pop culture trends in target markets (North America / Europe)

Требования

  • Bachelor's degree or above in Creative Writing, Literature, Media, Drama, Game Design, Digital Media or related fields
  • Native-level English writing ability (serving overseas markets; the copy IS the product)
  • Strong storytelling and character-building skills; must provide a writing portfolio / script samples
  • Understanding of and passion for narrative-driven casual games
  • Effective collaboration across cross-functional teams

Будет плюсом

  • Published work or internship experience in game narrative / scriptwriting / content creation / screenwriting
  • Multilingual ability (Chinese / others), beneficial for cross-market narrative and localization collaboration
  • Familiarity with the meta structure of merge / simulation / narrative-driven games
