Что предстоит делать

Strategic Product Management

  • Insights & Discovery: Synthesize market research and in-game player data into actionable strategies that drive product evolution.
  • Roadmap Governance: Prioritize high-impact initiatives and manage resources to maintain a high-performing product roadmap.
  • Operational Excellence: Partner with cross-functional departments—including Engineering, Music, and Art—to ensure efficient feature delivery while maintaining a high quality bar for all releases.
  • Iterative Analysis: Evaluate feature performance and test hypotheses to refine future roadmap decisions and drive continuous improvement.

Leadership & Talent Cultivation

  • Team Mentorship: Elevate the Product team's capabilities through clear goal-setting and direct, actionable feedback.
  • Organizational Growth: Recruit top talent and foster a collaborative environment that upholds Uplive's core cultural values.

AI Implementation & Efficiency

  • Scalable Systems: Design reusable product strategies and collaborate on infrastructure that supports automated, scalable operations.
  • AI Innovation: Stay at the forefront of AI trends to optimize production workflows and enhance LiveOps efficiency.

Требования

  • Experience: 4-7 years in mobile game or app development, with significant experience in LiveOps and hybrid casual titles.
  • LiveOps Expertise: Demonstrated success in designing and scaling LiveOps systems, including retention strategies and monetization models.
  • AI Knowledge: Experience implementing AI products or tools to enhance LiveOps and production workflows.
  • Leadership Experience: At least 1 year in leadership positions, known for building high-performing teams and providing clear direction.
  • Strategic Thinking: Ability to move from high-level strategy to tactical execution, balancing long-term goals with short-term needs.
  • Adaptability & Resilience: Skilled at handling ambiguity, shifting priorities, and working in fast-paced environments.
  • Communication: Strong verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to translate complex concepts into clear directives for diverse teams.
  • Mobile Game & Ad Tech Knowledge: Solid understanding of the mobile gaming ecosystem and ad tech.
  • Passion for Music & Games: Genuine excitement for crafting music-based game experiences that resonate with players.
  • Language: Fluent in English, both written and spoken.
