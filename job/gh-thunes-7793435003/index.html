О Thunes

Thunes is the Smart Superhighway for money movement around the world. Thunes’ proprietary Direct Global Network allows Members to make payments in real-time in over 130 countries and more than 80 currencies.

Thunes’ Network connects directly to over 7 billion mobile wallets and bank accounts worldwide, via more than 350 different payment methods, such as GCash, M-Pesa, Airtel, MTN, Orange, JazzCash, Easypaisa, AliPay, WeChat Pay and many more.

Members of Thunes’ Direct Global Network include gig economy giants like Uber and Deliveroo, super-apps like Grab and WeChat, MTOs, fintechs, PSPs and banks. Thunes’ Direct Global Network differentiates itself through its worldwide reach, in-house Smart Treasury Management Platform and Fortress Compliance Infrastructure, ensuring Members of the Network receive unrivalled speed, control, visibility, protection and cost efficiencies when making real-time payments globally.

Headquartered in Singapore, Thunes has offices in 12 locations, including Barcelona, Beijing, Dubai, London, Manila, Nairobi, Paris, Riyadh, San Francisco, Sao Paulo and Shanghai. For more information, visit: https://www.thunes.com/

Context of the role

Join our dynamic team as a Pre-Sales Engineer, where you will play a crucial role in bridging the gap between our sales, product, and onboarding teams. Your expertise will ensure that our merchant and pay-in partners are provided with tailored solutions that leverage Thunes' innovative products, while also gaining insights into industry trends and competitor strategies.

Обязанности

  • Work closely with the sales team during the pre-sales and technical pre-sales cycle.
  • Understand the merchant and pay-in partners' needs and propose suitable solutions using the company's product.
  • Gather the partner's requirements, validate the use cases, and confirm the project scope before handing it over to the inbound onboarding team.
  • Perform market research to become knowledgeable about our industry and competitors.
  • Be an expert on our different products at Thunes.

Требования

  • 3 - 5 years of experience in solution consulting and technical project management with external stakeholders.
  • Deep understanding of payment and merchant solutions in the fintech domain.
  • Excellent communication skills with the ability to pitch Thunes, explain our product clearly, and translate needs/requirements to the product team.

Based outside Barcelona? If you have the right to work in Spain, we can support your move with a relocation package.
