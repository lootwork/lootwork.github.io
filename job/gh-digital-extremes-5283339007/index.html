О THIS POSITION

Digital Extremes is looking for a detail-oriented and self-starting IT Support Technician to join our IT team onsite at our London, Ontario studio. Working alongside our IT leads, you’ll provide day-to-day hardware and software support across the studio, handle Jira requests, and set up workstations for new hires, helping our development teams stay productive.

Обязанности

  • Work under the guidance of the IT Support Manager to maintain efficient studio infrastructure and support operations
  • Daily frontline Tier 1/2 helpdesk triage and user support via Jira Service Management and Slack
  • Daily end user hardware support (laptop and desktop component diagnostics, hardware refreshes/upgrades)
  • Daily software troubleshooting (Windows 11, P4V, Visual Studio, Autodesk Maya/Max, Adobe CC, internal studio tools)
  • Remote and hybrid worker support (troubleshooting Parsec host/client streaming, remote software and hardware diagnostics, dispatching of parts/techs via warranty)
  • Setup, deployment, and basic troubleshooting of console dev kits
  • Basic user account management across Active Directory, Google Workspace, Slack, and Jira
  • Workstation imaging, OS deployment, and system setup for new hires and life-cycles
  • Hardware inventory tracking (ServiceNow) and stock maintenance
  • Accurate, thorough documentation of ticketing notes, troubleshooting steps, and knowledge base articles
  • Workstation setups, physical office/desk moves, and hardware organization tasks

Требования

  • Postsecondary in IT, Networking or equivalent experience
  • 2+ years experience in IT at an administrative level
  • Knowledge of research and development analysis techniques

PREFERRED EXTRAS

  • Strong communication skills as well as strong attention to detail
  • Be a team player and a self-starter with the ability to multitask.

О DIGITAL EXTREMES

Founded in 1993 by James Schmalz, Digital Extremes ranks as one of the world's top independent video game development studios. Originating with the co-creation of Epic Games' multi-million unit selling Unreal® franchise including Unreal and Unreal Tournament, Digital Extremes went on to develop Dark Sector®, BioShock® for the PlayStation®3, the BioShock 2 multiplayer campaign, and The Darkness® II. The studio has reached its greatest critical and commercial success with the free-to-play action game, Warframe®, boasting a global community of 75 million registered players on PC, PS4™, PS5™, Xbox One™, Xbox Series X/S™, Nintendo Switch™, and iOS™. The studio's most recently announced project is the much anticipated Soulframe®, an open-world action-fantasy MMORPG title that is currently under development.

Digital Extremes is headquartered in London, Ontario, Canada, and is proud to be selected as one of Canada's Top 100 Employers.

For more information and exciting news related to Digital Extremes, please visit www.digitalextremes.com.
