Что предстоит делать

We’re looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join our Core Infrastructure team. In this role, you will be responsible for designing and implementing core components crucial to the product's scalability.

As part of your role, you will be expected to find creative solutions to various architectural and implementation challenges, designing and executing solutions for a unique set of problems we encounter as a state-of-the-art technical product.

you will be expected to take full ownership of your work — from initial design and implementation through to deployment and production support. You drive features end-to-end, see them through to completion, and take responsibility for their success.

What You’ll Bring

  • Strong coding skills in C++/Rust (5+ years).
  • Proven experience designing and operating large-scale distributed services in production, on hyperscale cloud infrastructure (AWS preferred).
  • Hands-on experience with AWS managed services (EC2, EKS, S3, KMS) - strong advantage.
  • Active daily use of AI tools in your engineering workflow (Copilot, Cursor, Claude, etc.).
  • Proven ability to own a problem end-to-end: design, implementation, deployment, and production support.
  • Python - advantage.
  • Experience with blockchain, cryptography, or MPC - strong advantage.
