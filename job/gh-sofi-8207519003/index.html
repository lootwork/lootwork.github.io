О роли

As a Senior Staff Identity and Access Management (IAM) Engineer at SoFi, you will be at the forefront of securing SoFi’s IAM ecosystem—architecting, automating, and scaling identity solutions across our workforce, cloud, and rapidly evolving AI infrastructure. In this high-impact role, you will eliminate manual toil through automation, champion Zero Trust values, and ensure seamless regulatory compliance. Beyond workforce identity, you will pioneer the future of our security posture by maturing cloud and non-human identity (NHI) capabilities—designing scalable access controls for AWS workloads, governing the full lifecycle of service accounts and API credentials, and partnering directly with engineering teams to embed identity-first security into next-generation AI-enabled systems. If you're ready to tackle complex identity challenges at scale and define how humans, machines, and AI securely interact, this is your opportunity to make a lasting mark.

Что предстоит делать

As a Senior Staff Identity and Access Management Engineer, you will define and execute the multi-year architecture and strategy for SoFi's IAM, Cloud IAM, Non-Human Identity (NHI), and AI security capabilities across enterprise domains. Daily responsibilities and essential functions include:

  • Define and drive the multi-year technical roadmap and architecture for Identity and Access Management across cloud, SaaS, and on-premises systems utilizing Okta, AWS, and associated identity technologies.
  • Architect and automate secure, least-privilege identity controls for human and non-human identities across multi-account AWS environments, including federation, workload identities, credential lifecycle management, and infrastructure-as-code.
  • Pioneer AI security architecture aligned to OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications, designing scalable controls for agent repositories, agent gateways, MCP servers, and least-privilege agent access.
  • Partner with Engineering, Security, and Information Technology leadership to establish scalable identity and authorization standards for Okta, AWS, Secrets Management, and AI-enabled applications within a Zero Trust security architecture.
  • Lead cross-functional initiatives and coordinate with IAM leadership and project management on technical planning, execution, and reporting across concurrent enterprise programs.
  • Mentor and elevate Staff (IC4) and Senior (IC3) IAM engineers, leading design reviews and guiding the team to maintain our IAM governance and certification programs to meet strict financial services compliance obligations.
  • Evaluate and select tools, methods, techniques, and evaluation criteria to meet operational goals, reduce systemic complexity, and remediate excessive, orphaned, and persistent access.
  • Advise internal teams on third-party access architectures, security policies, and IAM best practices.

What you’ll need

  • Education & Minimum Experience Requirements: Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science, Information Security, or a related field + 4+ years of experience in Identity and Access Management (IAM) or Security Engineering OR Master’s Degree + 3+ years of experience
  • Proven experience designing, configuring, and deploying Okta’s Identity Platform at enterprise scale, including Okta Identity Governance and Okta Workflows.
  • Demonstrated experience designing and automating identity and authorization controls in AWS, including AWS IAM Identity Center, IAM roles, cross-account access, federation, and least-privilege policy design.
  • Hands-on experience managing the full lifecycle and security of non-human identities (NHI), such as service accounts, workload identities, API credentials, certificates, and secrets.
  • Experience securing access for AI-enabled applications or autonomous agents, including service identity, data authorization, API/tool permissions, auditability, and AI infrastructure (e.g., MCP servers, open APIs, agent skills, and agent gateways).
  • Deep knowledge of cybersecurity and IAM concepts, Zero Trust architectures, and regulatory compliance frameworks supporting medium-to-large organizations.
  • Proven experience providing architectural oversight, design review leadership, and mentorship to engineers supporting Cloud Identity, Privileged Access Management (PAM), or Secrets Management solutions.
  • Okta Certified Administrator certification.
  • Exceptional problem-solving, research, written, and verbal communication skills, with the ability to work both autonomously and collaboratively across cross-functional engineering and security teams.
