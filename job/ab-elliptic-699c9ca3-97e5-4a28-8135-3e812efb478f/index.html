The impact you will have

Elliptic is at an inflection point, shifting from crypto compliance tool to data and AI intelligence platform. As Senior Campaigns Manager, you will orchestrate the global campaigns that tell that story and move enterprise buyers through the journey across our four core segments: financial institutions, PSPs, crypto natives and government. Working closely with the Director of Marketing, you will turn campaign strategy into programs that field marketing can localize, sales can act on and leadership can measure in pipeline. You will also turn our industry associations into a real channel into our ICPs. With strong knowledge of the US market, you will bring campaign leadership closer to our largest market and ensure every global campaign lands with US buyers.

Что предстоит делать

Campaign orchestration

- Orchestrate global integrated campaigns end to end, from plan and messaging through launch, nurture and pipeline, bringing content, creative, paid media, events, partnerships and field marketing together around one plan.

- Give field marketing everything they need to localize each campaign for their region, including messaging, assets, audiences, timelines and measurement.

- Set channel strategy based on how enterprise buyers research and buy, tailor messaging by audience and geography, and test content formats across each stage of the buying journey.

- Own what happens after launch: nurture, the hand-off to sales and volume targets through to pipeline. Optimize campaigns while they're live, not just in the post-campaign review.

- Report on campaign impact against the objectives you set, and turn what you learn into clear recommendations for the next campaign.

Campaign operations and AI

- Run email sends, webinars and other repeatable campaign tasks expertly from day one, knowing how to optimize each one for its channel.

- Build effective, scalable workflows that cut manual effort across the campaign cycle, so the team spends its time on the work that moves results.

- Take an AI-first approach: build agents for repeatable campaign work and shape how the team uses AI across planning, production and optimization.

- Build in HubSpot without support, including audiences, workflows, landing pages and email.

Organic social, newsletter and content

- Own organic social strategy and execution, with a clear point of view on what Elliptic should be saying, where and to whom, and how every campaign shows up across LinkedIn, X and other channels.

- Own newsletter strategy and performance, partnering with our content team to build programs that earn their audience and move readers through the buying journey.

- Bring a content marketing lens to every campaign, partnering with our content team on which stories and formats will move enterprise buyers at each stage, and using social and newsletter performance to sharpen them.

Associations and memberships

- Get the most from our existing association partnerships, such as our ABA diamond partnership, American Banker and the Digital Chamber, using each one as a channel into our ICPs through content promotion, dinners, speaking and co-marketing.

- Identify new associations and membership groups, and build the business case for each based on the access it gives us to our ICPs.

- Work with our Global Policy and Regulatory Group to explore how the regulatory memberships they hold can work as a marketing channel, regionally and globally.

- Work alongside partner marketing, who keep ownership of partner management.

Cross-functional partnership

- Partner with the Director of Marketing on campaign strategy and own its delivery end to end.

- Work with paid media as a true partner on how paid fits the campaign strategy, from planning through to optimization.

- Work closely with creative, content, events, partnerships and field marketing so every campaign lands as one global program.

- Support product marketing and comms during rapid response moments.

You will be a great fit if you

- Orchestrate rather than execute. You get the right people moving in the same direction and hold the plan together across teams and regions.

- Care about the impact a campaign makes, not just whether it shipped. You set clear objectives, work out the best way to meet them and report on how well you did in a way that shows the business what it got.

- Reach for AI first and can explain how it changes the way you run a campaign.

- Look at a manual process and see the workflow that should replace it.

- Have real curiosity about crypto and digital assets because you find the space interesting.
