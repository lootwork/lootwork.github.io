At Rockstar Games, we create world-class entertainment experiences.

Become part of a team working on some of the most rewarding, large-scale creative projects to be found in any entertainment medium - all within an inclusive, highly-motivated environment where you can learn and collaborate with some of the most talented people in the industry.

Rockstar Games is on the lookout for a passionate Senior Full Stack Developer to join our Performance Capture Tracking team. You will be responsible for our performance capture and production web applications used by our teams worldwide.

This is a full-time, in-office position based out of Rockstar’s new state-of-the-art game development studio in Carlsbad, CA.

WHAT WE DO

  • We develop and maintain internal web applications to improve and standardize production workflows.
  • We help diagnose and troubleshoot unexpected issues that arise in our web applications and relevant tools that integrate with our applications.
  • We monitor and optimize web applications and databases to iterate on performance.
  • We collaborate with other departments and stakeholders to enhance application functionality.

RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Define objectives and key results and translate them into clear technical roadmaps.
  • Collaborate with Production to plan features and schedule initiatives.
  • Work closely with teams across Performance Capture, Animation, R&D and Tech Art to align systems architecture.
  • Provide technical mentorship and help the team address complex development challenges.
  • Contribute directly to codebases across Websites, APIs, and Databases.
  • Ensure systems are reliable, maintainable, and meet performance benchmarks.

REQUIREMENTS

  • 7+ years of professional web development experience.
  • 5+ years of professional experience in C#, .NET, and SQL for building scalable systems.
  • 5+ years of professional experience using React (TypeScript/JavaScript)
  • Established mentoring skills with track record of building and leveling up strong team members.
  • Outstanding communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Comfortable writing and consuming REST APIs.
  • Familiarity with Agile development practices.
  • Experience using version control systems such as GitHub.

PLUSES

Please note that these are desirable skills and are not required to apply for the position.

  • Experience with Next.js, Python or Kafka.
  • Familiarity with CI/CD tools like TeamCity or Jenkins.
  • Experience writing unit tests and improving legacy code.
  • Experience using Postman, Bruno, or similar API testing tools.
  • Familiarity with MongoDB or the Elastic Stack.
  • Experience or knowledge of Motion Capture/Film Production.
  • Passion for Rockstar Games and our titles.
