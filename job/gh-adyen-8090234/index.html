This is Adyen

Adyen provides payments, data, and financial products in a single solution for customers like Meta, Uber, H&M, and Microsoft - making us the financial technology platform of choice. At Adyen, everything we do is engineered for ambition.

For our teams, we create an environment with opportunities for our people to succeed, backed by the culture and support to ensure they are enabled to truly own their careers. We are motivated individuals who tackle unique technical challenges at scale and solve them as a team. Together, we deliver innovative and ethical solutions that help businesses achieve their ambitions faster.

Lifecycle Experience Team

The Lifecycle Experience teams aim to build a best-in-class customer experience for onboarding to Adyen's platform. Our product offers a one-stop solution to unlock access to a wide range of financial products and services. Tasks that we handle include identifying and verifying customer data, connecting payout accounts, ensuring compliance with all applicable local and global regulations, and securing legal entity data in motion and at rest.

At a high level, our team builds and maintains both API and UI integration methods and seeks to improve our automation and compliance processes for our users. Day-to-day, our work can span from adding a field, to integrating with internal or third-party services for data enhancement, to preparing to launch our platform in a new region, and much more. Successful onboarding is ensured via designing a systematic API and an intuitive UI to guide customers through the entire journey with minimal manual data entry from the user or manual intervention from Adyen's operations teams. You will be responsible for researching, creating PoCs, integrating with external providers, and anything else necessary to reach this goal.
