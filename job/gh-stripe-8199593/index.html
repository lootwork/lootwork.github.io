Кто мы

О Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.

О команде

The Cards team at Stripe is responsible for the infrastructure, user-facing features, and business of accepting credit, debit, and prepaid card payments. Delivering the best offering for processing credit, debit, and prepaid cards is critical to Stripe’s mission, given the central role card payments play in internet commerce.

Что предстоит делать

We are hiring a product manager to drive our card network efforts and strategy across Europe and Asia Pacific. Specifically, this product manager will own the products and infrastructure powering our processing for local and regional card networks, including domestic card schemes and co-badged cards. A world-class card processing offering is the foundation on which our business is built and what our customers continuously expect. As a result, it is critical that we continuously expand, improve, and maintain the edge of our card processing capabilities. We believe Stripe can truly succeed here and are searching for the right product manager to lead this product effort.

This role is an opportunity to truly “own” a critical platform within Stripe’s core business. This product manager will partner with experienced engineering leads to define and deliver the associated product and infrastructure for these efforts.

Обязанности

  • Work with go-to-market teams (Sales and Account Management) to define how to expand or improve our coverage of local and regional card networks to meet the needs of our customers in Europe and Asia Pacific
  • Work with Business Development and Partnerships to represent our product and users in negotiations with local and regional card networks
  • Engage with merchants to understand their current experience accepting local and regional card brands, associated pain points, and opportunities
  • Collaborate with Engineering to develop Stripe’s roadmap for local and regional card networks
  • Work with Finance to understand the costs of processing across Stripe’s local and regional card networks and drive improvements

Кто вы

We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • Product experience: 6 to 10 years of relevant experience
  • Business acumen and data-driven problem-solving: You enjoy digging into the details of how a business works, identifying how to make it better, and building product and technical solutions to do so. You dig deep into the data, think from first principles, and deliver the right results.
  • Customer- and partner-facing experience: You’re comfortable in front of customers or third-party collaborators and have experience navigating customer conversations.
  • Operational execution: You are strong operationally, capable of structuring and leading a cross-functional team and designing and driving processes. You enjoy doing whatever it takes to execute on complex projects.
  • Clear communication: You pay close attention to detail and communicate precisely and effectively, including with external customers and partners.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with SQL or a willingness and aptitude to learn
  • Experience in an external-facing role (sales or partnerships) and in business negotiations
  • Experience building ML-based products
