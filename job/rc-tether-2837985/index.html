О вакансии

We are looking for a Senior DevOps / Infrastructure Engineer with strong hands-on experience in Google Cloud, Terraform and Kubernetes. If you enjoy the challenge of working with globally distributed clusters in an environment where security and scalability are taken seriously, this role is for you.

Our team has invested in a stable, observable platform. The next step is to scale that foundation. That means moving more workloads onto IaC, hardening our security posture, and preparing our services for rapid user growth. This is a hands-on role in a small, senior, fully remote team. You will own services end to end, make pragmatic technical decisions, and help other teams become self-sufficient. If you like keeping things simple, standard, well automated and monitored, come talk to us.

Обязанности

  • Design, build and maintain our Google Cloud infrastructure using Terraform, with the goal of having all production infrastructure defined as code and reviewed through pull requests.
  • Run and evolve our Kubernetes (GKE) clusters, including cluster upgrades, capacity planning, autoscaling, networking and workload isolation.
  • Migrate remaining manually deployed services onto Kubernetes, with documented and repeatable deployment flows.
  • Consolidate configuration, access and user management across Google Cloud projects into a single source of truth.
  • Keep separate, clearly bounded environments for internal services, partner workloads and future community facing services.
  • Partner with the other internal teams to move their infrastructure onto infrastructure as code and Kubernetes.
  • Keep GitHub Actions secure and efficient: pinned dependencies, least privilege tokens, sensible runner usage and controlled costs.
  • Maintain and extend our metrics, logging and alerting stack so that every service has metrics, logs and actionable alerts.
  • Apply least privilege access across Google Cloud, Kubernetes and GitHub, and review temporary admin rights regularly.
  • Contribute to the team's quarterly and yearly roadmap and give realistic estimates, including proposing discovery periods for complex projects.
