🚀 WHAT YOU'LL BUILD AND WHO YOU'LL WORK WITH

You would join the team building Mo, Alan's AI health companion, as the single place where members come to talk about their health, their insurance and the prevention programmes Alan offers them.

Mo has been available to all our French members (1M+) since Q1 2026. Today it serves very different needs: answering a health question, insurance questions and needs, following a member's health program, and handing over to a doctor when needed. More teams at Alan now want to bring their use cases into Mo. Your mission is to build the core that makes this possible: one companion that knows the member, works across all these use cases and stays reliable, whichever team builds on it.

Concretely, you will

- Evolve the agent architecture behind Mo (orchestration across specialised agents, tool calling, context and memory), so Mo can take on new skills without getting slower or less reliable

- Create clean interfaces for other Alan teams (Customer Support, prevention programm, Alan Shop, Clinic) to plug their use cases into Mo

- Make the conversation itself delightful: fast answers, chat, voice and rich content such as cards and actions

- Own quality across use cases: evaluation, observability and monitoring, so we know where Mo is strong and where it needs work

- Make Mo available in all the countries where Alan operates, with one shared core and local adaptations

⚡ WHY THIS ROLE IS SPECIAL

- One companion, many use cases: Health, insurance, prevention and care meet in the same conversation. You build what connects them.

- Applied AI at scale: You'll ship an agent used by over a million members, in a regulated domain where trust matters.

- Platform thinking: Your work is used by several Alan teams. You design the interfaces, the guardrails and the tooling that let them build on Mo.

- Frontier problems: Agentic workflows, memory, context management, evaluation, cost and latency trade-offs - the hardest open questions of production LLM systems.

- Ownership & Growth: Influence the architecture, mentor other engineers, and lead technical initiatives that shape how members interact with Alan.

🔍 WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR

Technical Requirements

- 3+ years building scalable software systems, with real product ownership

- Strong backend experience (Python is a plus), and comfort across the stack

- Hands-on experience building products with LLMs (agents, tool calling, RAG, memory, prompt engineering, evaluation) - ideally shipped to production

- Experience designing APIs or platforms that other teams build on is a plus

- An evaluation mindset: you know how to measure whether an AI system is actually good, and how to make it better

What we value

- You love turning complex problems into elegant solutions

- You care about the "why" behind what you build, not just the "how"

- You stay on top of a fast-moving AI landscape and are pragmatic about what really works

- You like working with many teams and finding the simple design that serves all of them

- Humble and still willing to grow (no matter your seniority)

- Fluent in English (no need to speak French!)

Mission Alignment

- Healthcare technology excites you

- You want members to get one seamless experience of their health and their insurance

But above all, we are looking for high potential and curiosity: make sure to show us this when you apply! Everything else is a bonus.
