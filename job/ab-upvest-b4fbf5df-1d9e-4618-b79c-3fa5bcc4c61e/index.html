At Upvest, we are on a mission to make investing as easy as spending money. Upvest empowers businesses to offer a wide range of investment products and the best experience in the field of capital market investment and retirement planning. Upvest’s Investment API is easy to integrate so that fintechs and financial institutions can save resources and fully focus on their core business.

We are proud to partner with Europe’s leading Fintechs and financial institutions such as DKB, Revolut, N26 and Raisin. Founded in 2017 by Martin Kassing, Upvest now brings together over 270 talented professionals from more than 70 nationalities. Upvest is backed by €280M in total funding from world-class investors, including BlackRock, Tencent, Sapphire Ventures, and Bessemer Venture Partners, Earlybird, Notion Capital, and Motive. Our latest €105M funding round in March 2026 - led by Sapphire and Tencent - serves as a massive catalyst for our growth, allowing us to offer premier investment experience.

О роли

- Upvest builds the investment infrastructure behind some of Europe’s best-known fintechs and banks. It’s a complex, technical product, and you’ll be the one who makes it understandable, exciting and impossible to ignore

- Your mission is to make Upvest a brand of choice for prospects, clients, and partners across Europe and the UK

- You’ll turn investment infrastructure that most people never see into stories that people want to read, share and remember. You’ll own the Upvest brand end to end: how it sounds, how it looks, and how it shows up.

- The role has grown with the company. You’ll step into a brand with momentum, a lot of opportunity, and a team that backs you

Что предстоит делать

- Tell the story of our brand and look after our visual and verbal identity, keeping it consistent across all external channels

- Own the content strategy and calendar, and create content across social, blog and thought leadership in formats such as video and long- and short-form writing

- Create and distribute client case studies and testimonials, in video and written form

- Produce short- and long-form content that raises the profile of our thought leadership

- Own and write the Upvest newsletter, curating company news, content highlights and industry insights for clients, prospects and partners

- Grow our LinkedIn following and website traffic

- Work on creative assets for brand campaigns, the website and social media that convey our value proposition visually

- Measure and report on content performance and brand impact, and use the results to improve

Что вы приносите

- 3–4 years of B2B marketing experience with a focus on brand and content

- A background in finance or fintech, ideally in B2B SaaS, API-based products or a scale-up

- A strong track record of turning technical product features into engaging content for different audiences

- Excellent storytelling skills and a portfolio of high-performing content

- An outcome-focused approach: you track KPIs and adjust based on results

- Hands-on, proactive ownership: a strategic thinker with the drive to execute

- The ability to deliver high-quality work on time and within budget

- A strong sense of the financial industry, its trends and its competitors

- Fluent English
