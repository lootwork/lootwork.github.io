О роли

Seeking a highly motivated Senior Analyst, Product Finance to join product finance and based in Singapore. The role will partner with product, strategy, and finance teams to enhance operational efficiency, deliver data-driven financial insights and support our growth ambitions. The ideal candidate is expected to excel in fast-paced and high pressure environments, possess a solid accounting background, and be passionate about utilizing systematic solutions and AI to drive operational excellence.

Основные обязанности

- Assist in the development of system solutions for margin analysis; perform initial data deep-dives to investigate discrepancies and provide insights into commercial performance.

- Work closely with the team on new product launches, helping to document data requirements and testing accounting flows to ensure reporting accuracy from day one.

- Work closely with Finance Platforms and Product teams to track cost-saving initiatives and gather data to support business cases.

- Support the implementation of AI-driven tools and automated solutions; maintain meticulous project trackers to ensure all operational improvements reach the finish line.

- Actively participate in a high-performance culture, supporting senior team members on complex projects and contributing to the continuous improvement of team processes.

- Tackle complex product-finance issues with a high level of detail, performing root-cause analysis and suggesting professional, data-backed solutions.

- Maintain the "source of truth" by upholding the absolute accuracy and integrity of financial data, documentation, and audit trails.

- Demonstrate agility by managing multiple tasks and competing deadlines in a fast-paced, pre-IPO environment.

Будет плюсом

- Robust accounting knowledge; familiar with IFRS and US GAAP standards.

- Proven experience driving the adoption of systematic or AI-driven solutions in a finance context.

- Strong leadership and team management skills.

- Exceptional problem-solving abilities and keen attention to detail.

- Ability to communicate complex financial concepts clearly, both verbally and in writing, in both English and Chinese.

- Strong data analysis and programming skills are preferred.
