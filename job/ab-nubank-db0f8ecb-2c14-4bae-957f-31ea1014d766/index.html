О роли

We are looking for an L&D Senior Specialist to lead development initiatives for Nubank's Engineering teams. You will work closely with Engineering leaders and partners to understand their context, identify capability and performance needs, and turn business priorities into practical learning plans and programs.

This is a senior individual contributor role at the intersection of strategy and execution. You will own complex initiatives end to end, exercise judgment in ambiguous and changing situations, influence partners without relying on formal authority, and use evidence to improve outcomes. You will bring deep L&D expertise, creativity and an AI-first mindset to develop solutions that engage engineers and strengthen their ability to deliver business impact.

You’ll be Responsible for

- Understand the business and diagnose needs: Build a close understanding of Engineering's strategy, operating context, audiences and challenges. Combine stakeholder input, data and observation to identify capability gaps, distinguish learning needs from other performance factors, and prioritize opportunities.

- Translate strategy into action: Turn strategic priorities and identified needs into a clear, prioritized development plan, with defined audiences, outcomes, milestones, ownership and measures of success. Adapt the plan as the business context evolves.

- Design and deliver development programs: Lead the design, development, implementation and improvement of learning experiences for Engineering teams. Apply adult learning and instructional design methods, selecting the right mix of formal, experiential, social and on-the-job learning for each challenge.

- Own projects end to end: Manage scope, timelines, dependencies, resources, risks and decisions across concurrent initiatives. Coordinate internal experts, facilitators and external partners where appropriate, communicate progress and resolve obstacles to deliver quality outcomes.

- Partner and influence: Build trusted relationships with Engineering leaders, technical subject matter experts, Centers of Excellence, People partners and other stakeholders. Align expectations, navigate competing priorities and influence decisions through clear recommendations and evidence.

- Measure and improve impact: Define success measures and a practical evaluation approach from the start. Track reach, engagement, learning and application, as well as relevant performance or business outcomes where feasible. Interpret results and feedback, communicate insights and adjust solutions over time.

- Create and scale with AI: Experiment with AI and digital tools to design, personalize and deliver learning solutions, improve the employee experience, increase engagement and expand impact. Choose intentionally between scalable self-service and high-touch experiences.

- Bring expertise and fresh perspectives: Stay current on L&D practices, learning technologies and the evolving needs of technical teams. Share knowledge and contribute to improving how the L&D team works.
