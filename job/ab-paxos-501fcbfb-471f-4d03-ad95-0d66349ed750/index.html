О Paxos

Today’s financial infrastructure is archaic, expensive, inefficient and risky — supporting a system that leaves out more people than it lets in. So we’re rebuilding it.

We’re on a mission to open the world’s financial system to everyone by enabling the instant movement of any asset, any time, in a trustworthy way. For over a decade, we’ve built blockchain infrastructure that tokenizes, custodies, trades and settles assets for the world's leading financial institutions, like PayPal, Venmo, Mastercard, Interactive Brokers and Charles Schwab.

О команде

Paxos is positioning itself as a connected platform to create, custody, distribute and utilize digital assets, and Custody is the control plane of those assets. The Custody product team owns the capabilities that let institutional customers hold, control, and move assets securely, and works hand in hand with Engineering, Compliance/Legal, Operations, and Go-To-Market teams to win tier-1 financial institutions globally.

О роли

Custody is where institutions decide whether they can trust Paxos with their assets. As Senior Product Manager, Custody, you’ll own the product that holds and secures those assets, including wallet management and the controls around how assets move, and set its direction as demand grows from crypto-native firms to banks, asset managers, and wealth platforms.

You’ll form a point of view on where institutional custody is heading and what Paxos needs to build to lead there, then drive it from strategy through launch. That means deciding where to invest to meet the security bar institutions require while staying flexible enough for how customers actually operate. You’ll work through technically deep problems with Engineering and learn directly from institutional clients what it takes for them to go live, turning their requirements into the product.

This is an individual contributor role with real ownership of a strategy that drives institutional adoption of our custody products.

Что предстоит делать

- Own the Custody strategy and roadmap, and drive it through execution, from defining requirements to launch and iteration

- Define the security and control capabilities institutional customers rely on to protect and govern their assets, including wallet management and the controls around how assets are held and moved

- Drive the infrastructure and integrations that extend Custody to meet institutional requirements, including relationships with third-party providers and partners

- Collaborate with Engineering, Design, Compliance, Legal, and Operations to deliver capabilities that are secure, usable, and meet the regulatory expectations of institutional clients

- Work with Go-To-Market teams to drive adoption of new capabilities, from positioning and client onboarding to feedback that shapes what comes next

- Engage directly with institutional customers and prospects, and turn their requirements into capabilities that serve the broader client base

- About you

- 6+ years of product management experience at a high-growth financial infrastructure company

- Deep partnership with engineering teams on highly technical platform problems

- A demonstrated ability to make effective product prioritization decisions with imperfect information

- Demonstrated success getting buy-in from cross-functional teams, including senior and executive stakeholders, on your product vision and strategy in written and verbal forums

- Experience building enterprise B2B products and working directly with clients and client-facing teams

You might also have

- Experience with digital asset custody, wallet infrastructure, key management, or HSMs

- Experience supporting large financial institutions including banks, asset managers, or wealth managers
