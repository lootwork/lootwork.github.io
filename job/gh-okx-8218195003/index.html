О Opportunity

The Global VIP Growth Associate needs to support the global VIP business with data monitoring, analysis, and project execution, providing data and operational support to VIP Relationship Managers (RMs) globally.

We welcome curious, logical, hands-on fresh graduates or early-career professionals. You will learn user growth, data analysis, and cross-functional collaboration on real business problems, and grow toward owning analyses independently.

VIP增长专员需要协助VIP业务团队开展数据监控、分析与项目执行，为VIP客户关系经理（RM）提供数据与运营支持。

我们欢迎逻辑清晰、对数据有好奇心、愿意动手的应届生或早期职场人。你将在真实业务中系统学习用户增长、数据分析和跨部门协作的方法，并逐步成长为能够独立负责专题分析的增长经理。

Что предстоит делать

  • Based on business needs and insights, prepare the data, charts, and key points for reports, complete topic analyses for the global VIP business, and support the team in reporting to senior management.
  • Maintain global VIP core metrics and run daily monitoring; escalate unexpected metric movements promptly and complete the first round of investigation.
  • Using existing templates and dashboards, produce weekly and monthly reports, and independently build and maintain automated reports.
  • Based on analysis conclusions and business requests, document business SOPs, metric definitions, and working methods.
  • As PMO, communicate requirements with the global RM team, Product, Compliance, and other teams, follow up on progress, and see deliverables through.
  • Collect and organize feedback from frontline teams and users as a reference for the team's analysis and for improving product experience and service processes.
  • 基于业务需求和业务洞察，整理报告所需的数据、图表和要点，完成VIP业务专题分析，支持团队向管理层汇报。
  • 维护VIP核心指标，执行日常数据监控；发现指标异常波动时及时上报，并完成初步排查。
  • 基于既有模板和数据看板，产出周报、月报，能独立创建 / 维护自动化报表。
  • 基于分析结论和业务诉求，整理业务SOP、指标口径及工作方法。
  • 作为PMO，能与RM 团队、产品、合规等团队沟通需求并跟进进度，完成交付。
  • 收集一线团队及用户反馈，归类整理，供团队分析和优化产品体验、服务流程时参考。

What We Look For In You

  • Bachelor’s degree or above; a fresh graduate, or 1–2 years of experience in user growth, strategy operations, business analytics, data analysis, client operations, or a related field (relevant internships count). Any major; Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Finance, Business, or Computer Science preferred.
  • Solid SQL fundamentals and able to write common queries independently (filtering, aggregation, multi-table joins).
  • Data-minded with basic analytical logic; able to spot issues in the data and explain clearly what happened and the likely reasons; understands the difference between correlation and causation.
  • A fast learner who is careful and takes ownership of data accuracy; able to break tasks down and deliver on time with guidance.
  • Good communication and collaboration skills; proactively shares progress and asks questions early.
  • Able to use AI tools to scale output and improve efficiency.
  • Fluent in written and spoken English and Chinese; Chinese is used as a working language.
  • 本科及以上学历；应届毕业生，或具备1–2年用户增长、策略运营、商业分析、数据分析、客户运营等相关经验（相关实习经历可计入）。专业不限，统计、数学、经济、金融、管理、计算机等相关专业优先。
  • 具备SQL基础，能够独立编写常用查询（筛选、聚合、多表关联）。
  • 具备数据敏感度和基础逻辑分析能力，能够从数据中发现问题，并清晰表达“发生了什么、可能的原因是什么”；了解相关关系与因果关系的区别。
  • 学习能力强，认真细致，对数据准确性有责任心；能够在指导下拆解任务并按时交付。
  • 具备良好的沟通与协作意识，能够主动同步进度、及时提问并寻求支持。
  • 具备使用AI工具规模化产出，提高效率的能力。
  • 具备流利的中英文书面及口头沟通能力，能够使用中文作为工作语言。
