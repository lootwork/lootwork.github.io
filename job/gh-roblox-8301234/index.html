WHY DATA SCIENCE & ANALYTICS?

The Data Science & Analytics organization's mission is to increase our speed, frequency, and acumen of making decisions at scale by instilling a data-influenced approach to building products. We cover a wide area of the data spectrum including analytical data engineering, product analytics, experimentation, causal inference, statistical modeling, and machine learning. Aligned and partnering with product verticals, we use this extensive toolbelt to discover new opportunities and unmet use cases, influence and shape the product roadmap and prioritization, build data products, and measure impact on our community of players and creators.

WHY CREATOR?

At Roblox, the Creator team is responsible for the systems that determine how creators generate and distribute games, license and share their IP, protect against clones, and thrive on the platform. As we move into a new era of AI tooling, game creation becomes much more accessible to a broad audience. We need to develop new tools and products to harness these new capabilities, and put new thought into protection against clones. As a Principal Data Scientist, you will be the primary strategic data partner across some of the most business-critical areas in the Creator space.

This is a high-ownership, high-autonomy role. You will work across economy, measurement, data infrastructure, and AI-enabled analytics — owning end-to-end problem framing, model development, and executive-facing recommendations in areas that directly shape platform integrity and creator success.

Вам предстоит

  • Drive Build Analytics: Develop and refine the suite of metrics used to track product success for Build — our new platform for generating games directly from user prompts. Define success metrics, guardrail metrics, and directly inform the product direction, from model spec decisions, to UI improvements, to pricing structure.
  • Own Clone Detection: Serve as the primary DS owner for clone detection modeling and consequencing — developing systems that distinguish between derivatives and full-on clones, and informing policies for how to intervene when clones are discovered. Innovate on which signals to use to identify clones in production.
  • Own IP Licensing Measurement: establish framework for tracking adoption and impact of IP licensing. Help define the strategy for broader IP sharing, through avatars and licensed assets.
  • Drive Creator Data Foundations: Serve as DRI for core creator data infrastructure — documenting all key creator tables, designing trustworthy data contracts, and building the semantic layers and analytical frameworks that enable reliable self-serve analysis across the team.
  • Enable Agentic & AI-Powered Analytics: Lead DS AI enablement for the creator org — building skills, evaluation frameworks, and scalable AI-enabled workflows that reduce analyst toil and elevate the team's analytical capacity.
  • Create Zero-to-One Frameworks: Define analytical standards and measurement approaches for new, ambiguous product areas — translating complex technical systems into interpretable, movable metrics for product and engineering leadership.

You Have

  • Seniority & Breadth: Principal DS experience, with a track record of owning complex, cross-functional data problems end-to-end — from problem framing and data infrastructure through modeling, experimentation, and executive communication.
  • Communication & Influence: Excellent written and verbal communication skills; able to translate technical findings into clear product and business recommendations for senior executives. Experience interacting directly with VPs and Senior VPs.
  • Comfort with Ambiguity: Experience operating independently in zero-to-one environments — defining the problem, building the data, and owning the answer without a playbook.
  • Technical Leadership: Ideally, someone who can raise the bar for adjacent DS workstreams through mentorship, code review, and cross-functional partnership.
  • ML & Generative AI Chops: Proven ability to build, evaluate, and maintain ML models, especially for game generation and asset similarity modeling. Experience with ecosystem or marketplace settings is a strong plus.
  • Experimentation & Causal Inference: Strong statistical rigor, experimentation design, and causal inference skills — ideally applied in complex, mediated settings where simple A/B tests don't tell the full story.
  • AI for Analytics: Deep understanding of agentic and self-serve analytics use cases, including how to turn DS workflows into scalable, AI-enabled products. Familiarity with evaluation frameworks for AI outputs.
  • Data Foundation Expertise: Ability to contribute to the data foundation layer for AI — including table documentation, semantic layer design, data contracts, and trustworthy infrastructure for AI-enabled analysis.
  • Technical Depth: Expert-level SQL and Python, with enough engineering fluency to work effectively across data pipelines, model outputs, and production-facing systems.
  • Academic Background: BS, MSc, or PhD in Computer Science, Statistics, Applied Math, or a related quantitative field.…
