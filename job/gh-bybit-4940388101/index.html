Role positioning

This is a strategic technical brand position under the CTO Office, with the core goal of helping the CTO establish external technical influence from scratch.

This person needs to possess strategic thinking, structured expression ability, and technical understanding, and be able to transform the core technical capabilities of the CTO, engineering team, and company into externally understandable, credible, and influential technical narratives.

This is not an ordinary brand operation, nor is it simply a position for writing articles. Must be able to truly understand technology - Understand what engineers are saying, judge the accuracy of technical content, and be able to directly communicate with CTO on technical issues. Do not require writing code, but cannot stay at the level of marketing copy.

Main responsibilities

  • Build a CTO technology brand system from 0 to 1, including positioning, content direction, communication channels, and release rhythm
  • Assist CTO in sorting out external technical narratives and forming a long-term, stable, and recognizable technical voice
  • Plan and produce technical articles, white papers, industry perspectives, speeches, interviews, media articles, and social media content
  • Track industry trends such as Web3, Crypto Exchange, AI, security, and system stability, and provide CTO with topic selection and viewpoint suggestions
  • Benchmarking technology brand actions against similar exchanges, outputting competitive insights, aligning with CTO with action list
  • Collaborate with engineering, product, risk control, security, PR, compliance and other teams to extract technical highlights and ensure content accuracy and compliance
  • Participate in necessary technical discussions, key project recaps and stability governance discussions, and precipitate real engineering creatives
  • Proactively break down problems, promote cross-team alignment in ambiguous environments, and implement them into actionable plans

Job requirements

Must have

  • Have experience as an MBB Associate or above in the past 5 years, or have experience in strategy, business strategy, business analysis, and strategic operation of major companies such as ByteDance/Tencent/Pinduoduo
  • Possess real technical understanding ability, be able to read what engineers are saying, judge the accuracy of technical content, and directly communicate with CTO on technical issues
  • Possess excellent structured thinking, problem-solving skills, business judgment, and Chinese and English expression skills
  • Experience in driving complex projects from 0 to 1, able to independently judge and promote implementation when the direction is not completely clear
  • Have ghostwriting experience, able to output content from the perspective of CTO or technical executives, rather than writing content centered on oneself
  • Samples of public content (technical blogs, white papers, speeches, industry reports or brand campaigns) are required
  • Proactive, passionate, strong ownership, able to quickly learn and fill in gaps in new directions
