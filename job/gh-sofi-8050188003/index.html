О роли

Our Finance team is expanding, and we are looking for passionate, dynamic individuals who are ready to grow their career. We are seeking a tech-forward Senior State and Local Tax Analyst to join our finance team. In this role, you will combine strong SALT technical knowledge with a modern, AI-enabled approach to tax, using AI tools and automation every day to work smarter and more accurately.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own day-to-day state & local tax compliance processes and coordinate with external service providers.
  • Ensure accurate and timely filing of all state and local returns including income/franchise, sales & use and other business-related filings.
  • Identify, build, and maintain AI-enabled automations and workflows (e.g., Alteryx, Power Automate, Python) that streamline compliance, provision, and reconciliation processes.
  • Review and validate AI-generated outputs for accuracy and completeness, in line with data-privacy and internal control requirements.
  • Partner with Finance Systems and IT to implement technology tools and process improvements across the tax function.
  • Support state tax controversy matters including examinations, audit defense, notice responses, and VDA projects.
  • Support restructuring projects with tax apportionment analysis.
  • Assist with tax incentive credit opportunities, planning and compliance.
  • Serve as a point of contact for internal inquiries, assist with coordinating audits, and prepare responses to notices from taxing jurisdictions.
  • Assist in identifying and developing state tax planning opportunities, as well as monitoring legislative and regulatory developments.
  • Manage state & local tax registrations.
  • Perform or assist with nexus studies and apportionment analysis.
  • Build subject matter expertise in state and local income tax matters as they relate to the business operations.
  • Assist with state tax provision and preparation of SALT related accruals, journal entries, and related account reconciliations.
  • Ensure adherence with SOX 404 documentation for tax accounting controls and related enhancements where necessary.
  • Work with cross functional teams to further understand how business processes affect state and local taxes..

What you’ll need

  • Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field.
  • MS in Tax or CPA preferred.
  • 3–5 years of related experience required.
  • Strong technical knowledge of state and local tax including income/franchise, sales & use and other business-related filings.
  • A combination of public accounting and corporate experience is great.
  • Possess an understanding of ASC 740 principles, as well as federal and state tax concepts.
  • Advanced Excel skills.
  • Curiosity about emerging technology and a track record of adopting new tools to improve efficiency.
  • Hands-on experience with data and analytics tools such as Alteryx, Tableau, Snowflake.
  • Demonstrated experience using generative AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Claude) for daily tasks such as research, drafting, data analysis, and calculations.
  • Familiarity with AI and workflow automation (e.g., Power Automate, UiPath, or AI agents), with the ability to spot automation opportunities and build or support solutions.
  • Experience with Python, SQL, or other scripting/low-code tools is a plus.
  • High attention to detail with ability to handle multiple projects and meet deadlines.
  • Strong analytical and organizational skills.
  • Ability to communicate sophisticated tax concepts in ways that are meaningful to non-tax professionals.
  • Financial and/or Tech Sector experience.
  • Experience with Workday is a plus.
  • Self-starter and highly collaborative with a strong work ethic.
