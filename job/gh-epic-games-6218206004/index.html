PLAYER MARKETING INTERN (MBA)

Что предстоит делать

We're looking for an MBA Player Marketing Intern to help run player-facing marketing campaigns across the Fortnite ecosystem, focused on third-party games and content. You'll run campaigns for developer-built experiences that drive awareness, engagement, and retention. You'll also measure the results and turn what you learn into best practices the team can reuse. You'll work directly with the Director of Player Marketing.

In this role, you will

  • Run marketing campaigns for new developer-built experiences as they launch in the Fortnite ecosystem
  • Work with developer and studio teams to best represent their games to players
  • Define KPIs for each campaign, track performance, and report results and learnings
  • Partner cross-functionally with shared service teams, including brand, creative, partnerships, and others, to launch campaigns
  • Document what worked and what didn't, and turn it into best practices for future launches

Кого мы ищем

  • Currently pursuing an MBA
  • Genuine passion for games and an understanding of player and creator communities
  • Experience working with external partners, licensors, or IP holders
  • Ability to manage multiple internal and external stakeholders
  • Experience building marketing campaigns, especially for player-facing or consumer-facing products
  • Comfort with analytics and reporting: pulling data, building clear reports, and explaining what the numbers mean
  • Strong written and verbal communication
