О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

As an Account Executive, Payments & Billing, you'll drive net-new revenue across SME, Mid-Market, and Enterprise segments in Asia by selling Airwallex's Payments and Billing solutions, including card acquiring, alternative payment methods, invoicing, and subscription management. You'll own the full sales cycle across your territory, from prospecting and qualification through solution design, complex multi-stakeholder negotiation, close, and handover to post-sales teams. You'll lead structured discovery into customers' billing, payments, and revenue workflows, then partner with Product, Solutions Engineering, and other internal experts to architect tailored solutions across Payments, Billing, Global Accounts, and FX. You'll present and demo Airwallex's solutions to senior stakeholders, build your industry network across Asia, and partner with Strategy, Revenue Operations, Product Marketing, Product, and other cross-functional teams to accelerate Payments and Billing adoption. You'll also maintain accurate pipeline, forecasting, and CRM hygiene to provide clear visibility of coverage, risks, and opportunities across your regional territory.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- 4–7 years of experience in an Account Executive, Business Development, or Product Sales role in fintech or B2B payments, with a proven track record of selling into SME, Mid-Market, or Enterprise segments.

- Deep understanding of card acquiring and regional alternative payment methods across Asia, including how different payment rails affect conversion, risk, and economics.

- Demonstrated success selling and closing billing or revenue products, such as subscription management, invoicing, usage-based billing, or similar SaaS billing platforms.

- Strong solution-selling skills, with the ability to understand complex technical and commercial requirements across billing, payments, and multi-product solutions and translate them into compelling commercial proposals.

- Demonstrated success selling in cross-functional commercial environments, collaborating with Sales, Product, Operations, Legal, and Partnerships teams.

- Proven ability to structure, negotiate, and close high-impact, complex deals in regulated or fast-scaling environments.

- Strong presentation and senior stakeholder management skills, with confidence engaging CFO, CTO, and Head of Payments personas.

- Strong verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to align technical and non-technical audiences and lead multi-stakeholder discussions.

- A high degree of autonomy and structure, with a track record of executing effectively in fast-changing, ambiguous regional contexts.

Preferred

- Experience selling into Finance, Revenue Operations, Product, or Engineering teams at SaaS, marketplace, fintech, or subscription-driven businesses.

- Hands-on familiarity with leading billing or revenue tools such as Stripe Billing, Chargebee, Zuora, or Recurly, or equivalent enterprise software platforms.

- Exposure to global payments, FX, or multi-currency financial products and how they intersect with regional billing and revenue workflows in Asia.

- Comfort discussing APIs, developer integrations, and data flows with technical stakeholders.

- Experience in a high-growth environment with a strong sense of ownership, resilience, rapid experimentation, and a bias to action.…
