Что предстоит делать

As an Account Executive, Payments & Billing, you will drive net-new revenue across SME, Mid-Market, and Enterprise segments in Asia. You will own the full sales cycle end-to-end, unlocking adoption for Airwallex’s payments, invoicing, and subscription platforms. Partnering closely with Product and Engineering, you will solve complex revenue workflows for clients and shape how our core billing infrastructure scales across the region. This role is based in Singapore or Hong Kong.

Обязанности

- Revenue Generation: Drive net-new adoption of Airwallex's Payments and Billing solutions—including card acquiring, alternative payment methods, invoicing, and subscription management—across SME, Mid-Market, and Enterprise prospects across Asia.

- Full Sales Cycle Ownership: Own the complete sales cycle across your territory, from prospecting and qualification through to solution design, complex multi-stakeholder negotiation, close, and handover to post-sales teams, while consistently delivering against net-new customer acquisition and revenue targets.

- Solution Architecture: Lead structured discovery to understand customers' billing, payments, and revenue workflows, then collaborate with Product, Solutions Engineering, and other internal experts to architect tailored solutions that combine Airwallex's Payments, Billing, and broader financial capabilities (including card acquiring, local payment methods, Global Accounts, and FX).

- Executive Presentations: Lead high‑quality presentations and demos tailored to multiple stakeholders—including C-level decision-makers (CFO, CTO, CEO, and Head of Payments)—clearly articulating the commercial and operational value of Airwallex Billing & Payments.

- Network & Business Development: Leverage your industry network across Asia to open doors, secure new high-value customers, and create references, including case studies, early adopter testimonials, and joint success stories.

- Cross-Functional Go-To-Market: Partner closely with Strategy, Revenue Operations, and Product Marketing to build targeted campaigns, market segmentation, and localized sales plays that accelerate payments and billing adoption in the Asian market.

- Pipeline Management: Maintain accurate pipeline, forecasting, and CRM hygiene to give clear visibility of coverage, risks, and opportunities across your regional territory.

Кто вы

We're looking for people who meet the minimum requirements for this role. The preferred qualifications are great to have, but are not mandatory.

Minimum Qualification

- 4-7 years of experience in an Account Executive, Business Development, or Product Sales role in fintech or B2B payments, with a proven track record of selling into SME, Mid-Market, or Enterprise segments.

- Deep Payments Domain Expertise: Thorough understanding of card acquiring and regional alternative payment methods across Asia, including how different payment rails impact conversion, risk, and economics.

- Billing / SaaS Closing Experience: Demonstrated closing experience selling a Billing or Revenue product (e.g., subscription management, invoicing, usage‑based billing, or similar SaaS billing platforms) with clear ownership of end‑to‑end sales cycles.

- Solution Selling Ability: Ability to understand complex technical and commercial requirements across billing, payments, and multi‑product solutions, translating them into compelling commercial proposals.

- Cross-Functional Execution: Demonstrated success selling in cross‑functional commercial environments, collaborating with sales, product, operations, legal, and partnership teams.

- Deal Structuring: Proven ability to structure, negotiate, and close high‑impact, complex deals in regulated or fast-scaling environments.

- Executive Presence: Strong presentation and senior stakeholder management skills, comfortable engaging CFO, CTO, and Head of Payments personas.

- Autonomy & Agility: High degree of autonomy and structure, with a track record of executing effectively in fast‑changing, ambiguous regional contexts.…
