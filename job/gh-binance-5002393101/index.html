We're looking for a Data Analyst Intern to join the Risk/Data team. The intern will support data pipeline development, SQL-based analytics, and data standardization initiatives under the guidance of senior team members. This is a great opportunity to gain hands-on experience with large-scale data processing in a real business environment.

Обязанности

  • Assist in developing and maintaining SQL-based data pipelines and analytics workflows
  • Support data standardization and normalization projects (e.g., address parsing, entity resolution)
  • Help build and optimize data models, dashboards, and reports
  • Conduct ad-hoc data analysis under mentorship and present findings to the team
  • Assist in monitoring data quality and implementing validation logic
  • Collaborate with team members on cross-functional data projects

Требования

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or related field
  • Solid SQL skills, familiarity with Hive/Spark SQL is a plus
  • Basic proficiency in at least one scripting language (Python preferred)
  • Strong interest in data analytics, risk control, or fraud detection
  • Good communication skills, eagerness to learn and take initiative
  • Available for at least 3 days per week, minimum 3-month internship duration
