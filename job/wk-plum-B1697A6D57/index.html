О роли

This is a high-impact, fast-paced opportunity to join Plum’s investment operations team as a self-starter. The Trading and Settlement Specialist will drive operational agility by real-time monitoring of automated client order flows, swiftly resolving trading alerts and exceptions. You will leverage your deep subject matter expertise to challenge the status quo, strengthen end-to-end controls, and spearhead process automation across the entire order lifecycle.

Обязанности

  • Proactively monitor live trade flows end-to-end throughout the day, taking immediate ownership to troubleshoot and resolve order exceptions in real time.
  • Own the daily transaction reporting process, ensuring complete data accuracy, resolving discrepancies agilely, and continuously refining reporting controls.
  • Lead daily stock and cash reconciliations between Plum and external partners, rapidly investigating and eliminating root-cause breakages.
  • Partner directly with payment & FX providers to execute flawless settlement operations within tight deadlines in a dynamic multi-currency setup.
  • Identify operational bottlenecks and champion automation initiatives to continuously eliminate manual overhead and streamline workflows.
  • Investigate automated trade surveillance alerts, collaborating closely with compliance and platform partners to build next-generation monitoring frameworks.
  • Drive cross-functional project execution with Product and Engineering teams to launch innovative investment features and enhance customer experience.
  • Perform continuous execution quality monitoring against Best Execution principles, optimizing venue selection and order routing standards.

Ideally You

  • Have experience in a similar trading & settlement role at a fintech or other wealth manager. Have a good understanding of other investment operations areas
  • Experience of UK, EU & US stocks, mutual funds & ETFs order execution & settlement
  • Demonstrates a proactive approach to problem-solving and has the ability to efficiently collaborate with cross-functional teams
  • Are comfortable with analysing data to solve problems & produce useful MI.
  • Like to take responsibility and use your experience to support other team members
  • Confident working with other teams and partners to ensure issues are resolved promptly
  • Understanding of Best execution, Consumer Duty, MIFID and market abuse regulation
