О нас

Gismart is a value-driven mobile app developer with a strong presence in the Health & Wellness, Utilities, and Music app markets. We have recently achieved a significant milestone of over 1 billion downloads worldwide, taking a step toward our mission of cultivating the well-being of people worldwide. With headquarters in London, UK, Gismart is a dynamic global company with a reach extending across Europe and far beyond. Our team consists of 400+ professionals who bring diversity, creativity, innovation, and relentless drive to the company. Gismart unites professionals from diverse backgrounds in entertainment, music, and tech, allowing us to play to each other’s strengths and succeed as a team.

Обязанности

- Own features end to end in our in-house web funnel builder and the live funnels, from spec to release.

- Build new funnels and migrate existing ones from the old builder.

- Develop the checkout (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal) and marketing analytics events.

- Take over and develop the Go backend with the team lead: APIs, data model, PostgreSQL.

- Integrate payment, analytics, ad, A/B testing and translation services.

- Keep mobile funnels fast and reliable using Sentry and Prometheus.

- Write short specs and documentation; review AI-generated code before merging.

Требования

- 5+ years of commercial software engineering.

- React, Next.js (App Router, Server Components) and strict TypeScript in production.

- Mobile web performance: server vs client rendering, bundle size, Core Web Vitals.

- 2+ years of production backend on Node.js or Go; API design, webhooks, idempotency.

- PostgreSQL: schema design, migrations, query performance.

- AI coding agents as your main way of working, with your own setup (rules, skills, MCP).

- Ownership from spec to production, including debugging in Sentry.

Будет плюсом

- Production experience with Go.

- Payments and subscriptions: Primer, Stripe, Apple Pay / Google Pay, 3DS.

- Web funnels / web2app: quiz flows, paywalls, A/B tests.

- Marketing analytics: Meta Pixel and CAPI, GTM, Amplitude.

- Multi-tenant or internal platforms used by several teams.

- Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, CI/CD.

- Remote-First Culture: Our team is as diverse and international as our product portfolio. We offer a flexible schedule and the opportunity to collaborate remotely from anywhere in the world or from one of our corporate hubs.

- Coworking Compensation: We provide 100% compensation for coworking spaces in Ukraine (except Kyiv, where we have a cozy and autonomous hub) and 50% compensation for coworking spaces worldwide, ensuring a comfortable and inspiring workspace.

- Public Holidays Policy: We provide 12 public holidays per year, accrued at a rate of one day per month.

- Sick Leave Support: We provide 100% compensation for sick leave.

- Equipment Support: We provide the equipment needed for your projects or cover the maintenance costs of your own equipment.
