We want you to #JOINTHEREBELLION!

For 30 years we’ve been independently developing and publishing incredible video games at our multiple studios founded by Jason & Chris Kingsley, but Rebellion is more than just games. We have our own film studio, we create board games, publish books, and through 2000AD, publish comics and graphic novels such as the amazing Judge Dredd.

We're looking for an experienced and passionate Level Designer to bring their creativity and expertise to an exciting, unannounced project! This is a fantastic opportunity to join a studio renowned for creating acclaimed titles such as the Sniper Elite series and BAFTA-winning Atomfall.

Your role is to create game levels and implement gameplay based on the creative vision. You will contribute to the game’s vision by building levels and work with others to achieve a consistent flow throughout all levels built for the game.

This exciting opportunity can be based out of either of our studios in Oxford or Warwick offering a creative and relaxed atmosphere where teamwork drives our success. We can provide a relocation package, including moving assistance, temporary accommodation, and full support to ensure a smooth transition to your new environment.

Обязанности

  • Leverage visual scripting tools to create engaging objectives and combat scenarios.
  • Create immersive gameplay scenarios that support and enhance the core mechanics and creative vision of the game.
  • Develop and iterate level designs through close collaboration with Artists, Narrative, and other disciplines.
  • Collaborate with Game Designers to define and tweak game mechanics.
  • Understand the iterating brief over the lifetime of the project and apply style guides and consistent gameplay languages across the game.
  • Integrate the content produced by other disciplines for your designs into the game engine (new gameplay elements, AI, sound, etc.)
  • Conduct gameplay evaluations with the team providing feedback and suggestions to help fine tune and improve the game.
  • Manage the levels through the full cycle of production from conception to finalisation.
  • Maintain and balance the readability, difficulty, and accessibility of the levels.
  • Communicate and collaborate with key stakeholders to confirm task direction and consistency of own work.
  • Assist with optimisation and debugging to fix the issues raised by testers.

Требования

  • Knowledge of creating game design documents and prototype specifications.
  • Willingness and ability to deliver high quality results using tools and processes required to get the job done.
  • Knowledge of the video game development process.
  • Knowledge of production pipelines.
  • Professional experience working on at least one shipped game within the third-person action/shooter genre.
