О TIDE

At Tide, we help SMEs save time and money in the running of their businesses by not only offering business accounts and related banking services, but also a comprehensive set of highly usable and connected administrative solutions, from invoicing to accounting.

Tide is transforming the small business banking market and now supports over 2 million members globally across the UK, Germany and France.

Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love.

Tide facts

  • Tide is available for UK, German and French SMEs
  • Over 2 million members globally and rapidly growing
  • Over $300 million raised in funding
  • 3,000 Tideans globally
  • We have offices in Central London, with a member support and technology centre in Sofia, Bulgaria, technology centres in Serbia, Romania, Lithuania and Hyderabad and offices in Gurugram, New Delhi, Berlin, Paris and Luxembourg

О команде

Tide is hiring a Director of Engineering to lead the engineering organisation behind Commercial Services. You will report to the CDTO and partner with Product and Design leadership as one leadership team accountable for the outcomes of the domain.

Commercial Services is where Tide extends from helping members run their business to helping them grow it. You will shape how quickly every member becomes discoverable, how much of their buying and selling runs through Tide, and how far AI can turn that into a lasting advantage across every market we serve.

Sell gives every member a live, discoverable presence, from the Tide Business Pages we are launching for 2 million members across the UK and Europe, to Own Website, catalogue, bookings, CRM and lead capture, and embedded fulfilment through Delivery as a Service.

Buy turns that member base into a two-sided marketplace through the Tide Online Network, alongside Tide Promotions (gift cards, cashback and personalised offers), business supplies from partners, and Vendor Hub for e-procurement.

You will be the single owner accountable for engineering delivery, architecture and people across both.

О роли

This role sits at the intersection of SME commerce, marketplaces and banking. Our members sit on both sides of the marketplace, our partners extend what we can offer them, and our banking platform lets us layer payments, credit, insurance and rewards into the experience at the point of need, which few commerce players can do. We need someone who can bring both streams onto one consistent engineering bar, ship quickly and build the platforms that turn Commercial Services into a material recurring revenue line for Tide.

As Director of Engineering you will

  • Set the engineering strategy and technical direction across the Sell and Buy value streams, from launch through to scale.
  • Lead the Heads of Engineering for Sell and Buy, build the leadership bench underneath them, and own headcount planning, hiring and organisation design.
  • Partner with Product and Design leadership on one shared plan, jointly owning delivery and outcomes, including members activated through real sales and purchases, engagement and recurring revenue.
  • Scale the Tide Business Pages and Tide Online Network into a trusted, high-traffic marketplace, including search, listings, member-to-member leads, chat and buyer requests.
  • Build the partner integration platform behind our website, gift card, business supply and logistics partners, enabling new partners and offers to be onboarded quickly and safely.
  • Work with Payments, Acquiring, Credit, Insurance and Wealth to embed payments, credit, insurance and rewards into commerce journeys at the point of need.
  • Build trust into the marketplace in partnership with Ongoing Monitoring and Risk, from verified seller status to initial and ongoing monitoring of listings and member-to-member communication.
  • Set a consistent engineering practice and quality bar across architecture, delivery health measured through DORA metrics, reliability and security.
  • Lead our move towards AI-assisted and agentic commerce, where a member describes what they need and Tide finds, compares and sources it.
  • Apply our OnePlatform principles so we build once, deploy globally, and keep market-specific logic isolated.

Кого мы ищем

  • Director engineering leadership of a multi-team organisation of around 50 engineers across backend, frontend, data and analytics, led through at least one layer of engineering leaders.
  • Domain experience in e-commerce, two-sided marketplaces, CRM or procurement platforms, including the trust and safety controls they need, with fintech or SME banking experience a plus.
  • Strong track record of turning platform investment into measurable product and commercial outcomes.
  • Experience building partner and third-party integration platforms at scale.
  • Strong product and design partnership instincts.
  • Comfortable setting direction through ambiguity and launching new products into multiple markets.
  • Strong judgement across organisation design, delivery, architecture and technical quality.
  • Experience bringing AI or agentic experiences into customer-facing commerce products would be a bonus.
