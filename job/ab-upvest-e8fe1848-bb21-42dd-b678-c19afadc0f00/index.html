О роли

We are seeking a Treasury and Payments Associate to join our motivated team of experts who value innovation, excellence, and shared growth in a results-driven environment. In this role, you will be responsible for ensuring the seamless flow of day-to-day operations by managing our treasury, payments, and reconciliation processes.

Что предстоит делать

- Timely processing and reconciliation of all inbound and outbound payments and treasury processes, ensuring accuracy and efficiency

- Managing customer payments and monitoring all related transactions to support smooth and reliable cash flows

- Preparing and distributing cash & securities reconciliation reports to management, providing timely and accurate insights into our cash and securities holdings

- Conducting cash forecasts and offering insights on liquidity management strategies to optimize cash flows

- Investigating and resolving any discrepancies between internal cash records and external statements

- Proper monitoring of the operations bank accounts ensuring that balances are supported at all times.

- Providing support for customer queries, audit processes, and ensuring smooth communication with banking partners

- Championing process improvements and automation projects, collaborating with product and engineering teams to enhance our treasury and payments infrastructure

- Ensuring full compliance with all applicable laws and regulations in the payments landscape.

Что вы приносите

- Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Finance, Economics, or a related field

- At least 2 years of relevant work experience with a focus on Treasury, Payments, Reconciliations or related fields

- Very good understanding of the global payments space - Sepa, Target, UK payment flows, Swift cross border transfers, among others.

- Strong investigative and analytical skills; ability to identify errors

- Fluent in English - both written and verbal. German is a strong advantage

- Exceptional ability to communicate and foster positive business relationships with both internal and external stakeholders

- Experience in data analysis and process automation using data visualization tools, SQL, Python, or similar is a plus
