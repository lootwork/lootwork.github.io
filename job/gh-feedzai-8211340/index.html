You

Reporting to the FP&A Director, the Corporate FP&A Cost Analyst will be responsible for both quantitative and qualitative analysis of cost dynamics and drivers of the company. FP&A Cost Analyst consider economic and business trends, review past company performance, and attempt to anticipate obstacles and potential problems, all with an eye toward forecasting a company's future financial results. The FP&A Cost Analyst is responsible for financial, analytical and budget support, and for providing financial and operational thought leadership and accountability throughout the organization.

Your Day to Day

  • Report and Analysis on Profitability by client / product line
  • Working with individual departments to prepare budgets and consolidate them into one overall corporate budget, independently testing and challenging assumptions against contracts, headcount plans and operational drivers.
  • Preparing internal reports for executive leadership and supporting their decision making
  • Creating, updating, and maintaining financial models and detailed forecasts of the company’s future operations
  • Comparing historical results against budgets and forecasts, and performing variance analysis that separates timing effects, one-offs and structural changes, identifies the underlying cost drivers and assesses their impact on the forward forecast.
  • Generating short and long term financial forecasts, including mapping out growth plans, capital expenditures and investments.
  • Partner with business leaders and key stakeholders across the different business teams on Company strategic projects
  • Prepare and present to company leadership team and key stakeholders across the different business teams monthly Performance management metrics (Budget, Reporting, Analysis, Business Insights)
  • Improve predictability and systematization of Feedzai’s forecasting models using Excel, Statistical/data methods, SQL, ERP, and other tools
  • Drive Performance and Growth of Feedzai’s operations, key financial trends, business drivers, KPIs and factors influencing the business performance
  • Proactively identify and drive new initiatives/projects to increase operational efficiencies and support rapid growth
  • Maintenance and improvement of backend IT databases, data normalization and standardization, including business structure changes
  • Collaborate with the Accounting, Payroll, Procurement and HR teams to ensure accurate and timely reporting, reconcile financial and operational data, and investigate and resolve unexplained differences.
  • Develop a detailed understanding of vendor and contract economics, including recurring versus non-recurring spend, unit costs, usage or volume drivers, renewals and committed spend.
  • Test explanations independently using transactions, contracts, headcount plans, usage data and other source information rather than relying solely on budget-owner commentary.

You Have & You Know-how

  • 3+ years’ experience with a minimum of 2 years in FP&A, preferably within FinTech/Payments and SaaS is a plus
  • Masters Degree in Finance, Accounting or Business related or Bachelors Degree
  • Strong proficiency in advanced Excel, GSuite (Google Sheets), Financial modeling, and Data analysis
  • Strong interpersonal skills with experience collaborating and working on a team
  • Strong analytical curiosity and critical thinking. Naturally questions numbers, investigates inconsistencies and is not satisfied with surface-level explanations.
  • Demonstrated ability to analyse complex costs and identify the underlying drivers of spend across areas such as headcount, vendors, cloud or infrastructure, professional services and discretionary expenditure.
  • Strong financial judgement. Able to distinguish timing differences from structural changes, recurring from non-recurring costs, and accounting movements from underlying economic drivers.
  • Strong written, verbal communication and presentation skills
  • Ability to prioritize and thrive in a fast-paced and growth-oriented environment
