О Payoneer

Founded in 2005, Payoneer is the global financial platform that removes friction from doing business across borders, with a mission to connect the world’s underserved businesses to a rising global economy. We’re a community with over 2,500 colleagues all over the world, working to serve customers, and partners in over 190 countries and territories.

By taking the complexity out of the financial workflows–including everything from global payments and compliance to multi-currency and workforce management, to providing working capital and business intelligence–we give businesses the tools they need to work efficiently worldwide and grow with confidence.

О роли

Join Payoneer’s Data Product Unit as a hands-on Senior Data Product Manager owning the data product portfolio for the Enterprise domain.

You’ll turn Enterprise client needs and internal business priorities into reliable, scalable data products for external data sharing and internal analytics. Working closely with Product, R&D, Data Engineering, BI, and business stakeholders, you’ll lead the full lifecycle—from discovery and prioritization through delivery, adoption, and ongoing improvement.

A key part of the role is translating individual client requirements into reusable capabilities while keeping Enterprise solutions aligned with Payoneer’s core data products.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the Enterprise data product strategy, roadmap, and backlog, prioritizing business value, client commitments, and delivery dependencies.
  • Lead discovery with Product, business teams, and client stakeholders to understand workflows, challenge requirements, and define the right solutions.
  • Translate business needs into clear product requirements, business logic, data specifications, and measurable acceptance criteria.
  • Define data models and consumption requirements with Data Engineering and BI, supporting both external data sharing and internal analytical use cases.
  • Use SQL and hands-on analysis to assess feasibility, investigate data, validate business logic, and guide product decisions.
  • Lead delivery across teams, managing dependencies and balancing client-specific needs with scalable, reusable solutions.
  • Define and monitor data quality, freshness, and service-level expectations, including contractual commitments where applicable.
  • Measure adoption and business impact, using feedback and usage data to improve the product portfolio.

What You’ll Bring

  • 5+ years in data product management or related hands-on data roles, with demonstrated ownership of complex data products from discovery through delivery and adoption.
  • Strong product judgment and business acumen, including the ability to prioritize competing needs and translate business problems into data solutions.
  • Strong SQL and analytical skills, with experience independently exploring data, investigating issues, and validating complex business logic.
  • Strong understanding of data modeling, warehouse architecture, and data pipelines, with hands-on BigQuery experience.
  • Proven ability to lead cross-functional initiatives and engage effectively in technical and architectural discussions.
  • Experience defining data quality requirements, acceptance criteria, and service-level expectations.
  • Excellent communication skills and strong English proficiency.

An Advantage

  • Experience with Enterprise clients and externally consumed data products, such as reports, data feeds, or APIs.
  • Background in fintech, payments, or financial services.
  • Experience balancing client-specific requirements with shared product capabilities.
  • Experience working across globally distributed teams.
