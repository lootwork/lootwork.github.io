Что нам нужно

HB Studios is at the forefront of the creation of sports video games and the studio behind PGA TOUR 2K Golf – presently working on Console and PC. HB Studios has an opportunity for a Senior 3D Artist to join their team of developers, creators, and fans, who are passionate about the art and process involved in video game development.

As a Senior 3D Artist, you will work with a team of professionals to create realistic 3D Art for a AAA gaming experience, breathing life into one of the fastest growing sports games series in the industry!

Что предстоит делать

  • Work closely with the Art Manager to visualize and develop asset production pipelines.
  • Drive the team in a cohesive direction ensuring all assets are built with the highest quality of standards.
  • Assemble architectural crowd stands and tournament tent structures in Autodesk Maya using a pre-built library of referenced modular components and grid-snapped units.
  • Adhere strictly to standardized material pipelines, including multi-layer blend shaders, alpha trim sheets, tiling surface textures.
  • Set up technical hierarchy requirements: organize instanced prop locators, character/crowd spawn locators, and collision meshes according to strict naming conventions.
  • Experience utilizing trim sheets, tiling textures, vertex color/blendmask painting, and layered materials.
  • Work across multiple disciplines including modeling, UV mapping, texturing, optimizing, as well as scene organization and implementation.
  • Balance performance issues while working within design and technical constraints to maintain a high level of quality.
  • Create 3D models and textures for environments, architecture, and supporting props.
  • Build high and low-poly models using innovative techniques and pipelines.
  • Mentor and facilitate learning for less experienced team members on standard methodologies as well as company tools, techniques, and pipelines.
  • Research industry developments and explore new tools and techniques to improve efficiency and content production.
  • Write and maintain documentation of workbooks on Confluence.
  • Participate and engage in team and departmental meetings.

Кто нам подойдёт

  • Typically, 4 to 6+ years of industry experience in 3D Art.
  • Strong knowledge of 3D content authoring techniques, including high-poly and game-resolution modeling, UVing, texturing, and optimization.
  • Proven experience with Maya, 3DMax, Photoshop, ZBrush, and Substance Painter/Designer.
  • Proven experience working with game engines (Unity/Unreal).
  • An excellent ability to interpret 2D reference material into 3D textured models.
