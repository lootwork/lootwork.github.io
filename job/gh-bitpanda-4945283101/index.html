Кто мы

We simplify wealth creation. Founded in 2014 in Vienna, Austria by Eric Demuth, Paul Klanschek and Christian Trummer, we’re here to help people trust themselves enough to build their financial freedom — for now and the future. Our user-friendly, trade-everything platform empowers both first-time investors and seasoned experts to invest in the cryptocurrencies, crypto indices, stocks*, precious metals and commodities* they want — with any sized budget, 24/7. Our global team works across different cultures and time zones, bringing our products to more than 7 million customers, making us one of Europe’s safest and most secure platforms that powers modern investing.

Headquartered in Austria but operating across Europe, our products are built by fast-moving, talented, “roll-up-your-sleeves-and-make-it-happen” kind of people. It’s these diverse perspectives and innovative minds operating as ONE TEAM that keep Bitpanda at the cutting edge of our industry. So if you’re someone who thinks big, moves fast and wants to make an impact right from day one, then get ready to join our industry-changing team. Let’s go!

Your Mission

As our Senior Atlassian Engineer, you won't just be managing Jira, you will drive architectural strategy at an enterprise scale for a high-growth fintech ecosystem. You will serve as a key technical partner to cross-functional teams, solving complex integration challenges, building advanced automations, and shaping our governance model to support fast-moving business needs. Plus, you will have direct impact by mentoring junior engineers and helping elevate our team's overall technical capability.

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead Architecture & Governance: Design, configure, and optimise our Atlassian Cloud products at an enterprise scale while establishing standardised configurations, schemes, and governance models to prevent system bloat.
  • Drive Advanced Solutions: Act as a subject matter expert to analyze complex business requirements from cross-functional stakeholders, translating them into scalable, robust technical solutions from needs analysis to delivery and documentation.
  • Empower Data-Driven Decisions: Design advanced Jira reports, high-level dashboards, and customized planning views for leadership and various business units.
  • Automate & Integrate: Build advanced automations and integrations with other enterprise tools using 3rd party apps and REST APIs.
  • Mentorship & Enablement: Conduct specialised training for employees, advocate for Atlassian best practices across the company, and mentor junior team members to elevate the team's overall technical capability.

Кто вы

  • A Proven Expert: You have mastered Atlassian Cloud systems (no on-prem or data center Atlassian systems in house) and understand how to architect them for scale.
  • Experienced: You bring 3+ years of hands-on experience administering, engineering, and scaling Atlassian cloud systems in a large, fast-paced, and dynamic enterprise or scale-up environment, on top of several years as a Business IT Engineer.
  • A Technical Problem Solver: You have hands-on experience with Assets (formerly Insight), advanced automation (native, JMWE, ScriptRunner) and utilising REST APIs. Experience with apps like Extension for JSM, EazyBI, and Xray is highly desired, as well as scripting in Groovy, JavaScript, or Python.
  • A Strategic Communicator: You excel at stakeholder management. You are comfortable consulting with internal users daily, pushing back on anti-patterns, and steering teams toward sustainable best practices.

  • Exclusive perks and rewards – Enjoy discounts, rewards, and perks from partners worldwide across lifestyle, wellness, tech, and travel.
  • Support during life milestones – Take advantage of up to 8 additional weeks of gender-neutral new parent leave to welcome and bond with your new addition to the family.
