О роли

We are looking for a creative, proactive, and hands-on Marketing Manager to strengthen Binance TH's brand presence and drive meaningful engagement in Thailand.

This role focuses on bringing the brand to life through impactful marketing campaigns, events, experiential activations, and strategic partnerships. You will play a key role in building brand awareness, strengthening local relevance, and creating meaningful connections with our target audiences.

The ideal candidate combines creativity with strong execution skills, understands the Thai consumer landscape, and can independently turn ideas into impactful marketing initiatives. You should be comfortable managing multiple projects, working with internal and external stakeholders, and thriving in a fast-paced environment.

Что предстоит делать

Brand Marketing & Campaigns

  • Develop and execute local brand marketing initiatives to strengthen Binance's awareness, positioning, and relevance in Thailand.
  • Translate regional and global brand strategies into locally relevant campaigns that resonate with Thai audiences.
  • Identify cultural trends, consumer insights, and market opportunities to develop creative and impactful brand initiatives.
  • Work closely with internal teams and external agencies to ensure consistent brand messaging and high-quality execution across touchpoints.

Events & Experiential Marketing

  • Plan and execute end-to-end brand events, community meetups, educational workshops, sponsorship activations, and experiential campaigns.
  • Own event execution from concept development and budgeting to vendor management, logistics, on-site coordination, and post-event evaluation.
  • Identify relevant events, platforms, and sponsorship opportunities that align with brand objectives and target audiences.
  • Develop creative activation concepts that drive audience engagement, brand visibility, and measurable marketing impact.
  • Ensure all events and activations meet brand standards, budget requirements, and relevant regulatory guidelines.

Partnerships & Collaborations

  • Identify and develop partnerships with relevant brands, communities, organizations, and industry stakeholders to expand Binance's local reach.
  • Explore creative co-marketing opportunities that strengthen brand positioning and introduce Binance to new audiences.
  • Manage partnership initiatives from initial discussions and planning through execution and performance evaluation.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with external partners, agencies, and vendors.

Community & Local Engagement

  • Support initiatives that strengthen relationships with local crypto communities, enthusiasts, and brand advocates.
  • Collaborate with community, content, and digital marketing teams to amplify campaigns and events across relevant channels.
  • Gather audience feedback and local insights to identify opportunities for stronger brand engagement.

Performance & Project Management

  • Manage marketing projects independently, ensuring timely delivery, budget discipline, and high execution standards.
  • Track and evaluate the effectiveness of brand campaigns, events, and partnerships against agreed KPIs.
  • Monitor competitor activities, market developments, and emerging consumer trends to inform future initiatives.
  • Collaborate with regional marketing and cross-functional teams to ensure alignment with broader business objectives.

Кого мы ищем

  • 7+ years of experience in brand marketing, event marketing, experiential marketing, or integrated marketing, preferably within technology, fintech, consumer brands, or agencies.
  • Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Communications, Business, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Proven experience planning and executing brand campaigns, events, and marketing activations from concept to completion.
  • Strong understanding of the Thai consumer landscape, cultural trends, and local marketing ecosystem.
  • Experience working with external agencies, event organizers, vendors, and business partners.
  • Strong creative thinking, with the ability to translate ideas into practical, high-impact marketing initiatives.
  • Excellent project management and organizational skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities, budgets, and stakeholders.
  • Bilingual English/Thai is required to be able to coordinate with overseas partners and stakeholders.
  • Proactive, resourceful, and detail-oriented, with a strong sense of ownership and a hands-on approach to execution.
  • Comfortable working in a dynamic, fast-paced environment.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience in cryptocurrency, blockchain, fintech, or other emerging technology sectors.
  • Existing network of local brands, event organizers, agencies, or industry partners.
  • Experience managing sponsorships, brand collaborations, or large-scale consumer activations.
  • Familiarity with digital marketing, social media, and content amplification.
  • Experience working with regional or global marketing teams.
