О роли

We're seeking a 2D animator to enhance gaming visuals. You can expect to immerse yourself in the amazing worlds of our games along with our animation team. Your animations will captivate the imaginations of millions of players all over the world.

  • Create engaging animations for result screens, offers, widgets, pop-ups, transitions, effects, and micro-interactions

  • Open-mindedness and hands-on experience with AI pipelines
