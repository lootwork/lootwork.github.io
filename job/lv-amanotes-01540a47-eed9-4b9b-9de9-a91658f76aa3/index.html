OBJECTIVES

  • Tiles Hop, Dancing Road, and Magic Tiles 3 is a top-ranked music games with a passionate global audience. As a Senior Product Owner – LiveOps, you will play a pivotal role in shaping and growing games that have already reached millions of players globally. This role is for someone who’s not only passionate about games, but also thrives in data-driven decision-making, rapid experimentation, and cross-functional collaboration. You’ll be empowered to define the product vision, own the roadmap, and drive performance across retention, monetization, and user satisfaction.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

Product Ownership:

  • Define and own the roadmap for key features or game areas, ensuring alignment with product goals.
  • Write detailed user stories and acceptance criteria, and manage the backlog to prioritize the highest-impact features.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure features are implemented on time and at a high quality.

Game Development & Strategy:

  • Work closely with the Product Manager to shape the overall game vision and strategy.
  • Contribute to designing innovative gameplay loops, hybrid monetization systems, and engagement mechanics.
  • Act as a mentor to Junior Product Owners and other team members, providing guidance and support.

Data-Driven Decision Making:

  • Use analytics and player feedback to inform decisions and optimize features for retention, engagement, and monetization.
  • Collaborate with the analytics team to design and evaluate A/B tests for new features and updates.
  • Identify key metrics and set success criteria for features and updates.

LiveOps & Player Engagement:

  • Help plan and execute LiveOps strategies, including events, seasonal updates, and new content releases.
  • Partner with marketing teams to align player acquisition and retention initiatives with product goals.

QUALIFICATIONS

  • 5+ years of experience in product ownership, product management, or a related role in the gaming industry.
  • Self-starter who is energetic, confident, adaptable, and player-focused
  • Experience supporting or owning live features for mobile games—rhythm or hybrid-casual titles a plus
  • Demonstrated use of data, experimentation, and player feedback to boost product engagement or monetisation
  • Ability to produce wireframes, mock-ups, prototypes, and user-interaction diagrams (Figma, Miro, etc.)
  • Comfortable querying and visualising data to answer product questions
  • Solid Agile/Scrum skills: you write crisp user stories and acceptance criteria and can guide a sprint from planning to release
  • Excellent written and spoken English; strong problem-solving and presentation abilities
  • Nice to have:
  • Familiarity with AI-powered tools to boost productivity and creativity
  • Passion for music and rhythm games—enough to spot a good beat map or suggest music-driven event ideas.
  • Understanding of F2P economies, core-loop and progression design, and live-ops best practices

OUR CULTURE:

  • We believe in rapid iteration, honest feedback, and celebrating every win. You’ll find autonomy, supportive peers, and the chance to impact millions of players with your work. If crafting joyful, music-powered experiences excites you, we’d love to meet. Ready to join the beat? Send us your CV and a short note about a data-driven product success you’re proud of.
