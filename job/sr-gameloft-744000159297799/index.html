Job Description

Job Description

  • Take part in the development of games using Unreal Engine
  • Program, debug and optimize codes to implement gameplay or features.
  • Actively detect issues on programming side, take initiative and propose solutions to prevent and solve those issues.
  • Subject to weekly supervision.
  • Share, take part in and coordinate work with their peers.
  • Documentation all technical skills and experience to enrich team’s knowledge.
  • Cooperate with people from all over the world to deliver high-quality works.

Требования

Требования

  • Knowledge of C++ language, Blue print & Unreal Engine.
  • Good at Algorithm, Mathematics and Physics.
  • Passion at Game Creation.
  • Good problem-solving and analytical skills.
  • Good team-work, a high sense of responsibility and being result-oriented.
  • Ability to communicate clearly and share solutions with fellow team members.
  • Experienced of 2D/3D game development using Unreal Engine is a plus.
  • Experienced with iOS/Android platforms is a plus.
