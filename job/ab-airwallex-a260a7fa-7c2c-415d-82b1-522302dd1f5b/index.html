О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

Own Airwallex’s regulatory engagement strategy and regulatory compliance programme for the Korea business, supporting our payment services and money remittance activities. You’ll maintain licensing responsibilities, coordinate licence applications and renewals, manage regulatory reporting and requests, and work directly with internal stakeholders and regulators.

You’ll advise on regulatory change, new and existing products, conduct risk, ethics, and the design of fit-for-purpose second-line controls, while helping the first line mature customer-facing, operational, and product processes.

You’ll also lead compliance monitoring, targeted and thematic reviews, employee training, and high-priority projects that remove regulatory or operational barriers to growth.

You’ll work from our Seoul office five days a week.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- A bachelor’s degree and at least 6 years of compliance experience in a regulated financial institution, fintech, legal or consulting firm, or a combination of these.

- Familiarity with financial regulatory laws, rules, regulations, and industry best practices in Korea, with broader understanding across the Asia region.

- Experience developing or maintaining a compliance programme within a financial institution, preferably covering payments activity.

- Exposure to licensing processes, regulatory expansion, and RegTech or technology-driven compliance solutions.

- Understanding of financial products and related conduct risk, ethics, and controls.

- Strong data proficiency, with the ability to use data to support strategic decision-making.

- Strategic thinking and creative problem-solving skills for complex regulatory compliance challenges.

- Strong communication skills, with the ability to explain complex topics clearly to regulators, executive leadership, and front-line teams.

Preferred

- Demonstrable experience dealing with the FSC and FSS in Korea and other financial services regulators across the Asia region.

YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF

- You’ve operated in a compliance-heavy environment and know how to move fast without cutting the corners that matter.

- You’re comfortable making decisions with incomplete information and moving quickly anyway.

- You connect dots across functions and hold the big picture without losing grip on the details.

- You communicate clearly and directly across cultures, and you're thoughtful about how context shifts meaning across borders.

- You know when to align and when to just decide. Collaboration isn't a way to avoid accountability.
