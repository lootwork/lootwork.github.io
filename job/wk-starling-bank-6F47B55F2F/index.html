О роли

We are seeking a highly motivated and experienced Mobile Security Engineer to join our Cyber Security team. As a Mobile Security Engineer, your primary responsibility will be to design, build, and maintain security for our Android and iOS mobile applications, ensuring our customers are protected while using our services. You will achieve this by collaborating with cross-functional engineering teams to implement robust security controls and enhancements.

Обязанности

  • Design, implement, and maintain security controls and mechanisms to secure our Android and iOS applications, and the data held on customer devices.
  • Partner with internal engineering teams to identify and remediate security vulnerabilities across mobile platforms.
  • Provide technical consultancy and guidance to engineering and operations functions to ensure that all technical aspects of our mobile applications are built and operated securely.
  • Stay at the forefront of the industry by monitoring emerging threats and security trends for both Android and iOS.
  • Create automated solutions to streamline security testing and vulnerability management within the mobile development lifecycle.
  • Maintain strict adherence to global security standards and regulatory requirements.

Требования

  • Strong Mobile Security technical knowledge, including:
  • Deep understanding of both Android and iOS security (e.g., native APIs, file permissions, application sandboxing, and hardware security controls).
  • Proficiency with relevant mobile development languages (e.g., Swift for iOS, Kotlin/Java for Android).
  • Practical and theoretical knowledge of public cloud (IaaS) technologies.
  • Strong reverse engineering capabilities for mobile applications.
  • Practical working knowledge of cryptography used for protecting data and network communications
  • Awareness of mobile threat landscape such as malware and remote access
  • Experience with developing security integrations by interacting with APIs.
  • Ability and willingness to learn new technologies and adapt to evolving security landscapes.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills to effectively collaborate with cross-functional teams and stakeholders.
  • Experience fulfilling a client-facing security consulting role is highly desirable.

Interview process

Interviewing is a two way process and we want you to have the time and opportunity to get to know us, as much as we are getting to know you! Our interviews are conversational and we want to get the best from you, so come with questions and be curious. In general you can expect the below, following a chat with one of our Talent Team:

  • First stage with a Mobile Engineer
  • Technical Test
  • Second stage with additional members Security Mobile Engineers
  • Final stage with Security Engineering Lead and Information Security Director
