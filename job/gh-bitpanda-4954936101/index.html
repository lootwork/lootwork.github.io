Кто мы

We simplify wealth creation. Founded in 2014 in Vienna, Austria by Eric Demuth, Paul Klanschek and Christian Trummer, we’re here to help people trust themselves enough to build their financial freedom — for now and the future. Our user-friendly, trade-everything platform empowers both first-time investors and seasoned experts to invest in the cryptocurrencies, crypto indices, stocks*, precious metals and commodities* they want — with any sized budget, 24/7. Our global team works across different cultures and time zones, bringing our products to more than 7 million customers, making us one of Europe’s safest and most secure platforms that powers modern investing.

Headquartered in Austria but operating across Europe, our products are built by fast-moving, talented, “roll-up-your-sleeves-and-make-it-happen” kind of people. It’s these diverse perspectives and innovative minds operating as ONE TEAM that keep Bitpanda at the cutting edge of our industry. So if you’re someone who thinks big, moves fast and wants to make an impact right from day one, then get ready to join our industry-changing team. Let’s go!

Your mission

As Product Manager for Securities & Commodities, you will own key feature execution and user flows for selected traditional financial products. You will collaborate closely with Engineering, Design, and Business Operations to turn complex financial workflows into simple, transparent retail trading experiences. This is a high-impact opportunity for an execution-focused PM eager to build leading multi-asset investing experiences across Europe.

Что предстоит делать

  • Take direct ownership of specific product flows within securities and commodities, such as equity trading interfaces, margin trading touchpoints, or RWA execution steps.
  • Write clear, detailed product specifications, process flows, and user stories, serving as the primary day-to-day product contact for Engineering.
  • Partner with UX, Design, and Data teams to turn complex financial concepts into transparent, high-converting retail experiences.
  • Maintain fast shipping velocity and high task completion by actively collaborating with Engineering, Business Operations, and cross-functional partners.
  • Track feature adoption and performance metrics to identify UX friction points and drive continuous optimizations.

Кто вы

  • 2–4 years of Product Management experience building trading, retail brokerage, or fintech products (ideally within a MiFID II or regulated environment).
  • Good functional understanding of capital markets, order execution flows, clearing, and basic retail investing mechanics.
  • Solid data-driven, analytical mindset with practical experience using product analytics to guide feature iterations.
  • Strong structured communication skills to translate business and technical requirements into actionable product specs.
  • Familiarity with multi-asset investing platforms.
