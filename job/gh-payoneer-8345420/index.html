О Payoneer

Founded in 2005, Payoneer is the global financial platform that removes friction from doing business across borders, with a mission to connect the world’s underserved businesses to a rising global economy. We’re a community with over 2,500 colleagues all over the world, working to serve customers, and partners in over 190 countries and territories.

By taking the complexity out of the financial workflows–including everything from global payments and compliance to multi-currency and workforce management, to providing working capital and business intelligence–we give businesses the tools they need to work efficiently worldwide and grow with confidence.

Full Time | Hybrid | Hong Kong

Что предстоит делать

  • Collaborate with the regional team to manage the company’s daily cash flow, ensuring timely and adequate liquidity across APAC for the money-movement platform
  • Maintain strong day-to-day relationships with regional partnering banks and providers to ensure seamless service delivery and operational efficiency
  • Assist with the preparation of regulatory reporting and controls for various licenses and ensure operational activities are compliant with local regulations and internal controls
  • Maintain data accuracy and completeness within the Treasury Management System while supporting ongoing automation initiatives to enhance its overall functionality
  • Proactively support and resolve treasury-related queries from internal stakeholders across APAC
  • Coordinate and perform ongoing administrative duties relating to our Bank Account Management function, supporting the growth and stability of the bank network
  • Participate in the execution of other general team-related tasks, and projects

Кто вы

  • Bachelor’s degree in accounting/ finance or business administration
  • At least 3 years of relevant treasury/ finance experience
  • Proficient in spoken and written English and Chinese, with the ability to work effectively with regional and global stakeholders
  • Proficient in Excel and Power Query

Not a must but a great advantage

  • Working knowledge of FX and cash management
  • Familiarity with AI-enabled tools to enhance productivity, or process automation
  • Experience working with Treasury Management Systems (i.e. Kyriba), Microsoft Power Platform, and SQL
