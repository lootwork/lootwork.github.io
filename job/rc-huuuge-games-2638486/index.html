At Huuuge Games, we make top-grossing mobile games that bring people together through fun and social mobile gaming.

As a Senior Data Analyst, you'll join a Marketing team where you’ll be at the center of how we acquire players, invest our marketing budget, and understand what actually drives growth - turning data into decisions that reach millions of players worldwide.

This is a role with real autonomy, real stakes, and genuinely interesting problems. If you're excited by work that directly shapes strategy, a team that values initiative over instruction, and the chance to drive impact at scale - this is where you want to be.

We are an in-office-first company and operate in a hybrid model (3 days from the office).

Как вы повлияете на результат

As a Senior Data Analyst working in the Marketing team at Huuuge, you'll be a trusted partner to Marketing stakeholders across UA, ASO, Creative, helping turn data into decisions and identifying opportunities before they become requests.

This is a high-ownership role where your work directly shapes marketing strategy, budget allocation, and business performance.

  • Build and own analyses that answer the big Marketing questions: Is our spend efficient? Where should we double down? What's driving a drop in player acquisition?
  • Design and maintain dashboards that give Marketing stakeholders a clear, always-on view of performance - and flag anomalies before anyone asks.
  • Proactively identify opportunities and problems in data, propose solutions, and drive initiatives end-to-end - from a vague business question all the way to a stakeholder presentation
  • Collaborate daily with UA, ASO, Creatives, Data Science, and Product - being the analytical backbone that connects them.
  • Translate complex findings into clear, actionable recommendations that non-data audiences can quickly understand and confidently act upon.
  • Contribute to automation that reduces recurring manual work and frees the team for higher-value analysis.
