Scopely's Tokyo office is home to teams across Scopely Explore and Scopely Japan, working in Japanese and English every day. We are looking for a Project/Program Manager to own Workplace Experience in Tokyo: the office itself, the vendors who keep it running, how new hires land, how the team comes together, and a major office relocation over the next two years.

The big goal is a collaborative office culture that feels like Scopely Explore: a place where people across teams know each other, help each other and enjoy working together. Events, programs and shared meals are how we get there.

This role works behind the scenes so that everyone else can do their best work. When the office runs smoothly, a new hire feels welcome on day one, and a team celebration is something people talk about for weeks, that is your work. We are looking for someone who genuinely loves this kind of work: who gets energy from making other people's day better, finds creative ways to delight the team, and feels rewarded when people say thank you.

You will join our Operations team, reporting to the Head of Corporate Operations in Tokyo, and partner closely with Scopely's global Real Estate & Workplace Services (REWS), People, IT, Finance and Legal teams.

We expect you to use AI agents as part of your daily work. Let AI take the first pass on the paperwork and repeatable tasks, keep the decisions yourself, and turn each correction into instructions that make the next cycle faster, so that more of your time goes to the people you serve.

Что предстоит делать

  • Workplace support for everyone — Be the go-to person for anything workplace or general affairs (総務) related. Answer questions and requests quickly and kindly, solve problems before they grow, and make it easy for everyone to focus on their work.
  • Office and facilities — Run the day-to-day Tokyo office: building management, maintenance, security and access, health and safety. Manage vendors and on-site workers (cleaning, reception, maintenance, catering), including scope, quality and renewals.
  • Purchasing and spend — Order office supplies, food and drinks. Track spend against budget and handle POs and expenses in Oracle and our expense tools.
  • Office relocation — Act as the project manager for the Tokyo office move: work with the landlord, designers and contractors on interior design and construction (including the split between landlord-side and tenant-side works), build and track the schedule and budget, and keep stakeholders in Tokyo and at REWS informed in both languages.
  • Onboarding — Own the workplace side of onboarding in Tokyo (seating, access, equipment handoff with IT, welcome guides in Japanese and English), and keep improving it based on new hires' feedback.
  • Office culture, events and programs — Build a collaborative, Scopely Explore-style office culture. Plan and run events and programs as the means to that end, from team gatherings and cross-team programs to company-wide celebrations: venues, budget and bilingual communication. Success is how the office feels, not how many events we run.
  • Communal dining — Make shared meals a center of office life: catering, team lunches, snacks and drinks, and the space and rituals around eating together. Eating together is one of the simplest ways for people across teams to get to know and trust each other.
  • Contracts intake — Prepare and route NDAs, purchase requests and POs through Ironclad and Oracle, check them against our templates, and escalate to Legal and Finance when needed.
  • AI-first operations — Build and maintain AI-assisted workflows for this work, such as vendor quote comparison, PO and NDA intake checks, bilingual announcements and guides, meeting notes and issue logs for the relocation, and a Tokyo office FAQ. Keep the instructions and rules behind these workflows up to date so that others on the team can use them too.

Кто вы

  • You love taking care of people. Making the office a place people enjoy coming to is the kind of work you want to do, not just a job you put up with.
  • You bring energy and a can-do attitude. You say "let's figure it out" before "that's not my job", and you stay positive when requests pile up.
  • You are creative about delighting people: a better welcome for new hires, an event idea nobody expected, a small touch that makes the office feel like ours.
  • You get things done, including the paperwork. Contracts, POs and expenses are done right and on time, because that is what makes the fun parts possible.
  • When something keeps going wrong or a request keeps coming back, you turn it into a fix, an FAQ or a better process, and you speak up early rather than letting frustration build.

What you will need

  • 3+ years of experience in workplace, facilities, office management, general affairs (総務) or operations project management.
  • Experience managing vendors and contractors, and handling purchasing, budgets and expenses.
  • Business-level Japanese and English, written and spoken. You will write for both Japanese and global audiences every week.
  • Strong organization: you keep many moving parts on track, follow through, and communicate clearly about status and decisions.
  • Hands-on use of generative AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) in your work today, and the habit of making your workflows reusable. You do not need to code.
  • Good judgment on what to automate and what to keep in human hands: money, contracts, people-sensitive communication.

Plus if

  • Familiarity with Ironclad, Oracle, Workday or similar systems.
  • Background in hospitality, events or employee experience.
  • Experience at a game, entertainment or technology company, or in a global company's Japan office.
