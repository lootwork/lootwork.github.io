Associate Product Manager - Payments

Product

Ebury London Office - Hybrid: 4 days in the office, 1 day working from home per week

Do you want to solve the hardest problem when it comes to moving money globally supporting SMEs thriving with their international businesses? Do you want to become an expert of money flows, payments and accounts? Do you want to be part of an ambitious team that wants to democratize access to currencies and corridors to all Businesses and Entrepreneurs?

If you are result-oriented, the brightest of the brightest, a problem-solver hungry for learning more, then Ebury is the place for you!

The Product Graduate role is for high-potential individuals beginning their career in Product Management. This position blends foundational learning with product feature ownership, preparing you for rapid growth into an Associate Product Manager role. You will work closely with cross-functional teams to contribute to the product vision, execute on the roadmap, and ensure the successful delivery of our products that help SME move money around the globe to propel their growth.

Что предстоит делать

Product Discovery & Execution

  • Assist in the delivery of small features, helping to crack the big problems of moving money like a local for those who trade internationally
  • Own the end-to-end delivery of small to medium features impacting the payments and collections worlds, from ideation to launch and iteration paring up with more senior team members
  • Define and monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) for owned features and report on how the product performs
  • Support the team by writing clear user stories and resolving queries around those
  • Participate in user research, demonstrated a foundational understanding of our end users, SMEs and Entrepreneur, and market landscape
  • Gather feedback from our users and derive insights
  • Understand the product's basic architecture and technology stack
  • Communicate clearly within the team, providing updates and seeking clarity
  • Proactively ask questions and seek guidance from senior team members, learning from them

Product Strategy & Vision

  • Understand the money flows team's product vision and how your work contributes to it
  • Translate product strategy into a clear, prioritized roadmap for a small area or feature set
  • Effectively present feature plans and results to cross-functional teams

Must have

  • Academic background in Computer Science, Engineering, or a highly quantitative field from a leading institution (e.g., UC3M, ICAI, UMA, Imperial, UCL, or equivalent top-tier European universities).
  • You have an interest in payments and how money moves around the globe
  • You are curious and you want to make a difference

О нас

Ebury delivers sophisticated, integrated solutions — business accounts, hedging, and financing — on a single platform with a seamless workflow. Our success is built on a simple premise and singular purpose: To help businesses operate and scale globally.

Since its founding in 2009, Ebury has always been a fast-growing leader in fintech. Today, we bring together 2000+ Eburians across nearly 70 cities and we’re always looking to add to our team.

At the heart of our offering is a proprietary platform, purpose-built to help businesses seamlessly streamline and manage global cash flow. We focus on continuous product evolution and innovation to build the infrastructure for borderless growth and help our clients scale at every stage.

The opportunities at Ebury are as diverse as our people, ranging from business development to engineering roles across our tech pillars.

We believe in inclusion. We stand against discrimination in all forms and are against the intolerance of differences that makes us a modern and successful organisation. At Ebury, you can be whoever you want to be and still feel a sense of belonging no matter your story.
