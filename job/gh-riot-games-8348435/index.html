Manager, Software Engineering - League of Legends

As an Engineering Manager on the League of Legends’ Purchase Points theme, you will lead and grow engineers building player-facing experiences in the game client and engine environment.

You will be accountable for the team's people, technical direction, delivery, and operational health, including participation in incident response and root-cause analysis. Working with Engineering, Product, Design, Art, UX, QA, and Production, you will create clarity in ambiguous problem spaces and help the team deliver high-quality experiences that are cohesive with the League ecosystem.

Обязанности

  • Lead, grow, and support a high-performing team of software engineers.
  • Stay technically engaged by reviewing designs, guiding decisions, and helping the team navigate complex client, engine, UI, content, tooling, and service problems.
  • Partner across disciplines to deliver player value while making tradeoffs across scope, schedule, quality, maintainability, and operational risk.
  • Establish strong engineering and operational practices, including code review, testing, documentation, debugging, incident response, root-cause analysis, and adherence to broader organizational technology standards.
  • Identify technical risks, dependencies, staffing needs, and capability gaps early, and work with partners to address them.
  • Hire, onboard, coach, and retain engineers; set clear expectations, give regular feedback, support growth, and foster an inclusive team environment.

Required Qualifications

  • 6+ years of professional software engineering experience, or equivalent experience building and shipping software.
  • 2+ years directly leading an engineering team as an Engineering Manager, Technical Lead, or comparable engineering leader.
  • Experience building and shipping player-facing software, game-client features, interactive experiences, developer tools, or other complex user-facing products.
  • Experience working in a large and complex codebase and guiding technical decisions across architecture, quality, performance, and maintainability.
  • Experience collaborating across engineering and non-engineering disciplines to deliver products or features.
  • Demonstrated experience mentoring engineers, supporting career growth, and creating healthy team practices.
  • Strong communication skills, including the ability to explain complex technical topics clearly to audiences with different levels of context.

Desired Qualifications

  • Experience developing game-client, gameplay-adjacent, UI, player-facing, or interactive systems.
  • Experience with C++, Lua, game scripting, content pipelines, or comparable technologies.
  • Experience working with a game engine, game editor, content tools, or other systems where engineering and content development intersect.
  • Experience shipping and operating a live game or other globally distributed consumer product.
  • Experience partnering closely with designers, artists, technical artists, producers, and QA.
  • Experience improving developer workflows, build and integration processes, or tools used by multidisciplinary development teams.
  • Experience with distributed services supporting player-facing experiences, or familiarity with League of Legends and its player experience.

Success in this role

  • The team has a clear engineering direction, healthy engineering practices, and a strong connection between its work and the broader goals for the League Experience.
  • Engineers receive clear feedback, grow in their careers, and operate with an appropriate level of autonomy.
  • The team delivers reliable, high-quality player-facing experiences while making deliberate tradeoffs around scope, time, quality, and sustainability.
  • Technical risks, dependencies, and operational concerns are surfaced early and managed before they become delivery problems.

For this role, you'll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players' experiences, this could be your role!

Whether you’re working directly on a new player-facing experience or you’re supporting the company as a whole, everyone at Riot is part of our mission.
