About the Role: Own and execute the Country Risk dimension of the Group ERMF — the third face of the 3 Dimension risk model (Product × Function × Country). The role is accountable for country-level risk visibility across all licensed jurisdictions: defining regional risk reporting, developing country-specific financial and fraud risk indicators, reducing ERM implementation gaps in the regional dimension , and acting as the central risk team's primary interface with regional GMs, Risk Officers and Regional MLROs.

The role closes the gap between a centrally designed framework and locally varying business models, so risk is detected at country level early enough to act on.

Scope

  • Geographic: All licensed and licensed-pending jurisdictions. Tiered by licence type, revenue materiality, business model complexity and regulatory intensity.
  • Risk types: Primarily financial (credit, market, liquidity) and non-financial (fraud risk) at country level, plus operational, outsourcing and conduct risk as they manifest regionally.
  • Decision rights: Recommends country risk ratings, KRI thresholds and escalations; approves regional risk report content; owns country risk log integrity.

Что предстоит делать

  • Design and maintain the regional risk assessment methodology: risk report, taxonomy mapping, country risk rating criteria, country risk register standard.
  • Set jurisdiction tiering and proportionate reporting requirements per tier.
  • Translate Group ERMF and respective risk policies into regional implementation guidance.
  • KRI development and monitoring: Develop country-level KRI suites for financial and non-financial risk; extend from pilot markets to all licensed regions.
  • Own KRI definitions, thresholds, escalation triggers and review cadence; recalibrate annually and on material business change.
  • Operate the regional risk dashboard and automated monthly reporting, with AI-assisted interpretation for local teams and senior management.
  • Assess KRI breaches; drive closure through regional Risk Officers and functional owners with documented root cause and remediation.
  • Reporting and governance: Produce the periodic Regional Risk Report: risk profile, KRI performance, emerging risks, incidents, remediation status by jurisdiction.
  • Prepare and present regional risk content to the Risk Management Work Group, regional Risk & Compliance Committees and Board-level reporting where required.
  • Regional risk dashboard operationalised as a working tool: automated data feeds live, threshold alerting and escalation triggers functioning, and regional teams self-serving — evidenced by reduced manual intervention and a measurable fall in reporting cycle time.
  • Maintain the country risk log; track issues to closure with named owners and dates.
  • Support regulator engagement on cross-border and regional matters alongside entity Heads of Risk, Compliance and Legal.
  • Primary point of contact of Group risk for regional GMs, Risk Officers and MLROs.
  • Run regional risk forums; convert business-model-specific insight into KRIs and scenario content.
  • Drive Risk 101 and risk culture initiatives into regional populations.

Кого мы ищем

  • Degree-level qualification in Risk Management, Finance, Economics, Accounting, Law, Mathematics, Engineering, or another relevant field.
  • 7+ years in risk management, with meaningful time in regulated financial services or crypto/digital assets.
  • Track record building KRIs, risk registers and risk reporting from scratch, not just operating an existing framework.
  • Sufficient grounding in prudential/financial risk (capital, liquidity, credit, market) and non-financial (Operational, fraud, etc.) to define meaningful country-level indicators.
  • Experience engaging and influencing GM/CEO-level stakeholders without direct authority.
  • Demonstrated experience interacting with regulators, Boards, senior management, and other key stakeholders.
  • Strong analytical capability, with excellent communication, influencing, and stakeholder management skills. Multi-jurisdiction, multi-regulator experience — essential.
  • Forward-looking risk leader with the ability to connect regulatory expectations, business strategy, and practical risk controls.
  • Strong decision-maker who can assess complex issues, form clear views, and provide constructive challenges in a fast-moving environment.
  • Credible and confident communicator, able to engage effectively with regulators, Boards, senior management, and cross-functional teams.
  • Strong ownership mindset, with a disciplined approach to governance, documentation, escalation, and follow-through.
  • Comfortable working in a high-growth, highly regulated environment where standards, processes, and controls continue to evolve.
  • Naturally curious about financial markets, digital assets, market structure, and emerging risk themes.
  • Strong analytical writing — turns data into a clear risk narrative for executive and board audiences.
  • High autonomy across time zones with ambiguous local data.
  • Culturally adaptable; resists applying a single template across markets.
  • Constructive challenger — willing to escalate when a regional team under-reports.
