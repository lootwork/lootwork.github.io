Within our Crypto Operations organization, our Robinhood Canada team is building the compliant foundation for bringing Robinhood's brokerage products to Canadian customers — crypto-asset trading and traditional brokerage offerings alike — under CIRO and CSA oversight from day one, so we can grow into the market without cutting regulatory corners.

We're expanding our brokerage offering into Canada, and we're looking for a Product Manager, Regulatory to own the regulatory and compliance dimension of that build. In this role, you'll make sure every Canadian product — from crypto-asset trading to traditional equities, options, and registered accounts — is built and operated in line with CIRO, CSA and FINTRAC rules. This is a hands-on, high-visibility role at the intersection of product, engineering, and compliance, ideal for someone who has navigated both the crypto-asset trading platform (CTP) framework and traditional OEO brokerage regulation in Canada!

This role is based in our Toronto, ON office, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Partner with engineering, design, compliance, and operations to ship new Canadian products in a regulated environment.
  • Define, build, and maintain the ongoing processes that support the regulatory aspects of new and existing brokerage products.
  • Help define, maintain, and monitor adherence to firm policies and procedures across product lines.
  • Track regulatory change affecting the firm, and plan and deliver product updates so the business stays ahead of new requirements.

Что вы приносите

  • 5+ years working with brokerage products in Canada.
  • Deep familiarity with CIRO rules governing OEO brokerages, Canadian securities law, and the CSA regulatory framework for CTPs.
  • Experience partnering with engineering teams on technical projects; SQL or other Business Intelligence experience a plus.
  • Certifications such as Canadian Securities Course (CSC), Conduct and Practices Handbook (CPH), Trader Training Course (TTC), Branch Manager/Supervisor Course, or Derivatives Fundamentals and Options Licensing / Futures Licensing Course (DFOL/FLC) are a plus.
  • Direct exposure to both crypto-asset trading platforms and traditional retail brokerage (equities, options, margin, registered accounts — Tax-Free Savings Account [TFSA], Registered Retirement Savings Plan [RRSP], First Home Savings Account [FHSA] is a plus.
