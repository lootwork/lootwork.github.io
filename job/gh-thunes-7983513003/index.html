О Thunes

Thunes is the Smart Superhighway for money movement around the world. Thunes’ proprietary Direct Global Network allows Members to make payments in real-time in over 130 countries and more than 80 currencies. Thunes’ Network connects directly to over 7 billion mobile wallets and bank accounts worldwide, via more than 350 different payment methods, such as GCash, M-Pesa, Airtel, MTN, Orange, JazzCash, Easypaisa, AliPay, WeChat Pay and many more.

Members of Thunes’ Direct Global Network include gig economy giants like Uber and Deliveroo, super-apps like Grab and WeChat, MTOs, fintechs, PSPs and banks. Thunes’ Direct Global Network differentiates itself through its worldwide reach, in-house Smart Treasury Management Platform and Fortress Compliance Infrastructure, ensuring Members of the Network receive unrivalled speed, control, visibility, protection and cost efficiencies when making real-time payments globally. Headquartered in Singapore, Thunes has offices in 12 locations, including Barcelona, Beijing, Dubai, London, Manila, Nairobi, Paris, Riyadh, San Francisco, Sao Paulo and Shanghai. For more information, visit: https://www.thunes.com

О роли

We are looking for a hands-on, detail-oriented Temporary Digital Marketing Manager to join our team in Paris for an 8-month contract running from October 2026 through May 2027.

With a newly launched website and a busy calendar of external events throughout this period, you will support our digital growth initiatives by coordinating with our external LinkedIn ads and web/SEO agencies, executing email campaigns, exploring modern search visibility (AEO/GEO), and tracking marketing-sourced pipeline performance using our modern tech stack.

This role offers a fantastic opportunity for an ambitious digital marketer to take operational ownership across agency management, marketing automation, multi-touch attribution, and modern search optimization.

Основные обязанности

  • Agency Coordination: Serve as the primary day-to-day contact for external agencies managing our LinkedIn Ads, SEO, and website management to ensure campaign execution aligns with event timelines and post-launch web optimizations.
  • Email Marketing & Nurture: Execute and manage email campaigns and lead nurture journeys using Pardot (Salesforce Account Engagement).
  • AEO & GEO Strategy: Drive Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) tactics to secure digital visibility across modern AI search engines.
  • Attribution & Performance Reporting: Track marketing-sourced opportunity attributions and revenue impact using Dreamdata, building routine dashboards to analyze channel performance.
  • Event Digital Support: Deliver digital promotion and campaign execution surrounding our intensive external event schedule from October 2026 through May 2027.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with internal marketing stakeholders and external agency teams to keep all digital projects on schedule.

Требования

  • Language: Fluent in English (written and verbal) is strictly required. (French language skills are not required).
  • Experience: 1–3 years of experience in B2B digital marketing roles.
  • Tech Stack & Tools:
  • Marketing Automation: Hands-on experience or working knowledge of Pardot (Salesforce Account Engagement).
  • Attribution: Familiarity or hands-on experience with Dreamdata (or similar multi-touch revenue attribution platforms).
  • Paid Media & Search: Practical understanding of LinkedIn Ads management (working alongside external agencies) and core SEO principles.
  • Content Management: Practical experience with WordPress CMS for content updates, page optimization, and basic site maintenance post-launch.
  • ABM Platforms: Working knowledge of 6sense (or similar ABM intent platforms) to track account-level intent, align with target lists, and support targeted campaigns.
  • Emerging Tech: Curiosity and practical understanding of AEO/GEO concepts and using AI tools to optimize daily workflows.
  • Analytical Skills: Solid understanding of marketing attribution, CRM data structures, and performance metrics analysis.
  • Project Management: Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage external agency deliverables and multiple event timelines concurrently.
