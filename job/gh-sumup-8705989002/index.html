О команде

Our KYC Operations team sits at the heart of SumUp's merchant onboarding experience, making sure new businesses can get up and running quickly and compliantly. We review and verify merchant applications for the Spanish-speaking market, working to clear quality standards so that every case is handled accurately and on time. This is a great opportunity to build a solid foundation in financial compliance and operations, with the support of experienced colleagues around you from day one.

Что предстоит делать

  • Review and process verification cases for new and existing merchants, ensuring each case is completed accurately and within daily targets
  • Learn and apply KYC processes and Spanish market-specific requirements with the support of senior agents and quality assurance specialists
  • Use internal tools and databases to assess merchant information and flag any issues
  • Collaborate closely with team leads to ensure cases are handled in line with compliance standards
  • Contribute to keeping the team's case backlog under control for the Spanish market

You'll be great for this role if…

  • Fluency in both English and Spanish (written and spoken) at a professional level
  • Experience in a KYC, compliance, or customer support role is a plus, though not essential
  • Comfortable working with databases and digital tools, with solid general computer literacy
  • Ability to stay organised and meet daily targets in a structured, process-driven environment
  • Enthusiasm for learning new systems and processes, with a genuine willingness to ask questions and grow

Why you should join SumUp

  • 🌍 Opportunity to work with SumUppers globally on large-scale fintech products used by millions of businesses worldwide, from our modern Sofia office. This involves an office-first setup
  • 🌈 Commitment to Diversity and Inclusion: be part of a workplace that values and promotes diversity, fostering an inclusive environment where everyone's perspectives are respected and embraced
  • 📚 A dedicated annual L&D budget of €2000 and 10 paid days of educational leave which can be used to attend conferences and/or advance your career through further education
  • 🏖️ Generous time off: 25 days of paid leave, plus one extra day per year of service (up to 35), along with public holidays and special leave options

  • 💪 Stay active: on-site yoga and a co-sponsored Multisport card offering access to hundreds of sports facilities
  • 🍽️ Meal support: benefit from a tax-free food voucher program plus flexibility via our Re:Benefits platform
  • 🚌 Easy commute: free shuttle buses connecting directly to Joliot-Curie metro station
  • 🌴 Break4me: 1-month sabbatical after 3 years of service
  • 🔗 Referral Bonus: earn additional rewards by referring talented individuals to join the SumUp team

This role is office based in Sofia, Bulgaria.
