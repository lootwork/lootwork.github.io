At Kabam, we’re constantly raising the bar of excellence in free-to-play gaming. We hire for passion and diversity so that we can triumph in our collective skill. Our teams are made up of a fusion of personalities and interests bound together by a collaborative nature, fun-loving culture, and a drive to do what it takes to make great games.

The Operations Product Manager Co-Op plays a crucial role within the Operations Team by providing essential project and product management support for the success of both current and upcoming company products. This position requires close collaboration with cross-functional stakeholders to standardize product reporting templates and project decks, ensuring they consistently meet a high quality bar and include the necessary product insights, data points, and supporting information. Key responsibilities include comprehensive product and market analysis, deck creation and execution, preparation of supporting assets, and gathering and synthesizing the necessary data points and research to inform product evaluations and project development reviews.

This is a 4-month position from January to April 2027. This role is a full-time, on-site position based 5 days a week at our Downtown Vancouver studio.

IN THIS ROLE, YOU CAN EXPECT TO

Key Accountabilities

Market Research

  • Research market trends by analyzing data available across internal and external sources, tailoring insights to different business needs and research objectives.
  • Organize and maintain market data and key metrics in Google Sheets, providing raw data and supporting materials for senior stakeholder review.
  • Conduct in-depth competitor product analysis, reviewing product performance, player reviews, marketing strategies, and player needs to identify relevant insights from both product and marketing perspectives.
  • Support the development of clear, actionable reports that highlight key opportunities, potential risks, and data-driven recommendations.
  • Assist in creating assets from products needed to be included in the deck.
  • Create charts and graphs needed to illustrate the trends and needed assets.
  • Develop templates and frameworks to streamline future research.

Internal Playtests

  • Organize the scheduling, execution, and summarization of internal playtests to gather timely, high-quality feedback
  • Work with the UXR team to provide information needed and ensure all testing efforts run smoothly.

Publishing & External Product Evaluation

  • Assist in researching and evaluating external product opportunities.
  • Keep product evaluation schedules and frameworks up to date and organized.

Ops Group Assistance

  • Provide flexible support to other Operations teams by delivering timely operational assistance, helping the wider org meet shared goals.
  • Support in creating reporting decks, adjusting templates, and ensuring that there’s consistency throughout the design of the deck as well as the content.
  • Manage and organize all the templates needed for reporting as requested

YOUR BACKGROUND INCLUDES

  • Working towards a Bachelor’s degree.
  • Basic understanding of spreadsheet applications (Excel, Google Sheets).
  • Interest in data analysis related programs.
  • Strong communication skills and ability to interact professionally with team members across the organization.
  • Passion in the tech and gaming space.
