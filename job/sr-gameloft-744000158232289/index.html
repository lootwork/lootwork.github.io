Job Description

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON

What you will focus on

Do you bring characters and game objects to life through animation? Join Gameloft Saigon's Animation team. You'll create believable, high-quality animations for ongoing projects. You'll work with Lead Animators, Lead Artists, designers and programmers to get the best result on every platform. Middle-level animators will grow their craft in a strong art team. Senior animators will also help shape our guidelines and coach junior talent.

What it means on a daily basis

  • Create believable, high-quality key-framed animations for real-time and pre-rendered sequences.
  • Animate characters and game objects, in either a cartoony, stylized or realistic style.
  • Rig and skin your assets, including complex rigs.
  • Work with game designers and programmers to deliver the best animation within platform limits.
  • Work with the production team, Lead Animator and Lead Artists, and meet project milestones and deadlines.
  • Help establish art production guidelines, and spot and prevent future mistakes.
  • Coach and train junior animators.

What success will look like

  • Pipeline: Read the production pipeline documents and follow them.
  • Quality: Deliver animation assets that meet the high quality standards set by the 3D Leads.
  • Feedback: Take feedback and adapt to technical requirements with a positive attitude. Express ideas and concerns calmly.
  • Communication: Give your Animator Lead and peers regular verbal and written updates on progress.
  • Collaboration: Work closely with artists and developers and share your knowledge to improve production.

Who you would be working with

  • You will be reporting to the Animator Supervisor, Art Producer, and Art Lead/Director.
  • Closely collaborating with other disciplines in the Art team (e.g., 2D Art, 3D Art, Technical Art, VFX) and other teams in the project (e.g., Game Design, Programming).

Where can this role take you?

  • Advance vertically to higher level in the Animation team such as Senior or Principal position.
  • Transition to other disciplines within the Art team: 3D Artist, Technical Artist, VFX Artist, and so on.

Recruitment journey

  • Online screening call with recruiter
  • Distance art test
  • Offline interview with Animator Lead, Art Director, and Producer

Требования

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED

  • Games industry experience, with high proficiency in 3ds Max or Maya.
  • Academic background in animation or equivalent (hand-drawing, sketching and/or illustration.
  • Advanced knowledge of the principles of animation, character motion and physics-based motion.
  • Excellent skinning and rigging skills with low to medium polygon counts, and a solid understanding of topology and production constraints.
  • Strong grasp of human anatomy, proportion, perspective, composition, color, shading, lighting and post-processing.

Кто вы

  • Creative, original and attentive to detail.
  • Strong communicator, with negotiation, conflict resolution and decision-making skills.
  • Proactively follow a brief, work on your own initiative and solve artistic or technical issues.
  • Organized, estimate time accurately and meet deadlines.
  • Teamplayer, open to feedback, and calm under pressure.
  • Committed, self-disciplined and passionate about games across platforms.
