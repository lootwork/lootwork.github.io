HEALTH CAN’T WAIT.

Not for symptoms to get worse. Not for a six‑month appointment. Not for a system to catch up. But that’s exactly how healthcare works today. You wait, until you can’t.

Alan exists to end the wait.

Health is a universal right, and we believe this right can only become real when it’s coupled with prevention. We need to stop treating health as something we repair and start treating it as something we build, every day. It’s not solely a question of willpower. It’s the healthcare system itself that needs to work for everyone, in a sustainable way.

So we are building the new standard in prevention insurance. Alan is the first company that integrates insurance, prevention, and care into a single, acclaimed user experience.

We are on an incredible journey to build a global leading company, with a unique culture https://alan.com/en/careers. We already partner with 40K+ companies of all sizes, serving more than 1M+ members, and have reached €800M+ in ARR.

Prevention as the new norm. That's what we're building with our team of 800+ people. If it speaks to you: we're hiring across France, Spain, Belgium, and Canada. And beyond.

🎯 THE MISSION & IMPACT

Our system defines what Insurance coverage (health or life & disability) our members get, with a high level of customization:

- We equip our Sales team with tools to craft these offers, price them, and contractualize them in one go. We also power our self-serve subscription flow.

- Over time, we manage our contract lifecycles, making sure they are always up-to-date in terms of legal requirements and responsible consumption best practices, and at equilibrium in terms of loss ratio.

- Alan's claim engine, billing system and many other part of our stack relies on the offers we build to deliver a pristine experience to our members.

🚀 WHAT YOU'LL BUILD AND WHO YOU’LL WORK WITH

- Next-Gen Healthcare Framework: 4 countries (and more to come), multiple products (Insurance but also several other healthcare products) but one system. Come build the contract & service stack that will power Alan’s growth for the next 10 years.

- AI-powered Acquisition Engine: Sales teams are autonomous & assisted by AI to customize coverage, compute pricing, and generate contracts in real-time. No need for actuaries, no more waiting days for quotes.

- Automated Renewal System: Automatically reviews and adjusts coverage for our entire portfolio annually, accounting for legal changes, consumption trends, and economic shifts.

You'll work closely with insurance experts, business specialist, sales teams, and collaborate with other eng teams across Alan. We're the bridge between complex regulations and simple user experiences.

⚡ WHY THIS ROLE IS SPECIAL

- Direct Impact: Your decisions affect Alan’s scaling, growth, profitability and ability to provide easy access to healthcare to more and more people.

- Complex Problems: Modelling a complex world in a simple stack, real-time pricing, scale challenges

- Ownership & Growth: Influence product direction, mentor other engineers, and lead technical initiatives that shape the future of healthcare access
