О TIDE

Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love.

О команде

Ongoing Monitoring is building the capabilities Tide needs to detect, prevent and manage financial crime, fraud and risk across the member lifecycle.

Within Ongoing Monitoring, we are creating a new Acquiring Fraud & Risk team as part of the Ongoing Monitoring team. This team will focus on protecting Tide’s growing Merchant Acquiring business by reducing acquiring fraud, chargeback losses and operational risk across card-present and card-not-present payment journeys.

Merchant Acquiring is a fast-growing product within Tide. We are on a mission to provide the simplest and easiest ways for our members to get paid. As we scale Acquiring globally, we need strong monitoring capabilities that protect Tide from avoidable losses while keeping the experience smooth for genuine members.

О роли

As Senior Product Manager for Ongoing Monitoring Acquiring Fraud & Risk, you will own the roadmap for Tide’s acquiring transaction risk monitoring capabilities.

Working closely with Engineering, Data Science, Fraud Operations, Risk and Acquiring Product teams, you will build and scale real-time monitoring solutions using rules engines, machine learning models and Agentic AI to reduce fraud losses, improve chargeback outcomes and manage Tide’s acquiring risk exposure across card-present and card-not-present payment journeys.

Product Management and Product Development

You will be responsible for

  • Owning and independently defining the product strategy, vision and roadmap for acquiring fraud and risk monitoring within Ongoing Monitoring
  • Building scalable monitoring capabilities across card-present and card-not-present acquiring journeys
  • Working with Engineering and Data Science teams to define and deliver real-time rules, risk signals, machine learning models and decisioning workflows
  • Having end-to-end ownership of the acquiring fraud and risk monitoring product development lifecycle
  • Prioritising product work based on risk reduction, commercial impact, operational effort, member experience and technical feasibility
  • Creating clear product narratives for senior stakeholders across Product, Risk, Operations, Finance and Commercial teams

Data-Driven Product Development & Acquiring Fraud Reduction

You will be responsible for

  • Using data to identify acquiring fraud patterns, merchant behaviour, loss drivers and control gaps
  • Developing product capabilities that actively reduce acquiring fraud losses and help keep Tide within relevant card scheme thresholds
  • Establishing and monitoring performance metrics such as fraud loss rate, chargeback rate, scheme exposure, false positive rate and operational efficiency
  • Running experiments and targeted control changes to improve fraud prevention while minimising friction for genuine members
  • Working with Finance, Risk and Operations teams to quantify the commercial impact of fraud and risk controls

Chargeback Management and Operational Risk

You will be responsible for

  • Building product capabilities that improve chargeback prevention, monitoring, handling and recovery outcomes
  • Working with Operations to improve case management workflows, review quality and operational efficiency
  • Improving visibility of chargeback drivers, merchant risk profiles and recovery performance
  • Helping Tide reduce avoidable losses where chargebacks arrive after funds have already been settled to merchants
  • Tracking and improving metrics such as recovery rate, chargeback handling time, operational cost per case and manual review effort

AI, Automation and Risk Decisioning

You will be responsible for

  • Identifying opportunities to use Agentic AI and automation to improve acquiring fraud and risk operations
  • Working with Engineering and Data Science teams to build scalable, explainable and measurable AI-supported risk decisioning capabilities
  • Improving the speed, consistency and quality of acquiring risk reviews
  • Increasing automation where manual review adds cost without improving risk outcomes

Кого мы ищем

You are an experienced Product Manager with strong acquiring risk knowledge. You understand the commercial reality of fraud, chargebacks, scheme exposure and operational handling in a live acquiring environment.

You are comfortable working with complex data, real-time payment flows, operational teams and senior stakeholders. You can turn messy risk problems into clear product priorities and measurable outcomes.…
