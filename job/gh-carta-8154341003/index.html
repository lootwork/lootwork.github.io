The Company You’ll Join

Carta is the connected platform and AI-native ecosystem for private capital. Built to replace fragmented tools with a single system of record, Carta brings together the software, services, and legal infrastructure that founders use to manage equity, fund managers use to run administration and reporting, and legal teams use to close transactions. Trusted by 55,000 companies and 1.8M+ equity holders in 160+ countries, and 10,000 funds and SPVs representing $250B+ in assets under management, Carta is transforming how private capital operates. Recognized by Fortune, Forbes, Fast Company, Inc. and Great Places to Work.

For more information about our offices and culture, check out our Carta careers page.

The Team You'll Work With

Carta is looking for a Marketing Intern to join our Singapore team between January to June 2027.

Carta opened its Singapore office in 2021, and since then, has been building a world-class team to support the startup and investor ecosystem across Asia Pacific, the Middle East and Africa.

The Marketing Intern will report up to Carta’s Head of Marketing for APAC & MEA, and get the chance to work firsthand on a range of projects, including marketing campaigns, website optimization, marketing analytics, event support and more. This role will give you the opportunity to try out the different realms of marketing and gain the necessary skill sets to jumpstart your career in B2B tech.

The Problem You'll Solve

  • Campaigns: Work with the demand generation leads to launch campaigns across the region, and A/B test and optimize lead generation efforts across online and offline channels
  • Content & PR: Partner with the regional team to localize blog articles to the region, edit bylines, craft organic social media content and develop customer case studies to increase brand awareness
  • Events: Support on digital & physical marketing collateral ideation and design (presentation decks, swag) as well as provide on-the-ground support for Virtual Events & physical events
  • Other ad-hoc tasks as assigned

The Impact You will Have

As a Marketing Intern at Carta, you will be an integral part of growing the marketing function in this region. You will drive demand in the market and help oversee Carta’s growth in APAC & MEA.

О вас

  • Studying Bachelors Degree in a relevant field (business, marketing)
  • Previous internship or work experience in marketing would be preferred
  • Desire to learn and work in a fast-paced environment
  • Excellent time management and organizational skills
  • Interest in B2B Marketing as well as the startup space

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with marketing and sales platforms (such as Salesforce, Marketo, Figma, Google Analytics, Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads)
  • Proficiencies with Microsoft Office and/or Google Suite (GSuite)
  • Knowledge of the startup and venture capital ecosystem

At Carta, you’re not just an employee. You’re a builder who is creating infrastructure that accelerates innovation and empowers more ownership. Cartans are helpful, relentless, unconventional and kind; representing Carta’s Identity Traits. They work collaboratively and cross functionally to challenge the status quo; working towards a common goal of creating more owners in the private markets.
