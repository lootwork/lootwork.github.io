ABOUT YOU

We’re looking for a Senior 3D Animator & Rigger who is passionate about creating realistic, high-quality animations and technically complex rigs for real-time projects. You enjoy working at the intersection of animation and tech — bringing characters and creatures to life while building the systems that support them.

You have a strong understanding of animal movement and behavior, and you’re comfortable taking ownership of the full animation process — from crafting believable motion to implementing and fine-tuning it in Unreal Engine.

You’re also confident on the technical side: working with MetaHuman rigs, exploring facial animation workflows (including MetaHuman Animator), and building advanced rigs for animals, including muscle simulation. Whether using existing auto-rig tools or developing custom solutions, you’re able to adapt your approach to fit the needs of the project.

RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Create realistic animal animations (locomotion, behavior, reactions)
  • Ideally work with Animation Blueprints in Unreal Engine
  • Set up state machines and blend spaces
  • Integrate animations into gameplay in collaboration with programmers
  • Work with IK, root motion, and physically accurate movement
  • Optimize animations for real-time performance
  • Maintain a high level of realism and visual quality

REQUIREMENTS

  • 5+ years of experience in 3D animation (games / real-time / simulators)
  • Strong proficiency in Unreal Engine (UE4 / UE5)
  • Excellent understanding of animal anatomy and movement
  • Experience with Maya, Blender, or MotionBuilder
  • Understanding of Animation Blueprints, IK, and retargeting
  • Experience with Motion Matching
  • Ability to work independently and make animation decisions
  • English: working proficiency (Upper-Intermediate or higher)

Nice to Have

  • Experience working on simulators or realistic projects
  • Experience with motion capture (including animals)
  • Experience with Control Rig in Unreal Engine
  • Experience optimizing animations for performance
  • Experience with plugins and custom solutions in UE5
