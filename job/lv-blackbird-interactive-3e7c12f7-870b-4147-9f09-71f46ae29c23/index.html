Blackbird Interactive is a creative-fueled, future-driven, independent game studio where the best talent in the industry can find long-term careers to help grow their abilities while working on a wide range of projects, which encompass world-class IPs as well as our own creations across multiple genres.

Our studio operates as a remote-first workplace, offering flexible work arrangements.

PLEASE NOTE: At this time, we are only able to hire candidates who are legally eligible to work in Canada and reside within Canada.

Blackbird Interactive is seeking a passionate Technical Animator to join our team to deliver a large, exciting IP!

Do you have a broad range of creative and technical experience setting up character and mechanical animations in Unreal Engine 5? We’re looking for a technical animator who has the technical proficiency to rig, skin, and export animated meshes into Unreal Engine. On top of that, we are requiring a versatile set of skills to animate humanoid characters as well as machinery within a sci-fi setting.

We support both remote and hybrid working arrangements across Canada (Vancouver, BC). At BBI, you’ll find a flexible, forward-thinking environment where creative voices are valued and nurtured.

Please include a link to your showreel with your CV.

Обязанности

  • Develop and maintain Maya and Unreal-based pipelines that enable animation, rigging, and content export workflows
  • Produce high quality animation in gameplay to fit the overall style and established creative direction
  • Proficient in animating both mechanical and humanoid assets
  • Ability to take direction from the Art Director in order to maintain a coherent aesthetic thread throughout the project
  • Collaborate with technical artists to fill gaps in pipeline and tools where needed
  • Collaborate with 3D artists to create smooth handoffs between asset creation and animation set-up
  • Collaborate with production to provide estimates and timelines to ensure timely delivery of assets

Требования

  • 4+ years of experience in technical animation and gameplay animation specifically for games
  • Strong knowledge of rigging workflows
  • Well-versed in Maya and Unreal Engine 5
  • Familiarity with game-ready skeleton design, skinning, and animation scene structure
  • Comfort working in Perforce-integrated pipelines and managing scene referencing/export workflows
  • Comfort with quick iteration and problem-solving
  • Strong communication skills and a collaborative mindset

BONUSES

  • Passion for games
  • Passion for film and cinematography
  • Experience manipulating Unreal camera rigs and setups
