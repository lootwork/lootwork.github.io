О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

The GTM Partnerships team drives new customer acquisition through strategic partners and builds scalable partnership models that turn ecosystem relationships into business outcomes. In this role, you’ll help Korean businesses grow globally by converting partner relationships into qualified leads, customer onboarding opportunities, and repeatable collaboration models.

- Build and deepen partnerships across the cross-border e-commerce ecosystem, corporate secretarial firms, government institutions, industry associations, chambers of commerce, and other organisations that support Korean businesses expanding internationally.

- Generate qualified leads and customer onboarding opportunities through seminars, workshops, webinars, campaigns, referral programmes, and member education sessions.

- Design structured partnership plans that translate institutional relationships into repeatable collaboration models and tangible pipeline.

- Lead partnership discussions from relationship building through execution, including collaboration frameworks and formal partnership agreements where relevant.

- Work with external institutions to understand their members’ needs and position Airwallex as a practical solution for cross-border growth.

- Partner with Sales, Marketing, Product, and Operations to ensure partnership programmes translate into measurable pipeline and onboarding outcomes.

You'll work from our Seoul office five days a week.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- Experience generating leads through partnerships in the cross-border e-commerce space.

- Experience working with marketplaces, e-commerce enablers, agencies, logistics providers, tax advisors, settlement partners, government institutions, industry associations, chambers of commerce, or similar ecosystem players.

- An understanding of the merchant journey and how partnership collaboration can create commercial value.

- Experience working in a partnership-led sales environment and contributing to meaningful commercial outcomes, whether or not you’ve directly owned lead generation.

- Experience working across multiple stakeholders and converting ecosystem collaboration into tangible pipeline.

- Experience building trusted external relationships and connecting them to customer onboarding or business outcomes.

- Understanding of Korean businesses’ export, commerce, fintech adoption, or international expansion needs.

Preferred

- Experience with partner-led lead generation or co-selling in cross-border e-commerce.

- Strong understanding of merchant pain points across payments, foreign exchange, operations, logistics, tax, and reconciliation.

- Experience selling to or supporting Korean merchants expanding internationally.

- Experience collaborating closely with organisations such as KITA, KOTRA, fintech associations, beauty associations, chambers of commerce, or corporate secretarial firms.

- Experience discussing memoranda of understanding or establishing formal partnership frameworks.

- Experience running programmes that support Korean businesses expanding overseas.

- Knowledge of foreign exchange, cross-border business, or overseas entity establishment.

- Knowledge of competitive solutions and ecosystem dynamics in global commerce.

You'll thrive here if

- You like defining your own space rather than staying in one narrow lane.

- You're comfortable with ambiguity. Give you a problem, not a prescription, and you'll run with it.

- You communicate clearly and directly across cultures, and you're thoughtful about how context shifts meaning across borders.

Learn more about your team

SME & Growth at Airwallex is about more than closing deals. We help small and mid-market businesses solve real financial challenges across borders: getting paid globally, managing multi-currency accounts, paying suppliers, and running spend across markets from a single platform. You're not selling a feature. You're helping founders and finance leaders replace fragmented financial operations with one global system that actually scales.

It's a function for sellers who like to build, not follow a script: curious, resourceful, and comfortable figuring things out in markets that are still wide open. You get real autonomy to create value, build relationships, and move opportunities forward.

If you want to sell a serious product, solve real problems for ambitious businesses, and build your career fast, this is a place to do it.
