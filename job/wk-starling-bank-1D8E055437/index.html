О Role & Team

The Security Architecture team at Starling is driving significant impact across the organisation, actively guiding and empowering teams across Security and the wider technology landscape. We are looking for a mid-to-senior level Information Security Architect to join our ranks.

While you will cover typical end-to-end security architecture across our modern tech stack, this role carries a strategic focus: Cryptography & Post-Quantum Readiness. As quantum computing capabilities evolve, we are looking to ramp up our Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) efforts. We are looking for a leader who will own the strategic design of our cloud-native and on-premise cryptography roadmaps, conduct cryptographic assessments, drive crypto-agility, and define our technical roadmap to keep Starling resilient against emerging future threats.

What you'll get to do

  • Lead our Post-Quantum Strategy: Take ownership of Starling’s Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) initiative over the coming years; designing our crypto-inventory, evaluating PQC algorithms, and designing architectures built for cryptographic agility.
  • Act as the Cryptography SME: Provide expert architecture and design guidance on Public Key Infrastructure (PKI), Hardware Security Modules (HSMs), key management, service mesh security, and transport layer protocols across our cloud environments.
  • Enterprise Architecture Delivery: Architect secure, scalable solutions across AWS and GCP, translating complex security requirements into simple, pragmatic design patterns for engineering teams.
  • Secure Architecture Governance: Lead and assist with secure design reviews and threat modeling initiatives, delivering actionable security guidance and continuous support to Engineering teams.
  • Proof of Concept Development: Our architects enjoy a hands-on approach. When suitable, we design and build PoC solutions for engineers to refine or draw inspiration from.
  • Collaborate & Influence: Work closely with software engineers, cloud architects, and security operations to review RFCs and ADRs, conduct threat models, and embed security seamlessly into the development lifecycle without adding friction.
  • Drive Thought Leadership: Serve as a trusted advisor within our growing Security Architecture team, staying ahead of industry trends, threat vectors, and regulatory standards to elevate Starling's overall security posture.

Responsibilties

  • Architect end-to-end security solutions for cloud-native applications, microservices, and modern banking platforms.
  • Develop and lead our Post-Quantum Cryptography transition roadmap and crypto-inventory strategy.
  • Perform threat modeling and technical risk assessments on new and existing platform architectures.
  • Define, document, and promote enterprise cryptographic management standards (key lifecycle, hardware security, modern cipher suites).
  • Review architectural design documents, RFCs, and ADRs to ensure alignment with security guardrails and regulatory requirements.
  • Mentor engineering teams and provide guidance on cryptographic implementations, securely managing secrets, and data protection at rest/in transit.
  • Support the rest of the Security Architecture team with daily security architecture activities (e.g. adhoc security architecture advisory services).
