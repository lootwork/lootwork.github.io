Binance is a leading global blockchain ecosystem behind the world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume and registered users. We are trusted by 300+ million people in 100+ countries for our industry-leading security, user fund transparency, trading engine speed, deep liquidity, and an unmatched portfolio of digital-asset products. Binance offerings range from trading and finance to education, research, payments, institutional services, and more. Our mission is to increase the freedom of money and financial access for people around the world.

Обязанности

  • Design, build, and maintain backend services and RESTful APIs powering compliance and analytics products
  • Own features end to end, from technical design through deployment and monitoring
  • Build data pipelines that ingest, normalise, and index on-chain activity across multiple blockchains
  • Contribute to the architecture of new compliance capabilities — you'll be in the room where the approach gets decided, not handed the outcome
  • Model data and optimise queries against relational and NoSQL stores at scale
  • Work directly with compliance and product specialists to translate regulatory requirements into technical design
  • Improve reliability, observability, and performance of services already in production
  • Use AI-assisted development tooling as part of your normal workflow to raise output quality and speed

Требования

  • 3+ years building production backend systems in Java
  • Strong command of Spring Boot, JVM fundamentals, concurrency, and memory behaviour
  • Solid SQL and data modelling; practical experience diagnosing slow queries
  • Experience designing and operating web-facing services at scale — request handling, caching, rate limiting, idempotency
  • Working proficiency in both Chinese and English, spoken and written
  • Fluent, daily use of AI coding agents such as Claude Code or Codex, with a clear view of where they help and where they need supervision
  • Comfortable owning code you did not write, and reading unfamiliar codebases quickly
  • Genuine interest in the problem space — compliance products reward engineers who care why a rule exists, not only what it says

Желательно

  • A self-starting, exploratory mindset — you investigate problems before being asked to, and you go looking for the answer rather than waiting for a ticket
  • A willingness to contribute to how the industry develops, and to speak up with constructive proposals when you see a better way — we want engineers who challenge the design, not only implement it
  • AWS in production: ECS/EKS, RDS, S3, SQS, IAM, CloudWatch
  • Blockchain or crypto exposure — node RPC integration, on-chain data indexing, wallet or transaction infrastructure
  • Kafka or similar streaming platforms
  • Prior work in a regulated environment: financial services, payments, AML/KYC, or RegTech
  • Familiarity with FATF Travel Rule, sanctions screening, or transaction monitoring concepts

Why this role

Web3 compliance is one of the few areas where the engineering problem and the regulatory problem are genuinely unsolved together. The products you build here are used by exchanges, VASPs, and regulators, and the design choices you make have reach well beyond a single codebase. If you want work where the technical difficulty and the significance are both real, this is that.
