О нас

Many of the world’s biggest brands and publicly traded companies selling online rely on Riskified for guaranteed protection against chargebacks, to fight fraud and policy abuse at scale, and to improve customer retention. О роли

As the Product Lead for Riskified’s Dispute Resolve solution, you will own the Dispute Resolve domain end-to-end, driving Riskified’s mission to help merchants manage chargebacks effectively, improve win rates, and scale their dispute operations.

Dispute Resolve enables merchants to handle disputes efficiently - from ingestion and analysis, through evidence generation and submission, to performance optimization - helping them improve win rates, reduce operational overhead, and make smarter decisions about which disputes to fight.

In this role, you will take full ownership of shaping and driving the Dispute Resolve product - hands-on, from strategy through execution - defining, executing, and evolving end-to-end dispute flows across multiple merchant setups, and ensuring the product delivers clear value and measurable outcomes. You will expand system integrations, evolve our evidence capabilities, and lead the product analytics function for the domain: owning key metrics, making data-driven decisions, and setting the product benchmarks that Sales relies on to sell Dispute Resolve.

You will work closely with Engineering and Data Science to deliver robust, scalable solutions, translating technical constraints into business priorities and vice versa. You will also partner closely with the Go-to-Market (GTM) organization and Account Management to support product adoption, enable new deals, and ensure Dispute Resolve contributes to revenue growth, retention, and expansion. As the domain leader, your decisions and benchmarks will directly shape customer satisfaction, commercial success, and the long-term evolution of the Dispute Resolve roadmap.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the Dispute Resolve domain end-to-end, leading the definition, execution, and evolution of dispute management flows across different merchant setups.
  • Set product strategy and roadmap priorities for Dispute Resolve, working hands-on with Engineering, Product Design, Data Science, and BI to deliver against them.
  • Own product analytics for the domain: define the metrics that matter, monitor performance, and make data-driven decisions on prioritization and roadmap direction.
  • Set and maintain the product benchmarks used by Sales to represent Dispute Resolve’s performance and value in the different markets.
  • Lead the expansion and optimization of integrations with PSPs, gateways, and additional systems to enable scalable dispute ingestion and submission.
  • Drive the development and continuous improvement of evidence capabilities, improving quality, coverage, and win rates across reason codes and merchant verticals.
  • Translate complex dispute workflows and data challenges into scalable, intuitive product solutions that balance usability, automation and performance.
  • Partner closely with GTM and Account Management to support product adoption, onboarding, and ongoing usage, and to ensure Dispute Resolve supports growth and retention goals.
  • Engage directly with merchants and prospects to understand real-world workflows, validate solutions, and identify opportunities for improvement.
  • Own backlog prioritization and roadmap execution, focusing on high-impact initiatives that improve operational efficiency and dispute performance.
  • Serve as the go-to subject-matter expert on dispute management, representment strategies and product capabilities - internally and with customers.
