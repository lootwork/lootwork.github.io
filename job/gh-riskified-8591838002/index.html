О нас

Riskified is the AI platform powering the eCommerce revolution. We use cutting-edge technology, machine-learning algorithms, and behavioral analytics to identify legitimate customers and keep them moving toward checkout. Merchants use Riskified to increase revenue, prevent fraud, and eliminate customer friction. Riskified has reviewed hundreds of millions of transactions and approved billions of dollars of revenue for merchants across virtually all industries, including a number of Fortune 500 companies.

О роли

Our Product Management Team plays a pivotal role in our company, driving product success by working collaboratively with Riskified’s engineering, data science, design, marketing, analytics and Application teams. Product Managers at Riskified are required to solve complex problems, using a varied and multi-disciplinary skillset. We continue to grow fast, so we are looking for people who can learn and adjust quickly as the company and our product suite expand.

Being part of our team means working alongside smart and motivated colleagues who consistently work hard and execute. Our philosophy centers around end-to-end ownership, which means that every team member is empowered and accountable. We hold ourselves to high standards, have ambitious goals, and are not afraid of trying new things.

As an Fraud AI Applications Product Manager at Riskified, you will lead a broad and complex product domain at the core of Riskified’s business - the analytical delivery of our ML-based Fraud management offering. You will work with senior stakeholders to define our vision and build the strategy to achieve it. You will design products and processes to execute that strategy, work to automate and optimize complex analytical processes and develop Riskified’s next generation analytical performance platform. You will work with data scientists in order to solve optimization problems. You will envision and define the approaches and tactics in which operations teams - fraud experts and analysts deliver best-in-class analytical performance for some of the world’s largest eCommerce companies. You will engage directly with users - internal and external - to identify challenges and opportunities to increase the solution value. You will achieve alignment on the right solutions and build innovative solutions to tackle complex optimization challenges of fraud at scale while handling a large scale AI-based system.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build the next generation of Riskified analytical performance platform to automate and optimize the analytics processes in a way that creates control and visibility, and brings business results
  • Collaborate with our analytics and operations teams to define their needs and brainstorm potential solutions
  • Partner closely with Data Science teams to translate business requirements into technical solutions and drive the development of advanced ML-based fraud detection capabilities.
  • Work with clients to understand the business needs and define creative solutions to address those opportunities
  • Own the Analytics Applications roadmap to meet the business goals, while doing hands-on and end-to-end product management, working with developers and data scientists
  • Define and own KPIs that measure the solution accuracy and impact
  • ​​Lead the planning, scoping, and prioritization of new capabilities to achieve Riskified business goals

Требования

  • 3+ years of experience as a Product Manager working with complex data & analytics solutions - must!
  • Proven experience working with operational teams as users in an analytics-based B2B product - must !
  • Ability to envision, work with product designers and run the development of a complex system UI, with a knack for intuitive UX
  • Fast learner with strong interpersonal and communication skills
  • Familiarity with AI based products and technologies
  • B.Sc. in Computer Science or relevant Degree
  • Experience mentoring fellow product managers - an advantage
  • Experience in SaaS companies in a B2B setting - an advantage
  • Experience with eCommerce, FinTech, Online Payments, Fraud - an advantage
