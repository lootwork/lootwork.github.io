A day in the life (Responsibilities)

As an IT Service Desk Analyst (P2), you will provide autonomous technical assistance across software applications and hardware. Taking ownership of moderately complex issues.

Your responsibilities include

  • Operations: Log, triage, and resolve Level 1/2 incidents via Slack, Email, and ServiceNow while meeting SLA targets.
  • Asset Management: Oversee the IT asset lifecycle including inventory audits, new-hire provisioning, and equipment disposal.
  • Meeting Rooms & Events: Maintain AV/IT equipment in collaboration rooms and provide live technical support for company Town Halls.
  • Knowledge Management: Draft internal troubleshooting guides and run IT onboarding sessions for new employees.

What you'll need to thrive (Requirements)

  • Strong hardware and software troubleshooting methodology across macOS Tahoe, Windows 11, and Linux.
  • Proficient in ServiceNow for end-to-end ticket lifecycle management.
  • Working knowledge of Jira for task tracking and cross-team coordination.
  • Working knowledge of networking fundamentals (DNS, DHCP, VPN, Wi-Fi).
  • Familiarity with AV systems and video conferencing platforms (Zoom Rooms, Microsoft Teams Rooms, Google Meet).
  • Basic understanding of asset management principles and ITAM tools.
  • OS: macOS Tahoe, Windows 11, and Linux.
  • Productivity & AV: Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, Teams, and Zoom Rooms.
  • Management & Tracking: Active Directory/Azure AD, Intune or JAMF, ServiceNow, and Jira.

What will help you stand out (Nonessential Skills/Nice to Haves)

You will regularly work with and support the following environment

  • CompTIA A+ or ITIL Foundation v4 are highly preferred.
  • Experience working with AI systems and large language models, specifically Claude.
  • Experience in programming and coding with Python, Java, Node.js, and HTML.
