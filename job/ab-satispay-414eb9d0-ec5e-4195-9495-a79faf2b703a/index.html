О нас

Traditional banking has never really given you control over your financial life. Satispay exists to change that: to empower people through finance.

That means making financial services easy and accessible for over 6.5M users. We started with payments, then benefits, investing, cards... and we’re not done. Not even close.

We follow the same logic every time: find a real problem, solve it properly, then look for the next one. The destination? To build the most loved financial platform in the world.

If you own outcomes rather than tasks, stay close to what users need, and refuse to wait for someone else to fix what's broken — you'll find people here who work exactly in the same way. And together, you'll build something worth being proud of.

Что предстоит делать

As an iOS Developer in the Consumer Wealth team, you'll play a key role in shaping how millions of users save, invest, and grow their money, focusing on products like Funds, ETFs, and Stocks.

Here's what that looks like in practice

- Create impactful mobile experiences - Develop and implement native iOS applications to enhance engagement and operate mission-critical wealth and investment features.

- Collaborate within a team - Work closely with Product Managers, Designers, and Backend Engineers to integrate cohesive, high-performance mobile solutions.

- Focus on users - Transform complex financial data into intuitive designs and clear charts; collaborate with Data Analysts on A/B tests to optimize user experience.

- Drive continuous improvement - Enhance existing investment features and create new user-centric tools to keep our financial offerings cutting-edge.

- Ensure excellence - Maintain high standards for coding, performance, and security for absolute app reliability.

- Work in an AI-Powered Environment - Leverage AI tools daily to write and optimize code, speed up root cause analysis, and generate documentation.

Our iOS tech stack is built heavily on Swift and UIKit/SwiftUI, utilizing the iOS SDK. We value clean architecture, heavily leveraging MVVM, app modularization, Dependency Injection, and Reactive Programming (RxSwift/ReactiveSwift). You'll take ownership of your code from local development to App Store deployment.

Who we’re looking for

This role needs someone who is a problem-solver, loves teamwork, is passionate about financial markets, and is ready for real ownership.

- Relevant educational background - Degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field.

- iOS development experience - At least 3 years building and shipping high-quality native iOS apps (Swift, SwiftUI/UIKit, MVVM, Reactive Programming). App Store published projects are a strong plus.

- FinTech & Wealth mindset - Strong interest or experience in FinTech, trading platforms, or wealth management products, including handling complex data rendering or financial charts.

- Passion and adaptability - Passionate about startup environments and adaptable as our wealth product line grows.

- Structured communication - Ability to communicate clearly, adapting your style to build trust with stakeholders.

- Language proficiency - Fluent in English.

Don't worry if you don't tick every box. We believe in the power of different viewpoints and strengths. Your unique perspective is important as we build something special: if you're passionate and can make a difference, we truly encourage you to apply.
