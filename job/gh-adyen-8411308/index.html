Product Manager - Business Intelligence and Data DevEx

At Platform Engineering, our group is building the foundation that lets Adyen unlock the full power of its 20 years of payments data. That data is a powerful asset, but it only reaches its full potential when combined with the right platform and solid processes. That’s why Business Intelligence, Data Analytics and Data Developer Experience, within the Application Intelligence portfolio, are key for Adyen’s success.

Within the Application Intelligence portfolio, we provide the tools and foundations behind Adyen's data products: from insights dashboards and self-service analytics to data pipelines and data applications. Our tools serve 5000+ active users today. As Product Manager, you will own the end-to-end experience of three groups of users:

  • Business consumers - product managers, commercial, operations and second-line teams who need fast, trustworthy answers. You will evolve our conversational analytics experience, so anyone can ask questions of our data in plain language and act on the answer;
  • Data analysts - who explore large datasets, build dashboards, run experiments and automate insights. You will make it easier for them to go from question to reliable, shareable insight;
  • Data engineers - who build, test, deploy and operate the pipelines and data applications everyone else depends on. You will shape how they develop data apps on our platform, from the first line of code to production.

You will work hand in hand with engineering teams and senior product colleagues to turn user insight into a clear roadmap, and make sure what we ship is adopted.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the strategy for how people at Adyen use data, from business consumers to data analysts and data engineers, aligned with the broader Data Platform strategy.
  • Translate that strategy into a roadmap. Balance short-term user needs with long-term platform investments, and align engineering and stakeholders on direction and trade-offs.
  • Run continuous discovery with all three user groups (interviews, usage data, support channels) and turn what you learn into clear problem statements and priorities.
  • Define success metrics such as adoption, time-to-insight and developer productivity. Use them to steer the roadmap and to show the impact on how fast Adyen delivers data products.
  • Proactively identify emerging trends in data and AI to future-proof the strategy, keeping Adyen at the forefront of data productivity.
  • Plan and run internal launches, enablement and change management, so new capabilities become part of people’s daily workflows.

Кто вы

  • 3+ years of experience as a Product Manager, with a track record of shipping data products: BI and analytics tools, data platforms, or internal data and developer tooling.
  • Solid data foundations: You’re comfortable with data modeling, pipelines, data quality, and BI tools (e.g., Looker, Tableau), so you can work closely with analysts and data engineers on technical details.
  • Curiosity about how AI is changing the way people use data, such as natural-language querying, semantic layers and how to evaluate the quality of an AI-generated answer.
  • Empathy for both technical and non-technical users: you can translate a commercial colleague's question into a clear problem for an engineering team, and back.
  • A data-driven approach: you combine qualitative user feedback with usage data to make decisions and explain them.
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills, and the ability to align stakeholders with competing needs.
  • An ownership mindset: you take initiative, move fast in ambiguous situations and stay a collaborative team player in a cross-functional environment.

Bonus points for

  • Previous experience as a Business Analyst, Data Analyst or BI Developer: you have been a daily user of the kind of tools you will now build.
  • Background in platform engineering or developer experience roles.
  • Prior experience in fintech or other high-reliability regulated industries.
