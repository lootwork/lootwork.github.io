With multiple awards to our name, Larian Studios has proven that we’re dedicated to delivering high-quality role-playing games As we move on to new projects, the studio that brought you Baldur’s Gate 3 & Divinity: Original Sin 2 is looking for an Associate Lead Cinematic Artist to join the team and help us in crafting the high calibre games our fans have come to expect.

Обязанности

  • Author realtime cinematics and interactive dialog sequences in our proprietary engine
  • Develop junior talent through artistic feedback sessions and performance review cycles
  • Develop and enforce standard practices for an evolving pipeline and toolset
  • Design features to keep our workflows and technology on the competitive edge

Требования

  • The ideal candidate would have 3+ years in an artistic role with a focus on visual storytelling either in games or animation
  • A strong sense of cameras, staging and storytelling
  • Interest in film and long form media
  • Dedication to growing an artist in parallel with growing as a lead.
  • Experience with 3D authorship

Будет плюсом

  • 1+ years experience leading or coordinating a team of artists
  • Experience in a game engine

Что мы предлагаем

  • An international team of over 500 employees spread across 7 studios around the world, each of which have their own unique qualities, and maintain a small-studio feel
  • An open, collaborative environment where ideas are welcome and encouraged
  • A managerial team consisting of highly experienced game developers at the top of their fields
  • Sharing hobbies and passions with like-minded colleagues
  • Inclusive environment with many activities organized by the studios or the employees
  • Commitment to making the best game possible for the fans, including listening to community feedback
