Thought Machine's mission is bold – to properly and permanently rid the world's banks of legacy technology. To achieve this, we have developed the foundations of modern banking through core and payments technology which run natively in the cloud. What we are attempting is hard and means we need great people working together to build great technology.

We have grown rapidly in the past few years – growing our team to more than 550 individuals across offices in London, New York, Singapore, Sydney and our newly established Engineering Hub in Lisbon. We have raised more than £500m in funding and our investors include Molten Ventures, Eurazeo, Intesa Sanpaolo, Temasek, Nyca Partners, JPMorgan Chase Strategic Investments, Standard Chartered Ventures, and more.

We have created a culture that enables our team to produce the best work in the industry while ensuring we have fun along the way. This is a full-time, permanent position based in our Lisbon office, requiring four days a week onsite.

At Thought Machine, IT stands for Internal Technology and we are responsible for ensuring our colleagues have the tools they need to be productive - whatever form that takes. We are looking for an enthusiastic Mac / Linux support engineer to be our first IT hire in Lisbon.

The Lisbon office is a brand new location for Thought Machine, and you will be responsible for supporting this new site, as well as joining our global support team supporting the company as a whole. We provide support primarily in-person, because we believe that this solves problems more quickly, and gives a great social experience at the same time. We have a Mac laptop fleet and Linux desktops, with a mix of on-premises and cloud infrastructure. As well as supporting the existing technology, we are looking for someone who believes in continuous improvement, spotting what we can do better and proactively tackling issues.

DUTIES

- Providing internal in-person support for Thought Machine employees in Lisbon, and remote support to employees in international offices

- Provisioning and deploying hardware for new and existing employees

- Managing the IT hardware stock. Provisioning and deprovisioning equipment.

- Contributing to infrastructure improvements with members of the wider department such as:

- OS and configuration managements updates

- Systems integration

- Network upgrades

- Scripting and tool development

- Checking and maintaining meeting room and other office IT equipment

- Supporting events for both employees and external visitors

- Partnering with the other members of the global IT team to provide seamless support

Требования

Essential

- 3-5 Years relevant experience

- Experience with Linux and Mac OS

- Experience troubleshooting hardware and software

- Solid understanding of networking concepts

- Excellent command of written and spoken English.

Desirable

- Experience in delivering technical support, including at executive level

- Experience working in a customer-facing role

- Familiarity with Mobile Device Management systems like JAMF Pro

- Experience with network troubleshooting

- Audio Visual experience, preferably Google Meet

- Google Workspace applications and administration

- Bash, Python, Golang

- Experience with infrastructure-as-code

- Experience with hardware or software procurement and inventory management
