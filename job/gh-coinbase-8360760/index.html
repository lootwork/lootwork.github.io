Coinbase Credit Risk is hiring a Director to lead credit risk for our institutional financing and lending business. Reporting to the Head of Credit Risk, you will own counterparty underwriting for funds and the risk frameworks behind cross margin, portfolio margin, exchange lending, and extended settlement products. Our product surface is expanding fast and you will build the risk frameworks that govern it.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the credit risk framework across institutional financing products: cross margin, portfolio margin, exchange lending, trade finance, and OTC extended settlement.
  • Shape rating methodology, underwriting standards, and risk appetite for funds; set and defend counterparty limits.
  • Build risk frameworks for new products from first principles, embedding margin, collateral, and liquidation logic at the design stage rather than after launch.
  • Partner with Engineering to productionize those frameworks across the credit risk platform — counterparty data, ratings, limits, exposure monitoring, and automated controls.
  • Own risk-relevant contractual terms with Legal across master loan agreements, security interest and pledge structures, derivatives documentation, and prime brokerage agreements.
  • Drive stress testing and potential exposure analysis, and actively manage single-name, sector, and collateral concentrations across the portfolio.
  • Influence executives across Institutional Financing, Product, Legal, and Finance, making final risk decisions on complex exposures and developing the leaders on your team.

Required Skills and Experience

  • 12+ years in credit risk, counterparty risk, or securities financing at a global financial institution, with depth in secured lending, prime brokerage, and/or derivatives.
  • Demonstrated ownership of a credit underwriting framework — rating methodology, limit setting, and surveillance — for funds and corporate counterparties.
  • Track record of building risk frameworks from scratch for structurally complex products, not only operating established ones.
  • Fluency in collateral mechanics, LTV structuring, margin methodology, potential future exposure, and wrong-way risk.
  • Experience negotiating risk terms with Legal and counterparties, and partnering with Engineering to productionize risk systems.
  • Able to negotiate with and influence senior leaders, and to make and defend final decisions under ambiguity.
  • Strong interest in crypto and digital asset markets.
  • Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.
