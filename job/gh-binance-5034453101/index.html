О роли

We are looking for a Global Compliance Head of Business Advisory to help shape, strengthen, and scale a high-performing global team focused on driving a culture of compliance across the enterprise.

This senior leadership role reports into the Chief Compliance Officer and is responsible for the design and execution of the organization's global compliance program strategic plan. This is a forward-looking, senior leadership role responsible for advising Business teams on Compliance matters during new product development processes and ensuring the Compliance organization is ready to support new products prior to launch, including adjusting the vendor strategy accordingly.

The ideal candidate is a strong compliance leader with strong technical skills who can combine regulatory awareness, program management, stakeholder engagement, and practical execution in a fast-moving environment. Experience in crypto, fintech or financial services is strongly preferred.

Что предстоит делать

Compliance Strategy

  • Develop and maintain a global compliance strategic plan to regularly focus and adapt the Compliance program, processes, tools and vendor infrastructure in support of business growth and user experience.
  • Prioritize quarterly roadmap of compliance tools and infrastructure initiatives in support of business initiatives, productivity gains and user experience enhancements.

Product Governance

  • Maintain policies and procedures governing the Assessment Approval Document (AAD) process applicable to all new products, overseeing adherence and driving automation;
  • Risk assess new token listings and perform ongoing due diligence on existing offerings;

Product Advisory

  • Act as a single point of contact for Product Managers on compliance related matters, including requirements and risks associated with new products.
  • Engage Compliance teams to gather function and jurisdiction specific requirements needed to support launch of new products and features as well as expansion of existing products into new geographies..
  • Manage relationships with partners’ compliance organizations, including risk assessing new relationships and managing due diligence exercises by existing partners.

Leadership and delivery

  • Build, lead, and develop a global, multi-jurisdiction team; set clear accountabilities and a strong quality culture.

Кого мы ищем

  • 12+ years of relevant experience in regulatory compliance, with at least 6+ years leading a Compliance advisory function at scale in a regulated financial institution or VASP.
  • Deep knowledge of global AML/KYC, sanctions related tools and technologies.
  • Engineering degree or extensive experience in product development is a plus.
  • Proven ability to influence at executive and board level.
  • Strong strategic thinking with the pragmatism to operate in a fast-moving, evolving environment.
  • Exceptional communication and stakeholder-management skills.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience managing global or multi-market compliance initiatives.
  • Proven ability to lead complex programs with multiple stakeholders, timelines, dependencies, and governance requirements.
  • Strong project management skills, including planning, execution, reporting, issue tracking, and remediation management.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to simplify complex topics for different audiences.
  • Strong stakeholder management skills, including working with senior leadership, regional teams, external vendors, and cross-functional partners.
  • Comfortable operating in a fast-paced, remote, global, and rapidly evolving environment
