Кто мы

О Opportunity

We're building the next generation of open platforms at the intersection of Web3 and AI — secure, open, and high-performance products and infrastructure spanning DeFi and the AI Agent ecosystem, designed to bring smarter Web3 experiences to users worldwide.

The team is focused on two core directions

  • Wallet Earn — A one-stop on-chain aggregated investment platform that lets users easily access leading DeFi protocols such as Aave, Morpho, Pendle, and Pancake, with seamless deposit, redemption, and yield management, continuously lowering the barrier to DeFi participation.
  • AI Agent Marketplace — An open marketplace and infrastructure layer for AI Agents, built around core primitives like Agent, Task, and Order. It enables agents to autonomously accept tasks, complete work, earn rewards, build reputation, and collaborate with other agents to create value.

OKX is part of OKG. Through OKX, OKX Wallet, OKX Pay, and OKLink, OKG delivers leading Web3 infrastructure and digital asset services to users globally.

Обязанности

  • Design, build, and continuously optimize the backend architecture of the Wallet Earn on-chain aggregated investment platform.
  • Design and build the aggregated-investment risk control platform, ensuring system stability, fund safety, and business scalability.
  • Continuously improve protocol integration capabilities, raising both new-protocol onboarding efficiency and overall R&D velocity.
  • Contribute to the multi-chain user asset/position platform, supporting asset parsing and display across major public chains and DeFi protocols; expand new protocols and asset types while keeping position data accurate, real-time, and efficient.
  • Contribute to the AI Agent Marketplace core platform, owning the architecture and development of core modules such as Agent, Task, and Order, and explore integration scenarios between AI Agents and Web3.
  • Own stability across core business flows to ensure high availability and high performance; independently diagnose and resolve complex production issues including performance bottlenecks, data consistency, fund safety, and edge-case business scenarios.
  • Continuously improve system architecture, code quality, monitoring/alerting, observability, and exception-compensation capabilities to strengthen reliability, security, and maintainability.

Требования

  • Solid Java fundamentals with strong command of mainstream frameworks such as Spring Boot and Spring Cloud.
  • Hands-on experience with common middleware including MySQL, Redis, MQ, and RPC.
  • Proven experience building high-concurrency, high-availability distributed systems.
  • Familiarity with the design and mechanics of major DeFi protocols (e.g., Aave, Morpho, Pendle, Pancake); understanding of smart contract fundamentals, with on-chain integration or contract development experience a plus.
  • Familiarity with mainstream wallet products and account models, and a working understanding of on-chain assets and the DeFi ecosystem.
  • Strong systems analysis and architecture design skills, ideally from large-scale consumer (To C) products or financial systems; able to independently own core-module design and drive complex projects to high-quality delivery.
  • Ability to independently analyze and resolve complex production issues, with experience in log analysis, distributed tracing, performance tuning, fault recovery, and data-consistency governance.
  • Strong sense of ownership and accountability, proactively driving problems and projects to resolution.
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills, comfortable in a fast-moving business environment; genuine interest in Web3, DeFi, or AI Agents and a drive to keep exploring frontier technology.

Nice-to-Haves

  • Familiarity with account models, transaction mechanics, and underlying principles of major public chains such as EVM, Solana, and Tron.
  • On-chain protocol development, smart contract development, or protocol integration experience.
