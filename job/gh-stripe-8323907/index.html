О команде

The Fraud Data Science team focuses on merchant fraud detection, account reviews, and interventions to protect Stripe and its users. We partner with Fraud Engineering and Risk Operations to turn data science into production systems and measurable improvements in fraud prevention. Our work includes evaluating how AI agents can support fraud investigations and review decisions while maintaining decision quality.

Что предстоит делать

We're looking for a Staff Data Scientist to help set the technical vision and roadmap for Fraud Data Science. This role combines hands-on technical leadership with cross-functional influence, with a particular focus on evaluating agentic approaches to merchant fraud detection and account reviews.

You'll define how we measure the quality and impact of these approaches, translate findings into technical priorities, and align partners on where and how to apply them. You'll bring scientific rigor and practical judgment to decisions that balance fraud prevention, operational efficiency, and the experience of legitimate users.

Обязанности

  • Shape the team's technical vision and roadmap across merchant fraud detection, account reviews, and interventions, identifying opportunities where data science can have the greatest impact.
  • Lead cross-functional initiatives with Fraud Engineering, Risk Operations, Product, and other partners, turning ambiguous fraud problems into clear technical plans and measurable outcomes.
  • Define evaluation frameworks for AI agents used in fraud investigations and account reviews, including evaluation datasets, quality metrics, offline testing, and online experiments.
  • Combine model and agent evaluations with reviewer feedback and business outcomes to identify failure modes, guide iteration, and inform deployment decisions.
  • Provide hands-on technical leadership in machine learning, statistical modeling, experimentation, and measurement, helping teams work through complex problems from research to production.
  • Develop approaches to measure the impact of fraud interventions, accounting for tradeoffs among fraud losses, review efficiency, and friction for legitimate users.
  • Translate technical findings into recommendations that influence priorities and investment across teams.
  • Raise standards for evaluation, reproducibility, and production monitoring, and mentor data scientists in their technical and professional development.
  • Lead complex projects from problem definition through modeling, implementation, and evaluation. Stay hands-on with analysis, code, and model development, and partner with engineering teams on production deployment.
  • Establish strong practices for model evaluation, monitoring, reproducibility, and analytical rigor.
  • Mentor data scientists and help peers grow their technical judgment, business understanding, and ability to deliver results.

Кто вы

We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • 10+ years of data science experience or equivalent combined industry and research experience in a quantitative field.
  • Bachelor's, master's, or PhD in a quantitative field, such as Statistics, Mathematics, Economics, Operations Research, Physical Sciences, or Engineering.
  • Experience applying data science or machine learning to fraud detection, risk decisioning, or closely related adversarial problems.
  • Demonstrated experience leading initiatives spanning multiple teams or using deep domain expertise to influence technical roadmap planning and execution.
  • Strong grounding in machine learning and statistics, with experience designing rigorous evaluations and translating results into production or business decisions.
  • Demonstrated ability to create alignment across technical and nontechnical stakeholders in ambiguous, complex situations and drive measurable business outcomes.
  • Ability to balance execution and velocity with research, statistical depth, and scalable design.
  • Proficiency in SQL and a computing language such as Python or R.
  • Proficiency with AI tools to accelerate model development, analysis, and coding.
  • Experience mentoring and investing in the development of peers.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with merchant fraud detection, account reviews, or fraud interventions in payments or financial services.
  • Experience evaluating or deploying systems powered by large language models, including AI agents, with attention to reliability, failure analysis, and human oversight.
  • Experience developing evaluation datasets and quality metrics for complex decisions where ground truth is incomplete or delayed.
  • Experience taking machine learning or statistical systems from initial research through production deployment, monitoring, and iteration.
  • Experience designing experiments or applying causal inference to measure the impact of risk policies, review processes, or interventions
