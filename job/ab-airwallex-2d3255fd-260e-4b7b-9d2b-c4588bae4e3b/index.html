О команде

The Regulatory & Compliance team helps Airwallex grow responsibly as a global financial platform. We work at the intersection of law, regulation, risk, and business execution, partnering closely with teams across the company to navigate complexity, enable growth, and protect the trust our customers, partners, and regulators place in us. It’s a team for people who are thoughtful, practical, and energized by solving hard problems in a fast-moving, highly regulated environment.

Что предстоит делать

As Manager, Regulatory Operations, ANZ, you will build and run the engine that turns complex regulatory and partner obligations into real, tested, and well‑evidenced controls across our ANZ‑licensed entities and products. You will sit within Regulatory Legal & Compliance and work closely with Regulatory Compliance, Regulatory Legal, Risk, Product, Operations, Finance, and external financial partners to strengthen a control environment that is practical, scalable, and regulator‑ready. This role is hands‑on and execution‑focused: you will not just interpret rules, you will design, implement, and continuously improve the controls, documentation, and reporting that demonstrate we are meeting our obligations day‑to‑day.

This role is based in Australia.

Обязанности

- Identify, interpret, and operationalize regulatory and partner obligations across ANZ jurisdictions, products, and licensed entities, and translate those obligations into clear governance, controls, and execution requirements.

- Manage the end‑to‑end design of key regulatory and partner controls through implementation, partnering with Product, Operations, Engineering, Risk, and Regulatory Compliance to embed controls into real workflows and systems.

- Build and maintain regulator and partner‑ready documentation for your control scope, including obligation mappings, control descriptions, RACIs, testing approaches, evidence standards, and remediation tracking.

- Engage with third parties such as regulators, auditors, and financial partners in connection with inquiries relating to controls, confidently explaining our obligations, our control architecture, and how specific controls operate in practice.

- Support regulatory exams, audits, inspections, RFIs, and related supervisory engagements in ANZ by coordinating responses, curating consistent evidence packages, maintaining “exam‑ready” documentation, and partnering with Regulatory Compliance and Regulatory Legal.

- Support the governance process for material changes to controls, ensuring that relevant obligation owners and stakeholders across Product, Operations, Engineering and Compliance are notified and aligned before changes to shared controls are implemented, and that the obligations register reflects the updated control design.

- Contribute to the design and operation of regional regulatory reporting and obligations governance, ensuring that what must be reported, by whom, when, and from which systems is clearly defined and appropriately controlled.

- Identify gaps, inconsistencies, or duplication in obligations, controls, and evidence across ANZ, and drive pragmatic remediation or uplift plans that balance regulatory expectations, partner requirements, and business impact.

- Use and contribute to the team's GRC tooling and compliance dashboards, maintaining accurate and current records of ANZ obligations, controls, evidence and open issues within the central system, and supporting the transition towards an AI Native GRC tooling.
