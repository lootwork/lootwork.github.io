О project

MyGrowth is a personal development app that makes continuous learning simple and engaging by offering daily micro-lessons, practical exercises, and habit-building activities that fit easily into a busy day. MyGrowth makes it easy to stay consistent, helping users acquire new skills, educate themselves, and grow their careers in just a few minutes per day.
