The Role: Docs Experience Engineering Team

We are looking for Junior Full-Stack Developers to join our Documentation team in Chicago. Built on the pillars of experience and experimentation, this team is designed to give you ownership over the Adyen developer experience. You will maintain a strict focus on the human element of documentation - implementing front-end UI updates, tackling technical debt, and experimenting with new workflows to directly free up the wider team for other strategic initiatives.

Что предстоит делать

  • Enhance the Developer Experience: Give the Adyen Docs site a fresh coat of paint, align it with corporate branding, and build interactive, developer-focused elements.
  • Tackle Technical Debt: Streamline our docs-as-code workflows and assist with Grav CMS integrations, clearing overhead so the wider team can move faster.
  • Experiment with Data & AI: You will find new, interesting ways to proactively enhance the user and agent experience. You will also experiment with AI workflows to improve documentation speed.
  • Formula Fit Execution: Champion the Adyen Formula by launching fast and iterating based on real-world feedback.

Кто вы

  • Educated & Ready to Build: You have a 4-year Computer Science degree and a solid foundational understanding of full-stack web development, including modern JavaScript, HTML/CSS, and server-side/web technologies.
  • AI-Forward, but Grounded: You know how to accelerate your work with AI tools, but you possess the engineering rigor to validate AI outputs, catch algorithmic mistakes, and maintain clean code.
