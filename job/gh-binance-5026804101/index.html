О Binance Accelerator Program

Binance Accelerator Program (BAP) is a 3-6 month internship program designed for Early Career talent to have firsthand experience in the rapidly expanding digital assets space. You will be given the opportunity to develop your skills at Binance and understand what it’s like to work at the world's leading blockchain ecosystem. As part of your internship in the BAP, there will also be opportunities for networking and development, which will expand your professional network and build transferable skills to propel you forward in your career. Learn about the BAP Program HERE.

Who May Apply

Current university students and recent graduates.

*Terms of employment/ engagement shall be subject to contract and local applicable laws.

О роли

Binance is looking for an intern in the creative, data-driven marketer to own content, brand voice, social media, and organic search across the Philippines and wider SEA. You'll be the local voice for one of the world's most recognised crypto brands — shaping how we show up on TikTok, X, and Google in one of our fastest-moving regions, and turning that presence into measurable audience and organic growth. This is a hands-on, high-visibility role with direct regional exposure: your work ships in-market within days, not quarters.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build localized content across social, campaigns, blogs, and marketing collateral — written to land with a PH audience, not translated at it.
  • Own our local social channels (Facebook, X, Instagram, TikTok and beyond) end to end: content calendars, campaigns, community engagement, and trend response.
  • Grow organic search performance through keyword research, on-page optimization, and content that ranks — coordinating with regional SEO teams on strategy.
  • Protect brand consistency, ensuring every asset carries the right message and visual identity across SEA markets.
  • Partner with regional marketing on SEA market campaign execution, branding activities, and product launches.
  • Measure and report on content, social, SEO, and campaign performance — turning the numbers into clear recommendations.

Кого мы ищем

  • Internship experiences in content marketing, social media, branding, or SEO — agency or in-house.
  • Bilingual English/Tagalog is required to be able to coordinate with overseas partners and stakeholders.
  • Hands-on experience running social platforms for a brand: calendar planning, publishing, and community engagement.
  • Comfort with data: you can read a performance report and decide what to change.

Будет плюсом

  • Working knowledge of SEO tools and Google Analytics, and what content optimization actually means in practice.
  • Interest in or exposure to crypto, blockchain, or Web3.
  • Prior experience across multiple SEA markets
