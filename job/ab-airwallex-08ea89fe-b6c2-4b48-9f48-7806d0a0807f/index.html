О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

As a core member of Airwallex Korea’s Outbound team, you'll create qualified sales opportunities across e-commerce and consumer brands—including K-beauty and cosmetics—manufacturing, trading, and digital technology. You'll own the early stages of the sales journey, from targeted prospecting and qualification through meeting arrangement, while turning marketing and event engagement into pipeline.

- Build a qualified outbound pipeline across priority industries by identifying target accounts and decision-makers.

- Start relevant conversations through tailored cold calls, cold emails, and consistent follow-up.

- Lead initial discovery, assess fit and timing, capture the key qualification details used in the ChAMP process, and arrange qualified meetings for Account Executives.

- Turn marketing, campaign, and event leads into qualified AE meetings through timely follow-up and clear next steps.

- Partner with Account Executives, Marketing, and the team lead to support events, improve targeting and outreach, deliver effective handovers, and keep Salesforce records accurate.

You'll work from our Korea office five days a week.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- 1+ years of experience in an SDR/BDR role, inside sales—including a closing role—or another high-volume customer-facing role.

- A demonstrated track record of success in outbound prospecting.

- Energy, persistence, and resilience: you maintain momentum through repeated outreach and bounce back quickly from rejection.

- Confidence initiating conversations by phone and email, with strong follow-through and time-management skills.

- Commercial curiosity, a willingness to learn quickly, and the ability to ask clear questions about a prospect’s business.

Preferred

- Experience prospecting in e-commerce or consumer brands, manufacturing, trading, or digital technology such as SaaS, AI, or gaming.

- Experience using Salesforce or another CRM, as well as sales engagement or prospecting tools.

- Familiarity with cross-border payments, FX, global accounts, or international business.

- Experience supporting marketing or industry events and following up with event leads.

YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF

- You have a track record of hitting your numbers, and you’re motivated by stretch goals.

- You treat setbacks as information. You get back up quickly.

- You default to action. You’d rather course-correct in motion than wait for perfect clarity.

- You communicate clearly and directly across cultures, and you're thoughtful about how context shifts meaning across borders.

- You value in-person collaboration and are happy being in the office five days a week.

Learn more about your team

SME & Growth at Airwallex is about more than closing deals. We help small and mid-market businesses solve real financial challenges across borders: getting paid globally, managing multi-currency accounts, paying suppliers, and running spend across markets from a single platform. You're not selling a feature. You're helping founders and finance leaders replace fragmented financial operations with one global system that actually scales.

It's a function for sellers who like to build, not follow a script: curious, resourceful, and comfortable figuring things out in markets that are still wide open. You get real autonomy to create value, build relationships, and move opportunities forward.

If you want to sell a serious product, solve real problems for ambitious businesses, and build your career fast, this is a place to do it.

Как выглядит успех в этой роли

- A consistent flow of qualified, SDR-sourced pipeline across our target industries.

- Outbound and marketing/event leads converted into qualified AE meetings through timely, relevant follow-up.

- Clear qualification, reliable CRM records, and effective handovers that help AEs progress opportunities.

Что предстоит делать

Airwallex Korea Outbound 팀의 핵심 구성원으로서 이커머스·컨슈머 브랜드(K-뷰티·화장품 포함), 제조업, 무역업, 디지털 테크 분야의 적격 세일즈 파이프라인을 직접 만듭니다. 타깃 아웃바운드로 잠재 고객을 발굴해 초기 상담과 적격화, 미팅 설정까지 맡고, 마케팅 및 이벤트 접점도 파이프라인으로 연결합니다. Account Executive, 마케팅팀, 팀 리드와 긴밀히 협업합니다.

Обязанности

- 우선 공략 업종의 타깃 기업과 의사결정자를 발굴하고, 적격 아웃바운드 파이프라인을 구축합니다.

- 맞춤형 콜드콜·콜드이메일 등 관련 채널로 잠재 고객에게 접근하고, 꾸준히 후속해 대화와 적격 미팅을 만듭니다.

- 초기 상담을 진행해 고객 적합성과 도입 시점을 파악하고, ChAMP에 필요한 핵심 정보를 기록합니다.

- 마케팅 채널·캠페인 및 이벤트 유입 리드에 후속해 관심도와 적합성을 확인하고, AE 미팅을 조율한 뒤 다음 단계를 기록합니다.

- 팀 리드와 마케팅팀의 이벤트 기획·준비·운영을 지원하며, 관계자 조율과 행사 당일 업무를 돕습니다.

- 적격 기회를 AE가 이어서 진행할 수 있도록 핵심 내용과 다음 단계를 명확히 전달합니다.

- Salesforce 정보를 정확히 관리하고, 타깃팅과 아웃리치 개선에 도움이 되는 잠재 고객 피드백을 공유합니다.
