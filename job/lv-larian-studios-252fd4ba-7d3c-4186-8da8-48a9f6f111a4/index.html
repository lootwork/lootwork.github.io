With multiple awards to our name, Larian Studios has proven that we’re dedicated to delivering high-quality role-playing games As we move on to new projects, the studio that brought you Baldur’s Gate 3 & Divinity: Original Sin 2 is looking for a Machine Learning Engineer to join the team and help us in crafting the high calibre games our fans have come to expect.

RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Explore opportunities for the application of ML techniques to optimize and improve our game engine, game creation pipelines, and ultimately enhance the player experience
  • Maintain and improve the current ML systems that are critical to our development pipelines
  • Educate all developers across the global studio in the domain and application of ML and associated technologies
  • Generate, gather and build a knowledge base of research and expertise in the domain and application of ML

REQUIREMENTS

  • At least 4 years of experience in Machine Learning Engineering
  • Proficient in python
  • Degree in a technical field such as Machine Learning, Computer Science, Mathematics, or equivalent practical experience
  • Passionate about AI, ML, and computational statistics
  • Open minded, willing to experiment, collaborate and iterate within a technical domain
  • Good written and spoken English
  • Enjoy coaching and mentoring
  • This is a full-time, on-site role, requiring your presence in the office to support collaboration and creativity

NICE TO HAVES

  • Experience in the gaming industry
  • Proficient in C++
  • Experience in ML Ops (docker, K8s) and Reinforcement Learning

WHAT WE OFFER

  • An international team of over 500 employees spread across 7 studios around the world, each of which have their own unique qualities, and maintain a small-studio feel
  • An open, collaborative environment where ideas are welcome and encouraged
  • A managerial team consisting of highly experienced game developers at the top of their fields
  • Sharing hobbies and passions with like-minded colleagues
  • Inclusive environment with many activities organized by the studios or the employees
  • Commitment to making the best game possible for the fans, including listening to community feedback
