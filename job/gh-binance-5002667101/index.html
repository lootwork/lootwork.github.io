We are building AI Agent systems for core use cases such as AI conversations and AI-powered trading. We are looking for an AI Agent R&D Engineer to develop a conversational agent platform and its underlying capabilities, including tool calling, memory, orchestration, and more. The goal is to transform large language model capabilities into stable, reliable, and continuously evolving product features that can be applied to real business scenarios..

Обязанности

  • Develop an enterprise-grade conversational agent platform to support core business scenarios such as AI search, token insight conversations, trading assistant Q&A, and trading strategy generation.
  • Design and implement multi-turn conversation capabilities, including contextual memory, tool calling and orchestration, clarification and follow-up questioning, and fallback handling, to create a natural and coherent conversational experience.
  • Build and optimize the large-model application pipeline, including prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), tool calling / function calling, and workflow orchestration.
  • Work closely with product, algorithm, data, and trading teams to deploy conversational AI capabilities into real business scenarios and continuously drive iterative improvements.
  • Establish quality monitoring and evaluation systems for production environments, continuously improving key metrics such as first-resolution rate, conversation completion rate, response latency, and user satisfaction.
  • Ensure system stability, security, and compliance, while continuously improving performance, availability, and observability under high-concurrency scenarios.

Требования

  • Master’s degree or above in Computer Science or a related field is preferred.
  • More than 2 years of backend development experience, with solid engineering skills in Java or Python and familiarity with common microservices architectures.
  • Hands-on experience in deploying large-model / AI applications, with a deep understanding of key technologies such as prompt engineering, RAG, vector retrieval, and function calling.
  • Strong complex system design capabilities, with familiarity in caching, message queues, databases, and service governance.
  • Strong interest in digital asset trading, market structure, on-chain data, and token-related information, with a willingness to deeply understand trader needs and behaviors.
  • Strong data analysis and troubleshooting skills, with the ability to drive continuous system improvement based on logs and metrics.
  • Strong cross-functional communication and project execution skills, with a results-oriented mindset.
