О роли

We’re looking for a Senior Sales Compensation Operations Analyst to independently lead complex commissions administration across multiple plans and sales populations. You’ll own end-to-end commission cycles, resolve complex operational issues, and strengthen the controls and processes behind accurate, timely payments.

As a senior individual contributor, you’ll partner with Finance, Payroll, Sales Operations, HR, and technical teams to drive improvements, support approved plan changes, and develop the team’s operational expertise. This role owns the administration of approved sales compensation plans, not sales compensation plan design.

Вам предстоит

  • Independently own end-to-end commission cycles across multiple plans and sales populations, managing timelines, dependencies, approvals, and validated payment submissions to Payroll.
  • Interpret and apply approved plan rules for participant eligibility, quotas, sales crediting, effective dates, and adjustments; oversee accurate plan assignments and participant changes.
  • Own complex reconciliations across sales transactions, commission calculations, adjustments, and payment totals, resolving discrepancies before payment submission.
  • Serve as an escalation point for complex commission inquiries, explain calculations and outcomes clearly, and coordinate resolution within approved policies. Document exceptions and secure required approvals.
  • Design and strengthen operational controls, review procedures, and audit documentation to improve accuracy, consistency, and traceability.
  • Lead root-cause analysis of recurring issues and use operational reporting to prioritize improvements that reduce errors, manual work, and payment risk.
  • Lead operational readiness and user acceptance testing for approved plan, process, and system changes, partnering with technical teams to define requirements, validate results, and resolve issues.
  • Mentor teammates, guide complex cases, and improve shared procedures and training materials to strengthen team capability and consistency.

You Have

  • 5+ years of experience in sales compensation, commissions administration, or related operations.
  • Demonstrated experience in sales commissions administration, including independent ownership of complex payment cycles across multiple plans or sales populations.
  • Strong spreadsheet and analytical skills, including advanced formulas, lookups, pivot tables, and reconciliation of large datasets.
  • Ability to interpret complex compensation plan rules, investigate discrepancies, and distinguish routine administration decisions from exceptions requiring approval.
  • Experience developing operational controls, supporting audits, and leading process improvements through implementation.
  • Experience translating business requirements into test scenarios, validating results, and assessing readiness for plan, process, or system changes.
  • Strong cross-functional communication and influencing skills, including explaining sensitive payment issues and resolving competing priorities.
  • Sound judgment, discretion with confidential compensation information, and the ability to manage ambiguity while maintaining accuracy and meeting deadlines.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience administering incentive compensation platforms, such as Pigment, Xactly, CaptivateIQ, or Spiff.
  • SQL skills for querying, validating, and analyzing commission-related data.

Technologies We Use and Teach

  • Pigment for ICM/quota issuance, Salesforce as the system of record & end user UI in Looker.
