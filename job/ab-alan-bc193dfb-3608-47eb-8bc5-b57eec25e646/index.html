🤖 THE SECURITY TEAM

🎯 MISSION

Engineers ship secure products, safely, and we catch and contain attacks fast.

- We protect our members. Their health data and their money are with us. Nobody reaches data they shouldn't see, and a stolen password doesn't open an account.

- We accelerate Alan. A new deal, country or product gets its security yes by configuration, not by a new project.

- We use the attacker's tools to defend. Attackers use AI, so do we, on our own code and our own infrastructure, every day.

🔭 SCOPE, IN THREE STREAMS

Prevent: make the secure path the default one.

1. Authentication and authorization: our Keycloak-based stack for members, health professionals and admins (SSO, MFA, passwordless next), and endpoints that deny by default.

2. Vulnerability management: every signal (scanners, pentests, researchers, AI) triaged and routed to the owning crew. Agents open the fixes, humans review.

3. Sensitive data and AI: end-to-end encryption, secure file exchange, and AI agents and MCPs that only get the permissions they need.

Defend: see an attack early and contain it fast.

1. Detection: signals on our cloud, apps and identities that are worth waking up for.

2. Response automation: an access kill switch, one-click host isolation, scripted response on our cloud, automatic isolation at night and on weekends.

3. Investigation: AI-assisted triage, and the runbooks and tooling everyone on the incident rotation relies on.

Attack: find our weaknesses before someone else does.

1. Test our own products and infrastructure like an attacker, AI first.

2. Simulate the attacks on the rise: prompt injection, account takeover, automated attacks on our routes.

3. Turn every finding into a guardrail or a detection, with our pentest partners and security researchers.

👀 FOCUS FOR 2027

Security is growing at Alan. Attacks get faster and cheaper with AI, and we answer the same way: by building. Big topics on the table: authorization secure by default, detection and response automation, and a vulnerability management system that fixes itself.

EXPERIENCE WE VALUE

- 3+ years in software engineering roles, shipping and operating production systems (rollouts, on-call, incidents), or earlier in your career with an outstanding interest in security to show (CTFs, bug bounties, security side projects)

- You've built systems, libraries or tooling other engineers rely on

- Hands-on security work in past teams: fixing vulnerabilities at scale, designing auth or crypto flows, building guardrails, CI security gates or detections

- A plus: incident response, detection engineering, pentest or red team, OAuth/OIDC and IAM, vulnerability management, LLM or agent security

MINDSET WE VALUE

- Engineer first: whatever the stream, you solve security problems by building systems, not by adding process.

- Security is product work: engineers and members are your customers, and security should feel effortless.

- Hands-on: code is the bulk of the job (Python, TypeScript, Terraform), and you operate what you ship.

- You design and communicate: a framing, a sequence diagram, aligning 3 crews on a migration, and making security tradeoffs legible to non-security engineers.

- Enabler: you measure your impact by how fast crews ship secure features without needing you.

- AI native: you use AI daily, and you're curious about how it breaks.

- You are fluent in English (French isn't required).…
