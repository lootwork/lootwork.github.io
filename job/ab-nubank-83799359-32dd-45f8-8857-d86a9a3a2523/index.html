О роли

REGEX (Registration Experience) owns the longest and most conversion-intensive stage of Nubank Mexico’s onboarding funnel: the ~30-screen Registration flow covering identity verification, CURP, biometrics, address, consents and contracts. This is the stage that turns a qualified prospect into a Nu customer, so every improvement moves our Onboarding Rate and customer releases.

As a Senior Product Manager in Nubank Mexico’s Growth organization, you will co-own the strategy, roadmap and results of this flow, with Onboarding Rate and Registration conversion as your core metrics. You’ll work day to day in a multi-disciplinary squad (Senior PM, Tech Manager, engineers, Business Analyst, Design and 2nd line of Defense: Legal, Risk, Compliance) and report into Growth Mexico product leadership.

You’ll be responsible for

- Co-owning the vision, strategy and roadmap of the Registration flow, with Onboarding Rate and Registration conversion as your core metrics.

- Finding friction across the ~30 screens through funnel data, experiments and customer research, then prioritizing and shipping fixes and personalization.

- Running A/B tests end to end: hypothesis, design, rollout and read-out.

- Leading the squad’s planning, rituals and delivery, and acting as squad lead when needed.

- Partnering with Risk, Compliance, Legal and Operations so changes meet regulatory requirements (for example, CNBV rules) without hurting conversion.

- Aligning with adjacent squads and stakeholders on scope, handovers and dependencies.

- Communicating progress, risks and learnings to leadership in weekly updates.

We are looking for a person who has

Required

- 5+ years of product management experience, ideally in fintech, banking or consumer digital products, owning a high-traffic funnel and its conversion outcomes.

- Hands-on experience with SQL and product analytics, turning data into prioritized decisions.

- Experience designing and reading experiments (A/B tests) and tying results to conversion outcomes.

- Experience leading through influence, aligning engineering, design, risk and business partners without formal authority.

- Balancing conversion, risk and regulatory constraints with strong customer judgment.

- Clear written and verbal communication in Spanish and English.

Будет плюсом

- Experience in onboarding, KYC or identity verification (biometrics, document validation).

- Familiarity with Mexican financial regulation and the Mexican market.

- Background in personalization or lifecycle messaging.

- Experience leading a squad and working closely with Technical/Engineering teams.
