In 2018, Bitvavo launched the first version of its digital assets trading platform, to bridge the gap between traditional currencies and digital assets. By offering transparent fees, a wide range of assets and an easy to use platform, Bitvavo is making the currency of the future accessible for everyone.

In two years' time, Bitvavo has established itself as the market leader in the Netherlands and one of the largest in Europe, with over one million users exchanging tens of billions of digital assets a year.

О роли

Bitvavo is building dedicated product management for Crypto Custody, the team that keeps customer crypto assets safe and enables earning yield. As Crypto Custody PM Lead, you will own product strategy for our crypto safeguarding and transfer rails, working closely with Engineering (Crypto Safeguarding and Transfers) and Operations to balance security, compliance, growth and revenue.

EMPLOYMENT SETUP

This role can be done fully remotely, or based in one of our preferred hubs: Amsterdam, London, Limassol or Vilnius.

Что предстоит делать

- Own the product roadmap for crypto wallet security, custody transfers and new asset listings

- Partner with Engineering to ship hot and cold wallet security improvements

- Drive the crypto yield roadmap, covering staking, lending, stablecoins and on-chain products, together with Operations

- Set strategy for withdrawal fees, network fee policy and node infrastructure

- Manage key vendor relationships, including custodians, validators and infrastructure providers

- Track AuC growth and yield KPIs in partnership with Data

- Represent Product in incident response for wallet and crypto rail security events

Кого мы ищем

This role requires crypto experience, gained at a crypto exchange, custody provider or similar crypto native business, combined with experience gained in the European market. We are not able to consider candidates without this background.

- 8+ years in product management, at least 5 of which should have been gained at a crypto exchange, custody provider or similar crypto native business

- Relevant experience in the European market is a must-have

- Strong technical grounding, comfortable discussing wallet security, blockchain fundamentals and infrastructure trade offs with engineers

- Track record balancing security and compliance requirements with growth and revenue goals

- Confident managing stakeholders across Engineering, Operations, Compliance, Risk, Security and Treasury

- Solid understanding of crypto fundamentals, with custody experience a strong plus

DESIRABLE

- Experience with staking, lending or on-chain yield products

- Experience with crypto custody providers or cold storage operations

- Background in traditional finance custody or asset servicing
