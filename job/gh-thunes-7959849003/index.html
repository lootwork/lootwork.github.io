О Thunes

Thunes is a global, fast-growing, and innovative Fintech scale-up that uses technology to disrupt and transform the existing financial system.

We have a strong Mission: to create a better global payments network, and a great Purpose: to connect the world to economic opportunities and address systemic inequality when it comes to financial services.

We are proud to power payments for the world’s fastest-growing businesses and work with some of the amazing global brands - from Gig Economy giants such as Uber and Deliveroo and Southeast Asia's super-app Grab, to global Fintech leaders such as PayPal and Remitly.

Our Products help to drastically simplify the Payment integration experience for our customers: with a single, simple connection, businesses and consumers can send payments to – and get paid in – every corner of the world. Instantly.

Thunes is headquartered in Singapore with regional offices in London, Paris, Barcelona, Shanghai, Miami, Dubai, and Nairobi.

Context of the role

Reporting to the Software Engineering Lead, you will be part of the engineering team who is responsible for developing our core business products and getting them to production in the most efficient and fastest way possible. You will be involved in architecture, tools and platforms to solve business needs and tools around internal processes automation to improve quality and performance of internal clients.

As part of the Engineering team, based in Europe, this role will bring you great opportunities to work on various projects, technologies, with a diverse range of teams.

Key responsibilities

Thunes infrastructure runs in AWS, we use Kubernetes and docker. For Treasury Automation our technical stack is: Python, FastAPI, Vue.js, postgres, redis, Prometheus + Grafana + OpenSearch.

As an engineer, you’ll be responsible for

  • Developing and enhancing treasury automation product
  • Building and improving time series models for forecasting cash flow
  • Debugging production issues related to forecasting, configuration, data flow
  • Ensuring reliability and uptime of the service
  • Improving engineering standards and holding a high bar for code quality and simplicity

Кого мы ищем

  • 4+ years of professional software development experience – you have worked on systems that are deployed in production environments
  • A strong grasp of computer science fundamentals when it comes to dealing with API, web and backend systems
  • A bias towards shipping early and iterating. We believe in making small incremental changes to existing system instead of large quarter releases
  • Experience with python and pytest
  • Math and statistical background, in particular experience with time series forecasting or eagerness to dig into it
  • Experience with Vue.js and jest
  • Experience in creating and optimizing complex SQL queriesSound like you?

Наш стек

Python, FastAPI, Golang, Vue.js, postgres, redis, Prometheus + Grafana + OpenSearch
