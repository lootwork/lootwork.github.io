The Team

The DBA team sits within Zopa’s Platform Tribe, alongside Cloud Infrastructure and DevX. We support the databases and data stores behind critical customer and engineering services, while evolving how those services are delivered.

This is not a traditional, server-only DBA role. We are automating database operations, strengthening cloud and infrastructure-as-code practices, and giving engineering teams clearer, safer ways to use database services.

The team is also progressing a major transition from Microsoft SQL Server towards PostgreSQL, creating meaningful technical change for someone who enjoys both depth and improvement.

A DAY IN THE LIFE

  • Support mission-critical PostgreSQL and AWS database services with strong operational discipline.
  • Automate repeatable DBA work using infrastructure-as-code, CI/CD and deployment pipelines.
  • Improve backup, restore, monitoring and replication practices.
  • Partner with Cloud Infrastructure colleagues on AWS, Terraform and platform patterns.
  • Help engineers self-serve database capabilities safely and with clear guardrails.
  • Contribute to the move away from Microsoft SQL Server towards PostgreSQL.
  • Join the shared on-call rota and continually reduce avoidable alerting and toil.

О вас

  • Practical AWS experience supporting mission-critical database services.
  • Strong PostgreSQL knowledge and experience with backup and restore.
  • Terraform and CI/CD experience, with a focus on automation.
  • Experience participating in on-call support for production services.
  • A database-engineering mindset combined with practical platform automation.
  • Ability to reason about reliability, resilience, security and operability.
  • Collaborative working style and confidence partnering with platform engineers.

ADDED BONUS

  • AWS certification.
  • Experience supporting MySQL or SQL Server instances.
  • Database automation and PostgreSQL replication experience.
  • Monitoring experience with Redgate Monitor, Honeycomb or comparable tools.
  • Pragmatic use of AI in day-to-day engineering work.

Our approach to AI in interviews

At Zopa, AI isn't something we're testing out — it's part of how we work every day. As a proud partner of Jobs 2030, we're committed to building AI fluency across our workforce, and we expect Zopians to use AI as part of how they do their jobs.

Because of that, we want to be transparent about how we think about AI use during our hiring process.

Behavioural and competency-based interviews: please don't use AI. These conversations are designed to understand you — your experiences, your judgment, and how you've approached real situations. An AI-generated answer can't tell us that. What it can do is get in the way of us finding out whether we're the right fit for each other.

Technical interviews: it depends on the role. Some technical stages actively welcome AI use, others don't. Your Talent Partner will let you know what's expected at each stage. Where AI is part of the assessment, we'll be interested not just in the outcome, but in how you used it – the tools you chose, your reasoning, and the decisions you made along the way.
