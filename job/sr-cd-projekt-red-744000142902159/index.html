Job Description

CD PROJEKT RED is looking for a motivated and experienced QA Analyst to join our RPG team in the Gameplay area of the The Witcher 4. This role is perfect for someone with a strong background in game quality assurance, a passion for narrative-driven RPGs, and an interest in working closely with multidisciplinary development teams to help shape gameplay from the earliest stages of production.

As an embedded member of the Gameplay team, you'll work alongside designers, programmers, artists and production to ensure gameplay systems meet the highest standards of quality, player experience and consistency. You'll identify quality risks early, help improve testing methodologies, and actively contribute to creating unforgettable gameplay experiences.

Please submit your CV in English. Candidates must demonstrate English proficiency at a minimum C1 level.

Обязанности

  • Collaborate closely with Gameplay designers, engineers, programmers, animators, technical designers, producers, leads/managers, and directors throughout the feature development lifecycle.
  • Execute manual testing of gameplay systems, mechanics, features and content while identifying, reproducing and documenting defects.
  • Develop and maintain gameplay-focused test cases, checklists and test documentation using risk-based testing methodologies.
  • Participate in feature reviews and planning sessions, identifying potential quality risks and proposing mitigation strategies before implementation.
  • Verify fixes, perform regression testing and evaluate the impact of gameplay changes across interconnected systems.
  • Analyze gameplay quality trends using defect data and test coverage metrics, helping improve overall testing effectiveness.
  • Support milestone verification by ensuring gameplay features meet quality expectations before release.
  • Maintain clear and organized documentation of testing activities, known issues and gameplay quality standards.
  • Work with developers to investigate complex gameplay issues, helping isolate root causes and communicate actionable feedback.
  • Continuously suggest improvements to QA workflows, documentation, testing practices and collaboration between QA and development teams.
  • Share gameplay knowledge with other QA team members and contribute to establishing consistent testing standards across the project.

Требования

  • 2+ years of experience in QA within game development, preferably on AAA titles.
  • Experience testing gameplay systems, mechanics or player-facing RPG features.
  • Strong understanding of gameplay design principles and player experience.
  • Experience creating and maintaining structured test cases and test plans.
  • Experience working with Jira, Xray or equivalent bug tracking and test management tools.
  • Hands-on experience using Unreal Engine editor for gameplay testing and debugging.
  • Strong analytical skills with the ability to investigate, isolate and clearly communicate complex issues.
  • Excellent communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively across multidisciplinary teams.
  • High attention to detail with a structured, risk-based approach to testing.
  • English proficiency.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience testing open-world or systemic gameplay.
  • Understanding mechanics of character progression, itemization, crafting, economy & resources, and statuses & resistances.
  • Basic knowledge of QA automation or scripting.
  • Experience testing on multiple platforms (PC and consoles).
  • Passion for story-driven RPGs and an understanding of what makes gameplay satisfying, responsive and immersive.
