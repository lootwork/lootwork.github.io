ABOUT VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

TEAM

You will work within the Growth team, in cooperation with Engineering & Data teams, enabling our Growth operations to move faster, smarter, and at significantly greater scale. As Lead Product Manager you will be integrated in the VGP Team.

VGP automates repetitive and high-volume growth tasks, starting with the distribution of over 25,000 creatives across multiple ad networks and campaigns per year, and continuously improves decision-making, performance monitoring, and playable ads production. The platform is evolving to integrate AI-driven campaign launches, bidding automation, and expanded network coverage.

ROLE

We’re looking for a Lead Product Manager to join our Growth team. In this role, you’ll build scalable automation systems at the intersection of Growth, AdTech, and AI. You will own the vision, roadmap, and delivery of the Voodoo Growth Platform (VGP), a strategic internal product that powers the automation of our user acquisition operations at scale.

You will

- Define and drive the long-term vision and roadmap of the Voodoo Growth Platform.

- Build scalable systems to automate campaign launches, creative uploads, and cross-network distribution.

- Lead the integration of AI-driven features to automate bidding strategies, campaign setup, and performance optimization.

- Partner closely with engineers and tech leads to design and deliver reliable, high-performance systems.

- Collaborate with Growth teams to deeply understand operational pain points and turn them into automation opportunities.

- Work with Product Managers across Voodoo to anticipate dependencies and align roadmaps.

- Improve data-driven decision-making by enhancing performance tracking, reporting, and experimentation frameworks within VGP.

- Ensure platform robustness, monitoring, and operational excellence

- Balance short-term operational efficiency gains with long-term platform scalability and technical excellence

- Foster a culture of automation-first thinking across the Growth operations

PROFILE

- Strong experience as a Product Manager in Growth, AdTech, automation platforms, or similarly technical environments

- Proven experience building internal tools or scalable B2B/B2B2C platforms

- Understanding of user acquisition ecosystems (ad networks, campaign structures, creatives, bidding strategies)

- Strong analytical mindset with the ability to translate performance data into product decisions.

- Experience working closely with engineering teams on complex technical roadmaps.

- Interest in AI-driven automation

- Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills.

- Comfortable operating in fast-paced, performance-driven environments.
