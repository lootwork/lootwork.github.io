О роли

We are looking for a driven Trading Operations Specialist (with 1-3 years experience) to join our Trading Operations Team. You’ll take your operational experience further - learning how a fast-moving fintech runs its post-trade infrastructure, contributing to how it runs and developing the expertise to improve it.

Что предстоит делать

- Gain in-depth knowledge of daily processes across the trade lifecycle - not just execute them, but actively improve them where necessary.

- Act as a subject matter point of contact within the Trading Operations domain.

- Investigate and resolve discrepancies with a structured, analytical approach, and document your findings in a way that prevents recurrence.

- Write and maintain SOPs and control documentation to a high standard.

- Engage directly with brokers, custodians, and internal teams across the firm, representing Trading Operations with confidence and clarity.

- Support senior team members on firm wide developments and strategic projects.

WHAT YOU NEED TO HAVE

- 1–3 years of experience in trading operations, settlements, or a closely related post-trade function.

- An undergraduate degree.

- Experience in trade/position reconciliations and break resolutions.

- A track record of not just completing tasks but questioning and improving the processes behind them.

- Strong analytical skills with the ability to cut through complexity and communicate conclusions clearly to both operational and non-operational audiences.

- An appetite for automation and tooling; you'd rather build a smarter process than repeat the same manual task indefinitely.

- Confidence to engage cross-functionally and challenge existing approaches constructively.

Будет плюсом

- Knowledge of settlement flows across multiple markets.

- Familiarity with custodian or broker platforms.

- Proficiency in SQL, Python, APIs and/or similar tools.
