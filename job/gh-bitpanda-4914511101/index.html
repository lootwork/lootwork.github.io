Your Mission

As our Expert, Treasury ALM, you will run the day-to-day engine of a multi-entity treasury function spanning traditional finance and digital assets. Reporting to the Manager of Treasury, you will manage liquidity and balance sheet operations across a multi-regulatory structure handling 12+ fiat currencies and 700+ digital assets. You will serve as a key subject matter expert bridging the gap between traditional banking frameworks and 24/7 crypto liquidity.

Что предстоит делать

  • ALM & Risk Execution: Manage duration and liquidity mismatches across traditional and crypto markets, monitoring the IRRBB framework and stress-testing the balance sheet against aggressive market pivots.
  • Digital Asset Management: Monitor live crypto risk incidents under pressure, measuring real-time exposures and instructing prompt corrective measures on long and short positions.
  • Financial Risk Contribution & Regulatory Reporting: Deliver strategic proposals to the Financial Risk Forum covering long-term investments, ALM netting arrangements, and MMF allocations, while contributing directly to prudential requirements delivery.
  • Technical Ownership & Collaboration: Drive treasury framework optimizations, refine analytical models, and act as an individual contributor subject matter expert in close collaboration with key internal stakeholders.
  • Data Integrity & Reconciliation: Own the quality of the data feeding treasury and ALM reporting, ensuring figures reconcile cleanly across source systems, and diagnosing and remediating weak or semi-manual data ingestion processes so that reporting is timely, accurate and audit-ready

Кто вы

  • 3+ Years of Core Treasury Experience: You bring at least 3 years of hands-on experience in corporate treasury or financial institutions, specifically managing complex asset-liability management (ALM) structures.
  • Dual-Asset Fluency: Deeply comfortable operating simultaneously in traditional finance (SWIFT, SEPA, physical/notional pooling) and digital asset markets (handling stablecoins and a broad spectrum of crypto assets).
  • Regulatory Mastery: Strong working knowledge of multi-entity regulatory frameworks, specifically MiCAR, EMI, and MiFID II, with direct experience handling capital compliance and risk appetite frameworks.
  • Data & System Savvy: Experienced in leveraging Treasury Management Systems (TMS), banking APIs, and blockchain analytics to maintain early-warning indicators like Hedging Efficiency Dashboards.
  • Fluent in English: Professional working proficiency in written and spoken English.
