At SumUp, we believe small merchants are the heartbeat of our communities. By joining the Payments BR tribe, you’ll help shape solutions that directly solve merchants’ everyday challenges and redefine how financial products empower entrepreneurship in Brazil.

As an Android Engineer, you'll be joining a growing squad at the centre of a highly cross functional environment: every change you make touches code, teams, and decisions owned across the wider SumUp organisation. You'll work side by side with engineers from other disciplines and tribes, help shape how the Brazilian merchant experience connects to our global platform, and have real influence over technical decisions from day one.

Что предстоит делать

  • Design merchant facing Pix flows: Build in-app experiences that help merchants clearly understand and manage their Pix transactions, automatic Pix, and Pix credit
  • Work across the SuperApp ecosystem: Coordinate changes with other tribes, including Bank, Lending, Online Payments, and Platform, to deliver features that touch shared code and shared surfaces
  • Contribute to technical decisions as an equal: Take part in architecture discussions and help the squad decide how to build and scale the Pix domain
  • Write clean, tested Android code: Develop native features using Kotlin and Java, following modular architecture and solid testing practices
  • Collaborate with the Mobile Chapter: Share knowledge, patterns, and lessons learned with other mobile engineers across SumUp
  • Learn from experienced engineers: Be mentored by senior peers as you grow into the domain and build lasting relationships with stakeholders across tribes

You'll be great for this role if…

  • Native development experience with Kotlin and Java
  • Experience with modular architecture, MVVM, SOLID principles, and REST APIs
  • Comfort working with unit testing and dependency injection
  • Curiosity and genuine enthusiasm for technology, with a habit of asking questions and digging into how things work
  • Ability to work well in a multicultural, cross functional environment and communicate clearly with English speaking colleagues
