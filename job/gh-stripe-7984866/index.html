Кто мы

О Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies — from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups — use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

О команде

Every day, businesses across the internet need to answer one question with confidence: is this person real? Stripe Identity answers it millions of times a month for some of the world's largest artificial intelligence (AI) companies, marketplaces, and platforms. As fraudsters turn to generative AI to fake faces and forge documents, we're building the AI that catches them, while real people breeze through.

Identity is a full-stack product, and we build every layer. That includes the verification experience consumers complete on web and mobile, the application programming interfaces (APIs) and Dashboard businesses use to run and review verifications, the software development kits (SDKs) that bring Identity into any app, and the machine learning (ML) foundations that tell a real face from a deepfake. You'll shape all of it, from the first tap on a phone to the model that makes the call.

Что предстоит делать

You'll help define how the internet verifies people. You'll work with world-class engineering, design, ML, and data science teams, and directly with some of the largest and fastest-growing businesses on Stripe. Together, you'll build verification that's effortless for good users and hard for fraudsters

Обязанности

  • Set the strategy and roadmap for new ways to verify people, from digital identity to emerging global standards.
  • Work across the full stack: the consumer verification experience, the business experience and APIs, the Dashboard, web and mobile SDKs, and the ML foundations that power every decision.
  • Build AI-powered defenses with ML and data science teams to stay ahead of new fraud, such as deepfakes and synthetic identities.
  • Make smart trade-offs between stopping fraud and keeping verification fast for legitimate users.
  • Shape pricing and packaging, and own adoption and revenue for new products.
  • Build flexible experiences that let platforms bring verification into their own products.
  • Partner with go-to-market teams to win and launch with some of Stripe's largest users.
  • Expand Identity's reach across countries.
  • Be the go-to partner for teams across Stripe building on Identity.

Будет плюсом
