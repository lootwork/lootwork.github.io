О роли

As AI profoundly transforms how engineering teams work, we're looking for Full Stack Engineers to be the "seeds" of this shift across our product teams — among the first dedicated full stack engineers, expected not only to deliver end-to-end yourself, but to take root within a team: bringing full stack and AI-augmented practices to the engineers around you, and helping the wider organization grow into a highly autonomous, end-to-end development model.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the end-to-end development of product features spanning frontend and backend.Integrate mainstream AI coding tools (e.g., Cursor, Copilot, Claude Code) as a core part of your daily workflow to maximize development efficiency.
  • Translate product requirements into working implementations with minimal handoff friction between frontend and backend.
  • Act as a full stack seed within your team — champion full stack practices, mentor peers, and help scale AI-augmented development across the organization.
  • Proactively identify efficiency bottlenecks in cross-team collaboration and propose improvements to product, design, and engineering processes.
  • Contribute across the full product lifecycle — from ideation and design to deployment and iteration.

Кого мы ищем

  • Bachelor's degree or above, CS or AI-related major preferred; 1–5 years of experience.
  • Highly proficient with mainstream AI coding tools (Cursor, Copilot, Claude Code, etc.), able to clearly articulate requirements and constraints to AI, and to review, validate, and correct AI-generated code.
  • Self-driven and comfortable operating without clear boundaries; able to turn vague ideas into clear specs and drive them forward.
  • Strong logical thinking and communication; a curious observer of other teams who proactively suggests improvements, and passionate about learning new technologies.
