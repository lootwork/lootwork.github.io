О нас

Riskified empowers businesses to unleash ecommerce growth by taking risk off the table. Many of the world’s biggest brands and publicly traded companies selling online rely on Riskified for guaranteed protection against chargebacks, to fight fraud and policy abuse at scale, and to improve customer retention. Developed and managed by the largest team of ecommerce risk analysts, data scientists and researchers, Riskified’s AI-powered fraud and risk intelligence platform analyzes the individual behind each interaction to provide real-time decisions and robust identity-based insights.

Riskified is proud to work with incredible companies in virtually all industries including Booking.com, Acer, Gucci, Lorna Jane, GoPro, and many more.

We thrive in a collaborative work setting, alongside great people, to build and enhance products that matter. Abundant opportunities to create and contribute provide us with a sense of purpose that extends beyond ourselves, leaving a lasting impact. These sentiments capture why we choose Riskified every day.

О роли

Riskified is building a new product-led growth motion designed to bring our technology to a much larger universe of ecommerce merchants.

The right person will be comfortable operating like a startup product manager inside a larger company. You should be able to identify an opportunity, develop a point of view, build something, launch it, learn from the results, and improve it without requiring a large team around you.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build the next generation of Riskified’s product led growth motion
  • Drive execution from strategy to production
  • Work with customers, resellers and other strategic ecosystem partners to understand their business needs and define creative solutions
  • Own the roadmap for your product area, while doing hands-on and end-to-end product management, working with developers and data scientists

Требования

  • 7 years as a PM at a product led company, including +2 years of experience working on PLG or PLS in B2B/SMB SaaS.
  • Enthusiastic about working with AI based tools and technologies including hands-on experience as a product builder.
  • Growth experience across acquisition, activation and engagement.
  • Strong analytical skills.
  • A hands-on execution mindset.
  • Fast learner with great interpersonal and communication skills
  • Ability to operate in ambiguity, building new solutions in collaboration with PMs leading multiple product areas across the portfolio
  • Experience with : eCommerce, FinTech, Online Payment.
  • Proven experience in the payment ecosystem working with PSPs/Orchestrators
  • English mother tongue level
  • Advantage: Fraud, Shopify experience
  • B.Sc. in Computer Science, Economics, Industrial Engineering or related degrees
