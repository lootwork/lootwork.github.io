Обязанности

  • Develop and implement engaging UI animations, including transitions, panel animations, and interactive visual effects.
  • Create gameplay animations that bring life to in-game interactions and environments.
  • Work closely with designers and developers to ensure animations are aligned with the game's vision.
  • Optimize animations for performance across a variety of mobile devices.
  • Prototype and implement visual effects to improve the overall user experience.
  • Collaborate with the team to ensure seamless integration of animations into the game.
