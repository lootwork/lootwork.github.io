Since we opened our doors in 2009, the world of commerce has evolved immensely, and so has Square. After enabling anyone to take payments and never miss a sale, we saw sellers stymied by disparate, outmoded products and tools that wouldn’t work together.

So we expanded into software and started building integrated, omnichannel solutions – to help sellers sell online, manage inventory, offer buy now, pay later functionality, book appointments, engage loyal buyers, and hire and pay staff. Across it all, we’ve embedded financial services tools at the point of sale, so merchants can access a business loan and manage their cash flow in one place. Afterpay furthers our goal to provide omnichannel tools that unlock meaningful value and growth, enabling sellers to capture the next generation shopper, increase order sizes, and compete at a larger scale.

Today, we are a partner to sellers of all sizes – large, enterprise-scale businesses with complex operations, sellers just starting, as well as merchants who began selling with Square and have grown larger over time. As our sellers grow, so do our solutions. There is a massive opportunity in front of us. We’re building a significant, meaningful, and lasting business, and we are helping sellers worldwide do the same.

О роли

We are looking for a quantitative, AI-fluent early-career professional to fill a data science role focused on payments data across Square and Cash App. You will partner closely with Product, Operations, Finance, and Engineering, leveraging data tools to optimize our payments platform from success rate, to cost efficiency, to risk mitigation. In addition to vertical work focused on the Payments domain, we also expect you to contribute to horizontal initiatives across the broader Data Org from data systems, to process and tooling.

Вам предстоит

  • Leverage AI to create scalable data tools, agents, and automations
  • Partner with Product, Engineering, Finance, and Operations teams to systemize and make data-driven decisions
  • Measure and optimize the cost and performance of our payments stack
  • Serve as a subject-matter expert on our payments business and implementation
  • Proactively identify gaps and opportunities in our processes
  • Design, run, and analyze experiments
  • Independently research questions and produce actionable insights
  • Build ETL pipelines, dashboards, and machine learning models
  • Translate open-ended business needs into practical data solutions
  • Design, implement, and maintain data systems and system architecture
  • Stand up, maintain, and improve cloud data infrastructure and tooling
  • Operationalize data processes and systems

You Have

  • A bachelor’s degree in a quantitative field with a strong academic record
  • Experience working with code at an internship
  • Experience working with large datasets using code, including SQL and Python
  • Experience using AI tools to accelerate development, improve data workflows, and automate processes
  • Strong analytical expertise and business acumen
  • Strong ability to communicate with non-technical partners
  • Knowledge of systems architecture and cloud infrastructure
  • Bonus: experience with payments or banking
