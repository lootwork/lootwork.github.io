Основные обязанности

  • Review existing security policies and procedures for logical completeness, consistency across provisions, and alignment with local regulatory requirements.
  • Guide and oversee business teams in completing Control Self-Assessments (CSAs).
  • Lead the annual security compliance audit for the core platform, including audit evidence collection, remediation tracking, and validation of remediation outcomes.
  • Support security certification initiatives, such as ISO 27701 and ISO 42001.
  • Undertake other security governance initiatives, including but not limited to:
  • Configure, manage, and review access permissions for information systems across the Group, ensuring that access assignments are compliant and appropriate.
  • Conduct regular reviews of system permissions and access controls to ensure access is granted only to appropriate personnel and roles.
  • Continuously improve access-management processes and policies to enhance efficiency and security.
  • Conduct periodic access-security risk assessments to identify and address potential risks.

Требования

  • Bachelor’s degree or above; a major in Computer Science, Information Security, Network Engineering, or a related field is preferred.
  • Three to five years of experience in system security auditing, access management, security governance, or information security within a large enterprise or corporate group.
  • Familiarity with common identity and access management tools, such as LDAP and Okta, as well as access-control policies.
  • Good knowledge of information security standards, regulations, and compliance requirements, such as ISO 27001, GDPR, SOC 2, and ISO 27701.
  • Fluency in English, with the ability to read and write technical documents and communicate effectively in English.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a collaborative mindset.
  • Excellent communication skills and experience working across departments.

Будет плюсом

  • Relevant information security certifications, such as CISSP, CISM, or CISA.
  • Experience managing access permissions on cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Tencent Cloud.
