Scopely is looking for a Lead Product Analyst to join our new unannounced project in Barcelona, Spain, on a hybrid basis, working 3 days a week from the hub.

Что предстоит делать

As a Lead Product Analyst, you will partner with a team of Analytical Engineers and product analysts, to support the game team. In this role, partnering with our internal data platform and data science teams, you will use data to generate player behaviour insights and improve feature performance; from data modelling and event tracking, through to AB-testing, insightful analyses and recommendations that answer the game’s most complex and challenging questions.

  • Data Analysis: Dive deep into large, complex data sets to extract actionable insights that drive strategic decision-making. Analyse player behaviour, game performance, and trends to inform product strategies and optimisations.
  • Analytics Roadmap: Collaborate with cross-functional teams to develop comprehensive analytics roadmaps tailored to specific game verticals. Define key metrics, establish data collection processes, and implement analytical frameworks to drive continuous improvement.
  • Ownership of Analysis: Take ownership of end-to-end analytical projects, from data gathering and processing to interpretation and presentation. Drive the execution of analytical initiatives with precision and attention to detail.
  • Cross-functional Collaboration: Work closely with teams across the organisation to translate data insights into impactful business outcomes.
  • Product Roadmap Influence: Partner to shape the future direction of the game.
  • Leverage data-driven insights to prioritise features, optimise game economies, and refine product roadmaps for maximum player engagement and satisfaction.
  • Presentation and Communication: Effectively communicate complex findings and recommendations to stakeholders at all levels of the organisation. Present insights in a clear, concise manner through compelling presentations and reports.

Кого мы ищем

  • 7+ years of experience as a Data Analyst or Business Intelligence-related role
  • Gaming industry background
  • Working knowledge of SQL and comfort working in large data sets – exposure to BigQuery and Python/R experience a plus
  • Working knowledge of visualization tools: Looker (Preferred), QlikView, Tableau or PowerBI.

Please ensure that the résumé/CV you attach is written in English.

О Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others.
