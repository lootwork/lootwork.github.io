О GRIFFIN

Hi, we’re Griffin. We’re a fully regulated UK bank that powers the accounts and payments offerings of product-led fintechs and platforms like Uber, Yonder, Prosper, and Sidekick. By doing the heavy lifting behind the scenes, our customers can focus on what they do best - delivering amazing customer experiences.

We’ve grown rapidly since our launch in 2024, and today provide the banking backbone behind products in remittance, payroll, wealth, insurance, proptech, payments, neobanking and more.

We’re moving into card issuing, stablecoin infrastructure, and agentic finance next as we continue to support the next generation of product-led companies.

Learn more about what we do https://griffin.com

OUR CULTURE

We believe that smart, motivated, conscientious people thrive in high-trust, high-autonomy environments and have done our best to create such an environment at Griffin. People who thrive here don’t require much hand-holding, and go out of their way to figure out how to contribute in the most valuable way possible.

We've been remote-first since we were established in 2017 and we remain so today. Work here is asynchronous and fully flexible - because we want to be a part of your life in a way that helps you to get the most out of both your work and home life.

We understand that our culture and ways of working aren’t for everyone, and we’re very intentional about our hiring process as a result. We won't hire people who don’t seem like a strong fit for our core values, even if they're otherwise extremely qualified.

Our strong culture has helped us to remain a mission-driven company as we have scaled, and gives our people a clear, explicit operating framework and the space to focus on delivering real value for our customers.

Learn more about our culture. https://griffin.com/culture

THE MARKETING TEAM

You'll be joining Griffin's Marketing team at a critical point in our growth. We sit within the wider Commercial function and work closely with Sales and Customer Success to drive lead generation, brand awareness, and deepen customer love.

THE OPPORTUNITY

We're looking for a Marketing Associate who thrives in the engine room. You're not afraid to get stuck in, take ownership and get your hands dirty across a wide range of marketing activity. You'll work hand-in-hand with the team to turn ideas into content, campaigns, and coverage — often within days, not months. This is a role where you'll touch every channel: copy, video, social, PR, events, and customer co-marketing, including managing relationships with C-level execs at our customer and partner companies. If you want to build breadth fast and see your work go live immediately, this is it.

Как выглядит успех в этой роли

Within 6 months, you'll be running the social media calendar with minimal oversight and managing customer comms end-to-end via our website and HubSpot. You'll be producing high-quality video, graphics, and copy that sound unmistakably like Griffin, and you'll have secured editorial coverage for our execs while submitting award entries strong enough to make several shortlists. By month 12, you'll own clear reporting on what's working, and you'll be fluent in what marketing in a regulated environment actually requires — from BCOBS and financial promotions rules to Consumer Duty — confidently handling customer and product marketing reviews as a result.

TO DO THIS YOU’LL OWN

- Content creation — writing copy across the business, managing social media, drafting customer comms (release notes, emails), producing basic visual assets, and editing short and long-form video

- Events and awards — identifying and managing our industry events calendar, coordinating speaker pitches, and running award submissions

- Customer co-marketing — spotting opportunities to market Griffin's product alongside our customers and managing those campaigns

- PR and thought leadership — supporting drafting, pitching, campaign and agency management, and reporting, while identifying and creating opportunities to position our execs as thought leaders

- Reporting — tracking, analysing, and managing marketing performance reports

- General marketing support — jumping in wherever the team and business need you most

WE EXPECT YOU TO SHAPE THIS ROLE

This description reflects what we need today. How you achieve these outcomes, where you focus your energy, how this role evolves—that's for you to define as you learn what our customers need and where our technical gaps are.

YOUR BACKGROUND

To ensure this, we're looking for people who have

- Strong copywriting skills, with the range to write across different formats and channels

- Basic graphic design and video editing skills, so you can produce quality content independently

- 2-3 years' experience in a B2B (preferred) or B2C marketing role

- A basic understanding of fintech or financial services

- A self-starter mindset — you can turn ambiguity into opportunity and manage your own time, deadlines, and projects

- A love of writing - we have a major amount of written documentation that you will be contributing to, and we place a high degree of importance on clarity and readability (you can find out more here about how we write https://griffin.com/how-we-write)

Ideally, you would also

- Have experience managing social media accounts

- Have supported PR activity, ideally within a fintech or regulated environment
