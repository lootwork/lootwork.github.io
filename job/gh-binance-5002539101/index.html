О роли

As part of BINANCE TH, a leading digital asset platform committed to advancing the adoption of blockchain and cryptocurrency in Thailand, we are seeking a highly creative and detail-oriented Graphic Designer Freelance to support our growing marketing and content initiatives.

In this role, you will be responsible for producing compelling visual assets across digital channels, including both static and motion content. You will work closely with cross-functional teams to translate campaign concepts into engaging visuals that align with BINANCE TH’s brand identity and global standards.

This position requires a strong balance of speed, creativity, and precision, with the ability to deliver high-quality work in a fast-paced, dynamic environment while maintaining strict adherence to brand guidelines.

This is a contract position for 6 months to 1 year.

This advertisement is for a position at Gulf Binance.

Обязанности

  • Design high-quality static visuals including key visuals (KV), social media content, and other marketing materials based on briefs.
  • Create simple animation videos (e.g. motion graphics, short-form content for Tiktok & YouTube Short) to support digital campaigns.
  • Support video content proofing, ensuring visual quality, consistency, and alignment with brand direction.
  • Collaborate closely with the marketing team to translate ideas into compelling visuals.
  • Ensure all designs are aligned with brand guidelines and campaign objectives.

Требования

  • Proven ability to work quickly and efficiently in a fast-paced environment.
  • Strong creative thinking with a good eye for layout, typography, and composition.
  • Solid understanding of brand guidelines and ability to apply them consistently.
  • Experience with design tools such as Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, etc.)
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines.
  • Experience in digital marketing, crypto, or tech-related industries
  • Basic understanding of video editing or motion design trends for social media
