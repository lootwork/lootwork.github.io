О роли

We are hiring a Product Marketing Lead to own product marketing for our overall Platform and Data portfolio: the infrastructure, identity, data and entity-resolution capabilities that power everything else we build, from real-time screening to agentic case remediation. The portfolio also includes our emerging agentic and AI capabilities. Data is one of our differentiators in the market, and this role will shape how we tell that story.

You will report to the VP of Product Marketing and help build a newly forming, growing Product Marketing team. You'll partner closely with the Product Directors who own Platform and Data, and with Data Science and Content Marketing, turning technical concepts into competitive narratives, original content and market presence. The work is as much about explaining how we're built and why that matters, to technical and non-technical buyers alike, as it is about any single feature launch. That includes the headless story: how customers and partners get value connecting to our data and platform without our UI, through APIs.

You'll also support co-marketing with the technology and identity-verification partners that integrate with our platform, alongside competitive intelligence shared across the wider Product Marketing team.

This is a hands-on, individual contributor role with real ownership from day one. We are growing quickly, with a large go-to-market team and a high volume of launches, so you'll help shape the role, with room to grow into more scope as the team and portfolio mature.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own product marketing for Platform & Data. You are responsible for positioning, messaging, launch execution and enablement for this portfolio.
  • Tell the data and platform story. Translate a technical roadmap, set by the VP Product and Product Directors, into a narrative that Sales, analysts, prospects and partners actually understand: why the platform and data layer matter, not just the products built on top of them.
  • Make technical concepts land. Explain how our data works, from entity resolution and our intelligence graph to how we ingest and classify risk data, so it resonates with compliance leaders, engineers and analysts alike.
  • Tell the headless story. Position the value of connecting to our data and platform without our UI, through APIs, for developer, data and technical buyers.
  • Drive original content. Work with Data Science, Product and Marketing to publish original research, benchmarks and technical thought leadership that makes our data advantage credible. You won't write it all, but you will drive it.
  • Support partner marketing. Support co-marketing with the technology and identity-verification partners that integrate with our platform.
  • Contribute to competitive intelligence. Help build competitive positioning against established providers and AI-native entrants.
  • Use AI to scale your own work. We expect Product Marketing to use AI-assisted workflows for research synthesis, competitive analysis, and first-draft positioning and content. This is how we work, not an aspiration.

Basic Qualifications

  • Minimum of seven(7) years of overall professional experience, including four (4) years in product marketing
  • Experience marketing a portfolio, not a single product
  • Experience marketing technical, data, API or platform products, with enough technical understanding to explain them credibly to engineers, data scientists and compliance buyers
  • A strong analytical mindset, comfortable working with data, metrics and technical concepts
  • Demonstrated ownership of a launch or major narrative or content project end to end (positioning, enablement and go-to-market) with general guidance rather than close oversight
  • Strong independence and maturity, with good judgment about when to run with something and when to escalate, and comfort helping shape a role in a new function
  • Ability to translate technical, infrastructure-level capability into narrative that both technical and non-technical audiences can use
  • Excellent written and verbal communication
  • Comfort operating in a regulated, technical B2B environment, turning product and engineering input into content a commercial audience can act on
  • Demonstrable use of AI tools to scale your own work: already working differently, not just interested in it
  • Must be legally authorized to work in the United States without need for current or future sponsorship

Будет плюсом

  • At least six (6) years in product marketing at a high-growth B2B SaaS company
  • Experience at a high-growth technology company, ideally one that scaled quickly while you were there
  • Early career in engineering, data analysis or product management
  • Experience in fintech, fraud, security, identity or risk-data markets
  • Experience marketing AI or agentic tools, platforms, or features
  • Experience marketing API-first, MCP, headless or developer-facing products
  • Experience helping produce original research, benchmarks or technical content with data science or engineering teams
  • Experience with partner marketing or co-marketing programs
