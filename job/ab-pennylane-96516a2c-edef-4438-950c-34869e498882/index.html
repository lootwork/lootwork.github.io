Mission

As our Localisation Designer, your mission is to drive Pennylane’s visual expansion into Germany and Spain. You won’t translate text but you will ensure our brand assets are culturally and visually adapted for each market. You will ensure our brand feels native, impactful, and pixel-perfect in every market.

Обязанности

- End-to-End Localisation & Cultural Adaptation: Own the transcreation and adaptation of French master creative assets (ads, product videos, social media content) for the German and Spanish markets. Ensure the tone, visual pacing, and design resonate with local audiences.

- Scalable Asset Versioning: Build and maintain smart, well-organized master templates to efficiently deploy multi-language, multi-format variations of our campaigns.

- Custom Localisation Asset: R.

- Video Production & Market Support: Act as the brand team’s visual bridge to regional marketing managers. Own post-production, localised graphics, and AI-enhanced editing for all international market launches.

Profile

- Experience: 2–5 years of experience in Brand Design, with a proven track record in non-french speaking campaign, or multi-market versioning.

- Localisation & AI Savvy: You understand the challenges of localisation (e.g., German text being 30% longer than French, cultural nuances). You’re comfortable with AI tools to automate (such as video dubbing and asset creation).

- International Mindset & Languages: French is mandatory and Professional English is required . Professional proficiency in German or Spanish is a strong plus, you need an eye for what “looks right” in these markets.

- Technical Stack: You’re prolific on Figma, Jitter, Adobe CC. Strong organizational skills (naming conventions, clean templates) are key when handling multi-market assets.

- Creative Pragmatism: You know when a localised asset requires a deeper cultural redesign and when AI can do the heavy lifting for a fast, automated rollout.

Recruitment Process

1. First conversation with Marie, Senior Talent Aquisition Manager - 30 min (remote)

2. Interview with Benjamin, Brand Design Manager - 30 min (remote)

3. Case study: Localisation & Scale Strategy (how to adapt a French product announcement for Germany), followed by a debrief with Benjamin, Lian (Marketing Manager Deutschland), and Maxime Baumard (Chief Marketing Officer) - 60 min (remote)

4. Final interview with Benjamin and Maxime (remote)
