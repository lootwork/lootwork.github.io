О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

You’ll define and drive the product growth strategy, roadmap, and execution across Airwallex’s portfolio products and the customer lifecycle.

We’re building the product capabilities, operating framework, and underlying platforms that help customers discover value faster, adopt more products, engage more deeply, and grow with Airwallex over time.

In this role, you’ll own the product growth agenda end to end: identifying the highest-leverage opportunities across acquisition, activation, retention, expansion, and monetization; turning them into a clear roadmap; and building the platforms and mechanisms that let us run campaigns, experiments, and lifecycle programs effectively at scale.

- Own product growth across Airwallex’s portfolio products and customer lifecycle, including activation, adoption, retention, expansion, and monetization.

- Define the growth strategy, operating model, prioritization framework, and roadmap across customer journeys, product surfaces, and shared growth platforms.

- Lead and develop a high-performing team of Product Managers, setting clear goals, raising the quality bar, and ensuring strong execution against measurable outcomes.

- Partner with Engineering, Data Science, Design, Marketing, Commercial, Operations, and regional teams to turn growth opportunities into scalable product experiences and systems.

- Build and evolve the underlying growth platforms and engines that power experimentation, targeting, recommendations, rewards, cross-sell, lifecycle orchestration, and campaign delivery.

- Establish the KPI framework, instrumentation, reporting cadence, and experimentation model needed to make fast, high-quality product decisions.

- Drive a high-velocity experimentation culture, from hypothesis generation and prioritization through launch, measurement, and iteration.

- Identify where AI, automation, and intelligence can materially improve growth outcomes, and apply them pragmatically in customer-facing journeys or internal growth tooling.

- Balance short-term wins with longer-term platform investments so the team can scale growth efforts efficiently across markets, products, and customer segments.

- Work across multiple regions and functions to ensure growth products and programs are commercially effective, operationally sound, and locally relevant.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- 8+ years of product management experience in high-growth technology companies, with meaningful experience in growth, lifecycle, product-led growth, and complex B2B product environments such as B2B SaaS, financial services, marketplaces, or multi-product platforms.

- Strong track record of owning product growth outcomes such as activation, conversion, retention, expansion, or revenue, ideally in B2B products where customer journeys, stakeholder needs, and implementation paths are more complex.

- Experience building growth strategies and translating them into clear roadmaps, prioritization decisions, operating rhythms, and measurable execution plans.

- Experience running rapid, hypothesis-driven experiments and using data to make high-quality product decisions.

- Strong analytical rigor, including experience working closely with metrics, dashboards, funnel analysis, cohort analysis, and experimentation results.

- Experience building or scaling the platforms, tools, or systems that enable campaigns, personalization, lifecycle orchestration, targeting, or growth experimentation.

- Strong product judgment and customer empathy, with the ability to connect user behavior, commercial opportunity, and product strategy.

- Proven ability to work cross-functionally across Product, Engineering, Design, Data Science, Marketing, Commercial, and Operations teams.

- Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to influence senior leaders and align teams around priorities and trade-offs.

- Experience leading or mentoring Product Managers.

Preferred

- Experience in fintech, payments, financial services, or other regulated environments where trust, compliance, and operational constraints shape product decisions.

- Experience across B2B and, where relevant, B2C or prosumer-style growth models, with strong instincts for navigating the complexity of B2B customer journeys, buying motions, stakeholder needs, and product adoption barriers.

- Experience with rewards, referral, recommendation, or lifecycle engagement systems.

- Experience applying AI, machine learning, or automation to improve personalization, targeting, support, or growth operations.

- Experience launching growth products or programs across multiple markets, regions, or languages.
