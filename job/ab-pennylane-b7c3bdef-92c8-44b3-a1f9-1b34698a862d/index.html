WHY this position is of utmost importance to reach our mission

While some companies count on marketing and sales to gain market share, we are a product-led company and we are investing primarily on building an innovative and scalable platform. Tech teams have a crucial role to play to achieve this goal.

👪 Team and context

You’ll work within a DevOps team currently composed of 4 engineers, reporting to the Engineering Manager.

You’ll collaborate closely with 300+ engineers to define and elevate performance, reliability and observability standards across the organization.

You’ll be embedded in multidisciplinary product teams (Frontend & Backend), working closely with another DevOps Engineer and an Application Security Engineer.

This is a high-impact role : you’re joining early and will help shape how reliability works at scale.

🏠 Offices

The company's HQ are in Paris, but the whole Tech team is remote-first. You are thus free to work from wherever you want in Europe.

🎯 Your Missions

- Evangelize and simplify SRE practices across our engineering organization (300+ engineers).

- Proactively identify reliability and performance improvement opportunities - not just react to incidents.

- Define and evolve performance, metrics and observability standards (SLIs/SLOs, monitoring frameworks, alerting strategies).

- Scale production resources efficiently while ensuring resilience and cost awareness.

- Design, implement and continuously improve tooling and monitoring systems.

- Contribute to engineering guidelines and best practices around reliability and production excellence.

- Collaborate closely with Product, Backend, Security and Platform stakeholders to embed reliability by design.

🥇 You're the right candidate if you have experience

- Working with cloud environments (we use AWS).

- Building and evolving observability and monitoring systems (e.g. Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog, Splunk or similar).

- Scaling production systems and web applications, including asynchronous job systems.

- Operating in high-growth or product-led environments.

- Influencing engineering teams and driving reliability practices at scale.
