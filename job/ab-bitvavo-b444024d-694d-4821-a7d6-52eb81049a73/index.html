In 2018, Bitvavo launched the first version of its digital assets trading platform, to bridge the gap between traditional currencies and digital assets. By offering transparent fees, a wide range of assets and an easy to use platform, Bitvavo is making the currency of the future accessible for everyone.

In eight years time, Bitvavo has established itself as the market leader in the Netherlands and one of the largest in Europe, with over two million users exchanging tens of billions of digital assets a year.

What is the role about

As a fast paced, highly targeted and highly regulated company, security is more important here than most other companies. That means your impact can be much more material, this role is about working with our brothers and sisters in product and engineering and building security through collaboration. It’s about being an engineer who knows how to use automation and tooling to get more done at pace and to scale security’s impact.

The role includes ‘whole of product’ security - Application, architecture and infrastructure. You’ll understand deeply the threats posed to us by threat actors and where to focus our attention, you’ll be the kind of person who enjoys building long lasting relationships, making impact by really moving the needle and scaling through automation so you are always focussed on the highest value work.

How will you make an impact

- Threat modelling: Taking an attackers lens on what’s being built and understanding how it could be abused/targeted and being able to bring others on the journey through clear communication.

- Pen testing/Red teaming/Vuln discovery: Being the threat actor but with a whitebox approach, thinking about what tools can you build to help scale these processes. Thinking about how we bring in more data to find the right issues and surface them with appropriate prioritisation.

- Communication and relationships: Bringing teams on side by working together with the attackers lens, collaboratively designing best of breed security controls that mitigate threads and support the business and our customers by making them as slick as possible.

- Going deep on existing MO’s and industry attack knowledge: Understanding deeply the attackers, their motivations and evolving techniques, performing evaluations of our controls to ensure they hold up, continuously iterating and educating.

How will you be successful?

- Backend/frontend development background with a transition into security engineering

- 5+ years of experience in secure software development and AppSec

- Demonstrable record of delivering real world security improvements

- Deep experience of scaling security through automation including AI and slick processes

- Experience with Kubernetes, containers and CI/CD

- Strong AWS and/or GCP experience

- Strong understanding of AuthN/AuthZ, API security, AI Security and OWASP Top 10

- Experience with tools such as Burp, Wiz, Snyk, Semgrep, Bug bounty and SAST platforms

- Experience in fintech, payments, crypto or other regulated industries is a big plus

- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to think like an attacker to anticipate and prevent security issues

- Strong Communication: Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, capable of presenting complex technical concepts to both technical and non-technical stakeholders

- Bug Bounty participation/Hall of fame/CVE IDs are a big plus

- Any security certifications are a plus (CISSP, CPTS, OSCP, AWS Security+)
