🚀 We’re on a mission to make money work for everyone.

We’re waving goodbye to the complicated and confusing ways of traditional banking.

After starting as a prepaid card, our product offering has grown a lot in the last 10 years in the UK. As well as personal and business bank accounts, we offer joint accounts, accounts for 16-17 year olds, a free kids account and credit cards in the UK, with more exciting things to come beyond. Our UK customers can also save, invest and combine their pensions with us.

With our hot coral cards and get-paid-early feature, combined with financial education on social media and our award winning customer service, we have a long history of creating magical moments for our customers!

We’re not about selling products - we want to solve problems and change lives through Monzo ❤️

📍London or Remote (UK) | 💰£78,000 - £100,000 + Benefits | 12 month fixed term contract

The Integrated Marketing Lead will develop and deliver marketing campaigns that support Monzo Business's growth ambitions. You'll bring together marketing channels, product launches and commercial partnerships to drive customer acquisition, engagement and business value, working closely with teams across Marketing and Business Banking.

This is a 12-month fixed-term contract.

⭐️ Capabilities

  • Cross-functional leadership: Ability to bring together Marketing centers of excellence, Product, Sales and commercial partners to deliver coordinated B2B campaigns and shared business outcomes.
  • Strategic thinking & B2B expertise: Strong understanding of business customer segments, buying behaviors and go-to-market approaches, with the ability to apply B2B marketing best practices to commercial opportunities.
  • Commercial acumen in marketing: Understanding of B2B marketing economics, customer acquisition, engagement and lifetime value, with the ability to prioritize investment and resources based on business impact.
  • Integrated campaign design: Expertise in developing and delivering multi-channel campaigns and product launches, connecting customer insight, proposition, messaging and channels across the buying journey.
  • Performance and measurement: Ability to use data and customer insights to evaluate marketing effectiveness, understand funnel performance, test new approaches and improve business outcomes.

🔑 What you’ll be doing

  • Develop and deliver marketing plans and campaigns that support Business Banking's commercial priorities and wider go-to-market strategy.
  • Own the planning and execution of priority campaigns, product launches and commercial partnerships, coordinating teams across Marketing, Product and the wider business.
  • Translate customer and market insights into effective audience strategies, channel plans and customer journeys.
  • Manage campaign budgets, timelines, resources and dependencies, making recommendations on where to focus effort and investment.
  • Work with commercial partners on joint marketing initiatives, coordinating plans, managing relationships and measuring results.
  • Monitor campaign performance, share results with stakeholders and identify opportunities to improve commercial outcomes.
  • Contribute B2B marketing expertise, new ideas and external best practices to strengthen our go-to-market approach.
  • Explore how AI and new technologies can improve campaign planning, execution and measurement, challenging existing ways of working.

🤩 You’ll love this role if you

  • Have a strong track record in B2B marketing, delivering campaigns and product launches targeting SMEs that have driven measurable commercial results.
  • Understand different B2B go-to-market approaches, including self-serve acquisition and how marketing works with Sales to reach and convert businesses.
  • Have experience working across Marketing, Product and commercial teams, including managing external partners and agencies.
  • Are comfortable managing budgets, using performance data and making decisions based on customer and commercial impact.
  • Bring fresh thinking and a strong point of view on what great B2B marketing looks like, with the curiosity to keep learning and improving.
  • Actively use AI in your work and enjoy experimenting with new tools and approaches to improve how marketing gets done.
  • Can balance strategic thinking with hands-on delivery, managing competing priorities and getting things done.

A bit about you

  • ​You think strategically, but you’re not afraid to get hands-on.
  • ​You understand how to prioritise when there’s a lot to juggle
  • ​You bring people together and know how to get them excited about the work.
  • You care about doing the right thing for customers and for the business.
  • You’re humble and curious, asking for help when you need it and you’re always looking for ways to do things better.
