Кто вы

We are currently working on Project ETHOS, a new free-to-play, 3rd person rogue-like hero shooter and an exciting evolution on the genre!

31st Union is looking for a Senior Online Systems Engineer to build the game-side systems that connect our title to its online services. You will implement and own application-layer features across matchmaking, social, meta-progression, and telemetry, working in Unreal Engine against our hosted service suite.

Обязанности

  • Design and implement application-side online features: matchmaking flows, social features (parties, presence, friends), meta-progression, and telemetry instrumentation.
  • Integrate hosted services into the game client and dedicated server in Unreal Engine.
  • Make online features resilient to service degradation, latency, and platform outages.
  • Partner with backend engineers on API design and service contracts.
  • Partner with designers to factor feature requirements into implementable systems.
  • Debug and analyze issues in scenarios where multiple clients, servers, and services interact.
  • Ensure correct behavior across platforms, including platform social and privilege constraints.

Required Qualifications

  • 5+ years of programming experience with strong C++ fluency, complemented by a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent experience.
  • Production experience with Unreal Engine.
  • Shipped at least one AAA title, preferably competitive multiplayer or live-service.
  • Experience implementing online features against hosted services.
  • Strong understanding of asynchronous programming and client/server architecture.
  • Excellent communication skills and effective collaboration with design, backend, and platform teams.

Relevant Experience

  • Unreal Engine Online Subsystem and platform SDKs (PlayStation, Xbox, Steam, EOS).
  • Matchmaking, skill rating, or session management systems.
  • Telemetry pipelines and analytics instrumentation.
  • Commerce, entitlements, or progression services.
