⭐Our Risk & Compliance team

We’re looking for a pragmatic, collaborative and experienced Head of Compliance Advisory to join our second line of defence (2LoD) Compliance Team as part of our Risk and Compliance Collective.

The role is a 12 month fixed term contract providing interim cover for our current Head of Compliance Advisory - Payments and Mobile. This role will lead a team of compliance advisors, overseeing the day-to-day delivery of compliance advice and providing advice directly across Monzo’s Payments Collective (including: Payment Services, Cards, Acquiring, Open Banking, International Transactions, Multi-Currency Accounts, Agentic Payments, Digital Assets and Stablecoin) and Monzo Mobile. The role reports directly to Monzo’s Senior Director of Compliance.

This role will partner directly with our Product teams to ensure we fulfil our Consumer Duty requirements, launch products safely and meet our regulatory compliance obligations. This will require significant experience of advising on FCA requirements, deep technical expertise in retail banking and payments landscape and requirements, and the ability to understand, interpret new or unfamiliar regulatory topics.

We’re looking for someone (maybe you?!) to lead the team in delivering insightful advice in partnership with our 1st line colleagues. You’ll be taking a hands-on leadership role, managing a team of second line compliance advice managers.

🔑 You’ll play a key role by…

  • Leading, coaching and supporting a team of Compliance Advisory managers, helping them prioritise their work and deliver clear, high-quality advice.
  • Partnering with Product teams to advise on existing and new products, changes and customer journeys across Payments and Mobile.
  • Providing technical advice on the FCA Handbook, including PRIN, BCOBS, DISP, COBS and PERG where relevant, as well as the Payment Services Regulations 2017 and other applicable requirements.
  • Advising on payment services and infrastructure, cards, acquiring, open banking, international payments, foreign exchange and emerging digital asset propositions.
  • Supporting the development of Monzo Mobile, working with colleagues to identify and address relevant regulatory requirements and customer risks as the proposition grows.
  • Providing compliance advice on financial promotions, working with Marketing and Financial Promotions teams.
  • Helping Monzo continue to embed the Consumer Duty in product design, communications, monitoring and customer support.
  • Providing independent second-line advice and constructive challenge, including escalating material compliance risks where appropriate.
  • Supporting the response to significant incidents and regulatory breaches, including advice on conduct implications, customer outcomes and regulatory notification.
  • Building effective relationships across Compliance, first-line Risk, Legal and Audit to support joined-up oversight.
  • Contributing to the strategy and direction of Compliance Advisory, including the development and challenge of relevant policies and procedures.
  • Promoting a strong compliance culture through training, coaching and practical guidance.
  • Exploring ways to streamline, automate and improve how we deliver compliance advice and manage conduct risk.

🤩 We’d love to hear from you if you…

  • Have significant practical experience providing compliance advice in payments and retail banking.
  • Have a strong technical grounding in the FCA Handbook, the Payment Services Regulations 2017 and other relevant payments requirements.
  • Understand payment systems and infrastructure, cards, acquiring and merchant services, international payments, foreign exchange, open banking and payment security.
  • Can apply regulatory requirements to real products and customer journeys, including issues involving fraud, scams, payment disputes and customer outcomes.
  • Can interpret complex or evolving requirements and turn them into clear, practical advice.
  • Are an effective leader and people manager who can coach and develop a high-performing team.
  • Can think strategically while working in the detail with your team when needed.
  • Build strong relationships with stakeholders at all levels and can provide effective, respectful challenge.
  • Communicate clearly in writing and in conversation, adapting your approach for different audiences.
  • Are excited by technology and what we’re building at Monzo.

It would also be beneficial if you have

  • Knowledge or experience of cryptoassets, digital assets or stablecoins, including the regulatory perimeter and the developing UK regime.
  • Experience of advising on telecommunications or other non-financial regulated products, particularly knowledge of the UK telecommunications framework and Ofcom’s General Conditions of Entitlement.
