О Binance Accelerator Program

Binance Accelerator Program is a concise fixed-term program designed for Early Career Talent to have an immersive experience in the rapidly expanding Web3 space. You will be given the opportunity to experience life at Binance and understand what goes on behind the scenes of the worlds’ leading blockchain ecosystem. Alongside your job, there will also be a focus on networking and development, which will expand your professional network and build transferable skills to propel you forward in your career.

Learn about BAP Program HERE

Who may apply

Current university students and recent graduates

Обязанности

We are looking for someone who is careful, detail-oriented, and interested in LLMs and AI Agents.

What you will work on

You will support AI-related projects in compliance and risk control areas, including:

  • KYC (Know Your Customer): helping improve processes for identity verification, document review, and user onboarding
  • KYB (Know Your Business): supporting business entity verification and related due diligence workflows
  • EDD (Enhanced Due Diligence): assisting with more complex review cases, risk signals, and investigation support
  • Helping test and optimize LLM/Agent-based workflows for compliance operations
  • Reviewing prompts, automation logic, and model outputs
  • Documenting findings, edge cases, and process improvements
  • Supporting small-scale implementation, evaluation, and operational analysis tasks

Требования

  • Undergraduate or postgraduate background in business data analytics, computer science or a related field.
  • Some hands-on experience with LLMs, prompt engineering, or AI Agents
  • Basic coding or scripting ability is a plus
  • Interest in compliance, risk control, or operational efficiency is a plusIf you are reliable, practical, and excited about applying AI to real business workflows
  • Responsible and patient in repetitive or process-heavy work
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in Mandarin to be able to coordinate with internal and external stakeholders.
  • Capable of creating clear documentation and presentations.
  • Strong sense of ownership, with a collaborative, hands-on approach to problem-solving.
  • Ability to work effectively in cross-functional teams and adapt to a fast-paced, evolving environment.
