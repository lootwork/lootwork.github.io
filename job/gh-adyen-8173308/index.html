Senior Product Manager - Revenue Optimization

Adyen, a leading payment processor, is expanding its ambition to influence purchases and power the agentic commerce revolution. This requires a deep understanding of customer behavior, powered by billions of data points and leveraging state-of-the-art machine learning capabilities. As a Product Manager within the Revenue Optimization team, you will oversee this data platform's capabilities and our initiatives to provide a worldclass, high performing experience for agent-initiated searches and payments.

What you'll do, both for the Customer Data Platform and Agentic Commerce:

  • Own the strategy and roadmap
  • Define and track success metrics
  • Partner with teams across the organization at large
  • Engage with customers and partners
  • Adapt and prioritize as targets move and resources evolve

Кто вы

  • An experienced product leader with 8 + years in product management or adjascent functions, ideally within a fintech environment
  • Fluent with data, with the ability to access, manipulate, and analyze large corpuses of information on their own
  • Deep understanding of AI harnesses, and API best practices.
  • Comfortable driving deep technical discussions with engineers and strategic alignments with stakeholders across varying disciplines and cultures.
  • Knowledge of tokenization and crypto is a plus
  • Excellent and concise communicator, both in writing and in presentations
  • Hold strong opinions, loosely, and keen to have their mind changed by evidence
  • Naturally curious, data - driven analytical Problem Solver, comfortable with ambiguity
