О Business Banking at Monzo

Since its launch five years ago, Monzo Business Banking has been dedicated to delivering the very best of Monzo to small businesses. Today, it's an award-winning service, empowering over 1 million businesses across the UK.

As Monzo expands across Europe, Business Banking is entering its next phase: adapting our products and proposition for small businesses in new markets, while continuing our mission to make money work for businesses.

О роли

We’re looking for several Senior PMMs to own Product Marketing for entire products and experiences.

A useful distinction - at Monzo, Product Marketing starts upstream in product-market fit. We're embedded in squads with Product Managers, Data Scientists, Designers, User Researchers, and Sales, bringing the voice of the customer and market into what we build, how we position it, and how we take it to market. We own customer and market understanding, positioning, proposition, launch strategy, and sales enablement. We then partner closely with Integrated Marketing, who lead campaign strategy, creative direction, channel execution, and growth. Together, we build the right products and help them succeed in market.

This is a senior individual contributor role. You'll own Product Marketing for a product end-to-end: understanding customers, shaping product strategy, defining positioning, and helping bring launches to market.

Where you could join

  • Business Banking EU. Business Banking is entering its next phase of growth as we expand beyond the UK. In this role, you'll define how Monzo Business Banking succeeds in new European markets - understanding local customer needs, building the proposition that translates our core product into local relevance, and working across Product, Growth, Marketing, and local teams to bring those products to market. This is a senior role for someone with high autonomy who loves turning dense customer and market insight into practical decisions about what should launch.
  • Business Banking. Business Banking is growing quickly, serving more than one million businesses across the UK while expanding into increasingly sophisticated customer needs. In this role, you'll shape how our products are positioned, sold, and adopted—from defining compelling product narratives to enabling Sales and partnering with Growth and Marketing on launches. This is a role for someone who enjoys sitting at the intersection of Product and commercial teams, translating customer insight into positioning, propositions, and go-to-market strategies that help more businesses choose Monzo.
