Что предстоит делать

Project scope/overview

The intern will design, develop, and deploy production-ready capabilities for Airwallex’s Spend Cards and Issuing platform. The project may span card lifecycle management, spend controls, transaction visibility, APIs, or customer-facing card experiences..

Detailed Outline

Technical tasks/deliverables

1. Technical design scoping: Create workflow diagrams, identify dependencies, expected inputs and outputs, and define success criteria (e.g., latency, accuracy, cost).

2. Feature development: Build and integrate production-ready Cards and Issuing capabilities across backend, APIs, and frontend where needed.

3. Testing & observability: Add automated testing and set up observability dashboards with logging, metrics, and alerts to monitor performance and reliability.

4. Deployment: Deploy the completed capability to production and validate it with real users.

Project Milestones

1. Technical design sign-off: Finalise workflow diagrams, success criteria, and technical design documentation.

2. MVP development: Build Cards or Issuing capability and validate key flows.

3. Production readiness: Complete testing, observability, and integration requirements.

4. Deployment and showcase: Release the capability and present the outcome to the wider Engineering team.

Expected learning

- Card issuing concepts, including card lifecycle, spend controls, and transactions

- API and distributed systems design

- Production engineering including testing, observability, and reliability

- Frontend for customer-facing experiences

- Building within a global fintech platform

Planned role & responsibilities

- Actively participate in team standups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives to align with engineering priorities.

- Collaborate with cross-functional partners (e.g., Operations, Finance, Infosec) to understand workflow requirements and validate outputs.

- Contribute to code reviews, testing, and documentation.

- Deliver bi-weekly project updates and participate in mid-point and final showcases to demo project.

- Design, develop, and deploy an assigned Cards or Issuing project under mentor guidance.

Outline of onboarding process steps in team

- Buddy assignment: Pair with a senior engineer/mentor for end-to-end feature lifecycle walkthrough, and weekly technical check-ins.

- Tools orientation: Setup access to relevant AI and development tools

- Domain familiarization: Walkthrough of identified finance operational workflows (e.g., cash reconciliation, PO approvals) with relevant domain experts

Proposed schedule of Review Check-ins

- Monthly check-in for progress review and feedback sharing

- Final Review: at 24 weeks (End-of-project showcase)
