О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 676,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,300 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. ABOUT THE ROLE...

As Partnerships Director, France & Spain, you will lead and develop a team of four partnership managers:

- Two Accounting Partnerships Managers

- One Venture Capital Partnerships Manager

- One Travel & E-commerce Partnerships Manager

You will own the partnerships strategy and commercial performance across both markets, building a channel-led growth engine that generates qualified introductions, partner-sourced pipeline and net-new business for Airwallex.

This is a strategic, hands-on player-coach role. You will set direction, develop the team and build the operating model, while personally sourcing, negotiating, activating and growing strategic partnerships.

The role is focused on commercial partnerships and lead generation—not technical integrations. The initial priority is to build the Accounting and Travel & E-commerce channels across France and Spain. The broader scope includes Payments and Venture Capital and may evolve with business priorities.

The role is based in either France or Spain, with a minimum of three days per week in the relevant Airwallex office.

WHAT YOU’LL DO...

- Own the France and Spain partnerships strategy, aligned with the SME & Growth GTM.

- Set partner ICPs, commercial priorities, account plans, pipeline expectations and operating standards across the team.

- Lead, coach and develop the four partnership managers.

- Personally source and progress a portfolio of strategic partnerships in at least one priority vertical.

- Build repeatable referral, reseller, co-selling and partner-led outbound motions across Accounting, Travel, Payments, E-commerce and VC.

- Ensure signed partnerships become active commercial channels, not passive logos.

- Work with Sales on account mapping, lead routing, qualification, follow-up and conversion of partner-sourced opportunities.

- Track active partners, referrals, opportunities, sourced and influenced pipeline, conversion, time to first referral and revenue contribution.

- Test partner segments and commercial motions; scale what creates quality pipeline and stop what does not.

- Work cross-functionally with Marketing, RevOps, Product, Solutions, Risk and Compliance to remove friction from the partner journey.

WHAT YOU’LL BRING...

- 8+ years in partnerships, channel sales, indirect sales, partner sales, strategic business development or a comparable commercial role.

- A successful 0-to-1 partnership motion or vertical launch in Accounting or Travel & E-commerce, with measurable commercial outcomes.

- Strong channel-led execution and a personal track record of partner-sourced referrals, pipeline, opportunities or revenue.

- Commercial experience across at least two of these verticals: Accounting, Travel, Payments, E-commerce or Venture Capital.

- A deep, activatable network in at least one priority vertical, with access to senior decision-makers.

- Strong strategic judgement and hands-on execution: you can define the strategy and personally open the first doors, lead key meetings and progress strategic partnerships.

- Experience designing partner ICPs, activation motions, attribution and channel performance management.

- Experience leading, coaching or developing partnership professionals is preferred. Formal people-management experience is valuable, but not the only indicator of leadership potential.

- Experience in accounting, payments, fintech, ERP, financial software or another competitive B2B environment.

- Professional English plus native or bilingual French or Spanish. Native or bilingual proficiency in the other local language is a strong advantage, but not required.

- Comfortable with CRM, funnel metrics, partner economics and basic business cases.

WHO YOU ARE...

- Strategic and hands-on: you can set direction while staying close to partners, pipeline and commercial details.

- Channel-led: you care about net-new business and revenue, not only signed agreements or ecosystem visibility.

- A builder: you create structure from zero, test quickly and learn without waiting for a perfect playbook.

- Commercially accountable: you use data to decide where to invest, coach or stop.

- A player-coach: you develop people while continuing to win strategic partnerships yourself.

WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE...

- The four partnership managers have clear mandates, partner ICPs, account priorities and measurable pipeline objectives.

- Accounting and Travel & E-commerce have prioritised partner strategies and active channel motions in France and Spain.

- Partner cohorts are recruited, activated and connected to repeatable referral, reseller or co-selling motions.

- You personally own strategic partnerships that contribute materially to Airwallex’s growth.

- Partner-sourced and partner-influenced pipeline is accurately tracked and converted with clear Sales ownership.…
