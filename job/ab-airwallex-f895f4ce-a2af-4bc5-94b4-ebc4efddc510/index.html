О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

As part of our Risk Operations team, you’ll strengthen Airwallex’s global risk control strategy, lead a high-performing APAC merchant risk operations team, and ensure portfolio risk stays aligned with the enterprise risk appetite. You’ll own end-to-end operational performance across APAC merchant risk operations, including service levels, quality, loss metrics, and customer impact. You’ll improve merchant monitoring, investigation, escalation, tooling, automation, and AI-enabled workflows while partnering closely with Risk, Compliance, Legal, Commercial, Product, and Engineering. You’ll drive key performance metrics, including fraud and chargeback ratios, to support company objectives and continuously raise the standard of operational excellence.

- Lead the APAC Merchant Risk Operations team for Payments.

- Own end-to-end operational performance for APAC merchant risk operations, including service levels, quality, loss metrics, and customer impact.

- Oversee the design and execution of merchant monitoring, investigation, and escalation workflows across APAC.

- Partner with global Legal, Risk, and Compliance teams to implement new policies and control changes efficiently.

- Work with Product and Engineering to evolve risk tooling, including rules engines, case management, AI- and LLM-based workflows, and analytics surfaces.

- Champion automation and AI to reduce manual work, speed up decisions, and improve reviewer effectiveness while maintaining strong control standards.

- Drive key performance metrics, including fraud and chargeback ratios, to meet company objectives.

- Manage stakeholders proactively across Risk, Compliance, Commercial, Product, and other partner teams.

- Challenge the status quo and continuously improve operational excellence.

You'll work from our Singapore office five days a week.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- 8+ years of experience in payments, fintech, or acquiring, including at least 3+ years of people management experience.

- Deep expertise in one or more areas of merchant risk, including credit and fraud risk, reserves and exposure management, brand risk, acceptable-use policy, or compliance management.

- A proven track record of managing senior individual contributors across multi-location teams.

- Experience operating quickly in ambiguity, setting clear priorities, and driving outcomes in a fast-moving, high-growth environment.

- A strong desire to learn about new payment trends and technologies.

- A bachelor’s degree or equivalent practical experience.

Preferred

- Strong analytical and data skills, with comfort using dashboards and data tools to diagnose issues, design experiments, and measure impact.

- Experience building or scaling risk operations in a global fintech or top-tier payments company.

- Hands-on experience partnering with Product and Engineering to design risk tooling or automation, including the use of AI, machine learning, or LLMs in operations.

- Mandarin language proficiency.

YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF

- You're comfortable owning the roadmap yourself.

- You own the outcome and you don't wait for permission to fix what's broken.

- You're comfortable making decisions with incomplete information and moving quickly anyway.

- You've operated in a compliance-heavy environment and know how to move fast without cutting the corners that matter.

- You know when to align and when to just decide. Collaboration isn't a way to avoid accountability.

LEARN MORE ABOUT YOUR TEAM

Operations at Airwallex is where global ambition gets translated into systems that actually hold at scale.

This team runs the high-stakes flows businesses depend on — payments, accounts, and cross-border transactions. The work sits at the intersection of product, compliance, and customer experience. The problems are rarely simple: balancing speed with rigor, global consistency with local reality, and immediate execution with the stronger systems a growing company needs next.

It's a function for operators with a builder mentality: trusted to take ownership, work cross-functionally with product and risk, and create new processes for things that have not existed before.

If you want to help build the operational backbone of a global financial platform, this is work that keeps the whole machine moving.
