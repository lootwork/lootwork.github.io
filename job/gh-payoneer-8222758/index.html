О Payoneer

Founded in 2005, Payoneer is the global financial platform that removes friction from doing business across borders, with a mission to connect the world’s underserved businesses to a rising global economy. We’re a community with over 2,500 colleagues all over the world, working to serve customers, and partners in over 190 countries and territories.

By taking the complexity out of the financial workflows–including everything from global payments and compliance to multi-currency and workforce management, to providing working capital and business intelligence–we give businesses the tools they need to work efficiently worldwide and grow with confidence.

We are seeking a talented individual to join our Regional Finance Team. This role will be based in Hong Kong. The successful candidate will support accountants and auditors in daily accounting operations and ensure accurate financial records. This role is ideal for someone organized, responsible, and eager to grow in a dynamic work environment.

Onsite | Full time | Hong Kong

Что предстоит делать

Month end closing & financial analysis

  • Perform month end closing duties including financial analysis, reviewing full general ledger, P&L, Balance sheet according to US GAAP, and/or any local GAAP in the Asia Pacific region; experience in consolidation is added advantage.
  • Handle accounting and taxation enquiries from internal/external stakeholders.
  • Assisting audit requirements (internal and external).

Corporate payments

  • Process payment requests with relevant supporting documents and obtain proper approvals.
  • Handle invoice settlements and expense reimbursements.
  • Upload and reconcile bank balances and transactions
  • Assisting relevant purchasing requestors and procurement team across APAC region if needed.

Financial Projects and Process Improvements

  • Participate in finance transformation projects.
  • Define and deliver process improvements across Finance team, focusing on:
  • Process design/redesigning.
  • Time-saving opportunities.
  • Controls and risk assessment.

Payroll operations

  • Assist in payroll-related work and resolve employee inquiries relating to payroll matters.

Perform other ad hoc tasks as assigned.

For this role you are also

  • Number sensitive and an analytical thinker as well as good at multi-tasking.
  • With excellent interpersonal skills, able to liaise with both internal and external stakeholders across APAC region.
  • Self-motivated, well organized, independent, responsible, good communication skills and problem-solving skills.
  • Willing to take up challenges and work in a fast-paced environment.

Кто вы

  • Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Business studies or any professional accountancy qualification.
  • Minimum 3 years accounting experience.
  • Strong knowledge (and application) in accounting standards such as US GAAP, IFRS or equivalent.
  • Hands-on knowledge of MS Excel and accounting software/ ERP.
  • Well-versed in accounting & vendor payment processing procedures.
  • High level of written and spoken English & Chinese.
  • Experience in managing Asia Pacific region (or multiple regions).

Not a must but a great advantage

  • Experience in Power BI/ Business Central ERP / Microsoft Dynamics (NAV).
  • Experience in cross-border e-commerce, online payment industry.
  • Prior experience in engaging outsourced bookkeeping team.
