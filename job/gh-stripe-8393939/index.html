Кто мы

О Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.

О команде

Stripe’s Financial Systems organization builds the technology that enables Stripe’s Finance org to operate accurately, efficiently, and at Stripe’s scale. We develop trusted data products, internal platforms and products, and intelligent workflows that help Finance teams manage complex global operations while maintaining strong controls.

This role will join a product team focused on transforming finance operations. You will initially work closely with Stripe’s tax and treasury organizations with opportunities for future impact in other parts of the Finance org. The products you build will help experts find trusted information, coordinate work, identify exceptions, and make timely decisions without stitching together context and workflows across multiple systems.

Что предстоит делать

You will develop products that will enable Stripe to maintain a continuously updated understanding of financial performance, automate complex operations while maintaining rigorous financial controls that minimize risk, and reduce toil for domain experts to free them for higher-value work. You will develop a deep understanding of expert users and their workflows, own the product strategy, turn it into a focused roadmap, and lead products from discovery through adoption and continuous improvement. The transformation your products enable will reduce risk, improve efficiency, automate manual tasks, and better enable Stripe to make timely financial decisions, deliver on regulatory commitments, and run a global-scale business.

This role combines internal product development, data platforms, automated workflow design, and applied AI. You will help develop purpose-built solutions, platforms, and autonomous agentic capabilities that reason over governed data, drive end-to-end workflows, and support expert decisions. Success requires understanding the capabilities and limitations of AI and machine learning when applied to business-critical, regulated, and strictly governed problems. You will also use AI-enabled tools throughout the product development lifecycle to accelerate discovery, prototyping, requirements development, evaluation, and iteration without compromising quality or controls.

Обязанности

  • Develop a deep understanding of Finance users, jobs-to-be-done, compliance and regulatory considerations, and data dependencies, then translate that understanding into a clear roadmap for agentic automations.
  • Own products end to end, from problem discovery and strategy through delivery, adoption, measurement, and iteration
  • Build reusable data and self-serve workflow capabilities that eliminate manual overhead and enable future automated Finance use cases
  • Partner with Finance, engineering, data, security, legal, and others to make sound product and architecture tradeoffs in a business-critical and control-sensitive environment
  • Define measures to understand adoption, engagement, and impact for products and develop insights into the metrics to guide improvements to the product
  • Develop Finance products that enable Stripes to self-serve the creation and operation of agentic automations, while establishing appropriate boundaries for autonomous execution, evaluation methods, and controls for human review
  • Use AI-enabled tools throughout product development and help establish effective practices for AI-assisted discovery, prototyping, specification, testing, and iteration
  • Balance new product development with reliability, operational ownership, and the long-term health of the portfolio

Кто вы

We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • 5+ years of product management or equivalent experience leading the development of complex software products
  • Experience independently taking a product from problem discovery through launch, adoption, measurement, and iteration
  • Experience working closely with engineering and data teams on systems involving data models, integrations, APIs, or complex operational workflows
  • Demonstrated ability to synthesize the needs of multiple user groups and lead decisions across organizational boundaries
  • Ability to communicate clearly about product strategy, priorities, tradeoffs, risks, and results with both technical and nontechnical partners

Будет плюсом

  • Experience building products for tax, treasury, accounting, regulatory reporting, or other control-sensitive domains
  • Experience with enterprise data platforms, workflow systems, internal tool development, or systems of record
  • Experience applying AI or automation to expert workflows while preserving human review, auditability, and accountability
  • Experience maturing a product from active development into a reliable, sustainable operating model
