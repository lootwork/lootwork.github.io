О роли

Opening a new financial account is a moment of trust. People want to know what they’re signing up for, why it’s worth their time, and what happens next. As a Product Designer on Chime’s Growth team, you’ll shape those first impressions and help people go from exploring Chime to confidently taking their first steps as members.

Visual craft matters at every step. A clear hierarchy can make a complicated choice feel manageable. Thoughtful typography and layout can make important information easier to understand at a glance. A well-designed interaction can reassure someone that they’ve completed a step and show them where to go next. You’ll bring that level of care to the screens, flows, and small details that make the Chime app feel approachable and cohesive.

You’ll own design projects from the first sketch through launch and iteration. You’ll work with Product, Engineering, Research, and Analytics to find where people hesitate or drop off, explore different ways to solve the problem, and test what helps. You’ll bring a strong point of view on how the experience should look and feel, and you’ll use member feedback and experiment results to sharpen it.

In this role, you can expect to

  • Design consumer mobile experiences across the early member journey, from exploring what Chime offers to signing up, getting set up, and reaching a first meaningful milestone.
  • Bring a high bar for visual and interaction design to each release, with attention to typography, hierarchy, composition, motion, and accessibility.
  • Use funnel data and member research to understand where people get stuck. Explore different flows and visual approaches, then prototype and test the most promising directions.
  • Use and extend Chime’s design system to create cohesive experiences, accounting for the loading, error, and recovery states that matter when people are setting up a financial account.
  • Share work early, explain the rationale behind your design decisions, and partner with engineers to carry the details through implementation and launch.

To thrive in this role, you have

  • Experience shipping consumer mobile products and a portfolio that shows strong visual craft, interaction design, and attention to detail in shipped work.
  • A strong eye for typography, layout, color, and hierarchy, with examples of how you use them to make complex information feel clear and approachable.
  • Experience owning design work from discovery through delivery, including making tradeoffs and adapting your designs as you learn.
  • Comfort using qualitative research, funnel data, and experiments to form hypotheses and evaluate whether a design improved the experience.
  • The ability to communicate your design decisions, give and receive thoughtful critique, and work closely with cross-functional partners.
  • Experience designing signup, onboarding, activation, or other growth journeys is a plus.
