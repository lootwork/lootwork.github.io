Что предстоит делать

  • Lead interaction design for complex products or large-scale projects, with a deep understanding of product strategy and user needs.
  • Own end-to-end interaction design, including information architecture, user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity prototypes.
  • Build and maintain interaction design standards and guidelines.
  • Work closely with product, visual design, engineering, QA, and operations teams to ensure high-quality execution.
  • Support user research, usability testing, and data analysis to continuously improve the product experience.
  • Contribute design expertise, mentor junior designers, and help elevate team capability.

Кого мы ищем

  • Proven experience in financial product interaction design.
  • Strong logical thinking and ability to handle complex workflows and information structures.
  • Solid end-to-end interaction design skills.
  • Proficiency in user flows, wireframes, and prototyping.
  • Strong cross-functional collaboration and communication skills.
  • Experience in user research and usability testing is a plus.
