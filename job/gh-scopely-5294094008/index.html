Scopely is looking for an Lead Product Manager to join our MONOPOLY GO! Game team in Barcelona hub on a hybrid basis (3X week in office).

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues, or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing and innovating the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people around the world daily.

MONOPOLY GO! is a global phenomenon and the biggest mobile game launch in history, captivating millions of players worldwide.

Что предстоит делать

  • Direct the cross-functional group managing MONOPOLY GO! Live Minigames. You are responsible for every initiative from initial planning to live execution, ensuring exceptional experiences for players alongside stable, predictable operations globally.
  • Drive the strategy for live minigames, balancing player delight, brand vision, commercial goals, operational stability, and technical efficiency throughout the product lifecycle.
  • Coordinate execution across Design, Engineering, Art, UX, Economy, Analytics, Production, QA, and Localization, establishing clear milestones, accountability, dependencies, and launch readiness decision points.
  • Ensure high-quality launches using structured specifications, configuration checks, rigorous playtesting, prompt issue resolution, risk mitigation, and contingency planning.
  • Implement comprehensive product, economic, and technical telemetry for all events, establishing automated alerts, coverage schedules, clear escalation channels, and standard operating procedures.
  • Leverage analytics, player feedback, market trends, and post-launch reviews to optimize progression, rewards, engagement, and monetization while preventing recurring operational issues.
  • Expand our portfolio capabilities through reusable frameworks, streamlined content pipelines, automation, performance tracking, and mentorship that frees up bandwidth for innovation.

Кого мы ищем

  • 5+ years of product management leadership delivering complex live services at scale, with a proven track record of driving player satisfaction, product quality, and reliable execution. A degree in a relevant field or equivalent practical experience is preferred.
  • Deep expertise in free-to-play mobile mechanics, player motivations, core loops, progression systems, economy design, social features, and long-term retention strategy.
  • Proven operational success managing concurrent launches, data validation, quality assurance, live monitoring, incident management, and post-mortem analysis under tight timelines.
  • Strong analytical competency and technical proficiency across product data, segmentation, A/B testing, system architecture, deployment pipelines, observability, and debugging.
  • Exceptional communication skills and collaborative leadership ability, with a structured approach to resolving ambiguity, evaluating data objectively, and driving team alignment.
  • Active integration of AI tooling and automated workflows into daily tasks, demonstrating practical applications that elevate quality, speed, efficiency, and overall operational performance.

Дополнительный плюс

  • Prior experience operating top-tier mobile free-to-play titles, high-scale live-service games, or consumer platforms with rapid update cycles.
  • Hands-on familiarity with game economies, experimental frameworks, telemetry pipelines, monitoring dashboards, alerting setups, or incident response protocols.
  • Demonstrated success optimizing content delivery, configuration tools, build pipelines, or multi-region live coverage schedules across diverse time zones.
