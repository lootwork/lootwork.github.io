О команде

Marshmallow's pricing team applies quantitative methods to real insurance problems. In this role, you'll learn how pricing decisions work in practice and turn data into meaningful outcomes. You'll work closely with a senior pricing team member, bringing your quantitative background into an industry setting. The aim is for you to develop the practical judgement and independence to take on – and eventually lead – increasingly substantial work.

Что предстоит делать

- Working closely with pricing colleagues on scoped analytical and data-driven projects, taking on more responsibility as your experience develops.

- Applying mathematical, statistical and machine learning techniques to practical pricing questions, connecting your analysis to the decision it needs to inform.

- Explaining your findings clearly, keeping colleagues from different areas of the business updated on progress and following up on agreed actions.

- Using feedback to improve your work and build your understanding of data science methods, coding best practice and the commercial context of insurance pricing.

Кто вы

- You want to learn, seek feedback and build towards working independently.

- You communicate your rationale and findings clearly, and keep colleagues informed about your progress.

- You follow through on agreed actions and take ownership of your work.

WHAT YOU’LL BRING

- A strong academic record and a research master's or PhD in a quantitative field, such as mathematics, physics or econometrics.

- Experience applying quantitative methods or machine learning to a practical problem through your studies, research, a personal project or work.

- Industry experience is preferred - a placement, internship or experience whilst studying, or in between research masters / PhD.

- The ability to explain how your approach fits the problem, rather than relying on theory alone.
