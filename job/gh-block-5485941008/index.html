Square hardware is the physical connection between sellers and Square: Readers that turn any phone or tablet into a point of sale, Square Stand, and Android-based devices like Square Register, Terminal, and Kiosk. In this role, you'll own the quality bar for all Square hardware products. You'll decide when a release is ready for our factories and for our sellers, and lead the team that proves it.

Вам предстоит

  • Own the release quality bar for all Hardware programs. You'll sign off on builds going to the factory (NPI and production) and on OS, firmware, and app releases going to sellers in the field.
  • Define and oversee the validation strategy, both manual and automated, across the stack: applications, OS, firmware, and payment hardware. This includes device compatibility across the phones, tablets, and peripherals our sellers use.
  • Decide when product software is good enough for sellers. You'll evaluate risk at each phase of development, manufacturing, and deployment, and give clear go/no-go recommendations to product and engineering leaders.
  • Lead a team of about 10 contract test engineers and manage vendor partners. You'll set priorities, staffing, and expectations, and hold the team to a high bar.
  • Partner with Hardware engineering, product, program management, and manufacturing teams to plan releases, build testability into products, and drive critical issues to resolution.
  • Expand test automation on device test racks to lower the manual regression burden over time, and keep questioning what we test manually and why.

Требования

You have

  • 8+ years of verification, validation, or software engineering experience, including shipping consumer electronics or embedded devices.
  • Experience defining and owning a validation strategy for a complex product, from development through manufacturing and production release.
  • Experience leading test teams, including contractors or outsourced vendors.
  • Sound judgment on product risk, and a track record of identifying, prioritizing, and driving issues to closure across multiple products and releases.
  • Experience as a strong partner to development, product, and operations teams, with clear written and spoken communication.

Even better

  • Experience with NPI, factory test, or manufacturing release processes.
  • Experience validating Android/AOSP-based devices, firmware, or payment hardware.
  • Experience building test automation where off-the-shelf tools don't exist, including CI and hardware-in-the-loop test racks.
  • Experience supporting ongoing releases across multiple hardware products at the same time.
