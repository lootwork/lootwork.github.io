At Kabam, we’re constantly raising the bar of excellence in free-to-play gaming. We hire for passion and diversity so that we can triumph in our collective skill. Our teams are made up of a fusion of personalities and interests bound together by a collaborative nature, fun-loving culture, and a drive to do what it takes to make great games. And this is where you come in…

The Data Analyst utilizes their quantitative analytical skills and scientific thought process to interpret and

analyze large sets of complex data, while extracting meaningful insights for Kabam. This role works

closely with our internal gaming team to optimize every facet of player retention and monetization to

ensure the continued success of our games.

This role is a full-time, on-site position based 5 days a week at our Downtown Vancouver studio.

IN THIS ROLE, YOU CAN EXPECT TO

Provide Cross-functional Support

  • Collaborate with various teams across the organization to understand their business goals and data needs.
  • Improve reporting processes by identifying inefficiencies and suggesting enhancements.
  • Assist with larger-scale projects as required, contributing data-driven insights.

Data Exploration and Analysis

  • Use SQL to extract and manipulate data from various sources.
  • Perform hands-on exploration of data sets to answer business questions.
  • Utilize statistical analysis to identify trends, correlations, and patterns within the data.
  • Create data visualizations to communicate findings effectively.

Dashboard and Report Management

  • Develop and maintain a suite of dashboards and reports tailored to the needs of different teams within the organization.
  • Ensure that dashboards and reports are up-to-date and accurate.
  • Prepare ad-hoc reports as requested.

A/B Testing

  • Design, execute, and manage multiple A/B tests to gain insights into key performance indicators (KPIs) such as retention, acquisition, and monetization.
  • Analyze the results of A/B tests to draw actionable conclusions.
  • Make data-driven recommendations for optimizing business strategies based on results of A/B testing.

Product Feature Analysis and Optimization

  • Measure the success of newly released product features using key metrics and data analysis.
  • Drive data-driven iteration and optimization of product features to enhance performance and user experience.

Task Prioritization and Project Management

  • Assist in task prioritization within the data team based on business needs and project requirements.
  • Collaborate with team members to achieve project goals efficiently.

TO BE SUCCESSFUL IN THIS ROLE, YOUR BACKGROUND INCLUDES

  • Bachelor’s Degree in related field.
  • 3+ years of industry data analysis experience, with solid knowledge of statistical methods.
  • 1+ years of industry data experience in games (mobile, console, PC), online casino, slot games, or any high volume B2C mobile apps.
  • Expert SQL skills and experience querying very large data sets.
  • Proven ability to thrive using multiple mixed, varied, and inconsistent data sources.
  • Fundamental knowledge of project management methodologies.
  • Regular communication with stakeholders from other departments and drive actionable items for continuous improvement.
  • Experience with Google BigQuery, Tableau, JIRA and/or other project management software.
  • Experience working for a social or mobile game developer.
  • Understanding of game design concepts and principles.
