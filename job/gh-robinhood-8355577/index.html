Join us in building the future of finance.

Our mission is to democratize finance for all. An estimated $124 trillion of assets will be inherited by younger generations in the next two decades. The largest transfer of wealth in human history. If you’re ready to be at the epicenter of this historic cultural and financial shift, keep reading.

О Team & Role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world's biggest financial problems. We're looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn't a place for complacency, it's where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We're a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

Robinhood has been tapped as the brokerage and initial trustee for Government Products, and is working alongside BNY and The U.S. Department of Treasury to develop and operate the program, which aims to expand market access for the next generation of Americans. In this role, you will help build a world-class, intuitive platform, leveraging Robinhood's industry-leading financial technology to deliver a standalone web and app experience for this historic national initiative.

The Government Products team focuses on building and scaling systems that support long-term investing and account management for millions of customers. This team works closely with product, data, and infrastructure partners to deliver reliable, secure, and intuitive experiences. You will help shape systems that enable customers to plan for their financial future with confidence!

As a Software Engineer, Web, you will be an important contributor to this 0-to-1 initiative. You will be responsible for building the polished, reliable, and accessible customer-facing web experiences that families use to open and manage accounts, and for helping set the patterns of a brand-new front-end codebase. This is a high-visibility, high-impact role where you will directly shape a platform built to serve millions of future customers

This role is based in our New York, NY and Menlo Park, CA offices, with in-person attendance expected 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build, test, and ship web features using React and modern JavaScript for a brand-new, standalone product optimized for security, reliability, and scale.
  • Partner closely with Product, Design, and backend engineers to translate requirements into intuitive, reliable, and accessible customer experiences.
  • Integrate front-end experiences with our backend-for-frontend layer and the services behind it.
  • Contribute to front-end architecture, reusable components, and engineering best practices for a new codebase.
  • Participate in code reviews and help maintain quality, performance, and accessibility across the web experience.

Что вы приносите

  • Experience building production web applications with React and modern JavaScript, with a focus on performance, accessibility, and a polished customer experience.
  • Comfort working with APIs and collaborating closely with backend engineers to deliver end-to-end features.
  • Willingness to learn quickly and navigate ambiguity in a zero-to-one product space.
  • A habit of writing clean, well-tested code, and a strong desire to learn from feedback and grow as an engineer.
  • Clear communication skills with the ability to partner closely with Product, Design, and cross-functional teammates.
  • Experience with financial products or investment technology is a plus.
