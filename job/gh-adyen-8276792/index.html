Tech Strategy & Program Manager

Adyen is looking for a forward-thinking Tech Strategy & Program Manager to help drive cross-domain initiatives across our Tech organisation. You will join the Tech Strategy & Execution team, serving as an upstream tech function focused on driving strategic priorities across the organization. You are the bridge between commercial ambition and technical reality, ensuring that Adyen identifies the right strategic bets and defines the path to deliver them. You will work closely with senior leadership across Tech, Commercial and partner functions to ensure our growth ambition is technically feasible, aligned with our customers' evolving needs and executed in a timely manner.

В этой роли вы будете

  • Bridge Tech, Commercial, Operations and Compliance: Act as the Tech counterpart to stakeholder teams, ensuring a balanced perspective between business goals and technical reality moves towards the Tech organization
  • Act as Upstream Tech Lead: Act as the initial product and technical strategy lead for cross-solution initiatives—structuring the opportunity space, defining the problem, shaping the technical direction, and validating feasibility—before transitioning mature initiatives into dedicated solution teams.
  • Solve Holistic Merchant Problems: Look beyond individual products to synthesize merchant problems across solutions, identifying collective "blind spots" in the merchant experience and building frameworks to track improvements.
  • Drive Strategic Coordination: Shape and align strategic initiatives that cut across multiple solutions by clarifying trade-offs, surfacing system-level risks, and ensuring the technical direction remains coherent across the platform.
  • Product Execution: Creating mechanisms to track progress towards execution, between the Tech teams (for example on improving dependencies) and between Tech and our stakeholders (Commercial, Ops & Compliance)

Кто вы

  • Product Strategy: You have 5-7 years of experience in Product Management or Tech Strategy, specifically focused on shaping long-term product direction and future-scoping
  • Systems Thinker: You think in systems, not features—understanding platform dependencies, architectural trade-offs, and how decisions in one solution affect the broader merchant experience.
  • Commercial-Tech Translator: You excel at bridging the gap between customer/commercial needs and the internal technical organization, translating feedback into actionable product strategy
  • Entrepreneurial mindset: You are comfortable stepping into the unknown to lead new strategic bets before a formal team exists, driving them from concept to maturity
  • Strategic Influencer: You can drive alignment and coordination for major initiatives across multiple distinct product areas without necessarily owning the day-to-day execution teams
  • Clear Communicator: You can navigate high-stakes discussions with senior leadership and key stakeholders, providing clarity on dependencies, opportunities, and trade-offs
  • Execution Driven: You have a bias towards execution, and the ability to drive the delivery of large, cross-team programs at quality and speed.

Как выглядит успех в этой роли

  • New opportunities are clearly framed, prioritized, and aligned before execution
  • Cross-team trade-offs are surfaced early and resolved pragmatically
  • Commercial ambition is matched with realistic technical strategy
  • Leadership has clear visibility into progress on the Tech priorities across the teams
