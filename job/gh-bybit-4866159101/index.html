Role Summary

You are the player-coach who scales loan operations end-to-end. You’ll own the campaigns engine (Crypto Loan / UAT Loan / INS Loan), run the collateral listing/delisting program with Risk, and turn competitive intel into parameter and pricing changes. You ship reliably, read the data, and lead a small team.

Основные обязанности

  • Growth & Marketing Operations
  • Set quarterly campaign strategy and calendar; define mechanics, budgets, T&Cs; localize with regional teams.
  • Orchestrate execution across Product, Design, CRM (landing pages, push/email, banners).
  • Own UAT for campaign logic (rates, LTV tiers, rewards, attribution).
  • Measure and optimize: eFTD, active borrowers, AUM, ARPU, CAC/ROI; publish weekly dashboards and post-mortems.
  • Collateral Listing/Delisting Program (Margin Loans)
  • Chair the cross-functional workflow from intake → approval → rollout.
  • With Risk/Treasury/Compliance/Legal, propose and calibrate parameters: initial/maintenance LTV, haircuts, borrow caps, interest, regions.
  • Manage change management: staging verification, production checks, announcements, API/Docs updates.
  • Monitor after go-live (alerts, circuit breakers, user tickets); lead incident response and rollback when needed.
  • Pricing, Risk & Liquidity Partnership
  • Build data-backed proposals for pricing, LTV automation, and liquidation thresholds; align with credit risk.
  • Coordinate Treasury/capital-markets to optimize internal liquidity and cost of funds for promotions and new collaterals.
  • Maintain audit-ready records of approvals and parameter history.
  • Competitive Intelligence → Action
  • Maintain a living “rate & rules” tracker for Binance/OKX/Bitget and top regional players (rates, LTVs, VIP tiers, promos, liquidation logic, supported assets).
  • Produce monthly “so what” briefs; drive concrete changes (e.g., parameter updates, promo angles, new asset candidates).

Success Metrics

  • Growth: lift in eFTD, active borrowers, Loan AUM; campaign ROI vs. budget.
  • Reliability: 0 critical defects in parameter changes; % on-time launches.
  • Risk & Liquidity: parameter changes within approved bands; no uncontrolled exposure; utilization of borrow caps.
  • Intel to Action: adoption rate of monthly recommendations; time from insight → shipped change.
  • Ops Hygiene: SOP freshness, audit completeness, CS ticket reduction.

What You’ll Bring

Обязательно

  • 5–8 years in crypto/fintech/exchange operations or lending.
  • Proven record shipping multi-team launches (campaigns or listings) with rigorous UAT.
  • Strong data skills: Excel/Sheets; working SQL (BigQuery/MySQL) for self-serve analysis; dashboard familiarity (Looker/Metabase/Tableau).
  • Solid grasp of lending mechanics: LTV/haircuts, liquidation, collateral valuation, regional restrictions.
  • Cross-functional influence with Risk, Treasury, Product, Compliance, Legal.
  • Clear written comms; fluent English and Mandarin.

Будет плюсом

  • Hands-on experience with collateral onboarding/offboarding and pricing playbooks.
  • CRM/A/B testing (Braze/Iterable); Python for ad-hoc analysis.
  • Familiarity with KYC/AML frameworks and public announcements/API docs processes.

Инструменты

Jira/Confluence, BigQuery/MySQL, Looker/Metabase/Tableau, Sheets/Excel, Braze/Iterable, internal admin tools, Lark/Slack.
