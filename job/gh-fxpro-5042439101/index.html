Join FxPro: a leading international fintech company. Be a part of our expanding international team, with offices in Limassol, London, Nassau, Dubai, and Yerevan. FxPro boasts a diverse workforce of over 600 employees representing 50 nationalities, making it an exciting and dynamic workplace. At FxPro, we see each team member as an integral part of our success story.

As a Marketing Manager (AI-driven), you will own marketing for your business area end-to-end. Much of the day-to-day work is executed by AI agents - a developer builds them, and you are responsible for what they produce: you decide what each agent should do, shape its prompts, monitor how the workflows run, and ensure the results meet the standard required.

Обязанности

  • Develop and run the marketing strategy and activity plan
  • Identify which marketing tasks can be handled by AI agents (content, analytics, performance, communications, etc.) and write clear requirements for the developer: goals, inputs, expected outputs, and quality criteria
  • Write, test, and continuously improve the prompts and instructions the agents run on
  • Manage channels, budget, and contractors
  • Analyze results, optimize the funnel and overall efficiency
  • Continuously test new AI tools and propose the best ones for adoption

Требования

  • 4–5+ years of marketing experience, including project management
  • Hands-on experience with generative AI in marketing work: advanced prompt engineering and iterating on prompts based on output quality
  • Understanding of how AI agents and automated workflows operate, including their strengths, limits, and typical failure points, so you can work effectively with a developer (coding not required)
  • Ability to turn a marketing task into a clear specification for a technical team
  • Solid understanding of performance marketing, content, CRM, and analytics
  • Ability to build processes and measure results through metrics
  • Self-driven and comfortable working under uncertainty

Будет плюсом

  • Experience launching products or functions from scratch
  • Cases where AI significantly reduced time or budget spent
  • Familiarity with no-code automation tools (n8n, Make, Zapier) or workflow monitoring and logging tools

Interview steps

  • Recruiter screen (~30 minutes)
  • Technical Interview (1 hour)
  • Test task
  • Final Interview with managers (1 hour)
