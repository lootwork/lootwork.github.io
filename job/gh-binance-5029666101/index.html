Что предстоит делать

  • Develop and maintain the Risk SDK on Android (Java/Kotlin, NDK) or Web (JavaScript/TypeScript).
  • Build risk environment detection capabilities (HOOK, Debug, device tampering, etc.).
  • Develop code-hardening capabilities, including research and development of code obfuscation, VMP and related technologies.
  • Collaborate with the backend and strategy teams on signal reporting, data quality, and detection efficacy evaluation.

Кого мы ищем

  • Proficient in one or more programming languages: Java, C/C++, ARM assembly, TypeScript, JavaScript.
  • 3+ years of client-side development experience, with Android or Web development capability.
  • Reverse engineering skills on Android (Smali/DEX, native SO layer) or Web (JS deobfuscation, protocol analysis).
  • Familiar with Android system internals or the browser runtime environment (app lifecycle, signature mechanism, JNI, WebView, JS engine, etc.).
  • Familiar with common attack techniques: hooking frameworks, emulators/cloud phones, app multi-boxing/cloning, repackaging, automated clients, and the corresponding countermeasures.
  • Strong engineering discipline: code quality, performance and stability awareness (package size, startup time, crash rate, compatibility).
