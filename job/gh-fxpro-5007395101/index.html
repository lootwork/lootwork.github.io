Join FxPro: a leading international fintech company. Be a part of our expanding international team, with offices in Limassol, London, Nassau, and Dubai. FxPro boasts a diverse workforce of over 600 employees representing 50 nationalities, making it an exciting and dynamic workplace. At FxPro, we see each team member as an integral part of our success story.

As a Senior System Analyst, you'll play a crucial role in the design, implementation, and maintenance of our trading systems and infrastructure.

Обязанности

  • Collaborate with business stakeholders to understand their needs and requirements
  • Analyze and document business processes, workflows, and system functionalities
  • Design and propose technical solutions that align with FxPro's business objectives and technology strategy
  • Develop detailed system specifications, diagrams, and flowcharts to guide developers
  • Work closely with development teams to oversee the implementation of system solutions
  • Ensure seamless integration of new systems with existing infrastructure and platforms

Требования

  • 5+ years experience in a similar role, with 2 years experience working in mobile teams
  • Expertise in analyzing and documenting requirements for mobile and web applications
  • Experienced in creating detailed system specifications, wireframes, diagrams, and flowcharts
  • Skilled in RESTful APIs, Async API (WebSocket, Kafka, etc.) and third-party API integration
  • Understanding of UX/UI design principles
  • Proficient in creating comprehensive technical documentation
  • Proficient in responsive design and system architecture
  • Strong problem-solving and troubleshooting skills
  • Excellent verbal and written communication
  • Strong collaboration and teamwork abilities
  • Analytical thinking and data analysis skills
  • Adaptability to changing business needs
  • In-depth knowledge of trading systems is nice to have
  • Knowledge of industry standards and regulatory compliance is a plus
  • Fluent in English
  • Russian is an advantage

Interview steps

  • Recruiter screen (~30 minutes)
  • Technical Interview (~1 hour)
  • Final Interview with managers (~1 hour)
