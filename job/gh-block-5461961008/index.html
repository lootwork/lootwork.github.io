О роли

More and more of our engineering work now runs through autonomous agents, and those agents can only be as effective as the tools, context, and codebase around them. As a Developer Experience Engineer, you'll build the tooling and agent workflows that let humans and agents change our largest monorepos quickly and safely.

You'll own the interfaces engineers use to observe, steer, and give context to the agents working on our code. You'll also build the guardrails that keep complexity in check as those agents take on bigger changes. Large-scale work like framework upgrades, language migrations, and dependency health is still part of the job, but you'll do it by building and steering agents rather than making the changes by hand. This is a high-leverage role. Every improvement you ship multiplies across the whole engineering organization.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build and ship the tools engineers and their agents use every day, such as CLIs, desktop integrations, and interfaces for observing, unblocking, and steering autonomous agents, and own their adoption, distribution, and support
  • Design and evolve agentic loops (the plan, act, verify, and iterate cycles) and the harnesses, skills, and context that let agents do real engineering work reliably
  • Build agent workflows that drive large-scale changes across the codebase, including framework upgrades, language migrations, and dependency updates, with minimal disruption to product teams
  • Build the guardrails, tests, and automation that make large, agent-authored changes safe to land, and track the leading indicators of growing complexity
  • Find friction in the development workflow for both humans and agents, and fix the root causes
  • Partner with builders inside and outside of engineering to understand their pain points, and measure the adoption and impact of what you ship
  • Set technical direction and mentor other engineers on agent design, tooling, and codebase health

Кого мы ищем

We care more about what you've done than about which specific technologies you used to do it.

Обязательно

  • Shipping developer tooling at scale: You've shipped tools that engineers use daily, such as CLIs, desktop integrations, configuration management, or internal platforms. You understand what it takes to drive adoption, debug problems, and support users.
  • Scaling work with agents: You've built agent workflows that finished work that would normally take a team weeks. Examples include generating codebase summaries or world models, writing skills and loops, or orchestrating a team of semi-deterministic agents. You have a deep understanding of agentic loops (tool use, context management, verification, failure handling) and are eager to keep evolving them as the field changes.
  • Evolving frameworks and large codebases: You have deep experience with application or platform frameworks, including upgrading them and managing their evolution over time. You also have a track record of leading migrations or large refactors from planning through rollout.
  • Significant experience (typically 10+ years for Staff, 7+ for Senior) working in large, complex codebases
  • Strong communication skills and the ability to build trust with teams by owning the outcomes of your work

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with harness engineering or context engineering
  • Experience with agent observability, for example through OpenTelemetry
  • Experience distributing internal tools (versioning, release channels, auto-update) and measuring adoption with telemetry or surveys
  • Background in developer productivity metrics, test infrastructure, or CI/CD
  • A passion for intuitive user experience and good taste in elegant solutions
  • Open source contributions to developer tooling or agent frameworks
