About us

Hi, we are Ten Square Games, and we live games! No, not a typo - but a level up from loving! We are on a mission of giving players excellent outdoor experiences on their mobiles. With global hits, such as Fishing Clash, we know how to make the best of technology, fun, and fair play. With 77 million registered players in 2024 and 300+ people on board, we keep growing and learning, having the insatiable hunger for the next success. If you also like to explore the unknown and know how to own it, we want to work with you!

What is this job all about?

In the R&D team, our Data Scientists (f/m/d) help improve the gaming experience for our players. You’ll work hand-in-hand with product, design, and engineering teams to turn complex player data into deep analyses, experiments, and insights that shape what we build next. As a data enthusiast, you’ll translate business and product questions into analytical problems - and their results into clear recommendations for our Product Management colleagues. Whether you're building a segmentation model, designing an A/B test, or explaining your findings to team leaders, you'll play a key role in enhancing the gaming world.
