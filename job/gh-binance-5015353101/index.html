Что предстоит делать

  • Responsible for core derivative trading product management, specifically spot margin
  • Drive the development of product roadmaps, feature backlogs and product documentation such as PRDs, user stories, use cases, user flows, logic flows, and functional specifications
  • Prioritise development activities with engineering teams, assist in determining the best technical implementation methods, work closely with QA on acceptance testing and manage product launches
  • Conduct market research and competitor analysis to deliver best-in-class solutions and products
  • Manage cross-functional and cross-department communication and coordination to ensure product quality and progress, and completion of product milestones

Кого мы ищем

  • Minimum 5 years of proven experience as a product manager coupled with a good understanding of financial trading systems
  • Sound knowledge on financial products (especially on margin and futures product) and trading experience
  • Quick learner, can pick up new concepts quickly
  • High degree of initiative to work collaboratively across the team and independently
  • Familiarity with Agile workflow and ability to work within an Agile environment

  • Thrive in a results-driven workplace with opportunities for career growth and continuous learning
