О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

Lead the first-line financial crime operations function across Greater China and Southeast Asia, setting high standards for transaction monitoring and sanctions screening while protecting Airwallex from financial and reputational risk.

- Drive transaction monitoring risk assessments, rule tuning, segmentation and profiling to create a seamless experience for customers across Airwallex’s product suite

- Improve investigations and controls by applying a strong understanding of AML/CTF requirements, analysing patterns and trends, and using data to make risk-based decisions

- Manage escalations, stakeholder relationships, SLAs, deadlines and special projects while leading and inspiring local and remote transaction monitoring analysts

You'll work from our Singapore office five days a week.

Кого мы ищем

ESSENTIALS

- 5+ years of compliance experience in transaction monitoring, financial crime management, AML investigations or a related function

- Bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification in a relevant field

- Experience building efficient, well-documented procedures and workflows in a process-driven environment

- Strong investigative, analytical and problem-solving skills, with meticulous attention to detail

- Experience managing performance against objective targets and using data and analytics to make decisions

- Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to influence stakeholders and manage escalations

- Experience leading, mentoring or managing transaction monitoring or financial crime analysts

- The ability to prioritise competing tasks, meet SLAs and make sound risk-based decisions

- A comprehensive understanding of AML/CTF-related functions, regulatory expectations and compliance policies

Желательно

- AICB, CAMS or CFE certification

- Experience in fintech, payments or another highly regulated business

- Proficiency in SQL

- Experience working with local and remote team members across multiple markets

YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF

- You own the outcome and you don't wait for permission to fix what's broken.

- You're comfortable making decisions with incomplete information and moving quickly anyway.

- You hold yourself to a high standard even when no one is watching. Quality and accountability aren't external expectations, they're internal ones.

- You communicate clearly and directly across cultures, and you're thoughtful about how context shifts meaning across borders.

- You connect dots across functions and hold the big picture without losing grip on the details.

LEARN MORE ABOUT YOUR TEAM

Operations at Airwallex is where global ambition gets translated into systems that actually hold at scale.

This team runs the high-stakes flows businesses depend on — payments, accounts, and cross-border transactions. The work sits at the intersection of product, compliance, and customer experience. The problems are rarely simple: balancing speed with rigor, global consistency with local reality, and immediate execution with the stronger systems a growing company needs next.

It's a function for operators with a builder mentality: trusted to take ownership, work cross-functionally with product and risk, and create new processes for things that have not existed before.

If you want to help build the operational backbone of a global financial platform, this is work that keeps the whole machine moving.
