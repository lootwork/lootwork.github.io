➡️ Mission

Join us as an Android Engineer and help us build the mobile experience that 600,000+ European businesses rely on every day to manage their finances. You'll own product-critical features end-to-end — from technical design to rollout — in a large, modular Kotlin codebase where craft and velocity both matter. You'll work closely with Product, Design, Backend, and Frontend.

➡️ What you'll do

  • Own features end-to-end: You'll take ownership from discovery and technical design through implementation, rollout, and iteration on features that help businesses pay, get paid, and stay in control.
  • Drive technical decisions in your squad: You'll shape solutions with Product, Design, and Backend by clarifying tradeoffs across UX, performance, security, and time-to-market.
  • Raise app reliability and maintainability: You'll improve architecture, performance, and test strategy to scale the app without compromising stability.
  • Tackle complex mobile challenges: You'll contribute to real-time synchronization, resilience patterns, and smooth navigation in a modular codebase with ~1,000 modules.
  • Grow the team's Android craft: You'll lead code reviews and technical discussions, share patterns, and mentor peers to raise the bar on quality and delivery.

➡️ What we're looking for

  • Strong Android native experience: You've shipped and maintained production features with Kotlin and are comfortable owning technical decisions end-to-end.
  • Quality-driven mindset: You care about testing (unit, UI, snapshot), clean code, and pragmatic standards that keep teams moving fast.
  • Problem-solving approach: You thrive in ambiguity and turn complex constraints into simple, robust solutions.
  • AI-native workflow: You use AI coding tools daily to amplify your output — not just experiment with them.
  • Collaborative by nature: You're fluent in English and contribute through code reviews, knowledge sharing, and mentorship across technical and non-technical stakeholders.

➡️ What we can offer you

  • An AI-first engineering environment: Unlimited access to Claude Code, Cursor, and Copilot — and a culture that expects you to use them to raise the bar, not just go faster (click here to read our vision statement on AI)
  • Engineering standards that scale: Strong code review culture, continuous refactoring, and a focus on correctness in a regulated context.
  • A technically demanding domain: Fintech constraints — security, compliance, real-time data — push you to solve problems most mobile engineers never encounter.

➡️ Our methodology, the Qonto Way

  • We value upfront technical design, team reviews, and smart slicing — quality drives the velocity our customers need.
  • Our org assigns one team lead per project across stacks, giving your team a clear mission and full autonomy on how it operates.
  • Our lean toolbox, Kaizen and PDCA, lets you monitor and drive continuous improvement collaboratively with your peers and manager.

➡️ Our stack & practices

  • Kotlin-first & modern concurrency: 100% Kotlin codebase with Coroutines + Flow throughout.
  • Architecture: MVVM + Clean Architecture with modular design (~1000 modules).
  • UI direction: New screens built with Jetpack Compose, with a large share of legacy views already migrated.
  • CI/CD & quality: GitHub Actions, Bitrise, Firebase Test Lab, Sonar, Fastlane.
  • Testing culture: 26k+ unit tests, 14k+ snapshot tests, 500+ UI tests.
