SEON is the command center for fraud prevention and AML compliance, helping thousands of companies worldwide stop fraud, reduce risk and protect revenue. Powered by 900+ real-time, first-party data signals, SEON enriches customer profiles, flags suspicious behavior and streamlines compliance workflows - all from one place. SEON provides richer data, more flexible and transparent analysis, and faster time to value than any other provider on the market. We’ve helped companies reduce fraud by 95% and achieve 32x ROI, and we’re growing fast, thanks to our partnerships with some of the world’s most ambitious digital brands like Revolut, Wise, and Bilt.

Device Intelligence is at the center of our most strategic deals. When prospects evaluate us against competitors, the conversation gets deeply technical — and those are the moments that win or lose deals. We’re hiring a Senior Technical Product Marketing Manager to translate our technical differentiation into stories customers care about, across the full funnel.

You’ll work at the intersection of engineering, product, and GTM, building thought leadership that establishes SEON’s authority, creating campaigns and content that bring verticalized use cases to life, and enabling sales with the demos, collateral, and competitive positioning they need for head-to-head evaluations. If you’ve built or marketed device intelligence products, worked as a sales engineer or product manager in the space, or come from a fraud prevention startup where you wore multiple hats across product and GTM, this role was designed for you.

Что предстоит делать

Technical Differentiation & Positioning

- Partner with Device Intelligence engineering and product to define and sharpen our technical differentiators across SDK performance, fingerprint persistence, bot detection, behavioral signals, and network fingerprinting.

- Own the competitive narrative against device intelligence providers alongside the CI team — developing technical comparisons, differentiation frameworks, and objection-handling guidance grounded in real product capabilities.

Sales Enablement & Deal Support

- Build and maintain enablement assets for device intelligence deals: battlecards, demo environments, POC frameworks, and competitive one-pagers.

- Enable the sales team to navigate technical conversations around SDK integration, performance benchmarks, and architecture without requiring engineering on every call.

- Systematize cross-deal learnings so technical insights from competitive POCs are captured and distributed, not rediscovered deal by deal.

Thought Leadership & Content

- Develop a thought leadership strategy that positions SEON as the authority in device intelligence through original research, technical deep-dives, and industry perspectives.

- Collaborate with demand gen and content teams to weave device intelligence messaging and verticalized use cases into campaigns and mid-funnel content. May also serve as an external evangelist at industry events and in analyst conversations.

Product & Roadmap Collaboration

- Feed competitive and field intelligence back to product to inform Device Intelligence roadmap priorities.

- Support launches of new device intelligence capabilities with GTM strategy, positioning, and enablement materials.

WHAT EXPERIENCE YOU BRING

- 5–7+ years in technical product marketing, product management, sales engineering, or a related GTM role in B2B SaaS, with direct exposure to device intelligence and fraud prevention.

- Deep familiarity with the device intelligence landscape: how fingerprinting works technically, what differentiates providers, and how customers evaluate solutions in competitive POCs.

- Technical credibility with engineers: you can engage on SDK architecture, browser entropy, fingerprint persistence, latency optimization, and bot detection methodology.

- Proven ability to translate complex technical concepts into compelling narratives across sales collateral, competitive positioning, thought leadership, and demos.

- Strong cross-functional instincts — you thrive at the intersection of product, engineering, sales, and marketing.

- Self-starter who can operate independently, take ownership and build new programs.
