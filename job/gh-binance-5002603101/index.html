Binance is a leading global blockchain ecosystem behind the world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume and registered users. We are trusted by 300+ million people in 100+ countries for our industry-leading security, user fund transparency, trading engine speed, deep liquidity, and an unmatched portfolio of digital-asset products. О роли

We are looking for a Senior Marketing Manager to lead brand-led growth for Binance Australia. This role sits at the intersection of brand strategy, integrated campaigns, and localized execution, ensuring Binance’s global brand is translated into a trusted, culturally relevant presence in the Australian market.You will own brand consistency, narrative, and campaign excellence, while working closely with global marketing, product, PR, compliance, and local partners. This is a senior, hands-on role requiring strong judgment, structure, and maturity in a regulated financial environment.

Основные обязанности

  • Brand Strategy & Positioning
  • Own and evolve Binance Australia’s brand strategy, ensuring alignment with global brand standards while localizing for the Australian market
  • Translate complex crypto concepts into clear, credible, and consumer-friendly messaging
  • Act as brand guardian across all channels (ATL, BTL, digital, partnerships, community, events, content)
  • Integrated Marketing Campaigns
  • Lead end-to-end planning and execution of integrated campaigns (brand, product, education, partnerships)
  • Develop campaign narratives, creative briefs, and go-to-market plans across paid, owned, and earned channels
  • Ensure campaigns balance brand building + measurable growth outcomes
  • Local Market Leadership
  • Be the key marketing lead for Australia, with strong understanding of local consumer behavior, media landscape, and cultural nuances
  • Partner with PR, legal, and compliance teams to ensure messaging is accurate, responsible, and regulator-ready
  • Manage local agencies and creative partners (media, creative, production)
  • Stakeholder & Cross-Functional Collaboration
  • Work closely with global marketing, regional leadership, product, and comms teams
  • Clearly articulate local needs and feedback to global stakeholders
  • Collaborate with partnerships and community teams to amplify brand presence
  • Performance & Brand Health
  • Track and report on brand health metrics (awareness, consideration, trust) alongside campaign KPIs
  • Use insights to refine messaging, channels, and investment strategy
  • Balance long-term brand equity with short-term performance goals

Требования

  • 8–12+ years of marketing experience, ideally in financial services, fintech, crypto, technology, or regulated industries
  • Proven track record in brand marketing and integrated campaigns, not just performance marketing
  • Experience working with global brands and adapting them for local markets
  • Strong agency management experience (creative, media, PR)
  • Excellent brand judgment – understands tone, narrative, and long-term equity
  • Strong strategic thinking with the ability to structure complex problems clearly
  • Comfortable owning messaging in sensitive or regulated contexts
  • Execution & LeadershipHighly self-motivated, structured, and execution-oriented
  • Able to operate independently while aligning with global frameworks
  • Strong communication skills – confident presenting to senior stakeholders

  • Thrive in a results-driven workplace with opportunities for career growth and continuous learning
  • Competitive salary and company benefits
