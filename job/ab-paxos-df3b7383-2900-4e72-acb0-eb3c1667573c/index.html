О Paxos

Today’s financial infrastructure is archaic, expensive, inefficient and risky — supporting a system that leaves out more people than it lets in. So we’re rebuilding it.

We’re on a mission to open the world’s financial system to everyone by enabling the instant movement of any asset, any time, in a trustworthy way. For over a decade, we’ve built blockchain infrastructure that tokenizes, custodies, trades and settles assets for the world's leading financial institutions, like PayPal, Venmo, Mastercard, Interactive Brokers and Charles Schwab.

О команде

Product Security works across engineering to secure our applications and services. Partnering closely with engineering, we integrate security into every stage of development, from design documents to code reviews and CI/CD deployment pipelines. Our focus centers on secure defaults, intelligent automation, and pragmatic solutions rather than bureaucracy. We integrate AI tooling into our everyday testing, code review, and workflow automation. Operating within a highly regulated environment, our work safeguards customer trust and regulatory standing, requiring an exceptionally high standard of execution.

О роли

As a Senior Application Security Engineer, you will act as a key security partner across product and engineering teams, taking ownership of threat modeling, architecture reviews, and application code assessments, and supporting smart contract reviews. You will maintain and advance our automated security tooling to detect vulnerabilities early in the development lifecycle. Given our cloud-native infrastructure, you will continuously evaluate application and infrastructure security in tandem, analyzing deployment pipelines, service boundaries, access controls, and identity management. Rather than addressing vulnerabilities individually, you will focus on building scalable guardrails and secure design patterns, balancing robust security controls with product velocity and user experience. You will write code yourself, building the libraries, guardrails, and fixes, and you will be the security lead for the teams you support, mentoring engineers along the way. You will exercise significant autonomy and directly shape the evolution of our Application Security program.

Что предстоит делать

- Drive security design reviews and threat modeling initiatives for new products and major updates, evaluating both application architecture and cloud deployment strategies.

- Conduct comprehensive security assessments and secure code reviews on critical APIs and services, targeting business logic, data governance, and authorization mechanisms.

- Support reviews of blockchain integrations and smart contracts, collaborating closely with engineering teams and third-party auditors to remediate vulnerabilities.

- Build security guardrails into CI/CD (SAST, SCA, secrets scanning, and supply chain safeguards) and keep findings high-signal to maintain developer trust.

- Triage and prioritize security findings from bug bounties, penetration tests, and automated scanners based on contextual risk, steering them to resolution.

- Write code: build the libraries, guardrails, and fixes yourself instead of handing findings back to teams.

- Establish secure-by-default architecture, reusable libraries, and best-practice frameworks to systematically resolve entire classes of security vulnerabilities.

- Partner with cloud security specialists to enforce application-level controls, including service identity management, network segmentation, and secrets handling.

- Leverage AI platforms to automate workflows, accelerate review and triage capabilities, and expand program impact.

- Assist in incident response efforts for application-layer events and lead post-incident security strengthening initiatives.

- Act as the security lead for the teams you support, and provide actionable security guidance and mentorship to engineering teams to foster a culture of secure development.

О вас

- 5+ years combined in software engineering and application security, including hands-on experience building and shipping production software

- Strong software development and secure code review skills in at least one of Go, Java, Python, or TypeScript, with the ability to quickly evaluate unfamiliar codebases

- Practical expertise in threat modeling as well as web and API security, specifically targeting business logic, authentication, and authorization vulnerabilities

- Demonstrated experience securing cloud-native applications (preferably AWS), covering containers and Kubernetes, IAM, secrets management, service-to-service authentication, and CI/CD pipelines

- Proven ability to scale security mitigation using secure design patterns, automated tooling, and shared frameworks

- Track record of implementing and fine-tuning CI/CD automated security scanners to minimize noise and false positives

- Basic understanding of cryptography and blockchain; applied cryptography or hands-on smart contract audit experience is a plus

- Regular adoption of AI coding and static analysis tools for development and auditing, alongside an informed perspective on their effective application

- Strong verbal and written communication skills to collaborate effectively with software engineers, product management, and engineering leadership

- Proven history of partnering with engineering teams to resolve security vulnerabilities rather than simply cataloging them

- Experience mentoring engineers and acting as the security lead for an area

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience…
