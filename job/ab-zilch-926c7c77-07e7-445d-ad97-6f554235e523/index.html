О роли

As our Engineering Manager you'll play a pivotal role as a servant leader, directing, encouraging, and driving the team to excel. Your expertise will guide technical direction, mentor the team, and contribute hands-on. Day to day, you'll lead teams, design technical aspects, prioritise tasks, and support leadership while fostering best engineering practices. In essence, you'll be the catalyst for innovation and success within our engineering department.

Day-to-day responsibilities will include

  • Leading two agile teams to implement features that deliver significant impact for our customers.
  • Designing technical aspects and architecture of user stories and plans for delivery to production.
  • Prioritising technical debt alongside product deliverables to achieve the best outcomes for Zilch.
  • Supporting the Head of Engineering and CTO and interacting with key senior business stakeholders.
  • Take ownership for overall delivery cadence and quality for your teams.
  • Identifying and removing obstacles and bottlenecks from the team.
  • Drive adoption of best engineering practices to improve delivery.
  • Mentor and coach team of engineers in agile and lean practice.
  • Organising effective knowledge transfer, training, and documentation

Кого мы ищем

  • Someone with bags of energy, is happy, passionate and loves helping development teams deliver.
  • A person who lives and breathes agile and obsesses about continuous improvement.
  • A servant leader who can direct, encourage, assist, track, cajole, and drive a team to want to excel.
  • A technologist who can guide technical direction, mentor a team, and get their hands dirty.
  • A Bachelor and/or master’s degree.
  • 3+ years’ experience leading and managing software engineering teams, including coaching teams on agile practices.
  • Experience in Scrum, Kanban or other immediate type delivery.
  • Previous experience in developing either Java, React, C# etc.
  • 2+ years communicating to key stakeholders.

Bonus skills

  • Experience in solutions architecture.
  • Experience in managing projects.
  • Experience in cloud environments (AWS or equivalent)
  • Experience in managing DevOps, Platform or SRE teams.
