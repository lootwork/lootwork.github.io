We believe that the way people interact with their finances will drastically improve in the next few years. We’re dedicated to empowering this transformation by building the tools and experiences that thousands of developers use to create their own products. Plaid powers the tools millions of people rely on to live a healthier financial life. We work with thousands of companies like Venmo, SoFi, several of the Fortune 500, and many of the largest banks to make it easy for people to connect their financial accounts to the apps and services they want to use. Plaid’s network covers 12,000 financial institutions across the US, Canada, UK and Europe. Founded in 2013, the company is headquartered in San Francisco with offices in New York, Seattle, Washington D.C., Raleigh, London, and Amsterdam.

We are the Fraud ML & AI team within Plaid’s Fraud organization. We leverage the rich relationships and activity across Plaid’s financial network to develop advanced machine learning models that help customers detect and prevent fraud while minimizing friction for legitimate users. Our team operates at the intersection of research and engineering, exploring new signals and innovative modeling techniques, translating research into production-ready solutions, and continuously improving model performance through real-world insights and customer feedback.

As a Staff MLE focused on research, you will set research priorities for Plaid’s fraud models. You will develop methods for learning from connected entities, event sequences, and financial data, then lead the most promising approaches into production with our MLE partners.

You will stay hands-on in modeling and experimentation while guiding other researchers and engineers. You will test graph ML, sequence modeling, and representation learning methods against strong baselines and measure their performance across customers and fraud scenarios.

Обязанности

- Choosing research problems that address gaps in fraud detection across Plaid’s financial network.

- Developing and testing new architectures, training objectives, and learning methods for graph, sequential, and financial data.

- Solving difficult learning problems involving sparse or delayed labels, evolving attacks, and generalization across customers.

- Deploying research models that improve fraud detection within latency, cost, and scale requirements.

- Collaborating with Product, Engineering, Data Science, and DFAI to bring new modeling capabilities to Fraud products.

- Improving the team’s research methods through mentorship, technical reviews, shared code, and communicating findings internally and externally where appropriate.

Требования

- 5+ years of relevant industry or research-lab experience after a PhD, or 7+ years after a master’s degree, with demonstrated technical leadership and research-to-production impact.

- A record of original ML research with practical impact, demonstrated through publications, patents, open-source work, or internal research adopted in products.

- Deep expertise in graph ML, sequence modeling, or representation learning, supported by strong ML and statistical fundamentals.

- Experience designing rigorous experiments, meaningful baselines, ablations, and evaluations that address imperfect labels, leakage, and generalization.

- Strong Python and PyTorch or comparable framework skills, with hands-on experience training models on large datasets and building reproducible, well-tested research code.

- A track record of translating research into deployed models with measurable product or customer impact, including decisions about model quality, latency, cost, and scalability.

- Experience leading multi-quarter research initiatives with open questions: setting direction, coordinating with partner teams, and guiding other researchers and engineers through mentorship and technical review.

- Fluency with LLMs and AI-assisted development tools, using them to accelerate research, implementation, and debugging while validating generated code and results.

- Ability to explain research methods, results, and limitations to research, engineering, and product teams.

Preferred experience

- A PhD in machine learning, computer science, statistics, applied mathematics, or a related field is strongly preferred; equivalent research experience is welcome.

- Fraud, risk, abuse, security, or trust-and-safety modeling experience is strongly preferred.

- Experience with large-scale graph learning, self-supervised pretraining, foundation-model adaptation, or distributed GPU training.

- Publications, open-source contributions, or model interpretability work that demonstrates relevant research depth.

Our mission at Plaid is to unlock financial freedom for everyone. To support that mission, we seek to build a diverse team of driven individuals who care deeply about making the financial ecosystem more equitable. We recognize that strong qualifications can come from both prior work experiences and lived experiences. We encourage you to apply to a role even if your experience doesn't fully match the job description. We are always looking for team members that will bring something unique to Plaid!
