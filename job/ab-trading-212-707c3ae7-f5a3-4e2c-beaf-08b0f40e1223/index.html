О роли

We are looking for a Regulatory Reporting Manager to own our end-to-end regulatory reporting framework and regulatory reporting processes across the group. You will ensure compliance across multiple jurisdictions, drive the evolution of our reporting standards, and play a hands-on role in data validation and submission - all while pushing for greater automation and data quality.

Что предстоит делать

- Own and manage regulatory reports and disclosures across multiple jurisdictions, including Pillar 1, Pillar 3, and EU/German statistical reporting.

- Monitor regulatory developments and implement new requirements, defining the necessary data specifications and reporting logic.

- Drive continuous improvement and automation of reporting processes, controls, and data quality within the regulatory framework.

- Identify opportunities to streamline capital adequacy workflows and reduce manual effort.

- Lead interdisciplinary projects and take ownership of regulatory matters (e.g., processes for new products, expansion into new markets, licence applications).

- Serve as a key point of contact for external partners and regulatory authorities on reporting and capital adequacy topics.

WHAT YOU NEED TO HAVE

- Completed degree in Finance, Accounting, Law, Engineering, or Data Science.

- At least 3 years of relevant professional experience, gained either in regulatory reporting at a bank or in consulting/auditing at a Big 4 firm.

- Solid understanding of prudential regulatory frameworks (CRR/CRD, IFR/IFD).

- High reliability, a strong eye for detail, and excellent communication skills.

- Ability to work independently and thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic environment within a rapidly expanding organization.

- Fluency in English for internal team communication is essential.

IT’S A BONUS IF YOU HAVE

- Experience with ICAAP and/or ICARA processes and familiarity with Pillar 1 capital requirements and Pillar 3 disclosure obligations.

- Experience with regulatory reporting software.

- German proficiency.

Что мы предлагаем

- A unique opportunity to be part of the team that builds our presence in Germany from the ground up.

- Challenging projects with high impact on our international growth.

- Collaboration with a team of smart, driven, and supportive high-performers.
