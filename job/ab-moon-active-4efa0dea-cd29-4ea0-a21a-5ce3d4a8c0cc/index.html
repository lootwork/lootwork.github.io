Moon Active is a global leader in mobile gaming, creating entertainment experiences enjoyed by millions of players worldwide. Our games operate at massive scale, processing billions of events daily, and our engineering teams tackle complex technical challenges that push the boundaries of mobile game development.

As a Unity Developer you’ll be working on one of the mobile gaming industry’s most complex and successful products, a large-scale, highly-optimized Unity codebase that serves millions of concurrent users globally. This isn’t your typical mobile game project. It’s a sophisticated, ever-evolving system that demands exceptional architectural thinking, performance optimisation expertise, and the ability to maintain quality at scale.

Responsibilities

- Manage features from system design and data handling to production deployment.

- Design architectures and implement end-to-end client-side features, leveraging LLMs from day-to-day development processes up to system architecture.

- Use data-driven methods to resolve production issues and optimize performance across all devices.

- Manage the integration of native plugins and third-party services.

Requirements

- 2+ years of professional experience in software development.

- Hands-on experience with Claude Code, Cursor or similar.

- Experience with object-oriented programming, including design patterns, algorithms, and data structures.

- Strong knowledge of an OOP language (C# or equivalent) and memory management.

- Proactive, self-driven, with strong communication skills.

- Experience with Unity on mobile platforms, including its scripting APIs, profiling tools, UI, and animations.

- Native Ukrainian speaker.
