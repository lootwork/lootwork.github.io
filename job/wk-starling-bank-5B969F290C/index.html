We have a Hybrid approach to working here at Starling - our preference is that you're located within a commutable distance of one of our offices so that we're able to interact and collaborate in person.

Our Engineering Environment

Starling engineers are excited about helping us deliver new features, regardless of what their primary tech stack may be. Hear from the team in our latest blogs or our case studies with Women in Tech.

We are looking for engineers at all levels to join the team. We value people being engaged and caring about customers, caring about the code they write and the contribution they make to Starling. People with a broad ability to apply themselves to a multitude of problems and challenges, who can work across teams do great things here at Starling, to continue changing banking for good.

As an Infrastructure Engineer at Starling, you’ll be building and supporting infrastructure that spans across physical data centres and also our cloud estates. We have a presence in both AWS and GCP. Your focus will be on supporting our internal operations and interfacing with our software engineering teams to deliver the infrastructure that drives our business, all while building security in. This role is ideal for someone who has experience of working in physical data centres who is looking to gather enterprise multi cloud experience

Требования

  • Strong background in data centre network topologies, BGP routing, and VRF configuration for secure traffic isolation.
  • Experience of working with firewalls
  • Working knowledge of Linux administration
  • Experience or awareness Infrastructure as Code and infrastructure provisioning tools
  • System architecture and design
  • Experience with modern development practices and using Python/Golang or scripting languages like bash
  • Experience in building and maintaining containers

What skills are desirable

  • Knowledge of core cloud services
  • Experience running services in Kubernetes
  • Track record of maintaining compliant environments e.g. PCI-DSS
  • Experience of developing clean and highly maintainable code: creating and improving existing infrastructure services and tooling - primarily in Golang

Duties of the role will entail

  • Working with functional delivery teams to translate business requirements into technical implementations
  • Making changes to critical physical infrastructure that supports the bank
  • Designing and building patterns and frameworks to be used by other engineering teams
  • Introducing automation where appropriate to stop unnecessary toil
  • Taking ownership of projects and ensuring timely delivery
  • Diagnosing system and network failures under pressure
  • Familiarity with distributed systems and immutable infrastructure
  • Keeping abreast of new technologies and changes in the industry
  • Working with other teams, like our Security team, Developer Experience teams

Our process

Interviewing is a two way process and we want you to have the time and opportunity to get to know us, as much as we are getting to know you! Our interviews are conversational and we want to get the best from you, so come with questions and be curious. In general you can expect the below, following a chat with one of our Talent Team:

  • Initial phone call - ~45 minutes
  • Take home technical test to be discussed and extended during the technical interview - ~3 hours
  • Technical interview - ~1.5 hours
  • Final Interview - ~40 minutes
