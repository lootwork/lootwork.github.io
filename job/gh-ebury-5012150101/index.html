Senior Operations Analyst

Regional Operations

Ebury Sydney Office - Hybrid: 4 days in the office, 1 day working from home per week

Что предстоит делать

  • Reconciliation of client funds received across Ebury’s network of bank accounts, across all back office systems, in accordance with internal processes.
  • Instruction and verification of client payments, in accordance with internal processes and in line with internal SLAs.
  • Completion and verification of clients beneficiaries, in accordance with internal processes.
  • Conduct post-payment investigations, following up with Ebury’s Liquidity Providers and Partner Banks until full resolution.
  • Contact Ebury’s Liquidity Providers and Partner Banks to ensure transaction queries are prioritized and incidents are investigated promptly.
  • Conduct updates and 4-eyes controls on client information as part of Ebury’s Ongoing Due Diligence, adhering to the relevant processes and procedures.
  • Review of transactions and 4-eyes approval subject to Financial Crime Screening review, adhering to established processes and procedures and escalating as appropriate.
  • Help clients to resolve questions or issues regarding their transactions and payments, on email, phone and chat, in a manner appropriate to the nature of the query and the client in question.
  • Take and act on instructions from clients interacting with Ebury offline, in accordance with internal processes.
  • Communicate the status of tasks to colleagues, supervisors and managers, and escalate problems swiftly, as needed.
  • Support in the delivery of induction training for new starters as well as continue to support colleague learning and development.
  • Resolve day-to-day issues arising during daily processes, and escalating more complex challenges via the appropriate channel.
  • Support in ad-hoc projects within the department and wider business, as required.
  • Contribute constructive feedback on processes, with the aim of improving efficiency, service, and/or risk
  • Adhere to all internal policies, processes and procedures.
  • Quality standard, dealing with substandard work productively & constructively.
  • Uphold and reinforce company and departmental values.
  • Ability to consistently meet Quality KPI's / SLA’s as determined by the role.
  • Help all analysts to understand accountability and importance of owning outputs.
  • Takes responsibility and ownership of any allocated workload and takes accountability for the output of team members needing support.

What you’ll need

  • Understanding of the main FX products offered by Ebury, including spot, fixed forward, window forward and non-deliverable forward, and the cash movements associated with these.
  • Advanced knowledge of low and high-value payment clearing schemes in the different jurisdictions in which Ebury operates.
  • Advanced knowledge of SWIFT in a cross-border payments context, including MX and MT message types.
  • Strong communication skills, both verbal and written
  • Highly empathetic, with strong active listening skills
  • Understanding of fraud risks present in a payments environment, including red flags and indicators of suspicious or concerning activity.
  • Understanding risks relevant to the payments and FX market, and the red flags that are indicators of these.
  • Client-oriented and service-driven, with the willingness to go the extra mile to exceed client expectations.
  • Proactive, with the ability to work effectively within a fast-paced and high pressure environment.
  • Highly organised, with the ability to work to deadlines, multi-task, and prioritise and re-prioritise workload as needed.
  • Diligent, with excellent attention to detail.
  • Effective critical thinking skills, with the ability to find solutions to problems independently.
  • Co-operative, and comfortable working in a team environment.
  • Curious and engaged, takes ownership of own learning journey by asking questions and seeking out new information.
  • Adaptable, with the ability to cope with changing products, processes and procedures.
  • Motivated and driven, with a desire to make Ebury a success.
  • Experience with the FX products offered by Ebury, including spot, fixed forward, window forward and non-deliverable forward, and the cash movements associated with these will be considered.

Skills

  • Proactive problem solver who takes immediate ownership of daily operational bottlenecks and independently resolves or escalates complex issues.
  • Collaborative team player, with the energy to support team cohesion and mentor junior analysts to help scale Ebury’s Asia Pacific business operations.
  • Highly numerate and detail-oriented, with strong analytical skills to identify patterns, errors, or data discrepancies in operational workflows.
  • Strong organizational and time-management skills, with the proven ability to meet tight SLAs, multi-task, and efficiently execute daily deliverables.
  • Strong interpersonal and communication skills to build seamless working relationships with immediate peers, internal departments, and external banking partners.
  • Agile and highly adaptable, with a proven capacity to quickly learn, adopt, and master new products, systems, and changing operational procedures.
