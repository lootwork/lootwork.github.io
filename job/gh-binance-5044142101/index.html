О Binance Accelerator Program

Binance Accelerator Program (BAP) is a 3-6 month internship program designed for Early Career talent to have firsthand experience in the rapidly expanding digital assets space. You will be given the opportunity to develop your skills at Binance and understand what it’s like to work at the world's leading blockchain ecosystem. As part of your internship in the BAP, there will also be opportunities for networking and development, which will expand your professional network and build transferable skills to propel you forward in your career. О роли

As a User Communications Intern on the Binance User Comms team, you will help shape how millions of Chinese-speaking users experience Binance. You will review and refine the Chinese announcements and FAQs that business units publish to a global audience; ensuring every piece reads natively, and that it meets Binance's standards for accuracy, clarity, and tone. Your reviews will catch discrepancies against English source content before they reach users, and your feedback will help build the glossaries, guidelines, and AI-assisted tooling that raise the bar for Chinese content across the company. You will work closely with teams across every Binance business unit, alongside the wider content and social teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Review Chinese announcements and FAQs for linguistic accuracy, readability, and brand consistency for Chinese-speaking audience.
  • Cross-check Chinese content against English source materials to identify inaccuracies, inconsistencies, misleading expressions, and potential risks before publication.
  • Deliver reviews within tight turnaround times, prioritizing effectively and handling urgent and P0 content with speed and care.
  • Ensure consistent terminology, formatting, and style across all publications.
  • Support end-to-end publishing operations, uploading content to the CMS and coordinating localization and distribution to other channels.
  • Identify recurring content and language issues, review existing templates, and contribute feedback that shapes the team's review guidelines, glossaries, and SOPs; assist in developing AI-assisted tools for announcement review.

Кого мы ищем

  • Proficiency in Chinese with strong writing skills and a sharp eye for language nuances.
  • Good command of English (IELTS 6.5 or above), with the ability to accurately understand English source materials.
  • Meticulous and detail-oriented; announcements are dense with dates, numbers, and product details, and small errors matter.
  • Excellent proofreading, editing, and communication skills, with the ability to multi-task and meet tight deadlines in a fast-paced environment with shifting priorities.
  • Comfortable using AI-assisted writing and productivity tools to support reviews.
  • Stay up to date with trending topics on social media.
  • Interest in crypto and blockchain, and familiarity with Binance products, is a plus.
  • Availability: Full-time (5 days per week), remote, 6-month contract.
