Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy.

The Creative team brings the Toast brand to life across campaigns, product launches, events, and film. Video is one of our fastest-growing channels, and teams across Marketing and Sales want more of it.

As a Video Designer, you'll help teams across Toast make more on-brand video, faster. You'll build the templates that let non-designers create their own videos, design motion graphics and edits for campaigns, and manage video operations that keep our video library organized so great work gets found and reused. You love motion, care about craft, and get excited about building systems that help other people do great work.

A day in the life (Responsibilities)

Self-service video

  • Design, build, and maintain video templates in our self-service tool with reusable content blocks that help teams across Toast create on-brand video on their own.
  • Review videos made by cross-functional teams and give clear, constructive feedback that keeps quality high.
  • Create and update internal documentation like guides, checklists, and how-to resources that make self-service easy for others.
  • Explore AI tools that help teams make better videos, faster.
  • Continuously check in with teams to learn how templates are used and what questions come up, and turn that into improvements.

Motion design and editing

  • Create animations, motion graphics, and transitions in After Effects and Premiere Pro.
  • Edit short-form video for campaigns, paid, and events, including cutdowns, resizes, and captions.
  • Source and select b-roll, product UI, and imagery for in-house edits.
  • Deliver quick-turn video and motion assets for campaigns and creative tests.

Video operations

  • Manage our video library: uploading/publishing, tagging, and tracking final videos so they're easy to find and reuse in one place for sharing across the organization.
  • Manage naming conventions and folder structures for consistency across our creative tools.
  • Organize raw footage and b-roll.
  • Pull video performance data and help build simple, automated reporting.
  • Be a go-to resource when teams need to find a video or have a question about video.
