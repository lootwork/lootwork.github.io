Who is SkyBox Labs?

Recognized as one of BC's Top Employers (2025), SkyBox Labs is a game development studio that works on some of the world’s most iconic games. The studio is currently working on projects in the Minecraft universe, supporting the dev team at Bungie on Destiny 2, supporting development of Fallout 76, in addition to work on unannounced IP. Since our inception in 2011, SkyBox Labs has evolved from a small team of eight individuals to a fully established team of Engineers, Artists, Animators, Designers, Producers, and more. Come join our hybrid team in our Burnaby, BC studio today!

PLEASE NOTE: At this time, we are only able to hire candidates who are legally eligible to work in Canada and reside within Canada.

Join one of Vancouver’s fastest growing game teams, on a cutting-edge AAA title set in an upcoming immersive world! We are embarking on an ambitious new project within a highly anticipated universe. As an experienced World Artist on our team, you won’t just be following orders—you will have genuine agency and high impact over key world-building elements. If you are a proactive, collaborative artist ready to help define beautiful, highly playable environments, we want to hear from you.

Please note: this role is on a hybrid work team, requiring 3-days a week in our beautiful Burnaby studio.

A SNAPSHOT OF YOUR ROLE

  • Asset Modelling & Kit Creation: Create both hard surface and organic assets to build complex and diverse scenes across the world.
  • Layout & Composition: Create compelling layouts in Unreal Engine 5 while collaborating closely with Level Design to balance gameplay flow for spatial organization and composition.
  • World Integration: Build, populate, and refine world environments, open-world areas, and thematic Points of Interest (interior and exterior)
  • Optimization: Balance visual quality with technical performance limits to ensure scenes run smoothly at target frame rates.
  • Cross-discipline collaboration: Work actively with a multidisciplinary experienced team to ensure visual fidelity and seamless gameplay execution.

SOUND LIKE YOU? HERE'S WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

Must haves

  • Professional experience:
  • Multiple years of experience working as an intermediate or senior Artist on a AA or AAA game team.
  • Portfolio Requirements:
  • A strong, complete portfolio showcasing various environmental assets, complete environments with asset placement, strong compositional skills, and attention to detail.
  • Clear breakdowns showing your specific scene contributions alongside individual asset work is preferred.
  • Style & Aesthetic:
  • Proven experience with semi-realistic to hyper-realistic environments and assets.
  • Technical Skills & Tools:
  • Unreal Engine 5: Working knowledge of UE5
  • 3D Modeling & Sculpting: Proficient with Maya for hard-surface modeling and scene assembly, and ZBrush for high-poly organic sculpting.
  • Core Competencies:
  • Proactive communication: Highly engaged and articulate; driven by daily collaboration.
  • Drive & Adaptability: Self-motivated, highly adaptable to evolving project needs, and comfortable taking ownership of deliverables in a dynamic environment.

Nice-to-haves

  • Specialized experience with the foliage creation pipeline and Speedtree.
  • Organic asset modeling and Zbrush.
  • Knowledge creating or modifying materials in Unreal Engine to achieve visual targets.
