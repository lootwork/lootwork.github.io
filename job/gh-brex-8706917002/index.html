Что предстоит делать

As a Senior Software Engineer on the Accounting Engineering team, you will build the platform and product surfaces that turn Brex activity into accurate, usable accounting data. Accounting is a core component of our product offering and often a strategic differentiator. Your focus will be the systems finance teams depend on to close their books: reusable accounting records, rules, and mappings, reliable ERP integrations with NetSuite, QuickBooks, Xero, and others, and AI-native automation that predicts accounting work while keeping customers in control. You will work across APIs, data models, and distributed systems where correctness and auditability are non-negotiable, while collaborating with product teams across Brex to extend accounting capabilities without one-off solutions.

Where you'll work

This role will be based in our São Paulo office. We are a hybrid environment that combines the energy and connections of being in the office with the benefits and flexibility of working from home. We currently require a minimum of three coordinated days in the office per week: Monday, Wednesday, and Thursday. As a perk, we also have up to four weeks per year of fully remote work.

Обязанности

  • Build and maintain the core accounting platform, including reusable records, rules, mappings, and versioned APIs that Brex product teams and external partners extend without one-off solutions.
  • Own ERP connectivity end-to-end, building and scaling reliable integrations with NetSuite, QuickBooks, Xero, SAP, and Oracle, including sync, error recovery, and rate-limit handling.
  • Implement customer-facing close experiences for reviewing, configuring, reconciling, and exporting financial activity in our React/TypeScript dashboard.
  • Collaborate with Product, Design, Data Science, and Implementation teams to translate complex accounting and ERP concepts into experiences that feel simple for finance teams.
  • Design distributed systems where correctness is non-negotiable, making idempotency, reconciliation, traceability, and proactive issue detection first-class parts of the architecture.
  • Own problems end-to-end, from user experience and API design to data models, scalability, and ongoing observability.
  • Work with ML models in production, integrating predicted GL coding and mappings into accounting workflows while preserving explainability and human review.
  • Participate in on-call rotation and maintain high operational standards for mission-critical systems.

Требования

  • 5+ years of full-time software engineering experience with a focus on backend development.
  • 2+ years of experience building systems in a domain where correctness and reliability were core requirements, such as fintech, payments, financial systems, or another regulated industry (e.g. healthcare, legal).
  • Strong curiosity for digging deep into the technical problems, and a track record of owning significant technical scope and delivering high quality solutions.
  • Experience designing APIs, data models, and platform capabilities that serve many different customer workflows and configurations.
  • Proficiency in backend technologies (e.g., Kotlin, Java, TypeScript) and comfort with our stack (Kotlin and Micronaut).
  • Experience architecting, developing, and testing full-stack applications end-to-end.
  • Comfort working with databases (e.g., PostgreSQL), event streaming (e.g., Kafka), and distributed systems.
  • A high bar for engineering standards: code quality, testing, observability, and operational excellence, and a habit of raising that bar in others through review and mentorship.
  • Strong written and verbal English communication and interpersonal abilities.

Дополнительный плюс

  • Direct experience with accounting systems or ERPs such as NetSuite, QuickBooks, Xero, SAP, or Oracle, either building integrations or working with them as a practitioner.
  • Familiarity with accounting concepts such as the monthly close, general ledger coding, reconciliation, or double-entry bookkeeping.
  • Experience building third-party integrations at scale, including handling partner API limits, schema drift, and asynchronous failure recovery.
  • Experience shipping ML- or LLM-powered product features, particularly where explainability and human review matter.
  • Experience with platform or API products consumed by other engineering teams or external developers.
  • Experience leading technical design across multiple teams or mentoring engineers as a formal or informal tech lead.…
