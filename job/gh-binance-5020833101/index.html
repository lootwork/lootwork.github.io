Binance is a leading global blockchain ecosystem behind the world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume and registered users. О роли

  • We are looking for a Growth Marketing Manager for Kazakhstan to take full ownership of growth initiatives in one of Binance’s top priority markets. This role requires a hands-on, data-driven marketer who can combine user acquisition, lifecycle management, and reactivation campaigns with non-standard growth activations. You will design and execute experiments, optimise the funnel, and ensure Binance stays the #1 crypto brand in Kazakhstan.

Что предстоит делать

  • User Growth & Reactivation: Launch non-standard activations to engage and reactivate the existing userbase;
  • Unlock new user growth via creative innovations: new product offerings, interactive mechanics, A/B testing, and lifecycle optimisation;
  • Map and improve the entire user journey (Visit → Registration → KYC → Deposit → Trade) to boost business conversion funnel;
  • Promotions: Drive and execute series of educational and bonus-based campaigns;
  • Accountability & Reporting: Fully own market OKRs completion, frequently escalate Metrics Progress & Campaigns Performance with Marketing & Operations team.

Кого мы ищем

  • 2–4 years of relevant experience in Growth, Digital, or Performance Marketing (preferably crypto, tech/IT, fintech, e-commerce, or mobile apps);
  • Proven expertise on how activate audience of financial products (landing pages for activation creation, lifecycle campaigns, re-activation of churn audience)
  • Execution-oriented mindset with strong ownership and independence;
  • Analytical and data-driven, with hands-on A/B testing and product optimisation experience;
  • Native-level Russian / Kazakh + strong English proficiency;
  • Passion for crypto / blockchain / fintech, staying up-to-date with industry trends.

Будет плюсом

  • Background in traditional finance industries with proven growth playbooks;
  • Previous experience in crypto marketing or fintech products.

  • Thrive in a results-driven workplace with opportunities for career growth and continuous learning
