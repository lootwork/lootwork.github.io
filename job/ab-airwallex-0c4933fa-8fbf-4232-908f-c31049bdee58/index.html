О команде

As the engineering organization matures, the infrastructure teams are key to Airwallex’s ability to build a globally distributed platform, increase engineer productivity, and ensure effective use of resources.

Our team works to provide the best possible experience for our developers and customers while providing a safe and secure environment to ensure that our financial solutions are always available.

Что предстоит делать

- Maintain and expand Airwallex’s global cloud network infrastructure into new regions and markets while adhering to strict regulatory and compliance requirements.

- Help design the team structure and size for the future.

- Mentor and train junior engineers.

- Design, implement, and maintain highly available and secure production network infrastructure across LAN, WAN, VPN, and cloud environments spanning multiple global data centres and cloud regions.

- Manage global connectivity over international leased lines and domestic internet connections.

- Own the configuration and troubleshooting of complex network devices, including routers, switches, firewalls, load balancers, and cloud environments.

- Act as a first responder or escalation point for complex network issues, running incidents through to completion and producing after-action reports.

- Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including InfoSec, DevOps, Database, Corporate IT, and Facilities, to integrate network solutions and support business initiatives across multiple regions.

- Work with technical and non-technical teams to troubleshoot network issues across physical and cloud environments.

- Develop and maintain comprehensive network documentation, including diagrams, configurations, and operational procedures.

- Evaluate and recommend networking technologies and solutions to support business growth.

- Participate in a global on-call rotation, providing primary response for production and infrastructure issues.

- Support pre-scheduled network upgrades and other flexible scheduling requirements as needed to minimise business disruption.

- Investigate and propose practical solutions to improve the networking team’s visibility and operational capabilities.

MUST HAVES

- CCNP / JNCIP-level experience demonstrating a strong networking foundation.

- Demonstrated experience in a mixed networking-vendor environment, such as Juniper, Palo Alto, Cisco, Aruba, or equivalent, in a global company.

- Strong AWS network design and operations experience, with at least 3 years working with AWS networking in production.

- Strong documentation habits and an understanding of change-control processes.

- Basic understanding of GCP networking concepts.

- Understanding of AI-assisted workflows and practical use cases.

- Solid understanding of command-line configuration and troubleshooting for networking hardware and vendors, including JunOS, IOS, or equivalent.

- Willingness to learn new technologies and skills.

- Strong understanding of core BGP and route-filtering principles.

- An outcome-oriented approach.

- Collaborative and supportive work habits.

- Ability to assess the impact of changes and incidents and manage them appropriately.
