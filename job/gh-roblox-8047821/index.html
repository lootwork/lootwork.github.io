О команде

The CI and CD Foundation team is the backbone of our engineering velocity. Our mission is to engineer and deliver a CI/CD ecosystem that streamlines the integration and delivery of our core client code—including the Game Engine, Studio, and User Apps.

We are laser-focused on three goals: enhancing overall build reliability, maximizing developer productivity, and ensuring consistent software quality. We’re building and improving pipelines and tools to transform SDLC in the age of AI-driven software development.

О роли

As a Principal Software Engineer on the CI/CD Foundation team, you will serve as the chief technical strategist and architect responsible for transforming how Roblox builds, tests, and ships code at massive scale.

Our build and delivery ecosystem handles immense complexity: decades of C++ game engine code, extensive Lua applications, complex native platforms, and a sprawling monorepo with massive, growing test suites. You will lead the technical vision to modernize, decouple, and accelerate this ecosystem—driving radical improvements across our Local Dev Inner Loop (pre-commit/pre-push), CI (PR validation, speculative execution, merge queues), and CD (OTA updates and binary releases).

We need a visionary builder who deeply understands developer productivity engineering at scale—someone who can architect advanced systems (like custom Submit Queues or intelligent Merge Trains) and harness modern agentic AI workflows to build an autonomous, lightning-fast developer platform.

Вам предстоит

  • Architect CI/CD Strategy: Set and execute the multi-year technical vision for Roblox's build, test, and release platforms. Overhaul legacy pipelines into an automated, observable, and resilient foundation capable of scaling with our rapidly growing engineering org.
  • Master the Inner & Outer Loops: Dramatically reduce developer friction by modernizing the local inner loop (fast local feedback, smart caching, pre-commit optimization) and scaling the outer loop (distributed testing, fast artifact promotion, and automated OTA/binary deployments).
  • Build Concurrency & Merge Solutions: Solve complex, multi-repo merge and build bottlenecks by architecting high-throughput submission engines (e.g., modern Submit Queue systems) that handle high-velocity code insertion without breaking main branches.
  • Pioneer Agentic AI & Automation: Lead the integration of modern agentic AI tools and intelligent automation to autonomously diagnose build failures, optimize test suites, auto-remediate flaky tests, and eliminate repetitive developer chores.
  • Partner Cross-Functionally: Collaborate directly with Client, Engine, Mobile, and Infrastructure leads to understand their specialized stack requirements (C++, Lua, Native Mobile) and translate them into unified, world-class developer tooling.
  • Set Technical Excellence: Drive architectural reviews, establish technical standards, and mentor senior and staff engineers across the Developer Platform organization.

Кто вы

  • Domain Mastery (12+ Years): You have a proven track record as a Principal/Staff+ engineer architecting and scaling enterprise-grade CI/CD infrastructure, distributed build systems, and developer platforms at hyper-scale.
  • Deep Systems & Infrastructure Knowledge: You understand the mechanics of large-scale builds (C++, Mobile, Monorepos), artifact dependency graphs, distributed caching, container orchestration (AWS, Kubernetes, Nomad, Docker), and observability pipelines.
  • Algorithmic & Distributed Systems Problem Solver: You have hands-on experience (or deep architectural familiarity) with advanced CI concepts like speculative build execution, merge queues/submit queues, distributed test execution, and dynamic build graph generation.
  • Strong Polyglot Engineer: Proficient in systems and tooling languages (such as C++, Go, Python, or Rust) with a history of writing high-performance platform tools.
  • Pioneer in AI Tooling: You actively leverage and experiment with cutting-edge AI software engineering tools (e.g., Claude Code, Codex, agentic workflows) and know how to integrate AI to turn manual dev processes into self-healing, autonomous platforms.
  • Data & Observability Driven: You rely on build telemetry, pipeline tracing, and developer friction metrics to pinpoint bottlenecks and quantify developer velocity gains.
