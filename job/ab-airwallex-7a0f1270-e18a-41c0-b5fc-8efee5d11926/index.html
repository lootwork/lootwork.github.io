О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

How you’ll make impact

You’ll help build the infrastructure behind reliable, secure payment processing for businesses around the world. You’ll work across real-time transaction scenarios and contribute to systems that make online payments easier to integrate and safer to use.

- Build infrastructure services that improve payment processing capabilities, strengthen security, and increase customer trust.

- Design, develop, and maintain APIs, services, and systems that support a reliable global payment processing flow.

- Collaborate with engineering teams across Airwallex’s global offices, as well as users and financial partners, to deliver seamless integrations.

- Own critical components of Airwallex’s online payment processing flow, troubleshoot production issues across the stack, and continuously improve system reliability.

You'll work from our London office three days a week.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- 6+ years of software engineering experience.

- Experience designing elegant systems that abstract complex patterns, particularly in the financial industry.

- High standards when working with production systems.

- Experience making decisions with diverse stakeholders and subject matter experts in collaborative environments.

- Enjoyment of working with diverse groups of people and bridging gaps between teams and areas of expertise.

Preferred

- Proficiency with HTTP and RESTful specifications.

- Familiarity with OAuth 2.0 or JWT.

- Experience with Kafka or RabbitMQ.

- Familiarity with Nginx or Istio.

- Experience designing, implementing, and maintaining complex public API products, including user integration guides.

You’ll thrive here if

- You hold yourself to a high standard even when no one is watching. Quality and accountability aren’t external expectations, they’re internal ones.

Learn more about your team

Payments Platform at Airwallex builds the infrastructure that turns global payment complexity into reliable, scalable payment experiences, helping businesses accept and process money across markets with less friction. The challenge is balancing performance, reliability, local complexity, and commercial outcomes in the same system, where small improvements can materially affect conversion and failures are immediately visible.

Within Payments Platform, Airi builds and scales the identity and product capabilities behind secure, one-click checkout for shoppers. Dynamic tokenization helps strengthen checkout security, while Airi’s work lays the groundwork for a future where AI agents can complete purchases on shoppers’ behalf.
