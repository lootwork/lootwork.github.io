Join us in building the future of finance.

Our mission is to democratize finance for all. An estimated $124 trillion of assets will be inherited by younger generations in the next two decades. The largest transfer of wealth in human history. If you’re ready to be at the epicenter of this historic cultural and financial shift, keep reading.

О team + role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world's biggest financial problems. We're looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn't a place for complacency, it's where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We're a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

From day one, we've been breaking down barriers, empowering people with the tools, knowledge, and products to participate in the financial system. Our innovation doesn't stop — and we need bright, curious minds to help shape the future of finance!

As an Associate Product Manager, you'll be at the center of our product innovation. You'll work alongside top talent from engineering, design, research, and more to bring groundbreaking financial products to life. From simplifying complex financial processes to crafting seamless customer experiences, your work will help shape the future of fintech. This role offers direct mentorship from some of the most accomplished product leaders in the industry, giving you the chance to grow, learn, and make a real impact in a fast-paced, cutting-edge environment.

This role is based in our New York, NY office, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Collaborate with engineering, design, research, data science, and marketing teams to deeply understand our customers and their most pressing needs
  • Define and execute product strategies that align with business goals and user expectations
  • Be the voice of the product, facilitating communication and collaboration across teams
  • Lead product launches, ensuring they are delivered on time and meet a high standard of quality
  • Set and monitor KPIs to measure success and track product engagement and adoption

Что вы приносите

  • Currently enrolled in a full-time, degree-seeking program in Engineering, Computer Science, Business, Economics, or a related field, graduating between Winter 2026 and Summer 2027
  • 0–1 years of experience in product management, product development, or related roles; internship or co-op experience in fintech or tech is a plus
  • Sharp analytical and technical skills, a problem-solving mindset, and the ability to make data-driven decisions
  • Demonstrated leadership and communication abilities, with experience thriving in collaborative, cross-functional teams
  • An entrepreneurial spirit — takes ownership of projects, turns ideas into action, and drives innovation
  • A passion for fintech and a desire to learn more about financial products, markets, and emerging technologies
