О роли

We're looking for a Senior Product Manager who uses data to drive growth in our trading products. You'll own the trading experience, growth experiments, and market data features, turning user insights into solutions that are tested and shipped. You'll work across the full product cycle, from tracking and A/B testing to integrating third-party market data, and partner with Engineering, Design, Data, and Legal/Compliance to balance user needs, business goals, and regional regulations.

Что предстоит делать

  • Optimize the trading experience by analyzing user journeys and trading flows, identifying key pain points, and driving improvements in usability, conversion, and retention.
  • Drive growth experiments by building hypotheses, designing and running A/B tests, defining success metrics, and adjusting product direction based on results.
  • Build data tracking for trading products, including event instrumentation, metric definitions, and validation mechanisms that reliably support behavior analysis and impact measurement.
  • Analyze user behavior by independently exploring product data, including user segments, conversion funnels, and retention, and turn insights into product decisions and priorities.
  • Plan market data products by defining requirements for and integrating third-party market data, planning front-end market displays and data reporting features, and working with partner teams to validate data quality and user experience.
  • Coordinate cross-functional delivery by building the product roadmap with Engineering, Design, Data, and Legal/Compliance, and managing dependencies, timelines, and launch impact.
  • Implement regional compliance requirements by working with Legal/Compliance to translate local rules into concrete product changes and coordinating build, acceptance, and launch.

Кого мы ищем

  • 5+ years of product management experience, including hands-on experience in market data/financial data products or product growth.
  • Ability to independently explore and analyze data, using analytics tools to frame problems, form hypotheses, and support prioritization and impact evaluation with data.
  • Cross-functional collaboration experience with Engineering, Design, Data, or other business teams, with the ability to define clear requirements, align different viewpoints, and drive delivery.
  • Product leadership or team coordination experience, such as acting as Product Owner, Project Lead, or Mentor to guide product direction, coordinate work, or coach others.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience partnering with data teams to integrate third-party market data and deliver user-facing stock market data features.
  • Proficiency in SQL or another programming language for querying, processing, and analyzing data independently.
  • Experience working with Legal or Compliance teams to turn regulatory requirements into shipped product solutions.
