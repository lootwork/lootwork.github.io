Regulatory Compliance Officer

Our dynamic APAC Regulatory team is looking for a Regulatory Compliance Officer. The successful candidate will help build and scale Adyen’s business in Japan and be able to execute on the Regulatory Japan team strategy, be a trusted business partner and enable Adyen to create unique business opportunities while making ethical choices.

Что предстоит делать

  • Provide strategic regulatory advice towards internal and external stakeholders, as well as to the senior management, in line with Adyen’s commercial and strategic objectives which allow for scale;
  • Anticipate and identify key regulatory obligations and integrity risks, and ensure these are addressed in a practical and scalable way;
  • Transform identified obligations into relevant Policies and Procedures to be communicated and implemented across the global business;
  • Represent Regulatory on a variety of work streams and products to establish regulatory strategies that align with global objectives and ensure controlled and compliant growth;
  • Drive alignment with global stakeholders, including implementing new processes, leading process changes, or managing incidents; communicate effectively, ensure understanding of complex issues, and execute tasks with focus.
  • Act as a point of contact for regulator interactions. Your day-to-day main responsibilities may change according to Adyen’s strategy, focus and priorities. If this happens, it will be communicated to you.
  • Monitor regulatory developments in the legislative and policy environment and advise on its impact to Adyen.
  • Operationalise regulatory requirements in Japan.

Кто вы

  • You have a minimum 7 years of experience in a support function within a legal or compliance team
  • You have strong written and oral communication and interpersonal skills
  • You have strong research, statutory interpretation and organisation skills
  • You are a meticulous person with strong attention to detail
  • You will be a critical thinker, strategic and have innovative problem-solving skills
  • Your strong sense of initiative, independence and ability to take ownership enabling you to contribute to a fast-growing business
