Кто мы

О Privy

Our mission is to make privacy and user ownership the default online. We build simple, flexible developer tooling that make it easy to build products that put users first. By leveraging modern cryptography, we shift the status quo around digital ownership and protect the accounts and assets of millions of users.

Learn more about Privy: Privy and Stripe: Bringing crypto to everyone

О команде

Data at Privy is not just about running queries or building reports. We transform messy, high-volume data into scalable systems and clear insights that guide product decisions and business strategy. We design pipelines that are resilient, models that are intuitive, and tools that make data accessible and trustworthy for everyone across the company. We believe in clean abstractions, transparency, and sharing our work so teammates can build on top of it. We push each other to ask sharper questions, test bold ideas, and follow the signal in the data so we can shape better products and empower our customers.

Что предстоит делать

In this role you’ll partner deeply with product, engineering, and GTM teams across Privy to ensure that our users, our products, and our business have the models, data products, and insights needed to make decisions and grow responsibly. You’ll work daily with partners across the Privy business to extract insights and build pipelines from the rich and complex Privy data set which is a unique combination of SaaS and blockchain data. You’ll build data products, metrics, scalable data pipelines, dashboards and reports to inform and run the business. You’ll deliver actionable business recommendations through analyses and data storytelling while also providing data infrastructure that allows users and stakeholders to self serve their own analyses.

Кто вы

  • 5+ years in an analytics engineering or data analyst role, preferably in a SaaS or developer tools environment.
  • Proficiency in SQL and data modeling techniques.
  • Experience with ETL processes and tools (e.g., Snowflake/Databricks, dbt, or similar).
  • Familiarity with analytics tools like Looker, Tableau, or similar BI platforms.
  • Familiarity with AI native tooling (e.g. claude code, codex, MCPs)
  • Experience with blockchain and/or payments transaction data and an understanding of common pitfalls when interacting with transactional data
  • Ability to translate business requirements into analytical solutions.
  • Comfortable working with cross-functional teams, presenting technical insights to non-technical stakeholders.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience in an API-based business and in payments, fintech, or crypto.
  • Experience directly supporting Product and Engineering teams as an embedded data analyst
  • Experience in launching new products in fast-paced, scaling environments.
  • Experience with data governance and ensuring data quality at scale.
