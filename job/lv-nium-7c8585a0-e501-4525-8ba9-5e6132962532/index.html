Nium issues more than 50 million card tokens every year as a principal issuer on Visa, Mastercard, Discover and UATP. Those cards pay for travel, run payroll and fund procurement across dozens of markets. As Director of Product Management for Card Issuing, you will decide where that platform goes next, and you will bring the commercial, technical and regulatory judgment to get it there.

О Nium

Nium provides global infrastructure for real-time cross-border payments. Our mission is to deliver the global payments infrastructure of tomorrow, today: a platform that lets banks, fintechs and global businesses move money instantly, everywhere.

We are co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with offices in 14 markets. In March 2026 we delivered the largest month in our 11-year history, with record revenue, record volumes and EBITDA profitability. Today we move nearly $60 billion in payments a year, almost entirely for enterprises.

Our payout network spans 190+ countries and 100 currencies, with 100+ real-time corridors. We support local collections in 35 markets and hold regulatory licenses in 40+ markets. In 2024 we raised US$50 million in Series E funding at a US$1.4 billion valuation. We were named one of CNBC's World's Top Fintech Companies 2025, won Best Cross-Border Payments Solution at the PayTech Awards, and appear on FXC Intelligence's Top 100 Cross-Border Payments Companies list.

The B2B payments market is projected to reach US$175 trillion by 2030. We are building for that future with the scale of a market leader and the energy of a high-growth company.

THE OPPORTUNITY

This is a rare chance to own the strategy for a card issuing platform at a company that is scaling fast and has the track record to back ambitious bets. You will set the vision and commercial direction for Nium's Issuing Platform, serving travel clients who use cards for travel spend, and clients who use issued cards for everything from payroll to procurement.

You will bring together commercial strategy, engineering and compliance to meet the distinct needs of customers across geographies. You will lead a team of Product Managers and work with stakeholders across product, regulatory and commercial teams, up to executive leadership, to deliver the outcomes that matter most.

Why this role stands out

  • Scale that is already live. Over 50 million card tokens a year across four major networks. Product decisions here touch real money movement at real volume.
  • Problems worth solving. Travel, payroll, procurement and virtual card payments each carry different economics and constraints. You will turn them into reusable platform capabilities that serve many customers at once.
  • Global from day one. Launches across 40+ regulated markets with teams spread across time zones. You will develop a sharp sense of how regulation shapes product, and how product can shape the market.
  • Ownership of outcomes. You will be accountable for adoption, customer experience and commercial impact, not just the backlog, and you will work directly with senior leadership on the trade-offs.
  • Build a team and a practice. Lead, coach and grow a team of Product Managers, and help define how product gets done across the business.

Что предстоит делать

Shape the strategy and deliver it

  • Own the product vision, strategy and roadmap for Nium's global Card Issuing Platform.
  • Define and prioritise product investments by weighing customer value, commercial impact, technical complexity and regulatory requirements.
  • Lead the full product lifecycle, from discovery and requirements through delivery, launch and continuous optimisation.
  • Partner closely with Engineering to build card issuing capabilities that are scalable, reliable and high-performing.
  • Set clear objectives and metrics for adoption, customer experience, reliability and commercial impact.

Turn customer problems into commercial wins

  • Work with Commercial teams to understand customer requirements, market trends and competitive positioning.
  • Build deep expertise in B2B travel and virtual card payments, and translate complex requests into capabilities that scale.
  • Evaluate product economics with Commercial and Finance, including transaction revenue, interchange, scheme fees and processing costs.
  • Support commercial opportunities by assessing feasibility, identifying solutions and bringing product expertise to the table.
  • Partner with Partnerships teams on scheme capabilities and opportunities that strengthen the issuing proposition.

Lead a team and align the business

  • Lead, coach and develop Product Managers, building a culture of accountability, collaboration and customer focus.
  • Set the standard for product practice: discovery, prioritisation, roadmap planning and performance measurement.
  • Work with Engineering, Compliance, Risk, Operations and regional teams so every capability meets technical, regulatory and operational requirements.
  • Support new market launches, integrations and platform enhancements with internal and external partners.
  • Keep globally distributed teams aligned, managing competing priorities and dependencies to execute effectively.
  • Communicate strategy, progress and trade-offs clearly and confidently to senior stakeholders.
