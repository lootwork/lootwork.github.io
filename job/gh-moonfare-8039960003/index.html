Mission

Give every brief from across marketing the finished asset it needs, on brand, signed off and live on schedule.

О роли

Production at Moonfare covers fund launches, fund pages and around 24 webinars a year, alongside the ads, white paper layouts, brochureware, sales decks and event merchandise that marketing needs. This Content Producer role is the delivery for all of it: briefs come in and finished assets go out. About 60% of your time is Fund Marketing, where your scope begins once a launch has been positioned and its messaging agreed and ends when the last asset, page or session is live. The other 40% is the same production work for the rest of content and marketing. The hard part is running several streams at once on different rhythms: a raise open for months working toward a close, an evergreen fund with no close at all, a webinar with an external guest next week and an event kit due before the stand is built.

The team

This role sits within Content & Communications and works for Fund Marketing and other marketing teams. You report to the Head of Communications and Content. You work closely with the Investment Team and Fund Operations on accuracy, Legal & Compliance on approvals, Sales on activation and the design team and external agencies on creative.

Вам предстоит

Fund Marketing

  • The webinar calendar is yours. Around 24 sessions a year with the fund managers behind the funds on the platform. You hold the schedule, confirm guests and keep the run intact when someone pulls out three days before.
  • Each session runs through you in Livestorm, live or recorded, from registration to the moment it airs. The audience should never notice there was a producer.
  • Build fund and webinar pages in the CMS yourself and publish them on schedule. A recording that sits unpublished for a fortnight has lost most of the audience it was made for.
  • Direct the design work behind each launch: brochures, sales decks, ads and launch merchandise. You write the brief, work with designers or an agency and send back what doesn’t meet the standard.
  • Compliance sign-off on everything you produce is your process to run, with only the changes that shift risk or claims escalated.
  • Keep live materials current. Figures move, so you update them with the Investment Team and Fund Operations and audit what’s live on a rolling basis. An evergreen product has no close date, which means its materials need standing attention.

Wider content production

  • Briefs from across marketing become delivered assets: ads, white paper layouts, brochureware, sales decks and merchandise for events. You are the delivery.
  • Take each brief through the same process you run for fund launches: a written brief, a slot on the production calendar, designers or an agency directed to standard and compliance sign-off before anything goes out.
  • Turn finished copy into laid-out white papers and brochureware with the design team, and hold the version that goes live.
  • Produce event merchandise and materials against event dates, from the brief to the supplier to the stand.
  • Tell whoever briefed you early when a request will miss its date or needs a decision, before they have to ask.

Across both

  • Hold the production calendar and the materials repository, including the regulatory packs Singapore compliance requires, and coordinate every upload to the platform. You build your own automations for the recurring load with Claude and agentic workflows, so tracking status and chasing sign-off don’t eat into the calendar.

The Next Moonfarian

  • You come from marketing in financial services or fintech. You know what it takes to get material through a compliance review and you can present a fund to investors without overstating it.
  • You’ve produced live digital events and know where they go wrong. Livestorm, or a platform close enough that you’d be comfortable within a week. Guest confirmations, rehearsals, the platform on the day.
  • You can judge creative work and get it to standard. You can look at a brochure or a deck and say why it does or doesn’t meet the bar and you build pages in a CMS without waiting on anyone. Moonfare’s investors include UHNW individuals and family offices and the brand has to hold at that level.
  • You write cleanly under scrutiny. Copy that holds after a compliance read.
  • You keep several launches straight at once and know which stage each is at without being asked. When a schedule moves at short notice, you replan and flag what’s at risk before anyone has to chase you.
  • You’re AI-native. When a task repeats, you reach for Claude, automations and agentic workflows and you build the tool instead of waiting for one to be handed to you.
