О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

You'll own accounts payable operations across Airwallex entities, ensuring invoices, payments and reconciliations are accurate and comply with internal controls, SOX and company financial policies.

You'll partner with vendors, business units and internal stakeholders to resolve payment-related queries, support local audit and tax requirements, and provide documentation for internal and external audits.

You'll drive process improvements and finance transformation initiatives, including opportunities for automation, while maintaining reliable month-end and year-end reporting.

You'll provide guidance and training to junior team members and contribute to a consistent, scalable Finance Shared Services operation.

You'll work from our China office five days a week.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- 3+ years of accounting experience, with sufficient knowledge of accounts payable processes and the ability to apply them in practice

- A bachelor’s degree in accounting or a related field

- Experience processing and validating invoices, performing AP reconciliations, and supporting audit requirements

- Experience working with internal controls, SOX and company financial policies

- Proven experience managing complex local or international process-improvement projects

- Strong communication skills and the ability to interact with stakeholders at all levels

- Experience working independently and navigating ambiguity

Preferred

- Big Four experience

- CPA or equivalent qualification

- Good knowledge of ERP systems such as Oracle

- Advanced Excel skills

- Experience identifying opportunities for automation and participating in finance transformation initiatives

- Experience providing training or guidance to junior team members

YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF

- You own the outcome and you don't wait for permission to fix what's broken.

- You're comfortable making decisions with incomplete information and moving quickly anyway.

- You hold yourself to a high standard even when no one is watching. Quality and accountability aren't external expectations, they're internal ones.

- You connect dots across functions and hold the big picture without losing grip on the details.

- You over-communicate by design. You'd rather flag something early than let it become a surprise.

- You value in-person collaboration and are happy being in the office five days a week.
