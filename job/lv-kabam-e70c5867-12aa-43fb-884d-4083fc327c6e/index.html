The Contractor shall provide the following services on an independent, deliverables basis, exercising their own professional judgment, expertise, and methods in performing the Services.

IN THIS ROLE, YOU CAN EXPECT TO

  • Provide design input on assigned game features in alignment with core vision.
  • Pitch feature concepts and design rationale to internal leads for sign-off.
  • Write game design documentation (GDDs) for assigned mechanics, systems, and features.
  • Write narrative scripts and dialogue copy for assigned game content.
  • Detail technical specs and system requirements for implementation teams.
  • Define rules, formulas, and balance parameters for assigned game mechanics.
  • Implement and tune assigned systems, economies, and progression loops.
  • Present design specs and prototypes to cross-functional teams and stakeholders.
  • Hand off finalized design documentation and implementation guidelines to dev teams.

TO BE SUCCESSFUL IN THIS ROLE, YOUR BACKGROUND INCLUDES

  • 3+ years of experience designing systems, characters, and/or economies on at least one shipped title
  • Strong project management
  • Experience working directly within the Unity engine and building tools
  • Strong math skills
  • Advance technical excel expertise
  • Strong oral and written communication skills.

Excited by this opportunity? Kabam is dedicated to growing our diverse and inclusive workforce, so if your past experience doesn’t perfectly match the listed requirements we encourage you to apply anyways - you could be a great fit for this or other positions.

Kabam is a world leader in developing entertaining, immersive, and highly social multiplayer games, bringing high-quality graphics, next-generation technology, and revolutionary gameplay to players around the world.
