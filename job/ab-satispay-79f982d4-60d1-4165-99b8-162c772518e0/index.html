О нас

Satispay began by rethinking the simple act of a payment to remove the friction from our daily routines. But we didn’t stop there. Today, we are building a complete financial platform designed to empower people and concretely improve their lives. By giving our 6 million users a clear, open path to pay, save, and invest, we are evolving into the definitive destination for every financial need.

Что предстоит делать

As our Product Lead – Business & Merchant Domain, you’ll drive the evolution of our Business ecosystem and the products supporting the financial needs of companies. In this role, you will lead a team of business domain PMs while remaining directly hands-on with execution. This position requires a rare blend of product delivery, strategic leadership, and a powerful growth mindset to thrive in complexity, raise quality benchmarks, and continuously build product experiences that drive commercial success.

Here's what your day-to-day will look like

- Product Leadership & Execution – Own the end-to-end product lifecycle. Define the roadmap, design frictionless experiences, and deliver high-impact features.

- Strategic Coordination – Mentor and coordinate Product Managers, ensuring cross-team collaboration, alignment, and a unified product vision.

- Drive Commercial Growth & Engagement – Apply a strong growth mindset to design solutions that reactivate dormant merchants, increase feature adoption, boost upselling, and optimize conversion funnels.

- Business & Customer Understanding – Develop a deep understanding of businesses' financial needs, market dynamics and competitive landscape, translating customer and market insights into product opportunities.

- User-Centricity – Put merchants first by obsessing over user feedback, conducting continuous shadowing, and transforming pain points into seamless opportunities.

- Data-Driven Decision Making – Define, track, and analyze key product metrics to validate hypotheses, measure impact, and relentlessly optimize funnel performance.

Кого мы ищем

We need a problem-solver who loves teamwork and gets things done. If you're curious and ready for real ownership, you'll fit in!

Does this sound like you?

- B2B Product Management – 5+ years of experience in B2B Product Management, preferably within Business Banking, Fintech, Payments, Financial Services, B2B SaaS, or high-growth tech environments.

- Financial Services Experience - Experience building or scaling products in banking, fintech or other financial services environments is a strong plus.

- Leadership & Complexity Management – Experience managing people or leading highly complex, multi-stakeholder projects across organizational and technical boundaries.

- Commercial Acumen / Growth Mindset – A strong business sense with an understanding of how to leverage product experiences to drive monetization, acquisition, and merchant engagement.

- Obsession with Detail – High standards with a sharp eye for UX/UI details, copy, and operational excellence to constantly raise the bar.

- Analytical Power – Deeply data-driven mindset with experience using advanced data platforms to track metrics, optimize funnels, and turn raw data into actionable strategic growth insights.

- AI-Empowered Product Management – Strong AI fluency with the ability to integrate AI tools into daily workflows to accelerate product operations, research synthesis, and roadmap structuring.
