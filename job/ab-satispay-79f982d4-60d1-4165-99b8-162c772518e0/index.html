О нас

Traditional banking has never really given you control over your financial life. Satispay exists to change that: to empower people through finance.

That means making financial services easy and accessible for over 6.5M users. We started with payments, then benefits, investing, cards... and we’re not done. Not even close. We follow the same logic every time: find a real problem, solve it properly, then look for the next one. The destination? To build the most loved financial platform in the world. If you own outcomes rather than tasks, stay close to what users need, and refuse to wait for someone else to fix what's broken — you'll find people here who work exactly in the same way. And together, you'll build something worth being proud of.

Что предстоит делать

As our Product Lead – Business & Merchant Domain, you’ll drive the evolution of our Merchant & Business ecosystem. In this role, you will lead a team of business domain PMs while remaining directly hands-on with execution. This position requires a rare blend of deep banking and merchant acquiring domain expertise, product delivery, strategic leadership, and a powerful growth mindset to thrive in complexity, raise quality benchmarks, and continuously build product experiences that drive commercial success.

Here's what that looks like in practice

- Product Leadership & Execution – Own the end-to-end product lifecycle for our merchant acquiring, in-store/in-person acceptance, and business financial services. Define the roadmap and deliver high-impact features.

- Strategic Coordination – Mentor and coordinate Product Managers, ensuring cross-team collaboration, alignment, and a unified product vision.

- Drive Commercial Growth & Engagement – Apply a strong growth mindset to design solutions that reactivate dormant merchants, increase feature adoption, boost upselling, and optimise conversion funnels.

- User-Centricity – Put merchants first by obsessing over user feedback, conducting continuous shadowing, and transforming pain points into seamless opportunities.

- Data-Driven Decision Making – Define, track, and analyse key product metrics to validate hypotheses, measure impact, and relentlessly optimise funnel performance.

Кого мы ищем

This role needs a problem-solver who loves teamwork, gets things done, and combines deep banking and merchant acquiring expertise with strategic leadership and a high-growth mindset.

- B2B Product Management – 5+ years of experience in Product Management, with a proven track record in Banking, Merchant Acquiring, Payment Service Providers (PSPs), Card Acceptance, or similar domains within Fintech, B2B SaaS, or high-growth tech environments.

- Leadership & Complexity Management – Experience managing people or leading highly complex, multi-stakeholder projects across organisational and technical boundaries.

- Commercial Acumen / Growth Mindset – A strong business sense with an understanding of how to leverage product experiences to drive monetisation, acquisition, and merchant engagement.

- Obsession with Detail – High standards with a sharp eye for UX/UI details, copy, and operational excellence to constantly raise the bar.

- Analytical Power – Deeply data-driven mindset with experience using advanced data platforms to track metrics, optimise funnels, and turn raw data into actionable strategic growth insights.

- AI-Empowered Product Management – Strong AI fluency with the ability to integrate AI tools into daily workflows to accelerate product operations, research synthesis, and roadmap structuring.
