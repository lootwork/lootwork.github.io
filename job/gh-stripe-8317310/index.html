Кто мы

О Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.

О команде

The Stream Analytics team owns Stripe’s real-time online analytical processing (OLAP) solution. The platform serves low-latency analytics over large volumes of financial data, both to merchants through Stripe Dashboard products and APIs, and to internal teams through Stripe’s data products. The system is business-critical: its performance, data freshness, and reliability directly affect millions of businesses. The team tackles hard problems at a scale few companies reach, including real-time ingestion, query performance, multi-tenancy, cost efficiency, and onboarding a growing set of use cases. We value high ownership, technical rigor, and sustainable operations, and we move quickly while staying deliberate about changes that affect production.

Что предстоит делать

As an engineering manager at Stripe, you will play a pivotal role in driving the success of your team and the broader organization. Your responsibilities will include:

Обязанности

  • Leading engineering and design processes, coaching, mentoring, and supporting your team as they build innovative solutions
  • Collaborating with stakeholders across product, design, infrastructure, marketing, and operations to ensure alignment on projects and objectives, and shaping the team’s strategy and roadmap
  • Owning problems end to end by managing diverse systems, processes, and technologies, while continuously seeking opportunities to optimize efficiency and user experience
  • Upholding high engineering standards, ensuring consistency across codebases, and implementing best practices throughout your team
  • Recruiting top engineering talent in partnership with Stripe’s recruiting team to build a diverse and high-performing team
  • Supporting your team members’ career growth by providing insights and development opportunities tailored to their goals

Кто вы

We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • 4+ years of engineering management experience, directly managing and growing teams of 5+ engineers that build and operate infrastructure or data platforms at scale, and 8+ years of full-time software development experience
  • Experience building extensible, reusable software solutions that scale to different kinds of user needs, with the ability to empathize with users and advocate for exceptional user experiences; technical proficiency to engage thoughtfully with engineers about architecture and system design, offering valuable insights when necessary
  • Strong problem-solving skills, with a history of creatively tackling complex challenges, and experience dealing with ambiguity and executing on multiple high-impact work streams simultaneously in a fast-paced environment
  • Commitment to building a supportive, feedback-driven, and challenging work environment
  • Experience running and operating business-critical, high-scale production systems, including on-call ownership, incident leadership, and reliability improvements

Будет плюсом

  • Deep technical experience with OLAP systems such as Apache Pinot, StarRocks, ClickHouse, or similar
  • A proven track record of leading the adoption of a new technology to solve a business-critical problem, from evaluation and buy-in through production rollout and migration
  • You have a strong product and design sense and enjoy finding elegant solutions to complex problems
  • Experience thriving in cross-functional environments, demonstrating rigorous thinking, effective decision-making, and the ability to build strong cross-team relationships
  • You have experience building and leading a distributed team that works across multiple locations and time zones
