О Binance Accelerator Program

Binance Accelerator Program (BAP) is a 3-6 month internship program designed for Early Career talent to have firsthand experience in the rapidly expanding digital assets space. You will be given the opportunity to develop your skills at Binance and understand what it’s like to work at the world's leading blockchain ecosystem. As part of your internship in the BAP, there will also be opportunities for networking and development, which will expand your professional network and build transferable skills to propel you forward in your career. Learn about the BAP Program HERE.

Who may apply

Current university students and recent graduates.

*Terms of employment / engagement shall be subject to contract and local applicable laws

О роли

We are looking for a motivated individual to join the CIS team, focusing on growth initiatives, including CRM targeting, growth campaigns and performance marketing. The ideal candidate is a crypto enthusiast or junior growth marketer eager to deepen their skills in digital marketing within a fast-paced Web3 environment.

Что предстоит делать

  • Support internal communication initiatives, particularly within lifecycle marketing campaigns (emails, push notifications, in-app messages);
  • Help optimize and monitor paid advertising campaigns (Google, Meta, etc.);
  • Contribute to ASO (App Store Optimization) testing and improvements;
  • Assist with SEO strategy execution (keyword research, content optimization);
  • Participate in growth experiments and help analyze performance metrics;
  • Collaborate closely with the wider marketing team on recurring tasks and campaign execution.

Кого мы ищем

  • Interest in crypto/blockchain (previous experience in Web3, tech, Finance marketing is a plus);
  • Understanding of paid ads management (Google Ads, Meta Ads, etc.) and willingness to learn deeper;
  • Ability to leverage AI in daily workflows;
  • Basic knowledge of ASO/SEO;
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills;
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks with attention to detail;
  • Fluency in English & Russian is required to be able to coordinate with overseas partners and stakeholders. Additional languages would be an advantage;
  • Self-motivated, eager to learn, and a proactive team player.

Why Binance

  • Shape the future of finance
  • Collaborate with world-class talent in a user-centric global organization with a flat structure
  • Tackle challenging projects in a fast-paced and innovative enviornment with autonomy
  • Thrive in a results-driven workplace with opportunities for career growth and continuous learning
  • Competitive salary and company benefits
  • Remote-first, work-from-home arrangement (may vary depending on the nature of the role and team)
