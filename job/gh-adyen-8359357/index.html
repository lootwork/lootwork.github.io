This is Adyen

Adyen provides payments, data, and financial products in a single solution for customers like Meta, Uber, H&M, and Microsoft - making us the financial technology platform of choice. At Adyen, everything we do is engineered for ambition.

For our teams, we create an environment with opportunities for our people to succeed, backed by the culture and support to ensure they are enabled to truly own their careers. We are motivated individuals who tackle unique technical challenges at scale and solve them as a team. Together, we deliver innovative and ethical solutions that help businesses achieve their ambitions faster.

The Opportunity

Adyen commercial and operations teams serve as both an extension of our customer’s internal team and as a strategic thought partner. The pace of innovation in the industry, access to data, and emergence of agentic tools are elevating the game across all aspects of account management, financial operations, and technical support.

We need a Principal Product Manager to craft and execute on a vision for how we empower our internal teams with the data, insights, and workflows that enable them to elevate their partnership with our customers. The role will treat Ayden's internal teams as customers, working back from their needs and critical workflows to provide AI-enabled solutions - utilizing internal MCPs, documentation, data across our services, backoffice tools, and vendor software.

This is a newly funded team that will working with commercial, operations, and technical delivery stakeholders, you will rapidly prototype and launch solutions - partnering with data and engineering to move at the speed of AI; shipping constantly, iterating on feedback from your internal customers, and pushing the edge of what’s possible with frontier intelligence, agent harnesses, and enabling technologies.

Что предстоит делать

  • Define the Product Vision & Roadmap: Own the end-to-end product strategy for delivering solutions that elevate our commercial and ops teams; internal tooling, operational workflows, and data products. Translate cross-functional challenges into clear roadmaps with measurable business outcomes that drive efficiency, speed of resolution, and ultimately, delightful customer experiences.
  • Build High-Impact Internal Tools: Design and deliver internal tools and services—such as 360 views of the customer’s integration, proactive performance insights, workflows that manage common tasks and configurations.
  • Pioneer AI & Intelligent Automation: Champion the adoption of intelligent agents, LLM-powered workflows, and automated triage systems to accelerate the ship, iterate, and scale process of building solutions in an agent-led environment.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Act as the connective tissue bridging your customer’s needs with the internal data, tooling, and platform teams at Adyen that contain adjacent and relevant capabilities. Partner with them through data contracts, aligning roadmaps, and co-building to deliver solutions that scale.

Кто вы

  • Proven Product Ownership: 10+ years of product management experience, preferably building internal tooling, technical workflows, and B2B/API products in high-growth software or fintech environments.
  • Technical Acumen: Strong technical foundation and fluency with modern software architecture, APIs, web technologies, and data pipelines. Passionate about utilizing AI across your product craft and the products you build, with concrete evidence in personal and professional work being hands on.
  • Data-Driven & Problem Solver: You excel at breaking down ambiguous, multi-stakeholder operational problems into structured, scalable solutions. You rely on qualitative user insights and quantitative telemetry to prioritize ruthlessly based on ROI.
  • User-Centric Empathy: High empathy for internal users. You listen intently to commercial and operational teams to understand their daily friction and take pride in delivering software that makes your colleagues more productive.
  • Autonomy & Bias for Action: You thrive in fast-paced environments where ownership is distributed. You don't wait for permission; you identify opportunities, build consensus, and drive initiatives to launch.
  • Collaborative Communicator: Excellent written and verbal communication skills, capable of synthesizing technical complexity into concise narratives for executive leadership, engineering peers, and commercial counterparts across multiple time zones.
  • Formula Fit: You embrace the Adyen Formula—you value direct communication, team success over individual ego, long-term durable outcomes, and open collaboration.

The annual base salary range for this role is $330,000 - $462,000. Compensation is based on factors including qualifications, experience, and location. Total compensation package includes equity (RSUs), 401(k), health, dental, vision, and more.

This role is based in our San Francisco office. We're an office-first company and expect employees to work in-office at least 3 days a week on average. This approach helps strike a balance between bringing people together for in-person collaboration and learning from each other, while also supporting flexibility.
