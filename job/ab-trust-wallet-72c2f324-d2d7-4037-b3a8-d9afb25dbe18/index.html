О company

Trust Wallet is the leading non-custodial cryptocurrency wallet, trusted by over 200 million people worldwide to securely manage and grow their digital assets. Our vision is to give individuals the freedom to own their assets, confidently participate in the future economy, and access opportunities that enhance their lives. Our mission is to be a trusted personal companion — helping users safely navigate Web3, the on-chain economy, and the emerging AI-powered future. With support for over 10 million assets across 100+ blockchains, Trust Wallet offers a seamless, multi-chain experience backed by industry-leading self-custody technology, a vibrant community, and a growing ecosystem of partners.

О роли

Trust Wallet is looking for a Design Engineer to sit at the intersection of design and engineering — someone who can move fluidly between Figma and code to build and ship polished, high-quality UI directly in our React Native app. You'll close the gap between design intent and shipped product, working hands-on with both the design and engineering teams.

Обязанности

- Build production-quality UI components and interactions directly in React Native, translating designs into pixel-accurate, performant code

- Partner with product designers to prototype ideas quickly, test interactions, and iterate before full engineering handoff

- Own and maintain the design system/component library, ensuring consistency and reusability across the app

- Bridge design and engineering — flag feasibility issues early, propose technical solutions to design problems, and vice versa

- Improve UI performance, animations, and micro-interactions across web, mobile, and browser extension

- Collaborate with engineering on architecture decisions that affect UI flexibility, theming, and component scalability

- Help define and evolve design-to-dev workflows/tooling (Figma → code handoff, design tokens, etc.)

Требования

- 3-5+ years of experience in frontend/UI engineering, with strong hands-on React Native experience

- Strong eye for design craft — typography, spacing, motion, and interaction detail

- Comfortable working directly in Figma (or similar) to prototype and iterate on UI

- Experience building and maintaining component/design systems

- Track record of shipping polished, consumer-facing mobile products

- Strong communication skills — able to work fluidly across design and engineering teams and translate between the two

- Genuine interest in Web3/crypto products a plus

Будет плюсом

- Experience with design tokens, theming systems, or cross-platform component libraries

- Background as either a designer who learned to code or an engineer with strong design sensibility

- Familiarity with animation libraries (Reanimated, Lottie, etc.) in React Native

- Prior experience in fintech, crypto, or high-growth consumer apps

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION

This is a global and fully remote role. You must have the right to work for the country you are based.
