Кто мы

О Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.

О команде

  • The Financial Crime and Risk Oversight (FinCRO) team at Stripe exists to help protect Stripe and its users from crime. Our people play an important role in combating financial crime across a global footprint and in enabling Stripe to increase the GDP of the internet.
  • The global team works as a cohesive whole to manage and mitigate financial crime risks and meet and exceed the expectations of our users, our financial partners, and our regulators.
  • We are a deeply caring group of folks, slightly obsessed with our tradecraft, dedicated to protecting our users and the financial ecosystem from crime, and committed to our work, our lives, and our hobbies with equal passion.

Что предстоит делать

Help us protect Stripe and its users from crime and grow our Financial Crime and Risk Oversight (FinCRO) team!

We are looking for an experienced financial crime professional to lead our financial crimes program in Ireland (Stripe Technology Europe, Limited [STEL]). In this role, you will lead and be primarily responsible for managing financial crime risk at Stripe in Ireland and for our European operations. This will include branches of STEL in other European jurisdictions. You will be the principal decision-maker on financial crime risk management decisions, serving a critical role in ensuring Stripe’s continued safe growth and success in the region. You’ll also be heavily involved in the strategic design and planning of Stripe’s financial crimes program as we enhance and uplevel controls and operations. You will be the leading expert on financial crime matters, advising on the evolution of financial crime regulation.

Stripe is both a technology company and a financial services company, and you will need to be comfortable straddling both of those worlds every day. The right person for the role will be enthusiastic both about the mission of anti-financial crime and about the application of new and innovative technology approaches to protect Stripe, its users, the financial system, and the community at large from crime. The role holder will also enjoy seeking creative solutions and moving quickly, often in the face of ambiguity.

Additionally, in your role as the STEL Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO), based in Ireland, you will be the public face of Stripe’s financial crimes compliance team in the region.

Обязанности

  • Ensure Stripe’s global compliance and financial crimes frameworks, policies, programs, and controls (e.g., KYC, transaction monitoring, regulatory reporting, sanctions, and risk assessment) incorporate local regulatory requirements and industry best practices
  • Ensure that all transactions identified as potentially suspicious are investigated and reviewed in accordance with established procedures, and that appropriate escalations are made and measures are taken to mitigate Stripe’s risk exposure
  • Set, lead, and manage the culture of risk and compliance across the team in the local business, and work with our group and regional Legal, Risk, Financial Crime, and Compliance teams to define our risk appetite in the local market
  • Oversee financial crimes compliance for Stripe’s operations in Ireland and the EU, including the regulated businesses in Ireland
  • Provide guidance to the business teams on existing and upcoming regulatory requirements and resulting potential product changes, and provide strategic advice on expansion initiatives in Ireland and the EU from a financial crimes perspective, both enabling Stripe’s growth and advising on risk mitigation
  • Serve as a thought leader in supporting and driving innovative, technology-focused compliance solutions with regulators and financial partners, and build and maintain Stripe’s relationships with financial crimes counterparts at our partner financial institutions
  • Partner with the team’s other compliance officers to build a global program by contributing to strategic planning, goal setting, and more

Кто вы

We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • 10+ years’ experience in an AML/CFT role and comfort juggling numerous aspects of an AML/CFT compliance program
  • Demonstrated experience executing complex financial crimes initiatives and rolling out large-scale compliance initiatives (onboarding preferred)
  • Ability to work efficiently and independently in a fast-paced, high-volume environment and cross-functionally with internal stakeholders
  • An effective and relatable “culture of compliance” mentality
  • High professional fluency in English

Будет плюсом

  • PCF-52 experience in Ireland and equivalent experience in other European markets
  • Payments or fintech experience
  • Related regulatory experience, such as anti-bribery and corruption, payments, international policy, or audit
  • CAMS, CFE, or ICA certification
  • Additional European languages
