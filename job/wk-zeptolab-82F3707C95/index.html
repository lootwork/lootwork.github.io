At ZeptoLab, we're looking for a Senior Generalist Artist to join our team, working across our established titles and new game projects.

This is a hands-on role for a versatile artist who is equally comfortable working across 3D and 2D, implementing their work in Unity, and shaping the visual direction of a game.

Что предстоит делать

  • Create, iterate and implement 2D/3D/UI assets, animations and VFX in Unity for mobile games.
  • Help establish and maintain the visual style of new game concepts.
  • Work closely with Game Designers and Developers from early prototypes through production.
  • Turn gameplay ideas and design requirements into clear and effective visual solutions.
  • Iterate rapidly based on gameplay feedback, performance requirements and player needs.
  • Contribute your own visual ideas and proactively suggest solutions.
  • Estimate, prioritise and plan your work to support fast-paced prototyping and production.

Кого мы ищем

  • Strong experience in both 3D and 2D game art.
  • Hands-on experience implementing art assets in Unity.
  • Good understanding of mobile optimization and performance considerations.
  • Strong understanding of composition, readability and visual hierarchy.
  • Experience working on mobile games; Hybrid Casual, Casual or Hypercasual experience would be a plus.
  • Experience with AI tools in the creative workflow or a strong willingness to explore and adopt new AI tools.
  • Experience taking games or game features from prototype to production; shipped games would be a plus.
  • A strong hands-on approach — you enjoy creating and iterating yourself, rather than focusing only on art direction or management.
  • Ability to work independently, take ownership and make decisions in an environment with a high degree of autonomy.
  • A fast iteration and prototyping mindset.

Why this role is interesting

  • A highly hands-on and creative role with a lot of freedom, ownership and autonomy.
  • The chance to work across 3D, 2D and Unity rather than being limited to one area of art.
  • The opportunity to work on new game concepts from the very early prototype stage and see how they evolve based on gameplay and player feedback.
