О team + role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world’s biggest financial problems. We’re looking for bold thinkers, sharp problem-solvers, and builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn’t a place for complacency – it’s where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We’re a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

The Advisory Products team is redefining the advisor experience by bringing the best of Robinhood’s technology, design, and infrastructure to Registered Investment Advisors (RIAs) and the clients they serve. Following our TradePMR acquisition, we’re building a modern advisor platform – custody, tools, and a two-sided marketplace that connects Robinhood’s investors with trusted, growth-oriented advisors.

As the Product Manager for the Advisor Marketplace, you will own the product that helps investors find, meet, and fund accounts with the right advisor – and helps advisors win and onboard those clients efficiently. You’ll own the full marketplace funnel, from demand generation and matching through first meeting, onboarding, and funded account, and you’ll use data and experimentation to make every step work better for both sides. This is an opportunity to take a new, high-visibility business from early traction to scale, in close partnership with Engineering, Design, Data Science, Marketing, Operations, and Compliance.

This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA or New York, NY office, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the product strategy and roadmap for the Advisor Marketplace, balancing the needs of both sides – investors looking for advice and advisors looking to grow their practices.
  • Own the end-to-end marketplace funnel – discovery, matching and routing, scheduling, first-meeting show rate, advisor onboarding, and funded accounts – and drive measurable improvements at each stage.
  • Define and manage marketplace health: supply and demand balance, advisor quality and standards, lead allocation, and conversion, with clear metrics such as meetings booked and completed, accounts funded, assets under management, and net flows.
  • Partner with Engineering, Design, and Data Science to ship products from concept to production, using research, experimentation, and analytics to guide prioritization and measure success.
  • Work with Compliance, Legal, and Risk to embed regulatory and operational requirements directly into product design, advisor vetting, disclosures, and workflows.
  • Integrate the marketplace with the broader RIA platform, including TradePMR and Robinhood’s app, so that advisor and client data, accounts, and custody flows connect seamlessly.
  • Drive go-to-market execution with Marketing, Sales, and Operations across launches, advisor acquisition and onboarding, support readiness, and cross-functional communication.
  • Explore how AI can improve the client experience when working with an advisor.
  • Stay ahead of trends in wealth management, advisor technology, and marketplaces to inform long-term strategy.

Что вы приносите

  • 4+ years of product management experience, including building and launching marketplace, platform, or complex multi-sided products, ideally in fintech, brokerage, or wealth management.
  • Demonstrated success owning a funnel or growth-oriented product end-to-end, with a track record of improving conversion and engagement through experimentation and analytics.
  • Experience delivering products in a regulated or compliance-heavy environment, balancing innovation with operational and regulatory rigor.
  • Strong analytical skills: you define success metrics, write your own queries or dashboards, and use data to drive prioritization and continuous improvement.
  • Strong technical acumen and the ability to collaborate effectively with engineers, designers, and operations teams on system design, integrations, and scalability.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication, and the ability to align diverse stakeholders – including executives, advisors, and partner teams – around a shared roadmap.
  • An ownership mindset and a scrappy, self-starter attitude: comfortable with ambiguity, able to define what “great” looks like as you go.
  • Passion for empowering financial advisors and their clients, and for transforming the wealth management industry through modern technology.

Дополнительный плюс

  • Experience building two-sided marketplaces, lead-generation or referral products, or CRM and scheduling workflows.
  • Experience shipping AI-enabled features in B2B or consumer products.
