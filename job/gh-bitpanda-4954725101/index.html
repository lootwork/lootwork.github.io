Кто мы

We simplify wealth creation. Founded in 2014 in Vienna, Austria by Eric Demuth, Paul Klanschek and Christian Trummer, we’re here to help people trust themselves enough to build their financial freedom — for now and the future. Our user-friendly, trade-everything platform empowers both first-time investors and seasoned experts to invest in the cryptocurrencies, crypto indices, stocks*, precious metals and commodities* they want — with any sized budget, 24/7. Our global team works across different cultures and time zones, bringing our products to more than 7 million customers, making us one of Europe’s safest and most secure platforms that powers modern investing.

Headquartered in Austria but operating across Europe, our products are built by fast-moving, talented, “roll-up-your-sleeves-and-make-it-happen” kind of people. It’s these diverse perspectives and innovative minds operating as ONE TEAM that keep Bitpanda at the cutting edge of our industry. So if you’re someone who thinks big, moves fast and wants to make an impact right from day one, then get ready to join our industry-changing team. Let’s go!

Your mission

As Senior Product Manager for Securities & Commodities, you will own the end-to-end product strategy and execution for core traditional financial offerings—ranging from equity trading to Real-World Assets (RWAs). You will translate complex capital markets infrastructure and regulatory requirements into simple, transparent trading experiences for millions of European retail investors. If you are looking for high ownership building Europe’s leading multi-asset investing platform at the intersection of TradFi and fintech, this role is for you.

Что предстоит делать

  • Take full product ownership of end-to-end flows within the securities and commodities domain, including equity trading, margin trading, and Real-World Assets (RWAs).
  • Define detailed specifications, user flows, and delivery timelines, serving as the core product lead across cross-functional teams.
  • Connect front-end investing interfaces with complex back-end technical, operational, and clearing infrastructure, aligning execution with overarching business strategy.
  • Translate complex regulatory (e.g., MiFID II), market, and clearing topics into intuitive, transparent, and trust-building retail user experiences.
  • Drive high shipping velocity, task completion, and on-time feature delivery in close collaboration with Engineering and Business Operations.
  • Partner with UX, Design, and Data teams to continuously optimize product performance and user engagement across all touchpoints.

Кто вы

  • 4+ years of Product Management experience building retail brokerage, trading, or investing platforms (ideally within a MiFID II or heavily regulated fintech/banking context).
  • Solid domain understanding of capital markets, order execution flows, clearing and settlement, corporate actions, and taxation.
  • Practical knowledge of market infrastructure, including stock exchanges, market makers, custodians, and institutional intermediaries.
  • Strong data-driven and analytical mindset to inform product strategy.
  • Exceptional communication skills to translate complex financial, technical, and regulatory topics into actionable product direction.
  • A collaborative, pragmatic, and high-ownership approach suited for a fast-moving environment.
