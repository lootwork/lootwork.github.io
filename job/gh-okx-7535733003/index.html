О команде

OKX data team is responsible for the whole data scope of OKG, from techincal selection, architecture design, data ingestion, data storage, ETL, data visualisation to business intelligence and data science. We are data engineers, data analysts and data scientists. The team has end-to-end ownership of most of the data at OKx throughout the whole data lifecycle including data ingestion, data ETL, data warehouse and data services. As a data engineer of the team, you will work with the team to leverage data technologies to empower evidence-based decision-making and improve the quality of the company's products and services.

Обязанности

  • Responsible for the design and development of core modules of Data Governance platform, including but not limited to table lifecycle management, metadata management, data quality monitoring, CI/CD toolchain and other systems
  • Deeply understand business requirements, be able to deeply participate in product requirement design discussions, independently complete technical solution design, and lead the technical implementation of key functions
  • Coordinate cross-team resources and promote project lifecycle management (requirement analysis-development-testing-launch).
  • Participate in the development of Data Governance standards and specifications, precipitate technical documents and best practices

Требования

  • Familiar with Big Data ecosystem technologies (Hadoop/Hive/Spark/Flink, etc.), with experience in Data Governance platform development preferred
  • Able to quickly understand business requirements, translate them into feasible technical solutions, and provide product design optimization suggestions
  • Capable of abstracting complex business systems and independently designing highly available and scalable technical solutions
  • Excellent cross-team communication skills, able to coordinate multiple resources such as product and operation to promote projects

Good to have

  • Familiarity with modern governance/observability tools or frameworks (e.g., DataHub/OpenMetadata/Collibra/Alation, Great Expectations/Soda/Monte Carlo) and cloud/lakehouse stacks
  • Have experience in secondary development of Data Governance open source projects (Apache Atlas/DataHub, etc.)
  • Led the construction of enterprise-level Data Governance platform from 0 to 1
