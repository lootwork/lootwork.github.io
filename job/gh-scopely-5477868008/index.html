Scopely is looking for a Senior Technical Artist to join our new unannounced project in Barcelona, Spain to work on a hybrid basis 3 times a week. We can support with visa sponsorship and relocation support.

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues, or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a distributed team of game enthusiasts who are developing, publishing, and innovating in the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people around the world every day.

We are in the development stages of one of our new unannounced projects. We are looking for passionate game makers to collaborate, get in early and make their mark on this exciting venture

We are in the early stages of development on a bold, unannounced, cross-platform Strategy/MMO title, crafting a team of dedicated and hard-working game makers to join us on this exciting journey!

Что предстоит делать

  • Hands-On Technical Art Development
  • Create and iterate on complex, well-optimised materials and shaders for 2d & 3D game assets.
  • Create shaders and solutions for UI visual related work
  • Prototype technical solutions for content creation, game integration, visual systems, and production workflows.
  • Investigate and resolve complex technical issues across assets, shaders, materials, animation, prefabs, and runtime content.
  • Support implementation of art, VFX, and gameplay-facing visual systems in-engine.
  • Identify and troubleshoot technical issues related to art asset creation and performance. Engage in team-based problem solving to diagnose unexpected issues.
  • Stay up to date with the latest developments in art and technology and proactively identify opportunities for improving the visual quality and performance of our games.
  • Coordinate across distributed teams and time zones to keep communication and artwork aligned
  • Artist Support and Asset Integration
  • Provide day-to-day technical support to artists, helping them implement and integrate art assets, VFX, and UI content in Unity.
  • Guide artists in using project tools and production workflows, and review prefabs and asset configurations to ensure they meet project standards.
  • Review art content merge requests and help resolve technical issues before merging.
  • Use asset validation tools and ensure the required checks and validation requests are completed before art content merge requests are opened.
  • Support artists with Git workflows, including branches, commits, merge requests, and resolving merge conflicts.
  • Document recurring issues and practical solutions, and improve tools and workflows to prevent repeated problems.

Кого мы ищем

  • Experience as a technical artist for 5+ years in Technical Artist (Development, Game Development, or a related discipline.)
  • Strong knowledge of Shadergraph HLSL a bonus
  • Strong understanding of game content pipelines, asset integration, source control, and production workflows.
  • Strong understanding of 3D modelling and texturing process.
  • Hands-on experience building tools, scripts, automation, or pipeline systems.
  • Excellent problem-solving and troubleshooting skills.
  • Team focused with strong communication skills.
  • Able to bridge the gap in communication between programmers and artists and explain technical concepts to non-technical team members.
  • Positive, can-do attitude.
  • Experience with Adobe Suite (Painter, Designer, Photoshop)
  • Has experience writing code in C# - additional languages a bonus (esp Python)
  • Strong experience with DCC tools such as Maya, Blender, Houdini, or similar.
  • Experience optimizing game content for memory, performance, asset size, loading, or runtime efficiency.

Дополнительный плюс

  • Experience developing mobile games.
  • Experience with Unity editor tooling and custom workflow development.
  • Experience with shader, material, rendering, or asset bundle optimization.
  • Experience creating UI shaders & VFX
  • Experience with Git, Perforce, or similar source control systems.
