О роли

We are seeking an experienced compliance leader to serve as Compliance Manager and Manager-in-Charge (MIC) of the Compliance and AML/CFT core functions for Spark Fintech Limited, the Bybit group entity applying to the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) for a virtual asset trading platform (VATP) licence in Hong Kong.

You will be line managed by the Group Deputy Chief Compliance Officer and directly accountable to the Responsible Officer and Board of Spark Fintech Limited. You will lead the compliance workstream of the license application through to approval, own the compliance and AML/CFT framework end to end, lead the relationship with the SFC and hold the authority to make decisions, allocate compliance resources and represent the function with the regulator and senior management.

You will draw on Group Compliance and Group AML for systems, data and specialist support, while remaining accountable for outcomes in Hong Kong. This role suits a hands-on leader who builds and maintains the framework personally, challenges the business constructively and can take a platform from application to licensed operation.

Основные обязанности

  • Regulatory ownership and SFC relationship
  • Act as MIC of Compliance and AML/CFT, and as Money Laundering Reporting Officer, for Spark Fintech Limited.
  • Lead the compliance workstream of the VATP licence application, including SFC queries, external assessor reviews and readiness for SFC inspection, and implement licence conditions on approval.
  • Serve as the primary point of contact with the SFC on compliance and AML/CFT matters, managing inspections, information requests and notifications under the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) and the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (AMLO).
  • Own regulatory reporting and notifications, including periodic returns, changes in key personnel and the prompt reporting of material non-compliance to senior management and the SFC.
  • Coordinate external and independent reviews of the platform's compliance and drive remediation of findings to agreed dates.
  • Compliance framework
  • Own the compliance manual and supporting policies and procedures in line with the SFC Guidelines for Virtual Asset Trading Platform Operators, adopting Group policies with local addenda where Hong Kong requirements differ.
  • Maintain an obligations register that maps the SFO, AMLO, SFC guidelines and circulars to controls and named owners.
  • Track SFC policy developments and assess their impact on the platform's products, controls and licence conditions.
  • AML/CFT programme
  • Own the Hong Kong AML/CFT programme, including the institutional and customer risk assessments, customer due diligence, Travel Rule, transaction monitoring, sanctions screening and suspicious transaction reporting.
  • Keep the AML/CFT framework current and evidenced, working with Group AML for systems, data and expertise while retaining local accountability for decisions.
  • Monitoring and testing
  • Set and deliver a risk-based compliance monitoring plan covering client asset segregation and custody, token admission and review, market surveillance, conflicts of interest, employee dealing and AML/CFT controls, using Group Compliance Monitoring and Testing where appropriate.
  • Oversee market surveillance outcomes and escalations, ensuring suspicious activity is reviewed and reported appropriately.
  • Advisory and product governance
  • Sit on the Token Admission and Review Committee and provide independent challenge on admissions, reviews and removals.
  • Advise senior management, product and engineering on new products, platform features and group arrangements such as shared liquidity and affiliated market making, ensuring any required SFC approvals are in place before launch.
  • Review Hong Kong marketing and client communications for regulatory compliance.
  • Conduct, incidents and complaints
  • Apply Group conduct standards locally, including the conflicts of interest register, employee dealing controls and gifts and hospitality.
  • Oversee complaints handling, regulatory incident investigations and breach management, ensuring root causes are fixed.
  • Leadership, training and Board reporting
  • Manage and develop the Hong Kong compliance team, setting clear objectives and holding the team to the standard the SFC expects.
  • Deliver Hong Kong-specific compliance and AML/CFT training with Group Compliance Training, and support fit and proper and continuous professional training requirements for licensed staff.
  • Report regularly to the Board and relevant committees on compliance risk, monitoring results, audit and inspection findings and regulatory developments.
