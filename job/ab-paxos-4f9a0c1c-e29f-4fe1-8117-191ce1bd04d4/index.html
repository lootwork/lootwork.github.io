О Paxos

Today’s financial infrastructure is archaic, expensive, inefficient and risky — supporting a system that leaves out more people than it lets in. So we’re rebuilding it.

We’re on a mission to open the world’s financial system to everyone by enabling the instant movement of any asset, any time, in a trustworthy way. For over a decade, we’ve built blockchain infrastructure that tokenizes, custodies, trades and settles assets for the world's leading financial institutions, like PayPal, Venmo, Mastercard, Interactive Brokers and Charles Schwab.

О команде

Paxos Legal is a small, senior team embedded in the business. We advise on regulation, products, partnerships, disputes, and employment across the U.S., Europe, and Asia. We move fast, give clear answers, and build the systems that help the company grow safely.

О роли

As Associate General Counsel, Regulatory, Product & Litigation, you’ll lead our regulatory legal work and own our litigation and disputes. You’ll advise executives and product teams, and serve as a primary contact for our regulators. Over time, you’ll take on employment matters as well. You’ll report to the General Counsel.

Что предстоит делать

- Lead and develop a team of regulatory and licensing professionals

- Own regulatory strategy and relationships with our U.S. and international regulators, including new product approvals and product counseling, licensing, and remediation

- Oversee product counseling so new products launch on time and within our regulatory framework

- Manage litigation, arbitration, subpoenas, regulatory inquiries, and complaints, including outside counsel strategy and spend

- Learn and take on employment counseling and disputes

- Lead legal response to incidents, including regulatory and contractual notifications

- Build scalable processes and AI-enabled tools that make Legal faster and more consistent

О вас

- JD and admission to at least one U.S. state bar

- 10+ years of legal experience, including in-house experience at a regulated financial services or fintech company

- Litigation experience, either in house or at a law firm

- Regulatory experience, either in house or at a law firm

- Working directly with regulators; digital asset or stablecoin experience strongly preferred

- Managing and developing legal professionals

- Clear, concise writing and the judgment to give a recommendation, not just options

- Curiosity and appetite to learn new practice areas; employment law experience is a plus
