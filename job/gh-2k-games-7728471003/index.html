Что нам нужно

The Data Quality Engineer is a subject matter expert responsible for ensuring the reliability, accuracy, and integrity of data across the 2K gaming platform. This role involves designing and maintaining sophisticated systems to validate game telemetry, player data, and analytics pipelines. As a senior-level individual contributor, you will drive the technical direction for data quality standards, helping cross-functional teams make confident, data-driven decisions regarding gameplay, performance, and the player experience.

Что предстоит делать

  • Develop and maintain advanced automated data quality checks for game telemetry, player events, and backend systems.
  • Monitor complex data pipelines for anomalies, schema changes, and integrity issues, ensuring high availability and reliability.
  • Define, document, and enforce global data quality standards, validation rules, and SLAs across the data ecosystem.
  • Lead collaboration with engineering teams to ensure accurate and scalable instrumentation of in-game events.
  • Investigate and resolve complex data discrepancies, performing deep root-cause analysis to prevent recurrence.
  • Build and oversee dashboards and alerting systems to surface data quality issues in real time for stakeholders.
  • Partner with analytics and product teams to ensure data sets are optimized and fit for strategic decision-making.
  • Map and document data definitions, lineage, and quality expectations to improve organizational data literacy.
