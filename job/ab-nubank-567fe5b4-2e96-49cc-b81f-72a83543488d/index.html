О роли

You will lead the models and credit policy behind Nu’s new credit limit product in Brazil, Mexico and Colombia. It is in its growth stage, with a pilot already live in Brazil. You will turn that pilot into a multi-country program and decide which models to build and how credit decisions affect long-term value and customers. You will join the Credit Card division, which drives most of Nu’s profit and growth, and work with high autonomy in a fast-paced global team.

YOU’LL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR

- Own the program end-to-end: design, launch, measurement, iteration and scale.

- Deliver the models and credit policy to expand the Brazil pilot next year.

- Adapt and scale the approach to the Mexico and Colombia pilots

- Define which models to build and how to model credit risk and returns, maximizing long-term financial value and customer impact.

- Partner with design, product, engineering and marketing teams in each country.

- Lead and develop the team

- Contribute hands-on to modeling work when the team needs it

WE'RE LOOKING FOR SOMEONE WHO HAS

- Comfortable getting hands-on with the team

Будет плюсом
