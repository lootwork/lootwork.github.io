О FREETRADE

Freetrade’s mission is to become the default place to invest. Investing has been too complicated and expensive for too long, keeping millions from making the most of their savings.

We’re changing that.

We’re building our team and looking for people who are excited to reshape how our customers invest and grow their wealth. If you’re driven by solving complex problems and building innovative products, you’ll fit right in.

2026 is a big year for us. Last year, we announced a game changing deal to become part of the IG Group. We’ll continue to operate as an independent business while gaining access to the resources and support of an established leader in the space.

This year, we’re accelerating our roadmap and taking our products to the next level. We’re building new features like mutual funds, bonds, and family investment tools. We’re also exploring how we can leverage AI to deliver an even better experience for our customers.

Why join the Commercial Finance team at Freetrade?

You will be joining a small Commercial Finance team on a 6-month Fixed Term Contract (FTC) as Freetrade enters the ‘scale-up’ phase of its business. Our team is evolving from an FP&A reporting team to a commercial-oriented business partnering team. You will be providing commercial insight through impactful and insightful analysis to influence executive-level decision-making. You will report to the Head of Commercial Finance.

Вам предстоит

- Partner with our marketing leadership team, along with additional central departments

- Proactively highlight opportunities for efficiency, evaluating value for money, reviewing paybacks, CACs and ROIs

- Provide commercial challenge and recommendations, from both data analysis and competitor scanning

- Seek continuous improvement in reporting, finding areas for automation and opportunities to create self-serve dashboards

- Own the annual budget for your area along with monthly reporting against budget

- Forecast headcount spend in partnership with department heads in order to drive hiring decisions

- Forecast vendor-level spend, track renewal dates and run supplier negotiations

- Prepare models for business cases, projecting costs and impact for new products, new suppliers and new operations

Experience

- Recognised accounting qualification (ACA, ACCA, CIMA or equivalent) with 5+ years of relevant experience, including 3+ years as a Finance Business Partner.

- Experience working in financial services/fintech or similar industries

- Experience of business partnering with a marketing function

- Experience in building financial models and conducting scenario analysis

О вас

- A proactive, self-motivated problem solver

- An effective communicator with the ability to build strong relationships across all levels of the company

- An interest in the investments industry

- Highly numerate

Freetrade is the place to accelerate your career. Our benefits package and ways of working are designed to attract and retain the most talented people who are dedicated to making an impact at Freetrade.

Here’s what’s on offer in 2026

Hybrid working culture

We’re operating in a hybrid model: 3-days in-office / 2-days from home. We believe great collaboration happens in person and our beautiful offices in central London and Budapest are designed to bring people together.

🏢 Office days: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday

🏡 Work from home days: Wednesday, Friday

This setup helps us stay connected, collaborate more effectively, and keep building a culture where we all do our best work together, including dedicated days for focus work.

Flexibility for those who need it

We have a hybrid working model, but life happens. If you need flexibility let's talk about it, whether you're a parent or have other care responsibilities.
