You

We are looking for an AI-native Instructional Designer to create high-quality, scalable, and engaging learning experiences that help customers, partners, and internal teams get the most value from Feedzai’s products.

This role goes beyond traditional content development. You will combine instructional-design fundamentals with modern AI-enabled workflows to design, build, test, and continuously improve learning content. You will use AI responsibly to accelerate research, content drafting, adaptation, localization, multimedia production, assessment creation, and learning analytics—while ensuring that all materials remain accurate, inclusive, secure, and aligned with Feedzai’s product and brand standards.

A key focus of the role will be creating engaging video content, in-app learning experiences, tutorials, certifications, and product education journeys. You will partner with Product, UX, Documentation, Customer Success, and subject-matter experts to translate complex fraud-prevention and risk-management capabilities into clear, role-based, actionable learning.

You will be curious about how AI is changing learning design: from adaptive and personalized content to AI-assisted simulations, scenario-based practice, and embedded, just-in-time guidance inside the product. You will help evolve Feedzai’s learning ecosystem so that it is more efficient to create, easier to maintain, and more effective for global audiences.

Your Day-to-Day

1. Design AI-Enabled Learning Experiences

  • Design and develop learning journeys for customers, partners, and internal teams, including onboarding, feature adoption, role-based enablement, certifications, tutorials, and performance-support resources.
  • Apply adult-learning principles, learning objectives, assessment design, and instructional-design methodologies to create practical, engaging, and measurable learning experiences.
  • Use AI-enabled tools and workflows to accelerate content research, outlining, drafting, rewriting, summarization, assessment creation, scenario generation, and content adaptation.
  • Validate AI-assisted outputs with subject-matter experts and source documentation to ensure factual accuracy, product correctness, appropriate tone, and learner relevance.
  • Design learning experiences that combine self-paced learning, video, interactive practice, knowledge checks, live enablement, in-app guidance, and contextual performance support.
  • Explore responsible uses of generative AI to create realistic role-based scenarios, simulations, exercises, practice questions, and differentiated learning paths.

2. Create Multimedia and In-App Product Learning

  • Develop a range of digital learning assets, including video tutorials, product walkthroughs, microlearning modules, certifications, interactive guides, job aids, and in-app experiences.
  • Script, storyboard, produce, edit, and maintain video-based learning content using tools such as Synthesia, Descript, Camtasia, Adobe Creative Cloud, or comparable platforms.
  • Create and maintain in-app training content using Pendo, or similar digital-adoption platforms, to support contextual learning directly within the Feedzai product.
  • Work with UX and Product teams to identify high-impact moments for embedded education, onboarding guidance, feature discovery, and contextual help.
  • Apply usability, accessibility, and inclusive-design principles to all learning assets, including captions, transcripts, accessible visuals, clear language, and appropriate interaction design.
  • Help establish reusable templates, AI prompt libraries, content patterns, and production workflows that improve quality and reduce time to publish.

3. Partner Across Product, Documentation, and Enablement

  • Collaborate with Product Managers, Product Designers, Engineers, SMEs, Customer Success, Support, and Partner teams to understand learner needs and product priorities.
  • Translate complex product functionality, workflows, risk-management concepts, and technical information into clear and actionable learning content for different audiences.
  • Partner with Documentation to ensure that education content, product documentation, release communications, and in-app guidance are consistent, accurate, and easy to navigate.
  • Work closely with UX to embed learning moments into the product experience and reduce friction for users adopting new functionality.
  • Participate in feature-launch and release-readiness processes, ensuring that learning content is planned early rather than created only after a release.
  • Gather feedback from learners, internal stakeholders, and customer-facing teams to improve content quality and relevance.
