О роли

The Data Enablement team builds the platforms and frameworks that move and serve data across Block. We support four core capabilities — online and offline data projections, a third-party data proxy, and graph data stores — for customers across the company, including Risk, Compliance, Data Engineering, and Data Science. As a Staff Software Engineer on the team, you will become the subject-matter expert and a significant contributor to our online data projections and event-streaming capabilities — designing the platform, frameworks, and standards that turn Block's event streams into low-latency, production-grade data products. Your work sets the technical direction for how teams across the company build on streaming data.

Вам предстоит

  • Become the subject-matter expert for Block's online data projections and event-streaming capabilities — designing the platform, frameworks, and standards others build on
  • Lead the technical direction for real-time, event-driven data systems used by business units across Block
  • Build and operate streaming pipelines and projection frameworks on high-volume event data
  • Partner with engineering teams across Block to turn Kafka event streams into reliable, low-latency data products
  • Improve the reliability, observability, and data quality of streaming systems running in production
  • Mentor engineers on the team and shape platform architecture and standards
  • Contribute to and grow familiar with the team's other domains over time — supporting our third-party data proxy backend applications, graph data store capabilities, and offline batch data projections

You Have

  • 8+ years of professional software engineering experience (or Block equivalent, as referenced in go/levels)
  • Deep experience designing and operating distributed, high-throughput backend systems in production
  • Hands-on experience with event-driven architecture and streaming platforms such as Kafka
  • Experience building streaming or real-time data systems; hands-on experience with Spark Structured Streaming or Flink is strongly preferred
  • Proficiency in a JVM language such as Kotlin, or a comparable backend or systems programming background
  • Experience building on cloud infrastructure, ideally AWS
  • A track record of leading technical projects and mentoring other engineers
  • Bonus points for a data engineering background (building data pipelines, lakehouse or warehouse modeling, or large-scale batch and streaming systems)

Technologies We Use and Teach

  • Kafka, Spark, Spark Structured Streaming, Iceberg, Databricks, Snowflake, dbt, Prefect, block-forge, DataHub, Kotlin, AWS, CloudEvents
