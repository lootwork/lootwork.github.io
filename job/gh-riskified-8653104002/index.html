О роли

The Data Science department powers Riskified’s core value engine by building scalable, production-grade ML solutions that protect revenue and automate complex decisions. We leverage diverse analytical techniques - from advanced statistical modeling and graph theory to classic machine learning and deep learning - to extract maximum signal from complex datasets.

As a Data Scientist, you will own projects end-to-end: framing business problems, developing models, and deploying them to production. This role demands strong quantitative fundamentals, deep machine learning expertise, and a pragmatic problem-solving mindset to translate raw data into real business impact.

Что предстоит делать

  • End-to-End ML Development: Design, train, evaluate, and optimize scalable predictive models (classification, regression, clustering, deep learning) for complex business challenges.
  • Data & Feature Engineering: Clean, transform, and extract high-value features from large-scale structured and unstructured data to continuously boost model performance.
  • Collaboration & Production: Work within the Data Science team and alongside Data Engineers, Developers, and Product Managers to deploy models into production and translate complex insights into clear business actions.
  • Innovation & GenAI: Continuously explore emerging ML techniques, state-of-the-art tools, and GenAI workflows to elevate the team's analytical capability.

Требования

  • M.Sc. in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related quantitative field.
  • 2+ years of industry experience developing and deploying machine learning models in production environments.
  • Deep theoretical understanding and practical experience of ML algorithms on tabular and structured data.
  • Proactive team player with a strong curiosity and enthusiasm for adopting modern GenAI developer tools.
  • Proficiency in Python, SQL, and standard ML frameworks.
  • Strong hypothesis-driven problem-solving skills.
  • Excellent communication skills, with the ability to explain complex quantitative concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
