⭐ Our Risk team

The Head of Model Risk Oversight is a key Second Line of Defence (2LOD) leadership role responsible for establishing, scaling and managing the Model Risk Management (MRM) process across Monzo Bank Europe (MBEU).

As automated systems, advanced statistical models and Artificial Intelligence (AI) increasingly drive business activities and decision-making, this role ensures that all models supporting MBEU day to day operations including credit scoring, fraud and financial crime transaction monitoring, capital and liquidity stress testing, and AI Act-regulated use cases are mathematically sound, secure, compliant, and aligned with MBEU’s risk appetite and regulatory expectations.

This role reports to the Director of Credit Risk, MBEU.

🔑 You’ll play a key role by...

Model Risk Management (MRM) & Governance

  • Defining, implementing and maintaining the EU-wide Model Risk Management (MRM) Framework and associated validation standards. This includes developing and implementing the model risk appetite, model risk assessment process, model risk reporting and ongoing model reviews and validation schedules.
  • Serving as the primary driver and coordinator for the Model Risk Oversight Committee (MROC), ensuring material risk metrics, model validations and escalations are effectively reported to the Executive Risk Committee (ERC) and the Board Risk Committee (BRC).
  • Establishing and maintaining a centralised suite of model validation standards including those used for performance monitoring.
  • Maintaining MBEU Model Inventory including AI Use Case registries, ensuring appropriate tiering classification of material and high-risk models in alignment with the EU AI Act.
  • Managing the local embedding of the group-wide model risk governance frameworks, ensuring appropriate alignment and clear escalation protocols between global frameworks and subsidiary-specific policy standards.

Independent Model Validation & Oversight

  • Leading the technical 2LOD validation of all material and high-risk models prior to deployment, evaluating mathematical logic, data lineage, conceptual soundness, stability, performance and potential algorithmic bias.
  • Providing 2LOD independent oversight and technical validation across key risk domains including:
  • Credit Risk covering credit scoring, automated underwriting decision engines, customer affordability and IFRS 9 loan loss provisioning models.
  • Fraud & Financial Crime Risk covering digital onboarding risk assessment scorecards and real-time fraud/transaction monitoring models.
  • Financial Risk covering financial planning models, ICAAP/ILAAP stress testing, Pillar 2 economic capital models, macroeconomic forecasting, Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book (IRRBB) models.
  • Establishing ongoing monitoring standards, including back-testing, benchmarking, and sensitivity analysis, to detect model drift or performance degradation.
  • Issuing formal 2LOD Opinions on model changes, and track validation findings and action closures for regular reporting to the Model Risk Oversight Committee (MROC).

Advanced Modelling Technologies and AI Governance

  • Developing and operationalising independent 2LoD validation protocols for advanced Machine Learning (ML) methodologies and automated decisioning systems running on production platforms.
  • Establishing targeted risk oversight frameworks and registration protocols for Generative AI and Large Language Model (LLM) use cases deployed across bank operations.
  • Directing technical validation activities to meet compliance with evolving European AI Act legislation and Monzo's internal Artificial Intelligence Standards.

Regulatory Compliance

  • Reviewing all methodologies, modelling techniques and validation approaches to ensure continuous alignment with local and European regulatory expectations.
  • Leading the 2LOD assessment of evolving regulatory requirements with a specific focus on ECB and EBA Guidelines (eg, Loan Origination and Monitoring), IFRS 9 accounting standards, GDPR data privacy mandates and European consumer credit directives.

Committee Governance and Independent Challenge

  • Serving as the Secretariat function for the MBEU Model Risk Oversight Committee shaping technical agendas, tracking validation actions, and presenting validation reports and 2LOD opinions.
  • Managing formal reporting lines and ensuring the immediate escalation of material Model Risk Appetite breaches or validation failures to the Executive Risk Committee (ERC) and Board Risk Committee (BRC).
  • Providing clear, objective and evidence-based technical challenge to first-line (1LOD) model developers, model owners and accountable executive stakeholders throughout the validation lifecycle.
