Binance is a leading global blockchain ecosystem behind the world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume and registered users. We are trusted by 300+ million people in 100+ countries for our industry-leading security, user fund transparency, trading engine speed, deep liquidity, and an unmatched portfolio of digital-asset products. Binance offerings range from trading and finance to education, research, payments, institutional services, and more. Our mission is to increase the freedom of money and financial access for people around the world.

Обязанности

  • Own end-to-end development of product features spanning frontend and backend, supporting wallet marketing campaigns, growth tools, and user acquisition features

Integrate mainstream AI coding tools (e.g., Cursor, Copilot, Claude Code) as a core part of your daily workflow to maximize development efficiency

  • Translate product/marketing/BD requirements into working implementations with minimal handoff friction between frontend and backend
  • Own the quality of what you ship: define the test approach for each feature, write automated tests (unit, API, E2E), and deliver campaign features that are release-ready without relying on a long, separate QA cycle
  • Take part in requirement reviews to clarify acceptance criteria and edge cases early (campaign time windows, reward rules, eligibility, multi-region / multi-language)
  • Ship safely: use feature flags and gray releases, verify behavior after release through logs and monitoring, and keep a clear rollback plan
  • Act as a full stack "seed" within your team — champion full stack practices, mentor peers, and help scale AI-augmented development across the organization
  • Proactively identify efficiency bottlenecks in cross-team collaboration and propose improvements to product, design, and engineering processes
  • Contribute to the full product lifecycle — from ideation and design to deployment and iteration

Требования

  • Bachelor's degree or above; Computer Science or AI-related major preferred
  • 3–8 years of professional experience
  • Solid foundation in at least one backend language: Java or Golang
  • Proficiency in Node.js and React
  • Experience owning end-to-end projects in production — not just personal side projects
  • Familiarity with database design, data modeling, and core backend concepts
  • Highly proficient with mainstream AI coding tools (Cursor, Copilot, Claude Code, or similar)
  • Ability to clearly articulate requirements and constraints to AI tools; strong enough fundamentals to review, validate, and correct AI-generated code
  • Solid testing fundamentals: the test pyramid, test design techniques (boundary values, equivalence classes, state transitions), and writing code that is easy to test
  • Hands-on experience with testing frameworks, for example Jest / Vitest, React Testing Library, Playwright / Cypress, JUnit / Mockito or Go testing, and API testing tools
  • Familiarity with CI/CD quality gates (required checks, coverage, lint) and with troubleshooting production issues through logs, metrics, and tracing
  • Security awareness for user-facing pages: input validation, XSS, authentication / authorization basics (OWASP Top 10)
  • Self-driven and boundaryless — you don't wait for tasks to be assigned; you proactively identify what needs to be done
  • Product and operational mindset — you can turn vague ideas into clear specs
  • Curious observer — you pay attention to how other teams work and actively suggest improvements
  • Strong logical thinking and communication skills; passionate about learning new technologies
  • Bilingual English/Mandarin is required to be able to coordinate with overseas partners and stakeholders.
  • Creative and opinionated — willing to propose and stand by your own ideas, comfortable experimenting, with a sharp sense for market/growth scenarios

Будет плюсом

  • Knowledge of Web3/blockchain fundamentals
  • Experience with crypto wallets, DApps, or financial products
  • Experience building marketing pages, growth tools, or campaign features
  • Creative/growth-oriented projects
  • Performance / load testing experience (e.g., k6, JMeter) for peak traffic at campaign launch
  • Web3 testing experience: testnets, verifying on-chain transactions, multi-chain scenarios, wallet connection and signature flows
  • Experience with data reconciliation or testing financial-grade features (balances, rewards, settlement)

  • Tackle challenging projects in a fast-paced and innovative enviornment with autonomy
  • Thrive in a results-driven workplace with opportunities for career growth and continuous learning
  • Competitive salary and company benefits
  • Remote-first, work-from-home arrangement (may vary depending on the nature of the role and team)
