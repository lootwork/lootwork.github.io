Что предстоит делать

  • Security architecture and review: Take part in security design reviews for core business systems, infrastructure, and middleware. Identify architecture-level risks and propose practical solutions, covering dependency intake, service-to-service communication, and the integration of AI components.
  • Security baselines and standards: Define and drive application and supply-chain security standards, including dependency admission, secure middleware configuration (authentication, encryption, access control, disabling dangerous options), and SBOM and artifact signing requirements, and enforce them through pipelines.
  • Reusable security components and R&D: Build reusable security capabilities, such as secure-by-default framework settings, dependency and artifact verification tooling, and permission and audit components for AI calls. Track AI security and supply-chain attack trends and drive the evolution of the security architecture.
  • Risk governance and incident response: Lead impact assessment, root-cause analysis, and architectural hardening for major vulnerabilities. Coordinate staged middleware upgrades, canary rollout, rollback, and fix verification. Write and rehearse incident response playbooks to improve resilience under active threat.

Кого мы ищем

  • Bachelor's degree or above in computer science, information security, or a related field, with 5+ years in information security, including at least 2 years designing security architecture or delivering security solutions for large-scale systems.Theory and frameworks: Familiar with threat modeling (STRIDE or MITRE ATLAS), OWASP Top 10 and LLM Top 10, and security design principles for distributed systems.
  • Middleware and supply chain: Production operations and security configuration experience with at least two of MQ, registry, cache, or DB drivers. Familiar with SBOM and vulnerability standards (CycloneDX, PURL, CVE/CVSS, OSV), and hands-on with SCA tooling.
  • Identity and access control: Familiar with OAuth2 and OIDC; understands RBAC and ABAC trade-offs.
  • Cloud native and infrastructure: Familiar with security hardening in containers and Kubernetes, including authorization and isolation between microservices.
  • [AI, must-have] LLM and agent engineering: Engineering experience with LLM apps or agents (RAG, tool calling, MCP, or multi-agent orchestration); understands prompt injection, tool privilege escalation, and data exfiltration risk.
  • [AI, must-have] AI call governance**: Able to design permission, audit, and rate or cost controls for AI calls, and to evaluate agent accuracy and safety.
  • [AI, must-have] AI system threat modeling**: Able to threat-model and review AI systems, MCP servers, and third-party tools, identifying tool poisoning and trust-boundary issues.
  • Proficient in at least one of Java, Python, Go, or Rust. Able to write high-quality security components and perform secure code review.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience transforming infrastructure security at a large internet company, such as moving from network-perimeter models to zero trust or service-level identity.
  • Certifications such as CISSP, CCSP, CISP, or TOGAF are a plus.
  • Active contributor to the security community, or author of well-regarded security whitepapers or open-source projects.
