Job Description

CD PROJEKT RED is looking for an Audio QA Lead to lead and develop our Audio QA capability within Shared Development. This is a central role supporting multiple projects at different stages of development, from early production through release and live updates.

You will help projects deliver audio experiences that meet both technical requirements and creative intent. You will lead the Audio QA team, establish effective testing approaches, and work closely with project QA teams and partners across Audio, Development, Production, and Publishing.

The Audio QA Lead contributes to project QA direction and owns Audio QA planning and execution within the discipline. You will balance the needs of individual projects with the opportunity to improve shared practices, visibility, and consistency across the studio.

Обязанности

  • Lead, mentor, and develop Audio Design QA Analysts, setting clear expectations, priorities, goals, and quality standards.
  • Plan and coordinate Audio QA work across multiple projects, aligning coverage and resources with milestones, schedules, risks, and team capacity.
  • Translate project QA direction into practical Audio QA strategies, test plans, coverage, and measurable quality expectations.
  • Oversee testing across audio domains, including sound effects, music, dialogue, ambience, audio systems, and mixing.
  • Ensure audio is evaluated for both technical functionality and creative goals, including gameplay readability, immersion, consistency, and emotional impact.
  • Support the investigation of complex audio issues across assets, implementation, logic, and systems, using tools such as Wwise and Unreal Engine.
  • Ensure audio issues are clearly reported, prioritized, followed up, and verified through resolution.
  • Lead testing efforts for major audio features, establishing priorities, validation approaches, and suitable test environments.
  • Maintain visibility of Audio QA status, coverage, risks, blockers, and readiness; provide reliable input to project milestone and release assessments.
  • Identify quality risks, dependencies, and delivery concerns early, and work with project stakeholders to agree and follow through on mitigation plans.
  • Collaborate with Sound Design, Music, Audio Programming, Development, Production, project QA, and Publishing to align quality expectations and drive solutions.
  • Use testing results, issue trends, and stakeholder feedback to provide clear, data-informed quality recommendations.
  • Improve Audio QA workflows, testing methodologies, tools, documentation, and reporting practices across projects.
  • Support recruitment, onboarding, performance feedback, career development, and sustainable workload planning for the team.
  • Share effective practices across projects and contribute to consistent Audio QA standards within Shared Development and the wider QA organization.

Требования

  • Significant experience in game QA, audio, or a related development discipline, including responsibility for the quality of complex audio features throughout production.
  • Experience leading, mentoring, or coordinating QA professionals, with the ability to set clear priorities and support people’s development.
  • Strong understanding of interactive game audio, including sound effects, ambience, music, dialogue, and how they work together during gameplay.
  • A critical ear and the ability to evaluate audio beyond basic functionality, considering consistency, balance, creative intent, and player experience.
  • Experience creating and maintaining test strategies, test plans, and structured coverage for complex features.
  • Familiarity with audio middleware such as Wwise and game engines such as Unreal Engine, with the ability to investigate implementation-related issues.
  • Ability to assess quality risks and player impact, prioritize issues, and communicate clear recommendations.
  • Experience providing detailed, constructive, and actionable feedback to both creative and technical specialists.
  • Strong organizational skills and the ability to coordinate work across multiple projects and stakeholders.
  • Clear communication and collaboration skills, including the confidence to raise concerns, challenge plans constructively, and follow through on decisions.
  • Strong spoken and written English.
  • A proactive, accountable approach to problem-solving and continuous improvement.

Будет плюсом

  • A professional background in Sound Design, Technical Sound Design, or Audio Implementation.
  • Experience shipping at least one PC or console game.
  • Strong understanding of audio production fundamentals, including mixing, dynamics, and signal flow.
  • Experience with digital audio workstations such as Reaper or Pro Tools.
  • Experience with 3D or spatial audio and testing interactive music, dialogue, and ambience.
  • Familiarity with accessibility and gameplay readability considerations, and evaluating audio across different hardware and playback environments.
  • Experience coordinating testing with external QA teams or creating custom test maps and debug tools for audio validation.

Additional Information

Формат работы

Office-First: working primarily from the Warsaw studio in a full time or hybrid mode. Relocation is required.
