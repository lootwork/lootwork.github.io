Кто мы

Modulr is the payments automation platform that streamlines money movement with accuracy, control and reliability, built to scale. We process more than 200m transactions and over £180bn in payment value on an annualised basis, and we're trusted by over 6,000 businesses from fast-growing SMEs to global enterprises.

We automate how money moves into and out of a business (payroll, supplier payments, spend management and payment collection) through a single platform, connected to the accounting and payroll systems finance teams already use, and underpinned by principal memberships of Visa and Mastercard and direct access to central bank infrastructure.

As a regulated payments provider with over a decade of experience, we're the platform finance teams trust to run mission-critical operations. Modulr is backed by PayPal, FIS, General Atlantic, Blenheim Chalcot, Frog Capital and Highland Europe, with over 400 employees across London, Edinburgh, Amsterdam and Mumbai.

Find out more about us on our website.

Что предстоит делать

  • Set the product strategy and roadmap for Money Movement, Accounts and Cards, Onboarding and Identity, Data and Reporting, and Workflows and Verticals, with single-owner accountability for the converged portal, where vertical differences are delivered through configuration rather than code.
  • Own revenue, adoption and retention for our external products, working with Finance on pricing and driving the conversion of new B2B customers and expansion of existing ones.
  • Turn B2B customer and prospect insight into a prioritised roadmap and build the business case for each feature covering revenue, activation, retention and cost to serve. Within 12 months, the converged portal is live for its first vertical and revenue is tracking to plan.
  • Work with the Portal, Pay, Card and Trust engineering squadlets and their Engineering Managers on delivery and sequencing, and package products for SME and enterprise customers with Sales, Commercial and Finance.
  • Agree success measures for each product area and report them to the executive team.
  • Lead, coach and develop the product managers in your area.
  • Work closely with the Product Directors for Partners and API and Internal Experience.

Кто вы

What you’ll need

  • 7 or more years in product management, including at least 3 in a senior or director role in B2B payments, fintech, banking or B2B software.
  • Proven experience building and selling products to businesses of all sizes, including how B2B buyers evaluate, procure and onboard.
  • A track record of owning revenue or P&L outcomes for a product line, making commercial trade-offs explicit.
  • Experience running a multi-user portal or self-serve product with admin, approver and end-user roles, and working knowledge of payments flows and regulated controls including onboarding, identity and compliance.
  • Hands-on experience using AI to improve product discovery, decisions or delivery, with sound judgement on where it fits in a regulated financial services business.

Будет плюсом

  • Embedded finance, banking-as-a-service or partner-led distribution.
  • Vertical products in travel, lending, payroll or marketplaces.
  • Product operating models in scaled-up organisations.
