Compliance Officer

Compliance

Ebury Zurich Office - Hybrid: 4 days in the office, 1 day working from home per week

We are looking for a detail-oriented and proactive Compliance Officer to join our growing compliance team at Ebury Partners Switzerland AG, a professional financial intermediary operating in Switzerland as a member of the VQF SRO.

In this role, you will play a key part in protecting the business and its customers from financial crime, including money laundering, terrorist financing, fraud, and sanctions violations, within the 2nd line of defence (2LOD) and reporting locally to the Head of Compliance & MLRO - Switzerland. This position also serves as a vital link to our group 2nd line function and you will actively assist the Group Financial Crime Compliance (FCC) team. You will collaborate on group-wide initiatives, handle complex escalations, ensure cross-border alignment, and support multi-jurisdictional compliance strategies.

Что предстоит делать

  • Ensure financial crime risks are identified, assessed, managed, and reported in a timely manner for EDD, Transaction, Sanction, Screening, and PEP Escalations.
  • Assist and execute accurate pipeline management, ensuring workflow is handled in an efficient and timely manner.
  • Coordinate and review end-to-end 2LOD financial crime risk assessments on both client onboarding and client remediation accounts.
  • Conduct ad hoc deep-dive investigations on red flags raised by the First Line of Defence (1LOD) and as required by the business.
  • Make decisions and provide recommendations on appropriate actions in relation to financial crime issues raised within the Escalation process.
  • Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) research to support the analysis, preparation and submission of Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) to the Money Laundering Reporting Office Switzerland (MROS) in liaison with the Head of Compliance & MLRO - Switzerland
  • Support the Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) review of high risk customers including Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) in liaison with the Head of Compliance & MLRO - Switzerland
  • Develop, assess, maintain, update and ensure ongoing compliance with the local AML/CTF framework ensuring alignment to local Swiss regulatory expectation and best practice as well as overarching group risk appetites
  • Assist in the compilation and analysis of Management Information (MI) and compliance reporting for Local and Group leadership and committees.
  • Support group wide FCC projects such as the implementation of new compliance tools, system calibrations, and cross-jurisdictional financial crime investigations.
  • Horizon Scanning of local Swiss and broader international financial crime legislation

What you’ll need

  • 1-3 years of experience in AML, KYC, or financial crime compliance, preferably in the 2LOD within a payment institution, or fintech environment
  • Solid understanding of the Swiss regulatory model (SRO / FINMA), Swiss AMLA and related rules, ordinances and circulars.
  • Experience of scoping, conducting, documenting and communicating the outcomes of OSINT investigative work
  • Strong analytical and investigative skills with excellent attention to detail.
  • Ability to manage a caseload independently, prioritize effectively, and thrive in a global team structure.
  • Proficiency in English (written and spoken) plus one or more of German, French or Italian
  • Good working knowledge of Google Workspace.
  • EU or Swiss citizenship
