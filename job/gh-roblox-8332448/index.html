Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device. We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

The Trust & Safety Operations team safeguards Roblox’s community and creators. This role focuses on moderation operations in Trust & Safety. Within moderation, Game Safety, Creators and Compliance operations will be the primary focus. We are dedicated to improving Creator’s experience, protecting Intellectual Property and Digital Rights, ensuring strict regulatory compliance, while facilitating safe and fair ads and commerce activities for developers and users. We scale Operations and elevate our users’ experience through a multi‑year vision, rigorous execution, and data‑driven decision making.

You will be reporting to the Manager Data Operations and act as the data SME for moderation Operations, working closely with other domain-specific data analysts on the team. This position is critical to understanding, optimizing, and reporting on the operational performance and Platform Safety.

Вам предстоит

  • Own the analysis and reporting for Game Safety, Ads/Commerce, IP/DMCA, Compliance Operations and broader Legal Compliance Workflows. Deliver recurring KPI packs for senior leadership across Safety, Legal, and Developer Relations partners.
  • Identify opportunities through trend and cohort analysis; assess business cases and lead cross‑functional analytics initiatives from concept to impact.
  • Collaborate with Operations, Data Science, Product, Policy, and Developer Relations teams to define and maintain metric definitions, KPI hierarchies, and analytical guardrails that drive operational excellence.
  • Design, build, and maintain advance operational reports and dashboards, delivering critical insights to senior-level management and cross-functional stakeholders across Operations, Legal, Product, and Engineering.
  • Select and apply the most appropriate analytical methods to assess operations, extract relevant data, and derive actionable insights to ensure we meet all of Roblox's standards and obligations efficiently and accurately.
  • Monitor industry trends in digital rights management, regulatory compliance frameworks, and analytical best practices; incorporate them into our operational data strategies and tooling.

You Have

  • Experience: 5+ years of experience in a professional setting, with demonstrated experience in quantitative analysis and complex program management.
  • Domain Knowledge: Strong understanding of Trust & Safety operations and analytics. Good understanding of intellectual property operations (copyrights, trademarks, and Digital Rights Management), and legal compliance processes (such as regulatory response SLAs, privacy operations, or law enforcement guidelines) in the tech industry.
  • Technical Proficiency: Advanced proficiency in SQL, including complex queries, common table expressions, and window functions; working knowledge of Git and Python and/or R is highly desirable.
  • Cross-Functional Leadership: Proven ability to work effectively with cross-functional teams (specifically Legal, Product, Engineering, and frontline IP/Compliance Operations).
  • Problem Solving: Strong analytical skills with a track record of using data to inform decision-making, optimize complex workflows, and address operational bottlenecks.
  • Business Acumen: Deep understanding of business operations and the ability to align data strategies with strict legal, regulatory, and IP guidelines.
  • Strategic Prioritization: Strong multitasking and time management skills and ability to actively prioritize objectives based on business needs.
  • Education: Minimum of a Bachelor’s degree in a technical field / analytical discipline (preferred: Computer Science, Data Analytics, Information Systems, Statistics, Mathematics, Business Analytics or equivalent), with a strong preference for an MBA or MS.
