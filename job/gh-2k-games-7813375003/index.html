What we need

31st Union is looking for a Senior Gameplay Engineer to develop our Player Bots — AI agents that stand in for human players in our AAA free-to-play, cross-platform action game. Using C++, Unreal Engine 5, and our in-house utility AI framework, you will make bots that fight, traverse, loot, and team up convincingly enough that players second-guess whether they’re facing a human. The same bots power mode backfill and automated testing at scale.

Responsibilities

  • Develop combat behavior: target prioritization, hero-appropriate weapon and ability use, movement, and disengagement
  • Develop humanization: tunable aim, reaction time, and error models across difficulty levels
  • Develop macro behavior: team cohesion, traversal, pacing, events, looting, and progression
  • Extend our utility AI framework: new behaviors, scoring, and tuning
  • Keep bot populations performant: throttling, suspension, and LOD
  • Extend bots as automated test clients, from feature tests to match-scale load tests
  • Build bot evaluation tooling: debug visualization and playtest metrics
  • Partner with design to tune behavior through playtests

Who you are

  • 5+ years of gameplay or game AI engineering on shipped action games
  • Strong C++ and Unreal Engine fluency, including AI controllers, navigation, and EQS
  • Utility AI or comparable decision-making architectures
  • AI in networked multiplayer: client/server authority, prediction, dedicated servers
  • Humanlike combat AI: aim and error models, reaction time, believable movement
  • Optimizing many concurrent agents: LOD and tick throttling

Nice-to-Haves

  • Player bots in a competitive shooter
  • Unreal Engine 5, StateTree, and the Gameplay Ability System
  • Control systems (e.g., PID controllers) and input simulation
  • Automated test clients and load testing
