О роли

We are looking for a proactive and detail-oriented Trading Operations Analyst to join our team and help build and maintain robust processes whilst ensuring operational activities run smoothly and deliver a seamless customer experience.

Что предстоит делать

- Execute operational processes across all stages of the trade lifecycle.

- Identify, investigate and troubleshoot discrepancies in a timely manner.

- Collaborate with various internal teams to streamline operational processes.

- Manage broker and custodian enquiries efficiently and professionally.

- Create and maintain comprehensive documentation for all standard operating procedures (SOPs) and control frameworks.

- Develop expertise to become an internal subject matter expert for complex trading operations queries.

- Monitor internal systems to optimise operational efficiency and address any potential disruptions promptly.

- Contribute to business development projects under the guidance of senior team members.

WHAT YOU NEED TO HAVE

- Recently completed an undergraduate degree.

- An appetite for AI tools and automation where you'd rather build a smarter process than repeat the same task twice.

- A passion for continuous improvement where you're never quite satisfied with “good enough” when there's a smarter way forward.

- A creative and methodical approach to problem-solving, with a talent for cutting through complexity to find the most effective path forward.

- Strong analytical and organisational skills, paired with the ability to communicate complex ideas in a straightforward and engaging way.

- Someone who takes initiative but also values the benefits of collaboration within a team.

- A proactive mindset with the ability to take initiative and follow through without needing to be chased.

Будет плюсом

- Some exposure to financial markets, trading operations or a related field, whether through work, study or personal interest.

- A fascination with the intersection of trading and technology with a desire to play a part in shaping it.

- Proficiency in SQL, Python, APIs and/or similar tools.
