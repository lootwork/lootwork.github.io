О Opportunity

We are seeking a Market Surveillance Analyst to join our Compliance team. This role is focused on the day-to-day review and investigation of potential market abuse activity. You will use surveillance tools, trading data, and market research to assess alerts and produce timely, well-reasoned investigations supported by clear quantitative and qualitative evidence. The role requires understanding of markets, exchange products, and order-book dynamics, alongside sound judgment. You will work closely with Product, Engineering, Legal, Risk, Central Compliance, and local Compliance teams to support a robust and continuously improving surveillance framework. You will maintain complete, accurate, and audit-ready investigation records, while contributing to special projects and priority requests in a fast-paced global environment.

Что предстоит делать

  • Investigate potential market abuse activity, including wash trading, circular trading, price ramping, insider trading, spoofing, layering, and other related activities.
  • Review and analyze surveillance alerts using industry-leading tools and platforms.
  • Produce thorough, high-quality investigations supported by clear evidence and well-documented conclusions.
  • Use data, research, and analytical skills to conduct both quantitative and qualitative assessments.
  • Partner with Product, Engineering, Legal, Risk, Central Compliance, and local Compliance teams to strengthen and continuously improve the Surveillance function.
  • Maintain complete, accurate, and audit-ready documentation for alerts and investigations.
  • Support priority requests and special projects in a fast-paced, evolving environment.

What We Look For In You

  • BA/BS in a related technical field or equivalent practical experience.
  • 2-3 years of experience in market surveillance, trade surveillance, or closely related field.
  • Familiarity with end-to-end market surveillance workflows.
  • Strong understanding of the digital-asset industry and products offered by digital-asset exchanges.
  • Knowledge of market structure and order-book dynamics.
  • Working knowledge of relevant regulatory and compliance requirements.
  • Strong integrity, sound professional judgment, and the ability to recognize when an issue requires escalation.
  • A self-motivated, proactive approach, with the ability to work independently in a fast-paced global environment.

Будет плюсом

  • Familiarity with commonly used trade surveillance & reporting tools.
  • Professional AML/ATF compliance training or accreditation (e.g. ICA or CAMS).
  • Practical knowledge of CeFi trading products, including spot, derivatives, margin or leveraged products, institutional services, and OTC flows.
