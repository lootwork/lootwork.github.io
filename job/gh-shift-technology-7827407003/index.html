As part of the UK Project Management team, you will be central to how Shift delivers for its clients.

You will own complex, client-facing projects from planning through execution, bringing structure, clarity and momentum across Delivery, Data Science, Product, Engineering, GTM and customer teams.

This role is suited to an experienced Project Manager who is already comfortable operating independently, but who also values coaching, feedback and continuous development. You will be expected to anticipate issues before they escalate, challenge ambiguity, improve how we work and bring practical ideas that strengthen delivery.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the end-to-end delivery of complex enterprise software projects across the UK portfolio
  • Build and maintain credible delivery plans, milestones, dependencies, RAID registers, decision logs and governance rhythms
  • Connect Shift’s Delivery, Data Science, Product, Engineering and GTM teams with the customer’s technical and business stakeholders
  • Anticipate delivery, resource, commercial and customer risks early, then bring clear options and recommendations
  • Drive actions to closure with named owners and dates, rather than relying on broad email chains or unclear follow-up
  • Provide concise, fact-based reporting for customers and senior leadership
  • Maintain accurate project and portfolio information across the relevant systems, including Salesforce, Jira and delivery-planning tools
  • Lead customer workshops, governance meetings, Steering Committees and recovery discussions where required
  • Support pre-sales and opportunity shaping by validating scope, delivery methodology, assumptions, resource needs, timelines and Statements of Work
  • Challenge unsupported commitments constructively and ensure Delivery and technical assumptions are understood before plans are positioned externally
  • Manage several workstreams or clients at the same time without losing control of detail, priorities or customer outcomes
  • Improve delivery practices through reusable templates, smarter workflows, automation and practical use of AI
  • Contribute to strategic UK and wider Delivery initiatives that make execution more predictable, repeatable and scalable

Что вы приносите

  • 7+ years delivering enterprise SaaS and complex IT delivery: At least 7 years of experience delivering IT, enterprise SaaS, data, analytics, or digital transformation projects, with a strong background in complex client-facing implementations.
  • Independent cross-functional operator: The ability to operate independently across Product, Engineering, Data Science, and Go-To-Market teams, making sound judgments while knowing when to escalate appropriately.
  • Proactive risk management & project recovery: A proactive, pre-emptive mindset to identify risks before they occur, alongside proven experience in project recovery, managing escalations, and challenging scope when needed.
  • Strong ownership and execution: High personal accountability and follow-through. You do not wait to be chased.
  • Concise, impactful communication: Excellent written and verbal communication, with the ability to convey complex situations clearly, concisely, and actionably to senior customer stakeholders and internal leaders.
  • Commercial judgment & SOW management: Strong commercial awareness, including sharp commercial judgment and SOW awareness to balance scope, effort, cost, margin, and customer commitments.
  • Delivery governance & continuous improvement: Strong planning, governance, and risk-management skills, paired with a continuous-improvement mindset and a history of refining tools, templates, processes, or ways of working.
  • Adaptable & coachable collaborator: Comfortable navigating ambiguity, open to coaching and mentoring, and possess the maturity to challenge constructively while contributing original ideas.
  • Modern tooling proficiency: Experience using core delivery platforms such as Salesforce, Jira, Atlassian, Asana, Monday.com, or similar PMO systems.
  • Language skills: Native or full professional fluency in English.
  • Industry experience: Prior experience in insurance is strongly preferred; background in fraud, data science, or other regulated industries is beneficial.
  • Certifications: PMP, PRINCE2, Agile, Scrum, or similar project management certifications are advantageous, though not essential.

What Good Looks Like

  • Risks are surfaced early, not hidden
  • Customers receive clear, consistent and evidence-based updates
  • Plans reflect reality and are kept current
  • Actions always have an owner and a date
  • Delivery, Data Science and GTM remain aligned
  • Escalations come with options and recommendations
  • Repetitive work becomes templated, automated or removed
  • Lessons from one project improve the next
