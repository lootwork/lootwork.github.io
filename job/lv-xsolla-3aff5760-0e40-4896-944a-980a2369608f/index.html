О вас

We are looking for a Front-End Developer who is skilled, versatile, and passionate about crafting seamless experiences across web and mobile platforms to join our engineering team. The best candidate will be someone who thrives in a fast-paced, highly collaborative, and exceptionally dynamic setting and is excited to build responsive, high-quality interfaces that deliver exceptional user experiences for game developers and players worldwide.

Strong proficiency in React and TypeScript is essential, along with experience in mobile or hybrid development and modern front-end architecture. The ability to create scalable, maintainable components while collaborating effectively with designers, backend engineers, and product teams will be key to your success in this role.

If you're passionate about building beautiful, performant interfaces and love empowering the gaming industry with cutting-edge web and mobile experiences, we would love to hear from you!

О нас

Xsolla is a global commerce company with robust tools and services to help developers solve the inherent challenges of the video game industry. From indie to AAA, companies partner with Xsolla to help them fund, distribute, market, and monetize their games. Grounded in the belief in the future of video games, Xsolla is resolute in the mission to bring opportunities together, and continually make new resources available to creators. Headquartered and incorporated in Los Angeles, California, Xsolla operates as the merchant of record and has helped over 1,500+ game developers to reach more players and grow their businesses around the world. With more paths to profits and ways to win, developers have all the things needed to enjoy the game.

For more information, visit xsolla.com.

Обязанности

  • Develop and maintain complex, high-quality front-end components for both web and mobile platforms.
  • Implement responsive, adaptive interfaces that deliver exceptional user experiences across devices.
  • Integrate with existing systems using micro-frontends and module federation architectures.
  • Collaborate with designers, backend developers, and product teams to deliver robust, scalable features.
  • Build and maintain design systems and reusable UI components that ensure visual consistency.
  • Apply top-tier engineering standards for performance optimization, testing, and maintainability.
  • Participate in CI/CD pipelines, deployment workflows, and automated quality assurance.
  • Stay ahead of industry trends to continuously evolve the product’s technical and design standards.

Требования

  • 3+ years of experience in front-end development using React and TypeScript.
  • Experience with mobile or web hybrid development (React Native, Expo, or similar frameworks).
  • Strong understanding of modern front-end architecture, component design, and state management.
  • Familiarity with micro-frontends, modular architecture, and scalable design patterns.
  • Solid experience with responsive design, performance optimization, and accessibility standards.
  • Proven track record of collaborating in cross-functional teams and shipping high-quality features.
  • Familiarity with CI/CD, version control (Git), and modern testing tools (Jest, Cypress, or similar).
  • Excellent communication skills and a proactive, problem-solving mindset.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with design systems, Storybook, or component libraries.
  • Knowledge of progressive web apps (PWAs) and mobile-first development principles.
