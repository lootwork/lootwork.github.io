Head of Regulatory – EMEA

Adyen is seeking a strategic, high-impact, and visionary regulatory leader to serve as our Head of Regulatory – EMEA. In this role, you will be a senior leader in our Global Regulatory organization - including direct responsible for leading our Regional Counsel teams and Regulatory Strategy & Risk teams across EMEA .

Adyen is principally an EU-licensed credit institution with home-state supervision in the Netherlands. As such, Adyen’s regulatory footprint and licensing advantages in Europe form the strategic blueprint for what is possible for our business worldwide. You will lead a high-caliber team of senior regulatory experts who serve as the critical bridge between European supervisory expectations and our "Global-First" regulatory architecture. In this role, you will elevate our financial licensing position as a core differentiator in Adyen's right to win—transforming regulatory complexity into commercial opportunity and powering sustainable, controlled growth to achieve our ambition as the most customer-focused financial technology platform in the world.

Что предстоит делать

  • Unite & Lead the EMEA Regulatory Function
  • Provide overarching leadership and strategic direction across the EMEA Regulatory function - ensuring Adyen’s regulatory posture and supervisory relationships enable Adyen’s next phase of growth. ○ Serve as a true content and people leader for a senior team of regulatory experts, keeping them deeply connected to Adyen’s broader corporate strategy and global regulatory vision while navigating the dynamics of a fast-paced, highly regulated global business.
  • Cross-Functional 2LoD Leadership & Supervisory Presence
  • Act as a senior cross-functional leader across Adyen’s Second Line of Defense (Legal, Compliance, Risk, Internal Control and Data Privacy Office), building deep, collaborative relationships with leadership peers.
  • Position the EMEA Regulatory team as the essential first-analysis layer for emerging laws, regulations, and supervisory expectations across European markets, ensuring seamless translation into 2LoD risk frameworks and operational builds - in connection with their 2LoD counterparts.
  • Drive strategic engagement with key prudential and conduct regulators (including the DNB, FCA, ACPR, Bafin [and ECB]), positioning Adyen favorably and building long-term supervisory trust.
  • Global Regulatory Leadership Integration
  • Serve as a core member of the Global Regulatory Leadership Team, ensuring EMEA’s regulatory insights and licensing frameworks actively inform and shape our global baseline.
  • Partner seamlessly across our global domains: working in lockstep with Global Product Counsel (to orchestrate product enablement), Global Policy (to operationalize outcomes-based supervisory goals), and Global Partnerships (to enable scalable partner rails).
  • People Leadership & Adyen Leadership Expectations
  • Build a best-in-class team: Hire, develop, and retain top-tier regulatory leaders and SMEs; cultivate a diverse, inclusive, and agile environment; maintain an uncompromising quality threshold.
  • Define success for your team: Set a clear strategic direction for EMEA Regulatory aligned with Adyen's ultimate commercial ambitions; empower senior team members with autonomy while keeping them anchored to our long-term goals; communicate the "why" behind strategic decisions.
  • Deliver great results with your team: Direct team energy toward high-impact, needle-moving priorities; removing friction; act as a true multiplier for your team to accelerate business execution.

Кто вы

  • Senior Executive Leadership: 10+ years of experience, with at least 6+ years in a senior leadership position within a regulatory, legal, or supervisory function at a tier-1 bank, global credit institution, or complex fintech platform.
  • Deep EU/UK Regulatory Expertise: Comprehensive knowledge of European and UK financial frameworks (PSD2/PSD3, CRD/CRR, EMD, MiCA, DORA, Conduct rules) and credit institution supervisory dynamics. ● Strategic & Pragmatic Architect: Proven ability to balance fine regulatory detail with high-level corporate strategy. You possess a track record of transforming regulatory requirements into commercial speed and competitive advantages within the boundaries of risk appetite.
  • Cross-Functional Collaborator: Strong ability to build alignment across Second Line functions and First Line leadership without defaulting to consensus-seeking or creating operational delays.
  • Empowering People Leader: Exemplifies Adyen’s leadership principles—coaching senior contributors, fostering high autonomy and accountability, and maintaining a calm, steady presence in high-stakes environments.
  • Global Mindset & Fluency: Comfortable operating in a "Global-First" environment; fluent in English (proficiency in additional European languages is a plus).
