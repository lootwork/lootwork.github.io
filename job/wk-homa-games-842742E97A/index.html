Mission

You’ll join our Live Portfolio team, focused on bringing fresh energy to established games that don’t rely on continuous new content. Your role will be to reimagine existing creatives, identify new opportunities, and turn them into high-performing assets.

Working closely with the UA (User Acquisition) team, you’ll use performance insights to guide creative production and ensure ideas are built around what genuinely drives results.

  • Refresh and reinvent existing creatives through art revamps, new hooks, storytelling approaches, and trend-driven iterations.
  • Master the first three seconds by understanding what captures attention and continuously testing stronger hooks.
  • Bring multiple creative directions to the table, giving the team meaningful options to react to, test, and develop.
  • Benchmark relentlessly against top-performing creatives in the genre and stay ahead of emerging visual and creative trends.
  • Own high-quality video production end-to-end, creating compelling assets across both 2D and 3D.
  • Apply strong visual storytelling through composition, color, animation, pacing, staging, and overall art direction.
  • Push creative thinking further by challenging briefs, questioning assumptions, and proposing stronger alternatives.
  • Work within a structured, iterative creative roadmap, continuously incorporating UA performance data and learnings to improve future production.

The goal is simple: make existing games feel fresh, relevant, and impossible to scroll past.

Contract setup: This is a full-time freelance opportunity, structured around project needs, with flexible duration depending on the scope and evolution of the projects.

Требования

  • 2–3+ years of experience as a Marketing Video Artist, Motion Designer, or similar role, ideally within mobile gaming or performance-driven ad creative production.
  • Strong hands-on motion design skills across both 2D and 3D, with advanced proficiency in After Effects and working knowledge of Unity and/or Blender.
  • Solid animation fundamentals with a strong understanding of composition, color theory, animation principles, pacing, and a sharp visual eye.
  • A solution-oriented mindset — comfortable making the most of existing assets and finding creative opportunities without waiting for the perfect brief or a new set of resources.
  • Curiosity and a willingness to experiment, with an appetite for exploring new tools, formats, techniques, and creative approaches.
  • Autonomy and good creative judgment — able to navigate ambiguity, make informed creative decisions with limited feedback, and stay closely aligned with UA managers on what is being tested and why.
  • A strong team mindset — even as a freelancer, you’ll work in close, regular contact with the Live Portfolio team and be an active part of the creative process rather than working in isolation.

Дополнительный плюс

  • Experience using AI tools as part of the creative or production workflow.
  • Strong creative ideation skills — you’re comfortable challenging briefs, questioning assumptions, and bringing your own creative point of view.
  • An eye for static creative formats, including App Store screenshots, social visuals, and other performance-driven marketing assets.
