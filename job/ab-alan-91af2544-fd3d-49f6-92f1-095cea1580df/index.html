⭐ BRAND ASSOCIATE INTERNSHIP ⭐

The position is open for a six-month internship.

The Brand Associate joins Alan's Brand team at a pivotal moment: international expansion is accelerating, and the brand needs to scale with it. This role is built to flex across marketing strategy with a creative edge, building the playbooks that will let Alan's brand travel to new countries, launch new products at scale, and show up at the biggest international health event the industry has seen.

You will work in direct contact with Alan's Creative Director (Jérémy Clédat, co-founder & ex-CEO of Welcome to the Jungle) turning ambition into structured, actionable playbooks, and helping shape the team's own roadmap along the way.

⭐ DETAILED MISSIONS ⭐

As such, you can’t be exhaustive on the missions you will perform as a brand associate, as you can be asked to help on almost any brand related subject.

Possible projects include

1. Build the International Playbook

- Develop a recommended strategy on how Alan's brand should show up when entering a new market: M&A integration, hard launch, or partnering with a local provider

- Interview stakeholders from past expansions (Spain, Belgium, Canada) to capture what worked and what didn't

- Turn learnings into a reusable, structured playbook that future country launches can pick up and run with

- Cover brand naming/positioning decisions, local creative adaptation, and go-to-market sequencing for each entry mode

2. Develop the biggest health international event strategy

- Own the strategic thinking behind Alan annual health event: how do you build the biggest international health event, one that puts Alan's brand and mission on the map globally?

- Benchmark comparable flagship events (health, tech, and beyond) to identify what makes an event category-defining

- Build the concept, format, and multi-country activation plan

- Translate the event’s framing into a concrete, resourced event plan (content, guests, partners, logistics at a strategic level)

3. Build the Product Launch Playbook (GTM)

- Create a repeatable go-to-market playbook for future product launches: positioning, messaging, channels, sequencing, internal alignment

- Partner with Product Marketing and Product teams to pressure-test the playbook on a real, upcoming launch

- Ensure the playbook balances brand consistency with the flexibility needed for different product types and audiences

4. Support the brand’s team and the Creative Director

- Work in close, daily contact with the Creative Director as a thought partner and executor

- Support the definition and follow-up of the Brand team's OKRs, bringing structure and rigor to how progress is tracked

- Help translate the Creative Director's vision into concrete, actionable initiatives across the Brand team

⭐ PROFILE & COMPETENCIES ⭐

- Fluency in spoken and written English: all our communications are written and in English. French is a plus.

- Analytical sense and critical thinking: brand associate will have a lot of research and analysis to produce. This requires the ability to frame the right questions, objectively analyse and evaluate a problem, and problem-solve including simulating possible outcomes.

- Business & brand sense: understands what makes a brand credible and a launch successful, and connects that to commercial impact

- Creative sensibility: Genuine appetite for brand and creative work: can evaluate what makes a concept, a campaign, or an event idea strong. Comfortable partnering with creative profiles without needing to be one.

- Marketing & GTM fundamentals: good understanding of positioning, messaging, channel strategy, launch sequencing. Able to structure ambiguous, high-level briefs into clear frameworks and playbooks

- Execution and collaboration: given the scope, volume and timeline of work, the ability to direct people - including oneself - towards a particular objective that may require active orchestration is key.

- Versatility: comfortable moving between playbook-writing, event strategy, GTM thinking, and OKR structuring in the same week

- Communicate effectively: works with senior stakeholders (Creative Director, Product Marketing, international leads) and needs to represent ideas clearly and concisely

- Comfortable with ambiguity: several of these missions start as a blank page — energized by building structure where none exists yet

- Always looking for the extra-mile: as a brand associate, you will be expected to be able to deliver quickly and well on the tasks assigned to you, but also to be proactive in proposing new initiatives, going beyond the task assigned to you if you think it is necessary, as well as anticipating future needs to propose potential solutions…
