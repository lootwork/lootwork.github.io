О роли

Join the User Growth team to work on lifecycle operations and AI-driven personalized recommendations. You'll help put our recommendation strategies into action, coordinating with cross-functional teams, setting up and launching vouchers and tasks, and improving the new-user and active-user journeys to raise engagement and conversion. You'll work daily with global and Chinese-speaking teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Cross-functional alignment: Coordinate multiple teams (Operations, Product, UI/UX, Algorithm, Data, Legal, and regional teams) on the user
  • journey optimization project, tracking requirements and progress so every team delivers on time.
  • Voucher and task setup: Create, configure, check, and monitor vouchers and tasks so operations run smoothly and configurations are accurate.
  • Recommendation materials: Help build recommendation materials (education content, trending news, campaign guidance), track click and conversion performance, and suggest improvements.
  • User journey research: Walk through core user journeys, study the best experiences from competitors, identify friction points, and propose practical improvements.
  • Localization support: Work with regional teams to understand local user preferences and support localized content and strategy.

Кого мы ищем

  • Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's program. Business, Marketing, Data Analytics, Economics, Communications, or related fields preferred.
  • Strong communication and coordination skills, with the ability to manage multiple teams and tasks at once. Detail-oriented and organized.
  • Quick learner with a strong sense of ownership, comfortable taking initiative in a fast-paced environment.
  • Proficiency in Excel or Google Sheets.
  • Bilingual English/Mandarin is required to be able to coordinate with overseas partners and stakeholders.

Будет плюсом

  • Previous internship in user growth, user operations, campaign operations, or project management.
  • Basic data analysis skills, and familiarity with SQL or data visualization tools.
  • Interest in crypto, fintech, or internet user growth.
  • Experience using Binance or other exchange products.
