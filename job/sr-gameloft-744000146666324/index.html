Job Description

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON

Main challenge

We are looking for a Concept Artist to support the pre-production and visual development of our core game titles, creating concepts and artistic references that help align production teams around a clear visual direction. The role focuses on projects inspired by the visual standards of Disney Speedstorm and Asphalt 9.

What it means on a daily basis

  • Visual Concept Generation: Create character designs and illustrations, including line art and 3D blockouts, in line with project requirements and the distinct visual styles of Hyperdrive, Asphalt, and Disney.
  • Creative Ideation: Generate and develop artistic ideas while maintaining each game's unique visual identity.
  • Collaborative Design: Collaborate with fellow artists and designers to develop and enhance the game's visual style.
  • Feedback Integration: Work under the Studio Art Director's guidance, iterate on designs based on feedback, and deliver work in line with project goals and timelines.
  • Art Asset Planning: Plan the creation of art assets for characters, environments, and props, and clearly communicate the approach and requirements to the relevant team members.

What success will look like

After 3 months, you will have built a strong understanding of the projects visual directions and production workflows. After 6 months, you will independently deliver concepts and references that support production decisions. You will also contribute consistently to the evolution of the games' visual identity and support effective collaboration across art and design teams.

Who you would be working with

You will report to the Studio Art Director and work closely with concept artists, artists, designers, and production teams. Your concepts and visual references will support local and international collaborators throughout the production process.

Your recruitment journey

  • HR Screen Call: An initial conversation with our HR team.
  • First Interview: A 20-minute interview with the Hiring Manager to discuss your previous projects and experience.
  • Test: You will have one week to complete a practical test.
  • Second Interview: A discussion with the Hiring Manager to review your test, explore your approach in more detail, and share feedback.
  • Final Interview: A meeting with the Studio Manager to discuss alignment and answer any remaining questions.

Where this role can take you

This role can offer opportunities to deepen your expertise in concept art, explore broader artistic responsibilities, and access development opportunities, mentorship, coaching, or internal mobility depending on business and individual opportunities

Требования

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED

  • Demonstrated advanced expertise in concept art or a related discipline, supported by a strong portfolio
  • Advanced proficiency in Photoshop for digital art creation and proficiency with Blender or similar 3D software for concept development.
  • Solid ability to produce clear and efficient sketches and develop concepts for characters, environments, and props.
  • Ability to manage assigned work independently while collaborating closely with artists, designers, and production teams
  • Fluent professional English, written and spoken, for collaboration in an international environment.

Кто вы

  • You are comfortable developing ideas and adapting them based on feedback.
  • You communicate clearly and collaborate effectively with different team members.
  • You can manage your work independently while contributing to a collaborative creative process.
