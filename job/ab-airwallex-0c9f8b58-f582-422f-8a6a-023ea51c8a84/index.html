HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

Reporting to the FCC Transaction Monitoring Lead, you'll lead the Financial Crime Compliance Level 3 review function, applying sound judgment to complex cases and helping strengthen the quality and consistency of FCC operations.

- Lead and develop a team of Financial Crime Compliance Level 3 analysts, setting a high bar for case quality, timely resolution and consistent process governance.

- Independently review and resolve complex, risk-based cases escalated from first- and second-line operations across KYC, enhanced due diligence, name screening and transaction monitoring.

- Assess customer profiles, ownership and control structures, business activities, transaction patterns, counterparties and supporting information to reach clear, evidence-based conclusions.

- Conduct internal and external research, critically assess information from customers and internal stakeholders, and document investigative rationale, supporting evidence and follow-up actions.

- Identify financial-crime risks, recurring themes and opportunities to strengthen processes and controls, escalating higher-risk cases, potential sanctions matches and suspicious activity concerns through the appropriate channels.

- Partner closely with Operations and Compliance stakeholders, maintain accurate case records, apply global and local FCC policies and procedures, and stay current on relevant financial-crime risks and regulatory developments.

This is a remote role based in Dallas, Texas.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- A degree or at least eight years of experience in Financial Crime Compliance.

- Proven experience in an AML/CTF or fraud investigations role.

- Strong analytical and investigative skills, with the ability to assess complex information and reach well-reasoned conclusions.

- A solid understanding of financial-crime risks, typologies and risk-based review practices.

- Sound judgment, ownership and the ability to work independently in a fast-paced environment.

- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with strong attention to detail.

- Experience overseeing, mentoring or training analysts, with a focus on high-quality case resolution and process governance.

Preferred

- Experience in fintech or a start-up environment.

- Experience reviewing complex cases across KYC, enhanced due diligence, name screening and transaction monitoring.

- Experience conducting internal and external research and documenting clear investigative rationale, supporting evidence and follow-up actions.

- Experience working across global and local Financial Crime Compliance policies and regulatory requirements.
