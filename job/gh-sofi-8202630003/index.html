О роли

We are hiring a Sr Director, Enterprise Architecture to lead CIO-office Enterprise Architecture at SoFi. This person will own and mature the EA practice, and use it to drive meaningful business impact across Enterprise Applications, IT Ops, Data, and Applied AI.

You report to the CIO and operate as cross-cutting connective tissue for the CIO’s stack: partner peer-to-peer with VP IT, VP Data, and Sr Director Enterprise Applied AI (and with Enterprise Apps, Security/IAM, and GRC/HR Tech stakeholders). Your job is to make architectural inputs matter on roadmaps and spend, not to run checkbox IT-stack reviews.

What You'll Own

  • Lead the Enterprise Architecture practice under the CIO (small, hands-on team of Staff+ architects)
  • Facilitate the Architecture Review Board (ARB) as a speed bump, not a stop sign: coherent decisions, audit-ready posture, without slowing delivery
  • Drive standards, reference patterns, and paved paths across Enterprise Apps, IT Ops, Data platform partnership, and AI-enabled enterprise solutions
  • Own SaaS / application portfolio discipline: reduce sprawl, improve reuse, and align big-ticket platform choices to strategy
  • Influence nontechnical leaders (Finance, HR, Risk, Ops, and GBU partners) so architecture decisions change outcomes (cost, risk, speed, adoption)
  • Partner with Security/IAM and GRC so enterprise platforms stay control-aware in a regulated bank / fintech environment
  • Coach solution teams on enterprise patterns

Как выглядит успех в этой роли

  • Federated leaders seek EA early because it accelerates delivery, not because it gates tickets
  • Measurable impact on SaaS spend, consolidation, paved-path adoption, or risk reduction — not ARB volume alone
  • IT, Data, and Applied AI initiatives share coherent patterns instead of optimizing locally into incompatible stacks
  • ARB and standards feel like an accelerator inside a regulated environment

What You'll Bring

  • 12+ Years of experience leading Enterprise Architecture inside a CIO office (Enterprise Apps + IT Ops + Data + AI purview), not CTO/infra-only
  • Evidence of business-oriented influence: inputs that changed roadmap or spend outcomes with leaders who did not report to you
  • Fluency across business, applications, data, and technology architecture; comfort with SaaS portfolio maturity and integration patterns
  • Demonstrated impact (cost, risk, speed, consolidation, adoption), not framework theater alone
  • Credibility at Sr Director / Head / VP EA altitude; can partner peer-to-peer with VP-level IT/Data/AI leaders
  • Comfort operating governance as an accelerator in a regulated fintech or bank context
  • Hands-on practice leadership: willing to rebuild and run a small EA team, not only set strategy decks

Nice to have: enterprise SaaS / scaled tech / fintech background; prior ARB ownership; Workday / ServiceNow / Salesforce-class estate experience; TOGAF or similar as a tool, not a personality.
