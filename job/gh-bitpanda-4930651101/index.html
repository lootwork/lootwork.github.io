Кто мы

We simplify wealth creation. Founded in 2014 in Vienna, Austria by Eric Demuth, Paul Klanschek and Christian Trummer, we’re here to help people trust themselves enough to build their financial freedom — for now and the future. Our user-friendly, trade-everything platform empowers both first-time investors and seasoned experts to invest in the cryptocurrencies, crypto indices, stocks*, precious metals and commodities* they want — with any sized budget, 24/7. Our global team works across different cultures and time zones, bringing our products to more than 7 million customers, making us one of Europe’s safest and most secure platforms that powers modern investing.

Headquartered in Austria but operating across Europe, our products are built by fast-moving, talented, “roll-up-your-sleeves-and-make-it-happen” kind of people. It’s these diverse perspectives and innovative minds operating as ONE TEAM that keep Bitpanda at the cutting edge of our industry. So if you’re someone who thinks big, moves fast and wants to make an impact right from day one, then get ready to join our industry-changing team. Let’s go!

The Mission

We are on a mission to democratize finance for over 7 million users. As our Senior Product Designer for Securities & Commodities, your mandate is to take the high-stakes, data-heavy complexity of equities, derivatives, commodities, and real-world assets (RWAs) and architect them into seamless, intuitive user journeys. In our collaborative, "no-silo" culture, this role tackles the heavy lifting of investment products at scale—partnering directly with Product, Engineering, Data and Compliance to drive real-time impact on conversion, retention, and user trust without sacrificing functionality.

Что предстоит делать

  • The core of this role involves designing end-to-end trading and investment interfaces for TradFi and RWA products across mobile and web platforms, with the clear goal of increasing customer engagement and monetization.
  • Dense financial mechanics—including margin trading, commodities trading, and automated recurring investments—must be translated into accessible, low-friction journeys that educate and empower both novice and experienced investors.
  • Comprehensive user research, usability testing, and behavioral analysis will serve as your primary tools to diagnose funnel bottlenecks, validate product hypotheses, and continuously improve KPIs like trading conversion and drop-off rates.
  • Active contributions to our scalable design architecture are expected, applying the highest UX standards to ensure high-quality, consistent touchpoints across all investment areas.
  • Seamless partnership with Product, Engineering, Risk, and Legal teams in a fast-moving, regulated environment is critical to shape the product roadmap, validate customer problems, and ensure feasible, compliant feature implementations.

Кто вы

  • A strong background in Product/UX/UI Design is required, featuring mandatory experience designing for complex fintech, brokerage, trading, or wealth management ecosystems.
  • Exceptional proficiency in Figma, rapid prototyping, and interaction design is essential, alongside a proven ability to untangle data-heavy interfaces and intricate workflows into clean, elegant systems.
  • Behavioral analytics, A/B testing, and funnel metrics should naturally drive your design decisions to proactively identify opportunities that boost customer engagement, adoption, and retention.
  • A proven track record of thriving in fast-moving, highly regulated environments will demonstrate your ability to successfully negotiate solutions that satisfy strict Risk and Compliance requirements without compromising the end-user experience.
  • High customer empathy and excellent stakeholder management skills are necessary to build long-term user trust while championing a product-driven, highly collaborative culture across the organization.
