О RAMP

Ramp is building the smart infrastructure for finance teams, embedded in the transaction flow of every dollar a business spends. We automate how over $275B in annualized spend flows in and out of 85,000+ companies: authorizing payments, flagging risk, categorizing spend, and closing books.

The problems are high-stakes, data-dense, and unforgiving.

If you want to build systems that directly shape how companies move and manage billions, Ramp is the place to do it.

О роли

Getting paid is still one of the most manual parts of running a business. Finance teams chase invoices over email, match payments to invoices by hand in spreadsheets, and close the books late because cash data is scattered across banks, payment processors, and ERPs.

Ramp's Accounts Receivable team is building the system that automates all of it, from the moment an invoice is sent to the moment cash is reconciled in the general ledger. We own the full receivables lifecycle: invoicing, collections, payments, cash application, and accounting sync.

We're looking for a senior backend engineer to own core pieces of that system. The work sits where hard engineering problems meet real money: matching messy payment data to the right invoices, keeping ledgers correct to the cent, and syncing reliably with the accounting systems our customers depend on. You'll own projects end to end, from design through production, and work closely with product and design to turn complex finance workflows into software that just works.

Что предстоит делать

- Build the payment-matching systems that automatically apply incoming ACH, wire, card, and check payments to the right invoices, even when payment details are incomplete or inconsistent

- Design accurate data models where every dollar is traceable from invoice to payment to general ledger

- Build two-way integrations with ERPs like NetSuite and QuickBooks that stay consistent through retries, partial failures, and edits on either side

- Build collections workflows that help businesses get paid faster without hurting their customer relationships

- Own projects from problem definition and technical design through launch, monitoring, and iteration

- Raise the engineering bar through design reviews, code reviews, and mentorship

WHAT YOU’LL NEED

- Product sense: you care about the customer problem, not just the ticket, and you push back when the spec is wrong

- Experience designing and operating production backend systems, including APIs, data models, and asynchronous workflows

- Strong system design judgment and the ability to balance speed, correctness, reliability, and simplicity

- A track record of independently owning complex projects and shipping high-quality software

- Strong communication skills and a collaborative approach to technical decisions

Будет плюсом

- Experience with invoicing, billing, payments, accounting, or other financial workflows

- Experience building integrations that keep data consistent across systems

- Experience improving reliability and operational visibility in a growing product
