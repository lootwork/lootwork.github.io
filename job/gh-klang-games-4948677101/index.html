At Klang, we create products that inspire people to deepen their understanding of the emergent nature of society and collectively envision a better future.

At the heart of Klang is SEED, an accessible, persistent world in which players share a history and shape a future. We provide an environment for a broad audience to belong and develop relationships in a society simulation inspired by life itself.

We aspire to bring people together through engaging media experiences and by building strong communities.

It's a super exciting project, but in order for us to achieve a goal like this we are going to need a lot of help! From people just like you.

SEED entered Early Access in July 2026. We're a small publishing team with a fast-moving community, and we need someone who can help us keep up with it.

О роли

We're looking for a freelance designer/video producer to create social content across our channels, short-form vertical video, static graphics, motion, and channel assets. You'll work directly with the publishing team on a steady cadence of content, plus campaign moments around updates and events.

This isn't a role where you'll be handed finished storyboards. We'll give you direction, raw capture, and context; you'll bring the craft and the format instincts.

Что предстоит делать

  • Cut short-form vertical video for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts from gameplay capture, dev footage, and community clips
  • Design static and animated graphics for X, Instagram, LinkedIn, Discord, and Steam
  • Turn in-game moments and player stories into content people actually want to share
  • Adapt assets across formats, aspect ratios, and channels without losing the idea
  • Work within our existing brand guidelines while pushing new formats and finding what works

Кого мы ищем

  • A portfolio of short-form social video, games, or entertainment is a strong plus
  • Fluency in editing and design tooling (Premiere / After Effects / DaVinci, Figma / Photoshop / Illustrator, or your equivalents)
  • Platform-native instincts. You know that what lands on TikTok isn't what lands on LinkedIn, and you edit accordingly
  • Comfort working fast, self-directed, and from imperfect source material
  • Games literacy: you understand player culture well enough to tell when something reads as authentic and when it reads as marketing
  • Working English (German not required)

Будет плюсом

  • Gameplay capture experience
  • Sound design/audio editing
  • Experience editing community and UGC footage
  • Familiarity with MMOs, simulation, or systems-driven games

If you don’t feel that you quite meet all the requirements above, but the position still excites you and you think you would love to work for Klang, reach out! We still want to hear from you.

We explicitly encourage applications from applicants from groups underrepresented in games/tech spaces. We value all kinds of backgrounds and walks of life.
