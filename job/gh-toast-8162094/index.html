Toast is driven by building the restaurant platform that helps restaurateurs adapt, take control, and get back to what they do best: building the businesses that they love. As Toast continues to grow, we are seeking an experienced Solutions Engineer to help us continue to grow internationally expanding into mid - market and enterprise accounts.

Hybrid, London-based — with some travel to customer sites as needed.

О this role

  • Assist Sales teams in presenting the Toast product and platform to prospects and be able to demonstrate the value of a complete solution to each buying persona
  • Become a subject matter expert on Toast products with complete knowledge of the technologies, application software, and integrated partners, and future product roadmap
  • Work closely with Sales Enablement regionally to provide training and product expertise
  • Communicate with Toast product teams to clearly communicate gaps in product/market fit to drive strategic investments
  • Attend trade shows and other events as a Toast brand ambassador
  • Must have ability to work closely with Product Management, Sales Enablement, and Marketing teams
  • Drive cross functional collaboration across the business to ensure that expectations set during the sales process are met in delivery
  • Use restaurant industry knowledge / experience to consult with prospects on how Toast can help them outperform their peers

Do you have the right ingredients

  • Presentation Skills: Executive presence and the ability to articulate complex products and ideas with clarity, and handle complex customer communications fluently while maintaining high levels of customer satisfaction.
  • Strong understanding of APIs (coding skills not required)
  • Sales Experience: 3+ years of sales experience in a closing or closing support role within the Restaurant/Hospitality segment.
  • Collaborative: Highly collaborative, serving as a trusted partner to sales, onboarding, and product teams, with strong problem solving/troubleshooting skills.
  • Industry Experience: Must have experience with cloud POS systems, ideally within the Restaurant, Hospitality, or related industry. Experience with multi-location brands, enterprise/franchise IT systems, or complex organizational structures is highly desirable.
  • Must be willing to perform nationwide travel to support business needs.

Special Sauce (non-essential skills, nice to have)

  • Hospitality Experience: Must have experience working in hospitality in some capacity, giving you the ability to understand the challenges our customers face quickly.
  • Toast Product Knowledge: Prior experience with Toast POS is preferred, within sales, implementation, training, or support.
  • Must have the ability to work with minimal supervision: This role will require working independently as the first member of the team within the UK region.
