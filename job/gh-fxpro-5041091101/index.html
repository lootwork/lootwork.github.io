As a Payment Analyst (Alternative Payment Methods), you will play a crucial role in analyzing and optimizing the performance of our payment solutions, turning transaction data into actionable insights that improve checkout conversion, reduce processing costs, and detect fraud patterns early.

Обязанности

  • APM strategy: Use market trends and client transaction data to find, evaluate and prioritize new alternative payment methods that improve checkout conversion.
  • Performance & declines: Track daily, weekly, and monthly deposit and withdrawal metrics (approval rates, processing times, volumes, and average transaction values) by country, provider, and method. Categorize decline codes and failure reasons to find and fix recurring issues.
  • Provider benchmarking: Compare provider performance within each country and recommend routing changes, provider switches, and adding or removing methods. Work with Business Development on the order of methods at checkout and on method names and logos.
  • Dashboards & alerts: Build and maintain payment dashboards by country and provider in Excel, Google Sheets or BI tools. Set up automated alerts when performance drops below target, so the team can act quickly.
  • Reporting & insights: Prepare weekly performance reports and monthly management summaries explaining what changed, why, and what to do next. Present findings to the team and other departments.
  • Costs & fees: Track processing fees and cost per transaction across providers and methods. Provide cost analyses to Finance and Business Development for fee reviews and contract negotiations.
  • Risk & anomalies: Monitor transactions for unusual patterns such as fraud, abuse, chargeback spikes, or technical outages, and escalate them quickly to the right teams.
  • Settlements & reconciliation: Work with Finance to investigate settlement discrepancies, rolling reserves, and payout delays with providers.
  • Provider & cross-team support: Prepare data for monthly provider meetings and run ad-hoc analyses for Management, Finance, and Marketing. Support A/B tests of the cashier to measure their impact on conversion.
  • Documentation: Document report definitions, formulas, and data sources, and create templates and SOPs so recurring reports are easy to reproduce.

Требования

  • 2-3 years of experience as a Payment Analyst in Fintech, Forex/CFD, or iGaming
  • Strong analytical skills, with the ability to interpret data and present clear conclusions
  • Advanced Excel and Google Sheets skills; experience with BI tools (Power BI, Amplitude)
  • Practical knowledge of payment systems, gateways, and APMs
  • Understanding of fraud management, settlement reporting, KYC, and AML
  • University degree, preferably in Finance or Economics
  • Strong attention to detail, problem-solving, and communication skills
  • Excellent time management and follow-through until cases are resolved
  • Logical, business-oriented mindset
  • Excellent command of English; additional languages are an advantage
