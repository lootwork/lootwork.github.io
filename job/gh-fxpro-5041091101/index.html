As a Payment Analyst (Alternative Payment Methods), you will play a crucial role in analyzing and optimizing the performance of our payment solutions, turning transaction data into actionable insights that improve checkout conversion, reduce processing costs, and detect fraud patterns early.

Обязанности

  • APM Strategy & Evaluation: Analyze market trends and client transaction data to identify, evaluate, and prioritize new alternative payment methods that boost checkout conversion rates.
  • Performance & Decline Monitoring: Track daily, weekly, and monthly deposit and withdrawal metrics—including approval rates, processing times, transaction volumes, and average transaction values—across countries, providers, and methods. Categorize decline codes and failure reasons to identify and resolve recurring bottlenecks.
  • Provider Benchmarking & Optimization: Compare APM provider performance within each country to recommend traffic routing changes, provider switches, and method additions or removals. Partner with Business Development teams to optimize checkout sorting orders and update method branding (names and logos).
  • Dashboards & Automated Alerting: Build and maintain scalable payment dashboards (using Excel, Google Sheets, or BI tools) by country and provider. Configure automated alerts to flag performance drops below target thresholds so the team can respond quickly.
  • Executive Reporting & Insights: Produce weekly performance reports and monthly management summaries featuring clear commentary on what changed, why it happened, and recommended next steps. Present key findings to internal teams and cross-functional departments.
  • Cost & Fee Optimization: Track processing fees and cost-per-transaction across all providers and methods. Deliver detailed cost analyses to support Finance and Business Development during provider fee reviews and contract negotiations.
  • Risk, Chargebacks & Anomaly Detection: Monitor transaction flows for unusual patterns that indicate fraud, abuse, chargeback spikes, or technical outages, escalating issues promptly to the relevant teams.
  • Settlement & Reconciliation Support: Collaborate with Finance to investigate settlement discrepancies, rolling reserves, and payout delays across payment service providers.
  • Cross-Functional & Provider Collaboration: Prepare data packs for monthly provider catch-up meetings and deliver ad-hoc analyses for Management, Finance, and Marketing. Support A/B testing on cashier layouts to measure the impact of checkout changes on conversion.
  • Documentation & Standardization: Document report definitions, calculation formulas, and data sources, creating standardized templates and SOPs so recurring reports are easily reproducible across the team.

Требования

  • 2-3 years of experience as a Payment Analyst in Fintech, Forex/CFD, or iGaming
  • Strong analytical skills, with the ability to interpret data and present clear conclusions
  • Advanced Excel and Google Sheets skills; experience with BI tools (Power BI, Amplitude)
  • Practical knowledge of payment systems, gateways, and APMs
  • Understanding of fraud management, settlement reporting, KYC, and AML
  • University degree, preferably in Finance or Economics
  • Strong attention to detail, problem-solving, and communication skills
  • Excellent time management and follow-through until cases are resolved
  • Logical, business-oriented mindset
  • Excellent command of English; additional languages are an advantage
