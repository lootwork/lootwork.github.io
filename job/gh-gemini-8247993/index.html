О Company

Gemini is a global crypto and Web3 platform founded by Cameron and Tyler Winklevoss in 2014, offering a wide range of simple, reliable, and secure crypto products and services to individuals and institutions in over 70 countries. Our mission is to unlock the next era of financial, creative, and personal freedom by providing trusted access to the decentralized future. We envision a world where crypto reshapes the global financial system, internet, and money to create greater choice, independence, and opportunity for all — bridging traditional finance with the emerging cryptoeconomy in a way that is more open, fair, and secure. As a publicly traded company, Gemini is poised to accelerate this vision with greater scale, reach, and impact.

The Department: Marketing

The Role: Content Production Intern

We’re looking for a Content Production Intern to help bring our social engine to life. You’ll work alongside the marketing & product team to brainstorm ideas, spot opportunities, and help produce content across our social channels, getting hands on experience with everything from creative development and production to launches and social strategy. We’re looking for someone who’s curious, plugged into the internet, has that dog in them, and is excited to learn by making things.

This will be a semester long internship program with 3 days a week in person at our New York City, NY office from January to April 2027.

Обязанности

  • Edit guest segment recordings into podcast episodes formatted for Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and YouTube
  • Clip 2-3 moments per stream for use on X
  • Sit in the stream room during live broadcasts as a live producer, spotting standout moments in real time and clipping them
  • Run live stream graphics and on screen elements during the broadcast
  • Track trends, internet culture, and industry news to help pitch stream titles and topics
  • Collaborate with the social team on distribution strategy for each piece of content once it's produced

Требования

  • Pursuing an associate's, bachelor's, or master's degree in any relevant field
  • Genuine interest in media production, livestreaming, and internet culture
  • Working knowledge of video editing tools (Premiere, CapCut, DaVinci, etc.)
  • Comfortable working live, under time pressure, in a room where things move fast
  • An eye for what makes a moment clips and text engaging
  • Organized enough to manage multiple deliverables (podcast cut, clips, graphics) from a single stream
  • Has that dog in them
  • Genuine interest in finance, crypto, and prediction markets is a plus
