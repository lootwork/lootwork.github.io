At Kabam, we’re constantly raising the bar of excellence in free-to-play gaming. We hire for passion and diversity so that we can triumph in our collective skill. Our teams are made up of a fusion of personalities and interests bound together by a collaborative nature, fun-loving culture, and a drive to do what it takes to make great games.

The key function of an Animation Co-op is to deliver high quality animations for our games. This role will work collaboratively with other animators and artists to produce dynamic and polished art, and improve workflows and pipeline.

This is a 4-month position from January to April 2027. This role is a full-time, on-site position based 5 days a week at our Montréal studio.

IN THIS ROLE, YOU CAN EXPECT TO

Key Accountabilities

Deliver World-Class Assets and Animations for Our Games

  • Work under the guidance of experienced animators to help with various aspects of game animation, including character animations, creature movements, object interactions, and environmental animations.
  • Create, edit, and polish animations.
  • Work efficiently within deadlines and milestones.

Work Collaboratively with the Art Production Team

  • Work closely with other members of the game development team, including game designers, artists, and programmers, to ensure animations align with the game's design and technical requirements.

Learn the Animation Techniques & Delivery Pipelines

  • Build proficiency in game animation. Create seamless loops, squash and stretch characters, character interactions, and handle props. Learn how to implement these in the engine and have them ready for the next step in the pipeline.
  • Pursue opportunities to push skills and tackle new and interesting challenges to improve artistic and technical skills.
  • Develop the skills to use animation software such as Maya, and learn the Unity game engine.
  • Absorb advice, knowledge, and ideas from the other artists and designers to continually improve quality and speed of animation deliverables.

YOUR BACKGROUND INCLUDES

  • Working towards a post-secondary degree in a related field (animation, fine arts, computer graphics, etc.)
  • Introductory knowledge of game development.
  • Solid understanding of animation principles.
  • Good understanding of character animation and body in motion mechanics.
  • Experience with animation tools; experience in rigging is an asset.
