The opportunity

As Analytics Manager for Collections & Recoveries at Zopa, you’ll lead a team of three analysts. Their work helps colleagues understand performance and make informed decisions, with a real impact on customers in financial difficulty.

This is a chance to bring stronger data foundations and dependable reporting together, and use them to unlock new possibilities for automation, self-service and AI-enabled ways of working.

You’ll take ownership of the team’s data and reporting, defining a roadmap to build trusted data pipelines and establish a comprehensive semantic layer. Through better data quality, automation and accessibility, you’ll make analysis and insight possible at scale.

Working closely with Collections stakeholders, including Analysts, Product and Engineers, as well as Analytics Engineering and other central teams, you’ll align on data strategy, embed best practices and build solutions that scale. Balancing a reliable reporting service with longer-term improvements, you’ll agree priorities and deliver change that matters.

You’ll be at home working hands-on with data, especially as you get to know the landscape. By exploring datasets, understanding reporting logic and investigating data issues alongside the team, you’ll build the context needed to make sound technical decisions and set a coherent direction. Just as importantly, you’ll give your team the support and space to grow and deliver.

A DAY IN THE LIFE

  • Lead, coach and develop a team of three analysts, setting clear expectations, supporting their growth and creating an environment where people can do their best work.
  • Own the delivery and quality of recurring Collections & Recoveries reporting and insight, helping stakeholders understand performance and make informed decisions.
  • Get hands-on with SQL, GitHub and dbt to understand source systems, reporting logic and business context.
  • Shape and deliver a coherent data and reporting roadmap, improving the foundations for self-service, natural-language querying and AI-enabled workflows.
  • Partner with Analytics Engineering, Data Engineering and Engineering to improve data models, pipelines and data quality, and move manual or fragmented processes towards maintainable solutions.
  • Define and document clear, consistent metrics and contribute to a semantic layer that makes data easier to understand and use across reporting and analysis.
  • Improve dashboard capabilities, self-service insight and reporting, whilst also identifying opportunities to automate manual work.
  • Work with stakeholders and Central Analytics to prioritise improvements and share progress.

О вас

  • Experience managing and developing analysts, with a thoughtful approach to coaching, feedback and delivery.
  • Strong SQL skills with the ability to work hands-on with complex data models and pipelines.
  • Experience designing or improving data models and pipelines, with knowledge of dbt or a comparable transformation tool and sound data modelling principles.
  • Experience delivering reliable reporting and dashboards using a BI tool such as Tableau, Looker or Power BI, with an understanding of the data that underpins them.
  • The ability to set a clear direction, prioritise competing demands and make progress on longer-term improvements alongside an ongoing reporting service.
  • Strong analytical judgement and attention to detail, including an understanding of metric definitions, documentation and the importance of accurate, trusted data.
  • Confidence working with technical and non-technical stakeholders, translating business needs into practical solutions and explaining decisions and trade-offs clearly.
  • A proactive approach to identifying and adopting AI use cases, both in day-to-day work and more strategically.

Helpful experience

  • Experience in financial services, fintech or another regulated environment, including exposure to collections, credit, customer or operational performance.
  • Experience with semantic layers, self-service reporting or natural-language analytics.
  • Python experience for analysis or automation.
