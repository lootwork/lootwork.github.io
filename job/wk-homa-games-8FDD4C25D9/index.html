Homa is a global mobile game developer and publisher creating games people love. We partner with studios and internally develop games, having launched over 80 titles, reached over 2 billion downloads, and seen our game All in Hole break into the global top-50 grossing charts. These are milestones, not the finish line.

With deep expertise in product and technology, we built Homa Lab, our proprietary platform that gives developers the market intelligence, data tools, and game tech, with AI built-in, to find product–market fit fast and scale mass-market games into lasting experiences players enjoy for years.

This is our flywheel: Homa Lab helps studios spot the right ideas early, turn them into games quickly, and reach millions of players. Every launch brings back data and insights that fuel the next hit, making each turn of the loop faster, smarter, and more likely to win. AI supercharges this process, running through everything we do, from everyday tasks to our guiding principles.

What powers the loop is talent density. 220+ people from 30+ nationalities, bringing expertise from the world’s best studios and companies. We uphold ambition, curiosity, humility, and focus, and deliver with speed, excellence, and candor.

Since the start, we’ve raised $165M from Headline, Northzone, Eurazeo, Bpifrance, and the founders of King, Sorare, and Spotify, people who’ve built and backed the games and platforms we admire, now supporting our drive to create the next ones.

At Homa, we are building the next billion-player experiences from the ground up and shaping the future of entertainment.

О this opportunity

You will work alongside our Chief of Staff and senior leadership, driving key initiatives that shape the growth and success of Homa. This role offers a rare opportunity to gain hands-on exposure to high-impact strategic projects across the company — interacting directly with C-level executives and experienced leaders across every function.

You will develop a true 360° vision of a fast-moving gaming company, contributing to projects with direct business impact. Based on your profile, strengths, and evolving business needs, you will be embedded in one or several of our core teams — and your scope may shift over time as you grow and as the company's priorities evolve.

The types of assignments you could own include

  • AI: Embedding with our dedicated AI unit to explore, design, and implement AI-powered workflows across the company. A fast-moving, high-impact space at the frontier of what's possible in gaming.
  • Data & Tech: Partnering with our data and engineering teams to drive analytical or operational projects at the intersection of technology and business impact.
  • Business Development & Partnerships: Contributing to our external growth strategy, from onboarding new game studios and expanding into new markets, to structuring partnerships that broaden Homa's ecosystem and reach.
  • Product & Gaming: Embedding within product teams to support roadmap prioritization, market analysis, and go-to-market strategy on live or upcoming game titles.
  • People & Transformation: Acting as a business partner to HR leadership, supporting talent strategy, retention, team structure, and organizational change. Ideal for someone who sees people operations as a lever for business.
  • Growth & User Acquisition: Working with our UA and growth teams on performance strategy, creatives, monetization strategies, channel analysis, and scaling initiatives that directly drive revenue.
  • Finance: Supporting financial modeling, business case development, and strategic planning alongside our finance leadership. A strong fit for profiles from PE, consulting, banking, or corporate finance.

Mission

You will play a pivotal role in directly supporting our executive team on high-impact missions across the gaming industry. This is a unique opportunity to learn fast, take real ownership, and drive measurable change, not as support, but as a strategic contributor. Your missions will include:

  • Business Partnering: Act as a trusted partner to leadership, translating business needs into structured plans and driving execution across functions.
  • Transformation & Operational Excellence: Lead or contribute to cross-functional transformation projects that improve how the company operates and scales.
  • Strategic Analysis: Conduct market research, competitive benchmarking, and business modeling to inform executive decision-making.
  • Strategy & Execution: Support strategic roll-outs from design to implementation, ensuring alignment across teams and measurable outcomes.
  • Communication & Influence: Build high-quality strategic materials, contribute to reporting, and represent leadership in key internal and external forums.

Требования

We're looking for sharp, driven individuals who are hungry to learn and ready to own real work from day one. This role is open to a range of profiles, from recent graduates to more experienced candidates. What matters most is having the mindset, the rigor, and the ambition to make an impact in a fast-paced environment.

You are a great fit if you have

  • Degree from a top-tier institution in Business, Engineering, Strategy, Finance, or a related field
  • First experience in consulting, finance, tech, gaming, or a fast-paced startup environment
  • Big plus, if you've built something on your own: a game, an app, a side project you've actually shipped.
  • Strong analytical and srategic thinking skills: you can break down complex problems, conduct rigorous market research, and turn data into actions
  • Excellent communication skills: you present information in a concise, strategic, and straight-to-the-point manner, both written and verbal
  • Autonomous: you lead projects end to end, take initiative without being asked, and are comfortable figuring things on your own
  • Pragmatic and action-oriented: you move fast, make decisions with imperfect information, and focus on what actually moves the needle…
