GREAT GAMES DESERVE CREATIVE THAT MAKES PEOPLE WANT TO PLAY THEM. WE’RE LOOKING FOR A MARKETING CREATIVE LEAD TO SHAPE THE FUTURE OF USER ACQUISITION CREATIVE ACROSS SUPERCELL’S PORTFOLIO OF LIVE AND NEW GAMES. BASED IN HELSINKI, YOU’LL LEAD AND DEVELOP OUR UA CREATIVE TEAM, SET THE STRATEGIC DIRECTION FOR THE DISCIPLINE, AND HELP US MAKE AMBITIOUS CREATIVE BETS THAT CONNECT OUR GAMES WITH PLAYERS WORLDWIDE.

This is a senior leadership role with broad ownership. You’ll work across games, franchises and creative formats—giving talented Creative Producers the context, trust and support to do their best work while building the strategies and systems that help us learn faster and raise the creative bar. You’ll partner closely with Marketing Leads, General Managers, Creative Leadership, game teams and global partners to decide where our creative can have the greatest impact.

Что предстоит делать

- Lead and develop the UA Creative team. Lead, coach and inspire Creative Producers working across both live and new games. Build an environment where people have the autonomy, context and support to make strong decisions, take ownership and grow into future production leaders.

- Set the strategic direction for UA Creative. Own creative strategy across Supercell’s portfolio, developing playbooks, testing methodologies and repeatable frameworks that help teams identify opportunities, validate hypotheses and scale winning ideas.

- Shape creative across the player journey. Guide strategies spanning gameplay-based creative, playable ads, cinematic animation, App Store Optimization (ASO), interactive experiences, social-first creative and emerging formats—from early concept testing and soft launch through global launch and LiveOps.

- Own the UA Creative portfolio. Take a portfolio-wide view of investment across games, audiences, formats, channels and franchises. Help teams determine where to place ambitious creative bets, when to scale successful approaches and where experimentation can unlock something new.

- Turn games into compelling creative ideas. Champion a deep understanding of gameplay systems, player motivations and game design. Help teams find the mechanics, emotions, and stories that authentically represent our games while creating entertaining, culturally relevant, and performance-driven work.

- Drive prioritization and alignment. Partner with Marketing Leads, General Managers and Creative Leadership to connect UA Creative priorities with broader company and game objectives. Bring clarity when priorities compete, make thoughtful trade-offs and help teams stay focused on the opportunities with the greatest potential impact.

- Build ways of working that scale. Continuously improve how UA Creative is planned, prioritised, reviewed and delivered. Create systems that enable teams to move quickly without lowering the creative bar, and make sure insights and learnings travel effectively between games and teams.

- Champion franchise quality. Work closely with our Animation Supervision team in Seoul, game teams and external partners to ensure acquisition creative strengthens each franchise’s identity while continuing to evolve with players, cultures and platforms.

- Grow our creative ecosystem. Build strong relationships with agencies, production companies, AI partners and specialist vendors, using external expertise thoughtfully to complement our internal capabilities and expand what our teams can create.

Что у вас должно быть

- Extensive experience leading User Acquisition Creative, creative strategy or marketing production within mobile gaming, entertainment or consumer technology.

- A track record of leading, mentoring and developing high-performing Creative Producers, including helping others grow into broader leadership responsibilities.

- Demonstrated success setting creative direction beyond an individual project or campaign, with the judgement to influence priorities and investment across multiple teams, games or products.

- Experience building creative strategy frameworks, experimentation methodologies and scalable operating models that improve creative quality and marketing performance.

- Strong knowledge of UA Creative across gameplay-based assets, playable ads, animation, ASO, social-first creative and emerging acquisition formats.

- A deep understanding of mobile game design, player psychology and gameplay systems, and an ability to identify the mechanics and emotional hooks that can become compelling acquisition creative.

- Experience supporting games across their lifecycle, from concept validation and new-game incubation through soft launch, global launch and long-term LiveOps.

- Excellent judgement in ambiguous situations and the ability to prioritise across competing opportunities, stakeholders and business objectives without needing every decision prescribed.

- Experience partnering with Marketing Leads, General Managers and senior cross-functional leaders, influencing strategy and creating alignment around significant creative decisions.

- Experience collaborating effectively across globally distributed teams, animation studios and external creative partners.

- A passion for building exceptional teams and creating the conditions for others to produce the best work of their careers.

Будет плюсом

- Experience shaping UA Creative strategy across a portfolio of multiple games or globally recognised entertainment franchises.

- Experience connecting creative experimentation with performance insights and using those learnings to influence broader marketing strategy.…
