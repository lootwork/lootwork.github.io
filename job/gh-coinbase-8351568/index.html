As a Prime Financing Trading Analyst on the Institutional Prime Finance team, you'll help manage and grow Coinbase's institutional financing book across lending, margin, and shorting products. This team is building a full-service prime brokerage platform that enables institutions to deploy trading strategies and achieve capital efficiency at scale. You'll trade day-to-day across the financing desk, optimize net interest margin, and work directly with hedge funds, ETF issuers, market makers, and corporates to deliver bespoke financing solutions.

Что предстоит делать

  • Trade the financing book daily, managing long and short side liquidity across Prime Financing and CB Institutional products.
  • Optimize net interest margin on principal borrow/lend activity through active position management and pricing adjustments.
  • Support asset and client pricing for financing implementation across lending, margin, and shorting products.
  • Engage directly with institutional clients, including hedge funds, ETF issuers, market makers, UHNW individuals, and corporates, to structure financing solutions.
  • Build and refine custom client solutions that improve onboarding, internal processes, and overall client experience.

Required Skills and Experience

  • 2+ years of experience in equity or fixed income financing, prime brokerage, or derivatives at a bank, broker-dealer, or crypto exchange.
  • Proficiency in Python, SQL, or similar programming languages applied to trading analytics, pricing models, or data analysis.
  • Demonstrated knowledge of fiat and crypto funding market fundamentals, including repo, securities lending, and margin financing mechanics.
  • Proven ability to communicate clearly with institutional clients and internal stakeholders while managing competing priorities with urgency.
  • Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.
