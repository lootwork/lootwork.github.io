О нас

We are building a new digital payments service to make money flow more efficiently through businesses and the economy. This is a new type of payments account that is built for businesses that need faster, easier and more reliable ways to move money.

Our flexible platform and innovative approach allows our customers to utilise our payments account instead of a traditional bank account. О роли

The Financial Crime Operations function executes processes to mitigate risks to Modulr as they relate to Money Laundering/Terrorist Financing, Fraud, Sanctions, and other Financial Crime risks (such as Anti-bribery & Corruption).

We are seeking a detail-oriented and driven Financial Crime Analyst to join our Transaction Monitoring team. This role plays a key part in maintaining regulatory compliance by investigating flagged transactions to detect money laundering, fraud, and terrorist financing. You will review automated alerts, research account history, and prepare Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs) to ensure compliance with regulations.

You will report into the Transaction Monitoring Team Manager and work closely with colleagues across Financial Crime Operations to ensure payments held due to monitoring rules are investigated within required timescales, and that SARs and UTRs are submitted on behalf of Modulr to relevant external bodies where necessary.

This is a great opportunity for someone looking to deepen their expertise in financial crime risk management and compliance within a dynamic and fast-paced environment.

Основные обязанности

  • Alert Triage: Review automated transaction monitoring alerts to determine if the activity aligns with the customer's expected profile, escalating or closing alerts based on risk-based analysis.
  • Investigations: Perform deep-dive analysis by checking historical account behaviours, cross-referencing watchlists, and evaluating geographic and typology risk.
  • Reporting: Document case rationales clearly and, when necessary, draft and file SARs and UTRs with the FIU and other relevant external bodies within required timescales.
  • Process Improvement: Identify system and process trends, report false positives, and update internal compliance teams on emerging financial crime typologies.
  • Take ownership of quality control by reviewing your own cases for accuracy and completeness, addressing errors proactively, and meeting internal QC benchmarks.
  • Collaborating with other teams such as Screening, Fraud, Complex Investigations, and CDD/Onboarding where required to support risk assessments and process alignment.
