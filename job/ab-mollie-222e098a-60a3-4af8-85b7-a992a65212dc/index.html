Your impact

We’re looking for a Regional Marketing Intern - Benelux to be a crucial force in driving our growth in the Dutch and Belgian market. Your creative and skilled approach will directly support and realize impactful marketing campaigns and local initiatives that matter to these key regions.

This is a chance to leave your mark by supporting creative and strategic campaigns and executing high-value events and content. You'll connect the dots between regional marketing, partner marketing, sales, customer success, product marketing and design, gaining first-hand experience in a fast-moving fintech company, right at the heart of Amsterdam.

If you're ready to jump in, own key projects, and see your efforts translate into tangible results for our growth in the Netherlands and Belgium, this is your mission.

What you'll ship

- End-to-End Project & Event Management: Independently manage and execute your own projects and regional event logistics from conception to analysis, utilizing our systems to build localized content hubs, deploy full-funnel email campaigns, and execute targeted Account-Based Marketing (ABM) initiatives.

- Content Creation & Localization: Coordinate the production and publishing of diverse content (customer stories, white papers, videos, and more), ensuring absolute accuracy and consistency across the NL-NL, BE-NL, and BE-FR content hubs.

- BNL Newsletter Ownership: Own the end-to-end execution of the merchant newsletter based on standardized operational documentation; drive the entire process by compiling local market inputs, aligning copy variations with global stakeholders, and building the final campaigns.

- Stakeholder Management: Serve as the central point of contact for internal teams and cross-functional partners, driving alignment and updates.

- Financial & Admin Support: Manage campaign and event administration, tracking, and invoicing through our systems.

- Merchandise & Inventory Management: Oversee HQ merchandise stock, inventory distribution, and monthly volume across all international offices while tracking budgets versus actuals.

What you'll bring

- Fluency in Dutch and business proficiency in English to thrive in an international environment (French is a plus).

- Currently studying in marketing, business administration, communication, or a similar field.

- A proactive, creative, and results-driven attitude.

- Excellent communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams.

- Familiarity with the Dutch and/or Belgian market and the cultural nuances between them.
