О роли

This Senior Dialer Analyst role reports to the Senior Director, Collections and Recovery Business Planning, Project Management & Analytics (BP&A).

The Senior Dialer Analyst is a key member of our team here at SoFi, responsible for supporting all of our outbound/inbound dialer operations for Personals Loans, Credit Cards, and Loan Origination Outreach . Overall, work will also include driving Dialer improvement efforts in efficiency, effectiveness, and productivity, including implementing initiatives across 1LOD Business Controls.

Что предстоит делать

Operational Management

  • Help the team manage daily dialer operations, ensuring campaigns start on time, run efficiently, and meet intensity targets.
  • Identify and resolve technical issues, collaborating with IT and Telephony teams to implement upgrades and patches.
  • Monitor agent status in real-time to maximize productivity and reduce idle time.

Analytics & Reporting

  • Create and maintain dashboards to track MTD metrics and validate the effectiveness of calling strategies.
  • Analyze dialer metrics to provide recommendations for improving efficiency and effectiveness.

Risk & Compliance

  • Ensure all dialing activities comply with FDCPA, Reg F, TCPA, and state laws.
  • Partner with Risk stakeholders to establish control inventories, descriptions, and workflows for consistent testing.
  • Proactively monitor regulatory changes and support remediation efforts to address control gaps.

Strategic Collaboration

  • Work with business partners to identify staffing gaps, monitor agent patterns, and plan future initiatives.
  • Develop training materials and communication tools to share best practices and program enhancements with stakeholders.

Typical KPIs (A Senior Dialer Analyst is often responsible for improving):

  • Right Party Contact Rate (RPC)
  • Promise-to-Pay (PTP)
  • Dollars Collected
  • Roll Rates
  • Delinquency Cure Rate
  • Queue Penetration
  • Agent Occupancy
  • Average Handle Time (AHT)
  • Abandon Rate
  • Contact Rate
  • Connect Rate
  • Cost per Contact
  • Cost per Dollar Collected

What you’ll need

  • Experience working in a fast paced, collections call center environment
  • Some knowledge of outbound collection regulations and rules (FDCPA Reg F, TCPA, & State laws)
  • Proven ability to analyze complex data sets to identify trends, performance gaps, and opportunities for improvement. Experience with Tableau or similar data visualization tools is preferred.
  • A Bachelor's Degree in Telecommunications, Information Technology, or related field, or 5 years of relevant technical experience in lieu of a degree.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills, ability to articulate issues, and create reports used by senior management
  • Strong ability to create a culture of ownership, accountability, collaboration, and ability to influence at different levels.
  • Ability to work independently with limited daily supervision while meeting deadlines.
  • Ability to navigate through ambiguity, manage and coordinate multiple project assignments, and deliver on commitments.
  • Partnership mindset to ensure we have positive and productive working relationships with auditors and examiners.
  • Experience with risk data, reporting, and analysis; including systems.
  • Experience in highly-matrixed, fast-paced environments.

Желательно

  • Previous experience in Banking or Fintech
  • Experience working with an auto-dialer (Preferably the Genesys Outbound Dialer)
  • Utilize SQL queries to extract, analyze, and manipulate data for performance monitoring and strategic decision-making
