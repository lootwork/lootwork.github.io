THE WORK

Join Ripple Labs Inc. as an Analyst Trading and Markets in Miami, FL. You will be part of an ambitious team working to flawlessly develop world-class financial solutions. You will help invent the future of finance through collaboration and determination.

Что предстоит делать

  • Assist in pricing, quoting, and accomplishing trades across digital asset and crypto/fiat pairs
  • Support sales campaigns by managing client calls and converting relationships into trading flow
  • Monitor algorithmic execution and market-making activity, identifying and addressing anomalies

WHAT YOU'LL BRING

  • Proven ability to handle RFQs and grow client relationships
  • Familiarity with algorithmic trading and market-making strategies
  • Strong communication and analytical skills, with a focus on operational rigor and collaboration
