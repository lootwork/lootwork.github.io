As Software Engineers at Riot, we bring deep knowledge of specific technical areas and also value the opportunity to work in multiple broader domains. We work with exciting technology, overcoming high-volume obstacles and delivering value to players.

As a Software Engineer on the League of Legends Purchase Points team, you'll report to the team's Engineering Manager. You’ll build and improve the tools, services, and pipelines that help us configure, validate, test, schedule, and ship Store, Battle Pass, Loot, and other live content. You’ll partner with content coordinators and engineers to reduce manual work, catch configuration issues earlier, and improve confidence in releases across environments and regions. You will work across technology stacks to achieve reliable and performant player experiences. You will exercise your skills to work in a diverse group, being a trusted technology partner to product owners.

Обязанности

  • Building interfaces for creating and updating content configurations.
  • Adding schema validation, previews, and automated checks to catch configuration errors before release.
  • Supporting localization and asset workflows, and making it easier to test and promote content between environments.
  • Improving scheduling, publishing, change visibility, and recovery for live content updates.
  • Partnering with content coordinators, Product, QA, and engineers to identify manual or error-prone steps and improve them.
  • Write unit and integration tests
  • Code reviews
  • Participate in the team's on-call rotation

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent experience
  • 2+ years experience as a full stack software engineer
  • Experience working with Python
  • Experience developing software with C++
  • Experience building internal tools, content-management or configuration systems or workflows with validation and testing

Desired Qualifications

  • Experience in test automation, operational best practices, implementing automated builds, software packaging, automated deployments, logging, monitoring, and automated alerting
  • Experience shipping and operating a live game or other globally distributed consumer product
  • Familiarity with League of Legends or strong motivation to learn the game and its player experience

Success in this role

  • For this role, you’ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and choices that focus on our players, who are the customers of your work.

For this role, you'll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players' experiences, this could be your role!
