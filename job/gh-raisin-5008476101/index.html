Raisin is the world’s leading platform for savings and investment products. Founded in 2012, the FinTech connects consumers with banks in the EU, the UK and the US. This gives consumers better interest rates and banks a diversified form of refinancing. Our vision is to offer savings and investments without barriers and thus open up the global 160 trillion euro market.

Raisin currently employs more than 800 people from over 75 countries worldwide. Today, the platform holds over 80 billion euros in assets from more than one million investors which have accrued over 5 billion euros in returns.

Team

The Design team at Raisin brings together product designers and brand & marketing designers. We are united by a shared belief in respect, creativity, and our purpose to give people financial peace of mind.

As our Digital Brand Designer, you'll join the Brand & Acquisition team. We shape how Raisin looks, moves, and feels across every market we operate in: paid and organic social, our websites, product launches, and campaigns. You'll report to the Head of Brand and Marketing Design and work integrated with the US Marketing team.

Your main focus is conversion: designing and testing the landing pages, web journeys, and creative that turn interest into sign-ups. You'll also support broader marketing initiatives across paid social, web, and email, so Raisin feels consistent across the funnel.

Ваши обязанности

  • Conversion-Driven Web Design: Design, test, and optimize high-converting landing pages, reusable UI components, and end-to-end user journeys that elevate the brand and drive growth, while governing overall website design standards and brand alignment.
  • Data-Informed Experimentation & Testing: Partner with Marketing to turn hypotheses into A/B and multivariate tests, from concept to shipped variant, and iterate based on the results, using funnel analytics, heatmaps, test results, and customer insights to find friction and decide what to test next.
  • Creative Production: Produce static and animated creative, from hero assets to fast, iterative test variations within our brand design.
  • Email & CRM: Design email templates and email campaigns with the CRM team, from lifecycle journeys to product launches.
  • Templates & Systems: Build and maintain reusable templates, modules, and patterns for web, ads, and email, working with Product Design and the Raisin Design System so marketing and product feel like one brand.
  • End-to-End Ownership: Act as a solution-oriented design partner, independently guiding cross-functional stakeholders from problem to execution, taking projects from brief to launch, including prototypes and leveraging AI for implementation-ready tests.
  • Stakeholder Management: Present and explain design decisions with confidence, connecting them to the brand, the customer, and the business result.
  • Workflow Innovation: Use AI tools across concepting, variant production, prototyping, test-implementation and analysis to work faster and test more, without compromising visual quality or compliance.

Профиль кандидата

  • 4+ years in digital, marketing or web design, ideally in-house at a fintech, DTC or fast-growing tech company, or at an agency with performance clients.
  • A portfolio that shows strong visual craft across brand, web, landing pages, paid social and email, and that tells the story behind the work: the problem, the hypothesis, what you tested and what changed.
  • Proven experience designing for conversion. You have worked with growth or performance teams and know how to balance brand expression with commercial results.
  • A data-informed mindset. You are comfortable reading test results and analytics, and you use them to make better design decisions.
  • Excellent typography, layout and hierarchy skills, with attention to spacing, rhythm and detail.
  • Expert in Figma, including components, auto layout and prototyping. Solid skills in Adobe Creative Suite.
  • Practical use of AI in your design process today, for exploration, variant production or prototyping, not only for generating imagery.
  • Hands-on ability to build implementation-ready components and code snippets (HTML/CSS) to deploy tests directly with marketing via Optimizely and Bloomreach.
  • Speed without sloppiness. You can manage several projects at once in a fast-moving, feedback-rich environment.
  • Clear communication in English, written and spoken. You can present and defend your work to stakeholders.
  • Working knowledge of HTML/CSS, or experience with AI-assisted coding tools (for example Cursor, v0 or Claude Code).
  • Experience with experimentation tools (for example Optimizely) and analytics tools such as Google Analytics, Hotjar or Looker.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience in financial services or another regulated industry, where trust and compliance shape the work.
  • Basic motion design skills (After Effects, Jitter, Lottie, Figma Motion, or similar) for ads and web.
