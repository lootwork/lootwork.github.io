Роль

We're looking for a Treasury Project Manager to join our Corporate team on a 6-month fixed-term contract. In this role, you'll drive the transformation of our treasury operations by identifying inefficiencies, implementing automation, and strengthening financial controls across our global entities.

- Support day-to-day treasury operations when needed, ensuring continuity across the team

- Gain a deep understanding of daily cash management and liquidity monitoring across France and international affiliates using Kyriba TMS and electronic banking platforms

- Design and implement automated workflows leveraging AI and Kyriba to reduce manual workload (automatic bank statement analysis, automated bank notifications, budget code entry, etc.)

- Monitor and validate the effectiveness of newly implemented workflows, iterating as needed

- Analyse the current cash forecasting process and identify areas for improvement

- Propose and implement enhancements to the cash forecasting methodology, including the potential rollout of new Kyriba modules

- Assess the existing workflow between purchase orders, invoices, and payments to identify fraud risk exposure

- Design and implement controls and tracking mechanisms to mitigate fraud risk and ensure end-to-end traceability between POs and payments

Профиль кандидата

- Master's degree in Finance or Accounting (business school, engineering school, or university)

- Minimum 4 years of experience in cash management, combined with a proven track record in project management

- Hands-on experience with Kyriba TMS and electronic banking platforms

- Proficiency in AI tools, including the ability to craft effective prompts with clear context, expected output formats, and examples - and to design appropriate safeguards (e.g. preventing cell overwrites, balance discrepancy checks, alert setup)

- Strong analytical mindset with the ability to challenge existing processes and propose pragmatic improvements

- Rigorous, proactive, and autonomous, with a strong sense of ownership

- Comfortable working in a fast-paced, international environment

- Fluent in both French and English (spoken and written)
