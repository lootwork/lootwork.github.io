Кто мы

Let's have fun shaping your future!

We are Socialpoint, a world-renowned mobile game studio proudly part of the Zynga & Take-Two Interactive family. We’re the creators of hits like Dragon City, Two Dots, Top Troops, and Monster Legends, loved by hundreds of millions of players around the globe.We craft the best social games, bringing joy from Barcelona to the world. Now, we want to bring the world to Barcelona! At Socialpoint, work and play intertwine seamlessly, and we celebrate the diversity that makes us stronger. No matter who you are or where you’re from, you’re welcome here. Join us for a future where making games is as thrilling as playing them!

We’re looking for a Mobile Developer Intern to join our Game Development team and help us create and improve the amazing experiences enjoyed by millions of players worldwide.

As part of your internship, you’ll work alongside experienced C++ developers, learning how we build and polish our games from the inside out. You’ll contribute to real projects, support gameplay features, improve tools and workflows, and gain hands-on experience in how a world-class studio develops and ships mobile games.

This is an excellent opportunity to learn, grow, and level up your technical and creative skills in a supportive and fun environment.

Что предстоит делать

  • Learn to build and improve features in C++ alongside senior engineers.
  • Support the team with internal tools, gameplay systems, and optimization tasks.
  • Help test, debug, and polish gameplay experiences.
  • Participate in code reviews and team discussions to understand best practices.
  • Collaborate with designers, artists, and other developers on small but meaningful projects.
  • Explore new ideas and propose creative technical solutions under guidance.
  • Contribute to technical documentation and small improvements in workflows.

Что вы приносите

  • Currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Game Development, or a related field.
  • Experience with C/C++ through coursework, personal projects or other internships.
  • Knowledge of 2D/3D graphics, and computer mathematics/algebra.
  • Some knowledge in mobile platforms (iOS, Android).
  • Good interpersonal and communication skills.
  • Passion for games, learning, and teamwork.
  • Strong problem-solving mindset and curiosity to explore how things work.

What's in it for you as a Socialpointer?

  • Hybrid working model: we ask you to come to our Barcelona office at least 2 days per week
  • A generous 25 days of annual leave, plus public holidays
  • Free language lessons in English, Spanish and Catalan
  • Legendary social events and parties to let off some steam

Onsite perks include

  • State of the art gym facilities
  • Free breakfast, lunch, and specialty coffee
  • Onsite physiotherapist and hairdresser (no wallet needed!)
