Кто мы

О Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies, from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups, use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

О команде

Stripe Terminal helps businesses extend their online presence into the physical world. Our mission is to make it as easy for businesses to accept in-person payments as the Stripe API has made it for online payments. With Terminal, businesses can create flagship retail experiences, extend their websites to pop-up stores, or enable mobile point-of-sale payments at their next event. All of this is built on the Stripe Payments Platform, so businesses can maintain a single view of customers and sales across online and offline channels.

Our hardware engineering team in Taiwan designs and builds the physical payment devices at the heart of that experience, including products such as the Stripe Reader S700 and Stripe Reader M2. We are expanding the team and seeking an experienced Senior Mechanical and Industrial Design Engineer who can own both the structural engineering and aesthetic form language of our next generation of payment terminals. This is a blended role. You will be equally comfortable driving design for manufacturability (DFM) for a plastic enclosure and sketching form concepts that express Stripe's design identity in hardware.

Что предстоит делать

You will lead the industrial and mechanical design of next-generation Stripe Terminal payment devices, shaping their form, feel, usability, and structural performance from early concepts through mass production. You will partner with cross-functional teams and suppliers to deliver manufacturable, high-quality hardware, including enclosure design, color, material, and finish specifications, packaging, validation, and production readiness.

Обязанности

Industrial design

  • Lead ideation and concept development through rapid hand sketching, digital sketching, and physical foam or 3D-printed models to explore form factors early in the product lifecycle.
  • Define the visual and tactile design language of Terminal hardware, including proportion, form, surface transitions, button feel, and overall product character.
  • Produce photorealistic product renders using KeyShot or similar tools to communicate design intent to cross-functional teams and executive stakeholders.
  • Define and manage color, material, and finish (CMF) specifications. Work directly with suppliers to qualify target surfaces, textures, and coatings.
  • Manage packaging design, working directly with suppliers to qualify final deliverables and the unboxing experience.
  • Apply ergonomics and human factors principles to ensure products are intuitive and comfortable for a diverse range of users and environments.
  • Maintain visual consistency and brand coherence across the Terminal hardware product family, in alignment with Stripe's design identity.

Mechanical engineering

  • Design mechanical components and assemblies using SolidWorks or Creo, following professional engineering standards.
  • Hands on experience in mechanical designs and drawings, assess manufacturability, and resolve technical problems.
  • Lead and plan problem-solving activities using scientific approaches, such as tolerance analysis, computer-aided engineering (CAE) and finite element method (FEM) simulations, design of experiments (DOE), and physical prototyping.
  • Independently analyze and verify mechanical designs, identify risks, and propose mitigations.
  • Manage bill of materials (BOM) cost and design schedules from a mechanical engineering perspective.
  • Prepare mechanical engineering deliverables, including BOMs, component specifications, and support for failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA) activities.
  • Review Design fixtures and calibration processes for high-precision mechanical assemblies.
  • Verify and qualify components in first-article builds from suppliers, and participate in pilot production.
  • Produce and maintain engineering documents, including specifications, design-for-excellence (DFx) guidelines, technical reports, and gate-review packages.
  • Partner with Quality and Reliability teams to support successful validation outcomes and improve hardware durability standards.
  • Support product compliance and certification activities, including Payment Card Industry (PCI) and regulatory certifications.
