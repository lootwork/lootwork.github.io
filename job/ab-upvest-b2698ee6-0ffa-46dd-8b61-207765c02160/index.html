О роли

- Join Upvest Securities Ltd, our FCA authorised UK entity, as a Compliance Analyst Intern for an initial six month term (extendable), reporting into the Head of UK Compliance.

- You’ll help us maintain our compliance monitoring and outsourcing oversight and keep pace with Upvest’s growth, so that controls are checked systematically rather than reactively;

- Experience hands on exposure to the rules that matter most for our UK investment platform, understand regulatory requirements including CASS, Consumer Duty and the outsourcing requirements.

- Deep dive into financial crime and anti money laundering related topics to understand the real time impact of 2LOD oversight in an established financial institution

- Maintain monitoring and second line approval and work closely with our regulatory team to focus on strategic priorities and direct engagement with the FCA and our auditors.

- Enable our Growth and Product colleagues to onboard new clients and partners, so your work makes it easier for others to move quickly and safely.

- Start with a structured two week induction and a 90 day ramp plan, with the Head of Compliance as your named buddy and everything you produce reviewed by an experienced colleague.

- A midpoint check in will review your progress and the option to extend your term or move into a permanent junior role, so this is a real stepping stone into a compliance career.

Что предстоит делать

- Execute scheduled checks under our compliance monitoring programme, log and report the results, and flag any issues early so they can be resolved fast.

- Support due diligence and periodic reviews and keep the outsourcing register and risk assessments up to date.

- Keep actions and comments up to date for the Core UK Compliance initiatives in our UK readiness plan, and chase progress where it stalls.

- Maintain compliance registers, management information and supporting documentation so that they are accurate, consistent and audit ready.

- Assist with regulatory horizon scanning and help keep our policy documentation current, summarising what changes mean and why in plain language.

- Keep our UK Slite pages up to date and make sure updates are circulated widely, so colleagues across Upvest can easily find what they need.

- Take ownership of your outputs: make sure they are reviewed and approved by the Head of Compliance before they are finalised, and handle sensitive regulatory and client data with care.

Что вы приносите

- Currently studying, or recently graduated, in law, finance, economics, business or a related field, and available for a six month term (part time as a working student or full time as an intern).

- A genuine interest in financial regulation and compliance, and the curiosity to learn the rules behind them quickly.

- Strong attention to detail and an organised, methodical approach to recurring checks, registers and documentation.

- Clear written and spoken English, with the ability to turn complex regulatory topics into simple, concise summaries.

- A proactive, own the outcome mindset: you progress work without being chased, value progress over perfection, and know when to escalate.

- Openness to feedback and a drive to keep learning as you grow from first-line support to more independent work.

- Good working knowledge of spreadsheets and documents, and the discretion to handle confidential regulatory and client information responsibly.

- The right to work in the UK.

IT’S GREAT IF YOU HAVE

- Prior internship or project experience in compliance, risk, legal or audit, ideally in financial services or fintech.

- Familiarity with the FCA Handbook, for example CASS, SYSC 8 or Consumer Duty, from coursework or previous roles.

- Enthusiasm for using modern tools, including AI tools, knowledge bases like Slite and workflow tools, to work smarter and share knowledge with others.
