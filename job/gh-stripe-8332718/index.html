Что предстоит делать

Обязанности

  • Develop and manage platforms and solutions to minimize financial and regulatory risk while maintaining excellent user experience.
  • Diagnose and resolve production issues across various services and throughout the tech stack.
  • Proactively identify, analyze, and swiftly mitigate emerging risk patterns.
  • Partner with diverse stakeholders, which include: engineering, product, policy, legal, operations, and data science teams.
  • Collaborate with other engineers to build modular, composable Fraud control systems.

Кто вы

Minimum requirements

Must have a Bachelor's Degree or foreign equivalent in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field, plus five (5) years of post-bachelors, progressive experience in Software Engineering.

Must also have five (5) years of experience in each of the following:

  • One of the following programming languages: Java, Ruby, Python or Go;
  • Performing data-driven investigations and analysis to inform decision-making using SQL and visualization software;
  • Working with systems agnostic of programming languages;
  • Translating business requirements into tangible software requirements and system designs; and
  • Identifying and resolving production issues that span multiple services and stack layers.

Must also have three (3) years of experience in each of the following:

  • Building platforms and solutions to minimize financial and regulatory risk while maintaining high quality user experience; and
  • Working within the Trust and Safety industry.
