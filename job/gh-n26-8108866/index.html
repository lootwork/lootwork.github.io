О opportunity

We are seeking for a Backend Engineer with Full-Stack capacity for our The Agentic Platform Team. You will build the products that let hundreds of engineers and non-engineers at N26 work alongside AI every day. Any engineering background works: backend, frontend, mobile. We care more about how you think and build than where you came from. No prior AI product experience required; we expect you to use AI coding tools daily and push what they can do.

In this role, you will

  • Ship Agentic Workflows: Design and prove the core patterns that let AI agents complete real engineering work from start to finish: context engineering, memory, gated plans. Once proven, package them so any team can adopt them.
  • Enable Self-Service AI Tooling: Build the distribution layer that lets teams publish and pick up AI capabilities on their own schedule, without routing through a central team.
  • Connect Agents to Internal Tools: Build the integrations between AI agents and the systems engineers use every day: issue trackers, code review, CI and empower engineers to build that kind of workflow themselves.
  • Turn Problems into Patterns: Identify recurring engineering problems and turn them into repeatable, AI-powered solutions other teams can adopt.
  • Set the Standard: Define and document how engineers at N26 use AI responsibly: the guardrails, the practices, the shared understanding that lets the platform scale without surprises.
  • Measure What Matters: Track how AI is being used across N26, what it costs, and where it drives real impact. Use that data to sharpen what the team builds next.

What you need to be successful

  • An Ownership Mindset: You identify gaps, propose solutions, and ship without waiting to be directed.
  • Strong Coding Fundamentals: You write clean, maintainable code; language is secondary to craft. Our stack leans on Go and TypeScript, but what matters is that you care about what you ship and can pick up new languages fast.
  • Platform Intuition: You think in multipliers. When you see a repeated manual step, you build the tool that removes it for everyone.
  • Developer Tooling Experience: You have built or operated internal developer platforms: plugin registries, CI/CD integrations, package distribution, or configuration management.
  • AI Coding Habit: You use AI coding tools daily (Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, or similar). No background in building AI products required; you need the habit of using them and the curiosity to go deeper.
  • GitOps/DevOps Experience: GitOps and DevOps are your natural environment. Hands-on experience with GitHub Actions or AWS is a strong plus.
  • Communication Skills: You explain complex systems clearly to engineers and non-technical stakeholders.

What’s in it for you

  • Accelerate your career growth by joining one of Europe’s most talked about disruptors 🚀.
  • Employee benefits that range from a competitive personal development budget, work from home budget, discounts to fitness & wellness memberships, language apps and public transportation.
  • As an N26 employee you will have access to a Premium subscription on your personal N26 bank account. As well as subscriptions for friends and family members.
  • Additional day of annual leave for each year of service.
  • A high degree of autonomy and access to cutting edge technologies - all while working with a friendly team of peers of diverse nationalities, experiences, and backgrounds.
  • A relocation package with visa support for those who need it.

If you want to build the AI platform that powers N26, where agents ship features, teams own their tools, and the platform stays out of the way, we want to hear from you. Apply now!
