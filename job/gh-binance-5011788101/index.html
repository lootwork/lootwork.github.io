О роли

We are looking for a hands-on engineer who enjoys writing code, solving complex backend problems, and building reliable payment systems. The ideal candidate takes ownership from development to production, is comfortable debugging real-world issues, and can turn business requirements into robust technical solutions.

Experience with PIX is a strong advantage, but we also welcome strong Java backend engineers from payments, fintech, banking, and other transaction-intensive environments who can quickly learn the Brazilian payment ecosystem.

Что предстоит делать

  • Design, develop, test, and maintain scalable backend services using Java and Spring Boot to support PIX payment integrations and related payment flows in Brazil.
  • Implement and optimize payment processing capabilities, including payment initiation, transaction status management, reconciliation, and exception handling.
  • Integrate with Brazilian banks, payment service providers, and external financial systems through APIs and other integration mechanisms.
  • Build reliable, high-performance backend systems with a strong focus on transaction consistency, idempotency, fault tolerance, and system availability.
  • Troubleshoot production issues, identify root causes, and implement effective solutions to improve system stability and performance.
  • Monitor system performance, identify bottlenecks, and continuously improve the reliability, scalability, and efficiency of payment services.
  • Support payment-related security, risk control, and compliance requirements in collaboration with relevant stakeholders.

Кого мы ищем

  • Strong hands-on backend development experience with Java and PIX, preferably using Spring Boot and related frameworks.
  • Experience with PIX, Brazilian payment systems, or integrations with Brazilian banks and local payment service providers.
  • Solid understanding of RESTful APIs, microservices architecture, distributed systems, and database design.
  • Experience working with relational databases and SQL; familiarity with caching, messaging systems, and asynchronous processing is a plus.
