О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE AN IMPACT

Growth Engineering at Airwallex is building an AI-native platform that powers customer acquisition, activation, conversion, retention, and expansion. We're developing scalable infrastructure, intelligent decision systems, and high-performance web experiences that connect product, sales, and marketing to drive measurable business outcomes.

As an Engineering Lead, Full Stack, you'll lead a team of engineers to design, build, and scale the web experiences and growth platforms that power Airwallex's customer acquisition and conversion journeys. You'll own the technical direction and execution of customer-facing web applications, corporate websites, content management systems, and growth capabilities, combining strong full-stack engineering expertise with technical leadership and product judgment.

- Own web engineering and full-stack delivery, leading the design, development, and evolution of high-performance websites, landing pages, CMS-powered experiences, and customer-facing web applications.

- Drive technical architecture and direction, making pragmatic decisions across frontend applications, backend services, APIs, content platforms, and integrations to build scalable, maintainable, and reliable systems.

- Build and develop a high-performing engineering team, providing technical mentorship, coaching, clear expectations, and fostering a culture of ownership, collaboration, and engineering excellence.

- Enable growth and experimentation, building capabilities for rapid content iteration, A/B testing, personalization, conversion optimization, and marketing automation to accelerate customer acquisition and activation.

- Partner across functions, working closely with Product, Design, Marketing, Data Science, and other engineering teams to translate ambiguous business needs into intuitive experiences and scalable technical solutions.

- Drive measurable business outcomes, using performance metrics, experimentation, and customer insights to improve website performance, conversion, engagement, and overall growth effectiveness.

- Raise the engineering bar, establishing strong engineering practices for code quality, web performance, accessibility, SEO, security, reliability, observability, and operational excellence.

SG: You'll work from our Singapore office five days a week.
