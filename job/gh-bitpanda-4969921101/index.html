Кто мы

We simplify wealth creation. Founded in 2014 in Vienna, Austria by Eric Demuth, Paul Klanschek and Christian Trummer, we’re here to help people trust themselves enough to build their financial freedom — for now and the future. Our user-friendly, trade-everything platform empowers both first-time investors and seasoned experts to invest in the cryptocurrencies, crypto indices, stocks*, precious metals and commodities* they want — with any sized budget, 24/7. Our global team works across different cultures and time zones, bringing our products to more than 7 million customers, making us one of Europe’s safest and most secure platforms that powers modern investing.

Headquartered in Austria but operating across Europe, our products are built by fast-moving, talented, “roll-up-your-sleeves-and-make-it-happen” kind of people. It’s these diverse perspectives and innovative minds operating as ONE TEAM that keep Bitpanda at the cutting edge of our industry. So if you’re someone who thinks big, moves fast and wants to make an impact right from day one, then get ready to join our industry-changing team. Let’s go!

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the end-to-end acquisition and onboarding strategy for new Fusion customers, taking full ownership of the user journey from first touchpoint through registration, KYC, initial deposit, first trade, and early retention.
  • Build and continuously optimize acquisition funnels targeted at active traders and high-value crypto customers, identifying and removing friction across every stage of the onboarding journey.
  • Develop scalable acquisition channels across paid, owned, community, affiliate, referral, and partner ecosystems, with a strong focus on measurable customer growth. This includes building strategic partnerships with trading communities, educators, affiliates, and key platforms to drive qualified new traders to Fusion.
  • Design targeted CRM and lifecycle journeys that convert registered users into funded and actively trading customers, rather than primarily promoting individual product features.
  • Develop acquisition propositions, landing pages, campaigns, and onboarding materials that clearly communicate why active traders should choose Fusion as their trading venue, while monitoring competitor acquisition strategies, incentives, pricing, and trader propositions to continuously improve Fusion's ability to win customers from other exchanges.

Кто вы

  • 4+ years of experience in growth, customer acquisition, or product-led growth, ideally at a crypto exchange, broker, trading platform, fintech, or similar digital financial product, including experience working cross-functionally with Product, Marketing, Data, Compliance, and Operations to improve onboarding and customer conversion.
  • Proven experience building and optimizing digital acquisition and onboarding funnels, with a strong understanding of conversion optimization and funnel analytics, as well as a strong analytical mindset and ability to translate customer and funnel data into actionable growth initiatives.
  • Demonstrated ability to build acquisition through a combination of performance marketing, CRM, referrals, affiliates, communities, and strategic partnerships, alongside a strong commercial understanding of metrics such as CAC, LTV, activation rate, funded-account conversion, retention, ARPU, and trading volume.
  • Good understanding of active trading and the needs of experienced traders, including order types, technical charting, APIs, crypto derivatives, margin, and maker-taker fee models.
  • Personal background as an active trader or technical analyst, and an existing network within European crypto trading communities, is a plus.
