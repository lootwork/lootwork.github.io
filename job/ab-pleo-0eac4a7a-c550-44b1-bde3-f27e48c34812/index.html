О роли

We’re looking for an Engineering Manager to join our Authentication and Authorisation team where you'll be responsible for the strategy, delivery and outcomes for the Pleo’s product IAM.

This is a highly complex space with a legacy custom-built service and a future-state platform, currently being implemented in our Enterprise rewrite.

Success in this role means defining and executing a sensible migration path between SMB and Enterprise architectures and modernising our current auth capabilities while maintaining high security, compliance, performance and trust.

WHO YOU'LL BE WORKING WITH AND REPORTING TO

You’ll report to our VP of Engineering and you’ll lead, mentor and grow a team of 6- 10 software engineers. You’ll work closely with Product, Security, Compliance and Core Platform teams to align priorities and manage dependencies, and you’ll collaborate across the wider Backend & Infrastructure group to keep technical direction and platform standards coherent.

Ultimately, your role is to foster an environment where engineers can do their best work and grow - as individuals and as a team - by setting clear expectations, ensuring our ways of working enable delivery (rather than slowing it down) and holding a high bar for quality.

Что предстоит делать

As an Engineering Manager, you will

- Own the vision, strategy and execution roadmap for identity, authentication and authorisation across SMB and Enterprise.

- Drive delivery and outcomes for the Authentication & Authorisation team, ensuring we build secure, scalable systems the whole platform can rely on.

- Successfully roll out a new identity platform across our Enterprise offerings.

- Define and execute a pragmatic migration strategy between the SMB legacy service and the Enterprise architecture.

- Update legacy auth services, reducing risk and technical debt while maintaining high security, compliance, performance and trust.

- Partner closely with Product, Security, Compliance and Core Platform teams to align priorities, unblock dependencies and keep delivery moving.

- Stay close to the technology and architecture, stepping into technical discussions to guide the team through complex trade-offs.

- Continuously improve how we build software - strengthening operational excellence, quality and ways of working.

- Hire, coach and grow a high-performing team, helping people develop while building trust and strong team dynamics.

Что вы приносите

Must have

- Solid experience as an Engineering Manager (or equivalent people-lead role), with meaningful people leadership impact (including coaching and performance support).

- Strong hands-on technical depth in authentication and authorisation domains and standards (e.g. OAuth, SAML, OIDC) and modern identity platforms (e.g. Amazon Cognito, Auth0, Stytch) within a product centric organisation.

- Architectural credibility and a track record of guiding teams through technical complexity, including migrations and legacy constraints.

- Strong stakeholder management across engineering, product and risk-focused partners with clear and consistent communication.

- A bias for action: you can lead through ambiguity and still create momentum, rather than getting stuck in analysis

Будет плюсом

- Background as a software architect or staff-level engineer before moving into people leadership.

- Experience operating in security - or compliance-sensitive domains where trust and reliability are non-negotiable.

- Experience scaling teams (hiring and building clear ownership) while maintaining delivery quality.

WHY THIS ROLE IS A GOOD FIT FOR YOU

This role is a good fit for you if

- You care deeply about people's leadership and you want your team to grow, thrive and ship great software together.

- You enjoy cross-team collaboration and you’re energised by aligning stakeholders to solve platform-wide problems.

- You like staying close to code and architecture enough to guide technical decisions, while still leading through people and outcomes.

This role is NOT a good fit for you if

- You prefer roles where you can be removed from day-to-day delivery, team dynamics and technical decision-making.

- You’re looking for a well-defined problem space with a clear migration plan already in place.

- You’re uncomfortable with ambiguity, trade-offs and the reality of improving a critical legacy system while building the future state.
