О вас

We are looking for a Field Marketing Manager, Americas who is organized, relationship-driven, and results-focused to join our Regional Marketing team at Xsolla Global Headquarters in Los Angeles, California. The best candidate will be someone who thrives in a fast-paced, highly collaborative, and exceptionally dynamic setting and is excited to turn a calendar of 70+ industry conferences, trade shows, Xsolla Connects, and partner events across the U.S., Canada, and LATAM into meetings, leads, and pipeline for Business Development.

Strong project management, communication, and content skills are essential, along with experience in B2B, field, or event marketing, ideally in gaming or technology. The ability to own a high-volume event calendar and coordinate several teams with competing priorities will be key to your success in this role.

If you’re passionate about creating events that connect the right people and drive business outcomes and love supporting developers in bringing their games to market, we would love to hear from you!

О нас

Xsolla is a global commerce company with robust tools and services to help developers solve the inherent challenges of the video game industry. From indie to AAA, companies partner with Xsolla to help them fund, distribute, market, and monetize their games. Grounded in the belief in the future of video games, Xsolla is resolute in the mission to bring opportunities together, and continually make new resources available to creators. Headquartered and incorporated in Los Angeles, California, Xsolla operates as the merchant of record and has helped over 1,500+ game developers to reach more players and grow their businesses around the world. With more paths to profits and ways to win, developers have all the things needed to enjoy the game.

For more information, visit xsolla.com.

Обязанности

  • Speaking & Thought Leadership
  • Identify, secure, and own speaking sessions, panels, roundtables, and interviews for Xsolla leaders, working with event organizers and the global external communications team.
  • Brief and prepare speakers, and promote each session through event emails, landing pages, and social channels.
  • Strengthen Xsolla’s thought leadership and visibility at industry events across the Americas.
  • Event Content & Promotion
  • Partner with the integrated marketing team to deliver event emails, landing pages, and organic social.
  • Brief design and creative assets for event promotion and on-site materials.
  • Partner with the global external communications team on press releases and event-related PR.
  • Adapt event content and outreach for Canada and LATAM as needed.
  • Event Go-to-Market Planning
  • Develop and execute the event marketing plan for each event on the Americas calendar including goals, target accounts, and KPIs.
  • Coordinate event marketing across BD, Developer Relations, Integrated, and Experiential, and give input to the event calendar and the 2027 plan, including GDC.
  • Partner with the regional business development team on audience building and invitations.
  • Leads, Meetings & BD Handoff
  • Work events on site as marketing lead, running lead capture and meeting delivery.
  • Process leads after each event and hand them off to BD on time.
  • Report results for each event: meetings, MQLs, influenced pipeline, and cost per lead.
  • Sponsor & Paid Media
  • Own sponsor-bundled media: site skins, newsletter inserts, and sponsored social.
  • Partner with the integrated marketing team on paid media that supports events.
  • Partner Activations
  • Support Xsolla Connects, partner activations, scholarships, and VIP events with audience building, invitations, and promotion.
  • Develop joint promotion with partners and event organizers for co-hosted events and webinars.

Требования и навыки

Обязательно

  • 3-5+ years of experience in B2B, field, or event marketing
  • Proven experience marketing and working conferences, trade shows, roundtables, and other customer-facing programs, including on-site execution
  • Track record of using events to generate leads, meetings, and pipeline
  • Experience securing speaking sessions and working with speakers, organizers, and communications teams
  • Experience with contracts, sponsorship agreements, and budget tracking
  • Content and campaign skills: email, social, press releases, and blogs
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to engage multiple teams with competing requests and drive projects forward
  • A hands-on mindset, with the ability to build the plan and execute it independently

Желательно

  • Experience in games or technology
  • Proficiency with project management tools such as Atlassian/Jira, Asana, and Google Workspace
  • Comfortable owning and managing a high-volume event calendar
  • Spanish or Portuguese language skills for LATAM
