О роли

Binance is looking for an experienced Margin Operations professional to support its growing Equities business within TradFi. The role will oversee SOPs/processes related to margin calls, liquidations, collateral management and client fund reconciliation, while working closely with Risk, Product and Engineering teams. Candidates should have hands-on margin operations experience within a brokerage or financial institution.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build and run daily stock margin operations, including margin calls, collateral monitoring, and forced liquidations
  • Partner with Margin Risk to turn margin policies into clear, executable workflows
  • Own the reconciliation of client funds, collateral, and positions with finance and custody partners
  • Work with Product and Engineering to define requirements for margin systems and test new features before launch
  • Identify manual processes and automate them to cut errors and turnaround time
  • Track operational incidents, find root causes, and drive fixes
  • Mentor team members and set up SOPs as the team scales

Кого мы ищем

  • Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Business, Economics or a related discipline.
  • Past experience in managing margin, collateral, or securities operations at a brokerage, bank, or prime broker.
  • Good understanding of the concepts behind stock margin, liquidation, collateral management etc.
  • Strong project management skills, with the ability to drive initiatives independently and manage cross-functional stakeholders across teams.
  • Bilingual English/Mandarin is required to be able to coordinate with overseas partners and stakeholders.

Будет плюсом

  • A risk mindset: careful, detail-oriented, and aware of downside before acting
  • Familiar with the Crypto industry's landscape
  • Hands-on experience with trading
