О роли

We’re expanding our TradFi business into new markets and products, and the operations behind it need to keep up. In this role, you’ll take on daily settlement, reconciliation and product issues, so the team can respond to users faster and focus on growth.

Обязанности

  • Handle daily settlement and reconciliation for TradFi products
  • Investigate product issues end to end, working with PMs and engineers to find root causes and fixes
  • Manage listing and delisting of new tickers and stocks/funds
  • Work with Customer Service on TradFi user issues, escalations and feedback
  • Coordinate with Treasury, Clearing and regional teams on operational matters
  • Analyse market and product data, including regional performance, and prepare reports on request
  • Share recurring issues with VIP and Marketing teams

Требования

  • At least 1-2 years of TradFi experience (brokerage, equities, derivatives, or similar)
  • Solid communication skills, written and verbal, as the role involves lots of cross-functional collaboration
  • Bilingual English/Mandarin is required to be able to coordinate with overseas partners and stakeholders
  • Bachelor’s degree in business, finance, economics or data is highly preferred

Будет плюсом

  • A risk mindset: careful, detail-oriented, and aware of downside before acting
  • Crypto experience is not a must, but your interest/motivation plays a big part

Why Binance

  • Shape the future of finance
  • Collaborate with world-class talent in a user-centric global organization with a flat structure
  • Tackle challenging projects in a fast-paced and innovative enviornment with autonomy
  • Thrive in a results-driven workplace with opportunities for career growth and continuous learning
  • Competitive salary and company benefits
  • Remote-first, work-from-home arrangement (may vary depending on the nature of the role and team)
