О вакансии

Keet is a fully peer-to-peer messaging and video calling app built on Holepunch's P2P stack. There are no servers, no middlemen, and no data harvesting. People talk directly to each other, with end-to-end encryption by design.

We are looking for a Senior Technical Product Manager to own the product vision for Keet and drive its long-term growth. You will define where the product goes, build alignment with leadership and the team, and lead the execution that gets it there.

The ideal candidate brings three things: proven product leadership on consumer products, the technical depth to work closely with engineers on complex systems, and a hands-on approach that includes using AI tools to prototype and test ideas quickly.

You will stay close to the details: tracking dependencies, raising risks early, and keeping several workstreams moving at once. This role suits someone who enjoys both setting strategy and being directly involved in delivery, from concept to production.

Обязанности

Vision & Strategy

  • Own and communicate the long-term product vision for Keet as a leading private communication app.
  • Analyze WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, LINE, WeChat and emerging competitors in messaging and communication to find where Keet can differentiate, and shape the roadmap around it.
  • Present product strategy, metrics and progress to senior leadership.
  • Define a growth strategy across acquisition, activation, retention and engagement, with measurable goals.

Technical Product Leadership

  • Build a working understanding of the underlying p2p technology and translate its capabilities and constraints into product decisions.
  • Partner with engineering on complex technical p2p challenges such as offline sync, multi-device support, notifications and mobile performance.
  • Own the quality of core experiences, including messaging and real-time calling.
  • Define clear feature tiers, separating stable from experimental functionality.

Rapid Prototyping with AI

  • Use AI and design tools to create prototypes and demos that validate ideas before committing engineering resources.
  • Make ideas tangible for the team, users and leadership early in the process.

Execution & Team Leadership

  • Lead the product team and run the sprint cadence, including planning, prioritization, reviews and retrospectives.
  • Own deliverables from concept through release, testing and post-launch iteration.
  • Keep roadmaps and issue trackers accurate and aligned with priorities.
  • Coordinate across engineering, QA, design, marketing and business stakeholders, and manage cross-team dependencies.
  • Communicate status, risks and timeline changes openly, and address accountability when needed.

Release & Distribution

  • Own the mobile release process for iOS and Android, including store submissions, compliance and release management.
  • Oversee desktop distribution across macOS, Windows and Linux.
  • Establish privacy-conscious crash reporting and analytics that turn user feedback into actionable engineering input.
