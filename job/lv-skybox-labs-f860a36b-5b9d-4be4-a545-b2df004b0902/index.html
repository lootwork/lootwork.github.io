Who is SkyBox Labs?

Recognized as one of BC's Top Employers (2025), SkyBox Labs is a game development studio that works on some of the world’s most iconic games. The studio is currently working on projects in the Minecraft universe, supporting the dev team at Bungie on Destiny 2, supporting development of Fallout 76, in addition to work on unannounced IP. Since our inception in 2011, SkyBox Labs has evolved from a small team of eight individuals to a fully established team of Engineers, Artists, Animators, Designers, Producers, and more. Come join our hybrid team in our Burnaby, BC studio today!

PLEASE NOTE: At this time, we are only able to hire candidates who are legally eligible to work in Canada and reside within Canada.

Interested? Let's talk about what we're hiring for

We're looking for a Senior C++ Software Engineer to join our Minecraft Networking / Core Systems team. This isn't a traditional network engineering role; the team works across a broad range of core engine systems, platform integrations, and development infrastructure, with a focus on the foundational technology that supports Minecraft across console, PC, and mobile platforms.

In this role, you'll work on low-level C++ systems that support the performance, stability, and reliability of the game. Areas of focus include file system I/O, multithreading and asynchronous systems, networking technologies, platform integrations, and improvements to existing engine architecture and development infrastructure.

You'll work in a high-ownership environment, taking technical problems from investigation through implementation. This means independently navigating complex codebases, applying deep technical expertise to challenging problems, exercising sound technical judgment, and delivering robust solutions across a range of systems.

A SNAPSHOT OF YOUR ROLE

  • Core Systems & File I/O: Design, develop, maintain, and optimize foundational engine systems, with a focus on file systems, data handling, I/O, and related infrastructure.
  • Concurrency & Performance: Work with multithreaded and asynchronous systems, including thread management and job scheduling. Independently investigate and resolve complex performance and concurrency issues.
  • Networking & Communication Systems: Work with and improve a modern networking stack built on WebRTC (SDP, ICE, STUN/TURN NAT traversal, DTLS, SCTP), JSON-RPC, and serialization formats, applying deep technical knowledge to complex networking challenges.
  • Platform Integration: Contribute to complex platform integrations across console, PC, and mobile platforms, working with platform APIs, SDKs, and technical requirements.
  • System Design & Reliability: Design and optimize resilient, low-latency systems, applying strong technical judgment around security best practices, observability, maintainability, and performance.
  • Codebase & Build Infrastructure: Drive improvements to existing systems, tackle complex refactoring, and contribute to codebase modernization, build configurations, dependencies, and developer tooling.
  • Testing & Automation: Identify and implement improvements to automated testing and development infrastructure to support reliable builds, cross-platform quality, and efficient development workflows.
  • Cross-Team Technical Support: Work with engineers across disciplines to investigate and resolve broader technical challenges, which may include multiplayer systems, serialization, telemetry, and partner integrations.
  • Continuous Improvement: Proactively identify and implement improvements to engineering practices, tooling, and workflows, including the practical use of AI-assisted development tools.

SOUND LIKE YOU? THIS IS WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR

  • 7+ years of professional software engineering experience in a similar role, preferably within the games industry.
  • Extensive hands-on experience with C++ and a deep understanding of modern C++ development.
  • Strong understanding of low-level software engineering concepts, including memory management, file systems, file I/O, and concurrency.
  • Strong experience with networking concepts and protocols, ideally including WebRTC, JSON-RPC, and serialization formats.
  • Advanced debugging skills, with a track record of investigating and resolving complex issues across large, established codebases.
  • Extensive experience working with complex software systems, including refactoring, modernizing, and improving existing architectures.
  • Exceptional problem-solving skills, with the ability to independently investigate ambiguous and highly complex technical challenges and deliver effective solutions.
  • Strong ability to interpret technical documentation and translate complex platform or system requirements into robust implementations.
  • Experience designing and developing resilient, low-latency systems with consideration for security, observability, maintainability, and performance.
  • Experience with AI-assisted development tools and the ability to effectively incorporate them into everyday engineering workflows.
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to take ownership of complex technical work and work effectively across teams.

Nice-to-haves

  • Experience developing or shipping games or other real-time applications across console, PC, or mobile platforms.
  • Strong familiarity with first-party platform SDKs, platform integrations, or console development requirements.
  • Experience with build systems, dependency management, or build optimization tools, such as CMake.
  • Experience improving automated testing, CI/CD pipelines, or development infrastructure.
  • Familiarity with game networking, serialization, or live-service systems. Deep networking specialization is not required.…
