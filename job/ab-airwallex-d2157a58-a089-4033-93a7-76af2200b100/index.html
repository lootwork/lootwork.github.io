Что предстоит делать

We’re seeking a talented and experienced Marketing Operations Manager that will play a crucial role in developing and executing automated marketing campaigns to drive customer acquisition, engagement, and retention. You’ll collaborate with Marketing and Sales teams around the world to develop and execute ABM strategies, using Marketo and other platforms to effectively target accounts. You’re a strategic thinker with a strong background in Marketing Automation platforms, data analysis, and campaign optimisation. You’re a resourceful, creative go-getter who is eager to help shape the next phase of Airwallex’s growth.

Обязанности

- Design, build, and execute B2B marketing campaigns using Marketo, including email templates, workflows, segmentation, and automation rules. Oversee all technical aspects to make sure they are optimised for campaign success.

- Spin up landing pages using existing, team-maintained templates; maintain a strong working knowledge of landing page and email best practices.

- Build and manage target account audiences in Demandbase, Hightouch, Salesforce, and Marketo, and activate them across downstream channels by feeding audiences into LinkedIn, Google Ads, or Meta Ads for retargeting to drive ABM-focused marketing motions.

- Develop and implement complex automation workflows in Marketo, including lead nurturing, drip campaigns, and triggered communications.

- Provide day-to-day support to the regional Marketing and Sales teams on the use of Marketo, Salesforce, Outreach, and Hightouch, following established best practices and playbooks.

- Ensure all ABM activities comply with data privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR) and internal security policies, particularly in handling and processing account data.

Кто вы

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

- 5+ years of experience working with Marketing Automation tools (e.g. Marketo) and CRM systems (e.g. Salesforce), especially in a B2B environment.

- A track record of working with Marketing and Sales teams to deploy successful ABM campaigns.

- Strong analytical skills, a data driven orientation, and uncompromising attention to detail.

- Solid communication and collaboration skills, both internally and externally.

- Strong knowledge of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and email design best practices, with hands-on experience building landing pages and email campaigns.

- Experience working across MarTech platforms such as Mutiny, Outreach, Marketo, Contentful, or similar tools.

- Portfolio required: Please submit a portfolio showcasing relevant landing pages, email campaigns, or other digital marketing assets you have created.

Будет плюсом

- Experience with Hightouch or a similar CDP to build, manage, and activate target audiences as needed.

- Adobe Marketo Engage certification and basic SQL skills are good to have.
