О роли

  • Design and continuously improve VIP growth programmes across acquisition, activation, upgrades, retention and reactivation. Turn regional business gaps into scalable mechanisms that improve customer experience and generate sustainable commercial results.
  • This is not a pure strategy-writing or manual operations role. You own business definition, cross-functional problem-solving and growth delivery; regional RMs and CMs own customer relationships, frontline execution and local commercial accountability.

Основные обязанности

  • Find VIP growth opportunities and customer-experience gaps. Work directly with regions and RMs, combining customer insights, competitor intelligence and operating data to identify acquisition, upgrade, product adoption, retention and reactivation opportunities. Investigate blockers proactively rather than waiting for the analytics function to assign work.
  • Own VIP proposition, loyalty and programme strategy. Strengthen benefits, tier positioning, loyalty mechanisms and partnerships against customer needs and competitor offerings. Define the role of each programme in the VIP lifecycle, balancing premium experience, market competitiveness, delivery feasibility and sustainable economics.
  • Design and rationalise lifecycle programmes. Develop and optimise experience-to-upgrade journeys, Momentum, retention, Recall and other approved use cases. Maintain clear eligibility, triggers, thresholds, reward rules, budget, duration, exit and exception requirements. Use performance evidence to retain, redesign, merge or stop programmes; prevent conflicting benefits and duplicate subsidies.
  • Integrate business coordination with growth delivery. Own the coordination of VIP programme blockers involving benefits, task mechanics, reward fulfilment, approvals or cross-team handoffs. Establish accountable PICs, actions, timelines, escalation needs and closure evidence. Resolve recurring patterns through standardisation and productisation, not permanent ad-hoc manual work.
  • Drive end-to-end automation and CRM improvements. Translate priorities into business requirements, rules and acceptance criteria with Internal Tooling, CRM and relevant Product teams through the unified intake channel. Cover eligibility, task issuance, progress, rewards, exceptions and result capture; distinguish automated steps from RM actions. Automatic task distribution alone is not completion.
  • Develop commercially sound deal and incentive frameworks. Maintain VIP deal templates and guidance, evaluate rewards, costs and net contribution under approved budget rules, and work with Finance and Risk on incentive boundaries. Identify overlaps with global campaigns and other lifecycle tasks; recommend sequencing, exclusions or adjustments for approval.
  • Lead regional rollout and VIP GTM. Agree target segments, regional suitability, participation expectations and delivery plans with BUs, CRM, Marketing and regional teams. Coordinate business cases, customer lists, approved communications and launch readiness. Ensure local consent and applicable market restrictions are respected; RMs retain customer-contact ownership.
  • Improve RM service standards through programme insight. Use CSAT, response times, recurring customer cases and product feedback to improve programme design and identify service gaps. Collaborate with Analytics & Governance on execution monitoring and remediation evidence, and with Training on capability gaps and materials; do not take over RM line management.
  • Define benefits and event business requirements. For gifts, partnerships and VIP events, specify customer objectives, eligibility, service expectations and expected commercial outcomes. Work with Marketing/Event owners and Merch or fulfilment teams on delivery; support tooling and process improvements without personally owning routine stocking, shipping or logistics.
  • Validate incremental and sustained business impact. Partner with Analytics on pilots, comparable cohorts or phased experiments. Measure formal upgrades after trials, continued trading after rewards end, retention, revenue and net contribution. Separate natural market movement and overlapping campaign effects from programme contribution; scale, adjust or discontinue mechanisms accordingly.
