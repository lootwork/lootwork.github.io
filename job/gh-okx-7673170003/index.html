Кто мы

At OKX, we believe that the future will be reshaped by Crypto, ultimately contributing to every individual's freedom. OKX began as a crypto exchange giving millions of people access to crypto trading and over time becoming among the largest platforms in the world. In recent years, we have developed one of the most connected Web3 wallets used by millions to access decentralized crypto applications (dApps). OKX is a trusted brand by hundreds of large institutions seeking access to crypto markets on a reliable platform that seamlessly connects with global banking and payments. In the last year, OKX has expanded into new markets including Australia, Brazil, Netherlands, Singapore and Turkey, with plans to launch in the US, Belgium and the UAE.

We are deeply committed to shaping a fairer, more transparent and accessible society through blockchain technology. This is why we publish proof of reserves monthly, and continue to ship new innovative security features.

О команде

Trading Services Engineering team powers OKX's core trading infrastructure — the systems that keep millions of users trading reliably, 24/7, at global scale. You'll build the backbone that other engineering teams depend on.

Что предстоит делать

  • Design and build high-availability, low-latency distributed services that power core trading flows
  • Own service reliability end-to-end: architecture, performance tuning, monitoring, and incident response
  • Partner with Product and cross-functional engineering teams to translate trading requirements into scalable system design
  • Drive technical best practices through code reviews, design reviews, and mentorship of peers
  • Contribute to Kafka-based messaging architecture and event-driven system design at scale

Что предстоит делать

  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams including Product, Design, and Marketing to drive innovation and execution
  • Design and implement high availability, high-performance distributed services
  • Contribute to technology best practices and perform code reviews for peers

What We Look For In You

  • Bachelor's degree in Science, Technology, Engineering, or Mathematics
  • Demonstrated experience adopting AI tools in the engineering workflow, able to evaluate and integrate AI capabilities into day-to-day development
  • Hands-on experience in Microservice/API development using Java/SpringBoot/SpringCloud
  • Strong knowledge of multi-threading and distributed architecture, and understanding of messaging frameworks such as Kafka
  • Proficient in relational database design and development
  • Comfortable working in a cloud-based Linux environment
  • Proven experience in automated testing and continuous integration
  • Knowledge of integrating with 3rd party software via APIs/SDKs
