О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU’LL MAKE IMPACT

T:0 https://t0.ai is building an AI-native accounting platform that helps businesses close the books, reconcile, report, and operate with greater speed and confidence. You’ll own the product direction for T:0’s agentic finance experience, turning real customer pain into a clear roadmap and shipped outcomes.

- Define how AI-native accounting can help businesses close the books, reconcile, report, and operate with greater speed and confidence.

- Lead and develop a high-performing team of Product Managers, creating a culture of ownership, product quality, customer focus, and continuous learning.

- Lead product discovery and delivery across engineering, design, data, finance, compliance, and commercial teams.

- Translate complex accounting and financial workflows into simple, trusted experiences, balancing AI innovation with deterministic controls, auditability, and accuracy.

- Build from first principles in an early-stage environment, using customer insight, product data, and rapid iteration to shape a category that is still being defined.

- Own product outcomes from discovery through delivery, using qualitative and quantitative signals to test assumptions and iterate quickly.

You’ll work from our San Francisco office three days a week.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- 8 + years experience leading product strategy and delivery for complex B2B, fintech, accounting, financial infrastructure, or AI products.

- A track record of taking ambiguous problems from discovery through launch, with clear ownership of product outcomes.

- Strong understanding of financial, accounting, or operational workflows and the customer problems they create.

- Experience working closely with engineering and design to build and ship high-quality products.

- Strong product judgment, with the ability to balance customer value, technical constraints, business priorities, risk, and regulatory considerations.

- Clear, direct communication and the ability to influence senior stakeholders across functions.

- Comfort using qualitative and quantitative signals to make decisions, test assumptions, and iterate quickly.

Preferred

- Experience building AI-native products, intelligent workflows, automation, or agentic systems.

- Experience with accounting software, financial operations, enterprise SaaS, or products serving finance teams.

- Experience working in an early-stage or zero-to-one product environment.

- Familiarity with the challenges of applying probabilistic AI to workflows that require accuracy, controls, and auditability.

- Experience working in fintech, payments, or another regulated industry.

LEARN MORE ABOUT YOUR TEAM

T:0's mission is to make finance run itself. We're building an AI-native accounting platform that closes the books, reconciles, and reports continuously, so founders can stop managing their finances and start using them to run the business. The hard part isn't adding AI to accounting. It's making AI you can trust with the books. We use AI to understand how a business works, then run the actual accounting on strict, checkable rules, and no black box deciding the numbers. That's why customers pick us over both the legacy tools they've outgrown and the AI wrappers they don't trust. And bookkeeping is just the start: we're building toward a full financial operating system for how companies account, plan, and operate.

The team is small, elite, and builder-focused, led by operators with a track record of shipping AI products at scale. We sit inside Airwallex, which means startup speed with a scaled company's infrastructure, distribution, and capital behind us. You'll work directly from real customer pain, own outcomes end to end, and ship fast.
