Technical Program Manager, Platform Engineering

Platform Engineering builds Platform as a Product, empowering engineers to autonomously build and maintain secure, scalable, and reliable applications with speed and quality. Our platform includes the crucial infrastructure foundation that supports our entire product suite and all our merchants. Adyen operates its own global network of data centers, continuously evolving its footprint and technology.

This is a unique opportunity for an ambitious individual to grow into a program management leader. As a Technical Program Manager, you will be at the heart of our most critical engineering initiatives. You will be coordinating key projects, learning our complex technical landscape, and developing the skills needed to drive large-scale programs.

Что предстоит делать

  • Coordinate Technical Projects: You will be a central point of coordination for projects within our Platform strategies. You'll help manage timelines, track progress, and ensure clear communication across engineering teams.
  • Support Cross-Team Collaboration: You will act as a key liaison between various infrastructure, platform, and application teams, helping to align on technical requirements and ensure project deliverables stay on track.
  • Manage risks and technical dependencies: You will identify and track project dependencies and assist with risk assessment and mitigation.
  • Track and report progress: You will provide clear, concise updates to stakeholders under the guidance of senior team members.
  • Drive Clarity and Organization: You will leverage your natural organizational skills to help establish clear processes, documentation, and workflows that make our project execution more efficient.

Who You Are (What You Bring)

  • You have a natural talent for bringing structure to complex situations. You can demonstrate the ability to organize and drive outcomes through other projects and activities you've managed.
  • You are a highly motivated, ambitious individual with a strong desire to learn and grow within the program management field.
  • You possess strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to convey information clearly and build positive relationships with various stakeholders.
  • You have a proactive, can-do attitude and a strong sense of ownership. You are driven to see tasks through to completion.
  • You have a good understanding of technology concepts allowing you to effectively engage with technical leads and engineering teams
  • Experience with project management tools or methodologies is a plus
  • Experience working with infrastructure and platform engineering teams is highly preferred.
  • Experience in a high-growth, global technology company is a plus.
  • Experience with data center build-outs or migrations and network standardization projects is a significant advantage.
