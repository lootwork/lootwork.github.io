Data Analyst - Growth & Marketing

О нас

Traditional banking has never really given you control over your financial life. Satispay exists to change that: to empower people through finance. That means making financial services easy and accessible for over 6.5M users. We started with payments, then benefits, investing, cards... and we’re not done. Not even close. We follow the same logic every time: find a real problem, solve it properly, then look for the next one. The destination? To build the most loved financial platform in the world. If you own outcomes rather than tasks, stay close to what users need, and refuse to wait for someone else to fix what's broken — you'll find people here who work exactly in the same way. And together, you'll build something worth being proud of.

Что предстоит делать

As our Data Analyst, you'll be a key member of our Growth & Marketing (G&M) team, putting data and intelligence first to drive real business impact, from user acquisition and engagement to retention and monetisation. Here's what that looks like in practice:

- Drive Decision-Making Through Data - Analyse complex datasets to identify patterns impacting the consumer funnel (Acquisition, Engagement, Retention, Monetisation). Partner with Marketing Managers and other stakeholders to deliver in-depth analysis across the full product lifecycle to maximise ROI.

- Partner with Key Stakeholders - Act as a strategic partner for Marketing, Finance, Operations, and Product, bridging the gap between technical data and business goals and translating complex analysis into clear, executive-level recommendations.

- Build High-Impact Visualisation Tools - Define key metrics and develop dashboards and reports that communicate analytical insights effectively, empowering stakeholder self-sufficiency.

- Contribute to Data-Mesh - Actively work on the development of the federated data layer by designing robust data models and building pipelines that integrate into the company's data strategy, ensuring scalable and governed data ownership.

- Promote Agentic Analytics - Contribute to the enrichment and adoption of AI-powered agentic workflows for data transformation and exploration, accelerating analytical capabilities and enabling self-service for key stakeholders.

- Drive Testing Initiatives - Own the end-to-end testing pipeline, from opportunity identification to experiment design and recommendation delivery, operating effectively in a fast-paced environment.

Who we're looking for

This role needs someone who is a problem-solver with a strong analytical mindset, who is detail-oriented, and ready to take ownership in a dynamic, collaborative environment.

- Relevant Experience - 4 years in data-related roles spanning across Business Intelligence, Business Analytics, or Analytics Engineering, with hands-on experience in high-volume, or big data environments.

- Data Modelling & Data Mesh Expertise - Hands-on experience in data modelling, ETLs and data transformation layers. Real world exposure to Data Mesh and contributive data ecosystems is a plus.

- Technical Proficiency - Strong command of SQL and Git. Experience with dbt and notebook-based analytics (e.g. HEX, Databricks, Jupyter) is a plus.

- Analytical & Problem-Solving Skills - Comfortable with ambiguity, able to break down complex problems into unit workstreams and deliver evidence-based answers. Takes end-to-end ownership of analytical projects.

- Data Visualisation - Experience with HEX, Looker, or similar reporting tools to build clear and insightful reports for both exec-level and operational teams.

- Stakeholder Management & Communication - Able to independently lead initiatives across diverse business domains, efficiently translating technical findings into clear, actionable recommendations.

- AI Fluency - Proven ability to work with AI tools as coworkers, including prompting and context management, ideally applied to data and business analytics, or automation.

- Digital Consumer Lifecycle Knowledge - Sharp understanding of consumer lifecycle (Acquisition, Activation, Monetisation, Retention) and unit economics (LTV, CAC, ARPU).

Don't worry if you don't tick every box. We believe in the power of different viewpoints and strengths. Your unique perspective is important as we build something special: if you're passionate and can make a difference, we truly encourage you to apply.
