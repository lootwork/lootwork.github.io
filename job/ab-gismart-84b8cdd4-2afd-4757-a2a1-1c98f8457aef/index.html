О project

Testora is a self-discovery app that helps users explore and analyze their personality, intelligence, relationships, and career strengths through engaging tests. The app offers personalized challenges, progress reports, and habit tracking, making Testora your go-to tool for self-awareness, stronger relationships, and personal growth.

Обязанности

- Engage with key stakeholders (product managers, marketers) to identify needs and shape the analytics strategy.

- Translate stakeholders’ business questions into concrete metrics and analytical tasks; deliver decisions, not raw numbers.

- Build and maintain dashboards, reports, and key metrics to support data-driven decision-making.

- Build and validate LTV predictions for the web funnel, including early/predicted LTV on short cohort history.

- Design event taxonomy and Amplitude dashboards for specific PMM, PM, and UAM requests.

- Analyze the web funnel: diagnose drop-offs between steps, assess the impact of changes on conversion and monetization.

- Compute product unit economics: ARPU, retention, payback, and ROAS tied to UA data.

Требования

- 3+ years as a Product Analyst.

- Strong SQL: cohort analysis, retention, revenue aggregations.

- Hands-on LTV/pLTV experience: understands the methods (cohort extrapolation, survival, ML regression), their limitations with small data, and how to validate predictions.

- Amplitude: dashboard building, event taxonomy.

- Understanding of mobile/web subscription-product metrics and UA logic (CPI, ROAS, ARPU, payback).

- A/B testing methodology.

- Hands-on experience with BI tools such as Tableau or Power BI.

- Effective communication and presentation skills to convey analysis results clearly and compellingly.

Would be a plus

- Python skills (pandas, data visualization, statistics) for data manipulation and analysis.

- Experience with MMP data (AppsFlyer/Adjust).

- Hands-on experience with subscription products.

- Data engineering.

- Direct experience working with a UA team.
