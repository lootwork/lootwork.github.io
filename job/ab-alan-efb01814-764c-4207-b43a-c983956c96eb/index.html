О роли

As an Insurance Compliance Counsel at Alan, you will help Alan meet its insurance compliance and consumer protection obligations. You will work with the teammates who build our tools, processes and products to turn regulatory requirements into how they actually work. Working closely with senior members of the compliance team, you will run key parts of our compliance work day to day, flag risks early, and prepare the material we share with regulators on the topics you cover.

You will also help connect the compliance function with internal control and Alan’s audit processes. That means tracking audit findings, following remediation through to completion, and making sure the fixes become part of how we operate.

As Alan expands into new markets, you will help our compliance standards travel with us by adapting Group frameworks to local requirements, working with local counsel.

The role sits withinthe Corporate Unit. You will work closely every day with the Legal, Insurance, Operations, Sales, Care and Product teammates across all of Alan’s markets. It’s a great opportunity to build broad compliance expertise and take on more ownership over time.

Что предстоит делать

Contribute to Alan’s Insurance Compliance Posture

- Help translate regulatory obligations relating to Alan’s core product (IDD including duty of advice, product governance, AML/CFT, etc.) into Alan’s operational processes, product design and tooling

- Work with Product, Insurance and Care teams to find ways to meet regulatory requirements while improving members’ experience, from pricing components and options design to UX choices and complaint handling

- Spot compliance risks in your day-to-day work, document them, and contribute to action plans with internal stakeholders

- Run the complaints management framework day to day across France, Belgium and Spain: monitor complaints, analyse trends and propose improvements in line with ACPR recommendations and local equivalents

Reporting & Audits

- Document compliance-related risks and controls in the Group’s centralised mapping, and support the rollout of the internal control approach across teams (Insurance, Operations, Sales, Care)

- Help prepare internal and external audits: gather evidence, check Alan’s standing against planned improvements, and track remediation actions through to completion

- Prepare inputs for compliance reporting to Alan’s Board and governance committees, and contribute to regulatory reporting such as the ACPR Questionnaire Protection de la Clientèle (QPC)

- Support responses to regulatory inspections (ACPR, FSMA or equivalent) by collecting information internally and keeping documentation audit-ready

AML/CFT & KYC

- Support the AML framework: carry out second-level analysis on suspicious cases, prepare files for escalation (including PEPs and high-risk client relationships), and help keep the AML/CFT risk assessment up to date

- Contribute to the training and upskilling of KYC Ops referents, including on Belgium-specific AML requirements

- Assist the AMLO on documentation, monitoring tools and regulatory reporting to the ACPR and equivalent supervisors

Compliance Roadmap & Stakeholder Coordination

- Track progress on Alan’s compliance roadmap and flag changes in the regulatory environment that may affect priorities

- Drive well-scoped compliance projects with Insurance, Product, Sales, Care and Operations, turning regulatory requirements into concrete deliverables and timelines

- Research regulatory questions affecting our products and contracts, and propose pragmatic, well-reasoned positions for review

Regulatory Training & Internal Capability

- Help design, update and deliver mandatory regulatory training for Sales and Care roles (IDD competency requirements including duty of advice and product governance, Loi Evin, AML/CFT)

- Write guidelines, templates and decision-making tools that help teams handle compliance questions autonomously

- Contribute to Alan’s written culture by producing clear, reliable documentation

International Compliance & Localisation

- Support the adaptation of Group compliance frameworks to local requirements in Alan’s markets (France, Belgium, Spain, Canada and new countries), working with local counsel

Profile

Experience & Skills

- 2+ years of experience in compliance, ideally in an insurance or fintech company (a regulator, audit or consulting background also works); exposure to at least one of: AML/CFT, insurance distribution (IDD), complaints handling, regulatory reporting

- Good understanding of the French insurance regulatory framework, and curiosity to learn EU and local requirements

- Experience working with cross-functional stakeholders: you can explain regulatory requirements clearly and turn them into practical next steps…
