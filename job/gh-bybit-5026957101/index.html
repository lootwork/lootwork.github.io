Job responsibilities

  • Participate in project requirements and technical reviews, develop test plans, write test cases and test code, and control project risks and quality.
  • Deeply involved in black box and SAST white-box testing of the project, including functional testing, performance automation, stability testing, interface testing, etc.
  • Carry out the process quality management of the project, control the admission standards of each link in the R & D process, such as the QA standards submitted for R & D and the testing standards.
  • Sort out the quality and efficiency issues encountered during the project R & D process, including but not limited to related issues such as R & D, testing, and launch, provide analysis of the reasons, and assist in proposing solutions.

Требования

  • Majors related to computer science, software engineering, information and computing science, etc., with a solid foundation in computer knowledge.
  • Familiar with at least one programming language, such as Python, Java, C++, Golang, etc., with certain programming practice experience, able to understand and write basic code logic and algorithms.
  • Have a strong interest in software testing work, understand the basic concepts, processes, and methods of software testing, and have the ability to quickly learn and master new knowledge and skills.
  • Possess good problem analysis and problem-solving skills, be able to actively think and explore solutions when encountering problems, have a strong sense of responsibility and teamwork spirit, and be able to work closely with team members to complete work tasks together.
  • Possessing a good learning attitude and self-motivation, being able to actively adapt to changes in the work environment and task requirements, being brave enough to accept challenges, and constantly improving their professional skills and comprehensive quality in practice.
