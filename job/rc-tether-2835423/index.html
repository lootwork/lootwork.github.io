О вакансии

Senior Business Analysts specializing in M&A transactions and strategy consulting to support the origination, evaluation, and execution of acquisitions, divestitures, investments, and strategic partnerships. The role bridges corporate strategy with rigorous commercial and financial analysis, evaluating companies, assets, and transactions across a broad range of industries.

Обязанности

  • Conduct commercial, financial, and operational due diligence on target companies and assets, integrating inputs from legal, tax, technical, and other specialist advisors.
  • Build and maintain financial models (DCF, LBO, merger and accretion/dilution, sum-of-the-parts, comparable transactions and companies) for valuations and corporate transactions.
  • Support the full M&A deal cycle, from origination and screening through valuation, structuring, negotiation support, and closing, on both buy-side and sell-side mandates.
  • Deliver corporate and business-unit strategy work, including market sizing, industry and competitive analysis, growth strategies, portfolio reviews, and investment theses.
  • Prepare investment memoranda, information memoranda, pitch books, valuation summaries, and board- and investment-committee-level presentations.
  • Monitor M&A activity, capital markets, sector trends, and the competitive landscape to identify and prioritize opportunities.
  • Support deal execution, including data room management, coordination with legal teams, advisors, and counterparties, and review of transaction documents (term sheets, SPAs, shareholder agreements).
  • Assess regulatory, geopolitical, ESG, and integration risks, and contribute to synergy assessment and post-merger integration planning.
  • Structure and evaluate transaction alternatives, including asset vs. share deals, joint ventures, minority stakes, earn-outs, deferred and contingent consideration, and structured or hybrid financing (e.g., royalties, streams, prepays, offtakes), and assess their impact on value and counterparty risk.
