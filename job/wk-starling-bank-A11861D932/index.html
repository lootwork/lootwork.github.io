Hello, we’re Starling.

Banking was broken – so we decided to fix it. The vision? Fast technology, fair service and honest values. All at the tap of a phone, all the time. We built Britain’s first digital bank. One hard-won banking licence later, we set about giving people a new way to spend, save and manage their money (and take better care of the planet, too). We’re changing banking for good. Back then, we were obsessed with unravelling the knotty world of finance and solving people’s problems rather than selling them stuff. We still are.

Since then, we’ve grown. A lot. Over four million accounts (and four account types!). A team of thousands. Headquartered in London with offices in Cardiff, Dublin, Manchester and Southampton. Seven years voted Which? Recommended Provider and Britain's Best Banking Brand. Still zero branches. Our culture is open, inclusive and focused on solving real customer problems, with an emphasis on doing the right thing, even when it’s not always the easy thing. From our approach to working together and sustainability to how we build our products, our decisions need to make the world – and Starling – a better place to be. Everyone at Starling is essential to our mission, which is really quite simple: to solve our customer’s problems – and build the best bank in the world!

And now we're providing Starling to other banks, via a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) proposition through our subsidiary Engine, using the proprietary technology platform that it uses to power our own bank.

The Producer / Project Manager is a core delivery lead supporting Starling’s in-house Creative Studio, responsible for owning the planning, coordination and execution of complex creative projects. Operating with a high degree of autonomy, the role bridges production and project management, ensuring productions are executed seamlessly, on schedule and to the highest creative standard, while maintaining consistency across all brand touchpoints. The Producer / Project Manager independently leads mid-sized and multi-workstream projects, while collaborating closely with key internal stakeholders.

Основные обязанности

  • Own delivery of assigned projects including receiving and assessing creative briefs, scoping and managing end-to-end creative and production processes and ensuring final asset delivery is on brand, time and budget.
  • Coordinate integrated asset production across film, photography, motion, digital, social, print and physical outputs, managing and negotiating costs with third party production partners where required.
  • Translate complex creative and task briefs into clear delivery plans, schedules and resourcing requirements. Develop and manage detailed project plans, timelines, dependency maps and status reporting.
  • Act as the day-to-day delivery lead, maintaining clear process documentation (production schedules, budgets, call sheets, risk assessments & audit trails) throughout delivery. Identify delivery risks early and implement mitigation plans, escalating appropriately when required.
  • Foster strong and trusted relationships with key stakeholders including the Creative Studio, Brand team and Channel leads.
  • Manage feedback loops, approvals and sign-off processes, ensuring clarity and pace whilst maintaining positive relationships with stakeholders.
  • Ensure all outputs meet Starling’s brand standards, legal, risk and compliance requirements.

Требования

  • At least 5 years experience in a Producer / Project Manager role within an in-house studio, agency or production environment.
  • A solid understanding of creative and production processes
  • Demonstrable experience delivering and producing multi-channel creative projects and working with Creative teams.
  • Strong project management capability, with confidence managing timelines, budgets and dependencies.
  • Comfortable working with senior stakeholders and navigating complex organisations.
  • Experience operating within structured, process-led or regulated environments.
