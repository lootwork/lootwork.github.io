О роли

We are looking for a hands-on, entrepreneurial Marketing Manager to lead events, community engagement, and local brand activations for Binance Japan.

This role is critical in strengthening brand awareness, trust, and community engagement in one of the world's most sophisticated and highly regulated crypto markets.

You will design and execute high-quality, compliant, and culturally relevant experiences that bring Binance Japan closer to both existing users and new audiences — from educational meetups and community events to strategic partnerships and large-scale brand activations.

You understand that in Japan, trust, credibility, and long-term relationships are essential to sustainable brand growth. You can translate global brand ambitions into locally relevant initiatives while maintaining the high standards of execution expected in the Japanese market.

Что предстоит делать

1. Event Strategy & Execution

  • Plan and execute end-to-end events and brand activations, including:
  • Community meetups and user engagement events
  • Educational workshops (Crypto 101, blockchain, Web3, digital assets)
  • Strategic partner activations
  • Conferences, industry events, and sponsorship activations
  • Experiential marketing campaigns and pop-ups
  • Own the full event lifecycle: concept development → planning → vendor management → execution → post-event evaluation.
  • Ensure all activities comply with Japanese regulatory requirements and internal compliance policies.
  • Deliver high-quality experiences with strong attention to detail, brand consistency, and operational excellence.
  • Develop scalable event formats that can be replicated across different Japanese cities and audience segments.

2. Community Building & Engagement

  • Build and nurture an engaged and loyal Binance Japan community, both online and offline.
  • Develop initiatives that strengthen relationships with existing users while introducing crypto to new audiences.
  • Identify and collaborate with local community leaders, KOLs, creators, and Web3 advocates.
  • Create community programs that encourage education, participation, and organic brand advocacy.
  • Support local community channels and platforms, including X, LINE, Discord, and other relevant channels.
  • Gather community feedback and insights to inform marketing campaigns and improve user experience.

3. Brand Awareness & Local Market Engagement

  • Translate Binance's global brand positioning into locally relevant and culturally resonant experiences.
  • Develop initiatives that reinforce Binance Japan's positioning as:
  • Trusted and responsible
  • Compliant and security-focused
  • Innovative and accessible
  • Committed to user education
  • Support integrated brand campaigns through on-ground activations and community engagement.
  • Work closely with local marketing, PR, growth, and regional teams to ensure consistent brand messaging.
  • Identify opportunities to expand brand reach beyond existing crypto-native audiences.

4. Partnerships & Local Collaborations

  • Identify and execute partnerships with:
  • Universities and educational institutions
  • Web3 and blockchain communities
  • Financial education and investment communities
  • Technology and innovation organizations
  • Lifestyle, entertainment, and cultural partners
  • Build credible, long-term local relationships that strengthen Binance Japan's presence and reputation.
  • Collaborate with external partners to develop co-branded events, educational programs, and community initiatives.
  • Evaluate partnership opportunities based on strategic fit, audience relevance, and measurable business impact.

5. Performance Tracking & Optimization

  • Establish clear KPIs and evaluate performance across:
  • Event attendance, participation, and engagement
  • Community growth and retention
  • Brand awareness and audience reach
  • Registrations, KYC completion, and first-time trading conversion where applicable
  • Cost efficiency and return on marketing investment
  • Deliver timely post-event reports with actionable insights and recommendations.
  • Continuously optimize event formats, partnerships, and community initiatives based on performance data and local market feedback.
  • Manage budgets effectively while maintaining high execution standards.

Кого мы ищем

  • 4–7+ years of experience in events, community, brand, or integrated marketing.
  • Strong experience in the Japanese market is essential, with a deep understanding of local consumer behavior and business culture.
  • Proven track record of planning and executing successful events, partnerships, and community initiatives.
  • Experience working with agencies, production vendors, and external partners in Japan.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience in crypto, fintech, technology, or consumer-facing digital platforms.
  • Experience in regulated industries is an advantage.
