О команде

The Global Risk function at Nu is structured around two dimensions: country risk officers (horizontal) and global functional heads by risk discipline (vertical). This matrix creates a "risk surface" that allows the organization to scale efficiently without duplicating senior expertise across geographies.

The Head of Risk US will lead the US risk surface, owning all risk disciplines locally while maintaining close coordination with global functional heads: Global Credit Risk, the Global Market and Liquidity Head, and other risk domain leads.

The US risk team is in its early stages, with existing members covering Compliance, Financial Crime/AML, Regulatory, and is expected to grow in line with the business. This is a build-from-scratch opportunity for a senior executive who thrives in dynamic, high-growth environments.

О роли

The Head of Risk US is a senior executive responsible for the effective and independent oversight of all risk disciplines within Nu's US operation.

The role requires a leader who understands the US regulatory landscape deeply, can engage independently with regulators and senior governance bodies, and is capable of building a high-performing risk function in a fast-moving fintech environment.

This is a high-visibility, high-impact position that will interact frequently with the US Board/Risk Committee, senior regulators, and Nu's executive leadership team.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES AND EXPECTATIONS

Risk Framework & Governance

- Build and lead a comprehensive risk management framework for the US operation, covering credit, market, liquidity, operational, financial crime, and compliance risk.

- Develop a risk appetite framework and limit structure aligned with the US Board's stated risk tolerance, ensuring all business lines operate within defined parameters.

- Establish robust risk metrics, reporting dashboards, and early warning indicators to monitor exposures and escalate material issues in a timely manner.

- Serve as Secretary to the US Board Risk Management Committee; prepare and circulate agendas and minutes.

- Participate as a permanent member of key senior management committees including the Risk Committee, ALCO, Compliance Committee, and New Product Approval Committee.

Regulatory Engagement

- Serve as the primary independent risk voice in all interactions with US regulators, engaging with confidence and autonomy.

- Ensure Nu's control environment consistently meets and anticipates regulatory expectations in a rapidly evolving US regulatory landscape.

- Develop and maintain relationships with regulators, demonstrating transparency, preparedness, and a mature risk culture.

Risk Surface Management

- Oversee the day-to-day management of the US risk surface, coordinating with global functional heads on credit, market/liquidity, operational, and financial crime risk.

- Apply Nu's "target friction" philosophy: prioritize controls based on materiality, avoiding uniform friction in favor of risk-proportionate management that enables business growth.

- Monitor operational loss events, cyber incidents, and key risk indicators, ensuring mitigation strategies are in place and effective.

Team & Stakeholder Leadership

- Lead and develop the US risk team, fostering a culture of high standards, intellectual rigor, and ownership.

- Build a strong working relationship with engineering and technology teams to ensure risk infrastructure is prioritized and developed appropriately.

- Collaborate cross-functionally with the US Country Manager, Legal, Compliance, and Finance to embed risk awareness across the organization.

- Act as a business partner to the US leadership team — risk as an enabler, not a blocker.

Требования

Professional Experience & Education

- Bachelor's degree required; advanced degree (MBA, MSc Finance, JD) or professional certification (CFA, FRM) preferred.

- 15+ years of experience in risk management within financial services, with a significant portion of that in the US market.

- Deep expertise in the US regulatory environment (OCC, Fed, FDIC); experience engaging directly with regulators at a senior level is essential.

- Background in financial institutions, fintechs, or challenger banks strongly preferred. Candidates with exclusive experience at large traditional banks (without demonstrated adaptability) may not be the right fit for this stage of growth.

- Experience building or significantly transforming a risk function from an early/mid-stage into a mature framework.

- Solid understanding of all risk disciplines: credit, market, liquidity, operational, financial crime, and compliance — with particular depth in the control environment (AML/BSA, operational risk, cyber).

- Credit risk expertise is valued but not the primary focus; Nu's global credit function provides strong support in this area.

Competencies & Attributes

- Comfort with ambiguity and a proven ability to build structure from scratch in fast-moving environments — "generating balance based on chaos."

- Hands-on leader who is present on the ground and willing to be deeply involved in operational realities, not just strategic oversight.

- Independent thought leader capable of engaging directly with boards and regulators without relying on hierarchical support for every interaction.…
