Robinhood's Enterprise Security team is at the forefront of AI security, designing safety guardrails for cutting-edge AI and driving modern enterprise-wide security tooling standards. As a Senior Corporate Security Engineer on our Corporate Systems team, you will operate at the center of our enterprise security infrastructure, maintaining the control plane for user-facing security tooling and building innovative solutions that keep our employees and platform secure!

In this role, you will combine security engineering, systems administration, and hands-on AI agent development to drive key automation and safety initiatives. Joining a lean, high-impact team, you will have high visibility and direct empowerment to take strategic initiative, solve complex technical challenges, and shape Robinhood's AI security posture.

This role is based in our Menlo Park or Bellevue office(s), with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build, maintain, and evolve enterprise security tools that power key user-facing security flows, including endpoint security, login/authentication, and security telemetry.
  • Secure AI interactions across the company by designing safety guardrails and integrating agentic tools into enterprise workflows.
  • Automate evidence-gathering processes for Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) and internal/external regulatory audits.
  • Support enterprise vulnerability management programs and maintain operational resilience and uptime across core systems.
  • Tackle evolving enterprise security challenges, such as protecting against active persistent threats (APTs) and securing the digital footprint of our executives.
  • Collaborate cross-functionally with Security Operations, GRC, Legal, and IT engineering partners to maintain a seamless, high-trust security environment.

Что вы приносите

  • 5+ years of professional experience in information security, combining security engineering, IT systems administration, and enterprise infrastructure.
  • Proven experience in one or more of the following: managing and securing modern identity systems (e.g., Okta), or deep experience blending security engineering with systems/infrastructure engineering. (We're hiring for two closely related roles on this team — identity platform depth is a strong plus for one, broader security+ systems generalist range for the other.)
  • Exposure to or hands-on experience with AI tools and agent workflows (e.g., OpenAI, Claude, Cursor, or custom API integrations).
  • Practical experience working within cloud environments (specifically Google Cloud Platform) and modern software development processes.
  • Broad security generalist skill set, including device compliance enforcement, binary/plugin/extension evaluation, behavior tracing, vulnerability management, and security policy enforcement.
  • Strong ownership mentality with an adaptable "builder" mindset, capable of driving automation and technical initiatives independently.
