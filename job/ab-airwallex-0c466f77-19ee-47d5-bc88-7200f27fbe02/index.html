О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. GLOBAL QUALITY ASSURANCE, SENIOR MANAGER

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

Lead Airwallex’s global Operations Quality Assurance framework across compliance, risk controls and key customer-supporting functions, helping the business meet its regulatory obligations and risk management standards as it scales. You’ll work closely with regional and global Compliance, Risk, Product and Operations teams to design and continuously improve QA programmes across the end-to-end customer and transaction lifecycle, including KYC, transaction monitoring, name screening, underwriting and merchant risk monitoring.

- Lead a global team of QA analysts across different areas of expertise, including KYC, transaction monitoring, name screening, underwriting and merchant risk monitoring.

- Build and continuously improve QA programmes that cover key operational functions and support consistent operational standards.

- Oversee monthly QA testing and deliver clear, high-quality reports that identify operational risks, process gaps and system limitations.

- Diagnose root causes and impacts, then translate findings into data-driven recommendations, remediation plans and optimisation opportunities.

- Communicate QA outcomes clearly to key stakeholders and senior leadership, tracking remedial actions through to effective closure.

- Drive global projects and initiatives for operational excellence, using analytics and insights to support informed decision-making.

You'll work from our UK or Singapore office on a hybrid working model.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- 8+ years of relevant experience in financial services, fintech or consulting.

- Experience in one or more of the following areas: AML and compliance review or advisory; credit, fraud, brand or consumer risk management; or QA, audit or consulting with strong exposure to compliance testing and risk assessments.

- Strong understanding of AML/CTF regulations and/or risk management controls, industry practices, and regulatory or card scheme expectations in financial services, payments or fintech.

- Familiarity with AML processes such as KYC, transaction monitoring and name screening, and/or risk controls such as underwriting and merchant risk monitoring.

- Experience leading teams and managing QA, audit, compliance testing or risk assessment programmes.

- Strong analytical, problem-solving, project management and stakeholder management skills.

- Excellent written and spoken English.

Preferred

- Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Economics, Business Management, Statistics, Mathematics, Law or a related discipline.

- CAMS, AAMLP or another relevant professional certification.

- Experience working with data and analytics tools such as SQL or Looker.

- Experience working across regional and global teams in a fast-growing business.

YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF

- You're comfortable owning the roadmap yourself.

- You own the outcome and you don't wait for permission to fix what's broken.

- You hold yourself to a high standard even when no one is watching. Quality and accountability aren't external expectations, they're internal ones.

- You communicate clearly and directly across cultures, and you're thoughtful about how context shifts meaning across borders.

- You connect dots across functions and hold the big picture without losing grip on the details.

LEARN MORE ABOUT YOUR TEAM

Operations at Airwallex is where global ambition gets translated into systems that actually hold at scale.

This team runs the high-stakes flows businesses depend on — payments, accounts, and cross-border transactions. The work sits at the intersection of product, compliance, and customer experience. The problems are rarely simple: balancing speed with rigor, global consistency with local reality, and immediate execution with the stronger systems a growing company needs next.

It's a function for operators with a builder mentality: trusted to take ownership, work cross-functionally with product and risk, and create new processes for things that have not existed before.

If you want to help build the operational backbone of a global financial platform, this is work that keeps the whole machine moving.
