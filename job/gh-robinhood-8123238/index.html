О team + role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world’s biggest financial problems. We’re looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn’t a place for complacency, it’s where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We’re a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

The Business Analyst team shapes the strategy, growth, and performance of Robinhood's credit products, delivering top-tier experiences for our users. As part of the broader Credit organization, we work closely with product, engineering, risk, and operations to unlock sustainable business growth through deep analytical rigor. Our team fosters a collaborative, ownership-driven culture where every analyst directly influences business strategy and product direction.

As a Business Analyst Intern, you'll spend 12 weeks embedded within a team across our Money and Growth org, getting direct exposure to the metrics, stakeholders, and strategic priorities that drive Robinhood's core business. You'll own real work from day one and build the analytical and cross-functional skills that define our team. Top performers may be extended an offer to join our 18-month rotational Business Analyst program after graduation.

This role is based in our Washington, DC office, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Get hands-on with a specific business area within Money and Growth, working directly with product, engineering, risk, or operations stakeholders on live priorities
  • Analyze business performance metrics and economic drivers to identify growth opportunities and optimize business outcomes
  • Structure and solve complex, ambiguous problems across product strategy, pricing, user segmentation, and risk-return trade-offs
  • Partner with engineering, product, risk, and finance teams to execute key strategic initiatives and monitor program efficacy
  • Develop analytical models, dashboards, and presentations to inform decision-making and influence business direction

Что вы приносите

  • Experience or coursework in business analysis, strategy, management consulting, financial services, or a quantitative analytical role
  • Strong proficiency in SQL and quantitative modeling techniques
  • Ability to synthesize complex quantitative findings into clear, actionable recommendations for cross-functional partners
  • Fluency with AI tools, with a genuine interest in staying current as the tools evolve
  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Business, Economics, Finance, Engineering, Data Science, or a related quantitative field, with an expected graduation date of Spring/Summer 2028
