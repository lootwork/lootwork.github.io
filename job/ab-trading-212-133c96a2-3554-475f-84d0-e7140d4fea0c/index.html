О роли

As the Deputy Head of Compliance for Trading 212 UK, you'll lead Core Compliance (Advisory and Compliance Monitoring) day to day and deputise for the UK Head of Compliance (SMF 16) where required.

You'll help to ensure UK Compliance has the structure, capacity and expertise the FCA expects of a permanent, effective and independent compliance function, while acting as a trusted partner to the business on products, promotions and regulatory change.

Что предстоит делать

- Deputise for the Head of Compliance and act as the escalation point for the team.

- Oversee the Compliance Advisory and Monitoring teams day to day, setting priorities and making sure business and regulatory deadlines are met.

- Represent UK Compliance at governance forums, when required.

- Oversee second line advice and challenge on new products and changes, so Compliance is involved from design rather than at sign-off.

- Oversee financial promotions and client communications approval, including the affiliates framework, and act as senior sign-off where required.

- Ensure Consumer Duty outcomes, product governance and fair value are assessed and evidenced.

- Proactively monitor the regulatory landscape and the impact of new rules, working with the team and the business to ensure each change is assessed, owned and implemented.

- Oversee the team delivering the risk-based Compliance Monitoring Plan, including test completion, KRIs, findings and remediation.

- Ensure breaches and incidents are assessed, escalated and reported on time.

- Own the production of monthly Compliance MI.

- Support the Head of Compliance on FCA correspondence, engagement and internal audit.

- Ensure timely and accurate reporting to the FCA

- Champion a proactive, practical and ethical compliance culture across the business.

WHAT YOU NEED TO HAVE

- Substantial compliance experience (typically 8+ years) within FCA-regulated investment services, retail brokerage or wealth management, including time in a senior or deputy role.

- Proven experience managing others.

- Expertise in FCA regulation, including SYSC, COBS, PRIN, Consumer Duty, PROD, SM&CR, MAR and financial promotion requirements.

- Experience of new product approval and launch in a retail investment or trading business.

- Experience producing Board and committee MI and presenting to senior management.

- Direct experience of FCA engagement, regulatory change programmes and remediation.

- Experience in a scaling or high-growth financial services business is advantageous; comfort with change, ambiguity and a fast-moving environment is essential.

- Educated to degree level or equivalent; relevant professional qualifications (ICA, CISI Compliance Diploma, legal or equivalent) are advantageous.
