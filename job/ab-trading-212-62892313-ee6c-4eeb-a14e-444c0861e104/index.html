Что предстоит делать

- Brainstorm ideas with stakeholders and help define early feature concepts

- Express your design vision via wireframes, high-fidelity designs, and functional prototypes if necessary

- Present and explain the rationale behind your design decisions on regular peer design review sessions. Collect feedback and quickly iterate.

- Collaborate closely with engineers to ensure that your designs are accurately implemented according to Trading 212's standards of quality

- Base your design decisions on data by leveraging Trading 212’s analytics tools and on user feedback by conducting usability tests

- Evolve and maintain Trading 212’s design library

- Drive innovation by integrating new tools and workflows into our design process.

- Be autonomous and have full ownership of your work

WHAT YOU'LL NEED

- Expert knowledge of user-centric design principles and methodologies

- Strong visual design skills, including layout, typography, and colour theory

- Strong diagramming skills - you can reduce complex user flows into clean and easy-to-understand flowcharts.

- Proven proficiency with Figma, Sketch, or other design tools.

- Excellent collaborative skills - you can express your ideas verbally and in writing

- An extensive portfolio that you can share with us
