Your mission

As Markets Control & Analytics Lead, you will drive P&L transparency, pricing and hedging analytics, front-to-back operational KPIs, and robust control frameworks across traditional and digital asset classes. You'll get to turn complex trading data into actionable insights, strengthen P&L transparency and build automated, scalable processes that improve trading performance and support better business decisions.

Что предстоит делать

  • P&L & Performance: Own the daily P&L process across all asset classes, including production, validation, reconciliation, variance analysis, attribution and trader sign-off.
  • Trading Analytics & TCA: Analyse pricing, hedging, inventory and FX performance, including execution quality, spreads and trading costs, to identify opportunities for P&L improvement.
  • Operational KPIs & Dashboards: Define and monitor front-to-back KPIs across Trading, Operations, Custody and Settlement, building dashboards that drive transparency, efficiency and scalability.
  • Controls & Automation: Establish robust P&L and KPI controls, automate reporting and collaborate with Trading, Treasury, Risk, Data and Engineering to ensure data integrity and operational excellence.
  • GL Reconciliation: Own the monthly Trading P&L-to-General Ledger reconciliation across products, partnering with Accounting to ensure accurate valuation, realised P&L and financial reporting.

Кто вы

  • Education: Degree in Finance, Economics, Accounting or a related field; CFA, CPA or ACCA is a plus.
  • Experience: 7+ years in front-office (e.g., Quant Trading) or middle-office (e.g., Product Control, Trading Operations) roles within banking, trading or fintech.
  • Markets Knowledge: Strong understanding of trading P&L, hedging and inventory risk across asset classes, including P&L bases such as cash, FIFO, average cost and mark-to-market. Experience with crypto, stablecoins, precious metals or FX is a plus.
  • Technical Skills: Proficient in Excel, SQL, Python or similar tools, with experience in analytics, dashboards and automation.
  • Mindset: Hands-on, analytical and entrepreneurial, with the ownership and drive to build and scale a high-impact function.
