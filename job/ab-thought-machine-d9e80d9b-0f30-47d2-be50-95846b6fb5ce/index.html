As a Senior Software Engineer I at Thought Machine, you will own and deliver highly complex, end-to-end engineering work items. You will look beyond immediate coding tasks to consider long-term system architecture, product strategy, and client outcomes.

In this role, you will act as a technical anchor for your team, solving ambiguous problems, guiding less experienced engineers, and actively shaping our technical and product roadmaps. This is a role for an engineer who values clean, maintainable systems, embraces an egoless approach to collaboration, and is driven to deliver robust solutions to production.

Что предстоит делать

Technical Leadership

- Design & Build: Design and build highly reliable, secure, and scalable software solutions for complex problems.

- Architecture & Standards: Contribute to team guidelines ensuring our services meet top-tier security, performance, cost, and reliability standards.

- Code Quality & Peer Review: Set a high bar for code quality by providing constructive, detailed feedback on design documents and pull requests.

- Operational Excellence: Investigate issues across multiple services in production, drive learning from operational incidents, and feed improvements back into our prioritisation process.

- Hiring: Actively participate in our growth by leading technical interviews.

Product & Delivery

- End-to-End Ownership: Own work items from initial concept to production, creating structured delivery plans, managing dependencies along the way and owning delivery of these work items.

- Product Vision: Partner with Product Managers to prioritize features, translate client requirements into technical specifications, and ensure solutions align with our overarching product vision.

- Incident Management: Confidently own the resolution of incidents, coordinating mitigation, fixes, and post-mortem artifacts.

Collaboration & Mentorship

- Team Growth: Mentor and support less experienced engineers, helping them level up technically and adapt to Thought Machine’s engineering philosophy.

- Stakeholder Management: Translate highly technical problems and architectural decisions into clear concepts for non-technical stakeholders.

Кого мы ищем

- Technical Expertise: Strong proficiency in modern backend languages (ideally Go or Python) and experience building distributed, cloud-native systems (Kubernetes, AWS/GCP, gRPC, PostgreSQL).

- Problem Solving: A proven track record of breaking down ambiguous, and complex problems into clear, deliverable milestones.

- Production Operations: Experience supporting high-throughput systems in production environments and diagnosing complex issues across distributed systems.

- Egoless Collaboration: A highly collaborative, feedback-receptive mindset focused on finding the best outcomes for our clients and business rather than defending personal preferences.

- Communication: Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to build consensus and document technical designs clearly.
