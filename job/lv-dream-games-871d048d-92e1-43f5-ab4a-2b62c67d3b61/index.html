You’re invited to Dream Games Career Days!

If you are a 4th-year university student, take your first step into the mobile gaming industry. Join our event for an opportunity to gain global insights from our product and marketing teams. In this event, you will also get some hands-on experience and explore our new grad positions.

This event will be held on 20th March at Four Seasons Hotel Bosphorus. We are pleased to host with accommodation and arrange travel for those joining us from outside Istanbul.

Fill out the application form and ensure that you complete your application by the 4th of January.

Protecting your privacy and the security of your data is a top priority for Dream Games. We collect and process your personal data as confidential as part of the recruitment process. Please read our Statement of Personal Data Protection Law and Consent Letter to know more details.
