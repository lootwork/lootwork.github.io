О роли

Spend more time playing collection/simulation/RPG games than sleeping? If you obsessively check leaderboards, have opinions about gacha mechanics, and get genuinely excited by a well-timed live event - we want to talk.

Join the Dragon City team as a Product Manager Intern and turn your love for games into real player insights and product impact. You'll work side by side with experienced PMs, learning how to analyse game performance, track market trends, and help shape the future of one of the world's most iconic dragon-collecting games. From digging into live-ops data to benchmarking the competition, this is your chance to see how a top free-to-play title is built, measured, and grown from the inside out.

Что предстоит делать

As a Product Manager Intern, you'll be right in the action, working alongside experienced PMs to:

  • Benchmark competitor collection, social and RPG games to identify trends and surface new feature ideas.
  • Gather player feedback through community research, surveys, and in-game data to improve the player experience.
  • Assist with feature and content launches: testing, monitoring, and documenting learnings.
  • Dive into game metrics - retention, monetisation, engagement, and turn data into actionable insights.
  • Support live-ops planning, dragon event scheduling, and cross-team coordination.

Кого мы ищем

  • You're a current student (Engineering, Computer Science, Economics, Mathematics, Game Design, UX… or similar).
  • You're passionate about collection, social, RPG mobile games - Dragon City experience is a big plus.
  • You have strong analytical skills and are comfortable working with numbers and spreadsheets.
  • You're curious, proactive, and eager to learn about product analytics, game economy, and live-ops.
  • You're an excellent communicator in English (Spanish is a plus).
  • Bonus points if you've participated in game jams, personal projects, or uni assignments related to gaming or app design.
