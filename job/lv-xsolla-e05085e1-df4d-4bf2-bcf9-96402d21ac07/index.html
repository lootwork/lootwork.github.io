Backend Developer

Обязанности

  • Design, develop, and maintain scalable backend services for key components of the Xsolla platform.
  • Architect and evolve microservices-based systems to support large-scale, high-performance operations.
  • Implement and optimize database structures and queries for maximum efficiency and reliability.
  • Ensure high availability, performance, and fault tolerance across distributed services.
  • Lead efforts to improve CI/CD pipelines, deployment processes, and system observability.
  • Conduct code reviews, mentor team members, and foster a culture of engineering excellence.
  • Collaborate closely with product, frontend, and DevOps teams to deliver reliable and innovative solutions.
  • Drive continuous improvement through automation, testing, and proactive system monitoring.
  • Stay ahead of industry trends, adopting emerging technologies to enhance platform capabilities.

Требования

  • 3+ years of professional experience in in backend development (with Go).
  • Proven experience in designing and maintaining microservices architectures.
  • Strong background in SQL and NoSQL databases, with hands-on experience in query optimization.
  • In-depth knowledge of RESTful APIs, event-driven systems, and asynchronous communication patterns.
  • Practical experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD automation.
  • Solid understanding of software design principles, OOP, and clean architecture.
  • Familiarity with testing frameworks (e.g., Jest, Mocha, or similar) and monitoring tools (e.g., Prometheus, Grafana).
  • Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments with cross-functional teams.
  • Strong communication skills, leadership qualities, and a proactive, ownership-driven mindset.
  • Proficiency in English for technical communication and documentation.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with Go frameworks such as Gin, Echo, or Fiber.
  • Familiarity with GraphQL or gRPC.
  • Background in distributed systems, caching layers, or message queues (e.g., Kafka, RabbitMQ).
  • Exposure to gaming, fintech, or digital asset platforms.
