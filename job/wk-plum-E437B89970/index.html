We're now going after something even bigger: the £2 trillion+ advised market in the UK and beyond, currently locked behind high fees and complexity. By combining AI-driven financial guidance with the products people actually need, we're building a solution for everyone, not just the wealthy few.

О роли

The Product team shapes the experiences that power Plum. They turn complex problems into simple, scalable solutions - collaborating across Design, Engineering, and Data to build features our customers love. We're looking for a Senior Product Manager to own a core part of the Plum product end to end. You'll set the strategy for your problem space, run the roadmap, and day to day execution to ship features that measurably improve how people save and invest.

What You Will Dο

  • Own the roadmap for one of our core products, from ideation through to development, launch, growth, and maintenance
  • Run discovery: talk to customers, mine behavioral and support data, and use that to decide what to build next and what to explicitly not build.
  • Partner day to day with an engineering squad and a designer to ship, measure, and iterate - you're accountable for the outcome
  • Setting team goals and success metrics to align with company goals and represent your product area in company-wide planning
  • Prioritize ruthlessly against a roadmap that's always bigger than the team's capacity, and be able to explain the trade-offs to stakeholders
  • Work with Compliance and Risk to turn regulation into an innovation driver
  • Drive maximum impact for your product based on data analysis, market research, and company strategy

Кто вы

  • 5+ years in product management, including time in a high-growth tech company or banking/finance business
  • Experience shipping in a regulated or trust-sensitive space (financial services, health, or similar)
  • Outstanding written and verbal communication skills
  • High analytical and technical acumen along with solid business sense that guides ROI-based prioritisation
  • Experience formulating and driving a focused product vision, and owning the narrative with leadership, customers, and partners
  • A bias towards action with the ability to roll up your sleeves and get things done including working with data
  • The ability to provide insight and deliver measurable results for complex products under ambiguous situations in a fast-paced environment
  • Genuine interest in personal finance and in Plum's mission of helping people build wealth without having to think about it.
