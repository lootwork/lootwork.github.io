О роли

As a Graduate Compliance Monitoring Associate at Trading 212 UK, you'll start your career in a second line of defence team at one of the UK's fastest-growing investment platforms. You'll help deliver the annual risk-based Compliance Monitoring Plan (CCMP): carrying out monitoring tests under supervision, recording findings clearly and helping track remediation through to completion.

This is an entry-level role with structured training. You'll build a solid understanding of FCA rules and how controls work in practice, with a clear path to Compliance Monitoring Associate as your experience grows.

Что предстоит делать

- Carry out assigned monitoring tests under the Compliance Monitoring Plan, following documented procedures and completing them on time.

- Extract and check data, select samples and keep clear, complete evidence for each test.

- Draft findings, recommended actions and management responses for review by a senior colleague.

- Help track open remediation actions, follow up with owners and record evidence of closure.

- Log breaches and issues accurately and escalate anything unusual to your manager promptly.

- Help prepare monthly monitoring MI, including completion statistics and KRIs.

- Help keep monitoring procedures and test templates up to date.

- Support the wider Compliance team, including Advisory, with ad hoc tasks as required.

- Complete your training plan and build your knowledge of FCA rules, Trading 212's products and the firm's controls.

WHAT YOU NEED TO HAVE

- Educated to degree level (2:1 or above, or equivalent) in Law, Finance, Economics or a related field.

- A genuine interest in financial services regulation and compliance; an internship or work experience in financial services is an advantage.

- Strong attention to detail and accuracy, with a methodical approach to checking information.

- Clear written communication, including the ability to summarise findings concisely.

- Good IT skills, particularly Excel or Google Sheets.

- A self-starter attitude, with the willingness to ask questions and learn quickly.

- Ability to work under pressure and be adaptable.
