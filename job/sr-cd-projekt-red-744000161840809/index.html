Job Description

CD PROJEKT RED is looking for a Senior Gameplay Programmer to join our team to work on Cyberpunk 2, the follow-up to Cyberpunk 2077. In this role, you will help the team develop believable worlds by creating systems and features for vehicles. This role will be responsible for vehicle pathfinding and AI, managing vehicle physics and drive models, and integrating vehicles with other game systems such as missions.

Обязанности

  • Work closely with designers and other developers to understand their needs and ensure they have the ability to create believable traffic systems and vehicle behaviors.
  • Build and maintain major systems that define vehicle AI behaviour in an open world.
  • Develop debug and designer tools needed for vehicle AI systems.
  • Work with other programmers to ensure that vehicle drive models work across all use cases.
  • Write high-quality code that is optimised, bug-free, and aligned with project goals.
  • Foster cross-functional collaboration to ensure the tools contribute effectively to all game systems and components.

Требования

  • At least 5 years of professional experience as a programmer within the games industry.
  • Strong proficiency in C++ programming and knowledge of Unreal Engine.
  • Experience with pathfinding and navigation for vehicles, ideally in an open world game.
  • Experience with modeling vehicle physics and vehicle drive models.
  • A good understanding of geometric algorithms and data structures.
  • Experience profiling and optimizing code at the systems level.
  • A good understanding of 3D mathematics and physics.
  • Ability to think creatively and strategically to overcome technical challenges and drive innovation in technology.
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to effectively communicate complex technical concepts to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Будет плюсом

(Please note that these skills are not required to apply for the position)

  • Experience with ECS or the Unreal Mass Framework.
  • Experience with navigation in 3D.
  • Experience working in a large development environment.
  • Shipped at least 1 AAA title.
