О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,300 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

О команде

The Marketing team at Airwallex drives brand awareness and customer engagement through innovative and strategic campaigns. We work to communicate the value of our financial solutions, attract new customers, and strengthen relationships with existing ones. By leveraging data-driven insights and creative strategies, we ensure Airwallex stands out in a competitive market. Our team is passionate about telling the Airwallex story and supporting the company's growth and success.

Что предстоит делать

Reporting to the Director, Global Growth Content, you will own and scale Airwallex’s growth and demand generation content engine globally on the payments product. You will work closely with global and regional teammates across Growth, Product Marketing, Product, SEO, Performance Marketing, and Lifecycle to build content that drives qualified demand, pipeline, and revenue.

This role is ideal for a hands-on content leader who loves writing and editing SEO content, ebooks, guides, and one-pagers, and who is excited to go deep on technical product narratives—translating complex capabilities into clear, compelling, conversion-oriented content.

You will join the Airwallex Marketing team during a critical period of growth. As we scale, we are building a team that balances global consistency in content strategy, quality, and process, with local flexibility and speed in execution. This is a unique opportunity to help shape how growth content drives the next phase of Airwallex’s expansion.

This role is based in San Francisco.

Обязанности

- Build Growth Content Marketing as a global discipline at Airwallex, establishing best practices, scalable processes, and a high-performing team over time.

- Own the end-to-end demand gen content strategy, spanning SEO, gated assets, mid-funnel conversion content, and product-led narratives.

- Write, edit, and ship high-impact content including

- SEO pillar pages, cluster articles, and comparison content

- Ebooks, whitepapers, and long-form guides

- One-pagers, solution briefs, and product explainers

- Partner deeply with Product Marketing and Product to translate launches, features, APIs, and workflows into content that is searchable, understandable, and sellable.

- Use user, customer, and market insights to improve ICP targeting, segmentation, messaging, and content distribution strategies.

- Work closely with SEO and Performance Marketing to align content with keyword intent, funnel stages, and conversion goals.

- Be obsessive about measurement and iteration, using performance data to continuously optimize content for traffic quality, engagement, and pipeline impact.

- Hold a high bar for content quality and technical accuracy, ensuring clarity without oversimplifying complex concepts.

- Build a structured experimentation mindset—scaling what performs, refreshing or retiring what doesn’t.

- Balance short-term growth outcomes with longer-term investments in evergreen content, topical authority, and durable demand.

Кто вы

We're looking for people who meet the minimum requirements for this role. The preferred qualifications are great to have, but are not mandatory.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

- 6+ years of experience in growth content marketing, demand gen content, or SEO-led content roles, preferably at a high-growth B2B technology company.

- A proven track record of creating content that drives measurable business outcomes across the funnel—from traffic to MQLs to pipeline.

- Exceptional writing and editing skills, with the ability to explain technical products, platforms, or workflows clearly and credibly.

- Strong understanding of SEO fundamentals, including keyword intent, on-page optimization, internal linking, and content clustering.

- A data-driven mindset, with the ability to analyze content performance and optimize based on results—not just instincts.

- Experience partnering cross-functionally with Product Marketing, Product, SEO, Performance, and Lifecycle teams.

- Comfort working in a fast-paced, global environment and communicating progress and impact to key stakeholders.

- Experience managing freelancers or agencies and holding them to a high bar for quality and performance.

- Familiarity with common CMS, SEO, analytics, and experimentation tools (e.g., Webflow/WordPress, GSC, GA4, Ahrefs).
