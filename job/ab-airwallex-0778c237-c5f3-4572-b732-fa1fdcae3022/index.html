HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

As a Staff Data Scientist, you'll help Airwallex make better strategic and operational decisions through rigorous analysis, forecasting, and clear data storytelling. You'll combine hands-on analytical leadership with strategic influence, helping evolve data science into a more scalable, general capability across the business.

- Uplevel the data science team through technical mentorship, hands-on leadership on complex analytical problems, and a clear vision for the team’s longer-term direction.

- Act as a strategic thought partner to cross-functional teams, using data and technical expertise to inform, influence, and execute strategy and investment decisions.

- Apply quantitative analysis, data mining, forecasting, and clear data storytelling to answer important business questions and guide strategic decisions.

- Define goals, build forecasts, and monitor key business metrics to identify trends and assess performance across the organisation.

- Help evolve data science from individual analyses into scalable systems of decision support, establishing robust analytical approaches that produce reliable insights repeatedly.

You'll work from our London office three days a week.

LEARN MORE ABOUT YOUR TEAM

Data Science at Airwallex is evolving from individual analyses and standalone models into scalable systems of decision and action. We bring together two complementary capabilities: DS Analytics, which improves decision quality and reduces decision latency, and DS Algorithm, which directly optimizes product and business outcomes in real time.

You’ll build systems of decision that help teams understand what changed, determine what to do next, and learn whether the action worked. This means owning the AI analytics harness around the analysis—including semantic layers, context retrieval, evaluation infrastructure, validation gates, and governance—so decisions remain reliable, contextualized, and trustworthy at scale.

You’ll also build systems of action that use machine learning and AI to shape product behavior, ranking, pricing, risk, allocation, and customer experiences. The work extends beyond training models to designing how intelligent systems reason, use tools, operate within guardrails, execute workflows, and measure their incremental impact.

As AI makes ad hoc analysis increasingly accessible, the remaining advantage is scale, scientific method, and rigor. You’ll structure ambiguous problems, design experiments, identify confounding factors, challenge misleading conclusions, and build production systems that produce reliable outcomes repeatedly—not just one-off answers.

The work is moving up the stack: from analysis to system, from model to harness, and from answering a question once to building something that answers it correctly over time. If you want to create data science systems that compound decision quality and turn intelligence into measurable action, this is where that work happens.
