О team + role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world’s biggest financial problems. We’re looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn’t a place for complacency, it’s where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We’re a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

We are looking for a Social Media Manager, Brand to drive brand storytelling, social-first creative, and culture-defining activations across our key platforms. This is a role for an experienced social/brand professional who can translate Robinhood's mission into content and moments that resonate with Millennial and Gen Z audiences. You will balance a bold, culturally fluent brand voice with the rigorous compliance standards required in financial services. This role owns brand storytelling, social activations, and creative strategy — it does not include day-to-day community moderation or customer support, which are owned by dedicated teams. This role is equal parts brand storyteller, trend-spotter, creative strategist, and cultural translator. You’ll know how to turn a product moment into a scroll-stopping activation that Millennial and Gen Z audiences share, remix, and talk about.

This role is based in our New York, NY, and Menlo Park, CA offices, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own our social content strategy: Define the voice, tone, and creative point of view for Robinhood’s brand across X, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Reddit — ensuring every post feels distinctly Robinhood and resonates with a Millennial and Gen Z audience.
  • Lead social-first brand campaigns: Partner with Brand, Creative, and Product to conceive and execute social-native campaigns and always-on content that make Robinhood’s mission tangible and culturally relevant.
  • Spearhead social activations: Ideate and execute high-impact brand activations — cultural moments, platform-native stunts, creator collabs, live events, and surprise-and-delight drops — designed to build buzz and deepen affinity with Millennial and Gen Z audiences.
  • Speak fluently to Millennial and Gen Z audiences: Bring a deep, authentic understanding of Millennial and Gen Z culture, humor, platform behavior, and finance-adjacent trends (FinTok, FinTwit, money influencers) to keep Robinhood’s content native and credible.
  • Stay ahead of culture: Constantly track platform algorithm shifts, emerging formats, meme culture, and rising creators to keep Robinhood’s brand presence cutting-edge, timely, and relevant.
  • Translate insights into strategy: Use social listening and performance data to understand what resonates with our audience, and turn those insights into sharper creative and content decisions.
  • Champion brand and compliance judgment: Act as a critical line of defense to ensure all brand and activation content adheres to Robinhood’s Content Standards and financial regulations, ensuring information is always fair, balanced, and truthful.

Что вы приносите

  • 5+ years of experience in social media, brand marketing, or social-first creative roles, preferably at a fast-paced consumer tech, fintech, or culturally relevant brand, with a track record of leading campaigns or activations end-to-end.
  • Deep fluency in Millennial and Gen Z culture — you know what earns a share, a save, a remix, or a comment, and you can spot a trend before it peaks.
  • Experience partnering with brand, creative, and campaign teams — you know how to translate a brand or product story into content and activations that feel native and culturally relevant on social.
  • A native understanding of platform nuances (especially our priority channels: X, TikTok, Instagram, and Reddit) and how brand content performs differently across each.
  • Exceptional written and creative communication skills; you can seamlessly code-switch between a formal brand brief and a culturally sharp post built for Millennial and Gen Z audiences.
  • Data-driven mindset with the ability to translate social and campaign metrics (engagement, reach, sentiment, cultural buzz) into actionable creative strategy.
  • Ability to thrive in a highly regulated industry, balancing bold brand creativity with strict compliance and legal guardrails.
