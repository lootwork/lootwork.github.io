We are looking for a Social Media Manager, Brand & Influencers to help define how Robinhood shows up for the next generation of investors. This is a hands-on creative role for someone who lives and breathes internet culture, understands what resonates with Gen Z, and knows how to turn that understanding into original, engaging social content.

This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA or New York, NY offices, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

We're looking for a maker, not just a strategist. Someone who can spot an emerging trend, develop a creative concept, and bring it to life through content that feels native to the platform. You'll help build a distinct, culturally relevant voice for Robinhood through always-on content, social-first storytelling, and unexpected brand moments that earn attention and spark conversation. You'll combine strong creative instincts with an understanding of brand strategy, platform behavior, and audience insights. You'll move quickly, experiment often, and know when to jump on a cultural moment versus when to create something entirely new. This role owns social-first brand content, creative development, and activations, with a particular focus on engaging Gen Z audiences. It does not include day-to-day community moderation or customer support, which are owned by dedicated teams.

  • Create social-first content for Gen Z: Concept, develop, and produce original, culturally relevant content for Robinhood's social channels, with an emphasis on TikTok, Instagram, X, and emerging platforms. Bring ideas from trend or insight to execution, creating content that feels native to the internet, not traditional brand advertising.
  • Bring social ideas to life: Don't just brief ideas — make them happen. Write scripts, develop concepts, capture content, collaborate with creators, and work with creative partners to bring social ideas to life quickly.
  • Build Robinhood's voice with the next generation: Help shape a distinct, recognizable brand personality that resonates with Gen Z through storytelling, cultural relevance, and a deep understanding of how younger audiences engage with brands.
  • Turn trends into content: Stay immersed in internet culture, emerging creators, platform formats, and conversations shaping Gen Z. Identify opportunities for Robinhood to participate in culture in ways that feel timely, authentic, and ownable.
  • Develop always-on social programming: Build repeatable content formats, original series, and creative franchises that give younger audiences a reason to follow, engage with, and share Robinhood's content.
  • Drive social-first campaigns and activations: Partner with Brand, Creative, and Product to develop campaigns, creator collaborations, and unexpected cultural moments that build awareness and affinity with younger audiences.
  • Experiment, learn, and iterate: Test new creative approaches, formats, and ideas at a high cadence. Use performance data, social listening, and audience behavior to understand what's working and continuously evolve our content.
  • Balance creativity with strong brand judgment: Push creative boundaries while ensuring content aligns with Robinhood's brand standards and the regulatory requirements of financial services.
