⭐️ Our Third Party & Outsourcing Risk Management Team

We are a high-performing Global team focused on delivering pragmatic Third Party Risk Management (‘TPRM’) that protects Monzo and our customers globally. As Monzo expands across new markets and scales its product range, the operational footprint is growing fast, and the operating environment is becoming more complex.

We’re looking for a Senior Third Party Risk Manager to help shape and deliver our approach. As well as ensuring Monzo’s TPRM has well defined controls, processes and governance models in place to meet the continually growing and evolving demands of global Third Party Risk Management regulations - you’ll also set priorities and turn them into measurable improvements.

Reporting to the Director of Third Party Risk, this is a hands-on role, with strategic scope. You’ll work across the global team, and with a variety of stakeholders throughout Monzo, to strengthen our framework, manage complex risks and improve how we deliver TPRM both tactically and strategically.

🔑 You’ll play a key role by...

  • Delivering Core TPRM Operations Globally: Operating end-to-end operational activities, including supplier tiering, materiality assessments, due diligence reviews, exit planning support, and regulatory reporting.
  • Driving Operational Excellence: Taking ownership of process quality, making sure TPRM activities are completed accurately, consistently and on time. You’ll look for ways to improve how we manage third party risk in practice.
  • Helping shape our priorities: Identifying emerging risks, gaps and opportunities across third party risk management, and making recommendations to inform the team’s strategy and roadmap. You’ll develop practical solutions and help deliver improvements through to completion.
  • Enabling Strategic Delivery: Supporting and feeding into higher-level strategic initiatives and complex risk landscapes. Leading agreed initiatives from problem definition through implementation autonomously, working across teams and global markets to deliver measurable improvements and ensuring clear visibility throughout.
  • Collaborating Across Teams: Working directly with internal stakeholders across the 3LOD, and external suppliers to clear blockers, follow up on actions, and resolve issues effectively.
  • Sharing your expertise: Support colleagues and stakeholders with practical guidance, helping them understand how to deliver their third party risk responsibilities.

🤩 We’d love to hear from you if…

  • You know Outsourcing, Third Party Risk & Regulation: You have substantial practical TPRM experience (ideally in banking or financial services) operating in both the UK and EU financial markets.
  • You’ve contributed to a risk strategy, framework or operating model: You can demonstrate how you translated an identified gap into a practical improvement.
  • You’re comfortable leading complex assessments: You make balanced, well-reasoned recommendations when information is incomplete or stakeholders have different priorities.
  • You have experience working with various technologies: TPR tooling and AI to build innovative solutions and streamline operational workflows.
  • You hold a high quality bar: You pay close attention to detail and care about accuracy. You use data and reporting to spot patterns, make evidence-based decisions and improve processes.
  • You take ownership: You work independently, take initiative and prioritise your workload in a fast-moving environment. You use sound risk judgement and know when to seek guidance or involve others.
  • You focus on outcomes, not just activity: You measure success by what has materially improved whether that's faster turnaround times, clearer risk ownership, or cleaner supplier data.
  • You are a clear, pragmatic communicator: You build strong relationships with internal teams, third parties and regulators. You’re comfortable holding colleagues accountable and offering constructive challenges when needed. You bring together input from specialists across Information Security, Data Protection, Legal and Operational Resilience to form a clear view of risk.
  • You are comfortable escalating: You use sound judgement to know when to involve your team or manager in emerging issues or concerns, and communicate your priorities and workload clearly and regularly, defaulting to transparency and taking accountability.
