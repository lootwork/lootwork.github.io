Кто мы

We simplify wealth creation. Founded in 2014 in Vienna, Austria by Eric Demuth, Paul Klanschek and Christian Trummer, we’re here to help people trust themselves enough to build their financial freedom — for now and the future. Our user-friendly, trade-everything platform empowers both first-time investors and seasoned experts to invest in the cryptocurrencies, crypto indices, stocks*, precious metals and commodities* they want — with any sized budget, 24/7. Our global team works across different cultures and time zones, bringing our products to more than 7 million customers, making us one of Europe’s safest and most secure platforms that powers modern investing.

Headquartered in Austria but operating across Europe, our products are built by fast-moving, talented, “roll-up-your-sleeves-and-make-it-happen” kind of people. It’s these diverse perspectives and innovative minds operating as ONE TEAM that keep Bitpanda at the cutting edge of our industry. So if you’re someone who thinks big, moves fast and wants to make an impact right from day one, then get ready to join our industry-changing team. Let’s go!

Your mission

Run Bitpanda's paid acquisition hands-on and make experimentation the way we work. You will own the daily operation of our Meta, TikTok and Google campaigns across European markets, run a weekly creative-testing cadence with our in-house Studio, and prove what works with incrementality tests rather than platform dashboards. You join a performance team that has just restarted with a clear mandate and a measurement framework built on CAC and payback.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build, launch and optimise campaigns across paid social, app and search: account structure, bidding, audiences, pacing and budgets.
  • Run structured creative and landing-page tests with clear hypotheses, together with the Creative Studio.
  • Design and run incrementality and geo-lift tests; read results by cohort and customer quality.
  • Work with Growth Analytics and our MarTech Engineer to keep tracking and attribution right.
  • Report performance in our CAC and payback framework and turn learnings into the next experiment.

Кто вы

  • You have personally run paid social and app campaigns at more than €1m a month, in-house.
  • You have run incrementality or geo-lift tests and can say what they changed.
  • You judge performance by CAC, payback and customer quality, not by platform-reported conversions.
  • You have run a creative-testing cadence with a creative team and know how to brief for it.
  • Fintech, trading or gaming experience is a strong plus; hands-on depth is the bar.

  • Exclusive perks and rewards – Enjoy discounts, rewards, and perks from partners worldwide across lifestyle, wellness, tech, and travel.
  • Support during life milestones – Take advantage of up to 8 additional weeks of gender-neutral new parent leave to welcome and bond with your new addition to the family.
  • Fuel and focus on-site – Pandas in Vienna, Bucharest, Barcelona, and Berlin can enjoy free onsite dining, with freshly prepared lunches and snacks to keep you fueled and focused all day long.
  • Recognition for your contributions – Celebrate milestones and achievements with recognition and rewards for your Tenure at Bitpanda.
