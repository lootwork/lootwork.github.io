Кто мы

At OKX, we believe that the future will be reshaped by crypto, and ultimately contribute to every individual's freedom.

OKX is a leading crypto exchange, and the developer of OKX Wallet, giving millions access to crypto trading and decentralized crypto applications (dApps). OKX is also a trusted brand by hundreds of large institutions seeking access to crypto markets. We are safe and reliable, backed by our Proof of Reserves.

О Opportunity

This is an exciting opportunity to own and shape the end-to-end customer lifecycle strategy, driving activation, engagement, retention, and reactivation across key customer segments. The role combines strategic ownership with hands-on campaign execution, using data, experimentation, personalisation, and multi-channel CRM to identify and unlock growth opportunities. Working closely with Product, Data, Marketing, Compliance, Risk, and Customer Service, you’ll optimise customer journeys, improve campaign ROI, remove lifecycle friction, and build scalable growth programmes and processes in a fast-moving, regulated environment.

Что предстоит делать

Lifecycle Growth

  • Own the activation and retention strategy from registration and KYC through first deposit, first trade, repeat activity, dormancy, and reactivation.
  • Build segmented customer journeys across push, email, in-app messaging, SMS, incentives, and product experiences.
  • Develop onboarding, habit-building, churn-prevention, and win-back programmes for retail, high-value, dormant, and VIP segments.

Campaign Management

  • Own the lifecycle campaign calendar and manage campaigns end to end: business case, brief, audience, budget, approvals, QA, launch, reporting, and post-campaign review.
  • Coordinate CRM, product, content, creative, and promotional campaigns, ensuring clear timelines, ownership, and compliance with UAE requirements.
  • Manage incentive economics and campaign ROI, balancing customer value, commercial impact, and operational complexity.

Analytics and Operations

  • Use funnel, cohort, and behavioural analysis to identify drop-offs and prioritise the highest-impact opportunities.
  • Run hypothesis-led experiments across audience, message, timing, channel, offer, and product experience.
  • Partner with Product, Data, Marketing, Compliance, Risk, and Customer Service to remove lifecycle friction and translate insights into roadmap recommendations.
  • Build scalable playbooks, dashboards, reporting standards, and operating processes for lifecycle growth.

What We Look For In You

  • 4 - 8 years of experience in lifecycle marketing, CRM, growth, customer engagement, or campaign operations, with a measurable record of improving activation or retention.
  • Hands-on experience with CRM and journey tools such as Braze, CleverTap, Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Iterable, or equivalent.
  • Strong understanding of segmentation, lifecycle automation, experimentation, campaign measurement, and personalisation.
  • Analytical fluency across funnels, cohorts, dashboards, and campaign ROI; SQL experience is an advantage.
  • Strong commercial judgement, project management, and cross-functional stakeholder leadership.
  • Excellent English communication skills and comfort operating in a fast-moving, regulated environment.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience in crypto, fintech, payments, trading, marketplaces, or high-frequency consumer products.
  • Knowledge of the UAE customer and payments landscape; Arabic capability is an advantage.
