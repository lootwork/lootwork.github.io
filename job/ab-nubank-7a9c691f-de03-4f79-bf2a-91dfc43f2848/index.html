О команде

The Credit Risk Squad is part of the 2nd line of defense, responsible for managing and overseeing the credit risk function of our organization by providing independent oversight and challenge to the first line of defense. Within it, the Provisions team creates the Expected Credit Loss (ECL) models used to calculate loss allowances, participating end to end in model development, implementation, monitoring, data quality and support to Finance accounting teams. Our models feed Nubank's financial statements under IFRS 9 and local regulation, including CMN Resolution 4,966/2021 and BCB Resolution 352/2023 in Brazil.

О роли

As a Retail Credit Risk Senior Specialist focused on Brazil, you'll be the focal point for everything related to Res. 4966 expected credit loss provisioning. You'll proactively identify and control gaps in the models and in the processes built around them, from inputs and model implementation to closing and regulatory reporting. That means making sure models comply with regulatory expectations, produce the correct data for accounting and regulatory reports, and that ECL movements are understood, controlled and well explained.

You'll also be a primary point of contact for the local regulator (BCB) and the external auditors (KPMG) on the local models, working alongside the team's leadership. This is an individual-contributor role with high visibility among senior stakeholders in Finance, Risk and the local CRO's office.

YOU’LL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR

- Own the Res. 4966 ECL provisioning chain — be the team's focal point on the local models, monthly closing and the data they produce for accounting and regulatory reports.

- Identify and close gaps proactively — assess models and surrounding processes (inputs, implementation, closing, reporting) against regulatory expectations, and drive the plan to close them.

- Explain and control ECL movements — make sure monthly provision movements are understood, controlled and clearly explained to the Global Credit Director and the BR local CRO.

- Resolve BACEN demands and make them repeatable — answer ad-hoc regulatory requests promptly, and build the answers into standing processes and monitoring so the same question is never investigated twice.

- Be the face of the local models with BCB and KPMG — act as the named counterpart for regulator and auditor questions, and as backup to leadership in regulator meetings.

- Automate and scale with AI — automate well-established local processes (e.g., the monthly Highlights deck, closing steps, pipeline validation) and build an internal Res. 4966 context library so other teams can self-serve 4966 knowledge and analysis.
