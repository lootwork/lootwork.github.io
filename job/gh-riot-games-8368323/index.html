Gameplay Capture Artist II

At Riot Games, Gameplay Capture Artists combine their in-game mastery and filmmaking sensibilities to tell engaging stories within the virtual walls of Riot’s game engines. Where others see only the game, capture artists see opportunities to move the audience.

As a Gameplay Capture Artist II on the Marketing Creative Services team, you will report to the Gameplay Capture Manager. You will collaborate with product and publishing teams to identify the most compelling gameplay scenarios and visual storytelling opportunities. You will work with developers, creative leads, brand managers, producers, editors, and other gameplay capture artists, working to creative briefs while holding the line for the authenticity your audience expects. Your years of craft experience, collaborative spirit, and in-game skills allow you to calmly direct complex scenarios that demonstrate a game’s sky-high skill ceiling. Your passion for gaming and audience empathy will inform your work, resulting in compelling, player-focused videos for a passionate audience around the world.

In this role, you will contribute to the success of an upcoming R&D game from its early development through launch, and beyond. You will provide technical guidance on in-game camera systems, replay functionality, and other proprietary capture tools. You will uphold the bar for creative craft excellence within Riot and in vendor partnerships. As a leader within your craft, you will build long-lasting relationships with your collaborators and other capture artists will look to you for advice and perspective. You can be relied upon to proactively call out potential pitfalls that could occur in a project to avoid and/or preempt them before they arise.

Обязанности

  • Create detailed shot lists, storyboards, and capture plans aligned with marketing campaigns and creative briefs
  • Capture high-quality in-game content to create imagery which demonstrates the high level gameplay fantasy, design intention, and technical assets of each product
  • Direct capture artists through complex gameplay scenarios
  • Work closely with product team partners to QA and troubleshoot technical issues related to gameplay capture content
  • Collaborate with your colleagues in the gameplay capture discipline to improve your craft and the technologies that power it
  • Lead and mentor your colleagues in the gameplay capture craft
  • Provide your gameplay expertise to other marketing efforts to help us make all our content authentic to the game and resonant with players
  • Develop your familiarity with game engines to find new ways to achieve creative experiences
  • Manage a vendor relationship directly, serving as a subject matter expert for all craft related needs
  • Assist in the onboarding and mentorship of more junior teammates
  • Step in and function as a creative director when necessary, raising the quality bar of the production
  • Support in tactic allocation and resourcing

Required Qualifications

  • 4+ years experience playing Riot Games products and similar games
  • 3+ years experience creating gameplay video content as an independent content creator or for a studio, company, or channel
  • 3+ years experience with first and third person cameras, familiar with standard camera directions, tools, and techniques
  • 3+ years experience with capture software and hardware such as Fraps, OBS, Shogun Inferno, ElGato
  • 2+ years experience managing and organizing files and media
  • 1+ years experience working within Unreal Engine
  • Film, photography, or arts background

Desired Qualifications

  • Experience leading a team of capture artists.
  • Experience working at a game studio or creative agency
  • Experience in 3D animation, previsualization, layout, or storyboarding
  • Experience analyzing game content and traditional filmmaking

For this role, you'll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players' experiences, this could be your role!
