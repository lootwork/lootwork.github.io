Would you like to start your career in engineering while also learning more about business? If so, our QA Internship might be the perfect fit for you.

We've designed this three-month-long internship to introduce you to one of the most exciting engineering roles!

At Nordeus, QA Engineers aren't the last stop before release - they're in the room from day one. They bring deep software expertise, pair it with business context, and untangle tricky problems across a genuinely complex system. Testing the final product isn't only on them - the whole team tests - but by the time it ships, QA has already shaped how it got there.

You won't just have a dedicated mentor - you'll be part of a real team, working together on a live game played by millions. From day one, you'll contribute hands-on to delivering a top-tier experience to players around the world!

Ready to take the first steps in your career? Apply now!

Что предстоит делать

  • Learn and apply QA principles across different challenges on a real product with a large user base
  • Participate in developing testing tools
  • Design, manage, and execute test cases and report on defects
  • Implement test automation solutions to speed up manual testing
  • Get hands-on exposure to functional, regression, and performance testing in a live game
  • Collaborate with a team of diverse professionals to shape testing strategies across every stage of game development
  • Monitor the health of live products by detecting and investigating potential issues
  • Help create a better user experience by tackling user-related problems, cheats, exploits, and bugs
  • Gain hands-on experience with how AI is used in practice to shape the future of software and game QA

С кем предстоит работать

  • You'll work cross-functionally with QA Engineers, Backend Engineers, Client Engineers, Product Managers, and Game Designers to help test, validate, and improve game features used by millions of players.

Мы подойдём друг другу, если вы

  • Are in the final years of your studies (3rd or 4th) in Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, or a related field
  • Have knowledge of object-oriented programming, algorithms, and data structures
  • Are enthusiastic about games and game-making, not just software development
  • Have a genuine passion for becoming an extraordinary engineer and for continuous learning and expanding your knowledge across different fields
  • Can communicate well with both technical and non-technical peers

Дополнительный плюс

  • Understanding of software design principles and patterns, and experience using AI tools to assist with coding or problem-solving

Почему у нас хорошо

  • Paid internship with benefits: Full-time internship with flexibility around your faculty obligations.
  • The team behind the game: transparency and trust from day one, paired with a strong sense of teamwork- that's the essence of who we are. It's not just what you do - it’s how you do it and who you do it with. With 280+ teammates from around the world, we’re on a mission to bring the joy of winning to millions.
  • We make your life at Nordeus hassle-free: enjoy Nordeus provided breakfast, lunch, snacks, and beverages, a fully-equipped gym, organized sports activities (yoga, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, basketball, football), an ergonomic workstation, top-notch tech equipment, a kids’ playroom, a music corner, board and video games + latest consoles.
  • Make your mark on games played by millions: with over 300M registered users, Top Eleven is the world’s most successful football management game. Golf Rival, with more than 60M registered users, is the #2 mobile golf game, aiming to claim the top spot. With one more game in the works, we are continuing our efforts to create powerful mobile sports gaming experiences built to last.
  • Meaningful career experience: work with experienced game makers and mentors who will support you from day one, helping you map a career path that's true to you.
  • Impact beyond the screen: It is not just about us. We are part of something bigger than a job. We create a better future for generations to come through the efforts of the Nordeus Foundation.
