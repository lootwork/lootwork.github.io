Coinbase's Financial Services & Loyalty team builds the products that make Coinbase the best place to deposit, hold, and grow your money. Our Yield & Investments team makes onchain lending, borrowing, and earning accessible to everyday customers. We're looking for a Staff Product Designer to own design strategy and lead hands-on execution across these experiences and their shared onchain foundations. This is an individual contributor role: you'll shape product direction alongside Product and Engineering leaders, lead through influence, and turn complex financial mechanics into clear, trustworthy experiences.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the design vision and strategy for onchain Yield & Investments, connecting lending, borrowing, earning, and shared foundations into one cohesive customer experience.
  • Lead ambiguous, zero-to-one initiatives from customer insight through prototypes, polished interaction design, and launch.
  • Partner with Product and Engineering leaders to define priorities, shape roadmaps, and align teams around a long-term direction and achievable milestones.
  • Make yield, collateral, liquidity, and liquidation risk understandable so customers can manage their positions with confidence.
  • Set the quality bar across mobile and web, strengthen reusable patterns, and elevate other designers through critique and mentorship, without people-management responsibilities.
  • Use research, data, and experimentation to improve customer outcomes, and communicate decisions and tradeoffs clearly in cross-functional and executive reviews.

Required Skills and Experience

  • 6+ years of product design experience, with a portfolio of complex consumer products shipped at scale and evidence of strategic impact as an individual contributor.
  • Experience designing crypto, DeFi, lending, or investment products, with a practical understanding of onchain mechanics and financial risk.
  • A track record of shaping strategy with senior Product and Engineering partners, influencing without authority, and navigating ambiguity and regulatory constraints.
  • Strong systems thinking, interaction design, visual craft, and prototyping skills, with the ability to move between product vision and detailed execution.
  • Experience using customer research and product data to evaluate tradeoffs and improve comprehension, adoption, and retention.
  • A responsible approach to generative AI, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.
