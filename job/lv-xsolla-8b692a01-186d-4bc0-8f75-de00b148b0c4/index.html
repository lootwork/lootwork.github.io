О нас

Xsolla is a global commerce company with robust tools and services to help developers solve the inherent challenges of the video game industry. From indie to AAA, companies partner with Xsolla to help them fund, distribute, market, and monetize their games. Grounded in the belief in the future of video games, Xsolla is resolute in the mission to bring opportunities together, and continually make new resources available to creators. Headquartered and incorporated in Los Angeles, California, Xsolla operates as the merchant of record and has helped over 1,500+ game developers to reach more players and grow their businesses around the world. With more paths to profits and ways to win, developers have all the things needed to enjoy the game.

For more information, visit xsolla.com.

О вас

We are looking for an Investment Analyst to join Xsolla Video Games Credit Fund.

You’ll work across the full investment process, from sourcing and evaluating game studios to

financial modeling, due diligence, deal execution and portfolio monitoring.

The role offers direct exposure to game developers and publishers across the World, giving

you a behind-the-scenes look at how games are developed, financed and brought to market.

You’ll work closely with the investment team on real transactions from day one, develop

expertise at the intersection of gaming and finance, and have plenty of room to grow as the

fund expands.

Обязанности

  • Evaluate investment opportunities in PC and console gaming, including studios,

individual titles and business models;

  • Build financial models, forecast game revenues and cash flows, and analyze different

financing scenarios;

  • Conduct market research, analyze comparable games and assess audience traction

using Steam and other industry data;

  • Review financial statements, production budgets, business plans and publishing

agreements;

  • Support due diligence, deal structuring and preparation of investment committee

materials;

  • Monitor portfolio performance and prepare regular investment updates;
  • Help source new opportunities and maintain relationships with developers, publishers

and other industry participants.

Требования

  • 2-3 years of experience in corporate finance, venture capital, private credit or a

similar role;

  • Strong financial modeling skills and excellent Excel proficiency;
  • Solid understanding of financial statements and investment analysis;
  • Strong analytical thinking and attention to detail;
  • Ability to work independently, manage multiple tasks and communicate findings

clearly;

  • Strong command of English (B2 level or higher);
  • Genuine interest in video games and the gaming industry.

Prior gaming industry experience is not required. Strong analytical skills and a genuine

interest in games matter more.
