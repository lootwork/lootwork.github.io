Scopely is looking for a Senior 2D Marketing Artist to join our MONOPOLY GO! team in Barcelona on a hybrid basis.

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues, or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing and innovating the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people around the world daily.

MONOPOLY GO! is a global phenomenon and the biggest mobile game launch in history, captivating millions of players worldwide. The team is based in Europe and the US and works daily to create captivating new experiences for our players.

In this role, you’ll use illustration, composition, typography and design to connect MONOPOLY GO! with players across marketing and community channels. You’ll bring ideas from concept to final delivery, combining strong craft with rapid iteration and repeatable AI-assisted workflows that improve quality, consistency and efficiency.

Что предстоит делать

  • Deliver high-quality 2D concepts and production art that reflect MONOPOLY GO!’s established style across social posts, Discord content, email headers, push assets, challenges, infographics and event communications
  • Adapt existing art, including characters, stickers, pop-ups and backgrounds, into multiple formats while maintaining strong composition, visual hierarchy and readability
  • Develop, document and share repeatable workflows using templates, AI-assisted processes and practical automation, evaluating emerging tools against real production needs
  • Research and apply visual references to develop ideas for ambiguous or open-ended briefs and identify opportunities to improve the 2D production pipeline
  • Create original lettering and logos for campaigns, events and community initiatives
  • Own work from brief to final delivery by clarifying requirements, estimating scope, managing priorities and flagging risks early
  • Give clear, specific and actionable feedback to internal and outsourced artists, using references or annotations to maintain the expected style and quality
  • Partner with Art Production, Producers, Marketing and other teams to align briefs, priorities and deadlines, ensuring creative work supports campaign goals and business objectives

Кого мы ищем

  • 6+ years of experience as a 2D or concept artist in games or games marketing, including 2+ years in a senior-level capacity
  • Experience developing and applying repeatable AI-assisted workflows in production, with the judgment to evaluate where they improve quality, consistency or efficiency
  • A track record of delivering high-quality 2D work on schedule while collaborating with Design, Engineering and outsourcing partners
  • Advanced illustration and concept development skills, with a strong command of composition, form, color, typography and visual storytelling
  • Advanced proficiency in Photoshop and the ability to research, synthesize and apply visual references to develop compelling creative ideas
  • Experience designing logos and lettering for campaigns, events and player-facing initiatives, maintaining a consistent visual identity across channels
  • Ability to communicate clearly with non-art stakeholders, clarify or challenge unclear briefs, negotiate priorities, and give and apply constructive feedback

Дополнительный плюс

  • Experience working within the brand guidelines and approval processes of an externally licensed IP
  • Experience helping outsourcing partners meet established quality standards
  • Strong 3D modeling skills in Maya, Blender or equivalent tools to support AI-assisted concept-to-3D workflows
  • Experience in a creative studio, production company or advertising environment

Please include a portfolio or PDF/links to examples of your work in the application.

О Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company's "World's Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.…
