We're hiring a Senior Product Creative Designer to join the Product Creative team within the Consumer & Business design organization. Product Creative brings visual design, motion, and illustration together so Coinbase's brand language holds from the first marketing touchpoint to the deepest in-app experience. You'll drive creative work inside the Coinbase app, balancing ambition with feasibility, and turn big ideas into shipped experiences millions of customers see and feel.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own visual design and storytelling across the Coinbase consumer product, from concept through final craft
  • Partner with Product Designers to build delight and emotional connection into core product surfaces
  • Shape creative direction for high-impact product launches, holding the bar on visual craft and narrative
  • Evolve the Coinbase design language, covering icons, color, and UI components with Product Design
  • Direct motion design and illustration work with internal and external creative partners
  • Champion design excellence across teams, raising the quality bar through critique and shared standards

Required Skills and Experience

  • 5+ years of visual design experience shipping consumer product work, with a portfolio spanning multiple product surfaces
  • Deep command of typography, layout, color, and composition, evidenced by shipped work at scale
  • Track record of directing motion design or illustration work from concept to delivery
  • Demonstrated success aligning Product, Engineering, and Marketing stakeholders on creative direction
  • Fluency in Figma, Adobe Creative Suite, and prototyping tools
  • Experience presenting creative strategy to senior leaders and building support across functions
  • Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.
