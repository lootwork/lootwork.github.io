Looking for a way to kickstart your career in mobile game development? Our Junior QA Intern position is the perfect opportunity for you! As a Junior QA Intern, you'll learn the ropes of game testing and quality assurance from experienced professionals. You'll get hands-on experience with industry-standard tools, develop and execute test plans, and learn how to report and track bugs. The ideal candidate should have a basic understanding of software development, and be currently enrolled in a degree program in Computer Science, Game Design, or a related field. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, as well as excellent communication skills, are a must.

This role will report to the Head of Production. It’s a full-time position located in our office in Cracow, Poland.

As a Junior QA Intern, you will have the opportunity to work closely with our experienced team to ensure the quality and functionality of our games.

  • Assist in testing new features and functionalities of our games
  • Identify and report bugs and issues
  • Collaborate with developers and designers to improve game quality
  • Learn and apply testing methodologies and best practices
  • Contribute to the overall success of our game development process

If you are passionate about gaming, have a keen eye for detail, and are eager to learn and grow in the field of QA testing, we would love to hear from you. This internship will provide valuable hands-on experience and the opportunity to work on exciting projects in the gaming industry.

Basic Responsibilities

  • Perform Black Box testing
  • Analyzing specifications
  • Implementing and executing test plans
  • Report bugs
