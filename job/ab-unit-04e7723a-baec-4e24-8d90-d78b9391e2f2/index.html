О UNIT

Money is fundamental to how people and businesses operate, but it often sits outside the software they use every day. Unit changes that. Unit is a financial infrastructure platform that enables technology companies to store, move, spend, and lend money inside their product.

Unit processes over $100B in annual transaction volume, serves 2M+ accounts, and handles 11M+ API calls daily for category-defining companies including Wix, Highbeam, Wayflyer and Relay.

Today, leading technology companies use Unit to create smarter money experiences, deepen customer relationships, and unlock new revenue. We’re building the infrastructure that will make financial products a native part of software, and we’re looking for people who want to help shape what comes next.

We’ve raised $160m+ from top investors including Insight Partners and Accel, and 60+ angels. We are proud to be recognized on CNBC World's Top Fintech Companies. https://www.cnbc.com/worlds-top-fintech-companies-2026/

О роли

As a Senior Backend Engineer, you'll be a key owner of the product and building the infrastructure behind it. You'll work closely with product managers, designers, and other engineers to shape features from idea to production, building the financial primitives that power businesses. This is a hands-on role for engineers who care deeply about what they build and why they build it.

For the right candidate, it's a rare chance to leave a lasting mark on an industry-defining product.

Основные обязанности

- Own features end-to-end: from product design and API contracts through implementation, testing, and deployment.

- Collaborate directly with product and design to define requirements, push back when needed, and help make smart tradeoffs between speed, quality, and scope.

- Build reliable, well-crafted backend services that power Unit's Banking, Payments, and Cards products.

- Write APIs and backend logic that developers love to use: with a focus on clarity, correctness, and a great developer experience.

- Contribute to architectural decisions and help raise the engineering bar across the team.

Кого мы ищем

- 5+ years of backend development experience, ideally in a product-driven environment where you've owned features

- A product mindset: you ask "why are we building this?" and you care about the customer experience, not just the technical implementation.

- Comfort working across the full backend lifecycle: design, implementation, testing, deployment, and on-call ownership.

- Experience building and maintaining reliable systems in cloud environments (AWS, GCP, or Azure).

- Solid understanding of distributed systems concepts: consistency, availability, failure modes, and observability.

- Experience with CI/CD and modern deployment practices.

- Fintech, payments, or developer-platform experience is a strong plus.

- Production experience with Kubernetes — advantage.

- Experience with Scala, ZIO, Cats, or functional programming — advantage.

- Familiarity with DDD, Event Sourcing, or CQRS — advantage.
