Jam City is on the hunt for an experienced Growth Marketing Manager, located at our Los Angeles office, to work on several of Jam City’s top-grossing mobile games. The perfect candidate will drive Jam City’s mobile marketing growth by leveraging data-driven UA, creative, and ASO strategies.

We are looking for a high-impact marketer who can collaborate with cross-functional teams, analyze the competitive landscape, optimize both organic and paid conversion funnels, and think forward with machine learning and AI across the complete user lifecycle. If you are a talented growth marketer with a passion for creative innovation, search visibility, and games, we want to hear from you.

Обязанности

  • Budget & Campaign Management: Manage significant marketing budgets to maximize Jam City’s growth, efficiency, and business profitability across social channels, ad networks, and DSPs.
  • Creative Strategy Ownership: Own the end-to-end UA creative strategy. Build performance-driven creative roadmaps, brief production teams, and relentlessly test new formats to improve conversion rates and lower CPIs.
  • ASO Strategy Ownership: Define and execute App Store Optimization (ASO) strategies. Lead organic growth initiatives, including keyword optimization, A/B testing store assets (icons, screenshots, videos), and aligning ASO with paid UA hooks to maximize store conversion.
  • Performance Optimization: Optimize channel, creative, and store performance, championing KPIs critical to the business: ROAS, LTV, ARPU, and organic multiplier.
  • Partner & Channel Expansion: Manage advertising and ASO vendor partnerships. Seek, test, and evaluate new acquisition channels and optimization tools based on competitive and market intelligence.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with studios, creative services, and lifecycle marketing teams to bring together a holistic approach to growth.

Требования

  • 3–5 years of experience in mobile user acquisition, performance marketing, or growth marketing.
  • Proven track record of owning or heavily influencing creative strategy and ASO (App Store Optimization) for mobile apps or games.
  • Deep analytical mindset with strong data-driven decision-making skills; ability to turn creative and ASO test results into actionable growth strategies.
  • Hands-on experience running campaigns on major networks (e.g., Meta Ads, Google AC, AppLovin) and using ASO tools (e.g. AppTweak).
  • Advanced Data Skills: High proficiency in Excel or Google Sheets for data analysis. Knowledge of SQL, Databricks, and Tableau is a major plus.
  • Experience working in the social/mobile game industry is highly preferred.
  • on-site 5 days a week
  • BA/BS Degree in Marketing, Finance, Economics, or an equivalent quantitative fields

Будет плюсом

  • knowledge and passion for mobile games!
