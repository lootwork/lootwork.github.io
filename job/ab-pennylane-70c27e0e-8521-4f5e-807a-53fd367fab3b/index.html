WHY this position is of utmost importance to reach our mission

Autopilot is Pennylane’s AI add-on for accounting firms, designed to automate bookkeeping and move accountants from execution to supervision—with a clear ambition: a 1-click VAT return.

The challenge is no longer only to build a powerful product, but to activate it at scale, prove its value and turn adoption into revenue.

As the first PM of the new Bookkeeping / Autopilot Growth squad, you will play a central role in making that happen. You will help hundreds of thousands of companies adopt Autopilot, connect firms to the value it already delivers and shape Pennylane’s AI monetization strategy.

HOW you will contribute to the company as a Product Manager

As a Senior Product Manager, you will join a 100+ person Product organization, working with 300+ engineers to make Autopilot the default way firms run their bookkeeping.

Вам предстоит

- Understand what drives adoption. Talk to firm owners and accountants, analyse product data with SQL, Amplitude and Metabase, and identify where firms hesitate to activate Autopilot.

- Turn insights into measurable growth. Define clear hypotheses, metrics and stop criteria, then run B2B experiments through holdouts, cohort analysis and staged rollouts.

- Own the growth strategy. Set your OKRs, shape the roadmap and focus the squad on the initiatives that drive activation, trust, usage and revenue.

- Build with a cross-functional squad. Partner closely with a Product Designer, Engineering Manager and product-focused engineers, starting with MVPs and iterating based on what you learn.

- Work across the ecosystem. Collaborate with the squads building Bookkeeping and Autopilot, as well as Data, Product Marketing, Pricing, Sales, Customer Success and Account Management.

- Scale adoption beyond the product. Design in-product and lifecycle experiences that help firms self-serve, while providing commercial teams with the signals they need to support adoption.

- Build for France and Germany from day one. Design configurable solutions rather than simple translations, and work with the German team to make what works in France scalable internationally.

- Help define a new way of building product. Work agentic-first, design for supervision and stay relentlessly user-obsessed—using AI tools and agents every day to explore data, prototype and move faster.

WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT FROM YOUR LIFE AT PENNYLANE

WITHIN ONE MONTH

- You'll learn everything about our company, our teams, and our vision during the first onboarding week

- You'll familiarize yourself with the product, the accounting industry and the accountant channel (you will have access to a lot of documentation and will attend trainings organized by your peers)

- You'll understand how Autopilot works: what it automates, how confident it is, and where it still needs a human

- You'll have mapped the Autopilot adoption funnel, at company and firm level: eligible, enabled, expanded, paying

WITHIN 3 MONTHS

- You'll have set the adoption baseline and targets with your manager

- You'll have shipped and read your first experiments on activation, hand in hand with your Product Designer and Engineers

- You'll have impacted positively at least one OKR

- You will have set up your continuous discovery routine with firms and be ready to build your roadmap

WITHIN 6 MONTHS

- You'll be autonomous at defining your OKRs and your roadmap

- You'll have shipped at least one step change in how firms activate Autopilot

- You'll have made the time Autopilot saves visible to firm owners…
