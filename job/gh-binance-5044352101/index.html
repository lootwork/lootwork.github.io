О роли

We are looking for a senior compliance leader to take ownership of compliance for our Japan business.

The role covers both the regulatory relationship and the build behind it — policies, procedures and controls, AML/CFT oversight, and the remediation that follows an examination. It is hands-on, and the credibility that matters here comes from having personally handled regulator conversations, examinations and remediation programs.

Что предстоит делать

  • Regulatory Engagement & Examinations — Liaise with the JFSA and other relevant Japanese regulators; manage regulatory communications, enquiries and requests, and run examinations, inspections, audits and compliance reviews through to timely remediation and corrective action.
  • Regulatory Compliance & Reporting — Ensure business operations comply with applicable Japanese laws, regulations and guidelines, including requirements for crypto-asset exchange service providers; coordinate regulatory filings, periodic reporting, compliance assessments and responses to regulatory requests.
  • Compliance Framework — Develop, implement and continuously enhance compliance policies, procedures, internal controls and governance frameworks in line with local regulatory expectations.
  • AML/CFT Oversight — Oversee AML/CFT programmes covering customer due diligence, transaction monitoring, suspicious transaction reporting and sanctions-related controls, in coordination with the relevant compliance teams.
  • Business Advisory — Partner with senior management and cross-functional teams (Legal, Risk, Operations, Product) to assess the regulatory implications of business activities, new products and market initiatives.
  • Compliance Monitoring — Conduct ongoing compliance risk assessments and monitoring to identify regulatory gaps and proactively mitigate compliance risk.
  • Governance & Training — Promote a strong compliance culture through internal training, stakeholder engagement and regular reporting to senior management.

Кого мы ищем

  • Minimally 6-8 years of compliance experience within financial services, banking, securities, fintech or cryptocurrency/digital asset businesses. Preferred legal degree and practicing license.
  • Strong understanding of Japan's financial regulatory framework, particularly JFSA requirements and regulations governing crypto-asset exchange service providers.
  • Previous experience engaging directly with the JFSA or other Japanese financial regulators, including regulatory examinations, reporting and remediation activities.
  • Demonstrated experience developing and overseeing compliance frameworks, policies, internal controls and regulatory governance.
  • Strong knowledge of AML/CFT requirements, transaction monitoring, customer due diligence and financial crime controls.
  • Experience managing regulatory findings, compliance gaps and remediation programmes.
  • Ability to work effectively with senior stakeholders and global compliance teams in an international organisation.
  • Professional fluency in Japanese and strong English communication skills.
