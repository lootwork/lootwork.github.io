Riot Games was established in 2006 by entrepreneurial gamers who believe that player-focused game development can result in great games. In 2009, Riot released its debut title League of Legends to critical and player acclaim. As the most played video game in the world, over 100 million play every month. Players form the foundation of our community and it’s for them that we continue to evolve and improve the League of Legends experience.

We’re looking for humble but ambitious, razor-sharp professionals who can teach us a thing or two. We promise to return the favor. Like us, you take play seriously; you’re passionate about games. We embrace those who see things differently, aren’t afraid to experiment, and who have a healthy disregard for constraints.

That's where you come in.

О роли

As a Game Producer on the Wild Rift Gameplay team, you will lead the production and delivery of gameplay initiatives, partnering with cross-functional teams to turn product goals into high-quality player experiences. You will establish clear plans, manage dependencies, identify and mitigate delivery risks, and guide work through development, launch, and post-launch evaluation.

You will collaborate with Product Owners, Game Designers, Insights, Creative, Publishing, and external development partners to align priorities, coordinate execution, and make informed tradeoffs across scope, quality, timelines, and team capacity. Beyond managing delivery, you will continuously improve the processes and operating practices that help teams plan effectively, make decisions, and deliver sustainable results.

You will report into the Director, Game Production of the team.

Обязанности

  • Own end-to-end production and delivery for gameplay initiatives, from requirements and strategic alignment through design, development, testing, launch, and post-launch evaluation.
  • Translate product and gameplay priorities into actionable delivery plans with clear scope, milestones, ownership, dependencies, decision points, and launch criteria, aligning commitments with team capacity.
  • Establish and facilitate effective production practices, including project planning, milestone reviews, risk assessments, launch readiness reviews, and retrospectives.
  • Proactively identify and manage delivery risks, blockers, dependencies, and unresolved decisions; drive mitigation and escalation while maintaining clear visibility into progress, risks, decisions, and delivery health for team leadership and stakeholders.
  • Partner with Product Owners, Game Designers, and cross-functional teams to align product intent, design priorities, implementation requirements, acceptance criteria, and delivery plans.
  • Coordinate execution across Riot teams, external development partners, disciplines, and regions, establishing clear ownership, communication paths, and accountability.
  • Evaluate and manage tradeoffs across scope, schedule, quality, resources, and team sustainability; recommend phased delivery, contingency plans, or adjustments when needed.
  • Continuously improve development workflows, production processes, and team operating practices using delivery metrics, team feedback, and post-launch learnings to improve predictability, effectiveness, and sustainable delivery.

Required Qualifications

  • 5+ years of experience in game development, game production, program management, project management, or a related field, including experience leading delivery across multiple stakeholders.
  • Experience applying project management fundamentals, including scope, scheduling, cost, quality, risk, dependency management, and launch readiness.
  • Experience selecting and applying appropriate planning tools, methodologies, workflows, and project management practices based on team and project needs.
  • Experience maintaining and improving production processes, team operating practices, and project management frameworks.
  • Experience navigating ambiguity, identifying and mitigating delivery risks, resolving blockers, and establishing clear plans and processes for complex initiatives.
  • Experience communicating project status, facilitating cross-functional alignment, resolving stakeholder dependencies, and influencing decisions across teams without direct authority.

Desired Qualifications

  • Experience managing production and delivery for a complete gameplay module or player-facing gameplay system, ideally in a live-service, mobile, multiplayer, or frequently updated game.
  • Experience collaborating with co-development or external development partners across companies or regions; professional working proficiency in both Chinese and English is a plus.
  • Experience using delivery metrics, stakeholder feedback, and post-launch outcomes to improve production processes, planning effectiveness, and team performance.

岗位简介

作为 Wild Rift Gameplay 团队的 Game Producer，你将负责玩法相关项目的生产与交付，推动跨职能团队将产品目标转化为高质量的玩家体验。你将建立清晰的项目计划，管理跨团队依赖，主动识别并降低交付风险，推动项目顺利完成开发、上线及上线后评估的完整周期。

你将与 Product Owners、Game Designers、Insights、Creative、Publishing 及外部研发合作方紧密协作，对齐项目优先级、协调执行，并在范围、质量、时间和团队产能之间做出合理取舍。除了推动项目交付，你还需要持续优化团队的生产流程和运行机制，帮助团队更有效地规划、协作、决策，并实现高效、可持续的交付。

你将向团队的 Director, Game Production 汇报。

主要职责

  • 负责玩法相关项目从需求与策略对齐、设计、开发、测试、上线到上线后评估的端到端生产与交付。
  • 将产品和玩法优先级转化为可执行的交付计划，明确项目范围、里程碑、负责人、依赖关系、决策节点及上线标准，并结合团队产能合理安排交付承诺。
  • 建立并推动有效的生产管理机制，包括项目规划、里程碑评审、风险评估、上线准备评审及项目复盘。
  • 主动识别和管理交付风险、阻塞、依赖关系及未决事项，推动风险缓解和问题升级，并向团队管理者及利益相关方清晰同步项目进展、风险、决策和交付健康度。
  • 与 Product Owners、Game Designers 及跨职能团队合作，确保产品目标、设计优先级、实现需求、验收标准和交付计划保持一致。
  • 协调 Riot 内部团队、外部研发合作方及跨专业、跨地域团队之间的项目执行，建立清晰的责任分工、沟通机制和交付责任。
  • 评估并管理项目范围、进度、质量、资源及团队可持续性之间的取舍，并根据需要提出分阶段交付、备用计划或项目调整方案。
  • 结合交付指标、团队反馈和上线后复盘结果，持续优化开发工作流、生产流程及团队运行机制，提升交付可预测性、团队效能和可持续交付能力。
