Job Description

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON​

What you will focus on

This role exists because reactive execution is no longer enough. The OTT programming division needs a technical authority who can shift the team's positioning from "a team that delivers what it is asked to build" to "a technical partners that others actively seek out because of the value it brings to a game's success." That shift requires someone who can set a long-term technical vision, build scalable production ecosystems, and drive focused R&D including applied AI in game development, while simultaneously ensuring flawless deliveries across multiple live and co-development projects.

What it means on a daily basis​

  • Monitor the technical health of multiple projects, working closely with technical leads to surface risks early, prevent bottlenecks, and keep deliveries on track.
  • Define and enforce studio-wide coding standards, architectural patterns, and engineering workflows, and drive their adoption across programming teams.
  • Lead high-impact R&D initiatives, with particular focus on AI applied to game development, and ensure successful integration of outcomes into production pipelines.
  • Provide hands-on technical guidance and mentoring to programmers, conducting periodic competency assessments and building clear development paths.
  • Act as the primary technical counterpart for co-development partner studios, aligning on architecture, tooling, style guides, and Unreal toolchain standardization across shared pipelines.
  • Collaborate cross-functionally with Game Design, Art, and Production to improve how programming integrates with other disciplines and contributes to overall product quality.
  • Strategic resource planning and project allocation in partnership with the Studio Technical Director.

What success will look like​

Within your first year, you will have made a visible and assessable impact on at least one co-development project or on a strategic technical mission defined in alignment with the studio's long-term vision. That impact will be confirmed through calibration with multiple stakeholders across the organization, not through self-assessment alone.

Who you would be working with​

  • Report to the Studio Technical Director.
  • Operate accross a programming department of around 35 to 40 people, spanning multiple seniority levels and allocated to live service and co-development projects.
  • Collaborate closely with Department Heads and Team Leads across Game Design, Art, and Production, as well as Studio Directors and the Studio Manager.
  • Work directly with Technical Managers and Technical Directors from co-development partner studios.

Your recruitment journey

  • Screen Call with a Recruiter
  • First Interview with the Studio Technical Director and Division Principal/Manager
  • Second Interview with Studio Manager and HR Director
  • Final interview with a Technical Director from a sister studio

Where this role can take you

If the opportunity arises and the qualifications are in place, this position is a direct pathway to the Studio Technical Director role. That outcome is not guaranteed, but it is a realistic evolution for the right person.

Требования

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED​

  • Proven hands-on expertise in game development across multiple genres and platforms, including mobile, PC, and console.
  • High proficiency in Unreal Engine 5 and programming languages such as C++ and C#.
  • Demonstrated ability to design and lead large-scale technical systems, covering areas such as 3D rendering, AI gameplay systems, online and network scalability, and cloud infrastructure.
  • A track record of leading R&D initiatives from exploration through to production integration.
  • Demonstrated leadership of multi-disciplinary programming teams, including identifying skill gaps, building development plans, and raising the collective output quality of a department rather than just individual projects.
  • The ability to communicate technical direction clearly to senior leadership, partner studio counterparts, and non-technical collaborators, and to influence decisions at a strategic level while maintaining alignment across stakeholders.
  • Practical experience deploying AI and LLM-based tools in a production context.

Кто вы

  • Think in systems and long-term consequences, not just immediate fixes.
  • Proactive technical authority who anticipates what teams and partners will need before they ask.
  • Invested in developing others: raising the technical floor of a department matters as much as personal output.
  • Hold grounds in complex stakeholder environments, with clear, inclusive communication.
  • Hands-on by choice on the most strategic and complex challenges, not only in meetings.
  • Balances technical rigor with product thinking: treats quality, security, and compliance as conditions for sustainable creativity.
  • Steady and focused when priorities shift or organizational context changes.
