Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy.

The Organic Growth team is responsible for guiding and supporting restaurant and retail operators from their first question to their final decision – establishing Toast as the trusted authority through high-quality, organic content that turns discovery into opportunity.

You'll own the outreach and off-site authority-building work that sits between our content production and its reach – building the backlink, citation, and third-party mention profile that both search engines and AI systems already trust. You'll partner closely with your Organic Growth team members and cross-functional stakeholders to make sure what we publish earns a place in the conversations that matter, including how AI systems are increasingly answering questions about the food and hospitality industry.

This is a high-output role for a writer-outreacher who is also a clear thinker: someone who can draft a compelling guest post as comfortably as they can identify the right target and angle for it, treats writing and distribution as one connected job rather than two separate hand-offs, sees every new tool or article as a fresh outreach opportunity, continues to search for opportunities in external mentions.

A day in the life (Responsibilities)

  • Pitch relevant blogs and industry publications to earn coverage and backlinks organically, closing our referring-domains gap versus competitors.
  • Own outreach and ongoing relationship management with third-party review sites for inclusion and improved placement knowing LLM citations correlate heavily with off-site authority and structured mentions.
  • Write and place guest posts on relevant third-party sites — owning the piece end to end, from drafting to securing the placement — and source and execute content opportunities with cross-functional teams.
  • Run structured backlink outreach campaigns targeting our highest-value assets — free tools, On the Line and Data by Toast articles, compare pages, and ungated resources.
  • Systematically close competitive link and citation gaps by targeting the backlink and citation sources competitors hold that we don't.
  • Monitor the health of our backlink profile — tracking toxic links, lost links, and anchor text distribution, and flagging disavow or removal actions when needed.
  • Maintain an organized, up-to-date outreach and link-building tracker (source, target URL, link type, status) so pipeline visibility doesn't live in any one person's inbox.
  • Contribute to Organic Growth's Reddit strategy and engagement — monitoring threads for citation-worthy questions and working cross-functionally to build genuine community presence rather than link-dropping.
  • Keep an outreach pipeline and tracker moving continuously; when a new asset launches, you're the reason its reach doesn't stop at publish.
  • Collaborate with partners in Brand Content & Authority, PR, and Product to source material, identify link and citation gaps, and turn outreach targets into secured placements.
  • Set the vision for new outreach channels and partnership types — spotting where an asset or competitive gap can be extended next, and building the case for it.
  • Embrace AI tools to accelerate your workflow — target research, pitch drafting, and gap analysis — building faster and with higher quality.

What you'll need to thrive (Requirements)

  • 4+ years of experience in SEO or content marketing with a track record of building backlinks and earning organic coverage
  • Strong writing skills – comfortable drafting guest posts and pitches yourself, not just securing placements for content written by others
  • Experience running outbound outreach campaigns at scale
  • Familiarity with SEO/AEO/GEO tooling (Ahrefs or similar, Profound, Google Search Console, etc.) to identify link/citation gaps and prioritize outreach targets
  • Comfortable using AI and LLM-based tools in your workflow as well as understanding how AI/LLM citation ecosystems work
  • Strong written communication for pitches and outreach
  • Strong project management instincts – you can track multiple outreach workstreams and keep things moving without constant check-ins

Nice to Have's

  • Experience with Reddit or community-led growth strategies
  • B2B SaaS or restaurant/hospitality industry marketing background
  • A track record of closing competitive backlink or citation gaps with measurable results
