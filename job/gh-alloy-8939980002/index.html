О команде

The Data Partner Integrations team builds and runs the connections between Alloy and the third-party data partners our clients rely on to verify identity and stop fraud. Every integration depends on details that are easy to miss: credentials that expire, certificates that need renewing, partner versions that get deprecated. This role keeps those details from turning into outages, and then automates the work so it stops being manual.

Alloy operates in a hybrid-work environment. We look to foster collaboration and community by having our local employees onsite three days a week.

Что предстоит делать

Reporting to the Engineering Manager of our Partner Ecosystem Platform, you'll be the operational backbone of our partner integrations, working closely with engineering, product, and our partners and clients. You'll start by doing the work by hand, learning how each integration runs, and then work together with the team toward automating it away with AI tools and scripts, with guidance from the engineering lead.

  • Run intake for new integration requests from clients, prospects, and partners, and confirm documentation, credentials, sandbox access, and open questions are in place before engineering starts
  • Keep integration builds moving from intake to launch by following up with partners, clients, and internal teams until blockers clear
  • Track everything across our integrations that expires or changes, including credentials, mTLS certificates, IP allowlists, and partner version deprecations, and reach out to partners and clients before anything breaks
  • Monitor inbound partner communications and route each change to product for client impact or to engineering for build work
  • Maintain partner technical documentation, test credentials, sandbox access, and escalation contacts so engineering and Technical Solutions can test without waiting on a partner
  • Learn to automate the repeat work, such as expiration alerts and partner changelog tracking, using AI tools, scripts, and workflow automation
  • Own reporting on integration pipeline, delivery and upkeep health and publish a bi-weekly update

Who we’re looking for

Must-haves

  • 1 to 3 years in operations, technical support, implementation, or project coordination, ideally at a SaaS, fintech, or API-based company
  • Clear, professional written and spoken communication, and the confidence to get on a call with an external partner or client and drive it to a next step
  • Strong organization and follow-through. You track a lot of moving pieces and close the loop on every one
  • A willingness to do detailed, hands-on manual work now, paired with the instinct to ask how it could be automated
  • You’ve used AI tools or scripting to build something that saves you time, and you can explain how you checked it worked
  • Comfort reading technical material like API documentation and asking good questions when something doesn't add up

Nice-to-haves

  • Some scripting experience (Python, SQL) or experience building no-code automations (Zapier, n8n, or similar)
  • Familiarity with APIs, authentication, or certificate-based security
  • Exposure to fintech, identity, or fraud data
  • An interest in growing into a more technical or engineering role over time
