О роли

We're looking for a Senior Engineering Manager to lead our Decision Intelligence team at Pleo. Decision Intelligence owns Pleo's real-time fraud, AML and risk decisioning: when a Pleo card is used, this team's systems decide, in milliseconds, whether the transaction is legitimate. Every decision is a live trade-off between stopping fraud and letting a genuine customer spend, made under real latency and regulatory constraints against adversaries who constantly adapt. It's some of the highest-stakes engineering at Pleo.

Our ambition is to build a best-in-class fraud and risk capability: fast, accurate, explainable, and powered by clean data and machine learning at scale. This is a senior leadership role built to grow. You'll start by leading Decision Intelligence as a single team, but the area is set to expand into several teams spanning machine learning, real-time decisioning, and risk operations. We want someone with the seniority and ambition to lead that expansion and scale with it.

Что предстоит делать

As Senior Engineering Manager for Decision Intelligence, you will

- Own the delivery, quality, and technical direction of the fraud and risk decisioning platform, and the health and velocity of the team (or teams) that build it.

- Set the technical vision for how Pleo detects and prevents fraud: a data and ML-driven decisioning capability that is fast, accurate, and explainable at scale.

- Build a focused, high-performing team with clear ownership, sharp priorities, and a strong engineering culture.

- Directly manage, mentor, and grow the engineers and tech leads, setting clear competency expectations and growth paths for each person.

- Drive AI-native ways of working: set the standards for how AI tools are used, and stay accountable for the quality and safety of AI-assisted work in a fintech context (verification, audit trails, explainability).

- Partner with Compliance, Risk, and Security leadership, keeping clear lines between real-time fraud detection and financial-crime governance.

- Shape the organisation as it scales, growing Decision Intelligence into multiple teams and defining the specialist roles it hires next.

Что вы приносите

You'll thrive in this role if you have

- A track record of leading and growing engineering teams as an Engineering Manager or Senior Engineering Manager, with clear ownership of delivery health and people development.

- Domain judgment in fraud, risk, financial crime, trust & safety, or another real-time, high-stakes decisioning domain, and an understanding of what makes it hard: latency budgets, false-positive/false-negative trade-offs, adversarial change, and regulatory weight. Direct fraud or risk experience is a strong advantage; a genuine feel for the domain and a proven ability to get deep quickly matter just as much.

- Enough technical depth to set direction credibly and challenge it well, including data pipelines, model-driven decisioning, and the infrastructure (clean data through to graph and ML, streaming and batch architectures, MLOps) a modern fraud capability is built on.

- Sound judgment on build-vs-buy: knowing when to leverage vendors versus invest in in-house capability, especially for high-stakes, regulated systems.

- A genuine passion for people management and coaching, and the conviction that promotion is earned by operating at level, not handed out to retain.

- High proficiency with AI-enhanced development tools (such as GitHub Copilot or Claude) and a history of championing AI-native ways of working within a team.

- Willingness to keep deepening your AI fluency (workflows and craft) as Pleo scales AI adoption across the entire company.

- Strong cross-functional collaboration and communication, able to explain fraud and risk trade-offs clearly to technical and non-technical audiences alike.

WHO YOU'LL BE WORKING WITH AND REPORTING TO

You'll report to the Director of Engineering for Payments, and work most closely with the Product Manager for Decision Intelligence. As a fraud and risk domain, you'll also partner with our fraud, security, compliance, and risk leadership. Decision Intelligence sits within Pleo's broader Payments organisation, with regular touchpoints across the payments and platform teams whose data and flows feed fraud decisioning.

HOW YOU'LL DEVELOP IN THIS ROLE

In your first six months at Pleo, you'll

- Get deep into how real-time fraud decisioning works end-to-end, from authorisation through to the data and models behind each decision.

- Establish clear ownership, sharp priorities, and a strong delivery culture within the team.

- Build relationships across product, compliance, security, analysts, and the other teams the domain depends on.…
