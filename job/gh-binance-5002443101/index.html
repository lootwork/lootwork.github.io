Binance is a leading global blockchain ecosystem behind the world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume and registered users. We are trusted by 300+ million people in 100+ countries for our industry-leading security, user fund transparency, trading engine speed, deep liquidity, and an unmatched portfolio of digital-asset products. Binance offerings range from trading and finance to education, research, payments, institutional services, and more. Our mission is to increase the freedom of money and financial access for people around the world.

Обязанности

  • Participate in end-to-end product development across frontend and backend under senior engineer guidance.
  • Integrate AI coding tools (Cursor, Copilot, Claude Code) into daily development workflow.
  • Translate product requirements into working implementations with minimal frontend-backend handoff friction.
  • Act as a full stack "seed learner" — absorb practices, contribute ideas, and grow into an autonomous full stack engineer.
  • Proactively suggest improvements to product, design, and engineering workflows.

Требования

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree or above; Computer Science or AI-related major is a plus.
  • Hands-on project experience (personal, coursework, internships, or open-source contributions all count).
  • Foundation in at least one backend language (Java, Go, Rust, C++).
  • Familiarity with Node.js and modern frontend frameworks.
  • Basic understanding of database design and core backend concepts.
  • Ability to articulate requirements and constraints to AI tools clearly.
  • Self-driven, product-minded, and passionate about learning new technologies.
  • Strong logical thinking and communication skills.

Будет плюсом

  • Open-source contributions or notable GitHub projects
  • Cross-platform development experience (iOS/Android/Web)
  • Internship or work experience at startups
  • Track record of proactively building internal tools or personal side projects
