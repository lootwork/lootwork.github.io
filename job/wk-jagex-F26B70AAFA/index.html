Location: Cambridge, UK – This position offers flexible working options, including predominantly remote work. Applicants should be based (or willing to relocate) within a comfortable commuting distance of our office to attend onsite as required.

Do you want to build systems that millions of players depend on every day without necessarily knowing they exist?

Our Player Platform organisation is the foundation that powers all things RuneScape. From the moment a player logs in to the moment they complete a purchase, our teams own the technology that makes it work. We're looking for Full Stack Engineers to join our Player Platform team - you'll work across Java backends, React frontends, and AWS infrastructure - building software where reliability isn't optional and your work is felt at scale.

Что предстоит делать

  • Owning work from scoping and technical design through to release in production, often across several services
  • Delivering features and improvements in your team's domain with secure-by-design code, code review and documentation
  • Testing your own work with test plans, automated tests (unit, mock, integration) and manual testing where needed
  • Building and maintaining services that are monitored, scalable, robust and highly available, integrating REST APIs and publish-subscribe messaging
  • Working closely with senior engineers and product owners, and growing your skills as you go

Кого мы ищем

  • A hands-on engineer who wants to build and ship, rather than move towards product or delivery roles
  • Proactive, inquisitive and a clear communicator with both technical and non-technical colleagues
  • Experience delivering web applications or contributing to large established projects, where you took real ownership
  • Java (Spring Boot or similar) and JavaScript/TypeScript with React, Next.js or Node
  • Working knowledge of AWS, CI/CD (Git, Bitbucket Pipelines, Bamboo) and IaC such as Terraform, with familiarity with microservices
  • Experience in billing, payments or e-commerce is a nice to have but not essential
