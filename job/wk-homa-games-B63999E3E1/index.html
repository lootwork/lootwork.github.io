Mission

As a Creative Developer, you will sit at the intersection of game development, creative production, and performance marketing. Your mission is to use Unity and C# to rapidly modify existing games, prototype new gameplay ideas, and build tools that help the Creative team produce and test high-performing ads faster.

Your main responsibilities will include

  • Modify and prototype gameplay in Unity: new mechanics, visuals, features, UI, cameras, and gameplay variations
  • Build tools for Creative teams: create flexible Unity apps and internal tools for video and playable-ad production
  • Produce rapid iterations: create multiple game variations to test hooks, mechanics, visuals, and player experiences
  • Own technical quality: debug, fix issues, optimize performance, and ensure features work reliably
  • Iterate from performance data: use ad results and learnings to improve creative concepts and gameplay
  • Contribute to ideation: participate in brainstorming and propose new gameplay, creative, and technical ideas
  • Explore new technologies: leverage AI, and emerging tools to increase production speed and creativity

Contract setup: This is a full-time freelance opportunity, structured around project needs, with flexible duration depending on the scope and evolution of the projects.

Requirements

We are looking for someone with years of experience as a Creative Developer or Unity Developer in mobile games.

You're a great fit if you have

  • Strong Unity + C# expertise, confidently modifying existing games, gameplay, mechanics, visuals, and features while debugging and optimizing your work
  • Creative + technical mindset, able to turn creative ideas into engaging gameplay variations, prototypes, and high-performing ad concepts quickly
  • Strong mobile gaming and creative understanding, with a player-centric approach and knowledge of performance creatives, playable ads, and social/mobile ad trends
  • Fast prototyping and iteration skills, balancing speed and quality in a high-volume, test-and-learn creative environment
  • Autonomous and collaborative approach, working closely with Creative, Game Dev, and UA teams and using performance data to iterate on concepts
  • Bonus: experience with AI-assisted creative/development workflows, Blender, Photoshop, shaders, VFX, or building Unity tools for Creative teams
