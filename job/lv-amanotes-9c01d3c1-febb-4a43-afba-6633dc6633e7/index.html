ABOUT THE JOB

We are seeking a dynamic and innovative Product Owner to join our team, focusing on the development and delivery of outstanding mobile app experiences. In this role, you will collaborate with cross-functional teams to create seamless, high-quality apps that will engage and delight users worldwide. You will drive the product roadmap, monitor user engagement, and continuously improve the app’s functionality and performance.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

  • Data Monitoring & Analysis: Monitor, collect, and analyze app user data, conduct market research to synthesize insights, and transform these insights into actionable recommendations for product improvements and new features.
  • User Stories & Prototypes: Translate user stories into detailed requirements and collaborate with design teams to create prototypes, wireframes, and user interaction diagrams for app enhancements.
  • Product Roadmap Management: Maintain and prioritize the product roadmap, ensuring initiatives are aligned with business objectives and have the maximum impact on user engagement and satisfaction.
  • Cross-functional Collaboration: Collaborate closely with development teams and other departments (UX/UI Design, Creative, Marketing, Quality
  • Control, User Acquisition) to deliver app features and improvements efficiently, effectively, and with optimal resource allocation.

QUALIFICATIONS

  • Working experiences:
  • Minimum of 2-3 years of experience as a Product Owner in mobile app development, with experience delivering successful apps to the market.
  • Proven expertise in app design, user engagement, and monetization strategies for diverse revenue models (ads, in-app purchases, subscriptions, etc.).
  • Strong understanding of mobile app technologies, app performance metrics, and data-driven decision-making.
  • Ability to independently conduct research and stay updated on mobile app industry trends.
  • Skills:
  • A self-driven individual who takes full ownership and consistently delivers results
  • A continuous learner, eager to stay ahead of mobile market trends and apply new insights to drive product success.
  • A highly adaptable multi-tasker who proactively seeks opportunities to support the team and manage multiple game portfolios.
  • Demonstrates a strong interest in and understanding of technology, marketing, analytics, and data-driven solutions.
  • Experience creating wireframes, mockups, prototypes, and user interaction diagrams.
  • Practical application of Agile methodologies.
  • Fluent in English, both written and spoken.
  • An effective communicator capable of sharing ideas, discussing projects, and providing constructive feedback.

  • Competitive salary, 100% salary during probation
  • Performance bonus with 30% of company profit shared with no cap
  • Full SHUI as Vietnamese Law + Premium Private Medical Insurance and annual health check

  • 12 days working from home per year

  • Free daily lunch with 10+ menu options.
  • Free daily F&B: Snacks, juice, beverage, tea-time
  • Monthly/Bi-monthly team activity allowance
  • Company learning trip will be top of countries that we gain most profit. 1st is Korea, next destination is Japan
  • Sponsored sports activities: badminton, soccer, ...
  • Sponsored overseas conferences and industry events for all levels.
  • Modern equipments/AI tool provided.
