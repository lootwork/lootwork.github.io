We’re looking for a Head of 1st Line Financial Crime Risk to lead our first line financial crime function and shape the next generation of financial crime capability at Zopa.

This is a senior, hands-on role within our Fraud and Financial Crime tribe. You’ll work closely with leaders and teams across risk, product, operations and technology, helping us build products and services that are safe, commercially sustainable and deliver a great experience for our customers.

You’ll set the direction for how we manage financial crime risk across our products, while staying close to the detail where it matters. Bringing together strategic thinking, strong technical expertise and sound judgement, you’ll help us make informed decisions, build effective controls and support the business to grow.

A DAY IN THE LIFE

  • Manage and develop a small team of financial crime specialists, building the expertise and resilience needed to support Zopa’s growth.
  • Set the strategic direction for financial crime risk management across our products and services, including the development of the infrastructure and capabilities that support it.
  • Partner with product, operations, technology and risk teams from design through to delivery, ensuring financial crime considerations are embedded in how we build and improve our products.
  • Lead the design and development of effective, proportionate controls across screening, customer risk assessment, transaction monitoring and suspicious activity reporting.
  • Translate our risk appetite into practical decisions, helping teams understand and navigate trade-offs between risk, cost, customer experience and speed of delivery.
  • Champion an agile and proportionate approach to controls and documentation, aligned to product maturity and delivery timelines.
  • Work with data teams to define meaningful risk indicators, metrics, thresholds and monitoring plans, including post-launch checks.
  • Act as a senior escalation point for complex or sensitive financial crime matters, bringing clarity, perspective and balanced judgement to decisions.
  • Build capability across the first line by sharing expertise, supporting development and creating ways of working that support resilience.
  • Work closely with second line teams to align with policy, standards and regulatory expectations, while maintaining clear ownership of financial crime risk in the first line.

О вас

  • You have experience leading a financial crime risk function and setting its direction in a complex, changing environment. Experience in fintech or digital banking would be beneficial.
  • Have a strong track record of owning a broad range of financial crime controls, including transaction monitoring rule design, onboarding and know your customer flows, customer risk assessment models and suspicious activity reporting processes.
  • You combine strategic thinking with a hands-on approach and are comfortable moving between the bigger picture and the detail.
  • You’re a critical thinker with an analytical mindset. You can assess complex information, challenge assumptions, identify the underlying issue and use evidence to make sound, well-reasoned decisions.
  • Confident using data to inform decisions and can define meaningful metrics, risk indicators, thresholds and monitoring plans.
  • You understand how to balance commercial and risk considerations, clearly articulate trade-offs and help teams prioritise when resources are constrained.
  • You have a track record of translating regulatory expectations into pragmatic, product-aligned solutions without adding unnecessary complexity.
  • Are comfortable acting as a senior escalation point, applying sound judgement to complex financial crime issues and taking ownership of decisions.
  • Support the development of others, share knowledge and build capability and resilience across the first line.
  • You communicate clearly and with impact, connecting financial crime risks and decisions to product strategy, customer outcomes and business goals. You’re comfortable engaging directly with senior business leaders.
  • You thrive in fast-moving, product-led environments and work collaboratively with colleagues across operations, product, engineering and second line functions.
