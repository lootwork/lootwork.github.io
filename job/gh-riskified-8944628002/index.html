О роли

We're looking for a Staff Data Scientist to lead one of our most ambitious directions: bringing foundation models to fraud prevention. The goal is models that learn from Riskified's broad transaction data and from chains of related orders, giving many of our models a shared, reusable base. That could mean training our own foundation model, adapting existing large models and solutions, or a mix of both. Finding the right path is part of the job.

The Data Science department powers Riskified's core engine. Our models make thousands of real-time decisions per second, and we're moving from hand-engineered features toward models that learn from behaviour and populations across our network.

You'll bring hands-on experience building, fine-tuning or adapting foundation models or similar large-scale models. Just as important, you'll bring sound research judgement. You'll weigh building against reusing what already exists, test the options against our data and existing solutions, prove value early, and then drive the best one to production.

You'll be a senior technical voice across the department. You'll shape how we use deep learning, representation learning and research rigour well beyond this initiative.

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead the foundation model initiative: Own the research and roadmap for applying foundation models to our core problems, from proof of concept to real-time production.
  • Validate before you scale: Challenge assumptions, set success criteria tied to business metrics, and let evidence from early experiments steer the direction.
  • Build or adapt, whichever wins: Decide between training our own models and adapting existing ones, such as tabular foundation models, LLMs and pre-trained sequence models. Then develop the sequence, transformer, graph or deep tabular models that work on massive transaction and behavioural data.
  • Go deep on our domain and systems: Learn our fraud domain, data and production models, and find where learned representations add the most value.
  • Partner across functions: Work with Data Engineering, ML Platform, Core Software and Product on the data, training and serving infrastructure these models need.
  • Raise the bar: Bring the latest research into practical solutions, set standards for research rigour, and mentor data scientists across the department.

Требования

  • Education: M.Sc. or Ph.D. in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics or a related quantitative field.
  • Experience: 6+ years of hands-on experience designing, training and deploying machine learning models in large-scale production environments.
  • Foundation model expertise: Hands-on experience building, fine-tuning or adapting foundation models or similar large-scale models, such as pre-trained transformers, self-supervised sequence models, tabular foundation models or LLMs. You have sound judgement on when to build and when to reuse.
  • Research judgement: A track record of turning open-ended research questions into well-designed experiments and evidence-based decisions.
  • Problem-first mindset: You start from the problem, not the technique, and you're comfortable challenging ideas, including your own, with data.
  • Curiosity and learning speed: You're eager to learn new technology as well as existing solutions, data and domain, and you ramp up quickly in unfamiliar territory.
  • Big data: Strong practical experience with large-scale distributed data processing (for example Spark or Databricks) and with training models on large datasets.
  • Technical influence: Proven technical leadership beyond your own team, including setting direction, aligning stakeholders and mentoring senior scientists.
  • Communication: You can explain complex research and trade-offs clearly to technical and non-technical audiences, including senior leadership.

Advantages

  • Experience pre-training models on event, transaction or behavioural sequences (non-text data).
  • Experience with tabular foundation models, graph neural networks or embedding-based models at scale.
  • Experience in fraud detection, risk management, payments or fintech.
  • Publications, open-source work or talks in relevant areas.
