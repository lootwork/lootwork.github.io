Are you someone who wants to…

  • Own technical onboarding and turn complexity into smooth go‑lives.

Кто мы

Modulr is the payments automation platform that streamlines money movement with accuracy, control and reliability – built to scale. Processing over 200mn transactions and £180bn annually, Modulr is trusted by more than 6,000 businesses to automate payment and reconciliation workflows – removing manual work, lowering costs and reducing errors.

Modulr provides a single platform for automating how money moves into and out of a business - including payroll, supplier payments, spend management and payment collection. With over a decade of experience as a regulated payments provider, Modulr delivers the reliability needed to run mission-critical finance operations at scale.

Find out more about us on our website and careers site.

Что предстоит делать

  • Support the delivery of onboarding projects by owning and managing technical setup, resolving troubleshooting issues, and maintaining high levels of process accuracy
  • Provide working‑level technical support to customers and internal teams during onboarding and early go‑live, and act as second‑line support during BAU for integration and cards enquiries, escalating complex issues appropriately
  • Configure Modulr’s full suite of products, services, and integrations across all client types, ensuring accurate setup and consistent delivery standards
  • Work with third‑party partners (e.g. Visa, Thredd) to support successful integrations, troubleshooting, and go‑live readiness
  • Coordinate and validate customer access across sandbox and production environments, including credentials, permissions, and readiness checks
  • Use tools such as Postman to diagnose and resolve straightforward API and implementation issues
  • Manage and progress implementation/support tickets through Freshdesk, ensuring timely responses, accurate updates, and adherence to agreed SLAs
  • Manage multiple onboarding cases concurrently, ensuring SLA adherence and timely progression to go‑live
  • Maintain accurate, up‑to‑date records in Salesforce, including milestones, risks, dependencies, and executive summaries
  • Identify and manage internal dependencies across Product, Compliance, Finance, and partner teams, escalating blockers when required
  • Coordinate clean handover to Support or Account Management once onboarding criteria are met
  • Ensure all onboarding activity is well documented, auditable, and compliant with regulatory and internal governance standards
  • Continuously seek opportunities to improve onboarding efficiency and accuracy through automation and the effective use of AI‑enabled tools

Кто вы

What you’ll need

  • Experience in implementation, operations, or technical support within a fintech or regulated environment
  • Working knowledge of APIs and system integrations, with hands‑on troubleshooting experience preferred
  • Comfortable independently delivering standard tasks while operating within established processes and governance controls
  • Able to solve a range of straightforward problems using standard procedures and clear escalation paths
  • Strong organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities effectively
  • Clear and professional communication skills when working with both internal teams and external customers
  • A curious mindset with a drive to explore how automation and AI can improve delivery quality and efficiency

Будет плюсом

  • Experience using tools such as Postman, Salesforce, or Freshdesk
  • Exposure to payments products such as Cards, Direct Debits, or Faster Payments
  • Experience working in scale‑up or fast‑paced delivery environments
  • A curious mindset and drive to explore how AI can make a real difference.
