Mercury is building a complete finance stack for startups. We work hard to create the easiest and safest banking* experience possible to simplify entrepreneurs' and business owners’ financial lives. To enable this stellar banking experience, we need to have a deep understanding of our customers and how they interact with our product. We are looking to hire a card fraud strategist to bring deep domain expertise and an analytical approach to our Risk Strategy team, driving impact in this crucial space.

Risk Strategy is a hybrid team that combines analytics with thought leadership to identify, assess, and mitigate the financial, regulatory, and reputational risks Mercury faces. The team focuses on data-driven insights and process improvements while ensuring a stellar customer experience that fosters trust in our platform.

As a Senior Risk Strategist focused on card fraud specifically, you’ll drive key parts of Mercury’s card fraud and dispute strategies, combining data-driven analysis with strategic problem solving. You’ll partner closely with Product, Engineering, Operations, and Compliance to deliver scalable outcomes that enable the business and reduce fraud losses while protecting customer experience.

*Mercury is a fintech company, not an FDIC-insured bank. Banking services provided through Choice Financial Group and Column N.A., Members FDIC.

As part of the journey, we would expect you to

  • Own the day-to-day card fraud rules alongside the existing card fraud strategist. You'll monitor trends, analyze gaps, write and backtest rules, test them in shadow mode, and launch them live
  • Build new card fraud programs from scratch, such as card testing detection, card issuance abuse, and agentic spending controls. You’ll also stand up new coverage when partners or BINs change
  • Research emerging attack patterns, analyze gaps, and deploy new controls and rules to close them
  • Build observability across card abuse typologies, including scalable key risk indicators (KRIs) and alerting
  • Define and track metrics for both risk effectiveness and customer impact, and build executive-ready reports that tell the story of how we're performing.
  • Lead cross-functional efforts with Product, Engineering, Compliance, Legal, and Operations to design and ship scalable solutions. That includes operationalizing new card network rules and tools.
  • Advocate for policy changes backed by what the data shows, and partner with Compliance and Legal to keep solutions aligned with regulatory requirements.

Требования

  • 6+ years in analytical roles, including 4+ years in card fraud detection and strategy, with a track record of owning a program or domain end-to-end (startup or high-growth fintech experience a plus)
  • Deep expertise in issuer card fraud, disputes (including Reg E), and authorization strategy, including card network rules and liability frameworks
  • Hands-on experience authoring, backtesting, and tuning rules in a real-time decisioning system (e.g., shadow testing and staged rollouts), including setting thresholds on model scores and weighing false positives against fraud loss
  • Strong SQL proficiency, and comfort automating recurring analysis and monitoring (Python or similar), with the ability to turn data into actionable strategy
  • Track record of leading cross-functional projects with Risk, Product, Compliance, and Operations
  • Excellent verbal and written communication, with the ability to simplify complex risk concepts into clear recommendations for executives and partners
  • Proven ability to operate in ambiguity and make decisions with imperfect information
  • Curious and motivated to work on fraud problems at every lifecycle stage: new product launches, scaling, and legacy optimization
  • Working knowledge of fraud across adjacent domains (e.g., onboarding, ACH, checks, RTP, ATO) and how attackers pivot between them

Будет плюсом

  • Familiarity with tokenization and lifecycle controls (network tokens, passkeys, 3DS)
  • Interest or experience in agentic payments and non-human buyer flows, and in defending against AI-enabled attacks such as automated card testing
