The team

The Delivery function is responsible for the first-class delivery of priority projects across product and engineering. As an Associate Delivery Manager, you will work alongside experienced Delivery Managers, product and technical leads and a range of stakeholders to provide structure, coordination, visibility and momentum for some of the most exciting and impactful changes happening at Zopa Bank.

You will lead smaller, less complex projects and support larger programmes, with the mentorship and support of the wider Delivery team to help you build confidence, capability and judgement as you grow.

A DAY IN THE LIFE

With seven engineering domains, twenty squads, five core products and a customer base growing by the day, there is always work to be done at Zopa, the purpose of the Delivery function is to ensure that the right people are doing the right work, at the right time and in the most efficient way possible.

More than managing specific projects, the delivery function is tasked with creating an environment conducive to agility, predictability, momentum and continual improvement.

1.Delivery Support

A blanket term for numerous responsibilities that drive two key outcomes, these are increased predictability and increased momentum.

The role will be expected to work alongside product managers, engineering managers and technical leads and duties include facilitating objective setting, road mapping, stakeholder engagement, surfacing and resolving blockers, shielding engineers from distraction and generally doing what needs to be done to allow squads to plan, prioritise and execute effectively.

2.Agility

Agility is the ability to switch focus quickly and easily to meet the demands of the market, and this is now the expectation of any company operating within the Fintech industry and Zopa is no different. We are committed to the core agile methodologies and delivery frameworks which adhere to those principles.

A focus of this role is proactively working with teams to identify opportunities to embed, experiment and mature processes and ways of working, absorbing what is useful and discarding what is not.

3.Health and Happiness

The Delivery manager is not a line manager, but the role is responsible for being proactive in helping squads find time and space to reflect on and to improve team mood, morale and motivation.

О вас
