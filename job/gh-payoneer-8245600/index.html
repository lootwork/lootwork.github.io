О Payoneer

Founded in 2005, Payoneer is the global financial platform that removes friction from doing business across borders, with a mission to connect the world’s underserved businesses to a rising global economy. We’re a community with over 2,500 colleagues all over the world, working to serve customers, and partners in over 190 countries and territories.

By taking the complexity out of the financial workflows–including everything from global payments and compliance to multi-currency and workforce management, to providing working capital and business intelligence–we give businesses the tools they need to work efficiently worldwide and grow with confidence.

Risk Management Manager

Role summary -As a Risk Management Manager, you will support Payoneer’s payment business in China by identifying, assessing, monitoring, and mitigating risks across cross-border payment activities. You will focus on areas including merchant risk, transaction authenticity, abnormal fund flows, and fraud-related risk, while partnering with cross-functional teams to enable responsible, compliant, and well-controlled business growth.

Location: Guangzhou, China

On-site

Full-time

Что предстоит делать

  • Build and continuously enhance risk management controls for Payoneer’s cross-border payment business in China, covering merchant risk, transaction monitoring, fraud risk, and abnormal fund flow scenarios.
  • Conduct risk assessments for new products, business models, and process changes, and provide practical mitigation recommendations to support launch and implementation decisions.
  • Monitor and analyze key payment risk typologies, including transaction splitting, false trade, suspicious or abnormal fund flows, regulatory-sensitive scenarios, and fraud-related activities.
  • Prepare risk assessment reports, thematic reviews, and risk-event follow-ups, and work with cross-functional teams to close control gaps and strengthen processes.
  • Partner with Compliance, Legal, Product, Technology, Operations, and business teams to ensure local risk controls align with regulatory requirements, Payoneer’s global standards, and business needs.

Кто вы

  • Experience in cross-border payments, cross-border e-commerce, merchant risk, transaction authenticity reviews, or fraud and abnormal transaction monitoring.
  • Professional certifications such as FRM, CAMS, or an equivalent credential.
  • An ability to balance effective risk management with practical business needs in a complex and evolving environment.
  • Bachelor’s degree or above in Finance, Risk Management, Law, Accounting, International Trade, or a related discipline.
  • 6+ years of experience in risk management, payment risk, transaction monitoring, or a related role within a licensed payment institution.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, sound judgment, and the ability to translate risk findings into practical controls and remediation actions.
  • Strong communication, ownership, and cross-functional collaboration skills, with the ability to work independently in a fast-paced, global environment.
  • Good written and spoken English skills.
