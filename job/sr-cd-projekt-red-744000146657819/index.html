Job Description

CD PROJEKT RED is looking for a Senior Publishing Producer to ensure high-quality delivery of external and internal projects while forging strong ties with external partners. If you are well-organized and efficient in working across teams and you want to redirect your skills to working with 3rd party partners on some of the greatest games, we have a job for you!

Daily Responsibilities

  • Coordinating the development team in the preparation of milestones deliveries and console submissions.
  • Evaluating product milestones and providing regular feedback to the development team.
  • Planning and applying proper development processes, tools and tasks used to start and complete a project.
  • Identifying and actively mitigating project risks, playing whatever role is necessary to contribute to a successful outcome.
  • Synchronising the planning between teams to assure effective task-flow.
  • Ensuring the proper communication flow between external partners, stakeholders and the development team.
  • Actively contributing to business and legal negotiations, assisting with budgeting and contracts creation.
  • Working closely with the development teams and taking part in preparation of builds to external partners.

Требования

  • At least 6 years of experience as a Producer, Project Manager or Release Manager in the gaming industry.
  • Experience shipping complex game projects, including AAA titles or innovative mobile titles.
  • Proven ability to effectively work across teams involved in a game development life cycle.
  • Knowledge of the game development and production processes.
  • Understanding of software development lifecycles and tools.
  • Broad knowledge of global gaming trends and player expectations.
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills.
  • Detail-orientation with very strong organisational and tracking skills.
  • Excellent interpersonal skills.
  • Fluent English and familiarity with common industry terminology.

Additional Information

Working mode

  • In-office in a hybrid or full-time capacity in Warsaw, Poland. Relocation is required.
