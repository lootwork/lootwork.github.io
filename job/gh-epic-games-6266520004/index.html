Что предстоит делать

Epic Games is searching for a Principal Animator to join our Fortnite team and help shape the future of gameplay animation. In this highly influential, hands-on role, you'll create exceptional keyframed gameplay animation, build playable prototypes that explore new ideas for Fortnite, and help develop innovative animation techniques, tools, and emerging workflows. We're looking for someone with deep expertise in gameplay animation, strong creative and technical instincts, and a passion for exploring AI-assisted animation technologies. You'll collaborate across disciplines to elevate animation quality, explore new gameplay possibilities, and help define the next generation of animation workflows within Fortnite.

In this role, you will

  • Own gameplay animation from initial concept through implementation in Unreal Engine 5, with a focus on gameplay feel, responsiveness, style, and visual appeal
  • Independently come up with new gameplay ideas, build playable prototypes, and iterate on them through playtesting. Bring a strong sense of what makes Fortnite fun and where there's room to take it in new directions
  • Champion AI-assisted animation technologies and emerging workflows, identifying opportunities to enhance animation quality, creativity, and production efficiency
  • Experiment with, prototype, and help integrate innovative animation tools and techniques into Fortnite's animation pipelines
  • Serve as a subject matter expert in gameplay animation, providing creative and technical direction across teams while remaining deeply hands-on
  • Partner closely with Design, Engineering, Technical Animation, Technical Design, and Production to solve complex animation challenges and influence long-term animation strategies
  • Establish best practices, mentor animators, and raise the bar for animation quality, technical execution, and workflow innovation
  • Create and refine stylized and realistic gameplay animation in Unreal Engine 5 using Control Rig and Animation Blueprints

Кого мы ищем

  • Extensive AAA game development experience and a strong portfolio that demonstrates exceptional keyframed gameplay animation for both characters and creatures
  • Demonstrated hands-on experience exploring, prototyping, or applying AI-assisted animation technologies and emerging workflows, whether through professional work, independent projects, or personal experimentation
  • Strong curiosity and passion for emerging technologies, with a track record of independently exploring new tools and techniques to advance animation workflows
  • Deep expertise in Unreal Engine 5, Control Rig, Animation Blueprints, and modern gameplay animation pipelines
  • Strong technical understanding of animation systems, including state machines, blending, procedural animation, and runtime implementation
  • Proven ability to influence animation direction, establish best practices, and solve complex creative and technical challenges across teams
  • Exceptional understanding of animation fundamentals, gameplay responsiveness, timing, and stylized movement
  • Experience collaborating across Animation, Engineering, Design, and Technical Animation to deliver scalable animation solutions
  • Strong creative and technical problem-solving skills, with the ability to prototype and evaluate new approaches to gameplay animation
  • Experience mentoring and elevating other animators through feedback, knowledge sharing, and technical guidance
  • Experience shipping multiple AAA titles

This role is open to multiple locations across the US (including CA & WA).
