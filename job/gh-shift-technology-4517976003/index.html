О команде

This role is part of the Data Science team, which is the largest department in our organization, consisting of close to 200 Data Scientists throughout the world.

Our Data Scientists work in a full-lifecycle role across a broad range of subjects, acquiring extensive technical and professional experience in data science, data engineering, software engineering, business understanding, and client engagement.

Team Impact & Focus

  • The Data Science team actively participates in the definition and development of our suite of products focused on fraud detection, anti-money laundering, and claim automation.
  • We possess deep technical and professional experience across coding, data science, data engineering, and client interactions.
  • We work with a highly diverse set of data types, including structured data, free text, documents, and images.
  • Our company is small enough that each person’s achievements have an impact on our overall performance, yet big enough to be a world leader and innovator in our domain.
  • You will work alongside technical experts, playing a key role in deploying our enterprise-level solutions to our clients.
  • Local Context: You will be joining the Brazilian Data Science team, which works mostly for US Property & Casualty (P&C) customers. Our main focus is deploying solutions to new clients, maintaining client production, and contributing to the development of our Fraud Detection Solution.

Role Purpose & Responsibilities

Your primary purpose is to learn and assist in deploying Shift Technology’s solutions to new clients as well as support the maintenance of solutions for existing clients. Under the guidance of senior engineers, this includes participating in managing data pipelines, executing ETL processes, and developing, testing, tuning, and deploying fraud detection models.

Working alongside experienced team members within a designated pod of clients, you will execute the following duties:

  • Client Implementation Support: Assist in implementing Claim Fraud Detection (CFD) for new clients, focusing on ETL setup, detection implementation, and workflow automation.
  • Feature Deployment: Help roll out new platform features to existing clients.
  • Optimization: Contribute to continuous improvements across existing client environments and pipelines.
  • Stakeholder Engagement: Collaborate with internal teams to collect user feedback, help prepare business case materials, and assist in reporting regularly on solution performance to stakeholders.

Requirements & Skills

We are looking for a recently graduated candidate or a professional with 1 to 2 years of experience in a similar technical environment (such as deployment engineering, data science, data engineering, or software engineering).

  • Software Engineering: A strong background in an OOP language (such as C#, C/C++, or Java) and SQL. We thrive on people who can write maintainable, production-quality code that runs every day without breaking.
  • AI Competency: AI-savvy with a clear understanding of statistics, machine learning, and data solutions. Must be able to communicate these concepts internally and explain how the product works to clients.
  • Client Interaction: Client-facing comfort. You need to be open to communicating directly with our clients on a regular basis.
  • Business Acumen: Business smart. We do not expect prior knowledge of the insurance sector, but you must be eager to learn and master the business aspects of our products.
  • Collaboration: A dedicated team player who works effectively with a diverse group of people and collaborates well across multiple teams.
  • Language: Fluency in English alongside good overall communication skills is a strict requirement.
