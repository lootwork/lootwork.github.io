О нас

Riskified empowers businesses to unleash ecommerce growth by taking risk off the table. Many of the world’s biggest brands and publicly traded companies selling online rely on Riskified for guaranteed protection against chargebacks, to fight fraud and policy abuse at scale, and to improve customer retention. Developed and managed by the largest team of ecommerce risk analysts, data scientists and researchers, Riskified’s AI-powered fraud and risk intelligence platform analyzes the individual behind each interaction to provide real-time decisions and robust identity-based insights. Riskified is proud to work with incredible companies in virtually all industries including Acer, Gucci, Lorna Jane, GoPro, and many more.

We thrive in a collaborative work setting, alongside great people, to build and enhance products that matter. Abundant opportunities to create and contribute provide us with a sense of purpose that extends beyond ourselves, leaving a lasting impact. These sentiments capture why we choose Riskified every day.

О роли

Join our global analytics team at Riskified! As a Data Analyst, you’ll work directly with enterprise customers and be responsible for Riskified’s customers’ performance and online fraud prevention in real-time. You will own a major part in researching and investigating new online-fraud trends and stand at the forefront of Riskified’s work. You’ll leverage your analytical skills and knowledge of our product to provide meaningful insights to our merchants.

Что предстоит делать

  • Research and explore data, using high level analytical tools (R and SQL) in a high level technical environment
  • Effectively communicate analytical insights and complex technical methodologies to both technical and non-technical audiences
  • Collaborate with various teams within Riskified to optimize processes and meet customers’ needs
  • Engage with account managers, directly interface with clients, and deliver analytical insights to business questions
  • Define and execute end-to-end technical solutions for fraud-prevention challenges
  • Master the online fraud prevention domain through hands-on analysis of live data

Требования

  • 2-3+ years of relevant strong analytical experience with complex data
  • 2+ years of hands-on work experience with SQL (must be proficient)
  • Proven expertise in analysis tools such as R and Python
  • Experienced problem-solver with critical thinking skills
  • Ability to work simultaneously on different tasks and lead multiple projects
  • Full working proficiency (both written and verbal) in English
  • Effective presentation skills, with a track record of client-facing experience
  • Advantage - experience in fraud, payments, or ecommerce domains

*Please submit your CV/Resume in English

We include transparent salary ranges to support fairness, clarity, and informed decision-making early in the process.
