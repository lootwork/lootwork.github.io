Business Analytics & Strategic Partnerships (Tokenisation) Manager

Binance is a leading global blockchain ecosystem behind the world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume and registered users. We are trusted by 300+ million people in 100+ countries for our industry-leading security, user fund transparency, trading engine speed, deep liquidity, and an unmatched portfolio of digital-asset products. Binance offerings range from trading and finance to education, research, payments, institutional services, and more. Our mission is to increase the freedom of money and financial access for people around the world.

Обязанности

  • Turn data into insight — source, clean, and interpret TradFi market data, opportunities, and competitive positioning, working closely with our data team
  • Turn insight into proposals — develop clear, actionable recommendations: pricing strategy, market entry, deal structuring, go-to-market
  • Enable business development — package analysis into decision-ready formats our leadership uses directly when engaging external parties and pursuing TradFi opportunities
  • Build the market picture — maintain a working map of the TradFi landscape (banking, brokerages, market infrastructure) to drive our commercial push
  • Originate and source deals — proactively identify and engage issuers, companies, partners, investment banks, exchanges, and distribution platforms to develop new opportunities
  • Own the full deal cycle — from initial outreach and pitch, through negotiation, to closing and handover. You'll be measured on deals closed, not meetings had.
  • Lead commercial discussions — structure the economics of the partnership, model the value, and land terms that work
  • Build the strategy — feed market intelligence back to shape what we pursue next in tokenisation and partnerships
  • Work cross-functionally — align with product, legal, compliance, and engineering to get deals over the line

Требования

  • 5- 8 years of experience in finance, strategy, consulting or similar analytical roles — ideally in crypto, fintech, or financial services.
  • Strong commercial and operational acumen as well as critical problem solving skills.
  • Ability to build strong cross functional relationships - communications and engaging with internal stakeholders of various levels is key.
  • Project and change management experience.
  • Deep understanding of tokenisation — real-world assets, digital assets, token issuance, or the institutional side of crypto.
