Build with us

Businesses deserve better from finance. Less friction, more freedom. Since 2004, Mollie has been on a mission to make payments and money management effortless for every business in Europe.

Today, more than 250,000 companies trust our all-in-one platform to get paid, manage money and grow on their terms. Simple, scalable and built with real businesses in mind.

We're one of Europe's fastest-growing fintechs, with 900+ Mollies across 12+ locations. Our people make this growth possible. We move fast, build with purpose and care deeply about our customers. Whether you're solving problems, building market-leading products or exploring how AI can make work smarter, you'll have the freedom to create and the trust to deliver.

If you're ready to grow, shape the future of fintech and join an ambitious, high-performing team, this is the place for you.

Your impact

You'll be a key player in the Connect domain, one of the fastest-growing channels at Mollie. Your direct contribution ensures we maximize the value of our strategic partnerships, translating product potential into significant revenue growth across Europe. This is a chance to move fast and own the outcome of a critical expansion vector.

You will manage a dedicated portfolio of SaaS accounts, responsible for driving their complete lifecycle. Your focus won't just be retention; it's on market expansion and aggressive revenue growth. You'll work cross-functionally—collaborating with teams across Mollie to drive growth for our partners and increase adoption of key products like Mollie Capital, in-person payments, and more—ensuring these accounts thrive and contribute to Mollie’s overall success.

What you'll ship

- Drive new in-year net revenue from your portfolio of accounts, exceeding aggressive growth targets for the Connect channel.

- Master the churn rate across your managed partners, proactively identifying risks and implementing strategies for long-term retention.

- Increase product adoption across your portfolio by successfully cross-selling high-impact solutions like Mollie Capital and in-person payment tools.

- Improve technical integration quality in partnership with Solution Engineers and Product Managers to unlock greater value and performance for our SaaS partners.

What you'll bring

- Proven experience in Partner Management or Account Management, specifically in a high-growth, high-stakes environment.

- Strong background in Business development with a clear focus on managing and expanding existing accounts (farming).

- Mastery at networking and relationship building, demonstrated by the ability to cultivate high-trust, high-value commercial relationships.

- Comfortable with working with Product teams, not just commercial ones, and effectively translating partner needs into product feedback and priorities.

- Excellent communication and presentation skills in Dutch (mandatory) & comfortable interacting in English.

Grow your way

No matter if you're aiming for promotion, exploring a different career path or building new skills, you'll be supported with the tools, trust and opportunities to grow your way.
