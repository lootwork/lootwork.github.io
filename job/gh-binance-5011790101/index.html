О роли

We are looking for a Head of Compliance Programs, Training & External Assurance to help shape, strengthen, and scale a high-performing compliance culture across the global Compliance organization.

This is a senior leadership role within the Compliance function, responsible for driving key programs that reinforce trust, accountability, transparency, and consistent execution. The role will report into the Deputy Compliance Officer and will lead and support a growing number of global compliance culture initiatives, including conflict of interest and whistleblower programs, compliance training, independent review and audit coordination, and external reputation-building efforts, among others to be defined.

The ideal candidate is a strong compliance leader and operator who can combine regulatory awareness, program management, stakeholder engagement, and practical execution in a fast-moving environment. Experience in crypto, fintech or financial services is strongly preferred.

Что предстоит делать

Conflict of Interest and Whistleblower Programs

  • Manage the day-to-day operational aspects of the global Conflict of Interest program, including managing committee logistics, training and awareness, yearly declarations, escalations reporting, and communications as needed.
  • Manage the day-to-day operational aspects of the global Whistleblower program, including maintaining the anonymous reporting and case management tools, escalating reports with the highest level of confidentiality, and in accordance with applicable requirements, tracking case progress and reporter communications (as needed) and escalating reports as needed.
  • Coordinate with external investigation providers where independent investigations are required.
  • Ensure clear processes, documentation, and escalation protocols are maintained for Conflict of Interest and Whistleblower Programs related matters.
  • Prepare periodic reporting for senior management and relevant governance forums.

Compliance Training

  • Lead the Compliance Training team responsible for global mandatory compliance training.
  • Oversee training strategy, annual planning, delivery, tracking, reporting, and completion governance.
  • Coordinate global compliance training requirements while supporting local market needs and jurisdiction-specific obligations.
  • Work with subject matter experts to ensure training content is practical, relevant, current, and engaging.
  • Drive improvements in training completion, effectiveness, learner experience, and management reporting.
  • Manage relationship with Compliance-focused industry associations such as ACAMS and ACFCS to provide access to latest trends and topics to the Compliance team.

Compliance Reputation and External Engagement

  • Partner with External Communications, Marketing, Public Policy, Legal, and relevant business stakeholders on public-facing compliance initiatives.
  • Support public relations and reputation-building engagements that improve the external perception of the company’s compliance program.
  • Help develop narratives, materials, and messaging that communicate the company’s commitment to compliance, transparency, and responsible growth.
  • Identify opportunities to showcase compliance maturity, industry engagement, and thought leadership.

Compliance Culture and Engagement

  • Partner with Compliance and Human Resources leadership to identify culture gaps, engagement opportunities, and practical actions to strengthen team alignment.
  • Lead initiatives to build and maintain a strong culture of compliance across the global Compliance organization.
  • Design and execute engagement programs that reinforce ethical behavior, accountability, regulatory awareness, and ownership.
  • Develop messaging, campaigns, and internal initiatives that make compliance expectations clear, practical, and embedded into day-to-day operations.
  • Track and report on culture-related metrics, feedback, participation, and improvement actions.

Кого мы ищем

  • 7+ years of relevant experience in compliance, risk, legal, audit, regulatory affairs, governance, financial crime, ethics, conduct, or a related field.
  • Strong understanding of compliance program management in a regulated or high-growth environment.
  • Experience managing global or multi-market compliance initiatives.
  • Proven ability to lead complex programs with multiple stakeholders, timelines, dependencies, and governance requirements.
  • Strong project management skills, including planning, execution, reporting, issue tracking, and remediation management.
  • Experience overseeing or coordinating audits, independent reviews, third-party assessments, or regulatory reviews.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to simplify complex topics for different audiences.
  • Strong stakeholder management skills, including working with senior leadership, regional teams, external vendors, and cross-functional partners.
  • High judgment, discretion, and integrity, especially when handling sensitive matters such as whistleblower reports or conflicts of interest.
  • Comfortable operating in a fast-paced, remote, global, and rapidly evolving environment.
