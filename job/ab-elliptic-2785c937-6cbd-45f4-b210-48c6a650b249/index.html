Elliptic has been tracking down crypto crime since 2013, trusted by banks, businesses, and governments worldwide to keep the financial system safe. Today, our compliance tools cover the large majority of global crypto exchange volume, and a $120m Series D is fueling the next stage of that work.

Elliptic is building an AI fluent workforce. Across our Product, Engineering, Design and Intelligence we’re going beyond giving everyone the tools. We’re setting new standards. Using AI to transform how we build and work every day to deliver on our market opportunity. Put another way our teams iterate alongside agentic systems applying judgement to build world class systems.

This role is an Adoptive AI fluency role. That means AI has already changed how you work, not just how fast, and you enjoy using AI and agentic systems to operate more effectively.

You’ll own the vision for our Real-time Risk solutions, building capabilities that detect financial crime the moment it happens and let some of the biggest names in crypto, financial services and government act on emerging threats as they unfold.

What you’ll bring

- Experience in a highly technical product role, working with large datasets and/or complex engineering systems (5+ years of product experience)

- Strong communication skills: you make technically complex concepts clear to any audience

- An entrepreneurial mindset, and a genuine enjoyment of spending time with customers, prospects and partners

- Fluency with AI tools as part of your daily workflow, you use them to move faster and think better, and you know when not to trust them

Bonus points for blockchain analytics or intelligence experience in the public or private sector.

Что предстоит делать

- Design and deliver scalable, real-time solutions that detect compliance risks and alert customers when activity crosses their risk thresholds

- Work with Engineering and Design to break down complex analytics challenges and make the right technical trade-offs

- Build and maintain the roadmap, and become Elliptic’s subject matter expert on Real-time Risk

- Partner with Marketing, Sales and Customer teams on go-to-market, and use customer feedback and usage data to keep improving the product

Что мы предлагаем

- Hybrid working: work from almost anywhere for up to 90 days a year

- Home office budget: £500 to set up your space

- Learning & development: $1,000 a year for anything, agreed with your manager, that supports your growth

- Leave: 25 days of annual leave plus bank holidays, and an extra day off for your birthday

- Parental leave: 16 weeks fully paid for eligible employees, regardless of gender or whether you become a parent by birth or adoption

- Private health insurance: through Bupa

- Life assurance: cover of 4x your salary for your beneficiaries

- Mental health: full access to Spill

- Cycle to Work scheme

We hire for trajectory as much as track record. If this sounds like you, apply even if you don’t tick every box, we’d genuinely rather meet you than miss you.
