ROLE CONTEXT

Pennylane currently operates around 50,000 business accounts through a BaaS model with Swan and is applying for Établissement de paiement and Société de financement licences. As we prepare to onboard new customers under our own licensed entity from mid-2027, we are building our first line of defence across onboarding, transaction monitoring, fraud and risk operations.

As Transaction Monitoring & Fraud Operations Lead, you will be one of the first operational leaders in this greenfield setup. Reporting to the Head of FinCrime Operations, you will lead first-line transaction monitoring and fraud operations across Pennylane’s payment activities within a single team. You will own first-line alert handling and fraud case management, with clear escalation paths to Compliance and Risk.

Your mission will be to build and launch Pennylane’s first-line transaction monitoring and fraud operations, then scale the team and its workflows as volumes grow—while maintaining strong decision quality, auditability and customer protection.

This is a high-autonomy role with direct exposure to a strategic regulatory project and close collaboration with Product, Compliance and Risk. You will help shape how Pennylane protects the financial activity of French SMEs as it brings more of its regulated operations in-house.

Обязанности

- Own end-to-end first-line transaction monitoring and fraud operations for business customers, ensuring that alert triage, investigations, customer outreach and escalations are completed in line with policies set by Compliance and Risk and within agreed SLAs.

- Translate AML/CFT and fraud policies into operational procedures, decision trees and analyst playbooks, and provide evidence-based recommendations on alert quality and operational bottlenecks.

- Review complex or high-risk alerts within the first-line mandate, oversee post-onboarding fraud cases and ensure that escalations, customer complaints and law-enforcement requests are handled with complete, auditable records.

- Implement the QA and reporting framework, monitor operational and risk indicators, and support Compliance during controls testing, audits and regulatory reviews.

- Recruit, onboard and lead the transaction monitoring and fraud analyst team, combining hands-on support with effective coaching and a culture of rigour, accountability and continuous learning.

- Establish the team’s performance and capacity framework, covering SLAs, productivity, quality, backlogs, false-positive rates and hiring needs.

- Partner with Product and Tech to test and improve transaction-monitoring, fraud and back-office workflows, prioritising automation while identifying the controls and decisions that should remain manual.

FIRST 6–12 MONTHS

- Deliver approved operating procedures, analyst playbooks and escalation rules before go-live.

- Establish a capacity and hiring plan based on observed alert volumes and handling times.

- Validate that alert queues, evidence collection, account measures and second-line handovers work operationally before launch.

- By go-live, have first-line monitoring operational for new customers, with trained coverage, agreed SLAs, documented QA controls and named escalation owners.
