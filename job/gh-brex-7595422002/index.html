Why join us

Brex is the intelligent finance platform that enables companies to spend smarter and move faster in more than 200 markets. By combining global corporate cards and banking with intuitive spend management, bill pay, and travel software, Brex enables founders and finance teams to accelerate operations, gain real-time visibility, and control spend effortlessly. Brex’s AI-native automation and world-class service eliminate manual expense and accounting tasks for customers so they can focus on what matters most. Tens of thousands of the world's best companies run on Brex, including DoorDash, Coinbase, Robinhood, Zoom, Plaid, Reddit, and SeatGeek.

Working at Brex allows you to push your limits, challenge the status quo, and collaborate with some of the brightest minds in the industry. We’re committed to building a diverse team and inclusive culture and believe your potential should only be limited by how big you can dream. Design at Brex

Design at Brex creates the experiences that make complex systems feel effortless through thoughtful, scalable design. Whether crafting interfaces, shaping product strategy, or harnessing AI to empower our users, we obsess over quality and clarity. This is a team where creativity meets high craft, and where designers grow by building for ambitious customers.

Что предстоит делать

Brex is looking for a Staff brand designer to join the Design team, a cross-disciplinary group of creatives including product designers, brand designers, content design, operations, and marketing strategists. Our team’s mission is to build a category-defining brand and meaningfully move key business metrics by delivering world-class creative.

Your role will be to drive the evolution of Brex's brand identity system, pushing our visual language forward while maintaining cohesion across all touch points. You'll be instrumental in defining how our brand shows up in the world, from establishing new design patterns and brand expressions to reimagining our visual identity for the next phase of growth. This is a strategic and hands-on role that will require you to work closely with Marketing leaders, Web and Product teams, and creative partners to ensure our brand identity is distinctive, scalable, and reflects our ambition. You are a systems thinker who can envision the big picture while obsessing over the craft details that bring a brand to life.

Where you’ll work

This role will be based in our San Francisco, Seattle, or New York office. We are a hybrid environment that combines the energy and connections of being in the office with the benefits and flexibility of working from home. We currently require a minimum of three coordinated days in the office per week, Monday, Wednesday and Thursday. As a perk, we also have up to four weeks per year of fully remote work!

Обязанности

  • Own and evolve Brex’s brand identity system, including visual language, typography, color, illustration, and brand patterns
  • Define and maintain brand standards that balance consistency with creative flexibility across teams and channels
  • Translate company strategy and brand vision into clear, compelling visual direction in partnership with leadership
  • Design and deliver new brand expressions that advance the identity while preserving recognition and equity
  • Lead high-impact brand work beyond the core system, including special campaigns, sub-brands, and select product or service launches
  • Art direct and contribute to brand campaigns, collaborating with external partners and internal teams to ensure exceptional craft
  • Provide guidance and feedback across the organization to raise the quality and effectiveness of brand execution

Требования

  • 7+ years of experience in brand design or art direction, with a strong focus on identity systems and brand evolution
  • A portfolio demonstrating strategic thinking, system design, and high-quality execution across identities and campaigns
  • Deep expertise in typography, color, composition, and building scalable visual systems
  • Strong ability to articulate design decisions and build alignment with cross-functional partners and senior stakeholders
  • Experience working across multiple workstreams in fast-paced, evolving environments
  • Fluency in Figma and Adobe Creative Suite, with comfort prototyping and iterating quickly
  • Practical experience using AI tools in the creative process with sound judgment on when and how to apply them

Дополнительный плюс

  • Motion, video, or illustration experience applied to brand systems or campaigns
  • Experience art directing complex brand work with external creative partners
