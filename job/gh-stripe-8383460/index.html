Кто мы

О Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

О команде

The Filing and Remittance team within Stripe Tax is building systems and experiences that help businesses move from collecting tax to completing their filing and payment obligations. We bring together filing, remittance, and reporting to make that journey simpler, accurate and more reliable.

Что предстоит делать

You’ll lead engineers building and scaling filing, remittance, and reporting products. You’ll shape the roadmap with Product and tax-domain experts, develop a team with deep ownership, and balance new capabilities our users need with the reliability required of systems that handle financial data and users’ money.

Обязанности

  • Coach and develop engineers, build shared expertise, and hire strong teammates.
  • Shape and deliver a user-driven roadmap across filing, remittance, and reporting.
  • Guide technical decisions and uphold correctness, reliability, and operational quality as the products scale.
  • Partner with Product, Design, tax experts, Operations, and platform teams to solve problems end to end.

Кто вы

We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum Requirements

  • Experience directly managing and developing engineering teams, with a track record of delivering meaningful product outcomes.
  • A strong technical background in backend systems and the ability to guide architectural and operational tradeoffs.
  • Ability to turn ambiguous user needs into clear priorities and work effectively with cross-functional partners and domain experts.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with tax, payments, billing, payroll, accounting, or other financially sensitive workflows.
  • Experience scaling an early-stage product while maintaining established, business-critical systems.
