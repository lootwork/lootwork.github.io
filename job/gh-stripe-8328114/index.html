Кто мы

О Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.

О роли

Stripe helps businesses accept payments online and in person. We’re looking for a product management leader based in Singapore to build and lead a local payments PM team spanning cards, local payment methods, and Terminal.

This leader will help shape the full payments stack: the capabilities that enable businesses to accept payments across digital experiences and physical locations. You will own a defined portfolio of payment capabilities, partner closely with Singapore-based engineering leadership, and build a team of PMs who can take products from user insight through launch and adoption.

An important part of the role is establishing Terminal product leadership in APAC and connecting our online and in-person payment capabilities more closely. You will bring proximity to APAC users, partners, and engineering teams into global product decisions—building capabilities that address local needs and can scale beyond a single market.

This is a hands-on management role. You should be comfortable moving between product strategy, technical and commercial tradeoffs, coaching PMs, recruiting, and the details required to get a product into users’ hands.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build and lead the Singapore payments PM team. Recruit, develop, and manage PMs across a growing portfolio of cards, local payment methods, and Terminal capabilities. Establish clear ownership, a high-quality bar, and an effective operating rhythm.
  • Own product strategy and execution for your portfolio. Translate user needs and business opportunities into a focused roadmap, make explicit investment choices, and remain accountable for product quality, adoption, and results.
  • Connect online and in-person payments. Identify where shared capabilities can create a better experience across channels, while respecting the different requirements of each payment method and environment.
  • Partner deeply with engineering. Work closely with Singapore-based engineering leadership and global counterparts on product direction, technical tradeoffs, delivery, reliability, and long-term platform investments.
  • Build from direct user and partner insight. Spend time with businesses, platforms, and ecosystem partners to understand their workflows, uncover unmet needs, and distinguish repeatable product opportunities from bespoke requests.
  • Balance enterprise needs with scalable adoption. Help us serve complex businesses without building a collection of one-off solutions. Invest in reusable capabilities, clear APIs, and experiences that make payments easier to integrate and operate.
  • Drive decisions across teams. Align engineering, design, data science, risk, operations, and go-to-market partners around clear priorities, dependencies, and measurable outcomes.
  • Bring an APAC perspective to global product development. Ensure regional payment methods, market infrastructure, and in-person use cases inform the products we build.

Кто вы

  • An experienced product manager with a track record of shipping technically complex products and delivering meaningful user and business outcomes.
  • A people leader who has managed and developed PMs, provided clear feedback, and helped teams make better product decisions.
  • Comfortable being hands-on while building a team: you can personally lead an ambiguous product area without becoming a bottleneck for others.
  • Technically fluent, with experience working closely with engineering on APIs, platforms, infrastructure, or other complex systems.
  • Strong at prioritization. You can make difficult tradeoffs across customer commitments, product quality, growth opportunities, and foundational investments.
  • Able to understand commercial economics alongside user experience and technical feasibility.
  • An effective collaborator across functions, geographies, and time zones, with clear written communication and sound judgment.
  • Motivated to build a team in Singapore and work closely with local engineering leadership.

Preferred experience

  • Product leadership in payments, financial infrastructure, or commerce.
  • Experience with one or more of cards, local payment methods, in-person payments, or enterprise payment integrations. We do not expect expertise in all of them.
  • Experience building products for APAC markets or working with regional payment ecosystems.
  • Experience hiring PMs or building a product team through a period of expansion.
  • Experience turning complex enterprise requirements into capabilities that serve a broader user base.
