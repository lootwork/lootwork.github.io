Join FxPro: a leading international fintech company. Be a part of our expanding international team, with offices in Limassol, London, Nassau, Dubai, and Yerevan. FxPro boasts a diverse workforce of over 600 employees representing 50 nationalities, making it an exciting and dynamic workplace. At FxPro, we see each team member as an integral part of our success story.

As a Senior Motion Designer, you will play a pivotal role in crafting visually stunning animations, videos, and motion graphics that will elevate our brand and enhance our marketing efforts.

Обязанности

  • Responsible for the entire video asset production process, from concept to final delivery, ensuring high-quality output
  • Create, resize, and localize advertisements in various dimensions and languages to meet the diverse needs of our target audiences
  • Work alongside internal and external editors, producers, and designers to seamlessly execute and deliver projects on time and within scope
  • Ensure all created assets comply with company guidelines and design standards to maintain a consistent brand identity
  • Integrate AI-generated content into creative workflows, combining AI tools with traditional production techniques to deliver high-quality visual content
  • Explore and implement AI tools, MCP integrations, and automation solutions to optimize workflows, accelerate production, and reduce repetitive tasks
  • Develop custom scripts and automated solutions using AI coding assistants such as Claude and Codex to streamline animation processes and address specific production needs

Требования

  • Proven experience in motion design and video production, with a strong portfolio showcasing your skills and creativity
  • Proficiency in motion design software and graphic design tools (After Effects, Illustrator, Figma, Adobe Audition, Cinema 4D)
  • Strong creative and conceptual thinking skills with the ability to transform ideas into visually appealing content
  • Strong sense of composition, visual rhythm, timing, and pacing
  • Advanced video editing skills, with the ability to create engaging and well-paced content
  • Keen attention to detail to ensure the highest quality output and compliance with brand standards
  • Strong understanding of AI-powered content creation workflows, with the ability to produce high-quality AI-generated content and seamlessly combine it with traditional production techniques
  • Experience automating creative workflows using MCP integrations for tools such as Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma, Blender, and Cinema 4D
  • Experience developing custom scripts and tools using AI coding assistants such as Claude and Codex to automate animation tasks and optimize production workflows
  • Proactive approach to researching, testing, and implementing new AI tools and technologies to improve creative efficiency and production speed
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills in English

Interview Steps

  • Recruiter Screening call (~ 30 minutes)
  • Technical Interview with Lead Motion Designer & Creative Producer (~ 60 minutes)
  • Test task
  • Final interview with Lead Motion Designer & Creative Producer (~ 60 minutes)

  • Competitive compensation package
  • Flexibility of remote work
  • 21 days off and 10 sick leaves annually
  • Enjoyable break during Public Cyprus Holidays
