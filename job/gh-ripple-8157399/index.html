Every payment, every custody operation, and every line of code at Ripple runs on the infrastructure our platform teams build. We operate a multi-cloud, Kubernetes-native environment spanning multiple regions and regulatory jurisdictions, engineered for the availability and security standards our financial institution customers expect — and for the developer velocity our engineers need. The systems this organization owns are the substrate for everything Ripple ships.

We have a big vision and the ambition to match. We are seeking the same in engineering leaders. You see infrastructure not as plumbing but as leverage: the difference between an engineering organization that ships weekly and one that ships continuously, between a platform that survives a regional outage and one that doesn't notice it happened.

We're looking for a hands-on engineering leader to develop and guide the teams responsible for Ripple's cloud infrastructure, Kubernetes platform, and developer experience.

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead the technical and organizational strategy managing multi-cloud infrastructure and Kubernetes platform at Ripple, balancing reliability, security, cost, and developer velocity y
  • Lead multiple teams and managers across compute platform, cloud infrastructure, developer experience, and reliability engineering
  • Define and deliver the multi-year infrastructure roadmap, including cluster architecture, workload orchestration, service networking, infrastructure-as-code, and CI/CD
  • Set the standard for operational excellence — SLOs, incident response, on-call health, blameless postmortems, and dynamic rollout practices — and hold the organization to it
  • Drive multi-cloud strategy and execution: workload placement, portability, avoiding accidental lock-in, disaster recovery, and multi-region resilience
  • Partner with Security and Compliance to build infrastructure that satisfies SOC 2, ISO 27001, and jurisdiction-specific regulatory requirements without slowing engineering down
  • Own cloud spend for your organization, with clear accountability for efficiency and unit economics as the platform scales
  • Provide hands-on technical leadership — architecture reviews, design docs, incident escalations, and the occasional pull request
  • Mentor and grow the professional and technical development of your team, both managers and individual contributors
  • Establish team objectives and quarterly plans in alignment with business and organizational goals
  • Translate infrastructure investments into terms that resonate with engineers, product leaders, and executives alike
  • Scale the organization by attracting, hiring, and developing exceptional leaders and engineers at all levels

Кого мы ищем

  • 12+ years of hands-on software and infrastructure engineering experience, including 5+ years leading teams and managing managers
  • Deep, production-grade experience with Kubernetes at scale — multi-cluster and multi-tenant operations, upgrade strategy, workload isolation, autoscaling, and the failure modes you only learn about the hard way
  • Experience operating across more than one major cloud provider (AWS, GCP, Azure) and a clear point of view on where multi-cloud pays for itself and where it doesn't
  • Strong background in infrastructure-as-code and declarative operations (Terraform, Helm, GitOps tooling such as Argo CD or Flux)
  • Track record building highly available, secure distributed systems and the observability to run them (metrics, logging, tracing, alerting that people actually trust)
  • Experience with service networking and service mesh technologies, and with securing traffic and identity across cloud boundaries
  • Demonstrated ability to improve developer experience measurably — build and deploy times, environment provisioning, self-service platform capabilities
  • Experience partnering with security and regulatory oversight functions in a regulated environment; comfort with least-privilege, key management, and audit requirements
  • Experience with Agile development, with a focus on robust design, scalability, and security
  • Solid leadership and communication skills, with the judgment to know when to decide, when to delegate, and when to dig in yourself
  • Eagerness to work openly and collaboratively with a diverse team, with a positive attitude and a passion for sharing knowledge
  • BS or equivalent experience in Computer Science or similar…
