О роли

Fraud doesn't sleep. And neither does our ambition to stop it.

At Pleo, we've built one of Europe's leading spend management platforms. But with scale comes risk, and our Fraud Operations Team plays a critical role in investigating, responding to, and mitigating fraud, turning detection signals into action and ensuring our customers are protected. And now, we're growing the team.

We're looking for a Fraud Operations Analyst to join us. You'll work closely with our Fraud Operations Specialists and Fraud Operations Manager to process fraud alerts, investigate cases, and make sure nothing slips through the cracks. This is a hands-on, operational role, perfect for someone who wants to build deep fraud expertise in a fast-moving fintech environment, with the structure and guidance to grow.

Что предстоит делать

- Monitor and process fraud alerts generated by our detection tools in real time, within SLA

- Investigate card fraud cases end-to-end using our Back Office systems, identifying transactions, verifying card details, flagging suspicious activity, and taking containment actions

- Manage inbound fraud requests via our Freshdesk queue, triaging cases from Customer Support and routing them correctly

- Process and monitor the Fraud inbox and Customer Dispute Forms for chargeback-related cases

- Document cases accurately and thoroughly in our case management system so nothing falls through the gaps

- Conduct liability assessments on fraud cases, determining the appropriate outcome and escalating to our Legal team for final sign-off where required

- Proactively identify emerging fraud patterns and typologies from day-to-day casework, and collaborate with the Decision Intelligence team to feed insights into rule proposals and detection improvements

You'll thrive in this role if you

- Have hands-on experience with fraud monitoring tools

- Have a background in fraud operations, financial crime, payments, or customer operations

- Are highly detail-oriented and comfortable working within structured processes and SLAs

- Can stay calm and methodical when case volumes are high

- Communicate clearly in writing, both internally and when supporting external queries

- Are fluent in English

- Are familiar with chargeback processes and card scheme rules (Mastercard/Visa)

- Have knowledge of relevant regulatory frameworks around liability, including UK and EU requirements
