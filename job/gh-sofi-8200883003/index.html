О роли

As a Senior Staff Incident Response Engineer on the Cyber Defense team, you will serve as the principal technical authority and subject matter expert for incident response within SoFi’s Security Operations Center (SOC). You will lead investigations and mitigation actions against threats targeting crypto and digital asset platforms, while securing our core enterprise and cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP, applications, identity, and endpoints).

This position sits within Incident Response (IR) in SoFi’s Cyber Defense organization. In partnership with our Cyber Incident Commander, you will handle high-severity incidents, architect containment workflows, and lead technical forensics across distributed systems. Pairing specialized digital asset threat knowledge with an engineering skill set, you will perform forensic triage, build automated investigation tools, and translate findings into hardened detection mechanisms and playbooks across the enterprise.

Что предстоит делать

  • Serve as the SOC’s subject matter expert for digital asset and cryptocurrency security, investigating and neutralizing threats directed at digital asset platforms and services.
  • Apply blockchain investigation methods, forensic telemetry, and analytical tooling to execute scoping, containment, and recovery operations.
  • Investigate and resolve security alerts and incidents across digital asset, cloud, application, identity, and endpoint environments, driving issues from initial triage to root cause and remediation.
  • Perform forensic triage and deep data study, including cloud and application log review, endpoint artifact inspection, evidence preservation, and timeline reconstruction.
  • Write clean, maintainable code (Python, Go, or SQL) to test large datasets, correlate telemetry across disparate log sources, and automate evidence collection and enrichment workflows.
  • Partner cross-functionally with Detection Engineering, Application Security, and Core Infrastructure teams to construct and tune threat detections, address logging gaps, and track systemic remediations to completion.
  • Partner with SOC analysts and the Cyber Incident Commander on incident operations, providing technical direction on complex investigations and high-severity incidents.
  • Develop and maintain crypto-specific and cloud-specific incident response runbooks and response procedures.
  • Document investigations thoroughly and deliver technical findings and post-incident reviews to technical and executive stakeholders.
  • Lead technical simulations and tabletop exercises encompassing digital asset and smart contract failure modes.
  • Evaluate and engineer automation tooling, including SOAR and AI-assisted investigation workflows, to increase response efficiency, consistency, and coverage.
  • Mentor engineers, conduct peer reviews, and elevate the technical standards of the SOC through threat education and reusable software tooling.

What you’ll need

  • Education & Minimum Experience Requirements: Bachelor’s degree + 4+ years of relevant experience OR Master’s degree + 3+ years of relevant experience.
  • 8+ years of total experience in incident response, security operations, or related security engineering functions within enterprise environments.
  • Proven hands-on experience investigating security incidents involving cryptocurrency, blockchain protocols, or digital asset platforms.
  • Solid understanding of blockchain fundamentals, smart contract interaction models, and digital asset attack vectors.
  • Demonstrated experience utilizing blockchain analytics and forensic tooling.
  • Hands-on experience leading investigations across cloud (AWS, GCP, or Azure), containerized, application, endpoint, network, and identity environments.
  • Solid engineering background with proficiency in at least one modern language (such as Python or Go) and SQL, with the ability to build automated investigation tools and query high-volume datasets.
  • Solid grounding in digital forensics fundamentals, including chain of custody, evidence handling, memory inspection, and disk/log triage.
  • Comprehensive understanding of the incident response lifecycle and industry frameworks (such as NIST SP 800-61 and MITRE ATT&CK).
  • Hands-on knowledge of enterprise security platforms including SIEM, EDR, identity providers, and cloud security posture tooling.
  • Solid understanding of operating system internals (Linux, macOS, Windows) and enterprise networking fundamentals.
  • Proven ability to work cross-functionally, align partners, and maintain composure while managing high-pressure incidents.
  • Clear written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to communicate technical findings to varied audiences.
