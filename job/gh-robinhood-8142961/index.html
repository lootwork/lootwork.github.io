We're looking for talented and motivated Android Engineers to join our award-winning team and help expand Robinhood's mobile experience.

As a Software Engineering Intern, Android, you'll own meaningful work end-to-end — contributing to new features, shaping the Android platform, and building products that change how America views finance. You'll be embedded on a real team, partnering directly with experienced engineers and our award-winning design team to ship software that millions of people use every day.

This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA or New York, NY office(s), with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Partner with a dedicated mentor who will guide you from onboarding through shipping, deepening your understanding of our tech stack and financial domain along the way
  • Collaborate with our award-winning design team throughout the full product lifecycle
  • Contribute to new features and help build products that will change how America views finance
  • Explore and build innovative Android UI/UX
  • Work with technologies such as Kotlin, RxJava 2, Retrofit/OkHttp, Dagger 2, and Room

Что вы приносите

  • Currently enrolled in a full-time, degree-seeking program with an expected graduation date of Winter 2027 or Spring 2028
  • A solid foundation in software engineering
  • An interest in front-end work, specifically visually driven product design and shaping user experience
  • Familiarity with Android components and experience building or contributing to at least one Android app
  • Experience building, maintaining, or contributing to open-source projects
  • Familiarity with Kotlin, RxJava 2, Dagger 2, and other libraries in our tech stack
  • A self-starter mentality and a proactive approach
