Raisin is the world’s leading platform for savings and investment products. Founded in 2012, the FinTech connects consumers with banks in the EU, the UK and the US. This gives consumers better interest rates and banks a diversified form of refinancing. Our vision is to offer savings and investments without barriers and thus open up the global 160 trillion euro market.

Raisin currently employs more than 800 people from over 75 countries worldwide. Today, the platform holds over 80 billion euros in assets from more than one million investors which have accrued over 5 billion euros in returns.

Team

The People Solutions team serves as the strategic engine room of Raisin’s People & Culture team by building a secure, data-driven, and compliant infrastructure that scales the organization. By bridging the gap between strategy and technology, the team optimizes HRIS systems to eliminate manual work, leverages analytics and AI to drive smarter business decisions, and acts as a compliance anchor managing audits, policies, and risk.

Ваши обязанности

  • You establish People policy and process standards by authoring, reviewing, and maintaining the global and local People policy frameworks, ensuring all documentation is logical, accessible, and structured according to company standards.
  • You look after AI-readiness and ethical governance in the People domain by reviewing existing policies and processes to ensure they are compliant with emerging AI regulations, address corresponding risks, and protect employee data privacy.
  • You partner with the wider People Solutions team to ensure that automated People processes and AI-driven tools have clear human-in-the-loop overrides and ethical safeguards.
  • You design, implement, and maintain a Risk and Controls Framework specifically for People processes that is in alignment with Raisin’s global compliance framework.
  • You track upcoming changes in employment law, data protection (e.g., GDPR, local privacy laws), and AI regulations and proactively update policies and process accordingly before they take effect.
  • You align closely with the People and Culture leadership and conduct regular spot-checks and internal mini-audits to ensure teams are executing People processes strictly according to documented standards.
  • You serve as the primary point of contact for all internal, external, and third-party People audits and governance-related reporting requests.
  • You manage and fulfill documentation requests from external supervisory bodies, labor ministries, and regulatory agencies.
  • You design and oversee in close collaboration with Raisin’s People and Culture leadership the implementation of Corrective Action Plans (CAPs) across the broader People team if audit gaps are identified.

Профиль кандидата

  • You hold a Bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Psychology, Business Administration, Legal Studies, or a related field.
  • You have 5+ years of progressive experience in Human Resources, with at least 2–3 years focused explicitly on HR Compliance, HR Audit, or People Operations Governance.
  • You have strong People domain knowledge incl. People processes across the employee lifecycle.
  • You have direct experience drafting, structuring, and maintaining comprehensive HR policies, employee handbooks, and standard operating procedures (SOPs).
  • You have a deep understanding of regional employment standards, labor laws, and strict data privacy regulations (e.g., GDPR) as well as strong foundational knowledge of emerging frameworks impacting HR technology, such as the EU AI Act.
  • You have strong communication skills and you feel comfortable collaborating with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • You have the ability to "speak tech" and understand how data flows through an HRIS.
  • You have the ability to act as a bridge between rigid legal/audit teams and fast-moving People teams without causing friction.
  • You have a strong focus on data integrity, semantic clarity in documentation, and spotting subtle gaps in process controls.
  • You are fluent in English. Additionally, professional working proficiency in German is required to interpret local labor laws.
