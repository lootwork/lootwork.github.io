Job Description

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON

Main challenge

You'll help build and improve the AI features of a storytelling app for children that's live in many languages. Your main focus will be LLM-powered story generation: making stories creative, consistent, age-appropriate and safe, in every supported language. Depending on project needs, you'll also work on computer vision (recognising real-world toys), generative illustration pipelines, and translation and text-to-speech automation.

What it means on a daily basis

  • Design, test and improve prompts and LLM workflows for story generation, using structured evaluation (quality, consistency, age-appropriateness, safety)
  • Build small evaluation datasets and scripts to measure output quality and catch regressions
  • Help maintain the image recognition pipeline: collect, clean and annotate data, run training or fine-tuning experiments, and analyse edge cases (lighting, occlusion, motion blur)
  • Support generative illustration workflows (e.g. ComfyUI): automate pipelines and improve visual consistency across characters and scenes
  • Write Python tools to automate translation and text-to-speech for many languages, including validation and quality checks

Who you would be working with

  • AI engineers, Unity developers, QA and content/localization teams
  • Cross-functional and international teams, depending on project needs

Требования

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED

  • Solid Python skills (scripting, data handling, calling APIs)
  • Hands-on experience with LLMs: calling LLM APIs, prompt engineering, or basic RAG/agent projects
  • Basic understanding of computer vision tasks (classification, detection) and machine learning fundamentals
  • At least one real project (personal, academic or internship) with code or a demo you can show
  • Good written and spoken English (for writing prompts and documentation and working with international teams)

Кто вы

  • A versatile builder who's comfortable switching between LLM, vision and automation tasks
  • Curious and fast to learn, keeping up with new AI models and tools
  • Detail-oriented, especially about output quality and content safety for children
  • Organized in documenting experiments and results
  • Proactive in spotting problems and suggesting improvements
  • Comfortable working both independently and in a team
