О Opportunity

We are looking for an experienced engineer to lead the architecture and hands-on development of the ML and data infrastructure behind our risk and fraud prevention systems. You will help teams move models from experimentation into reliable production services, improve model execution performance, and build scalable data foundations for training, evaluation, and real-time decisions.

Что предстоит делать

  • Improve model inference latency, throughput, resource utilization, and cost. Profile the full prediction path —from feature retrieval and preprocessing to model execution — and deliver measurable improvements without unacceptable model-quality regressions.
  • Build reusable infrastructure for model packaging, versioning, deployment, serving, monitoring, and rollback across cloud platforms and regions.
  • Design and optimize offline data pipelines on Databricks, Apache Spark, and warehouse/lakehouse technologies, addressing data modeling, partitioning, joins, shuffles, skew, incremental processing, and cost.
  • Connect offline and online feature pipelines, ensuring point-in-time correctness, consistent feature definitions, freshness, lineage, and reproducible backfills.
  • Evolve reliable feature services and data integrations supporting risk models and decision systems.
  • Set technical direction, mentor engineers, and lead delivery across ML, data engineering, cloud infrastructure, security, and product teams.

What We Look For In You

  • A record of building and operating large-scale distributed systems, ML infrastructure, or data platforms, with technical ownership across teams.
  • Hands-on experience optimizing production model performance, supported by measurable improvements in latency, throughput, utilization, or cost.
  • Deep understanding of Databricks and Spark, or comparable offline data platforms, including execution plans, distributed query optimization, storage layouts, data correctness, and workload cost management.
  • Experience with the model lifecycle, including training/serving integration, validation, deployment, observability, and rollback.
  • Strong Python, SQL, and at least one backend or systems language such as Java, Scala, Go, or C++.
  • Ability to turn ambiguous requirements into clear architecture and deliver maintainable systems through hands-on engineering and technical leadership.

Будет плюсом

  • SageMaker, Alibaba Cloud PAI/EAS, or comparable ML platforms.
  • Inference optimization using tools such as ONNX Runtime, TensorRT, or Triton.
  • Delta Lake, MLflow, feature stores, Kafka, Flink, and low-latency data stores.
  • Fraud detection or financial risk systems.
  • Fluency in Mandarin
