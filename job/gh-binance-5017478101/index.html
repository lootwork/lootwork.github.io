О роли

We are looking for an Investigation Analyst to join our Screening Escalations & Geofencing team on a 12-month contract. You will investigate suspicious login activities, review risk alerts, and support timely, evidence-based decisions to strengthen platform security and risk controls.

Что предстоит делать

  • Investigate geofencing alerts and suspicious login patterns.
  • Analyze case information, IP addresses, and user activity to assess potential risks.
  • Conduct case reviews, issue Requests for Information (RFIs), and follow up where needed.
  • Make evidence-based block/unblock decisions in accordance with internal SOPs.
  • Document findings, escalate complex cases, and meet established SLAs.

Кого мы ищем

  • Experience in investigations, fraud, financial crime, compliance, or risk operations.
  • Strong analytical skills, investigative thinking, and attention to detail.
  • Good judgment and the ability to assess cases using multiple information sources.
  • Strong English communication and documentation skills.
  • Ability to manage case volumes in a fast-paced environment.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience in fintech, e-commerce, banking, payments, or digital assets.
  • Familiarity with IP analysis, geolocation, or suspicious account or user activity.
  • Experience with case management or investigation tools.
