Nium, the Leader in Real-Time Global Payments

Nium, the leading global infrastructure for real-time cross-border payments, was founded on the mission to deliver the global payments infrastructure of tomorrow, today. With the onset of the global economy, its payments infrastructure is shaping how banks, fintechs, and businesses everywhere collect, convert, and disburse funds instantly across borders. Its payout network supports 100 currencies and spans 190+ countries, 100 of which in real-time. Funds can be disbursed to accounts, wallets, and cards and collected locally in 40 markets. Nium's growing card issuance business is already available in 34 countries. Nium holds regulatory licenses and authorizations in more than 40 countries, enabling seamless onboarding, rapid integration, and compliance – independent of geography. The company is co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore.

О роли

The Sales Operations Analyst is responsible for optimizing sales processes, improving data integrity, and providing actionable insights that drive revenue growth and sales productivity. This role partners closely with Sales, Marketing, and Revenue Operations to ensure the sales organization operates efficiently, scales effectively, and makes data-driven decisions.

Основные обязанности

  • Support the definition, tracking, and measurement of sales goals and quotas.
  • Partner with sales leadership to translate strategy into operational execution.
  • Produce, maintain, and automate recurring sales reports and dashboards (pipeline health, forecasting, win/loss, activity metrics).
  • Analyze market trends, sales performance, and customer behavior to identify risks and growth opportunities.
  • Present data insights clearly to sales leadership and cross-functional stakeholders.
  • Evaluate sales representative performance against KPIs.
  • Identify coaching opportunities, productivity gaps, and best practices.
  • Support territory planning, capacity modeling, and quota allocation.
  • Identify bottlenecks in the sales process and recommend scalable solutions.
  • Implement and enforce sales policies, workflows, and best practices.
  • Leverage automation and technology to reduce manual effort and increase selling time.
  • Partner with Marketing on lead flow, conversion analysis, and campaign effectiveness.
  • Collaborate with Finance and RevOps on forecasting accuracy and revenue planning.
  • Provide recommendations that align sales execution with broader business objectives.
  • Ensure accurate and timely data entry within the CRM system.
  • Improve CRM adoption, data quality, and reporting usability.
  • Support tool enhancements, integrations, and new technology rollouts.

Требования

  • Strong analytical skills with experience interpreting large data sets
  • Proficiency in CRM systems (e.g., Salesforce, HubSpot) and reporting tools
  • Ability to translate data into clear business recommendations
  • Experience supporting sales teams in a fast-paced environment
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills
