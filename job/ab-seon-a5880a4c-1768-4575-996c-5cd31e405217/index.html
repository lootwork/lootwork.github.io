SEON is the command center for fraud prevention and AML compliance, helping thousands of companies worldwide stop fraud, reduce risk and protect revenue. Powered by 900+ real-time, first-party data signals, SEON enriches customer profiles, flags suspicious behavior and streamlines compliance workflows - all from one place. SEON provides richer data, more flexible and transparent analysis, and faster time to value than any other provider on the market. We’ve helped companies reduce fraud by 95% and achieve 32x ROI, and we’re growing fast, thanks to our partnerships with some of the world’s most ambitious digital brands like Revolut, Wise, and Bilt.

You will join SEON's Performance Engineering team reporting to the VP of Architecture. As an Engineering Manager, you will lead the Performance Engineering team, which owns the latency, throughput, scalability and cost efficiency of SEON's real-time fraud-scoring platform.

The team's work spans database migrations and tuning, pre-aggregation and query optimization, load testing in pre-production, and making sure the platform handles customer traffic spikes without affecting other customers.

This position will be based in Budapest, on a hybrid schedule.

Что предстоит делать

- Lead and grow a team of 4 senior engineers, owning both their technical direction and their professional development

- Design and implement high-performance, low-latency distributed systems, and review designs from other teams for performance risk

- Continuously analyze production performance (latency percentiles, throughput, resource usage) to find bottlenecks and scalability issues before customers notice them

- Lead root-cause analysis of latency and capacity incidents, and drive the fixes through to production

- Prepare the platform for high-load periods through load testing and capacity planning

- Own the performance engineering roadmap and backlog, and prioritize them against business impact

- Actively participate in coding and development tasks, dedicating approximately 50% of work time to hands-on programming

- Run regular one-on-ones and performance reviews, and give clear, constructive feedback

- Define and report the team's performance KPIs (e.g., p99 latency, throughput) to engineering and business stakeholders

- Work closely with development, operations, and product management teams to ensure performance objectives are clearly understood and addressed

WHAT YOU’LL BRING

- Strong working knowledge of at least two of Java, TypeScript and Python, with the ability to profile and optimize code in them

- Proven track record of optimizing complex software systems for performance and scalability

- Strong understanding of distributed systems principles

- Experience with measuring and improving the performance of distributed systems

- Experience with performance testing tools and methodologies

- Expertise with Amazon Web Services (AWS)

- Deep experience tuning relational databases (we use PostgreSQL and YugabyteDB), including indexing, query plans and connection management

- Experience with distributed database technologies (e.g., ClickHouse, Elasticsearch, YugabyteDB)

- Experience with large-scale data migration practices with zero-downtime cutovers

- Hands-on experience with observability tooling (e.g., Prometheus, Grafana) and with Kubernetes

- Strong organizational skills and ability to work together with both technical and non-technical stakeholders

- Experience leading engineers directly, including one-on-ones, feedback and performance reviews

- Ability to work independently and prioritize your team's workload

- Experience and continuous curiosity on utilizing AI tools to improve engineering practices and SEON's product

- Excellent written and verbal English communication skills
