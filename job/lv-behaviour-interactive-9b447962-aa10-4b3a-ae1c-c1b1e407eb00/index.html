Are you ready to dive into the world of cutting-edge video game development? Headquartered in Montreal, Behaviour Interactive is at the forefront of the gaming industry, crafting unforgettable experiences across PC, console, and mobile platforms. We’re a dynamic, independent studio of over 1200 passionate employees, with studios in Toronto (Behaviour Toronto), Rotterdam (Behaviour Rotterdam) and in the UK (Behaviour UK–North & South). Our day to day revolves around two core business focuses: Services and Original Games. Our Services team collaborates with gaming and entertainment giants like Disney, Sony, Activision, and Nintendo, delivering exceptional work-for-hire development and pioneering immersive location-based entertainment (LBE). Meanwhile, our Original Games unit pushes our creative limits, with our signature IPs, including the iconic Dead by Daylight. Join us and be a part of the future of interactive entertainment!

At Behaviour Interactive, we believe in giving everyone a fair shot. We embrace a diverse and creative environment where everyone is celebrated. We value folks of all ages, sex, ethnicity, colour, ethnic or national origin, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, civil status, disability, or any other protected characteristics as per applicable law.

Help shape the next era of an iconic survival franchise.

For over a decade, 7 Days to Die has captivated more than 20 million players worldwide. Now, with Behaviour Interactive joining forces with The Fun Pimps, we’re entering an exciting new phase of growth, bringing together deep live game expertise and the creative legacy behind the franchise. This is a rare opportunity to help elevate a beloved game, scale its long-term potential, and shape what comes next for millions of players.

As Community Manager, Social & Content for 7 Days to Die, you will own the game's social content strategy and day-to-day social presence. Working closely with Product, Production, Marketing, and Community teams, you will create compelling platform-native content that engages players, strengthens community connections, and expands the game's reach across key social channels.

В этой роли вы будете

  • Own and execute the cross-platform social content strategy and content calendar across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Twitch, X, Steam, and emerging platforms.
  • Create engaging, platform-native content, including short-form videos, livestreams, and community-focused content, tailored to each channel and audience.
  • Partner with Product, Production, Marketing, Community, and Creator teams to support game updates, experimental builds, livestreams, events, and major releases.
  • Manage the publication of unreleased content while ensuring compliance with embargoes, approvals, and licensor requirements.
  • Monitor social conversations and community trends to identify opportunities, emerging topics, and potential risks, and use these insights to inform content strategy.
  • Track social performance metrics and deliver recommendations to continuously improve engagement, reach, and community growth.

Кого мы ищем

  • 5 to 9 years of professional experience, including at least 3 years managing social channels or creating content for games.
  • Strong expertise across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Twitch, X, Discord, Reddit, and gaming-focused social platforms.
  • Proven experience creating gaming-focused content from concept to publication, including video production, gameplay capture, and content editing.
  • Proficiency with Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Premiere Pro, OBS, NVIDIA ShadowPlay, or similar content creation tools.
  • Experience using analytics and social listening platforms such as Gamesight, Power BI, Tableau, Sprout Social, or comparable solutions.
  • A portfolio showcasing social content created for gaming audiences is required. Experience with creators, influencer programs, survival or sandbox games, and bilingualism in French and English are considered strong assets.

DANS CE RÔLE TU DEVRAS

  • Définir et mettre en œuvre la stratégie de contenu social multiplateforme ainsi que le calendrier éditorial sur TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Twitch, X, Steam et les plateformes émergentes;
  • Créer du contenu engageant et adapté à chaque plateforme, notamment des vidéos courtes, des diffusions en direct et du contenu axé sur la communauté, en fonction des audiences et des canaux ciblés;
  • Collaborer avec les équipes Produit, Production, Marketing, Communauté et Créateur·rice·s de contenu afin de soutenir les mises à jour du jeu, les versions expérimentales, les diffusions en direct, les événements et les lancements majeurs ;
  • Gérer la publication de contenu non annoncé tout en veillant au respect des embargos, des processus d’approbation et des exigences des donneur·euse·s de licence;
  • Assurer une veille des conversations sociales et des tendances communautaires afin d’identifier les opportunités, les sujets émergents et les risques potentiels, puis utiliser ces informations pour orienter la stratégie de contenu;
  • Suivre les indicateurs de performance des médias sociaux et formuler des recommandations afin d’améliorer en continu l’engagement, la portée et la croissance de la communauté.…
