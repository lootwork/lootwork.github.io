О Starling

Hybrid working: All Starling colleagues spend a minimum of 10 days per month in the office with flexibility to work the rest from home.

The Opportunity

As a Motion Designer, you’ll help shape how Starling comes to life through motion. Working across brand, marketing, campaigns and social, you’ll create thoughtful, expressive and beautifully crafted work that brings our ideas and personality to life.

Motion is an important part of our brand identity. We’ve developed a distinctive motion language built around confidence, clarity and a playful human touch. You’ll help build on this foundation, finding new and interesting ways to bring consistency, character and creativity to everything we make.

You’ll have an exceptional eye for motion, with a strong instinct for timing, rhythm, typography and storytelling. Whether it’s a big campaign or the smallest animated detail, you’ll understand how purposeful movement can make an idea more engaging, memorable and effective.

You’ll work closely with designers, writers and marketers, taking ideas from initial concept through to final execution. You’ll be curious, collaborative and passionate about your craft, always looking for opportunities to push the work further.

Что предстоит делать

Motion Design and Creative Craft

  • Create high-quality motion design and animation across brand, marketing, campaigns and social
  • Translate creative ideas and static designs into compelling, beautifully crafted motion
  • Bring an exceptional level of attention to timing, pacing, transitions and animation detail
  • Develop and present motion concepts, storyboards and creative explorations
  • Take ownership of projects from initial concept through to final delivery

Collaboration and Team Contribution

  • Work closely with brand designers, writers, marketers and other creative teams
  • Contribute ideas, references and creative thinking throughout the design process
  • Collaborate with designers to explore how motion can elevate and strengthen their work
  • Respond positively to feedback and develop ideas through iteration
  • Share knowledge, techniques and inspiration with the wider studio

Brand Expression and Consistency

  • Apply and evolve Starling’s motion identity across a wide range of touchpoints
  • Bring our brand’s confident, purposeful and playful personality to life through movement
  • Understand how timing, easing and composition can communicate character and emotion
  • Maintain consistency in how the brand moves while finding opportunities for creative expression
  • Help develop and refine our motion principles, behaviours and guidelines

Creative Thinking and Development

  • Explore different animation techniques and approaches to solve creative challenges
  • Stay curious about developments in motion design, animation and emerging creative technologies
  • Bring fresh ideas, creative references and new perspectives into the team
  • Experiment with new tools and workflows, including 3D and AI where appropriate
  • Take initiative in developing your own skills and pushing the quality of your craft

Требования

What You'll Bring

  • A standout portfolio or showreel demonstrating exceptional motion design, creativity and attention to detail
  • Advanced proficiency in Adobe After Effects, with exceptional 2D animation skills and a good understanding of animation principles
  • An exceptional eye for timing, rhythm, movement and visual storytelling
  • Experience with video editing would be desirable
  • Strong design fundamentals, particularly typography, composition and colour
  • Experience creating motion for brand identities, campaigns, digital and social content
  • An understanding of how motion can communicate brand personality, not just add visual interest

Behaviours & Competencies

  • Ability to develop ideas independently and take work from concept to final delivery
  • Familiarity with Adobe Creative Suite and wider design workflows
  • Experience with 3D software such as Cinema 4D or Blender would be a plus
  • Curiosity about emerging creative tools and techniques
  • Attention to detail, a collaborative mindset and a genuine passion for motion design
