О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

Own Airwallex’s regulatory engagement strategy and licensing responsibilities for our regulated financial services entity in New Zealand, serving as the direct point of contact with the FMA and coordinating closely with internal and external stakeholders.

You’ll support Airwallex Group expansion by managing the regulatory compliance components of licence authorisations, applications, business plan updates, regulatory information requests, examinations and inspections. You’ll also maintain regulatory, risk and breach reporting obligations across the region, advise the business on regulatory change, conduct risk and ethics, and help ensure our compliance controls and processes remain fit for purpose.

Working across the three lines of defence, you’ll help mature customer-facing and internal processes, guide the first line business, deliver the Compliance Monitoring Program and employee training, and manage regulatory compliance risk within the Enterprise Risk Management Framework. You’ll also take on proactive special-operations projects that remove business or operational obstacles and help Airwallex execute its strategic goals more quickly.

You’ll work from our Auckland office five days a week.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- A bachelor’s or master’s degree in law from a recognised university, or analogous compliance qualifications and experience

- At least 10 years’ experience in regulatory compliance within a regulated financial institution, fintech, legal or consulting firm, or a combination of these

- Familiarity with financial regulatory laws, rules, regulations and industry best practices across the ANZ region, with a specific focus on New Zealand

- Experience dealing with regulators, including the FMA, DIA and other financial services regulators across the region

- Experience developing or maintaining a compliance program within a financial institution, preferably involving payments activity

- Experience managing regulatory engagement, licensing processes, regulatory expansion and regulatory information requests

- A deep understanding of financial products and services, conduct risk, ethics and controls

- The ability to analyse new and existing products and provide practical guidance on regulatory obligations and second-line controls

- The ability to operate strategically and think creatively when solving complex regulatory compliance challenges

- Strong data proficiency, with the ability to work with data and use it for strategic decision-making

- Outstanding time management, presentation and relationship-building skills, with the ability to understand the bigger picture while staying focused on detail

- The ability to work in a fast-paced startup environment, manage competing priorities and deliver under demanding deadlines

Preferred

- Exposure to RegTech or technology-driven compliance solutions

- Experience supporting or managing regulatory examinations and inspections

- Experience delivering compliance monitoring programs, targeted reviews, thematic reviews or compliance training

- Experience advising on conduct risk, ethics matters and regulatory change initiatives
