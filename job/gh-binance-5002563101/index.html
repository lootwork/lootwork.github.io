О роли

We are looking for a data analyst with strong comprehension ability and a solid command of databases and SQL to join our research team. You will work with large volumes of transactional, operational, and customer data, translating complex patterns into clear, actionable insights for operational teams and executives.

This is also a unique opportunity to gain hands-on exposure to a wide range of exchange businesses ,including RWA, stablecoin, and trading product lines, and to understand how data connects to real-world products and operations.

Prior crypto knowledge is good to have but not required, we welcome candidates with or without a crypto background.

Обязанности

  • Quickly understand the team's business context and requirements, and build data monitoring and analytics dashboards from the ground up to support operational and research needs
  • Analyze and interpret large volumes of transactional, operational, and customer data using proprietary and open-source data tools and analytical tool kits
  • Actively track industry news and market hotspots in real time; proactively surface relevant findings and turn them into data-driven analysis
  • Translate complex findings into simple, well-structured visualizations and recommendations for execution by operational teams and executives
  • Process confidential data and information according to guidelines
  • Troubleshoot the reporting database environment and reports

Требования

  • Bachelor's degree from an accredited university or college in Computer Science, Math, Statistics, Economics, or a related quantitative field
  • Strong academic record, solid GPA / coursework performance reflecting rigor and consistency
  • Strong comprehension and learning ability, able to quickly absorb complex business and technical concepts, reason from first principles, and synthesize ambiguous information into clear conclusions
