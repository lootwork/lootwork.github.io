As a SE II Engineer, Platform you will play a critical role helping to advance Ripple's production infrastructure and release pipelines. Infrastructure Engineers at Ripple are a hybrid of systems and software engineers who support the design and lifecycle of our hosted products. You will work closely with product engineers to support their infrastructure needs, ideally in a self-service environment.

Что предстоит делать

  • Contribute to platform features across compute, authentication, service discovery, observability, and redundancy
  • Implement architectural solutions under the guidance of senior engineers, with a growing awareness of tradeoffs between scale, cost, and maintenance
  • Support and mentor junior engineers through code reviews and knowledge sharing
  • Help deploy and maintain Ripple's multi-region, multi-provider service platforms with a focus on security and reliability
  • Build and improve tooling for automation, monitoring, and instrumentation to reduce operational friction
  • Contribute to platform and application lifecycle services, working toward automated, data-driven workflows
  • Participate in discussions around scalable, maintainable, and cost-effective solutions
  • Learn and apply DevOps and GitOps principles in your day-to-day work

Кого мы ищем

  • Bachelor's and/or Master's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field
  • 3–6 years of relevant work experience with development background in Go, Python, or Java, with a good understanding of software engineering best practices and system design
  • Hands-on experience with cloud providers like AWS
  • Working knowledge of orchestration platforms (Kubernetes, Rancher) and containerization technologies for deploying and managing distributed services
  • Experience contributing to deployment pipelines using common CI/CD tools
  • Familiarity with Infrastructure-as-Code tools such as Terraform or CloudFormation
  • Exposure to Observability platforms leveraging Prometheus and OpenTelemetry, with an interest in building instrumentation patterns and monitoring SLOs/SLIs
  • A collaborative team player with a customer service orientation and a willingness to build relationships across teams and cultures
  • Good communication skills — written, verbal, and interpersonal
