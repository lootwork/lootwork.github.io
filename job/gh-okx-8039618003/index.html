О Opportunity

The Compliance Analyst conducts day-to-day monitoring, triage, and execution of electronic communications surveillance across email, chat, messaging platforms, and digital communication channels. Working within established procedures and SLAs, this role executes high-volume alert processing, identifies potential risk indicators, and escalates non-standard cases. This role reports to the Director of Anti-Bribery, Corruption & Conduct and supports the team by leveraging AI-assisted tools and ensuring high precision in daily surveillance operations.

Что предстоит делать

Operational Surveillance & Risk Detection

  • Execute Primary Triage: Perform daily review and triage of e-communications alerts across email, chat, text, and messaging channels within target SLA timelines.
  • Identify Risk Indicators: Apply foundational compliance and regulatory guidelines to detect potential policy breaches, misconduct, or operational risk indicators.
  • Maintain Audit-Ready Records: Execute precise data entry and case logging to ensure complete, compliant documentation for audit trails.

Case Processing & Escalation

  • Gather Case Context: Conduct initial background gathering and evidence collection on flagged communications to support case evaluations.
  • Draft Clear Case Notes: Prepare structured, fact-based summaries outlining key findings for non-standard alerts requiring upper-level review.
  • Escalate Complex Issues: Promptly escalate ambiguous, high-risk, or complex matters to senior analysts and leadership.

Operational Efficiency, AI & Continuous Improvement

  • Meet Volume & Quality Targets: Consistently hit daily output targets (KPIs) while maintaining strong precision in case evaluations.
  • AI Tooling & Automation: Utilize AI-assisted surveillance platforms, prompt engineering, and automated workflow enhancements to streamline alert review and triage quality.
  • Support Lexicon & Model Tuning: Identify recurring false positives and operational bottlenecks, providing feedback to help refine alert parameters and ML/AI model accuracy
  • Drive Process Enhancement: Bring strong intellectual curiosity and a proactive mindset to continuously challenge existing surveillance workflows, proposing innovative ways to improve operational efficiency.
  • Expand Domain Knowledge: Actively participate in ongoing training to build technical expertise across crypto regulations, market conduct rules, and emerging compliance technologies.

What We Look For In You

  • Mandarin Language Proficiency: Fluent in Mandarin (written and spoken) to support regional, cross-border, and multi-lingual e-communications surveillance across global team channels.
  • AI & Technical Aptitude: Hands-on experience working with AI-driven compliance tools, large language models (LLMs), or automated workflow tools to enhance daily analytical tasks.
  • Mindset & Drive: Strong curiosity and an inherent drive to continuously refine processes, automate repetitive tasks, and elevate overall team performance.
  • Analytical Skills: Strong attention to detail with the ability to synthesize unstructured text data, spot nuances, and present concise findings.
  • Education / Experience: BA/BS in Finance, Law, Business Administration, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience).

Будет плюсом

  • Prior exposure to specialized e-communications surveillance software (e.g., Smarsh, Behavox, Relativity) or lexicon/keyword monitoring tools.
  • Understanding of market abuse indicators, insider trading, or conduct risk concepts within financial services or digital assets.
  • Background in crypto, digital assets, Web3, or multi-lingual operational support.
