Who is SkyBox Labs?

Recognized as one of BC's Top Employers (2025), SkyBox Labs is a game development studio that works on some of the world’s most iconic games. The studio is currently working on projects in the Minecraft universe, supporting the dev team at Bungie on Destiny 2, supporting development of Fallout 76, in addition to work on unannounced IP. Since our inception in 2011, SkyBox Labs has evolved from a small team of eight individuals to a fully established team of Engineers, Artists, Animators, Designers, Producers, and more. Come join our hybrid team in our Burnaby, BC studio today!

PLEASE NOTE: At this time, we are only able to hire candidates who are legally eligible to work in Canada and reside within Canada.

Interested? Let’s talk about what we’re hiring for

Are you the type of artist who looks at a blank canvas and sees endless possibilities rather than a roadblock? SkyBox Labs is looking for a Senior UI Artist to help define the visual identity of an exciting, unannounced new AAA project that we are co-developing alongside a globally recognized partner. In this role, you won’t just be inheriting an established style guide and churning out icons—you’ll be in the driver’s seat of visual exploration, pitching bold new concepts, and establishing the look, feel, and graphical language of our in-game interface from the ground up.

If you thrive on ambiguity, love generating a multitude of visual directions, and take pride in crafting high-impact UI that elevates the entire player experience, we want to hear from you.

This team works in a hybrid capacity, working three days a week at our studio in Burnaby, BC.

A SNAPSHOT OF YOUR ROLE

  • Shape the visual UI: Build the UI aesthetic from scratch. You’ll create high-fidelity target concepts and pre-visualizations that define the look and feel of all in-game UI visuals.
  • Ideate & iterate: Bring a plethora of creative solutions to the table. You are comfortable exploring wildly different visual tones and rapidly iterating based on team feedback.
  • Present & champion: Confidently pitch, explain, and validate your visual designs with key stakeholders and project partners.
  • Collaborate cross-functionally: Work in lockstep with UX Designers and Engineers to ensure designs are visually appealing, intuitive and performant.
  • Elevate the team: Support the team as needed, sharing advanced techniques in toolsets, and advocating for quality across the studio

SOUND LIKE YOU? THIS IS WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR

  • 5+ years of UI art experience in the gaming industry (AAA console/PC development experience strongly preferred).
  • Proven experience building UI visual direction from scratch—you’re a self-starter who thrives when given a blank slate and can run with a lack of initial clarity.
  • A body of work that showcases not just final production assets, but your creative ideation process—concept explorations, high-fidelity mockups, typography, composition, and game-ready UI art.
  • Expert proficiency in UI software such as Figma and the Adobe Creative Suite
  • A collaborative mindset with clear verbal and visual communication skills; you enjoy engaging with teammates, explaining your design decisions, and presenting to stakeholders.
  • Comprehensive knowledge of emerging UI trends and technologies/
  • Solid UX understanding: While this is a visual art role, you possess a keen understanding of UX principles, player ergonomics, and game accessibility standards.

Nice-to-Haves

  • Experience acting as a team liaison, POC, or lead
  • Familiarity or working knowledge of Unreal Engine 5 (UE5) and UMG

Along with your resume, we ask that you please provide an up-to-date portfolio that demonstrates your UI art skills.
