The Crypto expansion team is building Robinhood products for new regions, extending crypto and adjacent financial experiences to customers around the world. The team owns all critical parts of the app across onboarding, funding, account setup, trading product integrations, and platform capabilities required to operate in new jurisdictions. This team sits at the intersection of building an internationalization platform and region specific product execution, to help Robinhood be successful in new markets. Recent work in expansion effort has included Canada, Europe launches with multi-currency wallet capabilities, local funding rails, translations infrastructure, and new onboarding experiences for each region

As a Senior Android Engineer on the Crypto Expansions team, you’ll help launch Robinhood’s crypto and adjacent financial products in new regions by building critical Android experiences end to end. You’ll work across onboarding, local funding rails, account setup, multi-currency wallet capabilities, trading surfaces, and translations infrastructure, balancing region-specific execution with long-term platform thinking. You’ll play a key role in delivering reliable, intuitive mobile experiences that make Robinhood successful in global markets.

This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA OR New York, NY office, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build intuitive, elegant, and stable mobile experiences using Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and modern Android APIs
  • Lead the full lifecycle of product features — from ideation and technical design to implementation, testing, and release
  • Collaborate closely with Product Managers, Designers, Backend Engineers, and Data Scientists to create impactful solutions
  • Champion engineering best practices that ensure code quality, scalability, and maintainability
  • Mentor junior developers and help elevate team standards and collaboration

Что вы приносите

  • 6+ years of professional experience developing Android applications
  • Proficiency with Kotlin and/or Java, and a strong grasp of Android SDK and architecture patterns like MVVM
  • Experience using Jetpack Compose, Coroutines & Flow or RxJava, and libraries such as Retrofit and Room
  • Strong collaboration and communication skills with a product-focused mindset
  • Demonstrated ability to mentor peers, lead complex projects, and influence architecture decisions
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or equivalent experience
