Что предстоит делать

As our IT Engineer, you’ll be the person who keeps our devices, tools and access secure and running, so everyone can move fast and build great products.

You’ll treat IT operations as an enabler for the whole company, always looking to automate, secure and simplify.

Here’s what your day-to-day will look like

- Process Automation - Build and maintain automation workflows with tools like n8n and Make, connecting our business systems and removing repetitive manual work.

- Support & Troubleshooting - Take on the complex tickets that go beyond first-line support: triage, investigate, fix.

- SaaS Administration - Run and secure our core cloud services (Google Workspace, Slack, Keeper), including onboarding, offboarding and security policies.

- Endpoint Management - Configure and deploy devices through MDM (mobile device management) platforms (Jamf, Knox, Google Workspace), and monitor endpoint security with EDR (endpoint detection and response) tools to stop threats early.

- IT Asset & Service Management - Manage hardware from purchase to retirement, and use our ITSM (IT service management) platform to prioritise tickets by business impact and SLAs (service level agreements).

- Network Management - Manage and troubleshoot our local network, including Wi-Fi, firewall and DNS, so we rely less on external providers during outages.

Who we’re looking for

This role needs someone passionate about technology and automation, who treats IT operations as a key enabler for the whole company, always looking for ways to optimize, secure, and streamline.

- Workflow Automation - Hands-on experience building and maintaining complex integrations with tools like Make and n8n.

- IT Operations - Proven experience in Tier 2 IT support and in managing local network infrastructure.

- Identity & Access Management - Strong skills in managing user identities and access across core business tools like Google Workspace.

- Endpoint & Security - Familiarity with modern MDM solutions (Jamf, Knox) and EDR tools for proactive threat mitigation.

- Hardware Lifecycle - Solid understanding of IT asset management and ticket prioritisation methods.

- Professional fluency in English - Italian is a plus
