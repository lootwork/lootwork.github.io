We believe that the way people interact with their finances will drastically improve in the next few years. We’re dedicated to empowering this transformation by building the tools and experiences that thousands of developers use to create their own products. Plaid powers the tools millions of people rely on to live a healthier financial life. We work with thousands of companies like Venmo, SoFi, several of the Fortune 500, and many of the largest banks to make it easy for people to connect their financial accounts to the apps and services they want to use. Plaid’s network covers 12,000 financial institutions across the US, Canada, UK and Europe. Founded in 2013, the company is headquartered in San Francisco with offices in New York, Seattle, Washington D.C., Raleigh, London, and Amsterdam.

О команде

The Lifecycle Marketing team owns Plaid’s email channel strategy and partners with teams across the business. As part of the Growth Marketing organization, we focus on driving sustainable revenue growth and long-term customer retention.

We build data-driven lifecycle programs that engage Plaid’s customers and prospects at every stage of their journey. Using an integrated, multi-channel approach, we create great customer experiences that drive results and build lasting relationships.

О роли

You’ll build and launch the email campaigns that move prospects and customers from first touch to closed-won. You’ll own day-to-day execution of our nurture and event programs, using marketing automation, segmentation, and personalization to send the right message at the right stage.

This is a high-impact time to join. Our two largest lifecycle programs are growing in volume and sophistication, and you’ll help scale them. You’ll work with events, paid media, product marketing, customer marketing, and brand to launch integrated campaigns that generate high-quality pipeline.

You’ll be data-driven and always testing. You’ll run A/B tests, track performance, and use what you learn to improve engagement and conversion. You’ll have support from a growing lifecycle team and a clear path to more ownership.

Обязанности

- Build and launch email campaigns: Create nurture and event email programs that engage prospects and customers from lead to closed-won, using dynamic content to deliver relevant, timely experiences.

- Scale core programs: Help grow our largest lifecycle programs, including TechTalk and Effects, by owning day-to-day email builds as volume and complexity increase.

- Partner on integrated campaigns: Work with events, paid media, product marketing, customer marketing, and brand to launch multi-channel campaigns that generate pipeline. Serve as the email expert, recommending ways to maximize channel impact and keep messaging consistent.

- Segment and personalize: Use customer and behavioral data to segment audiences and send targeted, personalized emails.

- Manage marketing automation: Partner with Marketing Ops to build and optimize workflows, triggered campaigns, and tracking in our marketing automation platform.

- Test and optimize: Run A/B tests on messaging, offers, timing, and creative, and use the results to improve engagement, conversion, and pipeline impact.

- Report and recommend: Track and present key lifecycle metrics (e.g., engagement, conversion, pipeline impact) using BI tools, and turn them into clear, actionable recommendations.

- Maintain list health and compliance: Follow email best practices for deliverability, list hygiene, and regulations such as CAN-SPAM and GDPR.

Требования

- 2–3 years of experience in lifecycle, email, or CRM marketing, preferably at a B2B or SaaS company

- Track record of building email and nurture programs that improve conversion rates and pipeline

- Hands-on experience with a marketing automation platform (Marketo preferred) and an email builder like Knak (or similar)

- Strong project management skills and the ability to run a high volume of email programs from concept to launch

- Skilled in segmentation and personalization, with solid knowledge of email best practices, including deliverability and compliance

- Data-driven, with experience using BI tools like Tableau (or similar) to report on and improve performance

- Clear communicator who collaborates well across marketing and GTM teams and is a trusted email expert

- Self-starter who thrives in a fast-paced, changing environment and enjoys building and scaling programs
