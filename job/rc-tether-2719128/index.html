О вакансии

We're changing how the world moves money. Wallets are where that change becomes real: the first place millions of people truly own their money. You'll own design execution across our wallets, working directly with the Design Lead to shape where they go next.

Обязанности

  • Own design execution end to end: from concept and prototype to launch and iteration.
  • Turn complex, conceptual ideas into experiences that feel simple to someone using a wallet for the first time.
  • Prototype fast and often: high-fidelity and interactive, in days not weeks, using the best tools available, AI included.
  • Partner daily with product, engineering and research, and present your work clearly to leadership.
  • Raise the bar for quality across everything we ship.
  • Contribute to strategic decisions about the future of our wallets.
