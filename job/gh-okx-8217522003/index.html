О Opportunity

Boss is OKX's unified internal operations platform, providing shared operational infrastructure for business lines across the company — helping business teams serve global users securely and efficiently, and supporting the platform business to achieve rapid growth in more regions.

Что предстоит делать

  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams (product, design, operations, infrastructure) to turn what the business needs from the operations platform into platform capabilities that ship.
  • Architect efficient and scalable systems that drive complex applications, and drive large efforts to reduce technical debt.
  • Own the architecture and evolution of platform security and access: authorization models, data-scope control, privileged access and control over high-risk operations, sensitivity classification for resources and data, end-to-end operation auditing, and insider-threat detection.
  • Drive adoption & infrastructure of agents / LLMs across security governance and operations workflows, owning evaluation quality, permission boundaries, data safety and cost.
  • Drive technical reviews and design decisions, codify design standards and best practices, and provide technical guidance to the team and partner teams; analyze and optimize code for quality, performance and security, and provide feedback to peers during code reviews.

What We Look For In You

  • Bachelor's degree or above in Computer Science or a related field, with strong command of a mainstream programming language (e.g. Java, C++, Go);
  • Able to use data and analysis to explain technical problems and propose actionable solutions.
  • Have led architecture design and technology selection for mid-to-large systems, making deliberate trade-offs between performance, availability, extensibility and cost of implementation, and owning how those systems evolve after launch.Able to quickly grasp the operational flow and key risk points of the business you support, abstract business needs into general platform capabilities, and drive adoption across multiple teams.
  • 7+ years of backend engineering and architecture design experience.

Будет плюсом

  • Familiarity with the Java / Spring microservice ecosystem and hands-on experience with high-concurrency, high-availability systems; working knowledge of message middleware (Kafka), MySQL / Redis / Elasticsearch, and experience designing API gateways, unified interception layers or policy engines.
  • Experience designing and building enterprise-grade access, audit or internal risk-control systems; familiarity with authorization models (RBAC / ABAC); hands-on experience in insider threat or data security.
  • Experience building and governing AI agents, familiar with MCP and context engineering, able to articulate the permission boundaries and data-safety concerns of agents running in production, with measurable impact from AI adoption (e.g. efficiency gains, quality improvements).
