О вакансии

World of Warships is the best naval game around, letting you take command of legendary warships from the period of the 20th century as you battle your way across the open sea with other players.

The Deployment Team is responsible for the World of Warships game servers throughout all stages of their lifecycle. Additionally, the team supports the development team by streamlining various processes, including the automation of production environment deployment and the enhancement of development tools. They also handle incident resolution and perform a wide range of other operational tasks.

Что предстоит делать

  • Be responsible for the availability, performance, security, and reliability of our game and parts of the infrastructure around it throughout the lifecycle of its services.
  • Optimizing and tuning our highly loaded services.
  • Supporting and developing a large number of automation tools for builds, deployments, and other processes required for our game and related services.

Кого мы ищем

  • Professional work experience with Linux systems and system administration-related tasks.
  • Development experience in at least one programming language (Python, Golang, or others).
  • Understanding of CI/CD methodologies and experience with tools (for example, TeamCity, Jenkins, GitLab CI, or others).
  • Work experience with version control systems (for example, Git, SVN).
  • Understanding of principles and experience with containerization tools (for example Docker, Podman, LXC, OpenVZ, Jail, or others).
  • Work experience with automation tools (for example, Puppet, Ansible, Fabric, Chef, Salt, Terraform, or others).
  • Experience using Bash for routine operations and automation.
  • Work experience with relational databases (for example, MySQL, PostgreSQL, etc.).
  • Understanding the basic principles of DevOps and SRE methodologies.
  • Understanding the principles and experience with orchestration systems (for example, K8S, Methos/Marathon, Compose, or others).
  • Work experience with message brokers (for example, Kafka, RabbitMQ, or others).
  • Work experience with configuration and secret management systems (for example, Zookeeper, Consul, Vault, or others).
  • Work experience with various monitoring systems (for example, Zabbix, Prometheus, Grafana, or others).
  • Experience in debugging applications at the system and network level.
  • Understanding how modern systems and network protocols work.
  • Intermediate English level or higher.

Что поможет выделиться

  • Expertise in building large-scale, high-volume, secure, efficient, and resilient operations environments.
  • Advanced hands-on Python / Golang programming experience.

Формат работы

О Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming's titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
