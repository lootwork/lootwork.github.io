What We Need

The DataOps Engineer is responsible for the operationalization, automation, and reliability of 2K’s data ecosystem. This role applies DevOps principles to data engineering, managing CI/CD for data assets, Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC), and advanced data observability. focuses on complex problem-solving, optimizing cross-functional data flows, and ensuring that data infrastructure is scalable, secure, and highly available for global studio stakeholders.

What You Will Do

  • Data Pipeline Operationalization: Lead the design and maintenance of scalable ETL/ELT pipelines to ingest and transform data from diverse sources into central data warehouses.
  • Automation & CI/CD: Implement and manage robust CI/CD pipelines specifically for data assets; automate extraction, testing, and deployment processes to increase velocity and reduce manual intervention.
  • Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC): Utilize tools such as Terraform to manage and version cloud infrastructure, ensuring environment parity across development, staging, and production.
  • Data Observability & Reliability: Proactively monitor pipeline health and data quality (freshness, volume, and distribution). Design and build automated alerting systems to resolve bottlenecks.
  • Security & Compliance: Implement and enforce data security measures, including IAM roles, encryption, and access controls, ensuring all pipelines meet GDPR and CCPA compliance.
  • Platform Support & Collaboration: Partner with Data Scientists and Analysts to facilitate data availability and provide infrastructure support for complex modeling and insights.
  • Process Optimization: Identify and execute improvements in data engineering workflows, adopting software engineering best practices for stateful data flows.

Who We Think Will Be A Great Fit

  • Execution & SLA Excellence: Consistently meets and exceeds Service Level Agreements (SLAs) by streamlining data workflows and maintaining high system availability. Translates business requirements into measurable performance targets.
  • Operational Governance: Implements rigorous operational standards and best practices, leveraging ITIL frameworks—specifically Incident, Problem, and Change Management—to minimize downtime and ensure predictable system behavior.
  • Global Collaboration & Follow-the-Sun Support: Experienced in working with distributed "around-the-sun" teams, ensuring seamless handovers, consistent documentation, and 24/7 operational continuity across different time zones.
  • Innovation & Adaptability: Adapts DevOps and DataOps strategies to handle the unique challenges of data statefulness and rapidly evolving cloud-native technologies.
  • Strategic Problem Solving: Independently diagnoses and resolves complex architectural bottlenecks; moves beyond "quick fixes" to identify root causes and prevent recurring systemic issues.
  • Communication & Stakeholder Management: Clearly articulates system health, operational risks, and technical requirements to both technical peers and executive leadership, bridging the gap between data science needs and engineering standards

Required Qualifications, Knowledge, and Job-Related Skills

  • Experience: 6+ years of professional experience in DataOps, Data Engineering, or DevOps, with a significant focus on large-scale data systems.
  • Technical Proficiency: Expert-level Python and SQL skills.
  • Orchestration & Transformation: Hands-on experience with modern orchestration tools (e.g., Apache Airflow, Mage, or Prefect) and transformation layers (e.g., dbt).
  • Cloud & Containers: Deep experience with AWS (or GCP/Azure) and containerization technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes.
  • DevOps Tooling: Proficiency with Git for version control and Terraform for infrastructure management.
  • Data Architecture: Advanced knowledge of modern data warehouse/lakehouse environments (e.g., Snowflake, Databricks).

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in the gaming industry or high-volume consumer data environments.
  • Advanced certifications in AWS or relevant Data Engineering specializations.
  • Experience with real-time streaming technologies (e.g., Kafka, Kinesis).
