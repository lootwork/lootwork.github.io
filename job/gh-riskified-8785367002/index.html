О роли

The Dispute Team owns what happens after a chargeback lands. We build Dispute Resolve and the Chargeback Management System (CBMS): the platform through which merchants and Riskified's own dispute operators receive chargebacks, decide whether to fight them, generate compelling evidence, submit it to the bank through payment gateways, and track the outcome to financial close.

Our domain is the full dispute lifecycle: pre-dispute alerts, chargeback ingestion and order matching, a rules-based representment decision engine, evidence generation (templated and LLM-based), gateway submission, second chargebacks and arbitration, and reimbursement when Riskified carries the liability. Under the hood this is an event-driven system of Scala and TypeScript (NestJS) microservices on Kafka, backed by CockroachDB and Elasticsearch, with a React console on top.

This role requires strong technical execution paired with genuine curiosity about how card-network disputes work.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build the dispute platform: Design, develop, and maintain the services that ingest chargebacks, decide on representment, generate evidence, and submit disputes to payment gateways, with the reliability a money-moving flow demands.
  • Drive AI into compelling evidence: Extend our LLM-based evidence generation, turning a per-category, per-gateway problem into one AI capability that scales across dispute types and providers.
  • Embrace modern workflows: Actively use agentic coding tools and AI-assisted workflows to raise team velocity and code quality.
  • Bridge tech and business: Partner with Product, dispute operations, and payments domain experts to translate card-network rules, liability logic, and merchant configuration into clean software architecture.
  • Own production end to end: Ship daily through modern CI/CD, own the health of your services in production, and investigate real merchant cases when a chargeback did not go where it should have.
  • Shape the roadmap: Take an active part in planning the team's roadmap and products.
