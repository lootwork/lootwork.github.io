О роли

Our team builds and owns one of Riskified's core products: a platform that merchants and Riskified's own operations teams rely on to manage critical post-purchase financial flows, from intake through automated decisioning, AI-generated content, and integration with payment providers, all the way to financial close.

Our domain covers the full product lifecycle: real-time event ingestion and order matching, a rules-based decision engine, templated and LLM-based content generation, payment gateway integrations, and financial reconciliation. Under the hood this is an event-driven system of Scala and TypeScript (NestJS) microservices on Kafka, backed by CockroachDB and Elasticsearch, with a React console on top.

This role requires strong technical execution paired with genuine curiosity about how payments and the card-network ecosystem work.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build the platform: Design, develop, and maintain the services that ingest events, drive automated decisions, generate content, and integrate with payment gateways, with the reliability a money-moving flow demands.
  • Drive AI into the product: Extend our LLM-based generation capabilities, turning a per-category, per-provider problem into one AI capability that scales across use cases and providers.
  • Embrace modern workflows: Actively use agentic coding tools and AI-assisted workflows to raise team velocity and code quality.
  • Bridge tech and business: Partner with Product, operations, and payments domain experts to translate card-network rules, liability logic, and merchant configuration into clean software architecture.
  • Own production end to end: Ship daily through modern CI/CD, own the health of your services in production, and investigate real merchant cases when something didn't go the way it should have.
  • Shape the roadmap: Take an active part in planning the team's roadmap and products.

  • Passionate about leveraging AI coding assistants and agentic tools in daily development workflows
