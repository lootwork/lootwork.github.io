The Brokerage Risk team’s mission is to leverage data, automation, and AI to strengthen risk transparency, improve operational resilience, and empower Robinhood to innovate safely.

As a Brokerage Risk Intern, you will join a hands-on, technical environment where you’ll build and test real-world risk management tools, analyze live brokerage data, and help automate processes that safeguard our customers and firm. You’ll collaborate with risk managers, engineers, and product teams to design solutions that detect, visualize, and

This role is based in our Chicago, IL office with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build SQL and Python scripts to automate key risk and control processes—such as reconciliations, risk alerts, and supervisory checks.
  • Partner with data engineers and analysts to develop dashboards that visualize real-time risk metrics, exposures, and performance trends.
  • Support data-driven investigations into brokerage risks (e.g., trading anomalies, process breaks, or control failures) and propose solutions.
  • Assist with GenAI-enabled projects—like improving document classification or enhancing automation of books and records, access management, and test account oversight.
  • Present findings and prototypes to senior leaders, showcasing how technology can make risk management faster, smarter, and more scalable.

Что вы приносите

  • You’re pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Data Science, Computer Science, Finance, Engineering, or a related field with an expected graduation date of Winter 2027/Summer 2028.
  • You have foundational experience in SQL and Python and a strong interest in applying these skills to real-world financial and risk problems.
  • You have exposure to automation, machine learning, and AI—and excited to see how they can transform risk management.
  • You’re analytical, precise, and detail-oriented, with the ability to communicate complex data insights clearly to non-technical audiences.
  • You thrive in fast-paced, collaborative environments and demonstrate accountability, curiosity, and ethical judgment in your work.
