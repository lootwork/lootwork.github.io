О opportunity

We are looking for an experienced and empathetic Engineering Manager to lead and grow 3 teams within our Cards and Digital Wallets segment. You will manage around 15 backend engineers, helping them deliver critical, customer-facing features from start to finish.

We need someone who knows how to build high-performing teams, tracks the right engineering metrics, and works closely with Product and UX to turn goals into reality.

Что предстоит делать

  • Manage and Grow the Team: Support a group of ~15 backend engineers through regular 1:1s, clear feedback, and guidance on their career growth.
  • Drive Delivery: Lead 3 teams to deliver impactful, end-to-end projects smoothly and on time.
  • Use Metrics Wisely: Track engineering metrics and KPIs to spot bottlenecks, improve processes, and help the teams perform at their best.
  • Partner with Product & UX: Work side-by-side with Product Managers and designers to plan projects, manage dependencies, and remove blockers.
  • Keep High Standards: Bring a strong technical background to help the team maintain high code quality and build scalable systems.

What you need to bring

  • Solid experience managing and mentoring software engineers (handling a team of around 15 direct reports).
  • A strong track record of delivering complex, end-to-end projects in fast-paced agile environments.
  • Good knowledge of engineering metrics and how to use them to improve team performance.
  • A strong agile mindset, with the ability to successfully handle multiple priorities and frequent shifts in direction.
  • Exceptional collaboration skills, working hand-in-hand with Product Managers, UX, and peers.
  • High proactiveness, looking beyond your own segment to create a broader impact across the company.
  • A technical background that lets you earn the respect of strong engineers and support their technical decisions.
  • Fluent English.

Будет плюсом

  • Previous experience work in a FinTech company or exposure working in an environment enabling DevOps culture
  • A good understanding of infrastructure, operations and engineering.

What’s in it for you

  • Accelerate your career growth by joining Germany’s #1 Top Startup Employer, named by LinkedIn two years running 🚀
  • Personal development budget, discounted public transit pass, German language classes, fitness memberships, childcare services, N26 premium bank card, employee loans, and your choice of a Mac or Windows laptop
  • 26 vacation days per year in Austria & Germany, with an additional day for each year of service - up to 30 days ; and 24 vacation days in Spain (up to 28 days)
  • A high degree of collaboration and autonomy while working with a group of international peers, getting things done attitude, and access to cutting edge technologies
  • Rotating selection of hot and cold drinks and snacks in the office, Friday Celebrations once per month with pizza, wine, and beer, plus a monthly company-wide lunch and learn
  • A relocation package with visa support for those who need it
