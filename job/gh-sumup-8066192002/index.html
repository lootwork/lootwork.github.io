Accelerate with SumUp: Join Our Independent Sales Agent Team!

SumUp is expanding rapidly in the US—and we want you to be part of our success story! We have a contractor-based sales channel and are looking for high-performing sales professionals to join us as Independent Sales Agents.

What’s in it for you?

  • Uncapped Commissions: Earn as much as you want—your success drives your rewards.
  • Non-Exclusive Agreements: Flexibility to work with other brands; you’re in control.
  • Direct Engagement: Work directly with SumUp, a global leader in payment solutions.
  • Proven Product: Sell innovative, easy-to-use payment technology trusted by millions worldwide.

Кто вы

  • Strong existing networks in merchant/payment services (think credit card processing, Point of Sale, Loyalty, Invoicing, and Kiosks)
  • Self-motivated
  • Focus on merchant satisfaction and relationships

Why SumUp?

  • Fast-growing presence in the US market
  • Supportive team and proven sales resources (Regional Support Leads and US based support center)
  • More than 4 million merchants use SumUp globally
  • SumUp has 3,000+ employees with 26 offices across 36 global markets
  • Recognized by CNBC as one of the World’s Top FinTech Companies in 2025

Ready to accelerate your earnings and grow with us? Business owners are at the heart of SumUp's focus, so we create powerful, easy-to-use financial solutions to help them run and grow their business. Contact us today to become a SumUp Independent Sales Agent!
