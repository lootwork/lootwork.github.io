Job Description

Обязанности

  • Assist in sketching, coloring, or polishing assets under the guidance of senior artists.
  • Demonstrate the ability to deliver revisions on time after feedback.
  • Show visible improvement in technical skills and workflow over the course of the internship.
  • Adapt to at least one new art style or IP-specific guideline based on real projects.

Требования

Требования

  • Good portfolio showcasing drawing fundamentals (anatomy, color, perspective)
  • Good sense of visual storytelling, lighting, and composition
  • Able to take direction and apply feedback effectively

Additional Information

  • You want to be part of an exceptional experience, within a company that is constantly growing.
  • You want to work with talented people who are industry pioneers.
  • You want to join a global company and meet great people around the world from all walks of life.
  • Motivating, challenging and rewarding work on the next great frontiers in games.
  • Career-development opportunities.
  • Exceptional library of diverse top games with broad distribution.
