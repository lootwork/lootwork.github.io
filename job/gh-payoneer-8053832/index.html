О Payoneer

Founded in 2005, Payoneer is the global financial platform that removes friction from doing business across borders, with a mission to connect the world’s underserved businesses to a rising global economy. We’re a community with over 2,500 colleagues all over the world, working to serve customers, and partners in over 190 countries and territories.

By taking the complexity out of the financial workflows–including everything from global payments and compliance to multi-currency and workforce management, to providing working capital and business intelligence–we give businesses the tools they need to work efficiently worldwide and grow with confidence.

Senior Mobile Engineer

Job Summary

We are seeking a talented and experienced Senior Mobile Engineer to lead the design and development of high-impact mobile applications. You will work across iOS and Android platforms, driving technical excellence, mentoring junior engineers, and collaborating with product and design teams to deliver world-class mobile experiences. This role requires a deep understanding of mobile architecture, best practices, and a passion for building scalable, performant, and user-friendly applications.

Основные обязанности

  • Design and develop native or cross-platform mobile applications (iOS/Android) using modern frameworks and best practices
  • Lead technical architecture decisions and establish coding standards to ensure scalability and maintainability
  • Mentor and code review junior engineers, fostering a culture of technical excellence and continuous learning
  • Collaborate closely with product managers and designers to translate requirements into technical solutions
  • Optimize application performance, identify bottlenecks, and implement solutions for faster load times and smooth user experience
  • Implement and maintain robust testing strategies, including unit tests, integration tests, and automation
  • Participate in on-call rotations and respond to critical issues with urgency and technical depth
  • Stay current with mobile development trends, tools, and technologies; evaluate and recommend adoption of new tools
  • Contribute to architectural planning and improve mobile infrastructure to support future growth

Required Qualifications

  • 6+ years of professional mobile development experience on iOS, Android, or both
  • Strong expertise in at least one native language (Swift for iOS, Kotlin/Java for Android)
  • Deep understanding of mobile app architecture patterns (MVVM, MVP, Clean Architecture) and design principles
  • Proven track record of shipping production apps that scale to millions of users
  • Experience with RESTful APIs, networking libraries, and data persistence technologies
  • Proficiency in debugging, profiling, and optimizing mobile applications
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills; ability to explain complex technical concepts clearly
  • Experience mentoring junior developers and contributing to team growth

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with cross-platform frameworks (React Native, Flutter) is a plus
  • Hands-on experience with security best practices in mobile applications
  • Knowledge of financial or fintech domain
  • Experience with CI/CD pipelines and mobile app deployment processes
  • Contribution to open-source mobile projects
  • Experience with analytics, crash reporting, and monitoring tools

Core Technical Skills

iOS Development

Swift, UIKit/SwiftUI, Core Data, networking, performance optimization

Android Development

Kotlin, Android Architecture Components, Jetpack, Compose

Mobile Architecture

MVVM, MVP, Clean Architecture, dependency injection

Testing

Unit testing, instrumentation testing, test automation

DevOps & Tools

Git, CI/CD tools (GitHub Actions, Jenkins, Fastlane)

Debugging & Profiling

Xcode Instruments, Android Studio Profiler
