Riot Games was established in 2006 by entrepreneurial gamers who believe that player-focused game development can result in genre-defining games. Back in 2009, that vision gave birth to our debut title League of Legends, which achieved critical acclaim among players and became the most played PC game in the world. We are honored to serve over 100 million players worldwide every month.

We’re looking for humble but ambitious, razor-sharp professionals who can teach us a thing or two. We promise to return the favor. We embrace those who see things differently, aren’t afraid to experiment, and who have a healthy disregard for constraints. As a Rioter in Team China, you'll join a team of culturally diverse professionals that share our prioritization of player experience, rejection of convention and maniacal passion for gaming.

The publishing product management function within China Publishing helps drive initiatives and optimizations for in-game events, channels expansion, and around game product feature development. You will get deeply involved in every aspect of daily player experience as well as business management.

As a Publishing Product Manager on the VALORANT China publishing team with a focus on insights, you will drive impactful publishing initiatives by providing critical feedback through in-depth player research, data analysis in collaboration with cross-functional teams. You will report to the Director, Publishing Product Management.

Обязанности

  • Be responsible for continuous efforts to understand our players, closely monitor and analyze the performance with a focus on publishing product tactics in China, and extract actionable insights to inform better decisions.
  • Partner closely with publishing partners to acquire, define, validate, and responsibly use China in-and-out of game player data across the global ecosystem. Ensure all partners can speak/apply the same set of language and methodology, that all insights are transferable and comparable across global and local benchmarks.
  • Design and lead surveys, focus groups, player labs, field research, interviews, usability studies, diary studies, and other qualitative and quantitative research programs.
  • Translate complex datasets into clear player segments, behavioral frameworks, dashboards, and recurring insight reports.
  • Offer analytical support and engage in collaborative projects to optimize regional publishing execution and global product features, ensuring they align with the preferences and needs of Chinese players.
  • Stay informed on industry and competitor trends to enhance our strategic planning.
  • Develop trusted partnerships with global stakeholders, facilitate effective communication to bridge organizational gaps, support global China initiatives such as player lab.
  • Work with cross-functional teams to create player-centric local experiences and initiatives that meet the diverse needs of our player base.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor’s degree and 5+ years’ of professional experience in management consulting, strategic user research, insights or business intelligence.
  • Demonstrated experience using player, user, customer, or consumer data to generate insights and influence product or business decisions.
  • Experience designing and executing mixed-method research programs, including surveys and at least several of the following: focus groups, interviews, usability studies, player labs, diary studies, or field research.
  • Experience in advanced data analytics, modeling, statistics, etc
  • Hardcore gamer. Rich experiences in video games and e-sports, including League of Legends and deep understanding of their revenue strategies and monetization
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to present complex findings clearly to executives and cross-functional teams.
  • Fluent in Chinese and English, and can drive collaboration across language and culture

Desired Qualifications

  • Experience working directly with major platforms, publishers, or ecosystem partners of Riot.
  • Experienced stakeholder management and rapport-building skills
  • Willingness to travel frequently

For this role, you'll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players' experiences, this could be your role!
