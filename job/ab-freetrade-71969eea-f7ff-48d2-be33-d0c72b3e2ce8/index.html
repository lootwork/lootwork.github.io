О FREETRADE

Freetrade’s mission is to become the default place to invest. Investing has been too complicated and expensive for too long, keeping millions from making the most of their savings.

We’re changing that.

We’re building our team and looking for people who are excited to reshape how our customers invest and grow their wealth. If you’re driven by solving complex problems and building innovative products, you’ll fit right in.

2026 is a big year for us. Last year, we announced a game changing deal to become part of the IG Group. We’ll continue to operate as an independent business while gaining access to the resources and support of an established leader in the space.

This year, we’re accelerating our roadmap and taking our products to the next level. We’re building new features like mutual funds, bonds, and family investment tools. We’re also exploring how we can leverage AI to deliver an even better experience for our customers.

О роли

As a Senior Quality Engineer at Freetrade, you will be embedded in our mobile team, testing our iOS and Android apps and helping us move from largely manual testing towards a more automated approach. You will extend our existing TypeScript mobile test framework and help build quality in earlier, working alongside engineers in a collaborative team that wants to shift left. You’ll get coaching, mentoring and support from our Staff Quality Engineer.

Что предстоит делать

- Hands-on functional, acceptance and planned, risk-based exploratory testing of our native iOS and Android apps

- Be instrumental in the implementation of mobile tests that support the CI/CD pipeline for the software stacks and frameworks we support (TS, XCode, WebdriverIO)

- Work with the Staff QE to ensure the rollout and socialisation of proactive quality information, plans and reports to project teams, enabling them to make better decisions around processes, solutions and releases

- Advocate for, coach and educate project teams on the contextual use of testing techniques that allow us to embed shift-left and shift-right testing practices

- Bring a quality lens to ideas before they’re built, working with engineers, product and design (e.g. Three Amigos)

- Work comfortably in ambiguity: find gaps, chase down requirements and take initiative without being spoon-fed

- Contribute to upcoming projects, including acceptance testing to support the backend re-architecture and our quality strategy refresh

- Use AI tools to move faster and go broader, with appropriate guardrails to keep quality high

О вас

- Hands-on experience testing iOS and Android apps, and adding tests to an existing automation framework (we use TypeScript; familiarity helps and tool agnosticism is welcome)

- A blend of manual and automated testing, with breadth across testing types (functional, acceptance and exploratory, with non-functional a bonus) and the judgement to know which to use when

- You’ve owned testing and built things, such as a framework, automation or a test process your team adopted

- Strong written and verbal communication (fluent English) and influencing skills, comfortable sharing test plans and reports and advising engineers, product owners and project managers

- A self-starter who is comfortable with uncertainty, curious, and looks for gaps and negative cases

- Pragmatic: you know what “good enough” looks like and help teams ship

- Experience in exploratory testing and/or requirements discovery

- Strong independent testing skills and opinions, and a curious but pragmatic view of AI
