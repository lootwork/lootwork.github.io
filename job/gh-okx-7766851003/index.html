О Opportunity

OKX is seeking an experienced VIP Growth & Operations Lead to focus on creating exclusive product experiences and service solutions for high-net-worth individuals, institutional clients, market makers, and other VIP customer segments.

You will spearhead the overall strategy and product planning for the VIP Growth & Operations line, driving customer lifecycle management, personalized services, and continuous optimization of system functionalities to enhance customer retention and platform AUM (Assets Under Management).

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the day-to-day VIP Growth & Operations execution for high-net-worth individual clients, ensuring VIP lifecycle programs are planned, launched, and iterated effectively.
  • Build and run a VIP lifecycle operations framework: define segments, triggers, and journeys; set weekly/monthly operating rhythms; and ensure consistent execution with Local Markets.
  • Partner with Local Market teams to to co-create market plans, align goals and timelines, and drive follow-through, while ensuring global consistency and scalable playbooks.
  • Plan and execute VIP offsites and online events (e.g., product sharing sessions, community/engagement touchpoints), including end-to-end coordination, stakeholder alignment, and post-event performance review.
  • Develop and scale VIP growth programs and tools (e.g., VIP trial cards and similar programs) that improve conversion, deepen engagement, and increase VIP contribution to trading activity and AUM.
  • Establish a data-driven growth operating model with clear measurement, experimentation, and continuous optimization to improve VIP contribution to revenue and AUM.
  • Coordinate with cross-functional teams (Product, Data, Engineering, Operations) to deliver workflows, tooling, and process improvements that support VIP operations at scale.
  • Track performance and continuously optimize: define KPIs, monitor results, gather market feedback, and refine programs based on data and learnings.

What We Look For In You

  • Bachelor’s degree or higher with 5+ years of product management or product strategy experience.
  • Deep understanding of the Web3/Crypto industry, particularly exchange products such as institutional services, API trading, and VIP incentive programs.
  • Prior experience in CEXs, brokerages, financial institutions, or high-net-worth client-related products is preferred.
  • Strong cross-team communication and coordination skills, with systematic thinking and sharp user insights.
  • Proficiency in data analysis tools and data-driven decision-making.
  • Fluency in English and Chinese; Multi-market collaboration experience is a plus.
