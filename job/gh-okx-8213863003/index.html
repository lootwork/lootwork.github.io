О Opportunity

We are seeking a hands-on Structured Products Trader to manage and develop our structured products trading capabilities across digital assets and tokenized or traditional financial assets. The role combines trading and risk ownership with product design, cross-venue hedging, technology delivery, and workflow automation.

Что предстоит делать

  • Manage, price, and hedge structured products portfolios, including dual-currency products, vanilla and exotic options, and other yield or option-based solutions.
  • Monitor portfolio Greeks, PnL, scenario risk, liquidity, margin usage, settlement exposure, and venue-level concentrations; escalate material risks promptly.
  • Optimize pricing and hedging across multiple liquidity sources and trading venues, taking account of order-book depth, transaction costs, account margin, and operational constraints.
  • Translate trading requirements into clear product and technology specifications covering calculation logic, units, trade direction, account mapping, configuration priority, exception handling, and acceptance criteria.
  • Partner with Product, Engineering, Risk, Operations, Sales, and external counterparties to launch and improve structured products and supporting trading infrastructure.
  • Own trading-side readiness for the full product lifecycle, including onboarding, quoting, booking, hedging, corporate actions, expiry, settlement, reconciliation, and post-launch monitoring.
  • Identify production issues, assess customer and trading impact, provide evidence and interim controls, and coordinate investigation and resolution with relevant teams.
  • Build or improve automation for recurring trading, risk, monitoring, and reporting workflows while maintaining appropriate pre-checks, failure controls, auditability, andhuman oversight.
  • Track market developments and client needs, and proactively propose new products, pricing enhancements, risk controls, and process improvements with clear business value.
  • Maintain operating procedures, controls, and knowledge documentation to support resilient day-to-day and out-of-hours coverage.

What We Look For In You

  • At least three years of relevant experience in derivatives trading, structuring, quantitative trading, or a closely related role at a bank, market maker, hedge fund, proprietary trading firm, or digital-asset institution.
  • Strong understanding of options and structured products, including pricing, Greeks, volatility, barriers, settlement mechanics, and hedging risk.
  • Practical experience managing trading-book risk and making decisions under changing liquidity, market, and operational conditions.
  • Ability to analyze complex workflows and communicate precise requirements to engineering and product teams.
  • Strong quantitative and analytical skills, with sound knowledge of probability, statistics, and derivatives valuation.
  • High ownership, attention to detail, and the ability to prioritize effectively in a fast-moving environment.
  • Clear written and verbal communication skills in English; Chinese language capability is beneficial for regional collaboration.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with digital-asset derivatives venues, APIs, order books, cross-venue hedging, and exchange account or margin frameworks; familiarity with Deribit is a plus.
  • Experience with equity derivatives, tokenized equities, trading calendars, physical or cash settlement, and corporate-action handling.
  • Hands-on ability with Python and SQL; experience with time-series databases, data visualization, or production monitoring is beneficial.
  • Experience using AI tools or agent-based workflows to automate trading operations, analytics, monitoring, or documentation.
  • Experience drafting product requirements and supporting products from design and UAT through launch and post-launch optimization.
  • Master’s degree or PhD in Finance, Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science, Engineering, or another quantitative discipline.

Как выглядит успех в этой роли

  • Reliable day-to-day management of structured products pricing, hedging, and risk with strong operational discipline.
  • Measurable improvements in quote competitiveness, hedge execution, capital efficiency, product coverage, or workflow efficiency.
  • Proactive identification of business opportunities and risks, supported by practical proposals that can be implemented across teams.
  • Clear documentation and effective knowledge sharing that reduce key-person dependency and strengthen team resilience.
