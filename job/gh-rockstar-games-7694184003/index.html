At Rockstar Games, we create world-class entertainment experiences.

Become part of a team working on some of the most rewarding, large-scale creative projects to be found in any entertainment medium - all within an inclusive, highly-motivated environment where you can learn and collaborate with some of the most talented people in the industry.

Rockstar is on the lookout for a talented Technical Artist who possess a passion for technology and innovation.

This is a full-time, permanent and in-office position based out of Rockstar’s unique game development studio in the heart of Leeds, England.

WHAT WE DO

  • The Rockstar Games Technical Art team creates, maintains and enhances tools and workflows for artists across our Art, Animation, and VFX teams.
  • We create and maintain photogrammetry tools and pipelines, and support live scan sessions.
  • We drive innovation and efficiency in our pipelines by continuously researching and developing new technology.

RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Design, develop, and support tools and pipelines in DCC and standalone contexts.
  • Develop automated solutions for data processing.
  • Stay informed on the latest technology and research related to the art of data pipelines and machine learning.
  • Write clear and detailed documentation for tools and workflows.
  • Diagnose and solve technical issues affecting artists.

REQUIREMENTS

  • 5+ years of experience as a Technical Artist, Technical Director, Tools Programmer, or similar.
  • Experience developing tools for DCCs like Houdini, Zbrush, Agisoft, or Metashape.
  • Experience working through a full game production cycle or long-term project.
  • A strong understanding of Photogrammetry workflows and software.
  • Proficiency in Python.
  • Experience with build systems like Jenkins, Airflow, or Celery.
  • An understanding of a 3D asset pipeline and color management.
  • Ability to think creatively and develop innovative solutions to complex problems.

PLUSES

Please note that these are desirable skills and are not required to apply for the position.

  • A degree in Computer Science or related discipline.
  • C++ programming skills.
  • Experience working with package management and deployment systems like Rez or PyPi.
  • Experience with ML based photogrammetry workflows like Gaussian Splats or Nerfs.
