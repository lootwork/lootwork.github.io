We want you to #JOINTHEREBELLION!

For 30 years we’ve been independently developing and publishing incredible video games at our multiple studios founded by Jason & Chris Kingsley, but Rebellion is more than just games. We have our own film studio, we create board games, publish books, and through 2000AD, publish comics and graphic novels such as the amazing Judge Dredd.

We’re looking for a talented and driven Producer to join our passionate production team and contribute to our upcoming unannounced projects!

The Producer role is dedicated to proactively managing the creation and tracking of submission schedules and support production. As part of the Production team, you will play a key role in overseeing multiple overlapping projects and workflows. This position would suit someone with experience in the game submissions process and project management skills.

The Rebellion Production team are responsible for ensuring that we have a coordinated release process for all our console and PC game releases.

This role is based at our riverside headquarters in Oxford, offering a creative and relaxed atmosphere where teamwork drives our success. We can provide a relocation package, including moving assistance, temporary accommodation, and full support to ensure a smooth transition to your new environment.

Обязанности

  • To proactively manage the creation and tracking of submission schedules and checklists.
  • To plan scheduling and track assets required for submissions.
  • To effectively document and communicate with the production team to ensure all elements. of the submission process are managed effectively and on time.
  • Coordinate age rating submissions, including the preparation and collation of required. materials & liaise with external age-rating bodies and publishing/distributing partners.
  • Coordinate submission builds with QA, Build Engineers, and development teams. Ensuring builds are prepared, banked, uploaded, and tracked against release checklist requirements. Identify any associated risks/issues and coordinate the resolution of platform-holder feedback, including any required waivers.
  • To submit and publish additional content items.
  • Manage the requirements and processes for platform-specific content such as DLC items, achievements, age rating certifications etc.
  • Support QA and Production with the build, test and launch pipelines for platform releases of our games.
  • Ensure the studio is abreast of and proactive in handling changes to third-party platform processes or requirements.
  • Work with internal stakeholders across management, production, publishing, and communications.
  • Work closely with the internal & external development teams to establish submission timelines.
  • Maintain Release and Submission documentation, including tracking changes to requirements, plans, and submission activities.
  • Contribute to patch note preparation and delivery with the appropriate team members.
  • Liaise with the Digital Store Manager on relevant release and update activities and ensure simultaneous platform launches take place and any end user issues are resolved promptly.

Требования

  • Previous professional experience in game development environment
  • Experience in the game submissions process for Sony, Microsoft, Nintendo etc.
  • A full working knowledge of production methodology used in games development
  • Ability to use data to facilitate decision making and advise best practice
  • Ability to maintain relationships with, and manage process flows across, various internal departments, third parties and agencies
  • Project management skills, with a keen eye for detail
  • Ability to oversee multiple overlapping projects and workflows
  • Excellent prioritisation and multi-tasking skills, with an ability to work in a fast-paced environment with proficiency and professionalism
  • You will view challenges as opportunities, be solution oriented, and a creative problem solver
  • Strong interpersonal skills - a team player who can lead and work collaboratively within the group, as well as across business units/functions
