О TIDE

At Tide, we help SMEs save time and money in the running of their businesses by not only offering business accounts and related banking services, but also a comprehensive set of highly usable and connected administrative solutions, from invoicing to accounting.

Tide is transforming the small business banking market and now supports over 2 million members globally across the UK, India, Germany and France.

Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love.

Tide facts

  • Tide is available for UK, Indian, German and French SMEs
  • Over 2 million members globally and rapidly growing
  • Over $300 million raised in funding
  • 3,000 Tideans globally
  • Recognised with Great Place to Work certification three years in a row, and among India’s Top 50 Best Workplaces in Banking, Financial Services, and Insurance in 2026
  • We have offices in Central London, with a member support and technology centre in Sofia, Bulgaria, technology centres in Serbia, Romania, Lithuania and Hyderabad and offices in Gurugram, New Delhi, Berlin, Paris and Luxembourg

О команде

PCS helps small businesses find and take the right credit. The Credit Outcome vertical is the heaviest part of PCS: it owns the journey from a business's intent to a successful credit outcome, measured by our north-star metric, % 1st Outcome. It spans several markets and combines market-focused product teams (Scaled Markets, New Markets, India) with horizontal teams (Sales Engine and Platform). You will work hand in hand with a dedicated Engineering Director, Design, Product Operations, Analytics and Commercial Heads.

О роли

Vision, strategy and outcomes

  • Own the vertical's north star: set the strategy and roadmap to grow % 1st Outcome, balancing margin, efficiency and member experience.
  • Place the big bets: prioritise across routing, human connection, integrations and decisioning (e.g. pre-qualification), and be explicit about what we will not do.
  • Regional focus: make sound trade-offs between markets, including scaling established markets and building new ones such as India.

Leadership and team growth

  • Lead and grow product leaders: hire, coach and develop Product Leads and Senior PMs, giving each a self-contained area of the business to own.
  • Clear ownership: ensure one accountable PM/EM pair per workstream, with no ambiguity on who owns what.
  • Operating rhythm: run planning, OKRs and reviews that connect team work to vertical and company goals.

Partnership and delivery

  • Engineering partnership: work as a true pair with the Engineering Director on roadmap, capacity and technical investment, including platform health.
  • Cross-functional influence: align Design, Data, Risk & Compliance, Commercial and Operations on priorities and launches.
  • Data and experimentation: use funnel analytics and experiments to find friction, make decisions and show impact in commercial terms.
  • Applied AI: identify where automation and AI (e.g. lead scoring, agentic sales tooling) can raise conversion and efficiency.

Кого мы ищем

  • Experience: 8+ years in product management, with 3+ years leading product managers or product leads [TBC level/years].
  • Commercial acumen: you tie product decisions to revenue, margin and unit economics.
  • Data-native: comfortable with funnel analytics, experimentation and BI tools.
  • Technical fluency: you can engage confidently with engineers on APIs, integrations and data flows.
  • Leader of leaders: you coach strong PMs, set a high bar and create room for people to grow.
  • Influence without authority: you align senior stakeholders across functions and regions.
  • Nice to have: experience in lending, credit marketplaces, fintech or regulated financial services; experience building products in multiple markets, ideally including India.
