🛡️ THE ROLE

Most GRC roles are about keeping auditors happy. This one is about earning our members' trust: every day, they share their diagnoses, their claims and their care with us.

You'll own security governance and risk at Alan end to end. That means one clear framework across our certifications and regulations, which the whole company uses to decide what matters most. You set the direction, make the calls on risk, and help every team own its part.

We just earned our HDS certification, and there's much more to build as we grow from 1M to many millions of members, across more countries and regulators. That includes how we govern AI, which Alan uses across its products and operations.

This is a hands-on senior role. You do the work yourself, mentor the junior GRC engineer we're hiring alongside you, and grow the team over time.

🚀 WHAT YOU'LL DO

- Make our security framework a tool for the whole company. You own the full certification cycle (ISO 27001, HDS and those to come), and turn it into something teams use every day to prioritise, not a binder for auditors.

- Bring the security view on regulation. Legal and Risk own the regulatory relationships. You bring the security answer: what the rules mean for how we protect data, and which controls make it work in practice.

- Run risk as an ongoing programme. You map and prioritise security risks, including AI risks, turn them into clear treatment plans, and feed them into company-wide risk decisions with the Risk team.

- Help every team own its risks. You define the controls framework and the standards, while the teams who build and run our systems own the controls. You brief the board and audit committee, and you know which findings need an urgent call and which can wait for the quarterly report.

- Own incident governance. You triage and escalate incidents, own business continuity and disaster recovery, and shape the security side of our regulatory incident reports.

- Be a credible partner to engineering. You work with engineering teams early on new designs and spot control gaps (access, encryption, logging) before they ship. You don't need to be an architect, but you can hold the conversation.

🏗️ WHAT YOU'LL BUILD

- One governance model: clear ownership of risks and controls across teams, so security decisions don't bottleneck on one person.

- A living risk map: prioritised risks that actually drive company and engineering decisions.

- One compliance backbone: a single control system across our countries and regulators, built to scale.

- An automated evidence engine: evidence collected straight from engineering systems, so audits run continuously instead of as a quarterly rush.

You'll work closely with Legal, the Data Protection Officer, Internal Audit and Risk, and day to day with engineering and operations teams.

⭐ WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR

Your team

You'll join Alan's Corporate team, which brings together our control functions: Risk, Corporate Governance & Internal Control, Legal, Compliance and Security. They share one mission: building what lets Alan operate at scale, safely and responsibly. Within that group, you carry the security governance and risk dimension, working hand in hand with the security engineers who run our infrastructure and platform security, and with a junior GRC engineer we're hiring alongside you. You'll be hands-on every day, building and owning the work yourself. Along the way, you'll mentor the junior engineer and help shape how GRC grows at Alan.

Что вы приносите

- You want to build and own things yourself, and you enjoy helping someone else grow while you do it.

- 6+ years in security, with real GRC ownership.

- You've run a GRC roadmap in a certified or strongly regulated environment (ISO 27001, HDS, SOC 2, or regulated sectors like health, insurance or finance). You're comfortable working with certification auditors, and you can explain what a framework requires and why it makes sense for business teams.

- You take a stand on risks, and you'd rather close one real risk than write ten policies.

- You're fluent in French and English.

A big plus

- You've driven a certification or audit cycle end to end yourself.

- You've automated compliance work, or you're eager to, and you have a view on where AI can help.

- You've presented risk to senior leaders or a board.

- You've mentored or onboarded someone before.
