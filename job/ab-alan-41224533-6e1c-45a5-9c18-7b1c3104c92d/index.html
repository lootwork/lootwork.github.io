🛡️ THE ROLE

Most GRC roles are about keeping auditors happy. This one is about earning our members' trust: every day, they share their diagnoses, their claims and their care with us.

You'll join Alan's security team to help run our compliance and risk work across ISO 27001, HDS and DORA, across a growing number of countries and regulators. We just earned our HDS certification, and there's much more to build, including how we govern AI across our products: assessing AI risks, setting the controls, and keeping up with the AI Act.

You'll learn alongside experienced security engineers, with a dedicated coach from day one. You'll take on real ownership early, and your scope grows as we scale from 1M to many millions of members. And you'll learn how we work at Alan: teams own their risks and controls, we automate wherever we can, and we focus on real outcomes, not paperwork.

🚀 WHAT YOU'LL DO

- Keep our certifications on track. You run the day-to-day of the ISO 27001 and HDS audit cycles: collecting evidence, preparing internal audits, and following up on corrective actions until they're closed.

- Turn regulations into practical actions. With Legal and the DPO, you help translate requirements like DORA, HDS, and GDPR into clear tasks for the teams, and track that they get done.

- Learn the health sector rules. You build your knowledge of health-specific frameworks (ANS, HDS) and help apply them to how we handle health data.

- Contribute to risk management. You help build and update our risk mapping, including AI risks, and follow up on the treatment plans with the teams who own them.

- Support the teams who own the controls. You keep the controls framework up to date, answer teams' questions, and help explain security clearly to every stakeholder, from engineers to managers.

- Support incident governance. You help classify and document incidents, keep Business continuity and Disaster recovery plans up to date, and prepare inputs for DORA incident reports.

- Automate what slows us down. You spot repetitive compliance tasks and automate them with scripts, tools or AI.

🏗️ WHAT YOU'LL HELP BUILD

- One compliance backbone: A single control system for several regulators in several countries, so Alan can scale without rebuilding compliance each time.

- An automated evidence engine: evidence collected straight from engineering systems with scripts and AI, so audits run continuously instead of as a quarterly rush.

You'll work closely with Legal, the DPO, Internal Audit and Risk, and day to day with engineering and operations teams.

⭐ WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR

- You want to do the work yourself and grow with it: a permanent, hands-on role where you build things for the long run.

- 2+ years in security, GRC or compliance (an audit background is fine if you want to move into building).

- You've played a key part in a certification or audit cycle (ISO 27001, HDS, SOC 2 or similar).

- You're curious about how things work technically, and you've automated at least one manual task (a script, a tool, an AI workflow).

- You're fluent in French and English, and comfortable presenting in both.

- You learn fast, ask for feedback, and go into the details when it matters.
