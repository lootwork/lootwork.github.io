Blackbird Interactive is a creative-fueled, future-driven, independent game studio where the best talent in the industry can find long-term careers to help grow their abilities while working on a wide range of projects, which encompass world-class IPs as well as our own creations across multiple genres.

Our studio operates as a remote-first workplace, offering flexible work arrangements.

PLEASE NOTE: At this time, we are only able to hire candidates who are legally eligible to work in Canada and reside within Canada.

Blackbird Interactive is looking for a deeply experienced Studio Head of Production to lead game development and production across the company. As the most senior development role in the studio, you’ll be accountable for the successful delivery of every game in it, owning schedules, budgets, staffing, publisher and partner relationships, and ensuring every game ships at the quality our partners and players expect.

You’ll lead the studio’s production, technical, and operations leadership and work as a peer to the other studio senior leadership roles. You’ll hold final say on production, development and budget decisions for the company, and partner closely with studio leadership, Business Development, and our publishing and IP partners. This role blends portfolio-level business ownership with hands-on production leadership; you’ll set the studio’s priorities, make the hard calls on scope, resources, and investment, and build a healthy, high-performing dev team that delivers. If you enjoy owning outcomes across multiple games, building strong partner relationships, and leading teams through the realities of development, this role could be a great fit!

Responsibilities include

DEVELOPMENT LEADERSHIP

  • Hold overall accountability for the studio’s games and the teams that make them, from pitch and pre-production through launch and post-launch support
  • Hold final decision-making authority on production, development and budget across the studio, including schedules, scope, resourcing, and spend
  • Lead the studio’s production, technical, and operations leads through coaching, mentoring, hiring, and performance management
  • Set the studio’s dev priorities and goals, balancing project needs, partner commitments, and studio strategy
  • Represent the studio within company leadership, contributing to studio-wide planning, resourcing, and business strategy
  • Foster a healthy, inclusive, high-performing culture where teams do their best work at a sustainable pace

DELIVERY AND BUDGET

  • Own delivery of every game in the studio, ensuring milestones are met on time, on budget, and to the quality agreed with partners
  • Own the studio’s budgets and financial performance, including project budgets, forecasts, cost tracking, and margin, in partnership with Finance
  • Establish production plans, milestone schedules, and production processes across projects, giving teams the structure and clarity they need to execute
  • Identify and manage risks across the portfolio, making informed tradeoffs on scope, schedule, resources, and quality, and escalating early when plans change
  • Support teams in managing features and scope wisely, deciding where to invest and where not to

PUBLISHER AND PARTNER RELATIONS

  • Serve as the executive point of contact for publishers, IP holders, and platform partners on the studio’s games
  • Manage partner relationships end to end, including milestone acceptance, change requests, contract scope, and regular status and executive reporting
  • Partner with Business Development on new opportunities, providing production estimates, staffing plans, and budgets for pitches and proposals
  • Communicate the state of each project and the studio’s plans clearly to partners and studio leadership

STAFFING AND ORGANIZATION

  • Own the studio’s dev staffing plan, including headcount, allocation of people across projects, and ramp-up and roll-off as projects start and finish
  • Shape the studio’s team structure, composition, and recruiting strategy so teams adapt to deliver for players, partners, and the studio
  • Own hiring, performance, and compensation decisions for the studio’s dev team and direct reports, in partnership with HR

Требования

  • At least 12 years of experience in game production and development, including at least 8 years as an Executive Producer, Senior Producer, Development Director, or equivalent production leadership role
  • Experience shipping multiple titles on console and/or PC through the full development cycle, from concept to launch
  • Proven track record leading multiple projects or teams at the same time
  • Significant people leadership experience, including managing senior leaders and owning hiring, performance management, and compensation decisions
  • Experience owning multi-million dollar game budgets, including forecasting, cost control, and financial reporting
  • Experience managing relationships with publishers, IP holders, or platform holders, including milestone negotiations and executive-level communication
  • Strong understanding of production methodologies, with the ability to set up and evolve production processes across different team sizes and project types
  • Ability to make difficult decisions on scope, staffing, and investment, communicate them clearly at every level of the organization, and build strong peer partnerships with creative leadership
  • Excellent communication and presentation skills, with experience preparing and presenting plans to executives and partners…
