(Senior) Brand Designer

Moonfare is at an inflection point. We are 10 years in the making, with almost €4 billion in AUM and a new CMO recently in place, and the brand now carries more weight than it ever has. We are looking for a designer to make every surface we put in front of an investor look as though it was made on purpose.

The person you are designing for is a partner at Freshfields, a Goldman MD, the principal of a small family office. They read curated material every day and they notice slippage immediately. Our reference points are Loro Piana, Aman, Cartier and Hauser & Wirth — houses that wear their authority quietly and let the object carry the brand. We are a gallery rather than a supermarket, and the craft has to hold at that standard across a fund brochure, a thirty-two word invitation, a billboard at WEF and a single slide in a sales deck.

The Brand Designer joins Moonfare's Marketing team — a high-output team responsible for building the brand, setting standards of excellence, generating demand, and driving investor acquisition across global markets. You will work daily with Fund Marketing, Investor Relations and Compliance, and you will own the craft rather than coordinate it. This role reports to the CMO.

AI tooling is business as usual in this team — Claude and Figma in the daily stack. The gain we want is not speed for its own sake. It is your hours moving from the mechanical end of production to the thinking.

Вам предстоит

  • Own the craft on every investor-facing surface — fund brochures, the annual letter, invitations, event environments, sales decks, web pages, paid assets — and hold the line on quality when the deadline argues otherwise.
  • Tighten and maintain the design system: type, grid, colour, photography, motion and templates that let non-designers produce work which still looks like us.
  • Design the fund marketing design system alongside Fund Marketing, so that each launch is assembled rather than reinvented, and the material reads like an LP memo rather than a brochure.
  • Take long-form and content design seriously — due diligence summaries, research, the annual letter — where the job is clarity and restraint rather than decoration.
  • Design for physical objects and rooms as fluently as for screens: printed matter, direct mail, gifts, signage and event environments, GTM launches and private dinners.
  • Work inside compliance rather than around it, treating risk warnings and disclosures as a design problem to be solved properly.
  • Bring an AI-first mindset to the production stack — concepting, variation, retouching, resizing and versioning — so that more of your time goes to the thinking and less to the mechanical.
  • Art-direct external partners including photographers, illustrators, agencies and printers, and brief them well enough that the output needs little rescuing.

The Next Moonfarian

  • You bring six or more years of design experience, with a portfolio that shows editorial and brand craft rather than campaign volume.
  • You have designed for a luxury house, a gallery or museum, a serious publication or a premium consumer brand — or you can show that you understand that register and want to work in it.
  • You bring an AI-native mindset — you use AI tools in your day-to-day and you have views on where they sharpen the work and where they flatten it.
  • You are equally fluent in print and digital, and you have shipped physical work: something printed, bound, packaged or installed.
  • You bring hands-on command of Figma and the Adobe suite
  • You give feedback as well as take it, and you can explain a design decision to a non-designer without defensiveness or jargon.
  • You can work at pace inside a regulated industry and you see compliance as a design constraint rather than a blocker.

This role is unlikely to be a good match if you:

  • Need a fully written brief before you can start. A good deal of this work begins as a conversation and a deadline.
  • Prefer to direct rather than make. This is a hands-on role and the person in it designs every day.
  • Are happiest producing high volumes of performance creative. Our channel mix is deliberately narrow, and quality of execution matters more here than volume of message.
  • Find restraint frustrating rather than freeing. Our brand is built on hairline rules, whitespace and type weight rather than colour and ornament.
  • Prefer to work at arm's length from the business. You will sit with Fund Marketing, Investor Relations and Compliance constantly.
