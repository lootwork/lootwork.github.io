О роли

You will be the senior on-site reference for Endpoint Engineering in Brazil, Nu's largest endpoint operation: about 11.8K devices assigned to nearly 8,000 active Nubankers and 3,800 third parties, across 6 offices today and 2 more opening soon.

Every Nubanker depends on a device to do their best work. This role makes sure each of those devices is known, compliant and tracked from the day it arrives to the day it is retired, which supports Nu's international expansion and regulated-market requirements.

You will own the accuracy and audit-readiness of the Brazil device inventory, and the quality of the processes around it: onboarding, replacements, returns, disposal, vendors and new office openings. This is an individual contributor role. You will not manage people, but you will guide the team and our third-party providers on complex topics, and you will decide on site, within hours, the cases that cannot wait.

YOU'LL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR

Your main challenge is to keep a large, live operation reliable while turning recurring problems into repeatable processes. A typical week splits between the cases that need your judgment, the checks that keep the inventory accurate, and the work that prepares the next office or process change.

- Act as the senior on-site point of contact for Endpoint Engineering across Brazil's offices, and decide the complex cases: lost or stolen devices, devices in transit, vendor and warranty escalations, inventory discrepancies and disposal decisions.

- Own the accuracy of the device inventory, physical vs digital: reconcile procurement, logistics, MDM and asset records, run physical counts, and keep the inventory audit-ready.

- Own the device lifecycle end to end: setup and deployment, onboarding and offboarding, replacements, returns, secure disposal, and the criteria that decide when a device is refreshed.

- Prepare new offices so devices and MDM are ready before people arrive, including stock, provisioning and acceptance on site.

- Turn regulatory and security requirements into endpoint controls, and keep audit evidence current and reusable, working with Security and Compliance.

- Manage hardware vendors, resellers and logistics partners: SLAs, warranty and RMA performance, scorecards, and cost evidence for purchase and refresh decisions with FinOps and Procurement.

- Guide the Brazil team and third-party providers: keep runbooks current, define escalation boundaries, and write the automation requirements that remove manual work.

- Work with the Tech Center and the HIVE asset platform so requests that cross teams do not stall.

- Report on inventory accuracy, delivery lead time, return closure rate and cost per device, and escalate to the Global Endpoint Engineering Ops Manager only when a case exceeds agreed boundaries.

WE ARE LOOKING FOR A PERSON WHO HAS

- Fluent English, written and spoken. This is non-negotiable. English is your daily working language with global peers, vendors and leadership.

- Experience operating endpoint or end-user computing at scale, ideally across several sites and tens of thousands of devices at different stages of their lifecycle.

- Hands-on knowledge of device lifecycle management with MDM and zero-touch enrollment for macOS and Windows, including imaging and patching.

- Asset and inventory management practice (ITAM; CAMP is a plus): reconciling records across systems, and controlling loss, return and disposal.

- Experience translating security and regulatory requirements into endpoint controls and audit evidence.

- Hardware vendor and supply chain management: SLAs, warranty and RMA, logistics and contract compliance.

- The habit of working from data (cost per device, failure rates, lifecycle and utilization) to justify refresh and purchase decisions.

- A record of designing processes and runbooks that other people run, of guiding colleagues on complex technical cases, and of influencing peers without formal authority.

- Availability to work on site and to travel occasionally within Brazil, and less frequently abroad.

Будет плюсом

- Experience in regulated environments, such as financial services.

- Experience opening or expanding offices and provisioning devices there.

- Portuguese and Spanish.

- Experience defining automation requirements with an automation or RPA team.

- Familiarity with identity (Okta), service management (JSM) and BigQuery-based reporting.

- Experience with managed-service models and their escalation design.
