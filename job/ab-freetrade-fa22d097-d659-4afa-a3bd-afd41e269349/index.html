О FREETRADE

Freetrade’s mission is to become the default place to invest. Investing has been too complicated and expensive for too long, keeping millions from making the most of their savings.

We’re changing that.

We’re building our team and looking for people who are excited to reshape how our customers invest and grow their wealth. If you’re driven by solving complex problems and building innovative products, you’ll fit right in.

2026 is a big year for us. Last year, we announced a game changing deal to become part of the IG Group. We’ll continue to operate as an independent business while gaining access to the resources and support of an established leader in the space.

This year, we’re accelerating our roadmap and taking our products to the next level. We’re building new features like mutual funds, bonds, and family investment tools. We’re also exploring how we can leverage AI to deliver an even better experience for our customers.

Freetrade Engineers play a pivotal role in developing and maintaining a robust, scalable platform used by thousands daily. We tackle big technical challenges, drive innovation, and build solutions that make a real impact.

Here, your code doesn’t just run - it powers the decisions of investors across the UK.

О роли

We are looking for a Senior Backend Software Engineer to play a pivotal role in our engineering team. In this position, you'll lead the end-to-end delivery of complex, impactful features, showcasing a passion for independent problem-solving and a drive to scale our next-generation, user-focused product.

Our philosophy on hiring

We hire for engineering excellence, not just a specific list of keywords. Our interview process is language-agnostic; we care about your ability to solve complex problems, design scalable systems, and write clean code in whichever language you’re most comfortable with.

Наш стек

Once you join, you’ll be working primarily with TypeScript, Node.js, and PostgreSQL on GCP. We don’t expect you to be an expert in these on Day 1, but we do look for engineers who are excited to master this stack and contribute to its evolution.

Что предстоит делать

- Lead features from initial design and development through to post-production and validation.

- Level up the team through technical workshops, pair programming, and active mentorship.

- Define and uphold company-wide standards for code reviews and engineering best practices.

- Act as a senior escalation point for high-priority issues and participate in our on-call rotation.

- Conduct technical interviews to identify and hire top-tier engineering talent.

- Constantly evaluate the tech landscape to improve our development processes and tools.

О вас

- Involve others in design decisions and delegate responsibility to help your squad grow.

- Speak candidly when quality dips and work proactively with your team to raise the bar.

- Stay calm and effective when tackling complex, ambiguous technical challenges under pressure.

- Maintain a deep obsession with the "how" and "why" of the systems you build.

- Bring a genuine interest in the world of investments and fintech.

- Stay current with industry trends to influence better internal engineering practices.

Our interview process

We keep things simple and transparent. Our process starts with an initial conversation with one of our Senior Talent Partners, followed by:

1. Hiring Manager interview – A deep dive into your skills and experience.

2. Live Coding – Show us how you think and solve problems.

3. System Design – We’ll explore how you design systems to handle real-world challenges.

4. Final Values interview – A conversation with our stakeholders, where we also assess cultural fit.

Hybrid working culture

We’re operating in a hybrid model: 3-days in-office / 2-days from home. We believe great collaboration happens in person and our office in central London, Shoreditch designed to bring people together.

🏢 Office days: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday

🏡 Work from home days: Wednesday, Friday
