Position Overview

Join our Central Analytics team to build high-quality data marts, tools, and AI-powered workflows that shape player experience and drive game growth. You'll work alongside talented and experienced Analytics Engineers, partnering closely with game and marketing analysts to deliver solutions that make a real difference, all using a modern, cutting-edge technical stack.

To truly make an impact, you'll need to learn quickly, thrive in a fast-paced environment, and collaborate closely within the team and with the stakeholders.

What You’ll Take On

  • Write and optimize complex SQL queries to support, experimentation, and model feature engineering across large-scale player datasets.
  • Design, build, and maintain cross-game data marts, providing clean and transformed data ready for Analysts to use.
  • Develop scalable, reusable, and maintainable code, tools, and frameworks that can be adopted across different game teams.
  • Partner with game analytics teams to co-design, build, and maintain data tools and products used across multiple applications.
  • Build and maintain end-to-end AI pipelines to support the day to day Analysts tasks.
  • Share knowledge across the team by educating game analysts on coding and software engineering best practices.

Why should you join us?

  • We are committed to continuous improvement, you will never stop learning.
  • We are a collaborative team that learns from each other every day.
  • We run a large production pipeline spanning ETLs, analytical tools and AI/ML models.
  • We focus on delivering innovative solutions to real-world problems.

Что вы приносите

  • A bachelor degree in a quantitative field (e.g Engineering, Computer Science, Maths, Statistics or Physics).
  • Advanced SQL skills, including experience with CTEs, query optimization, and large-scale data warehouses.
  • Advanced experience building and maintaining data pipelines in production environments, using tools such as Airflow, Docker, S3, and CI/CD workflows.
  • Advanced experience in Python for developing production-level data analytics solutions.
  • Basic to intermediate experience integrating LLMs or working with generative AI tools and workflows.
  • A collaborative team player who builds trust, communicates openly, and contributes to shared success.
  • A fast learner who is proactive and comfortable working with ambiguity.
  • Detail-oriented, with a strong commitment to accuracy and high-quality outcomes.
  • Pragmatic, with the ability to balance short-term delivery needs with long-term technical quality.

Nice-to-haves

  • Experience creating and maintaining dashboards in Tableau or a similar BI tool.
  • Background in the Mobile Gaming industry.
  • Background in Marketing Analytics.
