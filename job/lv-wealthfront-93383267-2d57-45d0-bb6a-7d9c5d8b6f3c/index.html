We’re looking for a Senior Software Engineer with Networking expertise to join our Infrastructure team, where you will work with hardware and networking across our data centers, offices, and the cloud to build and maintain critical financial systems.

Wealthfront’s Infrastructure team is a team of “Generalists with Expertise” who adhere to the principles of infrastructure-as-code and automation. We operate both physical servers and cloud-based computing, and our network spans both environments.

О роли

As a member of the Networking team, you will play a key role in shaping future network architecture. You operate comfortably across hardware and software boundaries, writing code to automate infrastructure and bringing strategic expertise to designing, implementing, and maintaining systems. You approach Networking problems with a bias towards vendor-agnostic IaC solutions.

AS A KEY MEMBER OF THE INFRASTRUCTURE TEAM, YOU WILL

  • Manage our networking and server hardware, topology, and connectivity in our physical data centers and cloud
  • Design and build network telemetry, monitoring, and observability
  • Build and maintain an automated change management pipeline to deploy network configuration revisions and provision hardware
  • Ensure critical services operate reliably by triaging and fixing operational issues as an on-call engineer, participating in post-mortems and implementing improvements to prevent future issues
  • Make and collaborate on key decisions regarding the evolution of our infrastructure, such as our hybrid cloud architecture
  • Be responsible for liaising with third parties, vendors and internet service providers as well as traveling to our Bay Area and New Jersey data centers and offices for onsite networking projects

THIS ROLE COULD BE A FIT IF YOU HAVE

  • Proficiency with automation technology including Terraform, Ansible, and Chef
  • Proficiency with at least one language such as Go, Python, or Ruby and configuration management tools such as Git
  • Demonstrated experience with Linux systems
  • Experience with automating production data center environments, including hardware provisioning, network device configuration, and capacity planning
  • Proficiency with Networking protocols such as BGP, MLAG, VLANs, DNS
  • Excellent communications and organizational skills
  • BS or MS in Engineering or Computer Science or related field, or equivalent professional experience

STRONG CANDIDATE MAY ALSO HAVE

  • Experience with routing and switching in data centers
  • Familiarity with Palo Alto Networks Firewalls, Juniper switches
  • Background on VPN technologies, like OpenVPN, IPSec, TLS
  • Experience with AWS Networking technologies like Direct Connect, NACLs, Security Groups, VPC, etc

ONCE YOU JOIN, WEALTHFRONT WILL

  • Provide you with a mentor who who will support you in your onboarding journey
  • Encourage you to lead impactful projects that match your professional goals
  • Support your professional development by providing feedback during weekly 1:1s and our bi-annual reviews
  • Provide autonomy so you can be a happy and successful member of our team

Estimated annual salary range: $172,000 - $228,000 plus equity and a discretionary bonus.

Benefits include medical, vision, dental, 401K plan, generous time off, parental leave, wellness reimbursements, professional development, employee investing discount, and more!

О Wealthfront

Here at Wealthfront, our mission is to create a financial system that favors people, not institutions. We do this by leveraging technology to build powerful, low-cost, and easy-to-use financial products that help modern investors grow and manage their money.

We started with the ambition to transform the investment advisory business. By automating strategies typically reserved for the wealthy, we unlocked access to high quality investment advice for a digitally-native generation that was underserved by traditional institutions. Since then, we've expanded to a full suite of products designed to help our clients turn their savings into long-term wealth, including:

  • A Cash Account that, through our partner banks, offers one of the highest annual percentage yields on uninvested cash in the industry, while providing instant and secure access to your money with no account fees and a full suite of checking features.
  • A zero-commission Stock Investing Account with 50+ handpicked collections that help DIY investors discover new companies and make smarter investing decisions.
  • Multiple automated investing portfolios designed to unlock tax savings through sophisticated strategies like fixed income, tax-loss harvesting, and direct indexing—which we offer at industry-leading low costs and accessible minimums.

Our award-winning products have attracted over 1 million clients who trust us with more than $90 billion of their hard earned savings—and we're far from done. If you’re inspired to help us reshape the financial industry as we create our next chapter, let’s talk!
