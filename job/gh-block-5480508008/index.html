О роли

The Security Governance, Risk and Compliance (SGRC) team within InfoSec manages cybersecurity risk across Block. Its Sourcing Security Assurance (SSA) team assesses third-party software, services, and integrations, enabling informed decisions about vendors, data, and access.

As a Senior Sourcing Security Assurance Analyst, you will lead complex, risk-based provider assessments, evaluating products, integrations, data handling, and controls beyond vendor certifications and translating findings into practical recommendations.

Reporting to the Cybersecurity Risk, Vulnerability, and Vendor Security Assurance Lead, you will work with business owners, Procurement and Strategic Sourcing, Security, Privacy, Legal, and other partners. This senior individual contributor role requires independent judgment, stakeholder influence, mentorship, and scalable assurance.

Вам предстоит

  • Lead complex third-party assessments from intake and scoping through evidence review, risk analysis, documentation, and recommendation. Manage competing reviews and communicate progress and dependencies.
  • Assess the provider, product, and use case, including business criticality, data sensitivity, deployment, integrations, access, and existing use across Block. Apply the Provider Assurance Level framework to focus risk-based diligence.
  • Review questionnaires, SOC 2 Type II reports, ISO 27001 certifications, penetration test summaries, and policies; assess evidence relevance, identify gaps, and follow up with vendors. Document evidence, risks, limitations, and recommended dispositions or mitigations. Escalate exceptions and risk decisions to their owners without accepting risk on their behalf.
  • Assess identity and access management, SSO, OAuth, APIs, service accounts, cloud and on-premises deployments, browser extensions, and AI features, including data flows, permissions, and compensating controls. Engage security, privacy, and governance partners for specialized reviews.
  • Support ongoing assurance through risk-based reassessments, material-change reviews, vendor inventories and baselines, and monitoring signals. Revisit prior findings and coordinate follow-up.
  • Triage third-party incidents and breach notifications, assess relevance to Block, and coordinate escalation with incident response, Privacy, and other partners. Work across teams to resolve questions and identify practical security requirements and mitigations while supporting business timelines.
  • Improve assurance quality through playbooks, assessment methods, reporting, metrics, and automation opportunities. Mentor teammates and share knowledge across a geographically distributed team.

You Have

  • 6+ years of experience in security assessment, third-party security or vendor risk management, security assurance, or a related field, including ownership of complex assessments.
  • Experience interpreting SOC 2 reports, ISO 27001 materials, penetration tests, and questionnaires; identifying gaps; and applying frameworks such as NIST and PCI DSS proportionately.
  • Working knowledge of identity, authentication, authorization, SAML, OAuth, OpenID Connect, APIs, and service accounts; experience assessing data handling, cloud or on-premises deployments, security architecture, and compensating controls.
  • Ability to synthesize incomplete or conflicting information, make well-reasoned recommendations, and explain technical risk to technical and nontechnical audiences.
  • Experience working with vendors and cross-functional partners, managing an assessment portfolio and priorities, and improving processes, documenting practices, or mentoring teammates.

You May Also Have

  • Experience assessing AI-enabled products, SaaS applications, browser extensions, or other workforce software integrations.
  • Experience with platforms such as Zip, OneTrust, Jira, or Grip; or using data, reporting, scripting, or automation to improve assurance workflows and stakeholder visibility.
  • Relevant certifications such as CISSP, CISA, CISM, or CRISC, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Experience working across distributed teams and explaining security findings to senior stakeholders.
