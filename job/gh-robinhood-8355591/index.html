О Team & Role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world's biggest financial problems. We're looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn't a place for complacency, it's where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We're a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

The Government Products team focuses on building and scaling systems that support long-term investing and account management for millions of customers. This team works closely with product, data, and infrastructure partners to deliver reliable, secure, and intuitive experiences. You will help shape systems that enable customers to plan for their financial future with confidence!

As a Software Engineer, Full Stack, you will be an important contributor to this 0-to-1 initiative. You will be responsible for building and shipping the features and services that span our Go backend and React web experience, working alongside experienced engineers and product partners. This is a high-visibility, high-impact role where you will directly shape a platform built to serve millions of future customers

This role is based in our New York, NY and Menlo Park, CA offices, with in-person attendance expected 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build, test, and ship features across Go backend services and React-based front-end experiences for a brand-new, standalone product optimized for security, reliability, and scale.
  • Collaborate closely with Product, Design, and external stakeholders to translate requirements into secure, well-tested, and maintainable software.
  • Take ownership of well-scoped projects from design through release, growing your independence and technical judgment over time.
  • Move between backend and front-end work as priorities shift, helping the team stay flexible and deliver quickly.
  • Contribute to code reviews, documentation, and team engineering practices that raise the quality bar for the whole team.

Что вы приносите

  • 4+ Years experience building production backend services, ideally in Go or Ruby, with a working understanding of API design and reliable data flows.
  • 4+ years experience building front-end applications with JavaScript frameworks such as React, with familiarity with Node.
  • Comfort working across both the backend and front end of an application, and a willingness to learn quickly in a zero-to-one product space.
  • A habit of writing clean, well-tested code, and a strong desire to learn from feedback and grow as an engineer.
  • Clear communication skills with the ability to partner closely with Product, Design, and cross-functional teammates.
  • Experience with financial products, ledgering, reporting, or investment technology is a plus.
