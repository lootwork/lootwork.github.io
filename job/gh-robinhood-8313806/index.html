Robinhood's Platforms Governance team works to keep our platforms secure and trustworthy. We partner with business teams to design the structures, policies, and processes that put safety first, guide decisions, manage risk, and support compliance. Our work also covers fraud prevention, detection, investigation, and recovery. Together, these efforts protect the firm and our customers from the impact of fraud and protect the integrity of our operations. We take pride in the trust our customers place in us, and we work every day to earn it!

In your role as a Platform Risk & Control Specialist, you will partner with Governance pillar leaders to define, scope, and project manage critical cross-Governance initiatives, support international expansion across key functions, and facilitate departmental planning and organizational strategy to drive scalability and operational maturity.

This role is based in our Chicago, IL, Denver, CO and Westlake, TX offices, with in-person attendance expected 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Partner with Governance pillar leaders to define, scope, and project manage the most important cross-Governance strategic initiatives.
  • Oversee international expansion for Governance workstreams, ensuring proper coverage for all key functions across geographies and entities (including SVI, Complaints, Control Testing, Entity Syncs, and Policy support).
  • Deliver support for various Golden Eagle workstreams (brokerage, call center, fraud) across areas supported by Governance (Risk, QA, Controls, etc.).
  • Develop reports & trend analysis that monitor compliance and risk exposures at the agent, team & business level. Communicate trends to key stakeholders on a regular basis.
  • Ensure adherance to predetermined risk standards, complete required deliverables and comply with business standard operating procedures.
  • Collaborate with designated principals and team managers to share control issues and provide regular reports on overall performance and trends

Что вы приносите

  • Bachelor’s Degree from an accredited institution and/or 3+ years experience in Operations in a customer obsessive organization, either at a start up, fintech or financial services company
  • 4+ years experience in risk programs and/or customer-facing teams
  • Uncompromising integrity and strong dedication to professionalism
  • Ability to think critically and apply logical problem-solving skills to resolve issues
  • The ability to concisely and accurately communicate complex topics
  • Familiarity with GRC tooling (e.g. Diligent, Auditboard)
  • FINRA Licenses 7, 63 and 24 strongly preferred
