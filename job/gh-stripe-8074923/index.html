Обязанности

  • Lead or collaborate with cross-functional teams, including Product, Engineering, Legal, Compliance, Support, Operations Technology, and Vendor Management, to define operational requirements for new Link/Crypto onramp products, features, and experiments.
  • Partner with Product and Engineering teams to design and implement the infrastructure and tools needed to support operational workflows.

Minimum requirements

Must have a Bachelor's degree or foreign equivalent in Business, Management, Economics or a related field, plus 5 years of post bachelor's progressive experience in Operations. Must also have each of the following:

  • 5 years experience pulling data, creating queries and analyzing large datasets using SQL.
  • 4 years experience leading end-to-end execution of large, cross-functional projects to improve user and Support Specialist experience and operational efficiency.
  • 1 year of experience with designing and executing comprehensive test cases for new features, system updates, and process changes.
  • 1 year experience coordinating feedback loops between end users, support teams and product teams.
  • 1 year experience writing Product Requirement Documents (PRDs) and Business Requirement Documents (BRDs) and the ability to translate operational needs into technical requirements.
  • 1 year experience with CRM and support platforms including Salesforce, Case Studio, and CRV, including designing and implementing automations, workflows, and routing logic to reduce manual tasks for Support agents.
