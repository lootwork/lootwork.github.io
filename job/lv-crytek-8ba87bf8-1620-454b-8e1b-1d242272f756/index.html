As Global Community Manager, you lead community strategy and operations across all of Crytek's titles, reporting to the Head of Communication & PR.

You own how we show up for players day to day: the programs, the channels, the moderation standards and the tone. You lead the community management team and ensure the community’s voice reaches the teams and decision-makers who can act on it.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own global community strategy across the portfolio
  • Lead and develop the community management team across titles
  • Set moderation standards, escalation paths and channel operating practice
  • Own community programs and the community content calendar
  • Ensure player sentiment and issues reach product, development and leadership clearly
  • Partner with Creator Partnerships, PR and Marketing on integrated activity
  • Report on community health with evidence, not anecdote

Что вы приносите

  • 6+ years in community management, including team leadership
  • Experience with demanding, long-standing player communities
  • Strong moderation and crisis-handling judgement
  • Multi-title or multi-channel operating experience
  • Ability to represent the community credibly to development teams
  • Excellent written communication in English
  • Willingness to relocate to Frankfurt am Main, Germany
