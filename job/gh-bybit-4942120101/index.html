Role Summary

The EU BI Head serves as the single owner of all EU data management. This role is responsible for building and maintaining the data infrastructure that fulfills compliance, business, and financial requirements across multiple licensed entities. The position combines hands-on data governance with strategic advisory to management on business risks and opportunities.

Key Responsibilities

1. EU Data Ownership & Structure

  • Act as the sole owner of all EU BI data management across business lines
  • Design and implement data structures that fulfill compliance, business, and financial reporting requirements
  • Ensure data coverage spans all EU-licensed entities (MiCAR, EMI, MIFID, and future licenses)
  • Establish and maintain a unified data architecture that enables consistent cross-entity reporting

2. Reporting & Financial Analysis

  • Own and deliver all regular regulatory reports with accuracy and timeliness
  • Produce financial projections and business data analysis for management decision-making
  • Manage tax reporting data requirements and ensure data readiness for audits
  • Identify and resolve business data discrepancies; support management and teams in troubleshooting data issues

3. Data Governance & Compliance

  • Lead data governance framework for EU operations, including data segregation and data protection policies
  • Ensure full compliance with EU GDPR and DORA requirements
  • Implement and maintain data classification, access controls, and retention policies
  • Coordinate with legal and compliance teams on data-related regulatory obligations

4. Risk Alerting & Strategic Advisory

  • Proactively identify and escalate business risks through data insights
  • Support management and leadership in taking timely action on risk indicators
  • Monitor and report on important business impacts and competitor movements
  • Provide data-driven recommendations to inform strategic decisions

Требования

Education

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Business Intelligence, Data Science, Finance, Statistics, Economics, or a related quantitative field

Experience

  • Proven professional background in Business Intelligence, data processing, financial analysis, or business data analysis
  • Experience in the financial industry or at large exchanges, with exposure to big data environments
  • Track record of managing data governance, compliance reporting, or regulatory data submissions

Technical Skills

  • Strong proficiency with mainstream data analysis and processing tools (SQL, Python/R, BI platforms)
  • Solid understanding of data architecture and data structure design
  • Experience with financial data processing and reporting systems
  • Familiarity with data governance frameworks and EU regulatory standards (GDPR, DORA)
  • Understanding of data virtualization and structured data processing techniques

Soft Skills & Other

  • Fluent English (written and spoken)
  • Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving ability
  • Ability to communicate complex data issues to non-technical stakeholders
  • Proactive, self-driven, and comfortable operating with ambiguity in a fast-paced environment
