О роли

As Director, Fraud, you will lead Airwallex’s First Line of Defence (1LoD) Fraud function. You will own the design, execution, and continuous optimization of our global fraud strategy, policies, detection systems, and operational workflows across Airwallex’s financial infrastructure. In this role, you will lead a high-performing team responsible for stopping fraud in real time while keeping friction low for legitimate, high-growth global businesses. Airwallex processes billions in cross-border payments, multi-currency corporate cards, merchant acquiring, and embedded finance products. You and your team will directly defend our ecosystem against card fraud, account takeover (ATO), merchant bust-out, payment scams, and synthetic identities.

You will partner directly with Engineering, Data Science, Product, Legal, and Compliance and interface closely with Second Line Risk to build and operate scalable, AI-native fraud detection models, effective triaging processes and operational guardrails.

Что предстоит делать

- Define & Execute 1LoD Global Fraud Strategy: Direct and build out the operational fraud strategy across all Airwallex products (Global Accounts, FX, Acquiring, Cards, and Embedded Finance / BaaS). Translate enterprise risk appetite set by the board and 2LoD Risk into actionable rules, detection logic, operational policies, and loss-prevention workflows.

- Lead & Scale the 1LoD Fraud Team: Build, lead, and mentor a world-class team of fraud strategists, analysts, and operations specialists responsible for rule writing, alert triage, incident response, and dispute operations.

- Optimize Risk vs. Conversion: Own and execute real-time fraud decisioning rules, loss-mitigation frameworks, and chargeback monitoring programs. Drive precision in risk controls to reduce fraud losses without introducing unnecessary friction for legitimate customers.

- Drive Product & AI/ML Execution: Co-own the fraud product roadmap with Data Science and ML Engineering teams. Directly inform and test next-generation models for real-time transaction monitoring, device fingerprinting, behavioral biometrics, and anomaly detection.

- Own Partner & Scheme Operational Compliance: Ensure operational adherence to fraud rules, monitoring programs, and threshold limits established by card schemes (Visa, Mastercard, local schemes), banking partners, and regulators across all active markets.

- Incident Management & Root Cause Remediation: Lead real-time response to complex or high-value fraud incidents. Conduct post-mortems, deploy immediate policy/rule fixes, and implement long-term structural remediations.

- Metrics & Loss Budget Ownership: Own operational KPIs, fraud loss performance against targets, False Positive Rates (FPR), triage SLAs, and chargeback recovery rates. Serve as the primary operational presenter on 1LoD performance to risk committees and leadership.

ESSENTIALS

- 10+ years of hands-on Fraud Management experience in fintech, payment processing, acquiring, or modern banking infrastructure, with direct experience leading operational 1LoD teams and holding loss budget accountability.

- People Leadership: Proven track record of scaling, mentoring, and directing high-performing strategy and operational fraud teams in fast-paced environments.

- Deep Domain Expertise: Comprehensive operational knowledge of merchant acquiring fraud, card issuing fraud, CNP chargebacks, account takeover (ATO), and cross-border payment typologies.

- Card Scheme Mastery: Deep practical expertise in Visa/Mastercard dispute management, scheme monitoring programs (e.g., VFMP, VAMP, ECM), and payment gateway risk mechanics.

- Data & Technical Fluency: Hands-on experience working directly with complex data environments and decision engine tooling (SQL, Python/R, real-time risk platforms, rule engines).

- Cross-Functional Leadership: Demonstrated ability to drive operational execution across Product, Engineering, Data Science, Legal, 2LoD Risk, and external banking partners.
