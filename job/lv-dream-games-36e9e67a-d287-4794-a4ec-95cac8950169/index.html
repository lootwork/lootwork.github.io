We are looking for a DevOps Engineer, who will design and implement scalable systems. As a DevOps Engineer, you will work closely with our development teams to address their needs and scale out the infrastructure of our attribution pipeline and related technologies. You take ownership of our infrastructure over various locations and continuously scale it, making sure that our servers run smoothly and at their highest efficiency.

At Dream Games, all team members have a genuine chance to contribute to the final product and have an impact on the company. If you want to be a team member of a fast-growing company, you might be the person we are looking for!

Обязанности

  • Building and supporting infrastructure and developer tools to improve game development processes
  • Building advanced automated build, test, and release infrastructure
  • Providing troubleshooting and timely resolution of the system, network, and infrastructure problems
  • Identifying and solving critical problems and preventing them from reoccurring via root cause analysis and automation

Требования

  • Knowledge of building secure and scalable environments within GCP or AWS
  • Knowledge of build/release systems, CI/CD systems (Jenkins and Git)
  • Have experience with Containerized technologies and Container orchestration frameworks
  • Knowledge of Infrastructure as Code
  • Passion and interest in games
  • Appreciation and understanding of teamwork
