Join FxPro: a leading international fintech company. Be a part of our expanding international team, with offices in Limassol, London, Nassau, Dubai, and Yerevan. FxPro boasts a diverse workforce of over 600 employees representing 50 nationalities, making it an exciting and dynamic workplace. At FxPro, we see each team member as an integral part of our success story.

As a Payments Onboarding Specialist you will join our Operations team. This is a great opportunity to work with a dynamic team of professionals and develop your skills. You will be assisting our Payments System Manager with post-onboarding related tasks. The ideal candidate will have 2-3 years’ experience in a similar role within the financial markets industry, in-depth understanding and practical application of applicable laws and regulations, and excellent communication and organizational skills.

Обязанности

  • Assist the Payments System Manager with post-onboarding administration
  • Provide post-onboarding support to clients and partners, including collecting, reviewing and following up on legal and compliance documentation
  • Monitor payment systems to confirm they are operating as expected, and raise tickets with the correct team when issues arise, supporting diagnosis and testing of flagged issues
  • Communicate regularly with Payment Providers during onboarding, implementation and ongoing support
  • Support the preparation of technical specifications and documentation for business requirements related to Payment Systems, under the guidance of the Payments System Manager
  • Maintain accurate records of onboarding status, documentation and open issues
  • Follow all practices, procedures and regulations in performing daily duties

Требования

  • University degree, preferably in Finance or Economics
  • 2-3 years of experience in financial services, preferably including onboarding or client support
  • Excellent command of the English language
  • Working knowledge of payment and transactional systems and services
  • Good understanding of applicable laws and regulations, and the ability to apply them in daily work
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Ability to work in a dynamic and multicultural environment
  • Good communication and organisational skills, with the ability to juggle several priorities under tight deadlines
  • Computer literacy with very good working knowledge of Microsoft Office applications
