О роли

Chime's Product Security team is looking for a Senior Infrastructure Security Engineer II to help secure the cloud infrastructure and data platforms that power Chime. This is a hands-on engineering role: you'll work side by side with infrastructure, platform and data engineering teams to find real risks, build guardrails and automation that scale, and make the secure path the easy path. Your work will span AWS, Kubernetes, network edge and data security, and it directly protects the millions of members who trust Chime with their money.

In this role, you can expect to

  • Design, build and improve security controls, guardrails and automation across our AWS infrastructure, Kubernetes platforms and data systems.
  • Partner with infrastructure, platform and data engineering teams to find and fix security risks, with practical guidance that keeps teams moving.
  • Strengthen our cloud security posture across infrastructure configuration, network and edge security (including WAF and bot management), identity and access, and sensitive data protection.
  • Review architecture and technical designs, assess new technologies, and turn what you find into clear, prioritized recommendations.
  • Drive security vulnerabilities from discovery to fix, prioritizing by real exposure and exploitability.
  • Use AI and automation to speed up security reviews, triage and remediation, and cut down repetitive operational work.
  • Join an on-call rotation for vulnerability management, responding to issues outside regular hours when needed.

To thrive in this role, you have

  • Deep, hands-on experience securing AWS production environments
  • Recent production experience with Terraform, including rolling out security changes safely across many accounts or resources.
  • Hands-on Kubernetes security experience, ideally on EKS, and a solid understanding of container and CI/CD security.
  • Strong networking fundamentals (DNS, TLS, load balancers, firewalls) and hands-on experience with WAF or bot management.
  • Practical scripting skills for security automation, and the judgment to review and validate code and infrastructure-as-code, including code written with AI.
  • A track record of owning security initiatives end to end and explaining risks and tradeoffs in a way that earns buy-in from engineering teams.
  • Bonus: experience with Snowflake or data security concepts such as access control, masking or tokenization, encryption and exfiltration risk.
