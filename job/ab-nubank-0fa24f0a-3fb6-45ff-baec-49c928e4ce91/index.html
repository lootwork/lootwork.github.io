О роли

This role leads analytics for two scopes, Contestation and Fraud Security Features, and is accountable for the analytical side of both rather than the product roadmap. The work spans fraud victim reimbursement, chargeback resolution, operations time per complaint, AI adoption in operations, regulatory reporting, new security layers for customers, and a horizontal view of how fraud frictions affect customers' lives.

The Contestation Platform team is the analytical foundation for how Nubank handles customer reports and disputes, providing the best experience possible for customers who were fraud victims or had any issues with their credit card purchases. This includes an intuitive reporting flow, transparency in the process and fairness in reimbursements decisions. The focus for 2027 is how we can increase the AI usage in the platform to decrease total costs of our complaints, including decreasing losses and operational time spent to resolve a complaint.

The Security Features team is an innovative area, with the primary goal to make Nubank customers feel safer, and we do this by creating solutions that increase customers' perception of our defenses and also create new defenses. Our best example is Safe Mode (Modo Rua), an extra security layer that protects customers' investments and cash-out when we detect they are away from home/ safe wifi networks. The focus for 2027 is to understand why customers still believe Nubank is not the safest financial institution, even though we believe we have best-in-class fraud policies/models at least in BR, and after identifying the pain points, increase safety perception.

The Customer Analytics scope was once a dedicated pack and is now held by one of the Decision Intelligence analysts. It owns horizontal fraud views, trying to solve a historical gap we have in fraud, that teams are isolated in their silos, not having proper understanding of how different squads' defenses impact customers as a whole in their real life. The focus for 2027 is to ensure fraud squads understand their defenses' impact in customers' experience, and give CX the same weight as fraud losses for decision making.

YOU’LL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR

- Lead the team: run weekly planning with the BAs, set priorities across the groups, guide day-to-day analytical work, and own hiring, feedback and career development for eight BAs, plus informal leadership of the two Analytics Engineers.

- Partner with the three Contestation squads: work closely with the Squad Leads of Core, Conan and CCI to decide which problems to solve, how to measure them, and whether a rollouts or tests worked as expected or not

- Own analytical quality: review critical analyses and recommendations before they reach leadership and decision forums, such as reimbursement policy changes and their financial impact.

- Drive the AI agenda: lead the weekly forum where Product, Engineering, Operations and Analytics track the AI initiatives that automate complaint handling, and turn results into decisions.

- Shape strategy and goals: build the yearly objectives and metrics with Product and Engineering leadership, and review the roadmap every month against what the data shows.

- Represent analytics with partners: Operations in Brazil, Mexico and Colombia, Finance on how losses are booked and reported, and Risk and Controls on audits and regulatory reports.

- You will be accountable for the analytics of both scopes and for the people who deliver it. The team works with every country where Nubank operates, so English is part of the daily routine. In Contestation you are also the main driver of the AI reinvention of complaint handling
