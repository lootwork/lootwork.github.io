О роли

Binance is exploring opportunities to expand its licensed payment business in Kazakhstan and the broader CIS region. We are looking for a Operations Manager to set up, run, and grow the local fiat business: day-to-day fiat operations, product localisation, partnerships with banks and payment providers, and market development.

Обязанности

  • Set up, sustain, and manage the local fiat business and fiat operations, including product localisation, and support the business on regulatory matters;
  • Collect feedback from local users and communities, as well as competitor insights, to optimise the service offering;
  • Explore collaboration opportunities with local fiat partners, blockchain and crypto communities, and industry associations to expand fiat awareness in the market;
  • Develop further fiat-related partnerships, including fiat on-ramp providers and KYC vendors;
  • Design and implement go-to-market strategies to grow fiat volumes, including content and promotion strategies;
  • Maintain strong relationships with regulators, banks, and payment partners;
  • Maintain up-to-date knowledge of applicable regulations to provide strategic advice;
  • Lead local fiat growth and other business development activities.

Требования

  • 5+ years of proven success in a similar role leading a business unit, business development, or business growth function at a blockchain or crypto company, financial institution, or bank;
  • Deep understanding of local communities and the crypto space;
  • Strong work ethic and commitment to results;
  • Demonstrated ability to work as a team player;
  • Problem-solving and results-driven mindset;
  • Passion for crypto and digital assets;
  • Fluent Russian and English required; Kazakh strongly preferred.

Why Binance

  • Shape the future of finance
  • Collaborate with world-class talent in a user-centric global organization with a flat structure
  • Tackle challenging projects in a fast-paced and innovative enviornment with autonomy
  • Thrive in a results-driven workplace with opportunities for career growth and continuous learning
  • Competitive salary and company benefits
  • Remote-first, work-from-home arrangement (may vary depending on the nature of the role and team)
