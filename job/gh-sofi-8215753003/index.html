О роли

SoFi is seeking a Fraud Operations Support Lead — ATO, Identity & Scams to own the end-to-end operational process for account takeover, identity risk, and scams.

You will ensure that fraud strategy, policy, controls, workflows, tooling, communications, training, and frontline execution remain current, measurable, and consistently applied at scale.

You will partner across Operations, Product, Engineering, Risk, Legal, Compliance, and Controls to strengthen detection, decisioning, investigations, case management, and member communications. You will identify and resolve systemic control and process gaps, guide high-stakes risk decisions, and ensure that new capabilities are implemented with clear governance, measurable outcomes, and strong operational readiness.

Success in this role means reducing fraud exposure and operational friction while protecting members, enabling frontline teams, and building durable processes that can adapt to evolving attack patterns and business needs.

Что предстоит делать

Own Fraud Operations Strategy and Process Design (30%): Define and evolve end-to-end operating processes for account takeover, identity risk, and scams, balancing fraud-loss prevention, member protection, operational efficiency, and regulatory expectations.

Translate fraud strategies, policies, detection rules, risk models, and regulatory requirements into clear operating requirements, investigative procedures, decision frameworks, controls, and implementation plans that frontline teams can execute consistently.

Lead Tooling, Workflow, and Capability Enablement (25%): Own the development and prioritization of business requirements for fraud-specific tooling, investigative workflows, case-management automation, and agent-experience improvements.

Lead build-versus-buy evaluations and partner with Operations, Product, Engineering, Risk, and Controls to implement capabilities that strengthen detection, decisioning, investigations, and frontline execution.

Identify and Resolve Control and Process Gaps (20%): Evaluate fraud-prevention processes, identify systemic control gaps, quantify operational and financial exposure, and prioritize durable remediation across ATO, identity risk, and scam typologies.

Lead root-cause analysis of material fraud incidents, emerging attack patterns, control failures, and member-impacting defects; convert findings into clear control, workflow, training, and communication changes.

Provide Fraud Expertise and Risk Decision Support (15%): Serve as a specialized fraud advisor and escalation point for complex process-design challenges, high-risk operational scenarios, emerging vulnerabilities, and decisions involving identity compromise, account takeover, and scam prevention.

Provide risk-based recommendations and resolve tradeoffs among fraud exposure, member impact, operational feasibility, and control effectiveness in partnership with cross-functional stakeholders.

Establish Governance and Continuous Improvement (10%): Set decision rights, approval paths, and documentation standards for fraud-process changes, exceptions, escalation protocols, control requirements, and operational-risk acceptance.

What you’ll need

  • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent practical experience.
  • 12+ years of progressive experience in financial-services fraud prevention, fraud strategy, fraud-risk management, or fraud operations, with substantial direct experience in account takeover, identity fraud, and scam prevention.
  • Demonstrated expertise in sophisticated fraud attack methodologies, identity fraud, authentication, and scam prevention.
  • Advanced understanding of fraud-detection technologies, authentication frameworks, identity-verification solutions, case-management platforms, and the operational application of fraud rules and risk models.
  • Ability to analyze data, weigh risk and member impact, and make high-judgment recommendations.
  • Strong influence, program-management, and written and verbal communication skills.
  • Demonstrated ability to translate fraud strategy, policies, and regulatory requirements into scalable operational processes and controls.
  • Experience leading cross-functional work across Operations, Product, Engineering, Risk, Legal, Compliance, and Controls.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with identity-verification, authentication, fraud-decisioning, or case-management platforms.
  • Experience in a highly regulated consumer financial-services environment.
  • Experience with control testing, audit readiness, or regulatory remediation.
