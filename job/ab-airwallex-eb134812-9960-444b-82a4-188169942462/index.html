Что предстоит делать

We’re looking for a Senior Manager of Influencer Marketing to build and lead our global influencer strategy from the ground up. This is a high-impact role responsible for driving virality around major brand and product moments, architecting our creator strategy, and building the systems and partnerships needed to operate at scale across key markets (US, UK/EU, AU, SG, HK).

You’ll combine strategic insight, cultural instinct, operational excellence, and influencer-world fluency to make Airwallex the most talked-about fintech brand globally.

The role is based in San Francisco.

Обязанности

- Define and own Airwallex’s global influencer and creator roadmap across YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, podcasts, newsletters, and emerging platforms. Shape strategies for major launches, campaigns, and cultural moments to drive brand awareness, engagement, and virality, while enabling local teams to execute market-specific adaptations.

- Architect multi-layered creator campaigns that amplify major announcements, fundraises, product launches, partnerships, and company milestones. Identify and engage high-impact creators across business, fintech, tech, lifestyle, and culture who can authentically champion the brand.

- Design and oversee systems, workflows, and tooling for creator sourcing, outreach, contracting, approvals, tracking, and reporting. Develop playbooks and templates for regional teams and maintain a robust global creator database.

- Lead end-to-end relationship management with top-tier influencers and agents. Set expectations for performance, negotiate contracts, and ensure consistent brand safety, messaging alignment, and creative excellence.

- Define success metrics across awareness, reach, engagement, and content performance. Oversee dashboards and reporting frameworks, test formats and creators, and implement insights to continuously optimize campaigns.

- Partner with Brand, Product Marketing, Comms/PR, Social, Regional Marketing, and Executive Leadership to integrate influencer initiatives into broader brand and product strategies. Collaborate with Legal, Finance, and Procurement to streamline global creator operations

Кто вы

We're looking for people who meet the minimum qualifications for this role. The preferred qualifications are great to have, but are not mandatory.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

- 8+ years in influencer marketing, creator partnerships, digital marketing, or brand marketing (startup or high-growth tech preferred).

- Proven track record driving large-scale influencer campaigns that create real cultural impact and measurable growth.

- Deep understanding of the influencer ecosystem, platform dynamics, and what drives virality.

- Experience managing global markets or multi-country campaigns.

- Strong negotiation, relationship-building, and stakeholder-management skills.

- Operational excellence - you can design systems, processes, and frameworks that scale globally.

- Ability to move fast, thrive in ambiguity, and execute with precision under tight timelines.

Будет плюсом

- Experience in fintech, payments, B2B SaaS.

- Relationships with top-tier creators, agents, and podcast networks.

- Experience building a global influencer program from scratch.

- Ability to analyze performance data and turn insights into action.
