Team Lead - Payments Pricing

Adyen processed €1.39 trillion in 2025, and more than €800 billion in the first half of 2026 alone. Behind every one of those payments sits a fee, and for most of our volume that fee is Interchange++ (IC++): the exact interchange and scheme fees that Visa, Mastercard and local networks charge us, are passed through to our customers at cost, with full transparency. Getting that right at this scale is one of the hardest and most rewarding problems in payments, and it is the job of Payments Pricing.

Payments Pricing is a globally distributed team within Merchant Finance & Treasury (MF&T). We turn card network changes into live pricing logic, predict what every transaction will cost, reconcile that against what the networks actually charge, and close any gap. Every fraction of a percent matters to our financial health and to the trust our customers place in us.

You will be part of the leadership team of Payments Pricing, based in Amsterdam, leading the Business Analysts in EMEA. This role will report to the Head of Payments Pricing. This is a player-coach role: you lead and develop your team and stay close enough to the work to coach it.

We are in the middle of a significant automation transformation. AI agents and automated workflows are taking on more of the work, and our Business Analysts are becoming the experts who direct those systems, supervise their accuracy and solve the hardest cases. This role leads that transformation for a region and helps shape it for the whole team.

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead and grow your team. Hire, develop and retain high-performing Business Analysts. Set clear expectations, build a team that owns its outcomes, and make the tough calls that keep the bar high. Stay close enough to the work to coach it.
  • Drive operational excellence through automation. Find what to automate, challenge how we work today, and build your team's skills with the newest tools, including AI. Move manual work into automated workflows that your analysts supervise.
  • Protect financial accuracy. Keep a strong control environment. Resolve discrepancies and incidents at the root cause, and size the financial risk early.
  • Decide what the team takes on. Say what the team will and will not do, and hold that line with Product, Engineering, Commercial and leadership, including customer escalations and scheme changes that cannot wait.
  • Shape one global team. As part of the leadership team, help set the priorities, ways of working and automation direction for all of Payments Pricing, not only your direct team. Share coverage and capacity with the other Team Leads, make what your team builds visible and usable for the other regions, and adopt what they have built instead of rebuilding it.
  • Partner across functions. Build strong working relationships with Product, Engineering, Commercial and Group Finance. Our daily work depends on them, from the engineering changes our fee implementations need, to commercial escalations, to pricing new products and markets correctly from day one.

Кто вы

  • You know payments, ideally from a deeply technical corner of it such as pricing, fees, reconciliation or acquiring operations. If you do not, you have a proven record of mastering a new, deeply technical field and succeeding quickly in it. Experience with interchange and scheme fees is a strong plus.
  • You are a people leader who has hired, grown and promoted analysts, and has had hard conversations about performance. You lead with a collaborative, low-ego style and can align people who do not report to you.
  • You already use AI in your own work and know where it helps and where it falls short. You challenge how work is done, keep finding what to automate, and know what checks you would want before anything that affects customer fees runs unattended.
  • You are hands-on and curious, comfortable with data, and able to bring clarity to ambiguous problems. You explain complex topics simply and influence people across functions, regions and time zones.
  • You move with urgency, pause to weigh risk before committing, and stay steady under sustained pressure.
