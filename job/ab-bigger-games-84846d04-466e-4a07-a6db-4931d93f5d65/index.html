We're looking for a Game Creative Artist who is passionate about creating engaging mobile games, bringing creative ideas to life, and building experiences that players love. At Bigger, our artists play an important role in shaping games from the very first concept through development and marketing, working closely with a talented team to turn ideas into exciting player experiences.

If you're a creative, hands-on artist who loves games, enjoys experimenting with new technologies, and is excited about turning ideas into playable experiences, come and join us!

О THIS ROLE

- Develop creative game concepts and bring them to life by building playable HTML games as part of our Game Lab

- Create engaging marketing videos and playable ads to test new concepts and support our games

- Design and improve visual elements, animations, UI, and gameplay feedback to deliver satisfying player experiences

- Collaborate closely with developers throughout game development, providing creative input and improving gameplay experiences

- Analyze test results and team feedback to continuously improve and iterate on creative concepts

- Leverage AI tools and efficient workflows to accelerate creative production and experimentation

- Stay up to date with mobile gaming trends, creative technologies, and emerging AI tools

YOU COULD BE A GREAT FIT IF

- Strong portfolio demonstrating visual design, animation skills, and an understanding of engaging mobile game experiences

- Ability to independently build, test, and iterate on playable HTML games using AI-assisted coding

- Experience creating marketing videos and playable ads for mobile games

- Proficiency in visual design and motion graphics tools such as Photoshop and After Effects

- Ability to create and integrate visual assets, troubleshoot technical issues, and ensure a smooth playable experience

- Strong creative thinking, attention to detail, and problem-solving skills

- Passionate about mobile games and eager to explore new creative technologies

- Being a collaborative team player with a strong sense of ownership

BONUS POINT IF

- You have experience with casual or hybrid casual games and actively follow industry trends.

- You're familiar with Unity or 3D design tools.

- You have experience using AI-powered tools and automation to streamline creative production.

Условия и льготы

- Be part of a top-tier team, learn from the best, and create games players love.

- Competitive compensation.

- Health insurance with unlimited coverage.

- A beautiful, centrally located office in the heart of Istanbul.

- Opportunities to attend global conferences and stay connected with the industry.

- Relocation support to make your move hassle-free if needed.
