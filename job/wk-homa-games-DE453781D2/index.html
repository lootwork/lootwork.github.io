Требования

As a Senior 3D Gameplay Artist (Animation & VFX), you'll play a key role in owning the visual and motion game feel of our gameplay mechanics, from greybox to shipped asset, so that objects read as fun before a player ever touches them. You'll work directly with art direction, game design, and gameplay engineers on All in Hole, a top-50 grossing title, on the elements players interact with most. Your main responsibilities will include:

  • Own game feel end to end: take a mechanic from greybox and UX blockout through animation, VFX, and final sign-off
  • Animate gameplay objects by hand: idle, activation, interaction feedback, and state transitions, with real command of timing, weight, anticipation, and follow-through
  • Build VFX and shaders that land the hit: impact, feedback, and state change that communicate clearly without drowning the read of the object
  • Translate concept into 3D without losing its soul: match the intent, elevate the charm, and challenge the concept early when the gameplay asks for something different
  • Iterate directly in Unity: prefabs, colliders, animator state machines, and enough technical range to test your own ideas in minutes rather than hand them off

This is a great opportunity to own the craft that players remember most, on a game already in the global top 50, with no one ahead of you in the queue — the person in this seat sets the bar for how gameplay objects look, move, and feel.

Требования

We are looking for someone with around +5 years of experience building 3D core gameplay animations in mobile games, and the humility, curiosity, ambition, and drive to make an impact fast. You're a great fit if you have:

  • 3D animation craft: hand-keyed work that gives personality and weight to objects that aren't characters, grounded in animation fundamentals rather than tooling
  • Strong VFX skills: particle systems and impact effects timed to reinforce readability, not decorate it
  • A core gameplay brain: you're a gamer, you read design intent off a prototype, and you can hold a real conversation with designers about why a mechanic is or isn't fun
  • Working fluency in Unity: blockouts, prefabs, animator and state machines, and the technical range to unblock yourself
  • Genuine shader ability: you can write and debug your own, not just wire up existing ones
  • Fidelity to concept: you hit the intent of a concept closely while making it better through the 3D pipeline
  • Clear visual communication: storyboards and blockouts that get every stakeholder seeing the same outcome before work starts
  • Autonomy and initiative: you hold a quality bar without it being checked for you, and you bring the unrequested improvement alongside the requested one

Bonus points if you

  • Can concept the idea yourself and push it forward
  • Have worked with physics-based gameplay
  • Have mentored or reviewed other artists' work

Even if you don't check every box in our requirements, we encourage you to apply. We value diverse perspectives and backgrounds, and we're more interested in your potential and passion than a perfect match to our checklist.
