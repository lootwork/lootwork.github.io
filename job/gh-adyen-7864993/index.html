Senior Machine Learning Scientist

Adyen is looking for a Senior Machine Learning Scientist to join our team in Amsterdam, a person sitting at the cornerstone of algorithms, mathematics, and engineering, who can solve problems by designing and implementing production-ready machine learning solutions. You will be responsible for building, developing and deploying algorithms that power data products at Adyen.

Teams Currently Hiring

Optimise

Optimize Intelligence at Adyen is a specialized team of Machine Learning Scientists and Data Analysts focused on maximizing transaction success, minimizing transaction costs, and driving payment performance at a global scale. We build and scale advanced ML/DL solutions that operate over massive, complex decision spaces, determining the optimal path for every single transaction in real time. Our systems evaluate high-dimensional data streams to dynamically select the optimal authentication methods, apply smart payment message optimizations, and orchestrate intelligent transaction routing. Our models live-score millions of daily transactions, balancing coupled cost and performance objectives simultaneously.

If you are passionate about solving intricate combinatorial challenges and pushing the boundaries of adaptive decision-making systems, join us in shaping the future of payments.

Identity and Verification Decisioning

This is an opportunity to help define how Adyen makes identity decisions at global scale.

The team sits at the intersection of machine learning, distributed systems, fraud prevention, compliance, and customer experience. Rather than optimizing isolated models, you'll help build the intelligence that determines how millions of businesses and individuals are verified throughout their lifecycle.

You'll join a newly formed engineering team, giving you significant influence over the technical direction, architecture, and ML strategy from an early stage. Your work will directly impact fraud prevention, regulatory compliance, operational efficiency, and customer onboarding experience.

Dynamic Verification

We are looking for a Senior Machine Learning Scientist to help build the machine learning capabilities behind Adyen’s Identity Platform.

Adyen processes payments and financial events at global scale. To prevent fraud and financial crime effectively, we need to understand likely entities and relevant behavioural patterns across events, while maintaining a high bar for privacy, governance, reliability, and explainability. The Identity Platform develops shared capabilities that help Adyen understand which events and identifiers may belong to the same underlying person or actor. These capabilities serve multiple downstream teams and use cases, including real-time fraud prevention, AML detection, investigations, and future risk products.

You will help design and build the ML systems that make this possible. This includes applied modelling, large-scale data processing, low-latency serving, clear interfaces for downstream consumers, and the operational controls required for a platform used across teams.

This is a hands-on technical leadership role. You will influence long-term technical direction while remaining close to implementation, experimentation, and production systems.

In this role, you will

  • Research, design, implement, train, deploy and monitor machine learning algorithms, either for batch prediction or real-time. Examples include: on-line learning algorithms to pick the best optimization decision in a changing environment, clustering algorithms to group customers/shoppers, supervised and semi-supervised learning methods for inference on risk patterns or graph analysis, representation learning for behavior prediction and monitoring, Anti-Money Laundering (AML) systems and real-time anomaly detection based on time-series modeling;
  • Develop orchestrated pipelines for analytical purposes and machine learning training;
  • Contribute to ongoing automation efforts for our experiments, training runs, validation runs and monitoring before, during and after deployment. Furthermore, collaborate with MLOps to improve our machine learning tooling;
  • Collaborate closely with product managers and business stakeholders to understand requirements, define problems, and frame them as solvable machine learning tasks;
  • Explore and analyze large, complex datasets to identify patterns, insights, and opportunities for ML-driven solutions;
  • Define key performance metrics, design rigorous experiments (e.g., A/B tests), and perform statistical analysis to validate model performance and quantify business impact;
