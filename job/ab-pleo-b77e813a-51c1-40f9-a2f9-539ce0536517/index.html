О роли

We're looking for a Senior Operations and Collections specialist to join our Credit team at Pleo. You'll own collections end to end — from the first conversation with a customer who's fallen behind, through to external agencies and legal action — as we scale our credit product. If you're excited about running Collections as its own function and are passionate about B2B credit, this is the opportunity for you.

Who you’ll be working with and reporting to

You'll report to our Senior Operations Manager and join a team of four who own Credit Operations at Pleo. Most of the work happens across boundaries: you'll partner closely with risk, underwriting, finance, product and our customer-facing teams to recover what's owed and stop the same problems recurring.

Что предстоит делать

As a Senior Operations and Collections specialist, you will

- Monitor a portfolio of customers that are unwilling or unable to pay back their credit

- Engage with customers in the portfolio in a 1:1 capacity to resolve breach of credit terms

- Liaise with external agencies and legal firms in different jurisdictions when internal solutions are not found, including instructing legal actions and registering claims in insolvencies

- Build and maintain reports pertaining to collections activities, amounts collected, amounts written off, internal and external portfolio performance, and provide other reports and insights as required

- Collaborate with leadership, risk & underwriting and other operational teams to support policy changes and proactive monitoring based on data driven analyses

- Create and maintain documentation relating to the collections processes

Что вы приносите

You’ll thrive in this role if you have

- Experience in collections, risk and compliance, and/or data analysis.

- Background in customer-facing roles across B2B or B2C (such as Customer Support, CX, or Customer Success).

- Strong independent leadership skills: you can self-motivate and balance daily tasks with long-term projects effortlessly.

- Intermediate skills with Google Workspace, SQL, and GenAI (including agentic AI tools).

- Experience collaborating with product and engineering teams to fix bugs and scope new features.

- The skill to convey complex operational concepts with simplicity and empathy.

- Sharp prioritisation skills that let you weigh impact against effort every day.

- Familiarity with FinTech, collections, or legal environments is a strong plus.

Why is this role a good fit for you

This role is a good fit for you if

- You have the discipline to follow procedural workflows to a T - and know precisely when standard procedures apply.

- You are biased to action above perfection, and people rely on you to get things done.

- You’re not just fixing problems as they appear, you are looking to optimise current processes, and you challenge the status quo.

- You know when to use AI in your workflows - but most importantly, you know when to not use AI.

This role is not a good fit for you if

- You find fast-paced, iterative problem-solving challenging or frustrating.

- You require total clarity and broad consensus before making day-to-day decisions.

- You are comfortable flagging issues, but hesitate to probe for underlying causes or propose solutions.

How you’ll develop in this role

In your first 3 months at Pleo, you’ll

- Master the collections processes, legal partner interactions, and collections portfolio.

- Collaborate with operations teams and risk & underwriting to improve monitoring and policies.

- Grow your skills in agentic AI, planning to build sustainable workflows within collections.
