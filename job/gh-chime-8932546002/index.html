О роли

Availability and resilience are core to keeping Chime reliable for millions of members who count on us for their everyday financial lives. The Incident & Availability Engineering organization is responsible for the systems, tooling, and culture that keep Chime up, resilient to outages, and fast to recover when incidents occur. We're looking for a Director to lead this org and define what comes next: an intelligent, automated platform where AI and ML aid in real-time incident triage, root cause analysis, and automated mitigation. If you have experience building scalable availability, reliability, and incident response systems and want to lead this critical pillar of Chime's technical infrastructure, we'd love to talk.

In this role, you can expect to

  • Lead the Incident Operations and Availability Engineering teams, inclusive of tech lead managers (TLMs) and senior engineers across Incident Management, Availability Tooling, Resilience Engineering, and Observability. You'll report to the VP of Platform Engineering.
  • Set the multi-year vision and roadmap. Unify incident management, alerting, chaos engineering, and availability tracking into an intuitive, seamless reliability platform.
  • Drive a culture of ownership and accountability, both inside the larger Platform Engineering team as well across all Product Engineering functions.
  • Partner closely with Product Engineering, Security, Core Infrastructure, and Data teams. Co-own reliability SLAs/SLOs, drive cross-functional alignment on availability goals, and cultivate a blameless engineering culture.
  • Empower engineering teams to ship reliably. Build tools and practices that make service health, error budgets, and incident readiness transparent and low-friction for product and infrastructure teams.
  • Hire, coach and grow strong tech lead managers who stay hands-on technically while leading their teams. Build an inclusive, high-ownership team people want to join and stay on.
  • Ensure operational readiness, disaster recovery, and compliance. Partner with Risk and Security leaders to conduct game days, disaster scenarios, and maintain audit-ready incident governance.

To thrive in this role, you have

  • 10+ years in software or infrastructure engineering, including 5+ years leading teams and at least 2 years leading tech lead managers or engineering managers in Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), Infrastructure, or Availability domains.
  • A track record of building SRE, incident response, observability, or availability platforms operating at high scale.
  • Experience leveraging ML or LLM-based tooling for anomaly detection, root cause analysis, or automated remediation, along with a firm understanding of governance and safe execution in production environments.
  • Deep expertise in incident response lifecycle, disaster recovery, chaos engineering, and driving SLO/SLA frameworks across large engineering organizations.
  • Strong distributed systems, high availability architecture, and telemetry/observability background (tracing, metrics, logs, event processing).
  • Clear executive communication skills during high-stakes incidents and a proven ability to lead cross-functional post-mortems and reliability initiatives.
  • Bonus: experience in fintech, high-availability financial services, or managing mission-critical 24/7/365 infrastructure.
