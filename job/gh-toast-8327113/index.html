Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy. Toast team is committed to our customers. We’re taking steps to help restaurants navigate these unprecedented times with technology, resources, and community. Our focus is on building the restaurant platform that helps restaurants adapt, take control, and get back to what they do best: building the businesses they love. And because our technology is purpose-built for restaurants, by restaurant people, restaurants can trust that we’ll deliver on their needs for today while investing in experiences that will power their restaurant of the future.

Toast’s Hardware team designs and develops world-class hardware devices in conjunction with our JDM/ODM/CM partners in Asia. We are looking for a Hardware Program Manager to join our HW PMO team in Taiwan, to handle new product development, sustaining and cost-down programs for Toast devices already in production.

In this role, you will keep shipping products healthy and competitive: driving engineering changes, component replacements, and cost-reduction initiatives from idea through validation to mass production. You will work cross-functionally with Hardware Engineering, NPI, HW Ops, Sourcing, Quality, and manufacturing partners to manage schedule, scope, cost, and risk across multiple programs in a fast-moving environment. This is a great opportunity for an early-career program manager to grow within a global hardware organization.

A day in the life (Responsibilities)

  • Lead new Hardware product development program from concept to mass production.
  • Creates and executes project plans and revises to meet changing needs.
  • Deliver project objectives, including product performance, schedule, cost, and other KPIs.
  • Understand Toast's strategic priorities and drive alignment with the cross-functionally to deliver clear project plans. Keep the team focused and ensure commitment and objectives are met.
  • Hold teams accountable for their commitments, removing roadblocks to their work. Leverage organizational resources to improve capacity for project work.
  • Ensure teams are adhering to Toast standards and best practices
  • Manage sustaining engineering activities for in-production hardware products, including ECN/ECO execution, component EOL/last-time-buy responses, alternate part qualification, and field issue follow-up.
  • Coordinate validation builds, test plans, and change approvals to ensure changes reach production on time without impacting quality.
  • Drive cost-reduction programs with Engineering, Sourcing, and manufacturing partners, from idea collection and feasibility review to validation and production cut-in.
  • Identify risks to schedule, quality, or savings, and manage mitigation plans in the project risk register.
  • Serve as a key point of contact between JDM/ODM/CM partners and global cross-functional teams (Hardware Engineering, NPI, HW Ops, Quality, Product).
  • Run program meetings, publish status reports, and keep program documentation current in Google Workspace, Jira, Confluence, and PLM.
