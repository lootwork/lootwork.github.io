О GRIFFIN

Hi, we’re Griffin. We’re a fully regulated UK bank that powers the accounts and payments offerings of product-led fintechs and platforms like Uber, Yonder, Prosper, and Sidekick. By doing the heavy lifting behind the scenes, our customers can focus on what they do best - delivering amazing customer experiences.

We’ve grown rapidly since our launch in 2024, and today provide the banking backbone behind products in remittance, payroll, wealth, insurance, proptech, payments, neobanking and more.

We’re moving into card issuing, stablecoin infrastructure, and agentic finance next as we continue to support the next generation of product-led companies.

Learn more about what we do https://griffin.com

OUR CULTURE

We believe that smart, motivated, conscientious people thrive in high-trust, high-autonomy environments and have done our best to create such an environment at Griffin. People who thrive here don’t require much hand-holding, and go out of their way to figure out how to contribute in the most valuable way possible.

We've been remote-first since we were established in 2017 and we remain so today. Work here is asynchronous and fully flexible - because we want to be a part of your life in a way that helps you to get the most out of both your work and home life.

We understand that our culture and ways of working aren’t for everyone, and we’re very intentional about our hiring process as a result. We won't hire people who don’t seem like a strong fit for our core values, even if they're otherwise extremely qualified.

Our strong culture has helped us to remain a mission-driven company as we have scaled, and gives our people a clear, explicit operating framework and the space to focus on delivering real value for our customers.

Learn more about our culture. https://griffin.com/culture

Команда

Our vision is that our Finance team plays a central part of the business, somewhere the Board can rely on to support strategic planning. In this our treasury team keeps us safely funded and liquid and gives the Board and ALCO numbers they can trust. As we add products and markets, the team is growing. As second hire to the team, you’ll report directly to our Treasurer, sitting at the heart of how we capture, report, analyse and manage risk to support how we optimise Griffin’s balance sheet.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Treasury at a fintech bank is different. You’ll see real-time payment flows and deposit behaviour that a conventional bank never sees. You’ll work directly with our Treasurer to build our treasury processes, reporting and systems rather than inherit them. You’ll contribute to management of liquidity as a Direct FPS participant, use of facilities within the Bank of England’s Sterling Monetary Framework (SMF), card issuing and stablecoin infrastructure.

Как выглядит успех в этой роли

By month 3, you’ll understand our business and processes, run daily reporting with Michael’s review and handle daily cash positioning, including our Bank of England reserve account. By month 6, you’ll own daily and monthly reporting and run intraday cash management independently, including testing the SMF. You’ll have drafted ALCO papers and credit reviews, delivered your first automation improvements and contributed to the impact assessment for Direct FPS or a similar product launch.

TO DO THIS YOU’LL OWN

- Daily and intraday reporting on liquidity, capital, interest rate risk, funding capacity and asset encumbrance, with every output sense-checked before it goes out

- Risk monitoring against our risk appetite: limits, triggers and early warning indicators, forecast-versus-actual reviews, and stress tests you design with the Treasurer

- Intraday cash management and liquidity forecasting: cash positioning, payments and transfers, depositor behaviour and outflow assumptions

- Monthly management reporting, including interest income, margin and customer profitability, the ALCO pack, inputs to regulatory returns, and contributions to the ILAAP and ICAAP

- Dealing operations: pre- and post-trade checks, confirmations, settlements and collateral movements, plus credit analysis on counterparties and issuers. You’ll also cover for the Treasurer with brokers, treasury bill auctions and SMF drawdowns

- Automating manual steps, closing control gaps, and documenting procedures that mean knowledge never sits with just two people

- Maintaining our treasury bank and broker mandates

WE EXPECT YOU TO SHAPE THIS ROLE

This description reflects what we need today. How you achieve these outcomes, where you focus your energy, how this role evolves—that's for you to define as you learn what our customers need and where our technical gaps are.

YOUR BACKGROUND

To succeed in the role, we're looking for people who have:

- 5+ years in a bank treasury function (e.g. middle-office, ALM or balance sheet management), measuring and reporting liquidity and capital from economic and regulatory perspectives. You will have produced reports hands-on rather than reviewed them

- Strong prudential knowledge across liquidity, capital and leverage (e.g. CRR, Basel III, LCR, NSFR, IRRBB, ILAAP, ICAAP), and hands-on exposure to the dealing lifecycle: pre- and post-trade checks, confirmations and settlements

- Advanced Excel skills (e.g. data tables, scenario analysis, VBA), plus the attention to detail and reconciliation discipline to sense-check everything you produce

- An analytical, inquisitive mindset, with a habit of automating what’s manual

- The organisation and drive to work autonomously, remotely and asynchronously, and the appetite for the breadth and ambiguity of building in an early-stage bank

- A love of writing - we have a major amount of written documentation that you will be contributing to, and we place a high degree of importance on clarity and readability (you can find out more here about how we write https://griffin.com/how-we-write )
