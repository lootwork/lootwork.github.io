О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

Help build the next-generation billing platform that supports Airwallex internally and gives customers an easy-to-integrate way to manage billing and revenue workflows.

- Build high-quality products with React and TypeScript, using the latest versions and modern frontend engineering practices.

- Design and develop micro-frontend web applications using domain-based architecture and a single-SPA framework.

- Build scalable, high-performance Node.js backend-for-frontend services with Apollo GraphQL and well-defined contracts for backend integration.

- Partner with Design to develop a shared design system and reusable components, creating a consistent UI and UX across domain teams.

- Deliver NPM packages, reusable library widgets, and open-source projects that make customer integration easier.

- Deploy micro-frontends and Node.js services using GCP, Alibaba Cloud Kubernetes, bucket CDN, Cloudflare, and GitLab CI/CD.

- Build reliable unit and end-to-end test coverage using React Testing Library, test-rendering tools, and Cypress.

- Develop no-code and serverless CMS capabilities for corporate websites and branding landing pages using Contentful and Next.js.

- Engage with the frontend guild, research emerging technologies, drive engineering initiatives, and help reduce technical debt.

You'll work from our Singapore office

LEARN MORE ABOUT YOUR TEAM

The Billing team at Airwallex builds a single platform that charges for everything: how we bill our customers, and how our customers bill theirs. We own the quote-to-cash engine behind Airwallex's fastest-growing revenue stream, from usage-based billing and pricing to the checkout experience that collects across 130+ currencies and 160+ payment methods. The hard part: one system serves two masters, Airwallex's own books and the revenue of every business that bills through us, so every number has to reconcile to the cent, in real time, under audit scrutiny. You'll thrive here if correctness is non-negotiable and you think like a product owner as much as an engineer. If you want billing to be the growth engine of the AI economy, this is where it's built.
