Reality Games is a fast-growing mobile gaming company with a focus on harnessing big data to create immersive, location-based gaming experiences. We’re passionate about innovation, and we’re scaling globally with our latest project, Monopoly World.

We are looking for a talented Unity Developer to join our Product department at Reality Games. As a Unity Developer, you will be responsible for creating engaging and immersive gaming experiences for our players. You will work closely with our team to create and develop high-quality games that push the boundaries of creativity and innovation.

Join Our Team and work in a one-of-a-kind office located in a beautifully, historic railway station, nestled in the heart of the city’s charming old town. Immerse yourself in the vibrant atmosphere of Krakow’s cultural and historic center while working on-site, Monday through Friday, alongside a passionate and supportive team.

Coming from outside Krakow? We’ve got you covered with a relocation package to make your move seamless and stress-free.

Responsibilities:

  • Collaborate with the team to design and develop interactive gaming experiences
  • Implement game functionality as per design specifications
  • Optimize game performance for different platforms
  • Debug and fix issues to ensure smooth gameplay
  • Build innovative multiplayer big data-based games in Unity for mobile platforms in dedicated product teams
  • Take part in creating development workflows at every stage of production and maintenance of launched titles
  • Build dedicated location-aware and multiplayer-enabled game features
  • Create and maintain development tools
  • Guide junior developers in your project and exchange knowledge with other Unity developers working on other titles
  • Help game designers verify and enhance ideas for new games with your technical expertise
  • Work closely with backend engineers on implementing and improving client-server communication
  • Help create company-wide standards for Unity developers
