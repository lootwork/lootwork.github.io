Что предстоит делать

Epic Games is searching for a Senior Environment Artist to work closely with a team of world-building experts and support multiple Epic initiatives across games, technology demos, and long-term R&D. The team develops high-impact demo content, workflows, and prototypes that drive Epic's real-time world-building technology forward and help shape the future of scalable 3D world creation.

In this role, you will

  • Create visually stunning natural and manmade environments in Unreal Engine and UEFN, maintaining a high artistic bar across composition, storytelling, and world-building quality
  • Develop performant and scalable environment content designed for real-time experiences, large worlds, games, and technology demos
  • Collaborate with teams across Epic to support shared initiatives, strengthen content libraries, and build reusable world-building solutions
  • Help shape future pipelines and workflows for environment assembly, optimization, reuse, and large-scale content production
  • Mentor and support junior artists on the team

Кого мы ищем

  • Strong portfolio demonstrating specialization in natural environment creation and set dressing
  • Strong working knowledge of UE5 and its core environment features, including the material system
  • Strong communication skills, including the ability to give and receive constructive feedback
  • Experience delivering environment projects at scale in a team environment
  • Strong balance of artistic judgment and technical awareness
