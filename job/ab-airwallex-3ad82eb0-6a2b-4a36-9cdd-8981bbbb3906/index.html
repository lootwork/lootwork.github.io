MANAGER, ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT

О роли

As Manager, Account Management, you’ll play a key leadership role as we scale our commercial organisation. You’ll lead a team of Account Managers responsible for some of our most important customer relationships, driving growth and retention across a defined portfolio.

This is a player-coach role. You’ll set the strategy and standards for your team while directly managing a small number of high-value customers. Working closely with Sales, Product, Solutions Engineering, Marketing, Finance and Operations, you’ll deliver exceptional outcomes for both our customers and Airwallex.

Что предстоит делать

- Lead, coach and develop a high-performing team of Account Managers, including hiring, onboarding and performance management.

- Own commercial outcomes across your portfolio, driving revenue growth through cross-sell, upsell and expansion while managing retention, churn and key negotiations.

- Define and execute the account management strategy in partnership with regional leadership.

- Act as executive sponsor for strategic customers, building senior stakeholder relationships and supporting customers from onboarding through to scale.

- Lead structured portfolio reviews and WBRs, using data-driven insights to identify priorities and drive clear action plans.

- Partner cross-functionally to deliver a consistent, high-quality customer experience, resolve issues and improve processes.

- Use customer feedback and data to identify trends, surface product gaps and influence Product priorities.

- Own portfolio forecasting and reporting across revenue, pipeline, risk and customer health.

- Contribute to territory planning and coverage models as the organisation scales.

Кто вы

We’re looking for people who meet the minimum requirements for this role. Preferred qualifications are a plus, but not mandatory.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

- At least 5+ years of relevant working experience

- 3+ years of experience in account management, including managing complex, multi-stakeholder accounts, ideally within payments, fintech, SaaS or B2B technology.

- 2+ years of experience leading and developing Account Managers or similar commercial teams, ideally in a high-growth or scale-up environment.

- A proven track record of meeting or exceeding revenue targets.

- Strong commercial acumen, with experience leading negotiations, renewals, commercial structures and executive-level conversations.

- A strategic and analytical mindset, with the ability to analyse customer data, market trends and product usage to identify growth opportunities and develop structured account strategies.

- A bachelor’s degree or equivalent qualification.

Будет плюсом

- Experience in payments, FX, card issuing, treasury or financial platforms.

- Experience in B2B software or other complex technology solutions.
