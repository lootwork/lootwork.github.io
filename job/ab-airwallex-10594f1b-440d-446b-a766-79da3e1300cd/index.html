О AIRWALLEX

HOW YOU’LL MAKE IMPACT

As a Senior Corporate Security Engineer, you’ll help defend Airwallex’s enterprise systems and employees against threats including malware, phishing and unauthorised access. You’ll detect, investigate and prevent security issues across a modern corporate environment, using digital forensics, incident response and security tooling.

- Defend Airwallex’s enterprise systems and employees against malware, phishing and unauthorised access.

- Design, develop, test and evaluate corporate security controls across identity providers, endpoints, corporate networks and other platforms.

- Build security alerts and workflows, and hunt through log sources to identify emerging threats.

- Deploy, configure and operate security tooling with a focus on measurable impact.

- Secure internal AI tooling deployments and integrations.

- Improve the quality and depth of incident response investigations across the corporate environment.

You'll work from our Shanghai office 5 days a week

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- 5+ years of experience in security engineering, incident response, or digital forensics within a fast-paced technology company.

- Deep, practical knowledge of Mac and Windows internals, combined with the ability to conduct complex incident response from raw logs and forensic artifacts.

- Hands-on experience securing enterprise AI tools. Understand the unique threat landscape of LLMs and AI agents, including how to safely integrate them with corporate tools via OAuth and MCP.

- Strong scripting skills (Python, Bash, PowerShell, or Go) to build custom security alerts, automate incident response lifecycles, and drive detection engineering.

- Experience operating identity providers (Okta, Google Workspace), MDM platforms (Kandji, Intune), and common monitoring tools (CrowdStrike, Splunk).

- Experiences with at least one major cloud platform (GCP, AWS, Alibaba Cloud). Understand cloud architecture and can secure environments beyond pure corporate IT.

- The ability to simplify complexity and explain highly technical security concepts to a non-technical audience in English.

Желательно

- A blended background combining Corporate Security with Product/Infrastructure Security (e.g., CI/CD pipelines, DevOps, Kubernetes).

- Experience building proprietary security tooling using Large Language Models or agentic frameworks.

- Recognized cybersecurity training or certifications (OSCP, GIAC, etc.).
