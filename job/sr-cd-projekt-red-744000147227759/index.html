Job Description

CD PROJEKT RED is looking for a highly skilled and motivated Senior Shared Tech QA Analyst to join our Shared Tech QA function. Shared Tech QA supports studio-wide technologies used across multiple products and teams – covering engine systems, tools, pipelines, and cross-project features. This role is crucial for ensuring shared solutions are stable, scalable, and maintainable across platforms and long-term roadmaps. If you enjoy testing complex systems, collaborating closely with technical teams, and driving quality improvements that benefit multiple projects at once – we’d love to hear from you.

Daily responsibilities

  • Conduct comprehensive testing and verification of shared engine systems, tools, pipelines, and cross-project features, including performance, stability, integration, and cross-platform checks across multiple teams.
  • Develop, execute, and continuously optimize advanced, reusable test cases, test suites, and test plans (manual and automated) designed for long-term maintainability and consistency across multiple projects.
  • Collaborate closely with shared technology developers, tools programmers, technical artists, and downstream project teams to identify, document, and resolve issues – ensuring reliable solutions for studio-wide use.
  • Analyze test results, identify systemic root causes, and provide actionable insights that improve robustness, performance, and adoption of shared technologies.
  • Participate in risk assessments, quality strategy planning, and process improvements, adapting to shifting priorities across multiple projects and platform roadmaps.
  • Create and refine quality reports and dashboards using data/analytics tools to highlight cross-project trends and support strategic decision-making.
  • Contribute to maintaining and improving technical documentation for shared technologies, including test coverage guidelines, known limitations, and QA best practices for broad studio use.

Требования

  • Excellent communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively with multiple cross-functional teams in a shared services environment.
  • Proven experience coordinating QA activities across internal teams and external partners, including ownership of planning, prioritization, timelines, and risk management.
  • 5+ years of experience as a Tech QA Analyst or in a similar senior technical QA role, ideally supporting internal tools, platforms, pipelines, or shared technologies.
  • Hands-on experience contributing to or supporting at least one shipped AA/AAA title, with a solid understanding of production realities and dependencies between teams.
  • Deep understanding of testing methodologies for complex systems, including integration, regression, performance, stress, and long-term stability testing.
  • Strong experience validating technologies built on or integrated with game engines (e.g., Unreal, Unity, Godot, REDengine/REDkit), with awareness of engine-level constraints and workflows.
  • Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines, build system workflows, RoboMerge, and Perforce.
  • Solid knowledge of content and asset creation pipelines, and the ability to identify systemic pipeline risks.
  • Proven experience testing across multiple platforms and environments, including PC and major consoles (PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo), with an understanding of platform constraints.
  • Ability to design, own, and evolve test strategies for shared technologies used by multiple products and teams.
  • Experience leveraging QA tools, automation frameworks, and diagnostic solutions to improve coverage, efficiency, and signal quality at scale.
  • Strong analytical and systems-thinking skills, with the ability to identify cross-team risks and long-term improvement opportunities.
  • Proven experience creating and maintaining technical documentation, test plans, quality guidelines, and test reports for broad internal use.
  • Ability to mentor, guide, and support other QA team members through knowledge sharing and best practices (no formal people management required).
  • Fluency in English (spoken and written).
  • Passion for games and game development, with a strong sense of ownership over the quality of shared technologies.
