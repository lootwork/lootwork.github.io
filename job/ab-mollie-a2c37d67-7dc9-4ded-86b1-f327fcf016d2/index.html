Your impact

As an Application Engineer I in the Hosted Integrations team, you'll help bring e-commerce platform integrations that were built by external consultants back into Mollie's unified e-commerce gateway codebase. You'll work on key high-volume partner integrations, including Shopify, Wix, Lightspeed and Mirakl, and more to come, while we explore a native integration for Salesforce. This is primarily a backend role in Java, where you'll connect services into the wider Mollie ecosystem.

The work matters. By keeping high-volume payment processing smooth across major global e-commerce platforms, you directly support Mollie's commercial goals and partner relationships, and you remove financial friction for merchants. You'll have high visibility and a direct line to key revenue drivers. At this level, you move from individual tasks to owning small projects within the team, maintaining and extending the systems you work on, and speaking up when you need help to get unblocked.

You'll be part of the only engineering team in the Commercial Partnerships domain, so you'll have the chance to help shape the engineering craft there. You'll work with mature core gateway systems, help scale integration architectures, and learn alongside senior engineers who are stepping into technical leadership.

What you'll ship

- Get up to speed on the e-commerce gateway system

- Bring integrations built by external consultants back into Mollie's unified e-commerce gateway codebase

- Streamline and maintain existing e-commerce integrations, so high-volume transactions are processed reliably

- Deliver small, clearly defined projects within the team from start to finish

- Write testable, readable code and contribute to documentation when needed

- Monitor, maintain and extend the systems within the team's scope, and take part in support rotations with help from more experienced teammates

- Share progress and blockers with the team and stakeholders, and track the outcomes of your work with relevant metrics

What you'll bring

- 3+ years of professional in software engineering experience

- A strong professional background in full-stack software engineering

- Proficiency in Java and object-oriented programming

- Proficiency in React and/or React Native

- Experience working with scalable applications or gateway systems

- A good understanding of cloud environments, with Google Cloud Platform preferred

- Excellent English communication skills
