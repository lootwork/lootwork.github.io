О вас

We are looking for an experienced senior backend engineer to join the Xsolla Technical Integration Team (China). The team focuses on driving innovation and delivering tailored solutions for our major partners in China through customized integration projects, localized product and solution enhancements, and dedicated technical support to meet the unique needs of the local market.

If you are ambitious, undaunted by challenging tasks, eager to grow, and passionate about working in the video game industry, this could be the perfect role for you.

О нас

Xsolla is a global commerce company with robust tools and services to help developers solve the inherent challenges of the video game industry. From indie to AAA, companies partner with Xsolla to help them fund, distribute, market, and monetize their games. Grounded in the belief in the future of video games, Xsolla is resolute in the mission to bring opportunities together, and continually make new resources available to creators. Headquartered and incorporated in Los Angeles, California, Xsolla operates as the merchant of record and has helped over 1,500+ game developers to reach more players and grow their businesses around the world. With more paths to profits and ways to win, developers have all the things needed to enjoy the game.

For more information, visit xsolla.com.

Обязанности

  • Design, implement, and optimize technical solutions based on the needs of users in the China region, continuously improving service performance and stability.
  • Participate in research and discussions related to products in the gaming payment domain, and drive their implementation and promotion.

Требования

  • Bachelor's degree or higher in Computer Science or a related field, with at least 3 years of backend development experience.
  • Strong programming skills and a solid foundation in computer science, with familiarity in data structures and design patterns.
  • Proficiency in at least one programming language (Go or PHP) and familiarity with relevant mainstream development frameworks.
  • Experience in designing and developing large-scale distributed systems, with a deep understanding of middleware technologies such as message queues, Elasticsearch (ES), Redis, and MySQL, including their principles and optimizations.
  • Excellent teamwork, communication skills, and a strong sense of responsibility.
  • Strong problem-solving skills, clear logical thinking, and an ability to think critically, summarize, and synthesize information.
