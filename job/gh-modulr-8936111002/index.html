Кто мы

Modulr is the payments automation platform that streamlines money movement with accuracy, control and reliability, built to scale. We process more than 200m transactions and over £180bn in payment value on an annualised basis, and we're trusted by over 6,000 businesses from fast-growing SMEs to global enterprises.

We automate how money moves into and out of a business (payroll, supplier payments, spend management and payment collection) through a single platform, connected to the accounting and payroll systems finance teams already use, and underpinned by principal memberships of Visa and Mastercard and direct access to central bank infrastructure.

As a regulated payments provider with over a decade of experience, we're the platform finance teams trust to run mission-critical operations. Modulr is backed by PayPal, FIS, General Atlantic, Blenheim Chalcot, Frog Capital and Highland Europe, with over 400 employees across London, Edinburgh, Amsterdam and Mumbai.

Find out more about us on our website.

Что предстоит делать

  • Set the product strategy for the Platform and API capability, including the public API surface, versioning, developer experience and documentation.
  • Own the partner programme, covering integrations with accounting, payroll and software platforms, partner onboarding, certification and commercial terms.
  • Build and prioritise the partner roadmap, with the aim of reaching SME customers through integration partners rather than high-cost direct marketing.
  • Define the API product portfolio, including access tiers, rate limits, sandboxes and usage-based pricing, with Finance and Commercial. Set API standards for security, reliability and backwards compatibility with Architecture and Quality Governance.
  • Own partner-sourced and API revenue, track partner health, adoption and API reliability, and report them to the executive team. Within 12 months, the top partner integrations have a named owner and joint roadmap, and partner-sourced revenue is tracking to plan.
  • Lead, coach and develop the product managers in your area.
  • Work closely with the Product Directors for External Experience and Internal Experience.

Кто вы

What you’ll need

  • 7 or more years in product management, including at least 3 in a senior or director role in B2B payments, fintech, banking or B2B software platforms.
  • Proven experience selling through or building partner channels, such as integration partners, marketplaces, resellers or embedded distribution.
  • Experience owning an API product, including developer experience, documentation, versioning and API commercial models.
  • A track record of owning revenue or margin outcomes, with partner and channel economics you can explain clearly.
  • Hands-on experience using AI to improve product discovery, decisions or delivery, with sound judgement on where it fits in a regulated financial services business.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with accounting, payroll or HR software ecosystems.
  • Experience with embedded finance or banking-as-a-service platforms.
  • Hands-on familiarity with REST APIs, webhooks, OAuth and developer tooling.
