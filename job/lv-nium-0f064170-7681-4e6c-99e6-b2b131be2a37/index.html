Nium, the Leader in Real-Time Global Payments

Nium, the leading global infrastructure for real-time cross-border payments, was founded on the mission to deliver the global payments infrastructure of tomorrow, today. With the onset of the global economy, its payments infrastructure is shaping how banks, fintechs, and businesses everywhere collect, convert, and disburse funds instantly across borders. Its payout network supports 100 currencies and spans 190+ countries, 100 of which in real-time. Funds can be disbursed to accounts, wallets, and cards and collected locally in 40 markets. Nium's growing card issuance business is already available in 34 countries. Nium holds regulatory licenses and authorizations in more than 40 countries, enabling seamless onboarding, rapid integration, and compliance – independent of geography. The company is co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore.

О роли

Join our Risk & Compliance team as part of the Compliance PMO supporting the Chief Risk and Compliance Officer (CRCO). You will work closely with the Global and Local teams within the Risk & Compliance function to drive strong governance, impact management and leadership decision support.

This is a PMO role with a Compliance lens, as you ensure global and local Compliance initiatives are organised and translate requirements into clear actions, accountable owners and deadlines.

Основные обязанности

  • Support the tracking, coordination and maintenance of Risk & Compliance initiatives across all regions, including status updates, dependencies, and risk logs.
  • Maintain central SharePoint or Confluence spaces for document control and artefact management.
  • Assist in establishing effective communication channels with relevant stakeholders. This involves providing regular updates, facilitating collaboration, and addressing stakeholder concerns to ensure alignment and transparency.
  • Support the Risk & Compliance Monthly Updates to CRCO, including status updates, risks, issues, follow-up actions and timely resolution of issues & actions.
  • Assist in preparing leadership-facing materials such as dashboards, status decks, and summary write-ups under the direction of the team.
  • Support annual and quarterly planning activities, including data collation for regional reviews, budget and vendor tracking, and HC planning inputs.
  • Assist in managing external requests from banking and network partners globally from a Risk & Compliance standpoint.
  • Support the continuous improvement initiatives within Risk & Compliance function to increase overall effectiveness and efficiency.
  • Assist in documenting operating model, ownership and process maps, and escalation paths for key compliance processes.
  • Support relevant initiatives in execution, evidence gathering or validating outcomes.

Требования

  • Prior internship or full-time exposure to Financial Crime Compliance, PMO, Management Consulting, or a similar cross-functional role (internships count).
  • Strong analytical mindset with the ability to organise information clearly and support structured problem-solving.
  • Good written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to prepare clear, well-organised materials.
  • Detail-oriented, proactive, and comfortable working with data and spreadsheets/dashboards.
  • Strong Excel and PowerPoint skills for dashboards, reporting, and executive presentations.
  • Strong organisational skills and ability to manage multiple tasks in a dynamic environment.
  • Eagerness to learn, take ownership of assigned tasks, and grow into broader strategy and PMO responsibilities.
  • Proficiency in use of AI technology to assist in day to day operations.
