О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the only unified payments and financial platform for global businesses. Powered by our unique combination of proprietary infrastructure and software, we empower over 250,000 businesses worldwide – including Brex, Navan, Qantas, SHEIN and many more – with fully integrated solutions to manage everything from business accounts, payments, spend management and treasury, to embedded finance at a global scale.

Proudly founded in Melbourne, we have a team of over 2,300 of the brightest and most innovative people in tech across 27 offices around the globe. Valued at US$11 billion and backed by world-leading investors including T. Rowe Price, Visa, Mastercard, Robinhood Ventures, Sequoia, Salesforce Ventures, DST Global, and Lone Pine Capital, Airwallex is leading the charge in building the global payments and financial platform of the future. If you’re ready to do the most ambitious work of your career, join us.

О команде

The SME & Growth team at Airwallex is focused on helping ambitious Canadian businesses grow faster across borders. We work with companies that are scaling quickly and need better ways to manage payments, financial operations, and global growth. Canada is still an exciting market for us, with a lot of upside and plenty of room to build. That means this role comes with real scope, real visibility, and the chance to shape how we grow from here.

Что предстоит делать

As the Head of SME & Growth, Canada, you will own the commercial strategy and results for the market. You’ll lead the team across sales, account management, and partnerships, and help build a more consistent, repeatable growth engine. This role is a mix of strategy, leadership, and execution. You’ll set direction, stay close to the work, and play a key role in how Airwallex shows up and wins in Canada.

This role is hybrid, based in Toronto

Обязанности

- Be the face of Airwallex in Canada across customers, partners, and the broader market

- Build, lead, and develop a high-performing commercial team across sales, account management, and partnerships in Canada

- Own the go-to-market strategy and commercial outcomes for Canada, from pipeline growth through to customer expansion and retention

- Stay close to the business by coaching deals, improving execution, and raising the bar on hiring, performance, and team development

- Partner closely with Marketing, Strategy, Product, and regional leaders to improve market readiness, sharpen positioning, and build repeatable growth levers

Кто вы

We're looking for people who meet the minimum qualifications for this role. The preferred qualifications are great to have, but are not mandatory.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

- Experience leading SMB sales teams or scaling high-volume sales motions in a high-growth B2B environment

- 10 years of commercial or GTM leadership experience, including time in strategy, operations, or high-growth technology businesses

- 5 years of experience leading multi-functional teams of 10 or more across sales, account management, partnerships, or marketing

- Demonstrated success owning commercial outcomes and growing customers and revenue in a B2B environment

- Strong strategic and analytical instincts, with the ability to go from market strategy to execution detail when needed

Будет плюсом

- Experience in fintech or payments

- Track record of building, scaling, or turning around a market at a key inflection point

- Experience developing multi-channel growth strategies across new logo acquisition, customer expansion, and partnerships

- Strong cross-functional leadership across commercial, product, marketing, and strategy teams

- Player-coach mindset with strong commercial judgment and a practical, data-driven approach to growth
