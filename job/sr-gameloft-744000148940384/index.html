Job Description

Job Description

We are looking for a dynamic Project Manager to lead a high-impact, strategic project that is critical to our company’s future. This role involves managing and coordinating efforts across multiple departments, ensuring smooth collaboration and alignment throughout the organization. As a key driver of this transversal initiative, you will play a vital role in delivering results that shape the success of our company.

Обязанности

  • Coordinate internal resources and third parties/vendors for the flawless execution of projects;
  • Ensure that all projects are delivered on-time, within scope and within budget;
  • Developing project scopes and objectives, involving all relevant stakeholders and ensuring technical feasibility;
  • Manage the relationship with the client and all stakeholders;
  • Makes sure the requirements are properly documented;
  • Works closely with the developers and makes sure the requirements, priorities and estimations are properly understood and agreed across the team;
  • Ensure resource availability and allocation;
  • Have extensive customer contact during the project execution and build up a good relation with the customer;
  • Implement and lead a project plan based on Agile methodology;
  • Develop a detailed project plan to track progress;
  • Use appropriate verification techniques to manage changes in project scope, schedule and costs;
  • Measure project performance using appropriate systems, tools and techniques;
  • Report and escalate to management as needed;
  • Perform risk management to minimize project risks;
  • Demonstrated understanding of budgeting, scheduling and change management processes;
  • Lead and manage end-to-end project lifecycle, from planning to delivery
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure alignment on project goals.
  • Lead meetings and manage project documentation.

Требования

Требования

  • Experienced in BI visualization tools, preferably Looker;
  • Great educational background, preferably in the fields of computer science, engineering or accounting;
  • +2 years planning and/or management experience;
  • Experience in custom application development and all phases of the software development lifecycle (SDLC);
  • Projects implemented on BI area;
  • Good understanding of IT projects methodology – understanding of business and functional;
  • Proven ability to function effectively in a fast-paced environment and manage multiple projects concurrently;
  • Excellent client-facing and internal communication skills;
  • Solid organizational skills including attention to detail and multi-tasking skills;
  • Excellent command of English;
  • Strong working knowledge of Microsoft Office;
  • Experience in agile environments (e.g. scrum, kanban) will be a plus;
  • PMP / PRINCE II certification is a plus ;
  • Flexibility is key. Must be open to adapting past experience to Gameloft Romania engagements;
  • Problem solving, teamwork, transparent communications, self-directed management style and quest for constant learning are the key behaviors that drive success at Gameloft Romania;
  • Ability to define and ensure objective measures of complex programs and experience in tracking and assessing projects based on project plans, resources;
  • Experience leading requirements gathering sessions and establishing task prioritization;
  • Motivated to drive excellence in service delivery, methodology, and operations (track record of managing successful projects);
  • Strong leadership and decision-making abilities.
  • Familiarity with managing distributed or global teams.
