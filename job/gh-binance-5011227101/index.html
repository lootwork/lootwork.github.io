Что предстоит делать

  • Drive the development and implementation of key remediation initiatives across Compliance and supporting Binance’s Global Money Laundering Reporting Officer (GMLRO) office
  • Engage in special projects and initiatives, acting as liaison between global Compliance functions and regional Compliance teams
  • Support the execution of various Compliance governance forums and oversight programs
  • Prepare program oversight reporting and assist in development of board reporting for global and local entities
  • Oversee obligation mapping and horizon scanning for Financial Crime risks within Binance’s various regional functions

Кого мы ищем

  • 7+ years of experience, including 3+ years in financial crime compliance / AML, ideally at a global or multi-jurisdictional organisation
  • Hands-on experience building or overhauling AML governance frameworks, policies, assurance programmes, and governance committee structures (Board, RCC)
  • Experience handling SARs/STRs, high-risk customer decisions, and regulatory investigations
  • Deep knowledge of global AML/CFT frameworks and FATF standards, including KYC/KYB, EDD, transaction monitoring, and sanctions
  • Background at a global crypto exchange, bank, or payments company with complex multi-entity structures, plus working knowledge of AML technology, typologies, and financial crime risk assessments
  • Able to influence without authority, navigate jurisdictional tensions, and standardise without overriding local autonomy
  • Track record of building collaborative relationships across cultures and time zones, and comfortable in a decentralised, matrixed structure
