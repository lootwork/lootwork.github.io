О роли

We're hiring a Product Designer to lead the design of some of Chime's most important member experiences across Growth, Spending, or Lending. This is a highly influential individual contributor role for a designer who combines exceptional visual craft with systems thinking and product strategy. You'll help define not just how our products look and feel, but what we build and why—creating experiences that millions of members rely on to make financial progress.

You'll partner closely with Product, Engineering, Research, Data, and Content Design to solve complex customer problems, shape product direction, and deliver elegant, intuitive experiences from concept through launch. If you're energized by ambiguity, obsessed with craft, and excited to raise the quality bar across an organization, we'd love to meet you.

The base salary offered for this role and level of experience will begin at $174,000 and up to $245,000. Full-time employees are also eligible for a bonus, competitive equity package, and benefits. The actual base salary offered may be higher, depending on your location, skills, qualifications, and experience.

In this role, you can expect to

  • Own the end-to-end design vision and execution for complex, high-impact product initiatives across Growth, Spending, or Lending.
  • Shape product strategy alongside Product, Engineering, Research, and Data partners, helping define what we build—not just how it looks.
  • Transform ambiguous opportunities into elegant, intuitive experiences that simplify financial decision-making for millions of members.
  • Design polished, production-ready user experiences across mobile and web with exceptional attention to visual craft, interaction design, accessibility, and usability.
  • Use customer research, experimentation, analytics, and sound design judgment to make informed decisions and continuously improve member outcomes.
  • Contribute to the evolution of Chime's design system while establishing new patterns that scale across products.
  • Raise the quality bar for design through thoughtful critique, collaboration, mentorship, and a relentless commitment to craft.

To thrive in this role, you have

  • A portfolio that demonstrates exceptional visual craft, polished interaction design, and end-to-end product thinking across shipped consumer products. Visual excellence is a core requirement for this role.
  • Extensive experience designing and shipping consumer-facing digital products for mobile and web, with ownership of complex product areas from strategy through execution.
  • A track record of influencing product direction through close partnership with cross-functional leaders across Product, Engineering, Research, and Data.
  • Deep expertise with Figma and modern prototyping tools, with a strong understanding of design systems and scalable interaction patterns.
  • Experience using customer research, experimentation, and product metrics to inform design decisions and iterate on solutions.
  • Strong systems thinking with the ability to balance member needs, business objectives, and technical constraints in complex environments.
  • Exceptional communication and storytelling skills, with the ability to clearly articulate design rationale and influence decisions across a wide range of stakeholders.

A little about us

At Chime, we believe that everyone can achieve financial progress. We created Chime—a financial technology company, not a bank*—on the premise that core banking services should be helpful, easy, and free. Through our user-friendly tools and intuitive platforms, we empower our members to take control of their finances and work towards their goals. Whether it's starting a savings account, purchasing a first car or home, launching a business, or pursuing higher education, we're proud to have helped millions unlock their financial potential.

We're a team of problem solvers, dreamers, and builders with one shared obsession: our members. From day one, Chimers have worked tirelessly to out-hustle and out-execute competitors to bring our mission to life. Their grit and determination inspire us to work harder every day to deliver the very best experience possible. We each bring an owner's mindset to our work, refusing to be outdone and holding ourselves accountable to meet and exceed the highest bars for our teams, our company, and our members.

We believe in being bold, dreaming big, and taking risks, while also working together, embracing our diverse perspectives, and giving each other honest feedback. Our culture remains deeply entrepreneurial, encouraging every Chimer to see themselves as stewards of our mission to help everyday Americans unlock their financial progress.

We know that to achieve our mission, we must earn and keep people's trust—so we hold ourselves to the highest standards of integrity in everything we do. These aren't just words on a wall—our values are embedded in every aspect of our business, serving as a north star that guides us as we work to help millions achieve their financial potential.

Because if we don't—who will?
