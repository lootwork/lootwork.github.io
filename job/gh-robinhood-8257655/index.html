О team + role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world's biggest financial problems. We're looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn't a place for complacency, it's where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We're a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

The Trading Tools team builds the research and analysis tools active traders use every day on mobile: asset charts, asset pages, watchlists, portfolio analysis, and the trading ladder. The team also owns Say, Robinhood's shareholder engagement business, which lets investors ask questions at earnings calls and vote their shares. Together these surfaces are how millions of customers research, track, and act on what they own.

As a Product Manager, you will lead initiatives within one of Robinhood’s newest business lines—prediction markets. You’ll shape the future of how users engage with complex financial tools by simplifying decision-making and creating thoughtful, engaging experiences for our retail audience. This is an opportunity to drive new product innovation from the ground up!

This role is based in our New York, NY or Menlo Park, CA office, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the product strategy and roadmap for Trading Tools and Say
  • Lead a cross-functional team of engineers, designers, and data scientists to ship on mobile at high quality
  • Improve how active traders research and act on positions through charts, watchlists, research, and the trading ladder
  • Grow Say by making shareholder Q&A and proxy voting easy and relevant for everyday investors
  • Use data, experimentation, and user research to decide what to build and measure the results
  • Partner with compliance, legal, and operations on products that touch shareholder rights and market data
  • Communicate priorities and results clearly to engineering, design, and leadership

Что вы приносите

  • Bachelor's or Master's Degree
  • 4+ years of direct product management experience building high-quality consumer products on mobile
  • Demonstrated history of using research, experimentation and analytics to guide decision making
  • Interest in markets and how retail investors trade and manage their portfolios
  • (Nice to have) Experience in fintech, brokerage, or investing products
  • (Nice to have) Active trader or investor who uses these kinds of tools yourself
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills
  • Motivated self-starter and scrappy
  • Ownership mindset with a scrappy can-do attitude
  • Strong communicator and collaborator
  • User-centric, strong product intuition
