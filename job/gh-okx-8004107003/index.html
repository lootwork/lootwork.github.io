О Opportunity

OKX Data Team is responsible for the whole data scope of OKG, from techincal selection, architecture design, data ingestion, data storage, ETL, data visualization to business intelligence and data science. We are data engineers, data analysts and data scientists. The team has end-to-end ownership of most of the data at OKx throughout the whole data lifecycle including data ingestion, data ETL, data warehouse and data services. As a Product Manager of the team, you will work with the team to leverage data technologies to empower evidence-based decision-making and improve the quality of the company's products and services.

Что предстоит делать

  • Deeply understand business goals and pain points: Work closely with business teams to understand their day-to-day data needs, identify core metric frameworks and key insight opportunities, and proactively propose data-driven solutions.
  • End-to-end data solution design: Translate business requirements into data requirements, owning the full lifecycle from event tracking design, data modeling, and metric definition to dashboard design, launch, and acceptance.
  • Build and standardize business metric systems: Lead the definition of core metrics, unify calculation logic, and design and implement dashboards to ensure standardized analysis and end-to-end business traceability.
  • Drive data product adoption: Partner with business teams to promote data tools within departments and establish standard operating procedures (SOPs).
  • Data governance and quality assurance: Continuously monitor the stability of event tracking and metrics, ensure data consistency, and help drive standardized data governance processes.

What We Look For In You

  • 7+ years of experience in data product management or related roles; experience at leading internet/technology companies is a plus.
  • Strong familiarity with data ecosystems, including event tracking systems, A/B testing frameworks, metric systems, and BI data pipelines, with hands-on design or management experience.
  • Ability to quickly understand complex business logic and break down ambiguous requirements into actionable, executable solutions.
  • Excellent cross-functional communication and execution skills, with strong ownership and project leadership mindset.
  • English and Mandarin Chinese proficiency sufficient for use as a working language as the role involves liaising with various stakeholders in chinese-speaking markets.
