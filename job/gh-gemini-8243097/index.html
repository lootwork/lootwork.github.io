О Company

Gemini is a global crypto and Web3 platform founded by Cameron and Tyler Winklevoss in 2014, offering a wide range of simple, reliable, and secure crypto products and services to individuals and institutions in over 70 countries. Our mission is to unlock the next era of financial, creative, and personal freedom by providing trusted access to the decentralized future. We envision a world where crypto reshapes the global financial system, internet, and money to create greater choice, independence, and opportunity for all — bridging traditional finance with the emerging cryptoeconomy in a way that is more open, fair, and secure. As a publicly traded company, Gemini is poised to accelerate this vision with greater scale, reach, and impact.

The Department: Marketing

The Role: INSERT JOB TITLE

Gemini is looking for a Brand Design Intern to join our brand team. You'll work on real projects across social media, merch, landing pages, and email design, getting hands-on experience with the full design process from concept to execution. This is a chance to learn and participate in how a brand actually gets built day to day.

This Brand Design Intern should be genuinely interested in how design, technology, and culture intersect. Someone who can work independently, but is just as comfortable talking through ideas with people outside of design. Someone who can bring both a creative and analytical eye to problems.

This will be a semester long internship program with 3 days a week in person at our New York City, NY office from January to April 2027.

Обязанности

  • Design assets for social, email, and advertising
  • Explore design directions for web and mobile experiences
  • Take direction from design leads and produce original content
  • Ideate on new concepts that can be used for email, social, and merch designs
  • Develop multiple creative solutions to a problem and present your thinking to the team

Требования

  • Pursuing an associate's, bachelor's, or master's degree in Graphic Design, Art Direction, or a related field
  • Working knowledge of layout, typography, hierarchy, and motion
  • Some experience or interest in designing for web and mobile interfaces
  • Comfortable managing your time and juggling multiple projects at once
  • Familiarity with Figma, Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects)
  • A collaborator who's curious about how design, product, and marketing actually work together
