FinCrime Analyst

A key part of building our business is ensuring we do it ethically and sustainably. As a financial institution, we need to know at all times what happens on our platform and whether this aligns with regulations and our risk appetite - and all of this in a scalable way.

We are looking for a FinCrime Analyst to join our FinCrime Operations Department based in Madrid. Do you want to be at the forefront of the fight against financial crime by conducting in-depth investigations and contributing to a passionate team? Then this is the role for you!

Что предстоит делать

  • Investigate alerts on suspicious transactions, reviewing and resolving daily screening alerts using internal and external systems and databases;
  • Conduct thorough research and risk assessments, preparing clear, evidence-based investigations and case narratives in a SAR-ready format to support timely and accurate suspicious activity reporting;
  • Conduct ad-hoc analyses and investigations into merchants’ compliance risk profiles;
  • Collaborate with global teams to ensure consistency and alignment in AML, Screening and Fraud Investigations across all regions;
  • Contribute to team development by sharing knowledge and best practices, and assisting in onboarding new team members.
  • Propose ideas for improvement of our internal processes and workflows;

Кто вы

  • You have 1-2 years of experience in AML/CFT compliance and/or AML/Sanctions/Fraud operations, with an emphasis on financial crime investigations.
  • You have knowledge of AML/CFT laws and regulations, and an intrinsic motivation to follow trends and developments in the AML/Sanctions/Fraud domain(s).
  • Experience in the financial sector is a must; though experience with payments or fintech is a plus.
  • You possess strong analytical and problem-solving skills, consistently using data to inform your decisions and adopting a solution-oriented approach in all aspects of work.
  • You are adaptable and able to handle multiple priorities in a dynamic environment.
  • You have excellent communication skills in English, both written and verbal.
  • You are naturally curious, you have a keen eye for detail, a proactive approach to your work, and a strong drive to understand the “why” behind what you see.
  • You have a continuous improvement mindset - you proactively identify inefficiencies, suggest improvements to workflows or tools, and are motivated to help the team and processes evolve as we scale.

NB: Contributing to global, impactful and urgent work in AML/Screening/Fraud requires flexibility to rotate workload evenly over the team, sometimes including public holidays and weekends (never more than 5 working days in a week). When this kind of flexibility is required, you will be compensated additionally as per the applicable CBA (i.e. ‘overtime’).
