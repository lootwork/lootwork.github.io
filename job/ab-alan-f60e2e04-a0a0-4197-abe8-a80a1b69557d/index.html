HEALTH CAN’T WAIT.

Not for symptoms to get worse. Not for a six‑month appointment. Not for a system to catch up. But that’s exactly how healthcare works today. You wait, until you can’t.

Alan exists to end the wait.

Founded in Senegal in 2022, Tanel is the first digital health insurer in French-speaking West Africa. It operates in Senegal and Côte d’Ivoire, transforming a market dominated by paper-based processes and fragmented legacy players.

With more than 30,000 members, a network of over 1,200 healthcare providers and more than 400 pharmacies, as well as revenue that grew sevenfold in 2025, Tanel has demonstrated strong product-market fit in countries that were ready for a revolution in healthcare insurance.

In 2026, Alan, the European leader in prevention-focused health insurance, acquired Tanel to accelerate its expansion across the African continent. Alan serves more than one million members across France, Belgium, Spain and Canada, and generates more than €950 million in revenue.

Together, we are building the leading health insurance company in French-speaking West Africa, with the ambition of reaching $200 million in revenue by 2030.

THE BEST OF BOTH WORLDS

We bring together the best of both organisations: Tanel’s deep understanding of the local market, strong local relationships and trusted reputation, alongside Alan’s leading technology platform, actuarial expertise and experience operating across multiple countries.

⭐ THE ENGINEERING TEAM ⭐

Within our Engineering team, we build the infrastructure, interfaces and applications that allow us to deliver a first-class service to our members, healthcare providers and internal teams.

Being an engineer at Alan means joining a team of talented, committed and passionate engineers, with a strong connection to product.

We move quickly, with a high level of ownership and autonomy, and we are proud to tackle complex problems.

Our way of working is simple: the people who make product decisions are also the people who build the solutions.

Our technology stack includes Python/Flask, React, React Native and PostgreSQL.

Want to learn more? Read this article https://blog.alan.com/tech-et-produit/engineering-organisation-at-alan to discover what life is like as an engineer at Alan, as well as this article https://medium.com/alan/engineering-career-path-at-alan-e5de5c2e740c about our Engineering career path.

⭐ YOUR MISSION: BUILDING THE CONVERGENCE BETWEEN ALAN AND TANEL

You will play a central role in the gradual convergence of Alan and Tanel’s products and technologies.

The goal will be to preserve the best of both organisations: Tanel’s deep knowledge of the West African market, operational expertise and local relationships, together with Alan’s powerful platform, product practices and international experience.

You will contribute to

- Bringing together the product experiences and technology foundations of both companies.

- Building reusable components and systems that can meet the specific needs of local markets.

- Evolving our tools to support the growth of our product and operations.

- Simplifying and automating processes that are currently managed manually.

- Improving the flow and reliability of data between teams and systems.

- Finding the right balance between global standardisation and adapting to the realities of Senegal and Côte d’Ivoire.

- Laying the technical foundations for our medium-term expansion into new West African markets.

This is a rare opportunity to work on complex, concrete problems with a direct impact on how healthcare is funded, administered and experienced by thousands of people.

Est-ce vous que nous cherchons ?

Nous recherchons un(e) Software Engineer avec au moins trois ans d’expérience, capable de contribuer à des projets ambitieux dans un environnement en forte évolution.

IS THIS YOU?

We are looking for a Software Engineer with at least 4 years of experience, who is able to contribute to ambitious projects in a fast-changing environment.

You will be particularly well suited to this role if you:

- Speak fluent French and English

- Are legally authorised to work in Senegal and are based in Dakar, or are willing to relocate there.

- Have at least four years of experience as a Software Engineer. We are hiring at different levels of seniority, so senior profiles are very welcome.

- Are proficient in a programming language such as Python, Java, C# or an equivalent language.

- Are comfortable with modern web technologies and interested in working on full-stack applications.

- Have experience designing robust, maintainable and scalable systems.

- Are interested in product-related challenges and enjoy understanding user needs.

- Are autonomous, pragmatic and able to make decisions.

- Enjoy working as part of a team and collaborating with non-technical stakeholders.

- Are comfortable working in an environment where priorities can change quickly.

- Are eager to help build an ambitious local technology organisation.

Experience in insurance, healthcare, fintech, payment systems, operations or regulated environments would be an advantage. However, it is not essential.

Above all, we are looking for curious, responsible people who are able to learn and grow quickly. If you do not meet every requirement but this mission genuinely excites you, we would still love to hear from you.

- Health insurance: Comprehensive health insurance through Alan for you and your family.
