SEON is the command center for fraud prevention and AML compliance, helping thousands of companies worldwide stop fraud, reduce risk and protect revenue. Powered by 900+ real-time, first-party data signals, SEON enriches customer profiles, flags suspicious behavior and streamlines compliance workflows - all from one place. SEON provides richer data, more flexible and transparent analysis, and faster time to value than any other provider on the market. We’ve helped companies reduce fraud by 95% and achieve 32x ROI, and we’re growing fast, thanks to our partnerships with some of the world’s most ambitious digital brands like Revolut, Wise, and Bilt.

SEON is looking for a Head of SEO & GEO to own how we are found, cited and trusted across traditional search and AI-driven answer engines. You will set the organic strategy, lead a hands-on technical and content program, and make SEO, GEO and AEO understandable to everyone from practitioners to the executive team. This is a high-visibility role. You will build roadmaps tied directly to company OKRs, partner closely with Product Marketing, Web Strategy, Content, Digital PR and Paid Search, and lead and develop an SEO Strategist on your team. Recruiter input needed: add who this role reports to and, ideally, a clearer description of the team they will join

This role offers flexibility and can be based in Austin, TX with a hybrid schedule, or anywhere in the Eastern or Central time zones in the US with a remote setup, including occasional travel to our other offices.

Что предстоит делать

- Lead technical audits and fixes across crawlability, indexation, site architecture, internal linking, Core Web Vitals, structured data and JavaScript rendering

- Educate and align stakeholders at every level, including executives, on our SEO, GEO and AEO strategy and results

- Build and own the SEO, GEO and AEO strategy and a quarterly roadmap that ladders up to company OKRs

- Develop and run our approach to visibility in AI Overviews, LLM-based assistants and answer engines

- Define the metrics that matter, including organic pipeline, share of voice, and visibility and citations in AI answer engines, and report on them clearly

- Track shifts in search and AI discovery, and turn them into clear recommendations and priorities

- Work with Web Strategy and Engineering to scope, prioritize and ship technical improvements, and QA the results

- Direct keyword, topic and intent research and content optimization, from briefs through refreshes of existing content

- Apply EEAT principles across content, authorship, brand entity and site trust signals

- Partner with Content and Digital PR on authority building, earned mentions and the sources AI engines draw from

- Test, measure and iterate on what drives citation and inclusion, and separate durable practice from hype

- Work with the Paid Search team to share keyword and query data, close coverage gaps, avoid overlap and improve combined search performance

- Partner across Product Marketing, Web Strategy, Content and Digital PR

- Manage, coach and grow a direct report, and raise the technical and strategic depth of the SEO function

WHAT YOU'll BRING

- 10+ years in SEO, with deep, hands-on technical SEO expertise and a track record of shipping fixes that moved organic results

- Equal command of on-page and content optimization, from research and briefs to refreshes and measurement

- A working understanding of EEAT and how to build it into content, authorship, brand and site experience

- Current, credible knowledge of GEO and AEO, including how AI search and answer engines surface and cite sources, and what is proven versus speculative

- Experience working with Paid Search teams to combine organic and paid strategy for better overall results

- Experience building SEO strategies and roadmaps tied to business outcomes and OKRs

- Strong communication skills, with the ability to explain complex topics simply to executives and non-specialists

- Experience managing or mentoring SEO practitioners

- Fluency with Google Search Console, GA4, a major crawler and enterprise SEO platform, such as Screaming Frog, Ahrefs, Semrush, Botify or similar, and structured data

AMAZING IF YOU ALSO HAVE

- B2B SaaS experience, ideally in a technical, regulated or trust-sensitive category such as fraud prevention, security or fintech

- Comfort with SQL, Python or similar for log file analysis and large-scale data work

- Experience with international or multilingual SEO

- Experience with AI visibility tracking and tooling
