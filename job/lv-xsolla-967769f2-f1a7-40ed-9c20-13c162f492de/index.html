Xsolla is a global commerce company with robust tools and services to help developers solve the inherent challenges of the video game industry. From indie to AAA, companies partner with Xsolla to help them fund, distribute, market, and monetize their games. Grounded in the belief in the future of video games, Xsolla is resolute in the mission to bring opportunities together, and continually make new resources available to creators. Headquartered and incorporated in Los Angeles, California, Xsolla operates as the merchant of record and has helped over 1,500+ game developers to reach more players and grow their businesses around the world. With more paths to profits and ways to win, developers have all the things needed to enjoy the game.

Обязанности

  • Develop the functionality of our products and maintain it
  • Develop new modules and components
  • Improve current architecture, participate in design and prototyping
  • Writing autotests
  • Participate in testing of task
  • Participate in design and improvement of designs
  • Ensure the product runs smoothly and work to improve stability

Требования

  • Great experience in JavaScript + TS.
  • Commercial experience with email template markup: table-based layout, inline styles, responsive design within a fixed-width container, understanding of Outlook and Gmail limitations
  • Solid Node.js skills: Express, working with queues, JSON Schema or similar input
  • Experience with Handlebars or another logic-based templating engine, SCSS.
  • Experience with version control systems.
  • Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
  • Excellent communication and teamwork abilities.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced and collaborative environment.
  • 4+ years of experience

Будет плюсом

  • Foundation for Emails (Inky), Panini, Gulp
  • Cross-client testing in Litmus or Email on Acid
  • Docker, Kubernetes at the level of running locally and reading configs
