О team + role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world’s biggest financial problems. We’re looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn’t a place for complacency, it’s where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We’re a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards. The Prediction Markets team’s mission is to create a compliant and engaging sports prediction platform where customers can trade on their knowledge. We are focused on building a seamless experience that brings the excitement of live sports data directly into our consumer application. Our team values deep collaboration, rigorous ethical standards, and high accountability for every product launch. We work closely with engineering, design, and compliance to bring these new sports prediction features to life!

As a Staff Product Manager for Sports Prediction Markets, you will lead the strategy and product lifecycle for our sports-focused prediction tools. You will guide the product definition, plan for major sporting events, and design how we package and present live sports data to our users. In this role, you will partner directly with senior leadership and key executives, navigating high-visibility projects while balancing consumer preferences with fintech regulations. Your work will directly expand our product offerings and introduce prediction markets to millions of customers. We are excited to welcome an experienced builder who is ready to shape the future of sports at Robinhood!

This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA or New York, NY office(s), with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week. At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead the product strategy and roadmap for sports-focused prediction markets, turning complex sports data into simple, intuitive consumer experiences.
  • Collaborate with engineers, designers, and compliance partners to define product requirements, navigate fintech constraints, and deliver highly compliant features.
  • Plan and execute product launches centered around major sporting events, ensuring robust market liquidity and high system reliability.
  • Incorporate feedback from senior executive reviews and user research to iterate on feature designs and improve platform engagement.

Что вы приносите

  • Proven experience managing consumer-facing applications, with a strong background in sports-focused platforms, prediction markets, or gaming apps (such as DraftKings, FanDuel, or Underdog Sports).
  • Strong expertise in merchandising, presenting, and displays of live sports data to consumers in a highly engaging way.
  • Demonstrated ability to navigate complex regulatory environments and collaborate with compliance and legal partners to launch products.
  • Excellent communication skills to present product strategies and updates to executive leadership and align teams around shared goals.
  • Ability to adapt to evolving product requirements and thrive in a high-visibility, collaborative environment.
