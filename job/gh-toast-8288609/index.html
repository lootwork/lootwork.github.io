О Role*

Toast’s Hardware team designs and develops world-class hardware devices in conjunction with our JDM & ODM partners. We are a very lean team that focuses on the key elements of Android devices and accessories, and architect our devices to offer the best hardware solutions for our customers. From our close relationships with our customers, we have learned the important design features that make our devices truly restaurant-grade. We focus on the long-term reliability of our devices to ensure our customers do not have to be concerned with their Toast hardware always being ready for another busy day.

A day in the life (Responsibilities)

  • As a Staff Electrical Engineer on the Hardware Team, you will be laying the groundwork for our future products.
  • Will work closely with Product Management, Product Design Architecture, Industrial Design, and the executive leadership team on new product definitions.
  • Responsible for efficiently integrating electrical systems throughout the device to enable target use cases and hit other product metrics such as thickness, weight, and cost.
  • You have the technical experience to discuss engineering tradeoffs across multiple disciplines including antennas, cameras, sensors, displays, and batteries.
  • You will partner with our global Product Engineering teams as they bring these future products to market, offering ongoing support and bringing lessons learned back into the definition process of next-generation devices.
  • Leverage cutting edge AI tools to enhance your development workflow, improve velocity, and help pioneer new approaches to building - contributing to a culture of innovation and productivity across the team.

What you'll need to thrive (Requirements)

  • BS degree/ ME or equivalent practical experience.
  • Minimum of 7+ years of equivalent working experience in design, implement, debug, and characterize Electrical Engineering systems for use in current and future product architectures. Design prototype devices in conjunction with Product Design Architecture to validate new form factors, features, or other concepts.
  • Partner closely with Product Design Architecture on optimizing the integration of electrical systems to benefit one or more key metrics like size, performance, and cost.
  • Plan and lead validation of your designs. Lead complete functional verification, RF testing, signal characterization, power modeling/test, and system coexistence testing.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams such as Mechanical Engineering, Layout Engineering, Thermal Engineering, and Signal Integrity.
  • Advocate for new technology and component development that offer clear benefits to future product layouts.

Highly Preferred Skills

  • PCB layout & Schematic using Cadence and Allegro
  • WLAN & Bluetooth connectivity experience
  • Understanding of LTE connectivity
  • Payment certification knowledge
  • Solder skills – assembly and rework
