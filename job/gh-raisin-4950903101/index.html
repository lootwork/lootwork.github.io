Raisin is the world’s leading platform for savings and investment products. Founded in 2012, the FinTech connects consumers with banks in the EU, the UK and the US. This gives consumers better interest rates and banks a diversified form of refinancing. Our vision is to offer savings and investments without barriers and thus open up the global 160 trillion euro market.

Raisin currently employs more than 800 people from over 75 countries worldwide. Today, the platform holds over 80 billion euros in assets from more than one million investors which have accrued over 5 billion euros in returns.

Team

As a Corporate Communications Manager, you will play a central role in shaping our corporate narrative on a global scale with a strong focus on Germany. Coming from a fast-paced fintech, finance, or startup background, you know how to translate complex business and macroeconomic developments into compelling stories. You are a proactive networker who thrives on building strong relationships with both internal and external stakeholders. In this role, you will have the exciting opportunity to take ownership of corporate topics, drive innovation, and act as a reliable point of contact for the media.

Ваши обязанности

  • Corporate & Macro Topics: Take ownership of corporate and macroeconomic topics, positioning the company as a thought leader in the industry.
  • Data-Driven Storytelling: Analyze internal and external data to craft interesting stories, unique angles, and compelling narratives that capture media attention.
  • Editorial Planning: Create and maintain a full editorial calendar to ensure consistent planning, alignment, and execution.
  • Format Innovation: Be accountable for projects focused on introducing and developing new external communication formats (e.g., industry reports, executive positioning, creative campaigns) to engage our target audiences.
  • Media Relations: Serve as a dedicated point of contact for the media. You are happy to meet with journalists on a regular basis to pitch stories and build long-lasting relationships.
  • Stakeholder Management: Actively build, nurture, and manage relationships with key internal executives and external stakeholders to ensure strategic alignment and cohesive messaging.
  • Content Creation: Draft high-quality corporate materials, including press releases, briefing documents, statements, and opinion pieces.
  • Media Coordination: Coordinate media activities, including briefings and interviews.
  • Monitoring & Risk Management: Monitor the financial media landscape and competitive activity to surface PR opportunities and proactively manage potential risks to reputation.
  • Global Alignment: Ensure that all corporate communications are aligned with our global strategy, seamlessly collaborating with international teams.

Профиль кандидата

  • Bachelor’s degree in Communications, Journalism, Public Relations, or a related field.
  • Minimum of 3+ years of relevant professional experience in corporate communications, PR, or journalism.
  • Excellent communications skills, written and spoken, in both German and English.
  • Strong background in the fintech, finance, or high-growth startup scene.
  • Proven ability to build trust and strong relationships with both internal management and external partners.
  • Highly analytical with a knack for turning dry data into exciting, newsworthy stories.
  • Confident spokesperson with a proactive approach to building press relationships and pitching to the media.
  • Hands-on mentality with a strong drive to actively develop and implement new external communication formats from scratch.

Будет плюсом

  • An existing, warm network of media contacts (especially within DACH financial, tech, or business media).
  • Practical experience in crisis communication and reputation management.
  • Familiarity with AI-powered tools for communications, content creation, and video generation.
  • Familiarity with PR tools and media monitoring platforms.
  • Experience handling executive communications or CEO positioning.
  • Hands-on experience with video editing tools and visual media production.
