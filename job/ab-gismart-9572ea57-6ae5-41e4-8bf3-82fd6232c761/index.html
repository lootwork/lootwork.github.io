Обязанности

- Ownership & Strategy: Take full ownership of product features or registration/onboarding funnels from idea to execution.

- Hypothesis & Experimentation: Generate, prioritize, and run A/B tests to boost user conversion, retention, and LTV.

- Data & Analytics: Work extensively with product analytics (Amplitude), define key metrics, build hypotheses, and set clear tasks for data analysts to build or optimize dashboards.

- Research & RnD: Conduct market/user research and collaborate closely with cross-functional teams (Creative, User Acquisition, Performance) to explore new growth vectors.

- Proactive Collaboration: Actively drive product discussions, communicate openly in team channels, and push for continuous improvements across the entire product ecosystem.

Требования

- 1.5+ years of experience in Product Management, Growth, or Performance Marketing within fast-paced digital products (App or Web).

- Strong Analytical Mindset: Hands-on experience with Amplitude (or similar tools) and a deep understanding of unit economics and core product metrics.

- A/B Testing Expertise: Proven track record of designing, running, and evaluating A/B experiments.

- Unit Economics Expertise: Deep understanding of unit economics in performance-driven startups — you can easily break down complex metrics and explain them simply (even to a 5-year-old).

- High Ownership & Proactivity: You don't wait for tasks to be assigned — you identify root causes, bring new ideas to the table, and push solutions from start to finish.

- Strong Communication: Transparent, responsive, and structured communication within cross-functional teams.

- Fast Learner: Quick to adopt new workflows, AI tools, and automation to speed up research and launch cycles.

- Language: Upper-Intermediate (B2+) English or higher.

Будет плюсом

- Experience with GrowthBook or similar experimentation platforms.

- Entrepreneurial experience or a background in fast-scaling tech companies
