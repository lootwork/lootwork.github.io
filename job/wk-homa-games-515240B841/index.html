Mission

As a Unity Team Lead, you’ll play a key role in driving both the technical excellence and the engineering evolution of a live, revenue-generating mobile game. You will own the management of a senior Unity development team while contributing directly as a hands-on engineer: keeping the bar high on craft, embedding AI into how the team works, and ensuring every technical decision is made with live-game discipline.

Your main responsibilities will include

  • Own people management for your team: run performance reviews, support career growth, and create the conditions for a high-performing, engaged engineering team
  • Deliver as a senior IC on live technical projects: write production-quality Unity code, contribute to architecture decisions, and drive implementation on impactful features
  • Lead AI adoption within the team: proactively test and embed new AI tools, share wins, and accelerate the team’s ability to use what’s possible
  • Act as a technical knowledge multiplier: identify automation opportunities, expand the team’s understanding of backend and AI systems, and raise engineering standards continuously
  • Manage a live, scaling game with production care: balance technical ambition with revenue-conscious delivery, ensuring changes are robust, measured, and safe to ship

This is a great opportunity to own both the technical and human side of a small, elite team, shape how AI transforms day-to-day engineering on a live product, and make a direct, visible impact on a game played by millions.

Требования

We are looking for someone with proven experience as a senior Unity engineer who has also led and grown engineering teams, and the humility, curiosity, ambition, and drive to make an impact fast. You’re a great fit if you have:

  • Senior Unity expertise: you write clean, robust Unity/C# code, own architecture decisions, and set the technical standard for your team
  • Proven engineering management experience: you’ve led engineers through hiring, performance cycles, and day-to-day management, and you do it with structure and empathy
  • Clear, adaptable communication: you know how to translate technical complexity for any audience and keep collaboration sharp across functions
  • Active AI curiosity: you test tools on your own initiative, understand what’s possible, and think constantly about how AI can accelerate your team’s output
  • Strong ownership mindset: you go beyond your defined scope, spot problems before they’re raised, and drive solutions without waiting to be asked
  • Problem-solving depth: you break down complex technical challenges, structure clear hypotheses, and execute with rigour

Bonus points if you have

  • Experience shipping and maintaining live, revenue-generating mobile games, especially puzzle games at scale
  • Backend or server-side knowledge that enhances your ability to build AI-powered workflows and automations
  • A track record of navigating technically complex changes on a live product under production constraints

Even if you don’t check every box in our requirements, we encourage you to apply. We value diverse perspectives and backgrounds, and we’re more interested in your potential and passion than a perfect match to our checklist.
