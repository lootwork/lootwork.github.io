О роли

We're looking for a Risk and Controls Manager to join our Risk & Controls team, part of Pleo's Second Line of Defence. You'll report to our Head of Risk and Controls, helping build and run our enterprise risk management framework, from risk appetite linkage through to the risk reporting our senior leaders and committees rely on. Alongside that full ERM remit, you'll own second line oversight of security, technology and operational resilience risk, giving our technology and security teams informed, credible challenge. It's a rare mix: a broad enterprise risk role with a technology risk specialism, in a FinTech that's still shaping how it manages risk as it scales. We value people from a range of risk backgrounds, so if you match most of what's below, we'd love to hear from you.

WHO YOU'LL BE WORKING WITH AND REPORTING TO

You'll work closely with other members in our Risk and Controls team, the security GRC team in the First Line, as well as teams across Product, Engineering, Operations, Finance and Compliance.

Что предстоит делать

As a Risk Manager, you will

- Build and run our ERM framework: Work closely the Head of Risk and Control on our enterprise risk management framework, including risk appetite, how it links to our risks and controls, and the reporting that goes to senior management and governance committees.

- Own technology and security risk oversight: Lead 2LOD oversight of information security, technology and operational resilience risk, setting the methodology and standards and challenging the First Line on where the gaps are.

- Test our IT general controls: Run ITGC testing with our technology and security teams, assessing whether controls are well designed and working in practice.

- Lead high-risk testing when incidents happen: Partner with our Assurance & Controls Manager to lead incident-driven testing in our highest-risk areas and turn findings into clear actions.

- Run risk assessments and RCSAs: Lead risk assessments and risk and control self-assessments across the business, not just in technology.

- Shape our GRC tooling: Run GRC tools and help decide how we bring more structure and automation to how we manage risk.

- Be a trusted risk partner: Advise First Line teams on risk, regulatory change and the control environment, and support our engagement with regulators.

Right now we're broadening what the team covers. We already have strong financial and credit risk skills in the team. This hire closes our gap in technology risk and gives us a second person who can run enterprise risk management end to end. You'll work across a risk universe that spans technology, information security, privacy and operational resilience, alongside product, operational and financial risk.

Что вы приносите

YOU’LL THRIVE IN THIS ROLE IF YOU HAVE

- Enterprise risk management depth: Hands-on experience running an ERM or operational risk framework in a regulated financial services business (FinTech, banking, payments or similar), including risk appetite, risk reporting and RCSAs. You could step in for the Head of Risk and Control when needed.

- Technology risk know-how: Hands-on experience applying security, technology and operational resilience risk methodology and standards, with enough depth to challenge technology and security teams credibly. You don't need to be an engineer.

- Controls testing experience: A track record of ITGC testing or similar control testing and assurance work.

- The ability to influence: Confidence engaging stakeholders on complex or sensitive risk topics, challenging constructively and being heard.

- Clear, collaborative communication: The ability to translate risk concepts into practical language for everyone from engineers to senior leadership.

- A pragmatic mindset: A drive to find workable answers to real risk challenges rather than defaulting to theory.

Будет плюсом

- First Line experience, for example in an IT, security or GRC team before moving into second line risk

- Experience with GRC tooling, including selecting or rolling out a platform

- Exposure to data risk or AI risk

- Experience at both a FinTech and a traditional bank or payments business

HOW YOU’LL DEVELOP IN THIS ROLE

IN YOUR FIRST 12 MONTHS AT PLEO, YOU’LL

- Get under the skin of Pleo's risk landscape and take ownership of key 2LOD review and challenge activities across priority business areas.

- Play an active role in evolving our risk framework as the company continues to scale — contributing your expertise to initiatives that will shape how we manage risk for years to come.

- Build strong relationships across the business, establishing yourself as a credible and trusted voice on risk matters.…
