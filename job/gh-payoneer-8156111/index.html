О Payoneer

Founded in 2005, Payoneer is the global financial platform that removes friction from doing business across borders, with a mission to connect the world’s underserved businesses to a rising global economy. We’re a community with over 2,500 colleagues all over the world, working to serve customers, and partners in over 190 countries and territories.

By taking the complexity out of the financial workflows–including everything from global payments and compliance to multi-currency and workforce management, to providing working capital and business intelligence–we give businesses the tools they need to work efficiently worldwide and grow with confidence.

Technical Salesforce Project Manager

ROLE SUMMARY

We are looking for a talented Technical Salesforce Project Manager to join our Delivery Team as part of the Applications & Integrations group.

The group is globally responsible for business applications and cross-department integrations within the organization, ensuring the delivery of innovative, high-quality solutions that meet business and operational needs.

This includes managing Salesforce, our central CRM and business-process platform, which supports our managed customer portfolio and is used by customer-facing teams and other functions across the company.

Hybrid

Full-time

Что предстоит делать

  • Own and manage the Salesforce team’s scope of work alongside other Technical Salesforce Project Managers, balancing ongoing feature requests with larger project initiatives.
  • Lead cross-organizational technical Salesforce projects end-to-end, from discovery and design through implementation and hypercare.
  • Develop clear project plans aligned with business cases and company priorities, including timelines, deliverables, and milestones.
  • Lead relevant ceremonies, keep delivery on track, and manage trade-offs as priorities shift.
  • Lead technical design discussions, negotiate requirements, and drive alignment across business and technical stakeholders.

Кто вы

  • The ideal candidate is a highly motivated leader who drives initiatives end-to-end, with Salesforce implementation experience and technical depth to shape design decisions in partnership with developers, backed by excellent communication skills and a passion for delivering results.
  • At least 4 years of experience managing technical projects in an enterprise environment.
  • Salesforce Administrator Certification or equivalent hands-on administration experience, with demonstrated proficiency in Sales Cloud.
  • Ability to deeply understand technical concepts and credibly engage with engineers on architecture, dependencies, and risk Experience managing Salesforce integrations with third-party applications and systems.
  • Proven ability to translate business requirements into actionable technical Salesforce solutions.
  • A proactive approach to using AI tools throughout project delivery, from discovery and analysis to solution design and execution.
  • Strong leadership skills with the capability to lead and motivate stakeholders toward achieving project goals.
  • Detail-oriented with the willingness to deep-dive into technical solutions.
  • Advantages Experience leading Agentforce implementation projects.
  • Knowledge of Experience Cloud and Digital Engagement.
