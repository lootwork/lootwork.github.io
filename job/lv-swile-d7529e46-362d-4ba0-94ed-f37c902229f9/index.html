At Swile, we believe that good products can help reduce friction in daily professional life and boost employee satisfaction. Today, we provide innovative solutions in various areas such as Fintech, Travel, HR, and Employee Benefits to more than 5.5 million users in 85,000 companies in France and Brazil.

Your role as a Senior Software Engineer centers around creatively solving problems, ensuring a balance between speed, pragmatism and excellence. It's less about the specific technologies used and more about crafting innovative solutions to business and engineering challenges.

Mission & Key Challenges:The primary mission of this role is to lead the Engineering Payments team in Brazil, accelerate execution, and establish strong authority and credibility on payments-related topics.

Что предстоит делать

As the Engineering Manager for Payments, you will play a critical role in driving the execution and evolution of our payments ecosystem in Brazil. You will lead a team of engineers while partnering closely with Product, Finance, and other stakeholders to deliver reliable, scalable, and business-critical payment solutions.

Key responsibilities include

  • Driving efficient execution and delivery across the Payments domain, ensuring projects are delivered with quality, predictability, and impact.
  • Acting as the primary payments subject matter expert and trusted advisor for internal stakeholders, providing technical guidance and strategic input on payment-related initiatives.
  • Strengthening collaboration between Engineering and Finance teams to improve operational efficiency, payment processes, reconciliation, and business outcomes.
  • Supporting the evolution of our payments strategy, architecture, and systems to enable future growth and scalability in Brazil.
  • Leading and developing a high-performing engineering team while maintaining involvement in technical discussions, architecture decisions, and operational challenges.
  • Removing blockers, improving engineering processes, and creating an environment that enables teams to execute effectively and move quickly.

Кто вы

Technical & Functional Expertise

  • Strong software engineering background with solid technical foundations and experience building scalable systems.
  • Strong product mindset, with the ability to balance customer needs, business objectives, and technical considerations.
  • Deep expertise in payments, including experience designing, building, and scaling payment systems and related infrastructure.
  • Proven experience leading engineering teams while remaining close to technical decision-making and architectural discussions.
  • Fluent in Engilsh (C1-C2).

Leadership & Management

  • Demonstrated ownership mindset with accountability for team outcomes and business impact.
  • Hands-on leader who is comfortable engaging in operational and technical challenges when needed.
  • Execution-focused, with a track record of improving delivery, removing obstacles, and driving results.
  • Strong collaborator who can build trust and alignment across Engineering, Finance, Product, and other cross-functional teams.

Ways of Working

  • Comfortable working in an office-first environment with regular in-person collaboration (hybrid model, São Paulo office).
  • Effective at building relationships with stakeholders and facilitating cross-functional teamwork.
  • Thrives in fast-paced environments where prioritization, adaptability, and strong communication are essential.
