Powered by world-class technology and a user-first mindset, Bybit delivers a seamless ecosystem across trading, payments, wealth management, custody, institutional services, and Web3 — connecting users to the future of digital finance.

Our core values define how we build. We listen, care and improve to create products and experiences that put users first. Backed by a global team of ambitious builders, problem-solvers, and innovators, we foster a high-performance and fast-moving environment where talent is empowered to drive real impact at the global scale. Today, Bybit is recognized as one of the most trusted and transparent platforms in the digital asset industry, continuing to expand its global presence while building the infrastructure for the next generation of financial services.

Key Responsibilities

Exchange Platform Performance

  • Optimize order entry, risk, matching and market data processing paths.
  • Identify bottlenecks across order validation, risk checks, matching, execution reporting and market data publication.
  • Improve latency, throughput, jitter and scalability.
  • Translate exchange and market-structure requirements into high-performance designs.
  • Evaluate trade-offs between performance, resilience and maintainability.

Low-Latency Architecture and Software

  • Design architectures and data flows for latency-critical exchange components.
  • Optimize concurrency, thread scheduling, memory access and data structures.
  • Apply cache-efficient design, memory alignment, allocation avoidance and zero-copy processing.
  • Design lock-free or wait-free algorithms and high-performance queues.
  • Reduce unnecessary processing, copying, serialization and context switching.

Protocol, Network and System Optimization

  • Design and optimize binary protocols and communication between gateways, risk engines, matching engines and market data components.
  • Improve serialization, IPC, messaging, buffering, batching and backpressure handling.
  • Work with FIX, FIXP, SBE and proprietary exchange protocols.
  • Evaluate technologies such as Aeron, TIBCO FTL, DPDK and Solarflare OpenOnload.
  • Optimize NIC queues, interrupts, packet processing and memory buffers.
  • Apply CPU isolation, thread pinning, IRQ affinity and NUMA-aware resource allocation.
  • Analyze cache behavior, context switches, page faults, microbursts, queueing and tail-latency variation.
  • Collaborate with Infrastructure Engineering on Linux, firmware, BIOS, NIC and server configuration.

Performance Measurement

  • Establish representative benchmarks for exchange workloads.
  • Measure component and end-to-end latency, including p50, p99, p99.9 and p99.99.
  • Build profiling, tracing and automated performance-regression capabilities.
  • Investigate latency spikes, jitter and throughput limitations.
  • Reproduce production performance issues and translate findings into engineering improvements.
  • Use reliable performance evidence to guide architecture and protocol decisions.

Required Qualifications

  • 10+ years of experience as a software engineer.
  • 5+ years working with trading technology, exchange infrastructure, HFT, market making or financial-market infrastructure.
  • Experience building or optimizing production trading systems with latency below 100 microseconds.
  • Experience with order entry gateways, market data distribution, risk engines or matching engines.
  • Experience profiling latency-critical systems and analyzing latency distributions.
  • Ability to investigate performance across application, protocol, network, operating-system and hardware layers.
  • Strong hands-on technical expertise in:
  • C++ and Java
  • Linux systems
  • Networking
  • Concurrency
  • Low-latency architecture
  • Memory optimization
  • Lock-free systems
  • Binary protocols
  • Tail-latency optimization

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with FIX, FIXP, SBE or proprietary exchange protocols.
  • Experience with Aeron, TIBCO FTL or similar messaging technologies.
  • Experience with DPDK, Solarflare OpenOnload or equivalent user-space networking.
  • Knowledge of SPSC/MPSC queues, NUMA, huge pages, CPU pinning and cache-line optimization.
  • Familiarity with Linux perf, eBPF, flame graphs and hardware performance counters.
  • Understanding of exchange market structure, order lifecycle, pre-trade risk, matching engines and market data distribution.
