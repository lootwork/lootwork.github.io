О команде

Our Growth Team sits at the heart of Airwallex’s mission, focusing on user acquisition and strategic market expansion. We leverage data and cutting-edge technologies to craft seamless, intuitive experiences that help millions of users discover, engage with, and benefit from our products.

From new-user funnels to onboarding optimizations, our Growth team is all about experimentation, rapid iteration, and driving sustainable business impact. Whether it’s designing scalable architectures for high-traffic pages, building robust A/B testing frameworks, or integrating global marketing platforms, the Growth team’s contributions directly shape how we attract and retain customers worldwide.

Joining the Growth team means you’ll have a front-row seat to the most crucial stage of our product lifecycle and play a pivotal role in fueling the next wave of our global expansion.

Что предстоит делать

As a Backend Engineer within the Growth team, you will architect, develop, and maintain scalable microservices and backend systems powering our growth initiatives. You will enable experimentation and data-driven decision-making, collaborate closely with cross-functional teams, and drive robust, secure, and high-performing technical solutions that support our global user acquisition and engagement strategies.

This role is based in San Francisco.

Обязанности

- Architect, develop, and maintain microservices in Java/Kotlin (or similar languages), ensuring high-traffic, high-availability systems meet global user demands.

- Implement best practices for service-to-service communication, orchestration, and fault tolerance.

- Collaborate with data scientists and product managers to design data pipelines, analytics platforms, and event-driven architectures.

- Ensure Growth data is accurate, reliable, and easily accessible for key decisions.

- Translate business requirements into scalable backend solutions by working closely with Frontend, Product, Design, and Marketing teams.

- Participate in architectural reviews, code reviews, and strategy sessions to continually improve backend service quality and performance.

- Develop services that support rapid A/B testing, feature toggles, and real-time performance monitoring; continuously improve backend functionalities to enhance user engagement and drive conversions.

- Integrate secure coding practices and frameworks to protect user data and ensure global regulatory compliance. Proactively address potential vulnerabilities and security risks.

- Deep experience with high throughput/low latency/high availability distributed systems

- Cloud experience with GCP (preferred) or AWS (EC2, RDS, ELB, CloudFront, etc.), and familiarity with Docker and Kubernetes

- Familiarity with observability tooling such as Splunk, Grafana, and Prometheus
