О роли

We are looking for an experienced Payments Director to lead our Payments function and ensure that our payments services operate safely, reliably and in line with our regulatory and scheme obligations.

Sitting within Technology and reporting to the Chief IT Control Officer, this is a senior leadership role with responsibility for providing effective oversight and control of the Bank’s payments ecosystem. You will lead the Payments team, ensuring payment risks are understood and appropriately managed, regulatory and scheme requirements are met, and our critical third party relationships are effectively governed.

You will work across Technology, Operations, Risk, Compliance and the wider business providing clear ownership and leadership when issues arise and helping us maintain a resilient, well controlled payments environment as the Bank continues to grow.

Why this role matters

Payments are at the heart of our customers’ experience and are one of the most critical services we provide.

At Starling, we want to move quickly and continue to innovate, but we also need to ensure that our payments services remain safe, resilient, compliant and well controlled.

As Payments Director, you’ll play a central role in achieving the balance. You’ll bring together technology, risk, regulation, suppliers and schemes to ensure we understand our exposures, respond effectively when things go wrong and continue to strengthen our payments capability as the bank grows.

What you'll get to do

You will provide leadership and oversight across the Bank’s payments activities, with particular responsibility for:

  • Payment risk and control - ensuring risks across the payments estate are identified, assessed and effectively managed, with a strong and proportionate control environment.
  • Regulatory and scheme compliance - ensuring the bank understands and meets its obligations relating to payments, including applicable regulatory requirements, industry standards and payment scheme rules
  • Scheme Management - owning and overseeing key relationships with payment schemes and networks, including Mastercard, ensuring obligations are met and changes, issues and risks are appropriately managed
  • Supplier and third party management - providing effective oversight of critical payment suppliers and partners, ensuring appropriate performance, governance, risk management and escalation arrangements are in place
  • Incident Management - providing senior leadership and oversight of payment incidents, ensuring issues are managed effectively from identification through to resolution, with appropriate stakeholder communication, root cause analysis and remediation.
  • Operational Resilience - ensuring payment services are resilient and recoverable, with appropriate contingency arrangements, testing and oversight of critical interdependencies
  • Change and emerging risk - assessing the risk and control implications of changes to payment products, platforms, regulation, schemes and suppliers, ensuring appropriate controls are embedded from the outset.
  • Governance and reporting - providing clear and insightful reporting on payment risks, incident, supplier performance, compliance and emerging issues to senior management and relevant governance forums.
  • Leadership - leading and developing a high performing payments team, establishing clear accountability and building strong relationships across Technology and the wider bank.

Требования

You will bring significant leadership experience within payments, ideally gained within a bank, fintech, payments provider or similar regulated financial services environment.

You will have a strong understanding of the payments landscape and the risks associated with delivering critical payment services. You’ll be comfortable operating at the intersection of technology, operations, risk and regulation, and able to translate complex payment issues into clear decisions and outcomes.
