О TIDE

At Tide, we help SMEs save time and money in the running of their businesses by not only offering business accounts and related banking services, but also a comprehensive set of highly usable and connected administrative solutions, from invoicing to accounting.

Tide is transforming the small business banking market and now supports over 2 million members globally across the UK, Germany and France.

Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love.

О команде

Tide Payroll lets UK small businesses pay themselves and their staff, file with HMRC and run pensions from the same place they bank. Thousands of businesses rely on it every payday, so it has to be right every time. You will lead a small, cross-functional team and shape where Payroll goes next.

О роли

The engineering challenge here is correctness and reliability. Payroll works out tax, National Insurance, student loans and pensions, then sends them to HMRC and pension providers on fixed deadlines. A pay run is either right on payday or someone is paid the wrong amount. Holding that standard as member numbers grow, and as tax rules change every April, is the core of this role.

  • As an Engineering Manager at Tide you will design, build and run the products in your area. We have a long roadmap ahead of us and rarely run out of interesting problems to tackle.
  • This role offers an exceptional opportunity to make a real difference by taking ownership of engineering practices in a rapidly expanding company. We trust and empower our Engineering Managers to make real architectural decisions that affect their domain and shape the future of Tide's One Platform.
  • We work in full-stack teams, grouped under common domains that own products in Tide's product catalogue. Our engineers self-organise, gather together to discuss technical challenges, and set their own guidelines in our Communities of Practice.

As Engineering Manager for Payroll, you will be

  • Owning the full Payroll stack: a Go backend, a React web frontend, an Angular microfrontend, and the Payroll Backoffice tools. Defining the target architecture, including whether the React app stays or is replaced, and making the platform ready for expansion into other markets
  • Owning the reliability of every pay run, HMRC filing and pension submission, and building the monitoring that tells us something has failed before a customer does
  • Turning HMRC rules and their edge cases into clear product rules, working with our compliance advisers when a case is unclear
  • Leading the engineers in your team, carefully dividing your time between design, coding and coaching. You will be the person people turn to for advice and solutions to difficult problems
  • Running the escalation rota with Operations and fixing the causes of repeat issues, not each case
  • Regularly aligning your roadmap and architecture with other Engineering Managers to make multi-domain work succeed
  • Working closely with product, mobile engineers, operations and compliance to turn requirements into well-engineered solutions
  • Making key contributions to our Communities of Practice, including how teams use AI tools in their daily work

Кого мы ищем

Experience and background

  • Extensive experience (10+ years) building server-side applications. Go is ideal; strong experience in Java or another strongly typed language also works
  • Working knowledge of modern web frontends (React or Angular), enough to guide design decisions and review code
  • Experience running microservices and building and consuming RESTful APIs
  • Experience engineering scalable and reliable solutions in a cloud-native environment
  • Experience replacing or retiring part of a live system without disrupting customers
  • A track record of improving reliability: monitoring, alerting, incident response and service levels that reflect what customers experience
  • Comfort with detailed rules: you enjoy getting to the bottom of a complex case and making the answer repeatable
  • A mindset of delivering secure, well-tested and well-documented software, and owning your code and your team's processes from development to production
  • Exposure to complex solutions in financial or payment services, and to integrating with third-party providers and partners
  • Experience with relational databases and with continuous integration, delivery and deployment
  • The ability to explain technical concepts to a non-technical audience

Будет плюсом

  • UK payroll, tax or pensions knowledge (RTI, PAYE, auto-enrolment). We will support you to take the CIPP payroll course if you do not have it
  • Flutter experience
