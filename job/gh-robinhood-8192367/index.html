The Performance Marketing team’s mission is to drive efficient, data-driven customer acquisition and growth for Robinhood by optimizing paid marketing investment across performance marketing channels like Google and Meta.

As Performance marketing intern, you will own performance marketing campaigns across Google and paid social, and are responsible for the full-funnel performance of your campaigns.

This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Manage and optimize day-to-day performance marketing campaigns across Google Ads and paid social platforms (Meta, TikTok, etc.), monitoring spend, pacing, and performance against targets
  • Analyze campaign data to identify trends, surface actionable insights, and recommend optimizations to improve CAC, ROAS, and other key performance metrics
  • Build and maintain performance dashboards/reports to track campaign results and communicate findings to the broader marketing team
  • Conduct A/B tests on ad creative, targeting, and bidding strategies, and translate results into optimization recommendations
  • Partner with growth and analytics teams to ensure budget allocation and channel strategy are grounded in data, not assumptions

Что вы приносите

  • Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in a quantitative field such as Finance, Economics, Statistics, or a related discipline with expected graduation date of Winter 2027/Summer 2028
  • Strong analytical skills with the ability to translate data into clear, actionable insights
  • Familiarity with SQL preferred; comfort working with data and a willingness to learn new tools quickly
  • Genuine curiosity and a proactive mindset — you ask questions, dig into the 'why' behind the numbers, and proactively identify what to tackle next
  • Self-starter with strong initiative, able to take ownership of tasks and drive them forward with minimal oversight
