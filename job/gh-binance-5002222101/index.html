Binance is a leading global blockchain ecosystem behind the world’s largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume and registered users. We are trusted by 300+ million people in 100+ countries for our industry-leading security, user fund transparency, trading engine speed, deep liquidity, and an unmatched portfolio of digital-asset products. Binance offerings range from trading and finance to education, research, payments, institutional services, and more. Our mission is to increase the freedom of money and financial access for people around the world.

Обязанности

  • Participate in the full-stack development and maintenance of the KYC (Know Your Customer) system, covering both backend services and frontend features
  • Assist in drafting and implementing the interface documentation for KYC business modules
  • Participate in feature testing and regression for KYC releases, and help troubleshoot issues reported by the QA team
  • Support the CS team in resolving and classifying customer issues for system improvement

Требования

  • Computer-related major, bachelor's degree (final-year students and fresh graduates are both welcome)
  • Solid Java fundamentals and good understanding of OOP; basic awareness of multithreading and networking concepts
  • Some exposure to Spring / Spring Boot (coursework or personal projects are sufficient)
  • Familiar with basic MySQL usage; knowledge of NoSQL databases is a plus
  • Basic understanding of common middleware such as Redis, RabbitMQ or Kafka (hands-on experience is a plus, not required)
  • Full-stack interest: some frontend foundation (HTML/CSS/JavaScript); familiarity with React or Vue is preferred
  • Good logical thinking, teamwork and a strong willingness to learn
  • Able to read and write technical documents in English; good spoken English is an advantage
