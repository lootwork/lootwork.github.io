О роли

As a Brokerage Operations Intern you will dive into the fast-paced world of capital markets. You will support our Brokerage Managers in daily trading activities, risk management, order monitoring, and the operational rollout of exciting new investment products. This is a unique opportunity to learn the intricacies of trade execution, algorithmic trading, and investment management from industry experts.

Что предстоит делать

- Order Management & Best Execution: Support the daily monitoring of trade executions and order flows to help ensure client trades are processed accurately, on time, and in strict adherence to best execution policies.

- Risk Management & Algorithmic Trading Support: Assist the team in monitoring algorithmic trading flows and evaluating daily operational risks, helping to ensure our automated trading systems run smoothly and within defined risk parameters.

- Market Abuse Monitoring & Compliance: Support the continuous monitoring of trading activities to assist in identifying potential market abuse scenarios. Help prepare data for regulatory compliance reporting (such as MiFIR, MAR, or Best Execution).

- Investment Universe Management: Assist in managing and expanding our offered investment universe across various European and EMEA jurisdictions, ensuring new and existing assets align with local regulatory and operational requirements.

- Trade Execution Support: Assist with the daily operational processes surrounding trading, including the execution handling for equities, mutual funds, and ELTIFs (European Long-Term Investment Funds).

- Product Planning & Launch: Collaborate cross-functionally with Product and Operations teams to assist with research and operational readiness for new brokerage product launches across different regions.

- Ad-Hoc Brokerage Projects: Actively participate in team projects aimed at improving execution mechanisms, rectifying mistrades, and optimizing our internal trading infrastructure.

Что вы приносите

- Keen Interest in Markets & Data: A strong, demonstrable passion for financial markets, data analysis, trading, and the fintech ecosystem.

- Foundational Knowledge: Basic understanding of market structures, trade lifecycles, and different financial instruments (knowledge of mutual funds, ELTIFs, or algorithmic trading concepts is a great bonus!).

- Analytical Mindset: High attention to detail, excellent problem-solving skills, and a proactive, "hands-on" attitude when dealing with complex data.

- Tech-Savvy: Comfortable working with data and eager to learn about trading platforms, market data tools (like Bloomberg), and internal order management systems.

- Communication: Fluency in English is required; German is a strong plus.
