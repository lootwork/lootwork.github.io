О роли

We are looking for a strategic, structured, and collaborative leader to lead the Governance pillar within Adyen’s global Scheme Compliance team. You will manage a small globally distributed team, setting the direction for how we demonstrate compliance with card scheme and local payment method requirements through effective governance, control assurance, and coordinated external engagement.

This role combines people leadership with accountability for our global governance framework, audit and assessment coordination, internal control mapping and testing, and relationships with scheme Risk and Compliance counterparts. You will own committee and board reporting across regional management levels, oversee the global scheme bulletin intake and update process, and lead scheme communications, requests for information, and recurring governance engagements.

Working closely with the Brand, Financial, and Data Integrity pillars, Internal Audit, Internal Controls, and operational teams, you will ensure that requirements, findings, and commitments translate into clear ownership, timely action, and transparent management oversight.

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead and develop a global team. Manage, coach, and develop team members across regions, establishing clear responsibilities, priorities, and development opportunities. Balance planned assurance work with incoming scheme requests and emerging priorities.
  • Own the governance framework. Define and maintain consistent standards for scheme, debit network, and alternative payment method compliance documentation, control mapping, evidence management, issue tracking, and reporting across the program pillars.
  • Lead audit and assessment coordination. Oversee Scheme Compliance’s participation in scheme and local payment method audits, assessments, and due diligence reviews. Coordinate scope, subject-matter contributions, evidence, responses, and follow-up with accountable stakeholders.
  • Oversee control mapping and testing. Lead the mapping of scheme and payment method requirements to internal policies and controls. Establish a risk-based testing plan with Internal Controls and pillar leads, ensuring control gaps are documented, assessed, and escalated.
  • Serve as the global governance point of contact. Act as the central interface for scheme Risk and Compliance counterparts, connecting the appropriate regional and subject-matter owners while maintaining a consistent global approach.
  • Manage scheme communications and RFIs. Oversee the intake, routing, coordination, and quality review of scheme and local payment method communications and requests for information. Ensure responses are accurate, consistent, appropriately reviewed, and delivered on time.
  • Lead recurring scheme governance engagements. Own the preparation and coordination of weekly business reviews (WBRs), quarterly business reviews (QBRs), and annual meetings, including agendas, reporting, stakeholder preparation, and tracking of decisions and commitments.
  • Partner on assurance and remediation. Work closely with Internal Audit and Internal Controls to align assurance activities, support independent reviews, and reduce unnecessary duplication. Challenge remediation plans, monitor progress, and escalate overdue actions or unresolved risks.
  • Own committee and board reporting. Lead the preparation, consolidation, and delivery of Scheme Compliance reporting to committees, boards, and management forums across all regions and management levels. Ensure reporting provides a clear, consistent view of compliance risks, control effectiveness, audit findings, remediation progress, and scheme commitments, with appropriate escalation of matters requiring attention or decisions.
  • Own the global scheme bulletin intake and update process. Oversee the intake, tracking, distribution, and status reporting of bulletins and rule updates from all schemes globally. Ensure relevant owners assess applicability and impact, establish required actions and deadlines, and provide evidence of completion. Maintain a consolidated view of progress and escalate gaps, delays, or unresolved ownership.
