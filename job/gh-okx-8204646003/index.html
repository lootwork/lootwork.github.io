О Opportunity

Enterprise Efficiency builds the internal products and platforms that power how our company operates across People, Finance, Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC), Legal, Procurement, and related functions.

We are looking for a Product Director to lead the product strategy, roadmap, and team for this portfolio. You will combine enterprise domain expertise, product judgment, and organizational leadership to identify operational problems, redesign workflows, and deliver measurable improvements through enterprise systems, automation, and AI.

You will build a product organization that owns the full journey from problem discovery and process design through delivery, adoption, and business impact. Partnering with functional leaders and engineering, you will connect individual initiatives into a coherent enterprise product strategy.

Что предстоит делать

  • Turn domain expertise into enterprise products. Understand your function’s processes, policies, controls, and user needs. Translate them into internal products that simplify daily work and support business operations at scale.
  • Own product strategy and roadmap for your domain. Partner with functional leaders to prioritize system foundations and efficiency opportunities. Make clear investment and build-versus-buy decisions aligned with business goals.
  • Redesign end-to-end workflows. Identify bottlenecks, remove unnecessary steps, and reduce manual handoffs and spreadsheet work. Define clear business rules, approvals, exceptions, and ownership from initiation through completion.
  • Connect domain systems and data. Partner with engineering to integrate core systems and internal tools, improve data consistency, and deliver connected user experiences with appropriate permissions and auditability.
  • Drive efficiency through automation and AI. Identify, test, and scale capabilities that reduce repetitive work and improve decision quality, enabling your function to handle growing demand without proportional increases in manual effort.
  • Lead delivery, adoption, and business outcomes. Align engineering, design, and functional owners from discovery through rollout and continuous improvement. Measure results through cycle time, manual effort, accuracy, adoption, and control effectiveness.

What We Look For In You

  • 7+ years of experience in product management or closely related enterprise product roles, including experience leading product managers and managing a portfolio across teams.
  • A track record of taking enterprise products from discovery through launch, adoption, and measurable business outcomes.
  • Deep functional expertise in at least one of the following domains, with experience leading initiatives across adjacent functions:
  • Finance: FP&A, procurement-to-pay, record-to-report, accounting operations, or financial controls.
  • People: Core HR, recruiting, employee lifecycle, performance, compensation processes, or workforce planning.
  • GRC: Risk assessment, control management, audit, policy management, third-party risk, or remediation.
  • Demonstrated ability to translate complex business processes, policies, and controls into clear product models, workflows, and system requirements.
  • Experience defining product strategy, allocating resources, and making difficult prioritization and build-versus-buy decisions.
  • Technical fluency in enterprise integrations, data dependencies, identity, permissions, and auditability.
  • Strong executive communication and stakeholder leadership, including the ability to challenge assumptions and align teams without direct authority.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience building or transforming an enterprise product organization.
  • Experience with platforms such as Workday, Oracle, SAP, ServiceNow, or equivalent internal systems.
  • Experience deploying AI or agent capabilities into production workflows and measuring their business impact.
  • Experience in financial services, crypto, payments, or another environment with demanding control and audit requirements.
  • Experience building reusable platforms and consolidating fragmented internal tools.
