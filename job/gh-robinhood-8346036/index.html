О team + role

Growth is one of Robinhood’s highest-impact engineering areas where you’ll build the experiences and systems that shape acquisition, activation, and long-term customer engagement.

The team works on products tied closely to core company outcomes — including net funded accounts, net deposits, and Gold subscribers. This is where strong execution translates quickly into visible business impact.

As a Senior Backend Engineer on Growth, you’ll be expected to operate with outsized ownership and impact. This is the perfect role for an engineer who can lead high-leverage projects while also strengthening the systems, patterns, and technical foundations that make the broader organization more effective. You’ll partner closely with product, design, data, and peer engineering teams to build reliable backend services, unlock high-impact customer experiences, and raise the scale at which Robinhood can move. The right candidate will act as a force multiplier for the team: driving ambiguous initiatives, shaping architecture, elevating execution quality, and creating durable backend capabilities that extend well beyond any single project.

Что предстоит делать

  • Design, build, and evolve backend services and APIs that power Robinhood Gold experiences and growth journeys
  • Own projects end to end, from technical design through launch, measurement, and iteration
  • Partner closely with product, design, data, and peer engineering teams to translate ambiguous opportunities into scalable systems
  • Improve service reliability, performance, observability, and developer velocity for product-facing backend systems
  • Help shape architecture and technical direction for systems that support subscription experiences, member benefits, and engagement flows
  • Use metrics and sound engineering judgment to make thoughtful tradeoffs between speed, quality, and long-term maintainability
  • Mentor other engineers through design reviews, code reviews, debugging, and hands-on collaboration
  • Raise the engineering bar by driving high standards in system design, execution, and operational excellence

Что вы приносите

  • 6+ years of software engineering experience, with a strong track record building and operating backend systems in production
  • Strong proficiency in designing distributed systems, APIs, and data models for reliability and scale
  • Experience leading medium-to-large technical projects with multiple stakeholders and clear business impact
  • Ability to work in ambiguous, fast-moving product environments and turn open-ended problems into pragmatic execution plans
  • Strong product sense and a willingness to engage deeply with customer experience, experimentation, and business outcomes
  • A high ownership mindset and the ability to independently drive work from concept to production
  • Excellent collaboration and communication skills across engineering and cross-functional partners
  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience
