О вас

The Xsolla Offerwall puts rewarded offers in front of players inside the games and apps of our publishers. A player opens the wall, picks an offer, completes it, and earns in-game currency. Whether they do depends on what they see: the offer card, the creative, the video, the playable, and the reward moment.

Those units come from several places. Some are native offers we render ourselves. Some arrive as VAST video, HTML or MRAID creatives, or OpenRTB Native assets from demand partners. Each format has its own rules, and each one has to look right in a publisher's game, on a phone, without breaking what the advertiser sent.

You'll own how offerwall ads look and perform. You'll decide which formats we support and how they render. You'll design what the player sees, and turn that into specs engineers can build from. You'll measure whether a change made players more likely to engage, and you'll use AI to make better creative faster than a manual process allows.

Обязанности

Ad experience and creative

  • Own the look and feel of every ad unit in the offerwall: offer cards, detail pages, video, playables, interstitials, and the reward flow.
  • Set creative standards for advertisers and publishers: layouts, asset specs, safe areas, aspect ratios, and branding rules.
  • Balance three groups who want different things: players want a clean experience, publishers want it to match their game, and advertisers want their brand shown properly.

oRTB formats and rendering

  • Define how we support VAST, HTML and MRAID, and OpenRTB Native inside the offerwall, across Web, iOS, Android, and Unity.
  • Write the requirements for format support: which VAST events we track, which MRAID calls we allow, and how Native assets map into our own templates.
  • Work with Engineering on creative QA and rejection rules, so broken or non-compliant creatives get caught before a player sees them.

Design to engineering

  • Turn ideas into Figma designs and prototypes that engineers can build without guessing.
  • Write PRDs and acceptance criteria that cover states, edge cases, and device differences, not just the happy path.
  • Work with our designers on the offerwall's design system, and keep it consistent across publishers and platforms.

Experimentation and performance

  • Run A/B tests on layouts, creative treatments, and formats, and read the results yourself.
  • Track the metrics that show whether the ad experience works: CTR, video completion rate, offer start and completion rates, IPM, and eCPM.
  • Turn what you learn into a prioritized roadmap, and explain the tradeoffs to Engineering, Demand, and leadership.

AI in creative and workflow

  • Find where AI can improve ad units: generating creative variants, adapting assets to new formats, auto-cropping and localization, and checking creative quality.
  • Use AI tools in your own work: prototyping, writing specs, analyzing test results, and getting from idea to something testable faster.

Cross-functional work

  • Work with Engineering, Design, Demand, Publisher Success, and Data every week.
  • Give the Demand team clear creative guidelines they can share with advertisers.
  • Bring what publishers and advertisers say back into the roadmap, in a form engineers can act on.

Требования и навыки

  • Around 5 years of product management experience, with a significant part of it in AdTech.
  • Hands-on experience owning ad design or ad experience: ad units, creative formats, or how ads render in an app or game.
  • Working knowledge of oRTB ad formats. You know how VAST, HTML and MRAID, and OpenRTB Native creatives work, what breaks them, and what the specs allow.
  • Comfortable with data. You can design an A/B test, read the result, and write SQL or use a BI tool to answer your own questions.
  • You write clear specs. Engineers should be able to build from what you write without coming back to ask what you meant.
  • Good visual judgment. You can tell why one layout works and another doesn't, and explain it in terms of player behavior.
  • Clear communication with engineers, designers, and commercial teams.
  • Based in Los Angeles or elsewhere in the US, and able to work across US time zones.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience working with AI. This one matters a lot to us. Show us how you've used AI tools such as Claude, Midjourney, or Figma AI to make ads, prototype products, or get more done. Come ready to show a real example.
  • Experience with offerwalls, rewarded ads, or rewarded video.
  • Experience designing in Figma: building prototypes and engineering-ready designs yourself, not just reviewing someone else's.
  • Experience in mobile gaming or app monetization.
  • Familiarity with ad SDKs, mediation, and how they render third-party creatives.…
