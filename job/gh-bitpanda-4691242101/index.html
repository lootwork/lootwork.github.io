Your mission

As a Trading Operations Specialist at Bitpanda, your mission is to ensure a secure and smooth trading experience. You are well-versed in the foundations of financial instruments and trading systems and also possess a very strong, problem-solving-oriented mindset.

Что предстоит делать

  • Deep-dive into and quick understanding of our financial products, trading and hedging systems
  • Handle market alerts and execute OTC trades & blockchain transactions on behalf of Bitpanda
  • Perform daily settlement and reconciliation of trades
  • Monitor hedging and trading activity and maintain the relevant risk parameter settings; you will be the first line of defence for the sanity of our trading and hedging operations
  • Proactively document and improve processes & recommend system developments

Кто вы

  • Experience in trading operations or a similar area (trader certificate is a plus); you should have a great understanding of and/or interest in financial markets, particularly equities or cryptocurrencies/blockchain
  • Ability to identify problems and solve them quickly or escalate them as necessary
  • Strong data investigation skills (MS Excel in particular and SQL or working with databases is a plus; experience with Bloomberg, Refinitiv (Reuters) or FactSet is welcome)
  • Great team player with strong attention to detail, excellent organizational and communication skills (English is a must), thriving in an ever-changing environment
  • Ability to work in night shift (00:00-07:00) to ensure operational continuity in a 24/7 business
