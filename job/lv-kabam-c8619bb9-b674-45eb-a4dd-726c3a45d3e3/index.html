Senior Growth Marketing Manager

At Kabam, we’re constantly raising the bar of excellence in free-to-play gaming. We hire for passion and diversity so that we can triumph in our collective skill. Our teams are made up of a fusion of personalities and interests bound together by a collaborative nature, fun-loving culture, and a drive to do what it takes to make great games. And this is where you come in…

We are looking for a Senior Growth Marketing Manager who will be at the forefront of shaping and executing our growth strategy across various user acquisition channels, re-engagement tactics, and ad monetization efforts. Leveraging their expertise in data-driven decision-making, cross-functional collaboration, and strategic thinking, they will play a crucial role in driving Kabam’s games' success and expansion.

This role is a full-time, on-site position based 5 days a week at our Culver City studio.

IN THIS ROLE, YOU CAN EXPECT TO

Key Accountabilities

Strategy and Team Management

  • Develop and implement user acquisition strategies across diverse channels, driving diversification for optimized outcomes.
  • Formulate localized market strategies and tactics for major geos and regions, adapting approaches to match local nuances.
  • Generate clear, detailed long- and short-term projections, effectively communicating these projections to key stakeholders.
  • Demonstrate macro level thinking and be able to lead complex adhoc projects under guidance. (examples: Attribution windows analysis and adjustment on an app level, CPI projection forecasts, Yields projections etc.).
  • Be an avid sponsor for direct reports by providing support, advocacy, visibility, and promotion opportunities.
  • Able to monitor team growth and constantly providing support to encourage active learning.

Monetization, ASO, Creative Strategy

  • Lead App Store Optimization (ASO) projects to enhance storefront visibility and conversion metrics, formulating strategic approaches for success.
  • Have understanding of App Store Optimization (ASO) to strategize and execute synergy with paid marketing and storefronts.
  • Collaborate with the Marketing Creatives team to create and implement effective creative strategies that unlock growth potential.

User Acquisition, Re-engagement, and Retargeting

  • Define comprehensive strategies for new user acquisition and re-engagement/retargeting, tailored to different markets.
  • Implement retargeting campaigns to convert users who have expressed interest in our games.
  • Plan and execute channel strategy, budgets, KPIs and optimize campaigns to drive efficiency in CPI, ROAS, and LTV.
  • Deep understanding with AppsFlyer and working inside ad platforms such as DSPs, Influencers, Meta Ads, Tiktok, Google Ads, etc.
  • Be an expert in attribution models such as Mobile, iOS SKAN, Cross Platform, PC, etc. and be up to speed on Market Trends around Privacy, optimization models, etc.

Process Improvement and Automation

  • Spearhead the development of a Growth Marketing Automation function, enhancing processes, productivity, and scalability.
  • Identify opportunities for process improvements to support the organization's rapid scaling.

Analysis, Report, and Budget Management

  • Conduct thorough SWOT and competitor analyses for a portfolio of titles, providing insights to guide strategic decisions
  • Collaborate with the Data team to build reporting tools, automate growth marketing operational processes, and estimate projections and LTVs.
  • Utilize data-driven insights to inform decision-making and optimize growth strategies.
  • Manage and allocate budgets effectively across different marketing channels to achieve maximum impact for each project.
  • Raise flags for future potential issues that might arise if we continue on course or due to expected changes based on our current methodology
  • Ability to lead diagnosing game problems and creating actionable plans for problem resolution
  • Ability to work with Data to create data visualization views that allows team to run in-depth analysis of UA effort.

Cross-functional Collaboration

  • Collaborate closely with game teams to grasp product roadmaps, features, design, and economy, contributing insights for revenue growth.
  • Partner with the Marketing team to assess the impact of product marketing, branding, and consumer insights on ROI and growth.
  • Leverage expertise from the Business Development team to gain insights into platform partnerships, IP partnerships, and regional expertise.
  • Collaborate with the FP&A team to reshape growth marketing strategies and expectations using provided game metrics.
  • Awareness to maintain clear lines of communications between teams and actively foreseeing/preventing potential points of friction.
  • Actively leading cross-team projects across product, analytics, growth, and more.

TO BE SUCCESSFUL IN THIS ROLE, YOUR BACKGROUND INCLUDES

  • Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business, or a related field.
  • A minimum of 6+ years of experience in growth marketing, with a strong focus on the mobile gaming industry.
  • Proven track record of developing and executing impactful user acquisition, monetization, and retention strategies.
  • Exceptional leadership and team management abilities.
  • Effective collaboration and communication skills, with a demonstrated aptitude for working cross-functionally.
  • Proficiency in data analysis, generating actionable insights, and utilizing data to drive strategic decisions.
  • Experience in ad monetization, ASO, and creative strategy.
  • Proficient in market analysis, projections, and reporting.
  • Forward-thinking mindset with the ability to adapt and lead in a rapidly evolving industry.
  • Prior experience in setting strategic direction for growth marketing functions is a plus.…
