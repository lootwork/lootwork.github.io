О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

Shape the global performance marketing strategy that drives customer acquisition, pipeline growth, revenue, and marketing efficiency across regions.

- Build scalable frameworks, operating models, investment strategies, and testing methodologies across paid search, paid social, programmatic, and emerging digital channels.

- Partner with regional marketing leads across APAC, EMEA, and North America to translate global frameworks into market-specific plans.

- Lead quarterly planning, budget forecasting, scenario modelling, channel-mix decisions, and regional investment trade-offs.

- Develop measurement frameworks tied to pipeline, revenue, and LTV:CAC goals, including incrementality testing, marketing mix modelling, geo-experiments, and brand lift studies.

- Work closely with Analytics, Data, Finance, BI, Sales, RevOps, Product Marketing, and regional marketing teams to connect campaign performance with lead-to-pipeline conversion and broader go-to-market strategy.

- Build standardised playbooks, campaign QA frameworks, reporting templates, and communication cadences that improve operational consistency across markets.

- Synthesise regional learnings and share insights with stakeholders globally, using campaign data to influence lifecycle and go-to-market strategy.

- Coach regional marketers and agency partners to adopt global frameworks and test new growth opportunities.

- Champion continuous improvement through experimentation, automation, and tooling enhancements across platforms including Google Ads, LinkedIn, Meta, SA360, and Looker.

This role is based in Singapore.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- 5+ years of experience in B2B or fintech performance marketing, including strategic leadership of multi-channel global acquisition programmes.

- Proven success driving measurable pipeline growth and improving marketing efficiency through metrics such as LTV:CAC and payback period.

- Hands-on understanding of Salesforce and experience building Looker tables.

- Expertise in paid search, paid social, display, and emerging channels, with familiarity with ABM and demand generation.

- Strong analytical acumen, including experience with advanced measurement, attribution, and experimentation frameworks.

- Excellent communication skills, including experience presenting to executive leadership.

- Demonstrated ability to collaborate cross-functionally across time zones and influence without authority.

- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Statistics, Computer Science, Economics, or a related field.

Preferred

- Experience scaling global paid media in high-growth SaaS or fintech environments.

- Working knowledge of marketing automation and CRM systems such as Marketo, Salesforce, or HubSpot.

- Familiarity with BI and analytics tools such as Looker, Tableau, or Google Data Studio.

- Ability to synthesise data into strategic insights and clear recommendations.

- Experience managing or operationalising in-house performance models.

- Comfort operating in ambiguous, fast-changing environments.

YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF

- You're comfortable owning the roadmap yourself.

- You own the outcome and you don't wait for permission to fix what's broken.

- You're comfortable making decisions with incomplete information and moving quickly anyway.

- You enjoy working closely with people across multiple countries and time zones as part of one connected, global team, including flexing your hours occasionally to make that connection work.

- You communicate clearly and directly across cultures, and you're thoughtful about how context shifts meaning across borders.

LEARN MORE ABOUT YOUR TEAM

Marketing at Airwallex is a growth function, not a support one. The brief is ambitious: build the brand to match the platform, well-established in some markets and still emerging in others. This team leads across product marketing, brand, demand generation, and regional GTM: understanding what our platform actually does, then building the stories that make it land with real businesses, not just buyers on paper. They work closely with product, sales, and leadership, so the link between marketing activity and business outcome is direct.

If you want to build a global brand through insight, sharp positioning, and creative that earns attention and trust, this is where marketing has real leverage.
