Job Description

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON

Your main challenge

As a Game Cinematic Artist Intern, you will join our team in Barcelona and learn how cinematic sequences are created and implemented directly inside a real-time game engine.

Working alongside our artists and game team, you will help create compelling camera shots and sequences for our games, from race introductions and menu sequences to in-game cinematics. You will develop your eye for framing, composition and visual storytelling while gaining hands-on experience with our cinematic tools and pipelines.

What it means on a daily basis

  • Create and adapt 3D camera shots and cinematic sequences inside our real-time game engine, including race intros, menu sequences and in-game cinematics.
  • Work with 3D characters, vehicles and other game assets, positioning them within scenes and finding the best framing and composition.
  • Learn and work with our existing real-time cinematic tools and pipelines, maintaining and updating sequences as the game evolves.
  • Review and test your work directly in-game, identifying visual or technical issues and making the necessary adjustments.
  • Collaborate with the game and art teams, responding to feedback and supporting new cinematic needs throughout production.

WHAT SUCCESS WILL LOOK LIKE

In your first 3 months, you will become familiar with our game engine, cinematic pipeline and existing camera sequences, progressively contributing your own shots with guidance from the team.

By 6 months, you will be comfortable creating and adapting camera sequences for different areas of the game, testing your work and incorporating feedback with increasing autonomy.

As you progress, you will develop a stronger understanding of real-time cinematics and visual storytelling while contributing polished camera work that enhances the presentation of our games.

WHO YOU WOULD BE WORKING WITH

You will work closely with our Art and Game teams in Barcelona, collaborating with the people responsible for the characters, environments and game content featured in your cinematic sequences.

You will receive guidance from experienced team members while gradually taking more ownership of your work as you become familiar with our tools and pipelines.

YOUR RECRUITMENT JOURNEY

  • Recruiter interview
  • Hiring team interview
  • Portfolio/showreel review
  • Test

WHERE THIS ROLE CAN TAKE YOU

This internship will give you practical experience in real-time cinematics within a professional game development environment, developing transferable skills in camera work, composition, 3D workflows and visual storytelling.

You will also gain exposure to how Art and Game teams collaborate to take cinematic content from an initial idea to its final implementation in-game.

Требования

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED

  • Basic understanding of 3D environments and workflows, including navigating scenes and manipulating 3D objects.
  • Understanding of camera composition, framing and visual storytelling in a 3D environment.
  • Familiarity with at least one real-time game engine, such as Unity or Unreal Engine.
  • Ability to understand how positioning, framing and camera movement can help present 3D characters, vehicles and environments effectively.
  • Good attention to both visual and technical details.
  • Good level of English.
  • Ability to sign an internship agreement with a school or university.

Кто вы

  • Curious about video games, film and visual storytelling.
  • Eager to learn new tools, workflows and technical pipelines.
  • Creative, with a good eye for composition and presentation.
  • Comfortable receiving feedback and iterating on your work.
  • Organized and able to work towards deadlines.
  • Proactive while knowing when to ask the team for guidance.
  • Detail-oriented and willing to test and review your own work.
