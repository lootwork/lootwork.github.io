О команде

Twitch is all about community, and our Community team is a core pillar of what makes Twitch, Twitch. Community Discovery & ML (CDML) helps millions of viewers find the creators and communities they'll love. That covers search, browse, the mobile live and clips feeds, recommended notifications, and the recommendation systems behind them. When a viewer finds a new creator through one of these, it's often the start of a lasting connection. That connection is what keeps viewers, and the creators they watch, coming back.

О роли

We're looking for a data scientist with strong statistics and modeling skills to be the science partner for the machine learning and recommendation systems behind Twitch's discovery products. You will work with product managers, ML engineers and applied scientists to help build what our recommendation systems should optimize for. That means balancing what a viewer does today (a click, a play, minutes watched) against what keeps them coming back: return visits, daily habits, and lasting connections with creators. It also means weighing short-form clips against long-form live content, and each surface against the others. This role will draw from a variety of statistical learning techniques, causal inference, optimization and recommender systems evaluation, and it still owns the full path from question to decision.

Reporting to the Director of Data Science, you will work from our San Francisco, CA office.

Вам предстоит

  • Help define the objective functions and success metrics for ranking and recommendations across feeds, search and notifications. Make the trade-offs explicit between short-term engagement (clicks, plays, watch time) and long-term outcomes (return visits, retention, new creator connections), including how short-form clips and live content add to or take away from each other.
  • Evaluate ranking, recommendation and feed changes with ML engineers and applied scientists. This covers A/B tests and long-running holdouts, offline metrics that predict online results, and the cost and value of exploration and cold start. Handle the hard cases: novelty effects, interference between experiments, two-sided marketplace effects involving creators and viewers, and feedback loops in recommendation data.
  • Build statistical and ML models that explain viewer behavior and size opportunities, such as propensity, uplift, survival and retention models. Include proxy metrics that predict long-term retention, so teams can decide in days rather than months.
  • Turn ambiguous product questions into clear analyses. Quantify the uncertainty, and make recommendations that inform the machine learning and discovery roadmap.
  • Define the logging and data needed to measure discovery surfaces, and check its quality.
  • Contribute to the team's standards for experimentation, causal inference and model evaluation, and share what you learn with other data scientists.

You Have

  • A BA/BS in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Economics, Data Science or a related quantitative field, or equivalent industry experience.
  • 3+ years as a data scientist in a high-velocity, data-driven environment, with hands-on experience applying statistical modeling and machine learning (regression, classification, causal inference, time series), including the assumptions behind each method and when it fails.
  • Experience designing and analyzing online experiments, including variance reduction, power analysis and the pitfalls of interpreting results.
  • Expert SQL and proficiency in Python or R.
  • A track record of using data and models to influence product decisions and leadership.

Дополнительный плюс

  • An MS or PhD in a quantitative field.
  • Experience with recommender systems, search ranking or feed ranking, such as offline evaluation (precision and recall, NDCG), exploration versus exploitation, or cold-start problems.
  • Experience with multi-objective optimization or with defining objective functions for ranking systems, including choosing weights between competing goals.
  • Experience building proxy metrics for long-term outcomes, or estimating long-term effects from long-running holdouts or observational data (e.g., difference-in-differences, synthetic control, uplift modeling).
  • Experience with two-sided platforms or marketplaces, where changes for viewers also affect creators or supply.
  • Experience on a consumer content platform: streaming, short-form video, social or marketplaces.
  • Familiarity with Twitch, as a viewer or a creator (or both!).
