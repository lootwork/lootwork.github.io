Кто мы

О Stripe

Stripe, LLC. is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

Что предстоит делать

Обязанности

  • Scope, design, build, and maintain APIs, services, and large-scale systems that reliably and efficiently handle billions of money movement requests.
  • Debug and solve critical production issues across services and multiple levels of the stack.
  • Collaborate with stakeholders across the company to build new features at large scale while improving internal engineering standards, tooling, and processes.
  • Extend balance management platform and build out new fund accessibility platforms leveraged by enterprises and SMBs.
  • Collaborate cross-functionally across infrastructure and product teams to deliver innovative solutions that address evolving user needs.

Кто вы

Minimum requirements

Must have a Bachelor's degree or foreign equivalent in Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, or a related field, plus five (5) years of progressive, post-baccalaureate related work experience in software engineering.

Must also have five (5) years of experience in each of the following:

  • Large-scale financial tracking systems;
  • Cloud-based services including: gRPC, GraphQL, Docker/Kubernetes, or AWS;
  • Design, development, and operation of mission-critical gRPC services; and
  • Software Architecture and system design, debugging/troubleshooting, and API development and design.

Must have one (1) year of experience in the following

  • Design, development, and operation of public web APIs.
