Solaris is Europe's leading embedded finance platform. Solaris’ full German banking license and proprietary modular B2B tech stack empowers its partners – from SMEs to large, multinational, non-financial companies – to offer compliant, customer-centric banking services, providing seamless experiences to customers across all industries. Founded in 2016, Solaris pioneered the Banking-as-a-Service market with an unparalleled combination of tech and banking. Solaris is headquartered in Berlin and employs 300 people in Europe.

Your Team

You will join our process engineering team that is tasked with automating business processes. You will have the opportunity to dive into the complex worlds of operations of a bank. The team is driving process improvements with cutting edge GenAI implementations. Focusing on areas like customer support, fraud detection and many more use cases.

Your Role

We're looking for a Senior AI Engineer to join our process engineering team shaping the future of banking operations. In this role, you'll work at the intersection of operations, product and engineering, owning the design, delivery, and operation of complex process automations. You’ll collaborate closely with Product Management, Engineering Leadership, Operations and fellow engineers to build secure, resilient, and reliable banking solutions.

  • Revolutionize banking operations processes using GenAI.
  • Support operations in evaluating their business processes from a technical perspective.
  • Architect and deliver scalable, secure operations workflows based on user needs.
  • Collaborate with product, engineering and operations to ensure technical decisions align with business outcomes.
  • Take end-to-end ownership of systems ("You build it, you run it"), from design and deployment to operations.
  • Guide architectural decisions and long-term technical strategy within your domain.

We'd love to see

Depending on your level of experience, your responsibilities and scope of role will range. We don’t care much about fancy titles, but rather about real personal and professional development, as laid out in our learning framework. Let’s figure together out how you can contribute to our team.

  • Proficiency in TypeScript/Node.js, with a proven track record of developing scalable automation solutions.
  • Knowledge of Terraform and basics of DevOps.
  • Previous experience in integrating GenAI solutions.
  • Extensive experience in the development and operation of cloud-native applications across AWS.
  • Communication skills and a drive for aligning engineering efforts with business goals.
  • Ability to lead cross-functional initiatives, effectively balancing technical and product objectives in a secure and regulated environment.
  • Strong expertise in building and operating distributed systems, prioritizing performance, reliability, and maintainability.
