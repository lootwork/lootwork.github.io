О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

How you'll make impact

As an Associate Growth Account Manager, SME & Growth, you'll own growth across a portfolio of 1,000+ ANZ SME customers, unlocking opportunities across active, underpenetrated and dormant accounts.

You'll combine consultative selling, data-driven portfolio management and scalable customer engagement to analyse customer behaviour, product usage and transaction trends, segment your portfolio and prioritise growth opportunities. Through targeted outreach, structured engagement programmes and scalable campaigns, you'll drive customer reactivation, increase product adoption, develop upsell and cross-sell opportunities, and grow customer wallet share and gross profit.

You'll build relationships with founders, finance leaders and business operators, helping them get more value from Airwallex's product suite across accounts, payments, FX, spend management, treasury and API-enabled solutions. Working closely with Sales, Customer Success and cross-functional teams, you'll own opportunities end to end, from identification through to conversion, while maintaining disciplined pipeline management and forecasting.

You'll also help shape how Airwallex engages with and grows its long-tail customer base, building repeatable strategies that turn untapped potential into sustainable commercial growth.

You'll work from our Bangalore office four days a week and from home one day a week.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- 3+ years of experience in account management, inside sales or expansion sales with commercial ownership, ideally within a fast-growing technology, SaaS, fintech or financial services business. Candidates with 5+ years of relevant experience are also encouraged to apply.

- Experience managing a high-volume customer portfolio or working in a sales environment that requires prioritising and engaging a large number of accounts.

- A proven track record of meeting or exceeding commercial targets, with direct exposure to revenue, gross profit, upsell, cross-sell or expansion KPIs.

- Strong consultative selling skills, with the ability to understand customer business models, uncover underlying needs and connect those needs to relevant solutions.

- Demonstrated ability to identify, develop and close commercial opportunities, including upsell, cross-sell, reactivation and product expansion.

- Strong analytical and commercial judgement, with the ability to interpret customer data, product usage and transaction trends to identify opportunities, prioritise accounts and plan effective customer engagement.

- Ability to develop differentiated engagement strategies across customer segments, balancing personalised outreach with scalable, one-to-many engagement.

- Strong relationship-building, negotiation and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to build credibility with founders, finance leaders, operators and other decision-makers.

- Exceptional verbal and written English, with the ability to communicate confidently with technical, commercial and executive stakeholders.

- A proactive and resourceful approach to problem-solving, with resilience, a positive attitude and a strong bias towards action when navigating customer or internal blockers.

- Strong organisational skills, with the ability to manage commercial pipelines, maintain accurate CRM records and operate with discipline in a high-volume environment.

Preferred

- Experience working with customers in professional services, consulting, accounting, marketing, education, travel or healthcare.

- Experience designing and executing structured outbound engagement, customer reactivation initiatives or targeted customer campaigns.

- Familiarity with customer segmentation, lifecycle marketing, automation and data-driven engagement strategies.

- Experience in fintech, payments, SaaS or another fast-growing technology business.

- Familiarity with APIs, integrations or technical solution selling, and the aptitude to learn complex products and customer requirements.

- Experience managing or selling products such as payments, foreign exchange, business accounts, corporate cards, expense management, bill payments or treasury.

- Experience using Salesforce or a comparable CRM to manage customer portfolios, pipelines and commercial performance.

- Experience leading commercial negotiations involving multiple products, senior stakeholders, procurement, legal or other review processes.

- Experience using customer or transactional datasets to diagnose opportunities, identify patterns and improve account performance.

You'll thrive here if

- You have a track record of hitting your numbers, and you’re motivated by stretch goals.

- You're naturally curious about customers and their businesses, and enjoy finding opportunities that others might overlook.…
