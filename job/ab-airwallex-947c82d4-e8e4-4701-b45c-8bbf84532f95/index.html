О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the only unified payments and financial platform for global businesses. Powered by our unique combination of proprietary infrastructure and software, we empower over 250,000 businesses worldwide – including Brex, Navan, Qantas, SHEIN and many more – with fully integrated solutions to manage everything from business accounts, payments, spend management and treasury, to embedded finance at a global scale.

Proudly founded in Melbourne, we have a team of over 2,300 of the brightest and most innovative people in tech across 27 offices around the globe. Valued at US$11 billion and backed by world-leading investors including T. Rowe Price, Visa, Mastercard, Robinhood Ventures, Sequoia, Salesforce Ventures, DST Global, and Lone Pine Capital, Airwallex is leading the charge in building the global payments and financial platform of the future. О команде

The SME & Growth Business team at Airwallex is a passionate group of collaborators and business champions dedicated to empowering small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) to thrive in the global economy. We combine deep financial expertise with a growth mindset, working closely with our clients to understand their unique needs and develop tailored solutions that streamline cross-border payments and unlock new revenue streams. As a team, we are driven by a desire to make a positive impact and are constantly innovating to find new ways to support the success of our SME customers.

Что предстоит делать

Обязанности

- Own and grow a portfolio of retail, e-commerce, marketplace and consumer business customers in Singapore.

- Build trusted, multi-threaded relationships with stakeholders across commercial, finance, operations, e-commerce, product and leadership teams.

- Develop a deep understanding of each customer’s business model, payment flows, growth plans, key performance indicators and operational challenges.

- Use customer activity, transaction data, product adoption and business developments to identify whitespace and growth opportunities.

- Identify and qualify upsell and cross-sell opportunities across the Airwallex product suite, including cross-border payments, collections, FX, payouts, Global Accounts, cards, spend management and other financial infrastructure.

- Lead consultative discovery conversations to understand customer needs, current processes, desired outcomes, decision criteria and implementation requirements.

- Translate customer challenges into relevant commercial solutions that increase wallet share, revenue and product usage within existing accounts.

- Lead commercial discussions, manage objections, negotiate effectively and progress opportunities from qualification through agreement, activation and adoption.

- Partner with customers as they expand into new markets, helping them understand how Airwallex can support international collections, payments, FX, payouts and supplier or operational payments.

- Educate customers on relevant Airwallex products and features, and proactively drive activation, usage and ongoing adoption.

- Work closely with Sales, Product, Operations, Marketing, Strategy, Risk and Compliance teams to resolve customer issues and deliver solutions through to completion.

- Monitor transaction volumes, product usage and agreed success metrics after launch. Investigate gaps between forecast and actual adoption, and take action to improve realised customer value.

- Identify churn risks early and coordinate the appropriate commercial, product or operational response.

- Maintain accurate account plans, pipeline, forecasts, activation milestones and customer notes in Salesforce or other CRM tools.

- Use data, analytics, automation and AI-enabled tools to improve customer preparation, follow-ups, account reviews and portfolio prioritisation.

Кто вы

We're looking for people who meet the minimum requirements for this role. The preferred qualifications are great to have, but are not mandatory.

Minimum qualifications

- > 4 years of experience in account management, relationship management, customer success, sales or a similar commercial role.

- A demonstrated track record of driving revenue growth, expansion or wallet share within existing accounts.

- Strong commercial drive and motivation to identify and pursue growth opportunities.

- Confidence engaging directly with customers and business stakeholders to understand their needs and recommend relevant solutions.

- Ability to build meaningful relationships with customers across different functions and levels of seniority.

- Consultative selling and discovery skills, with the ability to understand a customer’s business model and connect their challenges to commercial opportunities.

- Strong verbal and written communication skills in English.

- Strong problem-solving skills, with the ability to identify customer issues, coordinate internal stakeholders and drive solutions through to resolution.
