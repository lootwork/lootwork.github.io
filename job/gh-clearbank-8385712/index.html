CLEAR is building THE secure identity company of the future. Our mission is to make experiences safer and easier—physically and digitally. With more than 43 million Members and a growing network of partners across the world, CLEAR's secure identity platform is transforming the way people live, work, and travel. Whether it’s at the airport, stadium, or throughout your everyday life, CLEAR unlocks the magic of frictionless experiences.

The Manager, Marketing Operations owns the systems, data, and processes that run CLEAR1’s marketing engine, turning campaign ideas into clean technical builds, reliable numbers, and dashboards people trust. Reporting to the Director, Demand Generation, you’ll lead CLEAR1’s shift to an account-based model and partner with Revenue Operations, Sales, Finance, and Legal to keep campaigns, data, and spend tied to revenue goals.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the foundation of CLEAR1’s marketing attribution, including tracking standards, UTM and naming governance, and the integrations that pass campaign, web, and ad data into HubSpot and Salesforce
  • Build and launch campaigns in HubSpot, including email, nurture, forms, workflows, lifecycle stages, and lead scoring, with clean data behind every launch
  • Drive CLEAR1’s shift to account-based marketing by partnering with Revenue Operations on ideal customer profile, account tiers, and routing, and by using intent data from tools like 6sense to prioritize accounts and trigger follow-up
  • Build dashboards that show spend, pipeline, conversion, and attribution by channel, campaign, and account, and turn them into recommendations for marketing, Sales, and executive leadership
  • Own the marketing tech stack and spend tracking, including integrations, renewals, and new tool evaluations, and partner with Finance on budgets and with Legal on contracts and compliance

How you'll measure success

  • Campaigns launched on schedule with accurate tracking, naming, and lead routing from day one
  • Improvement in cost per opportunity, pipeline per dollar spent, funnel conversion rates, and speed of lead follow-up
  • Trusted attribution of sourced and influenced pipeline by channel, campaign, and account, backed by clean HubSpot and Salesforce data
  • Increased reach and engagement with the priority accounts set by RevOps and Sales

What you're great at

  • 5+ years in B2B marketing operations or revenue operations, ideally in SaaS or technology
  • Expert hands-on HubSpot administration, plus working knowledge of Salesforce
  • Marketing attribution, UTM governance, and funnel and pipeline reporting
  • Account-based marketing with intent tools like 6sense
  • Evaluating, selecting, and implementing new marketing technology, including integrations and vendor management
  • Turning data into dashboards and clear insights for non-technical audiences
  • A detail-oriented problem solver who enjoys complex challenges, with strong project management and communication skills
