Scopely is looking for a Technical Artist to join our Yahtzee! With Buddies team in our Barcelona studio working on a hybrid model, 3 days a week from the hub.

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing, and innovating the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people worldwide daily.

Yahtzee! With Buddies is one of our casual free-to-play mobile games and is a classic board game with a new look. The team is based in Europe and the US, and works every day to create captivating new experiences for our players.

Что предстоит делать

Our TechArt & Tools team is currently composed of two people in the Barcelona office. We are looking to have a new Technical Artist join them to design, implement, and drive adoption of an end-to-end content pipeline that supports the live operations, design team, UI team, and 2D/3D artists.

  • Knowledgeable in HLSL and built-in render pipeline.
  • Great at implementing UI that scales to different mobile devices and supporting artists with UI implementation for when they do it
  • Collaborate closely with both art and engineering teams to optimize workflows and tools for creatives.
  • Define and promote technical best practices for content creation.
  • Own and maintain the technical budget of in-game assets, ensuring they are gameplay-ready and performance-optimized.

What you will need

  • 3+ years of experience as a Technical Artist in the entertainment industry (mobile experience preferred).
  • Good scripting and coding skills (e.g., Python, C#).
  • Well-versed in using Unity
  • Solid understanding of computer graphics fundamentals.
  • Familiarity with digital content creation tools (e.g., Blender, Photoshop, Figma).
  • Experience using version control and asset management tools (e.g., Git).
  • Strong problem-solving skills and a proactive approach to troubleshooting.
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with an ability to bridge art and engineering disciplines.
  • Great at documenting and presenting technical art best practices

Дополнительный плюс

  • Experience with real-time VFX.
  • Understanding of animation pipelines.
  • You're great at profiling on the device

Please include a portfolio or PDF/links to examples of your work in the application.

О Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others.
