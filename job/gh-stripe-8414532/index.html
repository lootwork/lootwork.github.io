О Stripe

Stripe, LLC. is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

Что предстоит делать

Обязанности

  • Expand the service platform’s asynchronous execution capabilities and make asynchronous systems easy to build, resilient, and scalable.
  • Improve productivity of the engineers using our systems and work towards ensuring a thoroughly considered developer experience.
  • Work with a team of technically sophisticated engineers to solve problems for fellow Stripe engineers by understanding their needs.
  • Use both qualitative and quantitative metrics to determine priorities and measure effectiveness.
  • Deliver practical, useful, and reliable tools, processes, and code refactorings to Stripe engineers.
  • Design and build client SDKs for developers all over Stripe.
  • Improve, deploy, scale, and manage services and databases.
  • Contribute to open source frameworks such as Temporal.
