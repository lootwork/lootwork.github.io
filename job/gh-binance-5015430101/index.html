О роли

Binance's Sanctions Compliance team is looking for a seasoned Financial Crime Compliance professional to lead sanctions and CTF investigations across our global business. The role suits a senior professional who is comfortable with ambiguity, takes ownership of complex issues, and drives them to a defensible resolution without waiting for direction.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the end to end investigations lifecycle from intake of alerts, referrals, and escalations, through evidence gathering, disposition, and documented closure, applying one consistent evidentiary standard across the team;
  • Applies sanctions regimes operationally, covering blocking, rejection, and reporting obligations;
  • Enforces pipeline discipline through triage and prioritisation rules, SLA and aging management, throughput, and quality metrics;
  • Leads lookbacks and remediation, including retrospective reviews, backlog clearance, root cause analysis on missed or mis-dispositioned cases, and feeding findings back into screening logic and procedures;
  • Brings deep subject matter expertise in sanctions and CTF investigations within a second line of defence environment, including investigative frameworks, evidentiary standards, and quality assurance over dispositions;
  • Manages internal stakeholders across all Compliance functions and more broadly across Binance, providing investigative findings and recommendations that inform how sanctions and CTF related risks are effectively managed;
  • Builds and manages the team through hiring and capacity planning, training and accreditation, calibration on judgement calls, and development of investigators.

Кого мы ищем

  • 10+ years of proven experience in the (international) compliance / financial crime compliance domain;
  • Knowledge of the cryptocurrency and financial institution business, processes, procedures and systems;
  • Fluency in English is required to be able to coordinate with overseas partners and stakeholders. Additional languages would be an advantage.
  • Strong analytical skills and sound judgement;
  • Ability to work across functional and geographic lines;
  • Learning agility and a critical and innovative mindset;
  • Natural authority combined with the ability to inspire, motivate and help others succeed;
  • A people-focused approach, connecting and interacting naturally with others;
  • Strong verbal and written communication skills, able to convey complex messages simply to explain why change is required for effective management of sanctions and CTF risks;
  • Adherence to Binance's values.

Будет плюсом

  • ACAMS or ACFCS certification is preferred.
