WHY THIS POSITION IS CRITICAL TO OUR MISSION

At Pennylane, Product is at the heart of how we create value for our customers.

We are now making a major bet on AI: not only to automate existing workflows, but also to fundamentally change how people interact with their financial software and extend what the product can do.

Our AI ecosystem is being built across several complementary layers

- A shared AI platform, or “harness”, that provides the foundations required to run reliable agents: context, memory, tools, skills, loops, guardrails and observability

- Studio Coworker, a conversational product experience that helps users analyse information and take action inside Pennylane

- Autopilot, which automates accounting and financial workflows to reduce manual data entry

- Studio Builder, the product you will own, enabling users to build and deploy personalised apps, automations and agents on top of Pennylane

Studio Builder is our most ambitious AI product initiative.

The idea is simple but powerful: give customers the ability to extend Pennylane according to their own needs, without requiring them to build everything from scratch. Users should be able to describe what they want to achieve and rely on AI to create, configure and deploy the right experience directly within Pennylane.

This could unlock a new way to use financial software: more personalised, more extensible and increasingly capable of adapting to every company’s specific workflows.

As Senior Product Manager, AI, you will shape this product from the ground up. You will define the vision, understand where AI can create genuine customer value and turn a highly complex technical ambition into a simple, useful and scalable product experience.

You will join one of the key teams shaping the future of Pennylane.

WHAT YOU WILL OWN

THE VISION AND STRATEGY FOR STUDIO BUILDER

You will define how Studio Builder should help customers create, personalise and extend Pennylane.

You will identify the most valuable use cases, understand where customers struggle today and establish a clear product strategy that balances ambition, usability and technical feasibility.

THE END-TO-END BUILDING EXPERIENCE

You will own the experience through which customers

- Define what they want to build

- Configure the relevant data, context, tools and actions

- Test and iterate on their app, automation or agent

- Deploy it within Pennylane

- Monitor its usage and improve it over time

THE FOUNDATIONS OF AN EXTENSIBLE AI PRODUCT

You will work closely with the AI platform team to ensure that Studio Builder makes the underlying capabilities of Pennylane accessible and useful to customers.

This includes thinking deeply about how users interact with

- Context and memory

- Tools and skills

- Agent loops and workflows

- Guardrails and permissions

- Reliability and observability

- Deployment and iteration

A CLEAR PRODUCT OUTCOME

You will define the metrics that matter and establish how we measure success.

Depending on the stage of the product, this may include

- Activation and adoption of Studio Builder

- Number and quality of apps, automations or agents created

- Successful deployment and repeated usage

- Customer value generated through personalised workflows

- Reliability, trust and user satisfaction

- Expansion of Pennylane’s product capabilities through customer-built experiences

HOW YOU WILL CONTRIBUTE AS A PRODUCT MANAGER

You will work in a highly collaborative environment with Engineering, Product Design, AI specialists and other Product Managers.

Вам предстоит

- Lead continuous discovery with SMEs, accountants and internal users

- Turn complex customer problems into clear product opportunities

- Define the product vision, strategy, roadmap and OKRs

- Explore and validate new ideas through prototypes, experiments and MVPs

- Work closely with engineers and designers from problem discovery through implementation and iteration

- Make AI concepts understandable and valuable for non-technical users

- Build strong product narratives and communicate them clearly to customers, stakeholders and leadership

- Balance long-term product ambition with pragmatic, incremental delivery

- Collaborate with the teams building the shared AI platform, Studio Coworker and Autopilot

- Develop strong product principles for building AI-powered experiences at Pennylane

- Measure the impact of what you ship and use those learnings to guide future decisions

- Influence without authority across a complex, fast-growing organisation

This is a hands-on product role. You will not only define what should be built — you will actively explore, prototype, test and help make the product tangible.…
