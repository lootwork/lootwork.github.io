О роли

Zilch's DevOps team is a small team with a large surface area: four engineers supporting an engineering organisation of over a hundred. The team's mission is to make engineering at Zilch faster, safer, and less painful, by building tooling and automation that lets product teams focus on shipping rather than fighting their environment.

We're looking for a software engineer who cares deeply about developer experience. Someone who's spent enough time building products to know where the friction is (slow feedback loops, flaky pipelines, manual toil, poor tooling) and who wants to fix those problems properly. Not with quick patches, but with well-built internal tools that engineers actually want to use.

You'll own problems end-to-end: understanding what's slowing engineers down, deciding what to build, building it, and driving adoption. The role blends product thinking with hands-on engineering; you'll shape what the team works on as much as you'll write the code.

One example of what this team builds: our ephemeral environments platform, which spins up full, sandboxed copies of the Zilch stack on demand for development, debugging, and automated testing. It's integrated into the PR workflow, backed by its own orchestration engine, and has fundamentally changed how engineers work here. You'd be working with and extending capabilities like this.

This is a role for someone who wants to shape how an engineering organisation works; not just keep the lights on.

Что предстоит делать

- Identify the biggest sources of developer friction across the organisation and turn them into well-scoped improvements

- Design and build internal tools, automation, and self-service capabilities that make engineers' lives measurably better

- Own adoption: build things that solve real problems and communicate them well enough that teams actually use them

- Work across engineering to understand workflows, pain points, and opportunities; act as an advocate for developer experience in technical decisions

- Raise the bar within the team: through code quality, design thinking, and setting standards for how the team approaches its work

- Contribute to the team's support rotation, and use that exposure to inform what the team builds next

Кого мы ищем

- Strong software engineering fundamentals. You write clean, well-structured code and care about maintainability, testing, and good API design

- Product-minded. You think about who's using what you build, what problem it solves, and whether people will actually adopt it. You'd rather build the right thing than the most technically interesting thing

- Empathy for developer experience. You've been the engineer fighting the tooling, and you want to be the one who fixes it

- Proactive and entrepreneurial. You see gaps and opportunities and move on them without waiting for permission; you make the case for what to build rather than waiting for it to be assigned

- Technically credible. You can hold your own in architectural discussions with experienced infrastructure engineers

- Familiarity with infrastructure tooling: Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD pipelines, cloud platforms (AWS)

- Organisational awareness. You know how to navigate a medium-to-large engineering org to get things built and adopted; shipping good tooling is as much about communication as it is about code

- Comfortable representing your team's work and priorities to senior stakeholders

Bonus skills

- Experience with GitOps delivery patterns, ideally with ArgoCD

- Observability platform experience, particularly OpenTelemetry

- Experience with other parts of our stack: GitHub Actions, Crossplane, Spacelift, Go, Liquibase

- Experience working in or with distributed teams across multiple locations

- Scale-up experience; we're growing fast and the landscape shifts with it
