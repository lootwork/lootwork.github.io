Who is SkyBox Labs?

Recognized as one of BC's Top Employers (2025), SkyBox Labs is a game development studio that works on some of the world’s most iconic games. The studio is currently working on projects in the Minecraft universe, supporting the dev team at Bungie on Destiny 2, supporting development of Fallout 76, in addition to work on unannounced IP. Since our inception in 2011, SkyBox Labs has evolved from a small team of eight individuals to a fully established team of Engineers, Artists, Animators, Designers, Producers, and more. Come join our hybrid team in our Burnaby, BC studio today!

Interested? Let's talk about what we're hiring for

We're looking for an intermediate C++ Software Engineer to join our Minecraft Networking / Core Systems team. This isn't a traditional network engineering role; the team works across a broad range of core engine systems, platform integrations, and development infrastructure, with a focus on the foundational technology that supports Minecraft across console, PC, and mobile platforms.

In this role, you'll work on low-level C++ systems that support the performance, stability, and reliability of the game. Areas of focus include file system I/O, multithreading and asynchronous systems, platform integrations, and improvements to existing engine architecture and development infrastructure.

You'll work in a high-ownership environment, taking technical problems from investigation through implementation. This means working from broader requirements, navigating complex codebases, collaborating with engineers across teams, and developing the ability to independently investigate and resolve technical challenges.

A SNAPSHOT OF YOUR ROLE

  • Core Systems & File I/O: Develop, maintain, and improve foundational engine systems, with a focus on file systems, data handling, I/O, and related infrastructure.
  • Concurrency & Performance: Work with multithreaded and asynchronous systems, including thread management and job scheduling. Investigate and resolve performance and concurrency issues.
  • Networking & Communication Systems: Work with a modern networking stack built on WebRTC (SDP, ICE, STUN/TURN NAT traversal, DTLS, SCTP), JSON-RPC, and serialization formats.
  • Platform Integration: Support development across console, PC, and mobile platforms, working with platform APIs, SDKs, and technical requirements.
  • System Design & Reliability: Contribute to the development of resilient, low-latency systems, with consideration for security best practices, observability, maintainability, and performance.
  • Codebase & Build Infrastructure: Improve and refactor existing systems, contribute to codebase modernization, and support build configurations, dependencies, and developer tooling.
  • Testing & Automation: Help improve automated testing and development infrastructure to support reliable builds, cross-platform quality, and efficient development workflows.
  • Cross-Team Technical Support: Collaborate with engineers across disciplines and contribute to broader technical needs, which may include multiplayer systems, serialization, telemetry, and partner integrations.
  • Continuous Improvement: Identify opportunities to improve engineering practices, tooling, and workflows, including the practical use of AI-assisted development tools.

SOUND LIKE YOU? THIS IS WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR

  • 3-5+ years of professional software engineering experience in a similar role, preferably within the games industry.
  • Strong practical experience with C++ and a good understanding of modern C++ development.
  • Working knowledge of low-level software engineering concepts, including memory management, file systems, file I/O, and concurrency.
  • Strong debugging skills and experience investigating and resolving bugs across established codebases.
  • Experience working with complex software systems and navigating existing codebases.
  • Good problem-solving skills, with the ability to independently investigate and resolve technical issues.
  • Comfortable working with technical documentation and implementing solutions based on platform or system requirements.
  • Experience with AI-assisted development tools and an interest in incorporating them into everyday engineering workflows.
  • Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to take ownership of assigned work and work effectively across teams.

Nice-to-haves

  • Experience developing or shipping games or other real-time applications across console, PC, or mobile platforms.
  • Familiarity with first-party platform SDKs, platform integrations, or console development requirements.
  • Experience with build systems, dependency management, or build optimization tools, such as CMake.
  • Exposure to automated testing, CI/CD pipelines, or development infrastructure.
  • Familiarity with game networking, serialization, or live-service systems. Deep networking specialization is not required.…
