О VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 1000 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publishers worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

Команда

Our Growth team is a transversal entity aiming to maximize and deliver the potential of any mobile product by leveraging performance marketing. It combines user acquisition, creative content production, monetization, and the tech capabilities required to turn applications into global hits. With over 150 experts, we operate at the forefront of mobile marketing and ad tech across various types of games (hybrid and casual).

Роль

Mobile gaming is a $100B+ market, with over 3 billion players worldwide and more revenue than console and PC combined. The marketing operations behind the biggest games, acquiring users at scale, optimizing monetization, iterating on creative, are among the most data-intensive in the industry.

As a Growth Associate, you'll work directly on products with big potential to become iconic (games or consumer apps), alongside Performance Marketing experts and getting hands-on with campaigns, creative, data and real business challenges from day one.

- Manage and optimize paid campaigns across Meta, Google, TikTok, and more.

- Run bid, audience, and creative tests, develop a sharp instinct for what hooks work and why, and scale what performs. Performance Marketing Managers at Voodoo are hands-on across the full creative process, from ideation to iteration, not just media buying.

A Growth Associate role provides structured entry into the Growth team. Succeeding here opens clear paths into expert-level or leadership positions, depending on your direction and performance.

If you thrive on challenges and want to join a fast-paced company that’s changing the future of mobile games and apps, now is your chance!

This opportunity starts as a final-year internship, and converts to a permanent contract based on performance.

Профиль кандидата

- Final-year student from a top engineering or business school (Programme Grande Ecole), looking for your end-of-study internship for January 2027

- Clear communicator, fluent both in English and French

- Strong analytical mindset; comfortable digging into data to make decisions

- Genuine interest in mobile apps, games, or performance marketing

- Autonomous and proactive; you'd rather figure things out than wait to be told
