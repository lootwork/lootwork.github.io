We're looking for talented and motivated iOS Developers to join our Apple Design Award winning team to help expand Robinhood's mobile experience.

As a Software Developer Intern, iOS, you'll build smooth, stable, and beautiful products used by millions of people every day. You'll own features end-to-end — from inception through design feedback, implementation, and launch — working directly with experienced developers on one of the most recognized mobile teams in fintech.

This role is based in our Toronto, ON office with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build smooth, stable, and beautiful products using Swift and the latest iOS and watchOS APIs
  • Own features from inception, through design feedback, implementation, and launch
  • Collaborate with Product Designers, Product Managers, Backend Developers, and Data Developers
  • Work with technologies such as UIKit, Auto Layout, RxSwift, Core Graphics, Core Animation, Lottie, Core Data, and a VIPER-esque Architecture

Что вы приносите

  • Currently enrolled in a full-time, degree-seeking program with an expected graduation date of Winter 2027 or Spring 2028
  • Strong product sense and experience working closely with Product Designers
  • Deep understanding of UIKit
  • Excellent communication skills and a passion for tackling tough technical problems
