The Team & The Role

We’re looking for a user-focused Android Engineer to join our engineering team and play a key role in building Plum on Android from its early days, giving our current and prospective customers a more intimate way to interact with their money.

This role is perfect for someone who values good engineering principles, imagination, quirkiness and healthy curiosity. We take a conservative approach to using external frameworks and we aspire to write simple, well-tested code, delivered in rapid iterations.

How We Work

We work in "squads" – a more startup-appropriate name for saying small, cross-functional teams. Each squad has a mission and a set of KPIs to monitor and improve. As an engineer you'll be working in a squad, but you can also reach out to and work with anyone in the company. You will also be part of a Mobile engineering team, consisting of Mobile Product Engineers who share the same vision and passion of building an exceptional mobile team that will help people be financially better off.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build a product that makes people better off
  • Build with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, leveraging your knowledge of the full Android stack and modern architecture patterns
  • Collaborate on a cutting-edge Android project
  • Work closely with design to turn ideas into well-crafted and delightful experiences
  • Think like a product person and understand the value and impact of your work

What We Look For

  • Solid experience with Kotlin programming language
  • Hands-on experience with DI and Android architecture components
  • Sound understanding of Git strategies, continuous integration setup, and automation
  • Experience with unit testing
  • An eye for design that you can transform into Material Design!
  • A genuine interest in agile development processes
  • Solid experience working with remote data via REST and JSON

We offer a compensation package consisting of base salary and equity, with compensation reviewed twice a year as part of our performance and growth framework.
