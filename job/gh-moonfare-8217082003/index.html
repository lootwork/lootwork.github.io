Investor Relations Analyst

Join the team rewriting the rules in private markets.

Moonfare delivers what few others can: the highly sought-after funds and hidden-gem investments that go beyond what most private banks offer. Every opportunity is subjected to a ruthless vetting process; the bar is unforgivingly high. The result? Institutional-quality portfolios for investors who demand more.

Our team combines finance veterans with talent from tech, consulting, law and industries you’d never expect. Headquartered in Berlin, we operate from eight offices across Europe, the US and Asia.

If you're ready to build what's next in private markets, let's talk.

О роли

Moonfare is experiencing exceptional growth in the North American market. This is a salaried, early-career position with a performance bonus - not commission-only - designed to build your foundation in private markets, client relations, and commercial execution. You will join a lean, fast-growing U.S. desk in New York, working directly alongside and reporting to the Head of U.S. Private Clients. In this high-visibility role, you will support existing client relationships and a pipeline of qualified leads while gradually taking ownership of your own book of investors.

Вам предстоит

  • Support the management and growth of relationships with key investment clients, ensuring exceptional service and engagement
  • Contribute to business development efforts, including outreach to prospective investors and support on new relationship origination
  • Take a proactive approach to investor portfolio management, including portfolio reviews
  • Handle client objections effectively, addressing concerns and providing tailored solutions
  • Conduct in-depth research on clients, industries, and markets to support strategic decision-making
  • Support private equity fundraising efforts alongside one of the fastest-growing companies in a high-growth sector
  • Prepare client reports, financial analyses, and presentations to strengthen relationship-building efforts
  • Identify opportunities to enhance efficiency and improve internal processes
  • Collaborate with internal teams (e.g. Account Management, Marketing, Product) to optimize client engagement
  • Contribute to a collaborative and high-energy team environment, continuously learning and sharing knowledge
  • Gradually take ownership of client relationships and build a portfolio of investors

The next Moonfarian

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Finance, Business, Economics, or a related field
  • Minimum of 1 years of experience in finance, investment management, private markets, wealth management, or a related area
  • FINRA Series 7 and Series 63 required
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English
  • Excellent ability to prioritise and manage multiple tasks in a fast-paced environment
  • Entrepreneurial mindset with a hands-on approach and a willingness to learn

If you feel you do not meet all of the requirements, we still encourage you to apply!

Our Commitment to a Rigorous & Fair Assessment

We believe in a transparent and comprehensive evaluation process designed to understand your unique skills and potential. Our approach is a two-way street: it allows us to see if you are the right fit for Moonfare, and just as importantly, it allows you to determine if we are the right fit for you.

Our process consists of the following stages

Screening

  • Talent Acquisition Interview (Virtual): An initial conversation to discuss your experience and motivations, and for you to learn more about the role, the team, and our mission.
  • Aptitude Assessment: A short, standardized assessment to understand your verbal, numerical, and abstract reasoning abilities, giving us insight into your problem-solving capabilities.

Hiring Manager Interview

  • Hiring Manager Interview (On-site): A conversation with the Head of U.S. Private Clients about your client experience, how you build and grow investor relationships, and what you would bring to a growing U.S. desk.

Team Interviews

  • Case Study Interview: This is your opportunity to showcase your skills. You will be presented with a real-world challenge relevant to the role, allowing us to see your thought process, skills, and approach in action. You’ll get to see our beautiful office space and meet more of the team.

Final Interview

  • Leadership Interview: A final conversation focused on our leadership principles, ensuring you will thrive in our high-performance culture.

The salary range for this role is $76500–$85000. Moonfare is committed to equitable compensation. Our model is built around a base salary and a performance-related bonus component, alongside the benefits above, and we set our ranges from independent market data that we review every year - so they reflect the genuine market rate for the work. Where you join within the range reflects the experience you bring relative to the role, with real room to grow: we’re performance-first, so your impact is what moves your pay. As a commercial role, it also carries a performance-related commercial bonus measured against on-target earnings, in addition to base salary. We never ask about your current or previous salary at any stage of the process. For more information about Moonfare’s compensation, please speak to your Talent Acquisition Partner.
