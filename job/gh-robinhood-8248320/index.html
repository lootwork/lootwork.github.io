The Options Web team builds the web experiences that let customers trade options on Robinhood. The team works across Robinhood.com and Legend, our web trading platform for active traders. Its work covers trading flows, options onboarding for new customers, and monitoring tools. On Legend, the team builds the options pieces, including options details in the positions widget and the Option Chain tool. You'll work closely with product managers, designers, and the mobile options team, and you'll see your work reach customers fast!

This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA office, with in-person hybrid attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Act as tech lead for options on web, setting direction on platform topics such as performance and observability
  • Design, build, test, and roll out features in React and TypeScript across trading flows, options onboarding, and monitoring tools
  • Own projects end to end, from options team projects to large efforts that involve cross-team collaboration.
  • Break larger projects into smaller pieces and guide other engineers as they build them
  • Diagnose complex performance problems and data issues, including options calculations, find the root cause, and set up monitoring to catch problems early
  • Partner with product managers and designers (and content designers when needed) to shape and ship customer-facing features
  • Work with the mobile options team so options features feel consistent across platforms

Что вы приносите

  • 7+ years of professional software development experience.
  • Strong web engineering fundamentals, with hands-on experience building production web applications in React and TypeScript
  • Strong problem-solving and debugging skills, with a record of finding root causes in performance and data problems
  • Experience owning projects end to end, from design through development, testing, and rollout
  • Experience leading projects and setting technical direction, such as improving performance or observability
  • Clear communication about technical tradeoffs with engineers, product managers, and designers
  • Nice to have: familiarity with options trading or other fintech and trading products (no options experience is required, and we'll teach you the domain)
  • Nice to have: prior tech lead experience, including at smaller companies
