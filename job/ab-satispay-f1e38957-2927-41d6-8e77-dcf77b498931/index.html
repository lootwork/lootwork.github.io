О нас

Satispay began by rethinking the simple act of a payment to remove the friction from our daily routines. But we didn't stop there. Today, we are building a complete financial platform designed to empower people and concretely improve their lives. By giving our 6 million users a clear, open path to pay, save, and invest, we are evolving into the definitive destination for every financial need.

Что предстоит делать

As a Software Engineer III, Backend in the Consumer Growth Team, you’ll play a key role in shaping the future of digital payments by designing and building scalable features that drive user growth and reduce friction for our community. Here's what your day-to-day will look like:

- Own the Story Lifecycle: Take full ownership of the end-to-end lifecycle—from technical design to production release—ensuring that product stories are robust and scalable.

- Accelerate Activation & Trust: Build and optimise high-reliability systems for onboarding, document verification, and security flows to ensure users can start transacting safely in minutes.

- Drive Engagement & Discovery: Enhance the app experience by developing core backend services for shop discovery and personalised consumer journeys.

- Scale Loyalty & Monetisation: Build the technical foundation for user retention, contributing to "Plus" subscription plans, referral programs, and loyalty mechanics.

- Master a Modern Tech Stack: Take ownership of our AWS-based infrastructure and participate in architectural improvements across our growth-focused microservices.

- Work in an AI-Powered Environment: Leverage AI tools daily to write and optimise code, speed up root cause analysis, and generate documentation to raise engineering efficiency.

- Ensure Quality and Stability: Conduct technical analysis, accurately estimate effort, and participate in incident resolution to ensure system health.

- Collaborate & Document: Create comprehensive technical documentation and collaborate cross-functionally with Product and stakeholders to align with the roadmap.

Who we're looking for

We need a problem-solver who loves teamwork and gets things done. If you're curious and ready for real ownership, you'll fit in! Does this sound like you?

- Experience: You have at least 3 years of experience as a Backend Engineer, with a strong track record of building and maintaining scalable microservices.

- Technical Foundations: Solid expertise in Java and Spring Boot, with a good understanding of relational databases (PostgreSQL) and cloud environments (AWS is a plus).

- Growth Mindset: You care about performance and user experience, understanding how technical improvements in onboarding or loyalty flows impact millions of users.

- Reliability & Documentation: You are structured in your approach, enjoy writing clear technical documentation, and focus on building maintainable, high-quality code.

- Teamwork and Communication: You excel at cross-functional collaboration and can communicate technical concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical peers.

- Language proficiency: You are passionate about FinTech, user growth, or startup environments and are fluent in English.
