Product Manager, Global Self-Serve Product

О Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

О команде

The Global Self-Serve Product team's goal is to make Stripe the #1 self-serve financial infrastructure provider for users outside the U.S. We drive product-led growth for the millions of users who adopt Stripe without a sales relationship, and we own self-serve activation goals across our ex-US business.

We're a cross-functional team of Engineers, Data Scientists, Growth Marketing Managers, Designers, and Product Managers, organized around three focus areas:

  • Growing our self-serve domestic business across a portfolio of our core, mature markets
  • Growing cross-border self-serve business across the next wave of emerging markets and launching bespoke registration, onboarding and upsell pathways for new markets
  • Raising the bar on the quality of the localized experience of our international users by building and improving our localization platform at scale

О роли

Most of Stripe's future users outside the US will never speak to a salesperson—they'll discover, sign up, and scale on Stripe entirely on their own. Getting that self-serve journey right, in every market and language, is one of the biggest growth opportunities ahead of us.

We're looking for a Product Manager to own self-serve activation and growth across our ex-US business. You'll set the strategy for how we win self-serve users in international markets and then make it real—turning funnel data and customer insight into a sequenced roadmap, and shipping the experiments and features that move the metrics.

The right person for this role thinks like a growth PM and a founder: you're fluent in funnels and experimentation, you have genuine taste for 0-to-1 product, and you move fast under ambiguity. You bring deep user empathy to localizing Stripe with contextual relevance, and you think in systems and platforms—turning experimental features into scaled growth levers

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the self-serve growth roadmap: identify the highest-leverage opportunities to grow activation across our ex-US markets, and build the case for investment
  • Build a deep understanding of our customers—from bootstrapped solopreneurs to well-funded founders—synthesizing funnel metrics, experiment results, and time in the product into sharp hypotheses about what will drive growth
  • Partner with engineering to ship—you'll drive specs, prioritization, and execution with a high degree of hands-on involvement, and run experiments to build conviction in your roadmap
  • Scale what works: take wins proven in small samples and roll them out through our localization and recommendation platforms, so the right experience reaches each market and use case
  • Collaborate with Stripe's global product teams to ensure regional needs are reflected in platform decisions, and advocate for regional investment at the right moments

Кто вы

  • You have 7+ years of product management experience, with meaningful time spent shipping 0-to-1 products
  • You reason from first principles—breaking ambiguous growth problems down to fundamentals, forming a sharp point of view, and moving fast under uncertainty with the ownership of a founder
  • You're data-savvy: fluent in funnels, metrics, and experimentation, you let the numbers sharpen your instincts, and you know when a test earns conviction and when it only adds noise
  • You have strong product taste and are technically comfortable—you sweat the details of a localized experience (message clarity, contextual guidance) and can engage credibly with engineers on API design and system tradeoffs
  • You're an exceptional communicator—verbal and written—who can influence without authority across a matrixed organization
  • You're based in Singapore or have a strong reason to be

Why Stripe

Stripe is still early in its presence outside the US, which means the person in this role will have genuine influence over how we show up in some of the world's most important emerging markets. You'll have the scope of a startup with the resources of a company that processes hundreds of billions of dollars a year.
