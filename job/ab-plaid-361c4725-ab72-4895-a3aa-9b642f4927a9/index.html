We believe that the way people interact with their finances will drastically improve in the next few years. We’re dedicated to empowering this transformation by building the tools and experiences that thousands of developers use to create their own products. Plaid powers the tools millions of people rely on to live a healthier financial life. We work with thousands of companies like Venmo, SoFi, several of the Fortune 500, and many of the largest banks to make it easy for people to connect their financial accounts to the apps and services they want to use. Plaid’s network covers 12,000 financial institutions across the US, Canada, UK and Europe. Founded in 2013, the company is headquartered in San Francisco with offices in New York, Seattle, Washington D.C., Raleigh, London, and Amsterdam.

We are the Fraud Data Science team within Plaid’s Fraud organization. We leverage Plaid’s extensive financial network data to uncover fraud patterns, measure product performance, and develop innovative data products that help customers better understand and mitigate risk. Our team works closely with customers and Go-to-Market (GTM) partners to demonstrate product value, identify opportunities for improvement, and translate data-driven insights into actionable solutions. We collaborate across Product, Engineering, and Fraud ML & AI to bring new capabilities to market and continuously advance Plaid’s fraud prevention solutions.

As a Staff Data Scientist, you will lead the development of new Fraud data products and improve existing ones. You will identify customer needs, build and validate prototypes, and work with Product and Engineering to launch products and measure adoption.

You will stay hands-on in analysis, experimentation, and prototyping while setting technical direction and guiding other data scientists. You will also lead fraud investigations, uncover emerging patterns, and work with partners and customers to act on the findings.

Обязанности

- Building new Fraud data products, from customer discovery and prototyping through launch and adoption.

- Turning findings from customer retrospectives and proofs of concept into features and tools that other customers can use.

- Using product metrics, experiments, and model evaluations to identify improvements in fraud detection and guide investment decisions.

- Leading fraud investigations with network and customer data, uncovering new patterns, and turning findings into actions that reduce fraud and improve products.

- Leading complex initiatives with Product, Engineering, Fraud ML & AI, and GTM.

- Raising technical quality through mentorship, analytical reviews, and reusable data and evaluation tools.

Требования

- 10+ years of relevant data science, product analytics, or applied quantitative experience, with demonstrated Staff-level technical leadership and product delivery.

- Fraud or risk domain expertise and demonstrated ability to lead fraud investigations, including framing hypotheses, analyzing suspicious activity, quantifying impact, and driving follow-up actions with partners.

- Experience supporting customers or GTM teams through analyses, retrospectives, proofs of concept, or assessments of product value.

- Strong SQL and Python skills for hands-on analysis and development.

- Experience defining product metrics and building dashboards and reporting that teams use to make decisions.

- Demonstrated experience with product analytics and experimental evaluation, such as A/B tests, backtests, or comparable methods.

- Ability to design and interpret backtests or offline evaluations for ML or rules-based systems, including limitations in the data and implications for customer decisions.

- Experience working with large, messy datasets and defining appropriate analytical data models.

- Clear communication and stakeholder management, including explaining complex analyses, agreeing on priorities, and guiding decisions with technical and nontechnical partners.

- A record of leading ambiguous, multi-quarter technical initiatives, aligning cross-functional partners, and improving other data scientists’ work through mentorship and review.

- Experience working with Product and Engineering to take a new feature or product from problem definition and prototyping through launch, then measure adoption.

Preferred experience

- Experience developing customer-facing data and analytics products or ML models, using causal inference, or applying graph analysis to fraud problems.

- Experience with dbt or similar transformation tools, or AI-assisted analytics and development workflows.
