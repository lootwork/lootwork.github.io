Кто мы

О Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies - from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups - use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

О команде

The Executive Content & Experiences team builds trusted relationships with leaders shaping the global economy. We design high-impact programs—including executive briefings, advisory boards, flagship convenings at Davos, and executive programming at Stripe Sessions—to bring Stripe's strategy to life. Beyond delivering premier high-touch moments, we turn leadership insights into actionable intelligence that directly informs Stripe’s narratives, product, and go-to-market direction.

Что предстоит делать

We are looking for a rock star product marketer to translate Stripe’s most complex technology and strategy into compelling, executive-ready narratives and scalable sales motions. In this role, you will lead the content strategy and enablement engine that powers our highest-stakes executive moments—from global Executive Briefing Centers (EBCs) to bespoke leadership workshops. Sitting at the intersection of Product, Sales, and Executive Engagement, you will equip Stripe leaders, account teams, and event hosts to clearly articulate Stripe as the complete financial infrastructure for the internet.

Обязанности

  • Develop Thought Leadership Narratives: Craft tailored, point-of-view-driven narratives and presentations for executive-level meetings, advisory boards, and Tier-1 convenings.
  • Drive Executive Briefing Center Enablement: Develop and scale sales enablement frameworks, messaging toolkits, and prep materials to empower account teams to lead impactful executive briefings.
  • Build Scalable Workshop Motions: Architect and scale interactive whiteboarding and workshop frameworks that simplify complex product architectures and showcase Stripe as a complete financial infrastructure.
  • Curate Executive Demo Library: Build, maintain, and package an executive-ready demo library—repurposing existing product assets into high-touch formats designed specifically for executive experiences.
  • Lead Speaker Training & Certification: Create and scale a formal speaker training and certification program to prepare Stripe executives and technical subject matter experts for high-profile speaking roles and EBC engagements.
  • Scale Content via AI: Design and deploy AI agents and workflows to automate content generation, personalize executive materials, and streamline enablement at scale.

Кто вы

A crisp storyteller, strategic thinker, and enablement innovator. You are a sharp, driven problem-solver with a relentless bias for action—someone who isn't afraid to get scrappy and is energized by building new capabilities for growing teams. You excel at taking deep technical complexity—like global payments infrastructure and financial software—and turning it into elegant, high-impact stories for C-suite audiences. You understand how executive sales motions work and know how to build repeatable systems, scalable tools, and AI-driven workflows that elevate an entire organization’s narrative capabilities.

Minimum requirements

  • 10+ years of product marketing, solutions marketing, or executive enablement experience in B2B tech, SaaS, or fintech.
  • Proven track record building executive-level narratives and high-touch customer workshop motions.
  • Demonstrated experience designing and executing sales enablement programs, speaker training, or certification frameworks.
  • Excellent written, verbal, and visual communication skills, with extensive experience presenting to and prepping C-suite leaders.
  • Demonstrated success working cross-functionally across Product, Sales, and Field Marketing teams.
  • Bachelor's degree
