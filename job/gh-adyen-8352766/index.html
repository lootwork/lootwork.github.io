Senior Partner Solution Engineer - Device Assurance

As a Senior Partner Solution Engineer within Hardware Partner Engineering, you will help to ensure that payment terminals supplied by our hardware partners meet Adyen's technical, reliability and quality expectations throughout their lifecycle.

You will own the system assurance capability for devices: translating product needs into clear technical requirements, defining how those requirements should be validated, and ensuring suppliers provide sufficient evidence to demonstrate compliance.

You will work closely with Product, SW Engineer, Partner Solutions Engineers, OEMs and external testing labs to create a lightweight and scalable technical-assurance model across our hardware partners.

Что предстоит делать

  • Translate product use cases and operational needs into clear, reusable device-level system requirements covering hardware, connectivity, power, diagnostics, environmental behaviour and reliability.
  • Maintain requirements throughout the device lifecycle, incorporating product changes, field learnings and reliability findings.
  • Define validation strategies, acceptance criteria and qualification approaches for new devices and significant changes.
  • Define what evidence should be produced by hardware partners and external laboratories, and review test plans, results and technical evidence for completeness and quality.
  • Challenge gaps in supplier validation, technical claims, RCA and corrective actions, and ensure additional evidence or actions are provided where needed.
  • Support technical assessment and qualification of new devices, suppliers and future RFPs.
  • Assess the technical impact of lifecycle changes and ensure field failures, RCA and corrective actions feed back into requirements and validation expectations.
  • Work across Product, Software Engineering, Partner Solutions Engineering, Integrity, Supply Chain, OEMs and external labs to provide clear technical recommendations, risks and evidence for product-readiness decisions.

Кто вы

  • 7–10+ years of relevant engineering experience in embedded systems, hardware products, systems engineering, product assurance or similar.
  • Strong experience defining and maintaining system-level requirements and translating product use cases into clear technical expectations.
  • Strong experience with verification, validation and qualification, including test strategy, acceptance criteria and technical evidence review.
  • Strong system-level understanding of embedded products across hardware, firmware/OS and software interactions.
  • Experience working technically with hardware manufacturers, OEMs or external engineering suppliers.
  • Able to critically assess supplier test results, technical claims, RCA and corrective actions and identify where evidence is insufficient.
  • Strong engineering judgement and stakeholder-management skills, able to introduce enough structure to make quality predictable without creating unnecessary processes.
  • Proactive, pragmatic and comfortable operating independently with incomplete information in a fast-moving environment where processes are evolving.
