О Paxos

Today’s financial infrastructure is archaic, expensive, inefficient and risky — supporting a system that leaves out more people than it lets in. So we’re rebuilding it.

We’re on a mission to open the world’s financial system to everyone by enabling the instant movement of any asset, any time, in a trustworthy way. For over a decade, we’ve built blockchain infrastructure that tokenizes, custodies, trades and settles assets for the world's leading financial institutions, like PayPal, Venmo, Mastercard, Interactive Brokers and Charles Schwab.

О команде

Paxos Legal is a small, senior team embedded in the business. We advise on regulation, products, partnerships, disputes, and employment across the U.S., Europe, and Asia. We move fast, give clear answers, and build the systems that help the company grow safely.

О роли

As Senior Regulatory Counsel, you will help shape how Paxos grows across jurisdictions. You will analyze laws and regulations in the US and abroad, translating them into clear, actionable guidance that supports new business opportunities in an area of law that is changing quickly. You will create templates, playbooks and other collateral that help Paxos scale internationally, and support the maintenance of our existing licenses and the application processes for new licenses and regulatory approvals.

Что предстоит делать

- Analyze laws and regulations in the US and foreign jurisdictions to support the development of business opportunities, translating regulatory complexity into clear recommendations for the business

- Create templates, playbooks and other collateral that help Paxos scale internationally

- Support the maintenance of existing licenses and manage application processes to obtain new licenses or regulatory approvals

- Partner cross-functionally with Product, Compliance, Engineering and Commercial teams to solve regulatory questions in real time

- Communicate effectively with internal stakeholders, clients and regulators

- Build a longer-term regulatory roadmap and strategy that anticipates where the law is headed

О вас

- Law degree and at least 6 years of legal experience at a law firm, a financial institution, a regulator or a high-growth technology company (ideally some combination)

- Ability to analyze complex and often unsettled regulatory questions, particularly where the law is still developing, and turn them into clear, practical recommendations that non-lawyers can act on

- Experience working cross-functionally with product, compliance, engineering and business teams on multiple projects at once, building strong, trusted relationships along the way

- Ability to meet tight deadlines without sacrificing quality, and comfort handling ambiguity and shifting priorities in a fast-paced, dynamic environment

- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to engage effectively with internal stakeholders, clients and regulators, and to explain complex regulatory issues clearly

- Exceptional attention to detail, especially in regulatory filings, license applications and the policies, templates and playbooks that others will rely on

- Ability to be both reactive and proactive: solving problems in the moment as they arise, while also building a longer-term regulatory roadmap and strategy that anticipates where the law is headed

- Efficient and judicious use of AI tools to improve speed and quality of research, drafting and workflow, with sound judgment about when human review is essential
