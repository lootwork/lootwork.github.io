Senior Data Engineer - Platform

Location: Madrid, Spain (Hybrid: 4 days in office / 1 day remote)

At Ebury, our ambitious strategic growth plan is powered by data. We are seeking a Senior Data Engineer to join our Data Platform team in Madrid.

Our mission is to build, maintain, and continuously evolve Ebury's Data Platform—focusing on scalable, robust, and innovative core frameworks that empower data teams and business units across the entire company.

Что предстоит делать

  • Architect & Scale: Oversee the end-to-end design, development, and maintenance of a central, high-performance Data Platform used across Ebury.
  • Drive Innovation: Explore and integrate emerging technologies and open-source frameworks to expand our data capabilities.
  • Collaborate & Empower: Partner with internal stakeholders (Data Scientists, Analytics Engineers, BI leads) to deeply understand platform requirements and deliver actionable solutions.
  • Govern & Secure: Establish and enforce data governance, quality standards, and access policies to ensure platform security and reliability.

Our Tech Stack

  • Cloud Infrastructure: Google Cloud Platform (GCP) — BigQuery, Cloud Run, Cloud Functions, GKE
  • Orchestration & Modeling: Apache Airflow, dbt, Dimensional & Graph Data Modeling
  • Engineering Tools: Python, SQL, Docker, CI/CD, Git, Open Source Tooling

Кого мы ищем

  • Proven experience designing, building, and operating production data platforms, data lakes, and cloud data warehouses at scale.
  • Advanced proficiency in Python (for data transformation, validation, and tooling) and SQL (for large-scale query optimization).
  • Deep expertise in traditional modeling for both structured and unstructured data. (Graph database modeling is a strong plus!)
  • Hands-on experience with GCP or equivalent cloud ecosystems (BigQuery, serverless compute, containerization).
  • Self-motivated, highly curious, and proactive about staying on the bleeding edge of data architecture and open-source practices.
  • Professional fluency in English. (Spanish is a plus!)
