Job Description

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON

What you will focus on

Love games and have the skills to draw, paint and bring ideas to life? Join Gameloft Saigon's Art team as a 2D Artist. You will create high-quality graphics for our projects and see your work go from first sketch to final in-game integration.

What it means on a daily basis

  • Create high-quality 2D assets that follow the project art direction and keep the art consistent throughout development.
  • Integrate your assets in-game, spot bugs and report them to your Lead or Supervisor.
  • Estimate your tasks and deliver them on time, to the required quality standard.
  • Take on assignments from producers, supervisors and directors, and report your progress regularly.
  • Join brainstorms, idea conception and brief creation.
  • Collaborate closely with the project team, artists and Artist Lead/Supervisor.
  • Share guidance with junior artists on your team.

What success will look like

  • Quality: Meet our quality standards in all your work.
  • Communication: Share ideas and give and receive feedback clearly, so nothing gets lost.
  • Teamwork: Build positive working relationships with colleagues, supervisors and producers.
  • Process: Follow our working procedures correctly.
  • Delivery: Deliver all assets for ongoing projects on time.

Who you would be working with

  • You will be reporting to the Artist Supervisor, Art Producer, and Art Lead/Director.
  • Closely collaborating with other disciplines in the Art team (e.g., 3D Art, Animation, Technical Art, VFX) and other teams in the project (e.g., Game Design, Programming).

Where can this role take you?

  • Advance vertically to higher level in the 2D team such as Senior or Principal position.
  • Transition to other disciplines within the Art team: 3D Artist, 3D Animator, VFX Artist, and so on.

Recruitment journey

  • Online screening call with recruiter
  • Distance art test
  • Offline interview with Artist Supervisor, Art Director, and Producer

Требования

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED

  • Advanced 2D art skills: hand-drawing, digital painting and illustration.
  • Advanced Photoshop skills.
  • Academic background in traditional or digital arts, concept art or equivalent.
  • Portfolio that shows solid art fundamentals: composition, shapes and forms, lighting, color theory, proportions, scale, perspective and anatomy.
  • Ability to follow and adapt to different art styles, guidelines and creative workflows.

Кто вы

  • Creative, original and attentive to detail.
  • Open to feedback and flexible in your thinking.
  • Proactive: you follow a brief and work on your own initiative.
  • Organized: you plan your tasks, estimate time accurately and meet deadlines.
  • Clear communicator who can express ideas, give constructive feedback and get along with others.
  • Able to solve artistic or technical issues in your projects.
  • Team player who works well under pressure and is passionate about games across platforms.
