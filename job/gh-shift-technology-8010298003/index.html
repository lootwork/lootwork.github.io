О ENGINEERING TEAM

Our Engineering team lies at the core of the value we offer to our customers. We solve complex problems by working not only within our squads, but also by working collaboratively with other teams across the organization.

Our tech stack includes .Net core (C#), Python, Cosmos DB, Service Bus, and more. If you are excited by solving complex technical challenges, this is the right place for you!

WHAT YOUR TEAM DOES...

You would join an internal squad in charge of building the foundations to help other squads to develop / deploy / monitor / secure their services. The team also develops transversal services such as the worker service, the realtime fraud detection service and monitors the Software Development Kit (SDK).

WHAT YOU'LL DO...

  • As a .NET C# Software Developer, your technical knowledge and autonomy will let you build complete designs for our applications and features.
  • You'll design and implement intuitive, scalable components that drive efficiency across our platform.

WHAT YOU BRING...

  • You have a first experience in software development.
  • You have used an OOP language, and ideally C# .Net
  • You have a good understanding of Microsoft Azure
  • You are able to build and modify high-performance APIs and program
  • Bonus:
  • Knowledge of Cosmos DB and Service Bus
  • Knowledge and understanding of AI/ML Fundamentals
