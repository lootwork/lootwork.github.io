We are looking for a passionate Illustrator to join our Marketing team. As an Illustrator, you will create characters, environments, and objects from concept to final artwork. In the studio, you will work closely with the marketing team and motion graphic designers to create ads that millions of people will watch daily.

At Dream Games, all team members have a genuine chance to contribute to the final product and have an impact on the company. If you want to be a team member of a fast-growing company, you might be the person we are looking for!

Обязанности

  • Developing visual concepts and ideas for new ads
  • Creating appealing characters, environments, and objects
  • Working on the development of a visual style, design, and overall look of the ads
  • Finding creative art solutions for the production challenges
  • Contributing to the development of user-oriented design culture

Требования

  • Passion for high-quality art production
  • Experience with Adobe Photoshop or Adobe Illustrator
  • Knowledge of composition, perspective, lighting, and color theory
  • Passion and interest in games
  • Appreciation and understanding of teamwork
