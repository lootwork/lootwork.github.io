The Role 🚀

We’re hiring a Creator Marketing Executive at Monzo. We’re looking for a brilliant creator marketing expert to help us scale our next chapter: showing up less like a standard bank and more like an authoritative, high-craft media publisher.

You’ll support the end-to-end execution of creator campaigns, manage always-on creator relationships, and help build deep trust around the culture of money.

If you are an energetic, detail-oriented creator marketer who loves collaborating with talent, knows how to drive real brand love, and can navigate the nuances of a regulated environment with strong judgment, we invite you to join our team.

The Team 💫

You’ll join a tight-knit, collaborative social and creator team. You’ll report directly to our Head of Social and Influencer, with direct support from our Creator Marketing Manager.

Monzo is growing up, and we’re ready for our next stage of growth. We’re moving away from the old playbook to build a high-quality, always-on social and creator ecosystem with partners at the top of their game. This role is a great opportunity to work with some of the best in the game, and bring a fresh perspective to creator marketing at Monzo.

You’ll play a key role by…

  • Executing high-impact campaigns: Support and deliver always-on, reactive, and integrated creator campaigns on time and on budget.
  • Driving trust & authority: Help shift brand perceptions and build long-term credibility through authentic creator partnerships and ambassador networks.
  • Supporting creator content & craft: Collaborate closely with talent and agencies to co-create high-performing, platform-native content across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, Substack and more.
  • Ensuring rigorous compliance: Act as a detailed guardian for brand alignment, risk, and regulatory guidelines across all creator output.
  • Tracking & optimise performance: Monitor campaign KPIs, analyse performance data, and help translate insights into actionable creative strategy.
  • Talent research and knowledge: Stay plugged into TikTok, Instagram, and wider internet culture to identify up-and-coming creators and social publishers who align with our brand values.
  • Campaign & content Support: Assist in briefing, coordinating, and supporting creators through execution to ensure assets meet creative standards and deadlines.
  • Creator management: Support talent onboarding and briefing, and day-to-day relationship management through our agency for our trusted ambassador roster.
  • Delivering compliant content: Review creator-generated content thoroughly to ensure full compliance with internal risk and controls teams and industry regulations.
  • Agency & cross-functional collaboration: Work seamlessly alongside agency partners, internal brand, writers, social, and growth teams to align creator work with commercial goals.
  • Performance reporting: Track metrics, build reporting dashboards, and extract insights on media efficiency, brand lift, and engagement.
  • Trend & competitor tracking: Monitor competitor activity and emerging platform features to bring fresh, innovative ideas to the table.

We’d love to hear from you if…

  • You have 1–3 years’ experience in creator marketing, influencer partnerships or digital social media.
  • You’re proactive and comfortable managing small projects independently, while knowing when to ask for guidance.
  • You understand platforms like TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn, and what helps creator content stand out.
  • You have a strong eye for detail and can stay organised when managing budgets, content deliverables and regulatory requirements.
  • You’re a great communicator who can build trusting relationships with creators and collaborate with teams across the business.
  • You’re comfortable using social analytics tools and keen to learn how creator storytelling contributes to business results.
  • You bring good judgement and empathy to your work, balancing creative, culturally relevant ideas with sensitivity to financial topics and brand risks.

Some nice to haves…

  • Prior experience coordinating creator events, activations, or ambassador programs.
  • Understanding of social SEO, keyword optimisation, and search intent in creator content.
  • Familiarity with creator management tools, social listening software, or analytics suites.
