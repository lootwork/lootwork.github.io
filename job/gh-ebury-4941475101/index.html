Senior Software Engineer (Money Flows)

Location: Sydney Office (Hybrid: 4 days office / 1 day WFH)

Department: Engineering

О роли

The Engineering team at Ebury is looking for a Senior Software Engineer to play a pivotal role in evolving our core transactional platform. As a key member of our global team, you will own the development, scalability, and optimization of our multi-currency account systems, incoming funds, and outgoing payment processes.

We are technology agnostic—we care about your fundamental software engineering craftsmanship, problem-solving abilities, and architectural mindset rather than the specific languages you’ve used in the past. If you understand how to build resilient, distributed systems, you will thrive here.

This is a critical role designed to take our high-volume transactional platform to the next level, delivering a seamless payment experience for both our global clients and internal operational teams.

Основные обязанности

  • Architect & Deliver: Lead the design and development of the next iteration of our global platform, streamlining complex cross-border payments and international fund collections.
  • Domain Ownership: Take full accountability for the services you build under our "You build it, you own it" philosophy—embracing everything from initial system design to deployment and production health.
  • Technical Leadership: Lead product initiatives end-to-end, working closely with Product and Operations stakeholders to translate complex financial business requirements into elegant technical solutions.
  • Mentor & Coach: Guide and mentor less experienced engineers, fostering a culture of high engineering standards, thorough code reviews, and continuous learning.
  • Collaborate Globally: Partner with engineering leaders and distributed teams across different regions to design robust, modular services and components.

О вас

  • Core Software Engineering Expertise: Proven track record of designing, building, and maintaining complex, highly available software systems at scale. You care deeply about clean code, design patterns, and testability (unit/integration testing).
  • Payments Domain Experience: Background in payments, fintech, or banking. You understand the unique challenges of transactional consistency, ledger systems, multi-currency handling, or external financial integrations.
  • Tech-Agnostic Mindset: Proficient in at least one major backend language and excited about choosing the right tool for the job. Strong experience with distributed architectures, asynchronous messaging, and relational databases.
  • Production & Observability Focus: You don't just write code—you know how to run it. Experience building standard APIs, integrating external systems, and setting up robust monitoring and alerting.
  • Communication & Collaboration: Ability to communicate complex technical ideas clearly and concisely in English (written and spoken) to both technical peers and non-technical business stakeholders.

Note: If you’re excited about this opportunity but your background doesn’t match every requirement perfectly, we still strongly encourage you to apply! You may be the right candidate for this or other open roles at Ebury.

Our Tech Stack (What you'll work with)

We don't expect you to know all of this on day one, but you should be excited to work with:

  • Languages & Frameworks: Python, Django, FastAPI
  • Data & Messaging: PostgreSQL, Kafka
  • Infrastructure & DevOps: AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform
  • Observability: Datadog

Что мы предлагаем

  • Compensation: Competitive base salary + discretionary performance-based bonus.
  • Global Impact: A dynamic, international environment offering massive scale and high-visibility exposure.
  • Growth: Continued personal development through structured training and professional certifications.
