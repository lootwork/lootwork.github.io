We're looking for a passionate and creative 2D Artist who will be producing high-quality 2D art assets for our chart-topping games. You will be responsible for creating all art assets utilizing your imagination and visualization skills. As a part of the Bigger team, you will be working closely with our product managers and game developers to architect outstanding gaming experiences that reach millions of players all around the world.

If you are a talented game artist who loves games and shares our passion for enjoying and crafting games, come and join us!

О THIS ROLE

- Create and implement concepts, game assets, characters or environments from scratch

- Collaborate closely with Product Managers and Game Developers to deliver the best gaming experience

- Design and evolve artwork from concept to production

- Research and analyze the market for the concept, art and game feelings

- Work on new technologies to create stunning game art

- Involve and contribute to all aspects of game development including Game Design, Logic, Feelings, Graphics and UI

- Contribute new ideas for game design, features, and projects

YOU COULD BE A GREAT FIT IF

- Bachelor’s or Master’s Degree in Arts or relevant fields

- Experienced in visual arts especially in 2D game art

- Strong knowledge of visual editing tools, like Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator to be able to deliver quality assets

- Great understanding of perspective, lighting, and composition

- Good sense of gameplay experience and special attention to detail

- A perfect team-player with excellent communication skills

- Previous experience in game art is a big plus

- 5+ years of industry experience is a plus

BONUS POINT IF

- You’ve played Tasty Match: Cook and Puzzle and explored the game. If not, now’s a great time to start!

- You’re curious about the gaming industry and eager to learn more.

- You enjoy working in fast-moving, dynamic environments.

Условия и льготы

- Be part of a top-tier team, learn from the best, and create games players love.

- Competitive compensation.

- Health insurance with unlimited coverage.

- A beautiful, centrally located office in the heart of Istanbul.

- Opportunities to attend global conferences and stay connected with the industry

- Relocation support to make your move hassle-free if needed.
