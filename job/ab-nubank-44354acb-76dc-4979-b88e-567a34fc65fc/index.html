OUR COMPLIANCE CHAPTER

The Compliance Team has a very special role in our mission: support the company’s growth by identifying the regulatory risks that NU faces; designing and implementing controls to protect Nu from such risks; and by providing advisory to the Business on regulations and internal policies to create the best product or service that follows with these external and internal regulations and standards.

Responsibilities assigned to you might be related to

- Research regulations and project management for its implementation.

- Project management related to new regulations and licensing processes.

- Lead efforts and align requirements towards new financial licenses.

- Evaluate from a compliance perspective new products and features to be launched.

- Keep other teams abreast of requirements by researching regulatory and filing information; writing and communicating guidelines.

- Provide advisory to other teams about the regulation to establish new processes or controls.

- Monitor and respond to inquiries received from Regulators, Auto Regulatory bodies or Auditors.

- Create and update Compliance policies and procedures.

- Liaise and work closely with various teams to enhance systems and processes related to Compliance.

- Coordinate or support regulatory reviews or audits.

- Support in identifying regulatory gaps and tracking its implementation.
