О project

Our product is an AI model orchestrator that brings dozens of AI models into one simple interface with a single token balance - no need for multiple subscriptions or separate tools.

We’re looking for a User Acquisition Manager to build and own the UA function from the ground up. You’ll have a lot of freedom to shape the strategy, test new approaches, build scalable acquisition processes, and directly influence the product’s growth.

And this is not just another UA role - you’ll have the opportunity to grow into a Lead position and build the direction around yourself.

Обязанности

- Own the User Acquisition process end-to-end, including campaign launches, day-to-day management, performance analysis, and ongoing optimization.

- Analyze campaigns, funnels, and creatives on a daily basis and use data to identify growth opportunities and define clear rules for campaign optimization.

- Develop creative ideas and work closely with the team to turn them into performance-driven concepts.

- Conduct audience research from a performance marketing perspective, including demographics, interests, behaviors, and pain points.

- Lead brainstorming sessions and develop marketing hypotheses across creatives, funnels, and ad campaigns.

Требования

- 3+ years of experience in paid acquisition within product companies

- Strong hands-on experience with AppLovin and Meta Ads

- Expertise in both web and mobile acquisition

- Experience with AI-powered automation tools, such as Birch

- Hands-on experience with attribution tools (AppsFlyer / Adjust)

- Strong understanding of full-funnel performance metrics and data-driven decision-making

- Analytical mindset with a strong focus on performance optimization

- Strong execution skills and attention to detail

Would be a plus

- Experience with TikTok acquisition

- Understanding of A/B testing
