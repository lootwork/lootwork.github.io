Job Description

Join CD PROJEKT RED as an Expert Environment Artist and play a pivotal role in the development of Cyberpunk 2. You will craft the future of atmospheric storytelling and serve as a key architect through world building. We are looking for a highly experienced artist who thrives on collaboration and teamwork. You will own the skyline to the streets, defining the visual identity of Cyberpunk 2.

Note

  • ​​This role is responsible for level art and world building.
  • If you are primarily an asset or prop artist, your skillset may be a better fit for our internal X-Dev team, with whom we collaborate daily.
  • Artists should include a portfolio to their application.

Обязанности

  • Design and execute environment art from sprawling city districts to intimate, high-detail interiors.
  • World-building by drafting large-scale city districts to define composition and player flow, embracing iteration as a core tool for quality discovery.
  • Define and uphold environment art standards across composition, lighting, color, materials, visual storytelling, and gameplay readability.
  • Collaborate with the Art Directors, Art Leads and teammates to maintain artistic consistency and vision for the game while building captivating worlds.
  • Create best-in-class modular kits that define Cyberpunk art styles.
  • Partner with Tech Art and Engineering to prototype, document, and establish new pipelines that build upon the studio’s robust technical foundations.
  • Collaborate effectively with the entire Cyberpunk 2 team, across disciplines. Including close collaboration with the Level Design and Narrative teams.
  • Strong communication skills, ownership and team-first attitude required.
  • Maintain and improve production methods, optimizing pipelines while consistently meeting deadlines.
  • Mentoring and providing feedback to other artists.

Требования

  • 8+ years of relevant professional experience (or equivalent skill demonstrated through previous work, portfolio, or comparable experience).
  • Mastery of Unreal Engine.
  • Industry leading DCC skills with Blender and Substance Painter. Maya & 3DS Max are valued however Blender is our primary 3D modeling package.
  • Excellent sense of composition, scale, proportions, color theory and lighting.
  • Strong awareness of the role materials play in constructing the final frame and how materials fit in a project’s context.
  • Deep, practical understanding of level design principles and how to craft environments to support gamer experience.
  • Thoughtfully crafting visually compelling, believable, and immersive environments that embed storytelling to support and enhance core narrative beats.
  • Exceptional communication and collaborative skills.
  • Passion for games.

Nice to have's: (Please note that these skills are not required to apply for the position)

  • Experience with Google Workplace, Miro, Jira, Confluence, P4V.
  • Experience with procedural content creation.
  • Experience using architectural principles to support storytelling, vibe, and tone in environments.
  • Experience using art principles in environments to enhance and support cinematics.
