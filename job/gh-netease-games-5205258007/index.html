Responsibilities

  • Own cross-border payment requirements, product solution design, and PRD documentation.
  • Drive the full product lifecycle from requirements and solution design through testing, launch, and post-launch optimization.
  • Lead the integration of new payment methods and payment providers, and support payment solutions for new business launches.
  • Monitor payment success rates, provider stability, and customer issues, and drive continuous improvements.
  • Analyze payment performance across countries, issuing banks, and payment methods.

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree with 2+ years of experience in payment products, transaction platforms, or digital products.
  • Proven ability to independently manage the full product lifecycle, from requirements to launch.
  • Familiar with payment, order, refund, payout, settlement, or account-related processes.
  • Strong analytical, project execution, and cross-functional communication skills.
  • Good understanding of payment operations and payment performance metrics.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in cross-border payments, international e-commerce, or virtual goods.
  • Experience in payment success rate optimization, routing, provider integration, PCI compliance, or risk management.
  • Familiarity with SQL, BI, or AI tools.
