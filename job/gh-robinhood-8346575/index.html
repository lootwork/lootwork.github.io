Join us in building the future of finance.

Our mission is to democratize finance for all. An estimated $124 trillion of assets will be inherited by younger generations in the next two decades. The largest transfer of wealth in human history. If you’re ready to be at the epicenter of this historic cultural and financial shift, keep reading.

О team + role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world’s biggest financial problems. We’re looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn’t a place for complacency, it’s where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We’re a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

The Brand Creative Team is evolving and elevating how the Robinhood brand shows up and reaches people – capturing attention, earning trust, and driving our next phase of growth. The next generation is figuring out their money, and we want them to do it with Robinhood. That means disruptive, culturally relevant work that cuts through where they actually spend their time and wins them over.

As Creative Director, Brand (Copy) you will lead the work that defines the brand and changes how people see Robinhood. You will concept big ideas and own them from insight to execution, with story and great writing at the core. The bar is world-class: work that breaks through, commands attention and moves the business.

This role is based in our New York, NY or Menlo Park, CA offices, with in-person attendance expected 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Concept and develop big ideas that break through. Create disruptive, culturally relevant brand campaigns and moments that are unmistakably Robinhood and impossible to ignore.
  • Win the next generation. Make Robinhood a brand young investors want to hear from, talk about and trust with their money. Meet them where they live with ideas they'll actually want to be part of, not just ads they scroll past.
  • Drive results with strategy. Start with the business goal and build ideas that move the numbers. Understand how people think about money, use data to sharpen the work, and measure it by what it delivers. Our company is highly strategic, and our creatives are too.
  • Make work the world talks about. Take ideas from first insight to finished work across film, social, out-of-home, experiential, product launches and events, and refuse to let anything go out less than great. Set the pace for the category with work that gets people talking and shapes the conversation in culture.
  • Raise the bar on writing. Hold every line to a world-class standard. Build stories that stick and make people think differently about money. Set the bar for writing across the team with sharp, useful feedback for writers, designers and freelancers, and never let good enough ship.
  • Shape Robinhood's voice. Build a tone that stands out in the category, sounds like the way the next generation actually talks about money, and stays razor-sharp across every channel.
  • Direct the making. Partner with directors, production companies, artists and agencies, and stay in the work through shoot, edit and finish. Push innovation in how we make, using AI and new tools to explore more ideas faster and make better work, without diluting the brand.
  • Get everyone behind the work. Inspire leaders, partners and teams to believe in the idea and stay behind it from first pitch to launch.
  • Build with the business. Partner with Strategy, Performance Marketing, Product, Social and Design so the work is rooted in real insight and built to move the company forward. Work with Legal and Compliance early so bold ideas get cleared.

Что вы приносите

  • 10+ years in advertising and brand creative, at a top agency, an elite in-house team, or both.
  • A portfolio of idea-led work that got made, got noticed, and moved a business, with a clear story behind each piece.
  • A copywriting background and the range to concept across film, digital, social, experiential and out-of-home.
  • Writing that is clear, distinctive and memorable, a gift for narrative, and a track record of making complex or regulated subjects feel human.
  • A record of owning and producing work end to end: working with directors and vendors, and getting ambitious ideas made on time and on budget.
