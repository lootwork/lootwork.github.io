Introduction

At Huuuge Games, we make top-grossing mobile games that bring people together through fun and social mobile gaming.

Become a part of an exciting adventure delivering fun & truly social experiences to millions of players around the world from our offices in Poland. We’re now hiring a Senior Product Manager to drive the continued success of one of our biggest titles.

This is a key, hands-on role for an experienced product professional who thrives in data-driven environments, loves working cross-functionally, and wants to see measurable impact from their idea.

Как вы повлияете на результат

  • Own the vision, strategy, and execution of core product features, ensuring they deliver strong player engagement, retention, and monetization results.
  • Translate business goals and player insights into actionable product roadmaps and feature concepts, working closely with Production, Design, Monetization, and Analytics teams.
  • Deeply analyze KPIs, player behavior, and A/B test results to guide data-informed decisions and continuously improve game performance.
  • Collaborate with internal teams (Content, UI, Economy, Marketing) to ensure cohesive player experiences and smooth feature launches.
  • Drive excellence in product execution - from ideation and prototyping through live operations and post-launch analysis.
  • Monitor competitors, market trends, and player feedback to identify opportunities for innovation and growth.
  • Champion the voice of the player, ensuring that business goals are achieved without compromising on fun or community engagement.
