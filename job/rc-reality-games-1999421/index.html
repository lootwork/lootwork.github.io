Reality Games is seeking a talented 3D Generalist to join our Product department and work on our exciting Monopoly World project.

Check out our trailer for the Monopoly World project

As a 3D Generalist, you will be responsible for creating high-quality 3D assets and animations that bring our virtual world to life.

  • Collaborate with the art and design teams to develop visually stunning environments and characters
  • Create and implement 3D models, textures, and animations for in-game assets
  • Optimize assets for performance while maintaining visual quality
  • Stay up-to-date on industry trends and best practices in 3D design and animation

By blending artistic creativity with technical expertise, you’ll contribute to delivering an unforgettable experience for fans of this globally recognized IP. If you're passionate about crafting stunning visuals for an exciting mobile game and thrive in a fast-paced, collaborative environment, this is your opportunity!

Work in a one-of-a-kind office, located within a beautifully restored historic railway station in the heart of Krakow’s charming old town. Immerse yourself in the vibrant atmosphere of the city’s cultural and historical center while collaborating with a passionate and supportive team, Monday through Friday.

This is a full-time, in-office position based in the iconic railway station building in Krakow.

Основные обязанности

  • Develop 3D city maps, environments, and characters.
  • Create dynamic 3D animations and cinematic motion design.
  • Optimize assets for mobile platforms, ensuring peak performance.
  • Collaborate with the creative team to deliver a world-class gaming experience.

Be a part of the team that is redefining mobile gaming with Monopoly World!
