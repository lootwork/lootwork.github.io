Что предстоит делать

We're looking for an Accounts Receivable (AR) Specialist to join our growing team. The AR Specialist will own billing and collections programs for Stripe’s growing mix of products that are invoiced. This role is responsible for projects within the end‑to‑end billing cycle, reconciliation of billing outputs to Finance reporting, and supporting AR collections strategy and improvement. The role works closely with Finance, Sales/GTM, Product, Data/Engineering, and Accounting to ensure invoices are accurate, timely, and collected.

Обязанности

  • Own and execute defined Accounts Receivable (AR) programs and projects from planning through delivery, establishing scope, milestones, success measures, and accountability.
  • Develop and maintain project plans, track dependencies, and coordinate cross-functional execution to deliver agreed outcomes on time.
  • Translate AR business priorities into actionable workstreams, aligning stakeholders on requirements, deliverables, and ownership.
  • Proactively identify project risks, resolve execution blockers, and escalate decisions or trade-offs with clear recommendations.
  • Analyze AR performance and financial trends to identify gaps, investigate root causes, and recommend targeted improvements.
  • Partner with Finance, Sales/GTM, Engineering, and other stakeholders to deliver initiatives that improve receivables performance and the customer experience.
  • Lead process improvement projects that standardize and simplify AR workflows, reduce manual effort, and strengthen controls.
  • Gather business requirements and coordinate the prioritization, testing, and rollout of enhancements in partnership with technical teams.
  • Drive change management for assigned initiatives, including stakeholder communications, documentation, adoption planning, and post-launch measurement.
  • Define and monitor program metrics and leading indicators to measure business performance, delivery progress, and realized benefits.
  • Provide concise updates to AR and Finance leadership on program health, milestones, risks, decisions, and results.
  • Maintain program documentation, decision logs, and ownership handoffs to support sustainable outcomes and continuous improvement.
  • Own and execute subsidiary AR integration projects, aligning stakeholders on requirements, milestones, and dependencies while driving process improvements to standardize workflows, strengthen controls, and improve scalability.

Кто вы

We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • 8+ years of relevant experience in billing/accounts receivable with usage-based billing platforms or complex SaaS billing systems.
  • Strong Excel/Sheets and SQL literacy; comfortable reading billing query outputs and reconciling to invoices.
  • Experience working with APIs, simple python scripts, or Retool automations preferred.
  • Familiarity with payment methods (ACH, wires, crypto/USDC, Stripe payment links) and cash application best practices.
  • Excellent attention to detail, organized, and able to manage a heavy month‑end cadence and ad hoc adjustments.
  • Strong communication skills and ability to partner cross‑functionally (Sales, Finance, Data, Product, Legal).
  • A proven ability to build strong collaborative working relationships with business partners
  • A proven ability to be a self starter capable of driving business results without significant supervision
  • The ability to maintain high performance under tight timelines and a “roll up your sleeves” mindset to get any job done
  • The ability to bring structure to ambiguous areas of opportunity and thrive in an unstructured, fast-moving environment
  • Excellent problem-solving skills and demonstrated ability to work independently, analyze problems and data sets to make complex investigation decisions
  • Demonstrated history of taking on various types of challenging projects and producing results
  • Solution-oriented mindset with enthusiasm for establishing best practices
  • Self-disciplined, diligent, proactive and detail oriented

Будет плюсом

  • Experience working at a high-growth technology or financial services company
  • Prior exposure to charge-through and pass‑through third‑party fees and complex fee constructs (platform fees, per txn, bps/% pricing, minimums).
  • Experience working at or supporting fintech or payments companies, especially crypto/stablecoin or cross‑border FX rails
  • Experience with Databricks, Snowflake, or similar data stacks.
