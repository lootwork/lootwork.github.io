Engineering Manager (Identity Team)

London Office - Hybrid: 4 days in the office, 1 day working from home

As an Engineering Manager for the Identity Platform, you will lead the team responsible for building high-throughput, mission-critical authentication and authorisation controls. You will architect and scale platform services that define and govern permissions across human, service, and bot identities, ensuring a secure, seamless banking-and-payments experience across Ebury.

Why This Role is Different

  • Own Client Outcomes: Take real client problems end-to-end, owning experience, APIs, data, and iteration with full trust and autonomy.
  • Player-Coach Early, Leader Always: Stay close to code early on (pairing, reviewing, shipping) and transition leverage into building people and systems as the team grows.
  • Platform-First Thinking: Build scalable, generalisable platforms rather than bespoke one-offs. Value retiring technical debt and flags as much as shipping features.
  • AI-Native Culture: Fluent in AI tools as force multipliers, guiding the team on effective adoption and designing AI into the product.

Основные обязанности

  • Grow the Team: Coach engineers through actionable feedback, clear growth paths, and performance management.
  • Delivery Ownership: Translate ambiguous business outcomes into clear roadmaps. Sequence work, manage risk, and deliver reliably at a sustainable pace.
  • Technical Leadership: Guide architecture and design reviews for high-scale, event-driven transactional systems on AWS and BigQuery.
  • Cross-Functional Partnership: Partner closely with Product, Design and Business Domains to shape customer solutions and influence strategic trade-offs.
  • Operational Health: Ensure production-grade stability, humane on-call loads, testing standards, CI/CD, and strong observability.
  • Responsible AI Adoption: Integrate AI tools into development workflows safely and effectively while maintaining complete ownership of shipped code.

Кого мы ищем

  • Engineering Leadership: Proven experience managing and developing software engineering teams with a track record of mentorship and high delivery standards.
  • Technical Depth: Solid CS foundation with hands-on credibility building/operating distributed systems at scale (AWS, event-driven architecture, APIs).
  • Hiring Instincts: Ability to define an interview bar, run rigorous hiring processes, and close exceptional talent from scratch.
  • AI Fluency: Hands-on usage of AI coding tools and strong opinions on how teams best leverage AI for productivity and product capabilities.
  • Product & Quality Mindset: Focus on client value, pragmatic scoping, and high discipline around money-movement systems (idempotency, reconciliation).
  • Communication: Fluency in English with exceptional ability to articulate complex technical ideas and align cross-functional teams.
  • Identity and User Management: Have worked on scalable platforms that define and operate authentication and fine grained authorisation functions.
  • Nice to Have: Fintech/payments/regulated domain experience, experience on early founding hub teams, or web & mobile channel exposure.

Что мы предлагаем

  • Competitive Package: Strong base salary, performance-based bonus, and comprehensive benefits tailored to a senior leadership role.
  • Career Progression: Clear leadership paths, senior mentorship, and room to grow across technical and organisational domains.
  • Challenging Problems: The opportunity to work on complex, intellectually stimulating projects that have a significant and measurable business impact.
  • Premium Equipment: High-end MacBook Pro and the required tools.
  • Collaborative Culture: An inclusive, high-performing environment rewarding technical excellence, decision-making, and constructive challenge.

Required Qualifications

  • Proven experience as an Engineering Manager or in a similar leadership role, leading and supporting engineers.
  • Experience working in fintech or within the financial services industry.
  • Experience working in a multi-channel, multi-product driven environment.
  • Deep understanding of software development methodologies and best practices.
  • Experience with agile development processes.
  • Excellent communication, interpersonal, and leadership skills.
  • Strong problem-solving and decision-making abilities.
  • Passion for technology and innovation.

Desirable Attributes

  • Experience building enterprise-level Identity, Access Management (IAM) platforms, Fine-Grained Access Control (FGAC) systems and defining authorisation policies.
  • Knowledge of advanced authentication techniques, such as device binding/attestation, and experience in consolidating fragmented user identities into a unified identity model.
  • Familiarity with cloud computing platforms (e.g., AWS, GCP, Azure) and containerisation technologies (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes).
  • Contributions to open-source projects or a personal portfolio demonstrating technical curiosity and skill.
  • Experience with Domain-Driven Design (DDD) and standardising communication patterns in a centralised architecture.
