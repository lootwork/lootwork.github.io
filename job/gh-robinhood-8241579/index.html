Our mission is to democratize finance for all. An estimated $124 trillion of assets will be inherited by younger generations in the next two decades. The largest transfer of wealth in human history. If you’re ready to be at the epicenter of this historic cultural and financial shift, keep reading.

О team + role

We are building an elite team, applying frontier technologies to the world's biggest financial problems. We're looking for bold thinkers. Sharp problem-solvers. Builders who are wired to make an impact. Robinhood isn't a place for complacency, it's where ambitious people do the best work of their careers. We're a high-performing, fast-moving team with ethics at the center of everything we do. Expectations are high, and so are the rewards.

Our AI team builds the models, agents, and platforms behind Robinhood's products and the tools our employees use every day. We work in small groups where product, engineering, and research sit together and ship in weeks, not quarters. We partner closely with design, data science, legal, and compliance, because financial products need to be useful and safe. We value clear writing, direct feedback, and decisions backed by data. It's an exciting time to join us!

As a Staff Product Manager, AI, you will set the product direction for an internal platform that drives measurable business impact. You will start with two of three focus areas: the developer lifecycle, personalization, and agent products. This is not a traditional product line PM role. You will build the platforms that make Robinhood's engineers faster and power what our customers experience. You will decide which problems are worth solving, write clear specs, and define how we know a model or agent is working, including quality, speed, cost, and safety. You will work every day with engineers, researchers, designers, and data scientists to turn early prototypes into products that people trust. As the area grows, you will coach other product managers and help lead a senior product manager, with room to expand your scope over time. We can't wait to see what you build!

This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA and Seattle, WA offices, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the vision, strategy, and roadmap for an internal platform that improves developer productivity, and explain the plan clearly to senior leaders in writing and in person
  • Tie platform work to business results by defining the metrics that show how developer and platform improvements drive outcomes for Robinhood and its customers
  • Turn open-ended problems into specs with goals, success metrics, and launch criteria
  • Define how AI features and agents are evaluated, including accuracy, response time, cost per request, and safety, and work with engineers and researchers to improve each one
  • Influence engineering teams across the company, and build a strong developer community that adopts and contributes to the platform
  • Ship in small steps with engineering, design, and data science, and learn from how people use each release
  • Partner with legal, compliance, and security so AI features meet financial regulations and Robinhood's standards
  • Track product metrics and run experiments, then use the results to decide what to build, change, or stop
  • Mentor other product managers, and as the area grows, help hire and lead a senior product manager

Что вы приносите

  • 8+ years of product management experience, including prior experience as a platform product manager setting the vision and roadmap for internal platforms that improve developer productivity
  • 3+ years shipping AI or machine learning products, such as features built on large language models, agents, personalization, recommendations, or ML platforms
  • A track record of taking a product from idea to launch and growing its use, with results you can describe in numbers
  • Experience influencing teams across the company without direct authority, to drive adoption and business impact
  • Working knowledge of how large language models are built, evaluated, and deployed, and the ability to discuss tradeoffs with engineers and researchers
  • Strong written communication, including explaining technical choices to people outside engineering
  • Comfort with data, including SQL or a similar tool, product metrics, experiment design, and metric definition
  • The ability and interest to grow into managing a senior product manager
  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience

Будет плюсом

  • Experience in fintech, brokerage, payments, or another regulated industry
  • Experience building AI agents or internal AI platforms
