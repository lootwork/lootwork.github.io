Что предстоит делать

As our Senior KYB Specialist — Business Account in Barcelona, you'll bring strong specialist expertise in corporate KYB and business accounts to shape our risk-based onboarding and ongoing due diligence processes. Reporting to the KYB Manager, you will serve as a senior point of reference for Business Account KYB—applying sound judgment to complex cases and connecting KYB needs directly with the teams building the product.

Here's what that looks like in practice

- Contribute to the KYB framework - Shape risk-based onboarding and ongoing due diligence processes for business customers, accounting for the specific characteristics and risks of the products they use.

- Support customer and entity assessments - Apply specialist judgment to complex ownership structures, legal representatives, and authorised persons to help the team evaluate relationships, authority, and relevant risks.

- Contribute to lifecycle reviews - Define how customer information and risk assessments are reviewed and updated over time, including when existing customers adopt additional products.

- Support complex KYB decisions - Provide specialist judgment on unusual, higher-risk, or unclear cases, guiding decisions and escalations within our agreed framework.

- Turn regulation and risk requirements into clear ways of working - Partner with relevant teams to translate regulatory expectations and risk appetite into practical checks, evidence requirements, decision criteria, review rules, and escalation points.

- Develop procedures and team capability - Create practical procedures, playbooks, and guidance that enable the team to work consistently and independently, while sharing case learnings, delivering training, and offering specialist support to colleagues.

- Work with Product and control teams - Translate KYB requirements into workflows, tools, and procedures, contributing to testing and operational readiness while identifying opportunities for standardisation, automation, or necessary manual review.

- Support ongoing improvement - Analyse case trends, operational data, and Quality team findings to spot recurring issues and improve controls, procedures, guidance, and customer journeys.

- Work across the organisation - Collaborate with Product, Compliance, FinCrime, Legal, Risk, and Operations to build solutions that balance risk, customer experience, and operational scalability.

Who we're looking for

This role needs someone who brings deep corporate KYB ownership, thrives on solving complex regulatory puzzles, and knows how to turn specialist judgment into scalable, practical workflows.

- Experience - At least 5 years in corporate KYB, business banking, fintech, payments, or another regulated financial-services environment.

- Entity & ownership assessment - Hands-on experience evaluating legal entities and complex ownership structures, including UBOs, legal representatives, authorised persons, and non-standard corporate arrangements.

- Product-specific due diligence - Proven background in KYB or customer due diligence for business accounts, payment accounts, or similar regulated financial products, understanding how product features impact customer risk.

- Account lifecycle expertise - Solid grasp of the business payment or banking account lifecycle beyond onboarding—covering activation, maintenance, ownership or signatory changes, ongoing reviews, restrictions, and closure.

- Regulatory translation - Experience interpreting regulatory or compliance requirements and converting them into practical controls, processes, and procedures.

- Risk judgment - Ability to apply risk appetite to real-world cases and make sound decisions in complex or ambiguous situations.

- Process design - Track record of designing or improving KYB processes, controls, decision frameworks, and operational guidance.

- Product collaboration - Experience partnering with Product teams on requirements, workflows, tools, or UAT.

- Data-driven mindset - Confidence using operational data, quality findings, and case trends to back up recommendations and drive improvements.

- Knowledge sharing - Ability to distil specialist expertise into clear procedures, training, and practical guidance for colleagues.

- Communication & stakeholder management - Pragmatic, clear communicator skilled at balancing risk, customer experience, and operational scalability.

- Language - Full professional proficiency in English.

Don't worry if you don't tick every box. We believe in the power of different viewpoints and strengths. Your unique perspective is important as we build something special: if you're passionate and can make a difference, we truly encourage you to apply.
