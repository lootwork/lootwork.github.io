The Company You’ll Join

Carta is the connected platform and AI-native ecosystem for private capital. Built to replace fragmented tools with a single system of record, Carta brings together the software, services, and legal infrastructure that founders use to manage equity, fund managers use to run administration and reporting, and legal teams use to close transactions. Trusted by 55,000 companies and 1.8M+ equity holders in 160+ countries, and 10,000 funds and SPVs representing $250B+ in assets under management, Carta is transforming how private capital operates. Recognized by Fortune, Forbes, Fast Company, Inc. and Great Places to Work.

For more information about our offices and culture, check out our Carta careers page.

The Problems You’ll Solve

We're looking for someone who doesn't wait for a problem to be assigned to them — they spot it first, get curious about why it's happening, and are genuinely energized by figuring it out. If you’re proactive, analytical, and have a solid technical foundation, this is the right position for you.

The Private Equity Support Analyst is responsible for providing best-in-class customer service for a portfolio of private equity backed companies and top PE firms. They will be responsible for the daily support of helping our clients navigate the Carta platform and assist them with all their equity compensation needs, using AI tools as a core part of how they investigate, resolve, and communicate solutions.

This role will be responsible for adhering to Service Level Agreements, conducting case reviews and training sessions with customers, and proactively communicating cap table errors and cleanup needs. The Private Equity Analyst approaches issues like a support engineer — diagnosing root causes rather than surface symptoms, and building lightweight scripts or tools to resolve recurring problems at scale instead of fixing them one at a time. You maintain a distinguished level of technical knowledge and utilize this knowledge, alongside AI-powered workflows, to manage projects and carry them through to completion, assist in strategic decisions, and serve as a subject matter expert for others within the Support Team.

The Impact You’ll Have

Become a Subject Matter Expert and the go-to front-line problem-solver for our Private Equity customers.

  • Provide timely, accurate, empathetic support across phone, email, and zoom — clearly communicating with a wide range of user personas, particularly within the private equity industry
  • Respond to support cases spanning a wide range of complexity surfaced from the cap table product
  • Incorporate AI tools into daily workflows as a core way of working, not an add-on
  • Context-switch comfortably between high-touch executive guidance and hands-on assistance (e.g., login issues) with a consistent white-glove service mentality
  • Manage high-urgency cases with ownership; prioritize critically and adapt comfortably to an evolving company and product environment
  • Operate in ambiguity, making informed decisions in never-before-seen situations
  • Bring a proactive, creative mindset to problem-solving — you don't just apply the existing playbook, you look for a better way when one doesn't exist
  • Read logs, error traces, and system output to troubleshoot technical issues beyond the standard playbook
  • Read between the lines of a user's question to identify their actual need and drive a speedy, satisfactory resolution
  • Reconcile cap tables and take on other equity projects such as repricing, stock splits, and share conversions
  • Drive improvements to support processes and product, partnering cross-functionally to close gaps and increase customer adoption and satisfaction
