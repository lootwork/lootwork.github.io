О роли

The Strategic Finance team provides analysis and recommendations that inform strategic and operational decisions across the company. We are looking for a Senior Data Analyst to be the primary analytical partner to our Sales organization. You will dig into pipeline, bookings, rep productivity, and plan attainment to give Sales and Finance leaders a clear, trusted view of how the business is performing and why.

You will frame ambiguous questions on your own, build the datasets and dashboards that answer them, and bring a point of view to senior leaders. The role offers regular exposure to Director and VP-level stakeholders and the chance to influence how a hyper-growth company plans, sets targets, and invests in its sales motion.

You love getting to the ground truth of what is happening in the numbers. You can pull together data from several systems, explain a gap to plan in plain language, and recommend what to do about it. You own your work from the first query to the final recommendation, and you have a critical eye for detail.

A day in the life (Responsibilities)

Partner with Sales and Finance leaders

  • Be a thought partner to Sales and Finance leaders by analyzing the drivers of bookings, pipeline conversion, win rates, and new business results against plan.
  • Build and maintain pacing views that show whether Sales is on track to plan, and explain gaps by volume, rate, mix, and timing.
  • Analyze why results vary between reps and what separates top performers, to inform coverage and enablement decisions.
  • Support planning and target setting with productivity models and quota inputs.
  • Size and measure sales incentives, including SPIFFs (short-term bonuses for a specific goal), before and after launch.
  • Analyze discount patterns and the impact of pricing initiatives on deals.
  • Analyze market size (TAM, or total addressable market) and saturation to show leaders how much room is left to grow.
  • Develop scenario models and sensitivity analyses that help leaders understand the range of outcomes and the tradeoffs between them.

Own the data and the metrics

  • Use SQL and BI tools to automate reporting and generate insights that let leaders see quickly and accurately how Sales is performing. This requires a deep understanding of our CRM and data warehouse tables, as well as the business sense to know which operating metrics matter.
  • Own the leadership-level view of sales performance, above the rep and manager-level reporting that Sales Operations maintains, and standardize core sales metrics so that Finance, Sales, and Sales Operations work from the same numbers.
  • Validate data integrity and pressure-test assumptions before presenting conclusions.
  • Partner with Data and Analytics Engineering to improve data models and pipelines, and use AI tools to speed up analysis, QA, and reporting workflows.

Communicate and influence

  • Present findings and recommendations to Director and VP-level stakeholders, turning detailed SQL analysis into a short, clear narrative.
  • Build trusted relationships with Sales, Sales Operations, Marketing, Finance, and Data teams.
  • Proactively spot issues and opportunities beyond the regular stream of requests, and investigate them independently.
  • Challenge assumptions constructively and influence decisions with a clear point of view.

What you'll need to thrive (Requirements)

  • 5+ years of experience in business intelligence, data analytics, strategic finance, or sales analytics, or a Bachelor's degree (or equivalent experience) in a quantitative field such as math, economics, finance, computer science, or a physical science.
  • Strong SQL & statistical data modeling skills (e.g. SQL, Python, R).
  • Proven track record building interactive dashboards in tools such as Hex, Looker, Tableau, or Sigma, and choosing the visualization that makes the insight clear.
  • Experience with sales data and the metrics built on it, such as pipeline, bookings, quota attainment, win rate, and sales productivity.
  • Strong business modeling skills, including forecasting, scenario analysis, and ROI analysis.
  • Experience with data engineering and the ability to navigate new data sources.
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication, with the ability to explain complex data to different audiences, including senior leaders.
  • A detail-oriented, collaborative approach, and comfort delivering results in a matrix organization.

Bonus ingredients

  • Experience supporting a Sales organization from within Finance, Sales Operations, or Sales Strategy.
  • Experience in a SaaS, fintech, subscription, or transaction-based business.
  • Familiarity with recurring revenue metrics (such as ARR) and the difference between bookings, billings, and revenue.
  • Hands-on experience with Salesforce, Snowflake, dbt, Hex, and AI tools.
