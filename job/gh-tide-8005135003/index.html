О TIDE

At Tide, we help SMEs save time and money in the running of their businesses by not only offering business accounts and related banking services, but also a comprehensive set of highly usable and connected administrative solutions, from invoicing to accounting.

Tide is transforming the small business banking market and now supports over 2 million members globally across the UK, India, Germany and France.

Using advanced technology, all solutions are designed with SMEs in mind. With quick onboarding, low fees and innovative features, we thrive on making data driven decisions to serve our mission: to help SMEs save time and money so they can get back to doing what they love.

О роли

We are looking for a highly skilled and data-driven Copywriter – SEO & ASO to lead our organic growth efforts across web and mobile. The ideal candidate will own end-to-end SEO and ASO strategy to drive high-intent organic acquisition for our MSME-focused financial products including Fixed Deposits, Insurance/Assurance, Lending, Prepaid Cards, and business banking solutions.

Основные обязанности

Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

  • Build, own, and execute organic acquisition strategy to drive high-quality traffic and conversions from MSME merchants and financial product seekers.
  • Conduct keyword research and competitor analysis across MSME, lending, FD, assurance, and prepaid card segments.
  • Optimize website architecture, internal linking, product landing pages, blogs, and performance pages to improve rankings & search visibility.
  • Lead technical SEO improvements: crawlability, indexing, Core Web Vitals, site speed, structured data/schema.
  • Build content strategy with product & content teams—blog articles, product guides, comparison pages, FAQ hubs, UGC, etc.
  • Track organic funnel performance and optimize for sign-ups, applications, and revenue impact.
  • Manage agency relationships, freelancers, and internal resources.

App Store Optimization (ASO)

  • Own the growth of organic app installs for Android & iOS.
  • Optimize metadata: titles, descriptions, screenshots, preview videos, and localized content.
  • Research and implement keyword expansion, competitor benchmarking, and A/B testing strategies.
  • Improve app rating, reviews management, and conversion from store listing views to installs.
  • Partner with product teams to align features and releases with store optimization opportunities.

Analytics & Performance

  • Analyze data using GA4, Search Console, App Radar/Sensor Tower, AppFollow, SEMRush, Ahrefs, SimilarWeb, and other tools.
  • Define KPIs and report on organic install growth, ranking improvements, conversion impact, and business outcomes.
  • Build forecasting models for organic acquisition ROI & revenue contribution.
  • Collaborate with performance marketing to reduce CAC via organic uplift.

Требования и навыки

  • 3+ years of hands-on experience in SEO & ASO, ideally in Fintech or BFSI.
  • Proven track record of scaling organic acquisition and driving measurable business impact.
  • Strong expertise in keyword planning, technical SEO, link-building strategies, and app store analytics.
  • Comfort with tools like GA4, GSC, Ahrefs, SEMRush, App Radar, AppTweak, Firebase, Data Studio, Hotjar, etc.
  • Good understanding of MSME user behavior, digital lending ecosystem, RBI compliance environment, and financial product search trends.
  • Strong analytical & stakeholder communication skills; ability to convert insights into action.

What You’ll Bring

  • Growth mindset with ownership attitude.
  • Ability to work cross-functionally with product, tech, and performance teams.
  • Passion for solving customer problems and building trust-first financial products.

Key Performance Indicators

  • Organic traffic & ranking improvements.
  • Growth in organic registrations / applications / revenue.
  • Increase in app store conversion rate & keyword rankings.
  • Reduction in dependency on paid acquisition & CAC improvement.
