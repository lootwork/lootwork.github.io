О PLEO

At Pleo, we believe that finance is the strategic force at the heart of how every business runs. When a finance team leads with its craft, they make better decisions and steer the business with more confidence. In order to do that, the busywork that comes with expense management needs to move out the way.

That’s why we built a spend management platform that streamlines the whole process, from employee spending to approvals and reconciliation. Finance teams get clarity and control of all the numbers and data in one place and employees get an experience that they actually enjoy.

The word “Pleo” in Greek means “more than you’d expect,” and that mindset has driven our growth since 2015 and our 40,000+ customers. Today we’re 750+ people from over 65 nationalities who take pride in understanding customer needs, turning complex problems into simple solutions, challenging the way things are done, and aiming for the highest outcome. We haven’t got everything figured out, but we’re committed to developing and delivering an experience as elevated as the craft of finance.

О роли

We're looking for a Business Applications Specialist (Marketing Technology) at Pleo. You'll own the technology behind Pleo's marketing engine. HubSpot Marketing Hub is at the centre of it, alongside the tools that feed it, extend it, and report on it. You'll be the person Marketing and Marketing Ops go to when they want to launch something, measure something, or fix something. You'll turn what they need into systems that are well designed, well documented, and built to last.

WHO YOU'LL BE WORKING WITH AND REPORTING TO

You'll report to the Director, Business Applications and work closely with teams in Marketing, Marketing Operations, BAT Engineering, and the Data team. Our team is highly collaborative. You'll also have the chance to partner across teams to ensure success.

Что предстоит делать

As a Business Applications Specialist (Marketing Technology), you will

- Own the Martech stack. Administer and optimise HubSpot Marketing Hub and connected tools such as Iterable, Segment, Amplitude, Appcues, Splash, Reachdesk, and our paid media and enrichment platforms.

- Turn requirements into solutions. Run discovery with marketing stakeholders, write process flows and user stories, and design solutions that match business goals.

- Configure and build. Deliver medium to large enhancements across workflows, lead scoring, lifecycle stages, segmentation, forms, campaigns, and properties, using standard functionality first.

- Protect data quality. Own marketing data hygiene, deduplication, lifecycle definitions, and attribution, so reporting is trusted at source rather than rebuilt in spreadsheets.

- Manage integrations and data flows. Work with BAT Engineering and the Data team on flows between HubSpot, Fivetran, BigQuery, Census, and the wider GTM stack.

- Test and release safely. Lead and document UAT, and follow our change management, peer review, and release practices before anything reaches production.

- Own vendor relationships. Manage 1–2 key Martech vendors, including support escalations, SLAs, licence usage, and input into renewals with Procurement.

- Enable the business. Train marketing teams on new systems and processes, and keep documentation current.

You can expect to work with the following tech stack: HubSpot Marketing Hub, Iterable, Segment, Amplitude, Appcues, Splash, Reachdesk, Fivetran, BigQuery, and Census.

WHAT YOU’LL BRING

You'll thrive in this role if you have

- 8+ years in business applications or systems administration, with a strong focus on marketing technology.

- Deep, hands-on HubSpot administration experience, especially Marketing Hub. Exposure to Sales Hub, Service Hub, or Operations Hub is a strong plus.

- A solid grasp of B2B SaaS marketing, including demand generation, lead management, lifecycle marketing, campaign operations, and attribution.

- Experience administering several Martech tools and the integrations between them, such as CDPs, marketing automation, product analytics, enrichment, and events.

- Strong data skills: data models, properties and associations, deduplication, and reporting. You can explain why the numbers don't match, and fix it.

- Confidence working with stakeholders. You can challenge a request, run a discovery workshop, and write a clear functional specification.

- Experience with structured delivery, including user stories, UAT, change management, and working in sprints.

- Clear written and spoken English. You'll work with teams across Europe every day.

Будет плюсом

- HubSpot certifications (Marketing Hub Implementation, Operations Hub, or Solutions Partner level).

- Experience with CRM re-implementations or large data migrations.

- Familiarity with SQL, BigQuery, reverse-ETL (Census), or iPaaS tools.

- Experience introducing AI into marketing workflows in a governed, secure way.

- Background in fintech or another regulated industry.
