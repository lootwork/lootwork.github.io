Senior Product Manager, Issuing

We have a massive opportunity ahead of us – to scale Adyen’s financial products by 10x, ushering in the next phase of growth for the company. Adyen has built a Financial OS spanning Issuing, Banking, and Capital, powered by our unified global platform, banking licenses, and dynamic identity data.

We are looking for a Senior Product Manager to help scale Issuing, which will be foundational for Adyen’s next generation Financial Products. The Issuing team is on a mission to enable businesses to monetize spend by providing fast, secure, and efficient payments using cards. The role is based out of Chicago and sits within the Issuing team, which is part of our Financial Services product team.

Successful candidates will have demonstrated passion for financial technology and are excited about Adyen’s opportunity to build a meaningful issuing business in the next 2-5 yrs. In this role, you will be responsible for building and executing the roadmap for the Servicing product. The Servicing team manages Adyen’s card and transaction auditability, program servicing and dispute management. In this role, you will collaborate closely with our Product, Commercial, Design, Engineering, Finance, Operations, Regulatory, and Compliance teams to deliver customer and business outcomes.

Что предстоит делать

  • Set strategy and roadmap for the Servicing team.
  • Understand customer needs and make investments in products and features that deliver customer value.
  • Lead platform-scale execution working closely with the Engineering, Product, Design, Finance, Operations, Regulatory, and Compliance teams to deliver an highly automated platform that scales globally without compromising reliability or trust.
  • Operate with a data and experimentation first mindset defining success metrics for the team and adopting an AI-first product development approach that uses experimentation to measurably improve outcomes.

Кто вы

  • Experience - 8+ years building high-scale products in fast growing tech or fintech environments. 2+ years of experience in cards or payments products.
  • Execution - track record of delivering complex product and platform initiatives at scale. Comfortable operating in ambiguity with high autonomy and ownership.
  • Customer centric - You are able to translate technical complexity back to customer impact and understand the needs of different customers.
  • Product - PM at heart with strong product instincts, data-based decision mindset, and highly focused on business impact. Demonstrated passion for AI-forward thinking.
  • Leadership - demonstrated ability of setting vision and strategy for teams. Strong sense of initiative and self-motivation.
  • Communication - a clear, compelling communicator who is comfortable interacting with both internal and external stakeholders from varying disciplines and cultures.
  • Mindset - entrepreneurial mindset with a strong bias toward action and ability to move fast in the midst of ambiguity.

The annual base salary range for this role is $180,000 - $250,000. Compensation is based on factors including qualifications, experience, and location. Total compensation package includes equity (RSUs), 401(k), health, dental, vision, and more.
