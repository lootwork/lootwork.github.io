Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy. Toast is building the platform that helps operators adapt, take control, and get back to what they do best: running the businesses they love. We've grown into a leading hospitality software company in the U.S., and we're now entering our next phase of growth, one defined by scale, complexity, and a new class of enterprise customers.

Toast is looking for a back-end focused Staff Software Engineer to join our Toast Connect team in Dublin. Toast Connect owns how everything outside Toast connects to Toast. We are the team that makes Toast a platform worth building on.

Our APIs, webhooks, frameworks, and development tools let partners and customers solve real problems fast, and our infrastructure lets the ecosystem scale safely. As the ecosystem grows, so does our charter: making Toast the source of truth for everything we know about the customer.

As a Staff Software Engineer, you'll be the technical lead for major parts of the architectural evolution of our Ecosystem. If you want to build something used every day by thousands of businesses, help those businesses grow and find success, and take on the challenge of scaling a platform, Toast is the place for you.

A day in the life (Responsibilities)

  • Lead the delivery of significant platform capabilities, from early design through production rollout
  • Design the systems that make development automated, self-serve, and fast for 3rd party developers, including sandbox provisioning and auth.
  • Anticipate how requirements will change and design systems that can absorb that change, exploring the problem fully before settling on a solution
  • Define best practices and help choose technologies to evolve our current tech stack while writing world-class applications
  • Evolve Toasts 3rd party developer-facing API surfaces and scale for agentic access
  • Lead efforts that measurably improve platform quality, reliability, and observability for the teams and customers who depend on it
  • Mentor other engineers and raise the bar on engineering practices across the organisation
  • Use AI tooling as a genuine force multiplier in your day-to-day work and help the team do the same

What you'll need to thrive (Requirements)

  • Proven track record (5+ years) in software engineering, with focus on data-intensive architecture
  • Proficient in Java, Kotlin or other object oriented languages
  • Platform-oriented mindset with expertise in designing robust APIs and scalable systems for both internal and external use
  • Deep technical expertise in microservices architectures, AWS ecosystem, distributed systems, and enterprise messaging infrastructure
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills to translate consuming teams’ requirements into developer-friendly solutions
  • Solid background in relational databases, with additional familiarity with columnar or document-oriented data stores considered a plus
  • Experience in developer ecosystems or collaborating with third-party developers is a distinct advantage
  • Strong customer empathy: you think about the developer integrating with our APIs and the restaurant owner who depends on that integration working every day

Our Tech Stack

  • A core of Java & Kotlin based backend services.
  • React SPAs leveraging Tailwind, GraphQL and Storybook.
  • OpenSearch, PostgreSQL, Dynamodb as core persistence.
  • Pulsar & Camel for message-based integrations.
  • And many more technologies that help us build a top class platform.
