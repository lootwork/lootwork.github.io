Adyen is looking for a data-driven, proactive, and collaborative Demand Generation Manager to join our team. This role will be based in our Stockholm office and report directly to the Head of Marketing.

О роли

The Demand Generation role is focused on driving measurable impact for Adyen’s new customer acquisition efforts. In this role, you’ll be responsible for planning, executing, and optimizing targeted Account-Based Marketing (ABM) campaigns across owned channels, paid media, and events.

The ideal candidate is experienced in meeting revenue-based goals through 1:Many, 1:Few, and 1:1 ABM campaigns, collaborating closely with SDRs, Sales, and Event teams. This is a matrixed-role requiring seamless collaboration across the region and Global Marketing teams to generate sales-accepted opportunities.

Что предстоит делать

  • Multi-Channel ABM: Plan, set up, and optimize 1:Many, 1:Few, and 1:1 account-based marketing campaigns to drive pipeline.
  • Event-Driven Demand Generation: Leverage both external trade shows and Adyen-owned events as core demand generation vehicles—driving targeted prospect attendance, designing pre-/post-event nurture tracks, and ensuring rigorous pipeline follow-up.
  • Targeting & Segmentation: Partner with SDRs and Sales to identify high-priority target accounts and build highly segmented lists based on market priorities.
  • Content & Messaging: Map, plan, and create compelling messages and creative tailored to prioritized accounts and specific event touch-points.
  • Digital Collaboration: Collaborate closely with the Digital Marketing Manager to create effective media strategies that surround target accounts.
  • Campaign Execution: Build and run regional email programs, direct marketing efforts, and optimized landing pages to convert interest into sales-accepted opportunities.
  • Global Alignment: Work closely with the Global Acquisition Marketing team to implement and scale localized strategies.
  • Analytics & Optimization: Leverage analytics tools to measure and determine the effectiveness of campaigns and event activations across all touch points.

Кто вы

  • 3+ years of experience in a B2B demand generation or integrated marketing role, with a proven track record of driving net-new customer acquisition.
  • 5+ years of experience in marketing or similar
  • Proficiency with ABM and marketing automation tools; knowledge of Marketo is a strong plus.
  • Analytical mindset with experience using data to optimize campaigns. Experience with Looker (or similar BI tools) is a plus.
  • A healthy balance between strategic thinking and hands-on execution—you can design the plan and build it, too.
  • Agile & Self-Motivated: A self-starting contributor with the ability to multitask, prioritize responsibilities, and navigate a fast-paced environment.
  • Native spoken and written Swedish is required.
  • Fluent spoken and written English is required.
  • Travel: Willingness and ability to travel domestically and internationally 20%+ (e.g., to support key trade shows and Adyen events).
