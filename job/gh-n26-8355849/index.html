О opportunity

Wealth is where customers grow their money with N26. It spans the full journey: saving day to day, putting idle cash to work in flexible cash funds, investing in stocks, ETFs and crypto, and understanding how it all adds up. That means the experience around savings, flexible cash funds, asset discovery, orders, and portfolio, and the connections between them. Customers increasingly move between these spaces, and our job is to make that feel like one clear, trustworthy experience. Assets under custody have more than doubled in the last year, and the number of products and initiatives in Wealth keeps growing.

You'll work across the domain rather than on a single product area. Depending on what the team needs, that could mean designing how customers discover and understand flexible cash funds, refining the order flow and order history, helping customers read and act on their portfolio, bringing clarity to asset discovery and asset detail pages, or designing the interaction patterns and data visualisations (like performance charts) that make complex financial information easy to grasp. You'll work on self-contained initiatives of low to mid complexity, with guidance from your paired designer, and your scope will grow as you do.

You'll be designing for a regulated financial product used by millions, where trust, clarity, and detail matter. The bar is high, and the work matters.

In your next challenge, you will

  • Design well-scoped features and improvements across Wealth, from understanding the problem through exploration to shipped work.
  • Design intuitive interactions and polished interfaces, with careful attention to edge cases and error states.
  • Help customers understand products like flexible cash funds, where clear explanation and the right moment to surface them make the difference.
  • Use and contribute to our design system, Pulse, keeping your work consistent with the patterns the team relies on.
  • Plan and run simple usability tests and interviews, and turn what you learn into clear, actionable insights.
  • Use data to understand how customers behave and to check whether your work is making a difference.
  • Partner closely with product, engineering, research, and data to ship work that lands, and learn from it once it's live.
  • Explain the "why" behind your decisions to your team, and seek feedback early and often.
  • Use AI tools as part of your design practice, for exploration, prototyping, and synthesis.
  • Share what you learn with your peers and suggest improvements to how the team works.

What you’ll need to be successful

  • You're fluent in Figma, and you keep getting better at it. You build with Auto Layout by default, so designs behave properly across screen sizes, languages and dynamic content. You work with components, variants and variables, and you move quickly with commands and shortcuts instead of reaching for the mouse. A tidy, well-named file is part of your craft. You're not satisfied with "good enough" in the tool and enjoy learning new features and techniques, then sharing them with the team.
  • You're curious about the problem before the solution. You ask who the user is, what they need, and what constraints you're working within.
  • You like to ship. You prototype to learn, share work early, and prefer real feedback over polishing in isolation.
  • You're curious about money, saving and investing, or willing to become so. You don't need to be an expert, but you want to make complex financial topics easy for everyone to understand.
  • You use AI to work better, not to skip the thinking. You're curious about how AI is changing design and product, and you use it to explore options, prototype and synthesise research faster. You also know it doesn't replace good craft or judgement. You can tell when an output is generic, inaccurate or wrong for a regulated financial product, and you take responsibility for the final result.
  • You think beyond the single screen. You see how savings, funds, orders, and portfolio connect, and you design with the wider journey in mind.
  • You're a generous collaborator. You give and receive feedback well, and you want to make the work better, not just your part of it.
  • You're organised and reliable. You manage your own tasks, meet timelines, and speak up early when something is at risk.
