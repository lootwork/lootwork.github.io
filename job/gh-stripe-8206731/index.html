Кто мы

О Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.

О команде

The Builder Experience Team makes it easier for Stripe’s product teams to build the right products, ship them with confidence, and help users realize value from them.

In order to build a great product, product teams must develop a deep understanding of users, shape and test ideas, coordinate across functions, navigate launch requirements, identify risks, gather feedback, and manage products after launch. Today, many of these workflows are fragmented, manual, or built around processes and tools that predate recent advances in AI.

We are a small, AI-native team reimagining how Stripe builds, ships, and ramps products. We experiment with new approaches across AI agents, automation, and process—and scale the ones that work. Our initial areas of focus include reinventing launch readiness from gates into guidelines, giving product teams richer and more timely context, and shortening the feedback loops between builders, users, Sales, and Support.

Our users are Stripe’s product managers, engineers, designers, researchers – and we help to simplify the interactions with the cross-functional teams that help bring products to market. We work closely with teams including all of our business unit PMs, Product Operations, and Kai, Stripe’s internal AI platform.

Что предстоит делать

We’re looking for product-minded engineers who want to rethink how products get built—not simply digitize existing processes. You’ll identify high-friction parts of Stripe’s product-development lifecycle, rapidly prototype new approaches, put them in the hands of real teams, and turn the strongest experiments into scaled solutions used by every product team at Stripe.

This is a hands-on role with broad product and technical scope. You’ll work across the stack, combining traditional software engineering with emerging AI capabilities. You’ll operate in ambiguous problem spaces where the right product, interface, and technical architecture must be discovered together.

Вам предстоит

  • Design and build tools that help Stripe teams shape better products, ship with greater speed and confidence, and learn from users more quickly.
  • Develop AI-native workflows and agents that synthesize context, automate repetitive work, surface risks, and help builders make better decisions.
  • Own projects end to end: identify high-value problems, develop prototypes, test them with users, measure their impact, and scale the approaches that work.
  • Work across backend systems, data sources, internal platforms, and polished user-facing experiences.
  • Partner directly with product managers, engineers, designers, researchers, and cross-functional teams to understand how work happens in practice.
  • Integrate with platforms such as Kai and Compass while identifying where new foundational capabilities are needed.
  • Apply strong judgment to the use of sensitive internal context, including appropriate attention to security, privacy, permissions, and user trust.
  • Help define how the team operates, including its technical foundations, product-development practices, and standards for experimentation and quality.
  • Contribute to the team’s roadmap by identifying systemic sources of friction and opportunities to improve Stripe’s product-development lifecycle.

Кто вы

We're looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • 5+ years of backend and/or full-stack software development experience
  • Demonstrated technical experience leading the design and delivery of new product experiences from prototype to production
  • Strong full-stack engineering skills, including experience building reliable backend services and polished user-facing product experiences.
  • Ability to operate effectively in high-ambiguity environments: framing problems, experimenting & adjusting, making right-sized technical decisions, and creating an actionable path forward.
  • Excellent cross-functional and cross-organizational collaboration skills, with the ability to align teams with different systems, priorities, and constraints.
  • Strong engineering judgment around system reliability, operational excellence, security, and maintainability.
  • A product-minded approach to engineering, with a focus on delivering meaningful outcomes for users and the business.

Будет плюсом

  • Experience with Java, Ruby, and/or TypeScript. We care more about strong engineering fundamentals and the ability to learn than expertise in a particular language or framework.
  • Experience delivering user facing products at Stripe (Payments, MM, RevSuite, etc), or experience delivering user facing products pre-dating Stripe.
  • Experience working on 0→1 products or taking new capabilities from concept through launch and iteration.
