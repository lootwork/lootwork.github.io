We believe that the way people interact with their finances will drastically improve in the next few years. We’re dedicated to empowering this transformation by building the tools and experiences that thousands of developers use to create their own products. Plaid powers the tools millions of people rely on to live a healthier financial life. We work with thousands of companies like Venmo, SoFi, several of the Fortune 500, and many of the largest banks to make it easy for people to connect their financial accounts to the apps and services they want to use. Plaid’s network covers 12,000 financial institutions across the US, Canada, UK and Europe. Founded in 2013, the company is headquartered in San Francisco with offices in New York, Seattle, Washington D.C., Raleigh, London, and Amsterdam.

Кратко

The Product Sales team at Plaid combines deep product and market expertise to drive adoption and revenue for Plaid's most strategically important solutions. We partner closely with Account Executives, Account Managers, and GTM leadership to develop and close deals across both new and existing customers. The Payments team specializes in bank payments, ACH risk, and money movement — products at the core of Plaid's growth that represent a significant and under-penetrated market opportunity.

Роль

As an Account Executive, Product Sales - Payments, you will drive revenue growth for Plaid's growing suite of Payments solutions– Account Verification, ACH Risk Optimization, and Money Movement. Your primary focus is partnering with account teams to develop and close opportunities.

You bring deep payments expertise and a commercial skillset to customer conversations at all levels, translating business problems into Plaid solutions and contributing directly to how we go to market.

Обязанности

- Drive ACV attainment for Payments solutions across your assigned segments, with clear accountability for pipeline generation and deal execution

- Partner with AEs and AMs on discovery through close; take direct ownership of select high-value or complex opportunities as needed

- Serve as the subject matter expert on Plaid's Payments products in customer meetings, competitive situations, and industry events

- Conduct deep discovery to uncover customer pain points and connect them to Plaid's Payments solutions

- Maintain real-time visibility into win/loss trends, competitive dynamics, and product-market fit across your segment

- Relay customer and market insights to Product and PMM to inform roadmap priorities and go-to-market strategy

- Support sales enablement by developing materials, coaching AEs on solution positioning, and sharing competitive intelligence

Требования

- 7+ years of B2B sales or commercial experience with a track record of quota attainment

- Deep expertise in payments, with a strong preference for bank payments (ACH, RTP) and payment risk

- Experience carrying a direct quota or commercial target with demonstrated results

- Strong discovery and solution-selling skills; ability to navigate complex, multi-stakeholder deals

- Ability to build relationships with VP-level buyers and above

- Strong cross-functional collaboration skills across Sales, Product, and Marketing

- Comfortable operating with ambiguity in a fast-moving, high-growth environment

- Nice-to-have: Experience in industries with billing & recurring payments use cases (lending, insurance, etc.)
