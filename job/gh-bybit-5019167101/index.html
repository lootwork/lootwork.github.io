Основные обязанности

  • Manage vulnerability scanning and day-to-day vulnerability management across all IT assets, including hosts, middleware, containers, and web applications.
  • Assess and prioritize vulnerabilities, drive remediation by business teams within agreed SLAs, and coordinate urgent responses to high-severity vulnerabilities and vulnerabilities actively exploited in the wild.
  • Establish an end-to-end vulnerability remediation process, produce regular operational reports, and continuously reduce outstanding risk exposure.
  • Define and implement security baselines for hosts, containers, and cloud platforms in line with CIS Benchmarks, and develop automated compliance checks.
  • Regularly scan for configuration drift and drive remediation. Support compliance audits by providing key metrics, including security baseline coverage.
  • Manage enterprise email security operations, including anti-phishing, anti-spam, email gateway policy management, and phishing incident response.
  • Implement security controls for instant messaging and other workplace collaboration platforms. Identify and address risks such as anomalous account activity, data leakage, and social engineering attacks.
  • Contribute to security awareness training and phishing simulation exercises to strengthen security awareness across the organization.
  • Participate in security incident investigations and response, supporting root cause analysis and remediation.

Требования

  • At least two years of experience in information security, with hands-on experience in vulnerability management, security baseline hardening, or workplace IT security.
  • Familiarity with vulnerability scanning tools such as Nessus, Qualys, and Nuclei, and an understanding of CVSS scoring and vulnerability prioritization methods.
  • Knowledge of CIS Benchmarks, with practical experience hardening Linux/Windows systems or cloud environments.
  • Familiarity with email security mechanisms, including SPF, DKIM, and DMARC, as well as leading email security gateway solutions.
  • Working proficiency in Python or shell scripting, with the ability to develop scripts that automate security operations.
  • Experience with compliance frameworks such as PCI DSS and ISO 27001 in the financial services or cryptocurrency industry is preferred.
