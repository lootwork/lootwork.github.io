Scopely is looking for a Director, Marketing Creative Intelligence to join our Creative Intelligence team working in our Barcelona studio on a hybrid basis.

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day—whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues, or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing, and innovating in the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people around the world daily.

Creative Intelligence, a central team within Publishing, leads the strategy, capabilities, and tools that turn creative performance data into actionable insights and recommendations, improving marketing and UA creative strategy across our entire game portfolio. The team helps explain what creative works, why it works, and what we should make, scale, stop, test, or iterate next.

This is an opportunity to shape how Scopely uses creative intelligence to make faster, more objective marketing decisions across a global portfolio of mobile games. The Director will build the team, operating model, and partnerships that turn creative performance insights into action.

Что предстоит делать

The Director of Creative Intelligence will lead the strategy, roadmap, and team responsible for turning creative, marketing, and performance insights into better UA and creative decisions.

  • Set the Creative Intelligence strategy and roadmap. Define the function’s priorities, goals, operating model, measurement needs, and roadmap across creative performance, creative testing, competitor intelligence, analytics, AI, and data capabilities.
  • Lead gaming UA and creative-performance analysis. Use creative review, tagging, UA performance data, and testing to understand what is working, why it is working, and where opportunities exist across concepts, hooks, formats, messaging, and executions.
  • Turn insights into action and influence teams. Translate analysis into clear recommendations for what to make, scale, stop, test, or iterate. Connect insights to creative briefs, testing plans, campaign decisions, and future creative development. Build trust in the recommendations and influence Marketing, UA, Flamed, and game teams to adopt them.
  • Own creative testing and measurement. Own, improve, and develop test plans and frameworks that answer important creative questions. Interpret A/B tests and other experiments, evaluate relevant performance outcomes, and recommend the next creative or investment decision.
  • Lead competitor creative intelligence. Establish approaches for tracking competitor creative, category trends, and creative patterns. Translate those findings into actionable recommendations for UA, creative, and game teams, and support relevant early creative questions for pre-launch games.
  • Build scalable tools and workflows. Prioritize tagging, data quality, dashboards, reporting, self-service tools, and AI-enabled workflows based on business impact and stakeholder needs.
  • Partner with Analytics, Data Science, and Engineering. Define business questions, hypotheses, requirements, success criteria, and desired outcomes. Lead technical partners through delivery, validation, adoption, and continued improvement without needing to build the underlying infrastructure yourself.
  • Lead and develop the team. Lead, coach, and develop a multidisciplinary team with a high bar for strategic thinking, analytical quality, creative judgment, communication, urgency, and follow-through.

Кого мы ищем

  • 8–12 years of relevant experience in mobile gaming, with a strong background in User Acquisition, game marketing, or performance creative. Gaming experience is required.
  • Hands-on experience using creative-performance data and A/B testing to understand what is working, why it is working, and what should be scaled, stopped, iterated, or developed next.
  • Ability to connect creative decisions to acquisition and downstream business outcomes, then translate findings into clear recommendations, briefs, testing plans, messaging, concepts, or campaign decisions.
  • Experience influencing UA, creative, and marketing teams to adopt evidence-based recommendations and change their plans or decisions.
  • Experience with competitor creative analysis and identifying creative patterns, opportunities, or market context that inform marketing and creative decisions.
  • Experience leading and developing multidisciplinary teams across areas such as creative analytics, creative testing, competitor intelligence, marketing intelligence,, or Data.
  • Experience partnering with analysts, engineers, and data teams to turn business questions into useful tools, insights, and workflows, with hands-on comfort applying AI to creative analysis and reporting.
  • Excellent communication, stakeholder-management, and problem-solving skills, with the ability to build trust, navigate ambiguity, operate with urgency, and follow through from question to measurable impact.
