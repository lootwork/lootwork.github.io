Not for symptoms to get worse. Not for a six‑month appointment. Not for a system to catch up. But that’s exactly how healthcare works today. You wait, until you can’t.

🎯 THE MISSION & IMPACT: LA MÉDECINE DU TRAVAIL ✨

In France, every employee is entitled to a doctor whose job is to ensure they stay healthy at work. On paper: yearly check-ups, prevention plans, workplace risk assessments, return-to-work visits. In practice: an under-invested corner of French healthcare, with medical teams drowning in paperwork instead of doing medicine, and expediting visit after visit with little care as a result.

We think that's backwards. If millions of French workers each get to see an occupational health doctor multiple times in their career, that's one of the biggest untapped prevention channels in the country - and we're going to make it live up to its promise.

Alan Tech is building the tech and operations behind Prévenir https://prevenir.fr/, a brand new occupational health service, since 2025. We're building a proprietary medical stack from scratch, scaling operations to dozens of thousands of workers, and just opened Prévenir's first physical medical center in Paris - inside the Alan building.

A startup inside the startup: Prévenir has its own product, its own P&L, its own regulatory framework, and its own medical team. You get the autonomy and pace of a 0-to-1 team, sitting inside one of Europe's fastest-growing healthtechs, with access to Alan's expertise, its stack, and its ecosystem.
