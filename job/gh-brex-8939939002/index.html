Why join us

Brex is the intelligent finance platform that enables companies to spend smarter and move faster in more than 200 markets. By combining global corporate cards and banking with intuitive spend management, bill pay, and travel software, Brex enables founders and finance teams to accelerate operations, gain real-time visibility, and control spend effortlessly. Brex’s AI-native automation and world-class service eliminate manual expense and accounting tasks for customers so they can focus on what matters most. Tens of thousands of the world's best companies run on Brex, including DoorDash, Coinbase, Robinhood, Zoom, Plaid, Reddit, and SeatGeek.

Working at Brex allows you to push your limits, challenge the status quo, and collaborate with some of the brightest minds in the industry. We’re committed to building a diverse team and inclusive culture and believe your potential should only be limited by how big you can dream. We make this a reality by empowering you with the tools, resources, and support you need to grow your career.

Engineering at Brex

Engineering at Brex is about building systems that scale with speed and intention. Our teams span Software, Data, Security, and IT, and operate with high autonomy and deep collaboration. We tackle hard technical problems, own our outcomes, and push for excellence at every level - from architecture to deployment. It’s an environment where engineering is a craft, and builders become leaders.

Что предстоит делать

As a Frontend Engineer on the Banking Experience team, you’ll build and scale the web and mobile features our banking customers rely on every day. We’re a customer-obsessed, full-stack team: you’ll own features from user experience through data models and metrics, working closely with Product, Design and various platform teams. You’ll use data to decide what to build, advocate for customers, and help us launch into new markets. We’re looking for engineers who care as much about the product as the code, and who want to drive direct business impact with a high quality bar.

Where you’ll work

This role will be based in our San Francisco office. We are a hybrid environment that combines the energy and connections of being in the office with the benefits and flexibility of working from home. We currently require a minimum of three coordinated days in the office per week, Mondays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays. As a perk, we also have up to four weeks per year of fully remote work!

Обязанности

  • Work with engineers across the company to build new features and products.
  • Work side-by-side with user facing teams (Sales, Support) to best understand the needs of our customers.
  • Design and implement experiments to improve our customer’s experiences.
  • Own problems end-to-end, thinking through everything from user experience, data models, scalability, operability and ongoing metrics.
  • Tune and polish features to a high-degree of excellence
  • Data driven outcomes

Требования

  • 4+ years of professional experience architecting, developing, and testing client-side code end to end
  • Experience developing software for the Web platform, including cross-browser compatibility
  • Experience with modern tools like ES6+, React, GraphQL, & Webpack, Vite, React Native (not a hard requirement)
  • Uphold our high engineering standards
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.

However, the starting base pay will depend on a number of factors including the candidate’s location, skills, experience, market demands, and internal pay parity. Depending on the position offered, equity and other forms of compensation may be provided as part of a total compensation package.
