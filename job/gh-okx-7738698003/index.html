О Opportunity

We are looking for a Senior Institutional Liquidity Product Manager to own the management and improvement of liquidity across OKX's spot, margin, futures, perpetual swap and options markets.

You will work closely with institutional traders, market makers, HFT firms and quantitative teams to identify liquidity gaps and develop market-structure, product and commercial solutions that improve execution quality and market competitiveness.

This is a strategic and hands-on role combining market microstructure expertise, quantitative analysis, product delivery and commercial ownership. You will partner with Quant, Data, Engineering, Risk, Relationship Managers , Operations and external liquidity providers to turn order-book data and client trading behaviour into measurable improvements in spread, depth, slippage, trading volume, market share and resilience during stressed markets.

Что предстоит делать

Liquidity Strategy, Analytics and Delivery

  • Analyse institutional client needs, market-maker and HFT trading behaviour, market trends, competitor liquidity, fee structures, order types and liquidity programmes to identify opportunities for differentiation.
  • Own the liquidity product roadmap across spot, margin, futures, perpetual swaps and options, including monitoring capabilities, order types, market structure, maker protection and liquidity improvement initiatives.
  • Translate quantitative analysis and institutional trading requirements into clear product specifications and solutions. Work closely with Quant, Data, Engineering, Matching Engine, Risk and Relationship managers teams to deliver scalable solutions from problem definition through launch.
  • Define targets, baselines, guardrails and post-launch measurement for each liquidity initiative. Use controlled or phased rollouts where appropriate, evaluate causal impact and ROI, and continuously adjust the roadmap based on data and client feedback.

Institutional and Market Maker Engagement

  • Understand participants' market-making, hedging, arbitrage, inventory-risk and latency-sensitive strategies, and translate these needs into product and programme requirements.
  • Partner with Institutional Relationship Management teams to onboard and engage liquidity providers, communicate product and programme changes, gather structured feedback and improve participation in priority markets.

What We Look For In You

  • Proven experience in liquidity management, electronic trading or market structure within a crypto or traditional exchange, market maker, HFT firm, proprietary trading firm, quantitative fund or institutional trading platform.
  • Strong understanding of institutional and HFT trading strategies, including market making, cross-venue arbitrage, price discovery, hedging, inventory and risk management, adverse selection, toxic flow and latency-sensitive execution.
  • A quantitative background in mathematics, statistics, computer science, financial engineering or a related field is strongly preferred.
  • Hands-on ability with SQL and Python, with experience analysing large-scale order-book, order and trade data. Experience with Level 2 or Level 3 market data is highly valued.
  • Deep knowledge of market microstructure, order books, matching, tick size, maker/taker economics, order types, execution quality and liquidity dynamics across spot and derivatives.
  • Front-to-back knowledge of crypto, FX, equities, fixed income or listed derivatives, with the ability to apply that knowledge to new products and market structures.
  • Strong product execution and stakeholder-management skills, with the ability to drive cross-functional delivery across Quant, Data, Engineering, Risk, Finance, Sales and Operations from problem definition to measurable outcome.
  • Able to communicate credibly with traders, quants, engineers, senior executives and external partners.
  • Fluent in English and Mandarin.
