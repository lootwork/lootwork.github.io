YOUR IMPACT

As a Treasury Specialist at Mollie, you play a critical role in managing our cash, banks, foreign currency positions and investments. You also actively support the design and roll out of our Financial Services products.

You're not just part of the CFO team. You're an integral part of the Financial Services Domain, and you're responsible for driving product improvements across multiple problem spaces, from Cash Management and Reporting to Banking Interactions. You bring a strong understanding of cash management, FX and investments, plus great drive and strong analytical skills.

This role suits you if you're not afraid of hands-on work and you also want to work on new strategic initiatives and product development in a fast-paced environment. You'll join our Merchant Finance and Treasury Team in Amsterdam.

WHAT YOU'LL SHIP

- Cash management activities that run smoothly, alongside better reporting and forecasting processes.

- Mollie's excess cash and multi-million investment portfolio, managed to get the most out of our money.

- Policies and procedures on Treasury and Financial Risk topics, developed and kept up to date.

- Uncovered FX risk positions identified, with clear recommendations on how to manage them.

- A banking infrastructure built and optimised for efficient, cost-effective payments.

- Company-wide growth projects and projects across domains, including new products and payment methods, that help Mollie build first class finance and treasury products for our customers.

- A treasury management system developed further, to support more automation of our Treasury operations.

WHAT YOU'LL BRING

- At least 5 years of relevant Treasury experience, with knowledge of international cash management, forecasting, foreign exchange management and internal controls.

- An advanced degree, preferably in Finance or Business Administration, a comparable study, or demonstrably a comparable level of knowledge and thinking.

- You like to get things done and bring energy to solve problems for our customers and internal stakeholders. You're proactive, analytical and take strong ownership.

- A mindset that looks for solutions, and the ability to come up with new ideas to improve our products and services.

- You're a confident communicator with excellent spoken and written English. You can align and influence stakeholders across Finance, Product and the wider business, and explain complex treasury topics clearly to non-specialists.

- You enjoy sharing knowledge and supporting less experienced colleagues, and you're comfortable contributing to domain-wide knowledge sharing.
