HOW you will contribute to the company as a Senior Software Engineer?

As a Senior Software Engineer at Pennylane, your squad is your primary focus. You will contribute to building reliable, scalable and user-focused products, while helping the engineers around you grow and raising the quality of our technical execution. You will:

- Understand and frame user and product problems, using your product knowledge to help your squad deliver simple, pragmatic and valuable solutions.

- Lead significant technical deliveries within your squad, from understanding the problem to designing, implementing, testing and operating the solution in production.

- Investigate and resolve complex production issues, using monitoring, observability and data analysis tools to identify root causes and prevent recurrence.

- Design and evolve robust solutions, identifying performance, scalability and reliability opportunities and turning them into pragmatic technical improvements.

- Bring strong expertise in at least one major part of our stack, while being able to join a new team and become autonomous on a high-impact initiative within a few weeks.

- Improve engineering practices across the organization, through high-quality code reviews, pair programming, documentation, RFCs and the adoption of AI-assisted development without compromising software quality.

- Act as a role model and a driver for other engineers, sharing knowledge, providing actionable feedback and helping the team continuously improve its technical skills and ways of working.

- Collaborate effectively with other teams when needed, proactively unblocking technical topics and clearly communicating progress, risks, decisions and results to the relevant stakeholders.

- Contribute to the Pennylane tech community, through technical content, onboarding, interviews, incident resolution, tech initiatives, meetups or other community-building activities.

🥇 You’re the right candidate if you…

- Have strong hands-on experience with Ruby on Rails (at least 7-8 years)

- Combine technical expertise with strong product and business understanding, enjoy solving complex problems and care deeply about your users.

- Are a collaborative team player, communicate clearly and take ownership of delivering reliable, pragmatic solutions.

- Like being challenged and solving complex problems (accounting is complex in Europe)

- Have strong communication skills and are capable of articulating your ideas clearly
