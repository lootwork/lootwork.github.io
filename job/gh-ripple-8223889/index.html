THE WORK

Ripple is expanding its Information Security function and is seeking an experienced Information Security professional to join us in building a world-class Information Security program.

This is a critical role based in our Brazil entity, integral to establishing Ripple's local operational presence and governance to operate as a Sociedade Prestadora de Serviços de Ativos Virtuais (SPSAV/VASP). This position will be key in building and delivering a strong information security and ICT risk program locally, supporting Ripple’s expansion of regulated digital asset activities across Latin America.

Что предстоит делать

  • Serve as the operational lead for cybersecurity governance, risk management, and compliance in Brazil. Work directly with local leadership on the entity's information security program and incident-response capabilities.
  • Prepare, coordinate, and directly support local leadership in regulatory authorization review, ongoing supervisory engagements, and examinations.
  • Provide periodic updates to Ripple Brazil's board and management on the InfoSec program, ICT risk posture, and regulatory developments.
  • Maintain compliance with Brazilian regulatory and security frameworks relevant to virtual asset service providers, such as central bank cybersecurity and data protection requirements.
  • Map new and evolving guidance issued by the BCB, COAF, and ANPD to Ripple's internal security controls library to identify gaps and enhancement opportunities.
  • Scope, plan, and independently conduct periodic technical control testing to validate the effectiveness of information security controls across our cloud, infrastructure, and application environments.
  • Directly access systems to pull technical evidence (logs, system settings, access reports) for control testing, audits, and independent assurance reviews.
  • Collaborate with global InfoSec teams to develop, maintain, and localize InfoSec Policies, Standards, and Procedures for local regulatory and data protection compliance.
  • Lead local oversight of outsourced IT, custody-adjacent, and security service providers, including third-party due diligence and contingency-planning obligations for relevant service providers.
  • Support Ripple Brazil's Data Protection Officer on security-of-processing matters.
  • Support group-wide Information Security GRC initiatives, ensuring cross-entity alignment as Ripple's regulated footprint in Latin America grows.
  • Partner with global Information Security, Engineering, Compliance, Finance, Product, Legal, and Sales teams to provide security guidance with direct impact on Ripple's product security and customer trust in Brazil.
  • Support regional customer-facing activities, including drafting Brazil-specific security messaging, responding to security due diligence questionnaires, and reviewing customer security contractual language.
  • Develop and maintain information security policies, standards, GRC process documentation, system workflows, and evidence collection procedures.
  • Attend and participate in Brazilian and regional digital asset industry events and discussions to stay current on evolving regulation, threats, and best practices.

WHAT YOU'LL BRING

  • 5+ years of experience in information security risk management and compliance, ideally including direct exposure to a BCB-regulated (or equivalent) financial institution's authorization or examination process.
  • A Bachelor's Degree in a relevant field or equivalent professional experience.
  • Based in Brazil, with proven experience in the Brazilian financial or FinTech sector and a strong working understanding of the BCB regulatory landscape.
  • Solid understanding of the BCB's PSAV/SPSAV framework and familiarity with the LGPD.
  • Proficiency with common information security frameworks, such as ISO 27001, SOC2, NIST, and CSA Cloud Controls Matrix (CCM).
  • A proven understanding of information security governance in the context of cloud-native FinTech companies operating in a distributed and fast-moving environment.
  • Comfortable working independently in technical environments, quickly learning new systems and processes.
  • Hands-on experience pulling and analyzing technical evidence, including system logs, configuration data, audit reports, and database queries.
  • Ability to develop clear, audience-tailored documentation, SOPs, and training content.
  • Experience collaborating effectively with multi-functional teams of engineers, product managers, and compliance experts.
  • Familiarity with tools such as Jira, Confluence, Okta, AWS, Tines, and integrated GRC platforms.
  • Desirable certifications include CISSP, CISM, CISA, and AWS Certified Security.
  • Fluency in Portuguese; professional proficiency in English.
