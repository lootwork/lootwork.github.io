Solaris is Europe's leading embedded finance platform. Solaris’ full German banking license and proprietary modular B2B tech stack empowers its partners – from SMEs to large, multinational, non-financial companies – to offer compliant, customer-centric banking services, providing seamless experiences to customers across all industries. Founded in 2016, Solaris pioneered the Banking-as-a-Service market with an unparalleled combination of tech and banking. Solaris is headquartered in Berlin and employs 300 people in Europe.

Your Role

  • Support Risk Controlling in the implementation and compliance of requirements (internal and external regulatory requirements) for the bank.
  • Assisting in the operational execution of risk management and controlling processes (identification, assessment, control, monitoring, and communication).
  • Assisting in the preparation of risk reports related to Risk Controlling.
  • Contributing to the ongoing development and refinement of methods related, including the creation and improvement of templates and internal policies
  • Contributing to the execution and documentation of controls.
