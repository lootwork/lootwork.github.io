At Zopa, our Analysts don’t just analyse the business – they help shape it.

From designing our products and customer experiences, to deciding our lending criteria and target markets, our Analysts are at the heart of every major decision we make.

You’ll combine creative problem solving with strong numerical and communication skills, turning data into insights and ideas into real-world impact. You’ll have meaningful ownership from the start, while learning from talented colleagues across the business.

HOW WILL WE HELP YOU GET STARTED?

You don’t need to arrive knowing every tool or technique! Your first four months will include a structured graduate training programme designed to give you a broad foundation across Analytics at Zopa. You’ll rotate through four placements and complete a project for each, giving you the chance to apply your learnings to real business problems. We make extensive use of AI-tools (both generic foundation models and skills developed locally) in all our work including knowledge discovery, code development, and task automation. Training in relevant AI tool use is also part of each of the placements:

  • Intro to Zopa & Technical Skills – get to know Zopa, our products and how we use data, while deepening your technical skills (including SQL and Python)
  • AI Applications – Learn how analysis using large language models and associated transformer-based architectures is applied to real business problems
  • Data Science – Develop your data science skills, including building gradient boosted and sequence-based models, and put them into practice in a competition to build the best model to address a real prediction problem
  • Product Economics – understand the economics behind our product design and lending decisions, and learn techniques for forecasting future value

Together, these placements provide broad training in the tools, concepts and business context used across our Analytics teams, preparing you to join any of the analyst teams within Zopa.

WHAT TEAMS DO GRADUATE ANALYSTS JOIN AT ZOPA?

After completing our training programme, you'll join one of our teams. As you grow, you'll have opportunities to explore new projects and internal moves. You can expect to:

  • Take real ownership from day one – using data to investigate problems, test ideas and recommend improvements to our products, customer experience and business performance.
  • Build your technical skills using cutting edge tools and techniques
  • Turn data into insights and communicate these to stakeholders across functions and levels

You'll learn more about each area of Analytics at Zopa throughout our hiring process and training programme. To give you a flavour of the different opportunities…

Credit Strategy 🎯

Shape the lending decisions that drive Zopa’s growth and profitability. You will develop, model and test lending strategies, explore how changes affect customers and commercial performance, and help turn analysis into real lending decisions.

Product Analytics 📊

Build insight and recommendations that drive product decisions, prioritisation and strategy. You will run in-depth analysis, design tests, monitor performance, and build self-serve tools to enable others in their work (for example, conversational analytics).

Data Science 🧪

Design and deploy cutting-edge predictive models that power Zopa’s products and decisions. You will be pushing boundaries in both the data sources and techniques that you apply to some of our most challenging problems, taking ideas from the experimentation stage through to production deployment.

Technology ⚙️

The roles of Zopians in our Technology teams are varied and rapidly evolving as the power of the tools we use to support building, testing, and deploying software grow more powerful. We have exciting opportunities across a broad set of disciplines including software engineering, data engineering, analytic engineering, information security, and technology delivery.
