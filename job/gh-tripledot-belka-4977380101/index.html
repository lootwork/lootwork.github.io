О роли

We're looking for a Product Scientist to join our small but fast-moving product analytics team. You'll own experimentation and data-driven decision-making across our mobile games portfolio — designing and analyzing A/B tests, building user segmentations, uncovering product insights, and directly influencing how millions of players experience our games.

This isn't a role where you'll wait for someone to hand you a task. You'll work shoulder-to-shoulder with product managers, game designers, and engineers — proactively identifying opportunities, challenging assumptions, and turning data into real product impact. We move fast, ship often, and actively use AI tools to accelerate our analyses and workflows.

Основные обязанности

  • Design, run, and evaluate A/B tests across multiple game titles — ensuring experiments are statistically rigorous and lead to clear, actionable outcomes.
  • Define and monitor success metrics (retention, monetization, engagement) and build analytical frameworks that drive product decisions.
  • Perform deep-dive analyses: user segmentation, cohort analysis, funnel breakdowns, and predictive modeling to surface growth and retention opportunities.
  • Translate business questions into data requirements — working with engineering to ensure proper event tracking and data reliability.
  • Build dashboards and reports that communicate insights to stakeholders clearly and effectively.
  • Collaborate with product teams to develop hypotheses, prioritize experiments, and foster a culture of continuous learning and rapid iteration.
  • Leverage AI tools and automation to speed up analyses, generate insights, and improve team efficiency.

Обязательные навыки и опыт

  • 2–4 years of experience in product analytics, data science, or a similar quantitative role — ideally in mobile gaming, apps, or a fast-paced tech environment.
  • Strong A/B testing expertise: you can design experiments end-to-end, choose appropriate statistical methods, interpret results, and communicate trade-offs.
  • Proficient in SQL — you're comfortable writing complex queries and working with large datasets.
  • Python or R for data analysis, statistical modeling, and automation.
  • Segmentation & cohort analysis experience — you know how to slice user behavior to find what matters.
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in a quantitative field (statistics, economics, mathematics, computer science, or similar). A thesis or research experience is a strong plus.
  • Self-starter mentality — you thrive with autonomy, take ownership, and execute quickly in a dynamic environment.
  • Excellent English communication skills (written and verbal).
