About us

Hi, we are Ten Square Games, and we live games! No, not a typo - but a level up from loving! We are on a mission of giving players excellent outdoor experiences on their mobiles. With global hits, such as Fishing Clash, we know how to make the best of technology, fun, and fair play. With 77 million registered players in 2025 and 300+ people on board, we keep growing and learning, having the insatiable hunger for the next success. If you also like to explore the unknown and know how to own it, we want to work with you!

What is this job all about?

As Head of Product (f/m/d) you’ll have the chance to shape a game from the very first idea through launch and beyond, setting the vision and guiding the team every step of the way. You’ll turn player insights and data into features that truly engage, delight, and keep players coming back. Working side by side with production, design, and marketing, you’ll make a real impact — not only on the success of your title but also on the future of TSG.
