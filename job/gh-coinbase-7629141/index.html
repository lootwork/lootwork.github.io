As a Staff Software Engineer, Frontend on the Advanced Trading team within the Consumer & Business group, you'll lead the front-end technical strategy for Coinbase Advanced, our trading platform for experienced traders. You'll own the performance, reliability, and scalability of our web and mobile trading clients, driving low-latency interfaces that serve traders across multiple instrument types. This is a high-impact role at the intersection of crypto, trading infrastructure, and consumer product, where your technical leadership will shape both the platform and the engineering team building it.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the front-end technical roadmap for Coinbase Advanced, defining architecture decisions that enable a reliable, scalable trading platform across web and mobile
  • Drive measurable performance improvements across rendering, network efficiency, and state management to deliver a low-latency trading experience
  • Lead complex, multi-quarter technical initiatives end-to-end, setting and enforcing high standards for code quality, system design, and engineering rigor
  • Strengthen the engineering team through hands-on mentorship of senior engineers, elevating technical decision-making and fostering growth across the subteam
  • Partner with Product and Engineering leadership to translate business priorities into actionable technical plans and ship high-impact features
  • Shape front-end engineering practices and patterns that scale with a growing team, establishing conventions that accelerate development velocity

Required Skills and Experience

  • 8+ years of iOS or Android mobile software engineering experience (Swift/iOS or Kotlin/Android), with working proficiency in the other platform; willingness to develop cross-platform fluency, as engineers on this team own both iOS and Android.
  • Proven track record optimizing complex UI systems for performance, including rendering optimization, network efficiency, and state management at scale
  • Experience leading technical strategy for a product area, including architecture decisions, trade-off analysis, and cross-team alignment for multi-quarter initiatives
  • Demonstrated success mentoring senior engineers and driving measurable improvements in team engineering standards and delivery velocity
  • Familiarity with high-availability, low-latency systems (trading platforms, financial systems, or real-time data applications preferred)
  • Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality.
