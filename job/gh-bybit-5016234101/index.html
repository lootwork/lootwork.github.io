О роли

Bybit EU combines crypto-asset services through its crypto-asset service provider (CASP) business with electronic money and payment services through its electronic money institution (EMI) business in the European Union. This role develops and manages banking and payment partnerships that let customers deposit and withdraw fiat money, while meeting our regulatory and compliance requirements.

The manager will work with banks, payment service providers, and electronic money institutions, together with our internal teams. The infrastructure includes bank account services for customer deposits and withdrawals, EUR transfers through SEPA, local payment methods, and connections between partner systems and our platform.

Основные обязанности

  • Support the onboarding and integration of fiat payment providers across European markets, including deposit and withdrawal services, local payment methods, and bank account services for customer funding and payouts.
  • Manage communication and commercial negotiations with existing and prospective partners, including pricing, fees, contractual terms, and service conditions.
  • Coordinate vendor onboarding processes, including due diligence, KYC/AML compliance checks, contract execution, and internal approvals.
  • Act as a liaison between external partners and internal Product and Technical teams, facilitating integration discussions, resolving operational questions, and supporting product launches.
  • Maintain ongoing relationships with fiat payment partners, addressing commercial and operational matters and coordinating improvements to existing services.
  • Work closely with Legal, Compliance, Finance, and Product teams to facilitate partner onboarding and support the expansion of fiat payment capabilities into new markets.
  • Identify and assess European banking and payment partners able to support regulated CASP and EMI activities, considering market coverage, payment capabilities, risk appetite, reliability, and commercial terms.

Требования

  • Experience in business development, partnerships, or account management within payments, banking, fintech, or digital assets, with exposure to European markets.
  • Understanding of fiat payment services, including SEPA transfers, local payment methods, deposit and withdrawal flows, and settlement and reconciliation processes.
  • Experience negotiating commercial agreements and managing partner onboarding from initial discussions through due diligence, contracting, integration, and launch.
  • Working knowledge of the EU regulatory environment for crypto-assets and payments, including MiCA, KYC/AML, and the Travel Rule; ability to coordinate due diligence and specialist reviews with Legal and Compliance.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to translate partner requirements into clear actions for Product and Technical teams.
  • Strong project coordination skills, attention to detail, and the ability to track owners, dependencies, deadlines, and open issues across multiple partner integrations.
  • Fluent English, both written and spoken.

Будет плюсом

  • An established network of European banks, payment service providers, or electronic money institutions.
  • Experience with crypto-related fiat services, customer bank account services, and API-based payment integrations.
  • Additional European language skills.
