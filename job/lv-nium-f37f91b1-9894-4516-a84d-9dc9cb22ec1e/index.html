О роли

Nium moves billions in payments every month for some of the world’s most demanding companies — banks, fintechs, and enterprise platforms operating across 100+ countries. We built that on fiat rails. The next layer is digital currency: stablecoins, on/off-ramps, and card products that let customers spend, settle, and move value in USDC as naturally as they do in dollars or euros.

We’re hiring a Staff PM – Digital Assets to own that build. Not the strategy document — the actual product. If you’ve shipped a stablecoin wallet, a crypto-funded card, or a fiat-to-digital conversion flow and you understand what it takes to get that through compliance, engineering, and go-live in a regulated environment, this is your rol

Обязанности

  • Own stablecoin-funded card products — physical and virtual cards funded from or settled in stablecoins (USDC and equivalents). Define the product, get it through compliance, ship it.
  • Build digital asset support into Nium’s multi-currency wallet — hold, convert, and send stablecoins alongside fiat currencies. This is a wallet extension, not a new product line.
  • Design and ship on/off-ramp flows — fiat-to-digital and digital-to-fiat conversion, including rate logic, settlement timing, and failure handling. Own the full customer experience.
  • Drive stablecoin-based cross-border settlement — payin and payout flows that clear on digital rails. Work with network and settlement teams to make these production-grade.
  • Evaluate and integrate custodians, exchanges, and blockchain infrastructure — as infrastructure decisions with integration timelines and SLAs, not as partnership strategy.
  • Own compliance product design for digital assets — MiCA, VASP frameworks, travel rule, FinCEN requirements. These get built into the product spec, not handed off to legal.

Требования

  • 7+ years in product management. Fintech, crypto infrastructure, or payments required.
  • You’ve shipped at least one digital asset product in production — a stablecoin wallet, a crypto card, an on/off-ramp, or a digital settlement rail. Not a prototype. Not a strategy deck.
  • Strong payments foundation — cross-border flows, FX, wallets, settlement mechanics. Digital assets are an extension of this, not a departure from it.
  • Regulatory fluency as a product skill — you know what MiCA, travel rule, VASP registration, and FinCEN MSB requirements mean for your spec, your launch sequence, and your go/no-go decisions.
  • Experience working with custodians, blockchain infrastructure, or card networks — enough to evaluate integration tradeoffs and write requirements that engineering can build against.
  • Comfortable operating in high-ambiguity, high-compliance environments — you know when to move and when to wait for legal sign-off.

HOW WE’LL MEASURE SUCCESS

  • Volume settled on digital rails (stablecoin payins + payouts) — tracked quarterly against target
  • Card activation rate and spend volume for stablecoin-funded card products
  • On/off-ramp conversion rate and time-to-settlement
  • Time-to-integration for new custodian or blockchain network partners
  • Regulatory coverage: number of markets with compliant, live digital asset product
