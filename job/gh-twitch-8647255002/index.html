О роли

Platform Core Engineering (PCE) builds the foundational systems, frameworks, and tooling that Twitch's engineers build on every day. As a Senior Product Manager on PCE, your customers are developers, and your success depends on earning their trust. You will own a platform product area and be measured on whether developers actually adopt, rely on, and advocate for what you ship.

This is a deeply technical, senior individual-contributor role. You are expected to be hands-on with modern AI development tools, to understand the technology you are shaping at a real level (not just at the surface), and to hold credibility in a room full of engineers.

You can be based out of one of the Twitch offices including San Francisco, Seattle, Los Angeles, Irvine, or New York City.

Вам предстоит

  • Own the strategy and roadmap for a PCE platform area, balancing near-term developer needs with a 1 to 2 year technical vision.
  • Treat developers as your customers: understand their workflows, earn their trust, and prioritize the capabilities that make them faster and more effective.
  • Work directly with Senior Engineering Managers and Principal Engineers to define technical and organizational needs and build a cohesive platform ecosystem.
  • Define and drive the platform metrics that matter.
  • Drive alignment across dependent teams, resolve competing priorities, and communicate technical tradeoffs.
  • Own launches end-to-end, including migration paths, documentation, and adoption, so capabilities land and stick.
  • Partner with tooling customers across engineering, ML infrastructure, MLOps, and data science to ensure the platform serves every developer persona.
  • Champion hands-on use of modern AI development tools, setting the bar for how the org builds with agents, MCPs, and context tooling.

You Have

  • 5+ years of product management experience, with a strong technical foundation.
  • Hands-on fluency with modern AI development tools and a habit of building with them.
  • A working understanding of how modern AI tools work and fit together (agents, MCPs, context, and tooling) and why they matter for developer products.
  • A track record building developer-facing or platform products where earning and keeping developer trust was the difference between adoption and failure.
  • Strong analytical skills and strong written and verbal communication, with precise articulation of technical customer problems and tradeoffs.
  • Demonstrated ability to operate independently and navigate ambiguity.

Дополнительный плюс

  • Experience building or supporting AI Developer Infrastructure, ML infrastructure, MLOps, or data and analytics tooling.
  • Background in software engineering or a related technical field (you have written and shipped code).
  • Familiarity with AWS and other builder frameworks, shared services, APIs, and platforms.
  • Familiarity with streaming and Twitch's ecosystem.
  • Proficiency with data-driven decision making and defining platform adoption metrics.
