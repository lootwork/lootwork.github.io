О FREETRADE

Freetrade’s mission is to become the default place to invest. Investing has been too complicated and expensive for too long, keeping millions from making the most of their savings.

We’re changing that.

We’re building our team and looking for people who are excited to reshape how our customers invest and grow their wealth. If you’re driven by solving complex problems and building innovative products, you’ll fit right in.

2026 is a big year for us. Last year, we announced a game changing deal to become part of the IG Group. We’ll continue to operate as an independent business while gaining access to the resources and support of an established leader in the space.

This year, we’re accelerating our roadmap and taking our products to the next level. We’re building new features like mutual funds, bonds, and family investment tools. We’re also exploring how we can leverage AI to deliver an even better experience for our customers.

Why join our Mobile Engineering Team?

Be part of a team that’s shaping the future of investing. As a Junior iOS Engineer, you’ll contribute to developing and maintaining a robust, scalable mobile application used by thousands of customers daily. You'll also work with experienced engineers, help solve technical challenges, build new features and develop solutions that make a real impact.

This is a unique opportunity to work with knowledgeable and experienced mobile engineers across both iOS and Android platforms, learn from their expertise and take on more responsibility as you develop your skills.

As part of our dedicated mobile engineering team, you will contribute to implementing new features as well as ensuring high levels of customer satisfaction by assisting in handling customer queries and feedback. In collaboration with Design, Product and QA teams, you will help build intuitive and engaging user experiences for our customers. You will also have opportunities to contribute to addressing technical debt, improving app performance and learning about architectural patterns and engineering best practices.

Our Mobile team’s tech stack

- Our iOS app is written fully in Swift

- Our app's UI is built using SwiftUI, with some UIKit code that we are in the process of migrating away from

- We organise our components using the MVVM-C pattern

- We use RxSwift for reactive data handling, with an ongoing migration toward Swift Concurrency

- We use Factory for dependency injection

- Screens are constructed with building blocks from our design system, built in SwiftUI

- We use a layered modular architecture using Swift Packages to optimise scalability

- We have recently transitioned to a modern, unified GraphQL API using Apollo

Our interview process

We keep things simple and transparent. Our process starts with an initial conversation with our Senior Talent Partner, followed by:

- Hiring Manager interview – A deep dive into your skills and experience with our Hiring Manager.

- Live Coding session – Show us how you think and solve problems in a collaborative session with two of our Mobile Engineers.

- Final Values interview – A conversation with our stakeholders, where we also assess cultural fit.
