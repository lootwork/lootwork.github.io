О роли

We are looking for a Head of Sanctions to help shape, strengthen, and scale a high-performing compliance culture across the global Compliance organization.

This senior leadership role reports into the Global Head of Financial Crimes and is responsible for the global sanctions programme end to end — the framework, the controls, the technology, and the team — covering sanctions advisory, list management, screening, investigations and reporting. In addition, hold executive accountability for the geofencing programme and for all name screening controls covering PEP and adverse media.

The ideal candidate is a strong compliance leader and operator who can combine regulatory awareness, program management, stakeholder engagement, and practical execution in a fast-moving environment. Experience in crypto, fintech or financial services is strongly preferred.

Что предстоит делать

Sanctions programme

  • Set and maintain the global sanctions framework, policy, and standards; ensure alignment with global and local sanction regulation requirements. Lead sanctions advisory on escalated matters: complex escalations, exposure analysis, licensing strategy, and voluntary disclosure. Lead sanctions-related regulatory filings, metrics, and board/committee reporting.

Name screening — PEP and adverse media

  • Lead all name screening controls globally: sanctions, PEP and adverse media — covering onboarding and ongoing screening. Drive screening technology strategy with Engineering and vendor management — evaluation, implementation, and continuous optimisation. Lead the design of adverse media taxonomy and severity tiering, ensuring consistency and defensibility in judgement.

Geofencing programme

  • Hold executive ownership of the geofencing and jurisdictional access control programme: residency and onboarding restrictions, IP and location controls, app store and web access, VPN/proxy detection, and perimeter integrity testing. Define the target-state control architecture and drive its delivery with Product, Engineering, and Data..

Leadership and delivery

  • Build, lead, and develop a global, multi-jurisdiction team; set clear accountabilities and a strong quality culture.
  • Represent the function in audits, regulatory exams, and independent reviews; own findings and remediation.
  • Partner with Training to build capability across first-line and compliance teams.

Кого мы ищем

  • 10+ years of relevant experience in financial crime compliance, with at least 6+ years leading a sanctions function at scale in a regulated financial institution or VASP.
  • Direct, demonstrable ownership of name screening controls across PEP and adverse media — not advisory-only exposure.
  • Experience designing or running a geofencing / jurisdictional access control programme, or equivalent perimeter control ownership.
  • Experience of regulatory engagement and exam management on sanctions matters.
  • Fluency with screening technology: matching algorithms, threshold tuning, transliteration handling, and alert workflow design.
  • Working knowledge of on-chain analytics and wallet screening for sanctions exposure (Chainalysis, Elliptic, TRM or equivalent).
  • Strong understanding of compliance program management in a regulated or high-growth environment.
  • Experience managing global or multi-market compliance initiatives.
  • Proven ability to lead complex programs with multiple stakeholders, timelines, dependencies, and governance requirements
  • Strong project management skills, including planning, execution, reporting, issue tracking, and remediation management.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to simplify complex topics for different audiences.
  • Strong stakeholder management skills, including working with senior leadership, regional teams, external vendors, and cross-functional partners.
  • Comfortable operating in a fast-paced, remote, global, and rapidly evolving environment.
