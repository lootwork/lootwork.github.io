Кто мы

О Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.

О команде

You will be joining Stripe’s ML Foundations and Gen AI team to incubate new ML applications and improve our Agentic and ML capabilities across Stripe. Our team is responsible for unlocking novel ML and LLM techniques and applications across Stripe’s product suite to drive business outcomes, as well as providing infrastructure, tooling and support for ML teams. Our team is also responsible end to end for delivering our internal productivity agentic tools.

Что предстоит делать

As a senior product leader, you will be responsible for a portfolio of products, comprising both platforms and end-to-end internal applications.

You will lead a cross-functional team in charge of radically transforming how Stripes do their day to day work. You will define the vision, priority use cases and adoption strategy for the internal agentic productivity tools thousand of Stripes use every day. You will obsess over crafting an exceptional end-to-end user experience and ensuring user adoption. This is a unique opportunity to have an unprecedented impact on Stripe’s ability to move fast in 2027.

You will also own our agentic platforms used by all teams building user-facing Agents. You will work closely with product leaders across business units to advance our core AI technology stack and empower those teams to infuse their products with new Agents and agentic capabilities. You will obsess over high quality platform primitives and drive internal adoption.

Обязанности

  • Define the vision for how Stripes work in 2027.
  • Deliver novel agentic internal capabilities and exceptional productivity tools for all teams at Stripe, that radically transform internal efficiency.
  • Obsess over craft and beauty, adoption and repeat usage of our internal tools.
  • Develop and execute on the Stripe-wide strategy for new Agentic applications across our product suite
  • Own customer discovery, product priorities, end-to-end UX, adoption, and outcome measurement across our entire Agentic platform.
  • Partner closely with internal stakeholders to understand platform requirements or adoption blockers and turn that into a roadmap.
  • Advocate for platform adoption and development of new internal or customer facing agentic applications at Stripe.
  • Communicate clearly and crisply with leadership stakeholders and drive alignment across multiple teams

Кто вы

We’re looking for someone who meets the requirements below, and has a passion for AI to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.

Minimum requirements

  • 10+ years of experience delivering highly successful and innovative software products which are ML powered
  • Solid understanding of ML, agentic and applied AI tech stacks
  • A passion and vision for how Agents will transform the way all Stripes will work
  • Demonstrated ability to influence company level strategy and work with business leaders to execute on the transformation
  • You push the pace. You take blame and pass the praise. People love working with you.
  • Proven ability to lead teams and work cross-functionally in a highly collaborative environment.
  • Ability to analyze and use quantitative and qualitative data to inform decisions.
  • A deep understanding and empathy for consumer and business users — you love building products that make our customers feel joy, delight and trust.
  • Relentlessly drives product quality
  • Capable of working on both 1P and 3P products
