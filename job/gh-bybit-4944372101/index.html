О нас

Established in 2018, Bybit is one of the world’s leading cryptocurrency exchanges and digital financial platforms, serving over 80 million users across more than 200 countries and regions. Powered by world-class technology and a user-first mindset, Bybit delivers a seamless ecosystem across trading, payments, wealth management, custody, institutional services, and Web3 — connecting users to the future of digital finance.

Our core values define how we build. We listen, care and improve to create products and experiences that put users first. Backed by a global team of ambitious builders, problem-solvers, and innovators, we foster a high-performance and fast-moving environment where talent is empowered to drive real impact at the global scale. Supported by 24/7 multilingual customer service and a strong commitment to innovation, we are shaping the future of finance through technology, collaboration, and bold execution.

Today, Bybit is recognized as one of the most trusted and transparent platforms in the digital asset industry, continuing to expand its global presence while building the infrastructure for the next generation of financial services.

О роли

We're looking for a Senior Product Manager to join our Platform Product team, working on mission-critical systems that power our global infrastructure. Depending on your background and strengths, you'll focus on one or more of the following areas:

  • Compliance & Regulatory Products — Translating global regulatory frameworks (MiCA, VASP, MAS) into scalable product solutions; KYC/AML systems; market launch and business migration
  • User Account & Identity — Account architecture, authentication, security & risk control, user lifecycle management
  • Fund & Asset Infrastructure — Deposit/withdrawal systems, unified trading accounts, multi-chain asset management, RWA integration
  • AI-Powered Products — LLM/RAG/Agent applications for customer-facing and internal efficiency use cases

Основные обязанности

  • Define product strategy and roadmap for platform-level systems serving millions of global users
  • Translate complex business, regulatory, or technical requirements into clear product specifications
  • Drive cross-functional execution across engineering, design, compliance, and operations
  • Own end-to-end product delivery from discovery through launch and iteration
  • Build scalable product frameworks that balance user experience, security, and regulatory requirements

Требования

  • 7+ years of product management experience, with 3+ years in relevant domains (compliance, account systems, payments/funds, or AI products)
  • Background in crypto exchanges, fintech, brokerage, or large-scale internet platforms
  • Strong systems thinking — comfortable with architecture-level product design
  • Proven ability to ship complex, cross-functional products in fast-moving environments
  • Excellent English communication skills for global stakeholder engagement
  • Data-driven decision making with strong analytical skills

Будет плюсом

  • Experience at leading crypto platforms or top-tier fintech/brokerage firms
  • Track record of 0-to-1 product launches in regulated or high-security environments
  • Understanding of blockchain infrastructure, multi-chain architecture, or on-chain asset management
  • Experience with LLM/AI product development and deployment at scale
