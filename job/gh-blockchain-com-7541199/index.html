Blockchain is connecting the world to the future of finance. As the most trusted and fastest-growing global crypto company, it helps millions of people worldwide safely access cryptocurrency. Since its inception in 2011, Blockchain has earned the trust of over 90 million wallet holders and more than 40 million verified users, facilitating over $1 trillion in crypto transactions.

We are looking for a Head of Business Intelligence to lead our BI function and help scale reporting and data-driven decision-making across Blockchain.com. This role is based in either London or Paris and sits at the intersection of analytics, finance, product, and business operations.

The Head of BI is a senior people leader responsible for building and managing a team of data analysts who own company-wide business intelligence and reporting. Your team will deliver high-quality dashboards, recurring reporting, executive insights, and self-service analytics capabilities that empower teams across Finance, Accounting, Product, Growth, Trading, Fraud, and Operations.

A key priority for this role will be partnering closely with our Finance and Accounting teams to support the company's evolving financial reporting, governance, and public company reporting requirements. You will help establish reliable, consistent, and auditable reporting frameworks, ensuring that business-critical metrics are accurate, well-defined, and aligned with the needs of senior leadership and external reporting stakeholders.

This role requires strong leadership, stakeholder management, financial and operational acumen, and a passion for building scalable reporting systems and a strong analytics culture across the organization.

Что предстоит делать

  • Lead and inspire a team of BI and data analysts, fostering a culture of collaboration, ownership, and continuous improvement.
  • Mentor and coach team members, supporting professional growth, skill development, and career progression.
  • Recruit, develop, and retain top BI talent; set clear performance expectations and provide ongoing feedback.
  • Own the company's business intelligence and reporting strategy, ensuring business-critical metrics and dashboards are accurate, consistent, and trusted.
  • Partner closely with Finance and Accounting leadership to develop and strengthen reporting capabilities that support financial transparency, audit readiness, and public company reporting requirements.
  • Support the development of robust financial and operational reporting frameworks, ensuring consistency, accuracy, traceability, and appropriate controls over key business metrics.
  • Collaborate with Finance and Accounting teams to streamline recurring reporting processes, improve reporting efficiency, and support internal and external reporting needs.
  • Define and maintain core KPI frameworks, metric definitions, reporting standards, and governance, establishing a reliable single source of truth across the organization.
  • Deliver executive-level reporting and actionable insights to guide strategic decisions, business growth, operational efficiency, and risk management.
  • Partner with leaders across Product, Growth, Trading, Fraud, Finance, and Operations to understand business needs and translate them into scalable analytics and reporting solutions.
  • Drive alignment across teams on reporting definitions, BI tooling adoption, and analytics best practices.
  • Oversee the planning and delivery of BI initiatives, ensuring alignment with business priorities, timelines, and high-quality standards.
  • Establish best practices for reporting quality assurance, metric validation, documentation, dashboard lifecycle management, and stakeholder communication.
  • Proactively identify reporting gaps, data inconsistencies, and inefficiencies, working cross-functionally to implement sustainable improvements.

WHAT YOU WILL NEED

  • Proven experience leading Business Intelligence, Analytics, or Reporting teams, ideally in high-growth, complex, or regulated environments.
  • Experience in fintech, financial services, trading, crypto, or other data-intensive industries.
  • Strong experience partnering with Finance and Accounting teams, with a solid understanding of financial reporting, business performance metrics, and reporting governance.
  • Exposure to public company reporting requirements, audit processes, financial controls, or IPO readiness would be highly valuable.
  • Demonstrated experience building scalable BI functions, including hiring, performance management, mentorship, and talent development.
  • Strong stakeholder management and communication skills, with the ability to translate complex data into clear, actionable business narratives for senior executives.
  • Deep understanding of KPI design, reporting frameworks, metric governance, and dashboard best practices.
  • Strong proficiency in SQL, Python, BigQuery, and Google Cloud environments.
  • Experience with modern BI and visualization tools such as Looker, Tableau, or Power BI.
  • Strong analytical mindset with the ability to identify and solve complex business problems through data.
  • Strong project management capabilities, including scoping, planning, prioritization, execution, and delivery.
  • Experience establishing reporting standards, data validation processes, and quality controls to ensure consistency and reliability across business functions.
  • Proactive, hands-on approach to addressing challenges, improving processes, and driving adoption of data-driven decision-making.
