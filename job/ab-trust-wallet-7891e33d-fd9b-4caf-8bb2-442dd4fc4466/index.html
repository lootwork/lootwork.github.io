О company

Trust Wallet is the secure, self-custody Web3 wallet that empowers people to own and control their digital assets. Whether you're new to crypto or an experienced user, Trust Wallet makes it easier, safer, and more convenient to explore Web3, securely access dApps, manage your crypto, and buy and sell digital assets, all in one place.

О роли

We're hiring a Senior QA Engineer to strengthen our release cycle across Trust Wallet's mobile apps (iOS + Android).

You'll work alongside our QA team on regression testing, bug triage, and release support, helping us deliver higher-quality mobile releases on a predictable schedule. This is a hands-on, execution-focused role: no automation coding is required, but strong manual testing skills and structured bug reporting are essential.

Что предстоит делать

- Execute regression test cycles on iOS and Android release candidates across our supported device matrix.

- File high-quality bug reports with clear reproduction steps, environment details, logs, and severity rationale.

- Perform exploratory testing on new features and flows, surfacing edge cases the automated suite misses.

- Triage incoming bugs from internal product testing, production reports, and user feedback channels.

- Coordinate with developers and release managers during RC cycles — confirming fixes, verifying blockers, unblocking release gates.

- Maintain and extend manual test documentation (checklists, test cases, regression matrices) in our test management tools.

- Support release-day validation including smoke tests on production builds and post-release monitoring.

MUST HAVE

- 3–5 years hands-on manual QA experience on both iOS and Android mobile apps.

- A high level of proactivity and a strong desire to deliver high-quality products

- Strong track record with structured bug reporting — reproduction rates, device/OS matrices, logs, severity.

- Experience owning or supporting full regression cycles on a scheduled release cadence.

- Solid exploratory testing skills — session-based, risk-prioritized, documented.

- Familiarity with test management and bug tracking tools (TestRail, Xray, Zephyr, Jira, Shortcut, or Notion).

- Experience with real-device testing via BrowserStack, Sauce Labs, or similar services.

- Excellent written English and async communication — contractors operate across timezones with written handoffs.

- Working-hours overlap with CET business hours (at least 4 hours daily).

Будет плюсом

- Experience with Selenium, WebdriverIO, and Appium.

- At least basic proficiency in Java, C#, JavaScript, Python, and Go.

- Prior experience testing crypto wallets, DeFi, or fintech applications (or sensitive financial/user-data products).

- Exposure to containerised or cloud-based test infrastructure.

- Familiarity with browser extension testing (Chrome, Firefox, Brave).

- Comfort with basic log/network inspection tools (Charles Proxy, Xcode Instruments, Android Studio Logcat).

- Exposure to release management workflows and RC-gating processes.

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION

This is a global and fully remote role. You must have the right to work for the country you are based.
