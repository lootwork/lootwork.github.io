Early Career Program Compensation Analyst I

Location: Office - New York

О Early Career Program

As part of Affirm’s Early Career Program, you’ll join a community of early career employees and receive dedicated support as you grow your career. You’ll be paired with a mentor from your team and collaborate in person three days a week from our New York office.

Throughout your first year, you’ll also take part in learning opportunities, networking events, and social activities with your ECP community. Past activities have included things like escape rooms, boat tours, mini golf, and other opportunities to connect with fellow early career employees outside of your day-to-day work. After your first year, you’ll have the flexibility to remain hybrid, transition to remote work, or continue working from an office.

О People team

The People team helps Affirmers cultivate a high-performing, inclusive organization. We focus on three core areas: attracting talent aligned to our mission, enabling growth through people and team development, and retaining employees through thoughtful experiences and rewards. We develop Affirm’s people programs and systems to create a workplace where all employees can thrive and contribute meaningfully. By investing in our people, we create a long-term advantage for the business.

О роли

The Compensation Programs & Partnerships (P&P) team designs, executes, and maintains some of the people programs and partnerships that enables Affirm to provide thoughtful experiences and rewards to Affirmers.

We’re excited to launch our early career program and are seeking someone passionate about learning, growing, and contributing within the Compensation area to help drive Affirm’s success. In this role, you’ll have the opportunity to build your knowledge of Affirm’s business functions, partner closely with the broader Compensation team on impactful initiatives, and build strong cross-functional partnerships through hands on projects that allow you to create scalable solutions that make a difference in the day-to-day experiences of our employees.

You’ll report directly to the Senior Manager of Compensation and be paired with an experienced compensation partner who will provide guidance, share their expertise, and help you navigate your early career while giving you the opportunity to take ownership of projects and bring your own ideas to the table.

Come join us if you’re excited about embarking on a new program that drives meaningful outcomes.

Что предстоит делать

  • Offer research and candidate analysis: Prepare concise offer-research briefs for selected hires, including the candidate’s current company and role, publicly available compensation information, relevant market data, internal peer comparisons, geographic considerations, and known upcoming vesting or compensation events.
  • Team compensation analysis and business-case support: Support team- and organization-level analyses by bringing together compensation, organizational, and market data and helping package the story into clear materials for key partner groups and business leaders. This could include preparing initial analyses on pay positioning, level distribution, retention risk, spans/layers, or other workforce questions before a senior team member develops the recommendation.
  • Annual and Mid-Year compensation cycle support: Provide analytical and operational support for major compensation cycles, including data validation, dashboard QA, manager-planning materials, exception tracking, recurring reporting, and post-cycle analysis.
  • Broader initiative support: Support broader job architecture and career-movement initiatives through research, documentation, data analysis, testing, and change-management materials.
  • Compensation knowledge and Employee Experience Hub content: Own ongoing maintenance of Compensation content within the People Employee Experience Hub / Comp Notion environment, help identify gaps or outdated content, and partner with the team to translate compensation programs into clear employee-facing materials.
  • Process documentation and operational excellence: Document recurring Compensation processes, create checklists and playbooks, identify manual steps, and help maintain a central library of procedures. Examples could include fiscal-year cutover activities.
  • Compensation dashboards and reporting: Support the maintenance and QA of Compensation dashboards, recurring reporting, and manager-facing tools.

What we look for

  • Pursuing or recently completed a bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Finance, Business Administration, Business Management, or a related or equivalent field.
  • An interest in Human Resources and Compensation or other people-focused business functions.
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to manage multiple tasks and keep projects moving forward.
  • Curiosity and an eagerness to learn, ask questions, solve problems, and take on new challenges.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with teammates and cross-functional partners.
  • Comfort working with data, spreadsheets, and presentations, or an interest in developing these skills.
  • A proactive, collaborative mindset and the ability to thrive in a fast-paced, evolving environment.
  • Passion and drive to make a meaningful impact on the employee experience.
