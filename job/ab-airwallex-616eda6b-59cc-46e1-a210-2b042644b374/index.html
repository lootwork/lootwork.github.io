О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

You’ll own a meaningful part of Airwallex’s product growth roadmap, helping customers discover value faster, adopt more of our products, and engage more deeply across their lifecycle.

In this role, you’ll identify growth opportunities in the customer journey, turn data into clear hypotheses, run experiments, and ship product improvements that move activation, conversion, retention, or expansion.

You’ll work across product surfaces and growth platforms, partnering closely with Engineering, Design, Data Science, Marketing, Commercial, and Operations to improve how we acquire, activate, engage, and grow customers.

- Own a defined area of the product growth roadmap across customer journeys, product surfaces, or shared growth capabilities.

- Identify growth opportunities through customer insight, funnel analysis, behavioral data, experimentation results, and commercial context.

- Translate ambiguous growth problems into clear product hypotheses, priorities, experiments, and execution plans.

- Design, launch, and iterate on experiments that improve activation, adoption, retention, expansion, or monetization.

- Partner with Engineering, Design, and Data to build customer experiences and internal capabilities that make growth programs more effective and scalable.

- Contribute to growth platform development in areas such as lifecycle orchestration, campaign tooling, targeting, personalization, rewards, recommendations, or cross-sell.

- Define success metrics, instrument the right events, and report clearly on performance, learnings, and next steps.

- Work cross-functionally with Marketing, Commercial, Operations, and regional teams to ensure growth efforts are relevant, coordinated, and effective.

- Help build repeatable product growth practices, not just one-off wins.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- 5+ years of product management experience in high-growth technology companies.

- Strong experience in experimentation, hypothesis-driven product development, and data-informed decision making.

- Track record of owning product improvements that drove measurable growth outcomes such as activation, conversion, engagement, retention, or revenue.

- Experience using product analytics, funnel analysis, cohort analysis, and customer insight to identify opportunities and prioritize work.

- Ability to structure ambiguous problems clearly and turn them into focused product plans.

- Strong cross-functional collaboration skills across Engineering, Design, Data, Marketing, and commercial or operational stakeholders.

- Excellent communication skills, with the ability to explain product strategy, trade-offs, and results to technical and non-technical audiences.

- High ownership, strong pace, and a bias toward action.

Preferred

- Experience in growth, lifecycle, onboarding, monetization, self-serve funnels, product-led growth, or adjacent product areas.

- Experience building or improving platform capabilities that support campaigns, personalization, segmentation, or experimentation.

- Experience across B2B, B2C, or hybrid growth models.

- Experience in fintech, payments, SaaS, marketplaces, or other data-rich digital products.

- Experience with website or self-serve acquisition funnels, where relevant, but not limited to those surfaces.

YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF

- You’re energized by finding leverage in the customer journey and turning it into measurable outcomes.

- You’re comfortable moving from insight to hypothesis to shipped experiment quickly.

- You care about both the craft of the product experience and the commercial result.

- You use data well, but you don’t hide behind it.

- You can work across teams, influence without authority, and keep momentum in ambiguous spaces.

- You’re not precious about your ideas and you iterate based on evidence.

- You own the outcome and keep pushing until the problem is solved.
