О команде

Risk Platform builds the decisioning infrastructure that sits between Airwallex and every dollar that moves through it, protecting 150,000+ businesses moving over US$260 billion a year across 150+ countries and 60+ currencies, and deciding, often in milliseconds, whether a new signup is real, a payment is safe, or a login is who they claim to be. The hard part is that fraud evolves fast, and every decision carries a two-sided cost: miss an attack and money is lost, over-block and a legitimate business can't get paid. We build this with streaming pipelines processing billions of events a day, graph databases exposing coordinated fraud rings, ML models scoring every transaction, and LLM agents that triage alerts. You don't need a fintech background, just an appetite for adversarial systems problems where the scoreboard is measured in dollars. If you want to help scale one of the world's fastest-growing financial platforms safely, this is the team.

О роли

As the first Quality Engineer on the Risk Platform team, you'll help the whole risk team decide and define what quality means for risk-critical systems — the standards, practices, and quality gates everyone builds around. You're an engineer first: you build the automation, tooling, and infrastructure that let us ship changes to these systems quickly and safely.

Обязанности

- Design, build, and maintain end-to-end and regression automation across the risk decisioning flows — onboarding (KYC/KYB, merchant risk), transaction scoring, and screening — and wire the suites into CI/CD so regressions are caught before release.

- Establish stable, reproducible test data and isolated environments, including realistic fraud, screening, and jurisdiction scenarios, so automation stays deterministic and trustworthy.

- Introduce and own contract testing between the Risk Platform and its upstream and downstream services to catch integration breakages early.

- Build jurisdiction-specific coverage that validates regional rules, thresholds, and regulatory requirements.

- Define and enforce release-quality gates, and design post-release verification (smoke tests, monitoring-backed checks) to confirm risk systems behave correctly in production.

- Own testing and release acceptance for major client-facing projects, end to end, partnering with engineering and product on acceptance criteria.

- Champion testability and quality across the team, and mentor engineers on test design and automation.

Кто вы

We're looking for people who meet the minimum qualifications for this role. The preferred qualifications are great to have, but are not mandatory.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

- Experience as an SDET / Software Engineer in Test, with strong programming skills and a track record of building test frameworks — not just writing test cases.

- Solid experience testing large-scale distributed backend systems: REST APIs, message-driven flows, and service-to-service integrations.

- Hands-on experience with API and E2E automation and CI/CD; experience with contract testing and managing test data and environments, or the ability to build these from the ground up.

- A strong sense of what makes tests reliable: deterministic data, isolated environments, and fast feedback.

- Use AI to accelerate testing — LLM-assisted test generation, coverage analysis, and failure triage — and help define how we test the team's ML models and LLM agents (evaluation, regression, and guardrails).

- Comfort working in a self-directed, Agile team, with excellent written and verbal communication.

Будет плюсом

- Exposure to risk, fraud, payments, compliance, or other regulated financial domains.

- Experience testing systems with jurisdiction- or region-specific behaviour.

- Experience with contract testing tools (e.g. Pact) and building test-data / environment tooling from the ground up.

- Experience using AI/LLM tools in the testing workflow, or testing ML- or LLM-based systems (evaluation harnesses, non-deterministic outputs).

- Performance and latency testing for systems on the critical path.
