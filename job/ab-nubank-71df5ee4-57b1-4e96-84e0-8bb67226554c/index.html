YOU'LL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR

As a Senior Systems Engineer working in a Cloud Network platform engineering team, you're expected to:

- Have solid troubleshooting and debugging skills with a passion for working creatively and analytically in a problem-solving environment with continuous improvement;

- Have familiarity with DevOps culture;

- Have the ability to analyze complex data and develop innovative solutions;

- Validate ideas and collaborates with in-house engineers and developers to promote creative solutions;

- Have familiarity with buy vs build approaches, evaluating new products and technologies;

- Execute high-level and low-level design for connectivity patterns for cloud network environments, considering multi-account and hybrid-cloud needs;

- Have experience with system administration (familiarity with Linux OS, scripting) and Infrastructure as Code tools;

- Have coding skills or interest to learn software development;

- Have previous PCI CSS experience or a similar security standard, considering the network security aspects of it;

- Support the Cloud Network topology, including troubleshooting incidents promptly, handling network changes, and participating in problem management processes including root cause analysis, and configuration management;

- Provide a reliable and stable infrastructure via best practices, standards, simplified support models, automated processes, operational excellence, and outstanding customer satisfaction;

- Have the ability to be data-driven before committing to a solution, taking relevant aspects like stability, reliability, scalability, and costs into account;

- Effective monitoring, implement a robust control and delivery of solutions services to identify issues proactively within the agreed SLAs using different methodologies, tools, and processes;

Have the ability to document the solutions for cross-teams knowledge sharing;

WE ARE LOOKING FOR A PERSON WHO HAS

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Systems Analysis and Development, or a related technical field.

- CCNP certification or equivalent experience, meaning you are comfortable designing, building, implementing, maintaining, and troubleshooting complex networking infrastructures.

- 2+ years of hands-on experience in infrastructure automation and cloud computing (AWS public cloud preferred), focused on AWS networking services.

- 2+ years of experience in planning, deploying, and troubleshooting network solutions (including IP addressing planning, BGP routing, and physical devices such as firewalls, load balancers, routers, and switches).

- English proficiency, with the ability to read, write, and participate in meetings.

YOU'LL FIT WELL IF YOU HAVE

- Leadership and a collaborative mindset—the ability to work with team members regardless of seniority level.

- Strong communication skills with both technical and non-technical audiences, a willingness to share knowledge, and openness to feedback.

- The ability to collaborate with customers when necessary to fulfill deliverables.

- Proficiency in English to facilitate clear and consistent understanding across time zones and language boundaries.

- A nonconformist, questioning, and innovative approach to problem-solving.

- The ability to move fast without compromising quality.

- An analytical profile—capable of identifying failures and proposing effective solutions.
