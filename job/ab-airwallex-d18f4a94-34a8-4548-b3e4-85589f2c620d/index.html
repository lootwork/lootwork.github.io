О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

As Staff Product Manager, Embedded Finance, you’ll lead Airwallex’s embedded finance products, helping platforms, SaaS companies, and marketplaces offer financial services to their end users.

- Own product strategy, solution design, roadmap, and delivery across products including payments, card issuing, banking-as-a-service, and FX.

- Partner with engineering, design, commercial, and external platform teams to launch scalable products that meet complex technical, regulatory, and commercial requirements across regions.

- Champion the developer experience, using customer and partner insight, data, and market trends to guide prioritisation and continuous improvement.

- Lead and develop Product Managers, building strong ownership, curiosity, and execution across the team.

You’ll work from our SF office three days a week.

Кого мы ищем

Essentials

- Experience taking products from ideation through delivery at global scale.

- Proven experience with API or platform integrations and technical go-to-market launches.

- A track record of balancing user needs and commercial strategy to deliver meaningful product growth.

- Experience leading complex, cross-functional product initiatives in a data-driven environment.

- Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to work effectively across multiple time zones.

- Experience leading, mentoring, or developing Product Managers.

Preferred

- Experience in fintech, SaaS, payments, embedded finance, or platform products.

- Experience working with platforms, SaaS companies, or marketplaces.

- A bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Business, or a related field.

- Experience working across payments, card issuing, banking-as-a-service, or foreign exchange products.

YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF

- You're comfortable owning the roadmap yourself.

- You own the outcome and you don't wait for permission to fix what's broken.

LEARN MORE ABOUT YOUR TEAM

As software, AI, and emerging technology reshape financial services, you'll have the chance to rethink familiar problems and build entirely new capabilities.
