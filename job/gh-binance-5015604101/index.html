О роли

We are seeking a highly motivated and experienced OTC Sales Trader to join our OTC Trading team. The ideal candidate will be responsible for managing and executing large-scale cryptocurrency trades for institutional and high-net-worth clients, providing exceptional client service, and driving business growth through strategic sales initiatives.

Что предстоит делать

  • Execute OTC cryptocurrency trades on behalf of institutional clients, high-net-worth individuals, and partners with a focus on accuracy, speed, and compliance.
  • Develop and maintain strong relationships with existing and prospective clients to understand their trading needs and provide tailored solutions.
  • Collaborate closely with the trading desk, risk management, and compliance teams to ensure smooth trade execution and adherence to regulatory requirements.
  • Monitor market trends, liquidity, and pricing to provide clients with competitive and timely trade opportunities.
  • Identify new business opportunities and contribute to the growth of the OTC trading desk.
  • Prepare and present regular reports on trading activity, client feedback, and market insights to senior management.
  • Ensure all trades comply with internal policies and external regulatory requirements.
  • Provide excellent client support and resolve any trade-related issues promptly.

Кого мы ищем

  • Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Economics, Business, or a related field.
  • Proven experience (8+ years) in OTC trading, sales trading, or institutional sales within the cryptocurrency or traditional financial markets in MENA.
  • Strong understanding of cryptocurrency markets, blockchain technology, and digital asset trading.
  • Excellent communication, negotiation, and interpersonal skills.
  • Ability to work in a fast-paced, dynamic environment with strong attention to detail.
  • Proficiency in trading platforms and tools; experience with OTC trading desks is a plus.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
  • Fluency in English is required to be able to coordinate with overseas partners and stakeholders. Additional languages would be an advantage.
