As a Content Producer on the VALORANT China Publishing team, you will lead production and delivery for China-facing, player-facing content across in-game experiences and out-of-game publishing and marketing materials. You will turn brand, publishing, and product objectives into clear production plans; orchestrate cross-functional and cross-organizational collaboration; and ensure high-quality content lands at the right cadence.

This is a senior production role for complex delivery. You will build effective operating models across local and global teams, navigate multi-party dependencies and changing priorities, and create a stable, scalable production system for a continuously evolving mobile product.

Обязанности

  • Lead end-to-end production management for China-facing, player-facing content across in-game experiences and out-of-game publishing and marketing materials—from intake and scoping through planning, cross-functional coordination, review, delivery, and retrospectives.
  • Translate brand, publishing, and product objectives into actionable briefs, priorities, milestones, and acceptance criteria that align creative expression, player experience, and product needs.
  • Orchestrate working rhythms and information flow across local, global, and external partners; manage critical dependencies, feedback loops, and decisions to keep complex projects moving forward.
  • Establish and continually improve asset- and project-management practices that strengthen visibility, version accuracy, delivery traceability, and team efficiency.
  • Balance scope, quality, timeline, and resources across concurrent initiatives; proactively identify risk, recommend tradeoffs, and drive timely resolution of critical issues.
  • Codify production workflows, templates, and best practices to raise the team’s efficiency, quality, and operating maturity for high-frequency content delivery.

Required Qualifications

  • 8+ years of relevant experience in games, digital products, content production, brand marketing, or creative production, with a successful track record of leading complex, multi-stakeholder projects from start to finish.
  • Deep understanding of digital-content, in-game experience, or marketing-asset production workflows, including dependencies across requirements, creative, design, copy, localization, development implementation, testing, and launch.
  • Strong project-management craft: able to bring clarity to ambiguity, build executable plans, manage priorities and critical paths, and sustain high-quality delivery amid change.
  • Proven ability to work across functions and organizations, build alignment through clear and pragmatic communication, drive decisions, and resolve complex collaboration challenges.
  • High standards for detail, quality, and player experience, with sound judgment and dependable execution in a fast-paced, multi-project environment.
  • Fluent in Mandarin and English, with the ability to independently drive day-to-day collaboration with global teams in writing and conversation.

Desired Qualifications

  • Experience with mobile games, live-service products, or integrated marketing-content production; familiarity with player-facing content delivery across in-game and out-of-game channels.
  • Experience partnering with global teams, development studios, or external collaborators in cross-time-zone and cross-cultural environments.
  • Experience with project-management, asset-management, or collaboration tools, and the ability to improve workflows based on project needs.
  • Understanding of China’s mobile-gaming market, content ecosystem, and player preferences.
  • A background in creative production, visual design, UX/UI, content operations, or product operations that enables strong judgment on asset quality and player experience.

Relationship to Gaming

For this role, you'll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players' experiences, this could be your role!

Employment Type: employment contract with FESCO Adecco, a professional personnel company in China
