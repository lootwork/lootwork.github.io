О Opportunity

Core product role within the Advanced Trading PM team. Own advanced trading product lines and trading features for our onshore compliant markets(EEA,US,SG,UAE and etc) — from strategy and PRD to launch and iteration — while keeping our product mechanics competitive with leading CEXs.

Что предстоит делать

  • Product Ownership:
  • Own the roadmap and delivery for assigned advanced trading product areas: account/margin model, order lifecycle, position management, market data surfaces, and trading UI on Web and App.
  • Write clear PRDs and user stories; drive designs through review, UAT, launch, and post-launch iteration.
  • Define success metrics and run continuous improvement loops on trading experience, liquidity quality, and risk outcomes.
  • Competitive & Market Insight:
  • Continuously benchmark against leading CEXs (Binance, Bybit, Coinbase, Kraken, Bitget, etc.) — deconstruct product mechanisms, fee/liquidity models, UX, and new launches.
  • Track TradFi derivatives and onshore compliant-market developments; surface actionable opportunities.
  • Trading Experience:
  • Improve speed-to-trade, order tickets, Kline/charting, and active-trader retention features on Web and App.
  • Partner with design and engineering to ship polished, high-performance trading surfaces.
  • Liquidity, Execution & Risk:
  • Contribute to liquidity parameters, fee tier design, and execution-quality tracking (spread, depth, slippage, fill-rate) under senior guidance.
  • Support risk framework work: price limits, funding rate, index/mark price logic, leverage & position tiers — translating requirements into product specs.
  • Compliant-Market Delivery:
  • Work with compliance, legal, and local entity leads to deliver product changes for licensed jurisdictions (SG, EU/MiCA, HK, US, BR, AU, UAE).
  • Translate regulatory requirements into product specs without over-degrading UX.
  • Cross-Functional Execution:
  • Partner day-to-day with engineering, quant/risk, design, ops, BD, and institutional client teams.
  • Communicate progress, risks, and tradeoffs clearly to the team lead and stakeholders.

What We Look For In You

  • 5+ years of product management experience, with at least 3+ years on crypto trading products (derivatives strongly preferred).
  • Solid working knowledge of CEX product mechanisms: perpetuals/futures/options, margin models, funding rates, index/mark price, liquidation, order types, market microstructure
  • Hands-on experience shipping trading features on Web and/or App at a top-tier CEX or comparable trading platform.
  • Basic understanding of exchange liquidity economics and risk frameworks (maker/taker fees, position tiers, leverage curves).
  • Strong written PRDs, structured thinking, and cross-functional execution.
  • Fluent English; Mandarin a plus.

Будет плюсом

  • Direct competitor experience at a leading CEX.
  • Familiarity with at least one onshore compliant market (EEA, US, SG, AU, UAE, TR, BR).
  • Prior experience at a traditional brokerage / TradFi exchange (e.g., IBKR, Futu, Tiger, Robinhood, CME, HKEX, SGX) — especially derivatives, margin, or trading UX.
  • Familiarity with smart contracts, token economics, or tokenized RWAs.
