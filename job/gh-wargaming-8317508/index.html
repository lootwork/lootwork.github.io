О вакансии

Wargaming is looking for a QA Automation Engineer to join the World of Tanks QA Automation Team in Vilnius. In this role, you will develop and maintain automated testing solutions, execute performance and stability tests for game server systems, and help improve the quality and reliability of World of Tanks, PC.

Reports to

QA Automation Team Lead

Что предстоит делать

  • Create, perform, and analyze the results of performance and stability tests of the World of Tanks PC game server using the internal test framework Bot-net
  • Work with prod telemetry and statistics about server performance and build test coverage based on it
  • Create new and support existing autotests on this framework
  • Dig deeper into the test automation for individual game modules, processing of test results, or preparing a test environment

Кого мы ищем

  • Highly motivated and fast-learning engineer ready to dive into World of Tanks PC QA Automation field
  • Knowledge of Python: language’s tips and tricks, restrictions, system of modules and imports, generators, decorators
  • Basic knowledge of game server architecture
  • Basic knowledge of Linux OS and gaming hardware
  • Pre-Intermediate or higher level of English (sufficient for reading technical documentation)
  • Good knowledge of automation with CI systems TeamCity/Jenkins
  • Strong analytical skills, ability to get deep into problems’ root causes and to offer system-wide decisions

Что поможет выделиться

  • Technical education
  • Knowledge/experience in automation with alternative programming languages (C++, C#, JS) education

Формат работы

  • This position is based in our Vilnius office
  • Onsite or Hybrid (3 days of work from the office: Wednesday–Friday)
  • This role does not include relocation or immigration support. We kindly ask candidates to ensure they have valid work authorization in Lithuania before applying

The salary range for this role in Vilnius is EUR 3500- 5000 monthly gross (The final salary within the range will depend on the candidate’s experience, skills, and performance during the interview.)
