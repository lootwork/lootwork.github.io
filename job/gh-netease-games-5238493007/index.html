Job Description

  • Proficient in 3D action design and production across multiple styles, with engine-related work experience;
  • Participate in specific action design tasks and review action assets submitted by outsourcing partners;
  • Responsible for designing in-game character actions, with in-depth analysis, understanding, and production experience regarding character motion principles;
  • Collaborate closely with the Lead Artist to define game action style, and produce action design templates and specifications;
  • Possess strong communication skills and a cooperative attitude, coordinate throughout the entire production pipeline, and deliver the product's art quality.

Job Requirements

  • Solid foundation in animation design; understanding of cinematography and camera techniques for animation; strong grasp of body mechanics, movement rhythm for both humanoid and non-humanoid characters;
  • Rich imagination with excellent action design capabilities, able to create smooth and dynamic action performances;
  • Strong teamwork awareness and good communication/collaboration skills;
  • Familiarity with action games, with deep understanding of impact feel (game feel) in combat actions;
  • 8+ years of professional experience in action design for large-scale games.
