Senior Product Manager - In-Person Payments Reliability

In-person payments at Adyen power the in-store experience for the biggest brands. As this solution is growing exponentially, we’re looking for a Senior Product Manager to join the Devices team and shape the platform capabilities that help us operate, release and scale our global in-person payments device fleet safely.

This role sits in our Engineering Integrity area: the team that builds the testing, release, observability, certification and security capabilities used by our In-Person Payments product and engineering teams. These capabilities are critical to improving fleet stability, launch quality and developer velocity as our product portfolio expands across terminals, SoftPOS and bring-your-own-device solutions.

The team focuses on building, enhancing and maintaining capabilities such as:

  • Emulators, robot testing and hardware-in-the-loop testing infrastructure
  • Release and rollout infrastructure for our fleet of devices
  • Monitoring and insights into device and payment application health
  • Certification tooling, capacity and strategy
  • Security and compliance readiness across device software, release and certification flows
  • Tools and processes that make launches safer, more predictable and easier to scale
  • This is not a ticket-management role. It is not a pure delivery role. You will define the product direction for a broad, engineering-heavy platform area: understanding the teams that depend on these capabilities, setting clear priorities, defining success metrics and connecting the work to broader Payments and business outcomes.

Что предстоит делать

  • Define the product vision and roadmap for the platform capabilities that support fleet stability, testing, release, observability, certification and security readiness.
  • Partner with Product and Engineering leadership to build a broader vision for device fleet stability and reliability across In-Person Payments.
  • Turn a broad engineering-led area into a clear product area with users, outcomes, priorities, trade-offs and measurable impact.
  • Own the product direction for testing infrastructure, including emulators, robot testing and hardware-in-the-loop setups.
  • Provide tools built around the needs of the teams that use them, by understanding their workflows, pain points and release risks.
  • Own the product direction for release and rollout infrastructure, including staged rollouts, automated release signals and rollback tooling.
  • Make release processes across our fleet of devices safer, more automated and more predictable.
  • Drive the product direction for monitoring and fleet health insights: the data and tooling that help teams understand device and payment application health, detect issues early and act before merchants are impacted.
  • Shape the approach to continuous pre-certification tooling, capacity and strategy, so certification becomes a final validation step rather than a place where issues are found late.
  • Collaborate with Security, Certifications, Engineering, Product and vendor-facing teams to embed quality, compliance and release readiness earlier in the lifecycle.
  • Define clear metrics for product stability, launch quality, release confidence and adoption of the platform capabilities you own.
  • Ensure the roadmap is tied to clear business outcomes and aligned with the broader Payments and company strategy.

Кто вы

  • Knowledge - You have a strong understanding of software delivery, testing, observability and release processes.
  • Experience with technical platforms, infrastructure, developer tooling, reliability products or device platforms is a strong plus.
  • Mindset - You are a self-starter who can create structure in an ambiguous environment and build product management for a technical area from the ground up.
