Job Description

Our Technical Support team helps connect player experiences with the teams working on our games. We support players, investigate reported issues, and turn player feedback into relevant insights for teams across the studio.

As a Junior Technical Support Specialist, you'll help players resolve technical and gameplay-related issues while providing valuable insights from player reports to our development and other teams. You'll communicate directly with players, investigate reported issues, document your findings, identify recurring patterns, and make sure relevant information reaches the right people.

We're looking for someone who enjoys video games, helping others, and solving problems. You don't need to have all the answers right away. What matters is your curiosity, willingness to investigate unfamiliar issues, and ability to find solutions, even when they're not immediately obvious.

We value attention to detail, clear communication, and a collaborative approach. You'll be part of a team where sharing knowledge, asking questions, and learning from feedback are all part of everyday work.

Daily Responsibilities

  • Respond to player reports and provide clear, helpful troubleshooting guidance.
  • Investigate technical and gameplay-related issues using available documentation, internal tools, testing, and independent research.
  • Identify recurring issues and patterns, ensuring they're appropriately categorised, prioritised, documented, and escalated.
  • Help develop and improve troubleshooting solutions, internal knowledge resources, and player-facing support content.
  • Collaborate with other teams, including development, legal, and communications, to share relevant information.
  • Organise and manage your workload while maintaining clear communication and accurate documentation.
  • Continuously expand your knowledge of our games, platforms, tools, and the teams involved in supporting them.
  • Put CD PROJEKT RED's values into practice through respectful communication, teamwork, and a player-focused approach.

Требования

  • At least 1 year of experience in customer-facing roles, such as customer service, customer support, community support, technical support, or similar positions involving direct interaction with customers or users.
  • Very good English communication skills, both written and spoken, with the ability to explain technical information clearly to different audiences.
  • Good analytical and problem-solving skills, with curiosity and a willingness to investigate unfamiliar issues.
  • Strong interest in video games and understanding of player experiences.
  • Attention to detail and the ability to document information clearly and accurately.
  • Ability to collaborate with others, ask questions when needed, and use feedback to improve your work.
  • Good organisational skills and the ability to manage recurring tasks consistently.

Будет плюсом

  • Basic understanding of PC hardware, software, and common troubleshooting methods.
  • Experience with ticketing systems, bug-tracking tools, game testing, modding, moderation, or supporting players in gaming communities.
  • Knowledge of additional languages that can help us support our international player communities, particularly French, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Mandarin Chinese, or languages spoken in Eastern Europe.
