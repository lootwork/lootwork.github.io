О VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company creating mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. With 800 employees, over 7 billion downloads, and 200 million active users, Voodoo ranks as the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in downloads, just behind Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes top games like Mob Control and Block Jam, as well as popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

Команда

Blitz, launched by Voodoo in 2021, is a gaming platform enabling players to compete in mobile games for real cash prizes through head-to-head matches, tournaments, and leagues. By leveraging a unique game library, Blitz engages users in a fun, competitive, and rewarding way.

Роль

Join Blitz as an Operations Engineer and become a driving force in our platform's operational success. You'll lead automation, technical product ownership, and data-driven decision-making across Payments, Fraud, Support, Finance, and CRM.

Задачи

- Serve as a technical product manager across Payments, Fraud, Support, Finance, and CRM, managing requirements, creating technical specs, and analyzing post-release data

- Analyze operational data to uncover insights and identify key business levers to enhance efficiency and user experience

- Proactively automate processes by identifying system bottlenecks, designing workflows, and developing Python scripts or Proofs-of-Concept (PoCs)

- Collaborate with engineering, product, and business teams to design scalable solutions to operational challenges

- Monitor platform performance and KPIs, flag risks, and recommend data-driven improvements

- Build internal tools and automate repetitive processes to boost team productivity

- Document systems, processes, and technical specifications clearly and systematically

Профиль кандидата

- Graduate of a top-tier French engineering school (École Polytechnique, Mines, CentraleSupélec, Ponts, or equivalent) with 1–3 years of professional experience

- Eligible for the V.I.E. program (EU citizen, under 28 years old at departure)

- Proficient in Python: able to write scripts, develop internal tools, interact with APIs, and manipulate data; familiarity with AI-assisted workflows is a plus

- Genuine generalist and “Swiss Army knife”—comfortable with both high-level strategy and hands-on implementation

- Highly proactive and accountable: thrive in ambiguity, identify challenges, and take initiative without waiting for direction

- Strong analytical mindset, attention to detail, and a structured problem-solving approach

- Previous experience in a US tech company or French tech scale-up is a strong asset

- Fluent in English (work language); French is a plus

Условия и льготы

- Compensation and benefits in line with the V.I.E. program (fixed structure — not at Voodoo’s discretion)

- Additional benefits depending on the country of residence
