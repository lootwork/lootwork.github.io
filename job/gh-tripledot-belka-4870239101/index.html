The role is working within our studio: Ludios

About Ludios

Ludios takes its name from the Latin, it means "The Game People". Led by Eyal, one of the co-founders of Tripledot Studios, and some of the most experienced people in mobile games, we are a studio that makes and publishes our own casual, free-to-play mobile games. Our team is based across APAC, with the majority of our development happening in Jakarta and Melbourne. We are data-driven, creative, and genuinely passionate about the industry we work in. Right now, we are working on a number of exciting projects, including one built around a globally recognised IP, alongside several original titles we are equally excited about. If crafting games and reaching millions of players is what gets you out of bed, we would love to hear from you.

Role Overview

You will work on creating hand-rendered 2D environment assets for a live game. The 2D Artist needs to be able to successfully produce any type of environmental art assets assigned to them, meeting deadlines. Quickly adapt to new art styles and any technical requirements and pipelines. Be diligent to deliver the quality following the art guidelines and style from the Lead Artist, and also be efficient in following the production schedule.

Key Responsibilities

  • Produce concept and 2D Painted Renders of assets and textures for the environment, props, objects, and scenes for the game engine
  • Understand and follow the art requirements and the specific needs of the project
  • Promptly learn any new tools or techniques
  • Execute tasks assigned to them according to the project schedule while meeting quality expectations
  • Using Checklists to perform initial art and technical quality checks before sending the art asset to the Team Leader for review
  • Fix all problems noted by the Team Leader and the Art Director
  • Promptly report to the Team Leader about any difficulties encountered
  • Support Junior Artists, helping them to deal with tasks when needed

Required Skills, Knowledge, and Expertise

  • Strong portfolio showing examples of 2D painted in-game props and environment examples
  • Familiar with creating 2D assets for a 3D game pipeline
  • Excellent 2D experience with Photoshop
  • Good basic art skills in art fundamentals (color composition and form)
  • Good understanding of architectural forms
  • Versatile in art style
  • Passionate about games
  • 3D software knowledge is a plus
  • Unity experience is a plus
