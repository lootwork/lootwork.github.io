О Starling

At Starling, we are on a mission to provide people with a fairer, smarter, and more human alternative to traditional banks. We are building a leading digital bank, driven by fast technology, fair service, and honest values. Our vision is to create a banking industry that serves everyone, which we accomplish by developing best-in-class proprietary technology, offering an exceptional customer experience, and establishing a strong, sustainable business.

Our five core values – Listen, Keep It Simple, Do The Right Thing, Own It, and Aim for Greatness – are at the heart of everything we do, guiding our strategy and shaping our culture. We're a branchless, largely paperless bank, committed to responsible banking, sustainability, and fostering an inclusive environment where everyone can thrive.

Hybrid working: All Starling colleagues spend a minimum of 10 days per month in the office, with flexibility to work the rest from home.

The Opportunity

The Marketing Executive plays a pivotal role in driving the execution, performance, and day-to-day management of Starling’s key brand partnerships, including Arsenal Football Club, talent ambassadors, and Transport for Greater Manchester (TfGM).

Reporting to the Head of Brand Partnerships, this role acts as the operational support for key partner accounts, centralises reporting and competitor insight across all partnerships, and provides agile support across broader brand planning, events, and SME initiatives.

Требования

Что предстоит делать

  • Account management and execution: Serve as the primary point of contact for TFGM, driving daily actions, campaigns, and contractual rights execution. Act as ongoing operational and event support for Arsenal (including community projects, ticketing, hospitality, and merchandise).
  • Performance reporting and insights: Own partnership performance tracking across all partners. Monitor, analyse, and report on key metrics in collaboration with internal stakeholders to build a central destination for research and reports.
  • Competitor intelligence: Lead the monthly tracking, analysis, and distribution of competitor partnership activities across Marketing channels to inspire innovative and unique Starling campaigns.
  • Cross-functional support: Act as an agile contributor to the broader Marketing team, assisting with Brand Integration, SME projects, and internal taskforces.
  • Inbox and operations management: Oversee the central Brand/Marketing inbox on a weekly basis, triaging external inquiries effectively.
  • Identifying, managing, and escalating operational, financial, and regulatory risks in alignment with Starling’s Risk Management Framework.
  • Ensures 100% compliance and accuracy across all marketing collateral approvals processed via JIRA, seeking clarity on regulatory considerations prior to escalation.
  • Completes mandatory risk training and ensures appropriate considerations are embedded into all managed partnership activations and events.

What You'll Bring

  • Analytical capabilities with experience interpreting data and performance metrics to generate actionable insights.
  • Proven track record in project management, campaign execution, and third-party stakeholder relationship management.
  • Experience in events management and activation is highly preferred.
  • Solid understanding of risk management, financial promotions, and compliance workflows within a regulated fintech or banking environment.

Behaviours & Competencies

  • Proactive and agile: Self-starter who thrives in a fast-paced environment and comfortably juggles multiple multi-faceted projects simultaneously.
  • Creative thinker: Brings "blue sky" ideas, innovative solutions, and fresh perspectives to campaign planning and activation.
  • Strong communicator: Clear, articulate communicator with strong presentation capabilities and public speaking confidence.
  • Collaborative and engaged: Eager to build strong working relationships across the wider marketing team and contribute to company culture initiatives.
  • Minimum of one - two years experience in a marketing campaigns and delivery role.
