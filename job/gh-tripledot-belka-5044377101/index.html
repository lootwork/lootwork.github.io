О роли

We're looking for a Product Analyst to join our analytics team and help shape how millions of players experience our games. You'll work closely with product managers, game designers and the UA team, turning player data into decisions on monetization, game economy, gameplay and live-ops.

This is a product-focused role: most of your time will go into understanding player behavior and driving product improvements, with a solid grasp of how user acquisition affects the player base and the business.

Основные обязанности

  • Monetization research: analyze both in-app purchases (IAP) and ad monetization. Look at conversion funnels, pricing, offers and bundles, ad placements and frequency, and the trade-off between ad revenue and retention.
  • Game economy: monitor and model sources and sinks, currency balance, inflation and progression pacing. Flag imbalances and recommend tuning.
  • Gameplay analysis: study level difficulty, churn points, session patterns, feature adoption and engagement, and turn the findings into concrete recommendations for game designers.
  • Segmentation: build and maintain player segments (payers vs. non-payers, whales, ad watchers, engagement tiers, cohorts by acquisition source) to tailor offers, content and live-ops.
  • A/B testing: design, run and analyze experiments end to end, including hypothesis, sample size, test duration, statistical significance and the business-impact readout.
  • UA collaboration: work with the UA team to understand cohort quality by channel, campaign and geo. Connect product KPIs (retention, ARPDAU, LTV) to UA performance (CPI, ROAS, payback).
  • KPIs and reporting: own core product KPIs, build dashboards, and investigate anomalies and trend changes.
  • Partnership: work daily with product, design, live-ops and UA, and present insights clearly to stakeholders and management.

Обязательные навыки и опыт

  • 3+ years of experience as a product or data analyst in mobile gaming.
  • Strong SQL skills, comfortable working with large event-level datasets.
  • Hands-on experience with A/B testing and solid statistics: hypothesis testing, confidence intervals, sample sizing.
  • Good understanding of mobile game KPIs: retention, ARPDAU, ARPPU, conversion, LTV, session metrics.
  • Familiarity with both IAP and ad-based monetization models.
  • Understanding of UA basics: CPI, ROAS, cohorts, attribution.
  • Experience with BI tools such as Tableau, Looker or Power BI.
  • Strong product sense and curiosity. You ask "why" and push for a clear recommendation, not just a report.
  • Excellent communication skills and the ability to tell a story with data.
  • B.Sc. in a quantitative field (Industrial Engineering, Statistics, Economics, Computer Science, Math or similar).

Будет плюсом

  • Hands-on experience using AI tools in analytics work (e.g. Claude, Cursor, AI features in BI tools), such as speeding up SQL and analysis, exploring data, and automating reporting.
  • Experience with game economy design or balancing, such as free-to-play economies and progression systems.
  • Experience with segmentation or clustering methods and predictive models (churn, payer propensity, LTV prediction).
  • A passion for mobile games. You play, and you think about why games work.

Why Join Us?

  • Real impact: your analyses directly shape features, economy tuning and monetization decisions in live games.
  • Broad scope across product, monetization, economy and UA, with plenty of room to grow.
  • A data-driven team that runs experiments constantly and moves fast.
