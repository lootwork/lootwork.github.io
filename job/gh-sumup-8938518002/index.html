Program Manager, KYC Operations

Location: Sofia, Bulgaria

Team description

Our KYC Operations team helps SumUp verify and onboard businesses safely, so merchants can start taking payments quickly and in line with regulations. As Program Manager, you'll report directly to the Head of KYC Operations and take real ownership of how initiatives are planned, delivered and adopted across Engineering, Product, Compliance and Operations teams.

Take a look inside our Sofia office.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own the delivery of complex KYC and compliance initiatives from first scope to final rollout, including plans and timelines
  • Design the change approach for major transitions, coaching sponsors and team leads and measuring how well new ways of working are adopted
  • Build trust with stakeholders across Operations, Compliance, Product and Engineering, balancing competing priorities and resolving disagreements.
  • Build and mantain regular reports, dashboards and provide data insights, so leadership can make faster decisions, and set documentation and process mapping standards that keep teams aligned.

What you'll bring

  • Experience leading projects or change programmes that involve several teams, with a track record of delivering complex initiatives from start to finish.
  • Comfort working with data, building reports from scratch, using evidence and reporting to influence decisions and build consistent processes.
  • Strong analytical thinking, discovering and fixing process and system inefficiencies.
  • Strong written and spoken English, with the ability to simplify complex topics and write clear briefs that help leaders decide.
  • Ability to make sound decisions when things are unclear and to keep work moving when priorities compete.

Nice to have: experience in onboarding, KYC, KYB or compliance-related programmes, SQL knowledge or comfort with tools such as Salesforce, Zendesk, ServiceNow and back-office platforms, and formal change management training or certification.

Why you should join SumUp

  • 🌍 Opportunity to work with SumUppers globally on large-scale fintech products used by millions of businesses worldwide, from our modern Sofia office. This involves an office-first setup
  • 🌈 Commitment to Diversity and Inclusion: be part of a workplace that values and promotes diversity, fostering an inclusive environment where everyone's perspectives are respected and embraced
  • 🚀 Enrolment onto our Virtual Stock Option programme: you will own a stake in SumUp's future success
  • 📚 A dedicated annual L&D budget of €2000 and 10 paid days of educational leave which can be used to attend conferences and/or advance your career through further education
  • 🏖️ Generous time off: 25 days of paid leave, plus one extra day per year of service (up to 35), along with public holidays and special leave options
  • 🏥 Peace of mind: additional health insurance and life insurance
  • 💪 Stay active: on-site yoga and a co-sponsored Multisport card offering access to hundreds of sports facilities
  • 🍽 Meal support: benefit from a tax-free food voucher program plus flexibility via our Re:Benefits platform
  • 🚌 Easy commute: free shuttle buses connecting directly to Joliot-Curie metro station
  • 🌴 Break4me: 1-month sabbatical after 3 years of service
  • 🔗 Referral Bonus: earn additional rewards by referring talented individuals to join the SumUp team
