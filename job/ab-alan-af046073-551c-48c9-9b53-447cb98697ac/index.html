⭐️ THE SENIOR ENGINEERING MANAGER JOB AT ALAN

At Alan, our engineering leaders are exceptional technical leaders who combine strategic vision with hands-on leadership to drive both team excellence and technical innovation. They are deeply involved in the strategic, technical and product direction of the team, as well as the human aspects of building high-performing engineering teams.

🚀 YOUR MISSION

As a Senior Engineering Manager at Alan, you'll be responsible for leading one of our engineering departments, comprising 6-15 engineers and organised in autonomous and nimble teams. You'll shape the technical vision, foster a culture of innovation, continuous learning, and ensure your teams deliver impactful solutions while maintaining engineering excellence.

In this role, you will

- Set and communicate a clear vision for why and what we're building, establishing high expectations and identifying key assets and opportunities.

- Bring daily energy to the team, providing context and fostering mutual help to create a motivated and engaged team.

- Own the engineering efficiency and excellence of your team by using productivity tools (including AI), zooming in and out depending on the needs of the team.

- Challenge what's "good enough" to build and ship, while removing sources of unnecessary defocus.

- Monitor team well-being and ensure psychological safety to maintain a healthy team dynamic.

- Own talent density by hiring exceptional talent, supporting team members' growth and addressing performance concerns promptly.

- Collaborate daily with stakeholders, product managers, designers, other engineering leads, etc.

This is the perfect role for you if you have exceptional people management skills, a great technical background and if you are passionate about building both great products and great teams. A minimum of 3 to 5 years of experience in engineering management is required.
