О Thunes

Thunes is the Smart Superhighway to move money around the world. Thunes’ proprietary Direct Global Network allows Members to make payments in real-time in over 140 countries and more than 90 currencies. Thunes’ Network connects directly to over 12 billion mobile wallets, stablecoin wallets and bank accounts worldwide, as well as 15 billion cards via more than 220 different payment methods, such as GCash, M-Pesa, Airtel, MTN, Orange, JazzCash, Easypaisa, AliPay, WeChat Pay and many more. Thunes’ Direct Global Network differentiates itself through its worldwide reach, in-house SmartX Treasury System and Fortress Compliance Platform, ensuring Members of the Network receive unrivaled speed, control, visibility, protection, and cost efficiencies when making real-time payments, globally. Members of Thunes’ Direct Global Network include gig economy giants like Uber and Deliveroo, super-apps like Grab and WeChat, MTOs, fintechs, PSPs and banks. Headquartered in Singapore, Thunes has offices in 14 locations, including Barcelona, Beijing, Dubai, Hong Kong, Johannesburg, London, Manila, Nairobi, New York, Paris, Riyadh, San Francisco and Shanghai. For more information, visit: https://www.thunes.com/

Context of the role

Thunes is seeking a technically fluent Product Manager - AI & Automation to help advance our AI-driven transformation across the organisation. You will contribute to our product strategy and own the delivery of a roadmap within your area, embedding AI and automation into our global cross-border payments platform and business operations to drive revenue growth and operational efficiency.

We’re looking for someone who combines established product management experience with a strong business mindset and practical depth in AI. You have helped build, deploy, or scale AI solutions in production and can own the “what and why” while working closely with engineering on the “how.” You understand how AI works and how to apply it meaningfully in a business context, prioritising what to build, what to automate, and what will deliver measurable impact.

Success in this role requires hands-on experience delivering AI products, the ability to connect technical solutions to business outcomes. You should be comfortable designing for automation, scalability, and regulatory compliance, and understand how APIs and AI infrastructure support effective financial products and operations.

Key responsibilities

Product direction and prioritisation

  • Contribute to Thunes’ established AI strategy through user insights, business analysis, technical assessment, and learnings from delivery.
  • Own the roadmap and backlog for your product area, translating strategic priorities into clearly scoped initiatives and achievable delivery plans.
  • Assess opportunities based on business value, user needs, technical feasibility, data readiness, cost, and risk.
  • Recommend whether to build, buy, or adapt existing capabilities, and select an appropriate approach across AI, conventional automation, and rules-based solutions.

End-to-end product delivery

  • Own initiatives from problem discovery and solution design through development, testing, launch, adoption, and ongoing improvement.
  • Work directly with users and functional teams to understand workflows, identify root causes, and define the problems worth solving.
  • Translate business needs into clear product requirements, user journeys, user stories, and acceptance criteria.
  • Partner closely with engineering and operational teams to shape solutions, make informed trade-offs, and manage delivery dependencies.
  • Take a hands-on role in prototyping, testing, and evaluating solutions, validating assumptions and resolving issues throughout delivery.
  • Drive stakeholder alignment and rollout readiness, including process changes, user guidance, and feedback collection.

AI solution design and responsible delivery

  • Contribute informed recommendations on AI solution design, including data sources, integrations, model workflows, evaluation methods, and human oversight.
  • Evaluate trade-offs across solution quality, reliability, latency, cost, scalability, and maintainability with engineering.
  • Work with Architecture, Information Security, Legal, and Compliance to incorporate security, data protection, and responsible AI requirements into product design and delivery.
  • Define expected behaviours, failure scenarios, escalation paths, and appropriate human review for AI-enabled workflows.

Performance and continuous improvement

  • Establish baselines and define success measures covering business outcomes, user adoption, operational efficiency, and solution quality.
  • Evaluate whether delivered solutions achieve the intended improvements and use evidence to guide further investment and prioritisation.
  • Monitor performance after launch, investigate gaps, and work with engineering and business teams to improve outcomes.
  • Communicate progress, risks, decisions, and results clearly to stakeholders.
