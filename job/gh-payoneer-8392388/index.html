О Payoneer

Founded in 2005, Payoneer is the global financial platform that removes friction from doing business across borders, with a mission to connect the world’s underserved businesses to a rising global economy. We’re a community with over 2,500 colleagues all over the world, working to serve customers, and partners in over 190 countries and territories.

By taking the complexity out of the financial workflows–including everything from global payments and compliance to multi-currency and workforce management, to providing working capital and business intelligence–we give businesses the tools they need to work efficiently worldwide and grow with confidence.

Role Summary

As a Software Engineer on the team building Payoneer’s organizational brain, you’ll help create a central, intelligent knowledge layer that connects the company’s many information sources, understands how they relate, and delivers relevant, trustworthy context to both employees and AI agents. At its core is an agent that uses tools such as GraphRAG and Code Graphs to surface relevant context and reliable sources. It’s already widely used across Sales, Legal, Operations, Engineering, Product, and our business units, and it’s the foundation other teams will use to build their own agents and AI solutions.

This is a small team with broad organizational impact. Expect plenty of zero-to-one work, systems thinking, and new technical challenges in information retrieval, semantic search, GraphRAG, Code Graph, agent memory, and agent evaluation. You’ll ship real AI to production, take part in architectural decisions, and shape how Payoneer uses knowledge, builds products, and adopts AI.

Что предстоит делать

  • Design, build, and ship production AI capabilities for Payoneer’s organizational brain, from retrieval pipelines to the agent and tools at its core.
  • Develop and improve information retrieval, semantic search, GraphRAG, and Code Graph capabilities that deliver relevant, trustworthy context to employees and AI agents.
  • Build the harness before you ship: evaluation frameworks, monitoring, and observability go in on day one, not after the first incident.
  • Measure agent quality objectively and systematically: define metrics, build validated test sets, and prove that your changes actually improve results.
  • Own your work end to end: understand the business problem, help define and architect the solution, ship it, and monitor it in production.
  • Take part in architectural decisions and design reviews, and clearly explain the reasoning and trade-offs behind your design choices.
  • Mentor and support others across teams working with different technologies, and non-technical departments as they build on the infrastructure we create.
  • Collaborate with Product, engineering teams, and stakeholders across the business to understand their needs and turn them into capabilities, not features.
  • Work AI-native, using tools like Claude Code and Cursor to get more leverage and move faster.

Кто вы

  • Backend engineer with 3-5 years of experience with solid, real-world production experience: you’ve watched systems run in the real world and fixed what breaks.
  • Hands-on experience building AI products, especially RAG and agents, and taking them all the way to production, including deployment, evaluation, and ongoing improvement.
  • Strong eval instincts: you know how to measure agent quality objectively and systematically, and how to tell whether a change truly improved results, rather than relying on a feeling that the answers look good.
  • Experience working in the cloud, especially AWS.
  • Team player who enjoys mentoring others, within your team and across teams working with different technologies.
  • Strong communicator: you’re comfortable standing in front of people, explaining architectural decisions, and justifying your design choices and the trade-offs behind them.
  • Curious and comfortable with ambiguity: you enjoy zero-to-one work, systems thinking, and taking on new technical challenges.

What Could Set You Apart

  • Experience with agent memory systems.
  • Experience with graphs (such as GraphRAG or knowledge graphs) and knowledge management.
  • Experience with our stack: Node.js, TypeScript, OpenSearch (for semantic search), Kubernetes, and MongoDB.
  • Fintech or regulated-environment experience: what matters is understanding why “probably fine” isn’t acceptable when money is moving.
  • Experience with messaging systems such as Kafka, RabbitMQ, or SQS.
  • Experience building SaaS platforms in a cloud-based, microservices environment.
