О роли

As a DevOps Engineer at Nium, you will be a key contributor within our engineering organisation, working closely with software engineering teams to build, automate, and operate the infrastructure that powers our card issuance platform. You will be maintaining our on-premises setup and will help move our workloads from on-premises to AWS, and drive our adoption of GitOps practices.

In this hands-on role, you will design and maintain cloud infrastructure, Kubernetes platforms, and CI/CD pipelines. You will empower engineering teams with the tools and self-service capabilities they need to deliver their work end to end, while keeping our systems reliable, secure, and scalable.

You will advocate for infrastructure-as-code and automation best practices, troubleshoot production issues, and promote collaboration and knowledge sharing across teams. Based in Malta, you will help shape how our engineering organisation builds and ships payment technology.

Основные обязанности

Cloud Migration & Platform Modernisation

  • Lead and execute the migration of workloads and CI/CD pipelines from on-premises infrastructure to AWS.
  • Support the transition from a monolithic architecture to microservices, ensuring the platform foundations are ready for it.

GitOps & Infrastructure Automation

  • Automate the provisioning of infrastructure using Terraform.
  • Implement and maintain application deployments to Kubernetes using ArgoCD and GitOps workflows.
  • Manage Kubernetes manifests and configuration with Kustomize, where applicable.

Continuous Integration & Delivery

  • Establish and employ Continuous Integration practices using tools such as CircleCI.
  • Build, maintain, and improve CI/CD pipelines to enable fast, reliable, and secure releases.

Engineering Enablement

  • Empower engineering teams with the tools, templates, and self-service capabilities to handle their deliverables end to end.
  • Provide the infrastructure required to run and scale the card issuance platform.

Operations & Support

  • Monitor, troubleshoot, and resolve infrastructure issues, providing timely support to customers and internal departments.
  • Contribute to incident response, root cause analysis, and continuous improvement of operational processes.

Knowledge Sharing

  • Contribute to team development through training sessions, technical talks, documentation, and informal mentoring.

REQUIRED SKILLS & EXPERIENCE

  • Hands-on experience with cloud solutions, ideally AWS.
  • Strong proficiency with Kubernetes, Docker, and Terraform.
  • Strong working knowledge of Linux operating systems and scripting in Bash, Groovy, and Python.
  • Experience building and maintaining CI/CD processes.
  • Experience with GitOps tooling such as ArgoCD is desirable.
