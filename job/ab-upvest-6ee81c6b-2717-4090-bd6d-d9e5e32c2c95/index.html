At Upvest, we are on a mission to make investing as easy as spending money. Upvest empowers businesses to offer a wide range of investment products and the best experience in the field of capital market investment and retirement planning. Upvest’s Investment API is easy to integrate so that fintechs and financial institutions can save resources and fully focus on their core business.

We are proud to partner with Europe’s leading Fintechs and financial institutions such as DKB, Revolut, N26 and Raisin. Our latest €105M funding round in March 2026 - led by Sapphire and Tencent - serves as a massive catalyst for our growth, allowing us to offer premier investment experience.

Что предстоит делать

Develop upvest's future trading infrastructure

You will drive the strategy to evolve and scale our trading infrastructure in line with our company strategy. You will own and support the decisions that shape it: which execution venues to connect in which markets, which assets and order types to support next, how to initiate post-trade, where to expand and, just as importantly, where not to. You will weigh client needs, market structure, cost, and regulatory requirements to place the right bets at the right time. The goal: a trading infrastructure that fits our clients' needs and growing expectations perfectly, built through fast, confident decisions and a focus on outcomes.

Accelerate our trading initiatives

You will drive the initiatives that matter to Upvest’s success: big trading bets, new venues and CCP connectivities, and the products and procedures our clients need to trade across Europe. You bring an up-to-date understanding of the European trading landscape (exchanges, MTFs and Systematic Internalisers, CCPs and their connectivity, and how it all links into clearing, settlement and custody) and use it to assess market places, their propositions, and where they are heading. You will develop our long-term trading infrastructure strategy and guide its execution: shaping trading and migration strategies for clients, running RfPs for potential partners where needed, and enabling the Product organisation to deliver the right results quickly.

Что вы приносите

- 4+ years in investment banking, product management, trading operations, at a financial institution or SaaS-company with the focus on trading infrastructure.

- Deep knowledge and understanding of market infrastructures, exchanges, CCPs and others, the connectivity options and current developments

- Proven ability to also understand client expectations and connect this to possibilities given by external trading infrastructures.

- Excellent written and verbal communication. You can write a crisp memo, highlighting different opportunities, and present to executives and boards.

- Proven ability to drive alignment and progress across teams without formal authority.
