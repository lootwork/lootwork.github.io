Frontend Software Engineer - Coinbase Card Team

As a Frontend Software Engineer on the Coinbase Card team within the Consumer Product Group, you'll build and ship the user-facing experiences behind Coinbase's card products, including the Coinbase One Card (credit) and the Coinbase Card (debit). This team is consolidating both products onto a unified card platform, and you'll join a small team with high ownership, greenfield feature work, and direct impact on how millions of customers discover, activate, and use their cards.

Что предстоит делать

  • Own end-to-end frontend development for core card experiences, including transactions, rewards, onboarding, and account management across web and mobile
  • Build new user-facing features - such as authorized card users, reward offers, real-time transaction status trackers
  • Partner with product, design, and backend engineering to scope, prioritize, and ship projects that reduce CX contact rates and improve the customer experience
  • Identify and resolve frontend performance bottlenecks across card UX journeys, driving measurable improvements in CUJ latency and availability SLOs

Required skills and experience

  • 5+ years of software engineering experience with deep expertise in React and React Native
  • Proven track record of shipping consumer-facing products end-to-end in a high-scale environment, with a focus on performance, reliability, and user experience
  • Experience owning SLOs and driving measurable improvements in latency and availability for user-facing flows
  • Demonstrated ability to mentor junior engineers and operate as a senior technical voice on a team of mixed levels
  • Utilizes generative AI responsibly, maintaining human oversight to deliver business-ready outputs and drive measurable improvements in workflow efficiency, cost, and quality
