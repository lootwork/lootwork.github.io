Blockchain is connecting the world to the future of finance. As the most trusted and fastest-growing global crypto company, it helps millions of people worldwide safely access cryptocurrency. Since its inception in 2011, Blockchain has earned the trust of over 90 million wallet holders and more than 40 million verified users, facilitating over $1 trillion in crypto transactions.

We’re looking for a Quantitative Trader to join our Market Making desk in London. In this role, you will help run, analyse, and develop the automated strategies that provide liquidity across digital asset exchanges and trading pairs. You will work directly with senior traders, quants and engineers, and take on strategy ownership as part of a compact and highly successful team.

You will sit at the intersection of trading, data and technology. This role suits someone with strong quantitative and coding skills, a real curiosity about how markets work, and the drive to grow into a fully fledged quantitative trader in a fast-moving 24/7 market.

Что предстоит делать

Market Making Operations

  • Research and develop market making strategies across CEX and DEX order books / AMM structures to be the most competitive market making desk we can be
  • Work with a successful trading team at the intersection of markets and one of the oldest crypto companies in the world
  • Monitor automated market-making strategies across multiple exchanges and trading pairs, flagging issues and escalating where needed
  • Learn how strategy parameters work, including spreads, quote sizes, inventory management and risk limits, and adjust them within agreed limits under senior guidance
  • Help troubleshoot live trading issues by working with traders and engineers to find root causes

Trading Analytics

  • Run post-trade analysis on execution quality, fill rates, inventory and profitability, and present your findings to the desk
  • Write Python and SQL queries to pull, clean and analyse trading and order book data
  • Help with backtesting and research into strategy improvements alongside traders and quantitative developers

Reporting & Automation

  • Build and maintain dashboards and reports that track trading KPIs such as uptime, spread, depth, volume and P&L
  • Use AI tools and automation to make routine monitoring, alerting and reporting more efficient
  • Suggest process improvements and new ideas to improve desk performance

Client & Internal Support

  • Prepare performance reports that show whether client strategies are meeting agreed KPIs
  • Contribute to market colour for clients and internal stakeholders, covering trading flows, liquidity conditions and price movements

WHAT YOU WILL NEED

  • Solid experience working in trading, quantitative analysis, data analysis or a related field; graduates and internship experience in trading or quant roles are welcome
  • Technical Skills: Working proficiency in Python and/or SQL; exposure to Rust or another systems language is a plus
  • Tools: Comfortable using AI tools, data visualisation and automation to improve workflows
  • Mindset: intense, and eager to learn
  • Communication: Ability to explain findings clearly to traders, and engineers
