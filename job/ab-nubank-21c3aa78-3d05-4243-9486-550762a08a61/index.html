О роли

The CC Factory, within Nubank’s Credit Card Business Unit, is responsible for internationalizing our credit card product and creating a reusable foundation for launching it in new markets. The team recently launched credit card capabilities in the United States and is building the frameworks, platforms and AI-enabled practices required to scale into future countries.

As a Staff Software Engineer, AI & Credit Card Platform, you will operate across multiple squads and technical domains, helping shape how credit card products adapt to new markets. This is a strongly cross-functional role involving Product, Engineering, Business, Legal, Compliance and platform partners.

You will combine deep technical leadership with hands-on execution: building reusable country-launch capabilities, driving AI transformation across the engineering lifecycle, and guiding architecture across three to four squads. This is a Staff-level role with potential to grow toward broader technical scope over time

YOU’LL BE RESPONSIBLE FOR

- Building the Country Launch Engine: Create reusable systems that reduce the time and effort required to launch credit card products in new markets, replacing country-specific documentation and repeated discovery with scalable capabilities.

- Automating country readiness: Help translate regulatory requirements and market capabilities into destination architecture and configurable product behavior.

- Driving AI transformation across CC Factory: Diagnose engineering friction, establish productivity baselines, run experiments, measure impact and scale the AI practices that work.

- Building domain-grounded AI systems: Develop context engineering foundations, domain skills and repository context so AI agents can support credit card configuration and international launches with reliable outputs.

- Defining AI risk and governance: Establish appropriate human-in-the-loop controls, evaluation, monitoring and auditability for AI used in regulated financial workflows.

- Leading technical architecture across multiple squads: Serve as a technical anchor across areas such as Collateral Lines, Cashback and adjacent credit card platforms, guiding architecture and unblocking complex work.

- Partnering across the organization: Align Product, Engineering, Business, Legal, Compliance and platform teams around technical direction, dependencies and launch priorities.

- Staying hands-on: Prototype, code and contribute directly when needed, while adapting quickly as business priorities and market opportunities evolve.
