Join FxPro, a leading international fintech company. Be part of our expanding international team, with offices in Limassol, London, Nassau, Dubai, and Yerevan. FxPro has over 600 employees representing 50 nationalities, which makes it an exciting and dynamic place to work. We see each team member as an integral part of our success story.

We are hiring Senior Business and Senior System Analysts across several teams. These include the trading platform behind our CFD and Invest products, order processing, and execution, and partner systems.

Обязанности

  • Gather, analyse, and validate requirements from business stakeholders
  • Own analysis through the full feature lifecycle, from discovery to delivery
  • Turn business needs into clear, development-ready documentation: specifications, user stories, use cases, and UML, BPMN, and sequence diagrams
  • Design and propose technical solutions that fit our business objectives and technology strategy
  • Describe system integrations and contracts between services, including REST APIs, gRPC, and message brokers
  • Analyse existing flows and legacy functionality, and document how they behave
  • Work closely with Product, Design, Development, QA, and Operations team
  • Own and continuously refine the technical documentation

Требования

  • At least 4 years of experience as a Business Analyst or System Analyst
  • Solid understanding of the SDLC
  • Proven record of writing documentation of detailed system specifications, user stories, use cases, and diagrams (UML, BPMN, sequence)
  • Analysis and documentation of requirements for backend and platform systems
  • Knowledge of web services, API types, and third-party integrations
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Strong time management, and the ability to onboard quickly into new domains and adapt to change
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
  • Fluent English

Будет плюсом

  • Background in the fintech industry, ideally CFD/Forex trading, with experience in client-facing trading platforms, admin tools, or back-office systems
  • Familiarity with API protocols (REST, WebSockets, gRPC, FIX) and message brokers (Kafka, RabbitMQ, Solace)
  • SQL and data skills, and experience with log analysis tools such as Graylog and Kibana
  • Knowledge of financial regulatory requirements
  • Understanding of UX/UI principles and CI/CD processes

Our benefits

  • Excellent compensation package
  • Medical insurance
  • Provident fund
  • In-house gym with a personal trainer
  • Free daily lunch catering, snacks, and beverages
  • Company discount card for various products & services
  • 21 days of annual leave and 10 days of sick leave annually
  • FxPro private shuttle bus service from Limassol
  • Birthday gift
  • Relocation bonus and visa/work permit support {for relo positions only}
  • Educational reimbursements & team building allowances
  • Monthly corporate events and activities
