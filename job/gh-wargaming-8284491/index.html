О вакансии

We are looking for an experienced Project Manager to join the World of Tanks team. In this role, you will collaborate closely with programmers, artists, designers, and other disciplines to ensure smooth production processes. You’ll be responsible for setting clear guidelines and success factors at every stage of development, balancing workloads, staying on top of issues, and proactively removing roadblocks to keep projects on track.

To succeed in this position, you should have solid knowledge of the game or software feature production pipeline. If you thrive in a fast-paced, multi-project environment and bring strong communication and people skills, we’d love to hear from you.

Reports to

PMD Director

Что предстоит делать

  • Create and maintain schedules to be followed and ensure a strong sense of ownership and accountability for each team member’s contributions
  • Set, manage, monitor and report on milestones for team members. Making sure all of the necessary greenlight passed successfully
  • Provide regular reports on team progress to multiple levels of management, share knowledge and experience to aid and improve integration process
  • Balance workload for some of the departments
  • Coordinate day-to-day operations, remove roadblocks in production activities
  • Collaborate on a corporate level with other studios based in Vilnius, Prague, Kyiv and Nicosia

Кого мы ищем

  • 3+ years of experience coordinating production activities within international teams
  • Solid understanding of project management methodologies (e.g., Agile, Waterfall) and risk management best practices
  • Extensive knowledge of tech pipelines, DLC, and related terminology
  • Hands-on experience with JIRA, MS Project, or other planning/project management tools
  • Experience collaborating with cross-functional and external teams on feature design and implementation

Что поможет выделиться

  • Passion for the video game industry
  • Experience driving process changes or implementing new workflows at a large team level

Формат работы

  • Hybrid (2-3 days of work from the office)

The salary range for this role in Vilnius is EUR 3800-5200 monthly gross.

О Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming's titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
