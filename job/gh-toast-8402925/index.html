Toast creates technology to help restaurants and local businesses succeed in a digital world, helping business owners operate, increase sales, engage customers, and keep employees happy.

Toast serves hundreds of thousands of restaurant customers. Those customers depend on our platform to operate successful businesses, deliver a fantastic guest experience, and provide their employees the tools to do their best work. The Vision AI team’s mission is to leverage video to accomplish all of these goals.

As a Senior Principal Product Manager for Vision AI Experiences, you will be a founding member of our team. You’ll be responsible for understanding the needs of Toast customers and creating Vision AI products that solve those problems and capitalize on their opportunities. The Vision AI experiences you’ll deliver will be across existing and new Toast products. You’ll operate at the strategy level all the way through to technical implementation. You’ll partner with teams across Toast to bring our product to market, validate product-market fit, and scale across our customer base.

A day in the life (Responsibilities)

  • Own the Vision AI product vision and strategy. Define the long-term roadmap for vision experiences within Toast products.
  • Drive zero-to-one product development. Lead discovery, prototyping, pilots, commercialization, and scaling of new Vision AI experiences.
  • Lead customer research and field validation. Spend time with Toast customers and in restaurants understanding operational workflows, validating solutions, and translating insights into product strategy.
  • Drive commercialization and adoption. Partner with GTM teams on packaging, positioning, pricing inputs, pilots, and rollout strategies that drive customer outcomes.
  • Lead across the organization. Influence Engineering, Design, Hardware Operations, Security, Legal, Customer Care, Sales, Product Marketing, and senior leadership to align teams around strategy and execution.
  • Embrace AI-first product development. Use AI tools to accelerate discovery and execution while identifying new ways AI can create value for restaurants and guests.

What you'll need to thrive (Requirements)

  • Product leadership experience. 8+ years of Product Management experience owning complex products, significant product areas, or multi-team initiatives from strategy through scale.
  • Zero-to-one leadership. Proven ability to take ambiguous opportunities from discovery through prototype, pilot, commercialization, and scale.
  • Technical fluency. Ability to partner deeply with Engineering teams on architecture, integrations, AI systems, and technical trade-offs.
  • Strategic decision making. Ability to balance customer impact, performance, scalability, operational complexity, development investment, and economics when defining product direction.
  • Customer obsession. Experience conducting deep customer discovery, field research, and pilots with strategic customers.
  • UX craftsmanship. Experience partnering closely with design to deliver elegant user experiences that simplify feature discovery, minimize cognitive load, and are a pleasure to use.
  • Leadership and influence. Strong communication skills and demonstrated ability to align senior stakeholders and cross-functional teams around a product vision.
  • Special Sauce (Nice To haves)
  • Experience with computer vision, camera systems, video analytics, or machine learning products, especially real-time video.
  • Experience building products that combine software and hardware.
  • Familiarity with AI model evaluation, experimentation, and performance measurement.
  • Experience in restaurant, retail, hospitality, or other operationally complex environments.
  • Technical background or deep experience partnering with Engineering on architecture, AI infrastructure, or hardware/software integrations.
