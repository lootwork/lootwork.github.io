We are seeking a Marketing Program Manager to join the Marketing team at Robinhood. This is a crucial role for enhancing & scaling our marketing capabilities — ensuring that our marketers, producers, creatives, and agency partners are set up for success in delivering the best work possible for our customers.

This role is based in our New York, NY office, with in-person attendance expected at least 3 days per week.

At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.

Что предстоит делать

  • Manage all internal workstreams across Marketing, Creative, Legal and Compliance teams to ensure cohesion for Brokerage related work.
  • Facilitate meetings to ensure information exchange and understanding across teams - creating and owning agendas, action items, and follow ups.
  • Supports Marketers to ensure all aspects of the program are on track, and flag delays or facilitate trade off discussions when necessary.
  • Manage a high volume of marketing assets and owns management of asset timelines in partnership with the producer.
  • Build and maintain marketing playbooks, trackers and templates.
  • Own the submission process for Legal & Compliance review, spearheading weekly check-ins, and ensuring materials are revised where necessary.
  • Lead QA Process of all assets, ensuring quality standards are met and work is complete.
  • Supports reporting cadence and facilitates retros, consolidating feedback and implementing optimizations for future work.
  • Collaborates with product-specific MPMs to ensure proper product representation when necessary.

Что вы приносите

  • 8+ years of experience in program and/ or project management.
  • Strong preference for a candidate that’s previously worked in a high velocity Events Marketing environment, and has previously worked in a highly regulated industry.
  • Demonstrate excellent communication, organizational, and time-management skills.
  • Able to organize information quickly and at a high level in order to address potential roadblocks, define the best way forward, and communicate trade-offs where appropriate.
  • Strong analytical skills - can analyze and interpret data to inform marketing decisions and identify risk in projects and programs early so that mitigation steps can be taken.
  • Experience operating and driving work independently in a dynamic environment, and comfortable navigating ambiguity.
  • Understanding of asset creation (i.e. Creative, Production, etc.) and approval cycles (Stakeholder Reviews, Legal Approvals, etc.).
  • Ideal candidate can build positive relationships with stakeholders.
  • Passion for democratizing finance and a deep understanding of Robinhood's mission, products, and target customers.
