О нас

GoCardless, a Mollie company, is a global leader in bank payments. Over 100,000 businesses, from start-ups to household names, use GoCardless to collect, manage and send bank payments through Direct Debit, real-time payments and open banking. With US$130bn+ processed annually across 30+ countries, we handle recurring and one-off payments without the chasing, stress, or expensive fees. Our end-to-end payment platform also features AI-powered solutions to improve payment success and reduce fraud, alongside connections to over 350 platforms businesses use everyday.

We are headquartered in the UK, with teams and operations spanning North America, Europe and Asia-Pacific. For more information, please visit www.gocardless.com and follow us on LinkedIn @GoCardless.

Mollie is the leading payments and financial services partner for business, rooted in Europe, with global reach.

О роли

We are in search of a dynamic and talented person to join us as an Integration Engineer. You will have the opportunity to grow within the developers' team and to evolve your skills in IT development or any other department.

We are looking for people that are passionate about what they do and don't come to work simply because they are paid to do so. If you enjoy hard problems to solve, value learning and want to create an impact, then this is a place for you.

What excites you

  • Dive into API documentation: Explore and review APIs, gaining a deep understanding of their intricacies. Contribute to seamless integrations with cutting-edge technologies.
  • Shape integration future: Create integrations adhering to predefined structures, ensuring smooth interactions between systems for an enhanced user experience.
  • Learn Python programming: Perfect your Python skills, writing clean, efficient, and scalable code. Be at the forefront of delivering high-quality solutions to complex problems.
  • Be a testing champion: Meticulously test and ensure system reliability and functionality. Maintain our high standards and deliver exceptional products to customers.
  • Tackle technical hurdles: Put your problem-solving abilities to the test, finding innovative solutions. Demonstrate resourcefulness in keeping our systems running smoothly.

What excites us

  • Build on your programming foundation: Join us to learn Python programming, no previous experience required! Expand your skill set and dive into a versatile language with our support.
  • Express yourself fluently: Strong written and verbal English skills are essential for effective communication within our collaborative team. Grow alongside talented individuals who share your passion for programming.
  • Fuel your passion for programming: At GoCardless, we value your enthusiasm. Bring your passion to the table and actively contribute to our projects. This is where your love for coding will be nurtured and celebrated.
  • Embrace your organisational skills: Be empowered to be organised. Take ownership of your projects, thrive, and excel in our supportive and flexible environment.
