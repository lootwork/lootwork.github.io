О нас

Riskified empowers businesses to unleash ecommerce growth by taking risk off the table. Many of the world’s biggest brands and publicly traded companies selling online rely on Riskified for guaranteed protection against chargebacks, to fight fraud and policy abuse at scale, and to improve customer retention. О роли

The Account Affinity team owns Riskified’s Identity domain. We build the systems that figure out who is really behind an order: linking accounts, devices, emails, and payment details into a single view of each customer across thousands of eCommerce merchants.

Our main product, Identity Engine, resolves and clusters identities in near real time and feeds them into Riskified’s fraud and risk decisions. We also build data platform services that give other teams a richer view of each customer.

Что предстоит делать

  • Build identity infrastructure: Design, build, and run high-throughput services that resolve and cluster identities in near real time.
  • Build data platforms: Develop large-scale data pipelines and the APIs that expose them to teams across Riskified.
  • Work across teams: Partner with Product, Data Science, and consuming teams to turn their requirements into clear, well-defined data contracts and services.
  • Own production: Ship continuously, and take end-to-end ownership of the reliability, performance, and data correctness of your services.
  • Use modern workflows: Use AI coding assistants and agentic tools day to day to move faster and raise code quality.
  • Shape the roadmap: Contribute to team planning, design reviews, and technical direction.

Требования

  • B.Sc. in Computer Science or equivalent experience
  • 3+ years of hands-on backend development
  • Proven experience building distributed systems and microservices on cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP)
  • Experience with streaming or event-driven architectures (Kafka or similar)
  • Experience with a JVM language (Scala, Java, or Kotlin). Scala is an advantage
  • Experience with large-scale data processing (Spark, Databricks, or similar) is an advantage
  • Experience with graph data, entity resolution, or identity systems is an advantage
  • Background in fraud, risk, fintech, or eCommerce is an advantage
  • Comfortable using AI coding assistants and agentic tools in daily work
  • Independent learner, proactive problem solver, and a strong team player
