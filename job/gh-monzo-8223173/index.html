⭐ Our Treasury team

Our Treasury team is growing and we’re looking for a Treasury Analyst with a focus on commercial performance to support our investment strategy. We are looking for an enthusiastic, detail orientated & collaborative Treasury Analyst to play a key role in supporting Treasury’s budgeting, stress testing, month end, and modelling. You’ll support all core Treasury functions- learning how Monzo manages liquidity, capital, interest rate risk, and the credit portfolio to maintain balance sheet resilience. This is a collaborative role and you will work across the Treasury, Finance and Risk functions.

🔑 You’ll play a key role by…

  • Supporting and enhancing our budgeting and forecasting process including stress testing and model development
  • Supporting our month end reporting including cost analysis and reporting
  • Enhancing our control environment
  • Designing brilliant ways to automate our work and leveraging technology to ensure we scale with the business
  • Partnering with teams internally to help them make informed business decisions

🤩 We’d love to hear from you if you…

  • Have experience working at a bank and knowledge of Treasury forecasting
  • Have a highly analytical and forward thinking approach
  • Are interested in identifying and using data, performing analysis, and understanding financial impacts on our bank
  • Are able to articulate and explain technical topics to diverse audiences
  • Have strong technical skills
  • Are interested in learning more about the wider Treasury worlds of investment and capital management
