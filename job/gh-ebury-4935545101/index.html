Engineering Manager - Money Flows Team

Porto Office - Hybrid: 4 days in the office, 1 day working from home

Ebury is a Global FinTech: we apply new technologies to enhance and automate financial services and processes. This allows small and medium-sized businesses to trade and transact internationally by eliminating boundaries related to more traditional.

О роли

We are seeking a highly motivated and experienced Engineering Manager to lead a team of talented engineers in the development and maintenance of our cutting-edge financial technology platform.

As an Engineering Manager, you will play a critical role in shaping the technical direction of your domain, ensuring the delivery of high-quality solutions that meet the needs of our customers. You will foster a culture of collaboration, innovation, and excellence while driving continuous improvement and professional growth within your team.

Key Responsibilities

Technical Leadership

  • Domain Ownership: Own a specific domain/subdomain, defining and setting the 0–6 month technical roadmap.
  • Architectural Oversight: Partner with senior engineers to ensure long-term architectural consistency.
  • System Integrity: Take accountability for the reliability, availability, and security of our systems.
  • Operational Excellence: Ensure all systems have appropriate support, SLAs, on-call rotas, and comprehensive documentation.

Delivery Management

  • Execution: Responsible for team delivery, including sprint planning and monitoring overall project health.
  • Flow Optimization: Assess the criticality of unplanned work to minimize context-switching and unnecessary pressure.
  • Capacity Planning: Balance team bandwidth with roadmap demands and business priorities.
  • Quality at Speed: Instill a sense of urgency while maintaining a healthy balance between velocity and code quality.
  • Incident Response: Ensure all incidents are owned and resolved efficiently.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

  • Product Partnership: Act as the primary liaison between Engineering and Product, ensuring full alignment on goals and strategy.
  • Communication: Proactively communicate progress and escalate blockers or risks effectively to stakeholders.

People Management

  • Team Stability: Lead and stabilize a team of engineers across various seniority levels.
  • Growth & Coaching: Provide active coaching, regular performance reviews, and clear career pathing.
  • Culture: Foster an environment of extreme ownership, psychological safety, and radical feedback.
  • Talent Acquisition: Demonstrate a proven ability to hire, grow, and retain high-performing engineering talent.

Process & Performance

  • Execution: Ensure company processes are executed effectively and in a timely manner.
  • Metrics-Driven: Accountable for team KPIs, including velocity, quality, on-time delivery, morale, and staffing.
  • Budgeting: Manage limited budgets for tooling or specific project-related costs.

Требования

  • Leadership Experience: Proven track record as an Engineering Manager or in a similar leadership role supporting engineers.
  • FinTech Expertise: Previous experience specifically within the payments industry is highly preferred.
  • Technical Depth: Deep understanding of software development methodologies, agile processes, and industry best practices.
  • Soft Skills: Excellent interpersonal, communication, and decision-making abilities.
  • Mindset: A strong passion for technology, innovation, and solving complex problems.

Что мы предлагаем

  • Compensation: Competitive salary and comprehensive benefits package.
  • Impact: The opportunity to work on challenging and rewarding projects in the FinTech space.
  • Environment: A collaborative, supportive, and high-energy work culture.
  • Growth: Be a key player in a dynamic and rapidly growing company.

О нас

Ebury delivers sophisticated, integrated solutions — business accounts, hedging, and financing — on a single platform with a seamless workflow. Our success is built on a simple premise and singular purpose: To help businesses operate and scale globally.

Since its founding in 2009, Ebury has always been a fast-growing leader in fintech. Today, we bring together 2000+ Eburians across nearly 70 cities and we’re always looking to add to our team.

At the heart of our offering is a proprietary platform, purpose-built to help businesses seamlessly streamline and manage global cash flow. We focus on continuous product evolution and innovation to build the infrastructure for borderless growth and help our clients scale at every stage.

The opportunities at Ebury are as diverse as our people, ranging from business development to engineering roles across our tech pillars.
