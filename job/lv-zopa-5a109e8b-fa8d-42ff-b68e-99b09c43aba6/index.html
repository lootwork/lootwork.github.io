О роли

As a Financial Crime Specialist, you will protect Zopa and its customers by investigating complex transaction monitoring activity, making clear and well-evidenced decisions, and escalating suspicion appropriately. You will apply sound judgement where automation is not appropriate, ensuring our controls remain effective, proportionate and customer-focused.

This role is central to how we’re evolving financial crime at Zopa. You’ll combine investigative expertise with the assurance of AI-enabled tools, challenging AI outputs, identifying control gaps and helping improve the quality and consistency of decisions across Financial Crime Operations.

Working pattern

This is a hybrid role, with 3 days per week in the office. You’ll work 7.5-hour shifts between 8am and 8pm, Monday to Friday.

You’ll also work one full weekend every four weeks, working remotely from 9am to 5:30pm, with weekdays off in lieu.

Some bank holiday working will also be required throughout the year, with time off in lieu provided for any bank holidays worked.

A DAY IN THE LIFE

Investigate and make risk-based decisions

  • Review and investigate Transaction Monitoring alerts across Zopa products, identifying activity linked to money laundering, fraud, terrorist financing and other relevant financial crime typologies.
  • Take ownership of complex cases from investigation through to decision, using contextual analysis, linked-account insights and appropriate risk mitigation.
  • Make timely, proportionate and well-evidenced decisions, including account restrictions and escalations where required.
  • Escalate suspicious activity in line with internal procedures and relevant legal and regulatory obligations.

Assure AI and strengthen our controls

  • Use AI-enabled monitoring tools in your day-to-day casework, critically assessing alerts, summaries and recommendations rather than accepting outputs at face value.
  • Assess whether AI-led decisions and customer journeys are delivering the right financial crime and customer outcomes, identifying and escalating quality, risk or control concerns.
  • Share insights from investigations to help improve monitoring rules, AI performance, customer questioning and operational controls.

Deliver strong customer and operational outcomes

  • Maintain clear, accurate investigation records and rationales, documenting the evidence considered, decisions made and risks mitigated.
  • Handle inbound and outbound telephone and chat conversations with professionalism and empathy, including conversations with customers whose accounts have been suspended.
  • Collaborate across Financial Crime, Fraud, Customer Support, Product and Risk to progress investigations, resolve issues and improve customer outcomes.

О вас

  • Proven experience investigating complex Transaction Monitoring and/or mule activity cases end to end, including making alert decisions and escalating suspicious activity where appropriate.
  • A strong interest in technology-led environments, with an understanding of how AI can support day-to-day decision-making and improve outcomes.
  • A sound understanding of UK financial crime requirements, including POCA, the Money Laundering Regulations and FCA expectations.
  • Strong analytical judgement, with the ability to interpret complex information, identify relevant risks and produce clear, evidence-based rationales.
  • Confidence in challenging AI-generated summaries, recommendations and decisions, while taking accountability for the final outcome.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to handle sensitive customer conversations with care and professionalism.
  • The ability to manage competing priorities in a fast-paced environment without compromising decision quality or control standards.
  • A good understanding of retail banking products, including payments, savings and investment transactions.

DESIRABLE

  • Experience investigating complex Mule activity, linked-account networks and suspicious payment behaviour.
  • Experience using AI-enabled tools to support financial crime investigations or operational decisioning.
  • ACAMS, ICA or an equivalent financial crime qualification.
