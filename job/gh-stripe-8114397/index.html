Кто мы

О Stripe

Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.

О our organization

The Growth organization drives Stripe's self-serve growth engine: from a user's first interaction with Stripe through onboarding, activation, and expansion across our products. Growth focuses on building the systems and surfaces that help any user find, understand, and adopt the right set of Stripe products, regardless of how or where they start.

Within Growth, the onboarding program is how new and existing users get to their first successful use of Stripe as quickly and completely as possible. A cohesive onboarding experience is critical to Stripe's ability to convert prospects, activate new products, and compete against increasingly sophisticated alternatives.

Onboarding spans several interconnected surfaces

  • Acquisition and intent capture — first-touch experiences that match prospects to the right products and entry points.
  • Account setup and activation — guided flows that get users live with the right integration shape.
  • In-product discovery — contextual surfaces that help new and existing users adopt additional products over time.

These surfaces are deeply interdependent, span dozens of cross-functional teams, and directly shape Stripe's new user activation and product attach rates.

Что предстоит делать

  • We are seeking a product-focused Technical Program Manager to drive Stripe's onboarding programs. This is a high-impact role at the intersection of growth strategy, product management, and cross-organizational program ownership.
  • In this role, you will own one of the most strategically consequential programs in Growth, driving the direction, health, and outcomes of Stripe's onboarding experience end-to-end.
  • You will set the program strategy in partnership with Growth leadership, holding organizations accountable to the success of the program.
  • Drive cross-org decisions across DRIs in Growth, Dashboard, Docs, Marketing, and product teams actively resolving the hard, ambiguous integration and ownership questions that fall between team boundaries.
  • Influence and shape product and engineering roadmaps bringing a program-level perspective to team planning cycles, and advocating for cross-cutting investments that no single team will prioritize on their own.
