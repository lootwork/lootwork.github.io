ABOUT YOU

We are looking for a Product Manager to own and develop the Quest Platform — a major company initiative that consolidates all quest-related functionality in one place and integrates it across all Xsolla products. This includes quests within products such as Web Shop, Xsolla Player, and Cloud Gaming. You will work closely with Engineering, Design, Marketing, and cross-product teams to create engaging quest experiences, drive user engagement, and ensure seamless integration across all Xsolla products.

ABOUT US

Xsolla is a global commerce company with robust tools and services to help developers solve the inherent challenges of the video game industry. From indie to AAA, companies partner with Xsolla to help them fund, distribute, market, and monetize their games. Grounded in the belief in the future of video games, Xsolla is resolute in the mission to bring opportunities together, and continually make new resources available to creators. Headquartered and incorporated in Los Angeles, California, Xsolla operates as the merchant of record and has helped over 1,500+ game developers to reach more players and grow their businesses around the world. With more paths to profits and ways to win, developers have all the things needed to enjoy the game.

For more information, visit xsolla.com.

RESPONSIBILITIES:

  • Own the product vision, roadmap, and execution for the Quest Platform
  • Create and manage new quests within the platform, acting as its primary advocate
  • Oversee quest UI implementation
  • Define and track key product metrics (engagement, completion rates, retention impact)
  • Translate business and user needs into clear product requirements
  • Collaborate cross-functionally to ensure smooth integration with Web Shop, Xsolla Player, Cloud Gaming, and future products
  • Continuously optimize quest experiences based on data and user feedback

QUALIFICATIONS & SKILLS:

  • Experience as a Product Manager working on engagement, gamification, or loyalty products
  • Strong understanding of gaming ecosystems (mobile, PC, console)
  • Ability to work with cross-functional teams and manage complex dependencies
  • Data-driven mindset with experience defining and tracking product KPIs

Nice to Have:

  • Engineering background
  • Background in mobile gaming or game studios
  • Experience with loyalty programs, gamification, or rewards systems
  • Experience building platform products that integrate across multiple services
