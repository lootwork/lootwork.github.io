At 11 bit studios, we're looking for an Expert Technical Animator who enjoys working at the intersection of animation and technology. You'll have the opportunity to design and develop animation systems in Unreal Engine, tackle technical challenges and help bring characters, creatures and machines to life.

Hybrid work in our Warsaw office is preferred for this role.

What will you be doing on a day-to-day basis?

  • Creating gameplay animations for characters, machines and animals, including locomotion, traversal, melee and ranged combat, interactions and narrative sequences.
  • Working with both keyframe animation and motion capture data.
  • Implementing and iterating on animations in Unreal Engine.
  • Collaborating with designers, artists and other team members to develop animation solutions and pipelines for the project.
