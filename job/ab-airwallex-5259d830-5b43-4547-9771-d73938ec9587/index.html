О AIRWALLEX

Airwallex is the AI-native financial operating system for a real-time, intelligent economy. More than 675,000 businesses, including McLaren Racing, Qantas, SHEIN, and TikTok, use us, directly or through our platform partners, to run their financial operations or build and monetize financial products of their own.

We started in Melbourne in 2015 to build the infrastructure global commerce runs on. We're the regulated backbone behind global payments: not by accident, but by design. A decade plus, 85+ licenses, and a financial infrastructure spanning North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

We're co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore, with more than 2,700 people across 27 offices. We hire builders with founder-level energy, people who move fast with good judgment, dig in with real curiosity, and make calls from first principles rather than waiting to be told what to do. Read our operating principles https://www.airwallex.com/en-us/operating-principles to see it in full.

HOW YOU'LL MAKE IMPACT

Lending is building the systems that help businesses access capital with speed and confidence. You'll lead engineering execution from roadmap to launch, shaping both the product and the technical foundations that support credit decisioning, underwriting, fund flows, servicing and collections.

- Own engineering execution for Lending, turning the roadmap into shipped products by planning, scoping, sequencing, unblocking, launching and iterating. Hold yourself accountable for outcomes, not just output.

- Shape the product with Product and Design by deciding what to build and what to cut, bringing your point of view on customer problems, credit economics and the metrics that matter.

- Set technical direction for resilient, high-throughput services across credit decisioning, underwriting, fund-flow orchestration, servicing and collections. Stay close to the architecture and step in hands-on when it counts.

- Balance speed and safety in a regulated domain by shipping incrementally, building controls in from the start, and making Risk and Compliance part of the delivery loop rather than a gate at the end.

- Build and grow the team by hiring, coaching and managing engineers, setting clear expectations and creating a culture of ownership. Scale the team's leverage as the business grows, including through AI-assisted development.

- Raise the bar on reliability through strong practices for testing, observability, incident response and security, so the platform scales across markets without breaking.

- Partner across Product, Data Science, Risk, Compliance, Finance, Sales and engineering leadership across APAC, EMEA and the US. Surface risks early and drive decisions.

You’ll work from our Singapore office five days a week.

Кого мы ищем

ESSENTIALS

- 8+ years of software engineering experience, including 2+ years leading engineers as a people manager, and a bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering or a related field.

- A track record of owning delivery of complex, business-critical products in a fast-growing environment, from ambiguity through to launch.

- Strong product judgement. You can weigh customer needs, commercial impact and risk, and prioritise what moves the needle.

- Strong backend engineering fundamentals, with experience in Java or Kotlin and Spring Boot, or strong transferable experience in another backend language and framework.

- Experience designing and operating high-throughput, highly available distributed systems, with domain-driven design, event-driven architectures and RESTful APIs.

- Experience with GCP or AWS, Docker and Kubernetes, and with observability tooling such as Splunk, Grafana and Prometheus.

- Proven ability to hire, mentor and develop engineers, and to build a culture of accountability and continuous improvement.

- Excellent communication and stakeholder management across Product, Risk, Compliance and executive teams.

Желательно

- Experience in lending, credit, fintech, payments or other regulated domains.

- Exposure to credit decisioning, underwriting, ledgers, collections or embedded finance.

- Experience building SaaS products offered as a platform.

- Experience scaling an engineering team through hyper-growth, and using AI tools to raise team velocity.

YOU'LL THRIVE HERE IF

- You have high agency. You own the outcome and you don't wait for permission to fix what's broken.

- You're comfortable owning the roadmap yourself and making calls with incomplete information.

- You're pragmatic. You balance quality, speed, and business impact, and you ship.

- You're a technical leader who builds, stays close to the details, and leads by example.

- You connect dots across functions and simplify complexity rather than hiding behind it.

- You're comfortable operating across time zones with a globally distributed team, including colleagues based in APAC and EMEA.

- You value in-person collaboration and are happy being in the office five days a week.

LEARN MORE ABOUT YOUR TEAM

Lending at Airwallex brings together flexible capital and financial infrastructure so fast-growing businesses can scale with confidence. We own the end-to-end credit platform: the backend systems, risk engines, and integrations that power business credit products and embedded financing. The challenge is balancing speed with safety, building resilient, high-throughput services that assess creditworthiness in real time, orchestrate fund flows seamlessly, and scale reliably across global markets. This is a team for product-minded engineers who want ownership and enjoy solving complex technical problems. If you want to help redefine the financial infrastructure that lets businesses around the world grow, this is where that work happens.
