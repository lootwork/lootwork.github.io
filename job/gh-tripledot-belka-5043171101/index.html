Кто мы

Tripledot Studios is one of the largest independent mobile games companies in the world.

We are a multi-award-winning organisation, with a global 2,500+ strong team across 12 studios.

Our expanded portfolio includes some of the biggest titles in mobile gaming, collectively reaching top chart positions around the world and engaging over 25 million daily active users.

Tripledot’s guiding principle is that when people love what they do, what they do will be loved by others.

We’re building a company we’re proud of. One filled with driven, incredibly smart and detail-orientated people, who LOVE making games.

Our ambition is to be the most successful games company in the world, and we’re just getting started.

О роли

We're looking for a Senior Technical Product Manager to own a backend product domain within our central platform: the technology that powers, configures and personalises our games across every title and studio.

You'll join a team of Technical Product Managers who act as an enabling function for the whole company. The services, APIs and back-office tools you own are used every day by game teams, studios and live-ops. Your job is to understand what they need, turn it into scalable solutions that work across many teams, and see them through to delivery and adoption.

AI is central to how we work. We're looking for someone who already uses it to move faster and think bigger, and who will help us keep raising the bar.

Основные обязанности

  • Own the roadmap for your backend domain, balancing priorities and trade-offs across studios, product and platform.
  • Partner with Studio Product Managers, fellow TPMs, Engineering and Delivery to gather requirements and use cases, and shape them into generic, scalable solutions.
  • Write clear, well-structured epics and user stories that give engineers the context they need to build the right thing.
  • Work closely with the team's Technical Project Manager on delivery – backlog refinement, sprint planning, reviews and retros – keeping scope, risks and dependencies on track.
  • Design intuitive back-office experiences through mockups, wireframes and interactive prototypes.
  • Work through API contracts, SDK integrations, JSON configurations and data models, and map systems with flowcharts and sequence diagrams.
  • Bring AI into every stage of the product lifecycle, from discovery and specs to prototyping and analysis, and share what works with the wider team.
  • Plan integrations and rollouts with other teams, then track adoption and usage to keep improving the tools.

Required Skills, Knowledge and Expertise

AI Proficiency (Essential)

  • You use AI tools such as Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor every day across your product work: research, specs, user stories, analysis and documentation.
  • You use AI to build working prototypes and proofs of concept quickly.
  • You understand the strengths and limits of LLMs, and can spot where AI can improve our tools and processes.
  • You can share concrete examples of how AI has changed the way you work.

Product Management

  • 5+ years of product management experience, ideally with backend, platform or internal tools products.
  • Excellent stakeholder management, with a track record of aligning multiple teams around a shared roadmap.
  • Strong experience building roadmaps, gathering requirements and writing user stories.
  • Hands-on experience with Scrum/Agile delivery and Jira or similar tools.

Technical Skills

  • A solid grasp of technical concepts such as APIs, SDKs, services and data flows.
  • Confidence reading technical documentation, JSON files and ERDs.
  • The ability to create clear flowcharts and sequence diagrams.
  • An understanding of how data structures and system architecture shape tools and operations.

UX and Prototyping

  • Experience creating mockups, wireframes and prototypes for back-office tools, with a strong eye for usability.

О вас

  • A genuine team player who enjoys collaborating and sharing knowledge.
  • An excellent communicator who can explain complex topics clearly to technical and non-technical audiences.
  • Professional-level English.
  • You love games, and you play them yourself.

Strong advantage

  • Experience in casual mobile gaming, such as puzzle, card or word games. This is a big plus.
  • Familiarity with game backend systems such as game configuration, live ops, personalisation or A/B testing.
  • A background in software development.
