О Binance Accelerator Program

Binance Accelerator Program (BAP) is a 3-6 month internship program designed for Early Career talent to have firsthand experience in the rapidly expanding digital assets space. You will be given the opportunity to develop your skills at Binance and understand what it’s like to work at the world's leading blockchain ecosystem. As part of your internship in the BAP, there will also be opportunities for networking and development, which will expand your professional network and build transferable skills to propel you forward in your career. Learn about the BAP Program HERE.

Who May Apply

Current university students and recent graduates.

*Terms of employment/ engagement shall be subject to contract and local applicable laws.

Что предстоит делать

  • Provide operational support for marketing initiatives and communications, among other program tasks.
  • Monitor marketing performance using data analysis and user feedback to ensure campaign effectiveness and user experience.
  • Assist in the tracking of competitor updates and community feedback.
  • Creating educational content including but not limited to blog posts, emails, video scripts and social posts.
  • Maintain tutorial content to make sure new updates are reflected timely.
  • Assist in preparing educational campaigns such as webinars and community events.

Кого мы ищем

  • Fresh graduate or final-year student in Business, Marketing, Finance, Economics, or a related field.
  • Fundamental to immediate knowledge in digital marketing (growth campaign management, content writing, SEO, GEO, paid ads, PR, etc.) is a bonus.
  • Ability to turn data and information into actionable insights and drive execution.
  • Ability to handle a fast-paced environment and multiple priorities simultaneously.
  • Strong ownership and accountability for the assigned tasks.
  • Strong communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Bilingual English/Mandarin is required to be able to coordinate with overseas partners and stakeholders.
  • Proficiency with Excel (Pivot, Vlookup) / Google Sheets / other data analysis tools.
  • Minimum 4 days per week commitment, for at least 6 months internship term.

Будет плюсом

  • Demonstrated use of AI tools to build workflows and improve personal productivity.
  • Strong interest and knowledge about crypto, blockchain technology, and the Binance ecosystem are desirable.
  • Previous experience in start-up or marketing agency is a plus.

Why Binance

  • Shape the future of finance
  • Collaborate with world-class talent in a user-centric global organization with a flat structure
  • Tackle challenging projects in a fast-paced and innovative enviornment with autonomy
  • Thrive in a results-driven workplace with opportunities for career growth and continuous learning
  • Competitive salary and company benefits
  • Remote-first, work-from-home arrangement (may vary depending on the nature of the role and team)
