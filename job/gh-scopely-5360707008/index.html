Role Summary

As a Senior Software Engineer, you will play a key role in developing and maintaining the game’s Unity client across mobile game codebase. You will contribute to high-impact initiatives spanning slots games, live-ops features events, client infrastructure, platform integrations, performance, build reliability and maintainability. This role is ideal for an engineer who combines strong technical execution with sound architectural judgment in a complex production environment.

What You’ll Do

  • Design, build, and maintain major client systems across gameplay, UI, live events, feature progressions, sales & offers, etc.
  • Contribute to the architecture of a large Unity/C# codebase with an emphasis on modularity, testability, performance, and long-term maintainability.
  • Partner closely with design, product, tech-art, QA, analytics, economy, backend, and live-ops stakeholders to deliver scalable client solutions aligned with roadmap and operational needs.
  • Improve engineering effectiveness through strong coding practices, tooling, CI/build processes, code reviews, debugging workflows, and technical documentation.
  • Investigate and resolve production issues across mobile client systems, platform integrations, monetization flows, and live event features.
  • Mentor junior and mid-level engineers, contribute to team technical decisions, and help raise the overall quality bar for the engineering organization.

What We’re Looking For

  • 5+ years of experience with mobile and Unity3D platforms and ecosystems.
  • Strong problem solving skills, with solid experience in C# programming and computer science fundamentals.
  • Ability to write well structured, efficient and maintainable code while following programming best practices.
  • Experience in building and maintaining client systems for gameplay, UI, event systems and data driven features.
  • Strong debugging and profiling skills across runtime behaviour, performance, memory usage and production stability.
  • Experience with version control systems such as Git, along with testing strategies and quality practices for medium to large codebases.
  • Familiarity with mobile native languages such as Swift, Objective C, Java and C++, with a strong passion for mobile games. Experience or hands-on use of AI tools are considered an advantage.
  • Good communication skills and passionate about mobile games and game development.
